# 11. TikTok and short video

## Is TikTok the Right Channel? Product Fit, Price and Audience

### Decide Whether TikTok Fits Your Store

- Set up the TikTok account as its own step in the store checklist, and test TikTok ads only after the store is ready. [L1288.007]
- Run a niche store that sells one product. General stores do not perform on TikTok ads, and niche stores selling one product perform best. [L1376.001]
- Do not expect organic social growth to produce sales. One store grew to about 1,500 to 1,600 Instagram followers and over 2,000 TikTok followers within 30 days with zero sales, while paid advertising drives sales. [L0549.043]
- Pay for ads only after a product has sales and profit to reinvest. [L1380.058]
- In the early stage with small budgets, do not split spend onto TikTok ads. Put the budget into Google and Meta, which deliver most of the results for this model. (Source A, 2025) [L0667.005]
- TikTok ads had not been tested for high-ticket drop-shipping in the lesson's experience, so the team focused its time on Google and Facebook. (Source A, 2020, older) [L0468.126]
- Short-form video on Instagram Reels and TikTok was not used for e-commerce marketing at that time, so budget went into Google and Facebook first. (Source A, 2021, older; the two identical units are merged) [L0671.095, L0671.232]

### Account Setup and Ban Risk

- TikTok ad accounts are generally easier to open and carry less account-ban risk than Meta accounts. [L0637.063]
- TikTok has a low barrier to account creation, fewer ban issues than Meta, and better support, with human replies to tickets. [L0296.021]
- Facebook has more targeting power than TikTok, so creative and audience drive results differently on each platform. [L0637.050]

### Audience and Targeting

- Most TikTok traffic comes from users aged about 21 to 30, so products should appeal to young buyers. [L0708.015]
- Open targeting works best on TikTok. Narrow targeting of buyers aged 30 to 40 plus is less effective, because the algorithm does not target them well. [L0708.015]
- TikTok uses broad audiences where the creative is the main variable. Facebook can find buyers through better targeting once the account is safe. [L0637.050]
- Products for an older audience are better tested on Facebook, while products for a younger audience should be researched directly on TikTok. [L1184.041]
- Widen product ideas beyond one audience. A bottle opener aimed only at beer drinkers over 21 is narrow, while showing soda or other beverage bottles can reach more age groups. [L0656.008]

### Product Types That Tend to Sell

- Unique, attractive or shiny-object products tend to sell on TikTok. [L0708.016]
- Life-hack tools sell well, as do niches such as jewelry, beauty and women-focused products. [L0708.016]
- Broader products tend to work better on TikTok. Competition level on the platform matters for a specific product. [L0950.061]
- A product that already won on Facebook can be tested on TikTok, where the audience has grown a lot since the earlier winning period. A product that sold well in an earlier year may still have an unreached TikTok audience that has never seen it. [L0819.029]
- A proven product can be tested on TikTok, which was a new platform in the case, because a new platform brings a new audience. Test the same product's hook and creative there before dropping it. [L0871.080]
- A product that struggles on Facebook may do well on TikTok, which can pick up the slack. [L0950.062]

### Product Types That Tend to Fail

**High-ticket products**

- For high-ticket products above about $200, return on ad spend is usually too low to justify the budget. [L0854.116]
- TikTok ads are not recommended for cold traffic to a website, since they reach people who may not buy high-ticket products. [L1285.060]
- The host did not put organic effort into TikTok for higher-ticket products. (Source A, 2023, older) [L0804.056]

**Print-on-demand products**

- For print-on-demand products, TikTok ads were reported to bring little success compared with other channels. [L1214.033]
- Organic TikTok reach is hard to achieve for print-on-demand products, and it has been slowing down in general. Plan to spend on paid ads rather than rely only on organic short video. [L1438.045]

**Older-audience and practical comfort products**

- Practical comfort products that sell well as Facebook ads often underperform as TikTok ads. Test such products on Meta first before adding TikTok to the plan. [L1185.041]
- Products that target an older audience are better tested on Facebook. [L1184.041]

**Ingestible and finished goods**

- Some brands found that TikTok ads, including Spark Ads, and Facebook ads did not become profitable for certain products, such as beverages and some finished goods. For those products, creator-led content was the way to keep sales going. [L1379.055]
- Buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website. (See the case below.) [L1429.051]

### Sequencing for Beginners

- Two units disagree on where beginners should start:
  - TikTok is beginner friendly, with fewer ban issues than Meta, better support, and a low barrier to account creation. [L0296.021]
  - Beginners should master Facebook ads first before running TikTok. TikTok ads die out fast, so the account needs a constant stream of new creatives, which is hard to produce well. [L1107.017]
- The stated constraint in the second view decides which applies: if the team cannot produce a steady stream of new creatives, start with Facebook. [L1107.017]
- Creative lifespan on TikTok is about one week, so scaling depends on a steady process for producing new creatives. [L0637.063]
- Run Facebook and TikTok in parallel for the same product to see which platform performs better, because results differ by product. [L0690.030]
- A product that works on TikTok may not work on Facebook, and vice versa, because the audiences are different. [L0950.060]
- In 2023, TikTok ads were a newer channel with less saturation. Cost per click, click-through rate and conversions were cheaper than on many other paid channels, but that advantage erodes as more advertisers join. (2023 conditions) [L1251.018]
- Retargeting on TikTok can work but adds an extra platform to manage. Meta and Google remarketing usually earn more profit. (Source A, 2024) [L1285.060]

### Testing Winners Across Platforms

Procedure: use TikTok to find winning creatives before paying for them on Facebook.

1. Test several videos as TikTok organic posts. [L0774.040]
2. Find the video that goes viral. [L0774.040]
3. Use that video in Facebook ads, cut to the Facebook formats if needed. [L0774.040]

Running one video on Facebook and posting one on TikTok without testing is not worth it. [L0774.040]

- Trending products from TikTok can be tested on Facebook with a different creative. The product stays the same, and only the ad creative changes. [L1431.046]
- Test the product on both channels, because TikTok can open a separate marketing stream even when a product works on only one platform. [L0950.060]
- For a product that already works on TikTok, TikTok's own ad platform may perform better than Meta ads for the same product. [L1380.058]

### Case: Viral Video, Few Sales

- Product: a ceramic ice cube.
- Hook: a video asked whether the cube makes drinks taste like metal, then showed the ceramic cube so viewers stayed to find out what it was. [L1429.051]
- Result: about 15 million views and only about 25 sales. [L1429.051]
- Cause of low sales: buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website. [L1429.051]
- Comment section: comments were left unfiltered, so complaints dominated it. [L1429.051]
- Competitor: a competitor who copied the video frame by frame also went viral, but the unfiltered negative comments damaged that account. [L1429.051]

## Account Setup for Organic Product Accounts

### Email and Sign-Up

- Register the account with an email address, not a phone number, Apple ID, or Apple Pay login, because each of those can only be linked to one account. [L1380.003]
- Use a dedicated email so that store and account matters stay separate. [L1380.003]
- Use a Gmail address that matches the email of the Shopify store, not a personal email, so the business accounts stay organized. [L1380.004]
- For an organic account, an ordinary email address is sufficient, and a US phone number is not required. [L1428.032]
- Avoid numbered variants of an address (the same name with added numbers), suspicious phone numbers, and changing IP addresses, because these can lead to shadow banning. Focus on legitimate content instead. [L1428.032]

### Username

- Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols, since symbols look unprofessional. Match it to the brand or store name. [L1380.005]
- Check username availability before creating the Shopify domain. [L1380.006]
- If the username is taken, add.com or.co to the end, or add shop before or after the brand name, since these variants are often still free. [L1380.006]
- A TikTok username can be changed after 30 days, so a poor choice at setup is not permanent. [L1380.007]

### Personal or Business Account

- Keep the account as a Personal account and do not switch to a Business account. [L1380.008]
- Business accounts lose access to trending and cooperative sounds, get less reach, and cannot post a link without 1,000 followers. [L1380.008]
- Business accounts run into copyright blocks when posting music, because the trending sound library is restricted for them. This is a main reason to stay on a personal account for product videos. [L1380.009]

### Profile Picture, Display Name, and Bio

- Set the profile picture to a high-quality product image, a product logo, or the brand name. [L1380.052]
- Set the display name (not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com. [L1380.052]
- Write a bio of 2 to 3 lines with emojis. [L1380.052]
- Place an emoji at both ends of each bio line. This format was used on TikTok product accounts and read well in the profile. Keep each line short so the slogan, offer, and link pointer stay readable on a phone screen. [L1380.061]
- Do not put a website link in the bio. The bio is not a link location. [L1380.013]

### Links and the 1,000-Follower Threshold

| Link type | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Website link in the profile link tab | About 1,000 followers. The link option appears only after this threshold, so a TikTok Shop is a practical starting point until then. | [L1215.018, L1380.013, L1380.052] |
| External website link before 1,000 followers | May be restricted. One seller avoided it and used Instagram instead, noting that the risk is not fully known. | [L1381.023] |
| Instagram link (social link) | No minimum follower requirement applied at the time of recording. | [L1381.024, L1380.014] |
| Any link on a Business account | Cannot be posted without 1,000 followers. | [L1380.008] |

- A store can be built later, once the link function is available. [L1215.018]
- To add links, tap Edit profile. The fields for a website link and other links (such as Instagram and YouTube) are located there. [L1380.014]
- Logging into Instagram from TikTok syncs the Instagram link, which then shows next to the Follow button. [L1380.014]

**Adding an Instagram link:**

1. Open Edit profile on the account. [L1381.024]
2. Scroll down to Social. [L1381.024]
3. Choose Instagram. [L1381.024]
4. Link the brand Instagram account, which should look similar to the TikTok account. [L1381.024]
5. An Instagram icon appears on the profile and opens the Instagram page with its products and shop-now button. [L1381.024]

### Comment Filters

**Turning on keyword filtering:**

1. Tap Settings at the top right of the profile tab. [L1380.016]
2. Open Comments. [L1380.016]
3. Switch on Filter keywords. [L1380.016]
4. Keep the Filter all comments option off. It forces manual approval of nearly every comment and lowers engagement. [L1380.016]

**Filter keywords:**

- Add these keywords to hide comments that reveal dropshipping or hurt trust: dropshipping, amazon, aliexpress, CJ dropshipping, cheaper, china, scam, shopify, dropshipper, fake, and don't buy. [L1380.017]
- Expand the list for each product, using negative words that appear in comments on that product. [L1380.017]

### Privacy and Suggestion Settings

- Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others. This stops people who receive a link or interact with you from seeing which account you run. [L1380.018]
- New accounts come with these options enabled by default, so check them at setup. [L1380.018]

### Upload Quality Settings

**Enabling high-quality uploads:**

1. On the post screen, tap More options (the last option in the bottom row). [L1380.020]
2. Turn on Allow high quality uploads. [L1380.020]
3. Do this before posting or drafting. [L1380.020]

- Record in 4K at 60 fps. If 4K or storage is not available, 1080p is sufficient. [L1380.020]

### Mobile versus Desktop

- Do product research and account setup on the TikTok mobile app, not desktop. [L1380.001]
- Desktop has far fewer filters and editing options. It is mainly useful for buying coins or looking at some analytics. [L1380.001]

**Desktop upload procedure:**

1. Log in to TikTok in a desktop browser with the account. [L1186.036]
2. Upload the video from the computer. [L1186.036]
3. Choose the cover, set public or private, and run the copyright check. [L1186.036]
4. Add the music inside the editing tool before uploading, because the desktop upload does not allow adding music. [L1186.036]
5. Add the hashtags in the caption. [L1186.036]

### Setup Checklist for a New Product TikTok Account

Complete these steps in this order:

1. Sign up with an email address, a Gmail that matches the Shopify store email, not a phone number or Apple ID login. [L1380.052, L1380.003, L1380.004]
2. Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols that matches the brand. If it is taken, try adding.com or.co, or shop before or after the brand name. [L1380.052, L1380.005, L1380.006]
3. Keep the account on Personal, not Business. [L1380.052, L1380.008]
4. Set the profile picture to a high-quality product image, product logo, or brand name. [L1380.052]
5. Set the display name (not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com. [L1380.052]
6. Write a 2 to 3 line bio with emojis. [L1380.052, L1380.061]
7. Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others. [L1380.052, L1380.018]
8. Turn on Filter keywords under Comments in Settings and add the blocked keyword list. [L1380.052, L1380.016, L1380.017]
9. Turn on Allow high quality uploads under More options on the post screen. [L1380.052, L1380.020]
10. Draft at least 3 to 5 days of videos before posting. [L1380.052]
11. Add a website link only after 1,000 followers, under Edit profile. [L1380.052, L1380.013, L1215.018]

### Accounts per Phone and Device Pool

**Three-account cap and its exceptions**

| Practical limit to avoid detection | About three accounts on one phone, with no engagement between them | Given as the practical limit for avoiding detection. [L0657.028] | [L0657.028] |

- Going beyond three accounts on one phone is considered a bad idea. [L0561.002]
- Several accounts on one phone is common and was reported without a clear ban. [L1186.033]
- Accounts can be shared across family members' phones as an additional device pool. Each device should still stay within the three-account cap. [L0561.003]
- A separate phone is not a guaranteed fix for shadow bans. Use an extra phone only if you already have one. Do not buy one for this purpose. [L1186.033]

### Separate Devices for Ad and Organic Accounts

- When you run an ad account alongside an organic account for the same product, use a separate phone for each. This keeps ad activity from putting the organic account at risk. [L0352.055]
- This recommendation applies only if you already have the extra phone. See [L1186.033]. [L0352.055, L1186.033]

### Cross-Account Engagement

- Do not log into a second account on the same phone to comment or like your own store account. Cross-account engagement on one device can hurt the account. [L0820.019]
- Do not engage with the accounts from each other. [L0657.028]
- Each new product test should run on its own fresh account. The same Wi-Fi may be shared across accounts, but an account must not be engaged from a device that is used for another account. [L0820.018]

### New Accounts and Content Reuse

- After about two weeks of consistent posting, the advantage of a new account can fade. Start a third account only after checking that the first two still perform. [L0352.043]
- Repurpose the same content on new accounts before creating new content. [L0352.043]
- Case: multi-account growth on separate accounts [L1381.002]
  - Starting point: a first account started gaining traction.
  - About 48 hours later: a second brand-new account was created. It took roughly three days to gain traction and go viral.
  - About a month later: a third new account was created.
  - Result: the first and second accounts each reached 100,000 followers in the same period. The third reached 100,000 within a month. Each account reached different people, and the second account became the largest. [L1381.002]
- Number on organic posting with the router setup: one account per phone is typical. The lesson's example recommended one account on the reset phone. [L0577.045]

### Resetting a Device for a New Product Test

- Testing a new product on a device requires a full factory reset and new accounts. The platform would otherwise link the new product to the old account footprint. Keep the data backed up before resetting. [L0561.005]
- Procedure to move a device to a new product test, or to clear a suspected device or account limit: [L0561.004]
  1. Back up the phone's data to the cloud, but do not include TikTok account information in the backup. [L0561.004]
  2. Perform a full factory reset of the phone. [L0561.004]
  3. Create new TikTok accounts for the new product and post at least three to five completely different pieces of content on them. [L0561.004]

### Diagnosing Device or IP Limits

- If new social accounts are suspended within minutes of posting the same product videos, the device or IP address is more likely the cause than the video content. [L0387.037]
- Test this by posting from a different phone, tablet, or network, or by posting an unrelated video from the same account. [L0387.037]

### Router and VPN Setup

- A VPN can make an account lose reach or be shadowbanned on TikTok. Test with a split setup: one account with a VPN and one without. Skip the VPN if you do not need it for your location. [L0372.032]
- A VPN is generally not needed to go viral from a first-world country. It may help only in certain third-world locations, and some sellers there have still gone viral without one. [L0657.022]
- A VPN that is only switched on in the phone's VPN app does not work for organic TikTok content. The VPN must run on the router so the device's connection is consistently routed. [L0577.044]
- Procedure: TikTok VPN setup to avoid shadowban (router method) [L0577.043]
  1. Test whether the VPN works on the router before the phone is changed. The VPN must be connected to the router, not only in the VPN app on the phone. [L0577.043]
  2. Set up the VPN on the router. NordVPN was used. A router setup guide is available from the provider. [L0577.043]
  3. Choose a rotating VPN so the phone stays permanently connected and the algorithm consistently sees the chosen country. [L0577.043]
  4. Once the router works, factory reset the phone, wipe it completely, and then set it up again. [L0577.043]
  5. When backing up the phone, do not restore Instagram and TikTok data. Log back in manually instead. [L0577.043]
  6. Check whether the account still struggles. Only create a new account if the old account still has problems. [L0577.043]
  7. Connect the phone to Wi-Fi via the router after the reset. [L0577.043]

### Posting from the Target Country

- TikTok organic accounts are not run through a VPN. Instead, content is posted in the target country so that TikTok first tests it there and then moves it to the US content pool if it performs well. [L1440.025]
- Store and account setup in the creator's own country is recommended, since VPN use for other regions can be frustrating. Start the first account locally, then expand to other regions once you have results. [L1384.048]
- Using a VPN to upload videos from a different region can cause shadow bans, because the platform detects the mismatch in IP location. Upload from the country where the account is intended to operate. Do not rely on a VPN to reach other regions. [L1077.028]

### Reaching US Viewers from Abroad

- To reach US viewers, upload at US times, use US hashtags, use US trending sounds, and stay consistent. [L0657.021]
- Expect US reach only after videos get roughly 100,000 to 200,000 views, and preferably over 1 million views. [L0657.021]
- To reach a US audience from outside the US, post English content with hashtags and music that are popular in the US instead of changing IP addresses. Reach then depends on the content's relevance and viewership. Videos from the seller's own location may still reach the UK first before spreading. [L1428.033]
- Do not use a VPN to make TikTok treat you as a US user. A VPN-driven spike was followed by a shadow ban that wiped out views. Posting from outside the US without a VPN still reached viewers, since US viewers make up a large share of TikTok traffic. [L0820.045]

### Country Availability

- At the time of the lesson (2023), TikTok was accessible in Pakistan, so TikTok ads could be run from there, and a VPN was an option. TikTok was banned in India. Check the current status in each country before running ads. [L1431.047]
- TikTok is blocked in India, so organic accounts cannot be opened from there. Instead, content creators from the UK and US were hired to run the accounts. [L1440.026]

## Warming Up and Launching a New Account

### Choosing Between a New Account and an Existing One

- A new account can reach more viewers than an older account with a similar follower count, even when the video is only average. [L1381.001]
- Multiple accounts are mainly useful for sellers who need reach quickly. Established creators usually do not need them. [L1381.001]
- If an established account stays flat for a long period, testing the same product on a fresh account is a reasonable next step. [L1444.063]
- An older account with weak reach for months can be outperformed by a new account that gets views right away. [L1444.063]

### Reusing an Existing Account for a Different Product

Use this procedure to make a reused account read as a fresh start:

1. Change the account name. [L1383.056]
2. Set all old videos to private or delete them. [L1383.056]
3. Post the new product consistently, without skipping days. [L1383.056]
4. Expect slow views for the first one to two weeks while the algorithm adjusts to the new content. [L1383.056]

### Warm-Up Waiting Period

**Standard warm-up procedure**

1. Create a TikTok account or warm an existing one. [L0309.022]
2. Wait 6-12 hours before uploading the first video, or up to a week. [L0309.022, L0309.023]
3. During the wait, follow accounts in your niche, follow competitors, follow influencers, and engage with your target audience's content. [L0309.022]
4. Do not upload videos or take any spammy actions during this period. [L0309.022]
5. After the warm-up, upload the first video. The account will receive views faster than a brand-new account with zero history. [L0309.022]

**New-email test procedure**

Use this when you are confident a product is viable but no account is gaining traction:

1. Create a new TikTok account with a new email address. [L0309.027]
2. Wait 12-24 hours. [L0309.027]
3. During the wait, do not upload videos. Instead, follow accounts related to your product category and engage with their content. [L0309.027]
4. After 12-24 hours, upload your first video. [L0309.027]
5. Repeat this process 5-7 times if the product still struggles to gain traction. [L0309.027]

**Wait durations by source**

| Variant | Wait before first upload | Applies to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard warm-up | 6-12 hours, or up to one week | General warm-up of a new or existing account | L0309.022, L0309.023 |
| New-email test | 12-24 hours | Repeated tests of a product on fresh accounts | L0309.027 |

- A warmed account that has engaged with other accounts before its first post receives the new account advantage earlier and performs better than an account posted to immediately. [L0309.023]
- The advantage applies as long as no videos have been uploaded. [L0309.023]

### New-Account Advantage and Its Timing

- A brand-new account can receive an early distribution boost during its first days or weeks. A sudden spike of views on a fresh account does not prove that the product converts. [L1385.019]
- The timing starts from the first video upload, not from when the account was created. Plan the first upload to use the early window. [L1384.049]
- Duration: the sources differ. One source says the advantage typically lasts about one week. [L1376.008] Another says the window lasts about one to two weeks. [L1374.039] Plan for the shorter figure as the guaranteed period and treat two weeks as the upper bound.
- Like-to-view ratios: new accounts can get away with weaker ratios, around 1:20 to 1:30. [L1384.002]
- Stronger push: new accounts can be pushed with weaker engagement ratios, such as 1:40 to 1:50, and sometimes even 1:100, depending on watch time. Proven content on a new account is therefore treated as stronger than it is. [L1429.026]
- Local-first distribution: new accounts are first distributed to their local area. Once a video passes about 1 million views, it is distributed across many countries and local targeting no longer applies. [L1429.019]

**Case: fast viral result on a second account**

- A product performed well on a first account for one week, so a second account was built on that proven product. [L1374.039]
- Paid Coins ran on the new account from its first day. By day three, it had gone viral with millions of views. [L1374.039]
- The team uses this boost only in the first three days of a new account, since the advantage window lasts about one to two weeks. [L1374.039]

### Preparing Content Before the First Upload

- Keep a content calendar with at least 9 finished videos ready before launching a new account. Nine videos cover three days when three uploads are made per day. [L0795.001]
- Use the buffer to react to trends or strong comments without missing the minimum posting schedule. [L0795.001]
- A new account should upload at least three videos per day during its first one to two weeks. [L0795.002]
- Film more videos than you post so a buffer is always ready. For example, filming about nine videos per day produces three publishable videos in advance, which keeps daily posting consistent. [L1444.062]

### Launch Boost with Coins

**Coins procedure for a new account (first three days)**

1. On day one, put about $10 in coins on the best video of the day. [L1429.044]
2. Wait until midday and compare views across the day's videos. [L1429.044]

## Hooks, Scripts and Video Structure

### Hook Design and First Seconds

#### The Opening Hook

- The first five seconds decide whether viewers keep watching; a weak opening makes people swipe away, so the hook must grab attention immediately [L0820.005].
- Retention rate is treated as the most important signal for pushing a video, so the first seconds deserve the most effort. Some early drop-off is normal, but the goal is to make the curve as flat as possible [L1186.024].
- Viewers often read on-screen text or an emoji in the first seconds before deciding to keep watching. A short caption with a visible emoji in the opening frame can hold attention for the hook [L1444.073].
- TikTok hooks need to be more shocking than standard ad hooks and should create curiosity that keeps the viewer watching. Other hook formats that work include before-and-after and point-of-view (POV) shots [L1427.007].
- A hook must promise something specific that the video then shows, so viewers stay to see whether it works [L1444.028].
- Proven hook structures can be borrowed from product videos in other niches, because the same opening works across different products. Copy the structure of the hook, not its exact wording, and adapt the content to your product [L1384.055].
- Virality depends more on video quality than on luck. An engaging hook, such as a surprising question that makes viewers want to finish, is a key driver [L1429.042].
- On TikTok, going viral depends on the hook, the wording, the angles and the sound choices in the video. Once these are found, they can be repeated across new videos [L1469.011].

#### Hook Pattern for a Short Product Video

Pattern: open with a short statement in the first seconds that makes viewers curious about a clear visible result, and keep the total length around seven seconds to raise watch time [L1444.028].

Example patterns to adapt to your own product:
- "Your phone screen is dirtier than you think, see it clean in 7 seconds." [L1444.028]
- "This drawer organizer fits any kitchen sink, watch it fit in one try." [L1444.028]

- On TikTok, content is judged as entertainment rather than as advertising. ### Judging a Product

**Check the signals that matter**

- Judge a TikTok account by video performance and conversion, not by follower count. [L0657.029]
- Followers on TikTok carry less weight than on other platforms: they do not guarantee views or sales. [L0657.029]
- One account with about 1,000 followers produced 56 store sessions in its first few days, while its average video reached about 5,000 views. Store sessions were the clearest signal of audience response. [L0352.002]
- Check how consistently the seller posts, not only the view count. A seller who posts at least daily and keeps uploading is usually still getting some sales, even when individual videos stall. [L0656.001]
- Read the comments for buying intent. Phrases like "where can I buy this", "I just bought one", "I placed an order" or "I want one" signal interest and possible sales. Complaints like "where is my order" signal fulfillment problems. [L0656.006]
- Use views only as an early signal and compare them with actual orders before scaling. [L0372.049]

**Check conversion before scaling a product**

- If a product gets good organic traffic (500+ views, strong engagement) but no sales, check product-market fit and the landing page before changing the creative. [L0309.035]
- If a product gets no organic traffic despite reasonable creative quality, create a new account rather than continuing on a shadowbanned or under-performing one. [L0309.035]

### Kill and Pivot Decisions

**Kill rules**

- Kill a product if a 3 to 5 day test shows no meaningful results (no views, no engagement, no conversions). [L0309.025]
- Kill a product when, after several days, the view count stays flat. Keep products whose views grow steadily and remove the product after a set number of posts without growth. [L0561.013]
- Do not kill a product on zero-view results alone when the creative is the likely cause. Test angles and hooks first. [L1469.012, L1469.013, L0309.040]

**Procedure for several products on one account**

1. Post each product on a per-post basis, roughly three to five videos per week or per day. [L0561.013]
2. Log engagement and views for each product in a spreadsheet over time. [L0561.013]
3. Keep products whose views grow steadily, for example from 1,000 to 5,000. [L0561.013]
4. After a set number of posts without growth, remove the product from the account. [L0561.013]

**Testing a product before paid ads**

- Post organic content to see which products gain traction in views and comments before investing in paid ads. Kill those that do not grow. [L0561.012]

### Comment-Driven Pivots

**Principles**

- Comments are the best feedback on what an audience wants to see. When one reference keeps repeating, pivot the whole angle toward it. [L0764.017]

**Procedure: audience feedback**

1. Read the comments on every video, especially the top comments. [L0764.016]
2. Look for the same reference, theme or question appearing repeatedly. [L0764.016]
3. Reply to the top comment with a video reply that uses that theme. [L0764.016]
4. If the reply performs well, go hard on that theme and keep repeating it across uploads. [L0764.016]
5. If the existing angle keeps underperforming after a few days, change the content completely instead of waiting. [L0764.016]

**Procedure: pivot from an unrelated trend in the comments**

1. Check videos with real views but weak engagement, and read the comments for a different topic or interest. [L1429.021]
2. Find comments on that topic that have many likes, which signal real interest. [L1429.021]
3. Make a video reply on that topic and connect it to your product. [L1429.021]
4. Take the top comments of the new video and make further video replies from them. [L1429.021]
5. Use trending sounds or trends on that topic, and create your own sounds if that helps. [L1429.021]

## Engagement, Comments and Driving Traffic to the Store

### Followers Versus Sales

- Below 1,000 followers, direct sales from TikTok profile traffic are small, because new viewers rarely trust a small account. Sales typically pick up after the account passes 1,000 followers, and a single video with roughly 5,000 to 10,000 views can be enough to reach that number. [L1380.015]
- Free-product and giveaway-style angles grow followers and views fast, but the audience that responds mostly wants free items and rarely buys. Use them to build early momentum, not as the main sales angle. [L0352.001]
- Giveaway posts grew the follower count quickly but mostly attracted people who wanted free items, not buyers. Sales-focused videos that followed stayed under 5,000 views and drew little traffic. Keep the content aimed at people who want the product, and use giveaways only when the audience matches the product. [L1077.054]
- Engagement drives distribution. When viewers like, follow, watch to the end and comment, the platform sends the video to a larger audience. Ads optimised for viewers who engage and finish watching the video tend to scale better. [L1383.054]

### Audience Matching and Steering

- Engaging with similar creators (liking, commenting and following in your niche) increases your exposure to the audience of those creators. [L0915.011]
- Steer the audience away from kids after a viral video attracts the wrong viewers:
  1. Find another viral video for a similar product and identify its angle. [L0706.053]
  2. Test new content angles that appeal to adults. [L0706.053]
  3. Use hashtags that adults engage with. [L0706.053]
  4. Once an adult-focused angle goes viral, replicate it and reduce the youth-focused content. [L0706.053]
- Redirecting an existing audience with coins is risky because it can drive away current viewers. Use it only when comments show a clear buying segment, such as all male commenters, and target that segment or an age group expected to buy. [L1429.014]

### Comments and Engagement

- Reply to as many comments as possible. Long comment sections raise watch time, and TikTok then pushes the video further. Replying to comments with new videos and asking questions in comments drives engagement. [L0501.035]
- To reply to a comment with a video, tap the comment under your viral video and choose **Make video**. The original comment then appears in the top-left corner of the recording screen, and you place your reply video there. [L0501.036]
- The TikTok video reply lets you answer a specific comment with a new video. Using it on a top comment that shares a theme can pull in viewers who are already engaged with that topic. [L0764.018]
- When a viral video's comment thread is building around its angle, a follow-up video that responds to those comments can extend reach while the trend is active. Only use this if the product really works, because a retraction-style follow-up can backfire. [L0655.028]
- Controversial content that prompts argument in the comments raises engagement and watch time, because the video keeps playing while people read and reply. Some creators deliberately point viewers to the comments to drive interaction. [L1384.025]
- Small deliberate imperfections in a video or caption, such as a visible slip, can draw comments and boost engagement. Keep them mild, because extreme behaviour can backfire on the account. [L0501.043]
- Do not share your own video by copying its link and sharing it on your own account, because TikTok can detect self-spam. Share actions should come from genuine viewer behaviour, not self-promotion. [L1384.026]

## Shadowbans, Restrictions and Account Health

### Symptoms of a Restriction

- A restriction shows up as a sudden drop in views compared with recent videos, and several consecutive uploads stay at a similar, very low level. Example: one video at 5,000 views followed by repeated uploads near 80 views is a strong sign of restriction. [L1381.003]
- Judge restriction by views relative to follower count, not by one low result. Views in the thousands on a small account can be normal, while an account with about 100,000 followers that gets only a few hundred views is likely restricted. [L1381.005]

### Diagnosis Before Assuming a Shadowban

1. Check whether the problem is platform-wide. Analytics and Creator Fund issues can affect many accounts and are not specific to yours. Before assuming a shadowban, check whether other accounts report the same delays or problems. [L0372.033]
2. For a new account whose videos get almost no views, check the account setup and VPN use before blaming the content. [L0501.041]
3. If a video stays in review for a long time, check its upload status with the Copy link step before assuming it failed. [L0501.042]

### Causes

- A glitching VPN or other account-level problem can get a new TikTok account shadow banned. [L0501.041]
- Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction. [L1381.008]
- Videos from new accounts can be shadowbanned or removed quickly if they contain risky content. [L0372.036]
- Posting a private copy and then publishing the same video again from the same account is risky, as TikTok may see the duplication as a problem. [L1444.030]
- Competitors can file reports against successful accounts, and enough reports can lead to flags even without a violation. [L1384.031]
- Most account removals happen after multiple violations or reports, not after a single incident. [L1384.032]

### Durations

The sources give different values. Read them together.

| Source | Duration | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Restriction in general | Usually one to two days; the longest observed lasted about two weeks. [L1381.004] | Ordinary restriction |
| Shadow ban in the described cases | Typically about 14 days before it lifts. [L0819.004] | Shadow ban; the length varies with the content posted during that time. [L0819.004] |
| Several videos removed in one day | Views stayed very low until the account recovered after about one to two weeks of waiting and re-uploading. [L1381.008] | Case of mass removal |

- Expect a few days for an ordinary restriction, and plan for up to about two weeks. [L1381.004, L0819.004]

### Recovery

When an account seems shadow banned:

1. Keep posting about once a day on that account. [L0819.028]
2. Run a new account in parallel. [L0819.028]
3. Expect the shadow ban to usually lift after about 14 days in the cases described, but note that the duration depended on the content being posted. [L0819.028, L0819.004]

- After mass removal, wait about one to two weeks and re-upload videos. [L1381.008]
- A single restriction usually recovers within a short period after a successful appeal. [L1384.032]
- Recovering from a shadow ban can cost weeks of work. [L1444.031]

### Appeals and Review

- Appeals are typically resolved in about 24 hours, sometimes three to four days when TikTok is backed up. [L1384.031]
- A video removed at least once may cause later uploads to go into review more often. [L0501.042]

### Reports and Violations

- Videos that violate Community Guidelines are not pushed to the For You Page and are shown only to the creator's own followers. [L1384.030]
- Repeated violations can lead to a permanent ban. Removal of an account means losing its followers and income. [L1384.030]
- Enough reports can lead to flags even without a violation. [L1384.031]

### Guidelines and Risky Content

- Keep the first videos of a new account safe and avoid borderline jokes until the account is stable. [L0372.036]
- Videos that break the rules lose their reach, so check every video against the Community Guidelines before posting. [L1384.030]

### Deletion Rules

- Never delete a video, because TikTok treats deleted videos as failed. [L1444.027]
- If a video is bad or has an editing mistake, set it to private instead. [L1444.027]
- Do not publish the same video again from the same account after posting a private copy, because TikTok may see the duplication as a problem. [L1444.030]
- If an account stops performing, test the same content on a new account, which is the safer option. [L1444.030]
- Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction. [L1381.008]
- Do not delete accounts that have brand, business or personal value right away. [L1381.009]

### Keeping or Abandoning Accounts

- A restricted account with thousands of followers can be kept. [L1381.009]
- For product accounts, a new account is often easier to make go viral than reviving an old one. [L1381.009]
- A large follower count builds credibility with shoppers, so an account with tens of thousands of followers is worth keeping as the main brand account even when its organic views drop. [L1381.010]
- Shoppers who click a Spark Ad often look at the profile first, which is why the follower count matters. [L1381.010]

### Account Roles and Separation

- Keep a separate account for paid ads and for testing new products. [L1381.012]
- Keep the personal account separate from ad and test work. This protects the main account and keeps the work organized. [L1381.012]
- The sources use "main account" in two senses. In L1381.010 it means the high-follower brand account that you keep. In L1381.012 it means the account kept personal and separate from ads and tests. Keep both roles apart. [L1381.010, L1381.012]

### Platform Rule Changes

- Platform rules and TikTok features change often, so check the current rules before relying on any setting or program. [L1380.051]
- Changes are a recurring reason setups fail. [L1380.051]

### Risk Principles

- Keep the risk of account bans low, because a ban can wipe out the views and progress of accounts on the same device. [L1444.031]
- Avoid the mistakes that trigger shadow bans, since recovering from a shadow ban can cost weeks of work. [L1444.031]

## Coins, Spark Ads and Paid Boosting of Organic Videos

### Choosing between organic testing and paid boosting

- Test the product with organic content first. Create organic TikTok videos and measure product performance over 3 to 7 days. [L0309.021]
- Boost only a video that is already performing. A video with strong engagement and views can be converted into a Spark Ad that runs from that organic video's URL. [L0309.021]
- Spark Ads typically outperform ads made from scratch because they use authentic engagement signals. [L0309.021]
- A two-day test with a small budget indicates whether a product gets sales and is likely to receive organic sales once views come in. [L1376.022]
- Coins help a new account's video reach the right audience faster, so organic reach can follow. The paid portion lets TikTok learn the audience early, and the organic portion can be much larger. [L0501.014]
- Coins are most useful once the content format already works and when you run multiple accounts on the same product. [L0501.014]
- For a product that has already produced proven sales, the approach is to start a new TikTok account for it, spend on coins only during the first few days so TikTok finds the right audience faster, and avoid overspending by limiting boosts to that window. [L1376.027]

**Procedure: accelerate organic reach for a product that already sells** [L1376.027]

1. Choose a product that has already produced proven sales. [L1376.027]
2. Start a new TikTok account for it. [L1376.027]
3. Spend on coins (paid boosts) during only the first few days so TikTok finds the right audience faster. [L1376.027]
4. Use the new account advantage during that window. [L1376.027]
5. Do not overspend on coins; limit the boosts to the first few days. [L1376.027]

**Example (one case, not a guaranteed result):** A product that had already passed six-figure sales was pushed on a new account with paid coins. The new account's video went viral with millions of views within three days. This suggests the early paid boost can compound with the new account advantage. [L1376.028]

### Timing: when to boost

- Spark Ads on a viral organic video are usually run after the viral effect has passed, once the video has slowed down. The video then carries social proof. [L1376.023]
- Organic reach on TikTok is inconsistent. Videos can reach very high views in the first hour and then stop. Whether running ads stops organic growth is unproven, so test it yourself. [L1376.024]
- Some videos go viral repeatedly. One reported video gained another 5 million views every few months. Such videos are good Spark Ad candidates after their viral phase has passed. [L1376.031]
- If a viral video's views start declining, wait a day or two, then add coins to it. [L1429.011]
- Use coins or Spark Ads on a video that is growing but not yet getting sales only after the audience has been checked. [L1429.011]
- A Reach campaign can kick-start a new account by putting a test video in front of many people in the target audience. If those viewers watch to the end and engage, average watch time rises and the platform distributes the video to larger groups. Use this to accelerate organic growth during testing. [L1383.005]

**Procedure: Spark Ads trigger for a test video** [L1186.026]

1. Pick the best organic video from the first days. [L1186.026]
2. If it reaches roughly 1,000 views on day two or three, set up a Spark Ads campaign for it. [L1186.026]

- Spark Ads were used by one operator on a product with about 50 million views per week. A 50 USD test on Spark Ads was run and then stopped after a few days. [L0819.017]

### Spark Ads and organic reach

Sellers disagree on whether Spark Ads reduce organic reach. One advertiser reported that they do. Another saw an organic boost after a small spend ended. [L1381.062] Opinions were mixed and based on small samples. [L0819.017]

- Test Spark Ads on a separate ads account and watch the organic views of the main account before scaling spend. [L1381.062]
- If you run both paid Spark Ads and organic content, keep them on two separate TikTok accounts. One account handles the paid ads and the other stays purely organic. [L1383.007]
- Never click the option to link your TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads. Once linked, the organic account should be considered lost, because the platform tends to limit organic views on accounts used for paid promotion. [L1383.014]
- A seller's other videos can show whether a viral result is repeatable. If they show no sign of the paid-boost pattern, reach likely came from organic posting alone. [L0657.044]

### Judging viral results and sales

- Spark Ads are a candidate explanation when sales arrive in short bursts despite huge view counts. Judging them unlikely just because an account has many followers is weak evidence. Compare the timing of sales with the timing of the views instead. [L1385.018]

## Ad Creative and Copy for TikTok Ads

### Ad Performance Weighting: Video Versus Copy

- TikTok ad performance is about 90 percent determined by video content and 10 percent by ad copy. The video carries the conversion weight. [L0204.015]
- TikTok requires video creatives. Images do not work on the platform. [L0577.042]
- Users have already seen the product in the video within the first 3-5 seconds, so the copy's job is to close. [L0204.016]

### Offer and Copy Rules

- Include the product offer or discount in TikTok ad copy in 9 out of 10 cases. The offer in the copy is the final trigger to click the shop button. [L0204.016]
- Keep TikTok ad copy to one line maximum. Longer text will not be visible on mobile. [L0204.017]

### Copy Strategies

The following template is reproduced unchanged:

```
TikTok ad copy strategies (one line each):

**Offer-focused:** [Discount]% off, get yours today | [Discount]% off, limited time only | Buy one get one free, limited time only | Offer ending soon, shop now

**Problem-solution:** Say goodbye to [problem] | Tired of [pain point]? Here's the solution, get yours today.

**Question-based:** Struggling with [problem]? [Product name] is your answer.

**Meme/controversial:** Use punchy, pattern-breaking humor or provocative one-liners that stop scrolling.
```

[L0204.018]

### Native Creative Style

- TikTok ad videos should look like organic TikTok content rather than traditional polished ads. The more native, fun and less salesy the video, the better the performance. Classic Facebook-style ad formats do not work on TikTok. [L1376.004]
- TikTok ads perform best with UGC-style videos that are already native to TikTok. Different creatives should be used per platform. [L1184.040]
- Organic-looking, creator-style videos were outperforming polished ads on TikTok Shop in 2025. Use the top-selling videos as a reference for the format, pacing and hook of your own creatives. [L1479.008]
- GIF-style clips and entertaining content often outperform static material in competitor ads on TikTok. Taglines that grab attention in the first seconds are worth noting and testing in your own creatives. [L0895.031]
- For digital products, the lesson suggested a hook showing someone experiencing a pain point, followed by a transition to the product. This approach is not proven. Check the TikTok Creative Center for competitor creatives. [L0577.046]

**Native clips on TikTok versus Facebook**

| Aspect | TikTok | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| What is rewarded | Very short, sharp content [L0524.051] | More depth [L0524.051] |
| Native clips as ads | Often underperform on Facebook; work when native and punchy [L0524.051, L1376.004] | Longer problem and solution videos often win [L0524.051] |

### Adapting Organic Posts and TikTok Shop Clips

- Focus on organic growth first, then promote the best-performing organic posts as ads. Ads performed poorly when the posts were not native to the platform. [L1077.020]
- When repurposing an organic video for ads, adjust the text and add an offer with a call to action at the end, such as an instruction to shop now. TikTok ads are judged on views and higher conversion. [L1376.005]

**Procedure: adapting a TikTok Shop video for a Meta ad**

1. Download the video from the source tool only for study, then edit a new cut rather than running the original.
2. Remove all TikTok Shop elements, such as the shop card, the product card and any shop-only buttons.
3. Remove on-screen price overlays that do not match your own selling price.
4. Cut the video down, since the sample testimonial ran almost two minutes and most of it would not be needed on Meta.
5. Keep the hook, the testimonial, the problem and solution, the benefits and the call to action.
6. Add your own call to action and link to your store.

[L0707.022]

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Do not run a TikTok Shop video on Meta unchanged. It still shows shop elements and a price that is not yours. Such mismatches confuse the viewer and can make the ad disconnect from your landing page. [L0707.023]
- Do not keep a video at close to two minutes when the hook and key claims fit in a much shorter cut. Long ads lose viewers before the call to action, so cut all filler and repeat lines. [L0707.024]

**Shop-badge example**

- Short vertical video ads for a seasonal one-size product can show a small TikTok Shop cart badge in one corner. This makes the ad look like it can be bought directly in the feed, which supports impulse purchases. [L0347.038]

### Static-to-Video Conversion

- Once a static creative proves an offer, put that offer into a video ad. Video is more engaging but takes longer to produce. [L0341.023]

### Ad Length

- Keep a TikTok ad under one minute. Short-form ads for TikTok should be quicker and more punchy than longer feed ads. Aim for 30 to 45 seconds when the content allows. [L0344.041]
- The one-minute cap is stated in the source as the limit for Reels and Stories at the time of the course. Check current platform limits before relying on it. [L0344.041]
- A near-two-minute cut is too long when the hook and key claims fit in less time. [L0707.024]

| Format | Target length | Source |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok ad | Under one minute; 30-45 seconds when content allows | [L0344.041] |
| Repurposed TikTok Shop testimonial for Meta | Cut from almost two minutes to the hook, problem and solution, benefits and call to action | [L0707.022, L0707.024] |

### Sourcing Creatives

- TikTok reduces the reach of duplicate content, and the same logic applies to ads that reuse the same video. Fresh creatives are therefore needed for ads. [L1436.025]
- Use creatives uploaded recently, ideally within the last 7 days, that few competitors are using. A video with a good like rate on PPAds is a sign that it is getting strong engagement. [L1436.026]
- A video that has run for more than about 15 to 20 days, or a month, and has gone viral usually gets much less reach when reused in an ad, because TikTok has already analyzed it. [L1436.027]
- Example: a creator video posted three days earlier with about 23,000 to 30,000 views (not yet viral) was reused for ads and scaled successfully, because TikTok had not yet picked it up. [L1436.028]

### Creative Lifespan and Supply

- Creatives on TikTok perform for 1-2 weeks before needing replacement. Plan for consistent creative supply to maintain campaign performance. [L0296.009]
- Creative lifespan on TikTok is about one week, sometimes up to two weeks. [L0637.049]

## Testing Structures, Budgets and Automated Rules

### Campaign budget type

- Set the TikTok ad budget type to **Daily**, not **Lifetime**. Lifetime budgets cause spending delays and inconsistent algorithm behavior, especially in high-competition periods such as Q4. [L0309.030]

### Campaign structure: ABO versus CBO

The sources use two budget structures. They differ in where the budget is set, not in what they measure.

| Structure | Where the budget is set | Values in the sources | ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| ABO (ad group budget) | Each ad group gets a fixed amount | $20 per ad group in a 7-15 video test; $20 per ad set on a product with a $10 margin | L1376.003, L1440.029 |
| CBO (campaign budget) | One budget at campaign level, divided by TikTok across the ad groups | $50 per day split across five ads or ad groups; about 50 pounds or 50 Euro per day split across five ads | L1375.001, L0552.025, L1378.017 |

- ABO assigns a fixed budget to each ad set. CBO sets one budget at campaign level, and TikTok divides it across the ad sets. The lesson recommends CBO for the five-ad testing structure. [L1378.016]
- The sources imply a split by test design. Where each video needs a fixed, equal share of spend (7-15 videos at $20 each), use ABO. Where one campaign tests five ads and TikTok should divide the total, use CBO. [L1376.003, L1375.001, L0552.025, L1378.016]
- The five-ad CBO structure is one campaign with five ads, a daily budget of about 50 pounds or 50 Euro split between the five ads, and the same campaign run consistently. [L0552.025, L1375.001] (merged: both sources describe the same structure)

**Setting up CBO**

1. Go to the campaign level, turn on Campaign budget optimization, and set the budget. The example uses a campaign budget of $50 (50 Swiss francs in the Swiss account), spread across all ad sets. [L1378.017]
2. After the campaign is published, a duplicate button appears on the ad group. Click it four times to create five ad groups in total. [L1378.025]

### Budget trick: low campaign cap, high ad-set budgets

**Procedure (use with the warning below)**

1. Create a TikTok Ads campaign and set a campaign budget limit (e.g., $100 for daily spend). [L0309.029]
2. Inside that campaign, create ad sets and set each ad set's budget to $2000-$5000. [L0309.029]
3. The algorithm believes each ad set has more budget than the campaign allows, forcing faster spend. [L0309.029]
4. The campaign-level cap ensures you never exceed your intended daily spend. [L0309.029]
5. To increase overall spend, gradually raise the campaign-level limit in increments (e.g., +$50 at a time). [L0309.029]

**What the trick does and does not do**

- The budget trick compresses the timeline for deciding whether a product is viable. It does not improve results or ROAS. Fast spending answers the question sooner; it does not change the outcome. [L0309.042]
- The result is the same whether you spend $500 gradually over five days or in rapid bursts. Only the timeline changes. [L0309.042]

### Warm-up spend

- TikTok ad accounts often need a daily budget of around 50 pounds or dollars to warm up the pixel. [L0552.024]
- Turning the campaign off early can damage its learning. Keep the same budget running for several days without increasing or decreasing it. [L0552.024]

### Creative testing

**Keep everything except the video identical**

- When testing several creatives, keep the offer, ad copy and audience identical across all ads, so that only the video differs. Otherwise a difference in results cannot be traced to the creative. [L1436.087]

**Creative-led structure (newer approach)**

- With a fully broad audience, the platform finds a pocket of buyers for each creative, so the creative is the variable to test. [L1194.016]
- The older setup (per source) used five ad sets, each with three to five videos and two to three ad copies, duplicated five times with the same creatives. It spread spend across shared assets, so it did not show which single creative was the winner. [L1194.055, L1194.016]
- The newer setup uses 10 ad sets with one unique video each on a fully broad audience. [L1194.055]
- The reset procedure that uses this setup is under "Resetting accounts and pixels". [L1194.015]

**Video and ad-copy test (ABO)**

1. Start with 7 to 15 different videos. [L1376.003]
2. Create one campaign using ad group budgets (ABO, ad-group level budget). [L1376.003]
3. Set the budget at $20 per ad group. [L1376.003]
4. Use only the TikTok placement. [L1376.003]
5. Set age to 25+ (adjust by product and gender). [L1376.003]
6. Use no interest targeting: completely broad targeting. [L1376.003]
7. Place one different video in each ad group. [L1376.003]
8. Use an ad copy built from a short customer-language phrase (four or five words) naming the main benefit, and test several of them. [L1376.003]

**Product test with a budget limit**

1. Choose 4 to 5 interests that correlate with the product, and narrow the targeting further when the product is niche. [L1440.029]
2. Set about $20 per ad set on a product with a $10 profit margin. [L1440.029]
3. When the spend reaches twice the cost per acquisition without a profitable result, switch that interest off and cycle it out. [L1440.029, L0298.041]
4. Spend about $200 to $300 per product with a well-made creative. If nothing works by then, the product is unlikely to take off. [L1440.029]
5. If the product still shows promise, try new creatives or a different marketing angle. [L1440.029]
6. Scale horizontally or vertically once the test works. [L1440.029]

### Evaluation window and daily noise

- Judge TikTok ad performance over a seven-day period rather than day by day. Daily results on low budgets fluctuate and can average out. Short-term swings do not necessarily mean a fault or a glitch. [L0552.022]
- Example: a test with a $50 or 50 Euro daily budget produced five to six sales on the first day, with seven add-to-carts and five checkouts (about 71% from add-to-cart to checkout). On the following day it produced zero sales despite four or five checkouts and add-to-carts. The speaker attributed this to random daily variation. [L0552.023]

### Day-of-week effects

- TikTok performance varies by day of week. Monday-Wednesday typically underperform, and Thursday-Sunday perform better. Adjust expectations and automation rules accordingly. [L0298.040]

### Automated rules

**Setting up rules in Ads Manager**

1. Go to Automated Rules in TikTok Ads Manager (top right). [L0309.031]
2. Select "Manage Rules" or "Create New Rule." [L0309.031]
3. For beginners, use two pre-built rule types: [L0309.031]
   - (a) Control Budget: set a threshold (e.g., "total cost in last 3 days > $X") and an action (turn off campaign or notify). [L0309.031]
   - (b) Enhance Performance: if CTR > 2-3% AND CPC < $1, increase daily budget by $50. [L0309.031]
4. Learn the interface before deploying, as wrong settings can disable profitable campaigns. [L0309.031]

**Kill rules based on break-even**

1. Rule 1: If spend equals 2x break-even cost per purchase with zero sales, pause immediately. [L0298.041]
2. Rule 2: If spend exceeds $80 and ROAS falls below break-even ROAS, pause the ad set. [L0298.041]

### Hour-level waste and bidding

- TikTok can spend budget in low-converting hours, unlike Facebook's delivery algorithm. Check spend by hour when TikTok campaigns run. [L0363.053]
- Manual bidding is not needed on TikTok in most cases. The exception is dayparting blasts later on. [L1440.029]

### Resetting accounts and pixels

**When a reset is justified**

- A reset to a new ad account with a new pixel is a fast fix when CPM and CPC stay far outside benchmarks. A new account showed better CPM and CPC after only $6.60 in spend. [L1194.014]
- This conflicts with the rule not to create a new account for each test. Reset only when CPM and CPC stay far outside benchmarks. Do not reset as routine practice between tests. [L1194.014, L0552.025]

**Reset procedure**

1. Create a new ad account with a new pixel. [L1194.015]
2. Launch 10 ad sets, each with one unique video (or one unique image) and a fully broad audience. [L1194.015]
3. Let them spend, then switch off the underperforming ad sets. [L1194.015]
4. When a winning creative appears, move it into its own campaign and duplicate that same creative into several ad sets. [L1194.015]

## Benchmarks, Diagnosis and Scaling TikTok Ads

### Creative First: The Precondition for Scaling

- TikTok depends heavily on creative and much less on the scaling strategy. Self-scaling, CBO and ABO work mainly because the creatives are strong, not because of the method itself. [L0298.037]
- Before scaling, secure high-quality creatives. Either film original content, or replicate competitor content in your own style rather than copying it directly. [L0298.038]
- Running the same ads that already work for a successful product gives no advantage. A test has to differ from the product's original winning approach in angle, platform or funnel. [L0561.011]
- Scale by producing more distinct video creatives, not by simply raising budget. Scaling budget too aggressively with too few creatives stops results quickly. Lower competition is also a reason to focus on creatives. [L1376.002]

### Benchmarks: CPM, CPC, CTR and Conversion

| Metric | Reference or target | Context | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPM | Typically $10-$15 | Costs are rising year over year | L0296.006 |
| CPM scale trigger | Below $10 | Part of the scale-readiness gate | L1375.006 |
| CPC scale trigger | Below $1 | Part of the scale-readiness gate | L1375.006 |
| CTR | Aim for 1.5% | Standard target | L0774.039 |

### Evergreen versus trend content

### Personal creator accounts and product accounts

- Keep one rule apart for personal creator accounts and product accounts. [L0819.027]
- A personal creator account should keep posting consistently. [L0819.027]
- A product or brand account that is not converting should be abandoned, and the product moved to a new account after a short test. [L0819.027]
- Follower count matters little, so a product account with 10,000 to 70,000 followers may be dropped without much loss. [L0819.027]
- Switching an existing account to a different product can cost followers, because followers follow the account's identity and not only the content. [L0657.027]
- Start a new account when the product or niche changes significantly. [L0657.027]

Follower thresholds, with each applying to a different situation:

| Situation | Follower count | Action | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early phase, first base | About 1,000 | Pass this mark with an easy angle first | L0352.003 |
| Established base | About 10,000 | Move to higher-converting product angles and keep testing new ones | L0352.003 |

- The 1,000 and 10,000 marks guide an account that is growing and has a base, while the 10,000 to 70,000 drop rule applies only to a product account that is not converting. [L0352.003, L0819.027]

### General social media growth principles

- Judge an influencer's TikTok performance over several posts rather than a single video, because short-form results rise and fall sharply from one video to the next. [L0479.032]
- A new account gets a broad mix of content, because algorithms do not know what the user wants at first. [L0915.010]
- Train the feed by interacting deliberately with the content type you want to create, such as watching and engaging with similar videos. [L0915.010]
- Buying followers is not recommended, because TikTok can detect purchased or foreign accounts, which can lead to shadowbanning. [L0657.026]
- Purchased followers on Instagram can affect reels at first, but they do not provide lasting reach. [L0657.026]
- Keep testing new angles constantly, since a good video can still break out at around 10,000 followers. [L0352.003]
