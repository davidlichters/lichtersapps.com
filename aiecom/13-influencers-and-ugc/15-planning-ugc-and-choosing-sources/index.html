13.15 Planning UGC and choosing sources

What UGC Is and Why It Matters

  • UGC stands for user-generated content: videos filmed by a real user or creator on a phone, with no professional studio. Such content is often more trusted than polished brand production, so it is a common format for high-performing video ads. [L0707.028]
  • Polished ad-style videos made for TikTok ads got almost no views in the referenced case. Reading the feedback and the zero-view results showed the format was wrong. Switching fully to a UGC style fixed the problem. [L0821.018]

When to Invest in UGC

  • Do not spend £120 or more on UGC creators until cheaper ad tests have shown sales. Use image ads or basic videos first. Pay for expensive UGC, such as Billow-style professional creators, only after product-audience fit is proven. [L0308.024]
  • Outsource full scripted creative production only for products already proven as winners, or for brands with inventory and data. Do not invest in this production for pure dropshipping tests, because clips and footage are easy to source for those. [L0638.040]
  • Buy UGC from marketplaces such as below.app, or hire local creators, only once the product has sales and the store is scaling. In the early phase, use supplier photos instead. [L0661.061]
  • Hire UGC creators or an agency once the product shows sales traction and you cannot make your own videos. [L1171.044]
  • Outsourcing video creation makes sense once money is available but time is scarce. An external creator team trained to produce converting hooks and calls to action usually beats cheap freelance marketplace creatives, which often lack hook and call-to-action skills. [L1376.016]
  • Validate a product with proof of concept before spending heavily. [L1376.017]

Scale-up trigger: owned creatives through UGC

  1. Reach the product's first sales of around $1,000 to $2,000. [L1171.043]
  2. Send the product to content creators or freelancers. [L1171.043]
  3. Ask them to market the product using different marketing angles than the competitors. [L1171.043]
  4. Use the resulting videos as your own creatives. [L1171.043]

Test Cheaply First

  • For a fast test, find the product on Amazon with fast delivery (one to two days), order it, and film UGC as quickly as possible. Fulfilling from Amazon speeds up UGC testing. [L0950.049]
  • Test an unproven product with cheaper options before paying for established creators. Look for cheaper UGC creators on Fiverr or film your own videos first. [L0950.048]
  • Freelance video creation on Fiverr can deliver two to three videos for about 30 to 50 in the lesson's example. Use this option to produce original creatives for testing. [L0638.041]
  • UGC-style TikTok ad videos from Fiverr sellers who research TikTok, YouTube, AliExpress and Alibaba can cost about 20 to 30 USD or less per video. Ask the seller about their research and content before placing an order. [L0363.026]
  • When outsourcing ads or graphics to freelancers, keep control of the ad copy. Give the designer the core benefits, the offer, and the direction, because graphic designers are not e-commerce experts. [L0950.050]

Bootstrap Your Own Content

  • Film your own videos first. A product can be shown by you, a friend, or in a product order from a store, which gives free creative while you test. [L0398.045]
  • A long-form UGC video of about six minutes, where a presenter explains how the product works, is mostly seen on affiliate-marketing products. Film a version yourself with friends, or use a UGC marketplace such as Billo or Fiverr. [L1216.034]
  • Creators can film their own videos with a small set of products from bulk orders instead of you paying for production. [L1376.017]
  • If a supplier offers free products in exchange for content, accept. You get the product and can use the content for product pages and social media. Post it on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube with the product name. [L0808.052]
  • Seasonal products often struggle to get on-season footage once the season has ended, so collect footage in advance. [L1077.053]

Source Creators at Low Cost

Procedure: find low-cost creators

  1. Find creators in your niche on Instagram with roughly 3,000 to 50,000 followers. Many of them will film a single video without a large fee. [L0398.045]
  2. Use a creator-discovery tool such as Modash to find creators with a high engagement rate. [L0398.045]
  3. Message promising creators directly on Instagram. [L0398.045]
  4. Order content through a UGC marketplace that lets you chat with creators, and ask for raw clips rather than a finished video. [L0398.045]
  5. Use your own footage, a friend, or a product order from a store to produce free creative while you test. [L0398.045]

Procedure: find TikTok creators

  1. Search the niche keyword on TikTok, for example fishing. [L0561.034]
  2. Open the accounts that appear and check their views. [L0561.034]
  3. If the largest accounts are too expensive, scroll down until views drop off and hire smaller creators with the same niche. [L0561.034]
  4. Small creators are usually cheaper. [L0561.034]

Compare Sources

Freelance Marketplaces: Fiverr and Upwork

  • Fiverr is a freelancer marketplace where UGC creators can be found by searching UGC. Buyers can review past work, and prices are described as fair. [L1402.011]
  • Fiverr and Upwork can be used to hire video creators. [L0321.018]
  • Fiverr and Upwork can be used to hire video editors and freelance UGC creators when footage and story already exist. [L0561.036]
  • Upwork offers a cheaper skilled-labour alternative. [L0308.025]
  • Fiverr and training a TikTok creator were suggested as sources of UGC talent. [L1214.054]
  • Freelance marketplace creatives often lack hook and call-to-action skills. [L1376.016]

Platform Marketplaces: Billow, Billo and below.app

  • Billow (also spelled Billo or Bilo in the sources) is a complete UGC platform. You define a video order, pay, and then choose from creators who apply. The speaker uses it for ads and says it offers the most options of the platforms shown. [L1402.014, L1077.030, L1214.054]
  • Billo UGC is an alternative for long-form videos, along with Fiverr. [L1216.034]
  • Bilo was once a recommended UGC source, but its quality was described as dropping after it grew. [L1214.054]
  • A UGC marketplace that lets you chat with creators is useful for requesting raw clips. [L0398.045]
  • below.app is a UGC marketplace, to be used only once the product has sales. [L0661.061]

TikTok Creator Marketplace and TikTok Creative Center

  • TikTok Creator Marketplace (creatormarketplace.tiktok.com) lets brands run campaigns that creators apply to. It is used to get UGC made by creators who respond to a brand brief. [L1402.009]
  • The TikTok Creative Marketplace requires a TikTok business account. [L0637.042]
  • Top-performing ads in TikTok Creative Center often use UGC-style videos made by creators or agencies. Many promote dropshipping products from AliExpress, so they are useful leads for sourcing. [L0706.019]

X/Twitter, LinkedIn and Facebook Groups

Procedure: find UGC creators on Twitter (variant A)

  1. Search for ugc or ugc creator. [L0561.035]
  2. Sort results by Top. [L0561.035]
  3. Use the advanced search to narrow results. [L0561.035]
  4. Contact the creators who post UGC services directly. [L0561.035]

Procedure: hire a UGC creator on Twitter (variant B)

  1. Open the Twitter search bar and type a keyword such as UGC. [L1214.047]
  2. Switch to the Latest tab to see recent posts. [L1214.047]
  3. Open promising profiles and check their work, such as an Etsy shop, YouTube channel or TikTok account. [L1214.047]
  4. Review their past UGC videos for editing quality. [L1214.047]
  5. Message them to offer a paid collaboration. [L1214.047]

Procedure: find UGC creators on Twitter (X) (variant C)

  1. Search for UGC content creator. [L1402.013]
  2. Open profiles of people who offer content creation services. [L1402.013]
  3. Check their posts and their Media tab to see past work. [L1402.013]
  4. If the work fits, send them a direct message to agree on terms. [L1402.013]

Other social and sourcing channels named in the source are X/Twitter hashtags such as #ugc and #ugccreator, LinkedIn job posts, and Facebook groups for local actors. [L0637.042]

Agencies and Full Production

  • Companies found by searching for UGC content creator on Google are a UGC option. Shop around and compare them. [L0561.039]
  • Outsourced video production for Facebook ads can work in this order:
    1. Hand the Facebook and Google ad management to an agency. [L0349.031]
    2. The agency sends scripts to content creators. [L0349.031]
    3. The creators shoot the videos based on those scripts. [L0349.031]
    4. An agency editor cuts the footage into finished ads. [L0349.031]
  • Hire an agency to produce UGC once the product shows sales traction and you cannot make your own videos. [L1171.044]

Local Creators, Micro-influencers and Models

Procedure: local platform production

  1. Send the product to a local platform that creates custom videos from user-generated content. [L0492.047]
  2. Hire several models to show unboxing, use and comfort. [L0492.047]
  3. Find an influencer from the target audience to show how to use the product. [L0492.047]
  4. Use the resulting videos as social proof for paid ads. [L0492.047]

Procedure: micro-influencer footage

  1. Identify micro-influencers who create content you would like to use. [L1077.029]
  2. Reach out to them with an offer. [L1077.029]
  3. Send merchandise in return for video footage and b-roll that you can use on your own social accounts. [L1077.029, L1077.053]
  4. Confirm you have secured the footage rights before relying on the partnership for content. [L1077.029]

Micro-influencers can be given merchandise in exchange for video footage and b-roll for the brand's own accounts. [L1077.053]
