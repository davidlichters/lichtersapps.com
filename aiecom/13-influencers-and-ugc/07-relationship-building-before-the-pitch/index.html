13.7 Relationship building before the pitch

Dream 100 Engagement Before Outreach

  • Rule of 10: Before contacting a Dream 100 prospect directly, aim for at least 10 engagements with their content, such as comments, replies or other interactions. Treat this as a guideline and use judgment rather than a hard rule. If the prospect produces content less often, reduce the requirement proportionally. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0202.002, L0525.019]
  • Reduced threshold for periodic publishers: The source gives two reduction rules:
    • Use the proportional reduction for any prospect who produces content less often. [L0202.002]
    • For prospects who publish only periodically, about 5 engagements before reaching out is enough. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.020]
  • Back catalogue counts: Engagements on older content count toward the threshold, and working through a prospect's back catalogue of posts speeds up reaching 10. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.021]
  • The threshold is a default: If a prospect replies to your emails and asks about your business, direct contact can start much earlier. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.022]
  • Engage only where you add value: Engage with a Dream 100 prospect only when their audience is one you can genuinely help. If you cannot add real value for that audience, skip the prospect rather than commenting just to be seen. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0453.022]
  • Relationships create opportunities: In high-ticket dropshipping, building relationships with people in your market uncovers many opportunities that stay hidden if the business stays isolated. Starting conversations makes you known to them, and every business in the space should do it. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0529.002]

Engagement Routine

  1. Interact with each prospect's content in their communities and forums. [L0202.003]
  2. Provide helpful responses to community members. [L0202.003]
  3. Continue until you reach at least 10 meaningful interactions. [L0202.003]
  4. Then proceed to direct outreach. [L0202.003]

(Older procedure, 2021-22.)

A simple daily Dream 100 routine, in order: (Older procedure, 2021-22.) [L0529.007]

  1. Open YouTube and check whether the creators you follow or subscribe to have posted new videos you can watch.
  2. Check your inbox for newsletters from the email lists you have joined and reply to any that are relevant.
  3. Visit the Facebook groups or forums you belong to and review the day's new posts.
  4. Where you have relevant knowledge of the market, products or space, write a short helpful paragraph on a few posts. You do not need to answer every post.
  • Low-effort option: Leave brief, sincere comments on a creator's new video, such as praising a specific point or asking a follow-up question. These take little focused thinking and still make you visible to the people in your market. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0529.012]
  • Patience: Dream 100 activity pays off over the long term, not as a quick win. Do not rush it or expect results within days. The only requirement is to keep doing it regularly. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0529.008]

Public Appreciation and Shout-Outs

  • Praise without strings: Public appreciation, such as writing about a creator's work, linking to it, or building on it and sharing the result, builds relationships without requiring a reply. Expecting a response creates pressure, so give praise without strings attached. [L1343.020]
  • Shout-outs grow the brand's account: Influencer shout-outs on a creator's account can grow the brand's own Instagram account. As that account grows, creators may start contacting the brand directly, which becomes an inbound source of partnerships. [L1379.051]
  • Name influencers in your content: Mention relevant influencers by name in your content, since people like being talked about and praised. A featured influencer is much more likely to share the content than to respond to a cold request. [L0899.015]

Procedure for featured influencers: [L0899.014]

  1. Build a Dream 100 list of the most relevant influencers in your niche.
  2. When a post mentions one of them, send them a direct message.
  3. Praise their account, tell them they were featured in your content, include the link and ask for a share. Do not send the same generic message to all influencers.

Template for the message to a featured influencer: [L0899.016]

Hi [Influencer name], I really love your account and your content about [their topic]. We featured you in our new guide [guide title] on our website, and I wanted to make sure you saw it. Here is the link: [URL]. If you think it's useful for your audience, I would really appreciate a share. Thanks, [Your name]

[L0899.016]

Thanks and Gratitude

  • Thank every creator: Thank influencers in every case, whether you pay them, send free product or work on a gifted basis. Building a long-term relationship makes creators more willing to help again later, even for free. [L0782.013]
  • Gratitude pays off later: Expressing gratitude and aiming for a long-term relationship pays off later. A creator who likes working with you may promote your product again, even for free. [L0792.010]

Contributor thank-you email, sent on the day the article goes live: [L0899.018]

Subject: Your article is live

Hi [Contributor name],

Thank you again for contributing to our article [Article title]. It is now live here: [URL]

If it fits your channels, I would really appreciate it if you could share it with your audience, for example on your blog, your Facebook page or group, or your website. Nothing mandatory, just a casual request.

Thanks again,
[Your name]

[L0899.018]

Mentions and Roundups

  • Best of the interwebs procedure: [L1421.018] (Older procedure, 2021-22.)
    1. Publish the collection and name each featured creator or site.
    2. After publishing, contact each featured owner or creator by email, using the outreach template.
    3. Tell them they were featured and that they are welcome to share the piece with their audience, email list or website.
    4. Expect that not all will share, but some will, and use the contact to build relationships in your market.
  • Roundups leverage others' attention: Best of the interwebs and expert roundups build relationships in the market and leverage other people's attention. This helps before you have earned attention yourself. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1421.027]
  • Attention matrix before roundups: Before creating roundup content, prepare a list of the experts in your market (an attention matrix) so you know whom to contact for quotes. Use this list as the source of contributors for expert roundup posts. (Older step, 2021-22.) [L1461.027]
  • Tagging brands in trend videos: Tagging a brand in a trend-based, meme-style video can earn a reply from the brand and free publicity if the video goes viral. Tag only when the content is strong, since brands mostly respond to big views. [L1429.041]

Community and Seeding Through Relationships

  • Community creates unpaid content: Build a sense of community and genuine care for creators so they feel close to the brand. Creators with a close connection often post unpaid content for the brand, which adds value beyond the paid deliverables. [L0783.009]
  • Open, ongoing relationships: The goal of outreach is a friendly, ongoing relationship in which creators feel comfortable sharing their own content ideas and bouncing ideas off the brand. This openness produces the best and most worthwhile content. [L0783.021]
  • Earn product through relationships: Celebrity wear was earned through relationships, not paid for. Attend artists' events, such as concerts, buy an extra ticket for backstage access where possible, meet their team, and explain your vision, who you are and what makes your designs different. [L1438.040]

Trust and Transparency Before the Pitch

  • Show numbers first: Build trust before the pitch by showing numbers. Without your own track record, relevant case studies from other industries can demonstrate that the approach works, but they must not be presented as your own results. [L0789.024]
  • No cold requests: Influencers rarely accept a deal based on a cold request. Build the relationship first with a call, and bring a prepared promotion script and brief, since influencers do not know how to promote, use scarcity or trigger emotion. [L0789.053]
  • Full transparency from the start: Be fully transparent about what the collaboration looks like from the beginning to build a solid foundation. Stay flexible in how you work together, especially with larger influencers, and frame the deal as mutually beneficial. [L0792.005]

The Partnership Call

  • Format: The call can be a phone call, a Zoom call or a Skype call. The platform matters less than agreeing on a time and having the conversation. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.025]
  • Purpose: The first partnership call is a getting-to-know-you conversation, not a sales call. Because outcomes are hard to predict, no fixed script is needed. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.026]
  • Core question: Ask how you can work together to benefit both businesses. You share an audience, your product is relevant to their customers, and their content is relevant to yours. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.027]
  • Preparation: [L0525.028] (Older procedure, 2021-22.)
    1. Review the prospect's content and learn what they do.
    2. Check whether they promote other products, run affiliate deals, or sell their own products.
    3. Use that knowledge to suggest suitable partnership options on the call.
  • Call after following for a while: Calling after you have followed a prospect's content for some time makes the conversation easier, because you already understand their business and topics. A cold call with little research is much harder. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0525.029]
