13.20 Tracking, measuring and iterating

Measuring Influencer Results: Principles and Priorities

  • Tracking traffic from influencer partnerships is the most critical step in determining profitability. It shows whether a partnership returns enough to continue long-term. Some influencers become so profitable that they stay active advertising partners for multiple years. [L0183.010]
  • Track referrals from brand ambassadors as their own traffic source, separate from paid social and search. This traffic is expected to be of higher quality than cold paid traffic, and it can feed back into the top of the funnel to create more customers. [L0676.019]
  • Judge TikTok performance over several posts rather than a single post, because engagement on influencer content can rise and fall sharply: attention spans are short and trends move quickly, so expect results to fluctuate. [L0480.029]
  • Organic Instagram posting that reached over 200 followers within a week signals content interest, but it is not sales evidence on its own. [L1016.065]
  • A cited survey claims that 86% of businesses using influencer marketing effectively report an immediate or long-term effect on their brand. The source and the exact definition of "effective" are not stated, so treat this figure as a rough indication only. [L0786.013]

Platform Analytics and Engagement Data

  • Check the built-in analytics of the platform for each influencer post:
    • Instagram Insights
    • Facebook Insights
    • Twitter Analytics
    • YouTube Analytics
  • Record for each post: comments, shares, likes and interaction rate. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  • Ask each influencer to send their engagement figures (comments, shares, likes, interaction rate) for the campaign posts, so you can compare them with your own platform data. [L0630.001, L0786.002, L0786.004]

Influencer Platforms and Campaign Analytics

  • Influencer marketing platforms such as Influency, Aspire IQ and Tracker provide detailed campaign analytics, including reach, engagement and metrics specific to each collaboration. [L0630.006]
  • These platforms are most useful when the whole campaign is managed through them. [L0786.007]
  • If the campaign ran through such a platform, pull its analytics for reach, engagement and collaboration-specific metrics. [L0630.001, L0786.002]

Brand Mentions and Audience Feedback

  • Track brand mentions over the campaign period, either with a social listening tool or by manually searching for the brand, keyword, hashtag or campaign name. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  • Survey the audience and collect feedback (surveys, social media polls) to find out where they first heard of the influencer and how they perceive the campaign. [L0630.001, L0786.002]

Sales, Conversion and Unique Codes

  • Track sales and conversion rate for each campaign. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  • Give each creator a unique discount code, ideally built from their name, so that sales can be traced back to that creator. A 25% code was affordable at that brand's margins. Any unique code, even free shipping, serves the same tracking purpose. [L0790.094]
  • Coupon codes unique to each influencer track campaign results and strengthen the relationship with the creator. Any coupon works, even free shipping, as long as it is tied to a specific influencer. [L0790.056]
  • Use one identical spelling of each creator name across all ads. Consistent creator names allow reports per creator and make creator commission payments easier to match. [L0885.006]
  • An influencer post that does not produce a sale can still be valuable if it earns a follower, an email or a visit that can be retargeted. Plan this fallback before paying for the post. [L0790.061]

Campaign Evaluation Sequence

Measure each influencer campaign in this order:

  1. Check the platform's built-in analytics for each influencer post (Instagram Insights, Facebook Insights, Twitter Analytics, YouTube Analytics). [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  2. Record comments, shares, likes and interaction rate. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  3. Ask the influencer to send their engagement numbers as well. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  4. Monitor brand and hashtag mentions with a listening tool, or search manually for the brand, keyword or campaign name. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  5. Pull detailed campaign metrics from the influencer marketing platform, if one was used. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  6. Track sales and conversion metrics. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  7. Survey the audience. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  8. Compare before and after the campaign. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  9. Compare results with your other marketing channels. [L0630.001, L0786.002]

Deciding Which Partners to Repeat

  • Use the results and audience insights from each campaign to decide which influencer strategies to repeat. [L0868.008]
  • Choose future partners and formats based on what has resonated most with your audience. [L0868.008]
  • Use the sequence above on every campaign before deciding, so that comparisons across creators rest on the same measurements. [L0630.001, L0786.002]
  • Influencers whose traffic and sales prove profitable can remain active advertising partners for multiple years. [L0183.010]
