13.14 Paid placements, sponsorships and joint promotions

Why Creators Sell Ad Space

  • Content creators and large-scale platforms earn revenue by placing advertisements across their websites, YouTube channels, Instagram feeds and audio podcasts. [L0183.003]
  • Offer advertisers a cost-free seven-day evaluation period to check whether the placement delivers measurable results before they sign a binding agreement. This appeals to most companies actively searching for additional advertising opportunities. [L0183.003]

Testing a Placement Before You Commit

Procedure for each prospect that runs paid ads:

  1. Complete your Dream 100 Worksheet, identifying people and companies in your niche across all influencer categories.
  2. Work through the list and segment out only those that run paid ads.
  3. Locate an "advertise" or "contact us" button on their site, or contact them directly via their preferred social channel.
  4. Send your outreach message proposing a free one-week trial.
  5. During the trial week, track traffic using UTM parameters in Google Analytics to measure sales generated.
  6. After one week, review your Google Analytics data for that traffic source to see sales and revenue.
  7. Compare the cost of the ad to the sales generated and decide whether to sign a contract. [L0183.007]

Advertising Early, Before Relationship Building

  • Take advertising opportunities with Dream 100 prospects early and directly, without waiting to complete relationship building, provided the cost is within budget. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0202.004]
  • Paid advertising can be offered before you reach the 10-engagement threshold. If a site shows an advertise-with-us option, you can buy ad space right away, and the purchase makes the owner aware of your business. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.030]

Booking Advertising on a Partner Site

Procedure:

  1. Find the advertise-with-us link, which is often in a footer or on a contact page of a forum, blog or website.
  2. Submit the contact form with your details.
  3. Wait for the rate card, which lists prices per placement, such as a banner ad or a sidebar ad.
  4. Compare the prices with your budget.
  5. If you can afford it, start the booking conversation and buy the placement. If you cannot, keep engaging in other ways and return to it once it fits your budget. [L0525.032]

Older advice, 2021-2022.

Banner Ads on Forums and Blogs

  • Forums and blogs often sell banner ad space. You pay the owner to display your banner, which places your brand in front of the same audience every day. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.012]

Podcast and YouTube Sponsorships

  • You pay for a sponsored episode or a sponsor mention, often read at the start of the episode. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.015]
  • Podcasters and YouTubers in your niche should be added to the Dream 100 list as well. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.015]
  • A sponsored YouTube video or podcast episode can stay online and rank for three to five years, delivering traffic and brand awareness long after it is published. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.016]
  • Sponsorships like this do not necessarily have to be expensive. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.016]
  • Example: a seller of wine coolers can add wine podcasts to the Dream 100 list and offer to sponsor an episode. Thousands of wine enthusiasts may hear that episode over the years, so the sponsorship reaches a relevant audience. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.017]
  • Pitch two placements to the same partner in one outreach, such as a podcast guest slot and a guest blog post, because one contact can open two traffic sources. [L0528.027]
  • Influencer traffic works better through advertising on niche authority blogs or websites than through paying random individuals in the niche, because authority sites bring more relevant readers. [L0597.010]

Paid and Free Product Promotion

  • Paid product promotion: some prospects openly publish rates for mentioning your product in a video or sending an email blast to their list. You pay the rate for the placement you choose, so the cost depends on what they offer. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.006]
  • Free product promotion: the prospect promotes your products to their audience or email list at no cost, usually because they like them. In return you promote their channel, business or content to your audience, for example with a recommendation or a blog post about them. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0525.005]

Flat Fee Versus Commission

  • A flat-fee ad placement can be cheaper than a commission deal. Example: a banner that costs a couple hundred USD per month but brings over 10,000 USD in monthly sales. Pay for placement first when you can afford it. Older advice, 2020. [L0961.073]

Product-for-Post and Celebrity Video Deals

  • A sponsored YouTube video can be paid in product, for example a board valued at up to $800 sent to a smaller creator with high views and engagement. In return the creator makes a video, posts it to the channel and gives a shout-out naming the brand. [L0528.013]
  • A celebrity can be paid to record a video that is sent to you rather than posted on their channel. You then post it on your own social media and website, so the celebrity's name and face lend credibility to your store. [L0528.015]

Endorsements and Specialist Reviews

  • Endorsements from recognized authorities or celebrities in your market, such as PGA Tour golfers endorsing golf products, provide credible third-party approval. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0295.008]
  • A specialist such as a chiropractor or doctor can review the product on camera; a 30 to 40 second review is enough. Pay the specialist and use the review on the site, landing pages and social media. [L0871.055]
  • Only ask a specialist for a review if the product quality is good, since their name will be attached to the product. A simple product with a clear problem is a good fit for this approach. [L0871.056]

Meme Pages and Repeating Promotions

  • Paid placements on TikTok meme pages can be used as a repeating promotion for a product, run about every one to two months. [L0819.026]
  • Launch a new batch when the audience gained from the previous batch has been used up and the follower base starts to recycle. [L0819.026]
  • Meme pages can post paid content for brands without issue, while influencers may refuse if their followers expect them to appear in the video. Use meme pages to repurpose UGC. [L1214.053]

Joint Promotions and Collaborations

  • Joint promotions let you get your message in front of another creator's audience while they reach yours. Most markets are large enough that this mutual exchange works without taking customers from each other. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0478.026]
  • Joint promotion formats include content collaborations, giveaways and competitions. A podcast can be sponsored for one episode, which places your link and product in front of its listeners. Older advice, 2021-2022. [L0478.027]
  • Collaborate on social media with suppliers, blogs, forums and Facebook groups in your niche by tagging them or working together on posts, which brings the right reach and followers early. Older advice, 2021. [L0801.129]

Free Resources for Influencers

  • Offer influencers a free training course, guide or educational resource to promote to their audience. This works as an alternative to affiliate commissions: influencers build credibility by offering exclusive free value, their followers gain access to the resource, and you gain traffic and backlinks. [L0226.045]

Lead Magnet Placements

  • Lead magnet traffic can come from blog banner ads, sponsorships of Facebook groups and forums, Facebook ads aimed at people interested in the niche or its events, and influencer posts. Test these placements with a free offer instead of only using direct discount offers. [L1076.008]
  • The lead magnet must match the site, community or influencer where it appears. An offer that fits the audience gets opt-ins, while an off-topic offer gets clicks that never convert to sales. [L1076.009]
