16.19 Retargeting existing customers and repeat purchases

Why Existing Customers Are Worth Targeting

  • Not retargeting existing customers is a major missed opportunity, because selling to an existing customer is much easier than acquiring a new one (older, 2021-22). Relevant upsells and resells should be promoted through ads to past buyers (older, 2021-22). [L0563.027]
  • After a first sale, retargeting and repeat email campaigns bring the same customer back for second and third purchases (older, 2021-22). Repeat sales are cheaper to win than new ones, so their profit margins are usually higher (older, 2021-22). [L0771.041]
  • Second and third purchases from existing customers usually generate more profit than the first sale, which makes existing-customer retargeting a key driver of customer lifetime value (older, 2021-22). Combine it with email marketing for the best effect (older, 2021-22). [L0563.030]
  • Do not assume existing customers will never buy again. Not all of them will, but some will, so test repeat offers rather than dropping them from campaigns (older, 2021-22). [L0563.032]
  • People who already use the product have already decided and rarely read acquisition ads, so repeated acquisition ads for current customers waste budget. [L1261.119]

Building Customer Audiences

  • Leaving existing customers out of retargeting means missing repeat sales. Build a separate customer audience and give it its own campaigns (older, 2021-22). [L1390.004]
  • Build a customer list from customer email addresses and names (older, 2021-22). Upload the list to the ad network, which matches it to users on its platform (older, 2021-22). [L0563.028]
  • Wait until the list reaches the network's minimum size before using it. Google Display needs 100 people (older, 2021-22). [L0563.028]
  • Show ads to this audience only when you have a relevant offer or content for these customers (older, 2021-22). [L0563.028]

Customer audience procedure (older, 2021-22):

  1. Build a customer list from customer email addresses and names. [L0563.028]
  2. Upload the list to the ad network, which matches it to users on its platform. [L0563.028]
  3. Wait until the list reaches the network's minimum size before using it; Google Display needs 100 people. [L0563.028]
  4. Show ads only when you have a relevant offer or content for these customers. [L0563.028]

Excluding Customers from Acquisition

  • If you have enough volume, build a customer audience and exclude it from new-customer acquisition campaigns (older, 2021-22). Customers also need their own retargeting campaigns matched to their stage in the customer journey, not first-purchase offers (older, 2021-22). [L1390.021]
  • Showing first-purchase retargeting ads to existing customers wastes spend, because they rarely buy the same product right away (older, 2021-22). Serve customers separate messages based on where they are in the journey (older, 2021-22). [L1390.022]
  • Target only first-time buyers by excluding customers with a purchase frequency above one. [L1243.037]
  • Use the same exclusion for the thank-you video so repeat buyers do not receive it again. [L1243.048]

Timing After Delivery

  • Timing for existing-customer ads can range from soon after the first purchase to six months later or longer (older, 2021-22). The right timing depends on the offer, such as an upsell or a reorder (older, 2021-22). [L0563.029]
  • The repeat-purchase funnel must start only after the customer has received the product, not when the order is placed. [L1243.035]
  • If products have different shipping times, separate the audiences by brand or product so each one is triggered by its own delivery. [L1243.035]

Goodwill and Thank-You Videos

  • Recent buyers get a goodwill video ad on reach and frequency, at around 2 impressions per day for 7 days. The goal is to be seen, not to sell, so conversion objectives are the wrong choice here. [L0649.022]
  • The thank-you video ad for customers in the first 7 days is set to low frequency, roughly twice a day per person. [L1076.066]
  • Customers from the last 180 days see the same content and promotions over time. [L1076.066]
  • Goodwill thank-you ad pattern: a short personal video that thanks the buyer, names the store, and says what to expect next. It works because it builds the relationship after purchase and gets high engagement. [L0649.068]
    • Example one: "Thank you for ordering from us, here is what happens next and how we will look after you." [L0649.068]
    • Example two: "Welcome to the family, here is what to expect from your first week with us." [L0649.068]

Thank-you video script for first-time buyers. Recorded by the store owner on camera and posted as a Facebook ad after the product has been delivered. Repeat-Purchase Funnel

  • Repeat buyers were cited as converting at 60% to 70%, compared with an industry average of 2% to 3% for new customers. Targeting past buyers is therefore presented as the main source of repeat sales. [L1243.036]

Repeat-purchase funnel procedure:

  1. Step 1, after delivery: Have the owner send a short thank-you video through Facebook, then follow up with an email containing a coupon code for the next purchase. Target only first-time buyers by excluding customers with a purchase frequency above one. [L1243.037]
  2. Step 2, 7 to 14 days after delivery: Run a cross-sell campaign with dynamic product ads. Set up pairings in the product catalog beforehand, such as when a customer buys product X, show product Y. Then send an email that recalls the coupon code from step 1. [L1243.038]

Cross-Sell and Upgrade Ads

  • Set up cross-sell pairings in the product catalog before launch, so that a purchase of product X triggers product Y (older, 2021-22 pairing logic is the same as the current funnel). [L1243.038]
  • Example: customers who bought a product a year ago are shown an ad for a newer model, presented as an upgrade to the product they already like (older, 2021-22). Some of them will buy again, so the offer is worth testing (older, 2021-22). [L0563.031]

Channel Split: Acquisition vs. Retention

  • Use paid ads to acquire new customers, and use SMS and email to retarget existing buyers with new products or better offers. [L1431.051]
  • Retargeting existing customers avoids paying again to reach them on Facebook. [L1431.051]
  • Use retention and reactivation messages separately from the acquisition campaign. [L1261.119]
