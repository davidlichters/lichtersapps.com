16.22 Measuring and optimizing retargeting performance

Core KPIs

  • Retargeting ROAS is revenue from the ads divided by ad spend:
ROAS = revenue from the ads / ad spend

[L0360.057]

  • Facebook Dynamic Remarketing and Google Remarketing should keep a conversion rate of at least 2.5% as a baseline. These are the warmest audiences and should outperform cold traffic. (Older advice, 2022.) [L0272.041]
  • Define business measurables for each horizon: customer count, average order value, cost per acquisition or cost per sale, repeat purchase rate, and number of products sold. Choose the measurables that directly drive revenue and profit figures. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1519.004]

Sample size and attribution caveats

  • With fewer than 300-400 qualified people in the audience, results appear random. Larger audiences show clearer conversion patterns. [L0276.057]
  • Retargeting audiences from Google and from Facebook or Instagram can be built from site visitors. Include them when calculating the conversion rate for paid traffic. (Older advice, 2022.) [L0963.138]
  • Traffic from days earlier can convert later through remarketing and abandoned cart emails. Do not judge a campaign on the last day's orders alone. (Older advice, 2021.) [L1279.070]
  • Mobile shoppers often add products to the cart on a phone and finish the purchase later on desktop. Retargeting brings them back, so mobile traffic should not be judged only by immediate sales. (Older advice, 2019.) [L1275.068]
  • If retargeting converts worse than cold-traffic campaigns in an account, the result is unusual and should be investigated: check audience size, lookback windows, and tracking errors. [L0867.006]

Testing retargeting against cold audiences

  • Retargeting is often the cheapest audience, but this does not always hold. One advertiser found that a retargeting audience cost 10 to 20 percent more than a cold audience of similar businesses in a similar niche that competitors had not yet targeted. Test cold audiences against retargeting audiences rather than assuming. [L0643.031]
  • Both results apply at once. Warm audiences should outperform cold traffic on conversion rate [L0272.041], but cost per result must be tested, because retargeting can cost more than a cold audience [L0643.031].

Diagnosing weak results

Signal First check Source
Retargeting underperforms How qualified the cold traffic is, and what the copy in the retargeting ads says (older advice, 2020) [L1277.092]
High click-through rate, zero conversions Traffic quality is usually fine. The bottleneck is the store, typically at product page level. Fix the conversion side before changing the audience. [L1083.011]
Retargeting converts worse than cold traffic Audience size, lookback windows, tracking errors [L0867.006]
  • The quality of the remarketing audience depends entirely on the quality of the traffic that creates it. The best remarketers are people who arrived through well-optimized cold traffic campaigns. [L0276.009]
  • Better cold traffic, such as traffic from Google Shopping, improves warm and hot retargeting results. Retargeting only works with the quality of the first visits that feed it. [L1083.013]
  • Keep Meta ads running for remarketing at a small budget even when they have not yet produced conversions. Remarketing only performs well when the initial traffic was qualified, so weak traffic from Google also weakens remarketing. [L0837.054]

Learning from non-buyers

  1. Create a custom audience of visitors who did not buy.
  2. Retarget them with a short video in which you ask why they did not buy and what would change their mind.
  3. Ask them to comment with the reason.
  4. Use the answers to improve the product, price or page, so more visitors convert.

[L0643.034]

Reviewing and improving ads on a schedule

  • Track remarketing ROAS and units sold as the retargeting KPIs, because more organic visitors grow the remarketing list. Compare them with the earlier month to see whether the growth is working. [L0941.080]
  • Review results across accumulated days rather than the last day's orders, since delayed conversions from earlier traffic also count. [L1279.070, L0941.080]

Scaling budgets

  1. Check that the audience is large enough for the result to mean something. Below 300-400 qualified people, results appear random. [L0276.057]
  2. Check the return. A retargeting campaign should reach a ROAS of at least 10 (10x) before its budget is increased. [L0360.057]
  3. Keep the Meta remarketing budget small until conversions appear, and only increase it once the campaign has passed the ROAS threshold. [L0837.054, L0360.057]
