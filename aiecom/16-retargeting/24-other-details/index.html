Retargeting Principles

  • Retargeting covers several ad types. Each type needs its own setup, and audiences should be segmented so every group sees the message that fits where it is in the buying process. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1497.006]

Retargeting Setup Sequence (Older Advice, 2021-22)

Work through these nine follow-up actions in this order:

  1. Set up all required pixels and tracking codes from Google Ads, Facebook and Google Analytics. [L0493.001]
  2. Build the relevant retargeting audiences in Google Ads and Facebook. [L0493.001]
  3. Plan the retargeting and email strategy before building anything. [L0493.001]
  4. Identify email opt-in opportunities across the site. [L0493.001]
  5. Review the email list and its segmentation structure. [L0493.001]
  6. Plan the email sequences for post-purchase and each opt-in. [L0493.001]
  7. Review and update the abandoned cart sequence. [L0493.001]
  8. Build the first 30-day master flow. [L0493.001]
  9. Replicate the master flow for each new entry point. [L0493.001]

Meta Account Setup

Set up a new Meta account in this order:

  1. Create a free Meta Business Manager at business.facebook.com. [L0649.061]
  2. Create a Facebook page for the business. [L0649.061]
  3. Create an Instagram page for the business. [L0649.061]
  4. Install the Meta pixel on the Shopify store. [L0649.061]
  5. Turn on auto advanced matching in the Meta account. [L0649.061]
  6. Create the audiences listed below in Business Manager. [L0649.061]
  7. Create the campaigns. If no lead generation or testing is running yet, start with hot dynamic, hot static, customers goodwill and promotions. [L0649.061]
  8. Fill each ad set with 3-4 ads that look similar to the creative examples. [L0649.061]

Landing Pages by Audience

  • Speak to different target audiences with separate landing pages when they have different needs or experience levels, for example beginner, intermediate and advanced. [L0918.016]
  • Match each landing page to the messaging of the ad that brings that audience in. This increases relevance and conversion. [L0918.016]

Traffic Sources and Affiliate Offers

Amazon Associate stores

  • Do not send traffic from Google Shopping, Google Performance Max or search ads to an Amazon Associate store. It is not allowed and can get the account banned. [L0923.023]
  • Use Amazon Associate stores only for retargeting people who already know your brand, because batch-imported products get almost no organic traffic. [L0923.023]

Raw affiliate links

  • Never run paid traffic to a raw affiliate tracking link, such as a long ClickBank hop link. Facebook dislikes affiliate promotion and the account can be lost. [L0923.028]
  • Instead, write a review or informative blog post on your Shopify store, add the affiliate link there, and retarget the blog post visitors. [L0923.028]

Display Placements

  • Remarketing display ads can appear on unrelated websites because they follow where the visitor goes next. (Older advice, 2021.) [L0968.065]
  • Unrelated placements are normal and are not an error on their own. (Older advice, 2021.) [L0968.065]

Video Production

  • Video editing is not a skill a store owner needs to learn. Freelance editors can cut a video cheaply, and at a rate of one or two videos per month the cost stays low. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1424.010]
