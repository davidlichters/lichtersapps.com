16.10 Dynamic product ads on Meta

Retargeting Window by Product Price

  • Dynamic product ad retargeting window depends on product price. Rule of thumb: [L0510.040]
Product price Retargeting window
Under $100 7 days
$100 to $500 14 days
$500 to $1,000 30 days
Over $1,000 Longer window
  • For most advertisers, 30 days is a suitable starting point. [L0510.040, L0510.042]
  • Test the windows. The longer the sales cycle, the longer the retargeting window should be. [L0510.040]

Setup Requirements

  • Dynamic retargeting shows people the exact products they viewed on the site. [L1203.025]
  • It requires the Facebook pixel on the site and an uploaded product feed. [L1203.025]
  • It is the fourth campaign type to launch. [L1203.025]

Format and Creative

  • The usual dynamic retargeting format is a carousel that shows the viewed product and related accessories. The product and price are visible, so people recognize what they looked at, which makes them more likely to click. [L0510.043]
  • Dynamic product ads are the main Meta remarketing format. They show the products a visitor actually viewed on the store, with product images, descriptions and text filled in automatically per visitor. They run across Facebook, Instagram and Messenger. [L1020.008]
  • Example: a jewelry store visitor views three specific rings or necklaces on the site and then leaves. Later, on Instagram, the visitor sees those three exact products in an ad with a Shop now button that leads back to them. [L1020.009]

Improving Dynamic Product Ads

  1. Review the dynamic product ads every two weeks and ask what can be improved. [L0510.042]
  2. Set the audience duration by product price and sales cycle, usually starting at 30 days. [L0510.042, L0510.040]
  3. Use dynamic text in the primary text that shows the product name and price. [L0510.042]
  4. Add a price overlay to the image to make the price visible. [L0510.042]
  5. If the audience is small, use link clicks as the optimization event instead of website conversions. [L0510.042]

Landing Pages

  • Mistake: sending paid traffic to the homepage loses buyers. Each product in a multi-product ad should link to its own product sales page where the customer can enter payment, not to the store homepage. [L0643.033]
  • Dynamic multi-product ads were a fairly new format, about six to twelve months old at the time. Facebook's automatic ordering of products by best sales was being phased out, so do not rely on it to show your top seller first. [L0643.065]

Cross-Device Behavior

  • Facebook can recognize the same shopper across devices. A visit on a work desktop can lead to a retargeting ad on a personal phone later. [L0510.067]
  • Results are less predictable for B2B products with longer sales cycles, because the ad may be seen at a different time and on a different device than the original visit. [L0510.067]

Remarketing Workflow

  1. A qualified visitor arrives at the store through a targeted search such as Google or Bing and views a product page. [L1020.006]
  2. The visitor leaves the store without buying. [L1020.006]
  3. Meta Ads Manager shows the visitor an ad for the product or products they looked at. [L1020.006]
  4. The visitor clicks the ad and is sent back to the exact page they were viewing. [L1020.006]

Combining with Other Ad Types

  • Older advice (2019, so outdated): use Facebook remarketing with a simple dynamic product ad group that retargets visitors who came from Google. Keep the daily budget small, such as $5 per day. [L1002.052]
