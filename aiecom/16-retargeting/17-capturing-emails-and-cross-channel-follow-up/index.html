16.17 Capturing emails and cross-Channel follow-Up

Exit-Intent and Opt-In Capture

  • Capturing email addresses from bounced visits, meaning visitors who leave without purchasing, gives a second chance to convert them and reveals customer hesitations and preferences. [L0154.001]
  • Collect email addresses from every visitor who reads content or opts in, so you can follow up later. Email follow-up is what makes peak-season sales strong. (Older: 2018.) [L1274.012]
  • Set up an email pop-up to capture visitors who are not ready to buy. This extends the relationship beyond the visit. [L1136.027]
  • A browse abandonment feedback survey is an optional extra channel. It is not a required part of collecting customer feedback. [L0682.028]
  • Set up a browse abandonment survey email only if the store already tracks browse abandonment. [L0682.028]

Lead Magnets

  • Run paid ads to a lead magnet landing page with an email capture form. Offer a piece of content or an ebook sample in exchange for the email address. [L0789.037]
  • Use this approach to grow the follower base and the email list at the same time. [L0789.037]

Email Lists and Invisible Audiences

  • Audiences fall into two groups:
    • Email lists: opted-in subscribers, for example in Klaviyo.
    • Invisible lists: platform audiences built from site visits, purchases and social engagement. They include people who never opted in.
  • You need both, because only a small share of visitors ever give their email address. [L1204.006]

Uploading Customer Lists to Ad Platforms

Procedure for adding your Shopify email list to remarketing (2024):

  1. Export your customer email list from Shopify. [L0965.046]
  2. In both Google Ads and Meta Ads, create a customer list audience from that file. The list may include people who have not bought yet. [L0965.046]
  3. Upload the list to both platforms. [L0965.046]
  4. In your remarketing ad group, stack the new customer list together with your website visitor audience so both are included. [L0965.046]

Procedure for improving Facebook remarketing performance (older: 2022):

  1. Set Shopify data sharing to maximum. [L1113.064]
  2. Upload the Klaviyo list of opted-in people who have not bought yet to a Facebook custom audience. [L1113.064]
  3. Add that audience to your remarketing campaigns. [L1113.064]
  4. Check frequency, and reduce spend if the same people see the same ads repeatedly. [L1113.064]

Syncing Email With Ads

  • Sync your ads with your emails so that everyone in the broadcast email audience sees the offer message, including people who do not open email. [L0757.028]
  • Post each email promotion or piece of content to Facebook and run it as an ad at the same time. This way the list and the audiences see the same message, which builds the relationship and moves people closer to buying. [L1076.073]
  • Run remarketing ads on Meta and Google, and email the visitor audience, to bring back people who visited the store. Use pixel-based audience data to build these lists. [L1115.089]

Retargeting Visitors and Abandoners

  • Retarget visitors who did not buy with ads and email campaigns. Use messages that follow up on the page they viewed. [L1016.045]
  • Follow remarketing visitors and abandoned carts from two angles at once: automated emails in Klaviyo and personal outreach by the owner. (Older: 2022.) [L1092.100]
  • Set up retargeting for visitors who arrive from Pinterest, so they can be reached again after leaving the site. [L1136.027]
  • Retargeting is a two-part development stage. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L0414.039]
    • Part one: retargeting ads on Google, Facebook and YouTube.
    • Part two: personal communication through email, SMS, push notifications and Messenger.
  • Smaller high-ticket businesses often neglect personal communication. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L0414.039]
  • Three main follow-up strategies exist: retargeting ads, email marketing and SMS marketing. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L1497.004]
    • Retargeting ads run mainly on a few networks.
    • Email is the classic follow-up channel.
    • SMS is an older channel that can still deliver strong returns when used well.

Personal Follow-Up Messages

  • Use this formula for personal follow-up: name the product, use the real time the visitor spent on that product page from your tracking tool, then ask one simple question about whether they still want it. The specific detail shows a real person is paying attention, so the message feels personal rather than automated. Keep it short and never reuse a fixed script. [L1472.069]
Hi {customer_name}, you spent about 20 minutes looking at the {product_name} yesterday. Are you still interested, or can I answer a question for you?

[L1472.069]

Hi {customer_name}, I noticed you came back to the {product_name} page several times this week. Would it help if I checked its availability for you before you decide?

[L1472.069]

SMS and Push: Channel Limits

  • SMS retargeting works only for customers who tick the consent box to receive SMS. If most customers do not tick it, the channel delivers almost nothing. Do not rely on it as the main recovery channel. [L1216.014]
  • SMS retargeting results depend heavily on the market. In the reported experience it worked in the US, Canada and Australia, but not in Switzerland, Germany and Austria. Test the channel per market before relying on it. [L1216.015]
  • Sources differ on SMS. The older Source B material calls it a channel that can still deliver strong returns when used well. [L1497.004] Source C limits its use to consenting customers and to specific markets. [L1216.014, L1216.015]
  • Push notifications are one of the personal channels listed alongside email and SMS for follow-up. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L0414.039]

Multi-Channel Coordination

  • Multi-channel marketing across email, SMS, push notifications and retargeting on platforms such as Google, Facebook, Pinterest and Snapchat works best when each message delivers value. Repeating the same product picture across channels mainly annoys customers. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L0949.049]
  • Run retargeting ads and personal messages together as one development stage, rather than as separate tasks. (Older: 2021-2022.) [L0414.039]
  • Keep ad and email messages consistent so that the list and the audiences see the same offer. [L0757.028, L1076.073]
