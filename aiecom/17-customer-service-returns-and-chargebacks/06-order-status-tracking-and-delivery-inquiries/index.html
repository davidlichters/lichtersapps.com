17.6 Order status, tracking and delivery inquiries

Why tracking and order status matter

  • Order tracking systems prevent orders from disappearing due to supplier delays, lost emails in inbox, or operational oversights. This becomes critical as transaction volume grows and when working with new suppliers whose reliability is unproven. [L0292.001]
  • Tracking questions are the most frequent support request, so prepare standard replies for the common cases: order not shipped yet, order shipped, shipped but not received, and shipped with an angry customer. [L0405.001]
  • Most customer calls are about legitimacy, order status and stock. Detailed product pages, an FAQ and automated tracking emails remove most of these questions. (2021, older advice) [L1112.033]
  • An order tracking tool can send tracking information to customers automatically after shipment, which reduces "where is my order" contacts. (2021, older advice) [L1112.034]
  • Automatic shipment updates sent through a tracking app reduce customer inquiries about order status. [L0966.093]

Order status check before every reply

  1. Look up the order status in the store admin and fulfillment system before replying to any tracking question. [L0405.033]
  2. If the order is not shipped yet, send the not-shipped reply: it is being processed, a tracking email follows once it ships, and orders take about 10-20 days after shipping. [L0405.033]
  3. If the order is shipped and the customer is asking about tracking, send the shipped reply with the shipping date, the 3-5 business day tracking delay, the tracking link and a screenshot of the current status. [L0405.033]
  4. If the order is shipped and the customer says they got no confirmation emails, send the junk/spam version of the shipped reply and ask them to check junk and spam first. [L0405.033]
  5. If the order is shipped but the customer is angry, send the angry reply with the current status, take responsibility for delivery and promise a free reship or full refund if the parcel is lost in transit. [L0405.033]
  6. If the parcel is delivered and the customer wants a refund, offer a free reship first unless they ask for a refund on first contact, in which case process the refund without pushing a reship. [L0405.033]
  7. If the tracking cannot be found at all and the parcel appears lost, send the lost-order refund reply and confirm the refund is already initiated. [L0405.033]

Delivery times: processing and transit

Sources give different values for the same stages. Each value applies to the case listed.

Processing before a dropshipped order ships About 4 to 7 days Dropshipping store, order not yet shipped; the value to quote in the AI responder and in the not-shipped replies [L0477.009, L0477.040, L1297.039]

International transit 10-20 business days after processing Used in the shipped templates that say "after processing" [L0405.009, L0405.022, L0911.006]
Transit to final address 10 to 20 days from the shipping date Used in the not-shipped reply that cites the shipping date [L0911.005]
  • Use one basis for transit time in every template, preferably business days, so customers do not receive conflicting estimates. [L0405.035]
  • Present delivery timelines as estimates, and separate supplier processing time from carrier transit time, so a deadline is not promised that cannot be met. When processing takes about 3 days before a shipment from Illinois to Philadelphia, a 5-business-day deadline cannot be guaranteed, so say that upfront. [L1472.064]
  • Set expectations with the customer before they become frustrated. [L0911.007]
  • Give customers a rough delivery window once a sale is made, such as one to two weeks, based on the parcel's position in the shipping process (for example, cleared customs and on its way), and update it when possible. [L1315.007]

Confirmation emails and spam checks

  • Confirmation emails for order, shipping and delivery can land in the junk or spam folder, so when a customer says they received nothing, ask them to check those folders first. [L0405.004]
  • Automated emails sent by a store or its platform can land in the spam folder. Before reporting a missing confirmation or account email, search both the main inbox and the spam folder for the sender's name. (2025) [L0807.052]
  • If support emails seem missing, check the spam folder first, since replies may land there. If you are in the mentor program, also post in the member Slack channel to get attention faster. [L0941.085]

[L0405.021]

Write a reply to a customer whose tracking shows the order has not shipped yet, using this structure: thank them for the message and say you are happy to help; explain the order is being processed and will ship shortly; state that a shipping confirmation with a tracking link will be emailed once shipped; mention that orders typically take 10-20 days to arrive after shipping; invite any further questions; sign off with the agent name and store name as Customer Support Representative. Do not invent a shipping date.

  • The standard response to a delivery complaint takes responsibility for the shipment, apologizes for the inconvenience, and then offers a free reship or full refund if the package is lost in transit. [L0405.006]
  • For a package lost in transit, offer the customer either a free replacement shipment or a full refund. Make this offer explicitly in the reply to an impatient customer whose order is still moving. [L0911.010]

Guarantees and refund policy

  • A guarantee covering every package should be stated to customers: if a parcel is lost in transit, they may choose a full refund or a free reship, and the store can add a store credit on top of the reship. [L0405.005]
  • The default refund policy is that a customer can get either a free reship or a full refund. The preferred first offer is a free reship, but if the customer asks for a refund on first contact, give the refund without pushing a reship. [L0405.007]

Lost parcels and refunds

  • For lost shipments, send the tracking information that shows the item was lost, then keep the customer updated on replacements, when they ship and when they arrive. Most customers only panic when they are not kept informed. (2020, older advice) [L0852.109, L0852.125]
  • If the fulfillment platform can no longer track an order and the package is considered lost, issue a full refund and tell the customer it has already been initiated. Do not wait for the customer to ask for a replacement before refunding. [L0911.017]
Write a reply for a lost order where the tracking has gone missing, using this structure: explain that the package appears to have disappeared from the system and that the reason the tracking cannot be found is unknown; state that a full refund is being provided and has already been initiated, with an email notification to follow; apologize for the inconvenience and thank the customer for their patience; sign off as the store customer support representative.

Late delivery and delay responses

  • When a customer complains about a late delivery, first find the cause before deciding on a remedy. Possible causes are outdated product page delivery times, slow order submission, or a supplier that took longer than usual, and each has a different fix. (2021, older advice) [L0853.018]
  • If a supplier never ships a paid order and the customer becomes upset, a partial refund such as 5% combined with a firm delivery date can settle the situation. The exact remedy depends on what went wrong. (2021, older advice) [L0853.019]
  • For delivery questions, promise only what the supplier and carrier can deliver. Contact the store team when a customer needs a quote instead of guessing a price. (2022, older advice) [L1281.118]
Hi [Customer name], thank you for your message and for your patience. Your order is on its way, but delivery is taking a little longer than usual. We are sorry for the delay and will keep you updated until it arrives. [Store name] Customer Support

Delivery promises and Christmas

  • Setting delivery times that are too short for the real transit time is over-promising. Shorter estimates may raise conversion, but customers who receive the parcel later than promised lead to complaints and chargebacks. Set the estimate to what the fulfillment center can reliably meet and improve it as the setup matures. [L1327.026]
Write a Christmas guarantee message for a shipment expected before the holiday: greet the customer by name; say the order is on its way and is promised to arrive before Christmas; state that if delivery takes longer, a full refund will be given; sign off with the agent name and store customer support representative title.

[L0405.020]

Warm regards,

Origin and trust after purchase

  • Customers who do not know their parcel is shipping from China become more anxious when they discover it later, so hiding origin on tracking and emails protects trust after the purchase. [L1315.006]

Tracking from day one

  • For dropshipping with physical products, shipping can take 5 to 14 days, so customers will often ask where their order is. Plan customer service for these tracking questions from the start. [L0911.001]
