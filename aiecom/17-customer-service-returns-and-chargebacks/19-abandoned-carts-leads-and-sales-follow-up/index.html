17.19 Abandoned carts, leads and sales follow-up

Who follows up, and how fast

  • Sources differ on who makes the follow-up call. Each applies to the setup it describes:

    • The owner personally messages every email lead when the store is new and traffic is low. The message asks what they are interested in, offers to answer questions, and shows you want their business. This closes more sales and reveals what customers want. [L0201.012]

    • The store owner or a customer service rep can make the follow-up call to an abandoned checkout. It should happen as soon as possible after the abandonment. The goal is to ask whether something went wrong and fix it, not to push a sale. (Source B, 2021-22; older advice.) [L1522.042]

    • A VA handles the callbacks in this setup:

      1. Abandoned cart alerts are sent to Slack as soon as a cart is abandoned.
      2. The VA calls the customer back right away.
      3. The VA asks whether the customer had any difficulty or questions and tries to recover the sale.

      The owner did not measure the exact recovery rate. [L1473.013]

Abandoned checkouts: where to find them and what to send

  • In Shopify, go to Orders > Abandoned Checkouts to see every cart that was started but not completed. Follow up manually with every person on this list. [L0208.010]
  • Abandoned carts are the easiest sales to win, so following them up raises conversions without spending on ads. [L0255.064]
  • Use Shopify's cart recovery email, which has a button that sends the reminder to the customer. Keep the default button and send it to all customers who entered an email address and did not complete the purchase. Template:

  • Include a contact address in every abandoned cart email so shoppers with questions can reach you before they leave the checkout. [L0567.030]
  • You can offer a discount in the abandoned cart email, for example 5% off. The source says this is promotional use and does not violate MAP restrictions on advertised pricing. (Source A, 2020; older advice.) [L0268.028]
  • Bot-generated abandoned-cart emails can be annoying. In 2020 no simple fix was known, so keep checking your tools for an option to block them. (Older advice.) [L1277.097]

Contact timing rules

  • Customer service must not contact customers after 8 pm in their time zone. For an abandoned cart, wait until the next morning to send the message or make the call. [L0964.108, L0964.109] (Source A, 2023.)
  • Abandoned-cart calls or texts happen only during daytime, using the customer's time zone. [L0964.109]

SMS and text messages

  • Do not automate SMS text messages to customers for marketing or abandoned cart recovery. One operator faced significant legal liability for automating customer text outreach. [L0286.020]
  • Sources differ on texting. L0964.109 allows calls or texts to abandoned carts during daytime in the customer's time zone. L0286.020 forbids automated texts. Both apply together: manual texts, sent only in the daytime window, never automated. [L0964.109, L0286.020]

Live chat, phone and service leads

  • Live chat and phone questions usually come from buyers who want to place an order or need a few answers to finish the purchase. Missed calls and chats cost real sales, so cover them as soon as traffic arrives. (Source A, 2019; older advice.) [L0467.064]
  • For service clients, invite prospects to call or visit the office. In-person or live selling produced a much higher success rate than email, which mostly serves to introduce the operator. (Source C.) [L0440.053]
  • High average order values, around 2,000 dollars and above, bring more customers who want to order by phone. Answer the phone yourself first and hire help later. (Source A, 2024.) [L0806.082]

Personal video follow-up

  • The purpose of a video follow-up is to open a conversation, not to close the sale immediately. Once the lead replies, answer their specific concern, whether it is a question, a discount request, or a delivery date. [L1049.012]
  • Use the same Loom method for other lead inquiries, such as whether a product is in stock or a request for a bulk order quote. Pull up the relevant product, record a short video, and send the link. [L1049.014]

Phone orders

  1. Add the product to the cart.
  2. Collect the name, shipping address, billing address and payment method.
  3. Enter the order into the checkout with the customer's email.
  4. Process the order like a web order and follow it up the same way.

[L1283.061] (Source A, 2023.)
