17.21 Documentation, record-keeping and operational safeguards

Order and communication records

  • Contact the customer first in any refund or dispute, then the supplier or carrier. Keep records of all communication, because they serve as chargeback evidence. [L0856.087]

Evidence for disputes

  • Keep complete records of every order, shipment and refund in Shopify. These records are the evidence needed to win a buyer appeal, and refund requests from platforms such as Facebook can be contested with them. [L1128.011]
  • Keep detailed records of tracking numbers, supplier legal names, locations (including exact China addresses) and company names. Payment processors require these in compliance investigations. [L0324.023]

Supplier information

  • Do not share customer email addresses with suppliers without caution. Suppliers will likely use them for marketing, and customers did not consent to receive supplier communications. [L0245.063]
  • Send customers only the supplier's order email and return policy. The company does not create its own supplier contracts. Sign the supplier's agreement and return it right away. (Older: Source A 2021.) [L0834.092]
  • As the store owner, you handle customer complaints and payment disputes, not the supplier. Agree return and payment terms with the supplier in advance. [L0660.033]
  • Set the defective return allowance on a supplier application to 100 percent, because a defective product is always replaced. [L0855.135]

Supplier relations and escalation

  • If a supplier does not respond after a few days, keep following up until you get a yes or no answer. [L0855.140]
  • When an order is lost or damaged, do not yell, curse or send hostile emails to the supplier, even if the fault is theirs. Work through the problem calmly and respectfully. Suppliers who are treated with disrespect will not give you special treatment, even if you sell a lot. [L0939.013]
  • If you cannot reach the decision maker at a supplier, ask the receptionist to connect you to a sales representative. Sales representatives have more product knowledge than call-center staff. [L0941.084]

Insurance

  • The sources differ on whether general liability insurance is required or only usual.
    • Optional view: general liability insurance is optional and not needed to open a business. It can be considered based on business assets. Get quotes from a large insurer or from the insurer already used for a home policy. [L0941.086]
    • Usual-type view (older): general liability insurance is the usual type for an online store. (Older: Source A 2020.) [L1277.098]
  • Be cautious with insurance brokers, and review what you are actually charged over time, since premiums can rise a lot after the first year. (Older: Source A 2020.) [L1277.098]
  • Limited liability insurance becomes more sensible once you have assets to protect. Consult a professional before deciding. (Older: Source A 2020.) [L1277.099]

Sales tax and payouts

  • Enable sales tax for each state before taking orders from that state. An order placed before tax is set up may need to be cancelled and redone, and refunds can cost real money, as in one case that cost 40 USD. (Older: Source A 2021.) [L1090.118]
  • A refunded or cancelled order can create a negative pending payout in Shopify. After logging in, check the options shown in Shopify Payments before paying out the balance. (Older: Source A 2021.) [L1090.119]

Refund exposure and cash reserves

  • Letting backorders stretch for months while income continues to be spent on normal costs and owner pay can leave no money for refunds or supplier bills. Track refund exposure from pending orders against the cash set aside for them. (Older: Source B 2021-22.) [L1200.024]

Sensitive files and AI-accessible folders

  • Dragging personal or financial documents into an AI-accessible folder exposes them to the agent and creates real risk. [L0366.006]
  • Keep such files outside the agent folder and out of any folder Codex can open. [L0366.006]
