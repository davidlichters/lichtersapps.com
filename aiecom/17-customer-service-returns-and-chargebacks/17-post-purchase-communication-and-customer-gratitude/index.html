17.17 Post-purchase communication and customer gratitude

Order and shipping confirmation

  • A post-purchase email assures the customer that the brand is legitimate, gives order updates, and can introduce cross-sell or upsell items. It is a good place to add a social media link to grow the following. [L0607.038]
  • Older advice (Source A, 2021): Confirm to the customer that the order was received, even when stock verification takes longer because of weekends or holidays, so the customer does not doubt the payment. [L0834.089]
  • State the shipping time clearly in the order confirmation, because customers ask about shipping most. A store that ships within 48 hours should say so, and should email customers when the order is dispatched and when tracking updates arrive. [L1076.055]
  • Customers in this model value fast shipping, being kept informed about their order, and small surprises. Use these three points deliberately in the post-purchase experience. [L1437.006]
  • Fast shipping and fast support build repeat buyers. One brand used two-day shipping and quick customer support to encourage repeat purchases. [L1431.052]

Access and onboarding for new buyers

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): The purchase confirmation email should contain a login link and all details needed to access the product, so it must be sent immediately after payment to the address given at checkout. [L1491.002]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Many access complaints come from buyers checking a different mailbox than the one used at checkout, so the confirmation appears to be missing. Always check the checkout email address first. [L1491.003]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Store a dedicated support address in the welcome and confirmation emails and direct all access problems there, so buyers have one clear channel for help. [L1491.005]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Moving a legitimate sender's email from spam to the inbox tells the mail provider to deliver future messages from that sender to the inbox. Tell buyers to do this so later order, access and service emails are not missed. [L1491.004]

Onboarding steps for a new buyer after a successful purchase (older, Source B, 2021-22) [L1491.001]

  1. Open the inbox of the exact email address used at checkout and look for the purchase confirmation.
  2. If it is not there, check the spam or junk folder.
  3. If it is in spam, move it to the inbox.
  4. Open the confirmation and use the login link and access details it contains.
  5. If access still fails, contact the support address and describe the problem.

Welcome email template (older, Source B, 2021-22):

Subject: Welcome, your access is ready

Hi {first_name},

Thank you for your purchase of {product_name}. Your order is confirmed and we are glad to have you with us.

To get started, check the inbox of {purchase_email} for the email with your order details and login link. If you do not see it within {minutes} minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

If you find the email in spam, move it to your inbox. This makes sure future emails from us reach you.

The email contains your login link and all the details you need to access your purchase. If you have any trouble accessing anything, reply to this email or contact {support_email}, and we will help you as quickly as we can.

Best regards,
{sender_name}

[L1491.006]

Post-purchase email machines and expectations

  • Post-purchase machines reduce buyer remorse and increase customer lifetime value by confirming the purchase, setting expectations, building relationships, and encouraging repeat purchases and referrals. They coordinate phone calls, video ads, and email sequences. [L0240.017]
  • The goal of post-purchase machines is to raise customer lifetime value, reduce buyer remorse, and earn sales, testimonials and referrals. Buyer remorse is common when competition is high, so reassurance after purchase matters. [L1076.054]
  • Avoid sending only sales pitches after purchase. Long-term success depends on happy customers. [L1076.060]

Avoiding buyer remorse

  • To reduce buyer remorse and maximize post-purchase customer lifetime value, underpromise on delivery times and product characteristics in your post-purchase communications. Then overdeliver by meeting those promises faster or with unexpected value, such as bonuses, premium packaging, or exclusive content that customers would love but never expect. [L0240.023]
  • Add extras such as digital files or unexpected perks after purchase. [L1076.060]

Thank-you messages and gratitude

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): A standard order confirmation email does not count as gratitude. The thank-you must go further and be overt and personal. [L0406.006]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Thank every new customer as a permanent part of the business, not only the first hundred customers. Making it a routine practice is worth it as the business grows, and it also encourages reviews. [L0406.007]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Say thank you whenever a customer takes an action you want, not only on the first purchase. Gratitude works best as a permanent routine in the business rather than a short campaign. [L0406.032]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Small and medium online stores have an advantage because large e-commerce companies rarely send personal thank-you messages. Customers do not expect personal gratitude from large brands, so it stands out when a small store does it. [L0406.004]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): The most effective way to get customers to refer others is an unusual post-purchase experience that surprises them, prompting them to talk or post about it on social media. Pre-purchase factors such as a working, good-looking site and good service still matter, but post-purchase gratitude is the focus. [L0406.003]
  • Thank-you messages should feel like personal communication from one person to another, not a mass-produced mailing. This applies to both handwritten notes and video messages. [L0406.022]

Thank-you videos

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Bonjoro is an affordable online tool for recording video thank-you messages with a webcam or phone camera and sending them as personal emails. The recipient watches the video directly inside the email. [L0406.009]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Video thank-you messages are rare for customers, so they create a strong wow moment and set the store apart from competitors. Doing them manually requires formatting and compressing video files, which is time-consuming, so a dedicated tool is worth using. [L0406.013]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Thank-you videos should be authentic and quick, recorded wherever you are, not overproduced. Customers value seeing a real person who appreciates their support. [L0406.014]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): A pre-recorded generic video can be personalized through email placeholders such as the customer's first name, which email tools insert automatically. The customer sees a personal address from a real person, while the video itself is recorded only once. Example: the video says "Thanks for supporting us," and the email subject and greeting include the customer's name. [L0406.015]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): A thank-you video should thank the customer, give useful tips for using the product, and explain how to get support. Invite customers to contact customer service if they get stuck or have questions. [L0406.017]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Video thank-you emails sent through Bonjoro get a good open rate, and recipients often share the videos with others. The video thank-you can therefore reach people beyond the single customer. [L0406.030]

Handwritten thank-you notes

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Decide when the note is sent. The timing options are below. [L0406.019]
Option When it is sent Arrival Use when
Right after the order Immediately after ordering About 1 to 3 days later Standard shipping
After expected delivery At the expected delivery date, e.g. day 7 for a 7-day shipping time After the customer receives the order Delivery is slow

[L0406.019]

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Do not sign handwritten notes with a generic "From the team" line. Sign with a real person's name and job title, such as owner or sales manager, so the note feels personal. [L0406.021]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): A low-cost option for businesses with few customers is to write short handwritten notes yourself and post them with a stamp, an envelope, paper and a pen. This is cheap and still effective, and it can be done before any online tool is needed. [L0406.023]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Scribeless is an online service that uses AI to turn typed text into a handwritten-looking note, including natural human imperfections. It can print and post the note for you, or you can print and mail it yourself. Pricing is about $2 per message and depends on message volume. [L0406.011]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Handwritten notes mailed by hand when a store had only a few customers were photographed and shared by recipients. Some later buyers said a friend who had received such a note had referred them, showing that a simple handwritten note at low cost can produce referrals. [L0406.033]
  • Older advice (Source A, 2021): Handwritten thank-you cards make the most sense when buyers are likely to reorder for their own clients, such as interior designers, especially after an earlier interaction. Automated thank-you emails can be sent through Klaviyo. [L0834.128]

Follow-up, support and first-product success

  • Following up with customers after purchase, for example with a call or a check that they are happy, builds relational and reputational capital. Satisfied customers then recommend the business, which supports trust and conversion. [L0390.029]
  • Help buyers succeed with the first product, because satisfied buyers are the main source of later purchases. A buyer who gets results is more likely to buy the next product from you. [L0378.099]
  • Good customer support, reliable shipping times and clear emails build the loyalty that brings repeat purchases and referrals, especially when new products are added to the same store. [L0871.081]

Repeat purchases

Consolidate component (repeat purchases) steps (older, Source B, 2021-22) [L0414.043]

  1. Map the consolidate pathway for each product: what the second and third purchases are, which can be a different product, such as an accessory for the first order or a complementary product later.
  2. Define what the customer must know before the second or third purchase, and whether education is needed first.
  3. Set the timing for the next offer. It depends on the original purchase and may be two weeks, two months or six months.
  4. Choose the delivery mechanism, such as email marketing or another channel.

Loyalty programme and referrals

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Mention the loyalty program in personalized post-purchase touchpoints, such as a thank-you video message or a post-sale support call. Explain that points are earned and can be turned into a discount on a future order. [L0956.010]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Thanking customers, showing gratitude, and following up after the sale with a post-sales support call each make customers more likely to recommend a store on their own. A referral reward adds an extra push on top of these steps rather than replacing them. [L1234.019]
