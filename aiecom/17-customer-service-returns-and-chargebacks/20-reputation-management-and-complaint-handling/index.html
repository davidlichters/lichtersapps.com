17.20 Reputation management and complaint handling

Principles of reputation work

  • Poor reviews and complaints are inevitable at scale: one complaint in a thousand customers is normal. [L0790.071]
  • Respond honestly, and interact with critics while the audience is still small enough to do so. [L0790.071]
  • Public negative comments cannot be fully prevented, so the practical response is to resolve each customer's complaint quickly and directly. Consistent service reduces the number of unhappy buyers over time. [L0492.067]
  • Happy customers refer other customers, while unhappy customers search out every public channel to complain. [L1472.014]
  • Customers judge a brand by how the retailer treats them. Suppliers approve retailers only if they will treat customers fairly, because a retailer represents the brand's name and must protect its reputation. [L1422.012]

Response standard and speed

  • Answer complaint emails quickly. Acknowledge the customer's frustration and offer a concrete solution such as a refund or fix. Response speed is part of the solution and prevents disputes. [L1141.028]
  • Reply to negative reviews within 24 hours. Acknowledge the specific problem, state the action taken and give a direct contact route. [L1164.025]
  • Trustpilot shows a company's reply rate and reply time. The example brand replied to 99% of negative reviews, typically within 24 hours. Use these figures as the benchmark for your own response standard. [L1164.023]
  • Update customers proactively about shipping delays, before they have to ask. [L0637.022]
  • Reply fast to Facebook comments, DMs and Messenger messages. Fast replies signal good business practice to Meta and can help move the business up a tier. [L0637.022]

Triage of negative reviews

  • Older advice (Source A, 2021), still valid as a sequence. Handle a negative review as follows: [L0968.097]
    1. Decide whether it comes from a real customer, a competitor or a troll. [L0968.097]
    2. For a real customer with a fixable problem, try to fix it calmly. [L0968.097]
    3. For fake or unjustified reviews, request removal. [L0968.097]
    4. For customers who cannot be satisfied, stop engaging and focus on good customers. [L0968.097]
  • The business cannot edit or delete reviews itself. Its only direct action on a review is a reply. [L0694.038]
  • Removal, as described in step 3 above, applies only to fake or unjustified reviews. [L0968.097, L0694.038]

Reply templates

Reply to negative reviews and complaint emails with this template, filling in the brackets:

Hi [Customer name], thank you for your review. We are sorry about [the specific issue, e.g. the unexpected charge, the difficulty cancelling your subscription, the wrong order]. We understand how frustrating that is, and it is not the experience we want for you. We have reviewed your order and [the action taken, e.g. cancelled your subscription, processed a full refund of [amount] for order [order number], refunded the remaining balance]. The refund should appear on your original payment method within [5-10] business days, depending on your bank's processing time. You are welcome to keep [the package you received] as a gesture of goodwill. If you have any other questions, contact us at [support email] or reply directly to our email and we will help. Kind regards, [Agent name], [Title]

Trustpilot: analysing the profile

  1. Read the star rating, the review count and the AI review summary. [L1164.015]
  2. Filter the reviews to 1, 2 and 3 stars to find product flaws and service problems. [L1164.015]
  3. Export or copy the filtered low-star reviews into a CSV file. Ask an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT to run a full gap analysis of the complaint themes. [L1164.017]
  4. Interpret the pattern:
    • When low-star reviews focus on cancellations, unexpected recurring shipments and refunds rather than the product itself, the product is probably acceptable. The real gap is service and subscription handling. [L1164.016]

Trustpilot: handling a negative profile

Procedure for a negative Trustpilot profile created about your store: [L0694.038]

  1. Check whether the business has claimed the profile. An unclaimed profile can be claimed by opening a new profile and proving ownership. [L0694.038]
  2. Accept that reviews cannot be edited or deleted by the business. You can only reply to them. [L0694.038]
  3. Send customers to your review page by email, so that new reviews improve the rating and the profile looks better. [L0694.038]

Subscription and cancellation complaints

  • Making cancellation hard is the most damaging pattern. This includes routing customers to a promotion instead of cancelling, or refusing to change an order after it is placed. Offer a self-service cancellation and order-change path. [L1164.020]

Public comments and ad moderation

  • Leaving comments unfiltered can damage an account. A comment section full of complaints or negative remarks can kill momentum and sales, so filter comments before a video goes viral. [L1429.038]
  • Procedure for moderating comments on the page: [L1431.055]
    1. Hide negative or inappropriate comments on the page. [L1431.055]
    2. Assign a moderator to the page to handle comments. [L1431.055]
    3. Reply to customer messages quickly using prepared templates. [L1431.055]
  • Respond to comments and messages on Facebook, Instagram and Messenger when running social ads, because these are often pre-purchase questions. [L1259.051]
  • Ignoring negative comments on ads and leaving them unanswered led to bad reviews piling up and the Facebook page being blocked. Answer comments and address complaints promptly. [L1131.046]
  • Procedure for filtering comments on every promoted video: [L1444.050]
    1. Add a list of banned words to the comment filter, and add words specific to your product. [L1444.050]
    2. If a comment gets through the filter, add variations of the same word to the filter. [L1444.050]
    3. If the same person posts many variations of comments, block that profile. [L1444.050]
  • Older advice (Source B, 2021 to 2022): reply to every genuine YouTube comment, including those expressing disagreement, because replies count as engagement. Ignore trolls or answer them lightly, and never argue. [L1511.014]

Imitators and public notices

Template for a notice to customers when imitators appear:

Draft a tactful notice to customers explaining that imitators are appearing, that our brand is the original, and that we have no partner ties with them. Anyone buying from us is buying from the genuine brand. Phrase it delicately so it does not come across as boasting.

Consumer complaint services

Google Reviews Both good and bad reviews Can go either way [L1472.014, L1472.062]

  • Focus your reputation effort on the platforms where your customers are most active. [L1472.062]
  • Prioritise resolving issues directly with the customer before they reach any public channel. [L1472.063]
  • When a customer files a complaint with a consumer complaint service:
    • Reply once through that service, in a calm and factual way. [L1472.067]
    • Keep a copy of every message. [L1472.067]
    • Write the reply as if the customer will read it, because the service forwards it to the customer. [L1472.067]
    • Do not argue about fault. [L1472.067]

Case: a complaint service filing after a pricing error

A store sold an appliance priced at about $1,000 too low because its price update was late. When the order came in, the store emailed the buyer that the item was out of stock. The buyer insisted on receiving the product and filed a complaint with a small business complaint service. The store offered a full refund, apologised again, and stopped the exchange. The complaint went off the web after about 30 days with no further action. [L1472.018]

Recovering customers and turning complaints into improvements

Procedure for customers who left a negative review or complaint: [L1377.068]

  1. Contact each customer who left a negative review or complaint. [L1377.068]
  2. Apologise and say the problem was a mistake on your side. [L1377.068]
  3. Offer a $5 to $10 credit or a small free product to rebuild trust. [L1377.068]
  4. Ask the customer for feedback through a short survey about the product and delivery. [L1377.068]

Mistakes that cause lasting reputation damage

  • Collecting customer payments without sending the orders to the supplier, then closing the store, leads customers to complain to the supplier and damages its online reputation. [L1422.011]
  • Ignoring negative comments on ads and leaving them unanswered led to bad reviews piling up and the Facebook page being blocked. [L1131.046]
  • Making cancellation hard, through promotion redirects or refusing order changes after placement, is the most damaging service pattern. [L1164.020]
