Payment, collections and chargebacks

Unpaid invoice after shipment (older advice, Source A 2021). If a customer does not pay an invoice after shipment, follow these steps:

  1. Call the customer. [L0247.095]
  2. Text the customer. [L0247.095]
  3. Check the tracking record to prove delivery. [L0247.095]
  4. Message the customer through LinkedIn or Facebook if they still do not respond. [L0247.095]

Pending PayPal payment. For a customer whose PayPal payment is pending, check the notification for a reason first. If none is shown, contact PayPal support. [L0964.107]

Chargebacks and refunds in the sales plan. Refunds, chargebacks and lifetime value often get too little attention during the front-end sale. Include them in every sales plan and report them with each offer. [L0378.098]

Chargeback risk from test orders. When placing a dummy test order on a competitor's store, contact their support first, say the order was placed by mistake, and request cancellation or a refund. Expect refusal, often with a claim that the order has already shipped. Some testers then consider a chargeback, which carries risk and should not be the default. [L0501.038]

Benchmark from a store with low disputes. A founder working from Thailand reported more than 1,000 orders with two chargebacks and fewer than one refund per month. The details are in the case study below. [L0910.057]

Collection letters: persuasion and threat in turn. Alternate persuasion with the threat of loss in a collection series. A strong threat frightens some debtors into paying, but it angers others, so follow it with a persuasive letter that restores goodwill before the next threat. [L1369.098]

Collection letters: appeal to honesty. Formula: remind the debtor of an honest trait he is proud of, such as paying one's debts, and then say that his account reflects that trait. Why it works: a debtor who sees himself as honest is more likely to pay than one who feels accused. [L1369.099]

  • Example one: Most of our customers pay their accounts promptly because they value their word, and we are confident you are one of them. [L1369.099]
  • Example two: Our records show your history of paying on time, and this one invoice is simply waiting for your confirmation to close. [L1369.099]

Collection letters: amount, date, consequence and benefit. Formula: state the exact amount owed, the specific date by which payment must arrive, the consequence if it does not, and the benefit of paying now. Why it works: a precise deadline and consequence are more likely to prompt action than a general reminder. [L1369.100]

  • Example one: Your balance of 25.00 dollars is due by the 17th. If payment is not received by then, your account will be recorded as overdue in our credit file. [L1369.100]
  • Example two: Your invoice of 340 dollars is due by the 30th. Paying by then keeps your account in good standing and lets you order next month without a hold. [L1369.100]

Collection letters: specific threats. Threats in collection letters must be specific: state the amount, the deadline date and the exact next step, such as a transfer to the legal department. A vague warning is easy to ignore. [L1369.101]

Collection letters: before and after record. In a payment reminder, show the debtor the exact current entry on his account and the entry it will become if payment does not arrive by a set date. A visible before-and-after record makes the consequence concrete. Keep the consequence limited to actions you are legally entitled to take. [L1369.123]

Shipping, delivery and checkout costs

Charging for shipping. Do not charge customers for shipping unless it is for an upgraded service, such as expedited shipping or white glove delivery. If free shipping margins are insufficient for specific suppliers, stop running ads to those products and rely only on organic traffic. (Source A 2023) [L0251.017]

Shipping cost as an expense. Shipping costs are an expense that comes out of gross profit, and they are not reduced by the store's own margin. (Older advice, Source A 2021) [L0834.093]

Default offer: free standard delivery. Offer free standard delivery by default and let customers upgrade to white glove delivery or white glove with assembly. This model has worked well compared with charging for every delivery option. (Older advice, Source A 2021) [L0853.096]

Checkout transparency. Use a checkout that shows shipping costs and fees clearly to avoid shock at checkout, since customers who do not expect extra costs may abandon or return. [L0950.072]

Residential or commercial address.

  • Customers may be asked whether an address is residential or commercial by using a Shopify app or native option if available. If none exists, contact Shopify support to check. (Source A 2024) [L1115.072]
  • For delivery questions about residential versus commercial addresses, check Shopify support for an app or native option before making changes to checkout. (Source A 2024) [L1115.073]

Customer accounts. Customer account login is usually enabled, but most stores see little use because purchases are one-off or infrequent. (Source A 2024) [L1115.074]

Installation.

  • When a customer needs installation for a product, avoid taking on installation liability directly. Build a network of local installers in major cities and refer customers to them. (Source A 2023) [L1094.094]
  • For a product that is basic furniture or not regularly installed by professionals, the freight-to-third-party-receiver model is not the norm. Ask the supplier about their standard delivery process first. (Source A 2023) [L1094.095]

Supplier quality and supplier relations

Invoices and wholesale prices in packages. Suppliers do not include invoices or receipts with wholesale prices in packages. The product box will show the brand name (normal, expected) but never the internal wholesale cost. (Source A 2022) [L0249.068]

Supplier approval before selling their products. Suppliers request approval before allowing you to sell their products. Explain that your store is not yet live, demo products are on the site to show suppliers what their product pages will look like, and you will launch on [specific date]. This is standard practice and fully disclosed. (Source A 2024) [L0253.035]

Reporting MAP pricing violations (procedure, older advice, Source A 2019).

  1. Find a competing store selling the supplier's product below the MAP price. [L0243.024]
  2. Use Loom to record a brief 20-second video stating the competitor's name, product, price, and that they are violating MAP policy. [L0243.024]
  3. Email the Loom video to your supplier contact at customer service. [L0243.024]
  4. The supplier will typically correct the violation quickly if they enforce MAP policy. [L0243.024]

Supplier mismatch case study. The founder of a store with a printed fashion item ordered the product after a video call showed the item. The founder asked the supplier for a factory photo or video, but the supplier said photos would slow production, and no sample was ever sent, even though one had been promised. The supplier then shipped a lower-quality product: the printed item was sent in place of a woven product. [L0694.039]

  • Within one day the store received six PayPal disputes, and more followed the next day. [L0694.039]
  • The founder spent many hours on customer service. A Trustpilot profile was opened by someone else with negative reviews, and the store was labelled as a scam. [L0694.039]
  • The supplier threatened to close the account if the founder opened a dispute and did not apologise. [L0694.039]
  • The founder decided to stop working with this supplier, postponed the planned relaunch from the 15th to around the 19th while checking the price with new factories, and planned to redesign the store and hire a virtual assistant. [L0694.039]
  • Lesson: the founder should have requested a photo or video from the factory before the first order, and should not have ordered bulk or trusted a supplier that refused to show the product. [L0694.039]

Early product example. A first product ran for about one month, with the first ad launched on 7 January, and produced about 11,000 EUR in revenue. This shows that a single well-chosen product can carry an early store while other markets are tested. [L0694.040]

Customer service staff and virtual assistants.

  • A customer support virtual assistant who handles chat, phone calls, email, and Zendesk tickets reduces seller dependence. If staff are willing to stay with the new owner, tell the buyer, because buyers highly value continuity. (Source A) [L0554.029]
  • A founder working from Thailand built a dropshipping business with over 500,000 dollars in sales in about 2.5 years, reportedly without answering calls or emails in real time. At the start he worked late nights and early mornings, calling suppliers and answering emails himself. [L0910.057]
  • He then added a visible phone number, an FAQ, personal product notes, a clear shipping page and detailed shipping-time emails, which reduced contact volume significantly. [L0910.057]
  • He hired a native English-speaking virtual assistant to answer calls for a flat 150 dollars per month, and he uses a shipping-tracking app and a Gmail scheduling and reminder tool. [L0910.057]
  • Over more than 1,000 orders, he had two chargebacks and fewer than one refund per month. [L0910.057]

Fulfilment model comparison. On FBA, Amazon handles most returns, refunds and customer calls, so the seller only answers occasional questions by email. Customer service load is much lower than running a dropshipping store. (Source A) [L1153.077]

Customer communication

Message templates. A ready-made PDF of customer service message templates covers the common situations. Adjust the store name, product name and customer service agent name in each template before sending it to customers. [L0911.004]

  • The PDF is grouped into four scenario types: tracking inquiries, returns and refunds, order modification or cancellation, and refunds for lost orders. [L0911.004]

Reply structure. Every customer reply follows the same frame: a greeting that thanks the customer and offers help, the order status with the specific facts, the expected timeframe or the action taken, a closing line that asks if they have further questions, and a sign-off with an agent name, store name and role. [L0911.018]

  • Pattern formula: Greeting + Status fact + Timeframe or action + Closing + Signature. [L0911.018]
  • Example for a delayed order: Hi Sam, thanks for reaching out, happy to help. Your order left our supplier on March 3 and is now with the carrier. Expect delivery within 7 to 10 business days. Let me know if anything else comes up. Best, Emma, Customer Care at [store name]. [L0911.018]

Response window for student or community messages. Answer student or community messages within a fixed window after the morning business checks, because members in other time zones may start their day while the operator is still in the middle of the workday. Schedule this as its own short block rather than answering whenever a message arrives. [L1181.028]

Opening a support ticket. When you open a support ticket, include your name, your goals and whether you are new or experienced, so the right helper can be assigned and help faster. [L1323.012]

Customer service as a differentiator. Customer service can be a differentiating part of the offer, and buyers expect service close to what they would get in a physical store. A distinctive service level such as extended phone hours can win sales when competitors do not offer it. (Older advice, Source B 2021-22) [L0411.026]

Customers referred from another marketplace (procedure, older advice, Source A 2020).

  1. Reply by email, acknowledge that Amazon referred them, and offer to help. [L0961.054]
  2. Offer to answer their question on a recorded video call, using FaceTime, Skype or a similar tool. An audio-only recording is acceptable if they are not comfortable on camera. [L0961.054]
  3. At the start of the call, ask how they found your store. [L0961.054]
  4. Answer their question and give practical advice. [L0961.054]
  5. End the recording with a call to action that points to your store. [L0961.054]
  6. Embed the recording on your About Us page and on the product pages for those products. [L0961.054]
  7. Run a remarketing ad to visitors of your site, so they remember you before buying elsewhere. [L0961.054]

Template: email reply to a customer referred by another marketplace (older advice, Source A 2020).

Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out about [product]. Amazon sent you to us for support, and I would be glad to help. Would you be open to a short recorded video call so I can walk you through it? If you would rather not be on camera, an audio call works too. Before we start, could you tell me how you found us?

[L0961.055]

Business mindset and service quality.

  • Poor customer service is a symptom of low business confidence, appearing alongside doing business only for money and running a business on plans that never get executed. Fixing the owner's confidence and self-alignment is part of improving service quality. (Source A) [L0701.041]
  • A competitive business should turn setbacks into advantages, so each problem becomes a chance to improve. Track each setback and record what it reveals about the customer experience or supply chain. (Source A) [L0920.029]
  • Speaking directly with real people who have bought the product is the most important way to make progress. These conversations reveal the bottleneck so it can be identified and solved. (Source D 2026) [L1042.015]
  • Good customer service and product quality can make shoppers buy from a dropshipping store even when many stores sell the same product. Satisfied customers also promote the store to others. (Source C) [L1213.077]
  • Scaling a product with poor quality or service makes unhappy existing customers see the ads and share complaints. Since iOS limits tracking, you cannot fully hide ads from past buyers even when they are excluded. (Source C) [L1213.036]

Customer targeting

Aim for the right buyers. Aim to attract the right buyers rather than the most buyers. Customers who do not fit the product create a poor business, complaints, and extra costs. [L0378.100]

Pain-point research. Find out what the product solves, what customers say in reviews, and what problems they face in daily life, to build a precise customer avatar. [L1158.020]

Customer conversations before private label. Before private labeling a main product, the store should already have a substantial sales history, many customer service interactions and ongoing contact with customers. These provide the data needed to find differentiation. (Older advice, Source B 2021-22) [L1155.005]

Platform support, accounts and policy compliance

Support channels for a training program. Support is available through the private, members-only Facebook group, where questions are answered by the instructor and experienced high-ticket dropshippers, and by email to support@dropshipbreakthrough.com (the link is in the page header). Before posting, search the group for the question, as it may already be answered. (Older advice, Source B 2021-22) [L1496.009]

Shopify theme and app support.

  • Shopify Support for theme edits: contact Shopify directly for a theme question, such as where to place a Klaviyo embed code in the Dawn theme, since Shopify built the theme and can give the exact location. (Source A 2024) [L0856.090]
  • Store and product issue support: email the support address with the theme version used. The developers can locate code added elsewhere, including custom code, that changed a setting. (Source A 2024) [L0856.091]
  • Send technical Shopify questions to the support address of the course provider, which routes to the developer team. This is not the coach's area of expertise. (Source A 2023) [L0964.112]
  • When business email support is needed, contact the store's support address and describe the page and the problem. The team then routes the request to the right designer or developer. (Source A 2022) [L1092.098]
  • For a support problem with Google Search Console or other tools, email the support team with the exact error message, since the team may already have a fix from earlier cases. (Older advice, Source A 2021) [L1090.121]

Unplanned Shopify theme code error (procedure, Source A 2024). Use this when red error lines appear in the theme editor after removing a third-party app's JavaScript.

  1. Find the extra or missing bracket or line that was removed. [L0971.036]
  2. If unsure, post a job on Upwork for a Shopify developer describing the exact red error. [L0971.036]
  3. A five-minute fix is typical; a budget of about $20 is usually enough. [L0971.036]

Bulk price changes. Shopify bulk edit and product export are the approach for price changes in bulk. No repricing app is used by the team. (Source A 2024) [L0971.077]

Digital content protection. When content is copied, first message the copier once to remove it. If there is no response, file a DMCA takedown with the host, for example Shopify for a Shopify store. (Older advice, Source A 2022) [L0969.039]

Store name collisions. If another store uses the exact same name and sells the same products, change the store name and buy a new domain to avoid consumer confusion. (Older advice, Source A 2022) [L0969.094]

Advertising account restrictions. Ad accounts and business assets can be restricted when the landing page or creatives break platform policy, for example with before and after claims or weight loss claims. Audit the landing page and creatives against the policy before launching or reusing creatives. (Source C) [L0638.049]

Google Merchant Center.

  • Google Merchant Center policy violations caused by automated bot scrapes can be contested. Request a manual review, then ask for the domain to be whitelisted so it is reviewed manually before suspension. (Older advice, Source A 2022) [L0665.088]
  • Merchant accounts can be suspended if the account is set up from abroad while listing a US address. If this happens, appeal and provide the correct details. (Older advice, Source A 2022) [L0665.089]

Facebook account bans due to ID issues. Submit the ID review in Meta Business settings. It usually takes 24 to 48 hours. If it does not work, contact the account seller or a support provider. (Source C) [L0773.052]

Pinterest. Pinterest's support team can help with account bans, restrictions and ad insights. The phone number on the business account is used for support, not verification. Do not follow Pinterest's general marketing advice for campaign decisions, because it is not aimed at direct response selling. (Source C) [L1134.036]

Ads and click quality

Click-through rate threshold. Do not scale ad spend when click-through rates fall below 2%. A low CTR indicates poor product-market fit and will exhaust budget without converting traffic. (Source C) [L0324.024]

Click fraud. A third-party click fraud service tested at the time was not worth the cost. The author cancelled after 30 days and does not recommend it. Use Google's Invalid Click analysis and account audit first. (Source A 2024) [L0856.089]

Marketplace delivery commitments (Google Shopping Actions)

Older advice, Source B 2021-22.

Joining Shopping Actions. Joining Shopping Actions requires the merchant to state specific delivery time frames for orders and to keep them. Other marketplaces use the same kind of delivery commitment. [L0738.010]

Delivery reliability before joining. Before joining, check that you can deliver within the stated time frame at least 90% of the time. Example: if you promise delivery in 7 days, at least 90% of orders must arrive within 7 days. If you cannot, do not join, because suspension will follow. [L0738.011]

Seller score. The seller score is driven by buyer feedback on delivery times, return rates and cancellation rates, each with thresholds. A poor seller score is hard to recover from. [L0738.016]

Retailer Standards score. The Retailer Standards score is calculated from order defects such as late deliveries and high cancellation rates. A merchant must deliver 20 items to receive a score the following month, and the score is checked on the first day of each month. [L0738.027]

Defect trends. Late deliveries and a high order cancellation rate both lower the Retailer Standards score. Check the defect trends in Merchant Center regularly so that problems are corrected before the monthly score is set. [L0738.028]

Daily order check. The Shopping Actions Today's Snapshot in Merchant Center flags orders as due today when they are pending shipment, missing tracking information or have wrong tracking information. Clear these flags daily, because late or untracked orders reduce the Retailer Standards score that determines Shopping Actions visibility. [L0738.040]
