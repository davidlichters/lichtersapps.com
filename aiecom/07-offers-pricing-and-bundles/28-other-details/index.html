Domains and store costs

  • Standard domain pricing is $16-$20 per year. [L0079.006]
  • Skip premium domains that cost hundreds or thousands of dollars, however appealing the name. [L0079.006]

Shopify plans and trial offers

Shopify prices and offers change over time. Check the current figures on the Shopify site before signing up. [L1304.008, L1304.009]

  • Standard recommended plan for a new store (USA, 2020 to 2026, as shown in the course): $39 per month when paid monthly, or $29 per month when paid for a full year in advance. [L1304.008]
  • Signup offer at the time of the course: a 3-day free trial plus a first month for $1 on select plans. [L1304.009]
  • Promotion at the time of the July 2025 call: a trial of 3 days free, followed by 3 months for $3 USD. This may have changed since. [L0844.001]
  • Store credit: Shopify gave new stores a $50 credit for the Shopify App Store at the time of the course. It was shown at the top of the store setup screen, and the bonus may not always be offered. [L1304.014]
  • Payment: the trial link required no credit card to start the free trial, but a payment method is needed to subscribe after the trial. [L1304.010]
  • Contracts: Shopify plans are pay-as-you-go with no contract. You can change or cancel the plan at any time before the trial ends, so no charge is made. [L1304.011]
  • Cancel-before date: this is 3 days after the trial was created. Cancelling before this date means no charge is made. [L1304.015]

Signup flow (setting)

Enter your email in the free-trial field and click Start free trial. On the quiz screen, click Skip all. Choose the business location (it defaults to the current location; change it to the country where the business will operate) and click Next. Create a password for a new account, or enter the existing password for an existing account, then click Login. [L1304.012]

Trial signup procedure

  1. Open the Shopify trial link from the course page so the offer is applied. [L1304.013]
  2. Enter an email address and click Start free trial. The email can be personal and changed later; no store name is needed at this point. [L1304.013]
  3. On the quiz screen, click Skip all. It does not affect the account. [L1304.013]
  4. Select the business location. It defaults to the current country; change it to the country where you plan to do business, then click Next. [L1304.013]
  5. If the email already has a Shopify account, enter the existing password; otherwise create a new password. Click Login. [L1304.013]
  6. Wait for the store setup process to finish. [L1304.013]
  7. Check whether the $50 Shopify App Store credit and the $1 first-month offer are shown at the top of the screen. The credit is not guaranteed and may not appear. [L1304.013, L1304.014]
  8. Click Select a plan (available at the top or further down the page). [L1304.013]
  9. Choose the Basic plan. [L1304.013]
  10. Enter a credit or debit card, or choose PayPal. [L1304.013]
  11. Check that the $50 app store credit is included and that the amount due is $1. [L1304.013, L1304.014]
  12. Click Subscribe. [L1304.013]

Trial and price timeline (USA, 2020 to 2026; check current offers before signing up)

  1. Days 1 to 3: the free trial runs with no charge, and no credit card is needed to start it. [L1304.020, L1304.010]
  2. Before the cancel-before date shown on the plan page (3 days after the trial was created): select a plan, or cancel to avoid any charge. The plan can be changed or cancelled at any time, and there are no contracts. [L1304.020, L1304.011, L1304.015]

Agency and outsourcing costs

Defining the partner relationship

  • Define the partner relationship you want before negotiating contract terms. Decide whether you need:
    • short-term tactical support,
    • a long-term scaling partner, or
    • a transition vendor, with a planned move to in-house within a defined timeframe.
  • The relationship type shapes contract length, flexibility requirements, and training obligations. [L0217.037]

Matching cost to scope

  • Cost structure must reflect the scope of work. [L0217.051]
  • Managing only paid media (pulling levers in ad accounts) costs less than full agency-of-record responsibility, which covers strategy, creative sourcing, landing pages, email setup, and analytics. [L0217.051]
  • Do not pay for breadth you do not need. [L0217.051]

Compensation structure

  • Base compensation lets the agency budget for labor costs, even if you reduce ad spend for profitability reasons. [L0217.052]
  • Percentage-of-spend compensation makes agency income volatile and creates resistance to spending discipline. [L0217.052]
  • Rule of thumb: the base fee covers labor, and incentives cover upside. [L0217.052]

Hiring a virtual assistant

  • When you are completely new to hiring a virtual assistant and only want to test a candidate, start with an hourly rate. [L1430.012]
  • Once you have found a good candidate, switch to a fixed monthly price, typically between 300 and 500 USD per month for Filipino virtual assistants. [L1430.012]
  • Offer changes can be delegated by stating the new offer directly, for example switching the offer to buy one get one free. [L1432.036]

Logo design on Fiverr (older, 2021-22)

  • Fiverr logo design services start at approximately 53 AUD (roughly 40 USD) for basic packages. Set your budget maximum at 100 USD. Above that, the investment is not justified for a startup brand. [L0323.015]
  • Fiverr organizes each designer's logo service into three package tiers:
    • Basic: the lowest price.
    • Standard: the mid-tier.
    • Premium: the highest price, with extra features.
    • You can upgrade for source file access or additional deliverables. [L0323.016]
  • Fiverr logo designers typically include up to 5 revisions in their packages. You can request design changes up to five times until you reach the final version you want to accept. [L0323.017]

Products, variants and inventory

Variants

  • Set up variants such as colors and sizes in the product's variant options. For example, switch a color to black and add a size range. [L1331.014]
  • For an early version with one color, a simple single-variant product is enough. Plan variants before launch. [L1331.014]

Pre-orders and uneven demand

  • For product variants with uneven demand, use a pre-order model for low-volume variants. This reduces inventory risk and lets you market all variants without holding slow-moving stock. [L0324.019]

Backorders

  • Accept backorders for products with low current stock. [L0249.096]
  • Tell the customer the expected delivery timeline and let them choose to wait. [L0249.096]
  • This converts sales you would otherwise lose by removing the product or marking it out of stock. [L0249.096]
  • Source age: older advice (2022). [L0249.096]

Economic timing and inventory planning

  • A current economic recession does not meaningfully reduce Q4 sales, because consumer spending shifts to credit and installment plans. [L0309.016]
  • Credit crises typically strike 6-12 months after high spending periods. [L0309.016]
  • Plan inventory conservatively from mid-2024 onward. [L0309.016]
  • Consider shifting to high-ticket products aimed at wealthy buyers, who are less affected by economic cycles. [L0309.016]

Reviews and trust

Incentives for video and image reviews (older, 2021-22)

  • Video and image reviews require a significant incentive, because they are more demanding for the customer and worth much more to the business. [L1012.022]
  • Examples of incentives:
    • a larger rebate on the reviewed order,
    • a cash-back payment,
    • a $100 Amazon gift card, or
    • another product sold at cost price, including delivery. [L1012.022]

Supplier reviews

  • When a store has high volume but very few reviews, ask suppliers for reviews. Use them alongside store reviews to build trust. [L1115.068]

Review schema (Judge.me)

  • Product reviews from Judge.me can be linked into product schema, so star ratings appear next to products in search results. [L1337.016]
  • This requires the Pro plan and Judge.me installed on the store. [L1337.016]

Reciprocity and gifts

Reciprocation

  • Give a valuable, unrequested gift first. The recipient feels obliged to respond with a purchase. [L0340.042]
  • The gift must be given without being requested. It should be valuable enough to feel thoughtful, not a cheap logo item. [L0340.042]

Example: moving service

  • A moving-service business sends gift certificates to new neighbors on behalf of local restaurants and auto shops. Examples are a free pizza, a free entree, or a free oil change. [L0340.043]
  • The gift introduces the business and starts the reciprocation effect. Satisfied recipients often return and become long-term customers. [L0340.043]

Common mistake

  • A reciprocity gift only creates a sense of obligation when the recipient did not ask for it and it feels valuable. [L0340.081]
  • A gift given in exchange for an action the customer already took is a thank-you, not a reciprocity trigger. [L0340.081]
  • A service provider can offer something like 30 minutes of free time, with no obligation, to create this effect. [L0340.081]

Upsells, anchors and entry products

Add-on upsell template

Use this only for an add-on that completes or speeds up [PRODUCT NAME].

Thanks for choosing [PRODUCT NAME]. Would you like to add [UPSELL NAME] to this order for [UPSELL PRICE]? It [SPECIFIC BENEFIT, e.g. gets you to your first result faster or supplies the part [PRODUCT NAME] needs to work fully], and it ships with your current order. Add it with one click, or continue without it.

[L0378.113]

Price anchoring for high-ticket services

  • Start from the price you feel comfortable quoting, then test a number roughly three times higher. [L0378.118]
  • If the higher figure still sounds credible when a prospect hears it, use it as your opening anchor for negotiation. [L0378.118]

Entry products

  • A low-priced digital template can be the first purchase for a buyer who later pays a much higher fee for a workshop. This works if the template solves an early step of the same process. [L0378.119]
  • Choose the entry product so that it demonstrates your method. This makes the higher-priced offer the natural next step. [L0378.119]

Case study: lemonade stand

Starting point: a lemonade stand run by a ten-year-old. Typical neighborhood stands brought in $10 to $15 per day and charged 10 to 25 cents per cup. [L0378.036]

  1. Phase one (sourcing): lemons were sourced at no cost, which kept cost of goods very low. [L0378.036]
  2. Phase two (product range): homemade cookies were packaged in packs of 1 and 3. Premium golf balls were collected and resold. [L0378.036]
  3. Phase three (location): the stand was placed on a busy street where regular cars and golf carts passed. [L0378.036]
  4. Phase four (pricing and pitch):
    • Lemonade: $1 for the first cup, 50 cents for a second.
    • Cookies: 1 for $1, or 3 for $2.
    • Golf balls: regular at 50 cents, premium at $2.50.
    • The pitch offered cookies with each drink, and golf balls to golfers who had lost balls. This matched a visible need. [L0378.036]

Result: sales reached $63 in one day, about 4 to 6 times the usual stand income. $58 was left after paying a helper $5. [L0378.036]

Lessons: small mistakes reduced results. These included missing samples, not adjusting pricing, and having a weak sign. [L0378.036]

Ad budgets and promotion

Daily budget guidance

  • Beginners starting with dropshipping should start around $25 per day, not $500. [L1374.018]
  • A $500 daily budget fits established brands that already have proven organic success and reinvest profits into high-quality, trend-aligned content. [L1374.018]
  • The speaker says $10 per day is the lowest workable level, but $25 has performed better. TikTok's own recommendation of $100 is not considered necessary. [L1374.018]

TikTok minimum budget

  • The minimum daily budget on TikTok is $25. [L1383.032]
  • The speaker kept the daily budget at $25 and set the ad-level budget higher, up to $50, so the ad could spend more. [L1383.032]
  • The setup in the lesson was described inconsistently. Verify the budget fields in Ads Manager before launching. [L1383.032]

Promoting a video with coins

  1. Open the video, tap the three dots at the bottom, and choose Promote. [L0501.012]
  2. Under the destination, choose Website visits (instead of the default profile option) to send traffic to your store. [L0501.012]
  3. Paste the store link, tap Shop now as the button, then tap Next. [L0501.012]
  4. Choose the audience. The default is TikTok's choice, but the author prefers Custom with minors removed. The common selection is:
    • ages 18-24, 25-34, and 35-44,
    • gender set to match the product (all, female, or male),
    • the broad interest option kept. [L0501.012]
  5. Tap Save, then Next, then set the budget. [L0501.012]

Creator video pricing on Billow

  • On Billow, the total price updates after every change to the order. This includes the number of creators, the video type, and the video length. [L1402.021]
  • The $99 base price shown for one creator and a 15-second video is only the starting point. [L1402.021]
  • Check the final price after each adjustment before clicking Next step. [L1402.021]

Order value and revenue benchmarks

  • A $10,000 daily revenue screenshot is usually a 24-hour total from several bundled orders, not one product. [L0854.036]
  • It can also be a high-ticket niche product, or a small business ordering several units of a $2,000 item. [L0854.036]
  • Source age: older advice (2022). [L0854.036]
  • The average order value in the coach's stores in 2020 was about 1,000 USD, aimed at upper-middle-class buyers. This is a 2020 benchmark. [L1277.085]

Charitable promises

  • A charitable promise based on net profit is clearer than one based on revenue. [L0855.050]
  • A pledge of 10 percent of revenue would be far too large for most stores, so the wording must say net profit. [L0855.050]

Done-for-you store builds

  • A done-for-you store build can typically be claimed within 12 months of the purchase date. [L0806.102]
  • Keep the purchase email with the claim instructions. If it cannot be found, contact the provider's support address. [L0806.102]

Marketplace limits (eBay)

  • A new eBay account is initially limited to 3,500 GBP in listed sales value. [L1441.037]
  • The limit can be increased after contacting customer service. [L1441.037]
  • The speaker's limit increased to 70,000 GBP after a few calls within about two weeks. [L1441.037]
