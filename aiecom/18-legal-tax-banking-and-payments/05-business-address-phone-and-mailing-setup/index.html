18.5 Business address, phone and mailing setup

Business Address Options

Address types compared

  • A UPS Store mailbox gives a business address with a suite number (example: 3200 B Cave Road Suite 137) instead of a P.O. Box, which looks more professional to suppliers. Cost is about $320 USD per year (2019 advice, older). [L0243.021]
  • Mailbox suites at a UPS Store cost about $100 to $150 per year and keep a home address off the public record (2019 advice, older). [L1069.070]
  • A non-US seller who needs a US address can use a UPS Store location set up remotely. The cheapest option is about $20 per quarter, depending on the location (2019 advice, older). [L1086.054]
  • A virtual business address is the recommended option over a P.O. box (2021 advice). [L0247.108]
  • A virtual mailbox is a real address with a suite number. It receives mail and forwards it or holds it for pickup. A P.O. box can be used for business mail alongside a separate address on the website. [L1092.018]
  • Business address options are a P.O. box, a UPS Store-style address with a suite number, or a virtual mailbox. Many LLC formation services also sell an address for a yearly fee. [L1092.017]
  • A virtual address from some providers appears as a street address followed by a mailbox number, such as "#406". Use the full address line exactly as the provider lists it on forms and on the website. [L1434.015]
  • Mailbox services such as PakMail can provide a business address when a UPS Store does not respond. Check their terms for business address use before signing up. [L0965.070]
  • Earth Class Mail receives, scans and forwards a business's physical mail. It can support a US address for a business without a physical office. [L0984.003]
  • PakMail receives parcels and letters at a US address and forwards them to the owner. [L0984.004]
  • UPS Store addresses provide mail forwarding and a professional business location without maintaining physical premises. [L0270.028]
  • A virtual office address in another city, a registered company address, or a correspondence address are all options for account verification. One seller reported about $20 for a virtual address and about $120 per year for a Swiss address service. Whether such addresses are accepted depends on the country and the platform's rules. [L1428.030]

Anytime Mailbox (US)

  • Anytime Mailbox (anytimemailbox.com) lists US locations by state and city. Browse the state, then pick a city or address and select a plan. [L1434.005]
  • Its basic US plan costs US$ 9.99 per month. The site advertises rates starting at US$ 5.99 per month across 1,127 locations. [L1434.005]
  • It gives a real street address for a registered business address, with mail forwarding, check deposit, and open-and-scan mail. The lowest-cost plan is enough because the address is not used as a mail service. [L1434.006]

Gordon Executive Centre

  • Gordon Executive Centre has a one-month notice period after the initial 1, 3 or 6 month term. [L1434.017]
  • Renewals are automatic. You are billed again for the same number of months you signed up for. [L1434.017]
  • A Gordon business address includes up to five names, such as a company name, a trading name and the names of two principals (four names in this example). [L1434.018]
  • Each additional name costs 10 per month, or a block of five names costs 35 per month, plus GST. [L1434.018]

Australia

  • Companies in Australia are registered federally, not per state. The virtual address can be in any city in the country. It is usually placed in a capital city, such as the Sydney CBD or Brisbane. [L1434.007]
  • Australian virtual business addresses typically cost AUD 30 to 40 per month. National providers with offices in every city often cost around AUD 50 per month. These figures are in local currency context and must be verified before use. [L1434.008]
  • To find a cheap Australian virtual address:
    1. Search for "virtual mailing address" plus the city name.
    2. Compare the local providers.
    3. Pick one with a location in your own state. This avoids time-zone confusion and customers asking whether you are in the wrong city. [L1434.009]

Choosing Between Home and Virtual Address

  • A home address can also be the business address. Running a business from a non-business premises is acceptable. The choice between home and virtual address is personal. [L1434.011]
  • A home address on supplier agreements is acceptable if customers do not send mail to the business. Changing it is optional in that case. [L963.124]
  • Do not use a home address as the business registration address. Registrations are published online and can be found by searching the business name. [L1092.016]
  • A PO box or business address can be used as the LLC address to keep your home address off documents. [L1113.047]
  • Use a virtual business address so your home address does not appear on the store. A mailbox at a UPS Store does this. [L0671.117, L0671.254]
  • Use a business address (such as a UPS Store suite address) on your website, supplier forms and documents instead of your home address, for privacy and professionalism. Do not advertise the address as a physical retail location or invite customers to visit. (2019 advice, older.) [L0243.022]
  • Digital nomads who need a US business address can choose a tax-friendly state such as Texas, Delaware, Nevada or Florida and use a UPS Store address in that state. (2020 advice, older.) [L967.063]
  • In the UK, a company's registered office can be changed at Companies House when a new one is set up. The speaker recommended a dedicated office address rather than a personal address, to keep private information out of public records. [L0552.041]

Matching Addresses Across Entity, Bank, Shopify and Merchant Center

  • Use the virtual address in the business bank account as well if the LLC is linked to it. Keeping them synchronized avoids mismatches. [L963.123]
  • Once you have a business address, update your business bank account with it, so the bank records match your business details. [L696.010]
  • A UPS Store or virtual address can be the business address for an LLC and for Shopify, as long as the address is consistent across all business documents. A virtual address may cause problems with Google Business Profile. [L971.069]
  • Do not use a registered address in a different state or city from where you actually operate. Customers and suppliers may ask questions, and calls may come in at inconvenient hours across time zones. Choose a provider in the same state. [L1434.010]
  • In the US, the virtual address should be in the state where the company is registered. A Nevada LLC should use a Nevada address. This avoids supplier questions caused by mismatched registration and postal addresses. [L1434.003]
  • If the business operates from outside the US but is based in the US, the company is often formed in a state such as Wyoming. The registered business address should be in that same state. [L1434.004]
  • Address and tax settings: a PMB or mail-forwarding address may cause Merchant Center to identify the store as being in a different region. Contact Merchant Center support to correct country settings. (2024 advice.) [L856.082]
  • Remove an old mailing address from business accounts once it is no longer used. Replace it with the current business address the store uses. [L857.065]
  • An online formation service can form an LLC and provide a virtual address. A mailbox service at a UPS Store can serve as a business address. (2020 advice, older.) [L468.150]
  • The address and phone number entered during Shopify sign-up are used only for the Shopify account and billing. Customers do not see them. The phone number can be a mobile or a business number. [L1314.012]
  • Google account setup asks for two contact details. A support email with a business address is a common combination. [L1428.031]

Verification Risks for Payments

  • A virtual business address is often not accepted by Shopify Payments or Stripe for verification. Stripe may require a physical address that proves the company exists. Buying a virtual address from an unknown online provider can block payment setup. [L1316.044]
  • Shopify may ask for proof of address during payment verification. Before choosing an address service, confirm the provider supplies documents that payment platforms accept. Check this before setting up Shopify Payments or Stripe. [L1316.045]
  • Setting up a company in a country (for example the US or UK) and using its address to verify Shopify Payments, Stripe or PayPal is a common path. Check the address type and provider with a qualified professional, because payment verification rules can change. [L1316.046]
  • If a supplier rejects a UPS or PO box address as a business address, ask whether the supplier accepts a PO box or a virtual forwarding address. Some suppliers and states may require a registered address. Confirm with a qualified advisor. (2022 advice, older.) [L854.089]

Google Ads and Website Address Requirements

  • A business address shown on the website is needed for suppliers and some advertising platforms. Google Ads requires a visible postal address on the site before it approves advertising. A virtual office address is acceptable if you do not want to use your home address. [L1434.001]
  • A virtual address is used only as a registered business and contact address. Expected mail volume is near zero, so the provider's service quality matters little. The address should look professional and exist as a real street address. [L1434.002]
  • Pick the most basic plan and sign up before moving on to the next setup step. [L1434.012]
  • The full setup procedure:
    1. Decide whether to use the home address or a virtual office address.
    2. If using a virtual address in the US, pick the state where the company is registered and find a location there on anytimemailbox.com.
    3. If in Australia, search for a virtual address provider in your own state, preferably in the capital city.
    4. Select the most basic plan and sign up before moving on to the next setup step. [L1434.012]

Business Phone Numbers

  • Landline or mobile numbers can both be used as business contact numbers on Shopify. There is no preference or requirement. [L245.067]
  • Phone numbers on the website build trust without overloading calls. A visible phone number in the header increases conversion rates and buyer confidence, especially for expensive products. [L268.078]
  • Most incoming calls are customers checking that you are a legitimate business before ordering, not complaints or rejections. Do not expect to be on the phone all day. [L268.078]
  • Phone number alternatives for business addresses:
    • Google Voice and Skype offer inexpensive local or toll-free numbers.
    • Mighty Call provides professional toll-free numbers with a 50 percent discount for the first three months (code provided with partnership deal). (2019 advice, older.) [L243.023]
  • A virtual business phone number rings through an app on an existing phone. No second device is needed, and it supports outbound calls. [L1116.011]
  • Use a local business address such as a UPS Store address and skip e-fax. A fax number is rarely needed for a dropshipping store. [L1116.010]

Business Email

  • Use a business email for corporate communications and for linking bank accounts and tools, not a personal email. This lets the business be sold cleanly later. [L1090.127]

Google Workspace and Microsoft Subscriptions

  • Enable two-step verification on your Google Workspace account to protect against unauthorized access. [L119.010]
  • Google Workspace Business Starter Plan costs $7 per month. [L125.002]
  • Google Workspace DNS setup: allow up to 72 hours for DNS propagation. If setup still fails after 72 hours, retry or check DNS settings. [L272.045]
  • Microsoft Office subscription costs $19 to $29 per month. Do not buy yearly plans, because you may need to cancel the tool if requirements change. [L218.010]
