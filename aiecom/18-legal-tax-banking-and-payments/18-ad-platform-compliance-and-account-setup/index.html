18.18 Ad platform compliance and account setup

Compliance checklist before traffic

  • Review the compliance checklist before launching any paid traffic, including Google, Bing and Facebook campaigns. Traffic campaigns must not start before this review is done. [L0393.011, L0532.015]
  • Running Google, Bing or Facebook campaigns before the compliance checklist has been reviewed risks account problems on the ad platforms. [L0393.025]
  • A compliance lesson covers the legal and platform rules a dropshipping store must follow before launch. Check these rules before running ads or taking payments. [L1089.009]
  • Review the Google Ads compliance policies before launch, because policy issues are the main cause of problems at first launch. Fix any issue before turning on ads. [L0972.053]
  • The checklist covers the most common reasons Google suspends Merchant Center or Google Ads accounts, especially misrepresentation. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L1145.050]
  • Setting up a parent LLC and a separate brand name (DBA) before starting is generally acceptable and does not by itself cause Google account problems. [L0808.046]

Store requirements

  • Put a privacy policy on the website, because Meta's terms of service expect one. [L0649.054]
  • Include a complete privacy policy on the site before setting up Google Merchant Center. (Older: Source A, 2021.) [L0671.069]
  • The store website must show a phone number, a contact us page and a privacy page in customer-visible places. Missing any of these is a common cause of Merchant Center suspension. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0727.006]
  • Use the same contact information on the store as is registered in Google Merchant Center. Phone, email and contact page options together can be enough even if no virtual address is displayed. (2023.) [L1283.056]
  • Footer social links that point to Shopify's social pages or platform home pages, instead of the store's own profiles, were linked in one case to a Merchant Center suspension. Replace them with your own profiles or remove them, then resubmit for review. (2023.) [L0934.146]
  • A financing or payment-option tab is typically not a reason for suspension, because financing options are common on stores without issues. (2023.) [L1283.055]
  • Each store's ad campaigns should match the store's selling region and currency. Keeping region, currency and traffic aligned avoids confusion about prices and shipping. (Older: Source A, 2021.) [L0853.103]

Google Merchant Center requirements

  • Merchant Center first performs an automatic bot-based review of the store and flags problems for manual review by a human. [L0377.023]
  • Tax setup is mandatory for the United States. See the tax section below. [L0731.005]

Google Ads billing setup

Setup sequence

  1. Select the billing country, time zone and currency. [L0184.004]
  2. Check the currency carefully before submitting the billing information, because it cannot be changed later. [L0716.005]
  3. Customer info: choose Individual or Business. For a business, enter the business name and the business address. [L0716.006]
  4. Create a payments profile by selecting Individual or Organization and adding a valid credit card. A temporary $50 authorization may appear. [L0184.004]
  5. Payment method: choose automatic payments and enter a credit card. Automatic billing means you do not have to pay invoices manually each cycle, similar to a phone bill. [L0716.007]
  6. Decline Google Ads strategist support, because the strategists are sales representatives, not strategic advisors. [L0184.004]
  7. Multiple payment methods can be added later. The billing page shows the account information and payment methods in one place. [L0716.008]

Alternative steps (Source B, 2021-22, older)

  1. Open Tools and settings (cogwheel, top right), then go to Billing > Billing info. [L1145.046]
  2. Enter the account name, the business address and the credit or debit card that will pay for the ads. [L1145.046]
  3. Campaigns can be built before billing is added, but ads cannot run until billing details exist. [L1145.046]

Rules and states

  • The Google Ads billing country must match the country where the business operates, not the country where the owner lives. For a US business, select United States. For a UK business, select United Kingdom. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0726.020]
  • A new ad account shows no payment methods, no recent spending and an outstanding balance of $0.00 until a payment method is added in Billing & payments. [L0651.017]
  • Keep a valid payment card on file in Google Ads. A declined bill can lock the account, and reopening it may require contacting Google directly. (2025.) [L0972.052]
  • A credit such as a 500 dollar Google credit usually appears on its own after the account processes it. If it does not, contact Google Ads support. (2024.) [L0856.081]
  • To restart a business in a niche with suspended Google accounts, call the Google Ads policy support number shown in your account to request a live review. (2023.) [L1283.054]

Google Merchant Center tax settings (US)

  • Tax setup in Merchant Center is mandatory for the United States. For sellers without a US business address, Google proposes a default tax setup that can be saved without entering US sales tax rates. Tax can alternatively be submitted through the product feed. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0731.005]
  • Tax details in Google Merchant Center must match the tax rates set in Shopify settings exactly. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L1145.016]
  • For a US-based dropshipping store, the tax setting in Google Merchant Center should copy the tax configuration used in Shopify. The state of business registration determines which tax nexus applies. In the setup described, no nexus is configured for all states. (2023 or undated, Source A.) [L0728.004]

Sales tax steps (Source B, 2021-22, older)

  1. Go to Merchant Center > Tax settings > Sales tax. [L0731.006]
  2. Turn on custom sales tax only for the states where the business must collect tax. [L0731.006]
  3. Enter each state's rate as a percentage (example: 7 for a 7% rate). [L0731.006]
  4. Turn off all other states, then click Save. [L0731.006]
  5. Check the rates with a tax advisor before saving. [L0731.006]

Which setup applies

Situation What to set Ids
No US business address Google's default tax setup can be saved without US rates, or tax can be submitted through the product feed. L0731.005
Business must collect tax in some states Custom sales tax on only for those states, rate as a percentage, all other states off, then Save. L0731.006
Shopify-matched setup, business registered in a state Copy the Shopify tax configuration; the registration state determines the nexus. L0728.004
  • US sales tax for Shopping ads applies in all states. Buy on Google requires collecting and remitting tax in Florida, Kansas and Missouri. Whether a seller must collect tax depends on the state where the business is registered. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0731.007]
  • The sample US setup shows the question "Is shipping and handling taxable?" with the answer No. The link "Need more tax options? Switch to advanced settings" opens the advanced tax configuration. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0731.035]

Google suspension appeals

Inventory mistake (older: Source A, 2021)

  1. Correct the inventory by restocking the item or removing products that will not be in stock from the product feed. [L0853.005]
  2. Make sure the feed no longer lists items customers cannot buy. [L0853.005]
  3. Request a review from inside Google Merchant Center. [L0853.005]
  4. If the review is denied, contact Google Ads support by phone or live chat. You must be logged in to reach that support. [L0853.005]

Misrepresentation (2023)

  • A misrepresentation suspension can be appealed by showing corrected changes. The appeal should list the changes made, explain what was wrong, and cite Google's policy documentation. [L1114.048]
  1. Identify what was not compliant in the store. [L1114.049]
  2. Make the required changes. [L1114.049]
  3. Review Google's full policy documentation and confirm the store complies. [L1114.049]
  4. Submit the changes with a clear explanation and links to the policy pages. [L1114.049]

Timing and resubmission (2023)

  • Google appeals about a Merchant Center suspension typically receive a response in one to two weeks. A failed appeal can be submitted again. Fix the cause before appealing so the first appeal succeeds. [L0934.148]

Meta Business Manager verification

Documents and profile

  • Verify the business manager with proper documentation: company registration certificate, certificate of incorporation, business address and contact details. Fill out all required fields in the business profile. [L0067.037]
  • Add the business documents, such as a certificate of incorporation and business address, and complete all contact details on the business page. An unverified or incomplete business profile can look like a scam to Meta. [L0640.056]
  • Documents that support verification include LLC or Ltd registration papers. A business registered in one country can still run ads targeting other countries, because verification only checks that a legitimate business is advertising. [L0637.008]
  • Verification needs a registered business with complete business details. Verifying it significantly reduces the chance that the ad account is banned or restricted. [L1024.009]
  • Entering registered business details builds trust with Meta and lowers the chance of bans. A beginner without a registered business may use personal information at first, but a proper business setup is recommended. [L1024.003]

One company, several accounts

  • Register one company and use it to verify all accounts, including Facebook and PayPal. The company name does not need to match the product or the store domain. [L1431.042]
  • One company can be used to verify several connected accounts, such as PayPal, a Facebook account and Shopify Payments. The company name does not have to match the store names. For every new store, the domain must be verified again in the ad account. [L1428.028]

Location and country of registration (sources differ)

  • The business location does not matter for verification. Verification needs to show that the business is registered and legitimate. [L1431.044]
  • For verification of a US business, the ad account asks for the business document matching the country where the business is registered. Examples: a Corporation document from Companies House or HMRC for the UK, or a GST certificate in Australia. Have the registration certificate ready, or Facebook will ask why it is missing. [L1440.041]
  • Apply both rules: the business location and the ads' target countries do not limit verification [L1431.044, L0637.008], but the document you submit must match the country where the business is registered [L1440.041].

Account access

  • Use an email address you check regularly for the Business Manager, because verification codes and one-time passwords are sent there. Losing access to that inbox can lock you out of the account. [L1024.007]
  • Facebook accounts can be banned for reasons outside the seller's control, including ownership verification issues with business documents. One seller lost an account a week after launching a winning product, after conflicting document requirements from Facebook. [L0690.027]

Meta ad policy and rejections

Policy training

  • To find the free Facebook Advertising Policy Blueprint course, go to the Policy, Data Privacy and Ads section of Meta's advertising resources. The beginner course takes about 15 to 20 minutes and issues a certificate on completion. [L0637.071]
  • Keep the certificate to show in appeals that the advertiser knows Meta's policies. [L0637.071]

Rejected ad appeal

  1. Read the rejection reason and the review points Facebook provides. [L0871.076]
  2. Remove or rephrase any flagged claim, skin exposure or comparison visual. [L0871.076]
  3. Submit an appeal if the ad did not break a rule. Appeals often succeed for ads with no real issue. [L0871.076]
  4. Relaunch the adjusted version after the appeal. [L0871.076]

Other policy rules

  • Ad platforms can detect a creative that was previously used in a suspended account. Reusing it may get it taken down. Resolve the policy issue at the root rather than relying on re-uploading creatives. [L0638.050]
  • Meta allows fictional characters on Facebook pages only if the character is clearly a brand persona and does not impersonate a real, identifiable person. Standard ad policy still applies to every ad run from the page. (Source D, 2026.) [L1476.003]

Meta billing and payment setup

  • Add payment info to the new ad account: select the country first, then choose a payment method among debit card, credit card, PayPal or online banking, and enter the details. The account is ready to run ads once payment info is added. [L1024.018]
  • Keep a valid credit card on file with Meta and pay all invoices, since unpaid balances are another reason ad accounts get restricted. [L0649.055]
  • Wise is flagged and declined by Facebook because it is not a bank. Avoid using Wise to pay Facebook ads. [L0690.023]
  • Paying Facebook in a local currency such as CAD is allowed, but it makes results harder to track because the local currency is weaker. Many marketers track metrics in USD for comparison. [L0690.026]

Late payments and restricted accounts

  • Late or unpaid ad account bills are a common reason for ad account disabling. Repeated overdue payments, around 3-4 times, can make Meta treat the account as suspicious, so pay due amounts on time. [L0637.023]
  • Pay Facebook ad account bills on time, because late payment can trigger spend limits such as a daily cap of 50 USD. [L1212.034]
  • Logging into a Facebook ad account from a different personal account, or switching between accounts, can cause Facebook to restrict the ad account's automatic payments. This produces failed payments, which can cap the daily budget and freeze ad delivery. [L1432.027]

Recovering a restricted account after a failed payment

  1. Pay the open balance through Facebook support chat, where a support agent opens the payment for you. [L1432.028]
  2. Make small advance payments to rebuild payment history. [L1432.028]
  3. Confirm that the spending limit and daily budget have been restored. [L1432.028]
  • After a payment failure, a Facebook account's spending limit can be cut to a low amount. One seller's limit went from 300 to 50 USD. Support may only promise an increase without applying it, so check the limit yourself after the payment is cleared. [L1432.029]

Microsoft Ads

  • If a new account is automatically suspended for "egregious violations," file an appeal as instructed by Microsoft support. [L0253.052]
  • Do not create duplicate accounts or try different credentials. Duplicate accounts are flagged as a violation of Microsoft's policy. [L0253.052]
  • Wait for the appeal to be approved and the account reinstated. [L0253.052]

TikTok payments

Account and tax requirements

  • TikTok payments may not ask for a billing address. If one is requested, a US address is required. US virtual cards or PayPal can be used as payment methods in that case. [L0363.062]
  • Owning an LLC alone may not be enough for a non-US seller to open a TikTok Shop, because a tax identification number is often required. Check the exact tax ID requirement for your country with a local tax advisor before applying. [L1215.019]
  • A verified TikTok Business Center account usually requires a business account plus legitimate business documents, and often an EIN. Receiving proposals requires no verification, but initiating proposals does. [L1379.044]
  • You need a bank account or a payment method such as PayPal to pay creators. Confirm first whether TikTok takes a commission on Business Center deals, since this was not verified. [L1379.045]

Automatic or manual payment

Mode How it works When the source recommends it Ids
Automatic Charges the saved credit or debit card automatically once a set threshold is reached. Recommended option in the general setup. L1373.004
Manual Adds budget to the TikTok wallet in advance. Automatic payment shows spend that updates a minute or two late and typically understates real spend by 10 to 30 percent. Recommended for aggressive scaling with higher budgets, and for scaling campaigns. L1382.045, L1436.057
  • The sources differ on the default: automatic is recommended in one lesson, while manual is recommended for scaling. Use automatic for ordinary spend, and manual when budgets are high or campaigns are scaling. [L1373.004, L1382.045, L1436.057]

Linking accounts

  • Linking an account may prompt a switch from a personal to a business account. One speaker reported that copyrighted sounds stop being usable after such a switch. This was described as a risk and was not verified, so check the current platform rules before linking. [L1383.015]

New ad account payment setup

  • Make the first four payments with a debit card on a new ad account, because early credit card use on a new account was linked to flagged behavior. Credit cards are better used later for their billing window. [L0351.051]
  • Use a new bank account and payment method dedicated to each business. Credit cards tied to a business address in a different country than the business operates in, or whose time zone and country differ from the Google account settings, can be flagged as potentially fraudulent. Cards already flagged elsewhere or with past failed payments can also trigger suspension. (Older: Source B, 2021-22.) [L0727.010]
  • The customer-facing billing descriptor (the statement name on the card) must be entered together with a number field. Do not use a personal phone number there, because customers may call that number heavily if the product goes viral. Use a separate business or alternate number. [L0655.007]
  • For Ads > Billing, enter the business details and click Save, then add a credit card, debit card or virtual card. PayPal is not accepted as a payment method. [L1135.024]

Agency accounts

  • Setting up an agency account costs $400+/month. It is named as one recovery option for a banned business manager. [L0067.037]
  • With an agency ad account you do not connect your own payment card. You top up the account by bank transfer or crypto instead. [L1021.017]
  • ROAS Agency accepts funding by international bank transfer or crypto only. Top-ups are arranged through WhatsApp with the agency contact. The account is ready for ads only after the first top-up has been made. (Source D, 2026.) [L1253.010]
  • Agency accounts are listed as a workaround for bans, but the same source advises against circumventing platform bans with agency accounts, new accounts or VPN workarounds. [L0067.037, L0577.067]

VPN and account safety

  • A VPN is only needed when researching search results from a different country. It is not required for normal business operation. (Older: Source A, 2020.) [L1111.147]
  • In one case, a TikTok ad account suspension was linked to using a VPN. Check your connection and login setup before managing ads. Once the cause is found and removed, the ads account can be run again without the same problem. [L0638.057]
  • Circumventing platform bans (Google, Facebook, TikTok) using agency accounts, new accounts or VPN workarounds is not recommended. The lesson advised against it in general. [L0577.067]
  • Keep one consistent login and contact setup for each account, and do not switch between personal accounts when logging into an ad account. [L1432.027, L1283.056]

Bans and recovery

Facebook business manager

  • If the business is banned and ID-verified, account recovery is difficult. The options named in the source are using a friend's account, setting up an agency account ($400+/month), or using third-party unlock services (Fiverr). The same source advises against circumventing bans in general. [L0067.037, L0577.067]
  • When a Facebook business manager account is permanently banned, the source describes this procedure:
    1. Contact a Fiverr seller specializing in business manager rental. [L0209.040]
    2. Request new account access. [L0209.040]
    3. Set up ads in the new account. [L0209.040]
  • Following a correct process for launching ads and setting up the business manager reduces the ban risk. A banned account can be recovered by following a specific appeal process. Several students reported few bans when the process was followed. [L1382.068]

Other causes

  • Reusing a creative from a suspended account can get it taken down. Resolve the root policy issue instead. [L0638.050]
  • Suspensions for circumventing systems are often linked to an earlier account. Call the Google Ads policy support number to request a live review. (2023.) [L1283.054]
