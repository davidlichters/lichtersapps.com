Tracking and analytics

  • Add campaign URL options and tracking templates only if you rely on third-party affiliate tracking, UTM builders or custom tracking software. If you use Google Analytics integrated with Shopify's native tracking, you already have sufficient performance data and do not need tracking templates. Most ecommerce stores get complete tracking through these platforms alone. [L0225.036]
  • Google sets the default deadline for GA4 setup. Universal Analytics stopped working in July 2023. [L0964.120]

Company, tax and compliance

  • Treat regulatory and platform-policy compliance as a standing check, not an afterthought. Compliance was the single lesson topic raised in a January 2026 coaching call. [L0817.001]
  • Business tax and personal tax guidance are in course lesson 0.5. The resale and state registration steps are in module zero. [L0964.116]
  • Payment, banking and company rules depend heavily on the country and on local tax law. Check any country-specific decision on company formation or tax with a local accountant or lawyer. [L1512.001]
  • General information only: this is not legal, tax, investment or financial advice. Each owner must evaluate the risks and consult a professional for their own situation. [L0903.025]
  • Before selling into the EU, check the shipping policies of EU stores that sell the same products. Confirm whether they ship to every EU country and whether shipping and taxes differ across countries before you commit. Older advice (2021). [L0801.145]
  • A supplier's name on the packaging is normal in drop shipping. You do not need to hide it. Older advice (2021). [L0834.082]
  • You may buy a supplier's product for yourself or a family member directly from the supplier at wholesale price. This does not need to go through your Shopify store, and it is not unethical. [L0966.092]

Payments and banking

  • Shopify Payments address verification: Provide a utility bill or any mail that proves you own the address. If you are outside the US but have a US address, provide a contract with the address company or mail from a supplier. Older advice (2022). [L0249.077]
  • Payment details at onboarding: These can include a bank account for transfers through TransferWise or a PayPal account email. Keep them in the standardized questionnaire responses so they are easy to find later. Older advice (2021-22). [L1054.012]
  • Online-only banks such as Mercury: Shopify Payments may not accept them, and Shopify support may be unsure. The practical test is to connect the account and see whether it is accepted. Then check community groups for other sellers' experiences with that bank. Older advice (2021). [L1279.093]
  • Shopify subscription payment methods: Credit card, debit card and PayPal are accepted. You can update the payment method later, for example when a business credit card is available. [L1304.016]
  • Email autoresponder billing: Autoresponder subscriptions are billed through the Shopify account. The charge appears on the Shopify bill after the trial ends. Older advice (2021-22). [L1298.034]
  • Gemini API billing: Usage is billed through a Google Cloud billing account that needs a credit or debit card. Charges go to the account linked to the key's project. If a tool runs on someone else's key, their account pays, so every user of a shared tool should enter their own key. [L0794.003]
  • Shared logins: Sharing one login across several people usually breaches the terms of service of paid software such as ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro or Helium 10. The provider can suspend or close the account, so read each tool's terms before you depend on shared access. [L1387.010]
  • Buying software bundles through Whop: The bundle is sold through a third-party checkout platform, Whop, using an affiliate-tracked sign-up link. The seller is therefore not the software vendors themselves. Before paying, check:
    • who the seller is,
    • the refund terms,
    • whether the subscription renews automatically, and at what price. [L1387.017]

Shipping costs and carriers

  • Shipping your own account: Open a UPS or FedEx account online, which is free. Give the supplier your account number so they can ship and bill it to you. You then pay the shipping bill. [L0933.019]
  • Cutting shipping costs for large or heavy products. Older advice (2021). [L0853.056]
    1. Ask the supplier whether they can give a shipping discount through their own carrier accounts.
    2. Get quotes from several freight brokers for recent shipments.
    3. Compare the quotes, because they can differ by hundreds of dollars.
    4. Use the cheapest reliable option.
  • Pricing a large item with a fixed carrier rate can waste a lot of money over time. Get shipping quotes regularly and avoid relying on one carrier, since UPS quotes for large, heavy items can be very high. Older advice (2021). [L0853.057]
  • Products that are unusually costly to ship or need special handling should be checked with the carrier. This course handles them as standard shipping with insurance, not as a separate cost line. Older advice (2022). [L1281.076]
  • Setting up shipping details in Smart SEO:
    1. Turn on the shipping details option.
    2. Enter the country code for the country you ship to.
    3. Enter the minimum handling days, for example 0 if some products ship the same day.
    4. Enter the maximum handling days, for example 3 if orders cannot ship on weekends.
    5. Enter the minimum transit days, for example 1 to 5 days.
    6. Choose the shipping price type: minimum, maximum or fixed amount.
    7. For a fixed amount, enter the currency (USD in the example) and the rate. For free shipping, enter a rate of 0.
    8. Leave the setting turned on and save. [L1337.018]

Shopify store setup: domains, SSL and licences

  • Connecting a domain in Shopify. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292.012, L1292.013, L1292.014]
    1. In the Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Domains.
    2. Click Connect existing domain.
    3. Enter the domain name you purchased (no https://), then click Next.
    4. If the registrar is a major one such as GoDaddy, choose Connect automatically. Shopify asks you to authorize the connection in the registrar's window. Then click Connect and close it.
    5. Shopify verifies the connection and alters the domain's DNS settings. Verification can take a minute or two.
    6. For a smaller registrar that Shopify does not connect to automatically, choose Connect manually and follow the instructions Shopify shows to change the DNS settings.
    7. Once the domain shows as connected, wait for propagation, which can take up to 24 hours.
  • Shopify detects and connects domains from major registrars such as GoDaddy automatically. Smaller registrars require a manual DNS connection. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292.014]
  • SSL: Shopify provides a free SSL certificate for connected custom domains, so you do not need to buy one separately. The status shows SSL pending for a short period, around 30 minutes. After that, HTTPS is active. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292.015]
  • HTTPS is essential for e-commerce. While the SSL status is pending, the site shows as not secure in the browser until the certificate is issued. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292.016]
  • Shrine licences: One Shrine licence per store is enough. Buying more licences for a single store is unnecessary. [L1330.006]
  • Shopify does not lock your store code. You can export all products as a CSV file and download image assets to move them elsewhere. A full rebuild on another platform is not a one-click process. Older advice (2021). [L0968.030]

Software costs and subscriptions

  • Shopify is the only paid software that is a must-have at the start. Canva's free version is enough for design. Stripe Atlas can help a founder operating from abroad set up a US company. Advice from 2023. [L0934.143]
  • Klaviyo offers a free tier, but billing starts once the contact list grows past a set size. Check the contact count against the pricing before the list grows. Advice from 2023. [L0934.144]
  • Canva's subscription cost about 11 GBP (roughly 14 USD) at the time of recording. It included a library of stock images, audio and stock videos that would otherwise cost extra. Check the current price and the licence terms before relying on this figure. [L0754.024]

Marketplace fees and paid promotion

  • Amazon Associates commissions: Rates range from about 4.5 percent to 8.5 percent, depending on the number of transactions sent per month. Lower-volume affiliates earn the lower rate, so commissions stay small until the volume grows. [L0923.022]
  • eBay fees: Commission depends on the product category. The category used in the lesson had about 7 percent commission. Payment processor fees such as PayPal's come on top of this. Calculate the full fee per category before you set prices. [L1441.039]
  • Paid boosts (coins): Coins can start at $5, but $10 per day for the first three days is the usual starting level. Avoid overspending on paid boosts, because heavy spending defeats the organic purpose. [L1429.045]

Health insurance abroad

  • Compare the total annual cost of a plan, meaning the premium plus the copay or deductible, rather than the monthly premium. One example US plan at about 500 USD per month carried a 7,000 USD annual copay. [L1515.019]
  • Many long-term travellers combine travel insurance for large catastrophic events, such as helicopter evacuation from a hike, with paying cash for routine care. Private care abroad can be affordable. For example, an emergency visit with ultrasound in Thailand cost about 120 USD in cash. [L1515.020]
  • SafetyWing offers health and travel cover built for digital nomads and expats. It is an alternative for people who need coverage without a home-country plan. [L1515.021]
