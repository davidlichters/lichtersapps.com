18.3 EIN, tax IDs and supplier paperwork

Which tax numbers apply to you

Your situation What applies Source
US business An EIN is required and free from the IRS. A sales tax certificate applies where your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one. [L0278.037, L0118.008, L1095.002]
Non-US resident starting a US business Apply for an EIN through the international route. An ITIN is used for tax filing without an SSN. [L1090.128, L0804.095, L0963.126]
European business not selling into the US A VAT number is enough. An EIN is not needed. [L0360.070]

Decision order: what to get and when

  1. Get the EIN from the IRS first. It is the minimum to have before contacting suppliers. [L0006.003, L0468.192, L0468.147, L1415.007]
  2. Get a sales tax certificate if your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one. [L0006.004, L0118.008, L0359.088]
  3. Keep a W-9 ready for suppliers that accept one. [L0667.080, L0972.048]
  4. Apply before supplier outreach where possible. Some suppliers require these documents and others do not. [L1116.008, L0808.045]
  5. If you are close to approval, have the documents ready within one to two weeks rather than re-contacting the supplier later. [L0934.004]

The core numbers and what they are

EIN (federal tax ID)

  • An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a 9-digit number the IRS issues to a business. It works like a Social Security number for the company and is given to suppliers instead of your personal SSN. [L0118.006, L0695.009, L1344.010]
  • The format is two digits, a dash and seven digits. A tax ID in that format is the same kind of number as an EIN. [L0855.124]
  • It is a federal tax ID. It is free from the IRS and is needed for tax reporting and for many business tasks in the US. [L0671.108, L1344.010]
  • A US business typically needs an EIN for tax filings and for opening a business bank account. [L0675.004, L1095.002]
  • It is needed to open a business bank account, apply for business licenses, file business taxes and fill in most supplier application forms. [L1415.007] (older advice, 2021)
  • It keeps business tax and accounting records separate from personal records. [L0468.147] (older advice, 2020)
  • Do not use your Social Security number in place of an EIN. Using the EIN keeps business and personal finances separate. [L1344.012]
  • US business suppliers require an EIN. [L0278.037]
  • Many suppliers ask for an EIN during approval. [L0247.102] (older advice, 2021)
  • Suppliers prefer an EIN over a personal SSN. [L0272.047] (older advice, 2022)
  • Suppliers use the EIN and the sales tax ID as interchangeable terms when they request a tax ID. [L0961.017] (older advice, 2020) See Conflicts below.

Sales tax certificate, resale certificate and related names

  • A sales tax certificate is the same thing as a reseller permit. [L0671.112, L0671.249] (older advice, 2021)
  • A sales tax certificate and a resale certificate are the same document, not two separate ones. They can be obtained online per state, and the process is typically fast and automated. [L0967.057] (older advice, 2020)
  • A sales tax permit and a reseller certificate are the same thing. They are based on the EIN and cover collecting and remitting sales tax. Suppliers asking for either are requesting the same document. [L0969.029] (older advice, 2022)
  • In the US, a reseller permit and a sales tax certificate are the same document. [L0934.006]
  • In many states a sales tax license and a resale certificate are the same document, and suppliers may ask for either one. [L0855.120]
  • A resale certificate and a sales tax ID refer to the same thing. Use them interchangeably when registering for sales tax. [L0963.125] (older advice, 2022)
  • A reseller number is essentially a sales tax ID. Some suppliers ask for it, and depending on your state you may need one. [L1274.045] (older advice, 2018)
  • A seller's permit is also called a sales tax permit, sales tax license or reseller's permit. [L1415.008] (older advice, 2021)
  • A business license, sales tax certificate and sales tax registration number usually refer to the same thing, though the name varies by state. [L1285.075]
  • A supplier's request for a business license number generally means the same thing as a reseller permit or sales tax ID. [L0971.015]
  • Names differ by state. Search for the state name plus "sales tax certificate" or "resale certificate." [L0667.079, L0934.006]

W-9 (form, not a number)

  • The W-9 is an IRS form that a US business uses to request the taxpayer identification number of a US-based supplier or contractor. It collects this information in a standard format and applies only when the business pays US-based payees. [L1008.005]
  • A W-9 is usually not needed, but fill it out if a supplier asks for it. [L1085.078] (older advice, 2018)

ITIN

  • An ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a tax ID for individuals who are not US citizens or residents and need to file US taxes. Use it when you are a non-US resident starting a US business. [L1090.128] (older advice, 2021)
  • An ITIN is used only for non-US citizens filing taxes without a Social Security number. [L0278.037]
  • For non-US residents, an ITIN works similarly to an EIN, and suppliers may ask for one or the other. Ask each supplier what they require, and check forums and communities for answers. [L1277.103] (older advice, 2020)

VAT and corporate tax numbers

  • VAT is specific to Europe and an EIN to the US. A VAT number is enough for European business. [L0360.070] (2024 advice)
  • A corporate tax number is not the same as a VAT number. Suppliers may ask for a specific one before authorizing a store. Asking which number a supplier requires before submitting the application avoids delays. [L0360.072]

Getting your EIN

Free application through the IRS

  • An EIN is free on the official IRS website (irs.gov). Many third-party sites charge for the same EIN, so use only official sources. [L0667.078, L1274.044] (older advice, 2018)
  • A third-party service is not needed. Some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing. [L0468.146] (older advice, 2020)
  • Paid services that advertise EIN setup are unnecessary for US residents, though non-residents may need paid help to obtain one. [L1405.027] (older advice, 2021-22)
  • Paid helpers are not needed for this simple process, since many online ads charge fees to do these steps for you. [L1415.016] (older advice, 2021-22)

Procedure: free EIN in the US [L1344.013]

  1. Search for "EIN number" on Google.
  2. Do not click the paid results marked as ads, because these companies charge a fee for setting up an EIN.
  3. Click the first organic result, which is the IRS site at irs.gov.
  4. Apply for the EIN online and answer the questions the IRS asks. No paid helper is needed.
  5. Expect to receive the EIN typically within about 15 minutes. [L1344.013]

Procedure: apply for an EIN (older advice, 2022) [L0963.126]

  1. Use only the IRS website, not paid third-party services.
  2. If you live outside the US, use the international applicant route, which requires a phone call.
  3. Check the IRS phone hours before calling. [L0963.126]

Procedure: apply for an EIN (older advice, 2020) [L0468.146]

  1. Go to the IRS website and apply for an employer identification number (EIN) for free.
  2. The application takes about five minutes.
  3. Do not pay a third-party service for the EIN, since some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing.
  4. Use the EIN instead of your social security number when applying for supplier accounts. [L0468.146]

  • An online EIN application usually takes 5 to 10 minutes and produces a 9-digit EIN. [L0695.011]
  • The IRS online EIN application only works during IRS business hours, even though it is an online form. If you try early in the morning or late at night, it may not be available, so plan the application for business hours. [L1344.014]
  • Get the EIN as early as possible. It is free on the IRS website, is required by many suppliers, and takes about 10 minutes online. [L0359.087]

Timing relative to your LLC

  • Apply for your EIN directly on the IRS website after your LLC is officially approved. [L0118.007]
  • An EIN can be obtained without an LLC. When reaching out to suppliers without an LLC yet, get an EIN from the IRS website for free in about five minutes. [L0272.047] (older advice, 2022)
  • Form an LLC first only if you want to give company details at the time of approval. [L0852.028] (older advice, 2020)
  • Not having an LLC or S-Corp usually does not affect supplier approvals. The coach had never seen that happen. [L1277.107] (older advice, 2020)
  • A business license and insurance are optional for an online store. However, get an EIN at a minimum if you do business in the US. [L1277.101] (older advice, 2020)

Sole proprietors

  • You can contact suppliers as a sole proprietor once the store is built, and no legal entity is needed. [L0852.028] (older advice, 2020)
  • A US business without a Social Security number can get an EIN from the IRS website. [L0801.140] (older advice, 2021)
  • Get an EIN even as a sole proprietor. It is only needed for businesses in the US. [L1277.100] (older advice, 2020)
  • A sole proprietor without employees does not strictly need an EIN. They can obtain one or use the owner's SSN instead. [L1415.006] (older advice, 2021-22)
  • A sole proprietor can be approved by some US suppliers, according to reports from store owners, but this was not verified by the lesson. [L0359.090]

Reusing an existing EIN

  • An existing EIN from a previous business may be reusable for a new store, although the business type on the EIN may need updating. Verify with a tax professional before reuse. [L1111.142] (older advice, 2020)
  • Reusing an old EIN from a business that never started is probably allowed, but verify it with a lawyer or a legal forum before you do. [L1277.105] (older advice, 2020)

Who applies and how the form is filled

  • An EIN can be applied for directly by the company. The title used on the form (president, CEO) is a matter of how you describe yourself. A partnership with several officers requires more care. [L0834.081] (older advice, 2021)

Why the EIN matters for pacing

  • Tax documents, EIN and state registrations can be completed after you start, because many suppliers still approve new retailers without them. [L0934.004] (2023 advice)
  • For a foreign founder, an EIN can take a long time, sometimes up to around 60 days. Keep building the store and contacting suppliers in the meantime, and hold back top-tier suppliers until your business documents are complete. [L0968.017] (older advice, 2021)

Applying from outside the US

Non-US citizens and residents

  • Non-US citizens must call the IRS to apply. US citizens can apply online at irs.gov. [L0270.026] (older advice, 2021)
  • Non-US residents cannot get an EIN online and must call the IRS, which begins its process at IRS.gov. [L0469.050] (older advice, 2021)
  • A non-US resident can get an EIN on the IRS website, though a phone call may be part of the process. [L0359.087]
  • Non-US applicants can get an EIN by calling the IRS international applicant number listed on the IRS EIN page. US citizens can use the same online process as in the course. [L0804.095] (2023 advice)
  • Applicants outside the US may be directed to call in instead of applying online, so check the foreign applicant route before applying. [L0968.018] (older advice, 2021)
  • Non-US businesses can apply for the equivalent tax identification number on the same site. [L0801.140] (older advice, 2021)
  • To obtain a US resale certificate as a non-US resident, form a US business entity first and apply through that entity. A virtual address is not enough, a real company address is required. [L0836.001] (2024 advice)
  • Stripe Atlas was named as one common formation service, but a state-issued resale certificate, not Stripe Atlas, is what suppliers usually ask for. [L0836.001] (2024 advice)

Procedure for the international route

  • See the international applicant procedure under Getting your EIN above. [L0963.126]

Canadian sellers

  • A Canadian seller does not need an EIN, because it is an IRS number for US businesses. [L1277.102] (older advice, 2020)
  • A Canadian business selling into the US probably needs a US tax ID, which is a FEIN that can be obtained online from the IRS. This is not certain, so confirm it with Canadians who already run US businesses or with a tax professional. [L0853.024] (older advice, 2021)
  • Some US suppliers ask Canadian sellers for an EIN or federal tax ID, which requires a short call to the IRS. Requirements differ by brand, so check with other Canadian sellers in community forums. [L1279.080] (older advice, 2021)
  • A business registered in Canada can get a US EIN from the IRS by phone and should not get a resale certificate first. When an application asks for one, leave that section blank or state that the business is based in Canada and has no nexus in any US state. A state tax ID and resale certificate follow later, once a state threshold is reached. [L1116.009] (2025 advice)

Foreign entities and sales tax

  • A foreign entity without a nexus in any US state probably does not need a sales tax certificate, but this is unverified. Submit the supplier application and check whether approval requires it. [L0853.025] (older advice, 2021)
  • Do not collect resale certificates for states where you have no obligation, just because a supplier asks for one. Only obtain them for states where you have physical presence or where you crossed the sales threshold in a year. [L0968.022] (older advice, 2021)

Sales tax certificate and state resale documents

When you need one

  • A sales tax certificate is required by most suppliers if your state has a sales tax. If your state has no sales tax, you do not need one. [L0118.008]
  • A sales tax certificate is needed only if the supplier requires it and the business is in a state that has sales tax. In states without sales tax, no certificate is needed. In other states, the certificate can be applied for online in about 10 to 15 minutes. [L0359.088]
  • Some suppliers ask for a state sales tax certificate and some do not. Requirements depend on the state where the business is based. Having the certificate does no harm and is required in some states. [L0664.026] (older advice, 2020)
  • Whether you need a resale certificate depends on the supplier and on your state. Some suppliers ask for one and others do not, and some states do not issue them. Before a state's sales tax threshold is reached, you usually do not need a tax certificate in that state. [L0961.018] (older advice, 2020)
  • Many suppliers ask for a seller's permit or sales tax certificate, but not all do. Requirements are state specific, and states without sales tax do not need a certificate there. [L1088.079] (older advice, 2020)
  • Taxes and sales tax: for each state, look up the state's reseller certificate. The certificate allows collecting and remitting sales tax and is required by most suppliers. [L0964.123] (2023 advice)
  • In a state where the business is based, a sales tax certificate is required, and suppliers may ask for a tax-exempt form. The tax-exempt certificate usually requires an existing sales tax certificate. [L0665.094] (older advice, 2022)
  • Whether suppliers require an EIN and a sales tax ID before approval varies by supplier and by state. Ask each supplier directly rather than assuming the same requirement. [L0359.089]
  • Some suppliers ask for a resale certificate and others do not, so ask each supplier before assuming you need one. [L0468.192] (older advice, 2020)
  • Get a resale certificate early, since many suppliers ask for one even though not all do. Requirements depend on the state where you do business. [L0808.045] (2026 advice)
  • Apply for a state resale certificate or sales tax permit early, as suppliers often ask for it when you are approved. [L0667.079] (2025 advice)
  • Do not apply for resale permits in every state a supplier asks for if you have no sales nexus there. Offer a W-9 instead, along with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667.080] (2025 advice)
  • Provide a resale certificate for the state where your business operates when a supplier asks for one. Do not treat the certificate as a general document for every state. [L0470.083] (2023 advice)
  • Get a sales tax certificate and an EIN tax ID eventually. Obtaining them sooner rather than later avoids delays with suppliers that ask for them. [L1088.080] (older advice, 2020)
  • A reseller number is not urgent in the early stage. [L1274.045] (older advice, 2018)
  • Purchases from suppliers are not taxed when you resell, because you give the supplier a W-9 if requested or your sales tax certificate, also called a resale certificate. Sales tax is separate from income tax. [L0972.048] (2025 advice)

Getting a state certificate

Procedure: sales tax certificate (general) [L0118.009]

  1. Search Google for "[Your State] sales tax certificate" and use only official.gov websites; do not pay third-party sites.
  2. Complete online registration if available in your state (most allow this and often free).
  3. You will receive a downloadable PDF or physical certificate by mail. [L0118.009]

Procedure: resale or sales tax certificate (2023 advice) [L0251.043]

  1. Go to your state's.gov website.
  2. Search for "[Your State] resale certificate" or "[Your State] sales tax certificate."
  3. Complete the online form (typically 5 minutes).
  4. Receive your certificate number.
  5. Provide this number to suppliers to purchase at wholesale without sales tax. [L0251.043]

Procedure: resale certificate (2025 advice) [L1116.008]

  1. Search Google for the registration state plus sales tax certificate or resale certificate.
  2. Open the official.gov page of that state.
  3. Complete the online form. Many states give a PDF immediately; some mail it, which can take about a week.
  4. Get it before supplier outreach, because some suppliers require it and others do not. [L1116.008]

Procedure: sales tax certificate online (older advice, 2023) [L0934.006]

  • In a state with sales tax, search for the state name plus sales tax certificate or reseller certificate and apply through the state's online form. [L0934.006]

Procedure: sales tax ID after your EIN (older advice, 2022) [L1113.050]

  • Get a sales tax ID after your EIN, as early as practical, because many suppliers ask for it. The setup is usually done online, and states without sales tax do not need one. [L1113.050]

Procedure: when a supplier asks for a resale number and the state has no certificate (older advice, 2022) [L0854.088]

  1. Search for an exemption form on the official state government website.
  2. If none is listed, call or email the state's contact number and ask for a document stating you are exempt.
  3. Send that document to the supplier. [L0854.088]

Procedure: resale certificate in a state with no registration yet (older advice, 2022) [L1092.006]

  1. Ask the accountant for the exact name of the state sales and use tax form.
  2. Search that form name online to check whether it can be filed online.
  3. If it is available online, file it there instead of visiting a city or county office. [L1092.006]

Multi-state and specific states

  • Do not apply for resale permits in every state a supplier asks for if you have no sales nexus there. Offer a W-9 instead, along with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667.080]
  • A seller's permit is required in your own home state, and most suppliers will ask for it. You generally do not need a permit in every state, although some suppliers may request one per state. [L1415.009] (older advice, 2021-22)
  • A supplier that does not receive a Florida annual resale certificate may offer to charge 7% Florida sales tax on orders shipped within Florida, or may refuse shipments to Florida. Send your home-state resale certificate first and state that you have no economic nexus in Florida. [L0835.136] (2023 advice)
  • Resale certificate rules differ by state. A business formed in Delaware may not need a sales tax certificate because Delaware has no sales tax. A Delaware wholesaler license was mentioned as an option for businesses set up there. [L0836.002] (2024 advice)
  • For a business in Delaware, the supplier example is handled with a W-9 instead. See the procedure under What to tell suppliers. [L0253.027]

W-9 forms

When and how to use a W-9

  • Fill out a W-9 when a supplier asks for one. [L1085.078] (older advice, 2018)
  • Give a W-9 if requested, or your sales tax certificate, when purchasing for resale. [L0972.048] (2025 advice)
  • Offer a W-9 instead of applying for resale permits in states where you have no nexus, together with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667.080] (2025 advice)
  • A D&B (Dun & Bradstreet) number is not necessary for the business. [L1085.078] (older advice, 2018)

Procedure: handling a W-9 request from a large business customer (older advice, 2021-22) [L1396.066]

  1. Keep a blank Form W-9 PDF in the PDF files section of the corporate information board.
  2. When a customer asks for it, print the form.
  3. Fill in the business name and the tax information.
  4. Send the completed form to the customer.
  5. Keep the file ready because large companies request it repeatedly. [L1396.066]

What to tell suppliers

Before you contact suppliers

  • Ask each supplier directly what they require before assuming a requirement. [L0359.089, L0468.192]
  • Get the EIN before contacting suppliers. The EIN is the minimum to have before contacting suppliers. [L0468.192] (older advice, 2020)
  • Get a resale certificate before supplier outreach, since some suppliers require it. [L1116.008]
  • Store EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. D-U-N-S numbers are not required for this model. [L0835.112] (2023 advice)
  • Ask which number a supplier requires, a corporate tax number or a VAT number, before submitting the application. [L0360.072]

What to give the supplier

  • Provide the official LLC name and the business address where the LLC is registered, not a private mailing address. [L0801.148] (older advice, 2021)
  • The company name and EIN are entered on supplier approval forms, which usually ask for company details in a one-page form. The company name is not shown to customers. [L0962.120] (older advice, 2021)
  • One LLC can run several stores. When calling suppliers, use the store name, and on approval forms list the store names you will sell under alongside the corporation's legal name. [L0853.044] (older advice, 2021)
  • Provide a resale certificate for the state where your business operates when a supplier asks for one. [L0470.083] (2023 advice)
  • Store EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. [L0835.112] (2023 advice)

Procedure: when a supplier asks for a state tax certificate and your state does not issue one (2024 advice) [L0253.027]

  1. Contact the supplier asking for their tax certificate requirement.
  2. If they ask for a state tax certificate, start with your business registration state (e.g., Delaware).
  3. If your state does not issue a sales tax certificate (e.g., Delaware), provide a W9 form from the IRS instead.
  4. Tell the supplier: "I am a Delaware business; please see the attached W9."
  5. Ask if the W9 is acceptable.
  6. If not, follow up with any documents your state requires. [L0253.027]

Credit, references and business details

  • Suppliers typically request credit and banking information as part of the retailer approval process. Use a business credit card or debit card for authorization rather than bank account details to maintain privacy and control. [L0243.040] (older advice, 2019)
  • Leave the business references blank if you are not applying for credit. Use cash or a credit card only, since no credit terms are needed in the dropshipping model. [L0274.046] (2023 advice)
  • Drop shipping stores do not require credit for supplier approval. A low credit score caused by past insolvency or bankruptcy does not block working with suppliers. [L0801.141] (older advice, 2021)
  • D&B numbers are not required for this model. [L0835.112, L1085.078]

Supplier agreements and dropship policy

  • For high-ticket items ($5,000+), you can request a supplier agreement. Search "dropshipping supplier agreement" on Google or check competitor sites for templates to adapt. The Source A material does not provide standard agreement forms. [L0268.046] (older advice, 2020)
  • You do not need your own dropship policy. Suppliers that ask for one are rare, and a simple agreement from a template is enough. [L0666.048] (2024 advice)

Conflict: [L0268.046] says high-ticket suppliers may ask for a supplier agreement, while [L0666.048] says a dropship policy is rarely requested. Use a template agreement for high-ticket suppliers, and a simple agreement otherwise. [L0268.046, L0666.048]

MAP, MSRP and coupons

Definitions

  • On a supplier price sheet, MSRP is the manufacturer's suggested retail price, while MAP is the minimum advertised price you may list at. [L0470.073] (2023 advice)
  • Check the case pack size, since the wholesale cost may cover a set of several units rather than one. [L0470.073] (2023 advice)
  • Some markets have no legally enforceable minimum advertised price (MAP), but suppliers may follow unwritten rules, and discounting can reduce their supply. In the UK, undercutting a supplier's price can cause the supply to dry up because of damage to the supplier's reputation with other retailers. [L1441.034]

Coupons and MAP compliance

  • MAP compliance is judged by the price displayed on the store, not by the price after a coupon. A coupon at checkout is usually allowed, but a displayed price below MAP is a violation unless the supplier agreement permits it. [L1112.057] (older advice, 2021)
  • Check the supplier's MAP agreement before offering coupon codes. A 20 dollar coupon on a 200 dollar MAP product is allowed as long as the product page still shows 200 dollars. [L1112.058] (older advice, 2021)
