6.22 Other details

Foundations for conversion

  • Market knowledge, content development and customer understanding feed directly into conversion rate optimization. Outdated basics therefore weaken every later marketing and sales effort. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0761.005]
  • A small store with good suppliers and steady traffic can make money before organic traffic builds. Organic traffic grows over time as Google recognizes the site as high quality. (Older advice, 2020.) [L1111.085]

Sales channels and store setup

  • The Shop sales channel can be added to Shopify sales channels. It rarely generates significant volume but does not hurt to include. [L0278.013]

Product images in Search product ads

  • Image extensions in Search product ads have an approval rate of approximately 50%, with no clear rejection reason. [L0210.037]
  • Upload product images anyway, because approved images generate more clicks. [L0210.037]

SEO titles and SKUs

  • Adding the SKU number to the SEO title is fine and helps SEO. [L0805.048]
  • The product title follows what the top competitors put in their titles. [L0805.048]
  • Products without variants show the SKU in the SEO title. Variant products do not include the SKU. [L0805.048]

Ad funnel (older advice, 2021)

  1. Run targeted Google Shopping ads to product pages optimized for trust. [L0270.051]
  2. Use dynamic Facebook remarketing ads and email marketing to bring back visitors who left without buying. [L0270.051]
  3. Optimize lead value by earning more from every sale once a customer purchases. [L0270.051]
  4. Repeat to maximize revenue per customer. [L0270.051]

Affiliate banners in Refersion (older advice, 2021-22)

  • When uploading a banner creative to Refersion, set the landing page URL to match the banner type. [L0288.017]
    • General business-wide banner: link to the homepage. [L0288.017]
    • Product-specific banner: link to the specific product page. [L0288.017]
  • This ensures visitors land on the appropriate destination, where they find details about the offer. [L0288.017]

Trade buyer capture (2024)

  1. Create a page with a name such as "interior designer discounts" or "to the trade", and link it from the homepage and the footer menu. [L0360.024]
  2. Add an exit-intent overlay that asks whether the visitor is an interior designer and collects their email for trade discounts. [L0360.024]
  3. Send the captured emails a personal reply that offers custom quotes. [L0360.024]
  4. Create a separate trade contact page with its own contact form. The form collects name, email, phone, products of interest and quantity. [L0360.024]

Returns benchmark (older advice, 2020)

  • Aim for a return rate under 1 percent. [L1277.080]

Case: high Facebook CTR, no sales

  • Starting point: A store with a high Facebook CTR but no sales. [L0773.061]
  • Changes: Changes were made to the store, including the store adjustments covered in this section. [L0773.061]
  • Result: Within a couple of hours, the store received two sales. [L0773.061]
