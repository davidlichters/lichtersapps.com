6.17 Live chat, forms and customer contact

Why live chat and fast answers matter

  • Live chat removes friction during the buying decision. It lets customers ask questions instantly, helps you close sales in real time, and is a more convenient alternative to contact forms or email. [L0151.002]
  • Live chat leads to extra sales because shoppers get answers in real time instead of waiting hours for an email reply. For a store built this way, live chat is almost a standard feature. [L0894.015]
  • Live chat lets visitors with a question get an answer while still on the site, instead of searching for an email address, filling in a contact form and waiting. Answering in near real time can close sales that would otherwise be lost, so live chat matters from the early stage of a store. [L1311.003]
  • A live chat button on the store lets shoppers ask last-minute questions on product pages. This recovers sales that would otherwise be lost, and real-time answers at the moment of doubt are the core benefit. [L0948.002]
  • Shoppers expect fast answers and fast delivery, so a visible way to get instant responses helps convert browsers into buyers. Competitors offering next-day delivery make slow answers and 2-3 day shipping a conversion risk. [L1399.003]
  • A live chat widget, for example the Tidio Live Chat app, lets visitors ask questions and get answers immediately while browsing. This turns browsers into buyers, since shoppers expect instant answers and fast delivery. [L1400.003]

Setting up Tidio

  1. Install Tidio from the Shopify App Store. [L0635.022]
  2. Turn on the automations so the chat answers basic questions such as pricing and delivery times automatically. [L0635.022]
  3. Customize the widget appearance. [L0635.022]
  4. Under Chatbot, turn on the Welcome series under Increase sales so visitors receive a welcome message. [L0635.022]
  5. Under Increase sales, open Cart abandoned and use a template. [L0635.022]
  6. Set the discount in the abandoned cart flow. The video changes 15 to 10 percent. Accept, activate, choose a coupon such as WELCOME10 with a value of 10, and save. [L0635.022]

Chat widget design and placement

  • In the theme customizer, open the App embeds section to change the chat widget. Options include the widget color, text color, button color, and the icon (chat bubble, smiley face or hand wave), with position set to bottom right and the lowest vertical position. [L1311.007]
  • Pick the icon and colors that fit the site, since no single option is better. [L1311.007]
  • A widget color that contrasts with the site's palette makes the chat icon easier to notice, which increases the chance of a click. Icon style is mostly a matter of brand fit and taste. [L1311.008]
  • Once chat is active, the chat icon appears on every page of the storefront, so visitors can start a conversation from any page. [L1311.009]
  • Older (2021-2022): chat widgets must not open as a large bubble that covers more than a third of the screen on mobile. Configure them to open only after the visitor has read part of the page or has stayed on the page for a set time. [L1099.006]

Chat greeting

  • The greeting shown next to the chat icon can be edited in the widget settings, and a preview updates as the text changes. The default text is a generic greeting that invites questions and promises help. [L1311.010]
  • Formula for a chat welcome message:
    • Name the store.
    • Invite the visitor to ask a specific question.
    • Add a short reassurance of a quick reply.
    • Keep it to one or two short sentences so it is easy to read in a small widget and gives the visitor a clear reason to click. [L1311.011]
  • Example 1: [L1311.011]
Welcome to [Store Name]! Ask us anything about sizes, shipping or your order.
  • Example 2: [L1311.011]
Hi, this is [Store Name]. Have a question before you buy? Send it over and we will reply shortly.

Capturing customer details in chat

Two sources give different rules for when to ask for contact details. Use the rule that matches your response speed.

  • Email before chat (Shopify Inbox): Shopify Inbox asks for the customer's email before chat. Keeping the email field is acceptable because people who ask buying questions usually give their email. The trade-off is fewer chat starts. [L0856.037]
  • Capture when you cannot reply quickly: Live chat should capture the customer's name and email if you cannot reply within a minute or two, so you can follow up later. [L1283.073]
  • No capture when you reply quickly: If you can respond quickly from your phone, this capture is not necessary. [L1283.073]
  • Which to use: Keep the email gate if chat starts matter less than lead quality. Use the name and email capture only when a reply within a minute or two is not realistic. Drop it when you can answer from your phone in that window. [L0856.037, L1283.073]

Recommendations in chat

  • Personalized product recommendations given in chat, based on a shopper's stated needs such as skin type, can raise conversion rate and create upsell opportunities. The AI agent can suggest a fitting product directly in the conversation. [L0753.004]

Response speed

  • Older (2020): Answer live chat, phone calls and emails quickly, because a slow reply can lose a sale that is worth $1,000 or more in net profit over a month. [L0468.090]
  • Older (2022): Customer service responsiveness affects conversions. When customers call or email with questions and receive no reply, they abandon the purchase. Monitor inquiries and respond quickly. [L0272.066]
  • Older (2022): Early on, a store's conversion rate is driven by manual follow-up with every inquiry. Even one or two extra sales from inquiries can put a young store in profit. [L1092.082]
  • Older (2022): In the first sales phase, handle every inbound contact manually. Answer the phone quickly, call back missed calls, and reply to every inquiry with real effort. This personal touch drives high conversion for a new store. [L0963.060]
  • Make the response fast and convenient, because even an interested reader will procrastinate when responding costs effort. Offer a toll-free number, consider staffing it around the clock, and consider fax, website or email response options. [L1370.093]

Quote forms for trade and bulk buyers

  • Custom quote forms built with Wufoo or Typeform trigger Zapier to send a private Slack message to the sales team and auto-populate a Google Sheets quote tracker. Respond to inquiries within minutes to maximize quote-to-order conversion. [L0286.018]
  • Quote reply template: [L0360.025]
Hi [customer name],

Thank you so much for your inquiry. We work with many [customer type, e.g. interior designers] and we are more than happy to provide custom quotes for your business.

I am the owner of [store name]. Please reply to this email with the products you are interested in and the quantities, and I will get back to you with a quote.

Best regards,
[your name]
[store name]
  • Trade program welcome template: [L0360.026]
Welcome to our trade program. We love working with [customer type]. If you ever need special orders or bulk orders, please email [trade email alias, e.g. trade@yourstore.com] or call [phone number].

Phone numbers on the site

  • Showing a phone number on the store tends to raise the conversion rate, even when visitors never call. It signals that the business can be reached and reduces doubt before buying. [L0700.001]
  • Add a phone number on the store's contact page. Customers rarely call, but its presence builds trust, and the few who call are more likely to buy. [L0359.060]
  • Place the business phone number in three spots: [L0700.012]
    • the header, which shows it on every page;
    • the footer, for visitors who look for contact details;
    • the contact page, for visitors who look for contact details.
  • Older (2021): Set a voicemail message that thanks the caller, says you are away or helping another customer, and asks for a name, email address and inquiry. Even if you rarely answer the phone, the number builds trust and makes visitors more likely to buy. [L1090.075]

Contact page

  • Older (2021-2022): A contact page should show several ways to reach the business, such as an email address, a phone number, and a physical address, alongside the contact form. This gives customers more than one option to get help. [L1264.015]
  • Older (2021-2022): The Contact Us page needs its Template suffix set to 'page.contact' instead of the default 'page' to display a contact form. The form shows fields for Name, Email, Phone Number, Message, and a SEND button. [L1264.013]
  • Older (2021-2022): Steps to add the contact form to the Contact Us page: [L1264.014]
    1. Open the Contact Us page under Online Store > Pages.
    2. Scroll to the Template section and open the Template suffix dropdown, which shows 'page' by default.
    3. Select 'page.contact'.
    4. Click Save.
    5. Click View page to check that the contact form appears.

Mid-page "We are here to help" section

  • Older (2021-2022): Build a mid-page help section with this structure: [L1310.032]
    • a heading that says the business is ready to help customers reach their outcome;
    • a line naming the outcome;
    • contact details: phone, email and live chat;
    • one button linking to the contact page.
  • Example: [L1310.032]
Ready to plan your next move?
[one line naming the goal]
Phone: [phone number] | Email: [email address] | Live chat
[Talk to our team]

Personal video follow-up

  • A personal video lets a lead see a face and hear a voice behind the brand. This makes a reply more likely than a generic reminder email. Most competitors do not send such videos, so this method also helps the store stand out. [L1049.009]
