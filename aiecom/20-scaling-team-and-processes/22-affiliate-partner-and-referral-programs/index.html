20.22 Affiliate, partner and referral programs

Long-term approach

  • Dream 100 outreach and affiliate marketing are long-term strategies. They succeed through small daily steps over months, not through intensive hours in single sessions. Consistency across all tasks matters more than the effort spent on any one day. (older, 2021-22) [L0202.009]

Setting up an affiliate program

  • Affiliate programs require proactive recruitment and management. Installing software and adding an affiliate link to your footer is not enough. You must actively identify and recruit potential affiliates. (older, 2021-22) [L0213.002]

  • Do not install affiliate software and wait for affiliates to find your program. They will not discover it without direct outreach, so you must recruit them even though the infrastructure is in place. (older, 2021-22) [L0213.016]

  • Setup procedure:

    1. Install affiliate software such as Refersion in your Shopify store.
    2. Identify your 10 bestselling, consistently in-stock products.
    3. Set commission rates for each product based on your margins (5-10% typical).
    4. Configure each product and its commission rate in the affiliate software.
    5. Proactively recruit affiliates and provide them with unique tracking links and dashboard login credentials.

    (older, 2021-22) [L0213.015]

  • Affiliates need dashboard access to monitor their performance, including sales count, click-through rates and pending payouts. Without visibility into results, they cannot judge whether their promotion works and will disengage. (older, 2021-22) [L0213.014]

  • Reversion is affiliate management software. It stores product details and information in a backend portal where affiliates can access everything when they log in. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.004]

Finding and recruiting affiliates

  • Identify potential affiliates by researching influential content creators in your niche: bloggers, YouTubers, podcasters and social media personalities with engaged audiences. Contact them directly with specific affiliate offers and commission details instead of expecting them to find a generic affiliate link. (older, 2021-22) [L0213.018]

  • Affiliate links are identifiable by extra URL parameters or query strings appended to the end, unlike plain domain URLs. Links with these additions indicate affiliate promotion. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.002]

  • Email is your primary contact target. For websites, check the Contact Us page. For social media users, try private messages. For YouTube creators, check the About tab for a business email. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.003]

  • To find potential YouTube affiliates:

    1. Search YouTube for best [product category], using the same approach as a Google search.
    2. For each result, go to the About tab of the channel.
    3. Look for a business email address in the About tab.

    (older, 2021-22) [L0212.004]

  • Use the affiliate outreach email template provided in the lesson resources. Customise it by filling in your affiliate program details, business information and specific offers before sending it to prospects. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.009]

  • If a potential affiliate agrees to join but has not yet signed up, create a follow-up trigger to remind them with a brief check-in message. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.007]

Tracking affiliates

  • When recording affiliates in your tracker, include the individual's name if you can identify it. Otherwise, record the site name. To find an individual, check the website's About Us page or, for blogs, look for a consistent author name across posts. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.001]

  • Affiliate tracking spreadsheet columns: (older, 2021-22) [L0212.005]

    Column Content
    Name Person or site
    Site/Channel URL
    Contact Details & Method How you contact them
    Target Product Type What they currently promote
    Contacted? yes/no
    Outcome? yes/no, became affiliate
    Comments Optional

  • Record the products each potential affiliate already promotes in the Target Product Type column. This helps you tailor your outreach to their existing audience and content. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.006]

  • Use the spreadsheet for two purposes: to avoid wasting time contacting people who declined, and to keep a roster of who has enrolled in your affiliate program. (older, 2021-22) [L0212.012]

Affiliate support packages

  • For each product offer, create an affiliate support package:

    1. Original high-resolution product images in a shared Google Drive folder, so affiliates can edit them for their websites.
    2. Detailed information about product features and benefits.
    3. Product videos from your supplier or self-produced, with clear directions on where to find them.
    4. The cookie window duration and affiliate commission amount for that specific offer.
    5. Multiple banner ad formats, so affiliates can choose between banners or custom content.

    (older, 2021-22) [L0211.005]

  • Audit each product page to confirm it contains original product images, feature and benefit descriptions, product videos with clear directions, cookie window and commission details, and banner ad options. If the product page includes all these elements, direct affiliates to it instead of assembling separate support materials. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.013]

  • In high-ticket dropshipping, do not give affiliate partners free products to test. Products become classified as used goods and cannot be resold afterward. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.006]

  • Educate each affiliate partner on the top-selling product features, key benefits and the specific problems the product solves. Affiliates rarely receive the actual product, so they cannot learn these points themselves. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.007]

  • If an affiliate has already bought and used your product, they may create their own promotional content instead of using your standard materials. This is acceptable and can be valuable because it reflects firsthand experience. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.012]

Managing and communicating with affiliates

  • Actively manage and support affiliate partners after recruitment. Passive approaches fail because affiliates have many other products to promote. Support helps them succeed and stay engaged long-term. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.001]
  • Affiliates maintain networks and communities where they share successful offers and partnership experiences. When you give strong support, partners refer others to your program by word of mouth without extra outreach. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.002]
  • Klaviyo and Drip are email management platforms. Use them to maintain a dedicated communication list with all active affiliate partners. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.003]
  • Expecting affiliates to research products and create all promotional content from scratch, without support, significantly reduces their success rate and engagement. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.011]
  • Communicate with affiliate partners regularly whenever you launch new offers, change or update products, release new models, or make other business changes. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.008]
  • Ask individual affiliates which products their audience is looking for, and invite them to suggest products for new offers. Do not promote only your pre-selected offerings. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.009]
  • Regular communication and two-way engagement build lasting relationships. This improves retention and increases active promotion. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.010]
  • Periodically check in with affiliates and ask whether you can help them with anything. Mirror your customer service approach instead of only pushing promotional opportunities. Combine this with regular product updates and new offer announcements. (older, 2021-22) [L0211.014]

Dream 100 outreach

  • Do not contact all 100 partners on day one. Reach out in batches over time, follow up with non-responders every week or every two weeks, and dedicate time to the partners who respond. [L0528.042]

  • Without logging the contact date, the response and the next follow-up in the sheet, outreach gets forgotten and responses are lost. Every contacted partner needs a date and notes. [L0528.043]

  • Dream 100 outreach is a recurring activity for the lifetime of the business, not a one-time setup. Put it in the calendar for one hour a week or one hour every two weeks. [L0528.044]

  • Working-sheet procedure:

    1. Email the partner's contact address with specific collaboration ideas.
    2. When a partner responds positively, copy their row from the master list into the working sheet.
    3. Enter the date you contacted them.
    4. Write a note on the potential collaboration, such as a podcast appearance or a guest post.
    5. Follow up on any partner who has not replied, using the same sheet.

    [L0528.045]

  • Dream 100 relationship work can only be done by the business owner. Comments, feedback and conversations written on your behalf lose their value when someone else writes them. A virtual assistant can still handle the research phase, for example by compiling YouTube channels above a set subscriber count on a given topic, if given clear instructions. (older, 2021-22) [L0529.003]

Referral programs

  • An automated referral flow is built once and then runs indefinitely in the background without ongoing manual work. It scales without adding staff time. [L0417.024]

  • A referral program can pay affiliate commissions to members who refer others. The referral links are tied to member logins. (older, 2021) [L0834.106]

  • Members refer others for three core reasons: they know people seeking financial freedom or a real business skill, they want someone to compete and build alongside, and they want peers in their community. [L0214.007]

  • When recruiting referrals, show results and evidence that the program works, such as wins and earnings examples, rather than making promises. Combine this with your personal testimony, because an existing member's authentic endorsement carries more weight than marketing materials. [L0214.010]

  • Referral procedure for E-Converts:

    1. Show the prospect results from inside E-Converts: pull up the wins channel, share earnings examples (16-year-olds earning $20,000+ monthly, 17-year-olds earning $500,000 in 90 days, adults earning six figures monthly), and share your personal experience. Members' endorsements are more convincing than marketing materials.
    2. Send them the referral link and direct them to book a call with the team, instructing them to enter your Discord name in the Did someone recommend you? field during booking.
    3. Once they join and complete their payment, you receive your 25% commission within 48 hours.

    [L0214.005]

  • Commission rates depend on the program. Product affiliate commissions are set per product on your margins, typically 5-10% (older, 2021-22) [L0213.015]. The E-Converts member referral commission is 25% [L0214.005].

  • Reinvest affiliate commission earnings into the business. Allocate the money to testing new products, scaling advertising or other growth activities, so referral income becomes leverage for your own scaling. [L0214.011]

  • You can refer anyone to E-Converts: a friend, family member, co-worker, or anyone in your direct messages whom you believe would benefit from the community and succeed in it. [L0214.012]
