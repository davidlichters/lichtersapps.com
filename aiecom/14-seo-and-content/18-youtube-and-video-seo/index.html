14.18 YouTube and Video SEO

YouTube SEO: Why Video Needs Its Own Strategy

  • YouTube has its own search system, so video keywords must be researched separately, even when the same topics are in the written content strategy. [L0758.017]
  • YouTube uses a simpler ranking logic than Google web search, which uses more than 200 ranking signals. For YouTube SEO, the practical focus is keyword-matched titles and descriptions, plus viewer engagement such as watch time and clicks. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.022]
  • YouTube works like a search engine. Videos can stay findable and keep attracting viewers for years. (older, 2021-22) [L1424.002]
  • A video can rank in Google results before a comparable text page does. For search terms where competition for text results is strong, a relevant YouTube video that answers the query can earn a first-page spot until the store's own page ranks. (older, 2021-22) [L1424.002]
  • Google often shows video results on the first page, sometimes two videos from the same channel, which is unusual for text results. Searches for reviews, best-of queries and how-to queries are especially likely to display YouTube videos. (older, 2021-22) [L1424.003]
  • Unlike Google web search, several videos from the same channel can rank for one search term on YouTube. Targeting the same keyword with more than one video is therefore acceptable. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.008]
  • Video is optional for content marketing, because written content alone can produce enough traffic. The easiest start is turning existing written content into video. A video can be made for YouTube specifically if it targets terms people already search for. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.017]
  • A YouTube channel does not need a big audience to work. A small number of relevant videos can still gain subscribers and website traffic. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.016]
  • Video is an extra traffic source. A channel with subscribers and engagement also increases business value if the business is sold later. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.016]
  • The number of subscribers is not tied to the number of videos. Five to ten good videos on topics people are interested in can already bring subscribers and website visitors. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.020]

Choosing Video Topics From Demand

  • Compare view counts with video age. A video with many views in a short time signals a topic people want. Use these comparisons to choose content ideas and to spot topics that draw little attention. (older, 2021-22) [L0478.018]
  • Example: in one surfing channel, a video posted about a month earlier had around 249,000 views, while a video posted three months earlier had around 13,000 views. The gap shows which subject resonates with the audience and guides the next content choice. (older, 2021-22) [L0478.019]
  • Use trend tools to choose video topics people already respond to, then make your own take that is better than what is trending. [L0487.061]
  • Watch time matters on YouTube, because longer viewing causes the platform to show the video to more people. [L0487.061]
  • Interest-based videos can target the broader topic around your products rather than the product itself. Formats include:
    • Top-10 lists, for example top 10 big wave spots. [L1511.025]
    • Compilations, for example worst wipeouts. [L1511.025]
    • Reviews of related items, for example the best movies in the niche. [L1511.025]
  • These formats attract viewers who share the interest, and they can later become customers or website visitors. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.025]

Keyword Research Procedures

Auto-suggest keyword method

  1. Type a core product term into the YouTube search bar. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.004]
  2. Read the suggested completions, which reflect commonly searched terms. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.004]
  3. Add variations by product type, beginner or intermediate level, use case, feature, and brand names. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.004]
  4. Check the results with the Report search predictions option only if you want to remove irrelevant suggestions. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.004]
  5. Record the chosen terms in a keyword research tracking spreadsheet, grouped by product or brand. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.004]

Keyword-to-video idea method for a product range

  1. List the products, categories and brands you sell. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.021]
  2. For each, search YouTube auto-suggest and note terms with volume signals. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.021]
  3. Add the terms to a keyword research spreadsheet. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.021]
  4. Turn each term into a video idea, for example beginner guides, reviews of a brand, comparisons, top-10 lists, or entertaining compilations for the niche. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.021]

Video Titles

  • The primary keyword must appear in the video title, as close to the start as possible, the same way it would on a website page. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.001]
  • Titles should be plain and descriptive, signalling what the viewer will learn, not hype. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.001]

Video Descriptions

  • The primary keyword should appear in the description no more than three times. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.005, L1511.002]
  • The description should stay under 300 words. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.005, L1511.002]
  • The description should explain why the viewer should watch and what benefit they get, and include a call to action. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.005, L1511.002]
  • Descriptions can contain as many links as needed, such as product pages and social profiles. Links in the description are not a ranking factor, but they let viewers click through to anything mentioned in the video. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.006]
  • When a YouTube video sends viewers away from the store, the description should link back to the store. A call to action with a link card should be placed at the end of the video so traffic still reaches the website. (older, 2021-22) [L1424.004]

Tags

  • The tag field accepts up to 10 relevant search terms. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.007]
  • Tags can include a range of similar terms, not only the main target keyword. The same keyword may be used across several videos. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.007]
  • Tags may repeat keywords used on other videos. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.002]

Captions, Closed Captions and On-Screen Text

  • Add on-screen text to the caption and closed captions of every video so the platform can tell what the video is about. Videos without this text are less likely to be distributed to the right viewers. [L1457.001]

Video Production and Retention

  • Lighting must be good enough that viewers can see the screen. Dark videos hurt engagement. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.011]
  • Production does not need to be polished. Keep the video moving by cutting irrelevant material and keeping audio and visuals active. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.011]
  • YouTube ranks videos partly on engagement and watch time, so viewers must stay on the video. Watching on YouTube rather than leaving for another platform is what the platform rewards. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.009]
  • Hold engagement with a clear intro and a fast-paced video. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.002]
  • Passive viewing without likes, comments, saves or shares makes a video far less likely to be pushed beyond the existing followers. Silent viewers are therefore a distribution problem, not only a missed interaction. [L1457.003]

Channel Setup

Channel setup on YouTube

  1. Use a Google account to log in and open your channel from the menu. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.018]
  2. Add a cover image, which can be a product photo, text, logo, or brand name, linking back to the website and social accounts. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.018]
  3. Fill in the About section with description, business enquiry email, location, and links to the website and social profiles. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.018]
  4. Upload videos regularly. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.018]
  5. Create a static welcome video rather than a changing one. A welcome video only shows to non-subscribers, and its purpose is to get them to subscribe. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.018]
  • Subscribers are notified each time a new video is uploaded, so the channel's main goal for non-subscribers is to get them to subscribe. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.019]

Embedding Videos on Store Pages

  • Embedding a YouTube video on a high-traffic store site can help the video rank on YouTube for the store domain name, for example when someone searches the domain on YouTube. The video can also appear on the first page of Google organic results. (older, 2019) [L0467.018]
  • Google favors pages that embed relevant videos. A small drop in the Shopify speed score is worth the SEO gain from a YouTube embed. (older, 2022) [L0854.065]

Embedding a product video without slowing the store (older, 2022)

  1. Create a YouTube channel for the store. [L0854.064]
  2. Upload the product videos to this channel. [L0854.064]
  3. Click Embed on the YouTube video and copy the embed code. [L0854.064]
  4. Paste the embed code onto the relevant product page. [L0854.064]
  • Do not host the video in Shopify Files, because Google favors YouTube embeds for SEO and the store page score drop is acceptable. (older, 2022) [L0854.064]
  • A video embedded on a product page shows the message not indexed due to not being on a dedicated watch page in Search Console. This is not an error. Google only indexes a video as its own result when the page is mostly about that video, so no fix is needed for supplemental videos. [L1287.033]

Video SEO Checklist

YouTube SEO procedure (older, 2021-22)

  1. Keyword research for each video, identifying at least one target search term. [L1511.002]
  2. Put that keyword in the title, near the start. [L1511.002]
  3. Write a plain, descriptive title that tells the viewer what they will learn. [L1511.002]
  4. Write a description that explains the benefit, uses the primary keyword no more than three times, stays under 300 words, and includes a call to action. [L1511.002]
  5. Add up to 10 relevant search terms in the tag field, which may repeat keywords used on other videos. [L1511.002]
  6. Hold engagement with a clear intro and a fast-paced video. [L1511.002]
  7. Use clickable thumbnails. [L1511.002]
  8. Reply to comments. [L1511.002]
  9. Promote each video soon after upload. [L1511.002]

Promotion

  • Promote each new video early after upload, for example via email list, social media profiles, and embedding it on the website where relevant. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.015]
  • An embedded YouTube video on a site counts as a view on YouTube, and an early boost of engagement helps the algorithm. (older, 2021-22) [L1511.015]
  • Plan the promotion of each video as part of the content plan before publishing, not after. Decide in advance which email list, social profiles, or relevant website pages will receive the video soon after upload, so it gets an early engagement boost. [L1511.027]
