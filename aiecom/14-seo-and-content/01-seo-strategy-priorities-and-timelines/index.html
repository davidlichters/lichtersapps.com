14.1 SEO strategy, priorities and timelines

What SEO and content do for a store

  • SEO means making a store's pages appear in the unpaid, organic results of search engines, as opposed to paid search ads for which the advertiser pays per placement. [L0594.001]
  • SEO targets Google and Bing. The lesson names Bing as the second major target after Google. [L0594.006]
  • Organic Google search means people find the store through unpaid text results, usually on page one. It costs nothing per click, but it depends on the store ranking. [L0586.005]
  • Organic search traffic comes when people search for a brand name, a product name or a SKU number and click through an unpaid result. It costs nothing per click but is not directly paid for. [L0676.006]
  • Organic traffic is any traffic that does not come from paid ads, including search results, organic social posts and free shopping listings. [L0855.151]
  • SEO produces free organic traffic from Google and Bing search results and is the free counterpart to paid search ads. [L0597.009]
  • SEO is one of the core traffic sources because it brings in visitors without paying for each click. It is treated as free traffic, although it requires work to build. [L0759.004]
  • Content marketing sits between publishing and advertising. You build an audience-owning platform and advertise your own products on it, instead of letting third parties monetize that audience. Owning the channel gives you control over how customers are influenced and serviced. [L0382.001]
  • Blog posts keep generating traffic for years after a one-time production cost, while pay-per-click charges for every incremental visit. Content therefore has a long-lasting return on investment. [L0382.002]
  • Ranking for keywords that matter to your business works like owning the best retail locations in your industry online. If you hold those search results, competitors are pushed out of them. [L0382.003]
  • Organic search traffic is one of the funnel's traffic sources, brings in visitors without paid ads, adds to Google Shopping traffic and works alongside email and retargeting. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0458.049]
  • A single piece of content that ranks on the first page of Google can deliver steady monthly organic traffic for years, potentially tens of thousands of visits over time. Judge content cost by this long-term return rather than the short-term price. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0379.019]
  • Content marketing is a medium- to long-term traffic strategy. Early on it brings only small amounts of traffic through promotion, and it can lift conversion on the site when the content solves a buyer's problem. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0383.001]
  • Content and SEO build awareness at the top of the funnel, so a shopper who later searches a high-value term already knows the brand. This raises the conversion chance on those high-value searches. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0740.017]
  • Once SEO is fully built, drive large volumes of top-of-funnel traffic from people with no loyalty to a brand who want the best product, and guide them to your product. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0768.015]
  • Low-value generic searches should be targeted through SEO and content marketing rather than by bidding on Google Shopping, because these channels reach that audience more profitably than Shopping ads do. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0740.016]
  • Organic search traffic from keywords that problem-aware people type can be won at a much lower cost than paid ads for the same keywords. This makes it affordable to target the problem-aware stage at scale. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1392.010]

How the search results page is laid out

  • On a Google results page for a niche search, the top product listings are Google Shopping product listing ads, the "Sponsored" sections are paid search text ads, and further down come product feed results and organic listings. Only the organic listings are the target of SEO. [L0594.004]

Ranking factors

  • Website search rankings depend on three primary factors: quality backlinks from other domains, quality content on the site, and onsite optimizations. [L0173.001]
  • Google's own description names three ranking factors above the rest: quality content, backlinks from other websites, and optimizing the site for the RankBrain algorithm. Other SEO activities can help a bit more, but without these three a site will struggle to rank. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0414.032, L1500.003]
  • The two sources phrase the third factor differently. Source A calls it onsite optimization; Source B, citing Google, calls it optimization for RankBrain. Both describe the same area of work. [L0173.001, L0414.032]
  • Neglecting any one of the three factors limits search rankings, however well the other work is done. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1500.003]
  • SEO work falls into three main categories: on-page SEO, technical SEO, and off-page SEO, which is mainly link building from other websites to your site. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1500.001]
  • On-page and technical SEO alone rarely produce strong organic search traffic. They multiply the results of content marketing and link building, so all three should run together. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1500.002]
  • Backlinks are a core ranking factor, and deep content earns more links. Building deep content is therefore a prerequisite for reaching the top of Google's organic results. Link building is a separate task that must also be done to generate significant organic traffic. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1461.007]
  • Backlinks matter most for very competitive keywords in crowded industries, not for niche e-commerce stores. (Older, 2022.) [L0429.067]
  • Read together, the backlink statements apply as follows: the core-factor view (Source B) applies to competitive keywords, and the 2022 crowded-industry view applies to niche stores. [L1461.007, L0429.067]
  • Ranking signals include user behavior: bounce rate, time on page and return visits. A good store experience can improve Google Shopping and organic performance. (Older, 2022.) [L0969.083]
  • Google evaluates aggregated, anonymized user interaction data to judge whether a result is relevant to a query. Visitors who click an organic result and leave within seconds signal a poor match, so keeping visitors on the page and having them interact further is a ranking lever. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1099.001]
  • Google states that it uses aggregated, anonymized interaction data to judge relevance, but it does not publish how much bounce rate or time on page weighs in ranking. Treat both as indicators of relevance and page quality, not as confirmed direct ranking factors. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1099.020]
  • Organic traffic depends on many combined factors, so a quick list of tips is not enough. The full SEO material should be studied in depth. (Older, 2022.) [L1092.101]
  • Established stores rank better in organic search mainly because they have existed longer, built more links and done SEO work for longer. A newer store can overtake them by applying the same SEO practices consistently over time. (Older, 2022.) [L0963.153]
  • Domain authority alone should not decide whether to enter a niche. Checking competitor scores only shows which keyword competition level is realistic for your store. [L1227.030]
  • Organic clicks concentrate on the first few Google results, and deeper result pages are rarely visited. Product pages should therefore aim for the top organic positions, not just to be indexed. [L1227.029]
  • Clicks concentrate at the top: about three quarters of traffic goes to the top three organic results, and the number one spot takes roughly two thirds of that. Reaching position one matters more than reaching position three. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1461.024]

On-page SEO

  • On-page SEO means optimizing each individual web page so that search engines can understand what the page is about, which context it provides, and which searcher it serves. The goal is to make the page match the search terms it should rank for. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1118.001]
  • On-page SEO works through many small adjustments to a page's content and structure that tell search engines which search terms the page should match. It is the first SEO work most sites do after technical SEO is in place, and it should be reviewed even if some of it was already done. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1118.002]
  • Product pages need SEO work for the search engine listing and the product description. Collection pages, standard pages and blogs also need SEO work, using the newest SEO guides. (2024.) [L0806.076]
  • Apply the SEO optimizations from day one, as part of setting up product pages and the store. They are not a later project. (Older, 2018.) [L1109.061]
  • For a new store, plan page titles, URLs and categories before launch, because it is much harder to add SEO structure to a finished site later. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0949.057]
  • Do not delay paid ads on a new Shopify store because most URLs show as not indexed. Only the key pages need to be indexed, and indexing duplicate URL variants would hurt search results rather than help them. [L0373.003]
  • Outsourcing alt text and meta descriptions to freelancers on Upwork is acceptable, because the writing does not require expert skill. AI tools can speed up the work where you can use them. (2024.) [L0965.081]

Target the products you sell

  • SEO should target the products, brands and SKU numbers you sell, because product pages are what bring organic buyers. Ranking for a broad niche keyword does not produce the same sales. [L0941.076]
  • A narrow customer focus simplifies content and SEO planning, because one buyer's interests answer the question of what to publish first. Broad stores with many product types and buyer types often stall because they cannot decide where to start. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0905.014]
  • For a nationwide high-ticket dropshipping store, geo-targeted local SEO is usually not worth it, because customers can be anywhere in the country. Local SEO normally covers a location within one state; once the area spans several states it is no longer local SEO. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0879.007]
  • Low-margin brand products can still rank organically if the site is set up correctly, which can produce some sales without paid traffic. (Older, 2019.) [L1068.074]
  • A new store without traffic data can find its likely best sellers by checking the top product placements in the category pages of established competitor stores. Optimize those product pages first, because top placement usually reflects strong sellers. [L1190.018]
  • A store can earn its first sale without any paid ads by ranking organically in Google for long-tail searches such as brand name, product name and SKU number. These rankings can be captured before Google Shopping product listings are set up, and the sale came at zero ad cost. [L1223.006]

  • Before buying traffic, use free channels such as SEO on product pages and free Merchant Center listings to bring visitors in. Paid ads can be funded from the profit those visitors generate. (2024.) [L0360.058]
  • Start organic SEO early, even while doing other work, because traffic takes a long time to build. (2025.) [L0857.047]
  • Free Google product listings can drive traffic without ad spend, so list products there early. Relaunching a stalled store also benefits from these free listings. (Older, 2021.) [L0853.077]
  • Do not prioritize getting content written immediately. Start with niche validation and supplier approval, and build content gradually as the store proves profitable. (Older, 2022.) [L0249.066]

  • SEO and content marketing are an advanced, later-stage layer. Reaching initial sales does not depend on them. [L0544.010]
  • Improve store pages and collection pages for SEO as the store grows, since organic traffic builds slowly. Free Google product listings and paid Google Shopping ads give data much faster than SEO. (Older, 2021.) [L0968.067]

What to prioritize first

  • A new store should prioritize product pages over blog content. Blogs and content pages should come only after product pages are optimized and the site is being crawled through Search Console. (Older, 2022.) [L0429.067]
  • For a store that has not done SEO yet, improve the collection pages and product pages first, since those are the pages you want people to land on. Build blog content around them afterward. (Older, 2021.) [L0801.085]
  • Start SEO work with the top 20% of products, which bring in about 80% of website visits. Find those products in Shopify analytics or Google Analytics and optimize them first. (2024.) [L0965.079]
  • Sources agree on the order of the work: product pages come first and blog posts come after. The first step differs by source. [L0594.005, L0245.045, L0962.154]

Sequence A, a Shopify store SEO order (undated):

  1. Install the recommended SEO app.
  2. Do organic keyword research.
  3. Optimize product pages.
  4. Optimize collection pages.
  5. Optimize standard pages.
  6. Optimize blog posts.
  7. Promote the content to get early traffic.
  8. Finally, optimize the search results once everything is set up. [L0594.005]

Sequence B, a 2020 checklist (older):

  1. Increase site speed (measure with Google Page Speed Test and fix identified errors).
  2. Mobile optimize (automatically handled by Source A theme).
  3. Customize top 20% of products by traffic with more photos, embedded videos, and Q&A.
  4. Create 3-5 anchor blog posts (massive value pieces) with contributions from niche authorities.
  5. Publish guest posts on high-ranking competitor sites (free or paid).
  6. Appear as guest on niche YouTube channels or podcasts, or start your own interviewing suppliers and customers. [L0245.045]

Sequence C, a 2021 priority order (older):

  1. Optimize product pages so they rank for buying keywords such as product names, brand names and SKU numbers.
  2. Create buyer's guides directly in Shopify.
  3. Add blog posts later, since they target lower-converting search terms and make up a smaller share of traffic. [L0962.154]

  • Organic traffic builds over time as Google recognises a site as a quality destination. Alexa rank improves as organic and direct traffic grow. (Older, 2020.) [L1111.118]

  • For high-ticket dropshipping, SEO usually becomes the biggest long-term traffic source, but it has to be started early and needs a medium to long time horizon before results arrive. It should run alongside paid traffic from the start. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0713.003]

Content strategy and cadence

  • Build content over time to become the authority in your niche: buying guides, tutorials and detailed product pages. This is a long-term strategy and does not need to be done on day one. (2024.) [L0806.075]
  • Start creating blog content only after your paid advertising campaigns are profitable. Prioritize paid traffic first and build organic traffic in the background. (2023.) [L0274.061]
  • Begin with one piece of content per month minimum, ideally weekly. (2023.) [L0274.061]
  • Content pieces work best when they are run alongside ongoing SEO work rather than as a separate effort. Operators who combined both reached a point where organic traffic exceeded their paid traffic. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0383.013]
  • Stopping or slowing content and SEO work lets competitors catch up and take the top market position. Organic traffic efforts must never be paused, even after success. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0383.009]
  • Do keyword research before writing content, since without it you do not know what the content should target. Content also performs better when backlinks point to it. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.030]
  • A realistic SEO cadence is one quality content piece per month, each followed by backlink building in the months after it is published, rather than many pieces in one month. Each piece gets some traffic on its own, but sustaining organic traffic takes a steady stream of pieces. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.031]
  • Build blog content around product and collection pages. Create buyer's guides directly in Shopify before adding blog posts. [L0962.154]

Outsourcing, agencies and ownership

  • Before hiring SEO help from freelancers or agencies, cover the basic on-page SEO lesson first, which takes about two and a half hours. (Older, 2022.) [L0429.033]
  • For these store types, simple on-page techniques are usually enough. Outsource only the specific tasks you still want done. Avoid broad e-commerce SEO services, which often cost a lot for few results. (Older, 2022.) [L0429.033]
  • Keyword research can be outsourced in raw form, but the owner must still decide which search terms to target and what content to produce. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.032]
  • Raw keyword research can be bought from freelancers on Fiverr. The store owner must still decide which search terms to target and what each piece of content should cover, because a freelancer cannot make that decision. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.043]
  • Content writing is commonly outsourced because most people write poorly for this purpose. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.032]
  • Backlink building is worth trying once yourself first. After that, it can be outsourced to an agency or to a virtual assistant trained on the process, which is cheaper. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.032]
  • Be even more cautious when hiring an SEO agency than when hiring a Google Ads agency. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1053.017]
  • Be cautious about SEO agencies that charge large monthly retainers for slow results. (Older, 2021.) [L0962.155]
  • SEO work on an existing store often comes down to a few tweaks to product pages. Hiring an expensive SEO agency rarely returns its cost. (2023.) [L0855.150]
  • Done For You Content Marketing is listed as an outsourced content marketing option in the December 2020 coaching call. The lesson names it without describing its scope or cost. (Older, 2020.) [L0426.014]
  • SEO work can be split into on-page and off-page SEO and assigned to a dedicated SEO person. Content such as blog posts can be written by a separate content writer who reports to the marketing manager. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1334.030]
  • The grow track includes a dedicated module on search engine optimization, content marketing and backlink building, and a separate module on Google Ads. An owner strong in one area but weak in SEO should go to the SEO module first. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0545.018]

From action list to work schedule

  • SEO works only through continuous work; occasional or cram sessions produce little effect. The three biggest SEO levers work together and should run at the same time: keyword research, content production and backlinks. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1456.029]
  • SEO, content marketing and link building each deliver value alone, but they work much better together. Plan them as one connected effort over time instead of choosing one over the others. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1270.003]
  • A full SEO action item list is often too long to work through in one go. Before starting, decide which items come first and which approach you will use for each one, so your time is not wasted. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1270.004]
  • Keep one action item spreadsheet that lists all SEO tasks for the product pages, collection pages and blog pages. Use it to track which tasks are open, in progress or done. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1270.005]
  • A 90-day rock for a six-month SEO priority can be completing six pieces of content, each with promotion. This turns the six-month target into a smaller quarterly milestone that can be measured. (Older, 2021-22.) [L0447.027]

Turning the action list into a work schedule:

  1. Go through every SEO action item and note what each one requires.
  2. Check the site and mark items that are already done so they are not repeated.
  3. Decide which of the remaining items you will implement.
  4. Sort the chosen items into higher-priority and lower-priority groups.
  5. Place the higher-priority items first in your work schedule, then add the lower-priority items after them. [L1270.001]
