14.20 Other details

Product research checks

  • When researching products through Google Shopping, do not judge market viability from the first page of results alone. If cheaper products dominate the first page, filter by price range to check that higher-priced variants exist in sufficient quantity. (older advice, 2020) [L0245.047]

Keyword research and listing copy

  1. On Amazon, research keywords with Amazon's own search suggestions. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0768.013]
  2. Include the chosen keywords in the product title, the description and the bullet points. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0768.013]
  • In 2021, Amazon was described as a pay-for-play system in which organic ranking was not realistic without paid ads. (older advice, 2021-2022) Plan an advertising budget for Amazon from the start. [L0768.014]

Store naming and file metadata

  • Including the niche keyword in the domain is preferred when possible but not required. Creative names that fit the store theme also work well. (2023) [L0934.098]
  • Set custom metadata in PDF documents, including the author, affiliated company and keywords. This metadata supports SEO and can later serve as proof of ownership if the file is copied. [L1357.029]

On-site SEO in Shopify

  • Site SEO is covered in the search engine domination lesson. The main focus is on-site optimization in Shopify and organic traffic tools. (older advice, 2022) [L0969.082]
  • Free SEO training resources are available as a starting point for improving a store's organic search visibility. (older advice, 2020) [L1087.008]

Traffic estimates and SEO tools

  • Ubersuggest (ubersuggest.com) is a cheap SEO research tool that estimates a domain's organic monthly traffic. Ahrefs or Semrush can be used instead if you already pay for them. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0893.011]
  • Traffic source findings are estimates, not facts, because competitors may run channels that are hard to detect. Treat search results and SEO tool numbers as a best guess and record that uncertainty in the comments. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0893.012]

Local search and listings

  • Local SEO tactics, such as citations in local newspapers, are specialized and rarely move rankings for a national store. Some local practices can still help a national business, but they are minor. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0879.008]
  • For Australian searchers, search engines tend to show.com.au domains ahead of.com domains. A local page with quality content and links can outrank overseas listicles that rank for the same term. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L0949.052]
  • Google Business Profile, 2021 view: it is not necessary for a dropshipping store to rank, so it does not need to be a priority at launch. (older advice, 2021) [L0962.157]
  • Google Business Profile, 2025 view: it is optional for this store model, and leaving it off is unlikely to cause harm. The two views agree that it is not a launch priority; the 2025 view is the more recent guidance. [L1116.054]

Feed work and reviews

  • If the store is already optimized for SEO, most of the feed work is already done. Google's organic search and Shopping matching work in similar ways, so the same title, image and description work help both. (older advice, 2021-2022) [L1170.022]
  • Junip can add a dedicated reviews page that gathers all store reviews in one place. The vendor says this page helps the store rank when people search for reviews of the brand. [L0589.009]
