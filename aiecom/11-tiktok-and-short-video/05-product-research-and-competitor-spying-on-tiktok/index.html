11.5 Product research and competitor spying on TikTok

Finding Winning Hooks for Organic Content

  1. Open the TikTok Creator Search or search your niche keyword on TikTok. [L0561.008]
  2. Look at posts with millions of views and note their intros, hooks and how they grab attention. [L0561.008]
  3. Filter results by most likes or views for a keyword, such as lamps, and study what the top posts do. [L0561.008]
  4. Pull relevant niche hashtags and follow trending sounds. [L0561.008]
  5. Copy the structural style, such as transitions, hooks or controversy, not the exact content. [L0561.008]

Creative Center and Video Ideas

  • The TikTok Creative Center is a tool for seeing what ads and creatives other brands run on TikTok. [L0577.047]
  • The Creative Center includes a video ideas section next to the search for top-performing ads in a niche. Use it for filming ideas after you have identified the winning hooks. [L1374.034]

Checking Whether a Competitor Still Sells

Judge a competitor's TikTok product by its latest video, not its most-viewed one. [L0656.031]

  1. Open the seller's most recent video, not the one with the most views. [L0656.031]
  2. Read the comments in order of date and focus on those from the last week. [L0656.031]
  3. Look for buying signals such as "where can I buy this", "I just bought one", "I placed an order" or "I really want one". [L0656.031]
  4. Look for complaints such as "where is my order" or "why hasn't my order shipped", which point to fulfillment problems. [L0656.031]
  5. Treat recent buying signals as evidence that the product is still selling, even if view counts are low. [L0656.031]

Building a Research Feed on TikTok

Use a separate account for product discovery, not your business account. [L0708.032, L1444.024]

Procedure A (hashtag-based feed):

  1. Create a new TikTok account for research only. [L0708.032]
  2. Search and follow pages for hashtags such as tiktokmademebuyit, Amazon finds, exposing dropshippers, Etsy finds and eBay finds. [L0708.032]
  3. Like and comment on the content to show TikTok your interest. [L0708.032]
  4. The For You page fills with similar product content over time, which turns the account into an organic research feed. [L0708.032]

Procedure B (daily engagement feed):

  1. Create a new account that is separate from your business account. [L1444.024]
  2. Like, share, comment on and engage with product videos every day. [L1444.024]
  3. Keep doing this until the For You page of that account is filled with product content. [L1444.024]
  4. Use this account to spot new trends, and keep it separate from your business account. [L1444.024]

Spying on TikTok Ads

  • Sellercount and PPAds are spy tools for TikTok ads. [L0708.033]
  • PPAds has a different library than Sellercount, so using both gives better coverage. [L0708.033]
  • Use these filters in Sellercount or PPAds:
Filter Setting Reason Source
Post duration 2, then 3, then 4 weeks Many advertisers are moving from Facebook to TikTok [L0708.034]
Engagement 50k plus to 100k plus impressions Proof of concept [L0708.034]
  • An ad with 50k to 200k plus impressions suggests the advertiser has been spending roughly 500 to 1,000 dollars, which usually means the product is profitable for them. [L0708.035]
  • Products with no clear profit signal should not be assumed profitable. [L0708.035]

Finding Products That Sell Without an Audience

  • The Low-Follower Sales Videos filter shows videos that sell even though the creator has few followers. [L1479.009]
  • A product that sells this way does not depend on an existing audience, which makes it a stronger test candidate. [L1479.009]

TikTok Shop and Video Reuse

  • For a TikTok Shop video, the Magic AI search looks for Facebook ads that repurpose the same video, sorted by thumbnail similarity. [L0892.031]
  • If no similar ads appear, it is unlikely that anyone has reused the video in paid ads. [L0892.031]
  • The Videos & Ads view lets you download all listed videos with one button. [L0892.032]
  • The lesson uses this to save a whole competitor video set at once. [L0892.032]

Reach Signals

  • TikTok open targeting reaches an audience of roughly 500 million to 800 million, sometimes up to about 1 billion people. [L0895.034]
  • Because of this reach, high ad impression counts for a product are a positive sign for a seller. [L0895.034]

Facebook Ad Library Cross-Checks

When to use both platforms:

  • The Facebook Ad Library is not the only source of product video inspiration. TikTok searches can find better-performing videos for the same product. [L1172.045]
  • Use both to find strong creatives. [L1172.045]

When to skip the Facebook checks:

Check TikTok product lists Replacement rule Source
Page creation date Skip None [L1183.022]
Ad library scaling stages Skip None [L1183.022]
Ads created within the last 14 days with strong likes and plays Use Treat as proof the product works [L1183.022]
  • For TikTok product lists, skip the Facebook checks because TikTok has no equivalent ad library page. [L1183.022]
  • List at least about 10 products to rank. [L1183.022]

Saving Viral Videos

  • Save the link, a screenshot and the view count of every viral video you research as soon as you see it. [L1385.022]
  • Save immediately because the creator can later set videos to private or delete them. [L1385.022]
  • A previously working link that now fails, combined with a changed account name or domain, signals that the account has pivoted. [L1385.022]

Case Studies

Case 1: Two Look-Alike Bottle-Opener Stores

Starting point: A bottle-opener product was promoted by two TikTok accounts with about 41,000 and 19,000 followers. Each was linked to a Shopify store on a different domain, and the two stores looked almost identical. [L0656.032]

Evidence that one operator ran both:

  • Store checks found no Terms of Service, no Privacy Policy and no full Contact Us page. [L0656.032]
  • Both stores used a free default Shopify theme with default colors. [L0656.032]
  • The product cost about 5 USD with shipping from AliExpress. [L0656.032]
  • Both stores listed the same contact email. [L0656.032]
  • Both accounts posted at the same times with the same clips, so the presenter treated them as one operator. [L0656.032]

Timeline and sales estimate:

  • The older account began posting in June. The newer one began on 14 July, and about two weeks later one of its videos reached 1.7 million views. [L0656.032]
  • The operator posted roughly once a day with gaps. [L0656.032]
  • The presenter estimated sales only as a range of about 1,000 to 10,000 USD, since exact revenue cannot be seen from outside. [L0656.032]

Store issues:

  • A leftover footer code line was visible. [L0656.032]
  • The header was changed, but the default theme colors stayed in other sections. [L0656.032]
  • There was no Shop Now button. [L0656.032]

Case 2: Drinks Product With Gaps in Posting

Starting point: A seller of a drinks-related product started posting on 26 April, first with two or three videos a day. [L0656.033]

Posting pattern and results:

  • After 27 April there were no uploads until 11 May. [L0656.033]
  • A video that reused a clip from an earlier post got only about 600 views. [L0656.033]
  • The seller's best video reached 700,000 views, and a second one reached 500,000 views. [L0656.033]
  • The account had only a few tens of thousands of followers at most, yet it kept posting about twice a day. [L0656.033]
  • Comments asking where to buy the product suggested sales were still coming in. [L0656.033]

Store issues:

  • The store had a visible HTML/CSS footer code line. [L0656.033]
  • It had several pop-ups and heavy use of GIFs. [L0656.033]
  • It used dark black and silver colors and lowercase brand lettering. [L0656.033]
  • There was no Shop Now button, so buyers had to add to cart and then open a separate cart page. [L0656.033]
  • The profile image did not show the product, which made the account look like a bot. [L0656.033]

Conclusion: The seller was probably still earning some sales despite low view counts. A new seller could beat those results with better consistency and cleaner content. [L0656.033]
