Product before filming

  • Before building an original book based on a public method, define its structure, page count and a visual hook that films well. This makes the product usable in short video from the start. [L0354.036]

Store setup before contacting suppliers

  • Before contacting suppliers, link the Shopify store to a purchased custom domain (for example a brand name.com) rather than leaving it on the default myshopify.com subdomain. A subdomain looks unprofessional when a supplier asks where you operate from. [L0767.001]

Technical limits on Shopify

  • Older advice (2021-22): On Shopify, the merchant has no access to hosting settings and cannot manage or change content delivery networks, so technical SEO work is limited to what the platform allows. Any optimization that requires server or CDN control is not possible on a hosted Shopify store. [L1362.003]

Testing before scaling

  • Traffic from TikTok is mostly mobile, so the mobile layout and offer placement drive most of the sales. Test the store on a phone before scaling paid TikTok traffic. [L1412.015]
  • Older advice (2020): The call gives no short-video platform guidance. Do not assume short video will work without testing. [L1111.115]

Local reach with TikTok's local feed

  • TikTok's local feed was being rolled out in the UK, France, Germany and Italy in the week of 29 June to 5 July 2026. [L1504.010]
  • The local feed serves content to users in the creator's region. This is useful for brick-and-mortar stores and local events such as conventions. [L1504.010]
