11.18 Benchmarks, diagnosis and scaling TikTok ads

Creative First: The Precondition for Scaling

  • TikTok depends heavily on creative and much less on the scaling strategy. Self-scaling, CBO and ABO work mainly because the creatives are strong, not because of the method itself. [L0298.037]
  • Before scaling, secure high-quality creatives. Either film original content, or replicate competitor content in your own style rather than copying it directly. [L0298.038]
  • Running the same ads that already work for a successful product gives no advantage. A test has to differ from the product's original winning approach in angle, platform or funnel. [L0561.011]
  • Scale by producing more distinct video creatives, not by simply raising budget. Scaling budget too aggressively with too few creatives stops results quickly. Lower competition is also a reason to focus on creatives. [L1376.002]

Diagnosing Add-to-Cart Without Sales

  • When TikTok traffic produces an add-to-cart rate of about 10% to 12% but almost no purchases, the audience is often the problem rather than the page. [L1345.098]
  • Excluding a younger group that browses without buying (for example 18 to 24) can lower add-to-cart volume while raising the purchase conversion rate. [L1345.098]
  • Judge audiences by purchase conversion, not by the number of add-to-carts. [L1345.098]

Scale-Readiness Gate

Start scaling when all of the following hold: [L1375.006]

  1. CPC is below $1. [L1375.006]
  2. CPM is below $10. [L1375.006]
  3. CTR is above 1.5%. [L1375.006]
  4. The store is making sales with a conversion rate above 1.5% to 2%. [L1375.006]

Before scaling, check the claims in the ads against platform policy. Do not wait for the first sales to find out. [L0638.054]

Scaling Methods

CBO scaling procedure

  1. Launch a CBO campaign at a $250-$300 budget. [L0298.039]
  2. Create 4 ad groups (ad sets). [L0298.039]
  3. Place your best-performing creative in each ad group. [L0298.039]
  4. Set up automated rules to manage performance. [L0298.039]
  5. If profitable, scale by self-scaling (for example 250 → 350 → 500 → 700), verifying profitability after each increase. [L0298.039]

Same-day budget increase procedure

  1. On a day when an ad set performs well, raise its budget that same day. [L1469.038]
  2. Check results within about three to four hours. [L1469.038]
  3. If results stay good, keep the increased budget and continue. [L1469.038]

Duplication

  • To scale an ad that works, duplicate the ad set into a CBO campaign with a budget of $51 instead of $50. The speaker's experience was that a $51 budget bids better against competitors. [L1469.036]
  • Open targeting usually performs well on TikTok. When a particular interest is working, duplicate it to reach the right audience. [L1469.039]
  • Duplicate winning creatives as well. [L1469.039]

Cost cap

  • A cost cap works well for scaling TikTok ads once a winning creative and audience have been identified. [L1469.037]

Targeting for new CBOs

  • Scaling can mean launching new CBOs with the same broad targeting. [L1375.006]
  • Alternatively, test interest targeting by filling the ad group audience instead of leaving it blank. [L1375.006]
  • Sources differ on the default: L1469.039 says open targeting usually performs well, while L1375.006 recommends testing interests. Use open targeting as the default, and test interests as a separate step. [L1375.006, L1469.039]

New countries

  • When ads meet the metric targets and the market is narrow, such as only the US, test additional countries like Canada, Australia and New Zealand, or entirely different markets. [L1375.007]

Spend Ceilings

  • Spend on TikTok ads alone reached about $1,000 per day in the source. Going higher became difficult without a different strategy. [L0637.064]
  • Products priced below $60 held up better at higher spend than products priced above $60. [L0637.064]
  • Plan a second channel or a new creative strategy before exceeding about $1,000 per day. [L0637.064]
  • The same level appears in euros in another unit: about 1,000 euros per day, consistently, for winning products in a niche. The source notes that this scale was limited by the niche. [L1440.023]
  • Treat the two figures as the same order of magnitude, since the currency differs between the units. The dollar figure applies to the US-dollar context, and the euro figure applies to the euro context of its source. [L0637.064, L1440.023]

Multi-Account Fallback

  • Running several TikTok ad accounts in parallel lets a seller move spend to a backup account when one is restricted. [L0638.053]
  • This is a fallback for account bans. It is not a sanctioned way around policy violations, so the underlying policy issue still has to be fixed. [L0638.053]
  • On TikTok, a strong product with strong creatives tends to perform even on a new ad account. Weaker products depend heavily on the standing and history of the account. [L0638.055]
  • When testing across accounts, judge the product and creative, since these are the most consistent variables. [L0638.055]

Ad Restrictions and Prohibited Claims

  • TikTok can flag and ban ads or products that make before-and-after claims or weight-loss claims. This often happens within a week or two of launch. [L0638.054]
  • Check the claims against the platform policy before scaling, not after the first sales arrive. [L0638.054]
  • If an account is restricted, the backup-account fallback applies, but the policy issue must still be fixed. [L0638.053]
