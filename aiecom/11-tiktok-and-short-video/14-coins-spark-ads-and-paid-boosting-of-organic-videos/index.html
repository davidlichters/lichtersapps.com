11.14 Coins, spark ads and paid boosting of organic videos

Choosing between organic testing and paid boosting

  • Test the product with organic content first. Create organic TikTok videos and measure product performance over 3 to 7 days. [L0309.021]
  • Boost only a video that is already performing. A video with strong engagement and views can be converted into a Spark Ad that runs from that organic video's URL. [L0309.021]
  • Spark Ads typically outperform ads made from scratch because they use authentic engagement signals. [L0309.021]
  • A two-day test with a small budget indicates whether a product gets sales and is likely to receive organic sales once views come in. [L1376.022]
  • Coins help a new account's video reach the right audience faster, so organic reach can follow. The paid portion lets TikTok learn the audience early, and the organic portion can be much larger. [L0501.014]
  • Coins are most useful once the content format already works and when you run multiple accounts on the same product. [L0501.014]
  • For a product that has already produced proven sales, the approach is to start a new TikTok account for it, spend on coins only during the first few days so TikTok finds the right audience faster, and avoid overspending by limiting boosts to that window. [L1376.027]

Procedure: accelerate organic reach for a product that already sells [L1376.027]

  1. Choose a product that has already produced proven sales. [L1376.027]
  2. Start a new TikTok account for it. [L1376.027]
  3. Spend on coins (paid boosts) during only the first few days so TikTok finds the right audience faster. [L1376.027]
  4. Use the new account advantage during that window. [L1376.027]
  5. Do not overspend on coins; limit the boosts to the first few days. [L1376.027]

Example (one case, not a guaranteed result): A product that had already passed six-figure sales was pushed on a new account with paid coins. The new account's video went viral with millions of views within three days. This suggests the early paid boost can compound with the new account advantage. [L1376.028]

Timing: when to boost

  • Spark Ads on a viral organic video are usually run after the viral effect has passed, once the video has slowed down. The video then carries social proof. [L1376.023]
  • Organic reach on TikTok is inconsistent. Videos can reach very high views in the first hour and then stop. Whether running ads stops organic growth is unproven, so test it yourself. [L1376.024]
  • Some videos go viral repeatedly. One reported video gained another 5 million views every few months. Such videos are good Spark Ad candidates after their viral phase has passed. [L1376.031]
  • If a viral video's views start declining, wait a day or two, then add coins to it. [L1429.011]
  • Use coins or Spark Ads on a video that is growing but not yet getting sales only after the audience has been checked. [L1429.011]
  • A Reach campaign can kick-start a new account by putting a test video in front of many people in the target audience. If those viewers watch to the end and engage, average watch time rises and the platform distributes the video to larger groups. Use this to accelerate organic growth during testing. [L1383.005]

Procedure: Spark Ads trigger for a test video [L1186.026]

  1. Pick the best organic video from the first days. [L1186.026]
  2. If it reaches roughly 1,000 views on day two or three, set up a Spark Ads campaign for it. [L1186.026]

Buying coins

Where and how to buy

  • Buy coins on desktop, not on the phone. Desktop is cheaper and gives more coins per dollar. Promote the video afterward from the phone app. [L0501.011]
  • Coins bought on the phone cost about 31% more for the same amount. Coins bought on the TikTok desktop version cost about 31% less than on mobile. [L0706.048, L1429.007]
  • Price points cited:
    • 1,000 coins cost about 10 USD and fund about 2 days of promotion. A thousand coins is a good starting amount. [L0501.011]
    • On desktop, $14.80 buys 1,400 coins, described as roughly three days of testing. [L0706.049]
  • TikTok sometimes sends notifications with coin promo deals of around 20% off. Check these before buying at full price. [L0706.051]
  • If you use a VPN, buying coins on desktop may cause problems. Try the purchase in the phone browser instead, and avoid the in-app purchase, which costs about 31% more. [L0706.068]

Procedure: buy coins on desktop [L0706.050]

  1. Log in to TikTok on desktop using the QR code login. [L0706.050]
  2. Open the coin purchase page and select the 1,400-coin package for $14.80. [L0706.050]
  3. Complete the purchase, and the coins are added to your account automatically. [L0706.050]

Login tip: Log into your TikTok account on desktop, using the QR code login for faster sign-in if needed. [L1429.008]

Return to the app: Buy the coins on desktop, then return to the phone app, where the coins boost prompt appears automatically. [L1429.008]

Coins: setup, objective and audience

Procedure: coins setup, in this order [L1429.009]

  1. Buy coins on the desktop version of TikTok, which is about 31% cheaper than mobile. [L1429.009]
  2. Go back to the phone app; the coins prompt appears automatically. [L1429.009]
  3. Choose the goal: More video views, More website visits, or More followers. [L1429.009]
  4. Choose the audience: TikTok's automatic targeting, or Custom. [L1429.009]
  5. For Custom, select interests, the age range, and gender (male, female, or both). [L1429.009]
  6. Choose the button call-to-action, such as Shop now, and enter the website URL. [L1429.009]
  7. Set the daily amount and number of days, for example $10 per day for 4 days = $40 total, and check the estimated click range shown before confirming. [L1429.009]

Objective

  • Choose the coins promotion objective before setting the budget. Website visits send paid traffic to your store. Video views only increase reach. [L0501.040]
  • Use website visits once the store is ready to take visitors. [L0501.040]

Age targeting

  • When targeting coins by age, exclude 13 to 17 year olds because they rarely buy. Typical adult ranges are 18 to 45 or 18 to 54, combined with a gender choice. [L1429.010]
  • You can target audiences aged 18 and up when boosting with coins. [L0706.047]

Review and link rules

  • TikTok reviews coin-boosted videos before promotion. Campaigns aimed at views face fewer restrictions than campaigns that add a website link, so keep links out of boosts when possible. [L0706.046]
  • Views-based objectives are more lenient than traffic objectives that include a link. [L0706.047]

Audience finding through comments

Procedure [L1429.013]

  1. Read the comments from people who say they want to buy the product. [L1429.013]
  2. Open those commenters' profiles and look at their photos and videos to identify who they are, for example a mom account. [L1429.013]
  3. Find other accounts that look similar to define the group you want to reach. [L1429.013]
  4. Set the age range and audience of your coins or Spark Ads to match that group. [L1429.013]

Coins: budget, duration and music

  • Coins promotion budget can be as low as 5 USD per day per video. [L0501.013]
  • The typical plan is 10 USD per day on one video for 3 days, for example 30 USD of coins spread over three days. [L0501.013]
  • Running coins longer than 3 days is not recommended. [L0501.013]
  • The setup walkthrough shows a 4-day example ($10 per day for 4 days = $40 total). [L1429.009] Use the 3-day plan as the recommended limit. [L0501.013] The 4-day example applies only as an illustration of the setup screens. [L1429.009]
  • A coins boost runs over a 24-hour window from its start time, spreading the budget across that period. [L1429.046]
  • Coins only work on videos with original or licensed music, not on copyrighted trending sounds. [L0501.013]

Coins: what they deliver

  • Paid TikTok views cost roughly 2,000 USD per 1 million views at the time discussed. At that rate, 8 million views would cost at least 16,000 USD in ads. This makes organic reach valuable for products with limited budgets. [L0764.023]
  • In the cited test, a $100 coins budget generated about 100,000 views, roughly a fifth of the Spark Ads result at the same spend. [L1429.005]
  • Coins deliver views more slowly over a roughly 24-hour window and showed no visible harm to organic reach. [L1429.006]

  • Choose Website conversions when the ad should generate sales. Choose Reach when the goal is views or an organic push. [L1383.006]
  • Do not mix the two goals within the same campaign. [L1383.006]
  • The Reach objective has no purchase option or shop button, so it cannot drive sales. Use it only to push views and visibility. [L1383.004]

Spark Ads: how they work

  • Spark Ads run an existing organic video directly from the connected TikTok account, so no new video needs to be produced. [L1376.006]
  • Spark Ads boost an existing organic post so it runs as an ad from your own account. Viewers see your profile and earlier videos when they tap through, and a link on the ad lets them buy directly. [L1429.002]
  • Spark Ads run under the account's own profile, so viewers can see the followers and buy directly from the ad with a shop-now button. This avoids sending viewers to the profile to find the link. [L1381.026]
  • Spark Ads can run without a TikTok pixel or Shopify store link, because the ad uses the authorization code of the organic video. A pixel can be added later if conversion tracking is needed. One seller skipped it for low-budget Spark Ad tests. [L1381.063]
  • Spark Ads deliver views fast and can slightly reduce organic reach at times. [L1429.006]

Identifying a Spark Ad

  • To tell a Spark Ad (a boosted organic post) from a copied ad, open the account behind the ad. A Spark Ad links to the real account that posted the video. A copied ad runs under a different name with no genuine TikTok presence. [L0530.038]

Spark Ads: authorization codes and authorized posts

Two routes appear in the sources. Linking the account in Business Settings makes it selectable as an identity in ad groups. [L1377.070] The authorized-post route runs a Spark Ad without linking the account. [L1383.016] One source warns never to click the option to link your account for Spark Ads. [L1383.014] Choose the authorized-post route when you want to keep the organic account unlinked. [L1383.014, L1383.016]

Connecting an account (linking route)

  • To use an organic TikTok account for Spark Ads, connect it in the business settings of TikTok Ads Manager. Once connected, it appears in the identity list when you set up an ad group. You can choose it instead of a brand name and logo. [L1377.070]

Generating a Spark Ad code [L1381.027]

  1. In the TikTok app, open the Profile and go to Creator tools. [L1381.027]
  2. Scroll down to Settings, add settings and enable the ad settings. [L1381.027]
  3. Open a video that is not copyrighted, tap the three dots. [L1381.027]
  4. Slide the bottom menu (Analytics, Save video, Pin, Promote) to the left. [L1381.027]
  5. Tap Add settings, then Add authorization. [L1381.027]
  6. Generate the code. [L1381.027]
  7. In TikTok Ads Manager choose the option to deliver content through Spark Ads, paste the code, and the account is recognized without logging in. [L1381.027]

Authorized TikTok Post method, without linking the account [L1383.016]

  1. In the TikTok app, open Creator tools and turn on Ad settings. [L1383.016]
  2. Open the video you want to run and confirm the music is not copyrighted, otherwise it cannot run. [L1383.016]
  3. Tap the three dots on the video, choose Ad settings, then choose Generate code. [L1383.016]
  4. Send the code to yourself and copy it. [L1383.016]
  5. In TikTok Ads Manager, open the Authorized TikTok Post option and paste the code into the field, then search. [L1383.016]
  6. Your account should appear. Select the option Use authorized account, not the option to link your account. [L1383.016]
  7. Under Add creative, choose TikTok post and select the video that appears. [L1383.016]
  8. Choose whether to duplicate the post, and edit how it appears on the For You page. [L1383.016]
  9. Enter the product page URL so viewers who click go directly to the product. [L1383.016]
  10. Choose the call to action. Standard is Shop now; Dynamic lets TikTok choose. [L1383.016]
  11. Scroll down and click Submit. [L1383.016]

Upload without the Spark option

  • If you upload a video without selecting the option to use a TikTok account for Spark Ads, the ad runs as a regular TikTok ad. In that case your organic account does not appear on the ad. [L1383.017]

Spark Ads: volume and budget

Procedure: scale one winning video into many ad groups [L1186.026]

  1. Pick the best organic video from the first days. [L1186.026]
  2. If it reaches roughly 1,000 views on day two or three, set up a Spark Ads campaign for it. [L1186.026]
  3. Create one ad group containing only that video with a $25 budget. [L1186.026]
  4. Set the payment method to manual and load $25 in advance so the ad spends within minutes. [L1186.026]
  5. Duplicate the ad group to create 20 to 30 copies, each with a $25 budget. [L1186.026]
  6. Do not add more money to the same video. [L1186.026]
  • Pouring repeated Spark Ads money into one video tends to suppress its later organic reach, because organic distribution usually picks up after two to three days. Spend on a video once and let it run rather than repeatedly topping it up. [L1186.027]
  • For Spark Ads volume, set up 20 ad groups with one creative in each. With a $100 spend, the cited test generated about 500,000 views, because the approach pushes the post into distribution. [L1429.004]
  • Spark Ads were used by one operator on a product with about 50 million views per week. A 50 USD test on Spark Ads was run and then stopped after a few days. [L0819.017]

Spark Ads and organic reach

Sellers disagree on whether Spark Ads reduce organic reach. One advertiser reported that they do. Another saw an organic boost after a small spend ended. [L1381.062] Opinions were mixed and based on small samples. [L0819.017]

  • Test Spark Ads on a separate ads account and watch the organic views of the main account before scaling spend. [L1381.062]
  • If you run both paid Spark Ads and organic content, keep them on two separate TikTok accounts. One account handles the paid ads and the other stays purely organic. [L1383.007]
  • Never click the option to link your TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads. Once linked, the organic account should be considered lost, because the platform tends to limit organic views on accounts used for paid promotion. [L1383.014]
  • A seller's other videos can show whether a viral result is repeatable. If they show no sign of the paid-boost pattern, reach likely came from organic posting alone. [L0657.044]

Judging viral results and sales

  • Spark Ads are a candidate explanation when sales arrive in short bursts despite huge view counts. Judging them unlikely just because an account has many followers is weak evidence. Compare the timing of sales with the timing of the views instead. [L1385.018]
