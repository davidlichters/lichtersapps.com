11.15 Campaign setup: objectives, targeting and placements

Setup order for a website conversion test campaign

  1. Open the TikTok ad account, go to Campaigns and click Create. [L1378.001]
  2. Choose Custom mode instead of Simplified mode. [L1378.001, L1378.002]
  3. Under Conversions, select Website conversions as the advertising objective and click Continue. [L1378.001, L1378.003]
  4. Name the ad group after the interest being tested, or "broad" if no interests are used, then continue. [L1378.001, L1378.015]
  5. Select the website pixel you created, or create a new one. [L1378.001]
  6. Set the optimization event to Complete Payment. [L1378.001]
  7. Under Placements, remove Global App Bundle and Pangle so only TikTok remains. [L1378.001, L1378.007]
  8. Under Demographics and location, choose the target countries. [L1378.001, L1378.008, L1378.009]
  9. Set gender and age (all ages from 18+), leave spending power and audience include/exclude unchanged, and turn on targeting expansion. [L1378.001, L1378.011, L1378.012, L1378.013]
  10. Under Budget and schedule, go back to the campaign level, turn on Campaign budget optimization and set the daily campaign budget (example: 50 dollars). [L1378.001]
  11. Click Continue, set the start date to the next day at 6 a.m. or midnight, and click Next. [L1378.001, L1378.018]
  12. Name the ad, leave Spark Ads off, enter the store name in the ad sign and upload the logo. [L1378.001, L1378.019]
  13. Upload the video, add the ad text with a simple call to action, and click Continue. [L1378.001]
  14. Add the product page link and click Submit to publish. [L1378.001, L1378.022]
  15. After publishing, click the duplicate button on the ad group four times to get five ad groups in total. [L1378.001, L1377.012]
  16. Let the campaign run for two days. [L1378.001]

Entry points and objective

  • Two entry paths lead to the same settings. Path A: click Create an ad, choose Website conversions, then select Custom mode instead of Simplified so the full set of options appears. Path B: go to Campaigns and click Create, then select Custom mode. [L1383.001, L1378.002]
  • Select Website conversions again inside Custom mode. [L1383.001]
  • In the ad group setup, after choosing the Website conversions objective and naming the ad group, the optimization location is set to the website. The store's pixel is then selected in the same section before the optimization event is chosen. [L1378.026]

Placements

  • Remove all placements except TikTok, so the ad runs only on TikTok. Remove Global App Bundle and Pangle. [L1378.001, L1378.007, L1383.010]

Countries and language

  • The countries available for targeting depend on the country where the TikTok ad account is registered, so only options for that registered market appear. [L1378.008]
  • German-speaking markets: target Switzerland, Germany and Austria. [L1378.009]
  • English-speaking markets: target the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and the UK. [L1378.009]
  • Keep the language setting broad, because users may have their phones set to a different language than the one they speak. [L1378.010]

Age and gender

  • Age for broad testing: select all age ranges from 18+. [L1378.012]
  • Age for open targeting, default: use 18+. [L1377.009]
  • Age for open targeting, larger audience: if the 25+ audience exceeds about 10 million, use 25+, because that group buys more. [L1377.009]
  • Age for open targeting, 18 to 24 group: it often gives strong engagement and clicks but weaker purchase conversion. [L1377.009]
  • The two age rules differ in when they apply. The broad-testing setup in the handbook uses all ages from 18+. The open-targeting rule uses 18+ as the default and switches to 25+ only when the 25+ audience is above 10 million. [L1378.012, L1377.009]
  • Gender: choose based on the product. If most buyers are female, select female, and let targeting expansion reach other genders where TikTok sees potential. [L1378.011]
  • Age and gender are the only filters kept on broad setups. Filter both by product. [L1376.030]

Targeting strategy

  • Broad targeting works better on TikTok than specific interest targeting because the algorithm is newer and still optimizing. Let the creative decide which customers it reaches rather than pre-selecting interests. [L0296.008]
  • TikTok has always used very broad targeting, so the creative and the message must carry the audience fit rather than the targeting settings. [L0999.055]
  • Testing TikTok can reach a younger audience than Facebook. [L0999.055]
  • Interest targeting used to perform well on TikTok but now works less well. The recommended setup is completely broad targeting with no interest filters. [L1376.030]
  • A broad audience works well only for products with very wide mass-market appeal. Otherwise use targeted interests. [L1440.031]
  • Start broad with no interests in the first test, rather than narrowing to specific interests from the beginning. [L1378.014]
  • Open targeting is usually the cheapest audience on TikTok, because the platform can show ads to anyone. Stacked interests usually have higher CPMs because they are narrower. Treat stacked interests as a second layer and use them to find a different pocket of buyers. [L1377.010]
  • Purchase intention: turn on the TikTok "Purchase intention" option within interest targeting and select broad interests under it. This reaches users who have shown purchase behavior and is meant to attract higher-intent buyers than plain interest targeting. [L1377.008]
  • Automated targeting can work for broad, relatable products that appeal to many people, such as simple life-hack or fitness products. Test it as an alternative, but keep it secondary to the open and interest structure for most products. [L1377.011]
  • Household income and interest targeting were left at default in the setup shown in the source. These can be tested but were not used as a standard setting. [L1383.012]
  • Spending power and audience include/exclude stay at their defaults for broad testing. [L1378.013]

Audience size and expansion

Situation Rule Ids
Interest or open audience, target size At least about 8 to 9 million people L1377.006
Audience under about 4 to 5 million Usually shows clearly high CPMs L1377.006
Interest audience below 8 million Turn on targeting expansion to widen toward about 10 million L1377.007
Open or broad, no interests or custom audiences selected Targeting expansion applies L1378.013
US broad open audience (18+, all genders, no interests) Estimated 150 to 200 million reachable people L1436.091
  • Smaller audiences tend to produce expensive CPMs and are hard to scale. [L1377.006]
  • Expansion lets TikTok reach beyond the chosen interests when the audience is small. [L1377.007]
  • Small budgets reach only a small pocket of the broad US audience. This is why separate campaigns are used to find the right pocket. [L1436.091]

Test campaign structure

  • Use 5 to 6 ad groups: two open-targeting ad groups (18+ with no interests), two stacked-interest ad groups, and one hashtag ad group. [L1377.012]
  • Split related interests across two stacked-interest ad groups with roughly five interests in each. Keep hashtags in a separate third ad group. [L1377.065]
  • Before launching, check that each combined audience reaches at least 8 to 10 million people. [L1377.065]
  • Duplicate a finished ad group and change only the audience, so creatives stay identical across groups. [L1377.012]
  • The setup order produces five ad groups in total by duplicating the first one four times. [L1378.001]

Hashtag audiences

  • Some TikTok hashtags do not appear as targeting options in Ads Manager, even when they have hundreds of millions of views. [L1374.037]
  • Hashtags tied to a brand or a specific product are likely to be excluded. A hashtag may need to exist for some time before it becomes available. [L1374.037]
  • The sources differ in emphasis: the hashtag procedure asks for hashtags that describe the product and its niche, while the availability rule warns that brand- or product-specific hashtags are likely to be missing. Use niche-level hashtags and check each one for availability. [L1377.066, L1374.037]

Procedure:

  1. Choose hashtags that describe the product and its niche. [L1377.066]
  2. Search each hashtag on TikTok without ads and note the ones that appear often in recent videos. [L1377.066]
  3. Keep the frequently used hashtags and add them to the hashtag ad group. [L1377.066]
  4. Check the audience size and turn on targeting expansion if it is below 8 million. [L1377.066, L1377.007]

Budget and schedule

  • Set the budget with Campaign budget optimization turned on, at campaign level. Example daily campaign budget: 50 dollars. [L1378.001]
  • Start date: set it to the next day at 6 a.m. or at midnight, then click Next. [L1378.001, L1378.018]
  • Default run hours: start the ad group schedule the next day at midnight and run all day, or run from 7 a.m. to midnight. [L1377.071]
  • Narrow the hours only when past data shows that traffic arrives at certain times without producing sales. [L1377.071]
  • Ad schedules can be set to run at chosen times instead of running continuously. [L1383.013]
  • Let the campaign run for two days before judging it. [L1378.001]

Naming

  • Name each ad group after the interest it tests, so results can be traced to that interest. If no interest is used, name it "broad". [L1378.015, L1378.001]
  • Give each video ad a sequential name such as "add 1" for the first video, so every creative can be identified later when comparing results within an ad group. [L1378.027]

Ad configuration

  • Name the ad, and leave Spark Ads off. The option "use TikTok account to deliver Spark Ads" is left off for this test structure. [L1378.001, L1378.019]
  • Enter the store name in the ad sign and upload the logo. [L1378.001]
  • Upload the video, add the ad text with a simple call to action, and click Continue. [L1378.001]
  • Destination: under the link field, add the product page URL, then click Submit to publish the campaign. [L1378.022, L1378.001]

Comment filters

  • Comment filters can be set on ads in TikTok Ads Manager. Use these settings when you run your own ads. [L0706.024]
