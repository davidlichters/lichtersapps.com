11.20 YouTube shorts, snapchat, Pinterest and other short-Video channels

Choosing platforms for short video

  • Snapchat works for very niche audiences such as the French market but is weaker for worldwide targeting. Pinterest can be tried when a product does very well. [L0690.031]
  • Each platform has a different audience, so a video can perform well on one and poorly on another. [L0784.015]
  • Two sources give different platform lists for short video. Use the list that matches your task:
Source Platforms When it applies
L0784.015 Core: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. Additional option: Snapchat Spotlight. Repurposing short video
L0796.009 Five platforms: Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Snapchat, YouTube Shorts, TikTok. Order is not important. Cross-posting short video
  • The same video can perform very differently by platform. One clip with weak reach on TikTok reached hundreds of thousands of views on YouTube Shorts and Snapchat Spotlight. [L1457.026]
  • UGC-style videos, in which a person talks or holds the product, perform well on TikTok and Snapchat. [L1431.045]

Multi-platform posting routine

  1. Choose the platform set. Use the five cross-posting platforms [L0796.009] or the core three plus Snapchat for repurposing [L0784.015].
  2. Make the video in UGC style, with a person talking or holding the product [L1431.045].
  3. Post the same content on each chosen platform [L0784.015, L0796.009, L1457.026].
  4. Do not drop the content after a weak first result on one platform. Keep posting it across platforms [L1457.026].
  5. On Snapchat, post Spotlight content every day [L1338.017].
  6. For Facebook, advertise the same product with new creatives [L1431.045].

YouTube Shorts strategy

  1. Post product videos, tutorials, unboxings, and reviews [L0226.027].
  2. Use descriptive titles and keyword-rich descriptions [L0226.027].
  3. No professional production is needed. Even simple videos with logos, music, and supplier footage rank in Google search results [L0226.027].
  4. Leverage user-generated content: ask suppliers for videos and add your own branding [L0226.027].
  5. Optimize for keywords to rank in both YouTube search and Google search [L0226.027].

YouTube Shorts ads (older advice, 2022)

The following procedure is from 2022 and is older advice. Placement rules may have changed since then [L1281.051].

  1. Upload the vertical video to YouTube [L1281.051].
  2. Create a YouTube ad campaign with the Video views objective [L1281.051].
  3. Use the video URL as the ad [L1281.051].
  • There is no manual Shorts placement. A vertical video under 60 seconds automatically qualifies for Shorts placements [L1281.051].
  • Run it alongside a Facebook version [L1281.051].

YouTube Shorts viewer and creator features

  • Custom thumbnails for Shorts are fully rolled out to all YouTube Partner Program creators. The earlier version had been unstable during testing [L1338.019].
  • Reactions: the Like and Dislike buttons were replaced by a single Heart icon. Viewers can choose Not Interested or Don't Recommend Channel in place of the old thumbs-down control [L1338.020].
  • Clear Screen hides all on-screen icons for distraction-free viewing. It launched in the week of 29 June 2026 and hides icons and captions with a single tap. Because viewers watch without overlays, this can affect how analytics-linked engagement is measured [L1338.021, L1504.021].
  • Viewers can watch at 2x playback speed by long-pressing anywhere on a Short. This faster option may change how long viewers spend watching each video [L1504.022].

Snapchat Spotlight and Now Playing

  • Now Playing on Snap Map: users can opt in to share their real-time Spotify listening activity. Friends can then discover songs through that activity [L1338.015].
  • In Now Playing, tapping a track opens Spotlight videos that use the same sound. Songs can also be saved to Spotify directly from Spotlight videos and Sound Pages [L1338.016].
  • Snapchat reports that creators who post Spotlight content every day grow followers about 10 times faster than creators who post occasionally. This is the platform's own figure [L1338.017].

Snapchat Snap Map sharing

  • When adding a Snap to Snap Map, the share screen lists "My Story" and "Best Friends" as audience options. Choose the audience before sending, because the two options reach different viewers [L1338.024].

TikTok feature updates

  • Creators can now upload original songs directly to TikTok. Previously this was possible only through a Spotify or TikTok representative. This is useful for a jingle or sound tied to a paid campaign [L1338.010].
  • Photo posts (carousels) can be created, published, and scheduled from the TikTok desktop web or app, not only from mobile. Use this to plan photo content in batches on a computer [L1338.011].
  • TikTok's feature list for this update names a "Songs of Live" option for uploading original songs for TikTok Live. Live content can be auto-clipped into shareable LIVE Gifts. Check the exact label in your own app before relying on it [L1338.023].

Pinterest with Autopin

  • Autopin.co automates Pinterest engagement, including pinning content, sending board invites, and following relevant users. Use it to grow Pinterest followers and drive recurring traffic to your website without daily manual effort [L0226.028].
  • Pinterest strategy:
    1. Create pins linking to your blog articles and product pages [L0226.029].
    2. Organize pins into boards by category [L0226.029].
    3. Use Autopin.co to automate pinning schedules, follower growth, and board invitations [L0226.029].
    4. Build backlinks by adding your website URL to pins. Approach this carefully to avoid policy violations [L0226.029].
    5. This is particularly effective for home renovation, home products, beauty, fashion, and sports niches [L0226.029].

Reddit

  1. Build subreddit karma by participating in relevant communities [L0226.030].
  2. Once you have sufficient karma or reputation, create your own subreddit focused on your niche [L0226.030].
  3. Post all blog content, new products, and updates to your subreddit [L0226.030].
  4. Each post generates backlinks and traffic to your site [L0226.030].
  5. Engage with the community to grow followers [L0226.030].
