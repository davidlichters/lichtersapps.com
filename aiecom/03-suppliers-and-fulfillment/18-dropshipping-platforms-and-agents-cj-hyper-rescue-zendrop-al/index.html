3.18 Dropshipping platforms and agents (CJ, hyper rescue, zendrop, AliExpress)

Platform roles and fit

  • Source C recommends a fixed sourcing check order: (1) AliExpress, (2) CJ (Global Sources), (3) Best Dealer. [L0209.030]
  • A 2021 Source A source (older advice) says not to source from AliExpress at all and to go direct to suppliers that stock products in the region you sell to, because this builds better supplier relationships and dropship approval. [L0270.042] The two positions conflict. The Source C order is the testing sequence, and the 2021 advice is the long-term direction.
  • CJ Dropshipping is the default starting supplier for beginners, because it is the easiest to handle and the fastest to learn. Other suppliers can be explored once sourcing is understood, for better prices and shipping times. [L1108.027]
  • Start with CJ Dropshipping for test products, then compare the others on offer, shipping times and convenience once sales grow. [L1171.034, L1171.055]
  • Alternatives to CJ and Hyper Rescue are Zendrop and USA Drop, and mentors recommend different starting suppliers. Choose the one that fits your target markets and product types. [L1172.063]
  • CJ Dropshipping and HyperSQ are suggested as the first fulfillment and sourcing tools for a beginner dropshipping store. CJ's sourcing price is the basis for calculating the selling price. [L1172.018]
  • The default supplier suggested for beginners is CJ Dropshipping. Zendrop, HyperSQ or BestDealer can be used if they offer better shipping times and a lower price. [L1219.012]
  • Common dropshipping suppliers for testing and scaling include CJ Dropshipping, HyperSKU, Zendrop, Best Dealer and AutoDS. [L1171.055]
  • Supplier and dropshipping platforms named in the lesson include CG dropshipping, Hyper SQ, Source Inbox and Zendrop. Each has its own app that lets the store owner send order data to the supplier from a laptop or desktop. [L0760.004]
  • Name spelling varies between units. HyperSQ is rendered for HyperSKU in the lesson, and the same company also appears as Hypersku and Hyper SKU. [L1171.034, L0552.011, L1017.031]
  • Dropshipping suppliers can handle quality checks, packing and direct shipping, so the seller does not need to meet manufacturers or handle goods physically. [L0760.005]
  • A fulfillment agent is necessary in dropshipping because it sources products directly from the factory and charges a small fee per order. This avoids the long delivery delays typical of AliExpress. [L1129.006]
  • Fulfillment companies such as CJ or Hyper Rescue are a shared-support model. They suit beginners, and at scale dedicated support with real-time updates, better prices and customization becomes more valuable. [L0685.020]
  • Dropshipping suppliers are not perfect. Their stock and return handling are often weak. Plan to move to private agents or your own warehouse once sales are high enough, and do not expect perfect service from a dropshipping supplier. [L1315.008]
  • Dropshipping suppliers are cheaper to start with and slower to ship. Private agents tend to be cheaper with faster shipping once the store has volume. [L1315.011]
  • Choose the supplier per product, because AliExpress and CJ Dropshipping are often close in quality and the better one can change by product. If Zendrop has the product at the same or lower price, Zendrop may be faster. If CJ offers more variety and better reviews, CJ is the better choice. [L0657.034]
  • Dropshipping platform suppliers may not be good for the long term, and service depends on the product. If a product has no good manufacturer, switch to an agent or a better supplier. [L1440.014]
  • Agents are third parties between sellers and direct factories. They have contacts with other agents and factories and can check whether a specific book is in stock in various locations, including provinces in China. [L0560.002]
  • When a product is not sold on AliExpress or similar platforms, a sourcing agent in China can supply it. [L1184.071]

Checking sourcing viability before committing

  • Before committing to a product, check manually whether it is available on AliExpress to confirm sourcing viability. [L0209.031]
  • Confirm that a supplier actually sources a product before building an ad or store around it. A sourcing agent may not carry marketplace items, so check availability with the supplier first. [L0524.032]
  • Before ordering a product for testing, check whether it is available on a platform like Amazon for faster shipping and variation options. Direct orders from CJ Dropshipping were described as unreliable, with long delays and products not matching expectations. Ordering a single sample to check quality and allowing extra time for arrival is a reasonable precaution. [L0819.024]
  • Before running ads, send the product to a supplier such as CJ to check whether it can be sourced. Ask the supplier whether it can be sold as a liquid, since liquids can have shipping restrictions. [L1171.036]
  • Products on AliExpress can be found by searching the product name with the key words, which helps confirm sourcing options. [L1176.031]
  • CJ Dropshipping sourcing request: it is free and easy to open, and it returns the supplier's actual price for a product. It is used to replace AliExpress estimates with accurate cost data. [L0693.020]
  • If CJ does not offer a product, contact several other suppliers to request quotes. Compare them by how fast they reply and how good their English is. A reply that takes three to four days is a bad sign. [L0361.037]
  • Choose a supplier by comparing product price, shipping time and service experience, and keep more than one supplier available. In the lesson's examples, CJ Dropshipping, Hyper SKU and Best Dealer were used alongside each other. A cheaper supplier is fine if it wins on these criteria. [L1017.031]
  • Some AliExpress suppliers operate several shops and can combine different products into one parcel when they are ordered together. The lesson's operator used this so multi-item orders look like they come from one warehouse. Before listing many products from such a supplier, confirm how it consolidates shipments. [L1131.062]

Quality verification

Quality check before scaling a product (procedure)

  1. On AliExpress, look for a listing with a rating of 4.6 or higher and at least 50 reviews. [L0335.032]
  2. If a listing has fewer than 50 reviews, treat it as doubtful and look for another listing of the same product. [L0335.032]
  3. On Amazon, look for a rating of 4 or higher. [L0335.032]
  4. Order one sample from the supplier before you scale. [L0335.032]

AliExpress quality check (procedure)

  1. Search for the product term. [L1183.012]
  2. Find a listing with at least 4.6 star rating and at least 100 orders. [L1183.012]
  3. Avoid relying on listings with high order counts but a rating below 4.6. [L1183.012]
  4. Record the AliExpress average rating in a column of the research sheet and use it to rank products. [L1183.012]

  • The review-count rule (50) is stated for AliExpress listings in one source and the order-count rule (100) in another. The sources do not say which applies in which case. [L0335.032, L1436.011, L1183.012]
  • Several listings of the same product at 4.6 or higher and 50 or more reviews suggest acceptable quality. Ordering a sample is the surer check but takes time and is optional. [L1436.011]
  • The same product can have several manufacturers, so one listing's rating does not describe the product as a whole. A listing rated 3.8 while another is rated 4.6 or higher suggests a weaker version of the product. [L0335.033]
  • Compare the sold count and review count of AliExpress listings before choosing a supplier. A listing with several hundred sales and reviews is more credible than one with only about 16 sales. [L1444.067]
  • Order and test the product yourself before promoting it with video or ads. If the product does not work as the content shows, buyers and commenters will find out and conversion will fall. [L1444.068]
  • Check supplier reviews on AliExpress with care, because many suppliers there use fake reviews. Cross-check the product on Amazon, eBay or other marketplaces where similar products are sold. Read the negative reviews, since some are unreliable. For a final check, order the product yourself and judge its quality. [L1215.023]
  • Ask an agent for videos and photos of each product part to check quality before you commit. Strong agents also work with the best suppliers and carry out quality testing. [L1217.023]

Choosing a fulfillment partner

Beginner recommendation

  • For beginners, the recommended fulfillment apps are CJ Dropshipping or Hyper Rescue, because they are more reliable and offer live agent support for refunds, returns and damaged packages. [L0635.005]
  • Automation-only apps such as AutoDS lack real after-sales support. [L0635.005]
  • Choose a fulfillment app with live agent support rather than a pure automation app, because refund, return and damaged-package cases are resolved through the supplier's agents inside the app. [L0635.012]
  • CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue both offer live agent support inside their apps for refunds, returns and damaged packages. CJ Dropshipping can also provide personal support once a store reaches good order volumes. [L0635.030]
  • Start with one fulfillment provider such as HyperSQ. Switch to CJ Dropshipping or Zendrop if service quality is poor. The right provider depends on the product and on the support agent assigned to you. [L0363.020]
  • Request quotes from three or four fulfillment companies before choosing one. Quotes typically arrive within 1-2 business days. Compare prices and the assigned support contact, not just the price per item. [L0363.019]
  • A supplier typically assigns an agent to the seller, who can check orders, fulfillment problems and orders lost in transit. Using a dedicated agent contact is a way to resolve supplier issues quickly. [L0678.026]
  • If a supplier's website gives only one support contact, post the question in the community chat and ask a mentor directly for their experience. Mentors often respond faster this way than through the supplier's support. [L1017.033]
  • Choosing a supplier by product is a valid approach, and sources differ on the fit of each platform (see the table below). [L0657.034, L1172.063]

Other fulfillment companies

  • Zendrop and Source Inbox are fulfillment companies in the same category as CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue. On platforms like these, the seller usually has to provide an AliExpress product link so the supplier can source the item. [L0635.029]
  • Source Inbox offers live agents. [L1217.066]
  • Other dropshipping fulfillment suppliers mentioned as alternatives to CJ Dropshipping are Best Dealer, Hyper Rescue and Zendrop, and AliExpress was mentioned as an option. [L0678.025]
  • Dropshipping suppliers named in the course include CJ, Hypersku, Zendrop and USA Drop. Manufacturers from outside the dropship model require paying a minimum order quantity (MOQ) upfront, so they do not fit a pure dropship model. [L0552.011]
  • Best Dealer, Zendrop and CJ were compared on price and shipping times across suppliers before one was chosen. [L1108.028]

AliExpress: role and limits

  • Older advice, 2019: AliExpress is not recommended as a long-term model because delivery takes too long, product quality varies, returns are hard to handle, chargebacks are common and prices cannot be controlled. Such a model can make money in the short term but is unstable for building a lasting store. [L0357.023]
  • Older advice, 2021: AliExpress as a supplier source brings slow shipping, inconsistent quality, mostly low-cost goods, difficult returns and no brand loyalty. It does not build a lasting business. [L0469.028]
  • AliExpress can be used only as a short-term supplier option. Move to better suppliers as soon as possible. [L0361.038]
  • Avoid AliExpress as a direct fulfillment source because its shipping times are very long and its support is unreliable. Use a dedicated fulfillment company that connects to the store instead. [L0685.001]
  • Sourcing from AliExpress requires a lot of manual work and gives little customer support. When a product is poor quality, AliExpress refunds are less reliable than those from dedicated dropshipping suppliers. [L1171.035]
  • A cheap product sourced from AliExpress with slow shipping leads to angry customers and poor reviews. Do not copy a competitor who sells this way if you want a lasting brand, and check shipping times before you commit to a supplier. [L1176.047]
  • Realistic AliExpress shipping was about 14 to 15 days in the session, which was considered acceptable. Delivery times of 4 to 6 weeks are a serious problem. [L1108.030]
  • Direct AliExpress orders could take up to about two months. [L1428.017]
  • Shein can potentially be used as a fulfillment source for dropshipped orders, especially for audiences on Facebook who are less aware of Shein. This was not yet tested by the speaker and needs to be verified before use. [L1439.007]
  • Before reselling Shein products in a dropshipping store and ordering to varied addresses, contact Shein support first and explain the plan. Reselling without this step carries the risk of the account being banned. [L1439.009]
  • A complete AliExpress rule set (older advice, 2021) is in the Source A sources, which prefer direct suppliers over AliExpress. [L0270.042, L0469.028]

Importing products into Shopify

  • AutoDS can import products from AliExpress and from CJ Dropshipping into a Shopify store. [L0363.083]
  • Alternatively, a fulfillment company's own Shopify app, such as HyperSQ or CJ Dropshipping, can be installed to import products directly from that company. [L0363.083]
  • To import products through a fulfillment app such as HyperSQ, install the app and send the AliExpress product link to their chat support, or source directly on their platform. Once they source the product, connect it to your store and the product list is created there. [L0363.018]
  • CJ Dropshipping can import listings from AliExpress, so a product found on AliExpress can be fulfilled through CJ. Use this when a product is available on AliExpress but you want CJ as the fulfillment partner. [L1444.070]
  • CJ Dropshipping can be used to source and fulfill products that were found on AliExpress. Pick the supplier that has the product in stock and connect it to the store as the fulfillment source. [L1439.006]
  • Zendrop can be used to request or import a dropshipping product into Shopify. Alternatively, you can add the product link now and connect it to Zendrop later. [L1331.010]
  • Older advice, 2020: Avoid product import apps that pull supplier catalogs into your store. They add a middleman between you and the brand, which reduces long-term profit compared with a direct relationship with the brand. [L0358.036]
  • The 2020 advice (above) and the fulfillment-app guidance in this chapter apply to different stages. Import apps are the beginner route for testing, and direct brand relationships are the long-term goal. [L0358.036, L0635.005, L0270.042]
  • Personalized products (for example engraved names) can be hard through CJ, which may require a developer to connect its API documentation. Print-on-demand providers such as Printful usually connect more easily to Shopify. [L1363.040]

CJ Dropshipping: listing a product in Shopify (procedure)

  1. Search for the product on CJ Dropshipping, or search by keyword to find similar listings. [L0685.010]
  2. Open the listing and select the specific variant (color and style). The variant changes the product price. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  3. Check the product price, the shipping cost and the total cost for the target country. [L0685.010]
  4. Compare shipping options and pick the one with the best balance of low cost and reasonable delivery time (for example 7 to 15 days). [L0635.009]
  5. Choose the List button, not Connect, for the product you want to add to the store. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  6. Set the store name, product type, collection, vendor and a target selling price. Set the Target Price (the selling price in the store), for example 39.99 USD. The vendor can be, for example, 'cj'. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  7. Click List it now. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  8. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for the product to appear in the Shopify store. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  9. Edit product title, images, descriptions and prices in Shopify as needed. [L0635.009, L0685.010]
  • On CJ Drop Shipping, the Connect option adds the product to the store with images, videos and descriptions that can be edited. The List option is used when adding the product with a target price directly through the listing screen. [L0685.011]
  • CJ Dropshipping has a very large catalog, so most products can be found and listed directly. [L0635.006]
  • CJ Drop Shipping has a very large catalog, so most products found on AliExpress can also be found there. Products can be connected to a Shopify store within minutes. [L0685.003]
  • CJ Dropshipping has a trending products page with product categories that is convenient for browsing dropshipping products. Its delivery times can be slow unless you pay for faster options. [L0706.031]

Hyper Rescue: setup (procedure)

  1. In the Shopify App Store, search for Hyper Rescue, click it and install it. [L0685.014]
  2. Create an account on Hyper Rescue. [L0685.014]
  3. Browse the dashboard catalogs (for example Top pet supplies, TikTok viral products and niche categories) to search for products. [L0685.014]
  4. If the product is not in the catalog, go to Smart sourcing or AliExpress sourcing. [L0685.014]
  5. Copy the AliExpress link of the product, paste it into the field, and click Save. [L0685.014]
  6. Wait about 1 to 2 days for sourcing to complete. [L0685.014]
  • Hyper Rescue has a smaller catalog and works mainly through sourcing requests based on AliExpress links. [L0635.006]
  • Hyper Rescue offers good shipping times, reliable support and better pricing, but it has a small catalog. Products not found in it are sourced by submitting an AliExpress link. [L0685.004]
  • Hyper Rescue's dashboard offers preset catalogs such as top pet supplies, TikTok viral products and niche-down categories. If a product is not in the catalog, use Smart Sourcing or AliExpress Sourcing, or try an image search with the help of the live agent. [L0635.031]

Sourcing products outside the catalog

Hyper Rescue sourcing (procedure)

  1. Copy the AliExpress product link. [L0635.007]
  2. In Hyper Rescue, open the AliExpress Sourcing section (or Smart Sourcing). [L0635.007]
  3. Paste the link and click Save. [L0635.007]
  4. Wait about 1 to 2 business days while the sourcing team finds the best factory, negotiates, obtains samples and verifies quality. [L0635.007]
  5. Review the quote, which shows the unit price and shipping cost per country (US, Canada, Australia and UK differ). Check stages such as 'sourcing in progress' and 'quote created'. [L0635.007]
  6. In Hyper Rescue's AliExpress sourcing, a submitted product moves through status stages: first 'sourcing in progress', then 'quote created'. The quote shows the price per unit and the shipping cost for each destination country, such as US, Canada, Australia or UK. Check the quote for your target country before accepting it. [L0685.026]

Request sourcing for a product not in the catalog (procedure)

  1. Try a keyword search first. If there is no match, use image search with a product photo. [L0685.017]
  2. If the product is still not found, contact the live agent support. [L0685.017]
  3. Alternatively, open the Sourcing section and click Post sourcing request. [L0685.017]
  4. Open the sourcing request section and choose Post Sourcing Request (Hyper Rescue) or the equivalent request option. [L0635.008]
  5. Enter the product title, a target price, and the AliExpress link. [L0635.008]
  6. For a product already in the Shopify store that should be sourced from another provider (for example from Hyper Rescue to CJ), select that product from the store and submit the request. [L0685.017]
  7. For a product that exists only as an AliExpress link, click the product request, enter the title (for example Cup washer), enter the target price, paste the AliExpress link and submit the request. [L0685.017]
  8. Submit the request. Sourcing takes about 1 to 2 days. [L0635.008, L0685.017]
  9. If neither keyword nor image search works, the live agent can help. [L0635.008]

CJ sourcing request (procedure)

  1. Submit a sourcing request on CJ with the link to the product, for example an AliExpress listing. [L1428.016]
  2. CJ contacts the producer or supplier and checks that the product is available for your store. [L1428.016]
  3. Once a customer orders, you pay CJ for that order. [L1428.016]
  4. CJ arranges the supplier's shipment, customs clearance and delivery to the end customer. [L1428.016]
  5. Check product quality yourself, because CJ's sourcing does not guarantee it. [L1428.016]

Sourcing request via CJ (procedure, short form)

  1. Find the product on CJ. [L1108.033]
  2. If it is not listed, submit a sourcing request with the AliExpress product link. [L1108.033]
  3. Wait for the answer, which is given within 24 hours and states whether the product is available. [L1108.033]
  • CJ is a fulfillment provider and not the manufacturer. Its higher unit price pays for handling, shipping, customs and delivery. The reported average CJ delivery time was 7 to 15 days, while direct AliExpress orders could take up to about two months. Choose the fulfillment route based on how much delivery time matters to your customers. [L1428.017]
  • CJ Dropshipping can be used to source and fulfill products that were found on AliExpress. [L1439.006]
  • A CJ product that is not in the catalog can be requested through a sourcing request. [L1428.016]
  • The sourcing request on CJ is free and easy to open. [L0693.020]
  • Negotiate and request in the live chat with a human agent (see the pricing section). [L1217.016]

Tracking and order status

  • Shipping status shows in the order list at each stage in the integrated app, such as 'sourcing in progress' and 'quote created' for sourcing requests. [L0685.026, L0635.007]
  • A supplier agent can check orders, fulfillment problems and orders lost in transit. [L0678.026]
  • Use the agent contact to resolve tracking or lost-order cases quickly. [L0678.026]

Order fulfillment

CJ Dropshipping: fulfilling store orders (procedure)

  1. Open the My CJ section. [L0685.013]
  2. Open Imported orders. [L0685.013]
  3. Shopify orders are imported into the My CJ section. Click Sync Store Orders (or Sync store orders) to pull all Shopify orders into CJ. [L0635.010, L0685.013]
  4. Open each order and click Fulfill (or Add to cart) to fulfill it. [L0635.010, L0685.013]

Hyper Rescue: fulfilling store orders (procedure)

  1. Orders from the Shopify store sync automatically into the Order list in Hyper Rescue, and new orders appear dynamically. [L0635.010, L0685.018]
  2. Open the order and click Fulfill. [L0635.010, L0685.018]
  3. Pay for the product price plus shipping cost. [L0635.010, L0685.018]
  4. Hyper Rescue handles the rest of the fulfillment. [L0685.018]

Multi-item orders

  • When a store has multi-item orders from upsells, ask the live CJ agent to ship items from the same supplier together. CJ may otherwise send them in separate packages from different manufacturers. [L1217.024]
  • At good upsell volume, switch to an agent. Agents can combine packages and customize orders, which dropshipping platforms typically do not. [L1217.025]

Costs per order

  • CJ Dropshipping example: a cup washer costs 5.95 USD for the chosen variant. The total cost including shipping to a US address is 18.85 USD. The price range across variants runs from 1.12 to 10.75 USD. Shipping times of 7 to 15 days were considered decent. [L0635.011, L0685.008]
  • CJ product prices vary by variant, ranging from 1.12 to 10.75 USD for the same listing. Check the price of the exact variant before listing. [L0685.009]
  • A product sold at 6.45 USD with US shipping had a delivery window of 7 to 15 days. A specific variant (color or style) cost 5.95 USD for the product, with a total cost of 18.85 USD including shipping to a US address. [L0685.008]
  • Pay the fulfillment provider pay-as-you-go with PayPal or credit card. It has no monthly plan. You can top up a balance (for example 200-300 USD) and then click fulfill per order. [L0363.021]
  • Faster suppliers can cost roughly 10% more per product than slower ones. [L0678.025]

Pricing, negotiation and agent selection

  • Supplier pricing does not automatically decrease in quality. If a supplier cannot lower price further, it will say so. Volume commitments, especially stock, provide negotiation leverage. [L0298.023]
  • Negotiate in the live chat of the supplier with a human agent, ideally during Chinese working hours. Automated replies rarely offer discounts, so get a live agent first. [L1217.016]
  • Listed prices on platforms such as Hyper Rescue and CJ are often higher than what you can get through direct negotiation. Do not accept the listed price without asking a live agent for a discount. [L1217.018]
  • Platforms and agents such as CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue often list higher prices to new dropshippers, because they prioritize long-term partners who bring steady volume. Negotiate by live chat and expect lower prices than the listed price. [L1217.065]
  • Listed prices are negotiable through live chat for CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue. [L1217.066]
  • Play agents against each other by sharing a competing agent's quote in chat and asking for a better offer. Ask each agent for a quote that beats the others to get the best price. [L1217.021]
  • Suppliers and agents do not mind when you send the same inquiry to several of them. They compete for good long-term clients, so the goal is to be a strong client to whoever you choose. [L1217.022]
  • Once a store reaches a consistent daily order count, suppliers may negotiate price and pre-order a week of inventory into their warehouse. [L1315.011]
  • Request stock inventory for at least one week when reaching 10+ daily orders. Immediately stop shipping new items from China to enable faster stock-based fulfillment if a customer cancels. [L0298.021]
  • Order stock starting at 10+ orders per day to save 0.5-1 dollar per item and reduce processing time to 12 hours (vs. 2-5 days normally). [L0298.019]
  • A dedicated fulfillment agent or personal agent can prioritize your orders at CJ Dropshipping when the default processing times are not reliable. [L1218.007]

Asking for better service with no sales history (procedure)

  1. State that you have sold before and that this is a new store you are testing with products. [L1440.013]
  2. Say that you want a long-term partnership. [L1440.013]
  3. Ask for better quotes and explain that you have experience. [L1440.013]
  4. Ask for a personal agent. The platforms provided one by Skype. [L1440.013]
  5. Send consistent sales, because suppliers give better service to customers who bring steady business. [L1440.013]

[L1217.017]

  • Negotiating with the 1688.com price: 1688.com is a Chinese domestic wholesale platform that shows the actual price suppliers pay. The site is in Chinese, so translate it and compare that price with your agent's quote to see the markup being charged. [L1217.015]
  • Personal agents usually require a minimum of about 5-10 orders per day. Move to a personal agent once your volume reaches that level. [L1217.019]
  • Ecom Ops requires a minimum order quantity of about 100 units per item. Other agents can work with volumes like about 5 orders per day or around 100 orders per month. [L1217.020]

Setting up a private supplier (procedure)

  1. Pick John or Joelle first. If neither carries the product, pick Bonnie or Veddo. [L1156.005]
  2. Get the supplier's WhatsApp number from the supplier list. [L1156.005]
  3. Send the WhatsApp direct message using the template, with a product photo attached. [L1156.005]
  4. Make sure the supplier confirms the exact product is available, that there is no MOQ, and that they ship to your target country. [L1156.005]
  5. Once the supplier agrees, go to shopify.partners and give the supplier partnership access to your Shopify store, following the instructions the supplier sends you. [L1156.005]
  6. The supplier fulfills the orders from your store. [L1156.005]
  7. Pay the supplier a fixed amount per order via Wise or bank transfer. [L1156.005, L1156.011]

Why and how private suppliers differ

  • Private suppliers are used instead of public dropshipping apps to get better margins, custom branding and more control over the product and fulfillment. [L1156.006]
  • With a private supplier, the supplier handles fulfillment of orders from your store. Your role is to grant store access and pay the supplier per order. [L1156.009]
  • Pay private suppliers a fixed amount for each order they fulfill, using Wise or bank transfer. [L1156.011]
  • The supplier's ship-from location and processing time are the first things to confirm (see the processing table above). [L1214.001, L1156.014]

Switching suppliers and leaving a platform

  • Supplier path for dropshipping (procedure):
    1. Start with CJ Dropshipping for product testing. [L1171.034]
    2. Once you make significant sales, switch to a supplier with better offers and shipping times such as HyperSKU (rendered as HyperSQ in the lesson), Best Dealer or a private agent. [L1171.034]
    3. Choose the supplier that gives the best offer, shipping times and convenience at each stage. [L1171.034]
  • Move from a fulfillment platform to a private supplier once you have consistent demand, for example at least about 5 orders per day or 3-4 days of steady sales. The private supplier must reply within its working hours. [L0363.024]
  • Keep the supplier that works best for each product, and consider using different suppliers for different products in the same store. [L0678.024]
  • Using two different suppliers in one store is possible, with a clear overview of which orders go to which supplier. Products can be split between suppliers depending on which one ships them fastest. [L0678.027]
  • Start with an easy-to-set-up supplier such as CJ Dropshipping, track processing and shipping times per order, and test other suppliers if delays become too long. [L0678.024]
  • A dropshipping supplier such as CJ Dropshipping is acceptable for early testing because it is easy to set up and offers workable prices and shipping. Only once demand is consistent should the store contact other suppliers and negotiate a better price. [L1426.033]
  • The right provider depends on the product and on the support agent assigned to you. [L0363.020]

Switching checklist (procedure)

  1. Before switching suppliers, stop running ads on the date the old supplier will stop receiving orders. Orders placed after this date may stay unprocessed and cause refund and delivery problems. [L1173.026]
  2. Check that all paid orders on the old platform are marked as fulfilled in Shopify. Unmarked orders can be paid for twice when the new supplier ships them. [L1173.027]
  3. For Day One specifically, request a quotation first. Expect that it will need access to the store, and close out unfulfilled orders on the old supplier before you connect it. [L1173.047]

Choosing between suppliers and the cost of switching

  • Choose the supplier that ships as fast or faster. [L1444.046]
  • Products can be split between suppliers depending on which one ships them fastest. [L0678.027]
  • Choose the fulfillment route based on how much delivery time matters to your customers. [L1428.017]
