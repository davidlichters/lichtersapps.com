15.17 Other details

Inbox and team tools

  • In Gmail, create filter rules that send unwanted email straight into a folder, so spam does not clutter your inbox every time you log in. [L1285.103]
  • To share a Google Docs or Google Sheets file for a team to copy, set the share link to view-only, so anyone with the link can read it. The recipient then opens File > Make a copy to save their own editable version in their Google Drive. [L1223.035]

Building and owning audiences

  • The email list and the lists of followers and engagers are the assets used to attract partners and sales opportunities. A brand with an engaged list can do business with related companies easily. [L0487.058]
  • Pinterest with a linked store and product pins costs nothing to set up, but it is not a major source of sales compared with Google and Microsoft. [L0855.155]
  • Instagram Broadcast Channels suit brands with an already engaged audience, and a minimum follower count may be required before a channel can be created. Check whether the format fits the page before investing time in it. [L0576.018]
  • Broadcast Channels work like a VIP club: followers feel they get behind-the-scenes access. The brand can post one-way, run quick polls and ask for future video ideas, while followers can answer but cannot freely send messages to the whole group. [L0576.019]
  • Broadcast messages are a one-way stream, so encourage emoji reactions on them rather than expecting full replies. Reactions are a low-effort way for followers to feel connected to the brand. [L0576.017]
  • Livecom's channel page with the stored RCPs is segmented inside Klaviyo, so the streams can be promoted to those segments. Videos embedded in the bubbles can also be reused in Klaviyo email campaigns. [L0951.010]
  • Messenger broadcasts were presented as having higher open and click rates than email, because most people already have Messenger on their phone. The source figures were shown only as a screenshot, so the exact percentages are not stated in the lesson. [L1243.019]

Affiliate follow-up emails

  • Affiliate follow-up emails serve two purposes: building trust through helpful content, screenshots and case studies, and asking the reader to take a specific action. Emails that only give content and never ask for anything usually produce no sales. [L0514.019]
  • Use this follow-up template:
Subject: Quick check-in

Hi {first_name},

How is your progress going? I wanted to see if you have started making money online yet. If you have not, here is the resource I recommend to start: {affiliate_link}

It helped me, and I can personally vouch for it. Let me know if you have any questions.

P.S. If you have not started yet, {affiliate_link} is the best place to begin.

[L0514.021]

  • Place the affiliate link at the end of follow-up emails, usually in a P.S. line, and link to the specific sales page. Owning a subscriber's email gives you a long-term contact until they unsubscribe, so the content must stay valuable enough that they keep opening. [L0514.022]

Collecting extra shipping charges after the order

Older advice (2020).

  • To collect expedited or upgraded shipping charges after the order, create a draft order in Shopify or a PayPal invoice, then send it to the customer for the additional payment. [L0268.055]

Subscriber opt-out and re-subscription

Older advice (2023), not marked as older by the 2019-2022 rule.

  • Opting out of a single email from a membership or course list can stop all further emails from that list. To receive updates again, give written permission to the support team so they can re-add the address. [L0804.110]

Product title tags

  • Add the words "buy" and "online" to product page title tags, as long as the store already has some traffic momentum. Expect roughly 10-30% more traffic and a larger sales lift. The typical template is "Buy [Product Name] Online", followed by the brand name if it fits. Some CMSs cannot do that order, so use "[Product Name] Online" instead. [L0575.001]
  • Add buy/online to product title tags in one of three ways: [L0575.002]
    1. Manually edit each product if you have few products (about 10 products in 15 minutes).
    2. On larger CMS stores, use an SEO plugin with a title and meta description template for product pages.
    3. For thousands of products, export to CSV, use formulas in Excel to build the new titles in spare columns, then import the file back.

Sales claims (not practice rules)

Older material (2021-22). This is sales copy from a course's own offer, not a practice rule. Do not copy its claims or its urgency tactics into your own store.

  • A Dropship Breakthrough offer was presented with a 42% discount from a $4,997 annual price to $2,987 annual, or a monthly option of $9.97 for three months, with a 72-hour deadline. The stated 42% does not match the prices: $2,987 is about 40% below $4,997. [L0414.046]
