5.6 Keyword and SEO research tools

Choosing Tools by Budget

  • Google Search Console is free and sufficient for starting out, before investing in AH Refs or SEM Rush. [L0255.060]
  • Free keyword research tools are sufficient for basic keyword research, and paid versions are not needed for it. [L0665.049]
  • At least one paid third-party SEO tool is required for an active SEO strategy, but you do not need to use all of them. Each tool has a different price point, and the return on investment is usually easy to achieve if the tool is used regularly. [L1272.006]
  • The choice of a third-party SEO tool depends on budget and preference, but at least one such tool is needed. Premium tools offer more data depth than budget tools. [L1272.014]
  • The data on links and search terms in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools is very limited. Third-party SEO tools provide far more data on links, search terms and competitor sites. [L1272.005]
  • Paid SEO and outreach tools are worth their cost because automating research and email collection can save hundreds of hours of manual work if the method is used regularly. Free or cheaper options can be used at first, and paid tiers can be added once the workload justifies them. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.013]
  • Free starting source: if you have run campaigns for some time, your own Google Ads search term data, for example from Google Shopping campaigns, already shows search volume for terms in your niche. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.009]
  • Outsourcing keyword research to a freelancer on Fiverr is a cheap alternative to doing it yourself. You give the freelancer your root terms, they pull the keyword data and send it back, and you then group and allocate the terms to pages. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.014]
  • Budget tier, Ubersuggest: the budget option among third-party SEO tools. A paid plan costs roughly $10 to $15 per month (price at time of recording). It offers less data depth than premium tools, but it is enough for many basic tasks. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.007]
  • Ubersuggest price points differ by source (older advice, 2021-22):
    • About $10 per month. [L0877.010]
    • Roughly $10 to $15 per month. [L1272.007]
    • About $7 US dollars per month, or about 10 Australian dollars per month. [L1485.009]
  • Free Ubersuggest accounts: the free account gives very little usable data, so a paid plan is recommended even on a tight budget. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.008]
  • Ahrefs, premium tier, price: a premium third-party SEO tool at around $100 USD per month (price at time of recording). It is essential for link building and content research and is used heavily when running an active SEO strategy. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.009]
  • Ahrefs, necessity, conflicting views:
    • Ahrefs is considered the best option for keyword research, but it is not strictly necessary. Ubersuggest is a lower-cost alternative whose reports cover the same information. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0878.010]
    • Ahrefs, Moz and SEMrush are expensive paid tools. They are more accurate and show more data points per term, but they are not necessary for content keyword research. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.013]
  • SEMrush: a premium alternative to Ahrefs at a similar price level. Its advantage is paid search features such as ad research, which Ahrefs did not offer at that time. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.010]
  • Raven Tools: a suite that costs somewhat less than Ahrefs and SEMrush. Its SEO audit tool analyzes a website and lists areas that can be improved. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.013]
  • Screaming Frog: an older, thorough SEO tool that runs as a paid desktop application, not online. It can handle most SEO tasks, but analyzing a very large website can slow down the computer and take a long time. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.011]
  • For large SEO projects, an online tool is preferable to a desktop application, because a desktop crawl of a very large website can strain the computer and run for a long time. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.012]
  • Most SEO research tools provide the same core reports: keyword, competitor and backlink reports. Their scoring of domain authority and keyword difficulty can differ slightly between tools, but the results should still be usable for the method. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.004]
  • Moz domain analysis: the free version of Moz.com's domain analysis tool shows the domain authority of any website. It allows 3 reports per day and needs no sign-up or payment. [L1227.004]

Free Keyword Research Tools

Google Keyword Planner

  • Google Keyword Planner is free for anyone with a Google Ads account and provides relevant search volume data. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.011]
  • It is used to check search volume for niche keywords before choosing a niche. (older advice, 2021) [L0801.114]
  • It is used, together with Ubersuggest, to check search volume and demand for niche keywords. (older advice, 2020) [L0664.074]
  • Volume ranges depend on account history:
    • New accounts: very broad volume ranges, for example 10,000 to 100,000. (older advice? Source unlabelled) [L0359.048]
    • After the account has spent money on ads: the range narrows to within about 1,000. [L0359.048]
  • Sign-up: Google Keyword Planner sign-up asks for a company name and a website. If you do not have a website yet, enter the domain name you plan to use. (older advice, 2019) [L1275.012]
  • Keywords Everywhere can replace Google Keyword Planner when that tool will not let you sign up. (older advice, 2019) [L1275.011]
  • Keyword and trend data pulled through third-party APIs can fail intermittently. One trend-data source was reported to return results only about 9 percent of the time over a few days. Retry the search after a few minutes first. If failures continue, switch to another data source such as Google Keyword Planner, which requires a Google Ads account. [L1097.047]

Ubersuggest

  • Free version: Ubersuggest has a free version with a limit on searches per period. It gives keyword volume ranges within roughly 1,000 right away, which makes it useful as a free keyword tool before a Google Ads account has spent money. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0359.047]
  • Free searches, 2021: as of 2021, Ubersuggest offered five free keyword searches per day, which was an alternative for countries not covered by the program's keyword tool. (older advice, 2021) [L0671.028]
  • Free access for full data: a free account is needed to see the full data. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1485.009]
  • Ubersuggest is used to estimate monthly Google search volume per country, and later for other marketing research during the business build. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1485.009]
  • Ubersuggest is used to fill the competitor analysis worksheet by showing where a competitor's website gets its traffic from. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0441.010]
  • Metrics: Ubersuggest shows search volume, SEO difficulty, paid difficulty, average CPC, keyword ideas and CSV export. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.010]
  • Keyword Discovery page: three checks per keyword. Use them together when judging a keyword, not volume alone. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1485.017]
    • Traffic volume: how many buyers search for it.
    • Trends: how search volume has changed over time.
    • Difficulty: whether a keyword is easy to rank for.
  • Market setting: in the language and country selector, choose the market (English / United States for a US store). It appears in the URL as locId=2840 for the United States. Change it to the target country, such as Australia, before every export, because search volume and difficulty differ by country. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0758.019]
  • Keyword overview: shows searcher age ranges, mobile versus desktop volume, a 12-month volume chart, and the average backlinks and domain score of the top 10 ranking pages. For 'surfboards' the top 10 averaged 14 backlinks and a domain score of 54, while for 'wetsuits' the figures were 33 backlinks and a domain score of 72. Use these benchmarks to estimate how much link building a term will need. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0758.021]
  • Top-ranking URLs: the keyword overview also lists the top ranking URLs with estimated visits, backlinks, domain score and social shares. Use it to see what kind of page ranks, such as marketplaces, Wikipedia, or store collection pages, before deciding whether to target a term with content, collection or product pages. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0758.022]
  • Feature gap: Ahrefs and Semrush both allow keyword ranking reports with a featured snippet filter. Ubersuggest does not offer this filter, so it is unsuitable for this step. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0662.005]

Google and YouTube Auto Suggest

  • Google and YouTube Auto Suggest are free and show popular searches related to a root term. They are a good source of keyword ideas, and YouTube keyword research works through its own search suggestions. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.012]

Keywords Everywhere

  • Free browser extension: Keywords Everywhere is a free Chrome extension (also described as a free browser extension for Chrome or Firefox) that shows keyword data next to Google search results. Use it during niche research and campaign planning, to research search volume and estimated cost-per-click before a new Google Ads account gains access to the keyword planning tool. (older advice, 2019) [L0243.042, L1275.011]
  • Pricing changed, conflicting views: Keywords Everywhere switched to a paid model, while free keyword tools with similar purposes exist. Use one of those alternatives for niche keyword research if the paid option is not worth it. (older advice, 2019) [L1110.021]
  • Where both views apply: the 2019 units describe the tool as free, and the later note says it moved to a paid model. Check current pricing before relying on it. [L0243.042, L1275.011, L1110.021]

Other Free Research Sources

  • Google Gemini was suggested as a starting point for keyword lists, based on the idea that Google's own model may have better access to Google search data. Verify any keyword list it generates before using it in campaigns. [L1287.035]

Paid Keyword and SEO Suites

Ahrefs (Keywords Explorer and Site Explorer)

  • Ahrefs trial: Ahrefs offers a 14-day trial, which is enough time to run the keyword process for 10 articles. You can cancel after the trial if the results are not useful. [L0382.046]
  • Ahrefs usage in plan, limits: Keywords Explorer shows these limits in the plan used: 100 daily reports and 1,000 monthly SERP updates. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0878.011]
  • Overview metrics: Clicks, CPS (clicks per search) and RR (return rate) are shown alongside search volume and CPC. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0878.011]
  • Bulk keyword checks: Keywords Explorer accepts a bulk list of keywords, pasted separated by commas or on new lines, or uploaded as a CSV or TXT file. This is useful for checking many candidate topics at once. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0878.025]
  • Keyword lists: you can save candidate keywords into keyword lists, with up to 20 lists per account. Use these lists to group topics for each niche or project. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0878.026]
  • Ahrefs for collection terms: Ahrefs and SEMrush are both used to quantify monthly search volume for a candidate category before a collection page is created. The same tools can check volume for hybrid keywords on a recurring basis. [L1389.016]
  • Backlinks: Ahrefs shows backlinks to any domain, including the sources of the links. Use it to see where competitors get their links and whether they publish content on other domains. (older advice, 2022) [L0963.051]
  • Ahrefs and the skyscraper method: Ahrefs is a paid SEO tool used for keyword research, competitor analysis, backlink reports for individual sites, and metrics such as domain authority and keyword difficulty. It is one of the more expensive paid tools in this link-building toolkit. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.001]
  • AH Refs (written as "AH Refs" in the source, most likely Ahrefs):
    • Used by teams to track organic keyword rankings and analyze SEO data. [L0255.058]
    • Costs approximately $300 per month for team access (2025 pricing). [L0255.059]
    • Price conflict: the $100 per month Ahrefs figure from 2021-22 and the approximately $300 per month figure from 2025 do not match. The 2025 figure applies to team access, and the 2021-22 figure applies to a single plan at that time. [L1272.009, L0255.059]
  • Bulk exports, procedures (Ahrefs), see the export and cleaning procedures below. [L1443.012, L1443.013]

Semrush

  • SEMrush is a widely used SEO and SEM research tool that offers keyword, competitor and backlink reports, though its report layouts differ and you may need to look around to find the equivalents. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.003]
  • Its advantage over Ahrefs is paid search features such as ad research. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1272.010]
  • Its featured snippet filter for keyword ranking reports is available. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0662.005]
  • It is listed among the expensive paid tools that are not necessary for content keyword research. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.013]
  • Ahrefs and Semrush used for collection volume checks. [L1389.016]

Moz

  • Moz is listed among the expensive paid tools with keyword research functions. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.013]

Other tools (Answer The Public, Source A, Research Vault, Source A, BuzzSumo)

  • Answer The Public (answerthepublic.com) generates question and preposition keyword variations for a topic and can be exported as CSV to plan content for subniches. (older advice, 2022) [L0665.046]
  • Source A keyword research tool: gives search data by country and keyword. It works for any keyword where data is available, and bugs can be reported to support. (older advice, 2022) [L0665.048]
  • Research Vault search volume tool: shows how often a keyword is searched. Use it to test whether generic, non-brand terms in a non-English product language are popular enough in English-speaking markets. [L0941.044]
  • Source A keyword tool:
    • Used to find search terms and niche ideas before building a store. (older advice, 2021) [L1089.006]
    • Keyword research is done with it, and it should always return results even with zero search volume. If it does not, contact support. (older advice, 2022) [L0969.054]
  • In-house keyword research tool: gives members unlimited searches when they log in with their member account and select the target country. Logged-out use is limited to a free version, so keyword checks should be done after logging in. (older advice, 2021) [L0962.150]
  • BuzzSumo does for blog content what vidIQ does for YouTube, showing trending articles and their performance. It is used to research topics before writing blog posts. [L0487.063]

Keyword Research Workflow in Spreadsheets

  • Export keyword ideas from Ubersuggest for each root term:
    1. Enter the root keyword in Ubersuggest and open the keyword ideas view. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.017]
    2. Check that the country setting matches your target market. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.017]
    3. Click Export to CSV and save the file to your computer. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.017]
    4. Repeat for every root term in your business, including terms for product types and product uses. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.017]
  • Keyword ideas view tabs. The results are grouped into five tabs: Suggestions, Related, Questions, Prepositions and Comparisons. Each tab has its own count. The SUGGESTIONS count shows how many similar terms were found (for example 255 for '4x4 accessories'). Each keyword row shows KEYWORD, VOL and CPC columns, a Filters option sits above the list, and an EXPORT TO CSV button exports the full list. The Questions tab is a good source for problem-based content topics. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.030, L1485.018, L0758.020]
  • Suggestions and Related: the Suggestions tab is the narrowest list of closest matches, while the Related tab can contain many times more terms, most of them off-topic. Scan Related for relevant terms instead of exporting it wholesale, and use the Questions tab for question-form terms that suit problem-based content. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.032]
  • Master keyword workbook layout (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.028]
Tab Columns Purpose
Raw Data Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty (from CSV export) All exported terms combined, sorted by volume, minimum 100 per month
Content Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty Informational, problem-based and product-use terms for content pieces
Collection Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty Broad product type, branded and specific product type terms

  • Summary formulas: summarise the volume and CPC columns in each tab with SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX and COUNTA. This shows the total search volume per page type, the average and range of CPC, and how many keywords each group contains. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0877.029]

Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

Google Search Console

  • Google Search Console is free (search.google.com) and is used to verify domain ownership, submit a sitemap, see indexed pages, free organic impressions and clicks, search keywords and site errors. [L0735.011]
  • Keyword tracking: Google Search Console is a free tool to track keyword rankings and organic traffic. [L0255.060]
  • Search terms vs Google Analytics:
    • Google Analytics shows many search terms as "not provided" (2020) or "not set" (2021), because of privacy. Use Google Search Console with the same Google account to see the keywords that bring clicks. (older advice, 2020-21) [L0468.079, L0834.052]
    • Google Search Console shows more search terms with impressions and clicks than Google Analytics. (older advice, 2021) [L0834.052]
  • Submitting new content: Google Search Console (Google webmaster tools) can be used to submit new content URLs so they get crawled sooner. [L0970.094]
  • Import into Bing: when importing from Google Search Console into Bing, see the Bing setup below. [L0303.006]

Bing Webmaster Tools

  • Bing Webmaster Tools, found at bing.com/webmasters, is Bing's free dashboard for seeing how Bing crawls and indexes a website. Its main areas are Search Performance, Site Explorer and error monitoring. [L0303.001]
  • Search Performance and Site Explorer: Search Performance shows how the site appears in Bing search results, and Site Explorer lets you inspect the site's pages. Both are used to find pages that have not been indexed and to spot issues that need fixing. [L0303.010]
  • Sign-in: Bing Webmaster Tools accepts sign-in with a Microsoft, Google or Facebook account. Use the same email address you used for your Google accounts so you can find and manage all accounts consistently. [L0303.004]
  • Same email, what it does and does not do: using the same email address for the Bing login does not link the accounts automatically. Its only benefit is consistent login credentials across services, which makes it easier to remember and access each account. [L0303.005]
  • Setup flow, button and label names as shown:
    • Get started: first screen. [L0303.003]
    • Import your sites from GSC or Google Search Console: import option on the Welcome page. [L0303.003]
    • Allow: sharing approval. [L0303.003]
    • Import: domain confirmation. [L0303.003]
    • Done: shown after the success message. [L0303.003]
    • Update now: in the profile section. [L0303.003, L0303.014]
  • Importing from Google Search Console:
    • Bing identifies the Google account by its email address, so choose the account that was used to set up the Search Console property. If several domains are linked to that account, Bing lets you pick which one to import. [L0303.006]
    • After importing, Bing may need up to 48 hours to process the data and reports. Until processing finishes, the dashboard data may be incomplete. [L0303.007]
  • Profile section: Update now opens fields for personal information, professional details and company information. The same section also contains a delete account option, which should not be clicked by accident. [L0303.014]

Competitor and Backlink Research

  • Backlink export, Site Explorer (older advice, 2021-22):
    1. In Site Explorer, open Backlinks. [L1443.013]
    2. Set the link filter to Dofollow. [L1443.013]
    3. Set Group similar links to One link per domain. [L1443.013]
    4. Click Export. Choose Quick export for the first 1000 rows or Full export for all rows, with CSV format. [L1443.013]
  • SERP export: in Ahrefs, the export button sits at the top right of the SERP results table. Clicking it downloads a CSV. Choose UTF-16 for Microsoft Excel or UTF-8 for Open Office and Libre. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.012]
  • Removing duplicates in Google Sheets: install the Remove Duplicates add-on via Add-ons > Get add-ons, then open Add-ons > Remove Duplicates > Find duplicate or unique rows. Choose Duplicates (exclude first instances), select the URL column, and tick that the table has headers. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.014]
  • Removing duplicates in Excel: Excel has a built-in Find duplicate rows feature that works the same way as the Google Sheets add-on, and it is the simpler choice when available. Apple Numbers does not offer an easy remove-duplicates function. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.015]
  • Excel duplicate modes: the duplicate tool is in the menu and offers four modes: Duplicates, Duplicates + 1st occurrences, Uniques, and Uniques + 1st occurrences. Choose Duplicates (exclude first instances) to keep one copy of each URL and remove the later copies. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.020]
  • Filtering by Domain Rating: in Google Sheets, apply a filter with Data > Create a filter, then use the Domain Rating column to keep only rows with values from 10 to 75. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.016]
  • Example case: when removing duplicates from a merged Ahrefs keyword sheet of just over 100 SERP results, delete the duplicate rows outright instead of copying them to another location. In the example, this removed 19 duplicate rows. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.021]
  • Other tools: the Site Explorer backlink report can be replaced by any SEO tool, and the same workflow works with those exports. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1443.017]
  • Competitor benchmarks, Ubersuggest: see the overview metrics above. [L0758.021]
  • Moz domain analysis, free: see the free-tools section above. [L1227.004]

Email Finding and Outreach Tools

Hunter.io

  • Hunter.io finds email addresses associated with a domain by searching the web. The single Domain Search page handles one domain at a time, while Bulk Domain Search processes whole lists. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.011]
  • Hunter.io is the primary email address scraper for the skyscraper method. It finds email addresses for websites you want to contact, and it works best for small to medium-sized sites and companies, including bulk searches. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.005]
  • Free plan: Hunter.io offers a free entry-level plan that is useful for testing the tool. Its paid plans are tiered by how many emails you need to collect, so you can downgrade to the free plan in months with no outreach and upgrade for a large outreach project. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.006]
  • Pay per result: Hunter.io charges only for successful results. Searching a domain or an email address that returns no result costs nothing, and you pay per email address actually found. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.007]
  • Plan requirement: Bulk Domain Search in Hunter.io requires the Starter plan or a higher plan tier. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.013]
  • Bulk setup, procedure (older advice, 2021-22):
    1. Log into Hunter.io on a Starter plan or higher. [L0601.023]
    2. Open Bulks and choose New bulk Domain Search. [L0601.023, L0601.014]
    3. Enter a List name; it is internal and any name works. [L0601.023, L0601.014]
    4. Set Type of input to Domain names. Company names can be used as input instead when you need contacts for one specific company. [L0601.023, L0601.014]
    5. Paste the copied root domain list into the domain field, or click Select a file and upload a CSV, Excel, Numbers or TXT file. [L0601.023]
    6. Set Maximum email addresses per domain to 10. [L0601.023, L0601.015]
    7. Set Email addresses type to Generic and personal. [L0601.023, L0601.015]
    8. Leave Departments on All departments; leave Include the sources in the result off unless you need sources. [L0601.023, L0601.015]
    9. Click Upload and wait while the file is prepared. [L0601.023]
    10. Compare the estimated deliverability tiers and select one. [L0601.023]
    11. Download the result as a CSV file. [L0601.023]
  • Bulk filter settings, in detail: Maximum email addresses per domain set to 10; Email addresses type set to Generic and personal; Departments set to All departments; Include the sources in the result is optional and was left off. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.015]
  • Do not set the maximum emails per domain to a high number such as 100. Hunter.io deducts one plan credit for every successful email it finds, so large domains consume credits for addresses you will never use. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.016]
  • Do not restrict the search to one email per domain, because companies often have several addresses and the single address returned may not be the best one for outreach. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.017]
  • Speed: a bulk search of 78 domains finishes very quickly, while bulk searches with thousands of domains can take up to 10 to 15 minutes. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.018]
  • Deliverability tiers from a bulk result for 78 domain names requested (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.019]

  • Threshold: estimated deliverability of 85 percent is acceptable for outreach, while anything below 80 percent is too low to use. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.021]
  • Trade-off: choose the tier that gives the most usable domains while staying above the deliverability threshold. Higher tiers such as 95 percent give fewer addresses, while 85 percent gives more addresses. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.022]
  • CSV result: the Hunter.io CSV result lists every requested domain with all email addresses found for it, including name, position and confidence score, and it is the file used for manual selection. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.024]
  • Confidence score: it is Hunter.io's estimate of how likely an address is to be deliverable. Use it together with the address itself rather than on its own. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.026]
  • Additional CSV columns: the bulk CSV also lists Department, Position, Type, Number of sources, Pattern, Twitter handle, LinkedIn URL and Phone number. Position and Department help check whether a contact fits a content or link request, and Pattern shows the naming format the domain uses for its addresses. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.034]
  • Bulks history: Hunter.io keeps every bulk search listed under Bulks with its list name, creation time and number of domains requested, and a completed search can be deleted from that list. This makes it easy to find an earlier run for the same project or remove test runs. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.036]

Voila Norbert

  • Voila Norbit (also written Voila Norbert) is a secondary email finder for locating the address of a specific person when you know their name and the website or company they work for. It is useful when a large business returns too many generic addresses in Hunter.io and you want to contact one named editor or employee. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.008]
  • It is not required for the skyscraper method and is only needed in rare cases where you want to reach a particular individual. Hunter.io should remain the default email finder. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.009]
  • List cleaning, Mailshake's List Cleaner, powered by Voila Norbert, checks addresses before sending and sorts them into Ok, Risky (higher chance of bouncing) and Bad (almost certain to bounce). An Ok result does not guarantee deliverability. The first 500 emails are free, then the price is $10 per 5,000 credits, with one credit per email. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0946.026]
  • List cleaning is optional, but it is recommended to protect deliverability. Always remove Bad addresses, and remove Risky addresses according to how much risk you accept. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0946.027]

Mailshake

  • Mailshake is a cold email outreach tool built for sending bulk emails to people who have not contacted you before. It also manages the outreach process and provides reporting on campaign results. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.010]
  • Mailshake is a bulk and automated outreach tool that sends personalised cold emails from your connected mailbox and runs automated follow-ups. It is used for sending link-building outreach, with the sending mailbox kept separate from the plan. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0574.011]
  • It is used to send the outreach emails to the matched contacts when pursuing backlinks for skyscraper content. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.032]
  • Do not send cold outreach emails through a regular email marketing or automation platform such as Klaviyo or Active Campaign. Cold emails to unknown recipients can generate spam complaints and dead addresses, which can damage the deliverability and performance of your main email account. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1388.011]

Mailshake plans and prices

  • Basic plan: 39 USD per month when paid monthly, or 29 USD per month when paid yearly, for one user (2021 to 2022 pricing). Basic includes Gmail and G Suite accounts, unlimited Gmail aliases, open, reply and click tracking, personalisation, advanced scheduling and throttling, automated follow-ups, Lead Catcher and CRM integrations via Zapier. Mailshake also offered a 30-day full refund guarantee at the time. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0574.012]
  • Pro plan: 44 USD per month per user (2021 to 2022). It adds A/B testing, conversion tracking JavaScript, higher API limits, Salesforce and Slack integrations and 5,000 list-cleaning credits monthly. Extra reopen-cleaning credits were sold at 10 USD per 5,000 reopens. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0574.013]
  • Pro also adds filter campaigns by teammate and campaign labels, which the Basic plan does not include. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0574.026]
  • Omni plan: 75 USD per month per user at the time (2021 to 2022). On top of Pro, it adds custom tasks, social selling, a contacts view, a higher recipient limit, an onboarding phone call, a phone dialer and 10,000 list-cleaning credits each month. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0574.025, L0574.013]

Outreach Spreadsheets

Outreach Email Filtering template

  • The Outreach Email Filtering template is a Google Sheets file that can be copied into Google Drive; the same layout also works as an Excel file. It strips URLs to root domains and holds the Hunter.io output for matching. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.004]
  • Keep the outreach filtering template blank and duplicate it for each new project, so every project starts from a clean sheet. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.005]
  • Template layout (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.006]
    • Column A: labelled 'Enter List of Referring Page URLs Here'.
    • Column B: returns the Referring Page URL Root Domain.
    • Column C: labelled 'Enter List of Link URLs Here'.
    • Column D: returns the Link URL Root Domain.
    • Columns E and F: hold Email Address and Name.
    • A second tab named 'Hunter Output' receives the Hunter.io results.
  • Row alignment: columns A and C must be filled from the Ahrefs output sheets with row alignment exact, so each referring page URL stays on the same row as its matching link URL. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.007]
  • Protected columns: deleting cells in the protected columns of the template triggers a warning. Only remove rows in the URL input columns and leave the calculated columns B, D, E and F alone. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.010]
  • Root domain formula, reconstructed from a blurry screen. It has the same logic as the sheet's own version, which is already in the file. It removes the protocol, a leading www., and everything after the first slash, leaving only the root domain. (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.008]
=IFERROR(IF(ISERROR(FIND("://www.",A2)),MID(A2,FIND("://",A2)+3,FIND("/",A2&"/",FIND("://",A2)+3)-FIND("://",A2)-3),MID(A2,FIND("://",A2)+7,FIND("/",A2&"/",FIND("://",A2)+7)-FIND("://",A2)-7)),"")

[L0601.008]

  • Preparing root domains, procedure (older advice, 2021-22) [L0601.009]
    1. Duplicate the blank Outreach Email Filtering template for this project.
    2. Copy the referring page URL column from the Ahrefs output and paste it into column A under 'Enter List of Referring Page URLs Here'.
    3. Read the root domain for each referring page from column B.
    4. Copy the link URL column from the Ahrefs output and paste it into column C under 'Enter List of Link URLs Here'.
    5. Read the root domain for each link URL from column D.
    6. Remove rows you do not need from the URL columns, but do not delete anything in columns B, D, E or F.
    7. Copy the complete root domain list from column B to use as input for Hunter.io.
  • Maintaining the email filtering spreadsheet, procedure (older advice, 2021-22) [L0946.021]
    1. Paste the SEO tool output into columns A and C, making sure each row lines up exactly with its source.
    2. Do not delete anything in columns B, D, E and F.
    3. Enter the Hunter output in the second tab, named Hunter Output.
    4. When finished, remove the URLs from columns A and D. The other columns refresh automatically.
  • Moving filtered data into the batch spreadsheet, procedure (older advice, 2021-22) [L0946.019]
    1. Open the email filtering spreadsheet and filter the rows so that rows with a blank email address are hidden or removed. Rows without an email cannot be contacted.
    2. Select all data rows, excluding the heading rows, and copy them.
    3. In the batch spreadsheet, click the first cell under the Prospects URL column, not under Your Article.
    4. Use Paste special and choose values only, because formulas do not work after they are moved.

Shopify SEO Apps

Smart SEO

  • Smart SEO is a Shopify SEO app built by a company called Sherpas. It bundles several SEO functions (meta tags, alt tags, image compression, page speed, broken links, backlink reporting, structured data) into one tool. Other SEO apps exist, so using it is a choice, not a requirement. [L1337.001]
  • Smart SEO is a Shopify app used for image optimization, meta tag generation and JSON-LD settings on pages, blog posts and articles. It handles the on-page technical steps in this workflow. [L1321.002]
  • Use Smart SEO for SEO work on product pages, blog posts, and pages, including image compression. Do not run Tiny SEO alongside it; uninstall Tiny SEO once Smart SEO is installed. [L0808.048]
  • Smart SEO is the Shopify SEO app used for setting up JSON-LD structured data in the current SEO lesson. The older JSON-LD app is no longer on the Shopify app store. [L0856.070]

Plans and pricing

  • Smart SEO has a free plan and a Pro plan at $9.99 per month. The JSON-LD structured data feature is only available on the Pro plan, so start with Free if you are unsure how much SEO work you will do and upgrade later. [L1337.002]
  • Choosing Free means the JSON-LD section is not available, so no structured data gets sent to Google. You can switch to Pro at any time later. [L1337.012]
  • Pro plan approval screen: when you select the Smart SEO Pro plan at $9.99 per month, the Shopify approval screen shows a 7-day free trial and the price that applies after it. Read that screen and approve the charge only if you accept the monthly price once the trial ends. [L1337.024]

Features

  • Image compression: Smart SEO compresses images so their file size drops while the visual quality stays acceptable. Smaller images load faster, which improves page speed scores and helps users on slow connections. [L1337.003]
  • Meta tags and alt text: Smart SEO can generate meta titles and descriptions with AI, but this is optional and can be done manually. The app also lets you edit image alt texts in bulk. [L1337.004]
  • Meta tag generation, procedure: [L0368.016]
    1. Open Smart SEO > Meta Tags > Collections.
    2. Select collections, then click Edit collections (bulk editing is available).
    3. Enter the main keyword, related keywords and tone of voice (for example playful).
    4. Click Generate to write the meta title and description. Try again gives alternatives.
  • Predictive page loading and broken links: Smart SEO includes predictive page loading, which preloads pages to make the site load faster. It also detects and fixes broken internal links, which becomes more important as the store grows over months and years. [L1337.006]
  • Backlink report: Smart SEO produces a backlink report that shows which sites link to your store. This becomes useful once content grows and more external sites link to you. [L1337.007]
  • Left navigation: after Smart SEO is integrated, its left navigation lists separate sections for meta tags, alt tags, image optimization, page speed, broken links and more. Each option has built-in video tutorials inside the app, so new users can learn each function there. [L1337.023]

Installation, procedure

  1. Open the Smart SEO listing in the Shopify App Store using the link provided. [L1337.009]
  2. If you have several Shopify accounts, make sure you are signed into the correct one. [L1337.009]
  3. Click Install. [L1337.009]
  4. Review the access the app requests and approve it, since it must be able to work on your store. [L1337.009]
  5. On the plan page, choose Free or Pro. [L1337.009]
  6. If you choose Pro, confirm the 7-day free trial and the price shown after the trial, then approve the charge. [L1337.009, L1337.024]
  7. Open the app and click Set up. [L1337.009]
  8. If the app status says Smart SEO is not fully integrated, click Integrate now. [L1337.009, L1337.010]
  9. Confirm the status shows the app as integrated in your store. [L1337.009]
  • After activation the app shows Current plan (for example Pro) and App status. If App status says Smart SEO is not fully integrated, the app is not yet installed correctly and you must click Integrate now. [L1337.010]

Schema, Noindex and Page Audit

  • Schema markup:
    • If a Shopify store already runs a working schema markup app, no action is needed. If it does not, install a schema app rather than relying on manual code or a developer. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1349.015]
    • Hiring a developer to add schema markup creates two problems: the store owner cannot easily verify that the work was done correctly, and every future markup change requires going back to that developer. A schema app avoids both because its developers update the markup automatically. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1349.016]
  • Noindex on Shopify: the recommended way to add noindex to selected pages is a low-cost Shopify app rather than hand-editing theme.liquid. Manual code edits carry a risk: a small error can noindex the entire domain, causing search traffic to disappear overnight and requiring a long re-indexing process. (older advice, 2021-22) [L1372.008]
