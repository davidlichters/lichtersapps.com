5.12 Email, SMS and customer lists

Choosing the Email and SMS Platform

  • Klaviyo is the email platform most sources in this chapter use. It has the deepest Shopify integration, handles automation, segmentation, SMS, and templates, and reports revenue from email [L0274.030, L0604.010, L0605.007, L0607.001, L0854.077, L1076.010].
  • Install Klaviyo before the store receives traffic so that automated sequences are running from the start [L0040.002].
  • Klaviyo covers all email: campaigns, abandoned cart emails, and messages to vendors [L0337.030].
  • Klaviyo sends abandoned cart emails and promotions directly, without pushing every contact into Shopify first [L0359.055, L0532.012].
  • Klaviyo is the platform for lead flows and campaigns, and its dashboard shows the revenue those emails generate, so you can check whether a lead flow works [L1076.010].
  • Klaviyo is used for email and SMS, including sign-up forms, flows, campaigns, and segments [L0604.010, L0605.007, L1092.092, L1114.035].
  • Klaviyo stores the addresses captured by exit-intent overlays and shows how many visitors signed up [L0963.055].
  • Klaviyo is a strong choice for stores of this size. It is used instead of a heavy CRM [L0468.076].
  • Klaviyo is the preferred email platform for Shopify because of its deep integration. Omnisend was not evaluated because Klaviyo met all needs (older advice, 2022) [L0665.052].
  • Klaviyo is the recommended email tool, and it supports exit-intent overlays and embeddable opt-in forms for sign-up pages (older advice, 2022) [L1281.090].
  • Klaviyo is recommended for abandoned cart flows and other automated messages [L1251.040].

Shopify Email, Shopify Messaging, and Omnisend

  • Shopify Email is a free, native email tool. It includes 10,000 emails per month free, then charges fractions of a penny per email. It lacks Klaviyo's template library but integrates natively and needs no extra app [L0274.031].
  • Shopify Email is included with a Shopify account, sends up to 10,000 emails a month at no extra cost, and is recommended over Klaviyo for cost reasons. Klaviyo is more advanced but becomes expensive as the list grows (2024 source) [L1285.041].
  • Shopify Email covers most of what Klaviyo does for new stores, and the first 10,000 emails every month are free. It is not necessarily better than Klaviyo, and switching is not required (2023 source) [L1114.036].
  • Shopify Email is free for up to 10,000 emails per month and can replace Klaviyo for most stores. Klaviyo remains a strong option, but Shopify Email now covers most of its functions at no cost (2023 source) [L1283.085].
  • Shopify Email is a free email marketing app built by Shopify. It sends automated follow-up emails and promotional campaigns to the store's list. It does not handle day-to-day one-to-one emails, which go through the regular mailbox [L1307.001].
  • Shopify Email includes Abandoned Checkout and Discount Reminder automation templates and a built-in AI tool that improves and customizes template messaging [L0156.003].
  • Shopify Messaging is a free app from Shopify for email and SMS, used mainly for marketing and follow-up messages [L0147.001].
  • Shopify Messaging is the primary app for abandoned checkout automation, accessed through Shopify Admin. Shopify Flow provides a visual editor to modify and customize the workflow [L0149.003].
  • The Shopify Messaging dashboard shows emails sent, open rate, conversion rate, and revenue, with a date range selector for reporting [L0147.004].
  • Omnisend Email & SMS is an email and SMS platform that is installed at a later stage, alongside other tools such as Klaviyo, not at the foundation stage [L1327.040].

When to Keep or Switch Email Tools

Sources disagree on whether Shopify Email should replace Klaviyo. Use the rule that fits your stage.

  • New store, no existing list: Shopify Email can be tested from the first day as a comparison (older advice, 2020). It can replace Klaviyo for most stores at no cost (2023) [L0967.055, L1283.085].
  • Automation depth: At the time of the call, Klaviyo still offered deeper email automation than Shopify's native tool. Switch to Shopify Email only if it matches Klaviyo's automation depth [L0941.046].
  • Large or growing list: Shopify Email is recommended for cost. Klaviyo is more advanced but becomes expensive as the list grows (2024) [L1285.041].
  • Existing Klaviyo user: There is no reason to switch to another email tool [L1114.035]. Switching large email flows is costly and results are uncertain, so keep Klaviyo (older advice, 2020) [L0967.055].
  • Any existing working tool: If an existing email or opt-in tool already works and you are comfortable with it, there is no reason to switch. Migration usually creates a lot of unnecessary work. Switching makes sense for a new business or when you are looking for something new (older advice, 2021-22) [L0608.002].
  • Other alternatives: Drip is a comparable alternative to Klaviyo, with a different interface for building flows and moving contacts between lists (older advice, 2021-22) [L0608.003].
  • Deciding factor: Email automation platforms such as Klaviyo and Drip do similar things with similar Shopify integration depth. The main deciding factor is usability, especially how flows are built and how contacts move between lists. A faster tool lets you produce more automations (older advice, 2021-22) [L0608.004].
  • Lock-in: Only the email automation platform is costly to change later. Flows and lists cannot be imported into another platform and must be rebuilt by hand (older advice, 2021-22) [L0608.013].
  • Klaviyo at the time of the source call: Klaviyo was still the platform used for welcome, upsell, and other flows, with triggers set per product or event [L0970.082].
  • Do not pay twice: Do not pay for both Klaviyo and Privy. Switch your opt-in forms to Klaviyo instead (older advice, 2020) [L1277.067].

Installing the Tools

Klaviyo for Shopify

  1. In Shopify, open Apps, then Apps and sales channels, then open the Shopify App Store [L0607.003].
  2. Search for Klaviyo, click Install, then click Install apps [L0607.003].
  3. Register with the same email address used for the Shopify account. The Klaviyo dashboard opens [L0607.003].
  4. Connect Klaviyo to Shopify if it is not connected yet [L0607.003].
  5. Turn on website tracking and enable it for the store [L0607.003].
  6. Under import, upload contacts (email contacts recommended) and newsletter subscribers, or skip this if none exist yet [L0607.003].
  7. Open the brand library and add the logo and brand colors. Klaviyo applies them to all email templates [L0607.003].
  8. Invite team members, or skip this step [L0607.003].

Shopify Email

  1. Log in to the Shopify admin panel of the store [L1307.006].
  2. Click Apps in the left navigation [L1307.006].
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the installed apps list and click All recommended apps [L1307.006].
  4. Under Made by Shopify, find Shopify email [L1307.006].
  5. If it is not listed, click Find more apps in the Shopify app store, search for "email", and select Shopify email [L1307.006].
  6. Click Install, then click Install app to grant the requested permissions [L1307.006].
  7. Confirm that "Email" now appears under Apps [L1307.006].
  • The Shopify Email app dashboard has Templates and Settings sections. Automations and campaigns are also created from this app, after its basic configuration is complete [L1307.007].
  • Open Apps in the Shopify admin and click the Email app. Click Settings to reach the sender email, double opt-in, open tracking, and template branding options. Template branding is at the bottom of the page [L1307.015].
  • The first email may ask the recipient to display images from the sender. After clicking this, images show in that email, and future emails display images by default [L1094.061].
  • Besides a browser color picker, look up the brand's six-digit hex color code in the store's own Shopify account settings. Paste that hex value into the Template Branding color field, such as the button fill, so emails match the site [L1317.015].

Other Store Apps Installed Alongside Email

  • Klaviyo and SMSBump are both installed. Klaviyo handles email automation and SMSBump handles SMS [L0693.028, L1360.013, L1432.010].
  • An assistant installed and implemented Klaviyo and SMS Bump in the store for the speaker [L1432.009, L1432.010].
  • Clavio and SMS Bump are recommended for installation. Tell the team which apps to install and how to set them up [L1358.012].

Abandoned Checkout and Cart Flows

  • Email and SMS apps are needed to win back visitors who added to cart or started checkout without buying. This improves customer retention [L0635.004].
  • Clavio's flows matter most when running paid or TikTok traffic. They send reminder emails every few hours to visitors who added to cart but did not buy [L0634.001].
  • Clavio can handle email remarketing and SMS for abandoned carts once the store has traffic. The presenter suggested a second phone number for SMS signups, using the text-free app, to keep personal numbers private [L0820.037].
  • Klaviyo is installed to send automated emails, including for bounced visits and abandoned carts [L0532.012].
  • Klaviyo builds the abandoned checkout flow, the email templates, and the A/B tests. Use Preview & test to send test emails, and Manage template to edit the shared layout. Save before going live [L0567.032].
  • ChatGPT drafts the abandoned cart copy from a brand primer. Keep the working prompts in one document and update them when a version performs better [L0567.035].

Checking Abandoned Cart Flows on Single-Page Checkout (2023 source)

  1. Opt in with a test account on the checkout form [L1114.038].
  2. Place a test order [L1114.038].
  3. Confirm the abandoned cart emails and Zapier triggers both arrive [L1114.038].

Flow Settings Before Going Live

  • The Klaviyo flow Settings panel lists Smart Sending, UTM Tracking, and Additional Filters as separate toggles. Check each one before the flow goes live [L0567.050].
  • Additional Filters was switched off in the lesson's flow. Add filters only when a specific audience rule is needed [L0567.050].

Welcome, Lead, and Post-Purchase Flows

  • Klaviyo flows are automations that fire after a customer action, such as an order or a delivery [L0417.015].
  • Klaviyo flows are used for many automatic messages, not only review requests. Common examples are birthday emails and shipping confirmations, all triggered by a customer action [L0417.027].
  • Set the flow trigger in Klaviyo to "When someone subscribes to" the sign-up pop-up's list. In the example, the pop-up was an OptiMonk offer of 10% and 15%, so the flow starts at signup [L1470.003].
  • Klaviyo can automate quote follow-up emails with flows and filters for separate brands. Building these requires a lot of setup for each brand's templates and images. Automate follow-ups only after the manual template system works, so the templates are already tested (older advice, 2021-22) [L1396.036].
  • Feedback Genius is an autoresponder that integrates with the Amazon account. It sends email sequences 5, 6, and 10+ days after purchase. It enables upselling, distributing e-book links, and requesting reviews without manual emails. At about $19 per month, it becomes essential once you reach 30-50 sales per day, as manual follow-up becomes impractical [L0228.029].

Sign-Up Forms, Pop-Ups, and Lead Capture

Choosing a Pop-Up Tool

  • Klaviyo is used for sign-up forms, including the exit-intent pop-up that collects phone numbers [L0363.030].
  • Klaviyo now includes its own sign-up forms and exit-intent pop-ups, so one tool can handle email capture and the flows. This replaces older standalone pop-up apps such as Privy [L1204.022].
  • Klaviyo's built-in sign-up form builder creates the welcome pop-up [L0605.007].
  • Klaviyo's embed code connects forms to a Shopify page [L1114.035].
  • Klaviyo handles exit-intent overlays in this setup, replacing Privy, which was dropped because it was no longer needed (older advice, 2021). Exit-intent overlays can be hidden on specific pages where discounts are restricted [L0247.078].
  • Klaviyo now covers both email marketing and coupon or pop-up offers for newer stores. Privy is no longer needed alongside Klaviyo, and an existing Privy paid plan can be dropped once Klaviyo is in use (older advice, 2021) [L1090.060].
  • Klaviyo now handles the email functions previously done by Privy, while After Ship remains part of the setup (older advice, 2021) [L0968.047].
  • Privy is a Shopify app used to capture emails from site visitors with pop-ups and offers. The brand reported an email engagement rate of about 20% from this approach [L0790.020].
  • Optimonk is a pop-up builder for multi-step pop-ups with email and SMS signups. It is chosen over Klaviyo pop-ups for stronger visual design and faster load speed [L0418.015].
  • OptinMonster builds content opt-ins: sidebar forms, inline forms inside blog posts or collection pages, and exit-intent pop-ups. The same tool can place a related download in the middle of an article [L0571.028].
  • Email opt-in apps such as OptinMonster and email campaign tools such as Drip can be turned on before a store drives paid traffic. This captures visitors who are not ready to buy (older advice, 2021-22) [L1456.028].

Building an Optimonk Campaign

Procedure:

  1. Ask the client to install Optimonk from the Shopify app store on their store [L0418.030].
  2. Have the client send you an Optimonk team invite, so you do not need Shopify admin access [L0418.030].
  3. Open app.optimonk.com with the invite and confirm you can see the client's account [L0418.030].
  4. Create a new campaign from scratch, select the relevant domain, and click Next to open the blank canvas [L0418.030].
  • The paintbrush tool copies the style of one element, such as a button, onto another. Use it to make a new button match an existing one, then change the label or color only where needed [L0418.031].
  • Known issue: Optimonk's hover effect on some buttons does not stick after the cursor moves away. After styling, move the cursor in and out of every button on desktop and mobile to confirm that the hover state behaves as intended [L0418.029].

Connecting Optimonk to Klaviyo

Note: the source spells the tool "Optimunk" in this integration procedure. Other units in this chapter name it Optimonk [L1138.006, L0418.015].

  1. In Klaviyo, click Settings in the left menu and open the API section [L1138.006].
  2. Copy the public API key and enter it in Optimunk under Integrations > Add new integration > Klaviyo [L1138.006].
  3. Back in Klaviyo, click Create Private API Key [L1138.006].
  4. Name the key Optimunk and set Select Access Level to Full Access. The options are Custom Key, Read-Only Key, and Full Access. Full Access gives access to every API scope [L1138.006].
  5. Click Create and copy the private key into Optimunk [L1138.006].
  6. Click Setup integration in Optimunk and continue [L1138.006].
  7. In Optimunk, open the Klaviyo integration, click Select a list, and choose the list you just created in Klaviyo. Then continue to field mapping [L1138.010].

Facebook Lead Ads

Setup in Facebook Creator Studio:

  • Navigate to Creator Studio → Tools → Lead Ads Forms. Create a new form, upload a brand image, add a headline, and set fields (Name and Email at minimum). Add a privacy statement, then integrate with Klaviyo via Integrations → Facebook integration [L0308.004].

Connecting the lead form to Klaviyo:

  1. Go to the Klaviyo dashboard [L0308.006].
  2. Click Brand (bottom left) → Integrations [L0308.006].
  3. Add the Facebook integration and log in [L0308.006].
  4. Attach the lead form created in Facebook Creator Studio [L0308.006].
  5. Anyone who submits the form automatically enters a custom Klaviyo list [L0308.006].
  6. Create lead generation campaigns targeting that list [L0308.006].

List Hygiene and Deliverability

Cleaning the List

  • Upload the email list as a CSV to a verification website. It shows counts of verified, spam trap, complainer, bot, and undeliverable addresses. Remove the bad ones. Do this about every six months (2024 source) [L0836.054].
  • Third-party validators check email lists for invalid and unreachable addresses before import. They are used during list cleaning, and the cleaned list is what gets imported into the new provider [L0633.017].

Warming Up the Domain

  • Warmup Inbox (warmupinbox.com) is a domain warm-up tool. It connects to a sending inbox, sends emails automatically, simulates opens, replies, and clicks, and moves emails out of spam. Run it continuously for a couple of months alongside the provider's own warm-up [L0633.015].
  • Warm-up tools that show inbox versus spam placement per mailbox provider give a second opinion on deliverability. This matters because the email platform's own metrics are not always fully accurate [L0633.016].

Platform Architecture

  • Each email marketing job is best handled by a separate tool. An email automation platform sends and segments. An opt-in form tool collects addresses through pop-ups and inline forms. A contest tool grows the list. A quiz or survey tool collects addresses through questions (older advice, 2021-22) [L0608.013].
  • Quiz and survey tools differ in price. When results are otherwise comparable, cost decides [L0608.011].
  • Automate only after the manual process works. Build flows once templates are tested [L1396.036].
