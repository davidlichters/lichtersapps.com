5.21 Affiliates, outreach and promotion channels

Refersion: what it does

  • Refersion is affiliate management software for e-commerce stores. You set it up on your site, place an affiliate signup link in your website footer, upload banner ad creatives for affiliates to download, and track commissions and sales [L0202.006].
  • Refersion is recommended for managing affiliate programs. It organizes banner creatives, tracks affiliate performance, handles payments, and gives partners a login portal where they access promotional assets and generate tracking links [L0288.002].
  • Affiliate software such as Refersion generates unique affiliate links, tracks cookies, and provides dashboards showing affiliate sales, click-through rates, and pending payouts. It integrates with Shopify [L0213.011].
  • Refersion is a Shopify app for tracking affiliate programs, managing partnerships, commissions, and approvals [L0270.029].
  • Refersion is a Shopify affiliate and referral app used to pay partners or customers commissions for sales they generate [L0430.002].
  • Refersion is an affiliate tool that pays creators or partners for sales they generate. It suits affiliate-style programs in which payment depends on results [L0790.019].
  • Refersion is an affiliate program platform used to manage affiliate partners and track their sales [L0970.087].
  • Refersion is a simple tool for running a store affiliate program [L1092.091].
  • Refersion is a Shopify app used to create an affiliate program. Niche blogs can join for free, link to the store to earn affiliate traffic, and create backlinks [L0666.012].
  • Refersion is preferred over loyalty-program apps for referral programs and affiliate tracking [L0470.088].

Refersion: pricing

  • Refersion requires a paid subscription, but its costs are easily recouped through affiliate-generated sales [L0213.011].
  • Refersion is a paid app, not a free one [L0270.029].
  • Niche blogs that join an affiliate program through Refersion do so for free [L0666.012].

Refersion: when to add it (sources disagree)

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Refersion is the recommended software. Place the affiliate signup link in the website footer and upload banners so affiliates can use them [L0288.002, L0202.006].
  • Older advice (Source A, 2022): Refersion is the affiliate platform recommended for stores large enough to run an affiliate program [L1281.092]. A footer link alone adds no sales [L1113.037].
  • Source A, 2023: Refersion is the affiliate program app the lesson recommends for managing affiliates, but it is not recommended during the first stage of a store [L0934.053]. Affiliate programs are not advisable in the early phase of a new store, and paid placements on relevant sites usually produce faster results [L0934.167].
  • How to apply: The 2021-22 sources describe how to set up the program. The 2022 and 2023 sources describe when a store is ready for one. Under the 2023 advice, do not start with an affiliate program in a new store's early phase. The sources give no numeric sales threshold [L0934.053, L0934.167, L1281.092, L1113.037].

Refersion: banners and affiliate links

  • In Refersion, go to Dashboard > Manage > Creatives. This tab shows all banner ads available to your affiliate partners. Partners click a banner to copy an embed code and paste it into their website's HTML to load the banner automatically [L0288.003].

Procedure: upload a creative to Refersion

  1. Go to Dashboard.
  2. Click Manage.
  3. Click Creatives.
  4. Click the button to create a new creative.
  5. Upload a banner image file.
  6. Enter the landing page URL where the banner links.
  7. Add a description.
  8. Assign a category if desired.
  9. Save.

Refersion automatically appends each partner's unique affiliate code to the end of your landing page URL [L0288.009].

  • Refersion personalizes the landing page URL for every affiliate partner by adding their unique affiliate code to the end. You enter the URL once, and the system handles the personalization [L0288.010].

Other referral and creator-payment tools

  • Referral Candy creates affiliate links for products in a Shopify store, tracks the sales generated by each creator, and lets you set per-order or percentage payouts and tiers [L1379.041].

Affiliate-site tools

  • Hosting and domain: Namecheap is used to buy the domain and first-year hosting for the affiliate site. At the time of the lesson, domain registration cost 10 dollars and first-year hosting cost 12 dollars [L0514.029].
  • Platform: Use WordPress with specific themes and plugins for affiliate sites. Shopify is built for taking payments in a store, so it is a poor choice when your visitors are sent to another merchant [L0514.028].
  • Amazon affiliate store: Shopper Press is a WordPress e-commerce theme that costs around 70 dollars and can import 20,000 or more Amazon products automatically, with prices, images, and descriptions. It is used to build a branded Amazon affiliate store that sits alongside a dropship store [L0923.021].
  • Prerequisites: Building an Amazon Associates WordPress store with an imported product catalog requires working knowledge of WordPress, WordPress themes, and affiliate marketing. Without these basics the store is hard to set up and maintain, so wait until you have them before building one [L0923.056].
  • Banner advertising (older advice, 2019): BuySellAds offers banner placements on different websites within many niches. It can be used to buy visibility on niche sites when paid search and social ads are limited [L0467.050].
  • Free consultation as lead bait: Skype is the video-call tool named for a short free consultation, such as 10 minutes on a specific topic. Limit the slots, because each lead costs you personal time [L0514.040].

Email tools for affiliate sites

  • Entreport: A powerful autoresponder with membership features and a high price. It suits membership sites, but it is more than a pure affiliate site needs [L0514.025].
  • Mailchimp: Avoid it for affiliate marketing. Its price is similar to GetResponse, but it bans accounts that send affiliate links in their emails [L0514.026].
  • Aweber: Be careful. On about half of accounts it requires a mailing history before you can disable confirmed opt-in. A new account would ask every subscriber to confirm, which adds friction [L0514.027].

Finding and verifying partner contacts

  • hunter.io: Finds email addresses that belong to a website. It helps when a site has no usable contact email for outreach. Use it only when the site itself does not offer an address you can use [L0332.018].
  • ZoomInfo: A business-information website that can reveal company contact details when a brand's site cannot be found. Search the brand name plus "Zoominfo" written as one word. The free view of contact details can be used without signing up, and the paid subscription is not required [L0940.029].
  • Sales CRM tools: Tools that have scraped the internet for contact details can suggest possible email addresses for influencers and public figures. Use them as a supplement to manual searches, not as the only source [L0790.021].
  • D7 Lead Finder: Collects a list of businesses in a chosen city or country, such as dentists, by pulling listings from Google and business pages. It also shows whether a business is running Facebook ads, and it exports the list to an Excel sheet after about three minutes of processing [L0440.020].

Building a partner target list

Dream 100 worksheet

  • To get an editable copy of the Dream 100 worksheet, open the template and choose File > Make a copy to save it to your Google Drive. For offline work, choose Download and pick Excel, which opens in Microsoft Excel or Apple Numbers [L0528.017].
  • Use these three sheets:
Sheet Columns
Sheet 1, master list Category, Name, URL, Email, Phone number, Guest posts and social shares, Paid ads, Notes, Date contacted
Sheet 2, ad trial Name, URL, Email, Ad types, Date trial requested, Date trial accepted, CPC
Sheet 3, guest posts Name, URL, Email, Date contacted, Notes
  • The master list is the starting point. The guest post and paid ad columns are filled in during research. Use the notes column for every response, follow-up date, and status [L0528.018].

AI-generated drafts

  • ChatGPT, or another AI tool, can generate a first draft of partner names, categories, URLs, and emails from a prompt. Its output often has broken links, wrong or missing emails, and fewer rows than requested, so it only seeds the sheet and does not replace research [L0528.019].
  • The free version of ChatGPT is enough to run a partner-list prompt that returns names, categories, URLs, and emails in a spreadsheet-ready format. Use the same prompt in any other AI tool you already have, but expect incomplete and unverified output [L0528.046].

Partner-list prompt

I run [store URL]. I'm building a target list of 100 people and companies who could send their followers to my online shop. Candidates include celebrities, websites, blogs, YouTube creators, course sellers, events, Instagram accounts, podcast hosts, and associations whose audiences are interested in buying [niche products]. Find these contacts and enter them in a spreadsheet with this layout: Column A holds the category, chosen from the types above such as celebrity, website, blog, YouTuber, course creator, event, Instagram account, podcast, or association. Column B holds the name of the person or business. Column C holds their website URL, when you can locate one. Column D holds their email address, when you can locate one. If no email turns up, leave the cell empty, and never make up emails or URLs. Aim for 100 rows of people and/or companies. If you can't reach 100, give back as many genuine rows as you can and state the exact number returned.

[L0528.020]

Procedure: run the partner-list prompt

  1. Replace [store URL] in the prompt with your store's URL.
  2. Replace [niche products] with the products you sell in your niche.
  3. Send the prompt to the AI tool. If it returns two responses, compare them and keep the one with more usable rows.
  4. Copy the chosen rows into the master list sheet.
  5. If the result has fewer than 100 rows, ask the AI to redo the list and include data for 100 people or entities, then compare the counts again.
  6. Keep a partial list as a starting point and fill the gaps through manual research.

[L0528.021]

  • In Google Sheets, use View > Freeze > Up to row 3 so the header rows stay visible while you scroll down the list [L0528.022].

Influencer and creator partnerships

Karo

  • Karo (getkaro.com, Shopify app "Carro") is a collaborative commerce network. Its influencer side finds people in your own customer base and email list who have an audience. Its basic functions can be used for free. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0869.001]
  • Brand partnership features were free at the time of recording, but some elements may be paid. Check the current pricing before using them. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0869.012]
  • Karo is listed in the Shopify App Store as "Carro" (apps.shopify.com/carro). Its website appeared on screen as getcarro.com, although the narrator called it getkaro.com. Check the address in the app listing before signing up. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0869.016]
  • Karo was built mainly for Shopify but also works with a few other e-commerce platforms. Its described setup path, including connecting customers and email lists, assumes a Shopify account. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0869.017]
  • On the Karo homepage, the main buttons are "Get Started" and "Learn More," and the Shopify listing button reads "Install On Shopify." In the brand partnership product view, the button "Add To My Store" changes to "Added To My Store" once the product is on your store. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0869.019]

Vetting creators

  • Tracker is an influencer-marketing platform that costs about $80,000, which is far too expensive for most brands. Many successful brands do without it and track influencer work in spreadsheets [L0790.022].
  • TikTok Business Center lets verified business accounts send direct proposals to creators. The speaker reported that around 95 percent of creators responded to such proposals [L1379.042].
  • TikTok Creator Tools analytics show a creator's views and follower data. Asking a creator to share a screenshot of this analytics page verifies their performance [L1379.043].
  • Social Blade is a third-party site that shows public statistics for social accounts and can be used as a rough cross-check of a creator's growth. Its accuracy was not confirmed, so treat it as a supporting check and verify key numbers with the creator's own analytics [L1379.056].

Giveaways and list growth

  • Gleam and Viral Sweep are contest tools used to grow an email list. They integrate contests closely with social media and email lists, and only one of the two is needed. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L0608.009]
  • WooRise is an instant giveaway tool for building the giveaway funnel. Any other instant giveaway tool can serve the same function [L0797.005].
  • Klaviyo holds the giveaway entrant lists, tracks orders and revenue with a 30-day attribution window, and stores the segments used to measure the funnel [L0797.014].
  • ManyChat automatically sends a direct message to every account that comments a set keyword on a post. It is used to deliver links, such as downloads, to commenters without manual replies [L1457.018].

Social channels

Posting and presence

  • Tailwind schedules social media posts to Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook in advance. A team member scheduled posts for the coming couple of weeks [L0337.036].
  • A free Facebook business page is used as a basic social presence for the store. It is the free version only, with no paid ads required at this stage [L0437.002].
  • A LinkedIn profile only makes sense if the target audience is active there, which is typically the case for B2B businesses. Consumer-focused stores can skip it [L0437.003].
  • Facebook offers an option to sell directly on the platform, but the lesson recommended sending traffic to your Shopify store instead, as of early 2023 [L0934.175].

Facebook groups

  • Facebook groups have a magnifying-glass search icon. Before posting a question, search the group for the same question, since members often have already asked it and received an answer. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1498.022]
  • Facebook groups cannot be set to read-only. Require admin approval for new posts to stop new questions, and send members to topic-based community channels instead [L0934.176].

Growth caveats

  • Some free-trial social-media growth campaigns can produce likes and followers cheaply, but these numbers rarely turn into sales. Focus on clicks and purchases, not followers [L0934.177].

Content research

  • vidIQ is a Chrome extension that adds data to YouTube pages, including trending videos and views per hour. It is used to find topics that are working right now before making a video [L0487.062].
  • Google Sheets can track content ideas: one column for the content idea and the next for the link to the source of inspiration. This is enough to track content research from forums and communities [L0472.041].

Live shopping and shoppable video

Livecom

  • Livecom 2.0 is a video player that lets marketers add shoppable video (video that can be bought from directly) to landing pages and product pages without waiting for the development team. Its vendor says it avoids the usual site speed loss from embedding video [L0951.006].
  • Livecom live streaming can broadcast one stream at the same time to TikTok Shop, Instagram Live, YouTube, Twitch, X, and any other platform that accepts RTMP streaming. Viewers can then be sent to the website to shop from the stream [L0951.007].
  • The Tap Cart integration lets a Shopify store host live streams inside the Tap Cart app and sends a push notification to customers when the stream starts. This makes the live stream available on the Shopify site, inside the Tap Cart app, and on the connected social channels [L0951.008].

LyveCom story video

  • LyveCom hosts the MP4 files itself instead of Shopify, and the story video is embedded with a script rather than a YouTube or Vimeo iframe. Because of this, the store is not slowed down, and shoppers can add variants to the cart and check out through the store's native Shopify checkout. The platform also works with any Shopify theme that accepts a custom HTML or Liquid snippet [L0959.001].

Procedure: set up a LyveCom story and embed it

  1. Open the LyveCom dashboard.
  2. Create a new story campaign.
  3. Name the story. The name appears as the overlay on the video (example: Collection Release).
  4. Click Next.
  5. Select the product to be tagged in the video.
  6. Click Finish.
  7. Upload the MP4 file to the story (no file size limit; keep the video short because of attention spans).
  8. Click Publish story.
  9. Choose the embed method: Floating Widget or Embed.
  10. For the widget, open the Widget tab, click Copy the code, and paste the snippet on any page of the website.

[L0959.002]

  • There is no file size limit for the uploaded MP4, but short videos are recommended to keep a social, app-like experience. Viewers tend to have short attention spans on product videos [L0959.003].
