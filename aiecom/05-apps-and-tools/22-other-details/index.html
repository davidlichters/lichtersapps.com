5.22 Other details

Learning and course tools

  • Video Speed is a free Google Chrome extension that adjusts playback speed of any video, faster or slower. Install it to watch instructional content at your preferred pace (older advice, 2019) [L0243.043].
  • Kajabi can show a student a list of videos to watch, mark each video as watched, and show mentors exactly what each student has completed. It is useful for tracking progress in a course or mentoring program [L1017.006].

Setup and official tool sources

  • Official websites for the stack: zapier.com for Zapier, trello.com for Trello, and Google Sheets through Google Drive. Slack and Shopify are used through their own sites and apps [L0437.010].
  • A basic laptop is enough to run an online store, including a Chromebook, since heavy software is not needed and large product spreadsheets can be handled in Google Sheets online (older advice, 2022) [L1092.094].
  • The Shopify App Store offers apps to add features to a store. A store credit (for example $50) can be spent there when offered [L1304.019].

Shopify apps and store features

  • Reviews and pop-up apps are updated in the course. The ecommerce course module on store setup was rebuilt for current Shopify interfaces (older advice, 2022) [L0969.092].
  • Shopify apps can lock parts of a store behind customer accounts, for example a dealer section with different prices and products. Shopify is not the right platform for a content membership site (older advice, 2018) [L1274.070].

B2B and trade customers

  • Shopify plug-ins can display hidden trade-only products and prices that only logged-in, approved trade customers can see. Use them to offer business buyers better deals without showing those prices to the public (older advice, 2018) [L1109.038].
  • B2B request-a-quote setup, in four steps (2023) [L1283.071]:
    1. Add a "request a quote" link in the header menu.
    2. Create a quote page with logos of past business customers.
    3. Add a contact form asking for name, phone, email, items of interest and quantities per item.
    4. Add a homepage section with the same business logos near the bottom of the page.

Offer and customer planning

  • The CURVE Offer Builder is a simple spreadsheet that records all nine offer elements in one place: marketing messages, product, price, shipping, USP package, risk reversal, customer service, trust factors, and bonuses. It also has a separate marketing-message section, which is completed after the offer structure is set (older advice, 2021-22) [L0411.040].

  • The avatar spreadsheet has space for three customer avatars, so you can keep distinct buyer profiles side by side. This allows separate profiles for, for example, a female and a male customer group, or for B2B and B2C buyers (older advice, 2021-22) [L0872.015].

Product map

  • Keep the product map as a spreadsheet, not a PDF or a separate document per product. A PDF needs a new file for every product and is harder to edit, while a spreadsheet with one tab per product keeps all products in one file (older advice, 2021-22) [L1160.018].

Email, orders and tracking apps

  • A wishlist or gift registry app should cost at most about $15 per month. Paying $99 per month for a registry app is excessive (older advice, 2021) [L0247.079].
  • Klaviyo (email marketing) and AfterShip (order tracking) both have setup documentation and responsive support teams. Follow their step-by-step setup guides before contacting support (older advice, 2019) [L0663.028].

Booking tools for service businesses

  • Fresha is a free booking app for service businesses such as barbershops and salons. It lets clients choose a service, pick a barber and book an appointment online, and it can be connected to Google [L0440.024].

Troubleshooting apps and store errors

  • When an SEO or store app shows errors that cannot be fixed directly, in four steps (older advice, 2022) [L0429.086]:
    1. Copy the exact error message.
    2. Search for that message together with the platform name, for example Shopify, including forums and the Shopify community.
    3. Look for fixes documented by users who had the same error.
    4. If no fix turns up, have a specialist handle it.
  • Troubleshooting a technical problem in a Shopify app or store setup, in three steps (older advice, 2021) [L0469.115]:
    1. Search Google for the exact product name or the exact error message.
    2. Search Reddit for the same problem, because people who cannot get help from official support often post there.
    3. Contact general support only if these searches do not solve the issue. Common problems are usually documented by others.

Backups

  • Rewind Backups is used to back up store data and code. Backups are not strictly necessary but are recommended (2024) [L1285.040].

Tools that need verification

  • StoreTasker, Niche Verification and Stubgroup were named as tools in the October 2024 coaching call, but the source gives no usage details for them. Check each one's current function before relying on it (2024) [L1105.012].

Product claims and compliance

  • Laboratory testing or expert review can verify product claims before they are advertised. This protects the claim from being challenged and lets it be stated with exact figures. Unverified claims can cause an ad to be refused by reputable media [L1261.050].

Work setup and cost of living

  • Replace car ownership with ride-hailing (Uber) and monthly Airbnb stays. This avoids car costs such as maintenance, smog checks and washing, and is a practical way to cut responsibilities while living abroad [L1515.023].
