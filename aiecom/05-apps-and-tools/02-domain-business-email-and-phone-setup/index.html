5.2 Domain, business email and phone setup

Domain: Choosing and Buying the Domain

  • Use a ChatGPT thinking model with deep research enabled to generate brand names. Deep research browses the live internet and checks which domains are actually free. Without it, most suggested.com domains turn out to be taken. [L0989.006]
  • Namecheap is the domain registrar used in this approach. It handles the purchase, domain privacy and DNS records. Any other domain provider can be used instead. [L0989.020]
  • Older advice (2019): buy the domain through Shopify if you want everything linked from the start. It costs a few dollars more than an outside registrar such as Namecheap, which is cheaper. [L0357.054, older 2019]
  • If the domain still does not link after the DNS records are updated, take a screenshot of the DNS records together with the exact error message. Paste both into a ChatGPT thinking model to get troubleshooting help. [L0989.027]

Google Workspace: Free Trial, Billing and Seats

  • Google Workspace starts with a free trial of at least 14 days. [L0119.005, L1145.005]
  • Billing can be added during the trial or after it. No charge is made before the trial ends, and Google sends a notice before the trial expires, so billing can be left until then. [L0566.015]
  • Billing begins 14 days after signup on both the annual and flexible plans. The annual plan is still billed monthly, so the monthly charge stays at $7 and is not paid up front. Users can be added or removed as needed. [L1196.005, L1197.006]
  • If billing is never activated, the mailbox stops receiving mail when the trial ends, and existing messages cannot be retrieved afterward. [L1145.005]
  • Billing setup asks for the billing address and a credit card on file. [L1197.008, L1196.008]
  • Create a payment profile with the address and add a credit card on file. Click Agree and continue. [L1196.008]
  • Each additional paid user license costs $7 per month. Aliases are not paid users and do not add to the monthly price. [L1196.006]
  • A Google Workspace account includes up to 30 alternate email addresses (aliases) at no extra cost. The user still signs in only with the primary address, and aliases are used to send and receive mail. [L1196.006, L1197.014]
  • Do not click "buy more licenses" in the admin panel when you only need extra email addresses. Every added license costs $7 per month. Aliases on the existing account cover multiple addresses without this cost. Add paid users only when an employee needs their own separate inbox. [L1196.007]
  • When only the first account is needed during setup, click Cancel on the prompt to buy more licenses. [L1197.007]
  • Creating extra user accounts is not needed during initial setup and can be skipped. Additional team members can be added later from the admin console. [L0566.016]

Google Workspace: Before You Sign Up

  • Sign up for the $7 per month plan first, before creating any other Google Workspace account for the same domain. [L0667.044]
  • If a Google Workspace account was created for the domain before signing up through the $7 per month link, cancel that account and sign up again with the $7 plan. Google will otherwise report that the domain is already in use. [L0667.045]
  • Create the business email address before signing up for Quo, because Quo asks for a business email and uses it to create the account. The same address is then used to log in to the app. [L0338.012]
  • Google changes the signup steps often. When creating a new business email account, follow the current signup screens rather than older guides. Existing Google Workspace business email accounts keep working and do not need to be changed after such an update. [L1049.020]
  • Signing up with a domain you already own leaves that domain and any existing website unchanged. Only the mail routing of the domain is connected to Google. [L0566.020]

Google Workspace: Connecting the Domain's Mail (Activation)

  • Activation routes the domain's email to Gmail. In the Google Admin console, go to Getting started, then click ACTIVATE under 'Activate Gmail for [yourdomain]'. The step is estimated at 5 minutes, with a possible wait of up to 60 minutes. [L0566.008]
  • If your domain is registered with GoDaddy, Google detects it automatically during Gmail activation and offers an automated connection. For a larger domain provider the automated option usually works too. Smaller providers may not be detected and need an alternative verification method. [L0566.010]
  • Connection with GoDaddy is confirmed automatically. Google updates the domain's mail settings and then verifies they are in place. The check can take a few minutes, so wait on the screen rather than closing it. [L0566.011]
  • The error 'There was a problem updating settings in GoDaddy.com' is common and usually means the two systems did not sync. It is not a sign of a setup mistake. Retry 'Sign in to activate' before changing the verification method. [L0566.012]
  • Google may ask for phone verification when it detects unusual activity on a new account. The code can be sent by text message (SMS) or automated voice call. The phone number is used only for security. [L0566.013]
  • Once the MX record for your domain points to Google (smtp.google.com with priority 1), all incoming mail for that domain is delivered to Gmail. Any older mailbox or forwarding setup for the same domain stops receiving new mail. Check what is still in use before switching the record. [L1196.020]
  • Send a test email in the Google Admin console after verification. This confirms that the domain's mail routes correctly before you use the address with suppliers and customers. [L0566.017]

Post-activation checklist (Google Admin console, Getting started page, in this order):

  1. Set up billing (5 min) [L0566.024]
  2. Send test email (after Gmail is activated and the domain is verified) [L0566.024]
  3. Create new users (5 min) [L0566.024]

  5. Set up your mobile devices (10 min) [L0566.024]
  6. Migrate your email and documents, importing existing email, contacts and data (1 hr) [L0566.024]
  7. Enhance your mail security, adding protection against spam and phishing (5 min) [L0566.024]

Email Addresses and Aliases

  • Use professional addresses on your own store domain instead of a personal inbox when contacting suppliers, and keep using them for customers. A simple setup uses three addresses on one account: sales@, returns@, and firstname@ on the store domain. [L1197.001]
  • Email aliases deliver all messages to a single inbox. You select which address to send from for each message, with no need to monitor separate accounts. [L0119.009]
  • Alternate email addresses (aliases) cannot be used to sign in to Google Workspace. Always sign in with the primary address (for example sales@yourdomain.com) and its password. Aliases receive and send mail through that primary account. [L1196.022]
  • Use the same Google Workspace email account for Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Merchant Center, and Google Ads so that all Google tools connect and share data cleanly. [L0171.002]

Add alias addresses (Source A, first version):

  1. In the Google Workspace admin panel, click the blue underlined name of the sales@ user. [L1196.012]
  2. In the left navigation, click Add alternate emails. [L1196.012]
  3. Enter returns as the first alias, which becomes returns@yourdomain.com. [L1196.012]
  4. Enter a personal alias using your first name (adding your last name is also recommended), for example firstname@yourdomain.com. [L1196.012]
  5. Click Save and check that all addresses are listed as alternate emails under the main account. [L1196.012]

Add alias addresses (Source A, second version):

  2. Open the Google Workspace admin panel and click the name of the primary account. [L1197.013]
  3. In the left navigation, click Add alternate emails. [L1197.013]
  4. Add the alternate emails returns and firstname (for example, the first name of the operator), each created on the store domain. Include the last name in the alias name if it is used for supplier contact. [L1197.013]
  5. Check that the list shows sales@, returns@, and firstname@ on the store domain, then click Save. [L1197.013]

Set up sending from the aliases in Gmail (Source A, first version):

  1. Click the gear icon in Gmail, then click See all settings. [L1196.014]
  2. Open the Accounts tab. Under Send mail as, click Edit next to the sales address. [L1196.014]
  3. Change the name from the generic account name to the brand name (for example the store name), then click Save changes. [L1196.014]
  4. Click Add another email address, enter the name and the alias returns@yourdomain.com, then click Next step. [L1196.014]
  5. Under When replying to a message, change the default from Always reply from the default address to Reply from the same address the message was sent to. [L1196.014]
  6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 for the personal alias firstname@yourdomain.com. [L1196.014]
  7. Click Compose, then choose the From address (sales, returns or personal) to send from it. [L1196.014]

Set up send-as aliases in Gmail (Source A, second version):

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then click See all settings. [L1197.015]
  2. Open the Accounts tab. [L1197.015]
  3. Under Send mail as, click edit on the primary address, change the name to the store name (for example, the store's name as customers should see it), and click Save changes. [L1197.015]
  4. Click Add another email address, enter the name (for example, Returns - Store Name) and the returns@ address, then click Next step. [L1197.015]
  5. In the settings for returns@, set When replying to a message to Reply from the same address the message was sent to, then click Next step. [L1197.015]
  6. Add the firstname@ address with the first and last name, then click Next step. [L1197.015]
  7. Open Compose and select the From address (sales@, returns@, or firstname@) before sending. [L1197.015]
  8. Leave Check mail from other accounts empty, because the aliases were already added in Google Workspace. [L1197.015]

Reply and sending mistakes:

  • Gmail's default is to reply from the primary sales address even when a message arrived at the returns or personal alias. Change the reply setting to reply from the same address the message was sent to, so customers see a consistent sender. [L1196.015, L1197.016]
  • Do not add anything under Check mail from other accounts, because the aliases already receive mail through Google Workspace. [L1196.015, L1197.015]

Use the account on a phone:

  • To use all addresses on a phone, add the account to the phone's email app using the primary sales@yourdomain.com address and its password. Once added, the app can send and receive from all aliases on the account. No separate setup is needed for each alias on the phone. [L1196.016]
  • In any email app on the phone, add an account using the primary address (sales@yourdomain.com) and its password. Send and receive mail from all three addresses, because the aliases are linked to the same account. [L1197.017]
  • After activation, the business address can be used at gmail.com or in desktop and mobile mail apps such as Apple Mail or Outlook, by adding the Gmail account to the app. This lets you send and receive on a phone or computer. [L0566.014]

Email Tools: Filters and Scheduling

  • Gmail filters and rules can apply labels and sort incoming emails automatically, so the assistant can action messages without sorting them by hand. Combine these rules with colour-coded priority labels so urgent messages surface first. [L1000.077]
  • Boomerang for Gmail schedules emails to send later and can send a reminder back to you if a recipient has not replied within a set number of days. The reminder is used to track supplier actions that would otherwise be forgotten. [L0910.040]
  • Boomerang's scheduled sending is optional for business email. In the store described, suppliers and customers did not object to emails sent late at night or early in the morning. The reminder feature is the more useful part for tracking supplier follow-ups. [L0910.059]
  • Boomerang for Gmail, a browser plugin linked to Gmail, lets you write emails at night and schedule their delivery for a set time in the recipient's time zone, such as 9 a.m. US time. Use it to show suppliers your email arrives during their business hours, for example by scheduling for 9:03 a.m. [L1441.021]

Security and Gemini in Google Workspace

  • The Gemini AI sidebar in Gmail can summarize emails, draft emails and auto replies, and find information in the inbox. It is included with the Google Workspace account and can be minimized or closed. It is optional and not required for the email setup. [L1196.019, L1197.023]

Google Voice: Setup and Use

  • Google Voice is added to an existing Google Workspace account that was set up earlier with the business email. It costs $10 per month, is managed in the Google Voice app on phone and computer, and offers only local numbers chosen by area code. Toll-free numbers are not offered. [L0700.005]
