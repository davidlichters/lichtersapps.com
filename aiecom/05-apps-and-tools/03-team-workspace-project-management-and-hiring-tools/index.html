5.3 Team workspace, project management and hiring tools

Google Workspace as the shared base

  • Google Drive is the central store for company files, including SOPs, FAQs, response templates, passwords and contact information, and the whole team can reach it from anywhere. [L0230.004, L0926.005]
  • Keep all company files in one organised, shared Drive folder that the owner and any assistant can open, so the business can be run from anywhere. [L0230.004, L1000.047, L1441.018]
  • Google Drive is used for most business tasks and file organisation. [L0835.123]
  • Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Hangouts and Gmail together form a cloud business system. [L1000.047]
  • A cloud folder system such as Dropbox can be used alongside Google Drive. [L1473.007]
  • Google Sheets is a free, cloud-based spreadsheet comparable to Microsoft Excel. It needs only a free Google account and is reached at drive.google.com. [L0737.001]
  • Google Sheets is stored in Google Drive and used to store and edit business data and to track orders and other records that automations can update. [L0437.004]
  • Google Docs, Google Drive and Basecamp can hold a project document and SOPs for each supplier, and can be shared with an assistant. [L0359.053]
  • Google Voice provides free phone service, and a US phone number can be set up through it. [L0230.004, L1515.022]
  • Cloud tools for a location-independent business are Google Docs for documents, Dropbox for files, Slack for team communication and Skype for calls, plus a US phone number through Google Voice. [L1515.022]

Chrome profile as the business workspace

  • A dedicated Google Chrome profile can act as a shared business workspace. It holds bookmarks to dashboards, spreadsheets, supplier price lists, contracts and account logins, organised by category. [L1473.008]
  • Each business or project can have its own browser window, and a new hire can be given the login to see the same setup. [L1473.008]
  • Setup procedure:
    1. Create a Google Chrome profile for the business as early as possible. [L1473.009]
    2. Bookmark every tool, dashboard and document used during daily work, such as the Shopify dashboard and Slack. [L1473.009]
    3. Start using a help desk tool like Remaze and a task board like Asana, with Trello as a possible alternative to Asana. [L1473.009]
    4. Once the processes run efficiently, document each one as a standard operating procedure. [L1473.009]
    5. Write FAQs from past customer emails and from the manufacturer's website, rewritten in your own words rather than copied. [L1473.009]
    6. Create on-brand response templates for the VA's email replies to reduce mistakes. [L1473.009]
    7. Keep one spreadsheet with all account passwords and one with supplier and service contacts, so the VA can answer questions without the owner being the contact point. [L1473.009]
    8. When these are in place, the system is ready for hiring. [L1473.009]

Shared templates, copies and checklists

  • Any shared Google document, whether Docs or Sheets, should be copied into your own Drive before you edit it. The shared original cannot be edited by you, and anyone with access to a shared version can see what you enter. [L0996.004]
  • Procedure to work in a shared Google Sheets tracker (older: 2021-22):
    1. Open the shared Google Sheets link. [L0996.003]
    2. Click File in the top menu. [L0996.003]
    3. Choose Make a copy. [L0996.003]
    4. Save the copy in your own Google Drive and work only in that copy. [L0996.003]
  • If copying a Google file feels too complex, use the Excel version of the tracker on your desktop instead. Choose whichever format fits your working style. [L0996.005]
  • The research tracker exists as a Google Sheets version and a Microsoft Excel version. The Excel file can be downloaded and used on the desktop, on Windows or Mac. (older: 2021-22) [L0996.002]
  • Keep your own copy of any shared checklist spreadsheet, either by copying it to your own drive or downloading it as Excel (preferred) or PDF. Edits then do not affect other users. (older: 2021-22) [L1145.003]
  • To reuse a shared Google Doc template, choose File > Make a copy. In the Make a copy dialog you can keep the same sharing ("Share it with the same people"), copy comments and suggestions, and include resolved comments and suggestions before pressing Make a copy. Edit only your copy, not the shared original. [L1323.016]

Team communication

  • Slack is the internal communication platform for instant team notifications and task assignments. It also sends abandoned cart alerts and organises group messages. [L0230.006]
  • Slack is used for daily team communication, with separate channels for work and a casual channel for team chat. Teams split across time zones use it as a shared office. [L0337.029]
  • Slack is a team messaging app that can be used free of charge even without employees. It works as a personal notes space and receives instant notifications from automations, so the owner does not need to check email for every event. [L0437.006]
  • Slack is a team messaging tool with channels, direct messages and group chats. It is used for internal team communication, including reporting outages and status updates. (older: 2021) [L0469.065]
  • Slack is used for daily VA communication instead of email, because it allows near-instant replies and constant availability. It keeps VAs engaged and makes it easy to check progress during the day. [L0921.018]
  • Slack is a team chat tool for communicating with remote team members and virtual assistants. [L0926.006]
  • Slack is used only for internal communication, one-on-one and in group channels, and keeps a searchable message history. Outside communication with customers stays in the help desk. Slack is preferred over Asana messaging, which was slow and gave poor notifications, and over Skype or Facebook Messenger for team chat. [L1473.005]
  • Keep the channels separate: customer messages go through the help desk, order and task management runs in Asana, and internal team talk runs in Slack. This makes it clear where each kind of work lives. [L1473.006]
  • Use WhatsApp as the main direct channel for quick questions from the team. Slack and Discord are alternatives. [L1431.013]
  • Source positions differ on the quick-question channel: one source uses WhatsApp for quick team questions [L1431.013], while another uses Slack for all internal talk with a searchable history [L1473.005]. WhatsApp fits fast one-line questions; Slack fits internal work that must stay searchable and separate from customer messages. [L1431.013, L1473.005]
  • Google Hangouts can be used to chat with a candidate, and for team chat that appears inside Gmail. It was also used for face-to-face team meetings. (older: 2020-22 advice) [L1000.035, L1441.018]
  • Skype calls that the assistant can listen to let the assistant learn the owner's tone when speaking with customers. [L1000.035]

Program community and coaching logistics

Discord (Source A program)

  • Discord is the community platform because other platforms were full of distractions and could not guarantee that members would see each other's posts. The space is dedicated to one community, where members ask questions, answer them and share wins. [L0517.002]
  • Discord works like an instant messaging service. The server is split into sections and channels in the sidebar, each for a different topic or program stage. [L0517.003]
  • Module-specific channels: questions about Module Zero go in the Module Zero chat thread, and questions about Module One go in the channel named 'One Niche Selection'. Search previous posts first, since the answer may already exist. [L0517.004]
  • The 'Social' channel, at the bottom of the channel list, is a free chat where members connect with each other in real time on any topic. [L0517.005]
  • Private or personal questions go by email to the source's own site Questions that would help the whole community go to the matching section of the Discord, where the coach or other members answer them. [L0517.009]
  • Discord can be used in a web browser, in a mobile browser, or through the Discord mobile app, which keeps the community accessible on the phone at all times. [L0517.010]
  • Procedure to join the members-only Discord community:
    1. Open the join link provided below the lesson, which opens Discord. [L0517.001]
    2. Enter the same email address you use to log into the program's member area. [L0517.001]
    3. Submit it so the membership can be verified and access granted. [L0517.001]
  • A resources channel in the program Discord holds tools, supplier lists, ad creative examples and research templates. A Meta ads guide built for the Source D system sits alongside it. [L1452.011]

Facebook group as a working channel

  • A Facebook group can serve as a working channel between mastermind calls for quick questions and answers, for sharing problems and asking for fast help. [L0832.039]
  • The private, members-only Facebook group is used to post questions, share what is and is not working, and get quick help from the host and other members. Members can also ask the host directly there. (older: 2021-22) [L0545.010]

Coaching portal (Source B program) (older: 2021-22)

  • Top menu: Dashboard (returns to the entry screen from any page), Group Coaching Schedule, Facebook Group, Coaching Recordings. Personal menu: My Courses (returns to the dashboard), Settings (basic platform changes), Log Out. [L0545.007]
  • The Group Coaching Schedule is a Google Sheet showing the current calendar month's coaching times, with a registration link per call. It is updated on the first day of every calendar month. [L0545.008]
  • Registration for a group coaching call in the schedule is required to attend, because the Zoom link for the call is only sent after you register. Registering is the only way to access a session. [L0545.009]
  • Recordings of all group coaching sessions are available to every member, so missed sessions can be watched later. Recordings are usually uploaded within one to two days after the session. [L0545.011]
  • Program video modules give step-by-step implementation guidance for action items assigned during coaching calls, enabling independent execution of recommendations. [L0035.008]
  • Members who are stuck can email the program's support team at support@dropshipbreakthrough.com. Questions can also be posted in the members-only Facebook group, where other members can answer quickly. [L0545.019]

Task boards and order flow

Trello

  • Trello is used for order flows (from order through fulfilment, shipping and invoicing), so the status of any order is visible on one board. [L0337.028]
  • Trello is also used for projects: a team member picks a card, works on it and moves it to completed, which removes status-check messages. The free version was enough for the example business. [L0337.028]
  • Trello is a free task-management tool for to-dos for each business. It lists clients and prospects to contact and colour-codes progress: red for not done, yellow for in progress, green for done. [L0440.021]
  • Each store or project gets its own Trello board. [L1431.011]
  • Trello is used to manage tasks: each task is placed in a category list, assigned to a team member and given a deadline, so the team can complete it overnight for review. Sort tasks into lists that match your workflow before assigning them. [L1441.017]
  • Trello is a visual board system for quotes, orders, projects and product uploads, with cards, lists, labels, comments, attachments and due dates. Due dates make cards turn red when overdue and can send email notifications. Cards can be dragged between lists to show progress. (older: 2021-22) [L1396.031]
  • Set a due date on each follow-up or order card so the team gets notified before and when it is due. Overdue cards appear in red on the board. Enable email notifications so reminders reach you outside the board. (older: 2021-22) [L1396.032]
  • Trello's free plan at the time of the lesson allowed five boards with a small team of about three members plus the owner. The speaker was unsure whether this was truly free, so check the current plan limits before relying on them. Trello and Asana were reported to cost about $10 per user per month with a minimum number of seats. (older: 2021-22) [L1396.033]
  • Zapier can connect Trello to Shopify or other tools so that actions in one app trigger updates in another. It was not yet working reliably for the speaker, so connect it step by step and test each trigger. Automations of this kind were the next planned step after manual boards. (older: 2021-22) [L1396.034]
  • Trello is a task board tool for organising projects and tracking work across a remote business. [L0926.004]
  • Procedure to set up a store project board in Trello (Source C):
    1. Create a board in Trello for the store project. [L1431.012]
    2. Create a big task card, for example Store creation, and list the sub-tasks: create Shopify account, install apps, prepare payment providers, marketing, and product research. [L1431.012]
    3. Add labels such as Not done, In progress and Done to show progress, using the pen icon to set the label text and colour. [L1431.012]
    4. Give each card a deadline. [L1431.012]
    5. Open Share and enter the email address of the assistant to invite them to the board. [L1431.012]
    6. Open Members and select the assistant so everyone knows who owns which task. [L1431.012]
    7. Use the filter to show only the cards assigned to you. [L1431.012]
    8. Attach any PDF or product criteria to the card if needed. [L1431.012]
  • Recommended board structure from a coaching program (older: 2021-22):

Asana

  • Asana is the core business tool for hiring, task management, reporting and team collaboration. Use it for the job application pipeline, candidate tracking, onboarding tasks, order tracking (each order = task), daily and weekly reporting, and automatic performance dashboards. [L0263.011]
  • Asana cost: free initially, then $10 per user per month for premium features with reports and advanced automation. [L0263.011]
  • Asana cost, conflicting source: the Reports tab is available in the paid plan only, at $10 per user per month. [L0263.027]
  • Asana is used to track order status through fulfilment stages and assign tasks to team members. Orders appear as cards on a board with status columns. [L0230.005]
  • Asana is a similar project board tool that also offers a list view for tasks and sorting by due date. Choose between Asana and Trello based on whether you want board-style moves or list views. Both can handle the same operational boards. (older: 2021-22) [L1396.035]
  • Asana tasks support due dates and extra detail fields, and personal projects in Asana are free, so it can also serve as a personal to-do list. [L1473.004]
  • Asana Reports tab: an automatic live dashboard shows task completion by person, open tasks remaining, and trend over time (4 weeks, 7 days, yesterday). Use it to see team performance and workload distribution at a glance. Available in the paid plan only ($10 per user per month). [L0263.027]

ClickUp and Basecamp

  • ClickUp can serve as the central task manager, with one list per area of responsibility, a calendar view that shows upcoming deadlines and calls, and filters or grouping by priority to work through tasks in order. Notion is an alternative for to-do lists. Keep all open tasks in one system so they do not spread across notes and memory. [L1041.032]
  • In a ClickUp task description, type / to insert tables, templates, bulleted lists, headings or embedded videos. Use this to keep the description as a dynamic page instead of plain text. [L1181.030]
  • In ClickUp, open the Everything view and group tasks by Priority to see all open work across lists in priority order. Save this grouped view and use it whenever free time appears. [L1181.031]
  • Basecamp is useful for breaking up tasks across many people with due dates, but it is not needed until the business has a decent-sized team. [L0835.123]

Screen recording and walkthroughs

Loom

  • Loom is a screen recording tool that captures your screen and optionally your camera, then gives you a shareable link to the video. It is useful for quick walkthroughs or explainer videos for team members or customers. [L0629.001]
  • Loom is a free screen recording tool available as a desktop app or browser extension. It records screen, microphone and camera for instructional videos. [L0276.036]
  • Loom is a free Chrome extension for recording screen, voice and optional webcam to document a process such as uploading products, for training a virtual assistant. (older: 2021) [L0801.111]
  • Loom records your screen and microphone so you can show a team member how to do a task. (older: 2021) [L0671.083, L0671.220]
  • Loom (free version), Google Meet or QuickTime can record your screen with microphone audio so that you can document how to perform a task. [L0359.052]
  • Loom is a free screen recording tool that gives a shareable link. Use it to record how a product should be uploaded so a freelancer can follow it. (older: 2020) [L0852.080]
  • Loom is a screen recording tool that records the screen and voice, useful for making quick tutorial or explanation videos. (older: 2020) [L1087.004]
  • Loom is a free Chrome extension that records the screen, optionally with a webcam and microphone. It produces a video link or embed code that can be placed in a step-by-step guide. (older: 2020) [L1088.065]
  • Loom is a free screen recording tool that adds a webcam bubble. Short Loom videos can be used to answer customer or lead questions more personally than email text. [L0972.030]
  • Loom is a free screen and video recording tool, available as a Chrome extension or as a desktop app from loom.com. It works well for answering questions with a short visual explanation instead of long text. [L1049.001]
  • When starting a Loom recording, choose whether to share your screen, your webcam and your microphone. Recording starts with one click and stops with one click, and the length can be anything from under a minute to several minutes. [L1049.002]
  • After you stop a Loom recording, Loom generates a unique loom.com link for that video. Paste this link into an email reply or a Discord post so the recipient can watch it. [L1049.003]
  • Loom records the product upload process; the videos are then sent to Fiverr freelancers. (2025) [L0255.053]
  • A short screen recording tool such as Loom can show a store funnel to an outside expert, who can spot obvious errors the owner missed. A five-minute walkthrough of the website and funnel is enough for this. [L0694.042]
  • Loom works when the owner also wants to appear on camera. [L0337.008]

QuickTime

  • On a Mac, QuickTime is a simple way to record a screen-based process for the team. Press Command + Shift + 5 to start screen recording. [L0337.008]
  • Videos do not need to be polished; they only need to show the steps clearly. [L0337.008]

Hiring and freelance talent

Hiring pipeline tools

  • Tools for the hiring process: onlinejobs.ph (job posting), Asana (hiring pipeline and candidate tracking), Asana forms (application screening) and Vocaroo (audio recording submission from candidates). These automate filtering and reduce manual work while maintaining visibility. [L0263.017]
  • Message-based interviews: Skype is the main chat platform, and you ask the candidate for their Skype ID. Slack can be used the same way by collecting the candidate's email and inviting them to a dedicated channel. (older: 2021-22) [L1055.015]

onlinejobs.ph

  • onlinejobs.ph is a hiring platform for virtual assistants based in the Philippines, used to find and hire customer service and admin staff. [L0970.091]
  • Online Jobs PH is the job site used to hire virtual assistants from the Philippines, which gives access to a large pool of experienced remote staff. (2021) [L1090.065]
  • onlinejobs.ph requires a monthly subscription of about $49 or $69 to access its VA database and message candidates before hiring. It is known for finding long-term, career-focused VAs, including many with Shopify skills. [L0921.027]
  • onlinejobs.ph is also used to find helpers for product uploads, and to hire hourly freelancers for product uploads and other tasks. (2022) [L0665.055, L0359.051]
  • onlinejobs.ph is a job board used to hire freelance video editors who make product creatives. It was the source of the editor used in the test. [L1194.022]
  • TimeProof on onlinejobs.ph is a free feature that records the VA's work session through screenshots taken roughly every 10 minutes. It lets you review what the VA did on the computer. [L0921.021]

Upwork

  • Upwork and onlinejobs.ph are used to hire hourly freelancers for product uploads and other tasks. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work. [L0359.051]
  • Upwork is used to hire Shopify developers for custom product tools or small theme changes, and bidders are usually available quickly. (2021) [L0671.081, L0671.218]
  • Upwork is the platform for one-off projects such as uploading products or hiring a virtual assistant. (2021) [L0834.055]
  • Upwork is used to hire freelancers for Shopify developer work, product uploads and site design, while virtual assistants can be hired from online-jobs.ph. (2020) [L0852.081]
  • Upwork lets you post a project and receive bids from writers and specialists. Run test projects with several writers before choosing one for ongoing work. (2021) [L1279.046]
  • Upwork can be used to hire a site speed specialist. Post the job tagged with Shopify, which costs nothing to post, and judge candidates by reviews for Shopify and speed work. (2022) [L0963.052]
  • Hourly or fixed prices for site speed work vary widely, from about 20 USD to several thousand. Price alone does not indicate quality, so reviews are the main criterion. (2022) [L0963.053]
  • Upwork and Fiverr are freelance marketplaces where a store owner can find first virtual assistants. The rate of bad hires there is high, so check the assistant's track record and consider a trial period before committing. [L1432.044]

Fiverr

  • Fiverr is pay-per-job, so a single task such as an upload or small edit can be bought without a subscription, with prices around $10 per task. Its freelancer pool covers a very wide range of skills, and quality varies strongly. [L0921.029]
  • Fiverr is a marketplace for hiring freelancers, used to outsource store builds, product pages, or purchasing pre-warmed Facebook ad accounts. [L1212.040]
  • Source positions on Fiverr differ. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work [L0359.051], while a single upload or small edit can be bought there for about $10 per task [L0921.029]. Use Fiverr for one-off tasks with a checked result, and use Upwork or onlinejobs.ph for recurring upload work. [L0359.051, L0921.029]

StoreTasker

  • StoreTasker (storetasker.com) is a marketplace for hiring developers to do custom Shopify work. For simpler needs, the Shopify App Store offers apps that can customise a store. (2021) [L0853.098]
  • StoreTasker is a freelance marketplace for one-off technical Shopify modifications and coding tasks, when a change is beyond what you can do yourself. (2023) [L0855.113]
  • StoreTasker.com is used for website development and code work. You post a job, developers submit bids, and you approve one or decline all. [L0921.030]
  • StoreTasker is listed as a tool for this business setup. The source does not describe its function, so verify what it does before using it. (2024) [L1095.006]

Hiring a VA: hand-over

  • Clarify upfront which tools or platforms are required for the agency to work with you. Determine whether they require access to proprietary software, dashboards or creative management systems, and ensure your data remains accessible to you if the relationship ends. [L0217.044]

Virtual assistant tools

  • Hootsuite lets a social media VA log into one dashboard and publish the same product visuals to all connected social media accounts. Giving the VA access only to Hootsuite keeps posting centralised and avoids sharing account logins. [L0921.035]
  • Google Drive, Google Sheets and Asana together provide the order and task view for the VA, with customer messages kept in the help desk. [L1473.006, L0230.005]

Monitoring and activity checks

  • Hubstaff is a time tracking tool that shows mouse and keyboard activity and time spent. Normal product listing metrics: 70% mouse, 30% keyboard. Abnormal: 100% keyboard and 100% mouse with no output, which is likely fake time tracking or off-task behaviour. [L0263.028]
  • Use Hubstaff to detect when someone fakes time tracking, for example by playing videos or using software to simulate activity. It is not used as a pay-per-second basis, but as activity validation. [L0263.028]

Access, backups and handover

  • Give the virtual assistant limited access to the Shopify admin panel, only to the parts needed to see order status and enter tracking information. Do not give full admin access, the same limited-access approach used for phone support staff. [L1430.005]
  • Rewinds is a Shopify app for backing up your store data before granting contractors access. (2024) [L0276.037]

Research VPNs

  • A VPN can be used during market research to see the search results and shopping results for the United States, if Google shows results for your own country instead. Any reputable VPN works, free or paid. (2020) [L0468.084]
  • A VPN or proxy set to another country shows Google search results as local shoppers see them. Use it to check foreign competitors and Shopping ads from outside that country. (2024) [L0806.108]
  • A VPN lets you see Google Shopping results as if you were in another country, which helps with price research where Google Shopping is unavailable locally. Proton VPN is free or costs about $10 per month. (2021) [L0853.052]
  • ExpressVPN and similar VPN services change the apparent country of the connection. Use them to see country-specific search results, such as when the Google Shopping tab is missing in the research country. (2022) [L0963.054]
  • ExpressVPN is a tool for securely connecting to the internet when working remotely from different countries. [L0926.003]
  • ExpressVPN can be used to view your Google Ads or store as shown in your target country when you are abroad, because geo-targeted ads will not display to a user outside the country. Use it carefully, because a VPN can trigger account security checks. [L1441.022]
  • NordVPN was named as the VPN to use on a desktop to access a US-region TikTok Ads Manager from a UK location. Reports on this setup were mixed and described as prone to issues, so test it with a small budget before relying on it. [L1186.042]
  • A VPN subscription can be used to log in with an IP from the target country, such as Australia, New Zealand, the UK or an EU country, before opening TikTok Ads Manager or TikTok Business Center. A yearly NordVPN plan was cited at about $1 to $2. Turn the VPN on before starting the session, not afterward. [L1377.060]
  • A VPN can be used to set an Instagram account to a target country, for example the US, so organic reels reach that audience. [L1212.044]
