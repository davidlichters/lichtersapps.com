21.26 Other details

Planning the sales process

  • Describe the sales process as it should look in 10 years: who sells, how customers and prospects are contacted, and whether a sales team works on the phone. (Older advice, Source B 2021-22) [L0439.021]
  • For B2B niches, also define how prospecting and offline conversion are handled and by whom. (Older advice, Source B 2021-22) [L0439.021]

Checking outside advice against your store

  • Advice from an external source should be checked against where your store actually stands. General courses distill information for everyone, while a personalized consultant tailors it to a specific store. [L0463.016]

Matching the offer to the buyer

  • Example: for parents selling exam-prep products, a low-cost ebook is more practical than an expensive class that requires travel, parking and time away from home. For this buyer, the ebook is more cost-effective. [L0513.038]

Negotiating with freight companies

  • Use this email to ask a freight company for a better price. (Source A, 2023) [L0855.158]
Hello, I am comparing quotes for this order. The current quote I have from [OTHER CARRIER/BROKER] is [AMOUNT], and the quote on your platform is [AMOUNT]. Is there any better pricing you can offer for this shipment? Thank you, [YOUR NAME].
