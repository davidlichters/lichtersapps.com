21.4 Showing the number and using phone calls to action

Why phone contact matters

  • For expensive products, a phone-based call to action can convert better than the buy-now button for many buyers, because they want to speak to a sales or customer service person before committing. Test "Call today" with the phone number in the copy against the buy-now button. Keep the buy-now button as the default option. [L0461.014]
  • Business buyers often want to call before they order, to confirm stock or negotiate a volume discount. If you sell B2B, put a phone number on your website. [L0769.006]
  • Visitors who call before ordering are usually close to buying. Leaving the number off can lower conversion. [L1088.085]
  • A visible phone number builds trust, and callers are often ready to buy. [L0960.042]
  • Older advice (2019 to 2022): Source A (2019) recommends a phone number at any price point, including products with an average order value around $500. Source B (2021 to 2022) limits the rule to expensive products. Use both: for high-ticket products, show the number prominently; for lower-priced products, the number still builds trust, so it does no harm to include it. [L0960.042, L0461.014, L1371.038]

Showing the number on the site

Header and contact page

  • Show a business phone number in the website header with a "Call us today" heading. High-ticket buyers want to speak to someone, and Google Shopping and similar marketing channels require a visible phone number. [L1371.038]
  • Keep the phone number on the website even if you cannot answer every call. [L0934.107]

Trade (B2B) page

  • B2B buyers usually want to talk on the phone and ask for bulk pricing. Make a phone contact visible on the trade page. [L0961.016]

Handling calls you cannot answer

Follow these steps when the store cannot answer every call:

  1. Keep the phone number on the website. [L0934.107]
  2. Set up a voicemail greeting that collects the caller's details. [L0934.107]
  3. Return every missed phone call on the same day. [L0961.016]
  4. Outsource phone support later, when the store earns enough to justify it. [L0934.107]

Offering a choice of channels

  • When customers can choose between writing, calling or visiting a store, the phone option is the one most people use. In one test, 70 percent of inquiries for a sample came by telephone. [L1261.052]
  • Offer the phone option as a primary response channel, since people find it easier than writing. [L1261.052]
