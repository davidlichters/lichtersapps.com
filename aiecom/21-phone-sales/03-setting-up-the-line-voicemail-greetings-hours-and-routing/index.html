21.3 Setting up the line: voicemail, greetings, hours and routing

Routing calls by time of day

  • The phone number is directed to different people at different times of the day, based on the business time zone. [L0404.004]

Overflow to voicemail or personal phone

  • Unanswered calls overflow automatically to voicemail or the owner's personal phone, so no manual switching is needed. [L0404.004]
  • Staff must check voicemail regularly during their answering periods. [L0404.004]

After-hours handling

  • Outside advertised business hours, the phone line plays a prerecorded message and goes to voicemail. [L0404.005]
  • Voicemail left after hours is checked in the next business period. [L0404.005]
  • Alternatively, outside advertised business hours the call diverts to the owner's personal phone. [L0404.005]

Staff answering rules

  • Staff must always state their first name and the business name when answering a call. [L0404.005]

Ringing several devices

  • Google Voice can be installed on multiple phones logged into the same Google account so that calls ring on several devices. [L0971.073]

Answering service

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22): Most number providers offer an answering service. When a call is missed, it plays a pre-recorded greeting that names your business and asks for a message. The service then notifies you of the message by email or app so you can call back as soon as possible. [L1198.005]

Voicemail transcripts

  • Quo's automatic voicemail transcripts convert incoming messages to text, enabling faster responses to customer and supplier inquiries without listening to full recordings. [L0122.007]
  • Example (Quo Starter plan): The Quo Starter plan includes voicemail transcripts, which turn each voicemail into text. A caller asking whether a product is in stock can be answered from the transcript later, so a sale is not lost while the owner is busy. [L0338.007]

Recorded voicemail greeting

Current version (Source A, 2023):

Hello, you've reached [store name, e.g. storename.com]. Thanks for calling. Our team is currently away from the desk or helping other customers, so please record a voicemail including your name, email address, phone number, and the purpose of your call. We'll respond as soon as we're able.

[L0934.103]

Older version (Source A, 2021):

Thanks for reaching out to [store name]. Our team is either away from the phone or busy assisting another customer at the moment. Leave a voicemail stating your name, email address, and question, and we'll call back as quickly as we can. Alternatively, send us a message at [support email address].

[L1090.140]

Missed-call text reply

Hi, this is {store_name}, and I'm sorry I missed your call. I'm helping other customers right now, so I'll reply by text or phone as soon as I can. If your call is urgent, you can reply to this message with your order number and a short note about what you need.

{your_name}

[L1472.068]
