21.23 Staffing, hiring and delegating phone coverage

Decide when to answer the phone yourself and when to hire

  • Handle calls yourself first so you learn them, then reinvest profits into hiring someone to take over. Do not outsource phone sales before the business is profitable. (Older advice, 2018.) [L1109.064]
  • Missed calls from customers mean lost sales. Whoever answers needs a set schedule and a quiet place to take calls. If that person is running errands, they carry a notebook to capture leads. [L0832.005]
  • Customer service calls can interrupt the owner about every 10 minutes, which makes focused work almost impossible. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1247.005]
  • Whether you need coverage depends on whether the calls make money:
Situation Action
Calls from customers turn into revenue Have someone answer the phone while you are away. [L0808.038]
Calls do not generate revenue You do not need to cover them. [L0808.038]

Timing the first hire

Sources give different triggers. Use the one that matches your situation:

Trigger Source Applies when
The site has traffic Older, 2019 [L0467.065] You need someone to cover live chat and phone calls during hours you cannot. The cost should be recovered quickly from the sales they handle. [L0467.065]
The business is profitable Older, 2018 [L1109.064]; older, 2020 [L0468.177] You are handing phone sales over to someone else, with profits funding the hire. [L1109.064, L0468.177]
  • Phone support must still be answered while you travel. If you work from another time zone, hire a virtual assistant rather than leave calls unanswered, because sales drop when calls go unanswered. (Older, 2019.) [L1002.070]
  • The first hire is usually a customer service assistant. [L1000.031]

Virtual assistants and remote coverage

  • Once the business is profitable, a virtual assistant can handle customer phone calls so the owner is not tied to the phone while travelling. (Older, 2020.) [L0468.177]
  • For short absences, such as a week away, have a friend or family member answer by forwarding calls to them. [L0808.042]
  • For ongoing phone coverage, hire a full-time person through onlinejobs.ph, a Filipino job board. [L0808.042]

Who to hire for phone work

  • Phone support staff should sound natural to the local market. When selling in the United States, for example, they should have no accent, because an accent can lower the conversion rate. Email and live chat do not carry this accent problem, so the person's location matters less for those channels. [L1430.006]
  • Phone staff are usually more expensive than staff who handle email, live chat and order processing, especially at the start. That is why the two roles are often separated when you start out, although one person can do both. [L1430.007]

Vetting and interview tests

Hiring and vetting a phone-support virtual assistant (older, 2019):

  1. Post a job on onlinejobs.ph for someone with phone and email sales or support experience. [L1002.071]
  2. Review resumes and prefer candidates with experience at large, well-run companies who are still employed there and looking for a better opportunity. [L1002.071]
  3. Test the hire by calling your own business at least a couple of times per week, from a withheld number and at unusual hours. Confirm they answer and handle the call correctly. [L1002.071]

Interview checks for the first hire:

  • Use a five-minute interview test. A call that ends in five minutes or less shows no rapport, and the candidate is unlikely to build rapport with customers. [L1000.031]
  • Reject candidates who are hard to understand on a call. They will be the voice of the business to customers every day, so poor spoken English or unclear speech is a reason to remove them from the list. [L1000.032]

Trial and pay structure

Pay structure for new phone or chat staff (older, 2019):

Stage Pay structure
Month 1 One-month trial. [L0467.066]
After the trial Add commission on sales. [L0467.066]
Once they prove reliable Base salary plus incentives. [L0467.066]

Daily performance tracking

Tracking a phone and live chat team (older, 2019):

  1. Log in each night and check how many calls came in and how many were answered. [L0467.067]
  2. Check the average response time for live chats. [L0467.067]
  3. Review the call logs in Mighty Call to confirm calls are answered and handled properly. [L0467.067]
  4. Confirm the quality of the work matches the standard you expect from the hire. [L0467.067]

Outsourcing outbound work

  • For B2B businesses, outbound sales calls to other companies and LinkedIn lead generation can be outsourced. Delegate them once the business can afford it. (Older, 2021-22.) [L1247.022]
