21.8 Taking orders over the phone

Staff authorisation and payment limits

  • Staff are authorised to take customer orders over the phone and place them on the website for the customer. [L0404.003]
  • Phone orders are limited to credit card or bank deposit payment. [L0404.003, L0404.002]
Rule Value Source
Who takes phone orders Staff L0404.003
Payment methods allowed Credit card or bank deposit L0404.003, L0404.002

When phone sales matter

  • Older advice (2021): most sales come through the website rather than the phone. [L0834.094]
  • Older advice (2021): phone calls are typically used to close bulk orders, for example 10 items for an office. A discount is offered, and the sale is then placed over the phone. [L0834.094]

Taking the call

  1. Answer the customer's product or stock question. [L1283.062]
  2. If they want to buy, add the item to cart in the store. [L1283.062]
  3. Collect the customer's name, shipping address, billing address and payment method. [L1283.062]
  4. Enter the details as if the customer were checking out, using their email. [L1283.062]
  5. Process and follow up the order like any other online order. [L1283.062]

The sources also give a fuller version of the call:

  1. Ask the customer what they would like to buy. [L0404.002]
  2. Open the website, put those products in the cart, then go to checkout. [L0404.002]
  3. Collect the customer's contact details with them: name, email address, delivery address and phone number. [L0404.002]
  4. Move on to the payment page and ask which payment method they prefer. Phone orders can be settled by credit card or by bank deposit. [L0404.002]
  5. If the customer is paying by credit card, ask for the card numbers, type them into the checkout page, then click finish to complete the purchase. [L0404.002]

Routes for entering the order

The sources describe two routes. Phone quotes are sent as draft orders, and direct orders are entered through normal checkout. [L1000.043, L0470.044, L1283.063]

Normal checkout

  • Take phone orders by entering them through the normal online checkout, asking the customer for the same information as on the site. No separate POS system is needed for this. [L0470.044]
  • A phone-only order can be placed through the normal checkout in the back end, so the order record matches a customer-placed web order. [L1283.063]

Shopify draft orders

Creating an order for a customer by phone in Shopify:

  1. Click "Create order" in the Orders section, which creates a draft order. [L1302.009]
  2. Add the products the customer wants. [L1302.009]
  3. Apply any discounts. [L1302.009]
  4. Send the customer a link so they can complete checkout themselves. [L1302.009]

Channel attribution

  • Phone quotes are sent to customers as draft orders in Shopify. [L1000.043]
  • When the customer buys from a draft order, the sale is attributed to the phone channel, not to the online store, Facebook or Pinterest. [L1000.043]
  • Because of this attribution, the phone sales commission can be tracked by channel. [L1000.043]
Sale placed via Attributed channel Source
Shopify draft order bought by the customer Phone L1000.043
Online store, Facebook, Pinterest Their own channel L1000.043

Referral questions

  • Ask about referrals only if they are part of the sales workflow. [L1283.063]
  • Referral status does not change the process. [L1283.063]
