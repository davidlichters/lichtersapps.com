21.12 Upselling and cross-selling on calls

Where phone upselling fits

  • Phone upselling and cross-selling can increase store profit substantially, but it is optional and is a different style of selling from the core approach. A store with a net margin of about 30% can run without it. (Older advice, 2021.) [L0834.095]

Upsell planner for agents

  • Give every customer service agent the full upsell planner: the list of core products and their related upsells. This lets agents know which products to offer with which core product. If you handle service alone, the planner applies to you too. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.021]

Answer first, then offer

  • Answer the customer's question first, then offer the related upsell product. For example, answer a shipping question and then mention the related product that goes with the item. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.022]

Relevance over frequency

  • Offer upsells only where they fit the customer's purchase. Relevance matters more than offering every time, so skip the offer when it does not fit. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.026]

Phone and chat upsell steps

Steps for customer service agents:

  1. Answer the customer's question first. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.023]
  2. Mention the related upsell product that pairs with the core product the customer is buying. [L1409.023]
  3. Ask whether the customer wants to add it to the order. [L1409.023]
  4. If the customer agrees on the phone, create the checkout for them and send it, or walk them through finding the product on the site, particularly when a deal applies. [L1409.023]
  5. If the customer is in online chat, send a direct link to the product page or checkout. [L1409.023]
  6. If a discount or special offer applies, give the customer the discount code or apply the offer. [L1409.023]

Agent scripts and training

  • Provide agents with a written script and instructions covering when and how to offer upsells. Build the upsell step into the customer service process or SOPs. Agents need practice before the upsell becomes routine. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.024]
  • Upsells in customer service fail when agents have not been trained or when the step is not part of the service process. Practice the script with agents and make the upsell step a standard part of their workflow. (Older advice, 2021-22.) [L1409.025]

Post-purchase calls and offers

  • A post-purchase call should add a helpful offer only when a better product or benefit makes sense for the buyer. Calling a customer just to tell them they should have bought something else damages trust. [L1076.048]
  • Case: a business owner with a sales background called every buyer after purchase for a few months, after selling the store.
    • The owner offered a different product, extended warranties and faster shipping over the phone. The owner had previously offered these on the site.
    • The owner saw sales climb steadily during that time.
    • The approach needs a team member or owner who is comfortable on the phone, and it takes little time. [L1076.049]
