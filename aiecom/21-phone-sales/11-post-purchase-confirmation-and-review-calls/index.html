21.11 Post-purchase, confirmation and review calls

Order confirmation calls

When to use an order confirmation call

  • Use a confirmation call only for orders that look suspicious or that need verification before shipping. [L0960.015]
  • The call checks the shipping destination, billing address, and connection location against each other, then asks the customer to confirm or correct the details. [L0960.015]

Order confirmation script (2019, older)

Hi, this is [Your name] from [Store name]. We received your order for [product] and want to confirm it is yours before we ship. Our records show it is going to [shipping state or city], the billing address is in [billing state or city], and the connection came from [IP location]. Could you confirm these details and tell me whether anything looks different from what you expected? Thank you, and we will get your order on its way once we have confirmed it.

[L0960.015]

Thank-you and satisfaction calls

Purpose and timing of the post-sale call

  • The first call is a post-sales support call, not a review request. Opening this way feels natural to the customer, and most businesses do not follow up at all, so the call builds goodwill before the request is made. [L1012.014]
  • Schedule a post-purchase call to every customer at the same point the automated review email would go out, typically around 7 to 14 days after the order or after the usual delivery time. For example, if most orders arrive within 7 days, call at day 7; if the review email goes out at 2 weeks, call at 2 weeks. [L1012.010]
  • Call new customers after purchase to thank them and confirm the order. Calls cost nothing if done with your own time or on commission, and take about 10 to 20 minutes each. [L1076.046]
  • Make an upsell only when a product truly fits the customer's purchase. [L1076.046]

Goals of the review call

  • A personal thank-you.
  • Gauging satisfaction with the business and product.
  • Resolving any problems.
  • Securing at least a text review.
  • Requesting a photo of the customer with the product.
  • Requesting a video review via VideoPeel. [L1012.017]

Structure of a post-sale call

  1. Open by asking a series of questions about whether the customer is getting the value they expected from the product and whether anything is wrong. [L1011.006]
  2. Answer any questions the customer has. [L1011.006]
  3. At the end of the call, ask the customer to leave a review. [L1011.006]

Post-purchase call script (Source A)

Use this post-purchase call script with placeholders filled in:

Hello [first name], this is [your name] from [store name]. Thank you for your business. I wanted to let you know that we have your order [order number] confirmed, and it usually ships within 48 hours. Here is what to expect: you will get tracking updates by email as soon as it ships. If you have any questions, you can call us at [phone number], email [email address], or use live chat on our site between 9 and 5. We appreciate you, and we are here to support you.

[L1076.047]

  • If the customer does not answer, leave a voicemail with the same thank-you message and the same contact details, and do not pitch a product in the voicemail. [L1076.047]

Use the script as a guide

  • Use a call script as a guide to the content only. Callers should speak in their own natural language rather than reading the script word for word. [L1012.015]

Review call procedure

Step-by-step review call

  1. Call the customer at the scheduled point after the order (usually 7 to 14 days). [L1012.013]
  2. Open with a personal thank-you for the order and say the call is a check-in to make sure everything arrived fine. [L1012.013]
  3. Ask whether the customer is happy with the product. [L1012.013]
  4. If the customer is satisfied, explain that reviews help the business serve customers better and ask whether they would leave a quick two-minute review. [L1012.013]
  5. If the customer is unhappy, listen and try to resolve the problem on the call, since a resolved issue often produces a stronger review. [L1012.013]
  6. Request a text review at minimum. [L1012.013]
  7. Offer the option of a video review (via VideoPeel) and/or a photo of the customer with the product. [L1012.013]
  8. Close the call and follow up with the email sequence that goes with the script. [L1012.013]

Finding unhappy customers

  • Callers must find out when a customer is unhappy, because customers often will not say so themselves. [L1012.016]
  • A problem that gets resolved on the call can turn into a more credible five-star review that mentions the good service. [L1012.016]
  • Ask whether the customer is happy with the product before asking for a review, so unhappy customers are identified first. [L1012.013]

Starting new staff on calls

  • Do not start new customer service staff on live calls straight away if the product is complex or technical. [L1012.012]
  • Start new staff with outbound scripted calls to existing customers. This lets them learn the product and customer language first. [L1012.012]
