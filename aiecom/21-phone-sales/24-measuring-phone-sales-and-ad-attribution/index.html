21.24 Measuring phone sales and ad attribution

Measuring offline and phone sales

When half or more of sales happen over the phone, Google sees too few conversions and undervalues the campaigns. Either attribute offline sales back to the clicks that caused them, or lower the Google target ROAS to account for offline revenue. [L0454.046]

Adjusting the target ROAS for offline sales

Formula

Google target ROAS = overall target ROAS × (1 − share of offline or phone sales) [L0454.048]

Worked example

  • Share of sales made over the phone: 50%
  • Overall target ROAS: 10x
  • Google target ROAS: 10 × 0.5 = 5x [L0454.048]

Trade-off

  • This method avoids CRM work. [L0454.048]
  • It requires a reasonable estimate of the offline share. [L0454.048]

Diagnosing the funnel before blaming the creative

  • Do not assume that a single poor result is caused only by the creative. Doing so leads to missed funnel problems. [L0361.010]
  • Even with a weak creative, one add-to-cart from a large spend means something in the setup is also wrong. [L0361.010]
