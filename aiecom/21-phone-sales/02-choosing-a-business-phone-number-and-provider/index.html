21.2 Choosing a business phone number and provider

Why a separate business line

  • Business numbers signal professionalism to suppliers and customers and keep business calls apart from personal calls. [L0122.001, L0664.084]
  • A dedicated business number receives less spam than a personal cell number, and business calls are easier to recognise and prioritise. [L0122.002]
  • Use a business phone line and business address on the store, not personal details. A business line also allows a phone team to handle inbound and outbound calls. [L0852.111]
  • Each store needs one phone number that belongs only to the business. Show it on the contact page or in the header. [L1092.088]

Toll-free numbers

Prefixes and rules

Country Prefix Notes
US 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, 833 Each prefix is assigned by a Responsible Organization (RespOrg) under FCC rules. [L1198.004]
Australia 1300 Equivalent of the US toll-free number. [L1198.003]
  • Toll-free numbers in the formats 800, 855 or 866 look more professional on store websites and work especially well for supplier-facing stores. [L0122.006]
  • Older advice (2019) lists 888 or 855 for customer calls. A phone line may be a Google number or a toll-free number. [L0960.043] (older advice, 2019)
  • For callers in the same country, a toll-free number is free. International callers pay charges. [L1198.003]
  • The provider forwards calls to any mobile or landline you choose, so callers hear an ordinary phone call. [L1198.003]
  • Toll-free numbers are portable. You can move one to a different provider without changing the number. [L1198.004]

Vanity numbers

  • Do not pay extra for a memorable or vanity number if the number only appears on your website and in supplier emails. The digits after the prefix do not matter for this use, and a plain number with a toll-free prefix looks just as professional. [L1198.010]

Providers

Mighty Call / MightyCall

  • MightyCall is a business phone system for handling calls. [L0518.002]
  • Entering the code dropship50off at checkout gives 50% off for the first 3 months. [L0518.002]
  • Mighty Call provides a toll-free business number with an app that rings the owner's cell phone and transcribes voicemails. It suits a single business number shown in the site header or on the contact page. [L1092.087] (source 2022)
  • If Mighty Call does not work in a country such as Australia, Google Voice is a workable alternative for a business phone line. [L1092.090] (source 2022)

Grasshopper

  • Grasshopper (grasshopper.com) is a US option for toll-free and vanity numbers. It has desktop and mobile apps, call forwarding and voicemail transcription. [L1198.006]
  • Pricing at the time of recording: plans from $26 per month for a business line, and up to $75 off an annual plan. [L1198.006]

Setup steps

  1. Open grasshopper.com and click Try Free. [L1198.007]
  2. Go to Types of Numbers and select Toll Free Numbers. [L1198.007]
  3. Pick a random number or specify your digits, then choose a plan. [L1198.007]
  4. Download the desktop and mobile apps. [L1198.007]
  5. Configure your settings, which the setup guides you through. [L1198.007]
  6. Place the number on your website and in supplier communication. [L1198.007]

Zintel

  • For an Australian business number, Zintel (zintel.com.au) sells 1300 numbers for about $10 per month. [L1198.008]
  • Sign-up takes under 5 minutes. There is no lock-in contract, and you can cancel at any time. [L1198.008]

JustCall

  • JustCall costs about $20 per month and lets you buy numbers in over 70 countries. Calls are answered through desktop and mobile apps from anywhere. [L1198.009]
  • Example: a US number can be answered from the UK or Thailand, which suits a business run from abroad. [L1198.009]
  • At the time of recording the provider advertised 70% off the first month. [L1198.009]

Google Voice

  • Google Voice is a workable business phone line where Mighty Call does not operate, for example in Australia. [L1092.090] (source 2022)
  • Google numbers are an accepted option for a phone line. [L960.043] (older advice, 2019)
