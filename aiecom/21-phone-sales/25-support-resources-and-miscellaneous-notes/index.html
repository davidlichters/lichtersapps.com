21.25 Support resources and miscellaneous notes

Testing products and offers

  • Do not conclude that a product is dead just because a competitor is scaling it while your test fails. Before drawing a conclusion, check the competitor's pixel data, their earlier entry into the product, their targeting, the product page and the creatives. [L0349.036]
  • Clothing brand and product tests should use cost-effective approaches first. A basic Facebook ad can start as low as five pounds a day to test quickly. [L0561.042]

Case: baby gyro ball

  • Starting point: A dropshipper found a baby gyro ball through Facebook keyword research. The product solved a problem, sat in the evergreen baby niche, and sold for about 25 USD. [L1251.045]
  • Action: The product was marketed on Facebook, and sales began arriving in a steady stream once the ad worked. [L1251.045]
  • Result: The store generated 50,000 USD in just over a month. The operator learned that logistics, supplier quality and customer service were critical, after fragile items arrived broken and suppliers had to be changed. [L1251.045]

Native ad networks

  • Native ad networks such as Taboola have few public tutorials, and agencies often claim the platform is very hard to master. Some brands do well with native ads, but beginners without a tested process should treat them as a supplement rather than a main channel. [L0349.037]

Creative formats and attention metrics

  • Meme-style video ads reached a 60 percent attention rate and a 4 percent click-through rate. They showed no direct sales but were kept running because Google Ads results improved after they went live, which suggested they fed other channels. [L0999.025]

Presenter-led video

  • A presenter-led product tutorial or course built from AI-generated videos can raise conversion because the buyer sees the product explained by a face and voice. [L0888.036]
  • The same approach works for a niche product such as pet health, where a how-to video shows the use of the product before purchase. [L0888.036]

Coaching calls and private mentorship (older advice, Source A 2022)

  • Coaching calls are held on the 1st Wednesday of every month at 2 PM Eastern time. [L0272.053]

Booking a coaching call:

  1. In the members area, hover over Activate. [L0272.053]
  2. Select Activate Coaching. [L0272.053]
  3. Book one of the available times. [L0272.053]

Private mentorship:

  • Private mentorship is available through the members area. [L0272.054]
  • If mentorship is not included with your membership, click Get Help and select Private Mentorship to enroll. [L0272.054]

Partner choice

  • Do not partner with someone who has the same skills as you, lacks experience with failure, or wants a quick win. Each of these mismatches tends to end the partnership when sales fall short. [L0413.046]
