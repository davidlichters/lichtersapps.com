21.18 Cold calling local businesses and agency clients

Niche choice and target selection

  • Choose a niche with healthy budgets and no shortage of money, such as dentists. [L1185.036]
  • Dentists are an example of a local business type that is often not thinking about ads or growth in business terms, which affects how the first conversation must be led. [L0440.055]

Cold email first

  • Send a cold email before calling, so the business has already seen the agency's name when the call arrives. [L0440.054]
  • The email should be short, open with one specific observation about the business's current online presence or ads, and ask for a 10-minute call. [L0440.068]

Call procedure

  1. Send a cold email first to introduce the agency. [L0440.054]
  2. Call the business. If they are busy, remind them that the email was sent. [L0440.054]
  3. Ask for 5 to 10 minutes to discuss their ads or online presence. [L0440.054]
  4. Ask about their goals for the coming year, for example more clients, fewer low-value treatments, or more high-value services. [L0440.054]
  5. Present how running ads can help reach those goals. [L0440.054]
  6. Propose a meeting, ideally at the business's office, to close the sale. [L0440.054]

Opening the conversation

  • Use a shared-interest opener. For example, note that the business owner and the agency both sell a product that solves a customer problem, and that the agency wants to help the business sell that product online. [L0440.056]
  • Local business owners are often not thinking about ads or growth in business terms, so the operator must lead the conversation toward growth and explain why ads and product offers matter. [L0440.055]

Leading the owner toward growth

  • Ask the owner about their goals for the coming year, then link ads to those goals. [L0440.054]
  • When the business already has an online shop, show a demo store with an easier checkout, relevant apps and automated customer emails, so the gaps in their shop become visible. [L1017.039]
  • Quote a price based on what the client is able to pay. [L1017.039]

Payment plan offers for expensive treatments

  • Offer local businesses a payment plan for expensive treatments, such as Invisalign, so that customers can pay monthly instead of a single large sum. [L0440.057]
  • Let a third-party installment provider take over collection of the payments to reduce the risk for the business. [L0440.057]
  • Example from a Swiss dentist's competitor: the offer was Invisalign on installments of about 60 francs per month, instead of a single payment of 3,000 to 5,000 francs. The installment provider paid the full amount to the dentist upfront and collected the monthly payments, charging late fees if payments were missed. [L0440.058]

Cold call and email scripts

Use this prompt to generate the scripts. Replace the bracketed placeholders before use.

Write me a cold calling script and a cold calling email script for my [type of agency, e.g. digital marketing agency] that offers [services, e.g. Facebook ad management and website design] to [target business, e.g. dentists] in [city]. The email should be short, open with one specific observation about the business's current online presence or ads, and ask for a 10-minute call. The phone script should be in the order it will be spoken and include: an opening line that introduces the agency and the reason for calling, one question about the business's goal for the next year, one sentence on how ads can help reach that goal, and a request for a 10-minute meeting at the business's office. Use simple, friendly language without jargon, and give me the script and the email as separate sections.

[L0440.068]

Lead magnet and monetisation

  • Offer a free sample of an improved website as a lead magnet. [L1185.036]
  • Upsell ad management once the website is sold. [L1185.036]
  • Charge a referral or per-lead fee for every client introduced. [L1185.036]

Delegated influencer outreach

  • Influencer outreach can be delegated to an assistant, using a cold-calling script and a course in the resources section. [L1432.038]
  • In the speaker's case, outreach led to two influencer partnerships. [L1432.038]
  • A DM alone rarely gets a response, because creators often do not see DMs. Combine the DM with an email and a comment on their latest video. [L1379.046]
