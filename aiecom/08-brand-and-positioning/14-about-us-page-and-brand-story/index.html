8.14 About Us page and brand story

Structure and Content of the About Us Page

Mandatory Elements

An About Us page for an ecommerce store must include the following: store name, niche product category, city location, a statement of nationwide or multi-regional presence, global shipping carriers (FedEx, UPS and First Class Priority Mail are standard), years or duration in business, team existence or operational strength, and a clearly linked Contact Us page for customer questions [L0093.001].

Before customers place an order, they decide whether to trust the business. The About Us page is the primary tool for building confidence that you are a legitimate, established retailer [L0145.001].

The About Us page builds trust with visitors who do not know the store yet. It should be written carefully because new buyers often read it before deciding to purchase [L1130.003].

Shoppers look at About Us pages mainly when they do not know the store, such as smaller or niche brands. A bland statement about what you sell weakens trust, while a photo of the founder or team and a personal voice makes buying more likely [L1522.033].

Length, Tone and Wording Rules

  • For a Shopify store selling high-ticket products, the About Us page should run 400 to 700 words, written as natural prose, with bullet points only where they make a point clearer [L0533.001].
  • Never use the word "dropshipping" on an About Us page. Write as a serious, long-term retail brand with an established tone, even if the business is new. Avoid generic template phrasing and any startup or hobby-store feel [L0533.002].
  • Older advice (Source A, 2020): the About Us page does not need an inspirational story or a made-up narrative. Focus on an educational approach instead: product benefits, the market gap you fill, and your commitment to service and quality [L0268.034, older].
  • The About Us page should tell a story that ties the store to the products and the audience of the niche, without lying, and should speak to the niche's customers directly [L0359.063].
  • High-ticket buyers need to feel safe before they pay. Build trust through clear supplier standards, service promises and delivery reliability. For this buyer group, confidence in these areas matters more than sales language [L0533.003].
  • If the brand is new, the years-in-operation statement can say "many years of combined industry experience" instead of a specific number of years. Use only experience the owner actually has [L0533.004].
  • A store that carries a person's name must make clear who that person is and what their role is. Visitors cannot tell what the brand stands for without this clarity [L0838.007].

Recommended Structure

  1. Strong brand opening: who we are, who we serve, and what makes us different [L0533.005].
  2. Mission and values: why the brand exists [L0533.005].
  3. What we specialize in: products, quality, and supplier standards [L0533.005].
  4. Customer experience and service promise [L0533.005].
  5. Logistics and fulfillment confidence: shipping partners, reliability, and nationwide reach [L0533.005].
  6. Closing paragraph with a soft call-to-action [L0533.005].

Source B's guidance (2021 to 2022, older) adds that the page should be personal and specific: who you are, why the company is worth buying from, how you serve your market, your USP and your primary promise [L1522.032, older].

Founder Story, Owner and Photo

  • The About Us page must tell a story: who owns the business, why they are experts in the niche, and a photo of the owner or a person representing the company. Its purpose is to build trust before the customer buys [L1293.012].
  • Create a brand story or about page that explains who runs the store and why it sells these products. It does not need to be long, but it helps visitors connect with the store [L1325.013].
  • The About Us page should use a real photo of the founder, a staff member or someone using the product, plus a personal story about why the store exists. A generic stock template page does not build the same trust [L0910.019].
  • Showing the owner's face and telling the origin story builds loyalty, because customers trust a visible founder more than an anonymous store selling an item they have not made or checked [L0871.057].
  • Story formula: tell the brand's story in a few sentences covering where it started, what it is committed to, and the impact it wants to have. A clear purpose builds customer trust [L1071.018].
    • Example: a founder who wanted better tools for home bakers and committed to safer materials [L1071.018].
    • Example: a team that started a repair service and committed to reducing waste [L1071.018].

Founder-Led and Curator-Style Stores

Choosing the Role

  1. Decide whether the named person is the designer of the products or a curator who selects products from different brands [L0838.008].
  2. Make the person the visible face of the business, with the chosen role stated clearly [L0838.008].
  3. Build an About page with a photo of the person, why they are passionate about the product category, and why they design or select the products [L0838.008].
  4. Tell the founder story on the About page so it explains why the store was built [L0838.008].
  5. Use real photos of the person. If none exist, use a model or stock images, but turn the store into a real personal brand [L0838.008].

Personal Founder Brand Pattern

A personal founder brand follows this pattern: a named person appears in photos and videos, has a bio page telling their story, and the site content centres on that person. It works because visitors trust a face and a story more than an anonymous product catalogue [L0838.009].

  • Example 1: a skincare store where the founder appears in her own videos and writes a blog about her routine [L0838.009].
  • Example 2: a jewellery store where the owner, a self-taught designer, tells how she started making pieces for her friends [L0838.009].

Curator Product Pages

On a curator-style store with a named person, product pages should include that person's own short assessment of each item and why it is worth buying. This supports the expert role and gives visitors a reason to trust the selection [L0838.022].

Honesty and Accuracy Rules

  • Only list differentiators that are true today in the About Us prompt. Claims such as better warranties should be removed until the suppliers providing them are confirmed, because the AI will otherwise write them into the page as facts [L1048.009].
  • Never invent a founder story to fill the optional field. If you have no real story, delete the entire section [L1048.011].
  • A story must not be made up. It should tie the store to real products and the real niche audience [L0359.063].
  • AI-generated About Us and policy text is a starting draft. The store owner must review it before publishing and check it against real business terms, because the text is written to sound professional rather than to match supplier terms [L1048.012].
  • Production footage should match the brand's main selling point. Eco-focused brands can use factory footage and tech pack visuals as part of the story. Other brands should focus on the quality of the final product. Showing production works best when a factory offers video production or when you film the received product yourself in close-up detail [L1438.055].

Shopify Setup

Creating the Page

  1. Go to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Pages and click Add page [L0509.007].
  2. Name the page About Us [L0509.007].
  3. Keep the theme template on default page, because the About Us page does not need a contact form [L0509.007].
  4. Paste your About Us text into the content area and edit it [L0509.007].
  5. Click Save, then click View Page to check the result [L0509.007].

Adapting a Template Before Publishing

  1. Replace the generic brand name with your store name [L0509.008].
  2. Remove bold formatting from the store name if the rest of the text is not bold [L0509.008].
  3. Replace the city with the city where the store is based, or delete the location sentence if you have no base yet [L0509.008].
  4. Keep the worldwide shipping sentence only if you work with suppliers that ship internationally, and name the carriers you actually use (the template mentions FedEx, UPS and First Class Priority Mail). Otherwise change or delete it [L0509.008].
  5. Replace the years-in-business line with your own experience, or delete it if you have none [L0509.008].
  6. Read the whole text and remove any leftover template wording [L0509.008].

Leaving template text, the sample brand name or placeholder years on an About Us page makes the store look unfinished to a supplier reviewing it. Check every template sentence and either adapt or delete it [L0509.009].

AI Prompt for Generating the Page

Steps

  1. Copy the full About Us prompt from its copywriter role line to the end [L1048.007].
  2. Paste it into ChatGPT or another AI tool [L1048.007].
  3. Replace every example value in the student inputs section with your own details: brand name, website domain, primary niche or category, target customers (the ideal buyer), brand personality, city where the business is based, years in operation, what makes the store different, customer service philosophy, shipping and fulfillment style and founder story [L1048.007].
  4. Delete the optional years-in-operation field if the business is new, and delete the founder story section if you do not want one [L1048.007].
  5. Send the prompt and wait for the full output [L1048.007].
  6. Copy the generated About Us text, read it through, and edit anything that does not match your store before pasting it into Shopify [L1048.007].

Inputs

  • The brand personality input takes two to four traits, which can be picked from the suggested list or written in your own words. For example, a premium and fun positioning uses the two traits premium and fun [L1048.008].
  • Example for a new single-niche store that works with domestic suppliers: niche specialization as a differentiator, fast response times, an educated and focused service approach, long-term customer relationships, domestic suppliers, insured delivery and tracked shipping. Freight, professional freight and warranties are removed when they do not apply yet [L1048.010].

Prompt Text

Write an About Us page for an online shop that sells high-ticket goods sourced from domestic suppliers. Act as a seasoned eCommerce brand copywriter. The page should feel established, reliable, customer-first and premium, even if the business is new. Never use the word dropshipping, and avoid any startup or hobby tone, so it reads like a long-term retail brand. Write in English with a professional, confident, warm, trust-building and premium voice. Cover these parts in order: an opening that explains who the brand is, who it serves and what sets it apart; a mission and values section that explains why the company exists; a section on specialties, covering the product range, quality and supplier standards; a section on customer experience and the service promise; a section on logistics and fulfillment, covering shipping partners, reliability and nationwide reach; and a closing paragraph with a soft call to action. The total length must be 400 to 700 words. Use flowing prose, and only use bullet points if they clearly improve readability. The text must sound human, not templated. If an optional input is empty, leave it out completely and do not leave any bracketed text. Mention the City or Region in the opening and the closing only, not repeatedly. Inputs to work with: the brand name; an optional website domain; the niche or product category (for example luxury outdoor furniture, massage chairs, surfboards, carports); the target customer (for example homeowners upgrading their outdoor space, wellness-focused professionals, surfers); 2 to 4 personality traits, chosen from options such as Premium, Friendly, Rugged, Minimalist, Family-Focused, Technical, Luxury, Relaxed or Professional; the City or Region (for example San Diego, Austin, Denver, Charleston); years in operation, which can be stated as over 5 years, nearly a decade, or many years of combined industry experience if the brand is new; differentiators (for example US-only suppliers, hand-selected products, expert support, faster shipping, higher quality, better warranties, niche specialization); the service philosophy (for example white-glove support, fast response times, education-focused, long-term relationships); the shipping and fulfillment style (for example domestic suppliers, professional freight, insured delivery, tracked shipping); and an optional founder story about why this niche and this business. Output only the finished About Us copy, ready to paste into Shopify, with no placeholders, no explanations, and wording that is different every time it is generated.
