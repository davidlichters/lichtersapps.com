Product testing

  • Keep product tests focused on one product at a time. If you must test several products at once, use a separate campaign for each product and never share one campaign across products, so the spending, traffic and results of each product stay readable. [L1347.041]
  • A product's failure can be a useful learning experience. Choose products you can test quickly with limited spend, then improve the product or move to a new angle or market. Not every product needs to succeed in its first market. [L0552.043]
  • To identify the best-selling product in a new or existing store:
    1. Open the store's sales data and sort or filter sales by product. [L1267.005]
    2. Identify the product with the highest sales. [L1267.005]
    3. If you are not sure, copy the URL of that product page and record it with your onboarding answers. [L1267.005]
    4. If the store has just launched and has no sales, skip this step. [L1267.005]

Brand foundations

  • Older advice (Source B, 2021-22). Set up the brand and website in this order:
    1. Choose and settle the business name. [L1060.008]
    2. Develop the branding. [L1060.008]
    3. Register and secure the domain. [L1060.008]
    4. Build the demo website on Shopify. [L1060.008]
  • Advanced branding and social media strategies are later-stage refinements. They build on a basic brand and on channels that already work. [L0544.008]
  • Once basic needs are covered, make the business bolder in every area, not only in branding. Hiring, sourcing and warehousing decisions should also aim bigger. Every bold move carries risk, and the risk should be chosen deliberately. [L0334.009]
  • Case: a supplement brand focused on cognitive health, with open-source formulas and full supplier disclosure.
    • The founders named it Natural Stacks. "Natural" reflects that all ingredients are natural. "Stacks" reflects that the simple formulas are meant to be combined. [L0334.010]
    • The homepage was not the most visited page. The ingredient traceability and label pages were among the most visited pages on the site. [L0334.010]
    • Results: over $100,000 revenue in the first 100 days, over $1,000,000 in the first year, and more than $1,000,000 per year on Amazon US and UK as a separate channel. [L0334.010]
  • Frame a purchase as an asset that keeps producing value after payment, such as a training course that keeps raising earnings or an investment that keeps growing. Buyers who see the cost as an investment resist it less than buyers who see it as spending. Describe the ongoing return in terms the buyer can measure. [L1369.115]
  • Build an idea file of mailings and ads you receive, and a swipe file of strong samples. With them you can show a designer or copywriter the exact look and copy you want. Reusing proven patterns is faster than inventing new formats. [L1370.108]

Storefront and content

  • Required content for a static affiliate site:
    1. Static pages, such as an About Me page and any disclaimer pages. [L0514.007]
    2. A product page for the main product, with a personal experience, a case study and proof screenshots from your own or a friend's store. [L0514.007]
    3. Three to five blog posts that show authority. [L0514.007]
    4. Affiliate links placed in the pages and posts. [L0514.007]
    5. Opt-in forms to collect emails from visitors. [L0514.007]
  • An affiliate store built on imported products looks and acts like an e-commerce store, but it is not one. It should not receive paid traffic from the main traffic sources. Its useful role is retargeting visitors who already came to your dropship store. [L0923.024]
  • If no recognized brand exists, you can still build search traffic. Get approved for brands in similar categories and list their products. Then guide shoppers toward your own products or the artisan products you offer. [L0941.055]

Traffic

  • To test a new traffic source with a paid banner on a relevant niche website:
    1. Find sites that already have the target audience and show banner ads. [L0971.025]
    2. Ask about buying ad space. [L0971.025]
    3. Negotiate a free trial of about one week. [L0971.025]
    4. Send the banner image assets and a UTM-tagged link. [L0971.025]
    5. If sales come in, pay the ongoing rate. [L0971.025]

Email and post-purchase

  • Shoppers check the order tracking page more than any other post-purchase touchpoint. This makes it a high-value place for cross-sells and upsells. [L0955.049]
  • To build the first abandoned cart email, use this order:
    1. Put the logo at the top to build trust. [L0567.037]
    2. Add the big desire or USP headline. [L0567.037]
    3. Add the product block with each abandoned item's title, quantity and line total. [L0567.037]
    4. Add a short urgency or scarcity line. [L0567.037]
    5. Add the CTA buttons "Complete your order" and "Return to cart". [L0567.037]
    6. Add a "Why choose us" section with three objection blocks: a quality claim, local or family ownership, and the 30-day money-back guarantee. [L0567.037]
    7. Add a help line with the contact email. [L0567.037]
    8. Close with a final CTA and the brand's unique value points, such as personal service, free shipping and quality. [L0567.037]
    9. Remove the header and announcement bar if the logo and products already appear. Delete filler lines that add no value, such as lines that repeat that the cart is waiting. [L0567.037]

Business-to-business sales

  • To set up B2B for a store with business customers:
    1. Create a page called "To the Trade". [L1094.111]
    2. Link it in the header menu and the footer. [L1094.111]
    3. Mention on the page that the store sells business to business. [L1094.111]
    4. Add a contact form asking which products and what quantity the business is interested in. [L1094.111]
    5. Follow up on large volume orders with special pricing or a bulk incentive. [L1094.111]
    6. Add logos of past business customers to the trade page and the home page to build trust. [L1094.111]
  • B2B customers search the same way as consumers, so the main marketing does not need to change. For business customers, only the trade page, contact form and logos are added. [L1094.112]
  • Targeted B2B ads through industry publications or associations, such as banner or print ads, reach the right audience but convert less. Treat them as a later scaling tactic. [L1094.113]

Team structure

  • Build the brand team starting with three core roles: [L0260.001]
    • Senior Creative: develops brand strategy and documents. [L0260.001]
    • Content Creator: produces narratives across mediums. [L0260.001]
    • Social Media Manager: handles campaigns, analyzes trends and closes the audience feedback loop. [L0260.001]
  • Hire a Senior Creative (senior graphic designer, art director or creative director). This role can align the brand across the entire business, assemble specialized team members as needed, and develop brand cues and documents that guide market positioning. [L0260.002]
  • A Content Creator must produce narratives across different mediums while maintaining specialist-level quality in execution. [L0260.003]
  • A Social Media Manager quickly identifies and acts on platform trends, leverages audience reach, and feeds insights back to the Senior Creative team so the feedback loop closes. [L0260.004]

Seasonal planning

  • For a seasonal brand, plan the next season's photo shoot, creatives and designs in the off-season, before the selling season begins. Reinvest in that off-season period so the new content is ready when demand returns. For example, a winter brand reinvests in summer. [L0661.067]

  • Before running a Black Friday campaign, you need:

    • a proof of concept showing that the product is selling; [L0308.002]
    • profitable upsells in place, ideally with profitability; [L0308.002]
    • ready-made unique offers, such as free gifts. [L0308.002]

    These offers ring-fence the campaign from competition. [L0308.002]

AI and process

  • Some dropshipping programs add AI as a side tool, such as using ChatGPT for product descriptions. This is not the same as building the business process around AI. A process-level AI approach uses AI in product research, store building and creative production. [L1452.012]
