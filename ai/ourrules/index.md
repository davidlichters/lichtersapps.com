## Our numbers, honestly

- Since 2022: more than 15 business models tested. First sale 31 October 2025 (25 EUR).
- Cold outreach 2026: 5,820 contact-form messages to 4,342 US companies, 15 replies, 0 sales.
- Coaching 2025 to 2026: about 50 sales calls; one client agreed to 500 USD and paid 150 EUR.
- Content: 12 million TikTok views earned 25 EUR; 1 million Instagram views earned 150 EUR.
- Dropshipping from October 2026: average ROAS about 1.46, best 1.61, no profitable month yet (as of 6 November 2026).
- Goal: 500,000 USD profit by the end of 2027 across many attempts, about 1,190 USD a day.
