# RIMA / Liquidationsradar: the history and lessons of the era before Porchnap (Aug to Sep 2026)

> **When to read this:** before you plan any cold-outreach business, any B2B data product, any autonomous Claude loop, or before you quote a conversion rate. This is the full post-mortem of ~5 weeks, ~3,000 USD and ~1,100 git commits that ended with 0 paying customers. Almost every rule we work by today was paid for here.

Written 2026-11-07 from the private repo `rima` (615 Markdown files, commit log 2026-08-30 to 2026-11-01). Numbers marked **[measured]** were counted from logs, inboxes or bills. **[estimated]**, **[guess]** or [RATE] means the source itself said it was not measured. All dates are 2026 unless stated. Money is in EUR unless marked USD.
