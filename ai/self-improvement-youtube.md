# Start a self-improvement YouTube channel

I was 13. In 2023 I started watching Hamza Ahmed's self-improvement videos and decided to start my own channel in his style. Then I spent most of my time planning. My notes are full of YouTube guides: titles, thumbnails, music volume, how to speak with charisma. In January 2024 my plan was one 7 minute video plus three Shorts every single day. I copied a big creator's plan without the habit of finishing even one video a week. What I actually made: scripts for about two videos, one about a morning routine, the other about cold showers. That is where the channel ended. **Result: 0 EUR.**

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
