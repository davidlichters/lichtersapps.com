# High-ticket coaching

In November 2025 I started selling cheap 1:1 coaching for 60 EUR per week. By December 2025 I had earned 374 EUR in total. Then I moved to high-ticket coaching at around 1,400 to 2,500 EUR. My first big closed deal in March 2026 was 1,400 EUR. The person agreed, then stopped answering. I received 0 EUR. I signed up with a paid mentor for 1,400 EUR, paid part of it, and the rest turned into debt. I also ran an Instagram account with a made-up coach persona and fake numbers. **Result:** in May 2026 my own note said "no sales to date" for the high-ticket offer.

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
