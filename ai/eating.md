# Eating

Only eat food that already existed 30,000 years ago.

## The rule

Tick **e** when everything you ate today passes one question: could a person 30,000 years ago have eaten this?

- Yes: meat, fish, eggs, fruit, vegetables, berries, nuts, water.
- No: chips, sweets, fast food, soda, energy drinks, anything with a long ingredient list.
- Cook meat, fish and eggs. Steak medium rare is fine. Raw is not part of the rule.

## Why

This is our own rule, not a study result. It is simple: no counting, no weighing, one question per meal. Most food that fails it is food made to make you eat more of it. More on the research: https://lichtersapps.com/ai/research.md

## How to start (at 13)

1. **Ask whoever buys the food.** "Can we get more meat, eggs and fruit, and fewer snacks?"
2. **Pack your own food for school.** Fruit, eggs, leftovers from dinner.
3. **Keep a water bottle with you.** Fill it before school and before training.

## Mistakes

- **Starving yourself.** The rule is about what you eat, not how little. Eat until you are full.
- **Diet rules and calorie counting.** Not needed. If eating or your weight worries you, talk to a parent or doctor.
