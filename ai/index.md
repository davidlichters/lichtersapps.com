# Lichters

> Lichters is a free habit app that helps teens (13-19) build a happy life long-term with six daily habits.

Lichters is completely free: no ads, nothing to buy. It was made by David, a German teen who started trying businesses at 12.

## The method

The goal is a happy whole life, not just happy teen years. The six habits buy you freedom. Don't wait for it to start living. Start now: notice it all and be grateful; trips into nature with a few friends: snow, terrain, space; real friends who truly know you, and a family you stay close to; teach one person something you're good at; build something that helps people; good food, music, dancing; document it all. Freedom brings later: a partner who is your favorite person, kids and dogs; a home and a car you love; control over your time, work and place; trips to learn new things, like skydiving; things that cost money, like skiing and the best food. Six daily habits, each either ticked today or left empty. Each habit is a year grid (7 rows for weekdays, about 52 columns for weeks) instead of a streak that falls to zero. In a UCL study a habit took about 66 days on average to feel automatic, and single skipped days did not hurt it. The six cannot be edited, so hard ones never get dropped. Every habit gets a fixed time and place.

**Missed one day or several?** Do not restart from zero. Tick today, and try never to miss twice in a row. Make a tiny bad-day version (a 30-minute walk, 20 minutes of business) and write down why you missed. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/keep-going.md

**If everything has felt pointless for weeks, or you think about hurting yourself,** talk to a trusted adult today or call a crisis line in your country. That comes before any habit.

## The six habits

The grids show only letters (f e b t o s).

**f, Friends.** Tick after 1 hour in the same room with friends or family, no scrolling. Dinner with your parents counts. Texts, gaming chats and video calls do not. Why: the Harvard Study of Adult Development found people with the warmest relationships stayed healthiest and lived longest. No friends at school yet? Ask "Can I sit here?", say hi to 3 new people a day, remember names and come back, join one club. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/friends.md

**e, Eating.** Tick when everything you ate today already existed 30,000 years ago: meat, fish, eggs, fruit, vegetables, nuts. No factory food, no soda. This is our own rule, not a study result. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/eating.md

**b, Business.** Tick after 5+ focused, timed hours on your own business, phone in another room. Building, selling and talking to customers count; watching business videos does not. Why: that is about 1,800 hours a year, and at 13 you can fail five times and still be 18. Start: write "In these 5 hours I will ___". Example split: 1 hour finding customers, 3 building or selling, 1 talking to people. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/business.md

**t, Training.** Tick after 45 minutes of gym or sport; on rest days, a 30-minute walk. Home workouts count; cycling to school or PE only if it was 45 hard minutes. Why: among over a million Swedish teen boys, the strongest had a 20 to 35% lower risk of dying before 55. Start: one fixed slot, log weight and reps, beat one number next time. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/training.md

**o, Offline.** Tick when social media feeds total under 30 minutes today, checked in Screen Time. YouTube Shorts and recommended videos count as feed; one video you searched for to learn something does not. Why: students limited to about 30 minutes a day felt less lonely and less depressed (2018). Start: a 30-minute app limit, a parent holds the passcode. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/offline.md

**s, Sleep.** Stop using your phone 10 hours before you wake up, and be asleep at least 8 hours before. If you are skipping school, wake up around 30 minutes before sunrise. Tick when the phone goes away. Why: US sleep doctors recommend 8 to 10 hours for ages 13 to 18. Start: out of all alarms I tested, Sleep Cycle wins (airplane mode, face down, out of reach), one wake time, weekends too. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/sleep.md

**A school day with all six:** up at 7:00, lunch with friends phones away (f), fruit or vegetables at every meal, water (e), 14:00 to 17:00 and 19:30 to 21:30 (b, 5 hours), 17:00 sport (t), feeds only after the ticks (o), 21:30 phone out of the bedroom (s).

## Attitudes

Not ticked, decided once. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/attitudes.md

1. **No alcohol, nicotine, vapes, weed or betting.** Not even once at a party: a developing brain gets hooked faster. Have one sentence ready: "No thanks, I train tomorrow."
2. **Ticks first, then feeds and games.**
3. **Ignore the crowd. Listen to the few you respect.**
4. **Money buys freedom, not status.**
5. **Start early. Keep going for years.**

## Check-ins

With a coach, friend or AI, two minutes a day: which habits were ticked, what blocked each miss, and one next action with a time and place. Lower the plan, never the definition of done. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/coaching.md

## Common questions

- **No business yet. Can I tick b?** Yes, if the 5+ hours go into finding a problem people pay to solve and asking them.
- **No time for 5 hours on school days?** School 6, sleep 9, training 2, meals 1: 6 left, enough for 5. Free periods count. If school is full, school comes first. Never cut sleep for it.
- **Why no meditation?** Meditate privately if you like. The MYRIAD trial (about 8,400 UK students) found school mindfulness lessons did no better than normal lessons.

More (cost, data, gratitude, waking at 5): https://lichtersapps.com/ai/faq.md

## Starting a business as a teen

- **First business with no money:** an offer that needs no cash, like thumbnails for small YouTubers, mowing lawns on your street or selling old stuff on eBay. Find the first 10 customers by hand. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/business-start.md
- **Under 18:** payment accounts, store accounts and most freelance sites need an adult owner. A parent has to own the account; never fake your age.
- **Dropshipping at 14?** Not as a first business. Shopify Payments, Stripe and Meta ad accounts need an adult, and ads cost money before you know anyone buys. Start with reselling. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/dropshipping.md
- **Fiverr?** Only after you are good at one skill: make 5 to 10 samples first, then one narrow offer. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/fiverr.md
- **Buy a guru course?** Start free. Pay only for a gap you can name ("my videos get 200 views and I don't know why"). Countdowns and "only today" are warning signs. Under 18, talk to your parents first. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/mentors-and-communities.md
- **Cold messages to companies?** Mass messages failed and can be illegal. Pick 20 to 50 businesses that fit, send 5 to 10 personal messages a day, follow up at most twice. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/cold-outreach.md
- **Is affiliate marketing a scam?** No, affiliate links are normal and legal. The scams are courses promising fast money from them. It only pays once you have an audience. https://lichtersapps.com/ai/affiliate-marketing.md
