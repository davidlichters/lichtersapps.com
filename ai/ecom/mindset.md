# Mindset and planning

> Pick one model, plan in short blocks, do a few important tasks every day, judge by data, and keep going through the bad weeks, because most people quit right before it works.

## Rules

1. Commit to one business model and one strategy, and give it 100% of your energy instead of splitting it across several projects.
2. Stop learning endlessly: in about 90% of cases you already know the next step, so do it instead of watching more videos.
3. Launch fast: a slow beginner needs two weeks to put ads live for one product, a fast one launches in two days and then acts on the data.
4. Treat ad spend as buying data, for example starting a test at 50 a day.
5. Do not expect a winner in your first 3 or 4 products; one 2026 example failed with more than 12 products before the first winner.
6. Decide with numbers, not feelings, and never switch off a test on day one just because the first cost per purchase looks bad.
7. Write down small, reachable goals; a fixed dream number like 1k a day or 10k a month makes you fragile when a bad day comes.
8. Keep your daily to-do list short: one planner works with no more than 3 items a day and 5 a week, and a 15-item list does not work.
9. Start each day with your single most important task and finish it first.
10. Never chase perfection: there is no perfect niche or store, and waiting for one makes you miss chances.
11. Talk to real customers; their questions show you the real bottleneck.

## Steps

1. **Write your goals.** Note your income goal and your main goal for the next 90 days. Keep them small enough to reach.
2. **Plan 90 days.** A 2026 roadmap splits them like this: days 1-14 basics, days 15-30 finding products, days 31-45 building the store, days 45-60 making creatives, days 60-75 ads go live, days 75-90 improving and scaling.
3. **Build in order.** Finish each module's task before the next one.
4. **Set up a daily routine.** Each evening, write the to-do list for tomorrow on paper or in any calendar or app. Each night, note: did you finish 100% of the tasks (yes or no), what stopped you, what went well, a focus score from 1 to 10, and your top 3 priorities for tomorrow.
5. **Track the tasks.** Aim for at least 75% done in your tracker and give yourself a small reward when you reach it.
6. **Plan the week.** Block about 15 minutes once a week to set the weekly list, and about 5 minutes a day for the daily list. Put both as repeating events in your calendar.
7. **When you hit a wall:** check the dashboard, dig deeper into customer research, diagnose instead of panic, and remind yourself what you are building.
8. **Review.** Look back every quarter and set new 90-day goals; review the big picture at least once a year.

## Key numbers

- **90 days:** enough to see progress, but only if you stay consistent (2026 roadmap).
- **After 90 days** you should own a brand and one tested product, have 15 to 30 ad creatives live, the skill to read data, and maybe your first profitable days.
- **Week 3:** when most students usually quit.
- **1 new product per day for 3 days:** a 2026 push to get tests live.
- **50 a day:** starting ad budget seen as "buying data" (2026).
- **75%:** minimum task completion rate to aim for in your daily tracker.
- **30 minutes:** minimum time per task in one 2026 daily tracker; you can double or triple it if you can.
- **3 items a day, 5 a week:** maximum to-do list length used by one planner (2021-22, still useful).
- **3 to 4 weeks:** how long a new habit usually takes to stick if you repeat it after every trigger (2021-22).
- **No more than 5 goals** for a 6-month plan, and no more than 7 big 90-day goals at once (2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- Spending weeks on logos, colors and a brand story for a short trend product.
- Watching videos and scrolling all day because it feels productive.
- Testing products forever without committing, because testing feels safe.
- Panicking when sales stall, an ad account gets blocked or payouts are paused; problems are normal in this business.
- Turning off a campaign after one day of bad numbers.
- Long to-do lists full of small jobs like answering emails.
- Spending time on what you are already good at instead of fixing your weakest area.

## FAQ

**How long until I make money?**
It can take a week, a month, a few months or a year. Plan for months of work, not a few days.

**Is it normal to fail many products first?**
Yes. One student had 8 months without success and then made 67,000 dollars in one month.

**Can I start without experience?**
Yes. You get better only by launching, so start fast and learn from real data.

**Can I do this next to school or a job?**
Yes, it can be built on the side. With 1 hour a day you move slower than someone with 4, and that is fine.

**Why does the start feel so slow?**
Getting from 0 to 500 a day in sales is much harder than going from 500 a day to 50k months. The first stretch is the hardest part.

**How do I handle the urge to give up?**
Once a week, note when you almost stopped, what set it off and how you reacted. Save the notes and reread them.

**Should I keep checking my store app all day?**
No. Opening the sales app dozens of times a day does not bring orders faster. Spend that time on new creatives and customer service.

**How big should my goals be?**
Long-term pictures can be ambitious; goals under one year should be realistic, specific and measurable.

**How much does mindset matter?**
One 2026 lesson says about 80% of success. Staying calm and executing through problems is the key skill.

**Who needs to own the accounts if I am under 18?**
Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so a parent. Check the rules in your country.

## Where courses disagree

- **Timeline to launch:** a 2026 lesson says some people finish the whole roadmap in about a week; older lessons (2021-22, high-ticket model, older advice) said launching within about 30 days is possible, and taking longer is fine.
- **Size of goals:** 2026 lessons say small, reachable goals; a 2021-22 lesson says goals that scare you a bit are usually the right size.
- **Planning horizon:** 2026 lessons work in 90-day blocks; older advice (2021-22) added a 10-year vision and a 180-day plan.
