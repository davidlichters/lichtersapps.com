### Category mapping and topical authority

- A 12-month content plan should aim at topical authority: becoming the go-to source for one subject area. Topical authority is built over time through a connected set of pages, not single articles. [L1389, 2025-26]
- One product can belong to several categories at once. A men's black running short, for example, fits men's running shorts, black running shorts, and running shorts with pockets. A category for each relevant search variant lets the store target more specific keywords and reach shoppers who search for exactly that item. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Broad head terms, such as "running shorts", face heavy competition and often attract shoppers with a different intent, such as a woman searching for women's products. Specific category terms are easier to rank for faster and convert better, which brings sales sooner. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Build a mix of broad parent categories and narrower niche categories, with narrow ones based on specific use cases. Furniture example: sectional categories for piece count (two-piece, three-piece, four-piece), color (brown, leather, black, canvas, white), and room (living room sectionals). A single product can sit in several of these at once. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Attribute categories pair a product kind with one or more traits, such as shade, cut, or purpose. In clothing, the structure runs gender, then product, then trait:
  - men's running shorts with pockets
  - men's running shorts with a lining
  - men's running shorts without a lining
  - men's compression running shorts
  - women's running shorts with a high waist

  For a swimwear shop, the same structure yields women's high-waisted swim bottoms with ruching and men's quick-dry board shorts with a zip pocket. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Granular category pages capture shoppers with very specific searches, and these shoppers are more likely to buy. Most competitors do not build pages at this level of detail, so the store gains a ranking and sales advantage. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Blog articles or Shopify pages can both serve as topical authority content, and either format works. Each article should link to the relevant collection pages, since that internal linking is how the collections are ranked. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Hybrid categories, such as a non-toxic version of a product, are only worth creating if people actually search for them on Google. SEO captures existing demand rather than creating it, so a hybrid category without search volume will gain little traction. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Claim-type modifiers, such as non-toxic or PFAS-free, can be tested as hybrid categories at the broad product level, for example "non-toxic running clothes", rather than at a narrow level such as "non-toxic running tights". Narrow claim-plus-product combinations often show too little monthly search volume on Google, so check the volume before creating them. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Map out every category you can think of, including an authority cluster for each, while skipping top-of-funnel and AI Overview-type queries. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Leave out top-of-funnel terms that everyone already understands, such as the basic product name in its most obvious form. Also leave out query types that an AI Overview would likely answer directly, because articles on those queries rarely earn clicks. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Put the exclusion rules into the prompt from the start, such as telling the AI to skip AI Overview-type queries and top-of-funnel terms. Do not let the AI generate every query and then delete the unwanted ones afterward. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Give the AI a list of direct competitors when building the map, since better input produces better output and speeds up the work. Choose competitors you actually want to model your categories on. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Firecrawl can scrape competitor websites so that their category structure and page content can be analyzed. The scraped data serves as input for mapping the competitor's categories. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Before creating pages, remove any category you do not stock or sell. Example: if the store does not sell high-waisted running leggings, drop that category from the map. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Once the category list is cleaned, put the final map into one shared document in a fixed format, so the whole team can read and review it before any collection page copy is written. This document is the handover point between the category map and the content work. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Category mapping procedure:
  1. Map out every category you can think of, including an authority cluster for each one, while skipping top-of-funnel and AI Overview-type queries.
  2. Give the AI your chosen competitors and have it map the categories against them.
  3. Refine and trim the map inside the chat.
  4. Validate each category's search demand with a keyword tool.
  5. Remove the categories you do not sell.
  6. Export the cleaned map.
  7. Create a collection page for each remaining category.
  8. Create blog articles or Shopify pages that link to these collections to build topical authority.
  [L1389, 2025-26]
- AI-generated map exports can contain multiple tabs and hundreds of rows with hallucinated, wrong, or irrelevant entries. Do the analysis and trimming inside the chat first, then export a cleaned version instead of reviewing a raw spreadsheet. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Case, hypothetical running brand: the brand had hundreds of non-toxic SKUs across all running subcategories, and the category mapping was tested on them. The competitors came from an athleisure strategy built a month earlier, with Happier Earth and Allware named as the main examples, and the AI was asked to map categories against them. The lesson reports no traffic or sales result for this map, only the planning setup. [L1389, 2025-26]

### Pitfalls

- Do not optimize one page around a single item from a set of similar topics, such as one fun fact out of many. Create separate content for each item instead, because a rewrite around one sub-topic can move rankings backward. [L1269, 2025-26]
- Common mistake: keyword stuffing across many pages. Keep one dedicated page per keyword and use the rest of the list on other pages. [L0881, 2021-22; L0879, 2021-22]

#### Further details

- Product pages should rank for product-specific terms such as a model name, but not for broad category terms that are very hard to rank for on a product page. Broad terms are targeted with separate content pages that link to the product page. [L0396, 2021-22]
