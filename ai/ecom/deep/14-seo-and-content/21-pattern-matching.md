### Pattern Matching

#### Matching formats that already work

- Research-based content process that avoids copying competitors:
  1. Find the most popular content of competitors, such as blog posts with the most comments or product pages with strong descriptions.
  2. Describe the intro, the body and the conclusion of that piece in one sentence each.
  3. Have the writer start from scratch with that framework, using their own words. [L0467, 2019]
- Match the format Google returns for your keyword. Build guides, videos and reviews that mirror the top formats and topics. [L1274, 2018]
- Pattern for content aimed at solution-aware and problem-aware buyers: a broad educational guide titled as the ultimate guide to a product category for a specific buyer type. It explains the buyer's needs, lists the characteristics to look for and recommends a short set of specific products that meet them. Two new examples: *The Complete Guide to Choosing a First Road Bike for Commuters* and *The Ultimate Guide to Picking a Beginner Espresso Machine for Small Kitchens*. [L1117, 2021-22]
- Page types to build on the store for SEO, in this order:
  1. Category collection page with buying-intent keywords, turned into a landing page with your product at the top.
  2. Best-of page for the category with the current year in the title.
  3. Listicle, for example a top seven list for the current year.
  4. Generic category page.
  5. For-sale page.
  6. Top seven page.
  7. Brand versus brand comparison pages. [L0768, 2021-22]
- Brand versus brand comparison pages are covered under Content Types and Formats above. [L0768, 2021-22]
- Style guides lead into product lists, so the top products for a themed room sit after the guide. [L0719, 2021-22]

### The Content Planner

#### Planner structure

- The content planner is the central sheet where content ideas, research status and promotion channels are recorded for each content piece. [L0381, 2021-22]
- The BSGR Content Planner is a spreadsheet with one row per content piece and one column per planning field. Keep the full multi-month list in this single sheet so the status and owner of every piece is visible in one place. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Columns of the BSGR Content Planner and what each one records:

| Column | What to record |
|---|---|
| Content Topic/Title | The working title of the planned piece |
| Overview | What the piece should teach or do for the reader |
| Target Keyword + Similar Terms | The main search phrase plus related search terms |
| Similar Online Content | Existing pieces that already cover the topic |
| Assets Required | Images, graphs, PDFs and other materials the piece needs |
| Sources, Stats & Links | Where statistics, images and external links come from |
| Promotion Plan | The channels the piece will be promoted through |
| Who Is Producing This Content? | The writer, freelancer, VA or self responsible for the piece |
| Additional Comments | Any other notes the producer or you need to track | [L0384, 2021-22]

- Content planner layout: a spreadsheet such as Excel or Google Sheets, or a project tool such as Trello. One row per content idea.

| Content Topic/Title | Overview | Target Keyword + Similar Terms | Similar Online Content | Assets Required | Promotion Plan | Who Is Producing This Content? | Additional Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Working title, may be broad at first | Purpose of the piece, what it should do, its goal | Primary keyword from keyword research plus synonyms, variations and similar terms | URLs of ranking pages and high-sharing pieces found in research | Materials the piece needs | How the piece will be promoted | Name of the writer, recorder or producer | Any other notes | [L0386, 2021-22]

- Example of one filled planner row for a home espresso equipment shop: working title *How to choose a grinder as a first-time espresso buyer*, overview of the features the reader needs to judge, main keyword *espresso grinder for beginners* plus close variants, and existing buyer guides listed as similar content. Assets could be a comparison graphic and a checklist PDF. Sources could be manufacturer spec sheets and a credited stock photo. Promotion runs through social posts, the email list and search text ads. The producer is a freelance writer, with a Fiverr designer for the graphic. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Example of a filled content planner row for a fictional fishing gear shop: the title is *Best Starter Fishing Rods Guide*. The overview says it should help first-time anglers choose a rod and understand key specs before buying. The target keyword is *starter fishing rod*, with similar terms such as *beginner fishing rod* and *best rod for beginners*. The similar online content column holds the URLs of the top five ranking pages plus any high-sharing pieces found in BuzzSumo. [L0386, 2021-22]
- Content plan size formula: months of planning multiplied by content pieces per month equals the number of ideas to fill in. Example: six months at two pieces per month gives twelve content ideas, which can be filled in at any time in advance.

  Ideas = months of planning × pieces per month [L0386, 2021-22]

#### Filling in the planner

- A content title can start as a broad topic and be refined later. The overview should state who the content is for, what it should teach or achieve, and the goal of the piece. [L0386, 2021-22]
- Target keyword and similar online content are recorded in this order:
  1. Take the primary target keyword from keyword research and record it.
  2. Add the similar terms, close variations and synonyms of that keyword, and plan to include them in the content.
  3. Search the keyword in Google and record the URLs of ranking content pieces.
  4. Check social share data for the topic with BuzzSumo or a similar tool and record strong pieces.
  5. Check competitor websites for content aimed at the same audience and record it. [L0386, 2021-22]
- Research phase output, in order:
  1. Content topic and title.
  2. Overview with the content's purpose and goal.
  3. Target search terms the piece might rank for.
  4. Similar online content that already exists for those terms. [L0386, 2021-22]
- Anyone who produces content must review all recorded similar online content before writing, recording or planning it, and their version must be better than that content. Share the planner with every writer or producer along with this instruction. [L0386, 2021-22]
- Record every asset a piece needs before production starts, such as images, graphs and PDFs, together with where each asset will come from. This lets whoever produces the piece find everything without asking. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Stock photo platforms such as Unsplash and Adobe Stock can supply images for content. Any third-party image used in a piece needs its source recorded and attribution given in the piece itself. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Record the statistics you plan to cite and the existing content you plan to link to in the planner, backed by enough research to support the idea. Empty fields are acceptable early on if the sources are not yet known. [L0384, 2021-22]
- For each planned content piece, choose three to four promotion channels and log them in the content planner. [L0381, 2021-22]

### Setup Sequence

#### Gameplan

- Simple content marketing gameplan, in order:
  1. Decide the content format: written content, video content, or a combination of both.
  2. Plan your first 12 content ideas (topics) in your content planning document.
  3. Decide how and by whom the content is produced. For written content, find a content writer. For video, find an editor and, if someone else records, a person to do the recording.
  4. Produce two quality content pieces per month, continuously and without an end date.
  5. After 12 months of consistent work, either increase output above two pieces per month (for example, add one more per month) or hire another person.
  6. Promote each content piece for at least two weeks, ideally up to 30 days, and build backlinks to content pieces where it is relevant. [L0383, 2021-22]
- Content marketing action plan, in order:
  1. Fill in the attention matrix document. It does not have to be finished in one month, but make a substantial dent.
  2. Do keyword research, or check whether it was already done. If not, do it yourself or outsource it.
  3. Enter the first 12 content ideas into the content planner, which covers about six months of content at two pieces per month.
  4. Complete research for the first four content ideas in the planner.
  5. Every two months, block up to 30 minutes in a calendar to research the next four ideas and add more content ideas, so about six months stay planned ahead.
  6. Post a job to find a content producer, such as a writer or video editor. Give candidates a test, hire one, and produce two quality pieces of content per month.
  7. For each planned content piece, choose three to four promotion channels and record them in the content planner. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Action steps to set up the content plan:
  1. Fill the planner with 12 content ideas that mix problem-solving and entertaining topics suited to your market or niche.
  2. Complete the research columns for the first four pieces.
  3. Add a calendar entry for two months from now to research the next four pieces.
  4. Make sure a producer is ready before content production starts. Keyword research is done alongside these steps. [L0384, 2021-22]

#### Planning horizon

- Keep a rolling plan of about six months of content in the content planner at all times, so new ideas are always added before the existing schedule runs out. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Plan 12 content ideas in the planner first. That equals about six months of content at two pieces per month. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Complete keyword and content research for the first four content ideas right after planning them, before production starts. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Plan topics six months ahead, and research them in batches of four. Do not research all six months in one sitting, because that takes too long. Schedule the next research session every two months, then research the following four pieces before their production window starts. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Every two-month research review should be a fixed calendar entry that is actually kept. Failing to schedule content research and planning in a calendar or management tool leads to missed updates. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Content should be produced from a researched, documented plan, so every planned piece should already have some research behind it. [L0386, 2021-22]

### Production System

#### Keyword-led weekly system

- Content marketing system with seven steps, repeated for each keyword every week:
  1. Define the target market and write the customer profile.
  2. Set up an email opt-in on the store.
  3. Build a keyword list from informational queries.
  4. Write a blog article for one keyword.
  5. Create a custom image for that article that contains the keyword and matches the brand.
  6. Write a plain text email that drives traffic to the article.
  7. Upload the article to the blog, then send the email to the list.
  [L0380, 2014]
- Create a separate blog for the store and publish each article as its own page. The email then links directly to that article page instead of the homepage. [L0380, 2014]
- Keyword-based content is produced in this order:
  1. Research the keywords yourself.
  2. Create the content. Most beginners should write it themselves. Later, collaborate with niche influencers who can contribute pieces, or hire an expert.
  3. Make basic graphics yourself in Canva, or outsource images on Fiverr.
  [L1120, 2024+]

#### Article workflow and quality control

- Content production workflow from start to finish:
  1. Choose keywords with the Ahrefs Content Gap method and vet them with RAW.
  2. Create the article in the Google Docs template and brief the writer with the brand guideline.
  3. Have the writer complete the article with an editor.
  4. Edit the article with a native English editor.
  5. Write the headline and meta description, often with a sales copywriter.
  6. Create a custom image with the keyword in its alt tag.
  7. Upload the article and check its SEO with a plugin such as Yoast.
  8. Publish the article.
  9. Track the article's email signups each month and use the best performers to find similar keywords.
  [L0382, 2017]
- Templates for customer surveys, blog post creation guidelines, podcast keyword research and podcast content processes systematise outsourcing and maintain quality at scale. [L0326, 2014]
- Writer brand guideline rules are covered under Writers and Outsourcing below. [L0382, 2017]

### Visual Assets and AI Assistance

- Stock photo platforms such as Unsplash and Adobe Stock can supply images for content. Record the source and give attribution in the piece. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Content created from a blog post can be turned into an infographic with ChatGPT. Steps:
  1. Copy the URL of the blog post from the store's blog.
  2. In ChatGPT, send the first prompt for an infographic outline together with the blog URL.
  3. Review the outline ChatGPT creates and tell it what to add or remove.
  4. Send the second prompt to turn the approved outline into an infographic image.
  5. Ask for revisions or a different colour scheme, such as the store's brand colours, if needed.
  6. Download the finished image by clicking it.
  [L1075, 2025+]
- Prompt for the infographic workflow:

```
Step 1: Please create an info graphic based on this blog post: [paste the blog post URL here]. Create an outline of the text that will appear on the infographic first.
Step 2 (after reviewing and approving the outline): Please turn this into an info graphic image. Keep all the points in the approved outline, make the main headings bold, and use the color scheme of [brand name / website colors].
```

[L1075, 2025+]

- Mixing text, images and video on each blog post suits readers who prefer different formats. [L0467, 2019]
- Every blog post should include text, images and a video, with the images and video placed within the text. Videos should come from the suppliers or the store owner. [L0467, 2019]
- Use a custom image for each article, with the keyword in the alt tag. [L0382, 2017]

### Writers and Outsourcing

#### Who can write

- Professional writing ability, marketing expertise or native English fluency are not requirements for content creation. These skills are learnable over time or outsourceable to freelancers, which removes a psychological barrier that stops many from starting a content strategy. [L0226, 2017]
- Keep research and planning in-house: decide which article titles, About Us format and product page elements the site needs. Outsource only the writing, because the owner must control the content direction. [L0467, 2019]
- Start content writer search on Upwork and specialised platforms, but recognise that great general writing does not produce great dropshipping content. Pivot to Facebook groups for niche enthusiasts who understand your category. They write with insider passion and knowledge that generic writers lack. [L0249, 2022]
- When outsourcing content writing, find niche enthusiasts in Facebook groups rather than professional writers. Niche passion produces better content for dropshipping stores than generic writing skill. [L0249, 2022]
- If the writer has no niche knowledge, find an experienced writer in niche communities such as Reddit or Facebook groups and post a paid gig request there. (2023) [L0855, 2023]
- ProBlogger has a jobs board where content writers can be hired for blog and written content. Its community is mostly full-time freelance bloggers, usually native English speakers, which gives better applicants than general freelance platforms. [L0379, 2021-22]

#### Finding writers step by step

- Finding writers for a broad, non-technical niche:
  1. Post a content writer job on Upwork with the outline of the pieces you want.
  2. Send the same piece brief to at least three writers.
  3. Compare the three samples and pick the writer whose voice fits best.
  4. Send the remaining work to that writer. [L0467, 2019]
- Finding writers for a technical or specialised niche:
  1. Search the niche term on Facebook and open the Groups results.
  2. Join all relevant groups.
  3. Identify members who post often and whose posts get many likes and comments.
  4. Send them a private message with an offer to write articles for your site. [L0467, 2019]
- Outreach template for a niche group member:

```
Hi [Name], my name is [Your name] from [your store domain]. I saw your posts in the [Facebook group name] group, and the feedback they get from the members is great. We are creating new content for our website, and I would like to know whether you would be interested in writing a few articles for us or [other content you need]. If you are interested, I can send you the outline and the list of article titles we need, and we can take it from there.
```

[L0467, 2019]

#### Pricing and cost benchmarks

- Freelance blog writers on Upwork were priced at $20-40 per article. [L0268, 2020]
- Hire a content writer on Upwork at $20-40 per article, or use voice-to-text with a virtual assistant to write articles. [L0268, 2020]
- A professionally written and Google-ranked blog article budget is about $100. [L0280, undated]
- A blog post priced at $500 is an example of a fair freelance price for quality written content. A comparable piece can be obtained for $250 if the writer's quality is proven. [L0379, 2021-22]

#### Standards for outsourced writing

- Do not use PLR (Private Label Rights) content. It is rewritten and republished everywhere, and you need unique content for your business. [L0268, 2020]
- For a US supplement store, article writers must not claim that a product cures a disease, because readers and the FDA will not accept such claims. Put this prohibition in the ground rules of the brand guideline given to writers. [L0382, 2017]
- A writer brand guideline can set key factors per article, such as a minimum of 1,000 words, three links to external sites, and the target keyword used a fixed number of times, for example three. Editors use the same list to check each draft. [L0382, 2017]
- Have the writer complete the article, then have a native English editor edit it. [L0382, 2017]
- Have the person producing the content review all similar online content before writing, and make their version better. Share the planner with them. [L0386, 2021-22]
- A virtual assistant can build the attention matrix and receive the planner. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Hire a content producer such as a writer or video editor by posting a job, giving candidates a test, hiring one, and producing two quality pieces of content per month. [L0381, 2021-22]
- For written content, find a content writer. For video, find an editor and, if someone else records, a person to do the recording. Make sure a producer is ready before production starts. [L0383, 2021-22]
- Beginners should write their own content first. Later, collaborate with niche influencers who can contribute pieces, or hire an expert. [L1120, 2024+]
- Example of a designer partner: a Fiverr designer makes the graphic for the freelance writer's piece. [L0384, 2021-22]
- For a beginner, a realistic minimum is two high-quality content pieces per month. Publishing one or two pieces per week is not realistic, and it also makes it hard to promote each piece properly. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Increase publishing frequency above two pieces per month only when you already have experience producing content and can keep it genuinely valuable. Without that experience, do not overreach. [L0384, 2021-22]

### Cadence and Volume

- Content marketing only builds results when it is published consistently. Irregular output loses momentum, while steady output compounds over time. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Produce two high-quality content pieces per month as an ongoing target, not as a one-off launch goal. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Target a minimum of two high-quality content pieces per month as an ongoing baseline, not as a campaign with an end date. [L0383, 2021-22]
- Publish about two high-quality content pieces per month. Daily publishing is not needed for a high-ticket dropshipping business, and posting more low-quality content adds no value. Quality matters more than quantity. [L0414, 2021-22]
- A content marketing plan of two high-quality pieces of content per month is a workable publishing rhythm. [L0943, 2021-22]
- Start publishing blog posts once every two to three weeks: publish 2-4 articles per week consistently rather than bulk publishing. [L0226, 2017]
- A blog needs about one quality post per week or every other week that is valuable to the reader. More posts do not help if the quality is low. (2025) [L0857, 2025]
- There is no fixed minimum or maximum for content articles per month. One high-quality piece per month can be a workable start, provided it is better than the existing results for its target search terms. (2023) [L0970, 2023]
- Blog posting frequency: every other week is effective. [L0272, 2022]
- Publishing more content on a fixed weekly schedule does not automatically improve rankings. SEO gains for a new site usually build slowly, often over several months. [L1274, 2018]
- Evergreen keywords are optimised once on existing pages. Trending keywords can justify one or two new blog posts per month. [L1120, 2024+]
- Move from two pieces per month to more after 12 months of consistent work, for example by adding one more per month, or hire another person. [L0383, 2021-22]

### Distribution and Promotion

- Promote each content piece for at least two weeks, ideally up to 30 days, and build backlinks to the content pieces where it is relevant. [L0383, 2021-22]
- For each content piece, choose three to four promotion channels and log them in the planner. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Content is one part of a connected marketing system. The same pieces feed email, SEO, link building and retargeting, so each piece should be promoted through those channels. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Each piece should have a place on the store's website, so it can later be reused for link building and SEO. [L0381, 2021-22]
- Publish three to five blog posts on a new affiliate site before buying traffic, so you have something to link to when you share the site elsewhere. [L0514, 2015]
- Content on social media can come from the blog, so the blog provides material for social posts. [L0835, 2023]

### Holiday and Seasonal Content

- Start publishing blog content for the promotion periods now. Target keywords in the form *niche* black friday deals and *niche* cyber monday deals, with the niche name in place of niche. This content should be live before the sale dates. (2025) [L1222, 2025+]
- Publish keyword-targeted blog posts for each holiday offer in the coming month, using the pattern [niche] + holiday deal, for example Black Friday deals or Cyber Monday deals. Publish early so Google can index and rank them before the holiday searches peak. [L1224, 2025+]
- Each holiday blog post should explain how to get the best deals for that holiday, which offers are available, which products they apply to, and how to take advantage of them. [L1224, 2025+]
- A page built for a time-limited event, such as a homebrewing trade show, can rank for the event name in search. After the promotion ends, keep the page as evergreen content and consider an affiliate arrangement with the organiser for ticket sales, or a note that the store attends each year. [L1204, 2022]
