### Outsourcing, agencies and ownership

- Before hiring SEO help from freelancers or agencies, cover the basic on-page SEO lesson first, which takes about two and a half hours. [L0429, 2022]
- For these store types, simple on-page techniques are usually enough. Outsource only the specific tasks you still want done. Avoid broad e-commerce SEO services, which often cost a lot for few results. [L0429, 2022]
- Keyword research can be outsourced in raw form, but the owner must still decide which search terms to target and what content to produce. [L1456, 2021-22]
- Raw keyword research can be bought from freelancers on Fiverr. The store owner must still decide which search terms to target and what each piece of content should cover, because a freelancer cannot make that decision. [L1456, 2021-22]
- Content writing is commonly outsourced because most people write poorly for this purpose. [L1456, 2021-22]
- Backlink building is worth trying once yourself first. After that, it can be outsourced to an agency or to a virtual assistant trained on the process, which is cheaper. [L1456, 2021-22]
- Be even more cautious when hiring an SEO agency than when hiring a Google Ads agency. [L1053, 2021-22]
- Be cautious about SEO agencies that charge large monthly retainers for slow results. [L0962, 2021]
- SEO work on an existing store often comes down to a few tweaks to product pages. Hiring an expensive SEO agency rarely returns its cost. (2023.) [L0855, 2023]
- Done For You Content Marketing is listed as an outsourced content marketing option in the December 2020 coaching call. The lesson names it without describing its scope or cost. [L0426, 2020]
- SEO work can be split into on-page and off-page SEO and assigned to a dedicated SEO person. Content such as blog posts can be written by a separate content writer who reports to the marketing manager. [L1334, 2021-22]
- The grow track includes a dedicated module on search engine optimization, content marketing and backlink building, and a separate module on Google Ads. An owner strong in one area but weak in SEO should go to the SEO module first. [L0545, 2021-22]

### From action list to work schedule

- SEO works only through continuous work; occasional or cram sessions produce little effect. The three biggest SEO levers work together and should run at the same time: keyword research, content production and backlinks. [L1456, 2021-22]
- SEO, content marketing and link building each deliver value alone, but they work much better together. Plan them as one connected effort over time instead of choosing one over the others. [L1270, 2021-22]
- A full SEO action item list is often too long to work through in one go. Before starting, decide which items come first and which approach you will use for each one, so your time is not wasted. [L1270, 2021-22]
- Keep one action item spreadsheet that lists all SEO tasks for the product pages, collection pages and blog pages. Use it to track which tasks are open, in progress or done. [L1270, 2021-22]
- A 90-day rock for a six-month SEO priority can be completing six pieces of content, each with promotion. This turns the six-month target into a smaller quarterly milestone that can be measured. [L0447, 2021]

Turning the action list into a work schedule:
1. Go through every SEO action item and note what each one requires.
2. Check the site and mark items that are already done so they are not repeated.
3. Decide which of the remaining items you will implement.
4. Sort the chosen items into higher-priority and lower-priority groups.
5. Place the higher-priority items first in your work schedule, then add the lower-priority items after them. [L1270, 2021-22]
