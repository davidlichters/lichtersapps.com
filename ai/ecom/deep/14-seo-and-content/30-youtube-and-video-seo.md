## YouTube and Video SEO

### YouTube SEO: Why Video Needs Its Own Strategy

- YouTube has its own search system, so video keywords must be researched separately, even when the same topics are in the written content strategy. [L0758, 2021-22]
- YouTube uses a simpler ranking logic than Google web search, which uses more than 200 ranking signals. For YouTube SEO, the practical focus is keyword-matched titles and descriptions, plus viewer engagement such as watch time and clicks. [L1511, 2021-22]
- YouTube works like a search engine. Videos can stay findable and keep attracting viewers for years. [L1424, 2021-22]
- A video can rank in Google results before a comparable text page does. For search terms where competition for text results is strong, a relevant YouTube video that answers the query can earn a first-page spot until the store's own page ranks. [L1424, 2021-22]
- Google often shows video results on the first page, sometimes two videos from the same channel, which is unusual for text results. Searches for reviews, best-of queries and how-to queries are especially likely to display YouTube videos. [L1424, 2021-22]
- Unlike Google web search, several videos from the same channel can rank for one search term on YouTube. Targeting the same keyword with more than one video is therefore acceptable. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Video is optional for content marketing, because written content alone can produce enough traffic. The easiest start is turning existing written content into video. A video can be made for YouTube specifically if it targets terms people already search for. [L1511, 2021-22]
- A YouTube channel does not need a big audience to work. A small number of relevant videos can still gain subscribers and website traffic. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Video is an extra traffic source. A channel with subscribers and engagement also increases business value if the business is sold later. [L1511, 2021-22]
- The number of subscribers is not tied to the number of videos. Five to ten good videos on topics people are interested in can already bring subscribers and website visitors. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Choosing Video Topics From Demand

- Compare view counts with video age. A video with many views in a short time signals a topic people want. Use these comparisons to choose content ideas and to spot topics that draw little attention. [L0478, 2021-22]
- Example: in one surfing channel, a video posted about a month earlier had around 249,000 views, while a video posted three months earlier had around 13,000 views. The gap shows which subject resonates with the audience and guides the next content choice. [L0478, 2021-22]
- Use trend tools to choose video topics people already respond to, then make your own take that is better than what is trending. [L0487, 2018]
- Watch time matters on YouTube, because longer viewing causes the platform to show the video to more people. [L0487, 2018]
- Interest-based videos can target the broader topic around your products rather than the product itself. Formats include:
  - Top-10 lists, for example top 10 big wave spots. [L1511, 2021-22]
  - Compilations, for example worst wipeouts. [L1511, 2021-22]
  - Reviews of related items, for example the best movies in the niche. [L1511, 2021-22]
- These formats attract viewers who share the interest, and they can later become customers or website visitors. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Keyword Research Tools

| Tool | What it gives for YouTube keywords | Source |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube search auto-suggest | Terms people already search for | [L1511, 2021-22] |
| Ahrefs Keyword Explorer | YouTube search volume | [L1511, 2021-22] |
| Ubersuggest | Does not show YouTube search volume | [L1511, 2021-22] |
| Google Keyword Planner | Does not show YouTube search volume | [L1511, 2021-22] |

- The tool information reflects 2021-22 advice and should be checked against current tool features. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Keyword Research Procedures

**Auto-suggest keyword method**

1. Type a core product term into the YouTube search bar. [L1511, 2021-22]
2. Read the suggested completions, which reflect commonly searched terms. [L1511, 2021-22]
3. Add variations by product type, beginner or intermediate level, use case, feature, and brand names. [L1511, 2021-22]
4. Check the results with the Report search predictions option only if you want to remove irrelevant suggestions. [L1511, 2021-22]
5. Record the chosen terms in a keyword research tracking spreadsheet, grouped by product or brand. [L1511, 2021-22]

**Keyword-to-video idea method for a product range**

1. List the products, categories and brands you sell. [L1511, 2021-22]
2. For each, search YouTube auto-suggest and note terms with volume signals. [L1511, 2021-22]
3. Add the terms to a keyword research spreadsheet. [L1511, 2021-22]
4. Turn each term into a video idea, for example beginner guides, reviews of a brand, comparisons, top-10 lists, or entertaining compilations for the niche. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Video Titles

- The primary keyword must appear in the video title, as close to the start as possible, the same way it would on a website page. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Titles should be plain and descriptive, signalling what the viewer will learn, not hype. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Video Descriptions

- The primary keyword should appear in the description no more than three times. [L1511, 2021-22]
- The description should stay under 300 words. [L1511, 2021-22]
- The description should explain why the viewer should watch and what benefit they get, and include a call to action. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Descriptions can contain as many links as needed, such as product pages and social profiles. Links in the description are not a ranking factor, but they let viewers click through to anything mentioned in the video. [L1511, 2021-22]
- When a YouTube video sends viewers away from the store, the description should link back to the store. A call to action with a link card should be placed at the end of the video so traffic still reaches the website. [L1424, 2021-22]

### Tags

- The tag field accepts up to 10 relevant search terms. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Tags can include a range of similar terms, not only the main target keyword. The same keyword may be used across several videos. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Tags may repeat keywords used on other videos. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Captions, Closed Captions and On-Screen Text

- Add on-screen text to the caption and closed captions of every video so the platform can tell what the video is about. Videos without this text are less likely to be distributed to the right viewers. [L1457, 2025-26]

### Video Production and Retention

- Lighting must be good enough that viewers can see the screen. Dark videos hurt engagement. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Production does not need to be polished. Keep the video moving by cutting irrelevant material and keeping audio and visuals active. [L1511, 2021-22]
- YouTube ranks videos partly on engagement and watch time, so viewers must stay on the video. Watching on YouTube rather than leaving for another platform is what the platform rewards. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Hold engagement with a clear intro and a fast-paced video. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Passive viewing without likes, comments, saves or shares makes a video far less likely to be pushed beyond the existing followers. Silent viewers are therefore a distribution problem, not only a missed interaction. [L1457, 2025-26]

### Channel Setup

**Channel setup on YouTube**

1. Use a Google account to log in and open your channel from the menu. [L1511, 2021-22]
2. Add a cover image, which can be a product photo, text, logo, or brand name, linking back to the website and social accounts. [L1511, 2021-22]
3. Fill in the About section with description, business enquiry email, location, and links to the website and social profiles. [L1511, 2021-22]
4. Upload videos regularly. [L1511, 2021-22]
5. Create a static welcome video rather than a changing one. A welcome video only shows to non-subscribers, and its purpose is to get them to subscribe. [L1511, 2021-22]

- Subscribers are notified each time a new video is uploaded, so the channel's main goal for non-subscribers is to get them to subscribe. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Embedding Videos on Store Pages

- Embedding a YouTube video on a high-traffic store site can help the video rank on YouTube for the store domain name, for example when someone searches the domain on YouTube. The video can also appear on the first page of Google organic results. [L0467, 2019]
- Google favors pages that embed relevant videos. A small drop in the Shopify speed score is worth the SEO gain from a YouTube embed. [L0854, 2022]

**Embedding a product video without slowing the store**

1. Create a YouTube channel for the store. [L0854, 2022]
2. Upload the product videos to this channel. [L0854, 2022]
3. Click Embed on the YouTube video and copy the embed code. [L0854, 2022]
4. Paste the embed code onto the relevant product page. [L0854, 2022]

- Do not host the video in Shopify Files, because Google favors YouTube embeds for SEO and the store page score drop is acceptable. [L0854, 2022]
- A video embedded on a product page shows the message `not indexed due to not being on a dedicated watch page` in Search Console. This is not an error. Google only indexes a video as its own result when the page is mostly about that video, so no fix is needed for supplemental videos. [L1287, 2025]

### Video SEO Checklist

**YouTube SEO procedure**

1. Keyword research for each video, identifying at least one target search term. [L1511, 2021-22]
2. Put that keyword in the title, near the start. [L1511, 2021-22]
3. Write a plain, descriptive title that tells the viewer what they will learn. [L1511, 2021-22]
4. Write a description that explains the benefit, uses the primary keyword no more than three times, stays under 300 words, and includes a call to action. [L1511, 2021-22]
5. Add up to 10 relevant search terms in the tag field, which may repeat keywords used on other videos. [L1511, 2021-22]
6. Hold engagement with a clear intro and a fast-paced video. [L1511, 2021-22]
7. Use clickable thumbnails. [L1511, 2021-22]
8. Reply to comments. [L1511, 2021-22]
9. Promote each video soon after upload. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Promotion

- Promote each new video early after upload, for example via email list, social media profiles, and embedding it on the website where relevant. [L1511, 2021-22]
- An embedded YouTube video on a site counts as a view on YouTube, and an early boost of engagement helps the algorithm. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Plan the promotion of each video as part of the content plan before publishing, not after. Decide in advance which email list, social profiles, or relevant website pages will receive the video soon after upload, so it gets an early engagement boost. [L1511, 2021-22]

## Monitoring, Reporting and Ongoing Maintenance

### Search Console Review Cadence

Sources give different intervals for checking Search Console. Each row keeps its own wording.

| Source | Interval | Scope | IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| one source | approximately once per week | General check for errors or warnings, fixing issues that could affect rankings, and monitoring which keywords drive free organic traffic | [L0171, undated] |
| one source | at least once a week | Errors and indexing problems, and tracking which keywords the store ranks for organically | [L0735, 2020-26] |
| one source 2023 | every couple of weeks | Errors and issues that affect organic traffic | [L0835, 2023] |
| one source | every two weeks; more often for high-traffic stores, less often for low-traffic stores; better scheduled in a calendar | Full routine for a Shopify store (see below) | [L1271, 2020-26] |

The general rules say weekly. The 2023 rule and the Shopify routine say every couple of weeks. The Shopify routine is the only source that adjusts the interval to traffic [L0171, undated; L0735, 2020-26; L0835, 2023; L1271, 2020-26].

**Weekly check (general procedure)**

1. Check Google Search Console approximately once per week [L0171, undated].
2. Look for errors or warnings in your site's data [L0171, undated].
3. Fix any issues that could impact your rankings [L0171, undated].
4. Monitor which keywords are driving free organic traffic to your store [L0171, undated].

**Fortnightly Shopify check**

1. Open Google Search Console > Sitemaps and make sure the sitemap is still submitted [L1271, 2020-26].
2. Open the Review snippets report and make sure all pages with reviews are valid and green [L1271, 2020-26].
3. Open the Core Web Vitals / Page experience report and check for errors or critical issues and that the site is secure [L1271, 2020-26].
4. Open Performance and review impressions, average position and CTR, then note which queries and pages changed [L1271, 2020-26].

### Triage of Search Console Issues

- Errors that prevent Google from indexing or ranking pages must be addressed [L0835, 2023].
- Issues such as soft 404 or missing fields are not urgent unless pages are de-indexed or missing from Google [L0429, 2022].
- Address non-urgent issues after ad performance is stable, or post screenshots for help [L0429, 2022].
- The Smart SEO audit score is a guide. Fix errors that can be resolved quickly, but do not chase a perfect score [L1116, 2025].

### Search Console Reports

**Performance report**

- The report shows impressions, clicks, and the search queries (keywords) that led to clicks and impressions, along with the pages involved. It is used to judge free organic traffic and which keywords rank [L0735, 2020-26].
- It shows total clicks, total impressions, average CTR and average position for queries, pages, countries, devices and search appearance over a chosen date range (for example, last 3 months) [L1333, 2021-22].
- Default settings: Search type "Web" and date range "Last 3 months". Tabs: Queries, Pages, Countries, Devices, Search appearance. It can be filtered by position and exported with the EXPORT button [L1333, 2021-22].
- Data lags. The "Last updated" label shows the delay, for example 5 hours ago, or several hours [L1333, 2021-22].
- Limits: the report has limited filtering and detail and works mainly as a quick overview. For deeper keyword and ranking analysis, Ahrefs or SEMrush give more usable data [L1333, 2021-22].

**Indexing and user-experience report**

- The report shows how many site pages are indexed, meaning they can appear in free Google search results. It also flags user-experience problems such as slow loading pages [L0735, 2020-26].

### Tool Choice

- Google Search Console is the main tool for tracking SEO results, including rankings for product searches versus competitors [L1112, 2021].
- Ahrefs is an optional advanced tool and is not required [L1112, 2021].
- Sources differ on third-party tools. The 2021 rule calls Ahrefs optional, while the older advice says dedicated tools give more usable data for deeper analysis. Both apply: Search Console is enough to start, and a third-party tool such as Ahrefs or SEMrush is added when deeper keyword and ranking analysis is needed [L1112, 2021; L1333, 2021-22].

### Checking Search Results Accurately

- To see how a product page appears in organic Google results, including price, stock, review stars and delivery details, search in an incognito or logged-out window [L1116, 2025].
- A logged-in search for a product term can return only Shopping results and hide the organic listings [L1116, 2025].

### Diagnosing Traffic Quality

**Checking where organic traffic really comes from**

1. Open Google Search Console and review which keywords produce impressions and clicks, since Google Analytics gives poor search term data [L0971, 2024].
2. Use Google Analytics to see which countries the visitors come from [L0971, 2024].
3. Judge whether the traffic is qualified (right countries, right keywords) before optimizing the site for conversion [L0971, 2024].

**Common mistake**

- A low sales count with many visitors can mean the traffic comes from unrelated search terms or from countries where the store does not sell, not from a weak store [L0971, 2024].

### Tracking Keywords and Organic Rankings

- Mapping keywords to pages gives SEO work a clear focus and replaces scattered effort [L0879, 2021-22].
- Once the top keywords are tracked, ranking progress and organic traffic show whether the SEO strategy works [L0879, 2021-22].
- Keep tracking which keywords the store ranks for organically during each Search Console check [L0735, 2020-26; L1271, 2020-26].

### Tracking TikTok Traffic with UTM Parameters

**Procedure**

1. Search "TikTok utm parameters builder" and use the TikTok Ads Manager link [L0298, 2023].
2. Enter your product URL and the builder generates a UTM-tagged link [L0298, 2023].
3. Use this link in TikTok campaigns [L0298, 2023].
4. View results in Shopify Reports > Sessions by Marketing and select UTM term and UTM content filters [L0298, 2023].

### Quick-Win Sequence for Top Blog Posts

**Procedure for the top five posts by traffic**

1. Run a diagnostic pass for about 30 to 45 minutes with no edits. Pull query-level Search Console data for the top five posts, identify queries with high impressions but few clicks, and check the brand name search results and the storefront setup for localized URLs (canonicals and hreflang) [L1269, 2025-26].
2. Rewrite the title tag, H1, meta description and first intro paragraph of those posts around the high-impression queries the diagnostic found [L1269, 2025-26].
3. In the same editing pass, add an on-topic call-to-action block and an email capture to each post [L1269, 2025-26].
4. In the same pass, add contextual internal links from those posts to the relevant collections and to the About or mission page, using descriptive anchor text [L1269, 2025-26].
5. Fix only the technical issues the diagnostic actually found, not a blanket clean-up [L1269, 2025-26].

**Scope of bulk rewrites**

- In a bulk blog rewrite, every blog article can get a row in the CSV, but full title, meta and intro rewrites should go only to the articles that actually carry search demand. In one case that was seven articles, and the rest did not need the same effort [L1269, 2025-26].

**Expected results**

- Average position in Search Console blends every query a page ranks for. Part of a low click-through rate can therefore come from weaker, lower-ranked queries [L1269, 2025-26].
- Do not project a multiple of current clicks from a target CTR. Present the result as a range and expect real but smaller gains [L1269, 2025-26].

**Case: content-led Shopify store**

- Starting point: top blog posts had high search impressions but low click-through rates and no next step for readers [L1269, 2025-26].
- The diagnostic pass checked query-level data, the brand search result and the localized storefront setup. The team chose five posts for changes [L1269, 2025-26].
- The plan: rewrite titles and intros on those posts around high-impression queries, add an on-topic CTA and email capture, add internal links to collections and the mission page, and fix only technical issues found in the diagnostic [L1269, 2025-26].
- Projection for one top post: about 33,858 impressions, 90 clicks, average position about 8, CTR about 0.27 percent. Reaching about 1 percent would mean about 340 clicks [L1269, 2025-26].
- Lesson: the case shows the plan and the projected CTR math, but no measured result from the rewrites [L1269, 2025-26].

### Content Refreshes

- Update existing blog posts annually by revising the title to indicate the new year (for example, change to "updated 2021") [L0311, 2021-22].
- Keeping content current helps maintain top search rankings [L0311, 2021-22].
- After the publishing pace is established, shift effort from creating new content to optimizing pages and posts that already perform well [L1321, 2020-26].
- Improving existing winners is preferred over constantly producing new content [L1321, 2020-26].

### Keeping SEO Work Continuous

- Run the Search Console routine on a fixed schedule, every two weeks for a Shopify store and adjusted for traffic, and place it in a calendar [L1271, 2020-26].
- Track keyword rankings and organic traffic sources each time, so that ranking progress shows whether the strategy works [L0879, 2021-22; L0735, 2020-26].
- Once publishing is established, direct effort to improving pages and posts that already perform well [L1321, 2020-26].

## Other details

### Product research checks

- When researching products through Google Shopping, do not judge market viability from the first page of results alone. If cheaper products dominate the first page, filter by price range to check that higher-priced variants exist in sufficient quantity. [L0245, 2020]

### Keyword research and listing copy

1. On Amazon, research keywords with Amazon's own search suggestions. [L0768, 2021-22]
2. Include the chosen keywords in the product title, the description and the bullet points. [L0768, 2021-22]
- In 2021, Amazon was described as a pay-for-play system in which organic ranking was not realistic without paid ads. Plan an advertising budget for Amazon from the start. [L0768, 2021-22]

### Store naming and file metadata

- Including the niche keyword in the domain is preferred when possible but not required. Creative names that fit the store theme also work well. (2023) [L0934, 2023]
- Set custom metadata in PDF documents, including the author, affiliated company and keywords. This metadata supports SEO and can later serve as proof of ownership if the file is copied. [L1357, 2018]

### On-site SEO in Shopify

- Site SEO is covered in the search engine domination lesson. The main focus is on-site optimization in Shopify and organic traffic tools. [L0969, 2022]
- Free SEO training resources are available as a starting point for improving a store's organic search visibility. [L1087, 2020]

### Traffic estimates and SEO tools

- Ubersuggest (ubersuggest.com) is a cheap SEO research tool that estimates a domain's organic monthly traffic. Ahrefs or Semrush can be used instead if you already pay for them. [L0893, 2021-22]
- Traffic source findings are estimates, not facts, because competitors may run channels that are hard to detect. Treat search results and SEO tool numbers as a best guess and record that uncertainty in the comments. [L0893, 2021-22]

### Local search and listings

- Local SEO tactics, such as citations in local newspapers, are specialized and rarely move rankings for a national store. Some local practices can still help a national business, but they are minor. [L0879, 2021-22]
- For Australian searchers, search engines tend to show.com.au domains ahead of.com domains. A local page with quality content and links can outrank overseas listicles that rank for the same term. [L0949, 2021-22]
- Google Business Profile, 2021 view: it is not necessary for a dropshipping store to rank, so it does not need to be a priority at launch. [L0962, 2021]
- Google Business Profile, 2025 view: it is optional for this store model, and leaving it off is unlikely to cause harm. The two views agree that it is not a launch priority; the 2025 view is the more recent guidance. [L1116, 2025]

### Feed work and reviews

- If the store is already optimized for SEO, most of the feed work is already done. Google's organic search and Shopping matching work in similar ways, so the same title, image and description work help both. [L1170, 2021-22]
- Junip can add a dedicated reviews page that gathers all store reviews in one place. The vendor says this page helps the store rank when people search for reviews of the brand. [L0589, 2025-26]
