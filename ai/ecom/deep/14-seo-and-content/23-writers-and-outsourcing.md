### Writers and Outsourcing

#### Weekly Assignment Cards

*Source: one source.*

- Assign weekly content work to a writer using this structure:
  1. Create a weekly project card, for example for a specific week in October.
  2. Add the target keywords in the card description.
  3. Set a length, such as 300 to 500 words for collection or product page content.
  4. Attach the link to each collection or product page to be written.
  5. Move each card to the done list when the writer finishes, so progress is visible.

  [L1396, 2021-22]
- Create blog or buying guide tasks using this structure:
  1. Write the blog name or topic.
  2. Add reference sources, such as manufacturer pages, reviews or related blogs.
  3. List the product links that must appear.
  4. Set the target keywords and the desired length.
  5. Move the card to done when published.

  [L1396, 2021-22]

#### Writer Capacity

*Source: one source.*

- Plan about twelve content items per week per writer as a starting point, one product category per week, adjusted after one week of measured results. [L1396, 2021-22]

### Holiday and Seasonal Content

*Source: one source.*

- Publish seasonal blog posts about each holiday promotion at least a month before it starts, so Google can index and rank them in time. Example titles are [niche] Black Friday deals and [niche] Cyber Monday deals, each explaining the offer, when it runs, which products it covers and how to use it. [L1287, 2025]

#### Further details

- Customer testimonial request email: 'Hi [Name], I noticed you recently purchased [Product]. We'd love to feature your story. Would you mind sharing: (1) a few photos of you using the product, (2) how the product improved your [activity/situation], (3) any advice for other customers considering it, (4) where/how you use it. In return, we'll offer you [free accessories/coupon/discount] and feature your story on our blog and social media.' [L0226, 2017]
