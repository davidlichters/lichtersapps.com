## Content Promotion and Distribution

### Promotion Planning

- Set the promotion plan when the title is planned, not after publishing. Typical channels are social media, a YouTube channel, a Facebook page, backlink outreach, search text ads and Google Display Network ads, and email to your list. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Publishing content is only the first half of content marketing. Without active promotion, new pages rarely get seen. Search engines and algorithms look for signals of quality and relevance, and those signals have to be generated by promoting the content on purpose. Early traffic gets the snowball rolling so that organic discovery can follow later. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Promote every content piece for at least 2 weeks, ideally up to 30 days, before moving on. [L0383, 2021-22]
- Each piece of content needs active promotion for a minimum of two weeks, and for up to 30 days after it is published. The promotion strategy should be planned during content research, before the piece is written. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Content promotion pursues five goals: generating initial traffic and sales, stimulating social sharing, earning backlinks, building an audience, and filling retargeting audiences and the top of the funnel. [L0385, 2021-22]
- For each piece of content, plan three to four promotion methods out of the available list, rather than using all of them. Choose based on the content type and resources, and start with the ones that are easy to execute. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Content must be actively promoted, because promotion helps discovery by real people and signals to search engines that the content is important enough to rank. [L0414, 2021-22]
- Promotion is optional, but it speeds up the organic ranking process that would happen anyway. Focus on the few methods with the highest effect rather than trying every channel. [L0899, 2020-26]
- The long-term goal is organic Google ranking, and new content can rank from the start. Each share and visit adds to the ranking signals over time, so promotion mainly accelerates a process that continues without it. [L0899, 2020-26]

#### The Twelve Promotion Methods

Use these twelve methods, in the order presented, when choosing the three to four methods for a piece. [L0385, 2021-22]

1. Outreach to other websites for sharing or linking (link building). [L0385, 2021-22]
2. Organic posting to social media (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube). [L0385, 2021-22]
3. Paid ads on Facebook, Google Display Network, Google Search and YouTube. [L0385, 2021-22]
4. Email to the existing audience. [L0385, 2021-22]
5. Sharing in forums, Facebook groups and online communities in the niche. [L0385, 2021-22]
6. Linking to new content from older content already receiving traffic. [L0385, 2021-22]
7. Promotion on content networks (Taboola, Outbrain, Quuu Promote). [L0385, 2021-22]
8. Expert roundup posts. [L0385, 2021-22]
9. Producing a video and posting it to YouTube. [L0385, 2021-22]
10. Converting the content into an infographic and sharing it. [L0385, 2021-22]
11. Submitting the content to online content communities. [L0385, 2021-22]
12. Outreach to people or sites that were featured in a best-of or list-style article. [L0385, 2021-22]

### Launch Sequence for New Content

- Launch each article by:
  1. Posting to your business social media channels. [L0274, 2023]
  2. Emailing your existing subscriber base with a link. [L0274, 2023]
- This accelerates organic traction beyond waiting for search engine rankings to build naturally. [L0274, 2023]
- Promotion means actively driving readers to newly published pages and blog posts from day one, without paid traffic. The aim is to send Google early engagement signals that the content is being seen, which raises the chance of a higher organic ranking. [L0899, 2020-26]
- A large number of visitors arriving on a new page on launch day tells Google the page is being checked out. If visitors stay on the page for a while, this is a further positive signal of authority. [L0899, 2020-26]

### Social Sharing

- A single organic social post reaches only a small share of followers. On Facebook it is commonly estimated that fewer than 10% of followers see an organic post. Posting once and considering the job done wastes most of the reach potential. [L0385, 2021-22]
- To maximize one piece of content on social media:
  1. Over a period of about one month (two weeks as a minimum), post 5 to 10 times about the same content. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Split the content into 5 to 10 small snippets of interesting facts, questions and comments related to it. [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Post each snippet as its own social post with a link to the full content piece. [L0385, 2021-22]
  4. Use a different image on each post, such as images from the article. [L0385, 2021-22]
  5. Make each post's copy give a reason to click by telling readers what they will learn. [L0385, 2021-22]
  6. Schedule all posts in advance with a tool such as Buffer or Hootsuite. [L0385, 2021-22]
- A handful of recent social media posts, about five, is enough to show activity. What counts is a page with recent content rather than the number of posts. (2024) [L0471, 2024]
- Free traffic can come from answering questions in forums and YouTube comments and linking back to your site. It takes more manual effort but costs nothing, so it works well for testing an offer before spending on ads. [L0514, 2015]

- Friday is used for Q&A sessions with the audience. YouTube and the blog host unique content, and social posts link to them. [L0487, 2018]

### Automation with Zapier

- Automatically publish new blog posts to Facebook and LinkedIn when they are created to maximize reach, maintain an active social presence, and eliminate manual posting. [L0236, 2018]
- Automate blog post sharing only to Facebook and LinkedIn through Zapier. Avoid automating to Twitter, Instagram, or Pinterest, because these platforms require custom image sizing and text formatting that native scheduling doesn't handle, making posts look bot-generated. [L0286, 2018]
- New blog posts published on Shopify or WordPress trigger Zapier to post immediately to Facebook, then repeat at 7-day, 30-day, and 60-day intervals using Zapier delays. This extends content lifespan across different audience segments. [L0286, 2018]
- When new podcast or audio episodes publish to an RSS feed, Zapier automatically creates an unpublished draft blog post and sends a Slack notification. This provides a content starting point without manual transcription uploads. [L0286, 2018]
- Example: The Ecommerce Lifestyle Podcast publishes at least one episode per week. When the podcast owner set up Zapier automation to post new episodes to LinkedIn, their personal Facebook page, and a business Facebook page, the initial setup took about 10 minutes. Over the next 20+ episodes, every new podcast posted automatically without requiring any manual team effort. [L0312, 2023+]
- Where sources differ: The Zapier rule above limits automation to Facebook and LinkedIn. The older scheduling method (2021-22) queues a different image and copy for each snippet post in Buffer or Hootsuite, including on Instagram and Pinterest. Use the scheduling method when each post is written and imaged separately. Use Zapier only for the Facebook and LinkedIn posts. [L0286, 2018; L0385, 2021-22]

### Email to Your List

- Five channels for promoting published content start with your subscriber list: email your subscriber list and ask them to share. [L0179, undated]
- Email your existing subscriber base with a link when each article launches. [L0274, 2023]
- Email to the existing audience is one of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]

### Contributor and Expert Outreach

- Five channels for promoting published content also include contacting people who contributed to the content and asking them to share it. [L0179, undated]
- When a post with contributor input goes live:
  1. Email every contributor on the same day. [L0899, 2020-26]
  2. Thank them, include the link and say you would appreciate it if they shared the post. [L0899, 2020-26]
  3. Ask them to share it through whatever channels they use, such as a blog, Facebook page or group, or their website. Keep the request casual and do not make it sound mandatory. [L0899, 2020-26]
- For expert roundups or quote-based articles, ask the top three contributors to share the post, since their audiences are the most relevant. Their shares carry more weight when the post is published on your site. [L0899, 2020-26]
- Process for an expert roundup post:
  1. Use the attention matrix to identify industry or niche experts. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Email them asking for a quote or answer relevant to the content topic (for example, one tip on a specific question). [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Include each answer in the content with a link to the expert's website or social profiles. [L0385, 2021-22]
  4. After publishing, email every contributor with the link and ask them to share it with their list or followers and/or link to it from their website. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Five channels for promoting published content also include emailing suppliers or companies mentioned in the content and asking them to share it. [L0179, undated]
- Five channels for promoting published content also include contacting relevant influencers and asking them to share. Each group has access to audiences or relationships that amplify your content. [L0179, undated]

### Outreach to Publications and Newsletters

- Five channels for promoting published content also include reaching out to industry publications and asking them to feature it. [L0179, undated]
- When an industry publication or newsletter sends a regular roundup of top posts, email its editor when you publish a strong post. Ask them to include your link in the next roundup, for example in a weekly Monday newsletter. [L0899, 2020-26]
- Publications and newsletter curators rarely share content on their own, so proactive outreach is required. Without a direct request, your post is unlikely to appear in their roundups. [L0899, 2020-26]
- Outreach email template for an industry publication or newsletter curator that sends a regular roundup of top posts:

```
Subject: Link for next week's roundup

Hi [Editor name],

I read your weekly roundup of the best posts in [Industry or niche]. We just published [Article title]: [URL]

Would you consider including a link to it in next week's newsletter? I am happy to send any details you need.

Best,
[Your name]
```

[L0899, 2020-26]

- Outreach to people or sites that were featured in a best-of or list-style article is one of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]

### Press Releases and Guest Posts

- Press releases drive traffic and ranking lift. One operator executed 10 per week with success and planned to scale to 15 per week. Each release was rewritten with a different angle but the same core message. This is an ongoing strategy with measurable impact on product visibility. [L0227, 2014]
- Press releases are not useful for a new store seeking brand recognition at launch. Guest posts or mentions on industry blogs and authority sites within the niche, not direct competitors, work better. [L0834, 2021]
- Where sources differ: The 10-to-15-per-week press release program [L0227, 2014] is an ongoing strategy with measured results. The launch-stage advice to skip press releases and use guest posts or industry mentions [L0834, 2021] applies to a new store seeking brand recognition. Use the first when the store is running the program and can measure visibility, and the second at launch.

### Authority Site Placements

- Paid placements on authority sites in your niche, such as blog banner ads or sponsored posts, can bring referral traffic and sometimes backlinks. Google discourages paid links, so choose relevant sites and treat the placement primarily as a traffic source. [L0856, 2024]

### Forums, Communities and Free Traffic

- Content shared in forums, Facebook groups and niche communities should be genuinely useful and not mainly a sales pitch, since most content is not built to close a sale. Share it where the target market already gathers, and let the value of the piece earn the click. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Sharing in forums, Facebook groups and online communities in the niche, and submitting the content to online content communities, are two of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Free traffic can come from answering questions in forums and YouTube comments and linking back to your site. [L0514, 2015]

### Infographics

- Process for an infographic:
  1. Pick a blog post, ideally a how-to or listicle, which suits infographics well. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Have it converted into an infographic, either by a freelancer on Fiverr or with a tool such as Piktochart. [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Put the source URL or the original post URL on the infographic. [L0385, 2021-22]
  4. Publish it as a standalone page on the website or blog and share it on social media. [L0385, 2021-22]
  5. Submit it to infographic directories such as visual.ly, Pinterest and the website. [L0385, 2021-22]
  6. Include the URL in the description or comments of every place it is posted. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Infographics made from a blog post can be embedded on the store's site, posted on social media as extra content, sent in email, posted on Pinterest, or offered to other websites in exchange for a credit link back to the store. [L1075, 2025+]
- Converting the content into an infographic and sharing it is one of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]

### Pinterest

- Pinterest can bring steady organic traffic if your niche suits the platform. Create a Pinterest account and make sure every product is pinnable. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
- About 80% of Pinterest interactions are repins, meaning users reshare a pin from inside the platform rather than a pin taken from a website. Repins are the main reason content can spread from one follower to the next. [L1136, 2014]
- Create vertical images, taller than they are wide, for blog post content that you pin to Pinterest, not only for product images. Blog pins that link back to the store can generate traffic in the same way product pins do. [L1136, 2014]
- Pinterest content should also be original, because shared unique content earns more sharing than republished material. [L0468, 2020]
- Pinterest organic growth approach:
  1. Post 15 to 20 posts at the start. [L0788, undated]
  2. Build a very specific niche audience, such as working moms, work-from-home entrepreneurs or nail enthusiasts. [L0788, undated]
  3. Use the Pinterest trends section to find trending keywords and the hashtags attached to each trend. [L0788, undated]
  4. Post consistently, about 5 to 10 pins per day. [L0788, undated]
  5. Aim for a target of 100,000 monthly viewers before expecting steady organic traffic. [L0788, undated]
- Where sources differ: The platform plan above sets Pinterest at once a week in the early phase and as the target [L0487, 2018]. The growth approach sets 5 to 10 pins per day after the first 15 to 20 posts [L0788, undated]. Use the weekly cadence for a brand-led plan that also runs a blog and social channels. Use the daily cadence when Pinterest is the main growth channel.

### Hashtags

- Broad hashtags such as #explore, #fyp and #foryoupage do nothing for discovery, because nearly everyone uses them. Do not rely on them. [L0796, 2025-26]
- Use only 3 to 5 niche-relevant hashtags per post. Choose hashtags that describe your specific topic rather than general reach tags. [L0796, 2025-26]

### Paid Promotion

#### Principles

- Paid traffic can amplify other channels such as blog posts, YouTube videos, and social media content by sending targeted paid views to content you already produced. Unlike organic reach, you control who sees it and pay for results such as views only when they happen. [L1455, 2018+]
- Informational searches that do not fit a product page, such as beginner-level questions, are better served by a content page and a search text ad than by shopping ads. The goal is to move these visitors into the email list and retargeting audiences, from where they are later converted. [L0385, 2021-22]

#### Facebook

- Paid Facebook promotion of content:
  1. Use Page Post Engagement or Traffic campaign types, or boost existing posts about the content. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Use the primary image from the blog post or a video, with less than 20% text in it. [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Write ad copy that creates curiosity and shows which problem the content solves. [L0385, 2021-22]
  4. Target interests identified in customer avatars, or lookalike audiences built from the email or customer lists. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Facebook content promotion can start with a budget of about $20 to $30 to get people engaging with a post and into retargeting audiences. Typical content ads on the Google Display Network and YouTube are also started on small budgets, and the test is run for a short time rather than for months. [L0385, 2021-22]

#### Google Display Network

- To promote content on the Google Display Network:
  1. Choose keywords that fit the topic of the content (the search phrase the content is optimized for), or audiences that resemble people who have already visited the site. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Run static image ads in the recommended sizes of 300 x 250, 300 x 600, 728 x 90 and 320 x 100. [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Either design the images yourself in Snappa or hand the entire job to an outside provider. [L0385, 2021-22]

#### YouTube

- YouTube content promotion: target people who watched YouTube channels that appeal to the target audience, identified during attention matrix research, to build audiences around those channels. [L0385, 2021-22]

#### Google Search Text Ads

- Google Search text ads for content:
  1. Target informational searches linked to the products and the content, such as `best charcoal grills 2019`. [L0385, 2021-22]
  2. Use only exact and/or phrase match keywords. [L0385, 2021-22]
  3. Match the ad headline as closely as possible to the headline of the content page. [L0385, 2021-22]
  4. Use the ad copy to build curiosity and name the problem the content solves. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Well-structured content search ads can reach a click-through rate of 20% or more, because most advertisers promote product or collection pages rather than content, so content ads stand out in the results. [L0385, 2021-22]

#### Headline Pattern for Content Ads

- Content ad headline formula: a headline that states the problem and a clear reason to read before buying, such as `Don't buy [product] until you've read this guide`, works because it reads differently from the sales-style ads around it.
  - Example 1: `Don't book a surf trip until you've read this beginner guide.`
  - Example 2: `Don't choose a running shoe until you've read our fit guide for this year.`

[L0385, 2021-22]

### Content Networks

- Taboola and Outbrain are content advertising networks whose ads appear on large media sites such as Forbes, entrepreneur.com and the New York Post. Outbrain is recommended as the starting point because it is simple to run: apply, open an account, and create an ad consisting of an image and a headline, with targeting guided by customer avatars and interest targeting. [L0385, 2021-22]
- On content networks such as Outbrain, the headline is the key lever and should state clearly what the reader will get from the content. Testing can start with a small budget of tens of dollars. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Quuu Promote is a paid content-sharing service that pushes content to people who look for material to share on social media or to link to. The campaign is set up by adding an RSS feed, the service then promotes content automatically, and its catalogue is split into market categories, with some niches covered better than others. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Promotion on content networks (Taboola, Outbrain, Quuu Promote) is one of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Other social and native networks where cold traffic can be developed include native ad networks like Outbrain and Taboola, alongside both paid and unpaid social platforms relevant to the business. [L0414, 2021-22]

### Tools for Promotion

- Buffer and Hootsuite are scheduling tools used to queue social posts in advance, so that the planned posting plan does not depend on remembering to publish each post. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Snappa is a tool for building ad images, and Fiverr is used to outsource infographic creation and similar design work. Piktochart is an infographic creation tool. [L0385, 2021-22]

### Repurposing Content

- Linking to new content from older content that already receives traffic is one of the twelve methods. [L0385, 2021-22]
- For a single article, turn 5 to 10 snippets into separate social posts, each with its own image and a link to the full piece. [L0385, 2021-22]
- Infographics, emails, Pinterest pins and outside sites can all reuse one blog post's visual, as set out in the infographic section. [L1075, 2025+]

### Creator and Influencer Content Repurposing

Repurposing channels for influencer content:

| Channel | Typical content |
|---|---|
| Website | Testimonials, images, videos that link to product pages |
| Email marketing | Testimonials, images, videos |
| Organic social media | Reposted stories, Reels, and carousel posts |
| Paid media | The same content as organic social, with ad spend behind it |
| Cross-promotion | Collaboration post with the creator, living on both the brand's and the creator's pages |
| In-store displays | Only relevant for brick-and-mortar brands |
| Audience submissions | Creators encourage followers to submit their own videos or try the product on camera |

[L0784, 2025-26]

### Publishing Across Platforms

- Publish each video on all relevant platforms, because the same video can perform very differently on each one. A weak result on one platform does not mean the video will fail on another. [L1457, 2025-26]
