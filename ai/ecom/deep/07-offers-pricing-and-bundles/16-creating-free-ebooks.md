### Creating free ebooks

#### Scope and content

- Keep the ebook short: 15-25 pages with 4-6 chapters of substantive, useful content. Each chapter must deliver real value, and the ebook must contain no placeholder text. [L0860, 2026]
- Ebooks cost almost nothing to deliver but noticeably raise perceived value. Customers feel they are getting expert information, and the offer feels more complete even if they never read the ebook. [L0860, 2026]
- Page count guidance: a free ebook should have around 15 to 30 pages to deliver perceived value, while 5 pages is too few. Show the page count on the landing page, for example "a 28-page PDF." [L1439, 2024+]
- Choose an ebook topic that matches the product's niche and the buyer's problem. A dog product gets a dog health guide, a fitness product gets a home workout plan, and a sleep product gets a sleep guide. [L0860, 2026]
- The ebook should read as a thoughtful gift, not a sales pitch. Use a warm, direct voice, short paragraphs, subheadings, and bullet points where they help readability. [L0860, 2026]
- Filler chapters and placeholder text make the bonus look cheap and weaken the offer. Every chapter should be useful on its own. [L0860, 2026]
- Ebook bonus titles should match the product's buyer. For a beauty product, a skincare guide such as "Five-Step Skincare Routine For Dry Skin" fits. For a posture product, a habits guide such as "6 Daily Habits For Sitting Taller At Work" fits. [L0860, 2026]
- For a book seller, a good free gift is a short e-book that teaches the reader how to create an e-book, offered as a bonus on the Buy 2 and Buy 3 bundles. [L1149, 2026]
- Make the ebook about how to properly use or optimise the main product. This justifies higher bundle prices and increases the perceived value of the tier. [L0232, 2026]
- In a free-gift ebook, place a short tracker or overview of the whole challenge, such as a 7-day reset map, before the day-by-day breakdown. The reader knows the full path before the detailed steps begin. [L0862, 2026]
- Example of a challenge ebook step: before bed, take a small flashlight and a small mirror. Spend about 30 seconds on three checks: the gumline where teeth meet gums, the back of the tongue for any white or yellow coating, and the gaps between the back teeth. Note one simple observation only, without changing your routine. [L0862, 2026]
- A short usage guide for a product that buyers may ask about can be made as follows. [L1432, undated]
  1. Find a store that sells the same product and has a strong product description.
  2. Use that description to understand the product's features and how it is used.
  3. Write and design a PDF of three to four pages that explains how to use the product.
  4. Deliver the PDF to buyers automatically, with a delivery app or a download link in the order confirmation.
  5. Prefer creating the guide yourself over buying Amazon seller material to reuse, since your own version is safer and more specific. [L1432, undated]
- Adding a small, unannounced free gift to the parcel costs almost nothing if it is printed cheaply, for example three items per A4 sheet in black and white with a simple recipe. Customers who receive such a surprise return more often, refer others, and leave better feedback. [L1437, 2018]

#### Sourcing and rebranding ebooks

- Ebook topics that buyers can use: a free ebook such as a resistance band exercise guide, an ab workout guide, or a 15, 30, or 60 minute workout guide. A resistance band seller added a mesh bag to solve an odour complaint and included three free ebooks. [L1153, 2016]
- Buy a PLR (private label rights) ebook on a health-related topic from a site such as plr.me, typically around $5 to $10 or less. [L0871, 2021+]
- A free-ebook sourcing procedure is set out below. [L0650, undated]
  1. Choose a topic related to your product or your customer's needs, such as wellness or a product-use subject.
  2. Search that topic on a free-ebook website and download a matching ebook.
  3. Hire a freelancer on Fiverr to change the cover and branding to match your store.
  4. Offer the rebranded ebook as a free bonus with a purchase, and do not sell it directly. [L0650, undated]
- A free ebook rebranded with a new cover and logo can be offered as a bonus. It should not be sold directly because of copyright concerns. The lesson describes this as a gray area and recommends the rebranding. [L0650, undated]
- Disagreement on selling rebranded PLR ebooks: one lesson says the ebook can be edited in Canva and kept as delivered, since the rights allow reuse, and then either added as a bonus or sold as a product on the site. [L0871, 2021+] Another says rebranded ebooks should not be sold directly because of copyright concerns and should be offered only as a free bonus. [L0650, undated] Use it as a free bonus in every case. Sell it only if the PLR licence you bought explicitly allows resale.
- A cheap-upsell strategy with a free ebook as the add-on follows these steps. [L0577, 2023]
  1. Search the supplier marketplace (AliExpress example) for a product related to your main product. Filter by reviews and find an add-on that costs between 10 cents and 1 dollar.
  2. Offer the add-on for free on the store, either beside the main product or as a free bonus when customers buy.
  3. Find a PLR ebook related to the product's benefit (for example, skin care for sensitive skin for a shower-filter product) on a PLR site.
  4. Buy the PLR ebook for about 2 dollars 70 cents, rebrand it with your own logo, and give it to customers as a downloadable bonus.
  5. State the ebook's value as 20 dollars in the offer, for example "Receive a 20 dollar ebook free with your order."
  6. Make the offer very visible on the site (banner, dropdown, or product gallery image as second image).
  7. Set up the add-on as a variant of the main product's Shopify listing, and use an automation app (Auto DS was named) so the supplier receives orders for both items together.
  8. Tell customers in the order confirmation email that the items may arrive on different days and not together. [L0577, 2023]
- A custom add-on packaging made specifically for a product can be expensive: if it must be manufactured separately, suppliers often require a minimum of 500 to 1,000 units. Only use custom add-ons if the supplier already has the item in stock. [L0577, 2023]

### Tiered and selectable gifts

- Tiered gifts make the buyer add more items. Each extra item unlocks an additional free gift, up to a set maximum. Removing an item removes the gift, which uses loss aversion to encourage larger orders. [L0919, undated]
- Example: a tiered gift offer in the cart unlocked three gifts as the buyer added bottles: a recipe book, a mystery gift, and a milk frother. Removing a bottle removed the matching gift. Gift tiers were tied to quantity and capped at a set number. [L0919, undated]
- Example: a necklace offer and selectable gifts with a choice of design, as above. [L0661, undated]
- Include a card that new customers return with their order to claim a gift, and give existing customers a different reason to receive it. The gift helped the catalogue beat competing catalogues and paid for itself several times through larger orders. [L1261, undated]
- Free gift price guidance: a base product at $45 with a free gift gave a total perceived price of around $60, which was a good sweet spot. A total of $80 to $100 was harder to sell because buyers did not see enough value. [L1315, 2023]
- Example: a free gift that costs about $2 was added to a product sold at $59.99 instead of $60, which skews the perceived value upward. This was the most profitable offer for that store. The gift must still be relevant to the customer and must not eat too much of the margin. [L0995, 2023; L0999, 2023]
- Bundle-stacking with value display, where the bonus is shown at its dollar value: offer the main product plus a free bonus item, show the bonus's original retail price in parentheses (for example "$19.99 value"), and add urgency language. This works because the free item increases perceived bundle value at minimal cost, and the specific dollar value makes the gift tangible. [L0313, 2026]
  - Example one: "FREE meal planning guide ($24.99 value) with every 90-day nutrition package."
  - Example two: "FREE stretching routine video ($14.99 value) with every recovery device-today only." [L0313, 2026]
- Example: a posture corrector bundle with a free gift. The free "Posture Fix Guide" was displayed at a $19.99 value and included with the corrector purchase. The ad used "limited time only" and "today only" language. The free guide was a low-cost value-add that increased perceived bundle worth without significant cost to the seller. [L0313, 2026]

### Presenting gifts on pages

- Show the free gift clearly on the product page and in the cart, with a headline, an image, and a claim message after adding to cart. Customers who do not know about the gift may not add the product. [L1315, 2023]
- Add free bonus items, such as a digital cheat sheet or e-book, as a visible line near Add to Cart and in the bundle block. State the bonus's stated value when it has one. Only promise a bonus that is actually delivered automatically after checkout. [L1188, 2026]
- Place an image labelled with the gift and its value under the product description on the product page. Use wording such as "free gift with your order, 39 USD value." [L0963, 2022]
- Bonuses should be shown on the product page as free gifts, not hidden behind extra steps. Do not make customers call or take extra steps to receive an offer, such as a gift voucher for large orders, because extra steps cause distractions and lost sales. [L1110, 2019] **
- Show the bonus on the landing page as part of the offer, next to the main product. [L0502, 2020+]
- Make the offer very visible on the site with a banner, a dropdown, or a product gallery image as the second image. [L0577, 2023]
- Present a bonus-offer pattern on an expensive product page: "If you buy [expensive product], you get [free item] included." The customer sees the bonus as added value to a purchase they already want. Two examples: "Buy the 4-seat sauna and get a free thermometer kit included." and "Order the commercial espresso machine and receive a free descaling set with it." [L1002, 2019] **
- Threshold gift pattern: "spend over [threshold] before [date] and get a bonus [gift] valued around [amount]." It works because buyers already plan to spend the amount, and the gift feels like extra value even when it has nothing to do with the store. [L1113, 2022] **
  - Example one: "Order over [threshold] this [holiday] and receive a free [gift] shipped with your order."
  - Example two: "Shop before [date] with over [threshold] in your cart and we add a bonus [gift] at no cost." [L1113, 2022]
- Gift-with-purchase promotion: offer a free trending product with any order placed before a deadline, such as 11:59 pm on Cyber Monday. It works even in niches that are not gift products, because buyers shopping for the main product also consider the bonus item as a present for someone else. [L1111, 2020] **
- Offer the gift as a bundle choice in the bundle block: the main product with the free bonus and the stated value shown at the bundle price. [L0313, 2026]
- Bundle presentation: list the offer as separate components stacked on the main product, not as one item. [L0683, undated]
- Bonus stacking for a 59.99 USD product: show the old price 119.99 USD next to the new price 59.99 USD, list a free ebook worth 19.99 USD, free shipping instead of 9.99 USD, and 24/7 customer service. Add a second unit for 39.99 USD instead of 59.99 USD to raise average order value. [L0683, undated]
- Each bonus should be given a stated value, such as the price it would cost on its own. Customers then see the total package as worth more than the price. [L0683, undated]
- Lead magnet pattern: keep the same product and the same price, and add a small free gift that matches the buyer's interest to raise conversion rate. Rotate gifts every two days so each gift gets a fair test. Example: a sold-out-style fitness band at the same price with a free water bottle; a kitchen knife with a free cutting board. [L0773, 2023]
- Bonus items can be presented as the "premium" in a sales letter, with the premium's price or worth named in the letter. [L1369, undated]
- Free gifts should be placed where the buyer sees them before deciding; the same bonus that is hidden in the footer does not increase conversion. [L1315, 2023]
- Add a free ebook with the shape of the offer: "Receive a 20 dollar ebook free with your order." [L0577, 2023]
- Show the gift image in the offer image, so the buyer knows exactly what they get. [L1363, 2023]
- Bonus delivered after purchase must be listed truthfully on the offer, so the buyer knows the bonus will arrive. [L1188, 2026]
