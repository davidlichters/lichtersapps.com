### Google Shopping and promotion display

- Google Shopping promotions can show offers such as free shipping or a discount code. Do not put a sale graphic over the product image, because the ad can be disapproved. [L0470, 2023]
- A Merchant Center promotion displays the offer in the Shopping ad, where the shopper can hover over or click it to see the promotion details. Shopping ads get the same extra incentive that promotion extensions give search ads. [L0712, 2020-26]
- Promotion extensions show a sale offer, such as a Black Friday sale, directly under the search ad, giving searchers an extra incentive to visit the site. The lesson calls them a strong way to increase CTR and notes the feature was recently rolled out, so check its current availability in Google Ads. [L0712, 2020-26]
- Google Merchant Center promotions: add a promotion such as a 5 percent off coupon code in Merchant Center. Google then shows a promo tab on your Google Shopping ad with the promotion details, which can increase click-through rate. [L0801, 2021]
- Price-tag caution for MAP suppliers: see the MAP note under price display. [L1092, 2022]

### Shopify setup and store-wide settings

- A store-wide discount code can be placed in the announcement bar. A product-specific code is shown through a dedicated product page template. [L0667, 2025]
- Use the product template procedure above for a unique offer on one product. [L0667, 2025]
- Use the free-gift banner procedure above for a free gift on a product page. [L0667, 2025]

#### Further details

- A free product offer that requires a $9.95 shipping and handling payment, placed prominently on the homepage, does not fit a store selling gifts and undermines trust. Review offers like this for fairness and fit before publishing them. [L0355, 2020-26]
