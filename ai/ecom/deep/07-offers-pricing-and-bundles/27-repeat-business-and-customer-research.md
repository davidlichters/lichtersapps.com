### Repeat business and customer research

#### Customer research

- *Older:* Customer research helps determine which upsells to offer, when to offer them, and how to generate repeat business from existing customers. [L0872, 2021-22]
- Test each product with different buyer qualification offers before deciding a market will not work. Track which offers convert for each product. [L0523, 2015+]

#### Survey rewards

- *Older:* Offer a small reward for completing the survey, typically around $10 to $15. It does not need to be large, since the goal is to thank customers for their time. [L1052, 2021]
- *Older:* If you run a loyalty program, reward survey completion with loyalty points, because points encourage a return purchase. Without a loyalty program, use a one-off discount code, a cash-back offer on the order, or an Amazon gift card. [L1052, 2021]
- *Older:* Cash-back and gift card rewards do not keep the money in your store, since customers may spend them elsewhere. Rewards that work inside your shop, such as loyalty points that can be used on upsell offers, drive repeat sales. [L1052, 2021]

#### High-value repeat customers

- Reward the best customers with a $50 coupon to encourage a fourth or fifth pair purchase; the discounted sale still carried about a 50% margin. Target this group as a VIP list, for example the top 700 customers who had bought four or more pairs. [L0790, 2017]
- Hyperactive buyers, identified fast after purchase, are the highest-value segment of a buyer list and should receive several upsells and downsells while their need is strong. [L0523, 2015+]
- Keep subscriber and buyer lists separate, and market to each differently. [L0523, 2015+]
