### Financing options: Shop Pay, Klarna, Affirm, PayPal Credit

#### Shop Pay Installments (Shopify rules)

- Shop Pay Installments is available for orders from $35 up to $30,000. Orders below $35 do not qualify, and the financing limit for a single purchase is $30,000. [L1295, 2020-26]
- Conflicting activation threshold: one source states that Shop Pay requires a minimum customer order value of $40-50 to activate as a payment option, and that products under $30 do not qualify. The Shopify tier above lists $35. Check the threshold in your own store's settings before setting any product price or messaging. [L0324, 2025+; L1295, 2020-26]
- For orders from $35 to $50, customers can pay in two interest-free bi-weekly installments of 50% of the total each. Customers can also simply pay the full amount at checkout. [L1295, 2020-26]
- For orders from $50 to $999, customers can pay in four interest-free bi-weekly installments. For example, an $800 order would be paid over about two months with no extra cost. [L1295, 2020-26]
- For orders from $150 up to $30,000, customers can pay in monthly installments. Interest rates depend on each customer's credit approval with Shopify. The merchant does not set or see these rates. [L1295, 2020-26]
- For orders above $25,000, the customer pays $5,000 upfront to Shopify, and the remaining balance is split into equal monthly installments that carry interest. This case is rare and mainly applies to large B2B orders. [L1295, 2020-26]
- Example: a hoodie priced at $44.50 falls in the $35 to $50 tier, so the product page shows an option to pay two interest-free installments of $22.25 each. Shoppers can ignore this and buy at full price, or choose the installment option at checkout. [L1295, 2020-26]
- The installment tiers and interest terms are preset by Shopify and cannot be changed by the merchant. The merchant decides only whether the option is switched on for the store. [L1295, 2020-26]

#### Shop Pay financing limit

- Shop Pay financing limit varies by store. It was originally $500 and is now higher on many stores, but some stores still have lower limits. Check the financing limit before assuming it covers your average order value. If the limit is below your average order, keep Klarna as a backup. (2023) [L0274, 2023]
- Conflicting limit: the $30,000 per-purchase figure above applies to the Shopify installment programme as currently described. The $500 original limit (2023) applies only where the store's own limit has not been raised. Use the limit shown in your store settings as the governing value. [L0274, 2023; L1295, 2020-26]

#### Deciding whether to add Klarna

- When deciding whether to offer Klarna alongside Shop Pay: first check whether your average order value fits within Shop Pay's financing limit. If Shop Pay's maximum financing covers your typical order size, Klarna is not necessary. Add Klarna only if Shop Pay's limit is too low for your average order value and you want to offer more customers financing options. (2024) [L0253, 2024]
- Buy-now-pay-later services (Afterpay, Klarna) increase customer acquisition, especially across the top five countries. The default risk falls entirely on the customer's credit. You receive full payment upfront from the BNPL provider, which eliminates merchant chargeback exposure. [L0324, 2025+]
- Offer payment options like Shopify Pay installments, Klarna or similar financing, so customers can spread payments, especially in weaker economic conditions. [L1113, 2022]
- A financing app allows customers to pay in installments. It directly increases conversion rate by removing upfront payment barriers for price-sensitive buyers. [L0040, 2020-26]

#### Affirm and PayPal Credit

- Offer PayPal Credit for customers. It gives six months with zero payments and 0% interest. Affirm integrates directly with Shopify, and both options can be offered together. [L1110, 2019]
- Payment plan via PayPal Credit: a visitor with a PayPal business account can offer this option on purchases above $99, with six months of no interest and no payments. The seller receives the full amount right away, and the buyer pays PayPal. [L1085, 2018]
- To accept PayPal Credit, use a PayPal business account and enable the option in the account settings. If the option is missing, contact PayPal through live chat to set it up. [L1085, 2018]
- PayPal Credit is enough for a typical order value. Stores with an average order value near $10,000 use more customised financing options. [L1085, 2018]

### Installment framing and payment plans

- Concealed price through payments: present the cost as a small monthly or instalment payment rather than the full price. In one test, "$39.95", "2 payments of $19.95" and "3 payments of $19.95" produced no difference in sales. Use payment framing carefully and test it, since it can lift the price a buyer accepts. [L1370, undated]
- Case: a $5,000 business equipment item was sold as four payments of $1,250. A premium version at $7,000 was offered as eight payments of $875, which is a lower monthly payment than the cheaper version. 72 percent of buyers picked the higher-priced premium version. [L1370, undated]
- For higher-priced items, split payment into a small first installment and monthly payments. Examples: $5 down with the balance over nine months, $1 a month for 12 months, or $2 a month for a large set. A low monthly amount makes the decision feel small. [L1369, undated]
- An illustrative $1,000 product could be offered on a 12-month payment plan with roughly $100 due at the first payment. Showing that smaller first amount next to the full price can make a high price feel affordable to shoppers who would otherwise hesitate. [L1295, 2020-26]
- Buyers who use installments carry the payment obligation, not the merchant. Keep the installment terms visible at the price point and the checkout so that the frame is not a surprise. [L1295, 2020-26]

### Testing and decision steps

#### Testing a payment option

Procedure:
1. Run the Shop Pay vs. Klarna test for one month. [L0274, 2023]
2. Review the last three months of sales and note the Klarna transaction count. [L0274, 2023]
3. Remove Klarna from the site by disabling the payment method. [L0274, 2023]
4. Enable Shop Pay installments. [L0274, 2023]
5. Run for exactly one month. [L0274, 2023]
6. After the month, compare: did Shop Pay transactions exceed, match or fall below the Klarna baseline? [L0274, 2023]
7. Decide whether to keep both, switch, or revert. [L0274, 2023]

#### Testing a fee or price level

- Test fee levels before choosing one, and measure uptake at each level. [L0955, 2025-26]
- Test guarantee length against conversion rate, since guarantee length is an offer element that can be measured. [L1164, 2026]
