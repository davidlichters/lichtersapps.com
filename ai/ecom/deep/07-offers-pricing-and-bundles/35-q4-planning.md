### Q4 planning

- Prepare five unique offers for the Q4 promotion season. Because Thanksgiving runs inside the Black Friday promotion, the five offers cover Halloween, Black Friday with Thanksgiving, Cyber Monday, Christmas and New Year. [L1221, 2024+]
- Run five unique offers across five promotions in Q4 instead of repeating the same discount. [L1285, 2024]
- Use five unique offers for the five holiday promotions instead of repeating the same discount, such as 5% off, because repeated offers stop converting after a few weeks. Each offer should give customers a different reason to buy now. [L1223, 2024+]
- Plan a promotion for each Q4 event: Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas and New Year. [L0626, 2025+]
- Create the promotional images and promotional email copy for each event before it starts. [L0626, 2025+]
- If a store has no Black Friday promotion ready, it is getting late, but it is still possible to plan and launch one before the event. [L0626, 2025+]
- Existing store offers, such as a site-wide discount code or free premium white-glove shipping, can be the basis for one of the five holiday promotions. Each promotion still needs a different offer, not the same discount repeated. [L1223, 2024+]
- Plan at least the next three months covering the holidays. To prepare holiday promotions:
  1. List every holiday that can carry a promotion. [L1113, 2022]
  2. Choose one offer for each event. [L1113, 2022]
  3. Create the Klaviyo emails for each promotion. [L1113, 2022]
  4. Create the images for Facebook remarketing ads. [L1113, 2022]
  5. Create the images for Google display ads. [L1113, 2022]
  [L1113, 2022] *Older advice (2022).*
- Set the start and end dates of the promotion before writing any promotional copy. [L1286, 2025]
- Use a single discount code applied to all customers, such as 10% off, combined with a delivery promise before a holiday deadline. Only promise a delivery date you can actually meet with your supplier and shipping times. [L0620, 2025+]
- Q4 is estimated to add about 30 percent to monthly revenue. The actual gain scales with the current revenue level. [L1116, 2025]
- Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales rise even without specific promotions. Once the first suppliers are live, add holiday-relevant promotions on the store to boost sales further. [L1116, 2025]
- Change the sale reason every three to four days, following a calendar: early November sale, 11.11, pre-Black Friday, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas and end-of-year sale. [L1194, undated]
- A monthly offer-brainstorming exercise can produce around 12 unique offers. Keep a running list so you always have spares. [L1224, 2025+]

### Black Friday and Thanksgiving

- Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales start before Black Friday and end at midnight on Cyber Monday. Long promotions tend to perform well, and one planned campaign included seven emails. [L1090, 2021] *Older advice (2021).*
- Start Black Friday promotions and ads at least one day before Black Friday. [L1092, 2022] *Older advice (2022).*
- Start the Black Friday sale on the Monday before Thanksgiving and run it through Black Friday, rather than extending it after the event. Longer sale windows produced more sales in this approach. [L1285, 2024; L1224, 2025+]
- Black Friday promotions perform better the longer they run, so start the sale before Thanksgiving rather than extending it after Black Friday. A five-day email run from Monday before Thanksgiving through Black Friday covers the Thanksgiving shopping window. [L1223, 2024+]
- A Black Friday promotion can run as a week-long event that includes Thanksgiving Day as one of its days, rather than as a single day. [L1115, 2024]
- Combine Thanksgiving and Black Friday into one roughly week-long promotion instead of a month-long Black Friday event. Start it on the Monday before Thanksgiving and end it on Black Friday. [L1224, 2025+]
- Thanksgiving is a US-specific holiday, so it is folded into the Black Friday promotion instead of getting its own campaign. The Thanksgiving-day email is themed around the holiday and repeats the discount code. [L1223, 2024+]
- For Black Friday and Cyber Monday, use aggressive discounts. Discounts of 60 to 70 percent perform best; discounts above 80 percent tend to be seen as too good to be true and convert worse. You may need to raise the product price beforehand so the discount still leaves a margin you can afford. [L0387, 2022]
- Escalating discount ladder for an event: start one month before the event with 20% off, then increase weekly to 30%, 40%, 50% and 60%. Raise prices incrementally as discounts increase to maintain margin and justify the perceived value of the offer. [L0309, 2024+]
- Run your main event (Black Friday or Christmas) with increasing offer pressure. [L0309, 2024+]
- Black Friday pricing on Google Shopping depends on the supplier agreements. Without a MAP restriction, lower the price in Shopify so the lower price shows in Google Shopping. [L1092, 2022] *Older advice (2022).*
- A bundle offer can run across the Black Friday to Cyber Monday window on the store, while Google Shopping ads stay unchanged. Promote the bundle on-site and through email and remarketing rather than as a Shopping promotion. [L1088, 2020] *Older advice (2020).*
- Pre-sale momentum: build it with a smaller offer that grows toward Black Friday, rather than a long sale that runs for weeks. Keep the largest offer for Black Friday itself. [L1225, 2025]
- A 24-hour sale extension is a common Black Friday tactic, especially for brands that rely on acquiring new customers, but it weakens urgency if the messaging is careless. [L1225, 2025]
- Black Friday and Cyber Monday are a sales dip-and-peak cycle. A sales dip before Black Friday and again in January and February is a normal ebb and flow for e-commerce, so plan cash flow around it rather than reacting with panic. [L1225, 2025]
- Free express shipping is used near shipping cut-off dates, for example before Black Friday, to let customers receive orders earlier and squeeze a few extra days of sales. [L1404, 2025-26]
- Source note on Black Friday timing: the minimum for ads is one day before (2022) [L1092, 2022]; the planned start is the Monday before Thanksgiving [L1224, 2025+; L1285, 2024]. Both apply, with the Monday start as the plan.

- The 2025 calendar runs Cyber Monday on Monday 1 December as a one-day promotion, with 2 emails (one in the morning and one in the evening) and remarketing all day. [L1224, 2025+; L1287, 2025]
- Cyber Monday 2024: early morning 2 December and about 17:00 on 2 December, two emails, one day only. [L1223, 2024+]

### Christmas planning

- Christmas promotions run before Christmas rather than on Christmas Day, with a message that the buy-by date for delivery before the holiday is closing. Allowing about two weeks for delivery, the emails go out in early-to-mid December. [L1223, 2024+]
- The target is gift buyers and customers who want high-ticket or bulky items, such as furniture or appliances, in time for the holiday. [L1223, 2024+]
- The Christmas offer message is "order now to get your product before Christmas". It drives a large spike, and it only works if suppliers can deliver within about two weeks. Confirm delivery times first. [L1224, 2025+]
- The Christmas promotion runs early in December because shoppers have usually finished their spending by Christmas and are less active online then. The main message is to order now to receive the product before Christmas, which drives a sharp spike in sales. [L1287, 2025]
- Example Christmas offer: discount code "xmas" gives 10 percent off, combined with a guarantee that the product will be delivered before Christmas. This only works if the supplier can deliver within about two weeks. [L1287, 2025]
- The 2025 September call paired a 10% off code with a guarantee that orders arrive before Christmas Day. [L1286, 2025]
- The 2024 Christmas calendar uses emails on Monday 9, Wednesday 11 and Friday 13 December, with the message as the last order date before Christmas. [L1223, 2024+]
- The 2025 Christmas calendar runs Monday 8 December to Friday 12 December, with 3 emails on 8, 10 and 12 December. [L1224, 2025+; L1287, 2025]
- Plan a Christmas sale for the second week of December so products can be delivered before the holiday. [L1090, 2021] *Older advice (2021).*
- Christmas offers can be smaller than Black Friday because most of the buying has already been done. [L1225, 2025]
- For a gift product, frame Christmas offers as gifting rather than as a buy-one-get-one deal. Use an experiential gift angle for buyers purchasing for a partner or themselves. [L1225, 2025]
- Example Christmas structure: free express shipping above a spend threshold plus around 20% off site-wide, with an added gift to keep the momentum. This should be lower than the Black Friday deal so the Black Friday buyers are not upset. [L1225, 2025]
- Gift wrapping: in Q4, offer free gift wrapping on high-tier bundles for giftable products to increase appeal to customers buying gifts for others. [L0232, 2026]
- Christmas packaging upsell: offer a simple checkbox on the checkout cart for "Add holiday gift packaging". It costs $0.50 to produce and sells for $3-4, adding $3-4 profit per order with no friction if presented as a non-intrusive option. [L0309, 2024+]
- Gift-friendly holiday bundle example: a holiday offer can bundle the main product with a free gift and a free ebook download. [L0692, 2023]
- Source note on gift wrap and packaging: free gift wrapping on high-tier bundles [L0232, 2026] and a paid packaging checkbox at $3-4 [L0309, 2024+] are both valid; the first is a bundle benefit, the second an add-on.

### Holiday shipping cut-offs

- Stop selling Christmas-delivery products by December 10-12 to guarantee shipping arrives before the holiday. Cutoff dates prevent logistics bottlenecks in late December when carrier capacity is exhausted and packages routinely arrive after December 25. [L0309, 2024+]
- Non-seasonal products (jewelry, gadgets, apparel, lifestyle items) can continue selling past December 10-12 for Christmas gifts if you clearly state on the order page that Christmas delivery is not guaranteed. Manage customer expectations through follow-up emails. [L0309, 2024+]
- Seasonal products (Christmas ornaments, holiday decorations) must stop by December 12 to prevent chargebacks. Organic channels with higher margins tolerate late-delivery complaints better than paid ads with lower margins. [L0309, 2024+]
- Procedure for the cutoff:
  1. Add a clear cutoff date to your website and order confirmation page stating "Orders placed after [date] cannot be guaranteed Christmas delivery." [L0309, 2024+]
  2. Include a 24-hour cancellation window on the confirmation email for customers who miss the cutoff. [L0309, 2024+]
  3. Update your refund policy to reflect that late-placed orders are ineligible for refunds due to shipping timing beyond your control. [L0309, 2024+]
- Holiday deadline: for the 2025 calendar, Christmas runs 8 to 12 December, within the 10-12 December cutoff range. [L0309, 2024+; L1224, 2025+]
- Expedited shipping offers: promote a cutoff time or a supplier ship-by date about a week before Christmas. [L1204, 2022]

### New Year

- Plan a New Year sale running from December 31 into January 1 as an end-of-year promotion. [L1090, 2021] *Older advice (2021).*
- Avoid ending a promotion on New Year's Day, when buyers are usually least active. [L1224, 2025+]
- Promotions should end on a Friday, because people are typically paid on Friday and spend more then. Avoid ending a promotion on New Year's Day, when many shoppers are not buying. [L1224, 2025+; L1287, 2025]
- The 2024 calendar runs New Year from Monday 30 December with Wednesday 1 January and Friday 3 January emails, closing Friday 3 January. [L1223, 2024+]
- The 2025 calendar runs New Year from 31 December, ending on Friday 2 January 2026 (see calendar version A, inferred). [L1224, 2025+]
- The 2025 calendar version B runs New Year from 31 December to Friday 2 January. [L1287, 2025]
- Source conflict: the 2021 advice ends the New Year sale on January 1 [L1090, 2021]; the 2025 rule avoids ending on New Year's Day [L1224, 2025+; L1287, 2025]. The later rule applies for new plans.

### Closing the Friday rule for promotions

- Close promotions on a Friday whenever possible, because people are paid on Fridays and will otherwise ask for more time. If a promotion must end on Wednesday or Thursday, send an extra email on the Friday after with the same offer extended by one day. [L1223, 2024+]
- Promotions should end on a Friday because people are typically paid on Friday and spend more then. [L1224, 2025+]

### Flash sales

- Flash sales are time-limited promotions with a strong offer, such as a high-ticket item at a deep discount, a product bundle, or a trending seasonal product. Pairing the time limit with limited stock creates urgency and triggers fear of missing out. [L1399, undated]
- Flash sale pattern: a promotion limited to a fixed time window, built around a high-value offer such as a high-ticket item at a deep discount, a product bundle, or a trending seasonal product. Pairing the time limit with limited stock creates urgency and taps into fear of missing out (FOMO). Example: a 48-hour sale on a premium bundle with only 50 units available. [L1400, undated]
- Pair flash sales with a fixed window: a 48-hour window on a premium bundle with 50 units, or a three-day window when quantity is limited. [L1400, undated; L1205, 2020-26]
- A monthly flash sale aimed at one email segment raises revenue in each month with a significant discount. [L0334, 2017]

### End-of-season clearance

- End-of-season discounts clear stock that needs to leave quickly. Shoppers also tend to wait for a sale before buying big-ticket items to get the largest savings. [L1399, undated]
- End-of-season discounts clear out stock that needs to leave quickly. Shoppers also tend to delay purchases of big-ticket items until a sale, so a seasonal discount captures that demand. [L1400, undated]
- Mystery box: a "trust us" bundle used to clear unsold or weaker stock. It only works if the brand keeps enough real quality in the box, otherwise the offer produces many returns and complaints. Because it can usually be run only once, the box can be a limited edition created for that one promotion. [L1404, 2025-26]
- A brand that runs frequent sales can keep its image by building a strong brand experience around promotions, including the ordering experience and merchandising, planned in advance so that the discounting is part of the brand. [L1404, 2025-26]
- Clearance pricing for an extension: 70% off, with the message "Almost out of stock: Black Friday stock still available". [L0309, 2024+]
- Sell out each collection, then move on to the next collection, rather than discounting constantly. [L1438, 2023+]

### Extending a promotion

- Continue the promotion for two weeks after the event concludes. In the extension period, message as "Almost out of stock: Black Friday stock still available" and offer 70% off to clear inventory. [L0309, 2024+]
- A 24-hour sale extension is a common Black Friday tactic. It weakens urgency if the messaging is careless. Extend only the same offer under the same terms, because adding deeper discounts or a new offer for late buyers beyond early loyal customers creates trouble. [L1225, 2025]
- Only change or extend an offer if the team can execute it fully, since a new offer means a website redo, updated email flows and new ad creatives. Extending a sale without proper execution hurts more than it helps, while a long sale period risks offer fatigue. [L1225, 2025]
- If you extend the sale or change the offer after Black Friday, make it exclusive to BFCM so the Black Friday offer keeps its value. [L1225, 2025]
- Extension email: if an event ends on Thursday or Wednesday, send a Friday email with the same offer extended by one day. [L1223, 2024+]
- The 2024 Halloween calendar uses a Friday extension email on 1 November, which is also the close. [L1223, 2024+]
- Source conflict: the extension of the 2024 Halloween and Black Friday sales is one day in the Friday rule [L1223, 2024+]; the two-week extension at 70% off is a clearance tactic [L0309, 2024+]. Use the one-day extension for offers still in their promotion, and the two-week clearance only for stock to be cleared.

### Closing a promotion

- Close an expired offer:
  1. Swap the expired offer for your default offer. [L0757, 2022]
  2. Revert every change made for the offer: remove it from the notification bar and the exit intent overlay. [L0757, 2022]
  3. Revert the offer-dependent changes: custom product pages, content pages, edited product pages, and the Bold Sales Motivator threshold display. [L0757, 2022]
- Leaving an expired offer on the site after it ends, including banners, overlays, pages and progress trackers, sends shoppers to a dead promotion. Always swap in the default offer and undo every promotion change. [L0757, 2022]
- Set the code to expire at the end of the promotion window so it stays time-bound and relevant to the holiday. [L0623, 2020-26]
- Always keep a default offer, such as 5 or 10 percent off, live after a promotion ends. People click promotional emails weeks later, and they should still find something to buy at a fair price. [L1204, 2022]
- Promotions should end on weekdays, with Friday used for the more expensive offers. [L0757, 2022]
- Use a sell-out close for limited stock: the offer runs for three days or until the 20 units sell out, whichever comes first. [L1205, 2020-26]

### Default offer after promotions

- Keep a default offer, such as 5 or 10 percent off, live after every promotion ends. [L1204, 2022]
- When an offer expires, swap in the default offer and undo every promotion change on the site. [L0757, 2022]

### Discount depth

- Escalate by week before a large event: 20%, then 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%. [L0309, 2024+]
- For Black Friday and Cyber Monday: 60-70% performs best; above 80% converts worse. [L0387, 2022]
- For monthly promotions: raise the usual coupon for a short window, for example 5% to 10% off all orders. [L1204, 2022]
- A repeated 5 percent offer loses responses after several uses. [L1285, 2024]
- Source conflict: 60-70% is the best range for Black Friday and Cyber Monday [L0387, 2022]; the 70% clearance is for stock after the event [L0309, 2024+]; the monthly rule is a 5-10% coupon [L1204, 2022]. Each figure applies to its own promotion type.

#### Further details

Run holiday promotions each month, with ad campaigns built for the biggest dates:
1. Plan a promotion for each month, with the largest ones around the holiday season.
2. Build ad campaigns for the specific promotion, such as Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas or New Year.
3. Create the promotion in Google Shopping promotions where available.
[L0470, 2023]

Running a bundle promotion that is not a Google Shopping promotion:
1. Leave the Google Shopping ads unchanged for the promotion period.
2. Show a notification bar on the website that promotes the bundle during the promotion window.
3. Use an exit intent overlay that announces the bundle price and links to the bundle page.
4. Email the store's list about the bundle.
5. Run remarketing ads on Facebook and Google to visitors who have not yet purchased.
[L1088, 2020]
