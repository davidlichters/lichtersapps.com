## Bonuses and free gifts

### Types of bonuses

- Bonus offers have four types: physical products (low-cost items customers need), digital products (online lessons, guides, e-books), expedited shipping upgrades (white-glove delivery), and extended warranties. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Physical product bonuses have historically given a stronger conversion lift than digital bonuses. Digital bonuses are covered under the digital versus physical comparison below. [L0314, 2020-26]
- A bonus is a free item tied to a purchase trigger, such as spending above a threshold or buying a specific product. Physical bonuses are shipped goods, and digital bonuses are delivered online. [L0757, 2022]
- Bonus items can include free ebooks, video guides, styling tutorials, complimentary gifts, or storage boxes. They increase perceived value at minimal cost and make rejection emotionally difficult for customers. [L0233, undated]
- Bonuses such as ebooks, video tutorials on how the product works, or videos on how it improves the buyer's life increase the value of an offer. Add them to the landing page as part of the offer. [L0502, 2020+]
- Free gifts, free upsells, installation services, and valuable content such as recipe books add perceived value. Consistently helpful free content throughout the buyer journey can build a higher perceived value than competitors. [L0411, 2021-22] **
- Free gift card: a gift card can serve as the free gift when a store wants to add value to an order and encourage a larger purchase. It works as an upsell because the buyer gets extra value without a physical product from the supplier. Show the gift clearly on the offer image so the visitor sees what they get. [L1363, 2023]
- Premium with its own value: add a premium that carries its own value, such as a one-year magazine subscription included with a set, so the same payment looks like more value. Name the premium's price or worth in the letter. [L1369, undated]
- Gift with purchase from another range: buying one product gives a free second product from a different range, typically a smaller, high-margin item that adds little weight, so shipping cost stays low. [L1404, 2025-26]
- Services the business already provides can be presented as bonuses at no extra cost to the seller. [L0683, undated]
- White-glove delivery means the delivery company brings the product inside, unpacks it, removes all packaging, and positions it. It costs an extra $100-$150 per order. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Physical bonuses should be items the buyer would need anyway, such as a seasonal ingredient pack, a protectant for leather furniture, or a book drop-shipped with the order. [L1204, 2022]

### Deciding whether a bonus pays

- Bonus offers must be strategised before traffic launch so they are built into the sales funnel as a proven conversion multiplier. [L0040, 2020-26]
- Offer free gifts or bonuses only when the margin allows it, such as on a best-selling product with strong profit per sale in a competitive niche. Free gifts make sense when competitors do not offer them and the product margin covers the cost. [L0941, 2022]
- Example: a free bonus gift worth about $50 can make sense on a product that earns roughly $400 to $500 profit per sale in a competitive niche, because competitors do not offer it and the gift drives extra orders. If the margin on the product is thin, do not offer a gift. [L0941, 2022]
- Include free gifts only when the margin still works after the gift cost. If a supplier charges state tax on shipments and this cannot be resolved, drop the free gifts. [L0968, 2021] **
- Keep the gift's cost low enough that it does not eat into the margin, and skew the value by setting a higher price point for the bundle. [L0999, 2023]
- Bundling a low-cost item with the main product, or adding a free gift that costs little, can raise perceived value and average order value while keeping margin healthy, as long as the gift is relevant and the customer wants it. [L0999, 2023]
- The bonus strategy is worth more when product margin is high. On a $300 or $800 profit product, a $20 bonus is negligible and can be given freely. [L1293, 2014]
- Very low-priced add-ons whose shipping cost is close to their price should not be sold alone. Offer them as a free bonus gift with high-ticket purchases, or sell them in multi-packs. [L0966, 2025] **
- Do not sell low-ticket accessories on their own. If the margin allows, include them as bonus gifts with a main product instead. [L0968, 2021] **
- Decide between a free bonus and a cross-sell by comparing the main product price with the price of the add-on. [L1110, 2019] **
- Cheap accessories on an expensive product should be listed as free bonuses on the product page, without their own product pages. Example: a 5,000 USD bike with a reflector and water bottle holder costing about 30 USD retail and 15 USD to the seller. [L1110, 2019] **
- Gift bonuses reduce the need for price cuts: a free accessory that costs the store $25 but is valued at $200 can be given with every order. [L1109, 2018] **
- Competitor stores add a free bonus item such as a guide ebook to a bundle. Use a bonus to raise perceived value without changing the base price. [L0600, 2026]

### Choosing gifts by cost and relevance

- Pick bonus items that cost little for you but solve buyer problems, such as storage, styling, cleaning, or wearing advice. Low-cost items like a jewelry box or an AI-drafted ebook can carry a large share of the perceived value. [L0502, 2020+]
- Bonus items should be inexpensive for you to offer, even if they look valuable to the buyer. Do not choose items with high wholesale costs or ones that threaten your margins, because bonuses are incentives for customers to choose you, not revenue-builders. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Bonus items that customers would need anyway with the product are the best choice. Ideally the bonus comes from the same manufacturer, so it can be added to the supplier's order at no extra effort. [L1293, 2014]
- Physical bonus pairings that work: a book for spending over $50; free ingredients or free bottles with a starter kit; a free protectant with a leather sofa. [L0757, 2022]
- Physical bonus ideas: a homebrewing starter kit can include a free ingredient sample pack or free bottles if bought before a deadline. Leather sofas can include a free leather protectant shipped with every order during a few promotion days each month. [L1205, 2020-26]
- The bonus should be closely related to the product's use or benefit. An unrelated free ebook is weak. For example, a shower filter should get an ebook on skin care for sensitive skin, linked to hard-water skin problems. [L0577, 2023]
- A free gift that is unrelated to the main product but valued by buyers can make an offer irresistible and help differentiate the store from competitors. Pick a low-cost gift that buyers want, such as a shaker or water bottle. [L1173, undated]
- Test whether the gift matches what the target buyer wants. Free packaging or free shipping insurance can be offered as a free-gift tab on some themes, but these are not always what buyers value. [L1173, undated]
- Choose a gift item that matches what the buyer needs, such as a cleaning product for a sofa. [L0963, 2022]
- Choose a gift the buyer will value, such as a popular consumer electronic item unrelated to the store's products, and check that its cost fits the margin on the main sale. [L1223, 2024+]
- Bonuses should be designed specifically for the main offer, not thrown together. Each one should be valuable enough that the prospect would pay for it alone. Start by asking which bonuses would perfectly complement the main product and add enormous value to the offer. [L1365, 2019+]
- Choose a promotion that feels valuable to customers and is still profitable. A free gift with high perceived value often moves more customers than a slightly larger money-off discount. [L1112, 2021] **
- A bonus gift with a perceived value of about $100, such as a leather desk mat, can persuade buyers to buy from your store instead of the supplier. Its cost to you is only the wholesale price of the gift, so it works best when the supplier does not offer the same extra. [L0934, 2023] **
- Choose bonus items from the same supplier as the main product to avoid extra shipping costs. Bonus items from different suppliers arrive separately and add shipping. [L0962, 2021] **
- Add-ons do not need to come from the same supplier as the main product. Choose the add-on that is cheapest to buy and has the highest perceived value for the customer. [L0577, 2023]
- Add-on pricing: the add-on's real cost is irrelevant to the buyer. Only the stated value matters, so label it with a clearly higher value. [L0577, 2023]
- Cheap free gifts such as a $1 item from AliExpress can be used as a bonus to entice buyers, as long as the business keeps a profit margin. [L0650, undated]
- Adding a complementary free item, for example socks with hoodies, or a free digital ebook raises perceived value and allows a higher offer price, as long as the item is cheap to produce or obtain. [L0650, undated]
- A free digital bonus, such as an ebook, can be added to every order as a zero-cost extra that makes the purchase more attractive. It works when the product margin is too thin for a bigger discount. [L0562, undated]
- If a margin is too thin for a $20 gift, give something that costs nothing, such as a digital recipe e-book, a video tutorial, or a free online training course, when it adds value for the customer. [L1293, 2014]
- Educational bonuses such as ebooks raise the perceived value of an offer, especially in health and wellness, kids, and parenting niches. This can allow a slightly higher offer price while still giving the customer a feeling of extra value. [L0650, undated]
- Example: a necklace offer gave the buyer one main product plus a second necklace of their choice for free. Most buyers chose a design linked to their home country. The offer raised perceived value while keeping the free item's cost low. [L0661, undated]
- Physical bonuses should be items the buyer would need anyway. Free books were also used as a bonus on orders over $50 in one store. [L1204, 2022]
- Two other examples of bonus items that carry perceived value: a free trending product offered with any order before a deadline, and a bonus of a snorkel with a mask. [L1111, 2020; L1293, 2014]
- Bonus pairings that match the main product's use: a free snorkel with a scuba mask, a free carrying case with a camera lens, a free air pump with a $300 air mattress (a $30 to $40 pump, since no air mattress comes with one and customers must buy it separately). [L1293, 2014]
- When a supplier includes a bonus item such as a bag or accessory, show it prominently as a "Free gift" block with a picture and its value. This makes the included extra visible and increases the perceived value of the offer. [L1130, 2020-26]
- Free accessories bundled with a main product are generally acceptable if the accessories are also sold separately at MAP. Check each supplier's policy if in doubt. [L1069, 2019] **
- Bundle low-cost extras as free bonus gifts rather than raising the base price, so the advertised price stays the same and MAP is not violated. [L1109, 2018] **

### Digital bonuses and free ebooks

- Digital bonus ideas: an e-book version of a guide given free for orders over $50; a free lesson, e-book, or downloadable video that matches what the customer needs; or a free extended trial of a related service through an affiliate partnership, such as a treadmill store offering a weight-management program trial. [L1205, 2020-26]
- Digital bonus pairings: spend over $50 for a free eBook; buy a drum set for free online lessons; buy a treadmill for an extended free trial of a weight-loss program such as Weight Watchers. [L0757, 2022]
- Digital bonuses (online lessons, guides, e-books) underperform physical bonuses in conversion lift, based on practical experience. When using digital bonuses, partner with external instructors or platforms rather than creating your own content. This reduces cost and leverages their expertise. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Example: a drum-set seller partnered with an online drum lesson platform to offer one or two free introductory lessons. The platform gains new customers, the store differentiates, and the bonus has minimal or no cost to the store. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Example: treadmill sellers can offer physical bonuses such as a free water bottle, towels, or apparel, or digital bonuses such as a free meal plan or an e-book on running technique. Test which types customers value most. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Educational bonuses should be checked against the product: the best bonus is directly linked to the buyer's use or benefit. [L0577, 2023]
- A free ebook worth $20 with a purchase increases conversion and can be delivered automatically after the order. [L1184, 2023+]
- A digital bonus such as an ebook was tested as extra value but did not beat a relevant physical free gift in one store. Prefer a physical gift that is closely related to the main product and its customer problem. [L1315, 2023]
- Free digital add-ons such as guides or templates can be listed at a stated value and given free with purchase, which makes the gift feel worth more. Customers who missed the add-on earlier can be offered it free later as a reason to buy again. [L0774, 2023]
- Free digital gift pattern: add a simple free guide, for example one made with ChatGPT and a couple of images, to the order to add value without cost. Some customers may pay a small price for the guide, after which the offer can be given free to previous buyers. [L0773, 2023]
- A free digital ebook that costs nothing to create can justify a $5.99 higher price for a bigger bundle tier, increasing perceived value at no cost. [L0232, 2026]
