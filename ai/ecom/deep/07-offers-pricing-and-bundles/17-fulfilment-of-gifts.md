### Fulfilment of gifts

#### Same-warehouse and separate shipments

- Fulfilment depends on where the gift ships from. [L0358, 2020]
  1. If the free gift ships from the same warehouse as the main product, bundle the gift into the product on your store so the supplier ships both together.
  2. If the free gift ships separately or from another warehouse, set up an automation in Zapier.
  3. Set the Zapier trigger to fire when a new order is placed with an order value above the threshold, for example above $2,000.
  4. Set the action to send an email to the fulfilment contact with the bonus gift and the customer's shipping address. [L0358, 2020] **
- Gift shipped separately from the main order: a store bought the gift directly from the brand and shipped it separately, with the business address as return address. The email list and ads promoted it. [L1113, 2022] **
- Gift-with-purchase fulfilment when a low-cost gift is added to a high-ticket order: [L1223, 2024+]
  1. Pick a gift the buyer will value (a popular consumer electronic item unrelated to the store's products) and check that its cost fits the margin on the main sale.
  2. When a customer completes a qualifying order, take the customer's shipping address from the order.
  3. Order the gift from the retailer and enter the customer's address as the delivery address, so the gift ships straight to the buyer.
  4. Let the buyer receive the gift as extra value on top of the main purchase. [L1223, 2024+]
- Gift-with-purchase value: a low-cost gift with purchase can drive pre-Christmas orders. The lesson's example was a roughly $80 iPod shipped to the buyer's address on top of a high-ticket purchase. The buyer sees the gift as extra value, and the seller pays only the gift and shipping cost. The gift should be desirable to the buyer and worth it to the seller at the chosen spend level. [L1223, 2024+]
- Check regularly that every order includes the free gift. A free gift that is not added to some orders creates refund and support problems. [L1173, undated]
- Thresholds such as $2,000 apply to the trigger for the Zapier automation step. [L0358, 2020]

#### Dropship SKUs and automation

- Setting up a free-gift or buy-one-get-one offer with a dedicated supplier SKU: [L1428, undated]
  1. Ask the supplier, for example on CJ, to create a variant that combines two items under one SKU.
  2. On the supplier's product page, choose the two-item variant. The total dropshipping price is shown at the bottom.
  3. Copy the SKU number of that variant.
  4. Add the variant to your store as the offer.
  5. Check that your margin covers the cost of two items before setting the price.
  With this setup the supplier ships both items on each order without any app. [L1428, undated]
- A temporary free-gift offer can use an automatic website discount instead, for example one free item when two are added to the cart. The supplier then ships each item separately, so the supplier must know the two items to ship. A permanent offer is better served by a dedicated SKU, because the supplier ships it automatically. [L1428, undated]
- Bonus setup steps: [L1293, 2014]
  1. List your top three bestsellers and the high-margin products you want to sell.
  2. For each, identify the accessory the buyer would normally need.
  3. Check the supplier's price list for cheap items you have not added to your store.
  4. Add the bonus to the supplier order confirmation, or to the SKU, so it ships with each order.
  5. Offer the bonus as a free gift once the purchase reaches the threshold. [L1293, 2014]

#### Shipping and compliance

- Customers may receive the add-on and the main product on different days, so tell them in the order confirmation email. [L0577, 2023]
- Free packaging, shipping, or extras that are not in the supplier's standard shipment need to be planned before launch so the order can be fulfilled. [L1173, undated]

### Digital versus physical performance

- Physical product bonuses have historically delivered stronger conversion lift than digital bonuses. [L0314, 2020-26] **
- Digital bonuses underperform physical bonuses in conversion lift, based on practical experience. [L0314, 2020-26]
- In one store, a digital bonus was tested as extra value but did not beat a relevant physical free gift. Prefer a physical gift that is closely related to the main product and its customer problem. [L1315, 2023]
- Physical bonuses can change with the purchase month. Each monthly window gives a different item that fits the season, such as a summer ingredient pack in one month and an autumn pack in the next. Buyers get a new reason to act, and the bonus is something they would probably buy anyway. [L1204, 2022]
- Physical bonus case: a merch store ran a five-day promotion in which every order over $50 received a free physical book. The offer raised average order value and turned old leads into customers. The bonus was given without any upsell and without extra charge. [L1205, 2020-26]
- Test different bonus types and combinations with different products over time. Do not expect to find the perfect bonus strategy immediately. Continuously measure which bonuses increase conversion and refine based on sales data. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Test whether the gift matches what the target buyer wants, and compare a physical gift with a digital gift on the same offer. [L1173, undated; L0314, 2020-26]
- In one store owner's experience, adding a bonus gift worth about $20 to the buyer tripled the conversion rate for the specific products it was attached to. The effect was tested on products the owner was already selling, not on new listings. [L1293, 2014]
- The $20 bonus strategy: give a low-cost item with each purchase to convert buyers who are comparing with Amazon and competitors. The formula is a product-specific bonus that the buyer would normally need anyway, worth about $20 to the buyer but cheap to the seller. A $50 to $100 margin product still earns up to $80 after the bonus. The reason: it removes the need to compete on price and makes the offer more valuable than the competitor's. [L1293, 2014]
- Scuba mask example: the bonus is a snorkel (wholesale about $5 to $10), offered free with any mask over $99. A podcast microphone can be bundled similarly with a stand, pop filter, and carrier case. A coffee maker example: a standard AeroPress versus a bundle that includes a reusable metal filter costing well under $1 to make, which the speaker chose over the competitor. [L1293, 2014]
- Free gift value comparisons: a free gift with high perceived value often moves more customers than a slightly larger money-off discount. [L1112, 2021] **
- Premium and bonus guidance from one source 2022: free gifts add perceived value without proportional cost. A guide valued at $19.99 costs the seller almost nothing to create, but the stated dollar value anchors customer perception of bundle worth. This is more effective than reducing the main product price by the same amount. [L0313, 2026]
- Free bonuses in product bundles work because they increase perceived value without proportional cost. This is more effective than reducing the main product price by the same amount. [L0313, 2026]
- A resistance band seller paired a $35.97 product with a $50 gift card for a partner weight-loss product, used to offer perceived value. The bonus was paired with an affiliate code so the seller could earn $50 to $150 when the buyer redeemed it. [L1153, 2016]
- Physical bonuses should match the buyer's need; a bonus that only looks valuable can be a waste. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Bonus items in a physical sense work when they directly address a real customer need related to the main product and cost much less than their apparent value to the buyer. Identify what customers need to use the main product effectively, then source an inexpensive version as a bonus. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Home-brewing starter kit: include a free ingredient pack (enough for one small batch) with purchases. Wholesale cost is minimal, customers see it as essential to getting started, and they are more likely to buy from you than from competitors not offering it. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Paddle boards with leashes: leashes cost $10-$15 each wholesale. Offering free leashes with board purchases provides genuine customer value while protecting margins. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Chandeliers priced at $1,000: offer a free pack of light bulbs with a wholesale cost of $20. Customers need bulbs and perceive high retail value. If competitors do not offer this, you gain a clear advantage. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Leather sofas: offer a free leather protectant to help customers maintain their purchase. This addresses post-purchase care concerns and adds perceived value without harming margins. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Treadmills: offer physical bonuses such as a free water bottle, towels, or apparel, or digital bonuses such as a free meal plan or an e-book on running technique. Test which types customers value most. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Free gift value: a bonus gift can also be a physical gift paired with a larger order. [L0999, 2023]
- Digital bonus versus physical gift: test both on the same product before choosing. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Airplane-style or large product purchases: the buyer may prefer a physical gift they need. [L0314, 2020-26]
- A physical gift that is cheap to print and delivered in the parcel can increase return visits and referrals. [L1437, 2018]
- Digital bonuses can be used when they are partnered with an external provider, so the store does not need to create the content. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Free ebook as a loss-prevention bonus: a free ebook on a related topic is a low-cost bonus that can be delivered automatically after purchase. [L1184, 2023+]
- A physical bonus would not be justified if the margin is too thin. [L0941, 2022]
- White-glove delivery: offer it free as a bonus for high-ticket items ($2,000 or above), where margins are strong and most competitors offer only curbside delivery. White-glove delivery costs an extra $100-$150 per order. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Do not offer expensive bonuses ($100-$150 upgrades) across your entire site. Target them to your highest-margin products or those priced $2,000 or above to protect overall profitability. [L0314, 2020-26]

### Common mistakes

- Do not choose bonus items that carry high wholesale costs or threaten your profit margins. Bonuses are incentives, not revenue-builders. [L0314, 2020-26]
- Offering a free ebook that is unrelated to the product is weak. The bonus should be directly linked to the product's use or benefit. [L0577, 2023]
- Filler chapters and placeholder text make the bonus look cheap and weaken the offer. [L0860, 2026]
- Free gifts that are not added to some orders create refund and support problems. Check regularly that every order includes the free gift. [L1173, undated]
- Customers who do not know about the gift may not add the product. Show the gift clearly on the product page and in the cart. [L1315, 2023]
- Do not promise a bonus that is not delivered automatically after checkout. [L1188, 2026]
- Bonus delivery must not be left to customers to claim through extra steps. [L1110, 2019] **
- Custom packaging made for one product is expensive. Only use custom add-ons if the supplier already has the item in stock. [L0577, 2023]

#### Further details

- Free gifts like 'Free mystery gift when you spend $X' or complementary items (travel bag with hair dryer bundle) increase perceived value at zero cost and make higher-price tiers more attractive. [L0232, 2026]

- A low-cost bonus closely tied to the main product, such as a recipe book included with an air fryer, can raise perceived value even when it costs little to give. The bonus works best when the buyer would naturally want it alongside that product. [L0411, 2021-22]

- Lead magnet test: 1) Keep product and price unchanged. 2) Add a free gift to the order. 3) If conversion does not rise, switch to a different gift every second day (e.g., Day 1 ring, Day 2 necklace, Day 3 ebook). 4) Keep the gift that produces the most sales; the winning gifts can suggest future bundles. [L0773, 2023]

Test a free gift as a lead magnet while keeping the same product and price:
1) Add a free gift to the product, starting with a low-cost item that fits the product, such as a yoga mat with a 29.99 USD stretching band, which sharply raised conversion in one case.
2) If conversion does not rise, change the gift every two days.
3) Rotate options in order, for example a ring first, then a necklace, then an e-book.
4) Keep the gift that produces the most sales; the winning gift also shows which bundle idea to test next.
