### Optional services and add-ons

- For freight shipments, offer optional services such as liftgate, inside delivery, and white glove service, and mark them up. Check with suppliers for any other custom options you can also charge for. [L1357, 2018]
- Item customizations such as custom colors can be marked up and work well for high-ticket items. Some customers send a hex code, which suppliers can often match. [L1357, 2018]
- If the manufacturer charges $100 for inside delivery, add a markup such as 10% to the customer's inside delivery line and record the base cost in the card notes. This keeps the owner's cost visible and the margin clear. Use the same markup rule for every quote of that type. [L1396, 2021-22]
- For an everyday free-shipping offer, do not add it as a line item in the cart. That creates a draft line item that blocks discount codes. Name the $0 shipping option instead, for example "free liftgate delivery." [L0963, 2022]
- Free shipping and paid add-ons: Shopify's shipping system and product options together can handle upgrades only as separate options, since free and paid shipping cannot both be offered on the same shipping setup. [L0247, 2021]

### Promotions that include shipping

- Free shipping can be used when margin covers it. For non-MAP products with high shipping costs, raise the product price to include shipping and show free shipping. [L0941, 2022]
- Free shipping is offered as a limited-time promotion, or through a discount code, as described in the free-shipping thresholds section. [L0502, 2020+]
- Free expedited shipping, free white glove in-home delivery, and free liftgate are $0 options that can be used as monetary promotions, restricted to chosen suppliers or to orders above a set price. [L1205, 2020-26; L0963, 2022]

### Merchant Center consistency

- When implementing shipping fees in Shopify, update the corresponding settings in Google Merchant Center with the same rules. Discrepancies between the two are flagged by Google as quality issues. [L0249, 2022]
- Shipping costs in Google Shopping ads are shown beside competitor prices, so the Merchant Center shipping settings must match the store's live shipping rules. [L0249, 2022; L0966, 2025]
- Where a heavy item uses a separate shipping profile (for example, to remain competitive on Google Shopping), that profile must be reflected in Merchant Center. [L0470, 2023; L0249, 2022]

#### Further details

- Use geo-targeting in paid ads to limit to regions near the supplier warehouse if shipping costs are high. This keeps shipping expenses lower and preserves profit margins. [L0249, 2022]

- When you offer free shipping, the customer is not charged for shipping but you pay it, and that cost comes out of your own margin. The supplier only gives free shipping if they choose to, so it is not automatic. [L1480, 2021-22]
