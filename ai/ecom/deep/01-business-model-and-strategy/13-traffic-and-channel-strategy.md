## Traffic and channel strategy

### Google First: The Core Channel

- **Rule:** Google is the primary traffic source because it is the fastest and most reliable channel. For a dropshipping store it is generally the fastest to produce results, so traffic work starts there. Do not use other platforms until Google traffic is highly profitable. [L0163, undated; L0586, 2020-26]
- **Principle:** Maximize Google traffic before expanding to other channels. All traffic sources funnel visitors to the store as the central hub. Splitting focus across channels too early dilutes results, so squeeze as much volume as possible out of Google first. [L0163, undated; L0586, 2020-26]
- **Expansion condition:** Other traffic platforms can still produce sales, but expanding to them only makes sense once the store is highly profitable and Google ads can no longer be scaled further. [L0586, 2020-26]
- **Proven methods first:** Use the proven traffic methods first in every country you sell in, not regional shopping comparison engines or local marketplaces. Even where a local platform is popular for e-commerce, the tested system is the default starting point. [L0710, 2020-26]
- **Blueprint:** The proven traffic blueprint contains roughly 30 lessons. Work through all of them and implement each one exactly as taught, rather than picking a subset, before testing any other channel. [L0710, 2020-26]
- **Reason:** Finishing the working system first gives it the highest likelihood of success, so it earns the money that funds later experiments. Switching to unproven channels while the existing system works adds risk without a clear gain. [L0710, 2020-26]
- **Common error:** Jumping to a new channel while the core traffic system is not yet profitable or running splits attention and cash before the business has a proven base. [L0710, 2020-26]
- **Google Business Profile:** Not necessary for running Google Shopping and Merchant Center campaigns. [L1115, 2024]
- **Local Inventory:** If the business operates physical retail stores alongside online sales, enable Local Inventory in shopping campaign settings. It shows products to customers searching near your stores, combining online and offline sales. For pure dropshipping with no physical stores, this setting does not apply. [L0225, 2015+]
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics is typically preferred over paid third-party attribution tools, which are expensive but can improve attribution at higher spend levels. [L0721, 2021-22]

### Channel Overview and Core Traffic Methods

Traffic methods for a Google-first store:

| Traffic method | Cost | Where the store appears | Who it targets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic Google search | Free | Unpaid text links in Google results, ideally on page one | People searching for a product, brand or SKU |
| Google product listings | Free | Product results in the Google Shopping section | People browsing products on Google Shopping |
| Google Shopping ads | Paid | Top of the Google Shopping section and the front of Google results, showing product image, store name and price | People searching for a product who are ready to compare prices |
| Google remarketing ads | Paid ads (ad cost not stated in lesson) | Ads shown after a visit, showing the exact product viewed | Visitors who left the store without buying |

[L0586, 2020-26]

- Traffic for a Shopify store can come from Google Shopping product listing ads, Google search text ads, organic search, Bing ads, paid social on Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest and Twitter, and a Dream 100 source. The list is expected to grow as more channels are added. [L0676, 2020-26]
- A store should use four traffic channels together: free organic search, free Shopping listings, paid Shopping ads, and remarketing. Each covers a different stage of the buyer journey. [L1393, 2020-26]
- Google search is the primary marketing channel for a high-ticket dropshipping store, and paid ads plus organic search make up most of its traffic. Bing and other search engines are much smaller and should not form a significant part of the effort. [L1486, 2021-22]
- A search-driven funnel of Google Shopping ads, organic search, email and retargeting, run without social media ads, is described as capable of growing a single high-ticket store to around $1 million a year in revenue. Other channels can be added later. [L0458, 2021-22]
- A mature business can run four or five profitable traffic streams, such as affiliate marketing, a presence on Amazon, organic search traffic and the own website. [L0439, 2021-22]
- Establishing profitable paid search first gives a strong base for later channels. SEO, social and content tend to perform better when added after paid campaigns already run profitably. [L0463, 2020-26]

### Platform Choice and Channel Fit

- **Three selection factors:** Platform choice depends on beginner level (Meta and TikTok), proof-of-concept status (Google Ads when the product already sells), and expertise in platform mechanics (Pinterest, Twitter/X, influencer marketing). [L0296, 2021+]
- **Message placement:** Even a strong product or skilled seller earns little when the offer appears in the wrong place. Put the message where buyers already spend time, both physically and online. [L0378, 2019+]
- **Platform audiences differ:** Different platforms reach different audiences, so the same product can perform differently on Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok or Pinterest. Match the product to the platform where its likely buyers spend time. [L0562, undated]
- **New sources:** New and unique traffic sources are found through market research in your specific industry, not copied from others. Some sources only work for certain niches, so test whether a source fits your customers. [L0413, 2016]
- **Omnipresence:** Attention drives sales, and because customer attention is fragmented and short, a business should aim to be omnipresent across the customer's online experience, so it is repeatedly encountered wherever the customer is. [L0414, 2021-22]
- **Omnipresence defined:** Being seen wherever your customers spend time online within your niche, so that you are the brand they encounter most often. The goal is to hold a larger share of your market's attention than competitors, because the most frequently seen brand tends to be chosen at the point of purchase. [L1117, 2021-22]
- **Why omnipresence:** Four reasons support it: customers take a journey before buying, that journey is hard to hold attention through, attention translates into revenue, and a brand that appears more often than competitors gains the edge. Journey length varies by price and type. A low-cost t-shirt needs less consideration than a $3,000 coffee machine. [L1117, 2021-22]
- **Six components:** The full marketing system is built from six components used together: Know, Convert, Educate, Promote, Develop and Consolidate. Each should make at least one other better, so they must run simultaneously rather than one at a time. [L0414, 2021-22]
- **Traditional funnel inputs:** Traditional funnels are fed by paid traffic (Google Ads, Bing Ads, paid social), organic search (SEO on Google and Bing) and outreach strategies such as Dream 100. After the sale, a customer can also be pushed to bring in new buyers, which raises the value of each customer. [L0417, 2020-26]

### Platform Account Setup and Location Rules

- **VPN rules:** A VPN is required when creating a TikTok account, because the platform detects location and assigns accounts to that location. For Pinterest, create the account with a US VPN, after which ads can run without the VPN. [L0788, undated]
- **Facebook and Google:** Facebook Ads do not require a VPN; target the country directly in campaign settings. Google Ads also does not require a VPN, but business details must match the location being targeted. [L0788, undated]

### Shopping and Search Intent

- **Commercial share of search:** Only about 20% of Google searches have commercial, bottom-of-funnel intent. The other roughly 80% are informational or directional. Paid traffic is easiest to scale by targeting buying-intent searches first, because people who already want to buy need less persuasion. [L0510, 2018]
- **Why Shopping works:** Shopping and Product Listing Ads are shown mainly for commercial searches because the platform filters queries by intent, so the advertiser reaches buyers without extra targeting work. [L0510, 2018]
- **Two sources of buying-stage traffic:** Informational searches are reached through content without ads. Commercial searches are reached through product listing ads (Shopping/PLA). [L1031, 2018]
- **Ad logic:** Google's ad logic moves people through four stages: awareness, interest, consideration and conversion. An ad only earns revenue when people click it at these stages, so the ad must make people want to click. [L1031, 2018]
- **Foundational hinges:** A small number of foundational changes to ads and product data can lower CPC, increase clicks and increase conversions at the same time. The lesson presents these as hinges that move the whole system. [L1031, 2018]
- **Quality over volume:** Visitors actively searching for the product convert far better than large untargeted audiences. One store earned a $4,935 order from only 26 website visitors. [L1223, 2024+]
- **Case, first sale from negative keywords:** A new store with no ad history reached its first sale of $2,135 from only 10 website visitors. The owner had onboarded a new supplier, then spent about two weeks on Google PLAs with negative keywords, restricting traffic to SKU-number and brand-term searches. The conversion came from people already searching for the exact product or brand. [L1223, 2024+]
- **Case, baseline before ads:** A coached dropshipping store first built the baseline of 100 products and Google Shopping traffic, then spent about $700 per month on ads while losing money. Because the foundation was in place, sales grew from a few thousand dollars to about $15,000 in one month after optimization. A working baseline makes growth much easier. [L1126, 2018]
- **Price fit for Google Ads:** Google Ads fits products priced around $200 to $300 and above, because shoppers usually search for the item before buying, so the purchase is intent-driven rather than impulsive. Search demand fits higher-ticket goods better than impulse-driven social feeds. [L1508, 2022+]
- **Shopping competition:** Competition in Shopping is measured per product. If one product is sold by 25 stores, appearing near the top is hard even with an aggressive bid. [L0856, 2024]
- **Undercut listings:** Do not spend on Google Shopping for products where competitors undercut on every listing, because traffic will not convert at the price difference. [L0969, 2022]
- **Keep paying on clicks without sales:** Do not stop paid ads when a niche product only gets clicks without sales. Move the effort into optimizing keywords, bids and product pages, since data is already coming in. [L0856, 2024]
- **Free-only stores:** A store running on free traffic alone is a valid option for products where ads do not convert. These products can stay on the site without ad spend. (Source 2023.) [L0855, 2023]

**Build order for paid traffic:**

1. Bottom-of-funnel Google Shopping for buying searches.
2. Facebook dynamic product ad retargeting for visitors who left.
3. CTR and quality score optimization to lower CPCs.
4. Email and lifetime value to raise revenue per customer.

Each step adds to the previous one. [L0510, 2018]

### Funnel Stages (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU)

- **Definitions:** TOFU is top of funnel, MOFU is middle of funnel and BOFU is bottom of funnel. Strategy and ads must match the audience's funnel stage, because a message aimed at a later stage than the visitor is currently in will not convert. [L1386, 2020-26]
- **Match the ad to the stage:** A bottom-of-funnel offer served to a top-of-funnel audience is not profitable, because the searcher still needs answers before considering a purchase. Send these visitors first to informational content that answers their questions. [L1386, 2020-26]
- **Example, TOFU (surfing):** A searcher typing "Do you need to be strong to go surfing?" is top of funnel, so an ad pushing a surfboard to that query is unprofitable. A top-of-funnel ad answering common beginner questions, with a landing page that captures the email address, is profitable within a few weeks, with the sale coming some weeks later. [L1386, 2020-26]
- **Example, BOFU (surfing):** A searcher typing "surfboard for beginner" is bottom of funnel. An ad saying "buy the best surfboard for beginners," sent to a landing page explaining why it suits beginners, can produce a sale within a few days. [L1386, 2020-26]
- **Start at the bottom:** Start paid traffic at the bottom of the funnel. Make campaigns profitable with people already ready to buy, and only then move up to awareness audiences. If a business cannot convert the small group of buy-now searchers, it should not spend large sums on awareness audiences. [L1386, 2020-26; L1031, 2018]
- **Do not convince non-searchers:** Do not try to convince people who are not looking for the product to buy it. Spending on this audience wastes budget and lowers results. [L1031, 2018]
- **Expand later:** Expand the funnel only after bottom-of-funnel campaigns run well. The lesson treats the rest of the funnel, labeled as 80%, as a later step rather than something to build first. [L1031, 2018]
- **Audience size by stage:** Audience size grows as you move up the funnel. Searches for a product to buy are fewer than searches for the product category, which are fewer than searches for a symptom or problem. Plan budget and messaging with this size gap in mind. [L1386, 2020-26]
- **Match channel to stage:** Paid traffic lets you reach a user at any stage with a message that fits that stage. Match each channel to a funnel stage instead of asking every ad for an immediate sale. [L1142, 2020-26]
- **Build bottom-up:** Start with Shopping campaigns to capture immediate sales, then add upper-funnel channels that build brand trust over time. Plan the sequence backwards from the sale, so each earlier channel feeds the next. [L1142, 2020-26]
- **Upper-funnel for unsearched products:** Starting with Facebook TOFU ads is possible but harder for products people do not search for, such as new unique products. These buyers need more touchpoints, and influencer content often works best as the first exposure before retargeting and an after-visit funnel. [L0510, 2018]
- **B2B, low-volume high-ticket:** For low-volume, high-ticket B2B products, search capture still comes first, because buyers who search are commercially minded. The audience is then extended up the funnel with display, Facebook or LinkedIn ads aimed at professional groups and industry news readers, since most potential buyers are not actively searching. [L0510, 2018]
- **B2B audience size:** B2B ads with small, specific audiences are harder than B2C ads because the number of people at each funnel stage is small. Social ads can still work when almost every business can use the product, because retargeting audiences then become large enough. [L0510, 2018]

### Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

- **Still valid, but pricier:** Facebook remains a valid platform for direct response ads, especially for cold traffic and retargeting, but it has become more expensive. Use interest-based targeting and strong creatives when pixel data is limited. [L0561, 2023+]
- **Cross-platform effect:** Google search and Facebook feed each other. Facebook ads can drive branded search on Google. [L0561, 2023+]
- **Retargeting priority:** Facebook dynamic retargeting was moved up to the second most important ad type for e-commerce stores, after Google Shopping, once the focus shifted to bottom-of-funnel audiences. It had earlier been ranked third, next to Google search retargeting. [L0510, 2018]
- **Warm audiences:** Building audiences from people who already acted on your site or social accounts lowers ad costs and raises conversion rates. Advertising to warm visitors is cheaper than cold prospecting. [L0757, 2022]
- **Shopper chain:** Shopping ads bring shoppers to the product page, then remarketing and trust-building emails bring them back. Set up each step to feed the next. [L0757, 2022]
- **Audiences per product:** Aim for at least 3 to 4 winning audiences per product. Single audiences can die after a few weeks or months. Several winners let you scale each one separately and later build lookalike audiences from them. [L0648, undated]
- **Social for high-ticket (2025):** For high-ticket products costing $1,000 to $3,000, a large social following matters less than showing up in front of people already searching to buy. Social media marketing is a weak main sales channel for this kind of store. [L1097, 2025]
- **Facebook and Google on the Shopify list:** Facebook paid social is one of the Shopify traffic sources listed under the core traffic methods. [L0676, 2020-26]

Older order advice (2020): Across Google results, the TikTok channel was not tested and the team put its effort elsewhere. Google Shopping and search came first, followed by Facebook and Instagram remarketing, with YouTube, Discovery and display as later expansions. [L0468, 2020]

### TikTok

- **Organic before paid:** Start with organic content to find a winning product before spending on Spark Ads. Paid promotion is most useful after organic results prove that a product and hook convert. [L1383, 2022]
- **Account age:** A newer account often performs better than an older one, but an existing account with a large follower base still has value. An account with about 10,000 followers can apply for the Creator Fund, use a playlist linked from the profile, and reach more viewers. [L1383, 2022]
- **Switching products:** When switching an existing account to a different product, expect slow results for the first one to two weeks. Keep posting without skipping days, and the algorithm usually picks the account back up. [L1383, 2022]
- **Age range:** Set the age range to start at 18 and never target anyone under 18, because they cannot complete a purchase. Examples: women 18 to 35 for a female-oriented product, or men 18 to 45 for a male-oriented product that tested well. [L1383, 2022]
- **Account differences:** TikTok ad accounts differ in performance, and some accounts show high CPMs for the same video. If a new account shows poor results despite good creatives and landing pages, the account itself can be the reason. [L1382, undated]
- **Older advice, 2020:** TikTok was not tested in that period; the effort went to Google, Facebook and Instagram first. [L0468, 2020]
- **Testing budget:** TikTok ads can be one of the untested channels in the roughly 10% testing share of a larger budget (see Experimentation Budgets). [L0970, 2023]

### Pinterest

**Fit and audience:**

- **Scale and intent:** Pinterest has more than 400 million active users, most of them using it for inspiration, which makes it suited to products tied to home decor, fashion, beauty and health. Roughly 25% of the time users spend on Pinterest is spent on shopping, so buying intent is high compared with platforms where people only browse. [L1135, undated]
- **Market:** The US accounts for about 50 to 60% of the Pinterest audience. The lesson states that around 55% of US users earn more than $100,000 per year. [L1135, undated]
- **Order value:** The average order value on Pinterest is above $50, which the lesson presents as higher than most other ad platforms. [L1135, undated]
- **Less saturated:** Pinterest is less saturated than Facebook, TikTok or Google, so many advertisers have not yet tested it, which can reduce competition for ad space. [L1135, undated]
- **Products banned elsewhere:** Products that often get banned on Facebook or TikTok can usually run on Pinterest, including traditional dropshipping products. [L1135, undated]
- **Regional costs:** Pinterest ads are cheaper in the UK and Europe than in the US, but the UK and Europe still have a solid market. The US is more competitive because it has the largest user base. [L0788, undated]
- **Account restrictions:** Pinterest account restrictions can usually be lifted within one to two days, or even a few hours, and personal support from Pinterest is available for account problems. [L1135, undated]
- **Account creation:** For Pinterest, create the account with a US VPN, after which you can run ads without the VPN. [L0788, undated]
- **Backup during bans:** Pinterest and Snapchat brought in some sales while Google and Meta accounts were blocked. Keep alternative channels ready as a backup when a main ad account is suspended. [L1214, 2023+]

**Testing and budget:**

- **Test budget and timing:** Pinterest Ads is a low-cost platform for specific niches and can be tested with a daily budget of about $5. Results usually take around one week, and no sales may appear in the first seven days. [L0788, undated]
- **Timing, second source:** Results take longer than on TikTok or Facebook, typically six to seven days. Not every product works on Pinterest, so only specific products should be tested. [L1135, undated]
- **Cost of the test:** A $5 daily budget for seven days costs about $35. In one case, CPM for a US audience dropped from $15 at the start to $2 after seven days, with a CPC of about 2 cents. [L0788, undated]
- **Low budgets win early:** On Pinterest, higher budgets do not help early on, because the platform needs time to learn the audience. In the speaker's tests the most profitable products ran at $5 per day, even though some campaigns at $100 per day were tested. [L1134, undated]
- **CPM and quality trade-off:** Pinterest ads can deliver very low CPMs, so the platform is cheap to test on. Early traffic quality is usually weaker because the algorithm is still learning the audience. [L1135, undated]

**Organic and free traffic:**

- **Free traffic:** Pinterest works best as a free traffic source for stores that already have an audience, since those visitors are the easiest to convert into quick wins. Stores without an audience can still get traffic through a viral pin, but that outcome is less predictable. [L1136, 2014]
- **User intent:** Pinterest users come for inspiration, discovery, new products and how-to content, so store content should be built for that purpose. Many save products as a wish list to buy later, so pinned products can convert after a delay. [L1136, 2014]
- **Peak days:** Pinterest referral traffic on Black Friday and Cyber Monday produced 2x to 3x the e-commerce revenue of normal days with the same traffic volume. This points to high buying intent in Pinterest traffic during peak shopping days. [L1136, 2014]
- **Viral spikes:** Pinterest traffic spikes of 100 to 500 visits per hour can happen regularly when content is shared well. Virality is easier to reach on Pinterest than on most other channels, according to the speaker's experience. [L1136, 2014]

**Pinterest setup procedure:**

1. Search pinterest.com/source/ with your store domain to see who has already pinned your products, then reach out to those users.
2. Make sure the Pin It button and Rich Pins are turned on in the store, which Shopify and BigCommerce do automatically.
3. Share your pins and your Pinterest profile everywhere your audience already is, including on the website, in email and on other social networks.
4. Build a great profile with product, inspiration, resource, behind-the-scenes and testimonial boards that support the store.
5. Create good-looking vertical images for your content and products.
6. Use group boards by finding them, getting access, posting your content, driving traffic and then retargeting those visitors.

[L1136, 2014]
