### Digital product tests

- Testing a digital product with ads that show a high CPM can still be worth it for a high-ticket item when the product's margin supports spending more and gathering more data. Patience is needed, since lower-ticket products need more spending to gather enough data. [L0678, 2023]
- Before building a digital product, confirm that buyers will work through it on their own. In one case, a paid digital course for young children turned into requests for one-on-one help, so the product became a service. Test self-study demand before investing in production. [L1017, undated]
- Before building a full ebook, check with research that the proposed solution really helps the problem, and only then treat it as a sellable product. [L1131, 2018+]
- Use ChatGPT to generate product, recipe and offer ideas quickly, but treat every idea as a hypothesis that must be validated with ads and sales data before scaling. [L1131, 2018+]
- Digital products such as recipe ebooks can be tested in markets where the main competitor is not yet advertising. A market with very high competition, such as the US in this example, can be skipped in favour of a smaller one like Germany. [L1131, 2018+]
- A digital product test could be a short PDF crash course that explains a topic most people have heard of but do not understand, such as the basics of crypto assets like Bitcoin and Ethereum, promoted with ads to see if people buy it. Clear, simple teaching of a popular trend is the core idea. [L1161, 2024+]
- A digital product such as an AI-tool guide can sell for 40 to 50 USD when it teaches how to get the most value from a tool. Subscribing to a tool is easy, but using it well is harder, which creates demand for guidance. [L1439, 2024+]
- An AI-tool guide ebook for dropshipping can be sold at 40 to 50 USD, with step-by-step blueprints for using a specific tool. The product works best when the niche and the problem it solves are clearly defined. [L1439, 2024+]

### Copying winners into new markets

**Copying proven videos and products**

- When starting from scratch, the fastest way to learn whether a product can sell profitably is to test proven top-performing videos from others, because you do not need original content for your first wins. At this stage speed matters more than originality. [L1252, 2026]
- Ripping proven videos is a starting point, not the long-term method. Collect sales data quickly and scale what sells. [L1252, 2026]
- Two similar brands scaled the same concept by entering different markets and adding unique selling points. This shows that a different market can work even with a similar product. [L1436, undated]
- A proven product from a successful brand can be copied into a new country where no local competitor is visible. This reduces the research effort needed to start, and the rest of the work lies in sourcing, photos and advertising rather than in inventing the product. [L1439, 2024+]

**Markets and stores**

- Find existing, verified winning stores in another market and sell the same products in a new market. The presenter's example: a store made about 1 million USD in the US, and the same products were sold first in Switzerland, then Germany and Austria. [L0642, undated]
- A product that already scales in the USA or Australia can be carried into German-speaking markets such as Germany, Austria and Switzerland. This works with a German-language store, German-language ads and the payment options local buyers use. [L0361, 2024]
- In the presenter's example, a home decor store had a price of CHF 59.99 (about 80 USD CAD) with a test spend limit set at 5 USD per ad set. The sales target per winning item was a few sales per day. [L0642, undated]

**Case: winter product in Switzerland**

- A seller with two years of dropshipping experience and some saved capital copied a proven winter product idea from a successful brand and entered Switzerland, where no local competitors were visible. They contacted Chinese suppliers and ordered 25 design variations, but first had one sample sent to check quality. [L1439, 2024+]
- A low-budget photo shoot with friends was made, and the supplier product photos were used in the first ads. The product launched on 1 January. The first ads brought around 6,000 USD in January and February, with the cat, koala and unicorn designs selling best and being reordered. [L1439, 2024+]
- In March and April, stock was reordered, the website was to be improved, and interests, creatives and a second photo shoot were prepared for the next season. From September to February, sales reached around 31,000 USD with a low Facebook ad budget, and every order was fulfilled manually from a warehouse. After ads were stopped, steady sales continued through word of mouth. [L1439, 2024+]
