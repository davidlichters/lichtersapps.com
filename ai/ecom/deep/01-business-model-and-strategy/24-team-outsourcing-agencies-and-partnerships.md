## Team, outsourcing, agencies and partnerships

### Skills to build before delegating

- Six core skills are needed for a dropshipping business: niche selection, Shopify store development, copywriting, deal-making, paid advertising on Google and Facebook, and search engine optimization. A weakness in any one of them limits the whole business. [L0831, 2019]
- A beginner first builds skills in product research, store building and marketing before chasing revenue. The business runs as three linked parts: product, store and marketing. Weak results in one part usually show up as a weak point in the funnel and should be checked in that order. [L1440, 2021+]
- Build the business on learned skills, not only on tools: consumer psychology, advertising best practices, design principles such as color theory, layout and Gestalt proximity, and persuasion principles such as scarcity and reciprocity. Books cited as starting points are *The Mini MBA* and Cialdini's *Six Principles of Persuasion*. [L1077, undated]

### Delegation rules

- Spend your time on tasks that directly produce revenue, such as writing sales copy or making sales videos. Hand off or drop tasks you are not strong at, because they take time away from the work that earns money. [L0378, 2019+]
- The biggest beginner mistake is trying to do everything in the business yourself. Map out which roles you enjoy and keep those, and outsource the rest, because doing everything alone can lead to burnout. [L0780, 2024+]
- Delegate only tasks that someone else can do without lowering the quality of the business.
  - Keep in-house: brand-shaping work such as choosing products, running ads and writing product descriptions.
  - Hand off: supplier negotiation, email replies, order fulfillment and tasks the owner dislikes. [L1431, 2020+]
- A first-time seller should not outsource all content creation to a freelancer right away. This blocks the owner from learning the platform and advertising, and outsourced content tends to cost more in the long run and rarely matches what the owner could produce. Learn to make the content and run ads yourself first, then hire once you know what good output looks like. [L1077, undated]
- Advertising skills are the foundation for hiring well. The owner must know consumer psychology, target audience needs and platform mechanics to judge the work of anyone producing content. Learn these before delegating. [L1077, undated]

### Time budgeting

- Target: a semi-automated online store that the owner can run in 30 minutes or less per day. It does not need to be fully hands-off, but it should not require daily involvement from sunrise to sunset. [L0592, 2020-26]
- Once a store is running, maintenance can be handled in 15 to 30 minutes per day, and later in a few hours per week. Building the store is far more work than this and includes supplier calls and setup. ** [L1068, 2019]
- Picking, packing and posting every order yourself can take 2 to 3 hours a day. This is a poor return on time and becomes the limit on growth. [L1437, 2018]
- Progress tends to happen in phases. At the start, time is traded for money through hands-on work. Later, money is reinvested to buy systems and help that generate more money. Moving from the first phase to the second requires systemizing what works. [L0819, 2020+]

### Automation order

- Automate repeatable business processes first, since they run the same way every time and can be copied without deep understanding. Automation comes before outsourcing, and it can be started very early. [L1447, 2020-26]
- Automation and outsourcing should only be added once the store is already profitable and the core setup tasks have been completed. A store that has not reached that stage is not ready for these steps. [L0592, 2020-26]
- The sources set different conditions for timing:
  - Single repeatable processes: automate early, with no profit requirement stated. [L1447, 2020-26]
  - Wider automation and outsourcing: only after profit and completed core setup. [L0592, 2020-26]

### VA hiring timing

- Once a store is profitable and orders are being fulfilled, the first reinvestment worth making is hiring a virtual assistant (VA) who takes over recurring day-to-day tasks. Make this hire only when the business can afford it. [L0970, 2023]
- Consider hiring a VA once the store has steady orders and monthly profit reaches roughly 3,000 to 4,000 EUR. At that level, outsourcing time-consuming work frees the owner to focus on ads and the offer. [L1431, 2020+]
- Order handling uses a different trigger: move it to suppliers or a team as early as possible, because picking, packing and posting can take 2 to 3 hours a day. [L1437, 2018]
- Case (student story "Carla: from $60K to $500K in 12 months", talk at a 2018 retreat): a non-technical parent went from hand-selling to a delegated store.

| Stage | What happened |
|---|---|
| Starting point | Sold small goods by hand on eBay and Facebook; made about $60,000 profit in one year; spent 2 to 3 hours a day packing and posting. [L1437, 2018] |
| Move | Built a dropshipping store on Shopify with Google search text ads. [L1437, 2018] |
| First sale | Came 45 minutes after the first ad launched: $300 revenue, $80 profit. [L1437, 2018] |
| Suppliers | About 10 to 15 suppliers contacted over the following months. [L1437, 2018] |
| Store audit | A coach's audit lifted sales by nearly $6,000 from January to February. [L1437, 2018] |
| First VA | Hired in March; April sales reached nearly $24,000. [L1437, 2018] |
| Second VA | Hired in June; July sales reached $75,000. [L1437, 2018] |
| Result | Grew from zero to over $500,000 in 12 months. VAs ran customer service and daily operations while the owner focused on the big picture. [L1437, 2018] |

### Team structure and partnerships

- Team at scale: 35 people across 3 stores (2 actively operating, 1 in growth phase), with hundreds of orders fulfilled per month. This size allows specialization, delegation, and delegation of hiring to team leaders. [L0263, 2022]
- Large dropshipping operations at nine-figure revenue commonly show this pattern:
  - They run five to ten stores in parallel rather than one, with several suppliers and sometimes one store per supplier.
  - They test new products weekly by seeing which go well.
  - Teams usually number 10 to 20 people, mixed across countries such as the Philippines and Pakistan, because a single-country team shares too much information in its own language.
  - Supplier choice is secondary to the ads and testing, and the business needs enough capital to fund the cash flow. [L0780, 2024+]
- Stores run day to day by partners, each reaching over 15,000 USD in revenue, let the owner keep the stores without daily operations. This model separates ownership from operating work. [L1515, 2019]
- Handing over operations: a store can be outsourced once it reaches roughly 50,000 to 80,000 per month in revenue. This was given as the target before handing over operations and opening another store. [L0694, 2024]
- Partnership: a founder with limited capital but strong skills can partner with someone who has money but no time to run a joint store. Such partnerships can be found through local meetups or existing relationships. Skills, time and money are interchangeable levers, so a missing resource can be replaced by another. [L0561, 2023+]

### Agency evaluation and incentives

- Structure agency incentives so both parties profit from growth. When the business succeeds and you earn more, the agency should also earn more. Misaligned incentives create friction because the vendor's financial interest diverges from yours. [L0217, 2025-26]
- Your account size relative to the agency's other clients affects priority and risk:
  - If you are their largest client, you receive high attention but risk de-prioritization if they land a bigger client.
  - If you are one of many small clients, your account may be deprioritized for their bigger accounts. [L0217, 2025-26]
- An agency's value lies in having time and experience with ad accounts. A person who has run ads for two years knows much more than someone who only studied them for months. [L1184, 2023+]
- Hire an agency only once scaling exceeds your own consistent knowledge. Until then, learning and testing yourself is the better path. [L1184, 2023+]
- Most ad agencies have little real experience with high-ticket dropshipping, a very small share of e-commerce. An agency that claims e-commerce expertise may never have run ads for high-ticket products. High-ticket has a different customer journey than low-ticket e-commerce and much thinner margins, which agencies used to high margins often struggle with. ** [L1053, 2021-22]
- Before revenue exists, invest in consulting, coaching or mastermind learning rather than hiring a paid traffic agency. Once the business has revenue, calculate whether an outside Google Ads specialist can add enough profit to justify the fee. [L1448, 2020-26]
- Do not hire a marketing agency to run or build ads for a new store. Agencies generally cannot create results from zero, so outsourcing makes sense only after the owner's own systems are already profitable. ** [L0468, 2020]
- Example of when an agency that scales ads profitably may be worth discussing: a store with about $100,000 monthly sales, $10,000 monthly ad spend and $20,000 monthly net profit. Such a stage is reached only after months of proven results. ** [L0468, 2020]

### Outsourcing ads

- Do not outsource paid ads until the campaigns are set up and deliver a return on ad spend you are satisfied with. Outsourcing earlier risks paying agencies whose model relies on signing many clients for a few months. [L0836, 2024]
- Even when outsourcing paid ads, learn the full traffic module first, so you can understand what the agency does and what a campaign should look like. [L0836, 2024]
- A marketing agency that offers done-for-you paid ads should be hired only after the campaigns are already profitable, to maintain or improve them. [L0836, 2024]
- Start paid growth services and additional marketing only after the store is live and already has traffic and sales, so optimization is based on real data. [L0855, 2023]
- Do not outsource paid ads until you have made them profitable yourself. An outside specialist can usually take an account from one to two or maintain a working account, but rarely builds it from zero. ** [L1112, 2021]
- Keep control of ad budgets in your own hands. Handing spending to an outside agency without oversight can quickly produce overspending and debt. [L0966, 2025]

### Coaching and mentors

- Single coaching sessions are one hour long and recorded; clients receive the recording afterward for reference. ** [L0035, 2021-22]
- Single coaching sessions can address any aspect of the client's high-ticket dropshipping business, from initial setup and build-phase questions through marketing, outsourcing, systems and finance management. ** [L0035, 2021-22]
- Monthly coaching frequency was optimized after testing various intervals. It gives sufficient implementation time between calls, allowing meaningful review of progress one month later, instead of frequent sessions where clients complete insufficient work. ** [L0035, 2021-22]
- Master Plan 12-month monthly coaching structure **:
  1. First call: analyze the current business state or building-stage progress, and establish topic priorities for the upcoming months. [L0035, 2021-22]
  2. Assign specific action items to complete in the next 30 days. [L0035, 2021-22]
  3. Direct the client to program video modules that give step-by-step execution guidance for each action item. [L0035, 2021-22]
  4. Next monthly call: review progress on action items, discuss obstacles encountered, assess completion status, and discuss why any items were not completed. [L0035, 2021-22]
  5. Identify the next business opportunity or priority to focus on in the following month. [L0035, 2021-22]
  6. Repeat the monthly cycle for 12 months. [L0035, 2021-22]
- Master Plan monthly coaching gives a competitive advantage by providing business-specific, market-specific and product-specific guidance. It deepens general program content without replacing core instruction. ** [L0035, 2021-22]
- Master Plan 12-month coaching is appropriate for entrepreneurs in the build-toward-launch phase, or for established operators seeking structured growth guidance over a defined period. ** [L0035, 2021-22]
- Coaching calls can save large amounts of time, money and energy, because you can learn from other operators' mistakes. Treat them as a core business resource rather than an optional extra. [L1441, 2018]
- Many dropshipping courses sell pre-recorded videos plus a dead community, so students hit a wall when applying the material alone. A paid program should provide live expert review at each major decision point, not only theory. [L1452, 2026]
- Use a mentor or course coach to review the product, offer and targeting before scaling. A mentor who knows the product can provide better guidance on audience choice. [L0678, 2023]

### Avoiding scammers

- Do not respond to cold emails from Shopify "experts" offering design, SEO or marketing services. These companies use volume models to contact all new Shopify stores. Delete the emails. If you need help, contact official one source support or use vetted service providers. [L0253, 2024]
- Unsolicited direct messages in online communities, especially ones asking for money, login details or your store's results, are a common scam pattern. Do not share account information or pay anyone who contacts you first. [L1323, 2026]

### AI tools

- ChatGPT Plus unlocks plugins, web access and more capable models, which the basic free version lacks. Plus costs around 20 dollars per month and is worth considering once automation tools such as Zapier are used. [L0353, 2023]
- Web browsing in ChatGPT may be temporarily disabled, and the model's knowledge may only reach back to 2021. Check current news and market information with a live search source. [L0353, 2023]
- AI tools help with brainstorming, brand name ideas, graphic mockups for team review and drafting email templates. Supplier relationships and direct customer relationships still need people, because the trust they build over time cannot be replaced by a tool. [L0857, 2025]
- AI tools can speed up product research, generate product descriptions and ad copy, and support ad targeting. They reduce guesswork and manual hours but do not replace the operator's own decisions. [L1487, 2026]
