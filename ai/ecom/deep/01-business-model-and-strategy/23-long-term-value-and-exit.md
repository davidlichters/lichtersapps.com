### Long-Term Value and Exit

- A brand built for long-term value can aim for about 1 million dollars in sales before it is sold, because a buyer pays for the audience and customer base. Early years may run at break-even while the brand builds awareness. Dropshipping stores are judged differently and are usually tested for profit from the start. [L0661, undated]
- A brand sold in Switzerland can be run for three to four years and possibly sold later to a larger seller or retailer, with brand partnerships adding value to the sale. [L0492, 2023+]
- Sell or partner from strength. Partnerships with other brands add value when the brand is sold, as in the Swiss example. [L0492, 2023+]

#### Further details

- A branded business can earn from many sources: dropshipped high-ticket products, private-label products, merchandise, paid community memberships, affiliate or referral links to related products, sponsored videos, and group trips. Diversifying income streams is a main reason to build the brand rather than only dropship. [L0487, 2018]
