## Plan promotions, seasons and content calendars

### Monthly Promotion Calendar

#### Building the 12-month calendar

1. Write the next 12 months in a spreadsheet or on paper, starting with the current month. [L1204, 2022]
2. Assign one offer per month from your arsenal and rotate through the list. [L1204, 2022]
3. In the notes column, write a short hook tied to a holiday, season or event. [L1204, 2022]
4. Define eligibility for each offer based on profit, such as a minimum order value or a specific product purchase. [L1204, 2022]
5. Create the emails and ads months in advance. [L1204, 2022]
6. Schedule the offers in Klaviyo, Google and Facebook. [L1204, 2022]

#### Sample calendar

| Month | Offer | Hook or reason |
|---|---|---|
| June | Free summer wheat recipe pack, eligible with any starter kit | Be ready for the 4th of July barbecue |
| August | Entry into a drawing for a trip to a homebrewing conference | Oktoberfest happens in September and October |
| September | 10 percent off | Labor Day sale |
| October | Free white glove delivery | Seasonal delivery offer |
| November | Bonus gift on orders over 1,000 dollars | Black Friday and Cyber Monday |
| December | Free bonus gift with all orders | Holiday shopping |
| January | 10 percent off again | Repeated offer with a new reason |

[L1204, 2022]

#### Offer windows and timing

- Run each monthly offer in a fixed window of about 3 to 5 days within the month, not for the whole month. [L1204, 2022]
- Use shorter windows for limited quantities or clearance stock to create urgency. Use longer windows for large purchases that need time to decide. [L1204, 2022]
- End offers on a weekday, such as a Friday. Open and click rates were highest on weekdays, possibly because people read email and social media during working hours. [L1204, 2022]
- Monthly campaigns on the Display Network run 4 to 5 days per month, for example October 29 to 31 for a Halloween sale. Use static ads instead of dynamic ads, and target a broader 180-day website visitor audience. [L0210, 2022]
- Use an evergreen URL such as /promotions rather than a time-specific URL such as /halloween-sale. The same link can then be reused across all monthly campaigns, and Google can build search authority on that page. [L0210, 2022]

#### Running and improving the programme

- A monthly promotion programme often needs several months before it reliably adds 20 to 30 percent of monthly revenue. A strong result can come in the first month, but expect three to six months of testing different offers before the programme is stable. [L1204, 2022]
- Do not rotate a weak offer back into the plan later. Replace it with a different offer based on the results, and keep repeating the offers that consistently win. [L1204, 2022]
- Even very small remarketing audiences are worth emailing for each promotion, because each small sale from existing contacts costs almost nothing. [L1115, 2024]
- Treat failed promotions as data, not waste. Early failures show which directions do not work. Reviewing each one for what did and did not work makes the next promotion more likely to sell. [L1365, 2019+]

### Preparing Promotions Ahead of Time

- Creating emails and ads in the week of the sale is a mistake. Spend one to two hours a week on promotion assets and schedule everything in advance in Klaviyo, Google and Facebook. [L1204, 2022]
- Start planning Q4 promotions about eight weeks before the first promotion, rather than a week before Halloween, to leave time for building offers, emails and ads. [L1285, 2024]
- Prepare the emails, ad copy and images for every Q4 promotion ahead of time, ideally by mid-October. Last-minute creation produces rushed assets and missed launch dates. [L1287, 2025]
- Waiting until the week before a holiday to plan the promotion leaves no time for offers, emails and ads. Set aside planning time several weeks ahead and work through the offers, emails and questions early. [L1223, 2024+]
- Quarterly planning across five promotions is easier when the team starts from historical best-performing copy and offers. Proven subject lines and headlines reduce the planning load and improve the chance of success. [L1223, 2024+]

### Seasonal Lead Times

- Seasonal products should be tested early. For example, Halloween items tested in early September sell significantly better than when competitors start testing. Starting 6 to 8 weeks before an event gives first-mover advantage and tests content performance in lower-competition inventory. [L0309, 2024+]
- Seasonal events such as Valentine's Day need preparation about one and a half to two months ahead. The store should be built and pre-ads running to pre-target the event. [L0561, 2023+]
- Valentine's Day is the next major sales window after the current period. Plan inventory, campaigns and bundles accordingly. [L0324, 2025+]
- Plan a product launch before the peak season, such as Q4. Prepare winning products in advance so they can be rolled out in about three days once demand arrives. [L1444, 2022]

### Q4 Preparation

- Start preparing Q4 promotions in September so the store, suppliers and promotion assets are ready about two months ahead, ideally by Halloween. Preparing in mid-October leaves no time to fix problems. [L1224, 2025+]
- Have the store, suppliers and promotion assets ready by about Halloween. That leaves roughly two months before the peak. Sales usually start climbing in late October and November, so earlier preparation is better. [L1287, 2025]
- Sources differ on the launch target. A 2023 source gives mid-November as the time to finish supplier approvals and store launch for holiday traffic. [L1114, 2023] The 2024 and 2025 sources ask for readiness by Halloween, with planning starting about eight weeks before the first promotion. [L1285, 2024; L1287, 2025] Plan to the Halloween target. The mid-November date is the later option named in the 2023 source. [L1114, 2023]
- Q4 pressure is often misplaced. One seller's first success came in Q1, not Q4, so do not panic about the holiday window. [L0820, undated]

### Black Friday and Cyber Monday

- Treat Black Friday as a deadline to launch by, not as the finish line. A store launched by then with a few suppliers, some traffic and some sales is on track. Plan the business as a long-term venture. [L1097, 2025]
- Prepare promotions around Black Friday and Cyber Monday in advance, and have stores ready before the holiday rush. [L1114, 2023]

### Supplier Timing Around Holidays

- Plan supplier outreach around the holidays. Late December and the days around Christmas through New Year are poor for approvals. Set up the store before the holidays and restart outreach about one week into January. [L0469, 2021]
- When suppliers go quiet during Black Friday and Cyber Monday, it usually reflects their busiest weeks, not a permanent refusal. Circle back in the following weeks, especially in the Q5 window. [L0468, 2020]

### Content Calendar and Posting Consistency

#### Why a calendar helps

- A content calendar helps creators produce ahead of time, protects against missed upload targets and keeps posting consistent when motivation drops. [L0795, undated]
- A content calendar should allow setting dates, times, a clear objective, goals and deadlines. Short bullet notes on what each video covers and why it is posted should sit alongside these. [L0795, undated]
- The phone's built-in reminders app, or the Android equivalent, can send an alert before each scheduled upload. [L0795, undated]

#### Minimum requirements before building a calendar

1. Choose the product you want to test.
2. Decide how you will create the videos.
3. Create a TikTok account.
4. Film videos on a phone.
5. Edit the footage in Splice.
6. Draft and finish the video in TikTok, adding text-to-speech, text and small edits.
7. Decide the upload times and the schedule that suits your audience.

[L0795, undated]

#### Launch and continuity

- A finished store and a chosen product give no advantage if no videos are posted. A competitor who started posting later can overtake a seller who built the store earlier. Launch with a posting schedule, not only with a finished store. [L0657, undated]
- Keep uploading consistently even when early videos underperform, because a viral result can arrive weeks later. One account went viral about two weeks after its first viral attempt. [L0764, 2022]
- Keep posting at a steady pace even when motivation and energy are low. The referenced creator uploaded 3 videos per day during discouraging periods, and that consistency led to the breakthrough. Consistency matters more than perfect planning. [L0821, undated]
- Results from a short-video channel should be judged after about three months of consistent posting, not after one or two weeks. [L1131, 2018+]
- Plan for roughly 2 to 4 hours of work per day on this Instagram viral process. [L1367, undated]

#### Systems over motivation

- Motivation fades, so rely on systems: reusable video formats, templates, batch production and a repeatable process. Systems keep output steady when inspiration is low. [L1457, 2025-26]
- Consistent posting feeds the algorithm. Good content without a posting strategy is often shown to the wrong people. Consistency and planning are weighted above the quality of a single video. [L1457, 2025-26]

#### Quantity versus quality

- For short-video posting, consistency and planning are weighted above the quality of a single video. [L1457, 2025-26; L0821, undated]
- For content marketing with articles, it is better to publish fewer pieces of very high quality than many shallow ones. [L1461, 2021-22]
- Both apply to different channels. Keep a steady cadence on short-video channels, and keep each long-form piece deep and high quality. [L1457, 2025-26; L0821, undated; L1461, 2021-22]

#### Product fit

- Many accounts that go viral do not sell because the content is not built around the product. Decide early whether the product fits your content plan and your skills. [L1444, 2022]

### Content Marketing: Long-Horizon Commitment

#### Patience and time

- Commit to a content marketing strategy for at least three months before judging it, and publish consistently during that period. One article does nothing. Results come from a steady stream of articles over several months, the same as with a blog. [L0380, 2014]
- Content marketing takes months to build traffic and sales, so judge it over a long horizon instead of by quick returns. Keep producing and refining the system while waiting for the compounding results. [L0382, 2017]
- Before committing, weigh the strategy against your available time, because content marketing is demanding to run alone. If the workload feels overwhelming, keep to the seven-step checklist and outsource one task at a time, starting with writing. [L0380, 2014]
- Brand building is not a one-time task. A single meme or post will not create a community. The content has to keep going, with new material added regularly over the long term. [L0487, 2018]

#### Compounding and simplicity

- Content and SEO work compound like a snowball. It starts slowly and gains momentum over time. Starting early gets the compounding going sooner, even though early results are small. [L0383, 2021-22]
- Building the full marketing system in an existing online store can take up to 12 months, depending on how much time the owner can commit. It is a long, steady build rather than a quick fix. [L0414, 2021-22]
- A content marketing plan should stay deliberately simple. Choose the format, plan the first 12 ideas, assign who produces the content, publish a steady number of pieces each month and promote them. Adding complexity early is usually unnecessary. [L0383, 2021-22]
- A pre-set content plan removes the daily decision of what to publish next, which is where content work usually stalls. Ideas are already chosen, so the producer can start work without re-planning. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Once a piece is locked into the plan with its promotion steps, carry those steps out as written. Change them only for a strong reason, because the value of planning is not having to decide again later. [L0384, 2021-22]
- Do not assume that putting a product on your website will bring visitors by itself. Have a written channel plan covering how customers will find the product from the start. [L0768, 2021-22]

### Email and Follow-Up

- Follow-up marketing is best built gradually. Start with email sequences for best-selling products, and expand the number of flows and retargeting audiences over time. [L0493, 2021-22]
- Once the loyalty program and its automated emails are set up, they run with little ongoing work. Expect to revisit the setup occasionally, but the main effort is at the start. [L0956, 2021]
- Post-purchase offers are worth testing even when most buyers decline. A small share of customers who said no to several offers can still convert on the final one, so this revenue is worth pursuing. [L1413, 2025-26]
- The time between a first visit and a purchase can range from days to weeks or even months, especially for higher-ticket products. Follow-up has to bridge that gap, because many competitors compete for the same customer's attention at any moment. [L1497, 2021-22]
- Holding a shopper's attention is what gives you the chance to get their money. Judge follow-up by whether it keeps the market engaged on the way to a purchase, not only by transaction count or conversion rate. [L1497, 2021-22]
- Full implementation of follow-up takes time, because emails must be written and put in place. Optimising follow-up is an ongoing process over months and even years, not something finished at once. [L1497, 2021-22]

### Attention and Opening Seconds

- Attention is increasingly fragmented because of growing social media use, full inboxes and far more websites and advertisers than a decade or fifteen years ago. Brands must grab attention quickly and then hold it, since people switch between things rapidly. [L1117, 2021-22]
- Average human attention span was about 9 to 10 seconds in 2020, compared with minutes measured 15 to 20 years earlier. Use this as a reason to make the first seconds of any ad, page or content count, not as a precise benchmark. [L1117, 2021-22]

### Referral and Thank-You Scheduling

#### Why referral and thanks come first

- Referral marketing turns past customers into a traffic source, because they recommend the store to family and friends who then buy. Plan a referral funnel as part of the traffic mix. [L0597, 2020-26]
- Thanking customers is the first step of a referral sequence because it is simple and rarely done by online stores. Expressing gratitude also supports the owner's own wellbeing and lowers stress, which helps balance the frequent setbacks of running an online business. [L0406, 2021]

#### Scheduling thank-you messages

- Thank-you messages must be scheduled as recurring tasks in a daily to-do list, a calendar or a team member's list. If they are not, they pile up and get postponed until the backlog becomes too large to handle. [L0406, 2021]

Procedure:

1. Choose the thank-you method: video, handwritten note, or both.
2. Set up the matching tool integration with Shopify.
3. Add the thank-you task to your own daily to-do list or to a team member's daily to-do list.
4. If you do not do the task yourself, write it into the SOP of the team member most relevant to it, such as the order processor or phone customer service.

[L0406, 2021]

### Dream 100 Time Blocks

#### Principles

- The Dream 100 process does not produce results overnight, with rare exceptions. It requires consistent effort over a long period, and the payoff comes later. [L0412, 2021-22]
- Dream 100 relationship building belongs in the important but not urgent category of a prioritisation matrix. Schedule it as a recurring task rather than treating it as something that demands immediate attention. [L0529, 2021-22]
- Consistency matters more than intensity for Dream 100 work. A small daily time block beats occasional long sessions. The goal is a sustainable routine that keeps running over months. [L0529, 2021-22]
- Dream 100 work compounds over time, similar to SEO, so the earlier you start, the earlier you see results. Begin conservatively with small amounts of work and build up gradually over the coming months. [L0529, 2021-22]

#### Time budget

| Workday length | Dream 100 time per day |
|---|---|
| Standard 6 to 8 hours | About 30 minutes |
| Shorter, about 4 hours | About 15 minutes |

[L0529, 2021-22]

- Spending two to three hours per day on Dream 100 activities is too much. That time is taken from tasks only the owner can do, so keep the daily amount small. [L0529, 2021-22]

#### Scheduling the block

1. Add Dream 100 time to your daily or weekly planning, as a block you can slot into your schedule.
2. Decide whether to do it at the start or the end of the workday, based on your own schedule.
3. Use the end of the day if your higher-level thinking is already used up, since the work mainly involves reading, asking questions and leaving short comments.
4. Keep the block short and move on to other tasks afterwards.

[L0529, 2021-22]
