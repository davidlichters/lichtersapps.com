### Store setup and launch steps

**First high-ticket store sequence**

1. Set up the business (store and supplier arrangements).
2. Launch the store.
3. Get the first sales.
4. Then shift focus to growth: marketing systems and scaling the business.

[L0438, 2021-22]

**Store launch checklist**

1. Secure approval from at least two suppliers.
2. Upload their products to your Shopify store.
3. Remove all demo products.
4. Test the checkout process.
5. Turn on Google Shopping ads.

Open for business and let customers find you. Continue adding suppliers and optimising in the background. [L0272, 2022]

**Supplier approval and launch readiness**

- The checklist requires at least two supplier approvals before launch. A launch that waits for every supplier approval is delayed, so the minimum is two approvals and the rest can follow. [L0272, 2022; L1446, 2020-26]
- Get enough supplier approvals to make money before starting a niche with few products. [L1085, 2018]
- When supplier outreach gets no response, change the call timing or approach first. Most brands can still be reached this way, so widen the niche only as a last resort. ** [L0970, 2023]
- A new seller should not delay contacting suppliers out of fear of rejection. Waiting too long often leads to abandoning the niche before any outreach. ** [L1094, 2023]
- Overthinking the call or feeling awkward about calling customers stops the programme from happening. These calls are positive, and customers who have bought generally like to hear from the business. ** [L1011, 2021]

**Launch from the one source self-onboarding form**

*Setting:* Work in your own copy of the self-onboarding Google Doc: File > Make a Copy. Fill in the copy so the original template stays clean. [L1268, 2026]

*Complete the form in this order:*

1. Answer what your business differentiator is and how you stand out from the rest, covering your products and mission.
2. List two to three things you wish every customer knew about your products.
3. Name your best-selling product. If you are unsure, check store sales by product in Shopify and paste that product's URL. If you have just started, skip this question.
4. Describe how you want your brand to be perceived by visitors, for example luxury, premium, wholesome or witty.
5. List what stops customers from buying: complaints, comments and feedback from people who viewed the website but did not purchase.
6. List influencers or authorities who work with or have used your products, including social media handles. Check Pinterest or Google to find them. Include social media influencers, actors, artists, celebrities and doctors.
7. Describe any seasonal buying patterns: the seasons when customers buy, or whether sales are year-round.

[L1268, 2026]

- Answer all seven self-assessment questions fully before moving on to scaling, ad production or any next stage. Skipping questions leaves gaps that later decisions depend on. [L1268, 2026]
- The self-onboarding questions clarify your niche, your goals and whether you are ready before you go deeper into a course or spend on ads. [L1268, 2026]

**Product chain and product choice**

- Plan the full chain from product idea to test-ready product, including research, recipes, covers, images and PDF, to take about three to four days. [L1131, 2018+]
- Choose products with proof of sales, run ads, and let Facebook data decide. [L1161, 2024+]

### Finishing steps and staying on one store

- Do not rush through setup just to look finished. Work through each step in order, using the checklist above. [L0272, 2022]
- Do not spend time polishing a step once its core output works; move to the next one. [L1446, 2020-26]
- Start with ready-made templates and move forward while learning in parallel. [L1348, 2023]

### Deadlines, time budgets and signs of being stuck

- Assign a realistic time budget to each launch step. If a step clearly takes longer than its budget, treat it as a sign of being stuck and ask a mentor, community or peers for help instead of continuing alone. [L0555, 2026+]
- Set a launch deadline for the first store, for example 30 to 60 days, to avoid delaying the launch indefinitely. ** [L0964, 2023]
- Set a fixed deadline for niche research, such as three days or one week, and choose on that date. ** [L1085, 2018]
- Use the time budgets in the table above as the reference points for each stage. [L1040, 2021-22; L1038, 2026; L1131, 2018+; L0438, 2021-22; L1279, 2021]

### Asking for help when stuck

- Market selection is about choosing a good-enough niche quickly, and seeking help along the way is encouraged, not a sign of weakness. ** [L1040, 2021-22]
- Rely on coaches and solve problems as they arise instead of trying to plan everything alone. [L0037, 2026]
- Use the verification or outside review when you are stuck between two niches, but do not use it to delay the decision further. [L0941, 2022; L1080, 2020-26; L1059, 2021-22]
- Once research is complete, either confirm a market or get an outside check rather than cycling alone. ** [L1058, 2021-22]

### Focus on high-leverage work

- Page speed work is not a high-leverage activity for a business owner. Once a site is near the 5-second mark, do not spend days or weeks tuning it. Move your time to core conversion work. ** [L1522, 2021-22]
- Time spent on low-leverage work is time not spent on launching and learning from real customers. [L1515, 2019; L1446, 2020-26]
