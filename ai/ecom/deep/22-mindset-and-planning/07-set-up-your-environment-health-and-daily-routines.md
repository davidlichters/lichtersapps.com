## Set up your environment, health and daily routines

### Environment before willpower

- Willpower is limited per day, so do not rely on self-control. Remove temptations and distractions such as unhealthy food, games or negative people from your surroundings. [L1041, 2023]
- Remove negative influences and obstacles from your environment, organise it to conserve resources, treat yourself well and reward progress. This is what makes behavioural change sustainable. [L0007, 2023]

### Content and feed curation

- Choose content deliberately to support your goals, and stop consuming content that does not serve them. Curate your own feed and teach the algorithm what you want to see. This takes practice but compounds. [L0007, 2023]
- Control the content you consume, because it shapes your perception and, through it, your reality. Choose educational and motivational content that supports your goals, and train the social media algorithm by watching the content you want to see. [L1041, 2023]
- Use social media for business only, and avoid personal content on business accounts. This approach works as an experiment and is not mandatory. [L0526, 2021-22]
- Why limit follows: a feed filled with thousands of followed accounts makes relevant business posts less likely to appear, because the platform limits how much content it shows and fills space with ads that cannot be removed. Limiting follows keeps business content visible. [L0526, 2021-22]

**Set up social media for business use only**:

1. Turn off all social media notifications, except those from Dream 100 groups. [L0526, 2021-22]
2. Follow only Dream 100 members on the platforms. Do not follow friends, family or others, unless a partner or close person would otherwise be upset (a judgement call). [L0526, 2021-22]
3. Join only groups that are Dream 100 groups or groups you manage for your business. [L0526, 2021-22]
4. Interact only with your business pages. [L0526, 2021-22]
5. Keep the feed limited to business-relevant content, so that the business connections you need are not crowded out by other posts. [L0526, 2021-22]

### Focus and work environment

- Practise mindfulness: focus on one task at a time and observe the present. This reduces anxiety and sharpens focus, and it can be used during set periods of the day without daily meditation. [L1041, 2023]
- Reduce distractions during focused work with headphones playing calm music or white noise, or by working in a coffee-shop-type environment. Noise-cancelling headphones are a worthwhile investment for people with children or noisy homes. [L1041, 2023]
- Work from a co-working space or a café with other people working. This makes it harder to drift into home distractions. [L0826, 2016]

### Sleep

| Item | Value | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum sleep per night | 7 hours | [L1181, 2023] |
| Ideal sleep per night | 8 hours | [L1181, 2023] |

- Aim for a consistent amount of sleep rather than extreme sleep schedules, and avoid oversleeping. [L1181, 2023]
- Sleep totals matter more than the clock. A late-night work session followed by a later wake-up is acceptable if total sleep is sufficient and productivity is high in the period when you work best. [L1181, 2023]
- Do not sacrifice sleep to build a store. Consistent daily action is more sustainable than working through the night. [L0671, 2021]
- **When the sources seem to conflict:** the late-night allowance in [L1181, 2023] applies only when total sleep still reaches the target in [L1181, 2023] (7 hours minimum, 8 ideal). The warning in [L0671, 2021] and [L0671, 2021] applies whenever working late would cut into that sleep. [L1181, 2023; L0671, 2021]

### Exercise and physical health

- Exercise every day. Base the decision to train on prior commitment, not on how you feel that morning. Discipline matters more than daily mood. [L0705, 2026]
- The type of training is flexible (gym, sports, cardio, pilates or similar). The essential requirement is that it becomes part of the daily routine. [L0705, 2026]
- Regular exercise raises self-esteem and self-confidence, improves the ability to complete difficult tasks, and builds physical and especially mental endurance. [L0705, 2026]
- Gym sessions of 30 minutes to 1 hour fit into the daily structure after waking. [L1181, 2023]
- Include a gym session in the morning routine. [L1041, 2023]
- Physical fitness supports the energy, focus and long working hours a business needs. Getting tired after only one or two hours of sitting work is a warning sign about health and should not be treated as normal. [L0826, 2016]
- Physical activity helps thinking. Combine work with movement, such as standing, walking or handling materials. Long stretches of sitting still at a screen reduce both energy and output. [L1343, undated]
- Protect your health as part of the work: sleep well, eat regular meals, exercise and get medical check-ups, because creative work needs energy. Harmful habits reduce long-term output. [L1343, undated]

### Food

- Cook your own food and pay closer attention to what you eat. Food is the fuel for your body, and you must live with its effects for the rest of your life. [L0705, 2026]
- Avoid fast food, frozen food, fried items and sugar. They make you feel worse after meals compared with healthy meals. [L0705, 2026]
- If you cannot cook all your own meals, home-cooked food prepared by family members still counts as a healthy diet habit. The aim is meals made from fresh ingredients at home rather than fast food or frozen products. [L0705, 2026]

### Reading

- Read at least 30 minutes of non-fiction every day. Choose books that build financial and business knowledge, and avoid light entertainment reading. [L0705, 2026]

### Meditation, mindset and personal development

- Meditation is strongly recommended as a daily practice in a fixed, pre-blocked slot of 5, 10 or 30 minutes. Ideally do it right after waking to start the day with more mental clarity. [L0705, 2026]
- Set a timer for the planned session length, for example 20 minutes. Ideally the session is in total silence, although the speaker used music to help. [L0705, 2026]
- Choose a practice time you will actually keep. Morning and bedtime are common favourites, but a lunch break or scheduled break is far better than skipping the practice altogether. [L1206, 2015+]
- Practise relaxation during the day by briefly recalling the physical feeling of relaxation several times, for example by mentally repeating that you feel more relaxed. Doing this several times a day can reduce fatigue and improve how you handle situations. [L1206, 2015+]
- For a solo owner in the early stage, business performance depends heavily on the owner's mental and physical condition. If the owner is not in a good state, the chances of getting the business running successfully drop significantly, so personal development belongs alongside business building. [L1256, 2021-22]

**Daily mindset routine**:

1. Sit calmly and silently repeat the mantra: *I am focused, I am committed, and I am patient.* [L0419, 2021-22]
2. While keeping your attention on reaching your goals, say: *I am focused on reaching my goals, I am committed to my goals, and I have the patience to reach my goals.* [L0419, 2021-22]

### Cold showers

**Cold shower routine for extra energy** (optional):

1. Take a normal warm shower. [L0705, 2026]
2. Switch to cold water for the last 2 to 3 minutes. Alternatively, wash only with the coldest water available. [L0705, 2026]
3. The speaker's own practice was once or twice per week, after cardio. It can be done every morning when extra energy is needed. [L0705, 2026]

### Daily routines

**Fixed routine**

- Wake up at the same time each day and leave home for a set workplace at the same time. [L0826, 2016]
- Keep a fixed daily routine when travelling or working from home, with set work hours and set family time. Mixing too much work into family time, or the reverse, throws the balance off. [L1000, 2017]
- Keep a fixed daily routine with a regular work slot, and avoid breaking it. Inertia stops progress and makes the work harder to restart. The aim is to do the work every day, even when it does not feel productive. [L1343, undated]
- Systems thinking applies to health and relationships. Fixed workout times, a consistent sleep schedule and scheduled time with a partner produce more consistent energy and results than leaving them to chance. [L1039, 2019+]

**Morning routine for setting up the day** (start before about 8:00):

1. Take a 30-minute walk while reading the one-year and five-year goal lists. [L1041, 2023]
2. Do a gym session. [L1041, 2023]
3. Review business metrics and finances. [L1041, 2023]
4. Block focused work time in the calendar before opening the task list. [L1041, 2023]

**Daily structure used by the speaker:**

1. Wake up around 5:00-6:00 a.m. [L1181, 2023]
2. First hour: no phone, notifications off, focus mode. Use it for mindset preparation: coffee, a 30-minute walk in daylight, and reviewing goals and the kind of person you want to be. [L1181, 2023]
3. Gym for 30 minutes to 1 hour. [L1181, 2023]
4. Shower and get ready. [L1181, 2023]
5. Start work around 7:30 a.m. with the business review: emails, finances, ad performance and order checks. [L1181, 2023]
6. Spend about 30 minutes on messages from students or the community. [L1181, 2023]
7. Work on ads for the first two hours after business and community tasks. [L1181, 2023]
8. Afternoon: handle ad hoc tasks such as student calls, mentoring work and strategy preparation. Book them into the calendar as they come up. [L1181, 2023]

### Breaks and burnout prevention

- Sustained motivation requires planned rest. Schedule breaks and avoid constant overwork to prevent burnout, and end each break with a defined next goal. [L0826, 2016]
- Rest is part of productivity. A short 15-30 minute break with quiet music and no screens, such as a power nap in the early afternoon, can reset energy levels when the body is tired. [L1181, 2023]
