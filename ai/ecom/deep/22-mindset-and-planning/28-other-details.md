### Planning and review routine

**Business fundamentals action plan:** Work through these steps in this order.
1. Write a vision document describing the business's 10-year vision. [L0441, 2021-22]
2. Based on the 10-year vision, set 3-year and 1-year goals and record them in the tracking document. [L0441, 2021-22]
3. Identify the first set of Rocks for the next 90 days, based on the goals. [L0441, 2021-22]
4. Build weekly and daily to-do lists from the Rocks, with the most important Rock task at the top of each list. [L0441, 2021-22]
5. Add to the calendar a fixed daily time for reviewing the to-do list, and a weekly time for planning the following week's list. [L0441, 2021-22]
6. Schedule quarterly review times to set new Rocks and an annual review time to assess the business against its goals and set goals for the next year. [L0441, 2021-22]
7. Complete the customer worksheet (what you know about your customer and what you still need to find out). [L0441, 2021-22]
8. Complete the competitor analysis worksheet using data from competitors' websites and traffic tools. [L0441, 2021-22]
9. Review the financial tracking system and replace it with a proven system if it does not work. [L0441, 2021-22]
10. Calculate the target cost per sale with the traffic calculator, record the numbers, and compare them with the financial tracking to monitor profit day to day. [L0441, 2021-22]

- Keep the action checklist as a printable document and tick off each item once it is done. This makes it easy to confirm that no planning step (vision, goals, Rocks, worksheets, calendar times) has been skipped. [L0441, 2021-22]
- Complete the customer worksheet before deciding on positioning and offers. It records what you already know about your target customer and which customer facts you still need to find out. [L0441, 2021-22]
- Three factors decide whether a dropshipper succeeds, because knowledge alone is not enough: [L0519, 2020+]
  - A clear vision of the future you.
  - Discipline: knowing when and how to do each task.
  - Organization: structures, delegation and clear next steps.

### Customer and offer definition

- Identify your dream customer before choosing products and audiences. Dotcom Secrets by Russell Brunson is recommended for this. [L0519, 2020+]
- Read the Alex Hormozi books for offer ideas, and the first part of Dotcom Secrets to identify the dream customer and dream offer. [L0650, undated]
- Do not sell a product on a topic you cannot teach. Learn a niche properly first, because real expertise lets you judge and improve competitor offers. [L0513, undated]
- Build credibility by showing who you are, your experience and why the buyer should trust you, such as your profession or years of practice in the niche. [L0513, undated]

### Brand building and store quality

- Build your brand gradually through iterative improvement over years rather than attempting overnight changes. This enables sustainable growth and continuous brand evolution. [L0260, 2025-26]
- The Oodie brand was built iteratively over 4 to 5 years and continues to be refined. Sustainable brand building requires long-term commitment and ongoing iteration. [L0260, 2025-26]
- First impressions determine customer lifetime value. Treat every interaction (shipping, support, unboxing) as brand-building, not as a one-time transaction. [L0324, 2025+]
- Good intentions amplify skill but cannot replace it. Buyers sense whether a seller is trying to help or to extract money, and that difference affects long-term results. [L0378, 2019+]
- The purpose of a design is to make the message understood and to lead the viewer toward the action the business wants. If too many elements compete for attention, none of them will be seen first. [L0755, undated]
- If a self-built store does not look close to competitor sites, get help rather than continuing with a poor result. Comparing your site with competitor sites is a quick check for quality. [L0921, 2022]
- Ask the supplier before using their reviews on your store, since reviews taken without permission can cause trouble with Google. Only use them after the supplier explicitly agrees. [L0853, 2021]
- Use an expert reviewer for scientific or technical claims before publication, because a writer may draw wrong conclusions from facts. Check any product-specific claim with someone who knows the science. [L1261, undated]

### Legal, banking and tax setup

**Legal and banking action steps, in order:**
1. Decide the country you will sell in: your own country if it has an e-commerce market, otherwise the US. [L1405, 2021-22]
2. Form the business entity: in the US an LLC or S-Corp; in Australia usually a sole trader, or a Pty Ltd if an accountant advises it. [L1405, 2021-22]
3. Obtain the tax ID for your country: in the US an EIN and, if your state has sales tax, a sales tax permit; in Australia at least an ABN. [L1405, 2021-22]
4. Find a business accountant if you do not already have one, preferably one who knows dropshipping. [L1405, 2021-22]
5. Open a business bank account with low or no fees (usually free to open) and a business credit card with rewards, or a debit card on the Visa or Mastercard network if no credit card is possible. [L1405, 2021-22]

### Supplier relationships

- Present your business to suppliers as a long-term operation, not a quick-money project, with customer service as its main priority. [L1422, 2020-26]
- Income from a store is not a reason to stop supplier approvals or product uploads. Growth needs a constant flow of new products and suppliers, not one push. [L0359, 2020]
- When a supplier issue occurs, evaluate the total relationship:
  - If the supplier ships correctly most of the time and is otherwise responsive, work through isolated problems.
  - If a pattern of poor behavior emerges, cut them off. [L0249, 2022]
- Never misrepresent your business. Do not claim years of experience, past sales or a track record you do not have. Lying to suppliers tends to become a habit that spreads to customers and staff and hurts the business in the long run. Being a new business is no disadvantage in itself and does not automatically lead to a no. [L0929, 2021-22]
- A supplier refusal for now does not mean failure. If a supplier says they are not taking new retailers, record the reason and the date they suggested, then follow up at that time. [L0928, 2020-26]
- A no from a supplier usually means no for now, not no forever. Stay open-minded, energetic and confident. Suppliers are businesses that want to make money and want customers who respect their brand. [L0933, 2020-26]
- When a supplier says no or gives vague reasons, follow up on a set schedule rather than giving up. Repeated, polite contact with a status update shows commitment and does not get you blacklisted. (2023) [L0835, 2023]
- Confidence on supplier calls comes from knowing your subject well. Do not fake knowledge; study the material until you can speak about the business with real understanding. [L0927, 2020-26]
- When a supplier asks about annual sales goals for a brand-new store, a reasonable answer is a figure in a range between 2 and 500,000. The currency was not specified. (2023) [L0934, 2023]

**Handling a no from a supplier, in order:** [L0928, 2020-26]
1. Thank them and make sure you have said everything you can to get approved. [L0928, 2020-26]
2. Note in the worksheet exactly what they said, including any date they suggested to call back. [L0928, 2020-26]
3. Set a reminder on your phone or in Google Calendar for that date. [L0928, 2020-26]
4. Follow up when the reminder comes. [L0928, 2020-26]

### Competitors and market awareness

- Study competitors as future rivals, but do not take their work. Success in dropshipping should come from your own effort and original work. [L0365, 2020-26]
- Viewing Google Ads updates as opportunities to optimize ahead of competitors gives a competitive advantage. Most businesses respond slowly to platform changes. [L0017, undated]

### Sales conversations (High Probability approach)

- Traditional selling aims to get the buyer to act, so every tactic becomes a form of pressure that triggers resistance. The High Probability approach replaces that goal with one question: is there a mutually acceptable basis for doing business? If not, both sides end the process early without a deal. [L0770, undated]
- Set aside old sales beliefs before judging a new method, because habits built on the old goal can conflict with the new one. Discomfort is normal while you practice and fades with repetition, so treat it as the cost of learning rather than proof that the method is wrong. [L0770, undated]
- Random negative reinforcement means a few sales won through ineffective tactics can strengthen habits that fail over time. Judge your methods by results across many calls, not by individual wins. [L0770, undated]
- Never try to convince a prospect that he needs or wants your offer. If he clearly understands what you offer and it does not match what he wants, end the visit. If he really wants it, he will not let you go. [L0770, undated]
- Avoid the five-step selling sequence of attention, interest, desire, conviction and action as a selling model. It is built on manipulation, wastes time on people who will not buy, and places the close at the end when it should be the whole process. [L0770, undated]
- When several prospects that sounded qualified all end without a commitment, check three causes:
  - You are still new to the method.
  - You read weak signals as strong ones.
  - You slipped into answering or selling.

  Each cause can be corrected, and only the last one is about your behavior on the call. [L0770, undated]

### Affiliate and lead value

- Affiliate marketing done only for quick money tends to fail. Promoting products you have not used, or do not believe in, shows in your emails and makes your recommendations less convincing. [L0514, 2015]
- Promote only products you have used, know someone who has used successfully, or have investigated thoroughly with a clear view of pros and cons. Recommending something you believe in is easier and more credible than recommending something you have not tested. [L0514, 2015]
- Focus on giving value to the audience instead of trying to extract money from them. A sincere recommendation is more likely to produce long-term sales than a short-term push. [L0514, 2015]
- The five lead value techniques are:
  - Cross promoting your own related stores.
  - Cross promoting affiliate stores.
  - Promoting information products.
  - Upselling add-ons at checkout.
  - Upselling intangible services.

  All of them depend on first collecting emails and installing tracking pixels on visitors. [L0923, 2015]
- Affiliate program setting: members recommend the program with their personal affiliate link, found under the resources section of the lesson page. Each person who joins through the link earns the referrer 500 USD, and four to five referrals can recover the cost of the course. Referred members' later spending can also pay out a share of their purchases. [L1498, 2021-22]

### Platform and ad settings

**Display campaign for dynamic remarketing (setting):** [L0210, 2022]
1. Check Dynamic ads for personalized ads. [L0210, 2022]
2. Select the Google Merchant Center data feed. [L0210, 2022]
3. Set focus to viewable impressions. [L0210, 2022]
4. Use no filters to show all products. [L0210, 2022]
5. Set the CPM bid to $10. [L0210, 2022]
6. Target the smallest recent website visitor audience available (7 days, 30 days, or larger if traffic is low). [L0210, 2022]
7. Turn off optimized targeting. [L0210, 2022]
8. Turn off audience expansion. [L0210, 2022]

- Do not include video extensions in display campaigns. Keep dynamic product display ads strictly as images with product links, not video. [L0210, 2022]

- On Instagram, a video uploaded as a Reel also appears as a regular feed post, so one upload covers both formats. Verify this in the upload screen when you create the Reel. [L1186, 2022]
- Low reach is often caused by strikes, restrictions or reports rather than poor content. Review account notices and guidelines before concluding that content is the problem. [L1384, 2022]

### Learning, skills and mindset

- Knowledge alone does not make a dropshipper successful. See the three factors under Planning and review routine. [L0519, 2020+]
- Habits that help owners grow a large business are the same for very large and very small companies. Developing yourself into a person capable of growing a large business applies regardless of current size. [L1256, 2021-22]
- Customer service skills improve with practice, so expect to become faster and more comfortable over the first months. Learning by doing is the main way to build this skill. [L0909, 2021-22]
- Mind OS, a book on the psychology of the self, was recommended as one of the few useful books for building inner confidence. The price quoted was roughly $200 to $500, so it is an expensive purchase. [L0701, 2015]
- Coding requires a real interest in the work, because progress is slow and frustrating on many days. Staying committed through setbacks is part of building the skill. [L0709, 2017+]
