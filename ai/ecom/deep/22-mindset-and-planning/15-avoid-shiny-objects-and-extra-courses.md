### Avoid shiny objects and extra courses

- While you are implementing a business plan, remove external distractions: do not buy additional courses, educational products or shiny objects that pull attention away from execution [L0419, 2021-22] (2021-22).
- Hopping from one business idea to the next (shiny object syndrome) stops progress. A beginner should commit to one business model and one strategy and run it through before judging it [L0631, 2026].
- Extra paid programs and upgrades are optional growth tools for a store that is already profitable. They are not required to build a successful dropshipping store [L0359, 2020].
- Trick-based methods can work for a time, while fundamentals hold up over the long term. Focus on fundamentals such as branding, creatives and ad basics rather than on individual tricks that may stop working [L0363, 2022+].
- Ad platforms change their features and rules almost every week, so no one can claim to have fully mastered them. Keep up with changes, but base decisions on the fundamentals of marketing and sales, which stay the same [L1213, 2023].
- Use a proven set of techniques instead of trying every technique in the books. Experienced writers keep a handful of techniques that work consistently, so start with those few and test others only when you have time [L1370, undated].

### Test products before judging

- Test multiple products and analyze the results with mentors instead of judging the business after a single attempt [L0440, 2023+].
- Students who make progress are the ones who put full effort into the product pitch and keep testing [L0691, 2024].
- Research at least 15 product ideas with Winning Hunter, looking for high emotional demand, strong margins and a wide audience [L0491, 2026].
- Learn a new product category with a few hours of basic overview videos, then learn most of it by running ads and answering real customers. Each unanswered question should be passed to the supplier and then added to your site [L1277, 2020] (2020).
- Learn from competitors and other sellers' errors through their reviews and experiences. This is safer than learning only through your own mistakes [L0962, 2021] (2021).
- Study documented results from people who already succeeded at the task instead of repeating their costly trial and error. Reusing proven methods saves years of testing and money [L0340, undated].
- Writing a strong sales letter is a learnable skill, not a talent: the mechanics are repeatable, and the business owner who knows the customer has a major advantage over a freelancer who must research them from scratch. Begin by writing blocks of copy and stacking them, rather than aiming for a finished letter at once [L1370, undated].
- Read the book 100 Million Offers by Alex Hermosi to learn how to build strong offers [L1220, undated].
- Ready, Fire, Aim by Michael Masterson is a book on direct response business thinking. Read it if you plan to build a private label product line [L0413, 2016].

### Discipline and following the process

- Discipline is essential in e-commerce. Adhere to the prescribed process precisely and avoid deviating from it before you have achieved significant results [L1394, 2017+].
- Follow the course program fully instead of freestyling [L1394, 2017+].
- Do not skip any module, and do not deviate from the sequence until the current module's action tasks are done [L0491, 2026], [L0491, 2026].
- Choose the same discipline for every module: start with the first module that applies to your stage, and finish its action tasks before the next [L0545, 2021-22], [L0545, 2021-22].
- Do not stop after rejections. Keep following the steps and contacting suppliers [L1088, 2020] (2020).

### Continuous learning

- Treat constant learning as part of the job: follow digital marketing groups, ask questions and answer questions in forums. Marketers who keep their skills current stay ahead of those who stop learning [L0489, 2020-26].
- The willingness to keep learning is the most valuable asset an entrepreneur can have, because nobody can take it away and it can carry you a long way. Success in this business requires constantly adapting and picking up new skills [L0926, 2018].
- The most valuable skill in dropshipping is a willingness to learn, because it cannot be taken away and it carries you through changing conditions. Expect platforms and rules to change and keep updating your knowledge [L1441, 2018].
- Investing in ongoing education, such as podcasts, reading, a mastermind group, a monthly local business breakfast and yearly conferences, is recommended. Even one useful idea per session can make a large difference to the business [L1437, 2018].
- When learning a platform such as Shopify from scratch, repeated basic questions in community groups and rewatching setup tutorials are a normal part of progress. Keep returning to the business goal rather than stopping when the technical steps feel overwhelming [L1437, 2018].
- Learning web development works best through a mix of YouTube tutorials, free online programming courses, and books, depending on whether the learner prefers video or reading. Consistent practice through trial and error builds the skill [L0709, 2017+].
- Learn people deeply. Study human psychology and people, because this knowledge directly improves your business decisions, marketing, and customer relationships [L0007, 2023].
- Copywriting is closely tied to general marketing. Reading classic copywriting and marketing books improves a marketer's overall skill, not only their writing, when the ideas are applied in practice [L1523, 2021-22].

### Reading routine

- Turn daily reading into lasting knowledge with this order [L0705, 2026]:
  1. Read non-fiction for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Highlight the key passages.
  3. Take written notes on the main ideas.
  4. Discuss what you read with other people so the concepts stay in memory.
- Daily reading of business-relevant non-fiction supports personal growth and business performance, and works like structured learning without a teacher [L0705, 2026].
