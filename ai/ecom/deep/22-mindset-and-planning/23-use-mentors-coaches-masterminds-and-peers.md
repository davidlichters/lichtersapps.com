## Use mentors, coaches, masterminds and peers

### Choosing a mentor and a single strategy

- Pay for mentorship only when you can ask specific, in-depth questions. Generic questions that a quick web search can answer waste the investment. [L0690, 2022+]
- Focus mentorship questions on what cannot be found by a basic search, because a mentor can share knowledge that is not easily found online. [L0690, 2022+]
- Pick a single strategy from one mentor and follow it instead of second-guessing among many approaches. This reduces decision fatigue for beginners. [L0690, 2022+]
- Learn the basics on your own before getting mentorship. Use the mentor to speed up progress, not to replace learning. [L0690, 2022+]
- Mentorship from an experienced coach is a practical investment for a beginner. It shortens the path through product research, ad testing and payments compared with self-funded trial and error. [L1440, 2021+]
- A beginner with no experienced person to ask tends to repeat avoidable mistakes and lose money on them. Active community channels where experienced operators answer questions and give feedback shorten that learning curve. [L1492, 2024+]
- Mentorship gives an exponential advantage. Successful people usually had a mentor, and a mentor helps you avoid repeating known mistakes. [L1041, 2023]
- Free YouTube content is enough to learn ads fundamentals and how to read ad logic. Add a mentor or consultation when you hit a specific problem, such as not knowing why ads fail after a first campaign. [L1213, 2023]
- Keep improving and keep working after the mentorship ends. Success often comes months after the programme, because results come from steady daily work rather than the course itself. [L1440, 2021+]
- The paid mentoring period lasts three months. Within it, a beginner should be able to build the store, launch ads and start making sales. If the student is not yet confident running Facebook ads at the end, support continues until they are. [L1017, undated]
- In a mentorship program, you choose which mentor to book for each call. You can book several calls with the same mentor or with different mentors. [L0934, 2023]

### Preparing questions and surveys

- Write the questions down before each call so you can check during the session that every topic you need is covered. Making notes is the minimum requirement even if you are unsure there will be enough time. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Ask questions that solve a real problem you cannot work out yourself. Do not ask for things you can look up. Better questions speed up progress more than waiting for answers. [L1454, 2026]
- If you are unsure whether you are reasoning about a niche or product correctly, ask a coach on a group or one-to-one call rather than deliberating alone. Bring the specific criteria you are unsure about so the coach can check them. [L1059, 2021-22]
- If you feel stuck during market selection, ask for help instead of letting the same doubts loop in your head. Asking questions is a normal and expected part of building a business. [L1040, 2021-22]
- Once you have a shortlisted niche, use a second opinion from a coach, a team review, or a market verification check. Outside input helps you test how you are thinking about the market. [L1040, 2021-22]
- Asking questions in a community is a good way to get a single stuck step solved. Rephrase the question and ask again later if it goes unanswered. [L0855, 2023]
- Pre-coaching survey, in order:
  1. Find the survey link below the videos in the resources section, or use the copy sent by email. [L1144, 2021-22]
  2. Open the link in a web browser. [L1144, 2021-22]
  3. Answer the questions one by one. [L1144, 2021-22]
  4. Click submit. [L1144, 2021-22]
  5. A confirmation message appears, and the answers are sent automatically to the coaching team. No further action is needed after that. [L1144, 2021-22]
- Complete the pre-coaching survey before the first coaching call. The coaching team uses the answers to learn about the person and business and to review the website in advance. The survey asks basic questions about the person and the business. [L1144, 2021-22]
- Coaching calls should not be spent on basic background questions, so the survey handles that information before the call. Each coaching session is one hour, so preparing in advance lets the time go straight to useful help. [L1144, 2021-22]
- A single coaching session lasts one hour. [L1144, 2021-22]

### Preparing for paid one-to-one sessions

- Paid one-to-one sessions only pay off when you plan before the call. Without a plan, the session is spent finding data and deciding what to discuss instead of solving problems. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Do not book a one-to-one session without a clear topic and a defined outcome you want to achieve. Booking a call just to have a chat gives uncertain value for the money paid. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Before each call, the basic pre-consultation survey must be completed after booking. Preparation for every upcoming call follows the same sequence: book, complete the survey, then email the topics and details. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Procedure for preparing a one-to-one coaching session:
  1. Book the call. [L0364, 2021-22]
  2. Complete the pre-consultation survey. [L0364, 2021-22]
  3. Decide exactly which topics you want to cover, which questions you want to ask, and what you need help with. [L0364, 2021-22]
  4. Write the questions down as notes on paper, a computer or a phone so you can refer to them during the call. [L0364, 2021-22]
  5. Decide which supporting information the reviewer cannot already see, using your own judgment and without worrying about getting it perfect. [L0364, 2021-22]
  6. Add links to specific website sections you want to discuss, grant access to Google Analytics and Google Ads if paid traffic data must be reviewed, and copy the emails you want reviewed or paste their body text. [L0364, 2021-22]
  7. Send one email to the support address listed under the course videos before each call, containing the list of topics and questions plus any attachments or links. [L0364, 2021-22]
  8. If you are unsure what material is needed or lack time, send at least the list of topics and questions. [L0364, 2021-22]
  9. If the reviewer replies asking for specific material, provide it before the call. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Before each call, email the support address listed under the course videos with your list of topics, questions, and any attachments or links. This is best practice and should take little time. [L0364, 2021-22]
- If you lack time or are unsure what material is needed, send at least the list of topics and questions. The reviewer may reply asking for specific material before the call. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Sending material in advance lets the reviewer read it before the call, so session time is used for discussion and advice rather than silent reading. Only material the reviewer cannot already access needs to be sent. [L0364, 2021-22]
- If there is more to cover than fits in one session, handle the leftover topics after the call. Do not let fear of running out of time stop you from preparing. [L0364, 2021-22]
- Every member has an onboarding call with the program host or a team member, booked through a link sent by email. The call clarifies your stage and which content to prioritise, so book it before starting the course. [L0545, 2021-22]

### Community rules and conduct

- The community must stay respectful. Avoid comments that an average person or the law would consider inappropriate, and avoid topics such as politics and religion, because the space is meant for building businesses. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Breaking any of the four core rules can lead to immediate removal from the program with no refund and no prior warning. The four core rules cover inappropriate conduct, selling services to members, direct messages for sales, and sharing content. The refund exclusion is part of the terms accepted at sign-up. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Sharing wins in the group is encouraged, because entrepreneurs often work alone and feel isolated. Celebrate milestones such as building a first website, making a first sale, or reaching a thousandth sale, and support others who are struggling. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Asking questions is expected. A question that feels basic is still better than getting stuck. Being active, including joining calls even as a listener, tends to increase results in the program. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Questions asked in the comments under a lesson video notify the person who asked when a team member or another member replies. The question and answer stay visible to everyone watching that lesson, so the same question does not need to be asked twice. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Searching before posting avoids duplicate questions and saves waiting time. Asking in the group or lesson comments benefits everyone, because other members with the same problem can read the answer later. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Check the community's announcements channel regularly. Updates are posted almost every day, not only before live calls. Missing an announcement can mean missing a call or a change to the schedule. [L0491, 2026]
- Group coaching is included in the standard membership with unlimited access. It is found in the learning dashboard menu under a group coaching link, and it shows the schedule for the current calendar month. [L1498, 2021-22]
- To join the members-only Facebook group, open the group link from the resources section below the lesson video, request to join, and enter the same email address used to buy the program when the form asks. Access is granted shortly after the request is reviewed. [L1498, 2021-22]

### Group and live coaching calls

#### one source group coaching

- Group coaching sessions are held regularly, each on one growth-phase topic such as SEO or conversion rate optimisation. A learner can join the session that matches the topic they are working on. [L1495, 2021-22]
- The group coaching schedule is published at the start of each calendar month and updated on the first day of every month. A notification email is sent when the new schedule is up. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Group coaching calls are split by business stage. One set is for people still building their first business, and another set is for the growth phase after launch. Choose the calls that match the stage you are in. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Group coaching calls are small, with about four to five other business owners plus the hosts. Members can attend just to listen or bring questions for live answers, and there is no requirement to speak. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Group coaching is reached from the learning dashboard. On desktop the link sits in the header, and on mobile it is in the drop-down menu. The page shows the call schedule for the current calendar month, with the call links. [L1498, 2021-22]
- One-to-one coaching is an optional paid add-on beyond the standard membership. It is bought on its own sales page linked from the header or dashboard menu, then booked and scheduled as needed. It is not required to succeed in the program. [L1498, 2021-22]
- All group coaching calls are recorded. Recordings are available only to members in a course called group coaching recordings in the learning dashboard, organised into one module per month and uploaded soon after each call. [L1498, 2021-22]
- Coaching recordings are useful for catching up on past calls and often contain practical insight beyond the core course material, so they build a library members can revisit. [L1498, 2021-22]
