### Supplier location and reach

- For suppliers that ship to customers only in a limited city or region, look for a freight or logistics partner that can offer nationwide shipping instead of limiting the store to specific cities. [L0665, 2022]
- Large or heavy furniture and similar bulky products from Europe are generally not shipped one by one to the US, because freight costs exceed the price. They are typically bulk-shipped to US fulfillment centers. Contact European brands directly and ask whether they have US fulfillment options. [L0665, 2022]
- Heavy and large products such as furniture are usually not practical to dropship from China, and standard furniture delivery can take about three months. Sellers who want large items should look for supply through local platforms that offer China-sourced large-item fulfillment. [L0780, 2024+]
- Freight-shipped items may need a freight broker in destination countries such as Canada. Ask the supplier which freight broker it uses for that country. If shipping is not cost effective, sell only in the country you can ship to profitably. [L0801, 2021]

### Price shipping for the customer

- Add markups to shipping options such as expedited shipping and freight services. Each freight option can be charged as a premium. [L0920, 2018]
- Heavy items cannot be sold at a low price with free shipping, if the nationwide shipping cost is 100 to 150 dollars (up to 200 to 250 dollars for huge items). [L0665, 2022]
- Shipping cost must be included when comparing a cheap product with an expensive one. [L0474, 2020-26]
- Set the selling price so a free-shipping offer still leaves the margin after the average freight cost. [L0801, 2021]

### Transit, delays, damage and returns

#### Delays

- Freight transit estimates, such as four to six business days, are not a fixed promise until a delivery window is confirmed, so delays can happen before that point. Compensation for a delay depends on the carrier's contract, so ask for it in writing, though carrier terms often limit the carrier's liability. [L0470, 2023]
- Freight carriers contact the customer to schedule delivery. Pass the tracking number to the customer as soon as you have it. [L0805, 2024]

#### Damage and packaging

Procedure: reducing damage risk for large items
1. Ask which carrier the supplier prefers, or which carrier other retailers use.
2. Ask what their damage rate is, so you know what insurance to plan for.
3. Ask how they package large items. For experienced shippers, this includes foam inside the box, plywood around the item, and strapping to a pallet.
4. Check online reviews of the supplier for patterns of damage.
5. Mark these products as not eligible for return on your site, since they cannot be returned easily.

[L0466, 2018]

- Example: a 3,000 dollar marble dining table is shipped safely only with foam around it inside a box, plywood nailed around it, and strapping to a pallet. [L0466, 2018]
- Ask suppliers for their packaging practice for large items, because damage risk depends on how the item is packed. [L0466, 2018]

#### Returns

- Mark large or heavy products as not eligible for return on your site, since they cannot be returned easily. [L0466, 2018]
- Accept the own-account risk only for large or heavy items when no other option exists. Do not write the supplier off immediately. [L0466, 2018]

### Special cases

#### Temperature-controlled freight

- Temperature-controlled products can be shipped with refrigerated freight trucks for ongoing smaller shipments rather than full truckloads. Costs must be quoted case by case through freight carriers that offer cold storage. [L1090, 2021]

#### Standard freight versus own account, and the Shopify question

- The ideal setup for freight is that the supplier quotes the order, creates the shipment and the BOL, and charges shipping on its own carrier account. The store does not set up freight shipping in Shopify. [L0854, 2022]
- A custom invoice in Shopify is used only when a customer accepts an add-on, for example liftgate, and the store sends it. [L0667, 2025]

#### Heavy and bulky items from abroad

- Large and heavy items shipped abroad need a freight broker or carrier at each leg. Check the carrier at each stage. [L0801, 2021]

### Work summary for the operator

1. Get the box size, weight and supplier location before you list a heavy item. [L0854, 2022; L0666, 2024]
2. Estimate the average freight cost from several destinations. [L0801, 2021; L0276, 2024]
3. Set a price floor so the margin covers shipping. Do not list cheaper items in that category. [L0357, 2019]
4. Decide who books the freight: the supplier's account is best; otherwise book through a broker. [L0255, 2025; L0854, 2022; L0805, 2024]
5. Get quotes from several brokers and the supplier's own carriers. Compare and negotiate. [L0966, 2025; L0855, 2023; L0970, 2023]
6. Ask which carriers the supplier's top retailers use, and contact them directly. [L0972, 2025; L0966, 2025]
7. Decide delivery level: curbside by default; liftgate and white glove as options. [L0834, 2021; L0667, 2025; L0961, 2020]
8. For ads, geo-target heavy products near the warehouse. [L0941, 2022; L0964, 2023]

### Freight Basics for Large and Heavy Items

- Large and heavy items that do not fit normal parcel shipping are quoted as freight. Request a freight quote before setting the price of a bulky item. [L1105, 2024]
- A freight calculator estimates shipping costs when you compare supplier or shipping options. [L1096, 2025]
- Less-than-truckload (LTL) freight has no fixed price. In a 2018 source, quotes for the same shipment differed by around $200 between companies. A 2020 source says the difference can be a hundred dollars or more. The gap varies per shipment, so compare quotes for each one, even when you offer free shipping to customers. [L1109, 2018; L1111, 2020]
- Overpaying for freight is a costly early mistake. Always compare quotes before booking a shipment. [L1111, 2020]
- For large and heavy items, freight brokers quote the shipping. Brokers are intermediaries, not the carriers themselves. Early on, quotes from different brokers tend to be similar. A quote of about $400 for a sub-$200 item shows that the selling price must be high. Competing quotes will not fix the margin on low-priced heavy goods. [L1114, 2023]

### Shipping Terms and Carrier Accounts

- Confirm each supplier's shipping terms before you build LTL costs into product prices. Estimate costs for the suppliers that do not cover them, because who pays changes the cost. [L1097, 2025]
- Some suppliers require the retailer to have its own freight account on file. They ship on that account and add the freight cost to the invoice. These charges can get expensive, so compare them before committing. [L1094, 2023]
- Shipping on your own account lets you track shipments, follow up with the carrier and file claims for the customer. This is easier when the supplier is not the point of contact for the carrier. [L1094, 2023]
- Setting up your own carrier account, when a supplier will not ship on its own:
  1. Ask first whether the supplier will ship on its own account, since that is simpler.
  2. Create a free UPS account at ups.com or a free FedEx account at fedex.com.
  3. Send the account number to the supplier, who enters it when creating shipping labels.
  4. Pay the supplier for the product, and receive the shipping bill directly from the carrier.

  [L1110, 2019]

### Estimating Freight Costs Before Launch

- Freightcenter's rate tool estimates LTL costs from the supplier warehouse ZIP code, the customer ZIP code, and the package size and weight. Use it to decide on free shipping before any orders come in. [L1092, 2022]
- Freightcenter also gives freight quotes for shipments to Canadian addresses, using postal codes. Canada-specific carriers recommended by other sellers can be compared against it. [L1094, 2023]
- Estimating LTL costs with Freightcenter (freightcenter.com):
  1. Find the warehouse location of the supplier.
  2. Open Freightcenter and choose LTL (less than truckload).
  3. Enter the supplier's ZIP code as the origin.
  4. Enter the ZIP code of a hypothetical customer, for example 90210.
  5. Enter an estimated package size and weight.
  6. Click 'View my rates' to get the quotes.

  [L1097, 2025]
- Estimating average freight cost across destinations:
  1. Go to freightcenter.com and open the freight rate calculator.
  2. Enter the supplier's zip code as origin and a test destination zip code in another state.
  3. Enter the shipment details such as weight and dimensions.
  4. Repeat with several destinations, such as one per region, and average the rates for the shipping cost estimate.

  [L1279, 2021]
- Testing parcel rates before the first order:
  1. Ask the supplier for the product size and weight of the packaged item.
  2. Create test shipments on UPS and FedEx to see what they would cost.
  3. Do this before the first order so no unexpectedly expensive shipment arrives.

  [L1279, 2021]

### Getting Quotes from Freight Brokers

- Gathering data for broker quotes:
  1. Look up product dimensions and weight on competitor product pages or the supplier price sheet.
  2. If they are missing, ask the supplier for them.
  3. Enter the shipment details into freight broker quote sites such as freightquote.com or freightpros.com.
  4. Use the averages in margin calculations.

  [L1111, 2020]
- Quoting large or heavy orders:
  1. Ask the supplier to ship on your own account if possible.
  2. If not, request quotes from at least three freight brokers for each large or heavy order.
  3. Compare the quotes, since prices for the same shipment can vary by a hundred dollars or more.
  4. Choose the lowest acceptable quote.

  [L1111, 2020]

### Finding Carriers Through Top Retailers

- Finding freight carriers for heavy products:
  1. Identify the top competing retailers that already sell the product at MAP and check whether they add a shipping charge or raise prices.
  2. Contact one of those competitors from a personal email, not a business email, as a prospective customer. Ask how the product ships and which carrier they use.
  3. Ask the supplier which top retailers it ships through for these products, and say that freight quotes received so far are too expensive.
  4. Request quotes from those carriers or retailers so you can compare them against your own freight broker quotes.

  [L1115, 2024]
- Template for the supplier message in step 3:

```
Hi [supplier contact name], we are interested in [product name] and want to confirm how it ships. We have requested freight quotes from several brokers, and the prices so far are too high for us to make it work. We have also seen that some other retailers sell these products at MAP. Could you tell us which top retailers you currently use for shipments on these products, so we can request quotes from them as well? Thank you, [your name]
```

  [L1115, 2024]
- For installation-heavy products, ask the supplier which installer it recommends. These are usually the same companies it ships with, so request quotes from them. [L1279, 2021]

### Heavy Products and Margin

- For very heavy products, roughly 300 to 1,200 lb, freight can consume most of a margin around 10%. Check the margin after shipping before ads run. A single item near 1,200 lb needs its own freight quote, because its cost cannot be estimated from lighter products. [L1115, 2024]
- Before testing bulky or large products, check the supplier or CJ shipping cost, since larger packages raise shipping costs. The product only makes sense if the supplier price and shipping still leave a viable margin. [L1185, 2023]

### Shipping Cost at Checkout and on Product Pages

- For heavy items, such as 25 lb or more with freight class pricing, do not calculate shipping at checkout. Add a fixed average shipping cost on the product page instead, so every customer sees it up front. [L1277, 2020]
- Average freight cost for large, heavy furniture-type products in the worked example is about $250 per order. With free shipping, this cost is paid from the store's margin. [L1179, 2020-26]

### Booking a Freight Shipment

- Booking a freight shipment through an online freight broker. The example uses Interparcel, an Australian broker, so check the equivalent for your country:
  1. Open the broker's website (example: www.interparcel.com.au).
  2. On the home page, request a fast quote by entering the package dimensions and weight, the pickup suburb (supplier) and the delivery suburb (customer).
  3. From the price list, choose the cheapest option whose name ends in 'ATL'. If no option ends in 'ATL', choose the cheapest option overall. Click 'Book Now'.
  4. Enter the collection address (supplier) and the delivery address (customer), then click next.
  5. Pick the pickup date. It must be the following business day, so a booking made on Wednesday is set for Thursday. You do not need to set a time. Click next.
  6. Once the pickup date and time are confirmed, click next again.
  7. Type a short description of the goods, such as 'lights', 'lamps' or 'chandelier'. Tick the box below it and enter the value of the items, which must match the customer's order price. Leave the Loss/Damage Warranty dropdown at its current setting. Click next.
  8. In the Payment section, select the 'Stored Debit/Credit Card' button. This bills the company account automatically.
  9. Leave the card dropdown on its default setting, which is the company card saved to the account. Tick the box, then click 'Pay Now'.
  10. On the confirmation screen, download the Shipping Labels and the Address Labels and save them to your computer.
  11. Send both labels to the supplier along with the payment confirmation for the order. Give the supplier the pickup time and the carrier.
  12. If you need to track the order for the customer, use the saved documents.

  [L1291, undated]
- On the price list, choose the cheapest option whose name ends in 'ATL'. If no option has that ending, choose the cheapest option overall. [L1291, undated]
- Freight pickup is booked for the next business day, so a booking made on Wednesday is set for Thursday. A pickup time is not needed. [L1291, undated]
- Loss/Damage Warranty dropdown: leave it unchanged at its default value. When declaring the goods, enter the customer's order price, not your supplier cost. [L1291, undated]
- After booking, message the supplier with this text, and send it together with the shipping and address labels and the payment confirmation:

```
This order will be picked up on [pickup day and date] by [carrier name].
```

  [L1291, undated]

### Freight-Only Suppliers, Receivers and Installation

- Freight-only suppliers may invoice the cost of goods plus freight to a commercial receiver of your choice, not to a residence. The receiver must have a loading dock and inspect the product for freight damage on arrival. Installation is handled separately and is not included on the invoice. [L1094, 2023]
- For heavy or freight-only items, the usual setup is that the freight carrier collects the product directly from the supplier's warehouse and delivers it straight to the end customer. Whether installation is needed decides if a separate installer is required. [L1094, 2023]
- In most cases the supplier books the freight with a freight company, is present when it collects the goods, and loads the truck. In some cases you must book the shipping yourself, but the supplier still packs and loads the box. [L1480, 2021-22]
- Handling heavy or installation-required products:
  1. Ask the supplier which top retailers they use for installation of that product.
  2. Find a nationwide installation company already familiar with the product.
  3. Contact that company directly for installation pricing.

  [L1094, 2023]

### White Glove Delivery

- White glove delivery suits large, heavy or assembly-heavy products that are hard for a customer to move in. The carrier brings the item inside, unpacks it, does light assembly if needed, and removes all boxes and packaging. [L1281, 2022]
- To offer white glove delivery for products shipped by suppliers that do not offer it:
  1. Ask the supplier which white glove delivery company other retailers use for their products.
  2. Contact that company and build a relationship, since it already knows the products and has quotes.
  3. If no partner is available, limit a free white glove promotion to the eligible brands and list those brands on a dedicated Shopify page.

  [L1092, 2022]
- To offer paid white glove at checkout:
  1. Open the Shopify shipping settings.
  2. Keep the default flat-rate shipping option at $0 (free shipping).
  3. Add a second shipping rate for the white glove upgrade with its price.
  4. Customers then see the upgrade choice when they reach shipping options in the cart.

  [L1281, 2022]
- For a custom white glove quote:
  1. Tell the customer on the store where to request a quote (email, phone or text).
  2. Give the default free shipping on the order.
  3. Send the custom white glove quote only if the customer chooses the upgrade.

  The method works for any niche and fits margin-sensitive products. [L1281, 2022]

### Regional Shipping Cost and Geo-Targeting

- Shipping cost varies with origin and destination. Shipping within the same state is cheaper than shipping across the country. Cost estimates for heavy items should use realistic average values, not a single fixed number. [L1180, 2020-26]
- For bulky products sold with free shipping, prefer a supplier located near the main customer region. In the example, shipping within one state cost $150 versus $300 across the country. That difference alone changed net profit from $300 to $450 per order. [L1180, 2020-26]

### Bulky Products: Packaging and Shipping Method

- Volumetric weight starts to matter above about 1 kg. Bulky items are charged for the space they occupy, so large or heavy products such as furniture can become very expensive and may need special shipping lines. [L1217, 2023+]
- Large high-ticket products such as outdoor structures usually ship disassembled in boxes, not fully assembled. Before listing a bulky product, confirm the packed form and shipping method with the supplier. [L1481, 2021-22]

#### Further details

Freight cost check for a new supplier:
1. Ask the supplier for a freight quote for the first order from the carrier they use.
2. Get a second quote on freightcenter.com using the product dimensions, the supplier's pickup location and the customer's delivery location.
3. Compare both quotes and choose the cheaper option, or negotiate with the supplier using the lower quote.
[L1094, 2023]
