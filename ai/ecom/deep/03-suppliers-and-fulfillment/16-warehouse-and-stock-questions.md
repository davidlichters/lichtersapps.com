## Warehouse and Stock Questions

- Suppliers ask about your warehouse to learn whether you are an online-only retailer who needs their products stocked and shipped by them. Your answer should explain your focus, not present the lack of a warehouse as a gap. [L0933, 2020-26]
- Do not answer the warehouse question by saying you are new and may have a warehouse one day. That sounds like a weakness and invites doubt, so state it as a deliberate business focus. [L0933, 2020-26]

#### Template: warehouse answer

```
That is not something we do. Our focus is on marketing, customer service and retail. We are an online retailer and would be a non-stocking dealer, which means you keep the products in stock and ship orders for us. Our focus is on being the best online retailer, bringing awareness to brands like yours, fulfilling orders and providing exceptional customer service. So we do not handle warehousing, and that is by design.
```
[L0933, 2020-26]

#### Template: how the model works (2022)

```
Here is how we operate. Each week our suppliers send us an inventory report listing what they hold in their warehouse, and we add their products to our store based on what is available right now. We pay for ads that promote these products. When a customer places an order, we forward it to the supplier, usually by email, and the supplier ships it through its own shipping account. If you cannot ship through your account, we can provide shipping labels for every future order.
```
[L0854, 2022] **

## Competing Brands and Other Suppliers

- Suppliers ask which other companies you work with because they want to know whether others have already approved you and whether competitors are already selling on your site. They do not want to lose sales to competing brands, so they want to be on your site too. Answer with the right information for that reason. [L0933, 2020-26]
- If you have not been approved by any supplier yet, name the two or three biggest players in your niche that you are reaching out to. If you are already approved with one or two suppliers, name them and say you will work with them for your launch. If you are in discussions with other companies, say you are in discussions. [L0933, 2020-26]

#### Template: competing brands answer

```
Right now, as you know, we have not launched yet, but we are reaching out to [top brand 1], [top brand 2] and [top brand 3], which we see as major players in this industry. [If approved with other suppliers: We have already spoken with [approved supplier A] and [approved supplier B], and we will be working with them for our launch.] [If in discussions with other companies: We are currently in discussions with other companies in this space.]
```
[L0933, 2020-26]

#### Template: why you, not just a listing

```
We are not just looking to list products. We are focused on the long term, and customer service is our priority. When we researched the market, we saw [number or description of competing brands], and what we love about your [specific product attribute, for example design, quality or uniqueness] is [why it stands out]. We will treat your customers with care and bring people to your store who want what you sell because of [specific benefits of their products].
```
[L0933, 2020-26]

- A broad approval email avoids over-explaining: When a supplier asks which companies you compete with or what your business does, keep the initial approval email broad and vague. Do not over-explain your business model, customer base or marketing strategy in early communications. Let the supplier focus on whether they can work with online retailers, not on your specific market positioning. [L0270, 2021] **

## Credit References, Prepay and Bank Details

**Credit terms**

- Make clear you are not asking for credit terms. Suppliers usually give credit terms of 30 or 60 days to physical retailers that buy in bulk, and a dropshipping business should not request them. ** [L0929, 2021-22]
- State that you pay upfront for each order, before the product leaves the supplier's warehouse. Because the supplier is paid before shipping, it carries no financial risk, which makes approval easier. ** [L0929, 2021-22]
- Use prepay terms with suppliers, meaning you pay for orders before they ship. (2018.) [L1085, 2018]
- If a supplier asks for trade references, you may use references from another niche. Tell the supplier you are not asking for credit terms, since references are usually requested to check payment history for credit. ** [L0853, 2021]

**Credit references on applications (2018 and 2023)**

- If a supplier application asks for three credit references, you can leave that section empty when you use prepay terms, because it only matters for credit terms. ** [L1085, 2018]
- When a supplier asks for bank details or a location, this is usually a credit check. If you are prepaying orders, you do not need to provide bank information. On the form, cross out the bank section and write prepay terms. *(2023.)* [L0970, 2023]

The template for revenue and credit references is in the section "Traffic and Sales Questions" above. [L0274, 2023]

## Follow-Up and the Contact Plan Tool

**The tool**

- The Supplier Contact Plan Tool generates a personalized 7-day outreach schedule with phone and email scripts, daily action guidance and follow-up timing. Access it by entering your business details (name, company name, store name, store URL, business phone and email address in first.name@domain format) and supplier information (company name, website, phone if available, and tier; select Silver for most suppliers). [L0126, undated]
- Day 1 outreach starts with a phone call asking about the supplier's application process and identifying the decision maker. If the supplier requests email instead, use the Day 1 email template. The 7-day schedule continues with follow-ups through Day 7 and stops when you receive approval, rejection or "not right now." [L0126, undated]
- Download and save each contact plan for your records. For multiple suppliers, generate a separate plan for each one by replacing the supplier details, and work through your list sequentially. [L0126, undated]

**Contact plan procedure**

1. Open your completed master supplier list and generate a contact plan for the first supplier. [L0126, undated]
2. Initiate Day 1 outreach with a phone call asking about their application process and identifying the decision maker (use the email template only if they request it). [L0126, undated]
3. Record the date, contact person's name and conversation notes. [L0126, undated]
4. Follow the 7-day contact schedule with the mix of phone calls and emails provided. [L0126, undated]
5. Stop contacting when you receive approval, rejection or "not right now." [L0126, undated]
6. Upon your first approval, proceed to the next lesson and upload that supplier's live products. [L0126, undated]
7. Return to this lesson and generate a new contact plan for your second supplier. [L0126, undated]
8. Repeat steps 1-7 until you have approval from five suppliers. [L0126, undated]
9. Continue this process with additional suppliers from your list to build silver and gold tier approvals over time. [L0126, undated]

**Follow-up timing**

- If there is no response, follow up every 2-3 days. If they say "we'll follow up," follow up with them after 1 week if they have not responded. ** [L0268, 2020]
- Follow up with a promised callback or email after a few days. [L0853, 2021; L1094, 2023]
- Follow up on the agreed date and log the result. [L0928, 2020-26]
- Trade account follow-up procedure: if the brand does not respond, follow up every few days; if it rejected you, follow up when the stated condition is met. ** [L0468, 2020]
- Note the sources: the 2-3 day cycle ([L0268, 2020]) and "every few days" ([L0468, 2020]) give the same rhythm. The contact plan tool ([L0126, undated]) sets the schedule within seven days.

**Onboarding after approval**

1. Call the supplier and ask to be approved. [L0671, 2021]
2. Once approved, request their product CSV or price list. [L0671, 2021]
3. Upload their products to the store the same day or the next day. [L0671, 2021]
4. Keep calling new suppliers every day. [L0671, 2021]

## Trade Shows, Meetings and Territory

**Trade show approach (2025)**

1. Remove the password from the store before the event so suppliers can see it live. [L1097, 2025]
2. Print about 200 business cards with your logo, name, store email and cell number (Vistaprint is one option). [L1097, 2025]
3. At each booth, introduce yourself and your company, and say you want to sell the brand's products online. [L1097, 2025]
4. Ask whether they work with online retailers and who to speak to if they do not. [L1097, 2025]
5. Collect each contact's business card. [L1097, 2025]
6. That evening, email each contact a short note asking them to get in touch once they are back home. [L1097, 2025]
7. If there is no reply after about a week, send one more follow-up. [L1097, 2025]

**Territory**

- When suppliers ask "What is your territory?", they are routing you to the correct regional sales representative. Simply state your location (city and state or geographic region). They will assign you to the territory rep responsible for your area. ** [L0243, 2019]

**Documentation**

- Document every conversation: the person's name, the date and all details discussed. Even if declined, the supplier will remember the call, which positions you for future contact. [L0133, undated]
- Record each answer, the reason for any rejection, and the date to follow up. [L0468, 2020]

### Phone-first outreach sequence

- Build a supplier list first, then contact each supplier by phone and by email. Once a supplier approves, upload its products to your Shopify store so customers can see them. In theory this is simple; the difficulty is carrying out the calls and emails. [L1248, 2021-22]
- Follow this order for each product in the target country:
  1. Find the suppliers of the product in the target country.
  2. Call each supplier and ask for approval to retail their products through your website.
  3. After approval, list the products on the store. [L1481, 2021-22]
- If calling feels uncomfortable, start with brands you care less about. This builds confidence before you call the brands that matter most. [L1113, 2022]

### Your position with suppliers

- Approach suppliers as a mutually beneficial business opportunity, not as someone asking for a favor or begging for approval to sell their products. Both sides can make money from the arrangement. [L1248, 2021-22]
- Suppliers benefit because you bring them sales and improve their cash flow. This win-win basis is what makes the relationship work; it is not a favor you ask for. [L1480, 2021-22]
- Suppliers do not have to pay to have their products listed on your website. You handle marketing, customer service and payment, and you pay them only for the products you order. [L1248, 2021-22]
- Suppliers already prepare and ship orders for their existing retail clients, so dropshipping orders add little new work for them. Their risk is minimal. [L1248, 2021-22]
- In the worst case you sell nothing for a supplier, which leaves their net result at zero. They cannot lose money from the relationship, but they can gain. [L1248, 2021-22]
- Suppliers compete with other manufacturers and distributors for the most prominent placement on retailer websites. This is why they want their products sold through a new store. [L1248, 2021-22]
- Suppliers judge a seller by professionalism and business seriousness, not by where the seller lives. A non-US seller who holds a US reseller permit and an EIN can approach US suppliers. Follow the call script closely and stay calm. [L1110, 2019]

### Preparing before the call

- Fix your website launch date before you contact any supplier, so you can give it without hesitation. (2024.) [L1115, 2024]
- Do not reveal your exact location in early conversations. In traditional industries this can reduce trust or cause resentment. Set privacy settings so suppliers who connect with you on Facebook cannot see personal photos, such as work from the beach. (2024.) [L1441, 2018]
- Prepare your own traffic ideas and a basic business plan. Suppliers need to believe you will add sales that require little work from them. (2024.) [L1422, 2020-26]
- Prepare a short explanation of how you will add sales to the brand and how you will provide excellent customer service. Brands approve a reseller when it can show both. [L1279, 2021]

### Presenting your business

- Suppliers unfamiliar with dropshipping need a pitch that explains the benefits and how the model works for them. Covering that education is a key part of getting them on board. [L1274, 2018]
- Present your business in this order:
  1. Show the supplier the value you will bring to their brand.
  2. Explain how you will market their products.
  3. Describe your business.
  4. Explain why you want to work with this specific supplier.

  [L1275, 2019]
- Explain that you run a long-term business with customer support as your top priority, and that you are not a short-term or fly-by-night operation. (2024.) [L1422, 2020-26]
- Approval depends on the supplier believing you will benefit its brand. Suppliers mostly want to know that you will look after customers and run a real business. (2023.) [L1283, 2023]

### Scripts and email templates

**First message to a supplier.** Keep it short, say you want a long-term partnership, and ask concrete questions. Questions on dropshipping, delivery time to each target market, shipping fee and method, and custom packaging or logo options let you qualify a supplier quickly before deeper negotiation. [L1353, undated]

```
Hi, I'm [YOUR NAME], a seasoned dropshipper. I'm already selling this product and want to find a supplier for a lasting partnership. If that interests you, could you help me with a few questions?

1. Do you provide dropshipping?
2. What is the typical delivery time to the USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and Europe?
3. What would shipping cost, and which carriers or methods would you use?
4. Can I add custom packaging and my own logo to the product?

Thanks in advance. I look forward to hearing from you.
```
[L1353, undated]

- Use the sentence "I'm already selling this product" only if it is true. (2024 source; honesty principle.) [L1283, 2023; L1111, 2020]

**Shipping and fulfilment offer.** Use this to offer the supplier a choice of how orders are shipped.

```
Hi [Supplier name], we work in a few different ways depending on what suits you best. If you prefer, we can take care of shipping: your team picks and packs the order and has it ready for dispatch, and we arrange our courier to collect it, and our virtual assistant can be trained on the exact steps. If you would rather ship it yourselves, that works too, and we are happy to go with whichever option is easier for you. We have had strong demand for your [product or brand] and have turned down some sales recently, so if you decide to work with us, let us know and we can set up the account.
```
[L1441, 2018]

- Only include the demand claim if it is true. Do not overstate the store's performance. [L1111, 2020]

**Monthly sales before the store is live.** Send this as written after filling in the placeholders. Do not invent sales figures.

```
Hi [Supplier name], our store is still being built and goes live on [launch date]. We do not have sales history yet, so I cannot give you reliable figures today. Once your products are live and traffic is running, I will share real numbers with you. If you need an estimate for planning, our goal is [target amount] per month for your brand, but the actual result will depend on traffic and listing performance.
```
[L1274, 2018]

**Company name and EIN before LLC paperwork is finished.** Fill in the placeholders before sending.

```
Hi [Supplier name], I am forming a new company for this business, and the paperwork should be complete in about [number] weeks. Could you send me the product files now so I can start preparing the uploads? I [have / will have] my EIN [and the date it was issued, if you have it].
```
[L1274, 2018]

### Answering supplier questions

**Yearly purchase budget.** Do not give a fixed number. Explain that your budget is effectively unlimited as long as products convert at a 10x ROAS, and that spending starts at a small daily test budget. (2024.) [L1115, 2024]

**Monthly sales estimate before products are live.** A usable answer is a rough target, such as $10,000 per month for the brand. Be clear that there are no real visitor data yet and that the actual result depends on traffic and listing performance. Never state zero sales as if it were a real figure. (2018.) [L1274, 2018]

- Use the wording in the monthly sales template above. [L1274, 2018]

**Website launch date.** Give the date fixed in advance, without hesitation. [L1115, 2024]

**Shipping and warehousing.** Ask for shipping on the supplier's account if possible. Ship on your own account only if the supplier requires it. For warehousing, ask the supplier to stock the products, provide inventory reports, and ship on its account. (2024.) [L1115, 2024]

| Option | Use when | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier ships on its own account | Your first request, if possible | [L1115, 2024] |
| You ship on your own account | Only if the supplier requires it | [L1115, 2024] |
| Supplier stocks, reports inventory and ships on its account | Your warehousing request | [L1115, 2024] |
| You offer to take care of shipping (supplier picks, packs, and your courier collects), or the supplier ships itself | An offer in the reply template; the supplier chooses | [L1441, 2018] |

- The two sources fit together: ask for the supplier's account first, and offer the courier option when the supplier prefers it or requires it. [L1115, 2024; L1441, 2018]

**Traffic.** Have your own traffic ideas ready, because suppliers need to see the extra sales you will bring. [L1422, 2020-26]

**Company name and EIN.** Use the template above if your LLC paperwork is not finished. [L1274, 2018]

### Application forms and terms

- When a supplier application asks what collaboration type you want, choose retailer, because you sell as a retailer on your own website. If asked about other sales platforms or physical stores, answer no when you only sell on your own website. (2024.) [L1115, 2024]
- You usually do not need to send your own terms. The supplier reviews the agreements it provides. (2024.) [L1115, 2024]

### What suppliers weigh

- Suppliers weigh approval by downside risk. A retailer that brings no sales costs them little time. A retailer that damages their brand is their worst outcome, so your pitch should show you will protect their reputation. (2022 source; the same point appears in two units.) [L1422, 2020-26]
- Suppliers are approving a future, not current results. Approvals are about the store's future potential and the niche, not current store results. (2020.) [L1111, 2020]

### Honest positioning

- State clearly during approval talks that you will never list a supplier's products below the MAP or price range it specifies. Suppliers need to believe this before they approve you. (2024 source.) [L1422, 2020-26]
- Present the plan honestly and do not overstate the store's performance. (2020.) [L1111, 2020]
- If the store is still in development and has only a few sales, describe it honestly as a store that is launching and plans to scale traffic and bring in the supplier's brand. Avoid sharing specific sales figures that would not impress a supplier. (2023.) [L1283, 2023]
