### Growing from the Launch Catalog

- Keep applying to more brands and uploading their products as approvals come in, aiming for around 20 brands over time. [L0941, 2022]
- There is no limit on how many brands you can sell, as long as each brand approves you. Keep applying to new brands while the store is live. [L1275, 2019]
- If you have few approved suppliers, keep adding brands and products rather than stopping at launch. Adjacent products in the same niche (for example ottomans or end tables next to leather chairs) can add more brands and products. [L1275, 2019]
- Set a goal of adding one or two new suppliers per week, even by circling back to earlier applicants. [L0429, 2022]
- Products from five different suppliers give a usable sample of items and brands to test and build on. This is more useful than relying on a single supplier's catalog when choosing what to sell. [L0689, 2020-26]
- Build the first product base in this order:
  1. Get approved by five dropshipping suppliers.
  2. Upload the products from those five suppliers to the online store.
  3. Once the approvals are in and the products are live on the store, mark this step as complete before starting conversion work on the store.
  [L0689, 2020-26]
- Verify that all products from all five suppliers are live and visible on the store before moving on. [L0132, undated]
- Upload products from each of the five approved suppliers to the store. [L0132, undated]
- Continue supplier outreach and approval requests until five suppliers confirm they will work with the store. [L0132, undated]
- Your store should have products from at least five different suppliers live before focusing on conversion optimization. [L0132, undated]
- Once the store is running, more suppliers usually surface on their own. [L0937, 2021-22]
- Keep the product catalog on the store as approvals come in. Approval of a supplier alone is not enough to make progress, and only approved suppliers with uploaded products add to the count needed before moving on. [L0495, 2020-26]
- Add the rest of the approved suppliers' products once the store is live. Upload all their products before ads go live. [L1092, 2022]
- Heavy or freight-shipped products priced around $1,500 to $3,000 can be a sound niche if they pass all niche criteria. Such orders can bring roughly $500 to $1,000 profit each. (2020) [L0664, 2020]

### When Too Few Suppliers Exist

- If you find fewer than 20 brands in your niche, validate viability before building:
  1. Call companies directly.
  2. Do not mention wanting to sell their products.
  3. Ask neutrally whether they work with online distributors.
  4. Use their answers to decide whether online distribution is feasible in this niche.
  [L0245, 2020]
- To check a market with no visible dropshipping, call the suppliers directly and have a proper conversation about dropshipping instead of relying on competitor stores as evidence. Approval from suppliers is the test that counts. [L0905, 2021-22]
- Some markets have little visible dropshipping because suppliers were not open to it in the past, while many suppliers are now more open. Such markets can be underserved and are worth testing. [L0905, 2021-22]
- If a niche has only one drop-ship-friendly retailer, check that it sells at least 20 brands before continuing. Then email from a personal address asking whether it has a warehouse or showroom, and proceed if it is online-only. (2023) [L0934, 2023]
- Count how many stores advertise the most popular products of a niche on Google Shopping. The competition threshold in that test is 10 stores. (2024) [L0971, 2024]
- Research the niche competition: at least 20 suppliers, then count stores advertising the top products on Google Shopping against the threshold of 10. [L0971, 2024]
- Expect approval rates to be low for a new store. In markets where most suppliers keep a warehouse, contact 50 to 60 suppliers instead of 20. [L0969, 2022]
- If you cannot reach about 10 suppliers after following up for roughly two weeks, move up one niche level to increase the number of products you can offer at launch. (2025) [L0966, 2025]
- Gold-tier suppliers that run out of stock: one option is to contact more suppliers in the niche before changing niches. [L0969, 2022]
- Fewer suppliers are acceptable only as a temporary stage in a smaller country, not as the target. (2023) [L0935, 2022+]
- Some stores abroad are built with well under 20 suppliers, but this is an exception. [L0935, 2022+]
- Estimated suppliers in a niche worksheet is the count of brands across the future competitors you found, not the number of competitor sites. Estimates are rough; if a brand list is long, a rounded figure such as 100 is an acceptable estimate. [L0459, 2020-26]
- The count of brands in a Google Shopping check across the product types in the niche must reach at least 10 in total. Procedure:
  1. Set the search to the target country, for example the US market.
  2. Read the seller and brand names in the Google Shopping listings and scroll through the next pages.
  3. Count the potential brands or suppliers; the lesson requires at least 10 in total across the product types in the niche.
  4. Open a retailer that sells the product category and note the brands it carries.
  5. Mark the local-brand criterion as yes if the count is met.
  [L0458, 2021-22]

### Broadening the Niche

- If too few suppliers approve you, broaden the niche to find more brands and products with better margins. This is a fallback, not a day-one move. (2023) [L0970, 2023]
- If a niche cannot reach 20 suppliers, niche up one level and add related products to the range. [L0359, 2020]
- When niching up because you found fewer than 20 suppliers, identify an additional 20 to 40 suppliers in related categories to expand the product range. [L0249, 2022]
- When a store has only a few approved suppliers with low margins or no MAP, grow the catalog with the steps in the "Growing from the Launch Catalog" section. [L0429, 2022]
- In markets where most suppliers keep a warehouse and too few suppliers exist, consider a broader niche to get enough products. [L0969, 2022]
- Before choosing a niche, make sure the number of brands is enough; if it is short, broaden rather than launch thin. [L0356, 2019]
- Before moving to the niche, confirm the supplier count first. Supplier availability is checked before product count, so the niche can still be widened if the count is short. [L0356, 2019]
- Expect that some niches will have too few suppliers for the target count. When that happens, the broader niche is the way to reach enough products. [L0969, 2022]
- Supplier-count thresholds below 20 are a temporary stage, so widen the niche to restore the standard. [L0935, 2022+]
- If a niche cannot reach ~10 suppliers after two weeks of follow-up, move up one niche level (2025). [L0966, 2025]
- Brands in a niche should be checked for drop-ship viability before broadening. A niche dominated by household names is not viable, so broadening should not lead there. [L0994, 2021-22]

### Niche Viability Checks

- The niche must be drop-ship friendly, meaning suppliers can ship orders directly to the end customer without the store holding stock. [L1080, 2020-26]
- Brands that sell through online-only stores are the ones that drop ship; brands that sell through distributors with warehouses usually do not. (2023) [L1094, 2023]
- Shipping is often the largest variable cost for heavy items, so shipping rates must be checked before choosing a niche. If shipping is charged out of a thin margin, such as a 35 percent margin on a large item, the product is generally not viable because no budget remains for ads. [L1109, 2018]
- The best suppliers and brands are known to enthusiasts within the market but unknown to the general public. Markets dominated by household name brands are not viable for drop shipping. [L0994, 2021-22]
- Televisions are not a viable drop shipping product, because the market is dominated by massive international brands such as Sony, Panasonic and LG. Those large companies do not need drop shippers to grow and rarely work with them. [L0994, 2021-22]
- Seasonal niches: for a seasonal niche (for example in Texas with a short winter), start with suppliers for year-round products that sell in all four seasons. Add seasonal products later, as the relevant season approaches. (2025) [L0472, 2025]
- Most industries see sales spikes around Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Christmas, so a holiday spike alone is not a reason to reject a niche. Plan around it by confirming with suppliers that stock will last through the peak. [L0357, 2019]
- Heavy or freight-shipped products priced around $1,500 to $3,000 can be a sound niche if they pass all niche criteria. (2020) [L0664, 2020]
- Before testing a niche's competition, source at least 20 suppliers. [L0971, 2024]
- The worksheet's Estimated Suppliers number counts brands, not competitor sites. [L0459, 2020-26]

### Single-Supplier Dependency Risk

- Do not depend on one supplier. A single supplier can stop selling to you and remove your whole catalog. Upload the first supplier's products and start ads, but keep contacting new suppliers continuously. [L1086, 2019]
- Do not start traffic with only one brand. Get at least two brands live before turning traffic on, and use the free time to get the rest of the suppliers approved. [L0671, 2021]
- Working with few suppliers raises business risk sharply. If two of three suppliers close or one raises prices, the store stays open but becomes far less profitable. A large supplier base protects margins and continuity against losing individual partners. (2023) [L0935, 2022+]
- Multiple supply sources reduce risk. A supplier can close, have an owner stroke, or move on to another project, so you need several brands and several suppliers in each niche. [L0801, 2021]
- Supplier depth is one of the two primary levers for growing revenue, so secure as many suppliers as possible within the niche. [L0512, 2020-26]
- Put paid ad budget only on products from suppliers that have real margins, and send MAP suppliers' products into paid ads while selling the rest through free traffic only. [L0671, 2021; L0429, 2022]
- Where a supplier enforces MAP, route its products to paid ads. Use free Google product listings for the rest. [L0429, 2022]
- Set up Google Merchant Center and free Google product listings for the products already approved. [L0429, 2022]
- Build a store with a large supplier base from the start; the larger the base, the less any single partner's exit can damage revenue. [L0935, 2022+]
- Keep the master supplier list continuously worked, since the long-term goal is authorization with as close to 100 percent of it as possible. [L0938, 2020-26]
- Supplier approval is a numbers game. Expect many rejections, and do not treat any single supplier as the business. [L0834, 2021]
- Expect the 80/20 pattern: about 80 percent of sales come from about 20 percent of suppliers, so keep approaching many suppliers and following up with them over time. [L1113, 2022]
- Some operators have built stores with few suppliers, but this carries more risk and should be an exception. [L0935, 2022+]

#### Further details
