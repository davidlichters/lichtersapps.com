- Order processing from China typically takes about 2-3 business days before the parcel ships, because items are moved to a warehouse, checked, stripped of Chinese labels and packed. [L0363, 2022+]
- CJ Dropshipping's warehouse is typically in Shenzhen, and the product must first travel from the manufacturing city, which adds time. [L1218, undated]
- Two or three large Chinese shippers, including YunExpress and Yun When Express, are the fast lines for the US market. [L1218, undated]
- Long delivery times from a China-based agent can come from where the agent is located, because parcels from some provinces pass through several cities before reaching the export airport. Ask where the supplier or agent is based before promising delivery times. [L1214, 2023+]
- For China-based fulfillment, Guangzhou and Shenzhen were named as the strongest provinces because they process goods faster on the way to the airport. [L1214, 2023+]
- Slow agent shipping can damage influencer campaigns, so check delivery times before promoting the product through influencers. [L1184, 2023+]
- Check shipping times before you commit to a supplier. [L1176, 2023]
- Products should be checked for delivery window. Shipping was listed as 7 to 15 days for the sample product. [L0685, undated]
- Some orders in the example took up to 45 days, which made it unacceptable for the seller. [L0678, 2023]
- Customers accepted the CJ delivery times of about two weeks. [L1444, 2022]
- Treat an order that is not shipping within your target window as a delivery problem and move the product to another route if it continues. [L0678, 2023]

### Order fulfillment

**CJ Dropshipping: fulfilling store orders (procedure)**

1. Open the My CJ section. [L0685, undated]
2. Open Imported orders. [L0685, undated]
3. Shopify orders are imported into the My CJ section. Click Sync Store Orders (or Sync store orders) to pull all Shopify orders into CJ. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
4. Open each order and click Fulfill (or Add to cart) to fulfill it. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]

**Hyper Rescue: fulfilling store orders (procedure)**

1. Orders from the Shopify store sync automatically into the Order list in Hyper Rescue, and new orders appear dynamically. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
2. Open the order and click Fulfill. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
3. Pay for the product price plus shipping cost. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
4. Hyper Rescue handles the rest of the fulfillment. [L0685, undated]

**Multi-item orders**

- When a store has multi-item orders from upsells, ask the live CJ agent to ship items from the same supplier together. CJ may otherwise send them in separate packages from different manufacturers. [L1217, 2023+]
- At good upsell volume, switch to an agent. Agents can combine packages and customize orders, which dropshipping platforms typically do not. [L1217, 2023+]

**Costs per order**

- CJ Dropshipping example: a cup washer costs 5.95 USD for the chosen variant. The total cost including shipping to a US address is 18.85 USD. The price range across variants runs from 1.12 to 10.75 USD. Shipping times of 7 to 15 days were considered decent. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
- CJ product prices vary by variant, ranging from 1.12 to 10.75 USD for the same listing. Check the price of the exact variant before listing. [L0685, undated]
- A product sold at 6.45 USD with US shipping had a delivery window of 7 to 15 days. A specific variant (color or style) cost 5.95 USD for the product, with a total cost of 18.85 USD including shipping to a US address. [L0685, undated]
- Pay the fulfillment provider pay-as-you-go with PayPal or credit card. It has no monthly plan. You can top up a balance (for example 200-300 USD) and then click fulfill per order. [L0363, 2022+]
- Faster suppliers can cost roughly 10% more per product than slower ones. [L0678, 2023]

### Pricing, negotiation and agent selection

- Supplier pricing does not automatically decrease in quality. If a supplier cannot lower price further, it will say so. Volume commitments, especially stock, provide negotiation leverage. [L0298, 2023]
- Negotiate in the live chat of the supplier with a human agent, ideally during Chinese working hours. Automated replies rarely offer discounts, so get a live agent first. [L1217, 2023+]
- Listed prices on platforms such as Hyper Rescue and CJ are often higher than what you can get through direct negotiation. Do not accept the listed price without asking a live agent for a discount. [L1217, 2023+]
- Platforms and agents such as CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue often list higher prices to new dropshippers, because they prioritize long-term partners who bring steady volume. Negotiate by live chat and expect lower prices than the listed price. [L1217, 2023+]
- Listed prices are negotiable through live chat for CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue. [L1217, 2023+]
- Play agents against each other by sharing a competing agent's quote in chat and asking for a better offer. Ask each agent for a quote that beats the others to get the best price. [L1217, 2023+]
- Suppliers and agents do not mind when you send the same inquiry to several of them. They compete for good long-term clients, so the goal is to be a strong client to whoever you choose. [L1217, 2023+]
- Once a store reaches a consistent daily order count, suppliers may negotiate price and pre-order a week of inventory into their warehouse. [L1315, 2023]
- Request stock inventory for at least one week when reaching 10+ daily orders. Immediately stop shipping new items from China to enable faster stock-based fulfillment if a customer cancels. [L0298, 2023]
- Order stock starting at 10+ orders per day to save 0.5-1 dollar per item and reduce processing time to 12 hours (vs. 2-5 days normally). [L0298, 2023]
- A dedicated fulfillment agent or personal agent can prioritize your orders at CJ Dropshipping when the default processing times are not reliable. [L1218, undated]

**Asking for better service with no sales history (procedure)**

1. State that you have sold before and that this is a new store you are testing with products. [L1440, 2021+]
2. Say that you want a long-term partnership. [L1440, 2021+]
3. Ask for better quotes and explain that you have experience. [L1440, 2021+]
4. Ask for a personal agent. The platforms provided one by Skype. [L1440, 2021+]
5. Send consistent sales, because suppliers give better service to customers who bring steady business. [L1440, 2021+]

**Negotiation template (verbatim)**

```
Hello [Name], I am an experienced dropshipper and have built multiple six-figure stores. I am now starting a new project in the [niche] niche, and I am looking for a long-term partnership rather than a one-off order. My current agent quotes [current price] for [product] at [volume] orders per month. If you can match or beat that price and offer a good discount for [product] at [volume] orders per month, I would like to move forward with you. Please tell me what minimum volume you need to offer your best rates.
```
[L1217, 2023+]

- Negotiating with the 1688.com price: 1688.com is a Chinese domestic wholesale platform that shows the actual price suppliers pay. The site is in Chinese, so translate it and compare that price with your agent's quote to see the markup being charged. [L1217, 2023+]
- Personal agents usually require a minimum of about 5-10 orders per day. Move to a personal agent once your volume reaches that level. [L1217, 2023+]
- Ecom Ops requires a minimum order quantity of about 100 units per item. Other agents can work with volumes like about 5 orders per day or around 100 orders per month. [L1217, 2023+]

### Private suppliers and agents
**Who to contact**

- The lesson lists four private suppliers for order fulfillment: supplier A, supplier D, supplier B and supplier C. Their WhatsApp contacts are on the course page and are not repeated here. [L1156, 2026]
- Make first contact with supplier D or supplier C, who are the recommended private suppliers. Use supplier A or supplier B only as a fallback if supplier D and supplier C do not carry the product. [L1156, 2026]
- Contact private suppliers by WhatsApp direct message, using the number from the supplier list. [L1156, 2026]
- For digital products such as books, supplier D and supplier C are the most suitable suppliers, because PDF versions of the content can be sourced from the internet. [L1156, 2026]
- The private supplier contacts on the course list use international phone prefixes (+86 and +852), which points to suppliers based in mainland China and Hong Kong. Confirm the supplier's location and shipping terms before committing, since the target market is usually elsewhere. [L1156, 2026]

**Message template (verbatim)**

```
Hello, I'd like to dropship this item: [PRODUCT NAME]. Photo of the product attached: [Attach product photo]. Could you verify whether this precise product can be supplied with no minimum order quantity (MOQ) and delivered to [COUNTRY, e.g. the USA]? I also need your price per unit, the shipping charge, and how long order processing takes. [Optional: referral line, only if someone referred you to this supplier, e.g. 'X sent me your contact'] Kindly share your contact information so we can move forward. Thank you.
```
[L1156, 2026]

- Send the supplier a product image in the first message so they know exactly which product you mean. [L1156, 2026]
- Before committing to a private supplier, confirm there is no minimum order quantity and that the supplier ships to your target country. [L1156, 2026]

**Setting up a private supplier (procedure)**

1. Pick supplier D or supplier C first. If neither carries the product, pick supplier A or supplier B. [L1156, 2026]
2. Get the supplier's WhatsApp number from the supplier list. [L1156, 2026]
3. Send the WhatsApp direct message using the template, with a product photo attached. [L1156, 2026]
4. Make sure the supplier confirms the exact product is available, that there is no MOQ, and that they ship to your target country. [L1156, 2026]
5. Once the supplier agrees, go to shopify.partners and give the supplier partnership access to your Shopify store, following the instructions the supplier sends you. [L1156, 2026]
6. The supplier fulfills the orders from your store. [L1156, 2026]
7. Pay the supplier a fixed amount per order via Wise or bank transfer. [L1156, 2026]

**Why and how private suppliers differ**

- Private suppliers are used instead of public dropshipping apps to get better margins, custom branding and more control over the product and fulfillment. [L1156, 2026]
- With a private supplier, the supplier handles fulfillment of orders from your store. Your role is to grant store access and pay the supplier per order. [L1156, 2026]
- Pay private suppliers a fixed amount for each order they fulfill, using Wise or bank transfer. [L1156, 2026]
- The supplier's ship-from location and processing time are the first things to confirm (see the processing table above). [L1214, 2023+; L1156, 2026]

### Zendrop

- Zendrop's Select plan offers a no-questions refund on returned or refunded items. After you refund the customer and send proof such as a screenshot, the supplier refunds the product cost. Ask other suppliers for similar terms if they do not offer it on their basic plans. [L0335, 2023]
- Zendrop contacted a store doing 30-50 orders per day and offered to match or beat supplier pricing. Suppliers track connected app installations to identify high-volume sellers for recruitment, and Zendrop and similar platforms use this data to proactively reach out. [L0298, 2023]
- Zendrop can be used to request or import a dropshipping product into Shopify. Alternatively, you can add the product link now and connect it to Zendrop later. [L1331, 2025-26]
- Zendrop is an option for single-order dropshipping. [L1217, 2023+]
- Zendrop and USA Drop are other dropshipping suppliers mentioned as alternatives to CJ Dropshipping and HyperSQ. [L1172, 2023+]

**Case: Zendrop's recruitment and the switch back to CJ (case)**

- Situation: a store doing 30-50 orders per day was contacted by Zendrop, which offered to match or beat supplier pricing. [L0298, 2023]
- Initial appeal: low prices, a private Slack group and multiple agents. [L0298, 2023]
- Problem: the appeal faded when service quality proved poor. Shipping lines were cheaper, deliveries were late, agents were not responsive, and shipping costs doubled compared with CJ. [L0298, 2023]
- Outcome: CJ maintained competitive pricing when shown competitor quotes and provided superior service and fulfillment times. [L0298, 2023]

### Other platforms and suppliers

**Big Buy and Spocket**

- Big Buy is a European all-in-one dropshipping platform with stock in Spain and Germany that integrates with Shopify. It ships with UPS, FedEx and DHL, so its shipping costs are higher than those of typical China-based suppliers. [L1214, 2023+]
- Big Buy accepts sourcing requests, where the company finds products that are not in its catalog through EU suppliers. Check its current catalog before relying on it, since the website had changed a lot after 2021. [L1214, 2023+]
- A US version of Big Buy exists, but the European version was rated better for sellers focused on Europe. [L1214, 2023+]
- Spocket offers a free trial of about three days. Use it to check whether the products you need are listed, and cancel if they are not. [L1214, 2023+]

**Other suppliers**

- Day One is a large agent supplier that high-volume dropshipping stores use for fulfillment. Before switching to it, request a quotation and expect that it will need access to the store, so close out any unfulfilled orders on the old supplier first. [L1173, undated]
- AutoDS can import products from AliExpress and from CJ Dropshipping into a Shopify store. [L0363, 2022+]
- A Shein-based fulfillment option (not tested by the speaker) should be verified before use. [L1439, 2024+]

### Switching suppliers and leaving a platform

- Supplier path for dropshipping (procedure):
  1. Start with CJ Dropshipping for product testing. [L1171, 2024+]
  2. Once you make significant sales, switch to a supplier with better offers and shipping times such as HyperSKU (rendered as HyperSQ in the lesson), Best Dealer or a private agent. [L1171, 2024+]
  3. Choose the supplier that gives the best offer, shipping times and convenience at each stage. [L1171, 2024+]
- Move from a fulfillment platform to a private supplier once you have consistent demand, for example at least about 5 orders per day or 3-4 days of steady sales. The private supplier must reply within its working hours. [L0363, 2022+]
- Keep the supplier that works best for each product, and consider using different suppliers for different products in the same store. [L0678, 2023]
- Using two different suppliers in one store is possible, with a clear overview of which orders go to which supplier. Products can be split between suppliers depending on which one ships them fastest. [L0678, 2023]
- Start with an easy-to-set-up supplier such as CJ Dropshipping, track processing and shipping times per order, and test other suppliers if delays become too long. [L0678, 2023]
- A dropshipping supplier such as CJ Dropshipping is acceptable for early testing because it is easy to set up and offers workable prices and shipping. Only once demand is consistent should the store contact other suppliers and negotiate a better price. [L1426, undated]
- The right provider depends on the product and on the support agent assigned to you. [L0363, 2022+]

**Switching checklist (procedure)**

1. Before switching suppliers, stop running ads on the date the old supplier will stop receiving orders. Orders placed after this date may stay unprocessed and cause refund and delivery problems. [L1173, undated]
2. Check that all paid orders on the old platform are marked as fulfilled in Shopify. Unmarked orders can be paid for twice when the new supplier ships them. [L1173, undated]
3. For Day One specifically, request a quotation first. Expect that it will need access to the store, and close out unfulfilled orders on the old supplier before you connect it. [L1173, undated]

**Choosing between suppliers and the cost of switching**

- Choose the supplier that ships as fast or faster. [L1444, 2022]
- Products can be split between suppliers depending on which one ships them fastest. [L0678, 2023]
- Choose the fulfillment route based on how much delivery time matters to your customers. [L1428, undated]
- Choose a supplier with reliable delivery before you scale ad spend. [L1127, 2024]
- If a product has no good manufacturer, switch to an agent or a better supplier. [L1440, 2021+]
- Dropshipping suppliers are cheaper to start with and slower to ship, while private agents tend to be cheaper with faster shipping once the store has volume. [L1315, 2023]
