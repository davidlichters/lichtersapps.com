### Backorders and customer communication

#### Accepting backorders

- Offer back orders for out-of-stock products when a reasonable delivery window can be stated. Many customers will wait for an item they cannot get elsewhere. [L1522, 2021-22]
- Under-promise and over-deliver on back orders: quote a longer lead time than the expected one. Example: if stock is expected in four weeks, promise six weeks, so early delivery earns better reviews and referrals. [L1522, 2021-22]
- Backorders can be accepted, but the order confirmation should state that the product is backordered and give the expected ship date. This prevents complaints and refund requests later. (2020) [L1277, 2020]
- Only pre-sell a backordered product when the supplier has given a reliable ETA, because customers will cancel or complain if a long backorder is not communicated. (2021) [L0942, 2022]
- Fast-selling products that run low at the supplier can be listed as back-order with a deposit option (see [L1069, 2019] above; older advice, 2019). [L1069, 2019]
- If a supplier is out of stock and gives no restock date, do not sell or advertise that supplier's products. If restock is coming soon in a meaningful quantity, pre-selling can make sense; decide supplier by supplier. (2021) [L0968, 2021]

#### Notice above Add to Cart when a supplier warehouse closes

- When a supplier warehouse closes, place a clear notice above the Add to Cart button stating due to the coronavirus, supplier ABC is currently not shipping until [estimated date] so customers make informed decisions before payment. (2020) [L0245, 2020]
- Warehouse closures in major hubs can delay shipping and require special arrangements with customers. (2020) [L0967, 2020]

#### Procedure: warehouse closure follow-up

1. Place warehouse closure notice above Add to Cart button. (2020) [L0245, 2020]
2. After orders arrive, manually follow up with phone calls and emails to each customer. (2020) [L0245, 2020]
3. Confirm customer willingness to wait for delayed shipment. (2020) [L0245, 2020]
4. Prevent customers from discovering shipping delays after payment. (2020) [L0245, 2020]

#### Procedure: handling a backordered product

1. Check the ETA for the backordered item with the supplier before speaking to the customer. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
2. Look for a similar product in your store, or find one from another supplier at a similar or lower price. A slightly more expensive alternative can also work if the extra cost is small. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
3. Contact the customer by phone, or have the phone team do it, and explain that the ETA applies to every seller of the product. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
4. If the customer will wait, offer to ship first out the door when stock arrives. If the wait is long, give the new ship date and make the same offer. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
5. If the customer needs the product sooner, offer the alternative. A cheaper alternative can come with a partial refund (for example $100) and ship within 24 to 48 hours. A slightly more expensive alternative can ship with no extra charge to the customer. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
6. If no similar product exists, look for similar products even from smaller or less established brands. (2020) [L0852, 2020]

#### Procedure: order check before taking payment

1. When an order arrives for a product that may be out of stock, check inventory with the supplier or warehouse before taking payment. (2020) [L1111, 2020]
2. If it is unavailable, email or call the customer that the card has not been charged yet. (2020) [L1111, 2020]
3. Tell the customer the backorder length. (2020) [L1111, 2020]
4. Let the customer choose to keep the order pending or cancel it, and release any pending charge. (2020) [L1111, 2020]

#### Procedure: out-of-stock product with no confirmed ETA, offer a similar item

1. Ask the supplier for an ETA and relay it to the customer if one exists. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
2. If there is no ETA, look through your own catalog for the closest similar product, matching type, size and color, for example a white standing desk about 80 inches long. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
3. Contact the customer and explain that the warehouse is out of the item with no confirmed return date. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
4. Offer the similar product, state the price difference in dollars, say whether it is in stock and can ship the next day. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
5. Ask whether the customer wants the order changed to the replacement product. (2024) [L0965, 2024]

Message to the customer (2024):

```
Hi [customer name],

Thank you for your order of [original product name]. Unfortunately, our supplier's warehouse is out of this item and they cannot confirm when it will be back in stock.

We found a very similar [product type, size, color] from another supplier that is in stock and can ship tomorrow. It costs [amount] dollars [more / less] than your original item.

Would you like us to change your order to this product? Please reply and let us know.

Thank you,
[your name / store name]
```
[L0965, 2024]

#### Procedure: pre-selling a product when a supplier gives an ETA

1. Use the supplier's ETA as the shipping date for the product. (2021) [L0942, 2022]
2. Turn the product's ads back on and let customers know it ships on that date. (2021) [L0942, 2022]
3. Show the shipping date in a clear but not overly loud notice right next to the Add to Cart button. (2021) [L0942, 2022]
4. Once orders come in, place them with the supplier so the goods are ready to ship when stock arrives. (2021) [L0942, 2022]

#### Procedure: backorder strategy with suppliers

1. Request inventory reports showing stock levels. (2023) [L0804, 2023]
2. Identify the top 20% of SKUs by sales. (2023) [L0804, 2023]
3. Find out when the next reorder arrives. (2023) [L0804, 2023]
4. When stock runs low, place a deposit to secure inventory before competitors run out. (2023) [L0804, 2023]

#### Refund and alternative email to a customer who asked for a refund

Use this when the updated ship date is known and the customer has asked for a refund. (2020)

```

If you would still prefer a full refund, just reply to this email and we will process it right away. We can also show you similar products that are in stock and ready to ship now: [links to similar in-stock products].

Please let us know which option you prefer.

Best regards,
[your name]
[store name]
```

```
This product is currently out of stock and is expected back on [date] / has no clear restock date. Here are similar products we have in stock and ready to ship today: [product 1 link], [product 2 link], [product 3 link].
```

#### Confirming a backorder in the order email

- After a purchase of a backordered item, send an order confirmation email to the buyer asking them to confirm they saw the backorder note and accept the estimated ship date. (2024) [L0805, 2024]
- After the order comes in, manually email the customer to confirm the ETA for shipping. (2024) [L0666, 2024]
- If a customer still orders organically, email them after the order to confirm they accept the ETA. (2024) [L0965, 2024]

### Handling payment for backorders

#### Shopify settings without an inventory app

Use this procedure when no inventory app is installed. (2021)

1. Keep a product listed as in stock while it is available or expected back soon, and show an estimated arrival date on its page. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
2. Mark the item out of stock only when its backorder typically lasts a month or more and you do not want new orders. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
3. Set Shopify so checkout preauthorizes the customer's card without charging it. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
4. Capture the payment only after the supplier confirms the item is in stock. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
5. For items with a long lead time, do not capture the payment, which cancels the charge without a refund. (2021) [L0853, 2021]

### Pre-orders and made-to-order products

#### Pre-orders

- Pre-order sales are a workable approach when suppliers are out of stock. Reserve inventory for pre-order customers and set up delivery deals with customers so shipments are not missed. (2020) [L0967, 2020]
- Pre-order buyers should be informed of shipping timing and warehouse status. Warehouse closures in major hubs can delay shipping and require special arrangements with customers. (2020) [L0967, 2020]
- Standard stock-based fashion needs a lot of cash because stock must be bought up front. A pre-order model reduces this cash flow pressure. [L0660, 2019+]
- When a dropship supplier says stock will arrive in about a week, the seller can start running a pre-order or a "high demand, secure yours before stock runs out" message in the meantime. Ask the supplier whether stock is limited per batch or can be replenished continuously, and whether they will hold inventory for the seller. [L0552, 2023]

#### Made-to-order and custom products

- Sell made-to-order products only if they are made fairly close to the customer and customers are told about the lead time before ordering. Presenting the custom production as a benefit works better than presenting it as a delay. (2021) [L0962, 2021]
- Made-to-order furniture that ships after about 10 days was accepted by customers when the page explained that it was custom made locally. Customers were willing to wait for it. (2021) [L0962, 2021]
- Products with a long lead time, such as 16 weeks for made-to-order items, should be marked on the product page right by the add-to-cart button, with the production and delivery estimate. Taking full payment at the time of order is appropriate when the item is custom made. [L1281, 2022]

#### Procedure: made-to-order item with a one-to-two-week production window

1. Put a note beside the add-to-cart button on the product page, quoting the supplier's exact phrasing, such as saying the item is made to order and dispatches within one to two weeks. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
2. Once a buyer places an order, email them by hand to get confirmation that they understand the build time. (2024) [L0965, 2024]
3. After the customer replies with their confirmation, forward the order to the supplier. (2024) [L0965, 2024]

Confirmation email for a made-to-order order:

```
Subject: Confirming your order [order number]

Hi [customer name],

Thank you for your order of [product name]. Just want to confirm that you're aware this product is built to order and ships in [lead time, for example one to two weeks].

Please reply to confirm, and we will send your order to production right away.

Thank you,
[store name]
```
[L0965, 2024]

#### Deciding whether to pre-sell

- If a supplier is out of stock and gives no restock date, do not sell or advertise that supplier's products. If restock is coming soon in a meaningful quantity, pre-selling can make sense; decide supplier by supplier. (2021) [L0968, 2021]
- Only pre-sell a backordered product when the supplier has given a reliable ETA, because customers will cancel or complain if a long backorder is not communicated. (2021) [L0942, 2022]

### Substitutions and replacements

#### Choosing a substitute

- If a supplier is out of stock but the product still shows as available on your store, offer customers compatible alternatives. This can turn orders that would have been lost into sales. Keep the availability status in mind when managing supplier inventory. (2025) [L0837, 2025]
- When an ordered product is out of stock, handle it in this order: 1) Order a similar product as a replacement. 2) If no similar product is available, find new suppliers that offer an alternative product. [L0920, 2018]
- When an ordered product goes out of stock, ship the in-stock items first and send the missing bundle component later. Suppliers usually restock accessories quickly, and customers accept the split if they got the deal. [L1357, 2018]
- Some dropshipping suppliers, when a product runs out, ship a different brand's product without asking the store owner. Check supplier practices early and set rules for substitutions, because this damages the brand and customer trust. [L1315, 2023]
- For an out-of-stock product with no ETA, the replacement offer is the step in the procedure above (see [L0965, 2024]). [L0965, 2024]

#### Procedure: similar in-stock products on the product page

1. Cut paid ad spend for the product page. (2024) [L1115, 2024]
2. Add a note next to the add-to-cart button saying the item is out of stock and showing the ETA if one is known. (2024) [L1115, 2024]
3. Keep the page live so free organic traffic can still arrive. (2024) [L1115, 2024]
4. Below the note, show a section with similar products that are in stock and ship within 24 hours, so organic visitors can still buy. (2024) [L1115, 2024]

### Discontinued products

- When a supplier discontinues a product, keep its product page but mark it as discontinued. Show two or three similar products that are still in stock, and stop running ads to that page. [L1281, 2022]
- Keep discontinued or unavailable products on the website as not orderable, with no ads, so they still pick up organic search traffic. (2020) [L1277, 2020]
- Products that are discontinued by a supplier are handled with the substitution steps above. [L1281, 2022]

#### Case: discontinued model with a phantom stock unit

A store received an order for a discontinued model, and the supplier's inventory system listed a stock unit that did not exist. The store explained the situation to the supplier's representative, who was not responsive at first. The store then renegotiated the price difference, got the old dealer cost, and charged the customer the new price. The store made more profit on that unit than it would have with the correct price. [L1472, 2022]

#### Case: a discontinued product on a government order

A government agency placed a sizable order, but the manufacturer had discontinued the product that same month, so the order could not be fulfilled as planned. Rather than cancel, the seller contacted two manufacturers who had earlier rejected them because they had not been in the industry long enough. Both agreed to fulfill the order. After the customer approved the change, the seller shipped a product of higher quality than the original, kept the customer as a repeat buyer, and added a new supplier with a new catalog. [L1357, 2018]

### Shortages and supplier reliability

- When a supplier runs short of stock, every seller who uses that supplier pushes the same limited inventory, so shift traffic and ad spend to suppliers that still have stock and strengthen those relationships. Suppliers that stay reliable during shortages tend to become the strongest long-term partners. [L0942, 2022]
- When supply is limited, work with the available suppliers, upload the approved products, and optimize them. Circle back to the other suppliers once supply normalizes. (2022) [L0963, 2022]
- If many product pages from one supplier are out of stock across different competitor stores, the supplier may be out of business, and it is worth reconsidering them. A single out-of-stock product is not a warning sign on its own. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
- Overseas dropshipping shipments can take several months to arrive, which damages trust and repeat purchases. Keeping your top 10 best sellers in stock locally, where possible, shortens delivery and reduces buyer hesitation. [L0390, 2019]
- In dropshipping, keep your top 10 best sellers in stock locally, if possible, to offer faster delivery to your best customers. This reduces the long wait that makes buyers hesitate. [L0390, 2019]
- Fitness and hobby niches had the longest supplier lead times around Q4 due to inventory and shipping congestion. Inventory is expected to normalize around Q1 of the following year. [L1112, 2021]
- Chinese suppliers often have a large backlog and slow replies in the weeks after Chinese New Year. Plan ad spend and stock with delays of two to three weeks in mind. [L1173, undated]
- Demand for home gym and workout equipment spiked during the 2020 COVID-19 lockdowns, and popular items sold out quickly. (See also the factory backlog note above.) [L0358, 2020]

### Price changes

- Price changes from suppliers are communicated by email to all retailers with the new prices and effective dates, so you normally do not need to check prices constantly. (2021) [L0834, 2021]
- Suppliers normally send price changes by email with an effective date, and product integrations for pricing or stock are often not available. Track price changes by watching supplier emails and their effective dates. (2025) [L0972, 2025]
- Suppliers usually announce price changes by email to their retailer list, stating the effective date and time and attaching the new price sheet. If a supplier changes prices without notice, ask them to include you in all future price-update emails so you can update prices before they take effect. (2025) [L0837, 2025]
- Most suppliers update pricing about once a year at most. They usually send a price sheet plus emails about new products or price changes, so weekly price checks are not needed. (2025) [L0966, 2025]
- Update product pages only when a supplier reports a change, such as a new size, SKU, variation or price change. Create a new product page for the new item and link to it from the old page. (2026) [L0808, 2026]

#### Procedure: new size, SKU, variation or price change

1. Wait for the supplier to notify you of a new size, SKU, variation, or price change. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
2. Create a new product page for the new item. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
3. Put a link on the old product page pointing to the new one. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
4. Update the old page as needed. (2026) [L0808, 2026]

### Pre-buying inventory

#### Principles and caution

- Do not pre-buy inventory from a supplier just to get a discount. Pre-buy only when your sales volume is high enough that you need to secure stock. (2020) [L0551, 2020]
- Buying inventory in advance only makes sense once you have a bankroll and a reliable sales history for a product. A common approach is to place a deposit before the busiest season such as Q4, while the supplier keeps the stock in its warehouse and continues to drop ship. (2021) [L0968, 2021]
- Pre-buying stock keeps inventory secured while you can still dropship the product. Other options are a third-party fulfillment center, which can get expensive, or your own warehouse, which makes you run a separate fulfillment business. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
- Do not ask a supplier to pre-stock inventory for a new store. Pre-purchasing inventory only makes sense for an established store with years of sales history with that supplier, checked before the Q4 peak season, and only for products where stock is running low. (2024) [L1115, 2024]
- Do not buy inventory to supply a marketplace such as Walmart. If you want to test it, list only a few brands whose suppliers reliably keep stock, instead of the whole catalog. [L1069, 2019]

#### Pre-purchase before the holidays

- Pre-purchase inventory from a supplier you sell consistently when stock runs low, such as before the holidays; the supplier holds it in its warehouse and ships when customers order, avoiding the need for own warehousing. [L0665, 2022]

#### Procedure: pre-purchased inventory for a best-selling product that sells out often

1. Estimate average monthly units sold, for example 20 units a month that mostly sell in the first 10 days. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
2. Ask the supplier to hold a higher monthly quantity in their warehouse, for example 40 units, and ship orders for you. (2026) [L0808, 2026]
3. Ask for a discount for ordering upfront. Suppliers often agree, since bigger orders give them faster cash and bigger manufacturer orders. (2026) [L0808, 2026]

### Q4 and holiday capacity planning

#### Why Q4 is the hardest part

- During Q4 the hardest part is not getting sales but delivering reliably and on time, since buyers are already in a buying mood. Stock availability and shipping time are the key bottlenecks. [L0780, 2024+]
- During the holiday season, do not add products whose delivery before the holiday cannot be guaranteed. The lesson used mid-December as the cutoff for products that would not arrive in time. [L1172, 2023+]

#### Procedure: Q4 logistics with a supplier-based approach

1. Do not jump on products that are already selling strongly without securing supply, because the supply often runs out first. [L0780, 2024+]
2. Limit the number of product variants (e.g., reduce from five colors to two). [L0780, 2024+]
3. Buy a small stock (roughly 50 to 100 pieces) and place it in a warehouse in China. [L0780, 2024+]
4. With guaranteed stock you can predict supply, fulfill orders faster and guarantee shorter shipping times. The same advice applies to Chinese New Year. [L0780, 2024+]

#### Weekly supplier calls and communication

- Schedule weekly Zoom or Skype calls with your supplier during Q4 planning and sales periods to verify fulfillment capacity, confirm inventory levels, and troubleshoot issues in real time. Missed communication during high-volume periods causes supply chain failures, leading to chargebacks and account suspensions. [L0309, 2024+]
- A $500,000-revenue store was lost in one Q4 when the supplier quietly ran out of stock but did not communicate for 2-3 weeks. Daily sales of $3,000-$4,000 continued but no shipments left the warehouse. Customer chargebacks accumulated, payment processor terminated the account, and Facebook shut down the ad account due to complaint volume. [L0309, 2024+]

#### Scaling capacity and event lead times

- Before scaling a product, have the supplier backend ready, including reliable suppliers and stock available to fulfill the orders that ads will generate. [L1469, 2023+]
- Ask the supplier for the maximum number of orders they can handle comfortably per day, and use that number as the scaling threshold. Suppliers often over-promise; set the threshold lower than their stated maximum. [L1363, 2023]
- Ask the supplier how long processing takes for the actual order volume; processing time can rise sharply (in the lesson from about one week to much longer) during high demand. [L1363, 2023]
- Running out of stock during scaling causes angry customers and a fall in revenue. Plan supplier capacity before increasing ad budgets. [L1363, 2023]
- For date-specific events such as Valentine's Day, count the supplier's shipping time, around two weeks in one example. Start ads about four weeks before the event so the product arrives in time and refunds are avoided. [L1469, 2023+]

### Mistakes that cost sales

- Running out of inventory means no sales and no income, so the seller must monitor stock levels closely. [L1153, 2016]
- Running out of stock during scaling causes angry customers and a fall in revenue. [L1363, 2023]
- Marking products as sold out with zero inventory while customers can still buy them can lead Google to suspend a Merchant Center account. [L0853, 2021]
- Some dropshipping suppliers, when a product runs out, ship a different brand's product without asking the store owner. [L1315, 2023]
- A supplier's system can show stock that does not exist, so do not rely on the inventory feed alone for an order. [L1472, 2022]
