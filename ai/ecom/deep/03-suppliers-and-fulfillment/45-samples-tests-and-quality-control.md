### Samples, tests and quality control

#### Ordering a sample

- Use a fast but economical shipping method when ordering a sample, not an expensive express courier. A sample only needs to arrive quickly enough to test the product. [L0552, 2023]
- Ask the agent or supplier to order one sample, have them film the item and take close-up shots of stitching and details, then decide if quality is good enough by your own standards. If yes, start selling and see whether demand exists. Ask the warehouse to take photos when the product arrives. [L0552, 2023]
- Expect to pay for samples, although a small discount may be offered. Some suppliers may never send them, so do not rely on a promised free sample. [L0694, 2024]

#### Sample check procedure

Procedure for checking a supplier sample before committing:

1. Ask for a photo or video of the sample, including close-up shots of the material and a stretch test for stretchable fabric.
2. If the product needs a logo, have the logo applied to the sample so you can check its placement and quality.
3. Have the sample shipped to yourself so you can inspect the product in person.
4. Place a test order through your own store, so you can see the real packaging, shipping and how the parcel arrives at the customer.
5. Expect to pay for samples, although a small discount may be offered; some suppliers may never send them, so do not rely on a promised free sample.

[L0694, 2024]

#### Inspecting your own first batch

Procedure for inspecting a first batch:

1. Ship your first smaller batch directly to yourself, via Amazon Partnered Carrier, typically about $50.
2. Inspect the product quality, packaging and branding.
3. If satisfactory, reship to Amazon, another about $50.

Alternatively, use a third-party inspection service in China or the United States to inspect before forwarding to Amazon. This shifts quality control risk to the service provider.

[L0228, 2015]

#### Substitutions and mismatches

- A substitute product, such as a printed item shipped instead of the woven product that was ordered, causes quality complaints and refunds. Confirm the exact material and construction before ordering, and check what arrives instead of trusting a video call alone. [L0694, 2024]
- A mismatch in product, color, material or country can lead to refunds and closing the store. Make sure the supplier actually ships the product you sell, and check quality before going live. [L0780, 2024+]

#### Ongoing quality control

- Start with tech packs and spec sheets, then run quality checks continuously. Get photos or videos of production regularly. [L1225, 2025]
- Procedure for production runs:
  1. Write a contract with clauses that hold the supplier accountable for the approved sample.
  2. Have the first lot of every production run sent for inspection.
  3. Request weekly photos or videos of the product during production.
  4. Check that each batch matches the approved sample.

  [L1225, 2025]
- Suppliers can change quality over repeat runs, for example by using less plastic in molds, substituting ingredients or prioritizing other customers. Without checks on every run, a brand may only notice after the damage is done. [L1225, 2025]
- Holding stock at a supplier warehouse in China means you can see the product before shipping it out. Pure dropshipping only pushes the product to another supplier. This visibility matters for quality control. [L0780, 2024+]

#### Tech packs

A tech pack is the key document for suppliers and manufacturers. It can be downloaded as a template, hired out to be made from scratch, or made yourself in Illustrator or Photoshop. It must include:

1. Digital sketch of the garment
2. Construction details and trims
3. Measurements
4. Fabrics
5. Size
6. Look inspiration
7. Colours
8. Artwork scales

[L0779, 2023+]

Essentials needed to run a clothing brand from the start:

1. A label maker for product labels.
2. Poly sender bags for shipping.
3. Home storage or a warehouse space to keep stock.

[L0779, 2023+]

### Payment terms and order risk

- Negotiate payment terms with factories as early as possible. Without them, the business has to fund every order upfront, which quickly strains cash flow when money is scarce. [L0790, 2017]
- Payment terms procedure:
  1. Use bank wire transfer, not Western Union, for secure payment.
  2. Offer a 30% deposit upfront and 70% on completion.
  3. Request photographic proof of your manufactured product with branding before releasing the final 70% payment.
  4. Verify the supplier's legitimacy through its verified badges.

  Check that the verified badges are Trade Assurance, Gold Supplier or Assessed Supplier. These indicate third-party inspections and legitimacy. [L0228, 2015]
- Negotiate a smaller initial order, for example 200-250 units, for a new supplier as a test batch to verify quality and reliability. [L0228, 2015]
- Once Amazon pays you after two weeks of sales, use that revenue to fund the 70% balance on a reorder while the supplier is still building the first batch. Maintain short lead times of 7-14 days so reorders arrive before inventory depletes. [L0228, 2015]

### Lead times and production planning

#### Lead time reference

| Source context | Lead time | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred lead times | 7, 14 or 30 days | [L0228, 2015] |
| Longer lead times (45-60 days) | Delay first inventory arrival; make quick reorders difficult during low stock or peak seasons | [L0228, 2015] |
| Supplier lead times, general guidance | Around one month; measure your own, since they differ by origin (US or China) and freight method | [L1154, 2021-22] |
| Bulk order from an Alibaba manufacturer shipped by sea to your own address | About 20-30 days to arrive | [L1217, 2023+] |
| Handmade sunglasses, one pair | About 90 days to produce | [L0790, 2017] |

- Lead time is the duration from payment to product completion. Longer lead times delay your first inventory arrival and make it difficult to reorder quickly when inventory runs low, especially during peak seasons. [L0228, 2015]
- Long production lead times must be included in inventory and promotion planning. [L0790, 2017]
- Supplier lead times can be around one month, so reorder early enough that stock never runs out. Lead times differ depending on whether goods come from the US or China and on the freight method. Measure your own lead times. ** [L1154, 2021-22]
- Longer supplier lead times of 6-8+ weeks reduce competition, because most sellers avoid the wait. Accepting longer lead times is a competitive advantage that makes ranking and dominating the listing easier. [L0227, 2014]
- Bulk stock should be ordered when there is still enough time to cover the production and transit. Plan the next order so the stock does not run out. [L1153, 2016]

#### Inventory in transit

- Inventory is in the pipeline at several stages at once: at the manufacturer, at the freight origin ready for shipping, on the vessel, at customs, and in your warehouse. Even at a steady sales rate, you must have several shipments ordered ahead to avoid stockouts. ** [L1154, 2021-22]

### Sea and air freight

#### Choosing the mode

- Air freight is faster but more expensive. Sea freight is cheaper but takes 30-45 days. Use air freight for initial orders and smaller shipments with short lead times. [L0228, 2015]
- Switch to sea freight only when ordering large quantities of 5,000-10,000+ units lasting 1-6 weeks. At that volume the total shipping cost becomes economical despite the longer transit time. [L0228, 2015]
- Shipping time from China by sea freight is about 14 days to California, plus about one more week to reach Amazon's warehouses. Air freight from China can arrive within three to five days. ** [L1153, 2016]
- Compare the total landed cost, including shipping and time, not only the unit price. A US supplier can reduce shipping cost and lead time, and products can be labeled Made in the USA, which helps on Amazon. ** [L1153, 2016]
- Amazon's paid carrier service can collect goods from a US supplier or printer and deliver them to Amazon warehouses. The seller reported this is cheap enough to beat sea freight costs for US-made products. ** [L1153, 2016]

#### Landed cost by mode (case)

- The landed cost of one blanket was about 20 to 25 USD by air freight. It fell to about 7 to 10 USD with sea freight at large order volumes. [L0492, 2023+]
- Order the next season's stock early and ship it by sea to lower the per-unit cost. For a winter season, order in April and ship by sea. [L0492, 2023+]
- Supplier production paused over Chinese New Year. After the pause, plan for 7 to 15 business days of shipping from the supplier to the customer. [L0492, 2023+]

#### Consolidated shipping to a warehouse

- Shipping from a supplier to a warehouse in China costs about $0.50 per piece for a single order. It drops to about $0.10 per product when local domestic shipping is consolidated across around 10 orders. Stock shipped in one consolidated batch therefore has a much lower per-unit logistics cost. [L0780, 2024+]

#### Sea freight and suppliers in China

- Sea freight from a supplier with a direct address takes about 20-30 days. For a fast-moving product, order in advance and keep stock on hand. [L1217, 2023+]
- Freight on board (FOB) terms for goods from China: map the full route and cost every stage before setting prices.
  1. Freight on board at the origin port, where the goods are loaded onto the vessel.
  2. Ocean transport to the destination.
  3. Customs clearance.
  4. Transport from the port to your warehouse.
  5. Preparation for shipping to customers.

  Add the costs of all these stages, plus the outbound shipping cost to the customer, into your product price. ** [L1154, 2021-22]

### Custom packaging, branding and inserts

#### When to invest

- Do not invest in custom packaging at launch. Wait until a product has produced good results for one to two weeks, then order a small run of about 100-200 pieces. [L0692, 2023]
- Supplier packaging is often low quality. Research the right box type for each product and invest in a sturdier box that protects the product during shipping and improves the unboxing experience. [L0403, undated]
- Many fulfillment companies offer personalized packaging, inserts and notes such as thank-you cards. These improve the customer experience and support a transition from a generic dropshipping store to a recognized brand. [L0760, undated]

#### Custom boxes

- Custom boxes from local or Chinese box makers can cost from about $0.50 per box. Ordering at least 50 to 100 units is needed to get bulk pricing. [L0403, undated]
- Order at least 50 to 100 units of custom packaging to get good bulk prices. [L0403, undated]
- Use supplier-provided or local box makers if the dropshipping supplier cannot produce custom packaging. [L0403, undated]
- Commissioning a custom box design:
  1. First ask the supplier for the exact box dimensions.
  2. Give the same box size to the freelance designer on Fiverr or Upwork, together with the logo, the website link and the brand colors.
  3. The designer produces the box design based on this brief.

  [L0403, undated]
- Fiverr and Upwork are used to commission design work for thank-you cards, tags and custom box designs. [L0403, undated]

#### Thank-you cards and tags

- Thank-you cards and tags usually come from the dropshipping supplier, so the store owner needs no design skills to add them. If the supplier does not offer them, they can be outsourced to a freelancer on Fiverr for under $5 to about $20. [L0403, undated]

#### Custom designs and product changes

- Custom branding and logos on hoodies typically require a minimum order of 500 pieces. [L1212, undated]
- Customization of a product design can usually be done by a supplier if the customer provides their own design. Changes in cut or direction, such as a hoodie that opens differently, may require a different supplier. [L1212, undated]
- Custom designs require a minimum order quantity of 500 pieces from the supplier. Plan for that volume before offering custom designs. [L0492, 2023+]
- Logo product customization usually requires a minimum order quantity of about 500 to 1,000 units, though the supplier may be able to reduce it to around 200 units. [L0403, undated]

#### Branding before a full packaging order

Procedure for branding a product before a full packaging order:

1. Ask the supplier for a sample with the packaging and a small logo, if possible.
2. Have the sample shipped to you.
3. Create product photos with it in a clean setting, such as a rented space or a simple background.
4. Edit the photos lightly in Canva to create a branded look.

[L0692, 2023]

#### Branding supplier

- Best Dealer (bestdealer.com) was the main supplier for the blanket stock. It also produced custom packaging and bags with branding from China. Its owner recommended it for both dropshipping and buying stock. [L0492, 2023+]
- Best Dealer is a Chinese supplier with strong service, fast shipping and good packaging. It is well suited for building a brand with bulk orders. A shipment of 25 large packages was delivered from China to Switzerland within 10 days. [L1184, 2023+]

### Inventory pipeline and stock levels

#### Staging stock

- Stock up a little before the holiday, because the warehouse is closed only for about three days but has limited capacity. [L0780, 2024+]
- Start with bulk stock from the supplier while testing. Switch to a 3PL warehouse once consistent orders have run for two to three weeks or more. A 3PL is an investment and carries risk, so it fits best with predictable sales. [L0871, 2021+]
- Consider holding stock in a local market only once a product brings consistent orders, for example at least 50 to 100 orders per day for a consecutive period of 10 days. Before that, buying a small stock locally or in China, around 10 pieces, keeps risk low and shortens delivery to about three days. [L0780, 2024+]
- Stock levels should be planned around the time it takes for inventory to move through each stage. Stock can be ordered ahead to cover the next period. [L1154, 2021-22]

#### Phased approach to moving stock

Phased approach to moving stock:

1. When entering a new region, fulfill directly from China. Buy customized goods, store them in China and ship from there.
2. When a product sells strongly in one country, move at least the best sellers to a warehouse close to the customers, for example the UK for UK customers, Germany for German customers.
3. For widespread demand across Europe, keep some stock in China, because shipping from China to a country such as Sweden can be cheaper than shipping from a neighboring country.
4. Scale later once the first country is serviced well.

[L0780, 2024+]

- Fulfil from China when margins are thin, because shipping a product to a local warehouse can eat up profit. Move fulfillment closer to customers when the product is high-ticket and margins are large enough to afford local fulfillment. [L0780, 2024+]
- Shorter delivery times improve the customer experience, so placing stock in a warehouse close to the buyers is worth doing once a product sells consistently. Wait until demand is proven before moving stock overseas. [L0780, 2024+]
- Moving stock to a US warehouse could cut shipping times by three to four days or more. It means shipping large containers that can be lost with the money inside. Only move stock once the store is stable and the extra speed justifies the container risk. [L0694, 2024]
- For faster shipping in the US, products should be stored in US warehouses. The products themselves do not need to be manufactured in the US. ** [L0804, 2023]
- Bulk stock held in China can help avoid delays around Chinese holidays. Keep the stock ready there so orders can still ship. [L1108, 2023]

### Chinese New Year planning

#### Timing

- Chinese New Year typically closes factories around 1 February. Warehouses keep running but also close for a few days. Stock is needed before the closure, and suppliers should be contacted early because they stop at different times. [L0349, 2024]
- Chinese New Year in the year of the lesson started around 20 January. Suppliers were barely available for about two weeks around it, while the warehouse itself was closed only for about three days. Plan stock levels and ad pauses around that roughly two-week window. [L0780, 2024+]
- During Chinese New Year, factories in China close for roughly 20 days to one month, and the exact end date differs by factory. Ask your supplier for it. For the case discussed, the holiday ended around the 17th or 18th of the month, and factories needed one to two weeks after that to resume normal operations. [L0694, 2024]

#### Before the holiday

- Communicate with your supplier about Chinese New Year closure dates. If you plan to restock after Q4, arrange shipment to a US-based warehouse instead of waiting for inventory to arrive after New Year. Confirm whether their warehouse will operate during the holiday period. [L0309, 2024+]
- Before Chinese New Year, stock a winning product in advance, because stocked orders still ship during the holiday. Without stock, orders take about a month, and the advice is to wait until the holiday ends. [L1161, 2024+]
- Plan bulk orders ahead of predictable slow-supply periods such as Chinese New Year. [L1017, undated]
- Stock up a little before the holiday, because the warehouse is closed only for about three days but has limited capacity. [L0780, 2024+]

#### During the holiday

- Stop running ads for products you cannot ship, because during the factory closure you cannot fulfil orders. If you have no stock, a pause of at least one week is advised around the holiday. [L0694, 2024]
- Slow down or turn off ads during the roughly two-week period around New Year when suppliers are barely available. [L0780, 2024+]
- Reduce product variants and keep only the two best-sellers, marking others as sold out without removing them from the site. [L0780, 2024+]
- During the factory closure, suppliers reply slowly, so ask questions early. Use the time to prepare creatives, optimise the website, improve existing products and research more products. [L0694, 2024]
- Use the quiet period to catch up on orders and negotiate a better price, because quantities per item are higher. [L0780, 2024+]
- Watch for suppliers who offer bulk orders as a fix for Chinese New Year without checking whether stock will be fulfilled during the holiday. The bulk order can become a money trap if workers are away. [L0780, 2024+]

### Warehousing, fulfillment and 3PL transition

#### Stages of the transition

- Start with bulk stock from the supplier while testing. Switch to a 3PL warehouse once consistent orders have run for two to three weeks or more. [L0871, 2021+]
- At $100k-500k per month, if a fulfillment agent is still used, the store should build its brand and move products into a US 3PL. This protects customer relationships, brand loyalty and LTV (lifetime value). [L1129, undated]
- For single-country sales, a fulfillment service can keep stock in a warehouse in that country. The owner can also store and ship stock locally if they live in the target country. [L1108, 2023]
- For stock kept in a warehouse rather than pure dropshipping, Daily Fulfill was named as a supplier another seller used and rated as solid. The seller pointed to a supplier that requires bulk orders. [L1127, 2024]
- Honest Fulfillment is a 3PL cited for the best per-order shipping rates in the industry. It requires at least 1,000 units per month for each SKU. Verify the exact company name before contacting it. [L1217, 2023+]

#### Warehouse selection and storage

- Physical inventory needs a storage solution: either your own warehouse or a third-party logistics (3PL) provider, which are easy to find. Where possible, choose a storage location in the US Midwest, or one facility on each coast. ** [L1154, 2021-22]
- With a white-label product, ask the manufacturer whether it will store the inventory and dropship orders directly to customers. Many manufacturers offer this. Raising it early can remove the need for your own warehouse. ** [L1154, 2021-22]
- Fulfillment center storage can be arranged as a retainer fee, a per-unit fee or free, negotiable. [L0324, 2025+]
- Running your own warehouse gave one brand control over fulfillment at scale. The team included about 20 warehouse staff alongside about 22 office staff, including customer service. [L0790, 2017]
- When selling on local marketplaces such as Amazon or eBay, buy stock in larger bulk and hold a baseline of goods in a local warehouse, because the marketplace needs fast local delivery. Dropshipping from China alone usually cannot meet that delivery standard. [L0780, 2024+]

#### Packaging when leaving Amazon

- Amazon branded shipping boxes were used for orders shipped from Amazon in the past. Amazon later offered blank-box shipping as an alternative. When selling outside Amazon, move away from Amazon-branded packaging as soon as possible. ** [L1154, 2021-22]

### Tariffs and country of manufacture

- Manufacturing in the US can avoid import taxes that change often. This matters for brands whose 3PL and customers are in the US. Compare the total landed cost, including tariffs, before choosing a country. [L1225, 2025]
- Not every product category suits American manufacturing. Check for a US manufacturer first, but accept a Chinese one for some products. ** [L1155, 2021-22]

### Unsold MOQ stock

- For products bought under a supplier minimum order quantity, ship orders quickly. Slow shipping drives chargebacks and RDR refunds. This applies especially to products sold on a custom brand. [L0351, 2026]
- Options for an MOQ product that is not selling well:
  1. White-hand method: refund all customers who ordered the product.
  2. Black-hand method: ask the supplier to send a similar product with a different book cover, and keep selling the same product under the new cover.

  [L0351, 2026]
- The black-hand method has downsides. Customers may complain, the total gross revenue and net margin are still kept, and chargebacks can still occur because customers say the product was not as described. It is worth doing when the order value is low, since profit is still profit. [L0351, 2026]
- Use the black-hand method only when the product suddenly stops selling and the proof of concept no longer holds, and not frequently. It is mostly a testing tool. [L0351, 2026]
