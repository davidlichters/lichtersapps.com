### Supplier management SOPs

#### Disagreements and unfair treatment

- The supplier relationship is central to the business, so every contact must be handled with care and professionalism. Team members should never escalate or argue with suppliers on their own, because a designated manager is responsible for resolving conflicts. [L1505, undated]
- Disagreement with a supplier on any subject is within bounds. Keep your composure, bring the discussion to a close and reach out to the designated manager for direction and support. [L1505, undated]

Procedure for a disagreement with a supplier:

1. Stay calm. [L1505, undated]
2. End the conversation. [L1505, undated]
3. Contact the designated manager for guidance and support. [L1505, undated]

Procedure for unfair or inappropriate treatment by a supplier:

1. Inform the designated manager immediately so the situation can be addressed. [L1505, undated]
