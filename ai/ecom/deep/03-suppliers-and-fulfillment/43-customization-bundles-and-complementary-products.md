### Customization, bundles and complementary products

**Complementary product (procedure)**

1. Search for accessories in the product's niche (for example fitness, jewelry, hair or nails accessories). [L1345, 2021+]
2. Apply the price filter with a minimum of $0.01 and a maximum of $4. [L1345, 2021+]
3. Pick an item that is light and adds little to shipping cost, and that complements the main product. [L1345, 2021+]
4. Add it to the store as a bundle component. [L1345, 2021+]

- Personalized products (for example engraved names) can be hard through CJ, which may require a developer to connect its API documentation. Print-on-demand providers such as Printful usually connect more easily to Shopify. [L1363, 2023]

### Testing products

- Explore products on CJ Dropshipping and AliExpress when TikTok search yields no good options. Buy the product you find promising and test it by creating videos about it. Use the test results to decide whether to continue promoting the product. [L1384, 2022]
- Order and test the product yourself before promoting it with video or ads. [L1444, 2022]
- Ordering a single sample to check quality and allowing extra time for arrival is a reasonable precaution. [L0819, 2020+]
- Before running ads, send the product to a supplier such as CJ to check whether it can be sourced. [L1171, 2024+]
- Ordering a sample is the surer check on AliExpress, but takes time and is optional. [L1436, undated]

#### Further details

- Sourcing with a fulfillment company means they find the best factory for the product, negotiate with it, obtain samples and verify quality. After that, they can fulfill future orders from that factory. [L0685, undated]

- 1) Contact each supplier before the first sale. 2) Show them the product you want to sell. 3) Use their customer support to ask what information they need for customization. 4) Provide that information clearly and completely. [L1108, 2023]

- CJ Dropshipping is a supplier and fulfillment platform used to place and process the orders received by the store. [L1432, undated]
