### Customs, Duties and Cross-Border Shipments

- When a product is shipped from stock in the same country, there are no extra import fees for the customer. Customs clearance only matters for cross-border shipments. [L1281, 2022]
- For cross-border shipments, carriers such as FedEx, UPS and DHL usually handle customs clearance and contact the customer when duties must be paid. In practice the seller typically pays those fees. Some carriers were not verified for this. [L1281, 2022]
- Delivery times can be affected by customs, weather and other factors outside the store's control. Naming these in the policy sets expectations and reduces complaints about delays. [L1250, 2026]
- For international orders where product dimensions vary widely, the store can give shipping prices on request rather than publishing one rate. The customer confirms the quote before the order and payment are taken through a secure online payment gateway. This approach suits low-volume stores that cannot yet predict shipping cost per destination. [L1264, 2021-22]
- Tell customers that international delivery can take longer than the stated estimate and that you only ship to selected countries. Customers should also receive a tracking number by email once the order ships. [L1250, 2026]
- Customers pay customs fees, taxes and import duties on international orders, and the policy should state this. [L1327, 2026]

### Shipping Policy and Customer-Facing Text

- Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on the customer's location and the shipping option chosen. The policy should say this instead of listing fixed prices that may not match the checkout. [L1250, 2026]
- Shipping policy template (replace bracketed placeholders; add the free shipping line only if margins and supplier costs cover it):

```
Thanks for buying from [Your Store Name]. We aim to get your order to you fast and reliably. Details follow.

1. Processing time
- Orders are prepared within [X-X] business days, with weekends and public holidays not counted.
- A confirmation email with tracking details arrives once the parcel has left us.

2. Shipping rates
- Checkout works out the cost from your address and the delivery method you choose.
- Worldwide delivery is free. [Keep this line only if your margins and supplier costs cover it.]

3. Estimated delivery
- Once processed, orders usually reach you within [X-X] business days.
- Transit can vary with location and outside factors we can't control, like customs clearance and weather.

4. International shipping
- We deliver to a limited list of countries. Overseas transit may be slower than the estimate given.
- When your order dispatches, a tracking email follows so you can watch its movement.

5. Shipping issues
- For any delivery problem, contact [your contact email]. Our team will help with tracking and make sure the package gets to you as planned.
```

Welcome! At {Store name} we are committed to getting your orders to you quickly and efficiently. Please read our shipping policy carefully to understand how we ship.

Shipping Methods and Delivery Times
We offer several shipping options to suit your needs:
- Standard Shipping: delivery within 14 to 21 business days.
- Express Shipping: delivery within 7 to 10 business days.
Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on your location and external factors. [Adjust these windows to match the delivery times set in Settings > Shipping and delivery. Order Processing Time
Orders are processed within 14 business days.
Orders placed after 12 PM PST, or on weekends or holidays, will be processed on the next business day.

International Shipping
We ship worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times differ by destination.
You are responsible for any customs fees, taxes or import duties.

Order Tracking
Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email so you can follow your package.

Lost or Damaged Packages
If your package is lost or arrives damaged, contact our customer service team at {Business email} within 7 days of the delivery date for help.

Incorrect Address
Please make sure your shipping address is correct before you place your order. We are not responsible for packages delivered to an address the customer provided incorrectly.
