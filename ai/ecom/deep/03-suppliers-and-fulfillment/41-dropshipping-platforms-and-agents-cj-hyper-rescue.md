## Dropshipping Platforms and Agents (CJ, Hyper Rescue, Zendrop, AliExpress)

### Platform roles and fit

- one source recommends a fixed sourcing check order: (1) AliExpress, (2) CJ (Global Sources), (3) Best Dealer. [L0209, 2023]
- A 2021 one source source says not to source from AliExpress at all and to go direct to suppliers that stock products in the region you sell to, because this builds better supplier relationships and dropship approval. [L0270, 2021] The two positions conflict. The one source order is the testing sequence, and the 2021 advice is the long-term direction.
- CJ Dropshipping is the default starting supplier for beginners, because it is the easiest to handle and the fastest to learn. Other suppliers can be explored once sourcing is understood, for better prices and shipping times. [L1108, 2023]
- Start with CJ Dropshipping for test products, then compare the others on offer, shipping times and convenience once sales grow. [L1171, 2024+]
- Alternatives to CJ and Hyper Rescue are Zendrop and USA Drop, and mentors recommend different starting suppliers. Choose the one that fits your target markets and product types. [L1172, 2023+]
- CJ Dropshipping and HyperSQ are suggested as the first fulfillment and sourcing tools for a beginner dropshipping store. CJ's sourcing price is the basis for calculating the selling price. [L1172, 2023+]
- The default supplier suggested for beginners is CJ Dropshipping. Zendrop, HyperSQ or BestDealer can be used if they offer better shipping times and a lower price. [L1219, undated]
- Common dropshipping suppliers for testing and scaling include CJ Dropshipping, HyperSKU, Zendrop, Best Dealer and AutoDS. [L1171, 2024+]
- Supplier and dropshipping platforms named in the lesson include CG dropshipping, Hyper SQ, Source Inbox and Zendrop. Each has its own app that lets the store owner send order data to the supplier from a laptop or desktop. [L0760, undated]
- Name spelling varies between units. HyperSQ is rendered for HyperSKU in the lesson, and the same company also appears as Hypersku and Hyper SKU. [L1171, 2024+; L0552, 2023; L1017, undated]
- Dropshipping suppliers can handle quality checks, packing and direct shipping, so the seller does not need to meet manufacturers or handle goods physically. [L0760, undated]
- A fulfillment agent is necessary in dropshipping because it sources products directly from the factory and charges a small fee per order. This avoids the long delivery delays typical of AliExpress. [L1129, undated]
- Fulfillment companies such as CJ or Hyper Rescue are a shared-support model. They suit beginners, and at scale dedicated support with real-time updates, better prices and customization becomes more valuable. [L0685, undated]
- Dropshipping suppliers are not perfect. Their stock and return handling are often weak. Plan to move to private agents or your own warehouse once sales are high enough, and do not expect perfect service from a dropshipping supplier. [L1315, 2023]
- Dropshipping suppliers are cheaper to start with and slower to ship. Private agents tend to be cheaper with faster shipping once the store has volume. [L1315, 2023]
- Choose the supplier per product, because AliExpress and CJ Dropshipping are often close in quality and the better one can change by product. If Zendrop has the product at the same or lower price, Zendrop may be faster. If CJ offers more variety and better reviews, CJ is the better choice. [L0657, undated]
- Dropshipping platform suppliers may not be good for the long term, and service depends on the product. If a product has no good manufacturer, switch to an agent or a better supplier. [L1440, 2021+]
- Agents are third parties between sellers and direct factories. They have contacts with other agents and factories and can check whether a specific book is in stock in various locations, including provinces in China. [L0560, 2026]
- When a product is not sold on AliExpress or similar platforms, a sourcing agent in China can supply it. [L1184, 2023+]

### Checking sourcing viability before committing

- Before committing to a product, check manually whether it is available on AliExpress to confirm sourcing viability. [L0209, 2023]
- Confirm that a supplier actually sources a product before building an ad or store around it. A sourcing agent may not carry marketplace items, so check availability with the supplier first. [L0524, 2023]
- Before ordering a product for testing, check whether it is available on a platform like Amazon for faster shipping and variation options. Direct orders from CJ Dropshipping were described as unreliable, with long delays and products not matching expectations. Ordering a single sample to check quality and allowing extra time for arrival is a reasonable precaution. [L0819, 2020+]
- Before running ads, send the product to a supplier such as CJ to check whether it can be sourced. Ask the supplier whether it can be sold as a liquid, since liquids can have shipping restrictions. [L1171, 2024+]
- Products on AliExpress can be found by searching the product name with the key words, which helps confirm sourcing options. [L1176, 2023]
- CJ Dropshipping sourcing request: it is free and easy to open, and it returns the supplier's actual price for a product. It is used to replace AliExpress estimates with accurate cost data. [L0693, 2024]
- If CJ does not offer a product, contact several other suppliers to request quotes. Compare them by how fast they reply and how good their English is. A reply that takes three to four days is a bad sign. [L0361, 2024]
- Choose a supplier by comparing product price, shipping time and service experience, and keep more than one supplier available. In the lesson's examples, CJ Dropshipping, Hyper SKU and Best Dealer were used alongside each other. A cheaper supplier is fine if it wins on these criteria. [L1017, undated]
- Some AliExpress suppliers operate several shops and can combine different products into one parcel when they are ordered together. The lesson's operator used this so multi-item orders look like they come from one warehouse. Before listing many products from such a supplier, confirm how it consolidates shipments. [L1131, 2018+]

### Quality verification

**Quality check before scaling a product (procedure)**

1. On AliExpress, look for a listing with a rating of 4.6 or higher and at least 50 reviews. [L0335, 2023]
2. If a listing has fewer than 50 reviews, treat it as doubtful and look for another listing of the same product. [L0335, 2023]
3. On Amazon, look for a rating of 4 or higher. [L0335, 2023]
4. Order one sample from the supplier before you scale. [L0335, 2023]

**AliExpress quality check (procedure)**

1. Search for the product term. [L1183, undated]
2. Find a listing with at least 4.6 star rating and at least 100 orders. [L1183, undated]
3. Avoid relying on listings with high order counts but a rating below 4.6. [L1183, undated]
4. Record the AliExpress average rating in a column of the research sheet and use it to rank products. [L1183, undated]

**Threshold conflicts**

| Check | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Rating and review count | 4.6 or higher and at least 50 reviews; more reviews are better | [L0335, 2023; L1436, undated] |
| Rating and order count | 4.6 or higher and at least 100 orders | [L1183, undated] |
| Sold count and review count | Compare both; several hundred sales and reviews are more credible than about 16 sales | [L1444, 2022] |
| Amazon rating | 4 or higher | [L0335, 2023] |

- The review-count rule (50) is stated for AliExpress listings in one source and the order-count rule (100) in another. The sources do not say which applies in which case. [L0335, 2023; L1436, undated; L1183, undated]
- Several listings of the same product at 4.6 or higher and 50 or more reviews suggest acceptable quality. Ordering a sample is the surer check but takes time and is optional. [L1436, undated]
- The same product can have several manufacturers, so one listing's rating does not describe the product as a whole. A listing rated 3.8 while another is rated 4.6 or higher suggests a weaker version of the product. [L0335, 2023]
- Compare the sold count and review count of AliExpress listings before choosing a supplier. A listing with several hundred sales and reviews is more credible than one with only about 16 sales. [L1444, 2022]
- Order and test the product yourself before promoting it with video or ads. If the product does not work as the content shows, buyers and commenters will find out and conversion will fall. [L1444, 2022]
- Check supplier reviews on AliExpress with care, because many suppliers there use fake reviews. Cross-check the product on Amazon, eBay or other marketplaces where similar products are sold. Read the negative reviews, since some are unreliable. For a final check, order the product yourself and judge its quality. [L1215, 2023+]
- Ask an agent for videos and photos of each product part to check quality before you commit. Strong agents also work with the best suppliers and carry out quality testing. [L1217, 2023+]

### Choosing a fulfillment partner

**Beginner recommendation**

- For beginners, the recommended fulfillment apps are CJ Dropshipping or Hyper Rescue, because they are more reliable and offer live agent support for refunds, returns and damaged packages. [L0635, undated]
- Automation-only apps such as AutoDS lack real after-sales support. [L0635, undated]
- Choose a fulfillment app with live agent support rather than a pure automation app, because refund, return and damaged-package cases are resolved through the supplier's agents inside the app. [L0635, undated]
- CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue both offer live agent support inside their apps for refunds, returns and damaged packages. CJ Dropshipping can also provide personal support once a store reaches good order volumes. [L0635, undated]
- Start with one fulfillment provider such as HyperSQ. Switch to CJ Dropshipping or Zendrop if service quality is poor. The right provider depends on the product and on the support agent assigned to you. [L0363, 2022+]
- Request quotes from three or four fulfillment companies before choosing one. Quotes typically arrive within 1-2 business days. Compare prices and the assigned support contact, not just the price per item. [L0363, 2022+]
- A supplier typically assigns an agent to the seller, who can check orders, fulfillment problems and orders lost in transit. Using a dedicated agent contact is a way to resolve supplier issues quickly. [L0678, 2023]
- If a supplier's website gives only one support contact, post the question in the community chat and ask a mentor directly for their experience. Mentors often respond faster this way than through the supplier's support. [L1017, undated]
- Choosing a supplier by product is a valid approach, and sources differ on the fit of each platform (see the table below). [L0657, undated; L1172, 2023+]

**Reported experience with named suppliers**

- Longer shipping times can still happen for a few orders even with good suppliers. [L0335, 2023]
- Choose a supplier with reliable delivery before you scale ad spend. [L1127, 2024]
- Choose the supplier that ships as fast or faster. If an AliExpress supplier ships as quickly as CJ, use the AliExpress supplier instead. [L1444, 2022]

**Other fulfillment companies**

- Zendrop and Source Inbox are fulfillment companies in the same category as CJ Dropshipping and Hyper Rescue. On platforms like these, the seller usually has to provide an AliExpress product link so the supplier can source the item. [L0635, undated]
- Source Inbox offers live agents. [L1217, 2023+]
- Other dropshipping fulfillment suppliers mentioned as alternatives to CJ Dropshipping are Best Dealer, Hyper Rescue and Zendrop, and AliExpress was mentioned as an option. [L0678, 2023]
- Dropshipping suppliers named in the course include CJ, Hypersku, Zendrop and USA Drop. Manufacturers from outside the dropship model require paying a minimum order quantity (MOQ) upfront, so they do not fit a pure dropship model. [L0552, 2023]
- Best Dealer, Zendrop and CJ were compared on price and shipping times across suppliers before one was chosen. [L1108, 2023]

### AliExpress: role and limits

- Older advice, 2019: AliExpress is not recommended as a long-term model because delivery takes too long, product quality varies, returns are hard to handle, chargebacks are common and prices cannot be controlled. Such a model can make money in the short term but is unstable for building a lasting store. [L0357, 2019]
- Older advice, 2021: AliExpress as a supplier source brings slow shipping, inconsistent quality, mostly low-cost goods, difficult returns and no brand loyalty. It does not build a lasting business. [L0469, 2021]
- AliExpress can be used only as a short-term supplier option. Move to better suppliers as soon as possible. [L0361, 2024]
- Avoid AliExpress as a direct fulfillment source because its shipping times are very long and its support is unreliable. Use a dedicated fulfillment company that connects to the store instead. [L0685, undated]
- Sourcing from AliExpress requires a lot of manual work and gives little customer support. When a product is poor quality, AliExpress refunds are less reliable than those from dedicated dropshipping suppliers. [L1171, 2024+]
- A cheap product sourced from AliExpress with slow shipping leads to angry customers and poor reviews. Do not copy a competitor who sells this way if you want a lasting brand, and check shipping times before you commit to a supplier. [L1176, 2023]
- Realistic AliExpress shipping was about 14 to 15 days in the session, which was considered acceptable. Delivery times of 4 to 6 weeks are a serious problem. [L1108, 2023]
- Direct AliExpress orders could take up to about two months. [L1428, undated]
- Shein can potentially be used as a fulfillment source for dropshipped orders, especially for audiences on Facebook who are less aware of Shein. This was not yet tested by the speaker and needs to be verified before use. [L1439, 2024+]
- Before reselling Shein products in a dropshipping store and ordering to varied addresses, contact Shein support first and explain the plan. Reselling without this step carries the risk of the account being banned. [L1439, 2024+]
- A complete AliExpress rule set is in the one source sources, which prefer direct suppliers over AliExpress. [L0270, 2021; L0469, 2021]

### Importing products into Shopify

- AutoDS can import products from AliExpress and from CJ Dropshipping into a Shopify store. [L0363, 2022+]
- Alternatively, a fulfillment company's own Shopify app, such as HyperSQ or CJ Dropshipping, can be installed to import products directly from that company. [L0363, 2022+]
- To import products through a fulfillment app such as HyperSQ, install the app and send the AliExpress product link to their chat support, or source directly on their platform. Once they source the product, connect it to your store and the product list is created there. [L0363, 2022+]
- CJ Dropshipping can import listings from AliExpress, so a product found on AliExpress can be fulfilled through CJ. Use this when a product is available on AliExpress but you want CJ as the fulfillment partner. [L1444, 2022]
- CJ Dropshipping can be used to source and fulfill products that were found on AliExpress. Pick the supplier that has the product in stock and connect it to the store as the fulfillment source. [L1439, 2024+]
- Zendrop can be used to request or import a dropshipping product into Shopify. Alternatively, you can add the product link now and connect it to Zendrop later. [L1331, 2025-26]
- Older advice, 2020: Avoid product import apps that pull supplier catalogs into your store. They add a middleman between you and the brand, which reduces long-term profit compared with a direct relationship with the brand. [L0358, 2020]
- The 2020 advice (above) and the fulfillment-app guidance in this chapter apply to different stages. Import apps are the beginner route for testing, and direct brand relationships are the long-term goal. [L0358, 2020; L0635, undated; L0270, 2021]
- Personalized products (for example engraved names) can be hard through CJ, which may require a developer to connect its API documentation. Print-on-demand providers such as Printful usually connect more easily to Shopify. [L1363, 2023]

**CJ Dropshipping: listing a product in Shopify (procedure)**

1. Search for the product on CJ Dropshipping, or search by keyword to find similar listings. [L0685, undated]
2. Open the listing and select the specific variant (color and style). The variant changes the product price. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
3. Check the product price, the shipping cost and the total cost for the target country. [L0685, undated]
4. Compare shipping options and pick the one with the best balance of low cost and reasonable delivery time (for example 7 to 15 days). [L0635, undated]
5. Choose the List button, not Connect, for the product you want to add to the store. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
6. Set the store name, product type, collection, vendor and a target selling price. Set the Target Price (the selling price in the store), for example 39.99 USD. The vendor can be, for example, 'cj'. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
7. Click List it now. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
8. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for the product to appear in the Shopify store. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
9. Edit product title, images, descriptions and prices in Shopify as needed. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]

- On CJ Drop Shipping, the Connect option adds the product to the store with images, videos and descriptions that can be edited. The List option is used when adding the product with a target price directly through the listing screen. [L0685, undated]
- CJ Dropshipping has a very large catalog, so most products can be found and listed directly. [L0635, undated]
- CJ Drop Shipping has a very large catalog, so most products found on AliExpress can also be found there. Products can be connected to a Shopify store within minutes. [L0685, undated]
- CJ Dropshipping has a trending products page with product categories that is convenient for browsing dropshipping products. Its delivery times can be slow unless you pay for faster options. [L0706, 2020+]

**Hyper Rescue: setup (procedure)**

1. In the Shopify App Store, search for Hyper Rescue, click it and install it. [L0685, undated]
2. Create an account on Hyper Rescue. [L0685, undated]
3. Browse the dashboard catalogs (for example Top pet supplies, TikTok viral products and niche categories) to search for products. [L0685, undated]
4. If the product is not in the catalog, go to Smart sourcing or AliExpress sourcing. [L0685, undated]
5. Copy the AliExpress link of the product, paste it into the field, and click Save. [L0685, undated]
6. Wait about 1 to 2 days for sourcing to complete. [L0685, undated]

- Hyper Rescue has a smaller catalog and works mainly through sourcing requests based on AliExpress links. [L0635, undated]
- Hyper Rescue offers good shipping times, reliable support and better pricing, but it has a small catalog. Products not found in it are sourced by submitting an AliExpress link. [L0685, undated]
- Hyper Rescue's dashboard offers preset catalogs such as top pet supplies, TikTok viral products and niche-down categories. If a product is not in the catalog, use Smart Sourcing or AliExpress Sourcing, or try an image search with the help of the live agent. [L0635, undated]

### Sourcing products outside the catalog

**Hyper Rescue sourcing (procedure)**

1. Copy the AliExpress product link. [L0635, undated]
2. In Hyper Rescue, open the AliExpress Sourcing section (or Smart Sourcing). [L0635, undated]
3. Paste the link and click Save. [L0635, undated]
4. Wait about 1 to 2 business days while the sourcing team finds the best factory, negotiates, obtains samples and verifies quality. [L0635, undated]
5. Review the quote, which shows the unit price and shipping cost per country (US, Canada, Australia and UK differ). Check stages such as 'sourcing in progress' and 'quote created'. [L0635, undated]
6. In Hyper Rescue's AliExpress sourcing, a submitted product moves through status stages: first 'sourcing in progress', then 'quote created'. The quote shows the price per unit and the shipping cost for each destination country, such as US, Canada, Australia or UK. Check the quote for your target country before accepting it. [L0685, undated]

**Request sourcing for a product not in the catalog (procedure)**

1. Try a keyword search first. If there is no match, use image search with a product photo. [L0685, undated]
2. If the product is still not found, contact the live agent support. [L0685, undated]
3. Alternatively, open the Sourcing section and click Post sourcing request. [L0685, undated]
4. Open the sourcing request section and choose Post Sourcing Request (Hyper Rescue) or the equivalent request option. [L0635, undated]
5. Enter the product title, a target price, and the AliExpress link. [L0635, undated]
6. For a product already in the Shopify store that should be sourced from another provider (for example from Hyper Rescue to CJ), select that product from the store and submit the request. [L0685, undated]
7. For a product that exists only as an AliExpress link, click the product request, enter the title (for example Cup washer), enter the target price, paste the AliExpress link and submit the request. [L0685, undated]
8. Submit the request. Sourcing takes about 1 to 2 days. [L0635, undated; L0685, undated]
9. If neither keyword nor image search works, the live agent can help. [L0635, undated]

**CJ sourcing request (procedure)**

1. Submit a sourcing request on CJ with the link to the product, for example an AliExpress listing. [L1428, undated]
2. CJ contacts the producer or supplier and checks that the product is available for your store. [L1428, undated]
3. Once a customer orders, you pay CJ for that order. [L1428, undated]
4. CJ arranges the supplier's shipment, customs clearance and delivery to the end customer. [L1428, undated]
5. Check product quality yourself, because CJ's sourcing does not guarantee it. [L1428, undated]

**Sourcing request via CJ (procedure, short form)**

1. Find the product on CJ. [L1108, 2023]
2. If it is not listed, submit a sourcing request with the AliExpress product link. [L1108, 2023]
3. Wait for the answer, which is given within 24 hours and states whether the product is available. [L1108, 2023]

- CJ is a fulfillment provider and not the manufacturer. Its higher unit price pays for handling, shipping, customs and delivery. The reported average CJ delivery time was 7 to 15 days, while direct AliExpress orders could take up to about two months. Choose the fulfillment route based on how much delivery time matters to your customers. [L1428, undated]
- CJ Dropshipping can be used to source and fulfill products that were found on AliExpress. [L1439, 2024+]
- A CJ product that is not in the catalog can be requested through a sourcing request. [L1428, undated]
- The sourcing request on CJ is free and easy to open. [L0693, 2024]
- Negotiate and request in the live chat with a human agent (see the pricing section). [L1217, 2023+]
