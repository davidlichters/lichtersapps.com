### Target Mix and Sequencing

**Target mix**
- Silver: 100% of your first five suppliers and about 95% of all suppliers long-term [L0110, undated; L0486, 2020-26].
- Gold: about 5 to 10% of your suppliers [L0711, 2020-26].
- Bronze: none; avoid entirely [L0112, undated].
- A store can be profitable with silver only, and gold adds strength but is not required [L1281, 2022].

**Sequence**
1. Build a store with silver suppliers, which should make up most of your supplier base [L0853, 2021].
2. Build sales, traffic and brand reputation with silver suppliers [L0711, 2020-26].
3. Once you have sales and traffic, apply to gold suppliers [L0251, 2023; L0853, 2021].
4. Keep contacting other suppliers while waiting for gold approval [L0938, 2020-26].
