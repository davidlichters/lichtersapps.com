## Sourcing creatives, creators and outsourcing

### Build or buy: in-house versus outsourced production

- Creatives are often the bottleneck, so the main problem is usually getting winning creatives into the account quickly. Outsourced creatives from freelance marketplaces do not guarantee performance, and building in-house skill takes weeks or months. [L0343, 2023]
- You do not need to make every creative yourself, but you should learn enough marketing to brief an outsourced designer or editor. A practical approach is to get a first set of creatives live quickly, then build basic copywriting and ad knowledge so you can judge and direct outsourced work. [L0552, 2023]
- At the test stage, paying someone to film is usually too expensive to justify. Start with self-shot or masked footage, and consider hiring only after a product proves itself. [L1186, 2022]
- High-quality video ads from outside agencies can cost thousands of dollars per month. Image ads let you test more creatives faster when budget is limited. [L0692, 2023]
- Video ads can come from four sources: [L0245, 2020]
  - DIY iPhone-style videos you film yourself
  - Product videos supplied by vendors
  - Web designer animations of your infographics
  - Static image ads, which work equally well and may be preferable for starting
- Creatives should be recorded or sourced from freelancers on Fiverr or Bilo when the owner cannot find the right people in their own circle. [L1184, 2023+]
- Outsource the parts of video production the owner dislikes, such as on-camera recording, editing, scripting or voiceover, especially if the owner feels nervous on camera. Not appearing on camera is fine: product videos and voiceover content work without the owner's face. [L1424, 2021-22]
- Email marketing can be outsourced to an agency once it shows revenue. In one example, the outsourced email campaigns were kept because they produced revenue, while Google Ads results were mixed for this product type and most traffic came from organic sources. [L0337, 2022]

### Sourcing order for a product test

Source and build creatives for a product test in this order: [L0349, 2024]

1. Look at competitor ads and supplier content for the same product.
2. Shoot your own content in the same style, using a smartphone, if the product allows it.
3. Use a script adapted from the content you saw, with only small changes, and test it for a short period.
4. Edit pictures with a simple tool such as Canva, or a freelancer for Photoshop work.

If the budget allows, the priority order for footage is: [L1377, 2022+]

1. Buy the product, shoot your own video with it, and return the product afterward. This custom content is the top priority.
2. If that is not possible, re-shoot a proven competitor video by keeping its hook, message, vibe and caption, and changing only the setting and optionally the music.
3. If neither works, search TikTok with 5 to 10 product keywords and hashtags and use videos from the last two weeks.
4. Build a creative folder of 5 to 10 or more videos from these sources.

### Own footage as a competitive advantage

- Ordering the product yourself and shooting original video creates a competitive advantage. You become the only seller with custom footage of that product, while competitors are forced to use generic supplier videos. [L0280, undated]
- Ordering products for content is not necessary at the start of testing. A sample from a supplier or Amazon can be used and then returned if you need product shots. Reusing existing winning creatives with clip changes saves time and money. [L0637, 2023+]
- Product video demonstrations can be created for dropshipping businesses only if you first obtain a physical sample to film with. Without the product, this proof point is not an option. [L0295, 2021-22]
- Ordering the product yourself is the recommended route for reliable supply, though it takes longer. [L0321, undated]
- Real customer photos, obtained with permission, can serve as ad creatives and look more professional than staged photos. Asking customers to tag the brand on Instagram gives a steady source of such content. [L1439, 2024+]

### Competitor and supplier footage when no own footage exists

- When no UGC is available, a product test can use YouTube, AliExpress, Alibaba or competitor videos, reshaped into the same 30-45 second structure with a strong hook and a call to action. This is a valid way to test a product before investing in creators. [L0398, 2023+]
- Source product images and videos from AliExpress, Amazon, Google Image Search, Instagram (search by hashtag #product-name), TikTok, or competitor stores. [L0321, undated]
- To test without your own designs, take product photos from AliExpress or Alibaba, or ask a manufacturer for pictures, and run ads with them. A test budget of around 200-300 USD is a typical starting point. [L1217, 2023+]
- When no creators are available for a classic product video, stock clips can be sourced from general websites and from marketplaces such as AliExpress and Alibaba. Captions and video structure are then added so the product is not presented as a plain sales pitch. [L1335, 2023+]
- Fashion product videos can be found on TikTok, where people sell products organically, which gives enough material for ad creatives. [L1161, 2024+]
- A Chinese short-video app is a source for new product clips, because fresh product videos often appear there before they reach other markets. Use it together with YouTube Shorts and Instagram when no own footage is available. [L1177, 2023]

**Freelancer workflow for footage from existing ads**

Use this when the seller cannot shoot their own footage. [L1194, undated]

1. Send the freelancer the AliExpress product link and ask them to find the product.
2. They search TikTok and Facebook for existing product videos and clip them.
3. They cut the clips together and remove the original ad copy text.
4. Deliver several variations where the first three to five seconds differ from each other.
5. Share the TikTok Ad Center or an ad library link with the creative editor, so they can study trending ads and how they are built before making variations.

**Mixing hooks from other footage**

- When the seller cannot shoot their own clips, search YouTube Shorts and Instagram for clips, mix hooks from them, or hire a creator on Fiverr for about 20-30 dollars to deliver content in two to three days. [L1177, 2023]

**Building an ad from sourced footage**

Source footage for an ad: [L1427, 2023+]

1. Write the narrative as a list of scenes.
2. For each scene, search for product or stock footage that matches the emotion.
3. Download each clip and store it in the correct folder.
4. Speed up slow stock clips so they match the ad's pace.

### UGC creators and creator casting

**Casting**

- Use the customer avatar to cast creators for videos. If buyers are women aged 40 and older, use a UGC creator from that group, because they resonate most with the exact audience. [L1158, 2023]
- When your own appearance does not match the target audience (for example, a Black African creator selling to Gulf or primarily European markets), create two test versions: one with self-made UGC and one with outsourced UGC creators who match the audience's appearance and accent. Run both in parallel and compare conversion rates to see which demographic resonance drives higher sales. [L0308, 2023]
- For a video ad aimed at a closing audience, the presenter can appear on camera, or a hired person who resembles the customer avatar can deliver the message. Freelance marketplaces such as Upwork were mentioned as a place to find such a speaker. [L1243, 2017]
- Creators who are building their own presence will often produce content for free product or small payments, which is a useful source of cheap content. Choose creators whose photo quality matches your brand. [L0790, 2017]
- Creators can supply high-resolution photos and video for your own social channels. Ask micro-influencers to send their photos and reshare the content, which reduces the need for a photographer. [L0790, 2017]

**Payment**

- Creator payment for video content is $45 to $60 per 60-second clip, or $45-60 for 12 five-second scenes broken down by hook, use case and call-to-action. Request raw clips rather than edited videos, so internal editors can combine scenes into 5-10 different variations. [L0321, undated]
- Fiverr creators for short content (see also the table below) can deliver content in two to three days for about 20-30 dollars. [L1177, 2023]

**Minimum spend and testing philosophy**

- Creators often demand a minimum spend per creative (for example, 3x average order value) to prove the ad works. Media buyers prefer letting the algorithm choose winners without spend minimums. Clarify your testing philosophy upfront and set budget guardrails to prevent mid-campaign conflicts. [L0217, 2025-26]

**Ordering UGC for Facebook**

- When ordering UGC for Facebook ads, the video ad type is the usual choice on Billow, since it is built for paid placements. [L1402, undated]

### Freelance marketplaces: Fiverr, Upwork and Bilo

**Fiverr for banners and design**

- Fiverr is a freelance platform where graphic designers create web banners at various price points and turnaround times. Use it instead of hiring an in-house designer, since designers deliver multiple banner sizes and formats. ** [L0288, 2021-22]
- To find designers on Fiverr, go to Graphics & Design, then select Web Banners from the subcategory menu. This section shows freelancers who specialise in web banner design. ** [L0288, 2021-22]
- When hiring a Fiverr designer, choose someone with at least 50+ completed jobs and a rating between 4.8 and 4.9 stars. Review their portfolio and select a designer whose style matches your brand. ** [L0288, 2021-22]
- Alternatively, Fiverr sellers are more cost-effective than agencies for creative production, and most agencies subcontract to Fiverr anyway. Hire Level 2+ sellers who speak English fluently to avoid revisions and quality issues. [L0309, 2024+]

**Commissioning banners on Fiverr** **

1. Provide the designer with your branding guidelines. [L0288, 2021-22]
2. Explain what the banner should communicate or convey. [L0288, 2021-22]
3. Supply product images if specific products must appear. [L0288, 2021-22]
4. The designer creates the banners in multiple sizes. [L0288, 2021-22]
5. The designer sends the finished files to you. [L0288, 2021-22]
6. Download the files and upload them to Refersion. [L0288, 2021-22]

**Affiliate banners** **

1. Determine which offer types you want to promote: business-wide offers or product-specific offers. [L0202, 2021-22]
2. Commission banner ad designs on Fiverr matching each offer type. [L0202, 2021-22]
3. Upload the completed banner ads to Refersion or your affiliate software. [L0202, 2021-22]
4. Make them available for affiliates to download. They can use them independently without further action from you. [L0202, 2021-22]

**Hiring ad designers**

- To hire ad designers, post a small test gig on Fiverr, or a job on Upwork, with one ad, and test several designers. Review each portfolio, and send examples of the style you want as inspiration, not copies of existing ads. [L0667, 2025]
- Bilo job posts usually need two to three days before applications arrive. [L1184, 2023+]

**Testing a copywriter or illustrator**

- Test a sales copywriter's efficiency before hiring. Give each candidate a small project, such as five headlines and five meta descriptions, measure the time, and hire the best one. In one test, candidates took between 2 and 6 hours for the same small task. [L0382, 2017]
- Custom illustration was budgeted at about $5 per image. Workflow: [L0380, 2014]
  1. The illustrator delivers an initial sketch.
  2. The owner gives feedback on the sketch.
  3. The illustrator delivers the final design.

### Agencies

- Most agencies subcontract to Fiverr, so a direct Fiverr seller is usually more cost-effective than an agency for creative production. [L0309, 2024+]
- A top outsourced ad creator cost about $3,500 per month. A brand that produced 100 creatives this way got about 3 winners, a hit rate of roughly 3%. Plan for many creative tests rather than a few polished ads, and consider producing creatives in-house to lower the cost per test. [L0995, 2023; L0999, 2023]
- Outsourced batches can have low hit rates. In one case, an outsourced batch of 100 creatives produced only 3 winners, about 3%. Budget for a large number of tests rather than a few high-quality pieces. [L0995, 2023; L0999, 2023]
- Viral Ecom Ads was described as about $50 per month, which covers a split test of three TikTok videos at about $12 each. Its quality was judged lower than premium production, which can cost thousands of dollars per month. [L1107, 2023]
- Creators and in-house production should be compared on the same test: create many creatives and test them. An entertaining, educational or value-first approach should be tested by analysing successful brand ads. [L0995, 2023]

### Copywriting and copy production

**Who writes the copy**

- In the early stage of an e-commerce business, the best-paid copywriters are usually unaffordable, so owners often have to learn to write product descriptions and emails themselves. Copy is a direct sales lever, so building this skill in-house can shortcut the cost of outsourcing it. ** [L0684, 2021-22]
- Product descriptions, sales emails and other sales copy can be written by owners, employees or virtual assistants who learn a tested copywriting method. Delegating copy production to a trained team member is a practical option for small teams. ** [L0684, 2021-22]
- Hire a sales copywriter to write headlines and meta descriptions for about $40 to $100 per hour. Large companies often employ people who only write headlines and meta descriptions. [L0382, 2017]

**Checking a copywriter**

- Excessive fluff and useless sentences often signal that the writer did not research the product. When hiring or testing a copywriter, check a trial text for this, and reject writers whose copy is full of empty language. ** [L0925, 2021-22]

**Product descriptions on Upwork** **

1. Post a job for product descriptions on Upwork. [L0961, 2020]
2. Hire at least three or four writers. [L0961, 2020]
3. Give each writer the same test product page to write. [L0961, 2020]
4. Compare the results and keep the writer who performs best. [L0961, 2020]

### Stock sources and assets

- Adobe Stock is a paid stock library with a monthly subscription and keyword search. It offers high-quality images, and it is the paid source the course's author uses across several businesses. ** [L1310, 2021-22]
- Unsplash is a free stock image source that works like a keyword search. Other free stock image sites can be found by searching for free stock image sites, but Unsplash is one of the better ones. ** [L1310, 2021-22]
- Canva's subscription includes stock photos, audio and stock videos that would otherwise be paid extras, which makes it a low-cost source of creative assets for a graphic designer. Confirm the current price and licence scope on the Canva site before budgeting, since the lesson's figure was taken at recording time. [L0754, undated]
- After store approval, obtain the creative assets for the Meta ads from the designated asset source before setting up the ad account and campaigns. The creative strategy for these assets is covered in the Meta ads module. [L0491, 2026]

### Customer and brand photos

- Collect real customer product photos in three steps: ** [L0968, 2021]
  1. Email customers who bought the product.
  2. Ask them to send photos taken with a good camera or a phone.
  3. Offer a payment for usable photos, for example 50 dollars via PayPal, to encourage participation.
- A friend who is a photographer shot the product for about 200. A typical professional shoot can cost about 1,000. Using a network to reduce costs is a practical option early on. [L0492, 2023+]
- A professional product photo shoot with editing costs around 1,000 to 2,000 USD. A low-budget shoot with friends is an alternative for an early-stage store, although picture quality will be lower. [L1439, 2024+]

### Influencers and authorities

Find influencers or authorities connected to your product: [L1267, 2026]

1. Search Pinterest or Google for influencers, actors, artists, celebrities or doctors who use or fit your product category.
2. Note each person's name.
3. Find and record each person's social media handle.
4. Note whether they already use or have used your products.

### Refreshing and testing creatives

- Freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr can supply replacement ad creatives when the original creatives stop converting. Refresh creatives as soon as CTR drops and CPM rises, because creative fatigue reduces sales over time. [L0660, 2019+]
- Create many creatives and test them. Budget for a large number of creative tests rather than a few high-quality pieces, since hit rates are often low. [L0995, 2023; L0999, 2023]
- A seller does not need to make every creative personally, but should be able to judge and direct the outsourced work. [L0552, 2023]

### Hiring checklist

- Fiverr designers: at least 50+ completed jobs and a 4.8-4.9 star rating, with a portfolio that matches your brand. [L0288, 2021-22]
