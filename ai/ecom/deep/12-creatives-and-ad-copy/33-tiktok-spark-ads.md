### TikTok Spark Ads

- Spark ads on an organic video can lower that video's organic reach, so use videos that are roughly 20 to 30 days old or older. Recent viral organic posts are better left out of paid tests. [L1377, 2022+]
- Spark Ads performed better than regular TikTok ads in the tests, so test both formats on the same product. Spark Ads are the preferred option when running ads from an organic account. [L1381, 2022]
- Spark Ads kept the comment filters set on the account, while comments on traditional TikTok ads are harder to monitor. Filter negative comments on the account before running ads. [L1381, 2022]
- After a Spark Ad was rejected by TikTok, organic views on the linked account dropped below 600 and stayed low. Rejected ads may coincide with a drop in organic reach, so review the rejection before testing more ads from the same account. [L1381, 2022]

### Instagram Reels and Trial Reels

- For Instagram Reels repurposed from TikTok, add a call to action such as Shop Now, and make the text more ad-like near the end of the video. The owner reported no account penalty on Instagram, while TikTok was more sensitive to reports. [L1077, undated]
- Trial Reels works as a low-risk testing ground for content styles and can be removed from the profile grid afterwards, so a polished brand page keeps its look. Creators also use it to test many hooks before pushing an ad on Facebook. [L1397, 2025-26]
- When Instagram is a main source of income, the ad can link to the Instagram page instead of the store, since the goal is for customers to check out the brand page first. This is an exception to the usual approach of linking directly to the store. [L1439, 2024+]

### Pinterest audience and targeting

- Pinterest audience size: 400+ million users. [L0296, 2021+]
- 80% of Pinterest users have previously purchased items on the platform. [L0296, 2021+]
- Pinterest's primary audience is women age 30 and older; this demographic is the majority and the best fit for products targeting this group. [L0296, 2021+]
- Pinterest requires 7-15 days to optimize; expect very high CPMs and poor results in the first 2-3 days as the platform tests. [L0296, 2021+]
- Not all products work on Pinterest; the platform works best for niches related to DIY, crafts, and inspiration content. [L0296, 2021+]
- Audience size: use stacked interests, selecting seven to ten (or five to twenty) specific sub-interests related to the product and target audience, not only broad parent categories. Keep audience size between 5 and 10 million as the sweet spot; up to 30 million is acceptable, and above 30 million is too big. [L1134, undated]
- Select individual sub-interests rather than only the parent interest, because Pinterest reports which interest drove sales only for individually selected interests; the breakdown view then shows which interest is performing. [L1134, undated]
- Demographics: if only women fit the product, select female only; an age range of 21+ is good, and 25+ was also reported to work well. Location can be all US or a specific country; language and device restrictions are not needed. [L1134, undated]
- Optimization and delivery: select Checkout as the conversion event and automatic bidding. [L1134, undated]
- Keep keywords and interests in separate campaigns. Start with interest-based targeting, and test keyword targeting only after the product has been confirmed as a winner. [L1134, undated]
- Keywords: Pinterest changed its search results so that keywords appear as separate, segmented items, which makes the platform more advertiser-friendly. Promoted pin targeting is built on these keyword segments, so choose keywords that match the exact terms users search for. [L1136, 2014]

### Pinterest creative

- Pinterest creatives should be TikTok-style vertical video in 9:16 format, 15 to 20 seconds long (20 seconds is seen as best), with quick transitions within one to two seconds. Show the product in use within the first three to five seconds; in the speaker's tests such clips reached over 3 percent CTR. [L1134, undated]
- Video outperforms static images on Pinterest in the speaker's tests across beauty, health, home decor and home improvement; fashion may be an exception where images can work. [L1134, undated]
- Creative can be reused for six or seven months because there is little saturation on Pinterest. [L1134, undated]
- TikTok-style videos produced the best CTR in the lesson's tests, and simple image pins and standard pins performed worse. [L1135, undated]
- Pinterest users recognize product images immediately, so the product must be visible. [L0577, 2023]

### Pinterest ad setup and copy

- Creating a Pinterest ad: click Select pins or Create pin (upload a video from the computer). Use the same headline as on TikTok ads as the title, and add a short description of three to four lines that names the pain point and the benefit and ends with a call to action, such as "flawless skin is just a click away." The description can be generated with ChatGPT. [L1134, undated]
- Creating a video pin at the ad level:
  1. Click Select pins. [L1135, undated]
  2. If you have no existing pins, click Create pin. [L1135, undated]
  3. Upload the video from your computer. [L1135, undated]
  4. Enter the ad headline in the title field (the same one used for TikTok ads). [L1135, undated]
  5. Enter the ad text in the description field. [L1135, undated]
- Pattern for Pinterest ad description copy: write three to four lines that name a pain point, state the product benefit, end with a call to action, and optionally finish with a short benefit-driven line. [L1135, undated]
  - Example one: "Tired of frizzy hair after every wash? This comb smooths in one pass, so tap now for salon-smooth results at home." [L1135, undated]
  - Example two: "Sore feet at the end of the day? These insoles add cushioning under every step, so shop today and walk easier tomorrow." [L1135, undated]

### YouTube ads

- YouTube ads need studio-quality creatives with a hook that grabs attention within the first six seconds. Videos that work on TikTok or Facebook often do not perform the same way on YouTube. [L0788, undated]
- Production polish is not a prerequisite. YouTube video ads can be produced cheaply, even with a phone and a plain setup where the presenter stands in front of the product and talks. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Authenticity can outperform high production values. A phone-shot video of a person talking in front of a product, with a near-zero production budget, can become one of the most-watched YouTube ads of its kind. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Simple YouTube ad formula: a product video with product promotion aimed at a retargeting list that has not purchased, plus a top-of-funnel video aimed at competitor fans. [L1510, 2020-26]

### Demand Gen ads

- Informational images should be paired with a headline that matches the image, since Demand Gen combines headlines with images. The image's job is to create curiosity, not to give the answer. [L0882, 2025-26]

### Native ad networks

- Native ad networks such as Taboola place advertorial-style ads on large news sites. This is a separate channel from Meta ads. [L0691, 2024]
