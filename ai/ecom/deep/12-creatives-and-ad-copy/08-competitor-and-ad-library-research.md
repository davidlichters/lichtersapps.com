### Finding winners: run time, spend and scale signals

#### Run time as a shortcut

- Ads that have run for a long time are usually profitable, so run duration is a useful shortcut for spotting winners. Model the headline, image, video and copy of ads that have been running for months. [L0523, 2015+]
- Any ad running for months has been validated as a successful winner in the free Meta Ad Library and can be swiped for analysis. [L0039, 2026]
- In the Meta Ads Library, the ads that have run longest are usually the ones with the best return on ad spend. Scroll to the bottom of the results to find them. Use them for inspiration on structure and copy, but do not copy them. [L0667, 2025]
- Caution: do not assume the longest-running ad is the winner. Some advertisers keep ads running without sales to mislead competitors. Check running days together with duplicates and traffic before copying an ad. [L0374, 2026]
- Running time and duplicates together give a stronger signal than either alone. Where an ad appears as many separate variations, treat it as proven. [L0707, 2026]
- Look for a creative that runs in many ads at once; the lesson uses 8 or more ads as the scaling signal. [L0398, 2023+]
- Recent-launch check: the Brands tracker counts new ads per week, and 3 to 5 new ads per week signals active scaling. [L0374, 2026]

#### Spend and brand size

- Prioritize big brands that spend heavily on Facebook, for example around 1 to 5 million USD per month, because they test creative constantly. [L0398, 2023+]
- Study what large brands run and copy their layout, fonts, colors and ad formats. They have spent heavily on testing, so their proven choices are a useful starting point. [L0692, 2023; L0646, 2023]
- Check the Facebook Ad Library for the ads large brands in your niche keep running, because those brands test heavily and keep what works. Use the winning styles as a starting point, then test them for your own product and niche. [L0646, 2023]
- Research direct competitors that sell the same product and, above all, large brands in the niche, because large brands spend heavily on testing creative ideas and marketing angles. Save their ads and landing pages in one sheet for reference. [L0902, undated]

#### Recency and engagement

- Find competitor creatives from the last two weeks by searching 5 to 10 product keywords and related hashtags on TikTok. Recent videos are less likely to have been shown to your audience. [L1377, 2022+]
- A good creative to test is one uploaded recently, not yet used by many competitors, and already getting a good like rate on PP Ads. A recent competitor video with a strong like rate shows real engagement. [L1382, undated]
- On TikTok, compare a creative's likes with its ad impressions. Strong impressions and a good like count mean the creative resonates and is worth replicating. [L0895, 2021+]
- Kalodata's Video & Ad list shows each ad with revenue, view count and post date. Use these figures to pick hooks from ads that already sold, not ads picked at random. It has an Export button for the list. [L0901, 2026]

#### Stopped ads

- If a competitor stopped a creative for a product, the cause may be account issues rather than poor performance. You can still test the product, but check results after a few days and change the creative, offer or market if sales do not come. [L1219, undated]

### Searching the Meta Ad Library step by step

#### Competitor research in the Ad Library

1. Open the competitor's Facebook page. [L0649, 2019]
2. In the right column, click Page transparency. [L0649, 2019]
3. Click See more to view the page creation date, name changes and the number of people managing the page. [L0649, 2019]
4. Click the Ad Library link, or the button to see all ads, to view active and past ads. [L0649, 2019]
5. Check the start date, format and copy of each ad, and switch the country to see regional ads. [L0649, 2019]
6. Revisit the page regularly to see which ads they keep running. [L0649, 2019]

#### Finding and replicating winning creatives

1. Open the Meta Ad Library and search your niche and the competitors and large brands in it. [L0398, 2023+]
2. Prioritize big brands that spend heavily on Facebook (see spend guidance above). [L0398, 2023+]
3. Look for creatives that run in many ads at once; 8 or more ads is the scaling signal used in the lesson. [L0398, 2023+]
4. Compare the creatives of the same brand and note the hook that repeats in the first seconds while the later clips change. [L0398, 2023+]
5. Replicate the repeated hook in your own ads and test it against your own video. [L0398, 2023+]

#### Using the Ad Library for wording and downloads

- Search the Facebook Ad Library for the niche, such as home decor, to find wording ideas for primary text and headlines. [L0642, undated]
- Search the product name in the Meta Ad Library with All ads selected, Active status filter on and country set to United States. [L0707, 2026]
- Check whether the same video runs many times as separate ad variations, since many active variations signal a proven ad. [L0707, 2026]
- To download a video from the Ad Library: play the ad, right-click it, choose Save video as, and type the file name with the extension.mp4. If the file is saved with another extension, download it and rename the extension to.mp4 so it opens as a video. [L0638, 2023]
- Meta Ad Library lets you download a competitor's ad creative for review. Save the files and study the hook, pacing and format before building your own test version. Do not copy the footage itself. [L0335, 2023]
- Download the video creatives of shortlisted products from the Meta Ads Library and save them in the shared research folder. Use them as a reference when briefing your own test ads, so the team works from the same examples. [L1173, undated]

#### AdvertSuite ad research (5 steps)

1. Define a S.M.A.R.T. objective for what ad types you want to find and why. [L0196, 2020+]
2. Select the 'Keywords' search mode and choose 'Ad Text', 'Comments', or 'Landing Page Text' as the search field. Filter by country, date range, ad position, demographics and technology if needed. Sort by 'Longest Running' or by engagement (likes, comments, shares). [L0196, 2020+]
3. Start with broad keywords related to your industry, then add filters. Examine the results to see what is working now. [L0196, 2020+]
4. Capture each ad in a Trello board: [L0196, 2020+]
   - screenshot the full ad from source;
   - copy the text to a Google Doc and link it;
   - add a link to the landing page;
   - screenshot the landing page (use FireShot Chrome extension for long pages) [L0196, 2020+];
   - screenshot the AdvertSuite details page;
   - capture relevant comments.
5. Analyze each ad by asking 30 questions, including: who the advertiser targets, what the short-term goal is, which features and benefits are shared, what the core attention grabber is, what mood is conveyed, which objections are addressed, and which calls to action are used. [L0196, 2020+]

#### Trello as the research database

- Create columns (lists) by keyword or category. Add cards with ad details, labels (platform, ad type), description (AdvertSuite search settings) and comments. [L0196, 2020+]
- AdvertSuite lets you filter by country, date range, ad position (newsfeed vs. sidebar), gender, language, age and technology used. [L0196, 2020+]
- FireShot is a Chrome extension that takes full-page screenshots of pages longer than the screen, used to capture complete landing pages for analysis. [L0196, 2020+]

### Tracking competitors in the brand tracker

#### What the tracker shows

- A tracked brand's detail page has these tabs: Overview, Ad Copies, Ad Headlines, Ad Hooks, Landing Pages, Personas, Concepts, Angles, Desires, Emotions, Awareness Stages and USPs. Each tab breaks down that element across the brand's ads, so you can check which hooks, angles or desires a competitor uses most. [L0772, 2026; L0892, 2026]
- Together these tabs show how a competitor is positioning and selling right now, based on its live ads. [L0892, 2026]
- A competitor's top personas, concepts, angles, USPs, emotions and desires come from its live ads, so they show what it is actually running. Copying or varying the strongest of these is faster than guessing the strategy. [L0892, 2026]
- The tracker's concept categories are Demo / How-It Works, Before-And-After, Educational Explainer, Social Proof / Reviews, Promotion / Discount, Problem-Agitate-Solve, Bundle / Multi-Pack Offer and Social Proof Emphasis. Check which of these a competitor scales most. [L0374, 2026]
- The tracker's angle categories in the example included Problem-Agitate-Solve, Customer Before-And-After Reveal, At-Home Microneedling Substitute, Budget Comparison, Comparison Vs Microneedling, Side-By-Side Comparison and After Demonstration. Each angle is tracked with the number of ads that use it. [L0374, 2026]
- In the awareness-stage view, the sort control offers Usage over 7 days or 30 days and Longest Running. Hooks and landing pages that appear across many ads or stay live for months are the strongest candidates to study first. [L0892, 2026]

#### Procedure: analyse a competitor brand

1. Open the brand and read the overview cards: top landing pages, personas, concepts, angles, desires, emotions, awareness stages and USPs. [L0374, 2026]
2. Count the new ads uploaded each week; 3 to 5 new ads per week signals active scaling. [L0374, 2026]
3. Check ad attribution by format; if almost all ads are video, the brand is scaling video. [L0374, 2026]
4. Open the Ad Copies tab and identify the copy with the most attributed ads; that is the main winning copy. Then check the headlines and hooks. [L0374, 2026]
5. Open the Landing Pages tab, note the number of pages and the share of ads sent to each. [L0374, 2026]
6. Open Personas and Concepts to see which audiences and formats the brand uses. [L0374, 2026]
7. Open Angles, Desires, Emotions, Awareness Stages and USPs. [L0374, 2026]
8. Open the top ads, read the ad details (running days, duplicates, landing page, price) and generate the transcript. [L0374, 2026]
9. Download the top video for frame-by-frame analysis. [L0374, 2026]

#### Reading a top ad's transcript

- In a tracked brand's overview, opening a video ad from the list gives you its transcript. Read the spoken hook and script word for word before rebuilding a similar ad. [L0772, 2026]
- Video ads in a brand's list can be opened for transcripts, speakable scripts, and framework or pattern analysis. Use these to see how a competitor's ad is structured before writing your own. [L0892, 2026]

#### Export hooks and generate variations

1. Open the brand and go to the Ad Hooks tab. [L0892, 2026]
2. Click Export to download the hooks as a CSV file. [L0892, 2026]
3. Import the CSV into an AI tool. [L0892, 2026]
4. Ask the AI for more hooks similar to the exported ones. [L0892, 2026]
- Pro tip: exporting top hooks, personas and angles from a brand and feeding them into an AI tool such as Claude can surface further personas and angles beyond those the tool lists. [L0892, 2026]

#### Worked case: acne-scar serum brand

- Case: an acne-scar serum brand was on the tracker with 133 active ads, tracked since 6 May 2026, and had been added 22 hours before the data was pulled, so counts were still filling in. [L0374, 2026]
- It uploaded 3 to 5 new ads per week, attributed almost only to video, with no image or carousel ads. [L0374, 2026]
- 133 ads ran the same main ad copy, whose headline reached 23,000+ people. [L0374, 2026]
- The top persona was people with acne scars, followed by budget-conscious skincare shoppers and adults with acne scars. [L0374, 2026]
- The top desire was fading acne scars, the top emotions were hope and relief, and the top USPs were a 90-day money-back guarantee, a microcrystal technology as the main mechanism, and bundle pricing under $40. [L0374, 2026]
- Traffic went almost only to product pages: 125 ads (81.2%) to the main product page and 8 ads (5.2%) to a second page, with no listicles, advertorials or pre-sell pages. [L0374, 2026]
- The analysis showed the brand lagging on its funnel, so a similar product with a stronger funnel and a better offer could compete. [L0374, 2026]
- The top video was downloaded, transcribed, analysed frame by frame in Gemini, and its script adapted to a new product. [L0374, 2026]

#### Worked example: supplement brand

- A supplement brand with 67 active ads had Promotion / Discount as its top concept, Fast and Free Shipping as its top USP, and a limited-time deal as its top desire. [L0892, 2026]
- This combination shows a brand that relies on urgency and price incentives. It can be copied directly or varied with a different angle. [L0892, 2026]

### Reverse-engineering a winning video in Kalodata

#### Procedure

1. In Kalodata, open the product page and set the region to United States and the date range to Last 30 Days. [L0707, 2026]
2. Check that the product sells at scale in Core Metrics: Main Revenue, Item Sold and Avg. Unit Price. [L0707, 2026]
3. Open the Video & Ad tab and pick the video ad with the highest views and revenue in the period. [L0707, 2026]
4. Watch the video and note the hook, the problem named, the testimonial structure, the solution, the benefits, the social proof and the call to action. [L0707, 2026]
5. Search the product name in the Meta Ad Library (see Ad Library settings above). [L0707, 2026]
6. Check whether the same video runs many times as separate ad variations. [L0707, 2026]
7. Use the findings to build your own version of the structure, not a copy of the video. [L0707, 2026]

#### Reference numbers

- Winning reference ad: about 8 million views and about 165k revenue in the last 30 days, with the video running about 1 minute 57 seconds on the player. [L0707, 2026]
- Kalodata's Video & Ad view lists competitor video ads for a product, and the Storyboard Export lets you export an ad's storyboard for analysis. Highest Revenue Videos and their conversion rates show which ad creatives generate the most sales. [L1187, 2026]
- Good ad editing and detailed bottom-funnel creatives indicate a product's ads are worth studying and possibly copying in a customized form. Look for creatives that show the product in use or in a result-focused way. [L1187, 2026]

#### Worked case: calcium multi-balm stick

- Case: a Korean calcium multi-balm stick, sold in the United States and on TikTok Shop, reached about 50k revenue per day and about 1.5 million in the last 30 days. [L0707, 2026]
- Its best ad, a phone-filmed creator testimonial, reached about 8 million views and about 165k revenue in the same period. [L0707, 2026]
- The ad ran about 1 minute 57 seconds and opened with a woman naming the product as a Botox-like balm to stop the scroll. [L0707, 2026]
- She then showed a before and after image, named smoker lines and under-eye darkness, told a story about an aunt who smoked for over 20 years, and added her own insecurity. [L0707, 2026]
- She then claimed she is a nurse, listed adenosine, glutathione and a patented calcium-vitamin D ingredient with about ten benefits in a row, cited five-star reviews and a four-week wait from stock shortage, and ended with urgency to act fast. [L0707, 2026]
- The same video ran as a repeated Meta ad, with about 6,500 keyword results in the Ad Library. [L0707, 2026]

### Selecting and editing test videos from Kalodata

#### Procedure

1. Open the Video & Ad list in Kalodata and set the time filter to Last 30 Days. [L0704, 2026]
2. Look for videos with high view counts and strong sales, which signal a viral, trending ad. [L0704, 2026]
3. Choose 12 to 24 videos for a test, starting with at least 12. [L0704, 2026]
4. Take half of the selected videos (about six) from page 1 and the rest from pages 2 and 3. [L0704, 2026]
5. Keep only videos that show a real person; drop abstract or product-only videos. [L0704, 2026]
6. Drop videos whose hook or demonstrated outcome does not match what your product delivers, even if the hook itself is strong. [L0704, 2026]
7. Drop videos whose spoken language is not English, since you cannot check or match their message to your store. [L0704, 2026]
8. Do not spend much time judging production quality; the sales data is the main signal. [L0704, 2026]
9. In CapCut, cut every part that mentions TikTok Shop or Orange Cart. [L0704, 2026]
10. Cut every offer that is not on your landing page, including buy one get one free if your website does not show it. [L0704, 2026]
11. Make no other changes: add no new hooks and no extra edits to videos that already sell. [L0704, 2026]

#### Why this selection works

- Do not judge a video mainly by how polished it looks. If a video shows strong sales, it is already working, so its quality matters less than its results. [L0704, 2026]
- Pulling some winners from deeper result pages targets ads that fewer competitors are looking at, which gives the test fresher material than only the top results. [L0704, 2026]
- Spending a long time deciding whether a video is good wastes time. Use the view and sales data first and sort the videos on that basis. [L0704, 2026]
- Kalodata's result list offers a Download Video (Without Watermark) option, which gives a clean file to edit before running the ad. [L0704, 2026]

### Analysing the psychology of winning ads

#### Prompt: psychological pattern analysis of a swipe file

Use this prompt unchanged when you have a collection of successful ads:

```text
When you receive a collection of successful ads, study each one to uncover the psychological pattern underneath the wording. For every ad, identify which emotion it activates (fear or desire), the core logic that makes its promise credible, the specific opening technique that halts scrolling, and the sequence of beats it follows. Then group the ads by shared emotional target and persuasion approach, identify fears the avatar likely holds that none of these ads address, and classify where the audience sits on Schwartz's five-stage awareness model (Unaware through Most Aware).
```

[L0039, 2026]

#### Swipe file size

- Gather 5 to 10 winning ad scripts to analyze as your swipe file. If using Kalodata, take the top performers. If using the free Meta Ad Library, any ad running for months is validated as a proven winner. [L0039, 2026]

#### Ad style analysis

- Always study what competitors are doing in their ad copy and improve on what is already working. Use competitive copywriting as inspiration for your own differentiation, not as a template to replicate. [L0206, undated]
- Ad style analysis compares each pair of styles with at least 10 examples each for the first pair, and tracks pattern changes over time. Pairs: [L0613, 2026]
  - professional vs user-generated content;
  - emotional vs logical appeals;
  - benefit-focused (bright side) vs problem-focused (dark side);
  - simple vs complex explanation;
  - short-form vs long-form;
  - serious vs humorous tone;
  - male vs female presenters;
  - solo presenter vs group;
  - studio vs real-world setting;
  - text-heavy vs image-focused;
  - technical vs lifestyle framing;
  - identity-led vs benefit-led.
- Also note outliers that succeed despite going against the trend. [L0613, 2026]
- Model proven landing pages and ads instead of inventing new ones and hoping they work. Find what competitors already run, such as opt-in pages, sales pages or quiz pages, and build something similar before testing refinements. Following a proven model saves a lot of time and money. [L0523, 2015+]
- Copying one source closely is imitation that reads as a knockoff, while borrowing from many sources is research that produces something new. Copy the reasoning behind a style, such as why an ad hook works for its audience, not just its surface look. [L1343, undated]
- Copying a competitor's ad without understanding why it works, and without setting expectations, is a common cause of failure. Competitor research is useful only to extract transferable lessons, not to clone their ads. [L1348, 2023]
