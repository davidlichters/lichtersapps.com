### Repurposing across platforms

- Repurposing can mean re-uploading an identical video on the same platform, or moving content from TikTok to Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts to reach more audiences. [L0795, undated]
- Repurposed content is valuable across platforms. A strong TikTok video can be reused as an Instagram Reel, and a successful Reel can be reposted. Use this to capture more reach from one production effort. [L1077, undated]
- Reuse winning TikTok videos on Facebook in the same format, or have an editor cut them into Facebook formats. Test organic TikTok first, then use the video that goes viral for Facebook ads. [L0773, 2023]
- Adapt each video to its platform in format and tone. Trends and jokes that work on TikTok may not work on Facebook Reels, which has an older audience. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Compilation method:
  1. Pick three videos that performed well. [L0795, undated]
  2. Extract only their highlights. [L0795, undated]
  3. Combine them into one clip of about 10 to 20 seconds. For example, three 3-second highlights make a 9-second video. [L0795, undated]
- Cross-platform workflow:
  1. Edit the video once in a mobile editor such as InShot. [L0784, 2025-26]
  2. Upload it to Instagram and add the overlay text using Instagram's own text tool, with the platform's font. [L0784, 2025-26]
  3. Upload the same video to TikTok and use TikTok's text tool and font for the overlay, keeping the same thumbnail. [L0784, 2025-26]
  4. Upload to YouTube Shorts and use YouTube's own text tool and font for the overlay. [L0784, 2025-26]
  5. Use the same wording on each platform, but match the text formatting to the native style. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Example: a DIY phone tripod hack was filmed on a camera, edited in InShot, then posted with the same thumbnail. The overlay "DIY phone tripod hack" was added with each platform's native text feature, which the lesson says boosts algorithm performance. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Mistake: uploading one video to every platform with the same unchanged text styling misses platform-specific optimisation. Use each platform's native text features and fonts, because format is part of what the algorithm rewards. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Watermarks: the animation tool's watermark (bottom left) and the subtitle tool's watermark (top) remain on the video unless covered. Cover them with a screenshot or overlay, and add your own logo in the bottom left corner. [L0888, 2023+]

### Thumbnails and hooks

- The thumbnail must grab attention, stop the scroll and show the product being used. It is the first visual impression of the ad before the video plays. [L0777, undated]
- Use the thumbnail to speak to the main pain point of direct customers. Keep the same thumbnail with a matching opening clip, since that combination can be a winning pattern. [L0871, 2021+]

### Quality bar and mistakes to avoid

- The quality of the video is the most important factor. A good video can be re-uploaded or repurposed later and may perform again, while a bad video will not succeed regardless of hashtags or posting tricks. [L0764, 2022]
- Clips must match what is being said. Low-quality or pixelated footage should not be used, because poor visual quality makes an ad look cheap. [L0693, 2024]
- Basic production is enough to start: a modern smartphone, a plain setup and consistent uploads. Film, edit and test before buying more. [L0349, 2024; L1381, 2022]

### Build the cut before the polish

- Build the cut structure first, then add transitions, sound effects and voiceover. Working in that order keeps the narrative clear before polishing it. [L1427, 2023+]

### Voiceover

1. Write the script first. [L1427, 2023+]
2. Record the voice on the phone's recorder app, reading the script over the edit. [L1427, 2023+]
3. Upload the recording to cloud storage and download it to the computer. [L1427, 2023+]
4. Import the audio into the Audio bin, place it under the visuals and apply the dialogue preset. [L1427, 2023+]

### Text and graphics in Premiere Pro

- Use the text tool in the Graphics panel to type text. [L1427, 2023+]
- Adjust the font, capitalization and scale, and use the alignment guides that appear to center it. [L1427, 2023+]
- Style the text with a fill color, a stroke (outline) and a background. [L1427, 2023+]

### Speed changes and motion

Speed ramp in Premiere Pro with keyframes:

1. Add a speed keyframe at the start of the section you want to change. [L1427, 2023+]
2. Keep the first part slow. [L1427, 2023+]
3. Add a second keyframe where the action should speed up and set that section to fast. [L1427, 2023+]
4. Play it back to check the timing matches the cut. [L1427, 2023+]

- Ease in and ease out the start and end of a movement or transition. A movement that starts and stops abruptly looks unnatural, while easing makes it feel smooth. [L1427, 2023+]

### Transitions

- In Premiere Pro's Effects panel, the video transitions folder contains presets such as Dip to Black, which fades a clip out to black. [L1427, 2023+]
- Drag the preset onto the cut point between two clips to apply it. [L1427, 2023+]
- Apply easing to the start and end of transitions as well as movements. [L1427, 2023+]

### Captions

- Animated word-by-word captions suit YouTube Shorts engagement, but they did not perform well for ads in the tests described. [L1427, 2023+]
- Test animated word-by-word captions against standard captions before using them for paid ads. [L1427, 2023+]

### Color and filters

- In Edits, the Paris filter is a Story-style filter that brightens the footage overall. [L1453, 2025-26]
- To keep one consistent look across all clips in a project, use the "Apply filter to all clips" option instead of filtering each clip separately. [L1453, 2025-26]
