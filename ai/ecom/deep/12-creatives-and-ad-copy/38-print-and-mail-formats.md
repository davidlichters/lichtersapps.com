### Print and mail formats

- Postcards suit consumer mailings with simple offers and existing relationships, but are weaker in business-to-business mail where gatekeepers discard B-pile mail. Large fold-out self-mailers that mail flat without an envelope, and roadmap postcards that unfold to about eight pages, are useful alternatives for longer copy. [L1370, undated]
- Tear-sheet or advertorial mailing: a piece made to look like a page torn from a newspaper or magazine, with a handwritten sticky note from a friend. Because people read articles more than ads, such mailings can start with 30 to 50 percent of recipients reading them, compared with about 10 percent for a standard sales letter. [L1370, undated]
  - Example: a sheet styled as a magazine article with a sticky note reading "Thought you would want to see this, J." [L1370, undated]

### Lead magnet and reward ads

- Ad copy for a lead magnet offers a free, related resource instead of a discount or a product. Formula: free + specific related topic + a short call to action such as "get your copy." [L1076, 2019]
  - Example: "Free guide: how to choose your first dog harness. Get your copy." [L1076, 2019]
  - Example: "Free checklist: the history of camping stoves. Grab yours." [L1076, 2019]
- Offer a reward item in the ad that the prospect claims by returning a request form with qualifying questions. The answers show which products each responder is likely to want and build a targeted list for follow-up. [L1261, undated]

### Giveaways

- Giveaway contests attract people who want free items rather than buyers, so avoid them when the goal is paying customers. [L1016, 2026]

### Social proof and post-engagement ads

- Formula for a post-engagement ad that asks for social proof: say that the customer loves the product, invite them to comment with a photo or description, and mention a small reward. Keep the request simple for older audiences by asking only for a written comment. [L1243, 2017]
  - Example 1: "Enjoying your new mug? Tell us what you think in the comments and get a free sticker pack." [L1243, 2017]
  - Example 2: "Got a photo with your jacket? Post it in the comments and we will send you a discount code." [L1243, 2017]
- Post-engagement ads are cheap to run and can be linked to Minichat for an automatic reward. Successful page posts can be reused in other campaigns as layered social proof, showing comments and photos to cold audiences who are asked to buy. [L1243, 2017]

### Trusted advisor and review headline ads

- Reuse the product image from the product reminder ad in the trusted advisor ad, since the shopper already knows it from the product page. Change the headline, copy and review so the ad feels new. [L1398, 2021-22]
- Formula for the opening line of a review-based ad: name a group of happy buyers, state what they say about the product, and name the product in the same line. It works because the first line is the only copy most viewers see, and a clear summary with a named product creates enough curiosity to tap more. [L1398, 2021-22]
  - Example 1: "Home cooks are raving about the Alpine cast-iron skillet, and it cooks evenly on any stove." [L1398, 2021-22]
  - Example 2: "Dog owners say the Cloud-soft bed is worth every penny for older pets." [L1398, 2021-22]
- Key attributes of a trusted advisor ad: [L1398, 2021-22]

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Image | The best quality image of the product, or an image supplied by a customer showing them with the product |
| Headline | The headline from a strong review, or a summary of one |
| Body copy | At least one review. Where there is more space, as on Facebook ads, add several reviews that highlight different things about the product or the business |
| Call to action | Test Learn More, Read More, Get Yours Today or Shop Now |

- Business-wide review headline: a plain statement of what customers like about buying from the store, placed before the review text so the ad is about the business rather than one product. It works because the shopper learns the ad's point in the first line, and social proof about the seller's service builds trust when product proof is not the main issue. [L1398, 2021-22]
  - Example 1: "Customers keep coming back because our shipping is fast and our support answers quickly." [L1398, 2021-22]
  - Example 2: "Buyers tell us they trust our store because returns are simple and the checkout is clear." [L1398, 2021-22]

### Creator and whitelist (native) ads

- Pitch the same product in two voices. The brand voice is a direct, company-led call to buy, while the whitelist voice is a first-person usage story that includes a time frame and a result. It works because the brand voice informs, while the personal voice reads as a recommendation. [L1474, 2026]
  - Example 1, brand: "Get the new 1 L steel bottle, now in stock." [L1474, 2026]
  - Example 1, whitelist: "I took this 1 L bottle on three 10 km runs this month and it has not leaked once." [L1474, 2026]
  - Example 2, brand: "Our new collagen drink is here, order today." [L1474, 2026]
  - Example 2, whitelist: "I added this collagen drink to my morning routine for 21 days, and my energy stays steady until lunch." [L1474, 2026]
- Formula for ads run from a character page: open with a personal aside that sounds like a real confession or a repeated question the character gets, then promise a specific number of tips or steps, then name the one tool or product the character uses. Show the character as the sponsor with a Sponsored label, a destination domain under the creative, a headline naming the concrete outcome, and a Learn More call to action. [L1475, 2026]
  - It works because it reads as content from a person first and an offer second, with a concrete promise that invites clicks. [L1475, 2026]
  - Example 1: "Honest confession: my knee pain was ignored for two years until I changed one habit with my running shoes. Here are the three checks I run before buying any pair now, plus the insole I still swear by." Headline: "3 Checks Before Buying Running Shoes". [L1475, 2026]
  - Example 2: "Every week someone asks me how I keep my kitchen grease-free without harsh chemicals. Here are four cheap habits that changed how often I clean, plus the one spray I keep on the counter." Headline: "4 Kitchen Habits That Cut My Cleaning Time". [L1475, 2026]
- Case: a fictional character page started at zero followers. Ads ran from the page for one month, from March to April. The page reached about 1,000 followers organically through the ads, and people chose to follow via the ad itself, without buying followers. Zero followers did not stop people from buying. [L1476, 2026]
- Native ads must always run from a whitelist page and should not be split-tested against a brand page. Running native ads from a branded page ruins the ad, because native ads only work when they look like an ordinary post by a person. [L1476, 2026]
- Whitelist ad copy is written in an organic, personal voice, not a brand voice. Brand copy uses direct selling such as "buy today, 50% off, see the reviews", while whitelist copy opens with a personal hook, names a question the character is often asked, promises a short concrete takeaway and mentions the product in passing. Keep the static ad layout the same and swap only the brand name and logo for the character's face. [L1476, 2026]
  - Example 1: "Real talk: I used to wake up exhausted every single day. Here are the 3 wind-down steps I still follow, and the one thing that made the biggest difference." [L1476, 2026]
  - Example 2: "I get DMs about my knife every week, so I finally wrote down why I stopped buying cheap ones. Here is what to check before you spend a dime." [L1476, 2026]

#### Further details

- Post-it Notes Hook Framework: (1) Create a handwritten hook on a physical post-it note. (2) Record a 15-30 second video showing the post-it with the product and its benefits. (3) Leverage the personal appeal of handwriting and the uncommon format to stand out. [L0258, undated]

- Handwritten post-it hooks are effective because audiences respond to the personal feel of handwriting, the format is uncommon and memorable, and the 15-30 second video length is short enough to maintain attention while conveying core benefits. [L0258, undated]
