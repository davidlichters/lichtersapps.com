### Hiring and freelance talent

#### Hiring pipeline tools

- Tools for the hiring process: onlinejobs.ph (job posting), Asana (hiring pipeline and candidate tracking), Asana forms (application screening) and Vocaroo (audio recording submission from candidates). These automate filtering and reduce manual work while maintaining visibility. [L0263, 2022]
- Message-based interviews: Skype is the main chat platform, and you ask the candidate for their Skype ID. Slack can be used the same way by collecting the candidate's email and inviting them to a dedicated channel. [L1055, 2021]

#### onlinejobs.ph

- onlinejobs.ph is a hiring platform for virtual assistants based in the Philippines, used to find and hire customer service and admin staff. [L0970, 2023]
- Online Jobs PH is the job site used to hire virtual assistants from the Philippines, which gives access to a large pool of experienced remote staff. (2021) [L1090, 2021]
- onlinejobs.ph requires a monthly subscription of about $49 or $69 to access its VA database and message candidates before hiring. It is known for finding long-term, career-focused VAs, including many with Shopify skills. [L0921, 2022]
- onlinejobs.ph is also used to find helpers for product uploads, and to hire hourly freelancers for product uploads and other tasks. (2022) [L0665, 2022; L0359, 2020]
- onlinejobs.ph is a job board used to hire freelance video editors who make product creatives. It was the source of the editor used in the test. [L1194, undated]
- TimeProof on onlinejobs.ph is a free feature that records the VA's work session through screenshots taken roughly every 10 minutes. It lets you review what the VA did on the computer. [L0921, 2022]

#### Upwork

- Upwork and onlinejobs.ph are used to hire hourly freelancers for product uploads and other tasks. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work. [L0359, 2020]
- Upwork is used to hire Shopify developers for custom product tools or small theme changes, and bidders are usually available quickly. (2021) [L0671, 2021]
- Upwork is the platform for one-off projects such as uploading products or hiring a virtual assistant. (2021) [L0834, 2021]
- Upwork is used to hire freelancers for Shopify developer work, product uploads and site design, while virtual assistants can be hired from online-jobs.ph. (2020) [L0852, 2020]
- Upwork lets you post a project and receive bids from writers and specialists. Run test projects with several writers before choosing one for ongoing work. (2021) [L1279, 2021]
- Upwork can be used to hire a site speed specialist. Post the job tagged with Shopify, which costs nothing to post, and judge candidates by reviews for Shopify and speed work. (2022) [L0963, 2022]
- Hourly or fixed prices for site speed work vary widely, from about 20 USD to several thousand. Price alone does not indicate quality, so reviews are the main criterion. (2022) [L0963, 2022]
- Upwork and Fiverr are freelance marketplaces where a store owner can find first virtual assistants. The rate of bad hires there is high, so check the assistant's track record and consider a trial period before committing. [L1432, undated]

#### Fiverr

- Fiverr is pay-per-job, so a single task such as an upload or small edit can be bought without a subscription, with prices around $10 per task. Its freelancer pool covers a very wide range of skills, and quality varies strongly. [L0921, 2022]
- Fiverr is a marketplace for hiring freelancers, used to outsource store builds, product pages, or purchasing pre-warmed Facebook ad accounts. [L1212, undated]
- Source positions on Fiverr differ. Fiverr is not recommended for product upload work [L0359, 2020], while a single upload or small edit can be bought there for about $10 per task [L0921, 2022]. Use Fiverr for one-off tasks with a checked result, and use Upwork or onlinejobs.ph for recurring upload work. [L0359, 2020; L0921, 2022]

#### StoreTasker

- StoreTasker (storetasker.com) is a marketplace for hiring developers to do custom Shopify work. For simpler needs, the Shopify App Store offers apps that can customise a store. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
- StoreTasker is a freelance marketplace for one-off technical Shopify modifications and coding tasks, when a change is beyond what you can do yourself. (2023) [L0855, 2023]
- StoreTasker.com is used for website development and code work. You post a job, developers submit bids, and you approve one or decline all. [L0921, 2022]
- StoreTasker is listed as a tool for this business setup. The source does not describe its function, so verify what it does before using it. (2024) [L1095, 2024]

#### Hiring a VA: hand-over

- Clarify upfront which tools or platforms are required for the agency to work with you. Determine whether they require access to proprietary software, dashboards or creative management systems, and ensure your data remains accessible to you if the relationship ends. [L0217, 2025-26]

### Virtual assistant tools

- Hootsuite lets a social media VA log into one dashboard and publish the same product visuals to all connected social media accounts. Giving the VA access only to Hootsuite keeps posting centralised and avoids sharing account logins. [L0921, 2022]
- Google Drive, Google Sheets and Asana together provide the order and task view for the VA, with customer messages kept in the help desk. [L1473, 2019; L0230, 2019]

### Monitoring and activity checks

- Hubstaff is a time tracking tool that shows mouse and keyboard activity and time spent. Normal product listing metrics: 70% mouse, 30% keyboard. Abnormal: 100% keyboard and 100% mouse with no output, which is likely fake time tracking or off-task behaviour. [L0263, 2022]
- Use Hubstaff to detect when someone fakes time tracking, for example by playing videos or using software to simulate activity. It is not used as a pay-per-second basis, but as activity validation. [L0263, 2022]

### Access, backups and handover

- Give the virtual assistant limited access to the Shopify admin panel, only to the parts needed to see order status and enter tracking information. Do not give full admin access, the same limited-access approach used for phone support staff. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Rewinds is a Shopify app for backing up your store data before granting contractors access. (2024) [L0276, 2024]

### Research VPNs

- A VPN can be used during market research to see the search results and shopping results for the United States, if Google shows results for your own country instead. Any reputable VPN works, free or paid. (2020) [L0468, 2020]
- A VPN or proxy set to another country shows Google search results as local shoppers see them. Use it to check foreign competitors and Shopping ads from outside that country. (2024) [L0806, 2024]
- A VPN lets you see Google Shopping results as if you were in another country, which helps with price research where Google Shopping is unavailable locally. Proton VPN is free or costs about $10 per month. (2021) [L0853, 2021]
- ExpressVPN and similar VPN services change the apparent country of the connection. Use them to see country-specific search results, such as when the Google Shopping tab is missing in the research country. (2022) [L0963, 2022]
- ExpressVPN is a tool for securely connecting to the internet when working remotely from different countries. [L0926, 2018]
- ExpressVPN can be used to view your Google Ads or store as shown in your target country when you are abroad, because geo-targeted ads will not display to a user outside the country. Use it carefully, because a VPN can trigger account security checks. [L1441, 2018]
- NordVPN was named as the VPN to use on a desktop to access a US-region TikTok Ads Manager from a UK location. Reports on this setup were mixed and described as prone to issues, so test it with a small budget before relying on it. [L1186, 2022]
- A VPN subscription can be used to log in with an IP from the target country, such as Australia, New Zealand, the UK or an EU country, before opening TikTok Ads Manager or TikTok Business Center. A yearly NordVPN plan was cited at about $1 to $2. Turn the VPN on before starting the session, not afterward. [L1377, 2022+]
- A VPN can be used to set an Instagram account to a target country, for example the US, so organic reels reach that audience. [L1212, undated]
