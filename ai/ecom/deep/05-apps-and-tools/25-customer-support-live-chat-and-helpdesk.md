## Customer Support, Live Chat and Helpdesk

### Choosing a live chat tool

- Live chat lets visitors message you during the shopping session, so you can answer objections and give immediate support, which supports conversion. [L0040, 2020-26]
- For a beginner on Shopify, Shopify Inbox is preferred over Tidio because it is simpler to set up. Tidio offers more features and automations but is more complicated to configure. [L0661, undated]
- Shopify Inbox's main advantage over Tidio is phone push notifications, which let the store owner answer customer questions quickly. [L1220, undated]
- One 2024 setup uses Shopify Inbox for live chat with customers instead of Tidio. [L0360, 2024]
- A live chat app can be used from a phone like a text conversation, so the owner can answer pre-sale questions such as whether an item is in stock. Put a live chat link on the product page. [L1346, 2020-26]
- Shopify live chat is the support channel for store-level problems, such as theme or code settings that affect how the store appears in search results. [L1090, 2021]

### Shopify Inbox

#### What it is and what it costs

- Shopify Inbox is Shopify's built-in live chat sales channel. It is free of charge ($0) and included with the store, so no paid third-party live chat app is needed. It is not active by default and must be added as a sales channel. [L0948, 2020-26]
- Shopify Inbox is a free live chat app from Shopify that adds a chat widget to the storefront. It is included with the Shopify plan at no extra cost. [L1311, 2020-26]
- Shopify Inbox is included with all Shopify plans and lets customers message you directly from your website in real time. [L0151, undated]
- The Shopify Inbox app is free. Install it early so that replies can be sent quickly from a phone. [L0661, undated]
- Shopify Inbox works like a live chat and sends push notifications to a phone whenever a customer asks a question. It is installed in the store and in the Shopify mobile app. [L1220, undated]
- Shopify Inbox replaces the need for Tidio for Shopify stores. [L1432, undated]
- Shopify Inbox asks for an email address before the chat begins. [L0856, 2024]
- The chat widget appears on every store page, so visitors can start a conversation at any time. Messages appear in Shopify Admin and are pushed to your phone through the mobile app. [L0151, undated]
- Shopify Inbox can replace third-party live chat apps. [L0971, 2024]

#### Install: route A (Apps menu, short version)

1. Log in to Shopify Admin.
2. Click Apps.
3. Search for "Shopify Inbox".
4. Select the app (free plan).
5. Click Install. [L0151, undated]

#### Install: route B (Apps menu, detailed version)

1. Log into Shopify Admin.
2. Click Apps.
3. Search for "Inbox"; the listing shows the label "Free plan available".
4. Click Install; the app is added to the store.
5. Optionally pin the "Inbox" entry in the sidebar navigation so it is easy to find.
6. Click Inbox in the sidebar and click "Turn on chat".
7. Customize the widget in the theme customizer (see the App embeds settings).
8. Click Save.
9. Reload the storefront to confirm the chat icon is visible. [L1311, 2020-26]

#### Install: route C (Sales channels)

1. Log into the Shopify admin.
2. In the left navigation, open Sales channels.
3. Click the plus icon, search for "inbox" and select Shopify Inbox.
4. Confirm with yes to grant the app access to store data.
5. Shopify Inbox then appears under Sales channels. [L0948, 2020-26]

Install in the Shopify App Store by searching "Shopify Inbox" and adding it to the store. Then install the Shopify Inbox app on the Shopify mobile app. [L1220, undated]

#### Turning chat on

- Under Sales channels > Shopify Inbox > Online store chat, chat is off by default. Click Manage settings and turn it on to activate the chat on the storefront. Setup takes about five minutes. [L0948, 2020-26]
- In Shopify Admin, Inbox > "Turn on chat" opens the theme customizer, where the chat widget is placed on the storefront. The widget does not appear until chat is turned on. [L1311, 2020-26]

#### Verify on the live storefront

To verify Shopify Inbox chat on the storefront after saving the settings:

1. Open a private (incognito) browser window.
2. Load the live store URL.
3. Check that the chat icon appears in the bottom right corner.
4. If it is missing, wait a few minutes, since setting changes can take time to show on the store. [L0948, 2020-26]

To test the full chain:

1. Open the storefront, click the chat icon and send a test question.
2. Enter a name and email address when the form appears and click "Start chat".
3. Open Shopify Inbox in the admin and confirm the conversation is listed.
4. Confirm the message arrives as a notification in the mobile app.
5. Reply from the admin and check that the reply shows in the mobile app as well. [L1311, 2020-26]

#### Common mistakes

- If chat is not switched on in the Inbox app, no chat icon appears on the storefront, so visitors cannot reach you. After switching it on, always check the live storefront to confirm the icon is visible. [L1311, 2020-26]
- The admin bar at the bottom of the storefront is visible only to logged-in admin users. Testing while logged in shows a view that visitors do not see. Hide the bar or test in a logged-out browser window to see the real visitor experience. [L1311, 2020-26]

#### Mobile app

- The Shopify Inbox mobile app (iOS and Android) sends push notifications when customers message, and lets you respond from anywhere by logging in with your Shopify credentials. [L0151, undated]
- Shopify Inbox has a separate mobile app for iPhone (Apple App Store) and Android. It is signed in with the same Shopify username and password used for the admin. [L1311, 2020-26]

To install the mobile app:

1. Install the Shopify Inbox app on the phone from the Apple App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Log in with the Shopify username and password.
3. Confirm that incoming chats arrive as phone notifications and appear in the app. [L1311, 2020-26]

The mobile app lets the owner answer messages from a phone, and the chat box can be turned off in its settings when the owner is unavailable. [L0971, 2024]

#### Responses, AI and availability

- Shopify Inbox responses can include text replies, links, discount codes, images, and emojis. [L0151, undated]
- Enable instant answers by turning on "Track my order", "shipping details responses", and "return policy responses" so the AI pulls answers automatically from your store pages. [L0151, undated]
- Shopify Magic is Shopify's AI tool integrated into Shopify Inbox. It can offer automatic instant answers, for example to contact-info questions (sourced from the contact page) and return-policy questions (sourced from the return policy page). [L0948, 2020-26]
- Set chat availability to 24/7 so visitors can message at any time. They can still leave messages outside business hours. The automatic first reply is optional, and marketing opt-in can remain enabled. [L0151, undated]

### Tidio

Sources disagree on the preferred tool. Recommendations from 2019 to 2021 favour Tidio. The beginner preference for Shopify Inbox appears in a 2024 source and in the one source course. Tidio has more features and automations but is harder to configure, so use Shopify Inbox for simple setup and Tidio when you need its extra features. [L0661, undated; L0360, 2024; L1002, 2019; L0663, 2019]

- Tidio is a live chat option for smaller stores. The presenter did not recommend the live chat tool his company uses, since it is built for large volume. [L0663, 2019]
- Tidio is the live chat software recommended for the store. Live chats and inquiries are an early sign that visitors are engaging with the site. [L1002, 2019]
- Tidio is a live chat app for Shopify stores. Avoid the native Facebook Messenger chat integration on Shopify. [L0852, 2020]
- Tidio has a free plan. Start with it and upgrade only when needed, without using the chatbot. [L0834, 2021]
- Tidio's free live chat can run alongside Klaviyo for email. Do not place discount codes in the chatbot greeting, because discounts already appear across the site. [L0247, 2021]
- Tidio is a live chat app that notifies you of incoming chats on mobile and can capture email addresses to import into Shopify. [L0962, 2021]
- Tidio can route live chat messages to the owner's phone so chats are answered as they arrive, which allows chat support outside office hours. [L1113, 2022]
- Tidio Live Chat adds a live chat widget to a Shopify store so visitors can contact you directly while browsing. [L1399, undated]
- ChatGPT can generate automatic replies for a live chat widget such as Tidio. This lets a store answer common visitor questions without a human agent being online. [L1416, 2023+]

### talk.to and tawk.to

- talk.to is a Shopify live chat app that lets visitors ask questions on a product page, and other Shopify live chat apps offer similar functions. Chat messages can reach the store owner's phone as a text or Facebook message, so quick replies from a phone are possible. [L1110, 2019]
- Talk.to (tawk.to) is a live chat app for Shopify that is low-cost and performs well on both chat functionality and page speed. [L1298, 2021-22]
- The tawk.to dashboard shows live visitors, page views, chats (answered and missed), engagement, availability and positive sentiment, with reporting for today or the last 7 days. Active chats open in a window in the dashboard for day-to-day handling. [L1298, 2021-22]
- tawk.to offers paid add-ons for removing branding, screen sharing, video and audio calls, or hiring agents to answer chats for $1 per hour. These are optional and not needed to launch. [L1298, 2021-22]

### Gorgias chat and helpdesk

#### Set up the Gorgias chat widget

1. In Settings, open Channels and click Chat.
2. Click Add chat.
3. Enter a chat title.
4. Select the platform type.
5. Connect a store.
6. Choose whether to allow live chat messages or only offline messages.
7. Click Create and customize. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Appearance

Settings > Chat > Appearance > Installation. Fields:

- Chat title.
- Language (English (US)).
- Intro message for business hours (for example "How can we help?") and outside business hours (for example "We'll be back tomorrow").
- Colors (main color and conversation color), font, company logo, and widget position on the website.

Labels, automated messages and offline capture subjects can be customized to match the brand's tone of voice. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Preferences

Settings > Chat > Preferences. Choose when live chat is available, for example only when agents are online. Outside those hours the widget shows the built quick responses and lets the visitor leave an email address for later contact. Also configure email capture, an autoresponder, and forwarding of chat replies to the customer's email. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Installation on Shopify

- 1-click installation for Shopify adds the widget to the store in one step and automatically enables Automation Add-on features if available. [L0752, 2025-26]
- Placement options are Show on every page, Show on specific pages, or Hide on specific pages. [L0752, 2025-26]
- Manual installation via embed code is used for other platforms or specific pages. [L0752, 2025-26]
- A store connection is required for Automation Add-on features in chat and for 1-click installation. [L0752, 2025-26]
- A single chat widget is enough for most brands. Separate chats can be created for different purposes if needed. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Campaigns

Gorgias chat campaigns prompt a message based on the page URL and can add conditions such as time spent on page. The example campaign shows a greeting when a visitor arrives on the site. The campaign name, audience (current URL), conditions and message text are set in the campaign editor, and the sender is a random agent's first name. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Offer

With a three-month plan, the second and third month of the Automation Add-on are free. For new Gorgias users, an annual plan gives two extra months of the regular platform free. [L0752, 2025-26]

#### Gorgias helpdesk

- Gorgias is the helpdesk used to find customer tickets and emails, to hold saved macros such as `CHARGEBACK - No Ticket`, and to print or save tickets as PDF evidence. Any email platform works if it can do the same. [L0350, 2025-26]
- Gorgias connects to Shopify so that the customer profile shows customer type (for example VIP), email, phone number, total spent, and the order list with payment status (for example PAID) and fulfillment status (for example FULFILLED). The Shopify integration allows creating orders from within the helpdesk. [L0753, 2025-26]
- Gorgias centralizes all customer conversations and customer data in one platform. Integrations with e-commerce apps keep workflows connected, so support and sales run from a single tool. [L0753, 2025-26]
- The Gorgias AI agent gives instant, personalized replies across the whole customer journey and chooses the right channel for each shopper at the right moment. It is used for both pre-purchase questions and post-purchase support. [L0753, 2025-26]
- The Satisfaction view under Statistics shows a "Latest Surveys" table with the columns Customer, Assignee, Date, Score and Comment, plus a "Go to ticket" link on each row. Use the link to open the original conversation behind a low score. [L0401, 2025-26]

### Shared inboxes and helpdesks

- Reamaze is a customer communication platform that centralizes all messages in one inbox. It houses an FAQ library, stores response templates, provides live chat, and integrates abandoned cart recovery. [L0230, 2019]
- Reamaze is a customer service software that handles emails, chats and social media messages in one list, showing who is working on each case. It should be adopted once the team has one to three people, since a shared inbox in Gmail becomes inefficient and loses track of who sent what. [L0337, 2022]
- Reamaze (spelled "Remaze" in the source) is a help desk inbox connected to Shopify that gathers email, social media messages and online chat in one place. Agents can see order status, change or cancel orders from the inbox, add internal notes and set follow-up reminders. It also stores reply templates that insert fields such as the customer name or tracking number automatically. [L1473, 2019]
- Zendesk is used for internal customer communication. Zendesk Chat is used on many of the stores, but it is described as overpriced and outdated for most sellers. [L0852, 2020]
- Zendesk is used as the customer email interface for support. Customer replies and outgoing support emails can land in spam, so check the spam folder for missing replies. (2024.) [L1285, 2024]
- Zendesk charges per seat, and a 20-person team made it expensive. Switching to Help Scout cut the monthly support software cost by about $1,000. (2025.) [L0807, 2025]

### Answering support requests and saved macros

- Shipment progress can be shown to customers through a multi-carrier tracking page such as 17track, where the tracking number is entered in the link. Agents attach a screenshot of the current status to the reply so the customer sees the same information. [L0405, undated]
- Reply templates in Reamaze insert fields such as the customer name or tracking number automatically. [L1473, 2019]
- Gorgias holds saved macros such as `CHARGEBACK - No Ticket`. [L0350, 2025-26]

### Chargeback documentation

- In Gorgias, find the customer's tickets and emails, apply the saved macro `CHARGEBACK - No Ticket` where it fits, and print or save the tickets as PDF evidence. Any email platform works if it can do the same. [L0350, 2025-26]

### Call tracking and phone support

- Mighty Call (mightycall.com) provides call tracking and chat monitoring. It shows call logs, how many calls were answered and how many chats came in, which lets the owner check a phone or chat team's performance. [L0467, 2019]
- MightyCall records phone calls and tracks how many calls are answered. This helps verify that a virtual assistant doing customer service actually answers calls. [L0854, 2022]

### Offline and after-hours messages

- Set up your online chat tool to take a message when no agent is available and forward it to your email. The forwarded message should include the visitor's question, their email address and any phone number they gave. [L0909, 2021-22]
- Gorgias shows the built quick responses outside chat hours and lets the visitor leave an email address for later contact. [L0752, 2025-26]
- Gorgias intro messages can differ for business hours and outside business hours. [L0752, 2025-26]
- Shopify Inbox chat availability can be set to 24/7, and visitors can still leave messages outside business hours. [L0151, undated]
- Turn off the Shopify Inbox chat box in its settings when the owner is unavailable. [L0971, 2024]

### CRM and when one is warranted

#### Current practice for small stores

- Keep customer data in Shopify and Klaviyo rather than a separate enterprise CRM such as Salesforce, which is not designed for drop-ship stores of this type. [L0468, 2020]
- A small store can manage customer profiles with Shopify's built-in customer records and send all customer email from a business Gmail account, without a separate CRM. [L0666, 2024]

#### Named CRM options

- Copper is a CRM that integrates with Gmail and suits a small team that wants a light, easy system. It is one option among several for organizing customer contacts. [L0949, 2021-22]
- Gorgeous is a customer-journey tool built for Shopify that offers more than the default Shopify features. It suits larger stores better than new ones, and users' opinions of it vary widely. [L0949, 2021-22]
- ActiveCampaign is a light CRM with strong email automation at a reasonable price and some CRM functions. At the time of the lesson it lacked the direct Shopify integration that tools such as Klaviyo and Drip offered. [L0949, 2021-22]
- Trello boards are a simple way to organize customer follow-ups and tasks in a small business. They are useful as a stopgap before a full CRM is justified. [L0949, 2021-22]

#### When a CRM is warranted

- A CRM is only needed when the Shopify-plus-Gmail setup stops covering the workflow. [L0666, 2024]
- Use Trello as a stopgap before a full CRM is justified. [L0949, 2021-22]
- Introducing a complex CRM before you have consistent sales adds work without a return, because good CRM tools are hard to set up and need data to be useful. Start with simpler tracking until sales are steady. [L0949, 2021-22]
- High-ticket stores have far fewer orders than typical e-commerce stores, so their customer records grow slowly. A simple tracking system is often enough for a long time, even at high revenue. [L0949, 2021-22]
