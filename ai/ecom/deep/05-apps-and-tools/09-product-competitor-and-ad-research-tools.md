## Product, Competitor and Ad Research Tools

### Choosing tools and limiting spend

- Pay for a research tool for about one month, then cancel if it does not produce useful products in that time. Use a strict rule: invest in one or two tools to find around 10 to 50 products in a month. [L0335, 2023]
- Start with only the one or two research tools you will use every week, because each subscription is a fixed monthly cost that must be covered before any sales arrive. [L1387, 2026]
- An ad spy tool is only worth its cost when you have a specific product or niche in mind, such as checking which shoes, slippers or bags perform best in one market before selling in another. For general product research without a clear goal, it is not worth the expense. [L1017, undated]
- Research and spy tools often cost $15 to $99 per month each, and some cost more, such as Pipiads or PPSpy at about $263 and $299 per month on the list prices shown. A new store should budget these as fixed monthly costs before it has revenue to cover them. [L1387, 2026]
- Pick one spy tool and use it consistently for product searches and keyword checks. The lesson names Dropispy, Peeksta, Adspy and Minea as options. [L0636, undated]
- Saturation-checking tools can help screen competition, but they do not replace a small paid test. The Facebook data from your own ads decides whether a product works. [L1161, 2024+]
- Some sellers hide their store from spy tools by paying the tool operators to remove them, or by adding code to the store that blocks scraping. The mentor reported these methods but has not tested them. [L0335, 2023]

### Niche validation and market data

- The one source Research Tool provides real data from trusted sources for niche research and saves hundreds of hours of manual research. Accessed via the online platform, it displays grade, average price, demographic appeal, brand loyalty, seasonality and demand metrics for any niche you enter. Use it for all market data in your niche selection process. [L0045, undated]
- The one source research software has unlimited use and returns a niche's monthly Google search volume, seasonality verdict and average price analysis within seconds. It can test any number of niches, not only a short shortlist. [L0451, 2020-26]
- Set up the research workspace with the worksheet on the left half of the screen and the research tool on the right half in split-screen view, or open the tool in its own browser tab. Copy each niche keyword from the worksheet and paste it into the tool without switching back and forth. [L1081, 2020-26]
- one source Research Software is a one source research tool named in an October 2025 coaching call. The document only lists it and does not describe its features. [L1106, 2025]
- Research tools for members are at the source's own site and use the same login as the blueprint course. [L0969, 2022]
- Niche Verification is a tool for checking whether a niche is worth pursuing before committing money and time to it. [L0851, 2025]
- Google Trends is the primary tool for checking demand and seasonality for a product. Set the region and a five-year time range to see whether demand is steady, spiky or truly seasonal. [L1090, 2021]
- The Fast Business Forum (fastbusinessforum.com) is used to search for niche selection advice from other sellers. You need to be logged in to post, and private messages are sent by clicking a member's name. [L1085, 2018]

### Free ad libraries

#### Meta Ad Library and Facebook Ad Library

- The Meta Ad Library is a free tool that shows active ads from advertisers, including how many ads a creative runs in. Use it to study competitors, find creatives that run in many ads and check which hooks big brands keep using. [L0398, 2023+]
- The Meta Ad Library shows every active ad a brand runs, with start date, format and copy. It is reached from a competitor's page under Page transparency, and the country can be changed to show regional ads. [L0649, 2019]
- The Meta Ad Library is a free tool that shows all running ads. Filter by country, search by keyword or brand, and screenshot ads for inspiration. [L0972, 2025]
- The Meta Ad Library is a free, public Meta database where you can search currently running ads by keyword and see which pages and stores run them. Use it to find competitor stores through the ads they run. [L1249, 2026]
- The Facebook Ad Library lets you search by country, category and keyword or advertiser name to see the ads a competitor is running on Facebook. [L0855, 2023]
- Check competitor ads for the same niche title in the TikTok Ads Library and the Facebook Ads Library as well as the Meta Ad Library. Bookmark them next to Kalodata for quick access. [L0354, 2026]
- Use the Ads Library filters active ads, sort by active ads and see summary details. Use the link from each ad to check the store and its other ads. [L1173, undated]
- AdLib integrates with the Facebook Ad Library. Click download in AdLib to save videos and thumbnails directly, or use the Facebook Ad Library link for manual downloads. [L0209, 2023]
- Winning Hunter offers a free Chrome extension that works with the Meta Ad Library. Install it when you want to pull ads from the Meta Ad Library into Winning Hunter's analysis views. [L0772, 2026]

#### Ad spy tools in general

- Spy tools track competitor ads and creatives, showing which ads are running for big brands. Use them to find the creatives a niche is scaling before you test similar hooks. [L0398, 2023+]
- Ad spy tools such as Pinterest, Snapchat and TikTok searches can show winning products. The speaker runs Facebook and TikTok side by side to see which platform works better for a product. [L0690, 2022+]
- Spy tools such as Minea and Dropispy show ads and competitor products across platforms and are used for product research. [L0788, undated]
- Spy tools show what competitors advertise and which products they sell. Tools such as Foreplay, Adspy and Dropispy are used for this kind of ad and product research. [L1387, 2026]

### Paid Meta and Facebook ad spy tools

#### AfterLive

- AfterLive builds extra filters on top of the Meta Ad Library data, such as active ads count, country, platform and a 30-day performance view. The mentor considered it the best tool for finding recent ads on Meta. It starts at about $50 to $80 per month, with a free trial. Other spy tools mostly show older ads, and some sellers can block them. [L0335, 2023]
- The source names this tool Afterlip, with a $9 per month plan, down from $99 per month. Its features may differ from the full plan shown in the tutorial, so compare the plan's feature list with the tutorial before assuming the same functions are available. [L0349, 2024]
- AfterLive setup: Technology set to Shopify; countries set to United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia; call-to-action set to Shop Now or Learn More; Active Ads minimum set to 10; Performance filter over the last 30 days. [L0335, 2023]
- The Performance view shows a progression of new active ads per day over the chosen period, so you can see a competitor's launch pattern. The Trending view highlights competitors scaling aggressively in a shorter time. A 7-day data option is available for very recent winners. [L0335, 2023]

#### DropiSpy

- DropiSpy research setup: date filter set to the last one month; likes, shares and comments thresholds; e-com platform set to Shopify; call-to-action set to Shop Now or Learn More; countries set to United States, Canada, Australia and United Kingdom. [L0335, 2023]
- DropiSpy costs about $29 per month at list price, or about $15 to $20 per month with a discount code. Rental platforms that resell tool logins sometimes list it for about $2 to $10 per month. [L0335, 2023]
- The bundle price capture lists Dropispy at $249 per month. The two figures conflict; the $29 list price applies to the provider's own plan, while $249 is the bundle's list price. Check the current price on the provider's site before buying. [L0335, 2023; L1387, 2026]

#### AdSpy

- AdSpy is a paid Facebook ad spy tool costing $150 per month. It lets you search by keyword, filter by a date range such as ads started since January 1, and sort ads by likes or shares to spot trends. [L0388, 2024+]
- AdSpy is an ad library for searching competitor ads by keyword, age, gender, country and store URL. A free account gives up to 20 pages of search per day. [L0661, undated]
- If AdSpy asks you to buy an account, do not pay for it. Create a new account and use the free search. If your email was used on an earlier account, sign up with a new email address. [L0661, undated]
- AdSpy finds older Facebook ads, such as those from 2019 and 2020. It was considered expensive, so compare its monthly cost with alternatives such as Peeksta. [L0706, 2020+]

#### Peeksta

- Peeksta (peeksta.com) and adtargeting.io help find niche-specific Meta interests for single-interest test ad sets. [L0376, undated]
- Peeksta is an ad spy tool used to find older Facebook ads, such as those from 2019 and 2020. It was presented as a cheaper alternative to Adspy, at about $50 per month. The price figures in the transcript are inconsistent, so check the current price before subscribing. [L0706, 2020+]
- Peeksta is used for product research, to find products that are performing well. It is a paid-style research tool referenced for locating winning products. [L1367, undated]

#### AfterLib

- AfterLib is a paid product research tool, costing about $150 per month, that ranks products by performance or by number of active ads. It is used to build the first candidate list before checking each product manually in the Facebook Ad Library. [L1172, 2023+]
- AfterLib is a paid ad spy tool recommended for its large library and data on how competitors scale their ads. It shows an ad's progression, meaning the growing number of ads per creative over time, which signals a profitable product. [L1174, undated]
- AfterLib shows how the ad activity of a product changes over time. Use it to spot products whose ads have risen by roughly 100 to 300 percent in the last 14 days. [L1215, 2023+]
- AfterLib is considered expensive compared with Peeksta, but it offers a free trial of about 7 to 14 days. Its data is pulled from the Ad Library itself, and experienced sellers consider its sales tracker accurate. [L1177, 2023]
- AfterLib sign-up accepts a public discount code, SAVE10, for 10 percent off. Sellers who want 25 to 30 percent can ask the vendor's support team directly for a larger discount. [L1177, 2023]
- AfterLib shared-access membership: a team plan allows several people to log in to one AfterLib account. The reported price for one person's shared access is $6.99. [L1216, undated]

AfterLib filter setup:

1. Click All filters. [L1174, undated]
2. Under Technology, choose Shopify. [L1174, undated]
3. Under Country, select the target country; for a general setup use United States, United Kingdom, Canada, New Zealand and Australia. [L1174, undated]
4. Under Language, choose English. [L1174, undated]
5. Under Call to action, choose Shop now and Learn more. [L1174, undated]
6. Under Creation date, prefer the last 30 to 45 days to find recently launched ads (results are already sorted by performance, so this step is less critical). [L1174, undated]

AfterLib steps to reach the competitor's Facebook Ad Library page:

1. Click the three dots next to the product result. [L1174, undated]
2. Click Go to meta page to open the competitor's Facebook Ad Library page. [L1174, undated]
3. Apply the Active ads filter. [L1174, undated]
4. Count the active ads for the product only. [L1174, undated]
5. Subtract any active ads that belong to other products the competitor is testing on the same page, then record the adjusted number. [L1174, undated]

#### Mynia

- Mynia is an alternative ad spy tool with data for Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok. It shows likes, comments, shares, page creation date, the audience targeted and other competitors fetched for the same search. [L1174, undated]
- Mynia combines Pinterest, Facebook and TikTok ads in one place. It is used to spot new products and see which competitors run ads across platforms. [L1377, 2022+]

Mynia filter setup for Facebook product research:

1. Log in and open the ad section, then select Facebook. [L1174, undated]
2. Under date, choose the creation date filter for the last 30 days. [L1174, undated]
3. Under CTA, choose Buy now, Shop now and Learn more. [L1174, undated]
4. Under ecommerce platform, choose Shopify. [L1174, undated]
5. Under language, choose English. [L1174, undated]
6. Set engagement minimums: at least 200 likes, at least 100 comments and at least 100 shares. [L1174, undated]
7. Select countries: United States, United Kingdom, Australia and Canada. [L1174, undated]

Mynia has no direct link to the competitor's Ad Library, so use this path:

1. Open the ad post in Mynia. [L1174, undated]
2. Click the page name. [L1174, undated]
3. Open the About section and then Page transparency. [L1174, undated]
4. Click See all and scroll down to Go to ad library. [L1174, undated]
5. On the Ad Library page, click active ads and count the ads for the specific product. [L1174, undated]

### Winning Hunter

- Winning Hunter is the AI product research tool recommended for finding candidate products. It is preferred over Kalodata, which was used earlier. [L0491, 2026]
- Winning Hunter is preferred for product research, but Kalodata remains useful as one signal source alongside TikTok Shop and competitor stores. [L0491, 2026]
- The Brands tracker shows one overview of a Meta advertiser: top ad copies, headlines, hooks, landing pages, personas, concepts, angles, desires, emotions, awareness stages and USPs, pulled from live ads by default. Each brand can be set to track Global Ads or EU/UK-only ads. [L0374, 2026]
- Newly added brands can take hours before all sections of the overview are filled in. [L0772, 2026]
- The brand tracker covers Meta advertisers only, and each brand page links to the same ads in the Meta Ad Library. Check the ads directly in the Ad Library before relying on the tracker data. [L0374, 2026]
- Each breakdown tab in a brand's overview (ad copies, headlines, hooks, landing pages, personas, concepts, angles, desires, emotions, awareness stages, USPs) offers time filters 7D, 30D, 3M, 6M, Custom and All, plus an Export CSV button. Sort options include Usage and longest running. [L0374, 2026]
- Winning Hunter lets you export a brand's top hooks, personas and angles. Feeding that export into Claude AI can uncover additional personas and angles. [L0772, 2026]
- Quick actions for a brand: open Brands, select the brand and use the Quick Actions menu. Track brand follows the advertiser, and Track store does the same for a Shopify store. Visit Product Page opens the store. Download Video and Download Thumbnail save creative files. Transcribe ad with AI produces a script of the ad. Other listed actions include Page Ad Library and Fulfill with Ecommerce. [L0374, 2026; L0772, 2026]
- The ad details panel shows running days, the number of duplicates (other advertisers using the same creative), the landing page URL, the product price, estimated monthly traffic and the first and last seen dates. Use these fields to judge how long and how widely an ad has run. [L0374, 2026]
- Winning Hunter's Ad Library filter limits results to one ad per brand by default, so you see fewer winning ads than exist. Turn that limit off when you want the full set of matches for a niche search. The limit is currently active, so expect smaller result counts when scanning. [L0374, 2026; L0772, 2026]
- The Ad Library search in the brand tool has a source dropdown with Meta Ads Library (marked recommended), Facebook Adspy, Pinterest Ads and TikTok Ads. Start with Meta Ads Library when you need ads that run on Meta. [L0374, 2026]
- The Ad Library filter panel has a Save current filters button, so a niche search can be reused without setting every field again. Preset shortcuts also exist for Week's biggest winners, European winners, US winners, Dropship Ads, Advertorials, Native ads, Quiz Funnels, Supplements and Top Branded. [L0374, 2026]
- Tracked brands can be sorted into folders and boards to keep competitor research organized. The account in the example allowed up to 200 brands and displayed the count as used of limit, such as 2 / 200 brands. [L0374, 2026]
- Brands Tracker lets you follow Meta advertisers, organize them into folders and boards, and track growth, formats, new ads and added dates per brand. The lesson shows a counter of 13 / 200 brands, which suggests a cap on how many brands one account can follow. [L0892, 2026]
- Winning Hunter Brands lets you follow Meta advertisers, organize them into folders and boards, and open each brand's details. The counter shows how many of the 200 brand slots are in use. [L0772, 2026]
- The Transcribe ad with AI action sits in the Quick Actions menu of an ad. Longer videos can take about a minute to produce the transcript. Wait until the full script appears before copying it, then check it against the video. [L0374, 2026]
- The ad details panel and Magic AI offer discovery shortcuts, Find Competitors with Magic AI and Find Shopify Competitors with AI. Use them to move from one winning ad to other advertisers and stores in the same space. Use them as an alternative way to discover competing brands for a product you already want to research. [L0374, 2026; L0772, 2026]
- Winning Hunter ad cards have three action buttons. Save ad keeps it in your library. Track in Brand Tracker opens the advertiser's full ad set. Recreate ad with AI generates a new version of the ad. Use Save and Track to build a swipe file of competitor ads before you write your own creatives. [L1164, 2026]
- Winning Hunter combines Brand Tracker, which shows a brand's personas, USPs, angles and ad copies, with Sales Tracker, which shows a store's revenue estimate, traffic, apps and pixels. Use it to go from an ad found in the Meta Ads Library to a full competitor analysis. [L1164, 2026]
- Winning Hunter also includes Trends, similar to Exploding Topics, and an AI ad image generator. The lesson presents ad research as the most used part of the platform. [L0892, 2026]
- Unlimited searches in the TikTok Shop explorer of Winning Hunter are a Premium-plan feature, so heavy product research needs the Premium tier. The explorer also lists trending products, top sellers and new arrivals with the same filters as the main product table. [L0892, 2026]
- Winning Hunter is listed at $90 per month on the provider's on-screen list but is marked as not included on its sales page. Check the current list and price on the provider's pricing page before budgeting. [L1387, 2026]
