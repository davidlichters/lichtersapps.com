### Running a Keyword Search in Ubersuggest

1. Open Keyword Ideas. [L1486, 2021-22]
2. Enter the keyword in the search field. [L1486, 2021-22]
3. Choose the country from the dropdown before clicking Search, because volumes are country-specific. [L1486, 2021-22]
4. Click Search. [L1486, 2021-22]
5. Read the results in the tabs SUGGESTIONS, RELATED, QUESTIONS and PREPOSITIONS. [L1486, 2021-22]
6. Export the list: click EXPORT TO CSV to download the keyword list with its volumes. [L1486, 2021-22]
7. Use the Filters button and the COMPARISONS tab, next to the export button, to narrow and compare keyword sets. [L1486, 2021-22]

### Reading the Keyword Columns

| Column | What it shows | Use |
|---|---|---|
| KEYWORD | The keyword suggestion | The list to work through |
| VOL | Estimated searches per month in the selected country [L1486, 2021-22] | Demand for the keyword |
| CPC | Typical cost per click for the keyword [L1486, 2021-22] | Planning paid ads later [L1486, 2021-22] |

### Assessing Competition with the Keyword Overview

- The Keyword Overview panel lists the Google top 10 results for a keyword, with their backlinks and domain authority. [L1486, 2021-22]
- The average values for the top 10 pages indicate how hard the keyword is to rank for and how much competition there is. [L1486, 2021-22]

### Trends and Difficulty

- Trends shows how search volume for a keyword changes over time. [L1486, 2021-22]
- Trends can show whether demand for a keyword is rising or falling. [L1486, 2021-22]
- Difficulty helps find keywords that are easier to rank for. [L1486, 2021-22]

#### Further details

- The Yoast SEO plugin for WordPress checks each article against SEO steps and shows a green or red light, for example whether the keyword appears in the headline. Its fit with Shopify was not confirmed in the lesson. [L0382, 2017]

- For keyword research, use free tools such as Google Keyword Planner or Ubersuggest, which provide search volume data without a subscription (2020 information; verify current access). [L0468, 2020]

- Ubersuggest is a lower-cost alternative to Ahrefs that can produce most of the same keyword, competitor and backlink reports needed for the skyscraper method. [L1388, 2021-22]
