#### Bundle Bear

- Bundle Bear is a Shopify app for volume-discount bundles, such as buy-two-get-a-percentage-off deals (for example 40% off). It is one of the common apps for this offer type. [L1345, 2021+]
- Bundle Bear creates product bundles with volume discounts and editable offer text. You can change the button text, for example from "Add to cart" to "Get my deal". The lesson reports high conversion with it and calls it simple to use. [L1412, 2023]
- Bundle Bear shows revenue per offer, so you can see which offer performs. [L1411, undated]
- Example tiers from Bundle Bear are buy 2 get 10% off and buy 3 get 15% off. [L1411, undated]
- Bundle Bear can combine products on the same product page or with items from another collection, for example a ring with a matching necklace. One store reported a clear rise in average order value after adding it. [L1428, undated]
- Bundle Bear's drawback is that it creates a discount for every order that already has a discount, which one seller found annoying. [L1428, undated]
- **Installation conflict:** The sources disagree. Two lessons say Bundle Bear needs a code line pasted into the Shopify product page template. [L1410, 2023; L1412, 2023] A third says it needs no added code, installs on any theme and works with page builders such as PageFly. [L1411, undated] Use the code-line route if you edit the product template directly. Use the no-code route if you build the page with a page builder. [L1410, 2023; L1412, 2023; L1411, undated]

#### Procedure: first volume-discount offer

1. Install a bundle or volume-discount app from the Shopify App Store. No code is needed, and it works on most themes and page builders. [L1411, undated]
2. Create a volume-discount offer, for example buy 2 get 10% off and buy 3 get 15% off. [L1411, undated]
3. Customize the label and layout of the offer. [L1411, undated]
4. Place the offer on the product page or in the cart drawer. [L1411, undated]
5. Check revenue per offer in the app's analytics and compare offers before keeping one. [L1411, undated]

#### Procedure: compare bundle apps and layouts

1. Search the Shopify App Store for "bundle" or "bundle offer". [L1345, 2021+]
2. Sort or pick the apps with the most reviews. [L1345, 2021+]
3. Open each app and check its demo layout. [L1345, 2021+]
4. Compare the layout with the competitor's bundle layout to find the closest match. [L1345, 2021+]

#### Wide Bundle and Wide Bundles

- Wide Bundle is a Shopify bundle app that shows a bundle layout and volume discount. Its layout can be compared with a competitor's bundle layout to find an inspiration template. [L1345, 2021+]
- Wide Bundles is a Shopify app for bundle and volume-discount offers, with a customizable layout, labels and discount display. It has a 14-day free trial and costs $16 per month after that. [L1411, undated]

#### White Bundles and the Catching or Coaching Bundle app

- White Bundles adds labels such as free shipping and savings text such as "you save $35". [L1410, 2023]
- White Bundles offers more options than the simpler alternatives, such as custom text like "you save X dollars". [L1412, 2023]
- The Coaching Bundles app is cheaper and simpler than Bundle Bear, and it needs no code pasted into the page template. [L1410, 2023; L1412, 2023]
- Catching Bundle has a 14-day free trial and costs $9 per month billed yearly or $14.99 per month billed monthly. It was described as the cheapest of the three bundle apps. [L1411, undated]

#### Kaching Bundles

- Kaching Bundles is a Shopify app for creating product bundles with quantity breaks. It highlights a best-selling bundle option on the product page. [L1324, 2026]
- Install Kaching Bundles when you sell bundles, since bundles are a core part of the store's average order value approach. [L1327, 2026]

#### Frequently Bought Together

- Frequently Bought Together is a Shopify app that adds a bundle box to the product page. The box shows the core product with related products and a bundled price. It can be placed anywhere on the page, usually below the Add to cart button. [L1409, 2021]
- In the box, the core product is labelled "THIS ITEM" and each related product has a selectable checkbox. The customer ticks the add-ons and presses "Add Selected to Cart". The box shows a bundled price for the selected items, and each product can show a sale price beside its regular price. [L1409, 2021]
- Other on-page bundle apps exist and can be used if they work well. Any on-page app should let each core product have its own specific upsells, matching the upsell planner sheet. [L1409, 2021]

### Product-page cross-sells

- Use a "customers who bought this also bought this" style recommendation widget on product pages to drive upsells and multi-product purchases. Shopify apps exist that provide this feature. [L1522, 2021-22]
- Shopify apps can provide both recommendation widgets and product bundles on product pages and on standalone bundle pages. Use them to set up cross-sells and value bundles without custom development. [L1522, 2021-22]

### Free gifts and cart progress

#### Free gift apps

- Easy Gift is a Shopify app that adds a free gift automatically when a product is added to the cart. It shows a pop-up and size selection. [L1315, 2023]
- Monster Cart is a cart app that can add free gifts and bundles. It opens in the cart and offers the free gift for adding to the order. [L1315, 2023; L1363, 2023]
- Free gift apps are optional. The lesson's alternative is to show the free gift in an image on the landing page, with the text "your order comes with a free gift, automatically added to cart" and the gift's title. [L1363, 2023]
- Free gift apps are not interchangeable. Use the one that fits your store and check its behaviour on the product and cart pages. [L1315, 2023]
- Two cart apps may conflict. A pop-up from one free gift app may not work with another cart app's own cart. Test the combination before running traffic. [L1315, 2023]

#### Checking the savings display

- Some gift apps show savings from the full compare-at price instead of the price you actually charge. This overstates the saving and makes the value look inflated. Check that the displayed saving matches the price charged. [L1315, 2023]
- Monster Cart shows savings based on the compare-at price, which some gift apps do not do. [L1315, 2023]

#### Cart progress and free shipping

- Monster Cart Upsell offers free-shipping thresholds, free gifts, percent-off tiers, cross-sells, bundles and pre-selected extras. [L1412, 2023]
- Monster Cart Upsell includes free-shipping upsells as one of its cart offers. [L1410, 2023]
- Monster Cart Upsell can add shipping offers alongside cross-sell items. [L1411, undated]

### Cart drawers and cart upsells

#### Monster Cart Upsell

- Monster Cart Upsell is a cart drawer app for cart upsells, free-shipping upsells, tiered offers, pre-selected add-ons and bundles on the cart page. It has a 10-day free trial and chat support. It is somewhat complex and suits simple funnels. [L1410, 2023]
- Monster Cart Upsell is a Shopify app for the cart drawer. It adds cross-sell items, several products at once, and shipping offers, and it allows detailed customization of the cart messages. [L1411, undated]
- Monster Cart Upsell supports multiple conditions. It is more complex than simple bundle apps, but its chat support can help build funnels. [L1412, 2023]

### Post-purchase upsell apps

#### Zipify One-Click Upsell (OCU)

- *Older advice, 2021-2022:* One-Click Upsell (Zipify OCU) handles pre-purchase and post-purchase upsells, including cart-page upsells. It lets you design the upsell page, set specific upsells per product, and split test offers and quantities. [L1409, 2021]
- Zipify OCU is built by the company Zipify, and Shopify promotes it as part of its own platform. It still has to be installed from the Shopify App Store. [L1409, 2021]
- **Price conflict:** The 2021-2022 source says One-Click Upsell is a paid app from $24.99 per month with a 30-day free trial. Additional charges may apply, and the cost is considered reasonable once the app handles meaningful sales volume. [L1409, 2021] The one source lesson says Zipify OCU has a 30-day free trial and then costs $35 per month, and it is the more advanced option with features such as custom sections. [L1411, undated] The lessons do not say which plan each figure belongs to, so check the current App Store listing before budgeting. [L1409, 2021; L1411, undated]
- Zipify OCU is somewhat more expensive than ReConvert and AfterSale, and it is an alternative to both. [L1412, 2023]
- Other post-purchase upsell apps exist in the Shopify App Store. Search for "post-purchase upsell" and compare them with One-Click Upsell before choosing. [L1409, 2021]

#### ReConvert Upsell

- ReConvert Upsell is a post-purchase upsell app with 24/7 chat support. Its funnel can be duplicated for other offers. [L1410, 2023]
- ReConvert offers post-purchase surveys to learn why a customer bought and their use case, which is useful for Facebook interest targeting. It also offers birthday gift options. [L1410, 2023]
- ReConvert Upsell and Cross Sell builds post-purchase funnels in which each offer leads to a different offer, depending on whether the customer accepts. It supports page and section customization, tutorials and customer support. [L1411, undated]
- Reconvert lets you duplicate a finished funnel and reuse it for other offers. It also supports post-purchase surveys, a birthday gift option, thank-you page customization and 24/7 support. [L1412, 2023]

#### AfterSell

- AfterSell is a cheaper post-purchase upsell app at about $7.99 per month with a 30-day free trial. Each funnel must be built from scratch, unlike ReConvert's duplication feature. [L1410, 2023; L1412, 2023]
- AfterSell is a budget option, suggested for a budget under $20 per month. Its features are similar to ReConvert and Zipify OCU. [L1411, undated]
- The sources spell the app AfterSell, AfterSale and After Sale. They refer to the same app. [L1410, 2023; L1411, undated; L1412, 2023]

### Rebuy: Smart Cart and post-purchase flows

#### Clean up old Smart Cart discounts

1. Set a Smart Cart without product or discount tiers as the live cart. [L1308, 2025-26]
2. Confirm that the discounts no longer apply, because Functions follows the settings of the live cart. [L1308, 2025-26]
3. Open Shopify Admin > Discounts and delete the old Rebuy discounts there. Clicking one redirects to the Rebuy admin. [L1308, 2025-26]

- Deleting or switching a Smart Cart does not remove the old Functions discounts from Shopify Admin automatically. Leftover entries stay in the Discounts list but do not apply when the active cart has no tiers. Delete them manually to keep the list clean. [L1308, 2025-26]

#### Test a Smart Cart copy without affecting live customers

1. Duplicate the live Smart Cart in the Rebuy admin. The test copy has the same layout but no effect on live customers. [L1308, 2025-26]
2. Change the discount tiers in the copy, for example to 4% order discount tiers, and save it. [L1308, 2025-26]
3. Open the store with `preview_smart_cart=<Smart Cart ID>`, `viewcart=true` and `cart_version=2` in the URL. This shows that copy's discounts at checkout, without switching the live cart. [L1308, 2025-26]

#### Post-purchase flows: how they work

- In Rebuy, upsells are offers shown right after a customer accepts an earlier offer. Downsells are offers shown after the customer declines one. Both live inside a post-purchase flow, and the flow pulls in widgets that hold the actual offers. [L1413, 2025-26]
- In the Rebuy admin, open the Post Purchase section in the left navigation. It has two areas. Post Purchase Flows is where the complete upsell and downsell sequences are built. Post Purchase Widgets is where the individual offers are created and then referenced inside flows. [L1413, 2025-26]
- The Rebuy admin left menu lists Dashboard, Widgets, Products, Landing Pages, Data Sources, Reports and Integrations, plus a Post Purchase section. Under Post Purchase, the three areas are Post Purchase Flows, Post Purchase Widgets and Post Purchase Analytics. Help Docs sits under Support. [L1413, 2025-26]
- Each flow in the Post Purchase Flows list shows a status of LIVE or NOT LIVE. Only the flow marked LIVE is shown to customers, so test and draft flows stay NOT LIVE until you switch them on. [L1413, 2025-26]

#### Procedure: build a post-purchase flow

1. Open the Rebuy admin and go to Post Purchase > Post Purchase Flows. [L1413, 2025-26]
2. Open an existing flow or create a new one. [L1413, 2025-26]
3. Set the initial offer, the first widget shown to every customer who reaches the checkout confirmation. [L1413, 2025-26]
4. Add a downsell branch that appears when the initial offer is declined. [L1413, 2025-26]
5. Add an upsell branch that appears when the initial offer is accepted. [L1413, 2025-26]
6. Chain further upsells and downsells off any accepted or declined offer to create multi-layer flows. [L1413, 2025-26]

#### When offers appear

- The initial offer in a post-purchase flow is shown in the Shopify checkout as soon as the customer clicks the Pay Now button. Widget settings and data sources decide which exact offer each customer sees. [L1413, 2025-26]
- Rebuy flows show offers only on the checkout confirmation page. They add revenue without changing the customer's original order. Keep upsell and downsell offers separate in the flow so the customer always sees the next logical step. [L1413, 2025-26]

#### Example: BOGO initial offer and downsell

- A simple initial offer can be a buy-one-get-one deal with 25% off the second product. Its rule can trigger when the cart contains the input product, and it is set up as the first widget in the flow. [L1413, 2025-26]
- Downsell setup: after a customer declines the 25% BOGO initial offer, show a second BOGO offer at 50% off with the message "Last chance, grab it 50% off". This second offer is a downsell because it appears only after a decline. [L1413, 2025-26]
