### Kalodata and TikTok research

#### Kalodata

- Kalodata is a TikTok product and ad research tool. It shows products with their revenue, items sold, average unit price, video and ad counts, and category, and lets you open the ad videos to study their hooks. [L0901, 2026]
- Kalodata shows products with product rank by country, units sold, revenue, average unit price and last-30-day figures, plus linked videos and ads. Use it to check whether a product sells before you invest time in building a store. [L1249, 2026]
- Kalodata setup: set the region to United States and the date range to Last 30 Days, then open the Video & Ad tab of the product page. The example list showed 4,487 video and ad items for one product. [L0901, 2026]
- In Kalodata, open the Product Insights panel and set the country dropdown to United States to see the product's rank in United States TikTok. Use the Last 30 Days, Last 90 days and Last 180 days filters to compare current and older sales data. [L0704, 2026]
- For the example NAD+ berry drink mix, Kalodata showed about 34.92k items sold, an average unit price of $23.19 and a price range of $26.36 to $78.95 for the Last 30 Days window from 07/05/2025 to 08/03/2025. Its Concentration Rate (L30D) was 22.3%, while the NAD+ drops in the other form showed 79%. [L0901, 2026]
- Kalodata limits how many videos you can download without watermark, based on your plan. When the limit is reached, the tool asks you to buy a recharge plan, so plan which hook and testimonial clips you need before downloading. [L0901, 2026]
- The Kalodata filter panel has a Save selected filters button and a Submit button, so a filter set such as the book research set can be stored and reused instead of rebuilt each time. [L0354, 2026]
- The Kalodata product detail page has buttons for Generate Promo Video, View on TikTok, Review Insights and Video Tutorial, plus a 'Why is this product underperforming' prompt. These help with a quick check on a candidate product. [L0354, 2026]
- Kalodata has a built-in AI assistant that answers research questions about a selected product or category. Typical questions include finding the top 10 competitors at the same price, the main pain points in customer reviews, why a product is not selling well and what could change, a product selection analysis report, a comparison of the product's competitiveness with a similar product, and the recent GMV and sales trends. [L1187, 2026]
- The Kalodata product detail page is organized into tabs and panels: Overview, Product Card, Reviews, Video Content, Video Toolkit, Creator, SKU Analysis, Concentration, Selling Points and Review Insights. The Product Card shows price range, commission rate, shipping fee, rating and creator count. The Video Toolkit includes Storyboard Export and video generation tools such as Video Remake and Promo Video generation. Shop information and a Local Shop label also appear on the page. [L1187, 2026]

#### PP Ads

- PP Ads offers a product search section and a large TikTok video library for finding ad clips. It has a trial of 1 USD for 3 days, and users sometimes cancel before the trial converts. [L0363, 2022+]
- PP Ads is a TikTok product and ad research tool used to find competitor listings and ads for a product. It typically yields 5 to 50 or more competitor listings per product, which makes it useful for building a creative folder. [L1377, 2022+]
- PP Ads offers a 3-day trial for $1, after which the subscription bills $155 per month unless cancelled. Cancel the subscription or remove the payment method before the trial ends. [L1377, 2022+]
- Search PP Ads for dropshipper phrasing such as '50% off', 'get yours today' or 'limited time offer' to surface dropshipping ads. Branded ads make up most results in broad searches, so the keyword search finds the right ads faster. [L1377, 2022+]

PP Ads procedure:

1. Open PP Ads and search 5 to 10 generic keywords from the product's name. [L1377, 2022+]
2. Apply the filters: post creation date within the last two weeks, around 500 to 1,000 or more likes, and e-commerce platform Shopify. [L1377, 2022+]
3. Open the listings with the latest ads and download the most recent ad videos for a creative folder. [L1377, 2022+]
4. Aim for at least 5 to 10 competitor creatives, with 7 to 10 as a safe range for testing. [L1377, 2022+]

#### Pixta and TikTok Creative Center

- Pixta is a product research website and app that lists products from TikTok ads, with tabs such as winning ads and product research. Each listing links to a TikTok video or ad, which helps you see whether the product is getting traction. Check the linked content directly on TikTok instead of relying on the listing alone. [L0372, 2022]
- TikTok Creative Center, reached through TikTok Business Center, shows trending hashtags, trending songs and top-performing ads. Use its filters for industry, country and time window to match your target market. [L0706, 2020+]

### Pinterest, multi-platform and other spy tools

- A Pinterest spy feature inside a product research tool, filtered by weekly winners, shows which products are currently running as Pinterest ads and is used to find test products. [L1135, undated]
- PPSpy and Ali Hunter estimate a store's sales and Pinterest activity, such as impressions and saves, to judge whether a competitor is performing well. [L0871, 2021+]
- Minea is a shop-analysis tool that fetches a store's ads from most platforms and shows how many ads run and how a store scales on Facebook. The lesson found 280 ads for one store this way. [L0871, 2021+]
- To analyze a competitor's store in Minea, open Shops, search for the store name and select the matching result to reach Shop analysis. The tool pulls ads from most platforms and shows how many ads the store runs and how it scales, for example 280 ads for one store. [L0871, 2021+]
- Pipiads is most likely the paid product spy tool that shows trending products across platforms. The transcript calls it 'pizza'. It is meant to be added after you have gained some experience with organic content. Its list price is $263 per month. [L0706, 2020+; L1387, 2026]
- WitTau is a spy tool for YouTube ads that shows which product types are working on the platform. [L0788, undated]
- Pizzazz tracks up to five competitor stores at a time: enter the store URL and it shows how many sales the store made in a chosen period. It also has a product spy feature for finding products that are already selling. [L0388, 2024+]

### Store sales and traffic estimators

- PPSpy is a free Chrome extension that shows live sales and lets you track up to five stores for free. It gives product-by-product sales over the last 24 hours and is accurate to roughly 50 to 70 percent. [L0335, 2023]
- PPSpy is listed at $299 per month on the bundle list. The free tier with five tracked stores and the paid list price apply to different plans, so check which plan includes the features you need. [L0335, 2023; L1387, 2026]
- PP Ads is used to estimate how many sales a Shopify store is getting. It only records sales while the app is open and you are browsing that store, so leave it running for a period, up to a couple of hours, before trusting the numbers. [L0372, 2022]
- Peak Star estimates the revenue of competitor stores by scraping their websites. A reading of about 1,000 sales suggests the store is doing at least that, and 1,500 to 2,000 suggests slightly more. The numbers are rough estimates, not exact figures. [L0639, 2023]
- In Peakstar, open the third menu item (Dashboard, AI content and store research) to check stores. Enter the product keyword and select the target country, such as United States, to find products that can be tested. The seller was unsure whether the store figures reflect real tracked sales, so verify them before relying on them. [L0678, 2023]
- Shop Hunter gives product-level sales for a store, which helps identify the individual product driving revenue. Use it together with another tool to confirm numbers. [L0335, 2023]
- Shop Hunter is an alternative to Peak Star for estimating competitor store sales. Its accuracy compared with other tools was not verified. [L0639, 2023]
- Peak (peaks) is a product research tool used to see products that are being sold and to shortlist product ideas. The lesson combined it with Amazon reviews and Facebook Ads Library checks. [L0577, 2023]
- To see the best sellers of a Shopify store, add the path collections/all with the parameter sort_by=best-selling to the store domain. [L0335, 2023]

#### Store Tracker and Sales Tracker

- Store Tracker setup: Store Tracker > Insert Store URL > Start Tracking. After that the tracker shows bestsellers, sales, traffic and market data for the store. [L1164, 2026]
- Sales Tracker Details shows the launch date, monthly revenue bracket, monthly visits, traffic by country, product and collection counts, pixels and stack, apps, similar stores and ad-rank partnership ads for a store. Use these fields together to judge the store's scale and marketing setup. [L1164, 2026]

### Website traffic and SimilarWeb

#### SimilarWeb

- SimilarWeb is used for competitor research and analysis. [L0255, 2025]
- SimilarWeb estimates a store's monthly traffic, traffic sources and rough revenue. Use it to check competitor traffic size before deciding on a niche. [L0871, 2021+]
- SimilarWeb shows a store's monthly visits, its traffic split between direct, search, social and paid sources, and its country ranking. Use the ranking to identify the store's main market before choosing your own ad country, and check the traffic size before committing to a niche. [L0871, 2021+]
- SimilarWeb shows a competitor's paid traffic sources, visitor demographics, sites where ads run, the first-seen date of each ad and how long it has run. Use it to collect most of the five campaign variables in minutes. The tool is the author's preferred choice at the time of writing, so check for alternatives if it stops working. [L0523, 2015+]

Free search limits differ by route, and the sources give different values:

| Route | Free limit | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SimilarWeb site, free account (2025) | 15 searches per day; no workaround without a paid plan | [L0255, 2025] |
| SimilarWeb Chrome extension, free tier | 15 searches per week, shown as a counter such as "1 out of 15" | [L1332, 2020-26; L0808, 2026] |
| SimilarWeb free account (2026 advice) | 25 searches per week | [L0808, 2026] |

Use the limit that matches the route you are on. The 2026 source gives 25 per week for a free account without naming the route, so check the limit shown in your own account. [L0255, 2025; L1332, 2020-26; L0808, 2026]

#### SimilarWeb Chrome extension

- The SimilarWeb Chrome extension is free to install from the Chrome Web Store and shows traffic data for the website open in the browser. After you click its icon, a panel opens on the right side with global and country traffic ranks. It removes the need for a paid subscription or a free trial account on similarweb.com. [L0687, 2020-26]
- SimilarWeb Chrome extension, country check: scroll down to the country rank section and read the rank for the country where you plan to do business (the lesson uses the United States). [L0687, 2020-26]
- Install the Chrome extension to see a site's traffic and country ranking without entering a URL. Click the icon on the website you are viewing to open a sidebar. [L0808, 2026]
- Install the SimilarWeb Chrome extension, open a competitor's website, click the extension icon and scroll to Country Rank to view their ranking in your target country. If you are not using Chrome, access SimilarWeb.com with a free account. [L0051, undated]
- The extension is free and needs no account or email address. It works only in Google Chrome and returns the same data as the free trial on similarweb.com, so it is a fallback when that trial cannot be set up. [L1332, 2020-26]
- Because the extension needs no account or email, it avoids the setup problems some users have with the similarweb.com free trial. Use it as an alternative route to the same data when the trial fails. [L1332, 2020-26]
- The extension's ranking section shows a competitor's traffic rank, including the country rank. It is used to run the traffic rank test on future competitors. [L1097, 2025]
- The SimilarWeb Chrome extension is used to view estimated traffic and related website data for competitor and niche research. [L0817, 2026]

SimilarWeb extension install procedure:

1. Use Google Chrome as the browser; download and install it first if it is missing. [L1332, 2020-26]
2. Open the SimilarWeb Website Traffic and SEO Checker listing in the Chrome Web Store (search Google for "SimilarWeb Chrome extension" if no link is at hand). [L1332, 2020-26]
3. Click "Add to Chrome". [L1332, 2020-26]
4. Click "Add extension" in the confirmation prompt. [L1332, 2020-26]
5. Click the Extensions icon in the Chrome toolbar and find SimilarWeb in the list. [L1332, 2020-26]
6. Click "Pin" so the extension stays in the navigation bar; its icon is a blue and orange logo. [L1332, 2020-26]

#### Alexa

- Alexa.com (free version) shows the global rank of a competitor's website. A global rank inside one million is considered enough traffic to be worth studying, and the tool does not show exact visitor numbers. [L1085, 2018]
- Use a competitor research tool that aggregates domain rank data instead of paying for Alexa. If a supplier or competitor domain shows no global or domestic rank there, it is simply not ranked. [L1086, 2019]
- Checking a competitor's traffic rank on Alexa.com: [L1085, 2018]

1. Open Alexa.com and scroll to the bottom right of the page. [L1085, 2018]
2. Click Browse or Search Sites. [L1085, 2018]
3. Type the competitor's domain name and click Find. [L1085, 2018]
4. Read the global rank: if it is inside one million, the site gets enough traffic to be worth your attention. [L1085, 2018]

- A competitor research tool returns no data when a competitor site is too small. Try larger websites in the niche instead, because a missing result means there is not enough volume for that site. [L0968, 2021]

### Technology stack lookups

- BuiltWith (builtwith.com) is a free tool that identifies all software, platforms and tools used by any website. Search for a competitor's domain to see their Shopify version, apps, payment processors and other integrations. [L0253, 2024]
- BuiltWith is a website lookup tool that shows which platforms, apps and technologies a competitor's site uses. [L0984, 2024]
- BuiltWith is a free website that shows the apps and technologies used on any URL, such as the Shopify apps on a competitor's store. Cold-email agencies use the same kind of tool to contact every Shopify store, which is why such outreach happens. [L0965, 2024]
- BuiltWith can show which apps and technologies a Shopify or other website uses, which helps identify stores built recently by agencies. [L1115, 2024]
- Cola is a Chrome extension that shows which apps a store has installed. Use it on a competitor's store to see whether it runs a print-on-demand supplier app, though some apps stay undetected. [L1214, 2023+]
- Pricing and testing apps seen on competitor stores: Intelligems for A/B testing of pricing and layouts, and Kaching Bundles for quantity-break bundles. [L1164, 2026]
- Marketing and review apps seen on competitor stores: Klaviyo for email and SMS, Judge.me for product reviews, a Product Amazon Reviews app for importing reviews, and Triple Whale for analytics. [L1164, 2026]

### Competitor creative research, swipe files and monitoring

- A swipe board, for example in the Foreplay app, lets you save competitor ads into boards for later reference. Build one board per angle or per competitor so creative briefs start from real examples. [L0524, 2023]
- Clipping services collect everything published about a product category, including competitor ads and claims, so the ad writer sees the full competitive picture. Use them as a source of competitive research before writing ads. [L1261, undated]
- Google Chrome 'Search image with Google' (right-click an image) is used to find the same product image on other sites, which often reveals the brand name of a product. [L0805, 2024]
- AMZ Shark and AMZ Tracker track competitor products, showing their sales velocity, reviews, pricing and trends over time. They are useful for ongoing competitive monitoring but not essential when starting. [L0228, 2015]
- SearchMonitor is a paid tool that monitors competitors' Product Listing Ads and can flag sellers pricing below MAP, replacing manual SKU-by-SKU price checks. It is an option for stores that want constant price data. The alternative is to audit ad accounts every one to two weeks and investigate when sales drop, which can reveal a competitor breaking MAP by around 10%. [L0429, 2022]

### Amazon research tools

- Amazon publishes a fee lookup tool on its seller site. Use it to check all fees for a product before choosing a niche and calculating profit. [L1153, 2016]
- The Amazon Best Sellers page shows the top 100 products in each category and its subcategories. Use it to find product ideas and check what sells. [L1153, 2016]
- Seller Pulse is an Amazon research tool used to find top-selling products on the marketplace. Amazon Ads sponsored listings can place products at the top of search results for shoppers. [L0788, undated]
- Jungle Scout is a product research tool for FBA. Like AMZ Shark, it is helpful for detailed analysis but not required to launch your first product. [L0228, 2015]
- Review Index is a Chrome extension that adds a summarize button to an Amazon listing and shows the pros and cons from customer reviews, which helps spot common complaints. [L1158, 2023]
- Amazon Reviews Summarizer setup: install the Chrome extension (find it by searching 'amazon review summarizer' on Google and opening the first result). On an Amazon product page, click the extension's summary just below the product name and price to list the pros and cons customers mention. [L1183, undated]
- A ChatGPT plugin that reads Amazon reviews returned summary themes such as product quality, effectiveness and a self-cleaning feature, which were judged shallow. Exporting all reviews to a spreadsheet with a Chrome extension and pasting them into ChatGPT in batches gave richer audience insight. [L0353, 2023]
- Axiom is a browser extension that scrapes product review data into Google Sheets by selecting fields such as star rating, date and review text on a product page. [L1115, 2024]

Procedure for scraping product reviews with the Axiom extension:

1. Open the Axiom extension on the product page and click Get current URL. [L1115, 2024]
2. Click Select for the first review field, such as star rating, then click a second matching element so the rest auto-fills. [L1115, 2024]
3. Repeat for other fields such as date and review text, clicking two matching elements each time. [L1115, 2024]
4. Set the scrape limit, such as 20 reviews. [L1115, 2024]
5. If reviews are paginated, click Find pager and select the show more reviews button so the extension moves through all pages. [L1115, 2024]
6. Click Complete, then paste the results into Google Sheets and sort by the rating column. [L1115, 2024]
