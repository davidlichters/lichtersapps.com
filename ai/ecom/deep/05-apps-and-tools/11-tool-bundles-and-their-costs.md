### Shared accounts and their risks

- Shared-account services such as ShareTool split the cost of an ad account among several users. For example, a tool priced at about 100 per month can cost about 20 per month when five users share it. Shared accounts are much cheaper, but other users can see anything saved in them. [L0361, 2024]
- Do not save winning products inside a shared ad account, because other users can see them. Keep saved product research in your own browser or elsewhere. [L0361, 2024]
- Access through shared accounts is not guaranteed, even when a bundle promises 24/7 access and no downtime. A suspended shared account can cut off a whole workflow, so export and save research data locally and avoid building a single critical process on one shared login. [L1387, 2026]

### TikTok Shop and Ad Discovery Tools

- PeakStar's TikTok ads search finds running dropshipping ads by country, like count, recency and keywords. [L1436, undated]
- PPAds is a larger ad library used for the same purpose. [L1436, undated]
- PPAds has offered a trial with full access for 3 days. The trial price was $1 earlier and later $7, so the figure that applies depends on when you sign up. [L1436, undated]
- FastMoss is used for product research. It scans TikTok Shop and Shopify data to reveal trending products and their sales figures instantly. [L1459, 2026]

### Kalodata

- Kalodata is a TikTok Shop research tool that ranks products, videos and creators by revenue, views and ad metrics. [L1479, 2026]
- Set the region to United States before reading any ranking, because results differ by market. [L1479, 2026]
- Newer versions add a product detail summary and a buyer review summary. [L1479, 2026]
