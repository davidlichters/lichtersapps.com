## Product Options, Personalization and Digital Products

### Choosing an options app

- Shopify's built-in option system is limited to three option types per product, so products with variations that cannot be handled natively need an app. [L0853, 2021]
- ** Bold Product Options is the default for any product with variations that cannot be handled natively. [L0853, 2021]
- Shopify allows up to 100 variants per product. For more variants, or for clickable color swatches, use an app such as Infinite Product Options or SC Product Options. [L0837, 2025]
- ** Having many product options is not a problem by itself. [L0551, 2020]

### Bold Product Options

- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app that creates product variants and bundling options. The Premium edition costs $19.99/month with a 14-day free trial. Plan 10-20 minutes of configuration per product and 30-60 minutes per week for analysis and optimization. [L0240, 2019]
- Bold Product Options adds checkboxes and drop-down menus to product pages. Use it to offer upgrades such as White Glove delivery on specific products only. *Older (2021).* [L0247, 2021]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for creating customization options such as sizes, colors and add-ons like assembly services. It can be combined with Bold Product Upsell. [L0251, 2023]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for managing many product options in a store. [L0551, 2020]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for setting up product options simply, such as sizes, colors or add-on choices. [L0855, 2023]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for adding custom product options, such as variants or personalization fields, to product pages. *Older (2021).* [L1089, 2021]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app for product add-ons and options, such as extra choices a customer can add to a product page. *Older (2021).* [L1090, 2021]
- Bold Product Options is used to display add-ons and product options on the product page. *Older (2020).* [L0468, 2020]
- Bold Product Options is listed as a resource in the December 2020 coaching call for adding product option selectors to a store. The lesson names it without describing its features. *Older (2020).* [L0426, 2020]
- Bold Product Options is an app that lets customers choose custom sizes and materials on a product page. Preset option values can update the price in real time, while free-text entry may not. Check with the app's support before relying on it. *Older (2019).* [L0467, 2019]
- Bold Product Options is an app that lets you add optional extras, such as bulbs, to a product page as a paid add-on. [L0894, 2020-26]

**Naming conflict:** Two 2024 sources disagree on which name came first. One says Bold Product Options was formerly SC Product Options [L0253, 2024]. The other says SC Product Options was formerly Bold Product Options [L0856, 2024]. Both names are used in this handbook as the sources use them.

**Pricing conflict:** The sources give different prices for different versions, so apply each figure only to the version it describes:
- $19.99 or about $20/month: the Premium version, with conditional logic and a 14-day free trial [L0240, 2019; L1076, 2019]
- About $10/month: the cheaper version without conditional logic. Do not use it for cross-sell [L1076, 2019]
- About 5 USD/month: shipping options and fixed shipping charges. *Older (2020).* [L1277, 2020]

### SC Product Options and Infinite Product Options

- SC Product Options is the Shopify app for product page upsells and variant options on product pages. [L0856, 2024]
- SC Product Options adds visual swatches to variant options on product pages, such as color circles or images chosen with a color picker. [L1116, 2025]
- SC Product Options cost $15/month in July 2025. It was the app used by the coaching team for more than 100 options with swatches. [L0837, 2025]
- SC Product Options is an app that displays other products on a product page so customers can add related items to their order, which is used for cross-sell bundles. It is quick to set up when you are already authorized to sell the items. [L1083, 2024+]
- Infinite Product Options costs about $13/month with a 14-day free trial. In July 2025 it had 4.8 stars from almost 2,000 reviews and supports more than 100 options and color swatches. [L0837, 2025]

### Global Product Options and personalization fields

**Setup procedure A (free app, product scope set per product):**

1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store, scroll down and click Install app, then click Continue as free. [L0476, undated]
2. Open the product you want to personalize, and click Add element. [L0476, undated]
3. Choose Text field as the element type and set the field label (example: type in your names). [L0476, undated]
4. Open advanced options and add a placeholder or help text showing the expected input, for example a list of sample names. The help text appears below the field. [L0476, undated]
5. Click Next. [L0476, undated]
6. Choose which products get the option. Pick All if the store sells only one product. [L0476, undated]
7. Open the theme editor, activate the app embed and click save. [L0476, undated]
8. Check the product page to confirm the text field appears. [L0476, undated]

**Setup procedure B (option sets, from a second lesson version):**

1. Install Global Product Options on the Shopify store. [L1220, undated]
2. Open Option sets and create a new option set. [L1220, undated]
3. Add a text box with a title, for example Name of boyfriend or girlfriend. [L1220, undated]
4. Click Show advanced options. [L1220, undated]
5. Add either a text placeholder (shown inside the box) or a help text (shown below the box) to explain what to type. [L1220, undated]
6. Optionally add an image upload option. [L1220, undated]
7. When an order comes in, forward the customer's text to the supplier exactly as typed. [L1220, undated]

**Notes on the two versions:**
- Both procedures set up the same app. Follow the menu names your app version shows. [L0476, undated; L1220, undated]
- Example for a single-product store: label "Type in your names", with sample names such as Daniel, Sarah, David, Michelle as the help text, so customers see how it works. Set the product scope to All. Then activate the app embed in the theme editor and save. [L0634, undated]
- Global Product Options adds custom input fields such as text fields to product pages, so customers can enter personalization details like names before they buy. [L0476, undated]
- Global Product Options is a free Shopify app for personalization, such as a name field on jewelry. [L1220, undated]

**Other personalization tools:**
- Global Variants is a Shopify app that allows product customization, such as choosing a mug handle, color or text, on a product page. It is used for personalized products rather than standard single-variant listings. [L1172, 2023+]
- A free Shopify text-box or photo-upload customization app lets buyers enter a name, number, message or photo on a personalized product. The seller then forwards the order details to the supplier. (The lesson does not name the app.) [L1469, 2023+]
- Bold Product Options adds text fields to product pages so customers can enter engraving or personalization text. The order arrives with that information, and the supplier engraves and ships the item, so you do not stock it. *Older (2021).* [L0962, 2021]

### Add-ons, upsells and cross-sells

**Setting up the offer:**
- Use the Shopify apps Product Accessories and Product Options by Bold Apps to add accessories and product options to product pages. These apps help raise AOV without changing the core product listing. [L0920, 2018; L1357, 2018]
- The Shopify apps Product Accessories and Product Options by Bold Apps work well for offering add-ons and product options. [L1357, 2018]
- Bold Product Options is the app used for cross-sell conditional logic on product pages. [L1076, 2019]
- Conditional logic example: if the customer is on Product A, show Product B; if they add B, show Product C. [L0253, 2024]
- Conditional logic is what makes the step-by-step cross-sell work. Do not use the cheaper version, which lacks it. [L1076, 2019]
- The premium version's 14-day free trial is worth using to test the setup before paying. [L1076, 2019]

**Upsell types:**
- Add-ons such as warranties, maintenance plans or accessories can be priced right on the product page. *Older (2022).* [L1281, 2022]
- Bold Product Options is a Shopify app that lets you add extras to a product page as a paid add-on. [L0894, 2020-26]

**Supplier-fulfilled and bundled items:**
- Bold Product Options adds bundled items or variant options to a product, so the extra item ships automatically with the main order. It is preferred over manual fulfillment when order volume grows. *Older (2019).* [L1002, 2019]

**Warranties via Bold Upsell (procedure):**

1. Create a product named extended warranty. [L1076, 2019]
2. Link it to the suppliers who provide the warranty. [L1076, 2019]
3. Add it as an upsell at checkout. The setup takes about 5 minutes. [L1076, 2019]

**Extended warranties with Extend:**
- Extend.com is a Shopify app for selling extended warranties on high-ticket products. You earn a commission on warranty sales, and the app provider handles warranty fulfillment. Multiple one source members recommended it. [L0251, 2023]

**Special cases:**
- Bold Product Options can offer White Gloves delivery on specific products only. *Older (2021).* [L0247, 2021]
- ** Bold Product Options can show shipping options and fixed shipping charges on product pages. [L1277, 2020]

**Mistake to avoid:**
- Product option apps such as Bold may become incompatible with split-payment apps that let customers pay with two cards. Ask both app providers for a compatible integration rather than assuming one will work with the other. [L0470, 2023]

### Creating ebooks and digital bonuses

**Tools for writing and layout:**
- Claude (claude.ai) can generate a complete, professional ebook in minutes from a single prompt, so the store owner does not need to write the ebook manually. [L0860, 2026]
- Canva lays out the AI-generated ebook text with a simple ebook template and exports the finished file as a PDF for upload to Shopify. [L0860, 2026]
- Canva is used to design ebook covers and the layout of the PDF product. [L1131, 2018+]
- ChatGPT generates ideas, lists and text for ebooks and offers. Give it concrete length targets, such as about 1000 words, to control output length. [L1131, 2018+]

**Prompt for a dog-recipe ebook (use as given, fill in the placeholders):**

```
Create a list of 20 recipes for dogs that support [PROBLEM, e.g. gut health, skin, dental health or joint problems] using everyday ingredients that are easy to buy in a regular supermarket in [COUNTRY]. For each recipe, give a title, an ingredient list with amounts, three to five short preparation steps, and one sentence explaining why the ingredients suit this problem. Write in [LANGUAGE] and keep each recipe under 100 words, with a total length of about 1000 words for the whole list. Do not make medical claims or promise cures, and add a note that owners should check any recipe with a veterinarian.
```

[L1131, 2018+]

**Sourcing ready-made content:**
- PLR sites are marketplaces that sell private-label-rights ebooks for about 2 dollars 70 cents each. They can be rebranded with your own logo and sold or given away as bonuses. [L0577, 2023]
- Free ebook websites provide educational ebooks that can be rebranded and offered as a bonus to customers. [L0650, undated]
- Fiverr can be used to hire someone to rebrand a free ebook to match the store, for a low cost. [L0650, undated]

### Delivering digital products

**Choosing a delivery app:**
- Digital Downloads is a free Shopify app made by Shopify. It delivers an uploaded file to the customer by email immediately after purchase. It can be used to sell a digital product alongside a bundle. [L0862, 2026; L0861, 2026]
- Digital Downloads is installed by searching for it in the Shopify App Store. [L0861, 2026]
- Digital Downloads automatically delivers a PDF or digital product after purchase, so it does not have to be emailed manually. [L1432, undated]
- Digital Downloads works for a free ebook offered as a bonus with a physical product. [L1428, undated]
- Install Digital Downloads only if the store sells digital files. The lesson's store installs it for its digital sales. [L1327, 2026]
- Digital Downloads has been used to sell and deliver digital files such as audiobooks, artwork and videos. [L0888, 2023+]
- EDP (Easy Digital Products) appeared as an alternative to Digital Downloads in the Shopify App Store search, with a free plan available. Compare its features and reviews with Digital Downloads if the free delivery workflow needs more options. [L0861, 2026]

**Setup procedure (Digital Downloads):**

1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store by searching for "digital downloads." [L0862, 2026]
2. Open the e-book product in Shopify, creating it first if it doesn't exist yet. [L0862, 2026]
3. Inside the app, open the product, choose Add asset, then File. [L0862, 2026]
4. Upload the PDF. [L0862, 2026]
5. In the product's Shipping section, switch off "Physical product" so it reads "Not a physical product." [L0862, 2026]
6. Click Save. [L0862, 2026]
7. Configure the notification email that goes out when a digital product is ready for download, plus the email used for updates. [L0862, 2026; L0861, 2026]
8. Set how digital products are accessed and downloaded after checkout. [L0862, 2026; L0861, 2026]
9. Add the e-book product as the free gift in the Kaching Bundles bar. [L0862, 2026]

**Settings and plan requirement:**
- The Digital Downloads settings let you write the notification email for a ready digital product and the email for when a purchased digital product has been updated. They also let you choose how digital products are accessed and downloaded after checkout. [L0861, 2026]
- Adjusting the checkout is only possible on a Shopify plan. If the app shows "To customize your checkout, you need to select a Shopify plan," select a Shopify plan first. [L0861, 2026; L0862, 2026]

**Link products:**
- Digital Downloads can create a link product that sells access to externally hosted content, sold alongside the files attached to a product. Use this when the gift is an online course or membership area rather than a downloadable PDF. [L0861, 2026]

**Bonuses without a delivery app:**
- Use Google Drive links to PDFs, unlisted YouTube videos, or unlisted pages to deliver free digital bonuses without an app. Paid digital products with automated delivery need a Shopify app built for that purpose. [L0934, 2023]

**Delivering a digital bonus after purchase with Klaviyo (procedure):**

1. Create a flow in Klaviyo triggered by the purchase of the product. [L0934, 2023]
2. Add an email containing the access link. [L0934, 2023]
3. Use a Google Drive link to PDFs, an unlisted YouTube video, or an unlisted page as the link. [L0934, 2023]
4. Test the flow before going live. [L0934, 2023]

**Online classes:**
- ** For a weekly class sold online, create a Shopify digital product with a price, then edit the order confirmation email with access instructions. For a private online class, a private Facebook group with video streaming is an option. [L0664, 2020]

**Free gifts and subscriptions (Kaching):**
- Kaching Bundles is a Shopify app that can deliver a free digital gift, such as an ebook, bundled with a product. Upload the finished file to the app to use it as a product bonus. [L1323, 2026]
- Kaching Subscriptions adds subscription purchases. It works well with products like supplements. For a book store it is not needed at the foundation stage. [L1327, 2026]

**Fallbacks and risks:**
- A digital product such as a PDF bundle may not reach customers if no delivery app or order confirmation link is set up. Check whether delivery is automated before selling it, and check support emails for complaints. [L1432, undated]
- Alternatives to a delivery app: place a download link to the PDF in the order confirmation so the buyer can click and download it immediately, or send each buyer a separate email with the PDF manually until automation is in place. [L1432, undated]
