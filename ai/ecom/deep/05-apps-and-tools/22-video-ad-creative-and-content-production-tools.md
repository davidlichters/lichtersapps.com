## Video, Ad Creative and Content Production Tools

### Planning the Project Folder and Assets

**Organise assets before building any ad**

1. Create a Google Drive folder for the campaign. [L0308, 2023]
2. Inside it, create two subfolders: "Image Assets" and "Video Assets". [L0308, 2023]
3. Gather all images: AliExpress product photos, Instagram posts, user-generated content, screenshots. [L0308, 2023]
4. Gather all videos: TikTok product clips, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, self-filmed footage. [L0308, 2023]
5. Add product images, lifestyle images and platform-specific clips. [L0308, 2023]
6. Reference this folder when designing ad creatives in Canva to keep creatives consistent and fast. [L0308, 2023]

**Editing project folder structure**

Set up the folders in this order before importing anything: [L1427, 2023+]

1. Create a separate assets folder for images, emojis and graphics. [L0343, 2023]
2. Create a separate audio folder for voiceovers, music and sound effects. [L0343, 2023]
3. Create a separate video folder for stock footage and product clips. [L0343, 2023]
4. Keep these folders stable. Moving or renaming files breaks the links in editing software such as Premiere Pro, which then reports that the files cannot be found. [L0343, 2023]

- Renaming or moving a source file or its folder after import breaks the link to the clip, so keep raw footage in fixed locations. [L0341, 2023; L0343, 2023; L1427, 2023+]
- Folders inside a Premiere Pro project bin only organise your work and do not affect links to source files. Use import folders such as assets, audio and video. [L0341, 2023]
- Google Drive, Dropbox or an external hard drive can store project folders. Cloud storage lets you reach the same files from a phone, for example to download clips or upload recorded voiceovers. [L1427, 2023+]
- When importing downloaded clips into CapCut, choose "Stay in original location" in the Imported media prompt to keep the files in their download folder instead of copying them. [L0901, 2026]

### Sourcing Footage: Downloads, Stock and Creator Discovery

**Downloading source clips**

- 4K Video Downloader saves YouTube videos to your computer. Copy the YouTube link, paste it into the app and download the file. The app asked for payment on at least one download, so check its terms before relying on it. [L1427, 2023+]
- The same app can download a sound effect as an MP3 (see the sound effect procedure under CapCut). [L1427, 2023+]
- SnapTik downloads TikTok videos: paste the TikTok link and save the file. SnapInsta is an alternative for similar downloads. [L1427, 2023+]
- Snaptik, ssstik and Downloader Bot download TikTok videos. The lesson's speaker uses Snaptik for this step. [L1252, 2026]
- A downloader such as SnapTik can save a finished TikTok so it can be reposted to Instagram Reels. [L1077, undated]
- A video-to-text extractor converts the spoken script of a direct-response ad into text. Paste the text into an AI chat as a model and ask for a new ad for your own audience. This saves typing the copy by hand. [L0995, 2023]

**Stock footage and images**

- Pexels and Unsplash are free stock footage sites. Use them to find pain-point clips when you have no product footage yet, then add grayscale treatment and caption overlays. [L0398, 2023+]
- Envato Elements is a third-party subscription library of graphics, stock video, imagery, sound effects and video effects for use in Photoshop and Illustrator projects. Photoshop and Illustrator do not include such a library, so a separate asset source is needed. [L0754, undated]
- Envato Elements is a royalty-free stock library. Build ad visuals by cutting out stock images, adding backgrounds and placing text on top. This is slower than generating images with ChatGPT from reference ads. [L0972, 2025]
- Envato Elements is a monthly subscription for stock photos and other design assets with unlimited downloads. It is an alternative to buying single images or using supplier images. [L0804, 2023]
- Envato Elements was a subscription source for images, design assets and templates used by the team. ** [L0665, 2022]
- Envato stock video subscription costs about 200 USD per year and includes graphics, sound effects and stock footage. [L1427, 2023+]

**Creator discovery**

- Modash finds creators by niche and engagement rate. Use it to shortlist creators for UGC content before contacting them. [L0398, 2023+]

**Principles for choosing**

- CapCut is quick to start on a phone or desktop. Desktop editors such as Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve suit serious production. If you plan to produce ads regularly, pick one desktop editor and learn it properly. [L1427, 2023+]
- Premiere Pro, Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve are heavier but offer more functionality. CapCut is cheaper and easier for beginners and already includes graphics, sound effects and auto captions. [L1423, undated]
- Splice is practical for beginners making short-form ads, but it cannot place sound effects or cuts at split-second precision. For frame-level timing, use DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro on Mac. Use Splice for most edits and switch to the desktop editor only when precise timing matters. [L1077, undated]
- Test an editor before committing. Principles transfer between tools, so switching later is low-risk. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Premiere Pro often requires buying a transition package before transitions can be used. CapCut ships a large set of transitions such as zoom, wipe and shake that can be added directly. For beginners who need quick transitions without extra purchases, CapCut is the faster starting point. [L0344, 2023]
- InVideo and Contagion are video editing tools. The lesson's presenter used one of them to edit short ads. [L0513, undated]

### CapCut

**Desktop or mobile**

- For ad creative work, CapCut desktop is preferred over the mobile app because all recorded footage can be imported at once, edited and saved in a project file. The free basic plan is sufficient for beginners. [L1423, undated]
- CapCut on a phone is fast and capable, but it stores everything on the phone and is less efficient for managing video projects than desktop software. Large projects are inefficient on a phone. [L0341, 2023; L0343, 2023]
- CapCut Pro costs 10 US dollars per month and adds more transitions, extra effects and a larger library of graphics and sound effects. Beginners do not need it to produce Facebook ad creatives. [L1423, undated]
- The free library covers basic transitions, effects and text animations. Text templates, some animations and additional pro transitions require Pro. For a beginner ad workflow, the free items are enough to start. Paid items are worth adding only once the basic edit is working. [L0344, 2023]
- Some animation options, such as advanced animated captions, may require a Pro account. Check whether a feature is locked before building an ad template around it. [L1427, 2023+]

**Layout (desktop)**

- The media bin holds imported footage, sounds and images. The timeline at the bottom layers clips, the player in the middle shows the output, and the right-side control panel changes the selected asset. The control panel holds volume, speed, animation and other settings for the selection. [L0344, 2023]
- The timeline is at the bottom and holds all video, audio and graphics tracks. The preview window is in the middle. The left panel has the tabs Media, Audio, Text, Stickers, Effects, Filters, Adjustment and Templates. The right panel is the Tools area, which changes with the selected clip and controls position, scale and other properties. [L1423, undated]
- Click any imported file in the Media panel to play it in the preview window. Review footage there before dragging it onto the timeline. [L1423, undated]
- Scrub footage by dragging across the preview area before placing it on the timeline to check what each clip contains. Dragging a clip onto the timeline makes it the active track for layering. [L0344, 2023]

**Layout (mobile)**

- The video preview sits at the top, the timeline in the middle and the main menu at the bottom with Edit, Audio, Text and Stickers. Under Edit, the Split option cuts the clip at the playhead. [L1427, 2023+]

**Frame rate and performance**

- Shoot and edit at 30 fps, but check the frame rate your camera or phone actually recorded and match the project to it. [L0344, 2023]
- Under the CapCut menu, Edit settings offers a proxy option that caches media so a weaker computer runs more smoothly. [L0344, 2023]
- Recorded footage at 1920 x 1080 pixels is the landscape format, useful when searching for horizontal footage. [L1427, 2023+]

**Shortcuts and timeline controls**

- A: selection tool. B: blade (cut) tool. Ctrl+B: splits the clip under the cursor without switching tools. [L0344, 2023]
- Space: plays from the playhead, and pressing it again stops playback. [L0344, 2023; L1423, undated]
- Left and right arrow keys step through frames. Shift plus arrow keys moves faster. [L1423, undated]
- A selects and moves clips. B activates the blade to cut. [L1423, undated]
- Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes. Ctrl/Cmd plus mouse wheel zooms the timeline. [L1423, undated]
- Zoom the timeline by holding Ctrl or Cmd and scrolling. This helps when checking small cuts or the audio waveform, and shows where speech starts and stops. [L0344, 2023]
- Keep Snap switched on as a beginner. It makes clips lock to the ends of neighbouring clips and avoids small gaps. Snap is the toggle near the timeline controls and can be turned off when free placement is needed. [L0344, 2023]
- Clips snap to the edges of neighbouring clips when you drag them. This helps line up B-roll and transition points without manual measuring. [L1423, undated]
- On mobile, hold a timeline item with your thumb and drag. The item vibrates when it nears an edge, which helps it snap into position. [L1427, 2023+]

**Cutting and trimming**

- The audio waveform under each clip makes pauses and silent gaps visible. Use it to find cut points for filler words before you play the A-roll back. [L1423, undated]
- Remove filler or a mistake:
  1. Play the footage and find the mistake or filler. [L0344, 2023]
  2. Switch to the blade tool with B and cut at the start and end of the unwanted section. [L0344, 2023]
  3. Switch back to the selection tool with A and click the unwanted piece. [L0344, 2023]
  4. Press Delete or Backspace to remove it. The gap closes automatically, so the remaining clips move together. [L0344, 2023]
  5. Replay the section and repeat until the take is clean. [L0344, 2023]
- In selection mode, grab the edge of a clip and drag it inward or outward to shorten or lengthen it. This trims without the blade. [L0344, 2023]
- Deleting a clip with Backspace closes the gap it leaves, so the next clip shifts left. [L0344, 2023]
- Drag a selection box across several clips on the timeline to move or delete them together. [L0344, 2023]
- To select several layers, drag a selection box over them or hold Cmd (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows) while clicking each one. You can then move them or change their text style together. [L1423, undated]

**Speed**

- To change a clip's playback speed, select the clip and use the Speed control in the video section of the right-hand control panel. The demo did not show a 2x timeline playback shortcut like the one in Premiere Pro, so speed changes are made on the clip itself. [L0344, 2023]
- On mobile, open Speed on a clip. Normal changes the whole clip evenly. Curve changes speed over time to create a speed ramp, for example slow at the start and fast at the end. [L1427, 2023+]

**Keyframes and motion**

- A keyframe stores a value such as position or scale at one point in time. The clip animates between two or more keyframes, which creates movement such as following a subject or zooming in on a product. [L1427, 2023+]
- Keyframes can animate more than scale, including opacity, position, rotation, size and alignment, using one keyframe at the start and one at the end. For example, a fade-in text is made by keyframing opacity from zero to full. [L0344, 2023]
- Add a moving zoom or pan in CapCut:
  1. Add a keyframe with the diamond button beside the play button. [L0343, 2023]
  2. Move the playhead. [L0343, 2023]
  3. Change the scale to zoom. [L0343, 2023]
  4. Repeat for each position change to make a moving zoom or pan. [L0343, 2023]
- The keyframe button sits beside the play button. Tapping it places a red diamond at the current position. To remove a keyframe, hover over the diamond until it turns red and tap the minus option. [L1427, 2023+]
- Motion blur in the adjustment settings adds blur to movement so it looks more like real camera motion. Use a light setting, because heavy blur looks unnatural. [L1427, 2023+]
- Fade out: grab the end of the clip, drag to adjust its length, then choose Fade from the bottom menu and select fade out so the clip ends by fading to black. [L1427, 2023+]

**Transitions**

- Split a clip at the playhead, click the small white marker between the two clips and choose a transition from the Movement category, such as Pull out, Pull in, Shake or Zoom. Adjust the transition length after it is applied. [L0343, 2023]
- On mobile, tap the point between two clips to open the transition presets, which include zooms, flashes and burns. Choose one to join the clips. [L1427, 2023+]

**Audio, sound effects and text-to-speech**

- Add a sound effect to a CapCut ad:
  1. Search YouTube for a sound effect, for example by typing sfx and the effect name. [L1427, 2023+]
  2. Copy the video link and download it as an MP3 with 4K Video Downloader. [L1427, 2023+]
  3. Import the MP3 into CapCut. [L1427, 2023+]
  4. Place the sound on the timeline at the moment it should play. [L1427, 2023+]
- Built-in text-to-speech: tap the text-to-speech button in the bottom menu, type the script and choose a voice preset. CapCut belongs to the company behind TikTok, so it often adds features suited to TikTok content. [L1427, 2023+]
- Add a voiceover from ElevenLabs in CapCut: [L1315, 2023]
  1. Type the script into ElevenLabs and generate the voiceover. [L1315, 2023]
  2. Download the generated audio file. [L1315, 2023]
  3. In CapCut, use the audio import option to upload the file. [L1315, 2023]
  4. Merge the imported audio with your video track in the timeline. [L1315, 2023]

**Building a UGC-style ad**

Recommended order in CapCut:

1. Record or film the main talking-head footage (A-roll), either as UGC or as a typical ad, from a finished script. [L1423, undated]
2. Cut out filler words, pauses and mistakes from the A-roll and play it through to check the script. [L1423, undated]
3. Overlay B-roll product shots on top of the A-roll while the speaker talks. [L1423, undated]
4. Add background music and sound effects. [L1423, undated]
5. Add auto captions and style them. [L1423, undated]
6. Add graphics such as icons, checkmarks and bullet points. [L1423, undated]
7. Add transitions, cuts, zooms and keyframed motion. [L1423, undated]

- CapCut is used to make video creatives by combining clips, text, descriptions and AI voiceovers. It is a common editor for building several ad variations quickly. [L1219, undated]
- CapCut cuts downloaded ad videos down to their hook, combines clips from different ads into one new creative and exports the result. The workflow runs entirely on the downloaded files. [L0901, 2026]

### Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve

**Premiere Pro**

- Premiere Pro is a professional video editor used alongside After Effects for motion graphics. [L0343, 2023; L0341, 2023]
- Panels: the Project panel (bottom left) holds imported media and bins. The Timeline (bottom) shows clips in sequence, with visual tracks above and audio tracks below a divider. The Program monitor (center) previews the combined result. The Effect Controls panel (top) edits the selected clip. [L1427, 2023+]
- A new sequence takes the aspect ratio of the first clip you add. Create a separate sequence for vertical video so the ratio is correct. Check the ratio before editing, because the sequence settings follow the first imported clip. [L0341, 2023; L0343, 2023]
- Set up a project:
  1. Create a dated project folder for the product. [L1427, 2023+]
  2. Create bins for video, audio and assets inside the project. [L1427, 2023+]
  3. Import the downloaded clip and drag it onto the timeline. [L1427, 2023+]
  4. Keep visual tracks above the divider and audio tracks below it. [L1427, 2023+]
  5. Place extra footage such as B-roll on higher visual tracks. [L1427, 2023+]
- The selection tool grabs and moves clips on the timeline. The razor tool cuts a clip where you click. Its shortcut is C in Premiere Pro and X in DaVinci Resolve. [L1427, 2023+]
- Effect Controls for a clip include position (left and right, up and down), scale, rotation, opacity or visibility, and clip speed. Adjust these per clip in the Effect Controls panel. [L1427, 2023+]
- Scrubbing means dragging the playhead along the timeline to move back and forth through footage. This is how you review and find cut points. [L1427, 2023+]
- Duplicate a text layer:
  1. Select the text layer. [L1427, 2023+]
  2. Hold Alt on Windows or Option on Mac. [L1427, 2023+]
  3. Drag the layer out to create a copy. [L1427, 2023+]
  4. Repeat to build a set of text layers, then edit each one's wording and position. [L1427, 2023+]
- Essential Sound panel: use it to set each audio clip's type, such as Dialogue, Background music or Sound effects, and apply the matching preset for balanced voice levels. [L0343, 2023; L1427, 2023+]
  - Set a voiceover clip to Dialogue and choose the preset "balanced male voice" to clean up and balance the recording. Adjust the volume afterwards if needed. [L0341, 2023; L1427, 2023+]
  - Lower the clip volume first if the audio is too loud. [L0341, 2023; L0343, 2023]
  - Keep settings basic. Adjust clip volume, and turn reverb or echo off if they are not needed. [L1427, 2023+]
- Tick-tok style text overlay: add a text layer with Open Sans Bold and adjust the line height in the text settings. Place the text with the Vector Motion position controls so it sits clear of the product. [L0343, 2023]
- A fast zoom transition can be added from the Effects panel using a zoom blur preset, placed between two clips. Third-party presets such as Film Impact or Premiere Composer provide more zoom transitions. [L0343, 2023]
- Create a zoom-in keyframe:
  1. Select the clip and open Effect Controls > Transform. [L0343, 2023]
  2. Move the anchor point onto the product or subject you want to zoom towards. [L0343, 2023]
  3. Set a keyframe on Scale at the start of the move. [L0343, 2023]
  4. Move the playhead forward and change the Scale value to zoom in, which creates a second keyframe. [L0343, 2023]
  5. Check the motion in playback and adjust the anchor point if the subject drifts off centre. [L0343, 2023]
- Right-click a keyframe and choose Ease In or Ease Out to create smooth speed curves. Use the speed graph for custom curves when a preset is not enough. [L0343, 2023]

**DaVinci Resolve**

- DaVinci Resolve by Blackmagic Design is free and includes most of the functions of Premiere Pro, making it a budget option for video editing. [L0341, 2023; L0343, 2023]
- It covers most functions of Premiere Pro for basic editing work and is a budget-friendly option for beginners. [L0343, 2023]
- It is a free desktop editor with a layout similar to Premiere Pro. It has a folder section for organising media and drags clips onto a timeline, but its shortcuts differ. [L1427, 2023+]
- The preset "balanced background music" applies a finished music mix once you place a music track. [L1427, 2023+]
