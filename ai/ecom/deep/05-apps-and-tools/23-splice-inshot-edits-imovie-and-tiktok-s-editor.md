### Splice, InShot, Edits, iMovie and TikTok's Editor

**Splice**

- Splice is a phone editing app used for cutting, voiceovers, speed changes, slow motion and extracting sounds. It is preferred over TikTok's editor for product videos. [L1380, 2022]
- Cost: about $12 per month. One statement in the lesson says $12 per year, so verify the price in the app store. [L1380, 2022]
- Splice is used to edit TikTok ads: it matches on-screen text to speech and adds non-copyrighted sounds before upload. [L1381, 2022]
- Splice is a mobile and desktop editing app. The speaker recommended editing on a phone and sending the file to a computer. [L1383, 2022]
- Splice works better on iPhone because TikTok allows higher-quality uploads from iOS, while Android uploads are limited. Android phones are still viable. [L1380, 2022]

**InShot**

- InShot is a mobile video editor used to edit creator content before uploading it to social platforms. [L0784, 2025-26]
- InShot is not tied to any single platform, so its exports are easier to reuse elsewhere. Use it for content that goes to several platforms. [L1453, 2025-26]
- Platform-native text tools in Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts add overlay text in each platform's own fonts and style when the video is uploaded. [L0784, 2025-26]

**Edits (Instagram)**

- Edits is a separate mobile video editing app made by Instagram, positioned as Instagram's counterpart to CapCut. It is a standalone download, not a feature inside the regular Instagram app. [L1453, 2025-26]
- Edits offers more editing options than Instagram's built-in reel editor, including more fonts and animations, and removes many of its limitations. The built-in reel editor is still more restricted. [L1453, 2025-26]
- The Edits app has five main tabs: Ideas, Reels, Edits, Record and Inspiration. Inspiration is opened with the plus icon at the bottom of the screen. [L1453, 2025-26]
- The Reels tab shows videos similar to your own or videos the app recommends you produce. [L1453, 2025-26]
- The Edits tab is the project gallery and holds current projects and drafts. Projects can be sorted by date created or alphabetically. [L1453, 2025-26]
- Recording inside the Edits app allows up to 10 minutes per clip. [L1453, 2025-26]
- How Edits' export metadata behaves after re-uploading to other platforms is unknown. Use InShot for content that goes to several platforms. [L1453, 2025-26]

**iMovie**

- iMovie covers basic cuts and transitions for simple video edits. [L0644, undated]
- iMovie is enough for basic cuts, cropping and simple transitions on UGC or repurposed footage. Use a separate online editor when a clip must be converted to vertical format for social placements. [L1426, undated]
- Online editors are used to convert formats, for example making a video vertical for ads. [L0644, undated]

**TikTok's built-in editor**

- TikTok's built-in editor is fast for quick trims and small fixes without re-rendering a full project. [L1077, undated]
- If TikTok's text-to-speech timing jumps around and will not stay in place, restarting the phone did not fix it, but uninstalling and reinstalling the app did. Try reinstalling before you change the content. [L1444, 2022]

### Voiceover and Text-to-Speech

**ElevenLabs**

- ElevenLabs converts a script into speech in a chosen voice and supports multilingual output. Non-English languages need the multilingual option. Several text-to-speech tools were compared, and ElevenLabs sounded the most natural to the creator. [L0888, 2023+]
- ElevenLabs generates AI voiceovers that can be added to short video creatives. Use it when you need narration without recording your own voice. [L1219, undated]
- ElevenLabs is used to make the voice in ads AI-generated. [L1131, 2018+]
- Setup: [L0888, 2023+]
  1. Choose a voice. [L0888, 2023+]
  2. Optionally change the voice settings. [L0888, 2023+]
  3. Check the languages offered under the multilingual option. [L0888, 2023+]
  4. Paste the prepared text. [L0888, 2023+]
  5. Click Generate. [L0888, 2023+]
  6. Download the audio as an MP3. [L0888, 2023+]
- Voiceover swaps: change a proven video's narration into another language while keeping the video itself. [L1195, 2026]
- Use a multilingual voice model and check the supported languages for any non-English video. The voice should sound familiar to the target audience, such as a documentary-style voice. [L0888, 2023+]
- Before producing audiobooks or other long audio for a store, check how many characters or words the text-to-speech plan includes. Choose the subscription tier so the full text can be converted without running out of allowance. [L0888, 2023+]
- The $5 plan is limited to 50,000 characters per month. [L1131, 2018+]
- Opening several accounts instead of buying a higher plan to stay within a character limit can break the tool's terms of service. Check the rules before doing this. [L1131, 2018+]

**Script source**

- Google Docs holds the script. The creator checks fluency, simplifies the language for listening and keeps the approved final text. It is the single source for the text used in voice generation. [L0888, 2023+]

**Other voice tools**

- CapCut's built-in text-to-speech is described under CapCut above. [L1427, 2023+]

### AI Video, Avatar and Script Tools

**Scripts and images**

- ChatGPT writes the video script from a prompt that specifies length, target age group, style reference and tone. Have a knowledgeable reviewer check the script for accuracy before use. [L0888, 2023+]
- Midjourney, used through Discord, generates portrait images of the character and offers variations of a chosen image. It is used to create the avatar that will later be animated. [L0888, 2023+]
- AI tools are used to produce faceless UGC and viral short content for the store's ads and social posts. [L0914, 2026]

**Avatar animation**

- D-ID animates a single portrait image with an uploaded audio file, moving the lips, blinking and adding facial expressions automatically. The portrait does not need teeth or manual animation setup. [L0888, 2023+]
- D-ID setup: [L0888, 2023+]
  1. Upload the portrait image and the MP3 audio file. [L0888, 2023+]
  2. Generate the talking video. [L0888, 2023+]
  3. The output carries a watermark in the bottom left corner. Cover it before publishing. [L0888, 2023+]

**Video generators**

- Visla creates videos from text scripts and can produce stock-style footage. It struggles with colloquial language and was not yet reliable enough for finished ads at the time of the lesson, but it can produce usable stock footage. [L0353, 2023]
- Visla asks setup questions before it generates a video: aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16 or 1:1), the platform the video will be published on, the tone, the type of video (for example entertainment) and the medium. Answering these first gives a more targeted result. [L0353, 2023]
- Runway is an AI video editing tool that can be used to edit product and marketing videos. [L1416, 2023+]
- Pre-built AI platforms such as Arcads, Higgs Field and Motion come with built-in instructions for specific tasks. They can create ads from a brand website, analyse ads or research ads in public ad libraries. Some allow limited customisation. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Pre-built platforms can replace whole traditional roles, such as a designer or manual static ad production, without custom instructions. Choosing which platform to use, or whether to use one, requires knowing which are best and how to prompt them. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Pre-built platforms work fairly similarly. Trying one for a month is a reasonable test before committing. [L0485, 2025-26]

### Captions and On-Screen Text

- Vide.io generates subtitles automatically from an uploaded video and lets you choose subtitle style, size and font. Its output is only a first draft and needs correction. [L0888, 2023+]
- Vide.io (written "vid.io" in one unit) adds captions to videos, including bold, social-style caption formats. Use it to make short ad videos easier to follow with the sound off. [L1219, undated]
- Auto captioning in CapCut and Premiere Pro generates captions from the spoken audio, which saves time on manual text entry. [L1427, 2023+]
- Style auto captions after generating them, as step 5 of the CapCut ad order. [L1423, undated]
- Platform-native text tools in Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts add overlay text when you upload. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Premiere Pro's TikTok-style text overlay is described under Premiere Pro above. [L0343, 2023]

### Graphics, Canva and Static Assets

- Canva is used for the landscape image layout, the simple logo, the text space and the thumbnail, using its thumbnail templates. It serves all static graphics in the video. [L0888, 2023+]
- Canva can be used to produce promotion ad creatives. Fiverr or similar freelance marketplaces can be used to outsource them. Google Sheets works for the 12-month offer calendar and for storing survey responses. [L1204, 2022]
- Canva Pro costs 11.99 USD per month and includes a stock video library. It is a cheaper option when budget is tight. [L1427, 2023+]
- Re-download AI-generated ads before uploading to Meta:
  - Upload AI-generated ads from ChatGPT or Claude to Canva and re-download them before uploading to Meta. [L0316, 2026]
  - This removes the metadata that Meta reads to detect AI-generated content, which prevents potential CPM penalties. [L0316, 2026]
  - It takes 2 minutes and has no downside. [L0316, 2026]
- Use Photoshop or Illustrator with an Envato Elements library when you need graphics and effects beyond Canva. [L0754, undated]

### Export, Compression and Format Conversion

**Format conversion**

- Aspect ratios for ads: 16:9 (landscape), 9:16 (vertical) and 1:1 (square). Visla asks for the ratio before generating. [L0353, 2023]
- Use a separate online editor to convert a clip to vertical format for social placements. [L1426, undated; L0644, undated]

**Converting video to GIF with ezgif**

- ezgif.com or any search result for "video to GIF converter" converts video clips to GIF format. Upload the video, select the time range, adjust quality and size, and download. [L0321, undated]
- Procedure: [L0321, undated]
  1. Go to ezgif.com. [L0321, undated]
  2. Click Upload Video. [L0321, undated]
  3. Select start and end time (in seconds or milliseconds). [L0321, undated]
  4. Click Convert to GIF. [L0321, undated]
  5. If the file size is too large, click Optimize and reduce quality (for example from quality 100 to 120). [L0321, undated]
  6. Check that quality is acceptable and not pixelated. [L0321, undated]
  7. Click Save or Download to save the GIF file. [L0321, undated]
  - Typical file reduction: 3.49 MB reduced to 1.27 MB by quality adjustment. [L0321, undated]

**Compression**

- Clipchamp was suggested as a free or low-cost video compression tool. [L0857, 2025]
- Paying a freelancer around 5 USD on Fiverr to compress videos is much cheaper than buying a professional editing tool that costs around 300 USD. [L0857, 2025]
