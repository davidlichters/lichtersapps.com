## Keyword and SEO Research Tools

### Choosing Tools by Budget

- Google Search Console is free and sufficient for starting out, before investing in AH Refs or SEM Rush. [L0255, 2025]
- Free keyword research tools are sufficient for basic keyword research, and paid versions are not needed for it. [L0665, 2022]
- At least one paid third-party SEO tool is required for an active SEO strategy, but you do not need to use all of them. Each tool has a different price point, and the return on investment is usually easy to achieve if the tool is used regularly. [L1272, 2021-22]
- The choice of a third-party SEO tool depends on budget and preference, but at least one such tool is needed. Premium tools offer more data depth than budget tools. [L1272, 2021-22]
- The data on links and search terms in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools is very limited. Third-party SEO tools provide far more data on links, search terms and competitor sites. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Paid SEO and outreach tools are worth their cost because automating research and email collection can save hundreds of hours of manual work if the method is used regularly. Free or cheaper options can be used at first, and paid tiers can be added once the workload justifies them. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Free starting source: if you have run campaigns for some time, your own Google Ads search term data, for example from Google Shopping campaigns, already shows search volume for terms in your niche. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Outsourcing keyword research to a freelancer on Fiverr is a cheap alternative to doing it yourself. You give the freelancer your root terms, they pull the keyword data and send it back, and you then group and allocate the terms to pages. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Budget tier, Ubersuggest: the budget option among third-party SEO tools. A paid plan costs roughly $10 to $15 per month (price at time of recording). It offers less data depth than premium tools, but it is enough for many basic tasks. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Ubersuggest price points differ by source:
  - About $10 per month. [L0877, 2021-22]
  - Roughly $10 to $15 per month. [L1272, 2021-22]
  - About $7 US dollars per month, or about 10 Australian dollars per month. [L1485, 2021-22]
- Free Ubersuggest accounts: the free account gives very little usable data, so a paid plan is recommended even on a tight budget. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Ahrefs, premium tier, price: a premium third-party SEO tool at around $100 USD per month (price at time of recording). It is essential for link building and content research and is used heavily when running an active SEO strategy. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Ahrefs, necessity, conflicting views:
  - Ahrefs is considered the best option for keyword research, but it is not strictly necessary. Ubersuggest is a lower-cost alternative whose reports cover the same information. [L0878, 2021-22]
  - Ahrefs, Moz and SEMrush are expensive paid tools. They are more accurate and show more data points per term, but they are not necessary for content keyword research. [L0877, 2021-22]
- SEMrush: a premium alternative to Ahrefs at a similar price level. Its advantage is paid search features such as ad research, which Ahrefs did not offer at that time. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Raven Tools: a suite that costs somewhat less than Ahrefs and SEMrush. Its SEO audit tool analyzes a website and lists areas that can be improved. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Screaming Frog: an older, thorough SEO tool that runs as a paid desktop application, not online. It can handle most SEO tasks, but analyzing a very large website can slow down the computer and take a long time. [L1272, 2021-22]
- For large SEO projects, an online tool is preferable to a desktop application, because a desktop crawl of a very large website can strain the computer and run for a long time. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Most SEO research tools provide the same core reports: keyword, competitor and backlink reports. Their scoring of domain authority and keyword difficulty can differ slightly between tools, but the results should still be usable for the method. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Moz domain analysis: the free version of Moz.com's domain analysis tool shows the domain authority of any website. It allows 3 reports per day and needs no sign-up or payment. [L1227, 2020-26]

### Free Keyword Research Tools

#### Google Keyword Planner

- Google Keyword Planner is free for anyone with a Google Ads account and provides relevant search volume data. [L0877, 2021-22]
- It is used to check search volume for niche keywords before choosing a niche. [L0801, 2021]
- It is used, together with Ubersuggest, to check search volume and demand for niche keywords. [L0664, 2020]
- Volume ranges depend on account history:
  - New accounts: very broad volume ranges, for example 10,000 to 100,000. [L0359, 2020]
  - After the account has spent money on ads: the range narrows to within about 1,000. [L0359, 2020]
- Sign-up: Google Keyword Planner sign-up asks for a company name and a website. If you do not have a website yet, enter the domain name you plan to use. [L1275, 2019]
- Keywords Everywhere can replace Google Keyword Planner when that tool will not let you sign up. [L1275, 2019]
- Keyword and trend data pulled through third-party APIs can fail intermittently. One trend-data source was reported to return results only about 9 percent of the time over a few days. Retry the search after a few minutes first. If failures continue, switch to another data source such as Google Keyword Planner, which requires a Google Ads account. [L1097, 2025]

#### Ubersuggest

- Free version: Ubersuggest has a free version with a limit on searches per period. It gives keyword volume ranges within roughly 1,000 right away, which makes it useful as a free keyword tool before a Google Ads account has spent money. [L0359, 2020]
- Free searches, 2021: as of 2021, Ubersuggest offered five free keyword searches per day, which was an alternative for countries not covered by the program's keyword tool. [L0671, 2021]
- Free access for full data: a free account is needed to see the full data. [L1485, 2021-22]
- Ubersuggest is used to estimate monthly Google search volume per country, and later for other marketing research during the business build. [L1485, 2021-22]
- Ubersuggest is used to fill the competitor analysis worksheet by showing where a competitor's website gets its traffic from. [L0441, 2021-22]
- Metrics: Ubersuggest shows search volume, SEO difficulty, paid difficulty, average CPC, keyword ideas and CSV export. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Keyword Discovery page: three checks per keyword. Use them together when judging a keyword, not volume alone. [L1485, 2021-22]
  - Traffic volume: how many buyers search for it.
  - Trends: how search volume has changed over time.
  - Difficulty: whether a keyword is easy to rank for.
- Market setting: in the language and country selector, choose the market (English / United States for a US store). It appears in the URL as locId=2840 for the United States. Change it to the target country, such as Australia, before every export, because search volume and difficulty differ by country. [L0758, 2021-22]
- Keyword overview: shows searcher age ranges, mobile versus desktop volume, a 12-month volume chart, and the average backlinks and domain score of the top 10 ranking pages. For 'surfboards' the top 10 averaged 14 backlinks and a domain score of 54, while for 'wetsuits' the figures were 33 backlinks and a domain score of 72. Use these benchmarks to estimate how much link building a term will need. [L0758, 2021-22]
- Top-ranking URLs: the keyword overview also lists the top ranking URLs with estimated visits, backlinks, domain score and social shares. Use it to see what kind of page ranks, such as marketplaces, Wikipedia, or store collection pages, before deciding whether to target a term with content, collection or product pages. [L0758, 2021-22]
- Feature gap: Ahrefs and Semrush both allow keyword ranking reports with a featured snippet filter. Ubersuggest does not offer this filter, so it is unsuitable for this step. [L0662, 2021-22]

#### Google and YouTube Auto Suggest

- Google and YouTube Auto Suggest are free and show popular searches related to a root term. They are a good source of keyword ideas, and YouTube keyword research works through its own search suggestions. [L0877, 2021-22]

#### Keywords Everywhere

- Free browser extension: Keywords Everywhere is a free Chrome extension (also described as a free browser extension for Chrome or Firefox) that shows keyword data next to Google search results. Use it during niche research and campaign planning, to research search volume and estimated cost-per-click before a new Google Ads account gains access to the keyword planning tool. [L0243, 2019; L1275, 2019]
- Pricing changed, conflicting views: Keywords Everywhere switched to a paid model, while free keyword tools with similar purposes exist. Use one of those alternatives for niche keyword research if the paid option is not worth it. [L1110, 2019]
- Where both views apply: the 2019 units describe the tool as free, and the later note says it moved to a paid model. Check current pricing before relying on it. [L0243, 2019; L1275, 2019; L1110, 2019]

#### Other Free Research Sources

- Google Gemini was suggested as a starting point for keyword lists, based on the idea that Google's own model may have better access to Google search data. Verify any keyword list it generates before using it in campaigns. [L1287, 2025]

### Paid Keyword and SEO Suites

#### Ahrefs (Keywords Explorer and Site Explorer)

- Ahrefs trial: Ahrefs offers a 14-day trial, which is enough time to run the keyword process for 10 articles. You can cancel after the trial if the results are not useful. [L0382, 2017]
- Ahrefs usage in plan, limits: Keywords Explorer shows these limits in the plan used: 100 daily reports and 1,000 monthly SERP updates. [L0878, 2021-22]
- Overview metrics: Clicks, CPS (clicks per search) and RR (return rate) are shown alongside search volume and CPC. [L0878, 2021-22]
- Bulk keyword checks: Keywords Explorer accepts a bulk list of keywords, pasted separated by commas or on new lines, or uploaded as a CSV or TXT file. This is useful for checking many candidate topics at once. [L0878, 2021-22]
- Keyword lists: you can save candidate keywords into keyword lists, with up to 20 lists per account. Use these lists to group topics for each niche or project. [L0878, 2021-22]
- Ahrefs for collection terms: Ahrefs and SEMrush are both used to quantify monthly search volume for a candidate category before a collection page is created. The same tools can check volume for hybrid keywords on a recurring basis. [L1389, 2025-26]
- Backlinks: Ahrefs shows backlinks to any domain, including the sources of the links. Use it to see where competitors get their links and whether they publish content on other domains. [L0963, 2022]
- Ahrefs and the skyscraper method: Ahrefs is a paid SEO tool used for keyword research, competitor analysis, backlink reports for individual sites, and metrics such as domain authority and keyword difficulty. It is one of the more expensive paid tools in this link-building toolkit. [L1388, 2021-22]
- AH Refs (written as "AH Refs" in the source, most likely Ahrefs):
  - Used by teams to track organic keyword rankings and analyze SEO data. [L0255, 2025]
  - Costs approximately $300 per month for team access (2025 pricing). [L0255, 2025]
  - Price conflict: the $100 per month Ahrefs figure from 2021-22 and the approximately $300 per month figure from 2025 do not match. The 2025 figure applies to team access, and the 2021-22 figure applies to a single plan at that time. [L1272, 2021-22; L0255, 2025]
- Bulk exports, procedures (Ahrefs), see the export and cleaning procedures below. [L1443, 2021-22]

#### Semrush

- SEMrush is a widely used SEO and SEM research tool that offers keyword, competitor and backlink reports, though its report layouts differ and you may need to look around to find the equivalents. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Its advantage over Ahrefs is paid search features such as ad research. [L1272, 2021-22]
- Its featured snippet filter for keyword ranking reports is available. [L0662, 2021-22]
- It is listed among the expensive paid tools that are not necessary for content keyword research. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Ahrefs and Semrush used for collection volume checks. [L1389, 2025-26]

#### Moz

- Moz is listed among the expensive paid tools with keyword research functions. [L0877, 2021-22]

#### Other tools (Answer The Public, one source, Research Vault, one source, BuzzSumo)

- Answer The Public (answerthepublic.com) generates question and preposition keyword variations for a topic and can be exported as CSV to plan content for subniches. [L0665, 2022]
- one source keyword research tool: gives search data by country and keyword. It works for any keyword where data is available, and bugs can be reported to support. [L0665, 2022]
- Research Vault search volume tool: shows how often a keyword is searched. Use it to test whether generic, non-brand terms in a non-English product language are popular enough in English-speaking markets. [L0941, 2022]
- one source keyword tool:
  - Used to find search terms and niche ideas before building a store. [L1089, 2021]
  - Keyword research is done with it, and it should always return results even with zero search volume. If it does not, contact support. [L0969, 2022]
- In-house keyword research tool: gives members unlimited searches when they log in with their member account and select the target country. Logged-out use is limited to a free version, so keyword checks should be done after logging in. [L0962, 2021]
- BuzzSumo does for blog content what vidIQ does for YouTube, showing trending articles and their performance. It is used to research topics before writing blog posts. [L0487, 2018]

### Keyword Research Workflow in Spreadsheets

- Export keyword ideas from Ubersuggest for each root term:
  1. Enter the root keyword in Ubersuggest and open the keyword ideas view. [L0877, 2021-22]
  2. Check that the country setting matches your target market. [L0877, 2021-22]
  3. Click Export to CSV and save the file to your computer. [L0877, 2021-22]
  4. Repeat for every root term in your business, including terms for product types and product uses. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Keyword ideas view tabs. The results are grouped into five tabs: Suggestions, Related, Questions, Prepositions and Comparisons. Each tab has its own count. The SUGGESTIONS count shows how many similar terms were found (for example 255 for '4x4 accessories'). Each keyword row shows KEYWORD, VOL and CPC columns, a Filters option sits above the list, and an EXPORT TO CSV button exports the full list. The Questions tab is a good source for problem-based content topics. [L0877, 2021-22; L1485, 2021-22; L0758, 2021-22]
- Suggestions and Related: the Suggestions tab is the narrowest list of closest matches, while the Related tab can contain many times more terms, most of them off-topic. Scan Related for relevant terms instead of exporting it wholesale, and use the Questions tab for question-form terms that suit problem-based content. [L0877, 2021-22]
- Master keyword workbook layout [L0877, 2021-22]

| Tab | Columns | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Raw Data | Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty (from CSV export) | All exported terms combined, sorted by volume, minimum 100 per month |
| Content | Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty | Informational, problem-based and product-use terms for content pieces |
| Collection | Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty | Broad product type, branded and specific product type terms |
| Product Page | Keyword, Volume, CPC, SEO difficulty | Branded, model-specific and very specific product terms |

- Summary formulas: summarise the volume and CPC columns in each tab with SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX and COUNTA. This shows the total search volume per page type, the average and range of CPC, and how many keywords each group contains. [L0877, 2021-22]

### Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools

#### Google Search Console

- Google Search Console is free (search.google.com) and is used to verify domain ownership, submit a sitemap, see indexed pages, free organic impressions and clicks, search keywords and site errors. [L0735, 2020-26]
- Keyword tracking: Google Search Console is a free tool to track keyword rankings and organic traffic. [L0255, 2025]
- Search terms vs Google Analytics:
  - Google Analytics shows many search terms as "not provided" (2020) or "not set" (2021), because of privacy. Use Google Search Console with the same Google account to see the keywords that bring clicks. [L0468, 2020; L0834, 2021]
  - Google Search Console shows more search terms with impressions and clicks than Google Analytics. [L0834, 2021]
- Submitting new content: Google Search Console (Google webmaster tools) can be used to submit new content URLs so they get crawled sooner. [L0970, 2023]
- Import into Bing: when importing from Google Search Console into Bing, see the Bing setup below. [L0303, 2020-26]

#### Bing Webmaster Tools

- Bing Webmaster Tools, found at bing.com/webmasters, is Bing's free dashboard for seeing how Bing crawls and indexes a website. Its main areas are Search Performance, Site Explorer and error monitoring. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Search Performance and Site Explorer: Search Performance shows how the site appears in Bing search results, and Site Explorer lets you inspect the site's pages. Both are used to find pages that have not been indexed and to spot issues that need fixing. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Sign-in: Bing Webmaster Tools accepts sign-in with a Microsoft, Google or Facebook account. Use the same email address you used for your Google accounts so you can find and manage all accounts consistently. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Same email, what it does and does not do: using the same email address for the Bing login does not link the accounts automatically. Its only benefit is consistent login credentials across services, which makes it easier to remember and access each account. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Setup flow, button and label names as shown:
  - Get started: first screen. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - Import your sites from GSC or Google Search Console: import option on the Welcome page. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - Allow: sharing approval. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - Import: domain confirmation. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - Done: shown after the success message. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - Update now: in the profile section. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Importing from Google Search Console:
  - Bing identifies the Google account by its email address, so choose the account that was used to set up the Search Console property. If several domains are linked to that account, Bing lets you pick which one to import. [L0303, 2020-26]
  - After importing, Bing may need up to 48 hours to process the data and reports. Until processing finishes, the dashboard data may be incomplete. [L0303, 2020-26]
- Profile section: Update now opens fields for personal information, professional details and company information. The same section also contains a delete account option, which should not be clicked by accident. [L0303, 2020-26]

### Competitor and Backlink Research

- Backlink export, Site Explorer:
  1. In Site Explorer, open Backlinks. [L1443, 2021-22]
  2. Set the link filter to Dofollow. [L1443, 2021-22]
  3. Set Group similar links to One link per domain. [L1443, 2021-22]
  4. Click Export. Choose Quick export for the first 1000 rows or Full export for all rows, with CSV format. [L1443, 2021-22]
- SERP export: in Ahrefs, the export button sits at the top right of the SERP results table. Clicking it downloads a CSV. Choose UTF-16 for Microsoft Excel or UTF-8 for Open Office and Libre. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Removing duplicates in Google Sheets: install the Remove Duplicates add-on via Add-ons > Get add-ons, then open Add-ons > Remove Duplicates > Find duplicate or unique rows. Choose Duplicates (exclude first instances), select the URL column, and tick that the table has headers. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Removing duplicates in Excel: Excel has a built-in Find duplicate rows feature that works the same way as the Google Sheets add-on, and it is the simpler choice when available. Apple Numbers does not offer an easy remove-duplicates function. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Excel duplicate modes: the duplicate tool is in the menu and offers four modes: Duplicates, Duplicates + 1st occurrences, Uniques, and Uniques + 1st occurrences. Choose Duplicates (exclude first instances) to keep one copy of each URL and remove the later copies. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Filtering by Domain Rating: in Google Sheets, apply a filter with Data > Create a filter, then use the Domain Rating column to keep only rows with values from 10 to 75. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Example case: when removing duplicates from a merged Ahrefs keyword sheet of just over 100 SERP results, delete the duplicate rows outright instead of copying them to another location. In the example, this removed 19 duplicate rows. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Other tools: the Site Explorer backlink report can be replaced by any SEO tool, and the same workflow works with those exports. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Competitor benchmarks, Ubersuggest: see the overview metrics above. [L0758, 2021-22]
- Moz domain analysis, free: see the free-tools section above. [L1227, 2020-26]
