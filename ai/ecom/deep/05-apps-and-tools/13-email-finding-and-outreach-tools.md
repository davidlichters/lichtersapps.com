### Email Finding and Outreach Tools

#### Hunter.io

- Hunter.io finds email addresses associated with a domain by searching the web. The single Domain Search page handles one domain at a time, while Bulk Domain Search processes whole lists. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Hunter.io is the primary email address scraper for the skyscraper method. It finds email addresses for websites you want to contact, and it works best for small to medium-sized sites and companies, including bulk searches. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Free plan: Hunter.io offers a free entry-level plan that is useful for testing the tool. Its paid plans are tiered by how many emails you need to collect, so you can downgrade to the free plan in months with no outreach and upgrade for a large outreach project. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Pay per result: Hunter.io charges only for successful results. Searching a domain or an email address that returns no result costs nothing, and you pay per email address actually found. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Plan requirement: Bulk Domain Search in Hunter.io requires the Starter plan or a higher plan tier. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Bulk setup, procedure:
  1. Log into Hunter.io on a Starter plan or higher. [L0601, 2021-22]
  2. Open Bulks and choose New bulk Domain Search. [L0601, 2021-22]
  3. Enter a List name; it is internal and any name works. [L0601, 2021-22]
  4. Set Type of input to Domain names. Company names can be used as input instead when you need contacts for one specific company. [L0601, 2021-22]
  5. Paste the copied root domain list into the domain field, or click Select a file and upload a CSV, Excel, Numbers or TXT file. [L0601, 2021-22]
  6. Set Maximum email addresses per domain to 10. [L0601, 2021-22]
  7. Set Email addresses type to Generic and personal. [L0601, 2021-22]
  8. Leave Departments on All departments; leave Include the sources in the result off unless you need sources. [L0601, 2021-22]
  9. Click Upload and wait while the file is prepared. [L0601, 2021-22]
  10. Compare the estimated deliverability tiers and select one. [L0601, 2021-22]
  11. Download the result as a CSV file. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Bulk filter settings, in detail: Maximum email addresses per domain set to 10; Email addresses type set to Generic and personal; Departments set to All departments; Include the sources in the result is optional and was left off. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Do not set the maximum emails per domain to a high number such as 100. Hunter.io deducts one plan credit for every successful email it finds, so large domains consume credits for addresses you will never use. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Do not restrict the search to one email per domain, because companies often have several addresses and the single address returned may not be the best one for outreach. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Speed: a bulk search of 78 domains finishes very quickly, while bulk searches with thousands of domains can take up to 10 to 15 minutes. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Deliverability tiers from a bulk result for 78 domain names requested [L0601, 2021-22]

| Estimated deliverability | Email addresses | Domain names |
|---|---|---|
| 95% | 102 | 28 |
| 93% | 134 | 37 |
| 89% | 181 | 44 |
| 85% | 200 | 50 |

- Threshold: estimated deliverability of 85 percent is acceptable for outreach, while anything below 80 percent is too low to use. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Trade-off: choose the tier that gives the most usable domains while staying above the deliverability threshold. Higher tiers such as 95 percent give fewer addresses, while 85 percent gives more addresses. [L0601, 2021-22]
- CSV result: the Hunter.io CSV result lists every requested domain with all email addresses found for it, including name, position and confidence score, and it is the file used for manual selection. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Confidence score: it is Hunter.io's estimate of how likely an address is to be deliverable. Use it together with the address itself rather than on its own. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Additional CSV columns: the bulk CSV also lists Department, Position, Type, Number of sources, Pattern, Twitter handle, LinkedIn URL and Phone number. Position and Department help check whether a contact fits a content or link request, and Pattern shows the naming format the domain uses for its addresses. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Bulks history: Hunter.io keeps every bulk search listed under Bulks with its list name, creation time and number of domains requested, and a completed search can be deleted from that list. This makes it easy to find an earlier run for the same project or remove test runs. [L0601, 2021-22]

#### Voila Norbert

- Voila Norbit (also written Voila Norbert) is a secondary email finder for locating the address of a specific person when you know their name and the website or company they work for. It is useful when a large business returns too many generic addresses in Hunter.io and you want to contact one named editor or employee. [L1388, 2021-22]
- It is not required for the skyscraper method and is only needed in rare cases where you want to reach a particular individual. Hunter.io should remain the default email finder. [L1388, 2021-22]
- List cleaning, Mailshake's List Cleaner, powered by Voila Norbert, checks addresses before sending and sorts them into Ok, Risky (higher chance of bouncing) and Bad (almost certain to bounce). An Ok result does not guarantee deliverability. The first 500 emails are free, then the price is $10 per 5,000 credits, with one credit per email. [L0946, 2021-22]
- List cleaning is optional, but it is recommended to protect deliverability. Always remove Bad addresses, and remove Risky addresses according to how much risk you accept. [L0946, 2021-22]

#### Mailshake

- Mailshake is a cold email outreach tool built for sending bulk emails to people who have not contacted you before. It also manages the outreach process and provides reporting on campaign results. [L1388, 2021-22]
- Mailshake is a bulk and automated outreach tool that sends personalised cold emails from your connected mailbox and runs automated follow-ups. It is used for sending link-building outreach, with the sending mailbox kept separate from the plan. [L0574, 2021-22]
- It is used to send the outreach emails to the matched contacts when pursuing backlinks for skyscraper content. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Do not send cold outreach emails through a regular email marketing or automation platform such as Klaviyo or Active Campaign. Cold emails to unknown recipients can generate spam complaints and dead addresses, which can damage the deliverability and performance of your main email account. [L1388, 2021-22]

##### Mailshake plans and prices

- Basic plan: 39 USD per month when paid monthly, or 29 USD per month when paid yearly, for one user (2021 to 2022 pricing). Basic includes Gmail and G Suite accounts, unlimited Gmail aliases, open, reply and click tracking, personalisation, advanced scheduling and throttling, automated follow-ups, Lead Catcher and CRM integrations via Zapier. Mailshake also offered a 30-day full refund guarantee at the time. [L0574, 2021-22]
- Pro plan: 44 USD per month per user (2021 to 2022). It adds A/B testing, conversion tracking JavaScript, higher API limits, Salesforce and Slack integrations and 5,000 list-cleaning credits monthly. Extra reopen-cleaning credits were sold at 10 USD per 5,000 reopens. [L0574, 2021-22]
- Pro also adds filter campaigns by teammate and campaign labels, which the Basic plan does not include. [L0574, 2021-22]
- Omni plan: 75 USD per month per user at the time (2021 to 2022). On top of Pro, it adds custom tasks, social selling, a contacts view, a higher recipient limit, an onboarding phone call, a phone dialer and 10,000 list-cleaning credits each month. [L0574, 2021-22]
- Choosing the plan: sign up for the Basic plan, not Pro or Omni. [L0574, 2021-22]

##### Mailshake account setup, procedure

1. Sign up for the Basic plan, not Pro or Omni, and enter payment and account details. [L0574, 2021-22]
2. On the Connect a mail account screen, choose GMAIL / G SUITE (any Google account) and click Next. [L0574, 2021-22]
3. Sign in to the Google account when asked and click Allow so Mailshake can access it. [L0574, 2021-22]
4. Set the sending calendar (sending limit, time between sends and sending pace) and click Next. [L0574, 2021-22]
5. Add the signature, including the unsubscribe placeholder, and click Next. [L0574, 2021-22]
6. Confirm the account under Mail Accounts. Check the sending limit there with the calendar icon before starting your first campaign. [L0574, 2021-22]

##### Sending options

- The Connect a mail account screen offers four sending options: [L0574, 2021-22]
  - GMAIL / G SUITE: any Google account.
  - GMAIL ALIAS: any address set up as an alias in Gmail.
  - MICROSOFT: Outlook, Exchange or Office365 accounts.
  - SMTP: most email addresses.
- For a G Suite mailbox, pick GMAIL / G SUITE and click NEXT. [L0574, 2021-22]

##### Sending calendar presets

On the sending calendar, the cold start preset is the default. Choose the warmed-up preset only if the domain and mailbox have an established sending history. [L0574, 2021-22]

| Setting | New email address or domain (cold start) | Warmed up, experienced sender |
|---|---|---|
| Sending limit | 20 / day | 300 / day |
| Time between sends | 12 min | 30 min |
| Sending pace | 1 email at a time | 5-20 emails at a time |

- Changing the daily send volume: change it in Mailshake under Mail Accounts, using the calendar icon next to the account, then edit the rules to change the maximum emails per day. Save, and also adjust the minutes between sends and the number of emails sent at once if needed. Start at 20 per day and raise the cap to 50 and later to 100 at the right times. [L0574, 2021-22]

##### Campaign creation and import

1. Never work in the template file. Make a copy of the outreach batching template for every outreach task. [L0946, 2021-22]
2. Rename the copy with the batch date, for example 2020.01.12. Any date format is fine. [L0946, 2021-22]
3. Keep all column names exactly as they are in the template. [L0946, 2021-22]
4. Open the Campaigns area and click the pink New campaign button. [L0946, 2021-22]
5. Enter an internal title, for example the topic and the start date. [L0946, 2021-22]
6. Select the from-address. Only one from-address should exist in the account. [L0946, 2021-22]
7. Drag in or select the CSV file and wait for the upload to complete. [L0946, 2021-22]
- CSV mapping: set the special column 'Email address' to the Email Address column and 'Full name (optional)' to the Name column. All other batch columns are imported as text replacement columns: Email Address, Name, Your Article, Topic, Prospects URL, Prospects Root Domain, Competitors URL, Competitors Root Domain and Date. Uncheck any column you do not want to import, then click Next. [L0946, 2021-22]
- Import time: importing a campaign list takes 1 to 2 minutes for a small list and up to about 5 minutes for a larger one. Mailshake may then ask whether to remove generic addresses, such as editorial@ or info@. Keep them when they are the only contact for a site, and click ignore. [L0946, 2021-22]

### Outreach Spreadsheets

#### Outreach Email Filtering template

- The Outreach Email Filtering template is a Google Sheets file that can be copied into Google Drive; the same layout also works as an Excel file. It strips URLs to root domains and holds the Hunter.io output for matching. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Keep the outreach filtering template blank and duplicate it for each new project, so every project starts from a clean sheet. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Template layout [L0601, 2021-22]
  - Column A: labelled 'Enter List of Referring Page URLs Here'.
  - Column B: returns the Referring Page URL Root Domain.
  - Column C: labelled 'Enter List of Link URLs Here'.
  - Column D: returns the Link URL Root Domain.
  - Columns E and F: hold Email Address and Name.
  - A second tab named 'Hunter Output' receives the Hunter.io results.
- Row alignment: columns A and C must be filled from the Ahrefs output sheets with row alignment exact, so each referring page URL stays on the same row as its matching link URL. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Protected columns: deleting cells in the protected columns of the template triggers a warning. Only remove rows in the URL input columns and leave the calculated columns B, D, E and F alone. [L0601, 2021-22]
- Root domain formula, reconstructed from a blurry screen. It has the same logic as the sheet's own version, which is already in the file. It removes the protocol, a leading www., and everything after the first slash, leaving only the root domain. [L0601, 2021-22]

```
=IFERROR(IF(ISERROR(FIND("://www.",A2)),MID(A2,FIND("://",A2)+3,FIND("/",A2&"/",FIND("://",A2)+3)-FIND("://",A2)-3),MID(A2,FIND("://",A2)+7,FIND("/",A2&"/",FIND("://",A2)+7)-FIND("://",A2)-7)),"")
```
[L0601, 2021-22]

- Preparing root domains, procedure [L0601, 2021-22]
  1. Duplicate the blank Outreach Email Filtering template for this project.
  2. Copy the referring page URL column from the Ahrefs output and paste it into column A under 'Enter List of Referring Page URLs Here'.
  3. Read the root domain for each referring page from column B.
  4. Copy the link URL column from the Ahrefs output and paste it into column C under 'Enter List of Link URLs Here'.
  5. Read the root domain for each link URL from column D.
  6. Remove rows you do not need from the URL columns, but do not delete anything in columns B, D, E or F.
  7. Copy the complete root domain list from column B to use as input for Hunter.io.
- Maintaining the email filtering spreadsheet, procedure [L0946, 2021-22]
  1. Paste the SEO tool output into columns A and C, making sure each row lines up exactly with its source.
  2. Do not delete anything in columns B, D, E and F.
  3. Enter the Hunter output in the second tab, named Hunter Output.
  4. When finished, remove the URLs from columns A and D. The other columns refresh automatically.
- Moving filtered data into the batch spreadsheet, procedure [L0946, 2021-22]
  1. Open the email filtering spreadsheet and filter the rows so that rows with a blank email address are hidden or removed. Rows without an email cannot be contacted.
  2. Select all data rows, excluding the heading rows, and copy them.
  3. In the batch spreadsheet, click the first cell under the Prospects URL column, not under Your Article.
  4. Use Paste special and choose values only, because formulas do not work after they are moved.

#### Outreach batching spreadsheet

Column order (do not rename any column) [L0946, 2021-22]

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Your Article | URL of the article you want linked |
| Topic | Topic from the original research |
| Prospects URL | URL of the prospect's page that links to a competitor or relevant source |
| Prospects Root Domain | Root domain of the prospect URL |
| Competitors URL | URL of the competitor page where the prospect found the link |
| Competitors Root Domain | Root domain of the competitor URL |
| Email Address | Contact email address of the prospect |
| Name | Contact first name, if known |
| Date | Date the outreach is sent |

- Filling the remaining batch fields, procedure [L0946, 2021-22]
  1. Enter the send date in the Date column and copy it down the column.
  2. Enter the topic from your original research in the Topic column.
  3. Enter the URL of your article in the Your Article column.

  Mailshake will insert these values into the emails through the matching fields. [L0946, 2021-22]
- Export for Mailshake: in Google Sheets, download the file as CSV. In Excel or another spreadsheet tool, save or convert the file as CSV before uploading. [L0946, 2021-22]
- CSV export from Hunter: see the Hunter.io bulk download above. [L0601, 2021-22]

### Shopify SEO Apps

#### Smart SEO

- Smart SEO is a Shopify SEO app built by a company called Sherpas. It bundles several SEO functions (meta tags, alt tags, image compression, page speed, broken links, backlink reporting, structured data) into one tool. Other SEO apps exist, so using it is a choice, not a requirement. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Smart SEO is a Shopify app used for image optimization, meta tag generation and JSON-LD settings on pages, blog posts and articles. It handles the on-page technical steps in this workflow. [L1321, 2020-26]
- Use Smart SEO for SEO work on product pages, blog posts, and pages, including image compression. Do not run Tiny SEO alongside it; uninstall Tiny SEO once Smart SEO is installed. [L0808, 2026]
- Smart SEO is the Shopify SEO app used for setting up JSON-LD structured data in the current SEO lesson. The older JSON-LD app is no longer on the Shopify app store. [L0856, 2024]

##### Plans and pricing

- Smart SEO has a free plan and a Pro plan at $9.99 per month. The JSON-LD structured data feature is only available on the Pro plan, so start with Free if you are unsure how much SEO work you will do and upgrade later. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Choosing Free means the JSON-LD section is not available, so no structured data gets sent to Google. You can switch to Pro at any time later. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Pro plan approval screen: when you select the Smart SEO Pro plan at $9.99 per month, the Shopify approval screen shows a 7-day free trial and the price that applies after it. Read that screen and approve the charge only if you accept the monthly price once the trial ends. [L1337, 2020-26]

##### Features

- Image compression: Smart SEO compresses images so their file size drops while the visual quality stays acceptable. Smaller images load faster, which improves page speed scores and helps users on slow connections. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Meta tags and alt text: Smart SEO can generate meta titles and descriptions with AI, but this is optional and can be done manually. The app also lets you edit image alt texts in bulk. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Meta tag generation, procedure: [L0368, 2020-26]
  1. Open Smart SEO > Meta Tags > Collections.
  2. Select collections, then click Edit collections (bulk editing is available).
  3. Enter the main keyword, related keywords and tone of voice (for example playful).
  4. Click Generate to write the meta title and description. Try again gives alternatives.
- Predictive page loading and broken links: Smart SEO includes predictive page loading, which preloads pages to make the site load faster. It also detects and fixes broken internal links, which becomes more important as the store grows over months and years. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Backlink report: Smart SEO produces a backlink report that shows which sites link to your store. This becomes useful once content grows and more external sites link to you. [L1337, 2020-26]
- Left navigation: after Smart SEO is integrated, its left navigation lists separate sections for meta tags, alt tags, image optimization, page speed, broken links and more. Each option has built-in video tutorials inside the app, so new users can learn each function there. [L1337, 2020-26]

##### Installation, procedure

1. Open the Smart SEO listing in the Shopify App Store using the link provided. [L1337, 2020-26]
2. If you have several Shopify accounts, make sure you are signed into the correct one. [L1337, 2020-26]
3. Click Install. [L1337, 2020-26]
4. Review the access the app requests and approve it, since it must be able to work on your store. [L1337, 2020-26]
5. On the plan page, choose Free or Pro. [L1337, 2020-26]
6. If you choose Pro, confirm the 7-day free trial and the price shown after the trial, then approve the charge. [L1337, 2020-26]
7. Open the app and click Set up. [L1337, 2020-26]
8. If the app status says Smart SEO is not fully integrated, click Integrate now. [L1337, 2020-26]
9. Confirm the status shows the app as integrated in your store. [L1337, 2020-26]

- After activation the app shows Current plan (for example Pro) and App status. If App status says Smart SEO is not fully integrated, the app is not yet installed correctly and you must click Integrate now. [L1337, 2020-26]

#### Tiny SEO

- Tiny SEO is an SEO app for when the store is scaling. The lesson says to add it once a store reaches about 30K gross in sales. [L1327, 2026]
- Conflict to note: the one source lesson (2026) suggests adding Tiny SEO at about 30K gross, while one source's rule says not to run Tiny SEO alongside Smart SEO and to uninstall it once Smart SEO is installed. Use Tiny SEO only as an alternative to Smart SEO, not alongside it. [L1327, 2026; L0808, 2026]

#### Shopify image sitemap

- A Shopify image sitemap app generates a separate sitemap listing all product images. On Shopify, install such an app from the app store and submit its image sitemap URL in Google Search Console alongside the standard sitemap, not instead of it. Other platforms may not have this limitation and need their own check. [L1333, 2021-22]

### Schema, Noindex and Page Audit

- Schema markup:
  - If a Shopify store already runs a working schema markup app, no action is needed. If it does not, install a schema app rather than relying on manual code or a developer. [L1349, 2021-22]
  - Hiring a developer to add schema markup creates two problems: the store owner cannot easily verify that the work was done correctly, and every future markup change requires going back to that developer. A schema app avoids both because its developers update the markup automatically. [L1349, 2021-22]
- Noindex on Shopify: the recommended way to add noindex to selected pages is a low-cost Shopify app rather than hand-editing theme.liquid. Manual code edits carry a risk: a small error can noindex the entire domain, causing search traffic to disappear overnight and requiring a long re-indexing process. [L1372, 2021-22]
- Google Sheets page audit tracker, columns [L1372, 2021-22]
  - Page Title
  - Page URL
  - Duplicate Content?
  - Thin Content?
  - Fix Content?
  - Delete?
  - If delete, redirect to where?
  - No-Index?
  - Separate sheets or columns for Page Audit and Orphan Pages.

### Bulk and AI-Assisted Tools

- SEO Gets MCP connects Search Console data to Claude, so query-level metrics can feed opportunity analysis and striking-distance keyword selection. It is used to build the targets for title and intro rewrites. [L1269, 2025-26]
- Firecrawl integration scrapes the current titles, meta data and intro text of blog pages. It is used to compare current content with the new rewrites before any change is made. [L1269, 2025-26]
- Matrix 5 is a Shopify app for bulk imports and is useful for large catalogs. Meta fields prepared in a spreadsheet can be imported in one batch instead of editing each page by hand. [L1269, 2025-26]
- Shop by Markets is a Shopify app that handles canonical and hreflang setup for market or language storefronts well. Use it only when the store runs localized subfolders or markets, such as /en-gb or /fr-fr. A single-market store with a standard domain can skip this step. [L1269, 2025-26]

### Ubersuggest: Access and Cost

- A free Ubersuggest account is not enough. You must sign up for an account to access the good data. [L1486, 2021-22]
- At the time of recording (2021-22, older pricing), the paid plan cost about USD 7 per month or about AUD 10 per month. [L1486, 2021-22]
- If the monthly cost is a concern, subscribe only for the market research phase and cancel afterwards. [L1486, 2021-22]
- Keeping the subscription is recommended, because it stays useful for ongoing marketing and keyword research after the store is built. [L1486, 2021-22]
