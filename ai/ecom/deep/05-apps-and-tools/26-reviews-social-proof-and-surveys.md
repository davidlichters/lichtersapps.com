## Reviews, Social Proof and Surveys

### Why reviews and social proof come first

- Product reviews build trust with new visitors, which helps them decide to buy. Collect both product reviews and store reviews. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Shoppers look for social proof and trust before buying, so a review app is treated as a must. [L0635, undated]
- Install a Shopify product review system on every product page to show social proof, even a basic one. The author calls this well worth the effort. [L1346, 2020-26]
- A review section does not need to be built by hand on a Shopify store. Use an app for the reviews and a page builder or theme section for the layout around them. [L0387, 2022]
- Review apps and urgency apps are installed on Shopify to add urgency elements and product reviews to store pages. [L0532, undated]
- A Questions & Answers app shows customer questions and answers on product pages. This creates user-created content for SEO and builds social proof to increase conversions. [L0176, undated]

### Ethics and rules for collecting reviews

- Ask only approved suppliers for reviews, and only if they have product reviews available. Request their permission before using any reviews, and do not copy reviews without explicit supplier approval. [L0155, undated]
- Supplier reviews can be entered with permission, so they appear on product pages in the same style as future customer reviews. [L0855, 2023]
- reviews provided by a supplier can be uploaded to Judge.me only with written permission from that supplier. [L1090, 2021]
- Editing or inventing reviews misleads buyers and can violate consumer-protection rules, for example fake-review rules in the US. Use review tools only for real customer feedback. [L0502, 2020+]
- **Conflict on seeding.** The seeding units say that initial reviews can come from suppliers, family or friends to establish credibility before paid traffic begins [L0040, 2020-26]. Review apps such as Ali Reviews or Judge.me can seed initial reviews on a new store, to be replaced later with real customer reviews [L0513, undated]. The ethics units require real customer feedback and supplier permission [L0502, 2020+; L0155, undated].
- **Operator reading:** the seeding units and the ethics units apply to different situations. A seeded review must not be presented as a real customer review, and supplier reviews need permission before they are shown. [L0040, 2020-26; L0513, undated; L0502, 2020+; L0155, undated]
- Review imports from supplier listings can show weak or irrelevant feedback. Screen each review before import and reject any that do not fit the product. Limit imports to four stars and up with photos to keep the store credible. [L0634, undated]

### Judge.me: profile and plans

- Judge.me Product Reviews collects, displays and manages customer product reviews. Find it by searching "judge.me" in the Shopify app store. [L1245, 2020-26; L0851, 2025]
- Judge.me is the recommended reviews app for dropshipping stores. It has been used successfully for 7-8 years and is the best fit for high-ticket dropshipping operations. [L0155, undated]
- The free plan is enough to collect and display reviews. The paid plan is not required, and no billing setup is needed to start. [L0155, undated]
- The paid "awesome plan" is optional and can be added later. Installing the free plan requires no agreement to a billing policy. [L1245, 2020-26]
- On the free plan, widget texts such as "Awesome" cannot be edited. Editing them requires the paid plan. [L0908, 2020-26]
- **Conflict on Google star ratings and the free plan:**
  - One unit says the Judge.me free plan can automatically email review requests and shows star ratings in Google and Google Shopping. [L0908, 2020-26]
  - A 2025 unit says the Google Shopping star integration costs $15 per month and the free plan does not include it. [L0972, 2025]
  - Use the 2025 value as the more recent one, and check the plan in the app before relying on Google Shopping stars. [L0908, 2020-26; L0972, 2025]
- Judge.me was chosen over the previous review app because it displays star ratings in Google and Google Shopping, which few low-cost review apps offer. Showing stars in search and shopping results can improve click-through. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Judge.me reviews can appear in Google Shopping ads through its Google Shopping feature. It is worth paying for once a store has a good number of reviews (2024). [L1115, 2024]
- Judge.me integrates more easily with Google Shopping star ratings than Stamped.io. [L0834, 2021]
- The paid Judge.me plan sends star ratings to Google Shopping. Stamped.io is an alternative with a similar function. [L1111, 2020]

### Judge.me: Google, search stars and trust signals

- Judge.me collects reviews and integrates with Google, so star ratings can appear in Google Shopping results. It is used as the review aggregator in the store setup. [L0803, 2022]
- Judge.me star reviews add schema markup to organic results, which can make star ratings appear in search and increase click-through rate (2023). [L1283, 2023]
- Judge.me displays reviews across the site and on a dedicated review page. This serves as the store's trust signal in place of paid security seals (2023). [L0934, 2023]
- Judge.me stars can appear in Google Shopping results and replace SEO Rich Snippets for reviews on new stores (2023). [L0804, 2023]
- SEO Rich Snippets and Minifier were removed from the Shopify app store. Replace them as follows: [L0804, 2023]
  1. Open the Shopify app store.
  2. Search for rich snippets and minifier.
  3. Choose a 5-star app that does the same job.

  Existing stores keep the old apps they already had. [L0804, 2023]
- Judge.me badges look similar to Trustpilot and give a more authoritative look on the homepage. Check that the badge links down to the reviews section on the page. Most review apps that work with PageFly support this. If not, contact the review app or PageFly support. [L1363, 2023]
- Judge.me needs the license number from the theme vendor entered in its setup. Without that license entry, the review block may not work correctly on the product page. [L1188, 2026]

### Installing Judge.me

Install steps in Shopify:

1. Open the Shopify App Store and click Visit the app store.
2. Search for Judge.me and open the first result, which shows the free plan.
3. Click Add app.
4. Agree to give the app access to the store so it works properly.
5. Click Install app.
6. Choose who installs it: yourself via the theme editor, or Judge.me's team.
7. Click Start installation.
8. Select where reviews should appear: the reviews widget on the product page, stars on the product page, and stars on the collection page.
9. Click Choose theme and install on the current live theme, unless another theme is installed but not yet published and should be used.
10. Click Install judge.me and wait for the installation to finish.

[L0908, 2020-26]

- Search for Judge.me in the Shopify App Store, open the app, click Add app, grant access to the store, and click Install app. Choose between installing it yourself through the theme editor or having the Judge.me team install it for you. [L0908, 2020-26]
- The Judge.me support team installs widgets manually and sends an email within three hours after the request. Installation may take about two hours in practice. [L0908, 2020-26]
- The Judge.me widget install (2024): after installing, add the review widget to product pages from the app's top-left box. If the automatic install does not show it, use the app's contact-developer box, and the developer can install it. [L0836, 2024]

**Widgets (procedure):**

1. Go to Judge.me → Widgets.
2. Locate the Review Widget.
3. Click Install.
4. The widget will be added to your Shopify theme automatically.

[L0155, undated]

- The product page review widget is found under Settings > Review display > Widgets. The first time, click Install; it adds the widget automatically to the Shopify theme. A separate star ratings badge widget is also available there, and several widgets can be used at once. [L1245, 2020-26]
- In Settings, the Other widgets section shows where each widget is toggled on or off on the site. Under the preview badge settings you can set badge colors and choose Hide badge texts. Leave it off so both stars and text are shown. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Branding: open Settings and scroll to the Branding section at the bottom left. Set the primary color to one of the colors used in the store's layout and color scheme. This primary color also styles the review request emails, including the stars. [L1245, 2020-26]

### Judge.me: review requests and emails

- Judge.me automatically sends a review request email 14 days after purchase. This timing ensures customers have received the product before being asked to write a review. [L0155, undated]
- Judge.me Settings groups requests under Review requests and Other emails. These control the automatic review request emails sent to customers. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Customers can send their review by email or leave it through the Shopify Product Review app, which should already be installed on the store. [L0417, 2020-26]
- Judge.me is a product review app that stores reviews for each product and also a general store review area. Use it to add reviews collected by email (2024). [L0965, 2024]
- Judge.me publishes product reviews on product pages (2023). [L0970, 2023]

**Email templates (procedure):**

1. Go to Judge.me → Email Templates.
2. Edit subject lines or review request copy if desired.
3. Leave default settings enabled, which is recommended.

[L0155, undated]

### Judge.me: moderation

- Judge.me automatically approves and publishes reviews by default. In the dashboard you can view all reviews, mark spam or unrelated reviews as hidden, and control what appears on product pages. [L0155, undated]
- New reviews appear in the review section and are published by default. Each review can be marked as Fake, Spam or Unrelated to the product, which hides it from the store. When hiding a review, choose the reason for hiding it. [L1245, 2020-26]
- Moderation steps:
  1. Open Judge.me and go to the review section.
  2. Check the new reviews that appear, which are published by default.
  3. For any spam, fake or unrelated review, mark it as Fake, Spam or Unrelated to the product.
  4. Select the reason for hiding the review; it is then hidden from the store.
  5. Edit any review if needed. You receive a notification each time someone leaves a review.

  [L1245, 2020-26]
- Mistake: leaving spam or fake reviews visible on the store. Marking them as Fake, Spam or Unrelated hides them from the product page and keeps unrelated content off the store. [L1245, 2020-26]

### Judge.me: entering reviews, imports and migration

**Entering supplier reviews directly (procedure, with permission only):**

1. Confirm the supplier gives permission to use their reviews.
2. Enter reviews directly on your product pages.
3. Submit them the same way a customer would submit a review.
4. They will publish like normal customer reviews.

[L0155, undated]

- Supplier reviews can be added one by one or imported with a spreadsheet upload (2025). [L0972, 2025]
- Judge.me can import reviews that were originally posted on eBay. Export the eBay reviews as a file first, then upload that file in Judge.me. This only works if you already have eBay feedback you want to show on your store. [L0941, 2022]
- The Judge.me importer is very important: install it so that existing reviews can be brought into the store. [L1327, 2026]

**Migrating from an old review app (procedure):**

1. In Judge.me, go to the import function and click Import reviews to bring in existing reviews from the old app. Confirm the import succeeded.
2. Wait until Judge.me confirms by email that the app is installed.
3. In Shopify, remove the old review app(s), such as product reviews apps, after the import is done.
4. Also remove the old app Stamped.io once it is no longer needed.
5. If no reviews are currently being collected, the removal steps can be skipped.

[L0908, 2020-26]

- Do not remove an old review app before its reviews are imported into the new app. Once the import is complete, exporting settings from the old app is not needed for Judge.me. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Moving existing reviews from Stamped.io to Judge.me: open the import function in Judge.me, click Import reviews, and confirm that the import succeeded. Remove Stamped.io from the Shopify store only after the import is confirmed. [L0908, 2020-26]
- running two product review apps on the same store can show each review twice on the product page. Keep one review app, remove the other, and check the product pages. [L0801, 2021]

### Luke's Reviews

- Luke's Reviews collects, imports and shows product reviews and photos. It sends automatic review request emails after fulfillment and can run a referral and photo-review incentive. [L0476, undated]
- The import-to-looks bookmark is a Chrome bookmark that copies review data from an AliExpress product page into Luke's Reviews for import. [L0476, undated]

**Setup (procedure):**

1. Go to Apps, open Luke's product reviews and photos, click Install, and scroll down to install the app.
2. Click Continue as free.
3. Follow the setup: in the theme editor turn on the app embed and click save.
4. Add the review widget below the product description and click save.
5. Add the rating widget below the product title and click save.
6. Choose the store language and icon color, then add your branding colors.
7. Set the review request email: choose how many days after fulfillment the email is sent. Pick a number based on your supplier's delivery time (the lesson used 21 days).
8. Set the photo and video incentive: a discount reward of 15 for photo reviews and 20 for video reviews, given automatically.
9. Optionally set up the referral program: the referred friend gets a discount (example: 10 off), the referrer gets a reward (example: $5), and you can set a minimum spend and choose whether to show it on the store.
10. Click continue through the remaining screens.

[L0476, undated]

- Parallel setting summary: install Luke's Product Reviews and Photos from the Shopify app store (free plan), turn on the app embed in the theme editor, then add the review widget below the description and the rating widget below the product title. Set the review request email to be sent 21 days after fulfillment (adjust for the supplier's shipping time). Offer a discount for reviews of 15 for photos and 20 for videos. [L0634, undated]
- Referral program settings: set the discount the referred customer receives (example: 10 off), the reward for the referrer (example: 5 dollars), and the minimum spend required. Choose whether to show this on the store. [L0634, undated]
- **Email timing across apps:** Judge.me sends its review request 14 days after purchase by default [L0155, undated]. In Luke's Reviews, the request is sent a number of days after fulfillment, set to the supplier's delivery time (21 days in the lesson) [L0476, undated; L0634, undated]. Use the app's own timing setting for each store.

**Import from AliExpress (procedure, version A):**

1. In Google Chrome, drag the import to looks button from the Luke's Reviews setup into the bookmarks bar.
2. Open the product page on AliExpress.
3. Click the import to looks bookmark, choose your store, and choose the product that the reviews belong to.
4. Set the rating filter to four stars and up.
5. Choose the countries the reviews should come from.
6. Turn on the option to import only reviews that include photos, if wanted, and set fetch most recent reviews to yes.
7. Click preview and import.
8. Accept each review you want with import, and reject the others.
9. Click Finish importing.

[L0476, undated]

**Import from AliExpress (procedure, version B):**

1. Open Google Chrome and drag the Import to Looks button to the bookmarks bar.
2. Open the matching AliExpress product page.
3. Click the Import to Looks bookmark, choose your store and the product.
4. Set the rating filter to four stars and up.
5. Choose the countries the reviews should come from.
6. Enable only reviews with photos and fetch the most recent reviews.
7. Click Preview and import.
8. For each review, click Import to keep it or Reject to skip it.
9. Click Finish importing.

[L0634, undated]

- The free Looks plan can import only 20 reviews per import. More reviews require a higher paid plan. [L0635, undated]

### Ali Reviews, Loox and other review apps

- Ali Reviews is a paid review app. It lets you edit review text, names, pictures, product links and target country. It costs about $19 per month at lower volume and about $39.90 per month at high sales volume. The lesson notes that the high-volume price is more than Shopify's 29 USD monthly plan. [L0502, 2020+]
- Judge.me and Loox are other review apps besides Ali Reviews. Ali Reviews allows editing of review text, names, pictures and target country. Compare these tools only on real customer reviews and conversion results. [L0502, 2020+]
- Ali Reviews can add review images. Use it to publish reviews that answer specific buyer doubts, such as fit or size concerns. [L0773, 2023]
- The same lesson uses Ali Reviews images to show that a product does not have a concern. Judge.me and Ali Reviews are both review apps for Shopify. [L0774, 2023]
- Judge.me also adds review images, which can strengthen trust on product pages. [L0773, 2023]
- Riview imports reviews from AliExpress and Amazon, with more customization options than Loox. Both apps can add review sections to product pages, but Riview gives more layout flexibility. [L0321, undated]
- Rightview can fetch 600 or more Amazon reviews for a product. Amazon Review Export takes only about 100 at a time. It exports a CSV that you can edit and re-import into a review app, and set to update automatically when new reviews appear. Most Shopify review apps do not allow editing. [L0335, 2023]
- Rivo was suggested as a cheaper alternative to Loox for early-stage stores. It has a 7 to 14-day free trial and a plan around $6 per month, and it offers review editing that other apps lack. [L1410, 2023]
- Right View is a product review app with review editing, presented as a cheaper alternative to Loox. It offers a free trial of 7 to 14 days and a plan around 6 USD, and it suits a store on a tight budget. [L1412, 2023]
- **Naming note:** the sources name this tool Riview, Rightview, Right View and Rivo. The exact name is not confirmed by the sources, so check the Shopify App Store before installing. [L0321, undated; L0335, 2023; L1412, 2023; L1410, 2023]
- Ali Reviews, Judge.me and similar review apps can seed initial product reviews on a new store. Later, replace them with real customer reviews. [L0513, undated]
- Stamped.io is a review platform. Its custom forms, menu and plans are as follows:
  - In the Reviews & UGC platform, go to Customize and then Add Custom Forms to add extra review questions, such as how the fit was, how the quality is, or the customer's age group. These questions collect buyer insight beyond the star rating. The platform recommends adding them once reviews start coming in. [L1298, 2021-22]
  - The main menu includes Home, Queue, Customers, Products, Analytics and Marketing. Under Moderation it has Reviews, Questions, Net Promoter Score, Checkout Comments, Visual UGC and Instagram. Loyalty & Rewards is a separate section. The Home page summarises the last 30 days of widget interactions, revenue and advocate traffic. [L1298, 2021-22]
  - The free-forever plan supports only standard product reviews, covering up to 50 orders. Photo reviews and other richer review types require a paid plan, starting with Basic at $23 per month for up to 200 orders. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Judge.me and Stamp.io are the most common review apps on Shopify. Ask other review tools, such as Yotpo, whether they can load the review title with the product name. [L1191, 2021-22]
- Stamped.io is an alternative review app with a similar function to Judge.me. [L1111, 2020]

### Junip

- Junip is a Shopify review app for collecting, managing and displaying product and store reviews. It runs review request flows and campaigns to start gathering reviews right away. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Junip sends customers an email asking them to leave a review. The email links to a mobile-first submission form. Review request flows and campaigns are set up inside Junip. [L0589, 2025-26]
- The submission form accepts a star rating and a text review by default. Customers are encouraged to add photos and videos. Custom questions can be added using product traits and customer attributes. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Photo and video reviews add more evidence than text alone. The vendor gives no figure for how much they raise conversion, so test them on your own store. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Junip review displays adapt automatically to the look and feel of the Shopify theme. No custom code is needed to match the brand. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Junip integrates with Klaviyo, OmniSend and PostScript, and offers API access for custom needs. These integrations connect review collection with existing email and SMS tools. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Junip offers a free 14-day trial that can be installed directly. Use the trial to test review requests and displays before paying. [L0589, 2025-26]

**Junip incentives:**

- Junip has a two-tier incentive system. The standard incentive covers all reviews. The immediate incentive covers only reviews that include a photo or video. [L1244, 2025-26]
- The standard incentive is given for any submitted review, regardless of its content. The reward does not depend on how good or detailed the review is. [L1244, 2025-26]
- The immediate incentive is given only when a review includes a photo or video. Text-only reviews do not receive this second-tier reward. [L1244, 2025-26]
- Junip generates a unique, single-use discount code for each eligible review that is submitted. The customer receives this code after submitting the review. [L1244, 2025-26]
- Path: Incentives > Add incentive > Add standard incentive. Under Incentive type, pick Free shipping, Percentage discount or Fixed discount, then set the amount. [L1244, 2025-26]
- Under Reward this to, choose "Verified buyers only (Recommended)" or "Anyone who leaves a review, including unverified buyers." The verified-only option is marked as the recommended choice. [L1244, 2025-26]

**Junip incentive setup (procedure):**

1. Go to Incentives and click Add incentive.
2. Click Add standard incentive.
3. Under Incentive type, select Free shipping, Percentage discount or Fixed discount.
4. Enter the amount for the chosen incentive type.
5. Under Reward this to, select Verified buyers only (Recommended), or Anyone who leaves a review, including unverified buyers.

[L1244, 2025-26]

### Video reviews

- VideoPeel lets customers record video reviews on their phone with very little effort. It is recommended because older methods of getting customers to record and upload video were more cumbersome. [L1011, 2021]
- VideoPeel records and uploads the video automatically, so customers can leave a video review from a phone in a few clicks. [L1012, 2021-22]
- **Conflict on dedicated tools:** review apps such as Judge.me or Stamp.io can also collect video reviews, depending on plan and features. A dedicated video tool is therefore optional if the review app already supports it. [L1011, 2021]

### Testimonial and UGC forms

- Typeform can host a testimonial request form. The form should collect first name, last name, a video upload, and a checkbox confirming that the customer agrees the content can be used in ads. [L0789, 2022]
- Jotform is used to build a customer review and UGC submission form with file upload, email field and category choice. A ready template can be cloned from the link provided and adapted with the store name. [L0605, undated]
- Testimonial collection feeds social proof on product and store pages. Pair it with the review apps above, so testimonials and reviews appear in one consistent place. [L0040, 2020-26; L0589, 2025-26]
