### Security and Gemini in Google Workspace

- Enable two-step verification for all Google Workspace users. It sends a code by text message when logging in on a new device. This protects the account from anyone who obtains the password. [L1196, 2020-26; L1197, 2020-26]
- The other optional items during setup, such as the Gemini sidebar and workspace app downloads, can be skipped or enabled later. [L1196, 2020-26]
- The Gemini AI sidebar in Gmail can summarize emails, draft emails and auto replies, and find information in the inbox. It is included with the Google Workspace account and can be minimized or closed. It is optional and not required for the email setup. [L1196, 2020-26; L1197, 2020-26]
- Gemini in Gmail is included in the Starter plan (Singapore list). [L1327, 2026]

### Alternative Mail Provider

- Zoho Workplace is a common alternative to Google Workspace for business email with custom domain aliases. [L1285, 2024; older]

- A phone-forwarding app can route the store's phone number to your cell phone, so customer calls are answered or returned quickly. It is useful when you choose to sell higher-priced products with a phone number on the site from day one. [L0835, 2023]

### Quo: Setup and Use

- Quo (formerly OpenPhone) is a cloud-based business phone system with iOS and Android mobile apps. It routes dedicated business numbers and messages through your existing smartphone. Incoming business calls are clearly labeled. [L0122, undated]
- Quo is a business phone system with a mobile app that gives a store its own phone number. Calls placed through the app show the business number to the other side. Incoming calls to that number ring in the app, so the owner can still use a personal phone. [L0338, 2020-26]
- Quo's Starter plan includes one business phone number with a choice of local or toll-free, calling and messaging in the US and Canada, and automatic voicemail transcripts. [L0122, undated; L0338, 2020-26]
- Quo's calling and messaging for business numbers works in the USA and Canada only. [L0338, 2020-26]
- Quo's Starter plan includes business messaging (SMS text) on the business number in addition to calling. Customers and suppliers can write to the same number as they call. [L0338, 2020-26]

**Sign up for Quo (first version):**

1. Click the Quo signup link. [L0122, undated]
2. Select Try for free. [L0122, undated]
3. Enter your business email address. [L0122, undated]
4. Choose the Starter plan. [L0122, undated]
5. Select your phone number type (local or toll-free). [L0122, undated]

**Sign up for Quo and install the app (second version):**

1. Open the Quo sign-up page using the link provided with the lesson. [L0338, 2020-26]
2. Click Try for free. [L0338, 2020-26]
3. Sign up with a business email address. [L0338, 2020-26]
4. Answer the setup questions, including the desired phone number. The account is created instantly. [L0338, 2020-26]
5. Select the Starter plan and claim the included toll-free number. [L0338, 2020-26]
6. Install the Quo app from the App Store on iPhone or from the Android app store, then log in with the same account. The app works right after login. [L0338, 2020-26]

**Set up the Quo mobile app:**

1. Download the Quo app from the App Store or Google Play. [L0122, undated]
2. Log in with your Quo account. [L0122, undated]
3. Calls and messages begin working immediately. [L0122, undated]

### Google Voice: Setup and Use

- Google Voice is added to an existing Google Workspace account that was set up earlier with the business email. It costs $10 per month, is managed in the Google Voice app on phone and computer, and offers only local numbers chosen by area code. Toll-free numbers are not offered. [L0700, 2020-26]
- Google Voice works well in the US but not while traveling abroad. [L0910, 2015]
- Google Voice serves as the business phone line, answered by the VA during business hours, while other inbound messages go into the help desk. [L1473, 2019]
- On Google Voice, a call recording starts only after the agent presses a key, and a recording notice plays to the caller. Staff often skip this step, so do not rely on Google Voice for consistent call recording. [L0910, 2015]
- Check the consent rules for recording calls in the countries where the store and its callers are located. [L0910, 2015]

**Google Voice setup for a US business number:**

1. Open the Google Voice sign-up page and confirm you are logged in to the Google Workspace account created with the business email. [L0700, 2020-26]
2. Scroll to the pricing section and choose the Starter plan at $10 per month. [L0700, 2020-26]
3. When prompted, select the location and area code you want. The number issued is a local number. [L0700, 2020-26]
4. Install the Google Voice app on your phone and computer to take inbound calls and place outbound calls. [L0700, 2020-26]

### OpenPhone (Quo): Setup with Toll-Free Line

**OpenPhone setup for a US business number with a toll-free line:**

1. Open the OpenPhone page, review the preview of how it works, and check the pricing. [L0700, 2020-26]
2. Click Try it free and create the account. The trial runs seven days, after which the Starter plan costs $15 per user per month. [L0700, 2020-26]
3. Choose the toll-free number offered in the plan during signup. [L0700, 2020-26]
4. Install the OpenPhone app on your phone and computer to take inbound calls and place outbound calls. [L0700, 2020-26]

### Mighty Call

- Mighty Call is a business phone app for a second number. Install it on a computer or phone so business calls ring in the app and can be told apart from personal calls. [L0664, 2020; older]
- Mighty Call provides business phone numbers and call handling for stores. At the time of the call it offered a 50% discount for the first three months through a promotional link. [L0852, 2020; older]
- Mighty Call provides business phone numbers and call handling. It replaced Grasshopper about a year and a half before the call. One reported issue was that it did not work with T-Mobile. Grasshopper is the alternative. [L0852, 2020; older]
- Mighty Call is a phone service used for the store's call line, chosen over Grasshopper after testing in 2019. It is used to route calls to the business. [L0960, 2019; older]
- Mighty Call was a cloud phone service used in 2019 to provide business phone numbers for online stores. [L1002, 2019; older]
- Mighty Call is a phone system that can route calls to voicemail. If callers hear two voicemail messages, the routing logic is misconfigured. Contact Mighty Call with the exact day and time the problem happens so they can fix the setting. [L1090, 2021; older]
- Mighty Call is a business phone service that sends voicemail transcripts right away, so messages can be read on the go and returned quickly. [L0970, 2023]
- Mighty Call with voice-to-text can turn voicemails into emails with a clickable phone number and email link to call or reply. [L1113, 2022; older]
- Mighty Call is a virtual phone service with staff in the United States and the Philippines who answer calls at the same time. It works from any country, so a store owner living abroad can run phone support from the same number. [L1111, 2020; older]
- A virtual phone service with staff in several countries can be used to answer calls from anywhere in the world. It allows a store owner in another country to run the same phone support. [L1111, 2020; older]

### Grasshopper and Aircall

- Grasshopper provides a business phone number (typically 1-800 format). It displays to customers while routing calls and texts to your mobile phone through the Grasshopper app. This maintains a professional identity without exposing personal numbers. [L0286, 2018]
- Grasshopper is a virtual phone provider that handles both calls and SMS texting for a business number. It has a text-back button for incoming calls that sends an automatic message saying you missed the call and will respond soon. [L1472, 2022]
- Grasshopper is a provider for toll-free business numbers, used when a store wants an 800 number. [L0356, 2019; older]
- Aircall integrates with Reamaze. Each incoming call is logged automatically, and the customer's order information appears on screen. This lets the team handle a call from one platform. [L0337, 2022]

### Skype, Text Free and Messaging Apps

- Skype was the most reliable phone solution while traveling abroad. It was compared with Google Voice, toll-free number forwarding and Grasshopper. Skype is a workaround for travel, not the general recommendation. [L0910, 2015]
- For SMS or sign-up numbers, use a free second number from the Text Free app instead of your personal phone. The app issues a free number tied to your email. The number stays active as long as you make a call roughly once a month. [L0820, undated]
- Text-Free was used to get a phone number for a new Ads Manager without using a personal number. [L1381, 2022]
- WhatsApp can be used for free calls and messaging with customers, including international calls. Its encryption is a selling point for privacy-conscious buyers. It is common for customers in Europe and can be used alongside an SMS provider. [L1472, 2022]
- Business phone numbers for non-US residents can be obtained through options such as MightyCall, Skype or Google Voice. Open Phone is recommended for Canada-based sellers shipping to the US. If none work, ask in the community forum. [L0804, 2023; older]

### Voicemail and Call Handling

- Use a phone service that sends an SMS or email notification whenever a voicemail is left. The notification should show the caller's number and let you play the recorded message. [L0909, 2021-22; older]
- Divert unanswered calls from your business number to a prerecorded voicemail greeting through your phone provider. Use a toll-free number such as a US 800 number, or an Australian 1-300 number if you operate in Australia. [L0909, 2021-22; older]
- Set up a professional-sounding voicemail for the store phone, either by hiring a voice actor on Fiverr or by having a friend record a scripted greeting. Avoid using the founder's own voice, because a third-party voice makes the business look like a larger company. [L0910, 2015]
- Grasshopper's text-back button sends an automatic message when a call is missed, saying you will respond soon. [L1472, 2022]
- The phone system's voicemail and transcript feature lets you read messages on the go and return calls quickly. [L0970, 2023; L0338, 2020-26]
