## Store name, domain purchase and domain connection

### Naming the store and the domain

- A domain is the web address for your online store and how customers reach it. Every store needs its own unique domain, for example the source's own site [L0079, undated]
- The domain is the root web address people type to reach the store. Check that the name is available and affordable before you commit to it. [L0336, 2021-22]
- Choose the domain before you invest in the store build or the logo. Check availability first, because the name you want is often already taken. [L0520, undated]
- Check availability before you finalise brand or logo work. The.com version of a good name is often unavailable, so try variations of your product or brand name until you find one that is free and fits the brand. [L0520, undated]
- Business name and domain: choose the business name first, then check whether the matching domain is free before you commit. Brainstorm five to ten name ideas so a backup is ready if the favourite domain is taken. [L0522, 2021-22]
- Keep the store name and the domain consistent. A business name does not have to match the domain, because the business name can stay broad enough for several businesses. Even so, keep the domain and the store as consistent as possible, ideally the same. [L0964, 2023]
- A custom domain should match the Shopify store name so customers can find and trust it. [L1250, 2026]
- The niche keyword is optional. Include it if it is available and inexpensive. A creative brand name that fits the theme is equally acceptable. [L0941, 2022; L0934, 2023]
- A store name does not need to list every category. Buyers who search for a specific product will still buy from a store whose name does not mention it. [L0801, 2021]
- The meaning of the domain matters little, as long as visitors can remember it. [L1325, 2020-26]
- Choose a simple, memorable name. The domain can be kept while the brand name changes. One example reused a basic name after a niche rename. [L0661, undated]
- For testing, start with a broad, neutral brand name and only a few basic branding elements, because the offer and the creatives decide the first sales more than the logo does. For unrelated products, a general name such as a female first name works for testing. Add full branding once a product has its first sales. [L1428, undated]

**Practical name rules**

- Use.com, no dashes or hyphens, three words or fewer, and a name that is easy to remember and type. [L0941, 2022]
- Do not use numbers or dashes, because they make the name harder to remember and to tell to others. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Keep the name short and easy to remember, with no hyphens. [L1111, 2020]
- Avoid unusual hyphens. Naming is free-form, but the name should be something customers remember. [L0964, 2023]
- If the.com is unavailable, avoid a shortcut name with an odd ending. A clear form such as shop.brandname.com or brandname.com is better. [L0530, 2022]
- Check that the name reads the same when spoken aloud. [L0468, 2020]
- Use a generic company or LLC name rather than one tied to a single store, so several related stores in the same niche can share it. [L1090, 2021]
- Google requires that an LLC's name is not used as a website. Build one website per sales store. The LLC itself needs no website. [L0934, 2023]

**Naming procedure**

1. Brainstorm 5 to 10 business name ideas that identify the market and stay within three words.
2. Check each idea against Google searches for your product keywords to confirm it is unique in the market and not a close variant of a competitor's name.
3. Pick the idea that resonates with you and whose matching domain is available.
4. Confirm the domain is affordable, then buy it so the business name and domain match exactly. [L0336, 2021-22]

**Domain-selection procedure**

1. Choose a short, memorable domain with no hyphens that ends in.com.
2. Check that the name reads the same when spoken aloud.
3. Check the US trademark registry for conflicts before buying.
4. Buy the domain through the Shopify admin, or through an outside registrar such as Namecheap at about $9 per year.
5. Link the domain to the store before contacting suppliers. [L0468, 2020]

### Domain extension

| Market or case | Extension | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US store |.com | [L0504, 2020-26; L0522, 2021-22; L0968, 2021; L1111, 2020] |
| Australia |.com.au (preferred) | [L0504, 2020-26; L0522, 2021-22] |
| UK |.co.uk | [L0504, 2020-26; L0521, 2020-26] |
| Canada, new store |.com, even when selling in Canada | [L1277, 2020] |
| Single country, other than US or Australia | local country-code extension | [L0522, 2021-22], [L0520, undated] |

- Match the top-level domain to the market where the customers are. [L0521, 2020-26; L0504, 2020-26]
- Google gives preference to domains that carry the local country designator. [L0522, 2021-22]
- A local extension is trusted more by local buyers than a.com. Example:.ch in Switzerland or.com.au in Australia. [L0549, undated]
- A country-specific extension can be chosen instead of.com when you target one specific country. [L0520, undated]
-.com is the first choice for a store domain because it is the most widely accepted..co is the second most common extension for e-commerce stores. [L0520, undated]
- A.com is recommended over.store or similar extensions, because customers type.com by default. [L0964, 2023; L0838, 2020-26]
- For a US store, do not use.net,.store or.org. Customers who come back later will type.com out of habit and land on another site or none at all. [L0504, 2020-26]
- For sales to US customers, do not use a.co.uk domain. Shoppers may hesitate to order from a store that appears not to ship to them. [L0967, 2020]
- In the US, only a.com is needed. In Australia,.com.au is preferred. Buying both.com and.com.au is possible but optional. [L0522, 2021-22]
- Use.com even when you sell in only one country, because shoppers remember and type.com by default. A.us domain is not recommended. [L0968, 2021]
- Meta Ad Library shows the domain to viewers. A.us or.store domain can make a pre-lander or advertorial look like a direct sales page, which can reduce trust. [L1327, 2026]
- Example: a store that sources and sells only within one country used that country's extension,.com.au for Australia, as in an early home lighting store. [L1014, 2021-22]

**When the preferred name is taken**

- When the local extension of a name is taken, the search also suggests.net,.org,.online or.us. For a store aimed at one country, keep the local extension and change the name instead of switching to a generic extension. [L0522, 2021-22]
- When the.com is already taken, do not switch to a different-looking extension. Change the store name, or buy the.com if the owner is willing. A.shop address causes customers to mistrust email from a mismatched domain. [L0468, 2020]

### Checking availability, trademarks and history

**Availability**

- Search the name in the store's domain search or at the registrar, with the correct local extension. If the result says available, add it to the cart. [L0522, 2021-22]
- In Shopify's Buy new domain search, the default results are suggestions. You can type a specific name you already chose straight into the search field. Delete any "http://" prefix and trailing slash before searching, then confirm the result says the domain is available. [L0521, 2020-26]
- If a candidate domain's website cannot be reached, that usually means nobody is using it. Still confirm availability in the store's own domain search, because an unreachable site does not prove the name can be bought. [L0521, 2020-26]
- A message that the domain is taken means someone already owns it, and it cannot be bought for that name. Do not force that name. Search the next backup name the same way and repeat until one shows as available with the correct extension. [L0522, 2021-22]
- AI chat tools cannot check real-time domain availability. Their suggestions may already be registered. Taken names often belong to someone else's site or are parked as premium domains for sale. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Avoid buying domains that other people hold to sell at high prices. Choose a name that is actually available after brainstorming. [L0964, 2023]
- Do not buy domain names for resale as a business idea. Many are held for thousands of dollars, and this is unnecessary. [L1111, 2020]

**Trademark**

- Before buying, search the US trademark registry for conflicts. [L0468, 2020]
- If a store name or domain may infringe a trademark, search the US trademark registry first. If it does, pick a new domain and link it to the existing Shopify store, which keeps all products and settings. [L0468, 2020]

**History checks (procedure)**

1. Pick the available domain you like best.
2. Google "site:" followed by the domain name. Zero results are normal for a new name.
3. Open the Google Transparency Report and enter the domain in the check site status field. "No data available" is normal.
4. Open the Wayback Machine at archive.org and enter the domain to view its browse history.
5. Only if all three checks show no problems, buy the domain. [L0504, 2020-26]

- An old site: string search that returns old pages is acceptable if they are relevant to the niche. Spammy or scammy old pages are a red flag, and you should pick a different name. [L0504, 2020-26]
- A domain can be available and still have a history of penalties, so the history checks are needed even when the name is free. A blacklisted domain, for example one that once carried a virus, is rare, roughly one in 500, but it can damage a store built on it. [L0504, 2020-26]
- If the Wayback Machine has archived a domain, open its old snapshots to check for spam or scam content. Then search Google for the domain's past reviews. If there is no archive, the domain is effectively new, and that is fine. [L0504, 2020-26]

### Price limits and premium domains

- Do not pay more than $20 per year for a domain name. Premium domains are often offered for $500, $1,000, $5,000 or more, and buying one at that price is not worth it for a new store. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Each store needs its own domain. Do not buy premium or parked domains at high prices. A similar domain can work just as well. [L0801, 2021]
- Avoid parked domains listed for resale, since they are often overpriced. Choose an open domain instead. [L0934, 2023] (2023 price: about $14 to $15 per year)
- Keep the domain cost low: spend no more than about $14 per year on a domain name. Choose a generic name or one that contains your niche keyword. [L1090, 2021]

### Buying a domain in Shopify

**Why Shopify**

- Shopify is the recommended place to buy a domain for a Shopify store. Common third-party registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap and Google Domains. [L0520, undated]
- A domain bought through Shopify is live immediately, with no waiting for SSL and no DNS changes. This makes it the simplest option for a beginner. [L0520, undated]
- Buying in Shopify is the intended way to get a low-cost name for a new store. [L0521, 2020-26]
- The Shopify purchase is quick and needs only a few clicks. [L0941, 2022]
- one source's 2019 advice was to buy the domain directly in Shopify for about $14 per year, rather than through an external registrar such as GoDaddy, which was advised against. Namecheap is another common registrar. [L0356, 2019]
- Buying in Shopify was used for ease of integration. [L0852, 2020]
- A domain bought in Shopify is added to the store's account and billed yearly at the price shown during purchase, for example $16 per year. The buyer owns the domain, so visitors who type that address reach the store. [L0521, 2020-26]
- A domain bought through Shopify can be used in the same Shopify account and set as the primary domain. [L1382, undated]

**Limits of a Shopify-bought domain**

- A domain bought from Shopify cannot be moved to other platforms such as WooCommerce or ClickFunnels. The lesson's view is that this rarely matters if you stay on Shopify. [L0520, undated]
- A domain bought through Shopify can be transferred to another registrar such as Namecheap or GoDaddy. Every domain registrar allows transfers. [L1094, 2023] (older, 2023 source; conflicts with the platform limit above, which concerns platforms, not registrars)

**Procedure A**

1. Log in to your Shopify admin.
2. Click Settings.
3. Click Domains.
4. Ignore the default myshopify.com domain.
5. Click Buy new domain.
6. Enter your chosen domain name.
7. Confirm availability and $16 per year pricing.
8. Click Buy.
9. The domain is added to your Shopify account.
10. Shopify automatically assigns it as your primary domain. [L0079, undated]

**Procedure B**

1. In the Shopify admin, click Settings in the bottom left corner.
2. Open Domains.
3. Click Buy new domain.
4. Type your desired name and check whether it is available.
5. If the name is taken, adjust it and check again. Test several options.
6. Select an extension, such as.com,.co, or a country-specific extension.
7. Review the details and the price, then click Buy domain.
8. Once purchased, the domain appears in the Domains section. You can set it as the primary domain so that visitors land on it. [L0520, undated]

**Procedure C**

1. Log in to the Shopify admin.
2. Click Settings.
3. Click Domains in the left navigation.
4. Click "Buy new domain" at the top.
5. Delete the "http://" prefix and the trailing slash from the entered name, then search for the domain.
6. Check that the search result says the domain is available.
7. Click "Buy" to add it to the account.

The domain is billed yearly, and it becomes the primary domain once purchased. [L0521, 2020-26]

**Procedure D **

- Open the Shopify admin, then go to Settings > Domains in the left navigation to view and buy domains for the store. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Once the purchase is made, the domain shows in the list. one source's 2024 lesson states that a new domain can be bought through Shopify (recommended for simplicity) or elsewhere, then linked under Settings > Domains, where it takes effect immediately. [L0971, 2024]
- A new store starts with a default address made of random characters followed by.myshopify.com, which redirects to the domain you choose. [L0504, 2020-26]

### Buying a domain at an external registrar

**Cost and reasons to choose it**

- External registrars such as Namecheap and GoDaddy typically cost about $6 to $10 per year, which is considerably cheaper than buying through Shopify. [L0520, undated]
- External registrars are the better choice when budget matters, or when the domain may later be used on other platforms such as WooCommerce or Wix. The trade-off is that they do not include free email forwarding, so a separate paid email service is needed for a professional address. [L0520, undated]
- A domain held at an outside registrar is easier to move later and makes business email simpler to set up. [L0989, 2025-26]
- one source's 2023 lesson: domains can be purchased from any registrar and connected to a Shopify store. You do not have to buy them through Shopify. [L0934, 2023]
- Buying a domain through GoDaddy and linking it to Shopify avoids TikTok warnings about links in the bio. A professional business email from the domain can be used for the store's social accounts. [L1381, 2022]

**Procedure E**

1. Open the registrar's website.
2. Search for the desired domain and confirm it is available.
3. Compare the price and look for a discount code.
4. Add the domain to the cart and complete the purchase. [L0520, undated]

**GoDaddy purchase**

1. Search the domain on the domain search page with the correct local extension.
2. If the result says available, click Add to Cart.
3. Check out and pay by credit card. Checkout creates a GoDaddy login account for the buyer. [L0522, 2021-22]

- Do not add any extra products or add-ons at GoDaddy checkout. Buy only the domain itself. [L0522, 2021-22]
- GoDaddy's domain search lists.com.au results with a "Restrictions apply" note, which signals eligibility rules for the registrant. Check that the buyer qualifies for the local extension before adding it to the cart. [L0522, 2021-22]
- GoDaddy may display prices in a currency that does not match the target market, such as euros for an Australian or US store. Confirm the currency and the final amount in the cart and at checkout before paying. [L0522, 2021-22]
- The cheapest first-year prices at GoDaddy are often tied to a two-year registration. For example, about €0.99 for the first year applies only when registering for two years. Compare the renewal price for each period before choosing the term. [L0522, 2021-22]
- GoDaddy's domain search may offer a broker service for a domain that is taken. Treat this as a last resort and move to a backup business name first, because restrictions apply. [L0522, 2021-22]
- GoDaddy is a domain registrar used to search availability, register domains and manage the account. Other registrars can be used instead if an account already exists there. [L0522, 2021-22]

**Namecheap purchase**

1. Go to Namecheap, search for the domain, and add it to the cart.
2. Check out with domain privacy kept on.
3. Leave auto-renew off for now.
4. Click Confirm Order.
5. Create a username, enter your details and click Continue.
6. Add a payment card and click Pay Now.
7. Open the domain and click Manage. [L0989, 2025-26]
