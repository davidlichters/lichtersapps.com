### Done-for-you build option

#### Two routes to a supplier-ready demo store
- There are two routes: build the demo store yourself step by step, or outsource the web design to a done-for-you service. The done-for-you service delivers a finished store that the buyer owns 100 percent. [L1062, 2020-26]
- The done-for-you web design service is a paid upgrade. Buyers who already purchased it submit the web design form. Buyers who have not purchased it can sign up through an information form to add it to their account. [L1062, 2020-26]
- Choosing the done-for-you service lets the owner skip the self-build lessons for the demo store and move straight to contacting suppliers. Choosing to build it yourself means working through every design step first; supplier outreach starts only after that step is complete. [L1062, 2020-26]
- The Ultimate Upgrade done-for-you store includes professional setup of Google Search Console, Google Merchant Center, Google Analytics, and Google Ads accounts. The standard done-for-you store does not include these setups. [L0278, 2025]
- A custom store build has typically taken three to five business days. Reply to the delivery email with any edit requests, since most owners are satisfied on day one. [L1116, 2025]

### Website checks before supplier outreach

#### What the store must look like before suppliers see it

- The store must look complete before suppliers see it. That means no placeholder phone numbers or emails, no filler or instructional text, and every link must work. [L1464, 2020-26]
- Real phone numbers and emails are not required yet. They are added before contacting suppliers. [L1464, 2020-26]
- Store branding (colors, color scheme, business name) must be configured in Shopify before any supplier contact. Finish branding first so the store can be shown as complete. [L1466, 2021-22]

#### Checking the store in order

1. Open your store and go through every page. Look for typos, broken links, placeholder phone numbers or emails, and filler text such as instructional placeholder sentences. [L1464, 2020-26]
2. Click every link on the store and confirm each one works as intended. [L1464, 2020-26]
3. Send the store link to a friend or family member, ask them to browse, and collect any feedback on things you missed. [L1464, 2020-26]
4. Fix everything they flag so the site looks complete. [L1464, 2020-26]
5. In Shopify Admin, go to Online Store > Themes and click Remove Password so the store is publicly reachable. [L1464, 2020-26]

#### Why an outside tester is needed

- After long work on a store, the builder tends to overlook obvious errors, so having an outside person test the site with fresh eyes helps catch them. [L1464, 2020-26]

### Password removal

#### Making the store publicly visible

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Where | Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes |
| Action | Click Remove Password |
| Result | Anyone who types the domain name can reach the store |

- To make the store publicly visible in Shopify, go to Online Store > Themes in Shopify Admin and click Remove Password. Once removed, anyone who types the domain name can reach the store. [L1464, 2020-26]

#### Common mistake

- A store still locked behind the Shopify password page cannot be shown to potential suppliers, so the password must be removed before supplier outreach begins. [L1464, 2020-26]

#### Further details

- Demo stores only need to look realistic for a supplier review, not to prove access to the products. Products can use free stock images and simple descriptions. [L0852, 2020]

- Before suppliers approve you, you can upload placeholder products with royalty-free images found through Google image search, so you can preview the store. If a supplier asks, explain that the store is being built and the placeholder products are not for sale, consistent with your message that you are launching a new store. [L1110, 2019]
