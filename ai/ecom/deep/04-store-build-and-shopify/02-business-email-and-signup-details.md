### Business email and signup details

**Email address**

- Use a business email address for the Shopify account and all business communication, rather than a personal inbox. The verification email must be opened and confirmed within about 24 hours. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- A personal email address can be used, including one from a personal account, because it can be changed later. [L1062, 2020-26]
- A business email is not required. A regular personal email such as Gmail works. [L1313, undated]
- In the sign-up page, type the business email address into the Email address field. [L1314, 2021-22]

**Verification**

- If the verification email from Shopify does not arrive, the Verify your email address card on the dashboard has a Resend verification email button. Clicking it sends the confirmation link again to the email address, so the account can be verified within the 24-hour window. [L1314, 2021-22]

**Business details**

- The business entity name does not need to relate to your domain name or brand. Simple names such as ER Stores LLC or Online Stores LLC are acceptable. The entity name appears on bank accounts and tax documents; the domain is your public-facing store name. ** [L0249, 2022]
- A US address is not required to open a Shopify account. It is optional for business setup, and a virtual or physical US address can be added later if needed. *(2024.)* [L0360, 2024]
- If you have no company yet, pick your home country as the business location (for example, the United States, or the country you live in). [L1313, undated]
- Onboarding Step 2 of 2 is titled Add an address so you can get paid. Its fields are First name, Last name, Address, Suburb, Country/Region, Phone, State/territory, Postcode and the optional Business or personal website. Below them is a checkbox labelled This store is a registered business, followed by the Enter my store button. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- Onboarding Step 1 of 2 offers sales-channel choices. Under Online Store: Online Store, Social media and Online marketplaces. Under In person: At markets and fairs, At pop-ups and In brick and mortar retail stores. Selecting any in-person option leads to point-of-sale offers. The question Are you setting up a store for a client? is for agencies building stores for others. A solo online-only merchant should select only Online Store. ** [L1314, 2021-22]

**Signup procedures (each source kept in full)**

Procedure A, one source 2026:
1. Enter your email on the sign-up page and click Next. [L1323, 2026]
2. Enter a password. [L1323, 2026]
3. Choose the Basic plan in the plan selection and click Try basic. [L1323, 2026]
4. In the checkout, check the trial end, the renewal date, the plan price (about $39/month plus tax) and the introductory $1/month rate, then click Subscribe. [L1323, 2026]
5. Dismiss the upsell offer with Not now. [L1323, 2026]
6. On the 'What can we help you do?' screen, select Sell online and Dropshipping, then click Continue. [L1323, 2026]
7. Enter a store name (the default is 'My Store' and can be changed later) and click Continue, or choose I'll do this later. [L1323, 2026]

Procedure B, one source:
1. Go to shopify.com, enter your email address and click 'Start free trial'. [L1313, undated]
2. A business email is not required; a regular personal email such as Gmail works. [L1313, undated]
3. On the first question, choose 'I'm just starting an online store' and select dropshipping products as the product type. [L1313, undated]
4. If you have no company yet, pick your home country as the business location (for example, United States, or the country you live in) and click Next. [L1313, undated]
5. Choose 'Continue with email', set a password and click 'Create Shopify ID'. [L1313, undated]
6. Once the account exists, open the settings options and fill in the business details: store name, time zone and main currency. [L1313, undated]
7. Under Domains you can buy a custom domain, which becomes the professional domain later. [L1313, undated]
8. If sign-up asks for a domain name, Shopify provides a default one ending in.myshopify.com. Any name works because this default domain is not used for promotion. [L1313, undated]
9. Open the Online Store section and then Themes, where Shopify's free themes can be selected. [L1313, undated]

- The name chosen at signup becomes the permanent myshopify.com address to which custom domains are attached. ** [L1002, 2019]

### Admin orientation

- The Shopify admin is the back end where storefront design, product upload, product page design and inventory tracking are managed. The front end of the store is designed inside this admin. [L1304, 2020-26]
- The left-hand navigation contains Home, Orders, Products, Customers, Analytics, Marketing, Discounts, Apps, Sales channels, Online Store and Settings at the bottom. Orders and products are managed daily here. Analytics and sales data start to fill in only after the store goes live. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- Until the store is launched, the storefront is protected by a password, so the public cannot see it. A new store shows only a basic home page with no design or images until the theme is customised. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- Practise Shopify edits on the free trial, which was 14 to 21 days at the time of recording, or on a copy of a store in progress. This prevents accidental deletions from damaging the live store. [L0921, 2022]
- The dashboard card titled Your trial just started lists four first setup tasks: Add your first product, Customize theme, Add domain and Set up payments. Working through these four items in that order gives a new store its basic setup after account creation. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- The Shopify admin home page shows a setup checklist for a new store with Add product, Customize theme, Add domain and Set up payments. These mark the main steps before launch. ** [L1292, 2021-22]
- Initial setup checklist on the Shopify admin Home page, in this order:
  1. Add store name. [L1323, 2026]
  2. Discover products to sell: install a recommended supplier app or add a product manually. [L1323, 2026]
  3. Set up Shopify Payments: click Activate. [L1323, 2026]
  4. Customize your online store: choose or generate a theme, then add logo, colors and images. [L1323, 2026]
  5. Review your shipping rates. [L1323, 2026]
  6. Customize domain: the store starts on a default myshopify.com address and a custom domain can be added. [L1323, 2026]
- Settings > Plan shows the current plan, the trial terms (in the example, 3 months at $1 per month), the date monthly billing starts, and the payment method. Plan changes and switching to annual billing are made on this page. [L1305, 2020-26]
- Settings > Billing shows the stored payment method, billing currency, active subscriptions including paid apps with their next bill, and previous charges. Use it to track what is paid to Shopify. [L1305, 2020-26]
- Settings > Users shows who has access. The Pending tab shows requests from the agency. [L0472, 2025]
- Shopify login problems can come from being logged into a different Shopify account. Use the admin URL (yourstore.com/admin) to log in to the correct store, or try an incognito window or a different browser. *(2025.)* [L0472, 2025]
- To deactivate a Shopify store you created by mistake:
  1. Open the Shopify admin. [L1113, 2022]
  2. Click Settings. [L1113, 2022]
  3. Open Plan. [L1113, 2022]
  4. Click Deactivate. [L1113, 2022]
  Shopify may put the store on hold for a while in case you change your mind. ** [L1113, 2022]

### Collaborator and staff accounts

**Staff accounts**

- Shopify Staff Accounts let the store owner give team members limited access to the store instead of sharing the main login. ** [L1089, 2021]
- Create staff accounts for contractors and designers. Set permissions to restrict access to customer data, payment settings and financial information. Each staff member can have different permission levels. [L0276, 2024]
- When bringing in a VA or helper, use staff permissions so they only get the access they need, such as editing store pages, and not financial or other sensitive areas. ** [L1090, 2021]
- Permissions to grant: Products, Catalogs, Online store and Store settings. Do not grant access to Orders, Draft orders or Customers. *(2024.)* [L1285, 2024]
- Settings > Users and permissions > Add staff: invite a person (for example, a hired store builder) by name and email, and choose which sections of the store they can access. This gives them a separate login instead of sharing your username and password. [L1305, 2020-26]

**Collaborator accounts**

- Shopify collaborator accounts give a staff member or outside partner access to a store without sharing your main login. Use them instead of sharing credentials. [L0675, 2025]
- The collaborator request arrives by email once assets are ready. [L0472, 2025]
- For agencies or partners on the 39 USD per month plan, use a collaborator invitation, since partner access does not require a staff account. [L0667, 2025]
- Give a private supplier access by going to shopify.partners and inviting them as a partner with partnership access to your Shopify store. The supplier provides the step-by-step instructions, and the lesson does not name the exact menu labels. [L1156, 2026]

### Cost of running the store

**Fixed costs**

- Shopify is a fixed cost that must be paid every month while the store is running. Stopping payment can lead to the store being shut down. [L0759, 2020-26]
- Shopify costs 29 USD per month when paid for a year in advance, or 39 USD per month when paid monthly. *(2023.)* [L0964, 2023]
- The Shopify subscription at 29 USD per month is small compared with the cost of a physical retail store, which needs a lease, a rent deposit, build-out and fixtures. A store that makes only one 1,000 USD sale per month still covers the subscription cost. [L0759, 2020-26]
- Shopify costs in the 2021 setting: a 14-day free trial, then 29 USD per month. ** [L0771, 2021-22]

**Startup costs before launch** **

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domain name | $5 to $20 | one-off |
| Shopify | 14-day free trial, then $29 per month | do not activate before you build |
| Branding and logo on Fiverr | about $20 to $30 | logo and branding |
| Total to launch | about $100 | time not counted |

[L0458, 2021-22]

- A premium Shopify theme can sell for around $300 when bought separately. Stores are built on a theme that handles the background code. ** [L0458, 2021-22]
- A hosted platform avoids the cost and time of a custom website, which typically runs to tens of thousands of dollars. ** [L1314, 2021-22]
- A domain name cost about 14 USD per year. ** [L1277, 2020]

**Transaction and add-on costs**

- Transaction fees per sale by plan are listed in the plans section: Basic 2.0%, Shopify 1.0%, Advanced 0.5% in the 2021 source. [L0370, 2021-22]
- Add-on prices: POS Pro USD 89/month per location; Remove online store USD 9/month. ** [L0370, 2021-22]
- Paid apps appear on the Billing page with their next bill. [L1305, 2020-26]
- For plans that cost more, Advanced was cheaper when billed yearly rather than monthly. [L0694, 2024]

**Keeping the cost under control**

- Do not commit to a multi-year term at the start. Pay monthly while the store is unproven. [L0370, 2021-22]
- Note the expiry date of any free period, because a reversion may cancel multi-market settings. [L0694, 2024]
- Cancel any unused second account to avoid paying for it. [L0471, 2024]
- Check plan limits regularly, since a plan change can break an existing store configuration. [L0694, 2024]

- Shopify is the platform used to build the online store, and a ready-made Shopify theme is used during the store build stage. [L0506, 2020-26]

- All stores in this model are built on the Shopify platform. Shopify is used to design the store, upload products, and manage orders. [L0759, 2020-26]

- Shopify offers a 14-day free trial before the monthly fee starts. [L0759, 2020-26]
