### Trust badges, guarantees and checkout banner

- Trust badge placement has changed across sources. Use the rule that matches your store's situation:
  - Footer, added through code: open the theme editor, edit the base code of the footer, and insert the trust badges into the footer code. If you do not know how to edit code, hire a developer on Upwork for a small job, which takes about 20 minutes. [L0671, 2021]
  - Below the add-to-cart button: not used. Only badges that come with the theme by default in the footer are kept. *(Newer, 2023.)* [L0970, 2023]
  - Product page: trust badges such as authorized retailer, free shipping and product warranty should appear on the product page itself, in the description, specifications or warranty section, and in the shipping tab. [L1281, 2022]
  - Near the buy area (shipping message, see Shipping section above) and on the landing or product page, where many more elements can be added. [L0916, 2022+; L1377, 2022+]
- Trust elements make buyers and suppliers feel safe. Clear branding, free shipping, easy returns and a visible contact number are added before the store goes live. ** [L0771, 2021-22]
- Shopify's checkout is very limited. Most apps cannot be added there, and the only practical change is an image in the logo area. Put additional trust badges, reviews and guarantees on the landing page or product page instead. [L1377, 2022+]
- Checkout trust banner in Canva and Shopify:
  1. Open Canva, choose Create a design, and select Custom size.
  2. Enter 1500 x 300 pixels.
  3. Add the store logo at the top or on the left.
  4. Search the Elements section for 'privacy protected' and add the matching icon, plus a secure-checkout or satisfaction-guarantee icon.
  5. Below the icons, add payment icons such as Visa, Mastercard and PayPal, and optionally a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  6. Export the design as an image.
  7. In Shopify, go to Settings > Checkout, search for checkout, and upload the image to the checkout image field. Other checkout parts cannot be edited this way. [L1345, 2021+]
- Alternative checkout logo procedure:
  1. Open Canva and create a custom design at 1500 x 300 pixels.
  2. Place your logo on the left side.
  3. Search Canva elements for 'privacy protected' and place the matching icon on the right side.
  4. Add further trust icons such as secure checkout or satisfaction guaranteed.
  5. Download the image.
  6. In Shopify, go to Settings > Checkout > Checkout settings and upload the image in place of the logo. [L1377, 2022+]
- To add trust icons to checkout, upload a custom image into the logo area under Settings > Checkout > Checkout settings. Create the image at 1500 x 300 pixels, place the logo on the left and the trust icons on the right, and upload it in place of the logo. [L1377, 2022+]
- Payment options must be shown in the footer and on product pages. A merchant account was flagged for not showing them clearly. ** [L1111, 2020]
- Place the privacy policy, shipping and returns policies and other required policies where they can be found easily. Some suspended merchant accounts were reinstated after the policy link was moved into the top menu under About us. ** [L1111, 2020]
- Case: a store had an add-to-cart rate of about 8% but a conversion rate of only 0.29%, with a plain, unbranded checkout. Trust elements were added to the checkout (privacy and secure-checkout badges, a satisfaction guarantee and payment icons) and the shipping text was changed. The conversion rate rose from 0.29% to 2.15% in one day. [L1345, 2021+]

### Authorized retailer, brand and charity claims

- Highlight brand partnerships and authorized retailer status on the site if you sell well-known suppliers. Include a 'Shop by Brand' navigation section to capture customers searching for those brand names. ** [L0272, 2022]
- Create an authorized retailer badge: use the supplier's logo with the text 'authorized retailer' below it. It can be built in Canva, which has a free version. ** [L0801, 2021]
- Authorized retailer badges use the same font, size, colour and shape throughout one store. The styling can differ between stores but must stay consistent within a store. [L0970, 2023]
- Brand or retailer logos and badges should show only the authorization or certification you actually hold. Claims that cannot be verified should be removed. [L0970, 2023]
- Charity claims on the website require written permission from the charity. Many large charities restrict who may claim to represent or donate to them. [L0970, 2023]

### Copied content and claim risk

- Copying a winning brand's sentences word for word, or keeping its health claims such as blood flow or circulation promises, can get ads rejected and creates legal risk. Rewrite all claims for your own product and check the claim rules of your market before launch. [L1479, 2026]
- Handling copied store content:
  1. Collect the link to the copying store and the link to your original page.
  2. Submit a DMCA takedown to the hosting platform, such as Shopify.
  3. Confirm that the claim is true, because a false claim can expose you to legal liability. Platforms usually remove a verified copy quickly and do not restore it. ** [L0968, 2021]

### Source conflicts

- Core page count:
  - Four pages (About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service) at the early supplier-approval stage. [L0509, 2020-26]
  - Six pages (adding Shipping Policy and Refund Policy) for a supplier-ready store. [L0090, undated; L1048, 2020-26]
  - Eight pages in the older 2021-22 pre-launch list, which includes price match and easy returns pages. [L1264, 2021-22]
- Where to generate legal pages: Settings > Checkout (bottom section, 'Refund, privacy, and terms of service') in one walkthrough, and Settings > Policies in another. Use whichever your admin shows and confirm in the Help Center. [L0421, 2020-26; L1048, 2020-26; L1327, 2026]
- Contact page: the automatic Contact page is renamed to Contact Us in one method, and a new page is created with the Contact template in another. [L0509, 2020-26; L0970, 2023]
