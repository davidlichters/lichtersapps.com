#### Further details

- To create a Facebook business page: 1. Log in to Facebook. 2. Click See more in the left navigation menu. 3. Click Pages. 4. Click Create Page. 5. Select Public Page. 6. Click Get started. [L0157, undated]

- Configure basic Facebook page details: enter your Store Name (not the full URL) in the Page name field; select a relevant Category for your business type (use AI to identify the appropriate category for your niche if uncertain); set Hours to Always open. [L0157, undated]

- Add Facebook contact information: enter your store URL in the Website field; enter sales@yourdomain.com in the Email field; optionally add a Phone number; do not use the Address field for ecommerce stores. [L0157, undated]

- Set your Facebook page's profile picture by uploading your store logo, cropping it to show a clean symbol or icon version if the original doesn't fit well in the circular profile format; upload your store header or banner image as the cover photo. [L0157, undated]

- Add a Facebook page action button: select 'Learn more' as the button label and link it to your store homepage. [L0157, undated]

- Configure your Facebook page's vanity URL: navigate to Settings & privacy > Page setup > Name to establish a username matching your brand (such as facebook.com/YourStoreName); if the exact name is taken, select the closest available alternative. [L0157, undated]

- Write a short Facebook bio based on your store's About Us page: use AI to draft the bio, then trim it to fit within Facebook's character limit if necessary. [L0157, undated]

- To create an Instagram business account: 1. Go to Instagram.com on desktop or open the mobile app. 2. Click Create new account. 3. Enter your store email (sales@yourdomain.com). 4. Create a password and complete the setup. 5. Enter your Store Name as the full name. 6. Choose a username as close as possible to your store name. [L0157, undated]

- To switch Instagram to a business account: 1. Click More. 2. Click Settings. 3. Scroll down and click Account type and tools. 4. Click Switch to a professional account. 5. Select Business. 6. Choose a relevant category (Shopping & retail is typical for ecommerce). 7. Confirm the switch to professional account. [L0157, undated]

- Configure your Instagram profile: upload the same logo or icon used for your Facebook profile picture; paste your Facebook bio into the Instagram bio field, trimming if necessary; note that Instagram prioritizes mobile viewing, so some settings like the website link may require configuration through the mobile app rather than desktop. [L0157, undated]

- Link your social media profiles to your Shopify store: navigate to Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Edit theme > Theme settings (gear icon) > Social media, paste your Facebook Page URL and Instagram profile URL, then click Save. [L0157, undated]

- Enable social media icons in your store footer: navigate to Theme Customizer > Footer settings, toggle ON the option 'Show social media icons', click Save, then preview your store to verify the icons link correctly to both Facebook and Instagram profiles. [L0157, undated]

- Only authentic products from brands you have the right to sell should appear in the shop. Demonstration or invented items can trigger Google policy problems and put your accounts at risk. [L0167, undated]

- Delete all demo products under Shopify Admin > Products. [L0167, undated]

- Broken links and incomplete pages are a problem for Google, so a single 404 or unfinished page can cause compliance flags. [L0167, undated]

- Remove every placeholder before submitting to Google: blank image boxes showing a circle with an X, 'image coming soon' graphics or text, and any unfinished section. The target is zero errors, zero placeholders and zero demo content. [L0167, undated]

- Show at least two contact methods (email, phone or physical address) on the site, and for this model use a real, working phone number and email address. Place them in the footer as a contact block containing both email and phone, and also on the Contact Us page. [L0167, undated]

- Edit the Contact Us page under Shopify Admin > Online Store > Pages > Contact Us. A default Contact Us page may only show a form, so add more contact methods to the page content. [L0167, undated]

- The Contact Us page content should include business hours (for example 9am to 5pm Eastern), a phone number labeled as the way to call, a support or sales email address labeled as the way to email, and a note that live chat is available if it is enabled on the store. [L0167, undated]

- Fake or placeholder contact details, such as dummy phone numbers or bracketed text, can lead to account problems on Google. [L0167, undated]

- Google Search Console blocking product variants is correct behavior: When you click product options (color, size) on a product page, the URL changes with query parameters (e.g., ?color=blue&size=large). Google Search Console marks these variant URLs as blocked by robots.txt, but this is the correct setup. Only the main product URL should be indexed; variants must not be indexed because they are duplicate content. [L0253, 2024]

- Shopify draft orders use a separate checkout system to which you cannot add conversion tracking tags; therefore, any sales completed via draft orders will never appear in Google Analytics or Google Ads conversion reports, even though they are real revenue. [L0256, 2021-22]

- When checkout links break during purchase, Shopify incorrectly attributes the conversion to itself as a traffic source; in the example shown, this false attribution accounts for 3 percent of total revenue. [L0257, 2020-26]

- Facebook Business Manager compliance check: Log in to business.facebook.com, click the hamburger menu (three lines) in the top left, hover over it, then click 'Account Quality' (or navigate via Manage Business > Account Quality). This page shows all compliance issues organized by type (ads, catalog, billing, etc.) and what needs to be fixed. [L0270, 2021]

- Google is taking over Shopify's native Google Channel app. This channel is less customizable and has reported glitches. Use Feed for Google Shopping app from Symprosis instead for more control and reliability. [L0272, 2022]

- Brand name in SEO title impacts Google Shopping display. If brand only in meta title, not in product title, it will not appear in paid or free Google Shopping results. Competitors appear with brand names; add yours too. [L0274, 2023]

- Link TikTok videos to the product page of the product getting attention, not to the catalog or homepage. Every extra step to find the product increases the chance that visitors leave. [L0352, 2022]

- Use a single direct product link for TikTok rather than a homepage, since the link then opens the product page immediately. A homepage is not required for an organic product store. [L0352, 2022]

- Shopify Admin > Reports > Sessions by marketing: use the filter to group sessions by country, region and UTM parameters to see where traffic comes from. [L0363, 2022+]

- Link the product feed to Google Merchant Center through the API so the feed updates about every 24 hours. Manual feeds can fall out of sync when prices change on the store. [L0468, 2020]

- The price in the Merchant Center feed must match the price shown on the product page. Compare-at prices are fine, but a mismatch between feed and site breaks Google's terms of service. [L0468, 2020]

- Switching themes normally keeps the Google feed intact when the feed is connected through the API and the new theme uses the same product structure. A manual feed may require re-review after a theme change. [L0468, 2020]

- Product pages should show brand name, product name and SKU number clearly, because Google Shopping pulls keywords from the content of the product page. [L0468, 2020]

- Before setting up Google Ads or Merchant Center, the store must not be password-protected and must have a contact page and a privacy policy. Missing pages are a common reason for account problems. [L0468, 2020]

- Google Ads optimization and Google Shopping feeds must not be set up before the store's contact page, privacy policy and product pages are complete. [L0468, 2020]

- Speed up indexing of a new or updated page in Google: open Google Search Console, paste the full URL into the 'Inspect any URL' field, click 'Test live URL' to confirm Google sees the live page, then click 'Request indexing'. Use this for individual pages you want indexed quickly. Google will crawl the site anyway, since the sitemap is submitted in Search Console. [L0472, 2025]

- Install Google Analytics on the store, because most buyers expect to see it and the broker requires it for listing. Shopify analytics is useful but does not replace Google Analytics for buyers. [L0554, 2019]

- Multiple TikTok accounts can link to the same Shopify store, but using two separate store links for different pages is generally recommended when running different pages. [L0561, 2023+]

- Build the store in Shopify and check that the pixel is connected: verify that the pixel ID matches on Shopify and in the Facebook app. [L0642, undated]

- Install the Meta pixel on the Shopify store and turn on auto advanced matching before creating any campaign, because campaigns depend on pixel data. [L0649, 2019]

- A Facebook page and an Instagram page are both needed before ads run, since Meta places ads on both platforms. [L0649, 2019]

Link the Facebook and Instagram pages to the Shopify store:
1. Open the Shopify admin and go to Online Store.
2. Click Edit theme.
3. In the sidebar, click Theme settings, the gear icon.
4. Scroll down to Social media.
5. Paste the Facebook page URL into the Facebook field. Use the business page, not a personal profile.
6. Paste the Instagram business account URL into the Instagram field.
7. Click Save.
8. In the footer menu, turn on Show social media icons.
9. Click Save and preview the store to check the footer links.
[L0654, 2020-26]

- Shopify social links path: Online Store > Edit theme > Theme settings (gear icon) > Social media, where the Facebook and Instagram URLs are entered and then saved. In the footer, the option Show social media icons must be switched on to make the links visible. [L0654, 2020-26]

- Linking the social pages to the Shopify store helps visitors reach them and helps Google know the pages exist, which can improve ranking. The main purpose of the link is to increase conversion rate. [L0654, 2020-26]

- In the Shopify theme's Social media settings, fill in only the platforms you actually use, such as Facebook and Instagram, and leave the other fields empty. Use the full URL of the business page or business account, not a personal profile. [L0654, 2020-26]

Verify the social icons in the footer:
1. Turn on the option Show social media icons in the footer menu, since it is off by default.
2. Click Save.
3. Open the store preview.
4. Hover over the Facebook and Instagram icons and confirm they link to facebook.com/[your page username] and instagram.com/[your account username].
[L0654, 2020-26]

- Star reviews appear in Google Shopping through judge.me; they increase click-through rate because they make the listing stand out. [L0665, 2022]

- Product titles for Google Shopping may include the supplier name; follow what the top competitors for that supplier's products do. [L0665, 2022]

- Multi-variant products: check the top Google Shopping and organic results for the product; if they use one page with variants, do the same; if they use separate pages per variant, do the same. [L0665, 2022]

- Social media links are entered in Theme settings > Social media, one box per network (Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest, Instagram, Tumblr, Vimeo, YouTube, LinkedIn). Paste the full profile URL, e.g. facebook.com/[business name] or instagram.com/[business name]; the icons then appear in the footer's social block and link to those profiles. Until accounts exist the social block stays empty. [L0681, 2021-22]

- The social icon style setting in the Footer colour group also applies to the password page, not only the footer. Choose Light on dark or Dark on light so the icons stay readable on both the footer and the password page. [L0681, 2021-22]

- Shopify path to the Google Analytics connection: Sales channels > Online Store > Preferences. The Google Analytics section there shows the existing account and a separate GA4 entry above it, which is managed through the Google channel app. [L0723, 2020-26]

Connect GA4 to Shopify, in this order:
1. In Shopify admin, go to Sales channels > Online Store > Preferences.
2. In the Google Analytics section, above the Universal Analytics entry, click Manage pixel.
3. Install the Google channel app when Shopify asks for it.
4. Click Connect and sign in with the same business email used for Google Analytics, Google Ads and Merchant Center.
5. Click Allow so that Shopify can access the Google data it needs to pass data both ways.
6. Open the GA4 property dropdown and select the correct property, for example the one named after the new store and ending in tracking tag.
7. Click Connect and wait for the confirmation message before moving on.
[L0723, 2020-26]

- If the Google account has several GA4 properties, Shopify may preselect the property of the main website. Always check the dropdown and select the property created for the store being connected. [L0723, 2020-26]

- The Google access request asks for access to many Google services. Allow it, because Shopify needs that access to send and receive data between the store and the Google properties. [L0723, 2020-26]

- Delete every demo product from the Shopify store before creating Google accounts, so that only real products from the supplier brands you are authorized to sell remain. Demo products left on the store can cause problems with Google. [L0724, 2020-26]

- Click every link in the header menu, every collection link on the homepage and every link in the footer menu, and confirm each one opens the correct page. No link may lead to a 404 page. [L0724, 2020-26]

- Replace all placeholder graphics before submission, including empty boxes with a crossed circle icon and labels such as 'image coming soon'. Every collection and product image must be real. [L0724, 2020-26]

- The left column of the footer navigation must show a real phone number and a real email address. This is one of the two contact methods Google checks on the storefront. [L0724, 2020-26]

Final compliance checklist before submitting to Google:
1. Delete all demo products in Shopify so that only real products from authorized supplier brands remain.
2. Click every link in the header menu, every homepage collection link and every footer menu link, and fix any link that leads to a 404 page.
3. Replace all placeholder images with real images.
4. Open Shopify Settings > Policies and review the refund, privacy, terms of service and shipping policies, replacing all placeholder text and confirming the email address and phone number are real.
5. Fill in the contact information section of each policy with a real email address, phone number and hours of operation, then publish.
6. Paste each policy page into Google Gemini and request a compliance review.
7. Apply every recommendation, then send the revised text back to Gemini for a second check and repeat until it reports full compliance.
8. Add a real phone number and a real email address to the left column of the footer navigation.
9. On the Contact us page, keep the contact form and add at least two more contact methods, then send the page to Gemini for review.
10. Submit the store to Google Merchant Center and Google Ads only after every step passes.
[L0724, 2020-26]

- Tax and shipping set up in Shopify must be mirrored in Merchant Center, because the settings in both places must agree. Mismatched tax or shipping information is a common reason for account issues. [L0731, 2021-22]

Fix product errors in Shopify when a feed app is installed:
1. Open Manage products in Shopify, where the app shows recommended actions for each product.
2. For a product with the error 'SKU not submitted', add the variant SKU to the product variants.
3. For products missing a Google product category, select the correct Google product category on each product.
4. For products with an invalid GTIN or barcode, correct the barcode value.
5. Select multiple products to view all their errors at once and work through them.
[L0732, 2020-26]

- To set social links: 1. Open Theme settings > Social media. 2. Replace each default URL with your own account link if you have one. 3. Delete URLs for accounts you do not have, so the footer does not link to Shopify's placeholder pages. [L0775, 2020-26]

- The Shopify integration for Google Analytics 4 was expected to be ready in March 2023, and the course would be updated after that. Until then, Universal Analytics stops being supported in July 2023. [L0804, 2023]

- Google Merchant Center product identifiers: if you use MPN instead of GTIN, leave the GTIN column blank and fill the separate MPN column. Make sure the MPN field is what you submit and what appears in the product feed. [L0804, 2023]

- If suppliers provide no GTIN or MPN, create your own naming convention such as APR-123 for each product, so product feed fields are not blank and do not cause rejections. [L0804, 2023]

- Adding Google Tag Manager to a Shopify store: 1. Create a Google Tag Manager container for the store and copy its two code snippets. 2. Paste the first snippet into the head section of the theme layout file. 3. Paste the second snippet directly after the opening body tag. 4. If the placement is unclear, ask a developer to put the code in the right place. 5. Add analytics, chat and heatmap tools as tags in the container instead of pasting separate code into the theme.
