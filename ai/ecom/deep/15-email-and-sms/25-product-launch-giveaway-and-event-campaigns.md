### Product Launch, Giveaway and Event Campaigns

**Campaign definition and ideas**

- Campaigns are one-time email blasts sent to a list or segment for events, launches, and seasonal offers, such as Valentine's Day, Father's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas, a new product launch, or early access for VIP customers. Unlike flows, they are not triggered by a visitor action. They are sent to an existing audience on a set date. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
- Campaign ideas for a store with a consistent seller: launches, offer launches, event launches (occasions such as Mother's Day or Valentine's Day), new product launches, specific collection promotions, bundle offers, giveaways and referral campaigns, and a weekly deal. Each should target a defined segment such as buyers, non-buyers, or VIPs. [L0605, undated]
- Run 3-4 email campaigns a month from this list: offer launch, event launch, new product launch, specific collection promotion, giveaways, and deal of the week. [L0604, undated]

**New product and category announcements**

- New product categories can be announced by email and SMS to existing customers, including the launch date and key features. Repeat purchases from these customers need no ad spend, so the sale is close to pure profit because the acquisition cost was already paid. Good customer service is a precondition for this to work. [L0434, undated; L0435, undated]
- Once you have a good customer email list, you can announce new products through an email campaign instead of paying for ads. This makes an existing customer base a low-cost channel for new product launches. [L1172, 2023+]
- Email your customer list when you launch new products or promotions, such as a new private-label brand, because email drives traffic to products that Google Shopping rarely reaches. [L0968, 2021]

**New product launch sequence**

1. One week before launch, send a VIP sign-up email announcing the upcoming product with its features and images.
2. Offer VIP sign-ups an extra discount on launch, for example 30% off an $80 product.
3. Send three to four emails over the one-week period.
4. Count the sign-ups to estimate how many purchases to expect (for example 200 sign-ups out of 1,000 non-buyers).
5. Send the next email to the people who signed up, and a separate email to those who did not, asking why and offering an extra benefit point. [L0605, undated]
- Campaigns should warm up the audience before asking for the purchase: educate first, then announce the launch, then ask for the sign-up. A direct buy request in the first email is too aggressive. [L0605, undated]

**Weekly deal**

- Weekly deal campaigns have a comparatively low conversion rate, but they keep existing customers warm and prevent them from forgetting the brand after the first purchase. Combine a benefit of a new product with a small extra discount each week. [L0605, undated]
- A weekly 10% off deal is part of a monthly structure of about four campaigns, plus one new product launch campaign per month. [L1213, 2023]

**Referral giveaway**

1. Run the giveaway on social media, asking followers to tag three friends and post to their stories.
2. In the email, ask the customer to refer three people using a referral or affiliate link.
3. Reward the referrer when a referred contact signs up with an email or phone number.
4. Add a larger reward when a referred contact buys. [L0605, undated]

**Event teaser**

- Add an event teaser at the bottom of a regular content email:

```
PS: [Event name] is coming. [Link to signup page]
```

- The linked page should ask only for an email address to qualify interested buyers. [L0334, 2017]

**Creating and scheduling a campaign in Klaviyo**

1. Open Campaigns and click Create campaign. [L0607, undated]
2. Enter a campaign name, for example the event and year, and set the start date (for example 15 January for a Valentine's Day push). Click Save and continue. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
3. Select the recipients: the Newsletter list or a segment such as VIP customers. Choose whether to exclude any groups. Choose whether to use tracking, and turn tracking on. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
4. Click Continue to content. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
5. Enter the subject line and preview text. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
6. Pick a template under Popular or from the template library. For an event, choose the event template from the Popular library. If there is no event template, change a similar template and edit the event name. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
7. Change the background color to white or your brand color, and add text, images, or a dynamic product section. [L0607, undated]
8. Click Next. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
9. Turn Smart sending off. [L0573, undated]
10. Choose Schedule or Send, then select Gradual send over several hours. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
11. Set the start time (for example 11 am Eastern) and the share of recipients per hour (for example 20 percent per hour). [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]
12. Click Schedule. [L0573, undated; L0607, undated]

- Gradual campaign sending: send to a set share of recipients per hour, for example 20 percent per hour, instead of pushing all emails at one time. Turn Smart sending off for campaigns too. [L0573, undated]
- Klaviyo is the email platform where offer emails are scheduled and sent to the broadcast audience. Scheduling of offers is done here alongside Google and Facebook. [L0757, 2022]

### Sponsored Blasts

- Sponsored email blasts: you pay to use a partner's email list to send your message. The price usually rises with the number of emails you send. [L0525, 2021-22]
- A sponsorship does not have to be a single email. You can plan a series of emails with the partner that promote your products, drive visitors to your site, or point to your content. [L0525, 2021-22]
