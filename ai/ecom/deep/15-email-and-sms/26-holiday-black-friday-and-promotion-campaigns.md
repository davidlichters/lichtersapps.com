## Holiday, Black Friday and Promotion Campaigns

### Q4 and Seasonal Planning

**Timing**
- Start preparing Q4 at least two months before the peak, and warm the email and SMS audience from about September or October. Interaction-based emails and surveys build the list before the sales period. [L1377, 2022+]
- Use plain-text emails with one link or one form instead of heavy designs and images, because designed emails are more likely to land in the promotions tab. Surveys can be built with Jotform and sent to subscribers. [L1377, 2022+]
- Plan the holiday content early. Steps:
  1. Create blog posts for major holidays (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas) 1-2 months before the event. [L0226, 2017]
  2. Write articles about upcoming sales, new inventory, and limited-time promotions. [L0226, 2017]
  3. Send an email to the list 2-4 weeks before the event with the article and promotional details. [L0226, 2017]
  4. Aim to rank in Google search when customers search "Black Friday [niche]" or "Cyber Monday deals." [L0226, 2017]
- Run a campaign for every major event or occasion, such as Christmas or Black Friday, because customers expect a discount at these times. This adds to the regular weekly and monthly campaigns. [L1213, 2023]
- Use seasonal events such as Valentine's Day for promotions and extra emails. Plan promotions and emails around holidays to create reasons to send more messages. [L0934, 2023]
- Plan a monthly promotion to the email list and build it into the regular calendar. Monthly promotions lift sales and help the store scale into other platforms later. [L0941, 2022]
- Follow up holiday promotions in a later month. A promotion for a holiday can close sales that a first visit did not convert. [L1325, 2020-26]
- Match email promotions with the same offer on Meta at the same time. Past customers who bought months ago, such as six months back, should receive both the email and the Facebook promotion. [L0649, 2019]

**Event campaigns**
- If Klaviyo's template library has no template for a seasonal event, pick a template for a similar occasion and adapt it, for example a New Year layout for Valentine's Day. Replace the headline, offer text, and background color so the email matches the event. [L0573, undated]
- A seasonal gift promotion, such as a Father's Day offer, fits stores whose products appeal to dads. Promote it with a short series of emails to the subscriber list. [L0857, 2025]
- An event-based promotion sent to the email list, such as 5% or 10% off on May 5 for Cinco de Mayo, is a quick way to earn extra sales. [L1002, 2019]
- Event campaign for occasions such as Mother's Day or Valentine's Day, in this order:
  1. Choose a specific marketing angle for the event. [L0605, undated]
  2. Send the first email to educate the audience and announce the upcoming offer, without a hard pitch. [L0605, undated]
  3. Send the next email with a benefit of the product and a sign-up request. [L0605, undated]
  4. Count the sign-ups to estimate the sales. [L0605, undated]
  5. Send the follow-up to sign-ups, and a separate email to non-sign-ups asking why they did not sign up, with an extra benefit point. [L0605, undated]

**Monthly promotion procedure**
1. Choose a high-ticket product from a supplier with good margins. [L0963, 2022]
2. Send an email offering a discount on that supplier's products, for example 10 percent off. [L0963, 2022]
3. Link directly to the product collection. [L0963, 2022]

**Black Friday and Cyber Monday announcements**
- Send an email to your list, post on social media, and use every channel you have to announce a Black Friday sale and a Cyber Monday sale. Even a small email list or a small social following can produce extra sales from these promotions. [L1086, 2019]
- In 2019, Black Friday fell on November 29 and Cyber Monday on December 2. Plan holiday promotions around these dates in the year they apply. [L1086, 2019]

**Abandoned cart contacts (2023)**
- Abandoned cart contacts who are not subscribed to the main list can receive about one promotional email per month, tied to a holiday or niche event. [L1094, 2023]

### List Building Before the Peak

- Build an email and SMS list before the Q4 peak, because acquiring customers gets expensive then. Start retargeting ads after roughly two to three weeks of traffic, once you have stats to target from. [L0692, 2023]
- List building avoids high CPMs. During the holiday season, CPM and CPC spike and CTR drops on all ad platforms (Facebook, Google, Pinterest, TikTok). Instead of direct sales ads, send traffic to a landing page that collects email addresses, which builds a qualified audience for engagement and future sales. [L0307, 2023+]
- Email marketing is a free or low-cost channel during BFCM and delivers strong ROI. Prioritize email list building and VIP segmentation before peak season. [L0298, 2023]
- Build the email list from organic and paid visitors, since subscribers can be promoted to during monthly promotions. Sync the monthly promotions with your ad accounts so buyers see them on social too. [L0941, 2022]
- Holiday promotion emails should go to the existing list regardless of its size, whether it has around 50 or around 50,000 contacts. Each email should include the discount code and aim to bring back visitors who have not decided to buy. [L0623, 2020-26]
- A small email list is still worth using for Q4 promotions. The remarketing budget for a small audience is about $5 a day, and the sales are close to pure profit. [L1285, 2024]
- A small email list still makes the holiday sends worth doing, because the remarketing spend with a small audience costs about $5 per day, and the sales it brings are close to pure profit. A few responses out of 100 to 300 contacts across five promotions justify the work. [L1223, 2024+]
- Set up abandoned cart emails, a content opt-in capture, and promotional follow-ups before a high-traffic sales period. This lets you contact visitors who did not buy on their first visit. [L1274, 2018]
- Black Friday and Cyber Monday results depend heavily on remarketing to the audience built up during the year, not only on first-time visitors. Give that audience a reason to return with a promotion. [L1274, 2018]
- Expect slow sales from influencer digital products. Collect emails for one to two months while keeping the audience engaged with the influencer, then blast the promotion to the list and on stories to create strong sales days. [L0789, 2022]
- Case: A store spent about $50,000 on Facebook ads in Q4 but got most of its sales through its email and SMS marketing. It warmed its list from October with surveys, plain-text emails and a launch of one or two offers per month, then sent VIP bundle offers before the sales period. Retargeting campaigns with Black Friday and Cyber Monday offers reached roughly 8x ROAS in some campaigns. [L1377, 2022+]

### Dated Promotion Calendars

**Black Friday and Cyber Monday sequence**
1. November 24 (48 hours before launch): send "Going live in 48 hours. Last year everything sold out in 4 hours. You're among our first 100 subscribers, so take advantage." Include SMS. [L0307, 2023+]
2. November 25 (24 hours before): send "Going live in 24 hours." Tease a product image with a blurred price (e.g., show "9.99" to create curiosity). Include SMS. [L0307, 2023+]
3. November 26 (Black Friday, launch day): send the launch email and SMS with a link to the hidden category page. [L0307, 2023+]
4. November 27 (day after Black Friday): send "We had huge success, received many requests, some items sold out. We just restocked limited pieces. Shop now." [L0307, 2023+]
5. November 28 (Sunday): send "This is your last day. Only a few items left. Shop now." Send SMS. [L0307, 2023+]
6. November 29 (day after): send "It's finished. Thank you for your trust and business." Send SMS. [L0307, 2023+]
7. Monday (Cyber Monday, one week later): launch a new offer with a different discount structure (example: change from 50% off to 40% off plus a 50% cashback voucher for the next purchase). Send the new offer email and SMS, then a 48-hour countdown, then a last-chance email, then a closure email. [L0307, 2023+]
8. Optional Black Week extension (November 29-30, only 2 days): send an email plus SMS, then a UGC email, then a closure email "It's over." [L0307, 2023+]

**Black Friday sequence for a store**
1. Before the sale, send an email announcing Black Friday week and asking customers to decide now what they want. [L1363, 2023]
2. On the day, send the main announcement with the bigger discount. [L1363, 2023]
3. Retarget visitors who did not buy with a message saying they missed the offer. [L1363, 2023]
4. After the sale, send an email saying the sale has been extended for people who missed it. [L1363, 2023]

**Hidden Black Friday category page**
- Create a product category page that is not linked in website navigation or menus. It is only accessible via direct links from email, SMS, or retargeting ads. Include all Black Friday-discounted products, a countdown timer showing the promotion end time, and low-stock quantity labels on each product to create urgency. [L0307, 2023+]

**Welcome series and standard flows during BFCM**
- Standard email flows (abandoned cart sequences, welcome series, post-purchase) remain active and run in parallel during Black Friday promotional campaigns. Do not pause them. They operate on their own triggers and segments, separate from the VIP Black Friday list. [L0308, 2023]
- For ongoing welcome series, if new leads enter after Day 3 emails have started, pause emails 2 and 3 for those new subscribers to prevent clashes with Black Friday email blasts, which have priority. Resume the welcome series after the promotion period ends. [L0307, 2023+]
- The welcome series runs automatically on fixed dates (e.g., Nov 1-3) regardless of when individual subscribers join. For subscribers entering late (e.g., Nov 22), pause emails 2 and 3 to avoid overlap with high-priority Black Friday blasts. Resume normal sequences after promotions end. [L0307, 2023+]
- Reconciling the two: standard flows stay active [L0308, 2023], and the welcome-series pause applies only to late joiners' emails 2 and 3 during the promotion period [L0307, 2023+].

**Campaigns versus flows**
- When time is short, use campaigns instead of flows for Black Friday emails. Campaigns are simpler: send an initial email to the list, analyze results, then send a new campaign to the list minus unsubscribes. There is no need for conditional flows. [L0308, 2023]

**Q4 2024 US holiday calendar (2024 dates)**
- Halloween (Thursday 31 October):
  1. Send emails on 29 October and 30 October. [L1285, 2024]
  2. Send an email on Halloween, 31 October. [L1285, 2024]
  3. Send an extension email on Friday 1 November saying the offer runs one more day. Pair this with Meta and Google remarketing ads. [L1285, 2024]
- Black Friday and Thanksgiving:
  1. Send one email each on Monday 25 November, Tuesday 26 November, Wednesday 27 November, Thursday 28 November (Thanksgiving, themed around the holiday with the coupon), and Friday 29 November (Black Friday, when the sale closes). [L1285, 2024]
  2. Run remarketing ads synced with these emails. [L1285, 2024]
- Cyber Monday (2 December, a one-day promotion):
  1. Send one email early in the morning on 2 December. [L1285, 2024]
  2. Send a second email around 5 PM the same day saying the sale is about to end. [L1285, 2024]
  3. Run remarketing ads alongside. [L1285, 2024]
- Christmas:
  1. Send emails on Monday 9 December, Wednesday 11 December, and Friday 13 December. [L1285, 2024]
  2. Make the message that this is the last time to buy and still get the item delivered before Christmas, since about two weeks of delivery time remain. [L1285, 2024]
- New Year:
  1. Start the campaign on 30 December. [L1285, 2024]
  2. Send an email on New Year's Day, 1 January. [L1285, 2024]
  3. Send a final email on Friday 3 January, when the offer closes. [L1285, 2024]
  4. Run remarketing ads alongside. [L1285, 2024]
- Each promotion uses its own email count and send dates from the calendar. Send the planned emails on dated sends, and extend the offer with one more email the day after the event when needed, as with Halloween's extra email on 11/1. [L1221, 2024+]
- Send one email per day with a distinct angle rather than repeating the same message. Each email should carry the same offer and coupon code so customers can act on any of them. [L1105, 2024]

**Christmas promotion**
1. Launch the holiday promotion email as early as possible, ideally right away. [L0468, 2020]
2. Include the message that products arrive before Christmas if ordered now. [L0468, 2020]
3. Send one or two reminder emails as the holiday gets closer. [L0468, 2020]
4. Do not send the main holiday promotion on the holiday itself, because most customers have already bought. [L0468, 2020]

**Five-day holiday promotion**
1. Send an email at the start of the promotion announcing the sale and including the discount code. [L0623, 2020-26]
2. Send an email in the middle of the window, or on a following day, saying the sale was extended to the final day. [L0623, 2020-26]
3. Send a last email stating that the code expires at midnight on the final day. [L0623, 2020-26]
4. Every email should include the code. [L0623, 2020-26]
- For a five-day promotion, spread a three-email sequence across the window: the first email on the launch day, the second about two days later, and the third on the final day. Each email should match the stage of the promotion it is sent in. [L0620, 2025+]

**One-week December promotion**
- For a one-week December promotion with three emails, send the gain email on the first day, the logic email two days later, and the fear email on the last day. For example, December 8, December 10, and December 12. [L1224, 2025+]

**High-ticket promotion calendar**
- Close offers on a weekday. For more expensive, high-ticket products, the offer should close on a Friday. [L1205, 2020-26]

**Timers and extensions**
- Klaviyo email campaigns can include countdown timers that are reset in each send. When a sale is extended, update the timer and the matching offer text in every campaign so the messages stay consistent. [L1225, 2025]

**Repeat buyers**
- Email customers who bought a single item with a targeted discount to buy a gift or a second item for a partner or family member. This extends the value of first-time buyers in the holiday season. [L1225, 2025]

### Promotion Segments and Exclusions

**Standard promotion segment (2024-2025 rule)**
- For a promotion email, send to all contacts in the list, except people who purchased in the last 30 days and contacts currently in an evergreen automation, such as the abandoned cart flow. This works with either Shopify Email or Klaviyo. [L1221, 2024+; L1222, 2025+; L1223, 2024+; L1285, 2024; L1287, 2025]
- Segment settings:
  - Include: all contacts.
  - Exclude: contacts who purchased in the past 30 days.
  - Exclude: contacts currently in the abandoned cart flow (or any evergreen automation).
  - Segment size does not matter; a list of 500 or 10,000 contacts uses the same rules. [L1223, 2024+]
- Promotional emails go to all contacts except two groups: contacts who bought in the last 30 days, and contacts currently inside an evergreen automation. [L1221, 2024+]
- Recent buyers and automated flows should not receive the promotion sequence. [L1222, 2025+]

**Tools for segments and sends**
- Shopify Email or Klaviyo can run the holiday promotions. Only one of the two is needed. Either tool handles the segments and the scheduled sends for the Q4 campaign, and the same tool must handle the promotional sends. [L1223, 2024+; L1221, 2024+]

**Matching timer and offer rules**
- Each flow needs one reliable discount code, and the expiry you promise in the copy must match the expiry configured in the store. [L0606, undated]

### VIP Early Access

**BFCM VIP early access email**

```
BFCM VIP Early Access Email: Subject emphasizes exclusivity (e.g., 'Your 24-Hour VIP Early Access Starts Now'). Body: Explain that subscribers get 24-hour first access to upcoming BFCM offers; this creates urgency and FOMO without requiring truly different pricing-simply send the email 24 hours before the public campaign launches. Landing: Direct to a dedicated BFCM collection or campaign page. Effect: Drives eager subscribers to site before the main sale begins.
```
[L0298, 2023]

**VIP positioning for lead capture**
- For Black Friday lead capture in Facebook Creator Studio, use a VIP positioning: "Enter details to get exclusive discounts up to 60% off with early access." This entices email submission without over-promising. [L0308, 2023]

**VIP access sequence**
1. Start VIP access at least 72 hours before the sale begins. [L1225, 2025]
2. Give VIP access first to ambassadors. [L1225, 2025]
3. Then open it to organic followers. [L1225, 2025]
4. Then open it to the email list. [L1225, 2025]
- Do not run VIP access paid for 30 days. [L1225, 2025]

**Trade-off of early access**
- Replacing a welcome discount pop-up with an early-access sign-up usually softens sales in the short term. The trade-off is a larger list of interested buyers that converts strongly once the sale starts, so the fair value exchange must be clear to the subscriber. [L1225, 2025]

**VIP pre-Black Friday email sequence**
1. Email 1 (day of signup): "Welcome to the Club." [L0308, 2023]
2. Email 2 (2 days before): "What you're going to Choose" (explore products). [L0308, 2023]
3. Email 3 (day before): "It's around the corner." [L0308, 2023]
4. Email 4 (day of): "Here's your exclusive early access." [L0308, 2023]
5. Mention in the final email that the discount releases to the public tomorrow, so act now. [L0308, 2023]
6. Final email on Black Friday: "Here we go, buy now." [L0308, 2023]
