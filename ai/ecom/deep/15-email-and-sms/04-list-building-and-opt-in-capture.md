## List Building and Opt-in Capture

### List Building and Opt-in Capture

### Why the list matters

- Around 80 percent of store visitors leave without buying and never return, so collecting their email address or phone number is needed to retarget them later. Email is the main way to turn these visitors into paying customers. [L0573, undated]
- Roughly 80 to 90 percent of website visitors leave without buying, so a sign-up form that captures email addresses is the main way to recover them later. [L0607, undated]
- A list built from visitors and offers gives you a traffic source that does not depend only on ads or organic social posts. [L0476, undated]
- A list of subscribers can be sold to repeatedly over time, so lead ads should first collect the opt-in, and direct sales on the first click matter less. [L0641, 2014]
- Build an email list from pre-purchase visitors and keep first-time buyers engaged with email so they return for a second and third purchase. [L0609, 2021-22]
- Use the email list you collect from visitors for follow-up, because a visitor who does not buy on the first visit may still buy later. [L1002, 2019]
- Capture the visitor's email address while they are on the store and send follow-up emails to encourage them to complete the purchase. This recovers buyers who did not purchase during their first visit. [L0771, 2021-22]
- Use email campaigns for visitors who did not convert, because the list is an extra channel to bring back warm traffic after a cold ad. [L1016, 2026]
- A large promotion such as a Black Friday sale brings the most cash once a business has an established email list. That list should be built from abandoned carts, exit-intent overlay sign-ups and remarketing audiences. [L1446, 2020-26]
- An incentive that gets visitors to opt in to email is tied to a working opt-in and email program, which the lesson links to a 10% boost in customer value. [L1031, 2018]

### Prospect lists versus customer lists

- Prospect lists, made of visitors who have not bought yet, do not grow on their own the way customer lists do. They need deliberate opt-in offers and several list-building methods running at the same time. They take time to grow, so an early start and persistence matter. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- Customer lists build by themselves through purchases, so the list-building methods below target non-buyers only. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- Seven proven methods build a prospect list: content opt-ins, discount offers, quizzes, surveys, contests and giveaways, sales event opt-ins, and lead magnets as the content-based variant. Content opt-ins are the main workhorse. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- Content opt-ins work best for visitors at the top and middle of the funnel who are researching and need education to solve a problem. For visitors near the point of purchase they are often unattractive, because they need less education. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- Interest-based newsletters on top-of-funnel articles are a useful capture. Subscribers can receive discounts or new-launch messages later, once trust is built, which turns anonymous readers into future buyers. [L1269, 2025-26]
- Capture email addresses in as many places on the site as possible, because these emails are needed for remarketing to shoppers who do not buy on the first visit. *Older advice (2019).* [L1275, 2019]
- Use email capture at multiple points, such as the footer and an exit-intent overlay, to build the list that later drives revenue. *Older advice (2022).* [L0854, 2022]
- Use the collected emails for remarketing and not just as a newsletter list. *Older advice (2018).* [L1085, 2018]
- Collect emails with exit-intent overlays and at purchase. Use the list to make offers and to remarket to past buyers and opt-in visitors. *Older advice (2019).* [L0663, 2019]

### Choosing the offer

#### Match the offer to the goal

- Match the opt-in offer to what subscribers want in the long term, not only to a first-order discount. A 5 or 10 percent coupon can work in the short term, but an offer tied to the customer's real goal keeps subscribers closer to the brand over time. [L0380, 2014]
- A bribe only earns an email address when it delivers something the visitor already wants. Build it from the site's topic and the visitor's likely stage, not from what the affiliate would like to promote. [L0514, 2015]
- Offer value that moves the customer toward the goal behind the product, not only the product itself. A podcast microphone buyer wants a successful show, so guides on launching and growing a podcast add value. The list works as a two-way exchange: the reader gets what they want, and you get their attention and eventually their purchase. [L0380, 2014]
- Asking visitors openly for their email in an opt-in pop-up is appropriate when the offer is genuinely useful and matches what the subscriber will receive. Visitors who want the content expect the request, so hesitating to ask weakens the list-building step. The promise in the pop-up must be exactly what the list delivers. [L0380, 2014]
- Use a community or cause in the offer. An offer that pairs a small incentive with a cause the reader already cares about gives a reason to subscribe that fits the article's topic. [L1269, 2025-26]

#### Incentive types

- Common incentive opt-in offers on Shopify stores include 10% off the next order, $10 off the first order, and a spin-the-wheel pop-up that gives a random discount in exchange for an email address. A concrete discount makes the sign-up feel worth it, while a generic newsletter invitation gives the visitor no clear reward. [L0510, 2018]
- A sign-up offer that gives a discount in exchange for an email address works because a clear, concrete reward makes the sign-up feel worth it. Two examples in the same pattern: `Subscribe now and get 15% off your first purchase.` and `Join the list for free shipping on your next order.` [L0510, 2018]
- The sign-up incentive is a discount, typically 5 percent, 5 dollars or 10 percent off, offered in exchange for the email address. [L0573, undated]
- Offer an incentive such as 10% to 20% off in exchange for signing up on a signup form page. [L1213, 2023]
- Offer free shipping at the moment a visitor arrives to exchange their email address. This captured emails at a rate of about 20% in the example. [L0790, 2017]
- Where a discount does not fit the business, a buyer's guide or product quiz can replace it as the reason to sign up, followed by an automated sequence. [L1204, 2022]
- Offer a valuable lead magnet, such as a buyer's guide or a quiz with results that unlock after entering an email, to collect emails when a discount alone does not work. *Older advice (2022).* [L1113, 2022]
- Three list-building offer types for campaigns: [L0307, 2023+]
  1. Membership or subscription: exclusive member-only deals, limited spots (for example 100), free to join.
  2. Contest or giveaway: enter to win a high-value prize (for example a $1,000 gift card), subscribe to enter.
  3. Lead magnet (evergreen): a valuable free resource (PDF, guide, template, checklist) usually priced at $19 to $29, available free for a limited time or limited quantity (for example 100 copies).
- Pattern: replace the default footer sign-up text with a prize-entry line that names a monthly giveaway, because people sign up for a chance to win more readily than for updates. Two examples: `Subscribe for your chance to win a $100 gift card this month` and `Join the list to enter this month's giveaway of a free starter kit`. [L1204, 2022]

#### Discount codes and their rules

Sources give different minimum-spend rules. Both are kept; each applies to the setup it describes.

| Source setup | Discount | Minimum spend | Usage limit | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify, Amount off order, generic sign-up | 5%, $5 or 10% off | None | One use per customer | [L0573, undated] |
| Welcome code example `welcome_10` | 10% off | None | One use per customer | [L0573, undated] |
| Shopify fixed-amount code for a sign-up pop-up, example `save five` | $5 off | Optional, example $50 | Once per customer if desired | [L0477, undated] |

- Create the discount in Shopify under Discounts > Create discount > Amount off order. [L0573, undated]
- A 10 percent welcome code named `welcome_10` with no minimum order value and one use per customer is used as the sign-up discount. Set the coupon type in the form to static coupon when one code is shared by all subscribers. [L0573, undated]
- For a fixed-amount sign-up discount, follow these steps. [L0477, undated]
  1. In Shopify, create a discount with a fixed amount ($5 in the example).
  2. Give it a memorable name (for example `save five`).
  3. Limit usage to once per customer if desired.
  4. Add an optional minimum purchase (for example $50).
  5. Put the exact code into the Klaviyo success step.
- A sign-up form can issue a unique discount code to each subscriber instead of one shared static code. Unique codes let you track redemptions per person. A static code is simpler to set up and is the usual starting choice for a new store. [L0573, undated]
- Replace the offer text with a fixed dollar amount, for example `save $5 with your first order`. [L1297, 2023]

### Lead magnets, buyer's guides, quizzes and giveaways

#### Lead magnet design

- A lead magnet should give real value to the reader. A useful resource that people want to keep works better over the long term than a single discount for a referring site. It should also resemble a marketing brochure, with the company introduced and an offer included. [L1076, 2019]
- About 30 to 40 percent of people who opt in open the email and click the download link for a lead magnet. Make the content worth reading for that group, while people who do not read it still receive the promotional emails. [L1076, 2019]
- A free downloadable buyer's guide for the product category can serve as a lead magnet, exchanged for an email sign-up. Adding a coupon after sign-up gives the prospect a second reason to convert and builds trust before Shopping ads are shown. [L1142, 2020-26]
- A free buyer's guide for a niche works well as a content asset, because it captures emails that can be used later in promotions. *Older advice (2022).* [L1092, 2022]
- Content pieces should include an opt-in form to collect email leads, even when the page also links out to third-party sites. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L1421, 2021-22]
- Every blog post should offer readers a chance to join the email list, for example through a free download in exchange for their email address. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0462, 2021-22]

#### Buyer's guide procedures

Procedure A: lead magnet with a Klaviyo flow. *Older advice (2022).* [L1092, 2022]
1. Create a buyer's guide PDF for the niche.
2. Build an opt-in page on Shopify with an embedded Klaviyo sign-up box that asks for the email address and promises to send the guide there.
3. Set the sign-up form to add the subscriber to a list named for the guide, such as buyer's guide, or to apply a tag.
4. Create a Klaviyo flow triggered when the tag is applied or the subscriber joins the list.
5. Add a short wait of 5 minutes, then send the email containing the PDF.

Procedure B: Shopify page or exit-intent overlay. *Older advice in the source year range (2023 source).* [L1094, 2023]
1. Create a Shopify page titled `Buyer's Guide` with an opt-in form asking for an email address.
2. Or set up an exit-intent overlay that appears as visitors leave and offers the guide in exchange for an email address.
3. Connect the form to a Shopify email automation that sends the PDF guide to the subscriber.

Procedure C: buyer's guide on a dedicated page. *Older advice (2022).* [L1281, 2022]
1. Create a content page on the store that describes the buyer's guide, not a product.
2. Embed the Klaviyo opt-in form on that page.
3. Send the guide by email through Klaviyo when the visitor submits the form. You can also use the exit-intent overlay alongside it.

Exit-intent text for the buyer's guide, ready to use. Keep the email field as the only input, and send the guide automatically after submission. *Older advice (2022).* [L1281, 2022]

```
Before you go, enter your email and we will send you our free buyer's guide.
```

#### Quizzes, surveys and contests

- Offer a quiz with results that unlock after entering an email. [L1113, 2022]
- In a contest or giveaway, extra entries can be earned through actions such as social sharing and referrals, which increases the number of opt-ins. Promote the contest on social media to grow both the entries and the reach. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- Instagram giveaways can collect email addresses for a new brand's list: followers enter the draw by submitting their email. This suits a business that still needs to grow its audience before it has a large list. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0462, 2021-22]
- Contest, giveaway and membership offers are set out in the three-offer structure above. [L0307, 2023+]

#### Sales event opt-ins

- Opt-in form for a sales event: one field for an email address, a "Sign Up" button, and directly beneath the form this note. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]

```
*Submitting this form means you agree to get our emails, and you may opt out whenever you like.
```

- A large promotion such as a Black Friday sale brings the most cash once a business has an established email list. [L1446, 2020-26]

#### Gamified capture

- Besides standard sign-up boxes in the sidebar, footer and abandoned-cart emails, lead capture can use exit-intent overlays and gamified spin-to-win pop-ups such as Wheelio that offer a prize in exchange for an email address. These tools collect leads from visitors who already come to the site, so they add to the reach of paid traffic instead of replacing it. [L1076, 2019]

### Where to place capture points

#### Placement principles

- Install an opt-in on the store before publishing content, because articles alone do not build an email list. [L0380, 2014]
- The email sign-up should be on the store even before you plan email campaigns, because it collects subscribers into the Shopify admin from day one. Keep the heading short and clear about what visitors get. [L0775, 2020-26]
- Each email opt-in on the site should have its own email list. Specific opt-ins can be placed on relevant content pages, and exit-intent pop-ups should be reviewed as an opt-in opportunity. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0493, 2021-22]
- Map every page of the site and note which opt-ins already run there. [L0571, 2021-22]
  1. Map every page of the site (home page, collection pages, blog page, blog posts, product pages) and note which opt-ins already run there.
  2. Identify the pages without any opt-in opportunity.
  3. Decide which opt-in to place on each gap, matched to the visitor's funnel stage.
- Content opt-ins need their own list or segment, with an automated email sequence behind them. The sequence should move the subscriber from the content toward a purchase, for example by leading a how-to downloader toward the relevant product. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]
- A newsletter works as an opt-in when it is specific about its content and frequency, for example one issue each month with a stated topic. Offering an immediate download or a sample of the newsletter on the page lets visitors judge its value before they opt in. *Older advice (2021-22).* [L0571, 2021-22]

#### Footer

- Add an email opt-in to the footer for the mailing list. Niche theme stores rely heavily on email to follow up, run holiday promotions and win repeat sales. [L1325, 2020-26]
- In the theme's Newsletter section, set the heading, subheading, email placeholder text and button label, and include a short promise such as `stay updated with offers` so visitors know what they sign up for. [L1188, 2026]
- On a simple product store, place an email sign-up box in the footer with the heading `Sign Up And Save` and the line `Get early access to new product releases and special discounts.` Add a `40% OFF` sale banner with `Sale Ends SOON` next to it to create urgency, and ask visitors where they are based in the sign-up form. [L0347, 2026]

#### Exit intent

- An exit-intent overlay that collects emails from visitors who are about to leave is the main source of email leads for a store. It builds a list from real visitors who opted in. *Older advice (2024 source, same principle repeated across sources).* [L0471, 2024]
- The exit-intent overlay produced most of the collected emails in the source example. [L1204, 2022]
- Exit-intent overlays on the store help gather new email subscribers organically. This is how a list grows after launch, alongside abandoned cart contacts. [L1285, 2024]
- Exit-intent overlays on the store capture visitors' email addresses when they try to leave. Together with the abandoned cart flow, they build the email list organically after launch, even if the store is new before Q4. [L1223, 2024+]
- Use an exit-intent overlay that captures emails and starts an automated follow-up sequence. Pair it with abandoned cart emails. *Older advice (2020).* [L1088, 2020]
- If it is unclear whether a pop-up comes from Shopify or Klaviyo, check Klaviyo first, because a Klaviyo pop-up is visible there. [L0855, 2023]

#### Blog and content pages

- After an email signup section is added to the Manhattan blog post template and saved, the opt-in appears at the bottom of every blog post, below the content. It is visible on the live store after a page refresh. [L1265, 2020-26]
- Build an opt-in form on the content page, not only on product pages, to collect leads from research visitors. [L0571, 2021-22]

#### Dedicated sign-up page

- Build an email list with a sign-up form page on the Shopify store that asks for email, name and optionally phone number, instead of a buy-now product page. Offer an incentive such as 10% to 20% off in exchange for signing up. [L1213, 2023]
- Lead generation traffic should be sent to a page whose only goal is to collect name and email, not to sell a product. Ads that promote a discount or a best-seller page skip the list-building step and lose the buyer who is not ready yet. [L1076, 2019]
