### Lead-magnet follow-up flows

- Collecting opt-ins without a follow-up email sequence produces leads who never buy. The money from a lead magnet comes from the follow-up, so every opt-in must enter a flow. [L1076, 2019]
- Build a separate flow for every lead magnet, so each requested item receives its own follow-up sequence. A single flow is enough at the start, and you add more as you create more free items. [L1076, 2019]
- Lead-magnet email flow in Klaviyo:
  1. Immediately send the requested free item with the download link. [L1076, 2019]
  2. Wait 24 hours. [L1076, 2019]
  3. Send a welcome email introducing the company, with a first coupon such as 5 percent off. [L1076, 2019]
  4. Wait 24 hours between each further email and keep making offers. [L1076, 2019]
- A new lead should first go through a welcome flow of about four to five emails, after which they move into the main broadcast list. Beginners can start with about one broadcast email a week and increase the frequency as the list grows. [L1076, 2019]
- Download-delivery emails: in one published benchmark, welcome and thank-you emails that contained a download link for a lead magnet had an 88.7% open rate and a 35.07% click-through rate. Use these figures as a reference point when measuring your own download-delivery emails. [L0571, 2021-22]
- The email that delivers a lead magnet is opened by most subscribers, so it can carry a second offer. Use it as a second conversion point, for example to promote a membership discount or a related product. [L0571, 2021-22]
- Influencer digital products:
  1. Send a five-email sequence that provides value first. [L0789, 2022]
  2. Then send the new subscribers a 24-hour promotion with a coupon to convert them into buyers. [L0789, 2022]

### Ongoing welcome-series handling

- Tag-based and purchase-based exits: keep the welcome flow limited to new subscribers, using the buyer and abandoner filters above, so the flow does not mail people who already bought or who sit in a separate abandoned cart sequence. [L0201, 2020-26; L1470, 2025-26; L0573, undated]
- Use separate codes per channel and check redemption by channel in reporting. [L1470, 2025-26]
- Smart sending and tracking: set Smart sending off and UTM tracking on for every welcome email, so the flow sends on the set schedule and shows its traffic in analytics. [L0476, undated; L0607, undated; L1297, 2023]
- Saving a reusable store template: save the first email as a template so later emails reuse the logo, images and layout. [L0476, undated; L1297, 2023; L0634, undated]
- Reviewing the flow after launch: check each email's delay, the discount link, and the send time, then review opened and clicked results. [L1470, 2025-26]
- Duplicate flows: confirm that the old welcome flows are off before switching the new flow on, and notify the client that the flow is live. [L1470, 2025-26]
- Discount link test: test the link itself, because the preview in Klaviyo does not make discount links clickable. [L1470, 2025-26]
- Ongoing frequency after the welcome series: continue with one message every 2-3 days until the subscriber opens an email or buys. [L0605, undated]
- Ongoing sequence after the welcome series: move new leads from the welcome flow into the main broadcast list. [L1076, 2019]

#### Further details

- Build one autoresponder sequence that delivers the bribe immediately and then follows up on a schedule. Each email is scheduled for a specific day, so email one goes out on day one, email two on day two, and so on. [L0514, 2015]
