## Devices, VPNs, IPs and Multi-Account Limits

### Accounts per Phone and Device Pool

**Three-account cap and its exceptions**

| Setup | Limit | When it applies | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testing on one device | Maximum of three TikTok accounts per device | Default rule for testing. Use two or more devices when running several accounts. [L0561, 2023+] | [L0561, 2023+] |
| Practical limit to avoid detection | About three accounts on one phone, with no engagement between them | Given as the practical limit for avoiding detection. [L0657, undated] | [L0657, undated] |
| Organizational limit | Up to three accounts per device | Recommended for organization. [L0820, undated] | [L0820, undated] |
| Observed higher count | Up to seven accounts on one device | Reported in the lesson, where the presenter ran up to seven. The device limit is not a hard rule if you do not engage across accounts. [L0820, undated] | [L0820, undated] |
| Seven logged in at once | Seven accounts logged in on one phone | One seller uploaded to only some of them. Separate devices are safer and easier to manage. [L1381, 2022] | [L1381, 2022] |

- Going beyond three accounts on one phone is considered a bad idea. [L0561, 2023+]
- Several accounts on one phone is common and was reported without a clear ban. [L1186, 2022]
- Accounts can be shared across family members' phones as an additional device pool. Each device should still stay within the three-account cap. [L0561, 2023+]
- A separate phone is not a guaranteed fix for shadow bans. Use an extra phone only if you already have one. Do not buy one for this purpose. [L1186, 2022]

### Separate Devices for Ad and Organic Accounts

- When you run an ad account alongside an organic account for the same product, use a separate phone for each. This keeps ad activity from putting the organic account at risk. [L0352, 2022]
- This recommendation applies only if you already have the extra phone. See [L1186, 2022]. [L0352, 2022; L1186, 2022]

### Cross-Account Engagement

- Do not log into a second account on the same phone to comment or like your own store account. Cross-account engagement on one device can hurt the account. [L0820, undated]
- Do not engage with the accounts from each other. [L0657, undated]
- Each new product test should run on its own fresh account. The same Wi-Fi may be shared across accounts, but an account must not be engaged from a device that is used for another account. [L0820, undated]

### New Accounts and Content Reuse

- After about two weeks of consistent posting, the advantage of a new account can fade. Start a third account only after checking that the first two still perform. [L0352, 2022]
- Repurpose the same content on new accounts before creating new content. [L0352, 2022]
- Case: multi-account growth on separate accounts [L1381, 2022]
  - Starting point: a first account started gaining traction.
  - About 48 hours later: a second brand-new account was created. It took roughly three days to gain traction and go viral.
  - About a month later: a third new account was created.
  - Result: the first and second accounts each reached 100,000 followers in the same period. The third reached 100,000 within a month. Each account reached different people, and the second account became the largest. [L1381, 2022]
- Number on organic posting with the router setup: one account per phone is typical. The lesson's example recommended one account on the reset phone. [L0577, 2023]

### Resetting a Device for a New Product Test

- Testing a new product on a device requires a full factory reset and new accounts. The platform would otherwise link the new product to the old account footprint. Keep the data backed up before resetting. [L0561, 2023+]
- Procedure to move a device to a new product test, or to clear a suspected device or account limit: [L0561, 2023+]
  1. Back up the phone's data to the cloud, but do not include TikTok account information in the backup. [L0561, 2023+]
  2. Perform a full factory reset of the phone. [L0561, 2023+]
  3. Create new TikTok accounts for the new product and post at least three to five completely different pieces of content on them. [L0561, 2023+]

### Diagnosing Device or IP Limits

- If new social accounts are suspended within minutes of posting the same product videos, the device or IP address is more likely the cause than the video content. [L0387, 2022]
- Test this by posting from a different phone, tablet, or network, or by posting an unrelated video from the same account. [L0387, 2022]

### Router and VPN Setup

- A VPN can make an account lose reach or be shadowbanned on TikTok. Test with a split setup: one account with a VPN and one without. Skip the VPN if you do not need it for your location. [L0372, 2022]
- A VPN is generally not needed to go viral from a first-world country. It may help only in certain third-world locations, and some sellers there have still gone viral without one. [L0657, undated]
- A VPN that is only switched on in the phone's VPN app does not work for organic TikTok content. The VPN must run on the router so the device's connection is consistently routed. [L0577, 2023]
- Procedure: TikTok VPN setup to avoid shadowban (router method) [L0577, 2023]
  1. Test whether the VPN works on the router before the phone is changed. The VPN must be connected to the router, not only in the VPN app on the phone. [L0577, 2023]
  2. Set up the VPN on the router. NordVPN was used. A router setup guide is available from the provider. [L0577, 2023]
  3. Choose a rotating VPN so the phone stays permanently connected and the algorithm consistently sees the chosen country. [L0577, 2023]
  4. Once the router works, factory reset the phone, wipe it completely, and then set it up again. [L0577, 2023]
  5. When backing up the phone, do not restore Instagram and TikTok data. Log back in manually instead. [L0577, 2023]
  6. Check whether the account still struggles. Only create a new account if the old account still has problems. [L0577, 2023]
  7. Connect the phone to Wi-Fi via the router after the reset. [L0577, 2023]

### Posting from the Target Country

- TikTok organic accounts are not run through a VPN. Instead, content is posted in the target country so that TikTok first tests it there and then moves it to the US content pool if it performs well. [L1440, 2021+]
- Store and account setup in the creator's own country is recommended, since VPN use for other regions can be frustrating. Start the first account locally, then expand to other regions once you have results. [L1384, 2022]
- Using a VPN to upload videos from a different region can cause shadow bans, because the platform detects the mismatch in IP location. Upload from the country where the account is intended to operate. Do not rely on a VPN to reach other regions. [L1077, undated]

### Reaching US Viewers from Abroad

- To reach US viewers, upload at US times, use US hashtags, use US trending sounds, and stay consistent. [L0657, undated]
- Expect US reach only after videos get roughly 100,000 to 200,000 views, and preferably over 1 million views. [L0657, undated]
- To reach a US audience from outside the US, post English content with hashtags and music that are popular in the US instead of changing IP addresses. Reach then depends on the content's relevance and viewership. Videos from the seller's own location may still reach the UK first before spreading. [L1428, undated]
- Do not use a VPN to make TikTok treat you as a US user. A VPN-driven spike was followed by a shadow ban that wiped out views. Posting from outside the US without a VPN still reached viewers, since US viewers make up a large share of TikTok traffic. [L0820, undated]

### Country Availability

- At the time of the lesson (2023), TikTok was accessible in Pakistan, so TikTok ads could be run from there, and a VPN was an option. TikTok was banned in India. Check the current status in each country before running ads. [L1431, 2020+]
- TikTok is blocked in India, so organic accounts cannot be opened from there. Instead, content creators from the UK and US were hired to run the accounts. [L1440, 2021+]

## Warming Up and Launching a New Account

### Choosing Between a New Account and an Existing One

- A new account can reach more viewers than an older account with a similar follower count, even when the video is only average. [L1381, 2022]
- Multiple accounts are mainly useful for sellers who need reach quickly. Established creators usually do not need them. [L1381, 2022]
- If an established account stays flat for a long period, testing the same product on a fresh account is a reasonable next step. [L1444, 2022]
- An older account with weak reach for months can be outperformed by a new account that gets views right away. [L1444, 2022]

### Reusing an Existing Account for a Different Product

Use this procedure to make a reused account read as a fresh start:

1. Change the account name. [L1383, 2022]
2. Set all old videos to private or delete them. [L1383, 2022]
3. Post the new product consistently, without skipping days. [L1383, 2022]
4. Expect slow views for the first one to two weeks while the algorithm adjusts to the new content. [L1383, 2022]

### Warm-Up Waiting Period

**Standard warm-up procedure**

1. Create a TikTok account or warm an existing one. [L0309, 2024+]
2. Wait 6-12 hours before uploading the first video, or up to a week. [L0309, 2024+]
3. During the wait, follow accounts in your niche, follow competitors, follow influencers, and engage with your target audience's content. [L0309, 2024+]
4. Do not upload videos or take any spammy actions during this period. [L0309, 2024+]
5. After the warm-up, upload the first video. The account will receive views faster than a brand-new account with zero history. [L0309, 2024+]

**New-email test procedure**

Use this when you are confident a product is viable but no account is gaining traction:

1. Create a new TikTok account with a new email address. [L0309, 2024+]
2. Wait 12-24 hours. [L0309, 2024+]
3. During the wait, do not upload videos. Instead, follow accounts related to your product category and engage with their content. [L0309, 2024+]
4. After 12-24 hours, upload your first video. [L0309, 2024+]
5. Repeat this process 5-7 times if the product still struggles to gain traction. [L0309, 2024+]

**Wait durations by source**

| Variant | Wait before first upload | Applies to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard warm-up | 6-12 hours, or up to one week | General warm-up of a new or existing account | [L0309, 2024+] |
| New-email test | 12-24 hours | Repeated tests of a product on fresh accounts | [L0309, 2024+] |

- A warmed account that has engaged with other accounts before its first post receives the new account advantage earlier and performs better than an account posted to immediately. [L0309, 2024+]
- The advantage applies as long as no videos have been uploaded. [L0309, 2024+]

### New-Account Advantage and Its Timing

- A brand-new account can receive an early distribution boost during its first days or weeks. A sudden spike of views on a fresh account does not prove that the product converts. [L1385, undated]
- The timing starts from the first video upload, not from when the account was created. Plan the first upload to use the early window. [L1384, 2022]
- Duration: the sources differ. One source says the advantage typically lasts about one week. [L1376, undated] Another says the window lasts about one to two weeks. [L1374, undated] Plan for the shorter figure as the guaranteed period and treat two weeks as the upper bound.
- Like-to-view ratios: new accounts can get away with weaker ratios, around 1:20 to 1:30. [L1384, 2022]
- Stronger push: new accounts can be pushed with weaker engagement ratios, such as 1:40 to 1:50, and sometimes even 1:100, depending on watch time. Proven content on a new account is therefore treated as stronger than it is. [L1429, 2022]
- Local-first distribution: new accounts are first distributed to their local area. Once a video passes about 1 million views, it is distributed across many countries and local targeting no longer applies. [L1429, 2022]

**Case: fast viral result on a second account**

- A product performed well on a first account for one week, so a second account was built on that proven product. [L1374, undated]
- Paid Coins ran on the new account from its first day. By day three, it had gone viral with millions of views. [L1374, undated]
- The team uses this boost only in the first three days of a new account, since the advantage window lasts about one to two weeks. [L1374, undated]

### Preparing Content Before the First Upload

- Keep a content calendar with at least 9 finished videos ready before launching a new account. Nine videos cover three days when three uploads are made per day. [L0795, undated]
- Use the buffer to react to trends or strong comments without missing the minimum posting schedule. [L0795, undated]
- A new account should upload at least three videos per day during its first one to two weeks. [L0795, undated]
- Film more videos than you post so a buffer is always ready. For example, filming about nine videos per day produces three publishable videos in advance, which keeps daily posting consistent. [L1444, 2022]

### Launch Boost with Coins

**Coins procedure for a new account (first three days)**

1. On day one, put about $10 in coins on the best video of the day. [L1429, 2022]
2. Wait until midday and compare views across the day's videos. [L1429, 2022]
3. Put the next $10 in coins on the video performing best at that point. This may be a day-one video rather than a newer one. [L1429, 2022]
4. Repeat for three days, for about $30 total. [L1429, 2022]
5. If a video goes viral on day one or two, ease off the coins. The boost has broken the barrier of a few thousand views and helped TikTok find the audience. [L1429, 2022]

|---|---|---|---|
| First 24 hours (minimum) | 500-1,000 views for a posted video | Floor for a new account with a posted video; below it, investigate | [L0309, 2024+] |
| Initial test per video | About 500 viewers | Standard distribution test, applies to any reasonably presented video | [L0309, 2024+] |

- By day 3-4, this trajectory shows whether the product has viral potential or should be abandoned. [L0309, 2024+]
- If views fall below these benchmarks, the cause is account problems (shadow-banning, insufficient warm-up) or product-market misalignment, not content quality. [L0309, 2024+]

### First 24-48 Hour Checks

**Checks in order**

1. Do not judge a video at zero views too early. It can sit at zero for up to about 24 hours while the platform identifies the audience. Most views arrive within a few hours. [L0501, 2022]
2. Confirm the video is live. [L0501, 2022]
3. Compare the first 24 hours against the minimum. If a new account with a posted video gets fewer than 500 views, investigate. [L0309, 2024+]
4. Investigate for shadow-banning, spammy activity, poor content, or product-market misalignment. [L0309, 2024+]
5. If none of these is found, create a new account and retry. [L0309, 2024+]
6. If the first video gets under 100-500 views, especially after several uploads, the account may be shadow-banned or the content is weak. [L0501, 2022]
7. Decide on the product at day 3-4, based on the growth trajectory. [L0309, 2024+]
8. Confirm actual sales before judging the product. [L1385, undated]

**Thresholds and when each applies**

| Threshold | What it indicates | When it applies | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fewer than 500 views in first 24 hours | Investigate account and content | New account with a posted video | [L0309, 2024+] |
| Fewer than 500 views | Platform issue (shadow-ban, spam, warm-up, restrictions), not content | Initial distribution test | [L0309, 2024+] |
| Under 100-500 views on first video | Shadow-ban or weak content | After several uploads | [L0501, 2022] |

- Poor content still clears the 500-viewer distribution threshold. A low count therefore points to the platform or account, not to content quality. [L0309, 2024+]

### Checking Whether a Video Is Live

**Procedure**

1. After uploading, tap Copy link. [L0501, 2022]
2. If a banner at the top says the video is under review, it is not yet published. Review can take a few minutes to a couple of hours. [L0501, 2022]
3. If you cannot copy the link, the video has not been fully reviewed. [L0501, 2022]
4. As a second method, have someone else look up the video on their phone. [L0501, 2022]

- A publishing delay of about 1.5-2 hours before the video appears publicly is normal. [L0501, 2022]
