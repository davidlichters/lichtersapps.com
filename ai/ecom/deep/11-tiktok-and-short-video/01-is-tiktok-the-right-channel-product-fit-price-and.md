## Is TikTok the Right Channel? Product Fit, Price and Audience

### Decide Whether TikTok Fits Your Store

- Set up the TikTok account as its own step in the store checklist, and test TikTok ads only after the store is ready. [L1288, undated]
- Run a niche store that sells one product. General stores do not perform on TikTok ads, and niche stores selling one product perform best. [L1376, undated]
- Do not expect organic social growth to produce sales. One store grew to about 1,500 to 1,600 Instagram followers and over 2,000 TikTok followers within 30 days with zero sales, while paid advertising drives sales. [L0549, undated]
- Pay for ads only after a product has sales and profit to reinvest. [L1380, 2022]
- In the early stage with small budgets, do not split spend onto TikTok ads. Put the budget into Google and Meta, which deliver most of the results for this model. [L0667, 2025]
- TikTok ads had not been tested for high-ticket drop-shipping in the lesson's experience, so the team focused its time on Google and Facebook. [L0468, 2020]
- Short-form video on Instagram Reels and TikTok was not used for e-commerce marketing at that time, so budget went into Google and Facebook first.  [L0671, 2021]

### Account Setup and Ban Risk

- TikTok ad accounts are generally easier to open and carry less account-ban risk than Meta accounts. [L0637, 2023+]
- TikTok has a low barrier to account creation, fewer ban issues than Meta, and better support, with human replies to tickets. [L0296, 2021+]
- Facebook has more targeting power than TikTok, so creative and audience drive results differently on each platform. [L0637, 2023+]

### Audience and Targeting

- Most TikTok traffic comes from users aged about 21 to 30, so products should appeal to young buyers. [L0708, undated]
- Open targeting works best on TikTok. Narrow targeting of buyers aged 30 to 40 plus is less effective, because the algorithm does not target them well. [L0708, undated]
- TikTok uses broad audiences where the creative is the main variable. Facebook can find buyers through better targeting once the account is safe. [L0637, 2023+]
- Products for an older audience are better tested on Facebook, while products for a younger audience should be researched directly on TikTok. [L1184, 2023+]
- Widen product ideas beyond one audience. A bottle opener aimed only at beer drinkers over 21 is narrow, while showing soda or other beverage bottles can reach more age groups. [L0656, undated]

### Product Types That Tend to Sell

- Unique, attractive or shiny-object products tend to sell on TikTok. [L0708, undated]
- Life-hack tools sell well, as do niches such as jewelry, beauty and women-focused products. [L0708, undated]
- Broader products tend to work better on TikTok. Competition level on the platform matters for a specific product. [L0950, 2023]
- A product that already won on Facebook can be tested on TikTok, where the audience has grown a lot since the earlier winning period. A product that sold well in an earlier year may still have an unreached TikTok audience that has never seen it. [L0819, 2020+]
- A proven product can be tested on TikTok, which was a new platform in the case, because a new platform brings a new audience. Test the same product's hook and creative there before dropping it. [L0871, 2021+]
- A product that struggles on Facebook may do well on TikTok, which can pick up the slack. [L0950, 2023]

### Product Types That Tend to Fail

**High-ticket products**

- For high-ticket products above about $200, return on ad spend is usually too low to justify the budget. [L0854, 2022]
- TikTok ads are not recommended for cold traffic to a website, since they reach people who may not buy high-ticket products. [L1285, 2024]
- The host did not put organic effort into TikTok for higher-ticket products. [L0804, 2023]

**Print-on-demand products**

- For print-on-demand products, TikTok ads were reported to bring little success compared with other channels. [L1214, 2023+]
- Organic TikTok reach is hard to achieve for print-on-demand products, and it has been slowing down in general. Plan to spend on paid ads rather than rely only on organic short video. [L1438, 2023+]

**Older-audience and practical comfort products**

- Practical comfort products that sell well as Facebook ads often underperform as TikTok ads. Test such products on Meta first before adding TikTok to the plan. [L1185, 2023]
- Products that target an older audience are better tested on Facebook. [L1184, 2023+]

**Ingestible and finished goods**

- Some brands found that TikTok ads, including Spark Ads, and Facebook ads did not become profitable for certain products, such as beverages and some finished goods. For those products, creator-led content was the way to keep sales going. [L1379, 2020+]
- Buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website. (See the case below.) [L1429, 2022]

### Sequencing for Beginners

- Two units disagree on where beginners should start:
  - TikTok is beginner friendly, with fewer ban issues than Meta, better support, and a low barrier to account creation. [L0296, 2021+]
  - Beginners should master Facebook ads first before running TikTok. TikTok ads die out fast, so the account needs a constant stream of new creatives, which is hard to produce well. [L1107, 2023]
- The stated constraint in the second view decides which applies: if the team cannot produce a steady stream of new creatives, start with Facebook. [L1107, 2023]
- Creative lifespan on TikTok is about one week, so scaling depends on a steady process for producing new creatives. [L0637, 2023+]
- Run Facebook and TikTok in parallel for the same product to see which platform performs better, because results differ by product. [L0690, 2022+]
- A product that works on TikTok may not work on Facebook, and vice versa, because the audiences are different. [L0950, 2023]
- In 2023, TikTok ads were a newer channel with less saturation. Cost per click, click-through rate and conversions were cheaper than on many other paid channels, but that advantage erodes as more advertisers join. (2023 conditions) [L1251, 2024+]
- Retargeting on TikTok can work but adds an extra platform to manage. Meta and Google remarketing usually earn more profit. [L1285, 2024]

### Testing Winners Across Platforms

Procedure: use TikTok to find winning creatives before paying for them on Facebook.

1. Test several videos as TikTok organic posts. [L0774, 2023]
2. Find the video that goes viral. [L0774, 2023]
3. Use that video in Facebook ads, cut to the Facebook formats if needed. [L0774, 2023]

Running one video on Facebook and posting one on TikTok without testing is not worth it. [L0774, 2023]

- Trending products from TikTok can be tested on Facebook with a different creative. The product stays the same, and only the ad creative changes. [L1431, 2020+]
- Test the product on both channels, because TikTok can open a separate marketing stream even when a product works on only one platform. [L0950, 2023]
- For a product that already works on TikTok, TikTok's own ad platform may perform better than Meta ads for the same product. [L1380, 2022]

### Case: Viral Video, Few Sales

- Product: a ceramic ice cube.
- Hook: a video asked whether the cube makes drinks taste like metal, then showed the ceramic cube so viewers stayed to find out what it was. [L1429, 2022]
- Result: about 15 million views and only about 25 sales. [L1429, 2022]
- Cause of low sales: buyers hesitate over products that go into food or drinks, even with a decent website. [L1429, 2022]
- Comment section: comments were left unfiltered, so complaints dominated it. [L1429, 2022]
- Competitor: a competitor who copied the video frame by frame also went viral, but the unfiltered negative comments damaged that account. [L1429, 2022]

## Account Setup for Organic Product Accounts

### Email and Sign-Up

- Register the account with an email address, not a phone number, Apple ID, or Apple Pay login, because each of those can only be linked to one account. [L1380, 2022]
- Use a dedicated email so that store and account matters stay separate. [L1380, 2022]
- Use a Gmail address that matches the email of the Shopify store, not a personal email, so the business accounts stay organized. [L1380, 2022]
- For an organic account, an ordinary email address is sufficient, and a US phone number is not required. [L1428, undated]
- Avoid numbered variants of an address (the same name with added numbers), suspicious phone numbers, and changing IP addresses, because these can lead to shadow banning. Focus on legitimate content instead. [L1428, undated]

### Username

- Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols, since symbols look unprofessional. Match it to the brand or store name. [L1380, 2022]
- Check username availability before creating the Shopify domain. [L1380, 2022]
- If the username is taken, add.com or.co to the end, or add shop before or after the brand name, since these variants are often still free. [L1380, 2022]
- A TikTok username can be changed after 30 days, so a poor choice at setup is not permanent. [L1380, 2022]

### Personal or Business Account

- Keep the account as a Personal account and do not switch to a Business account. [L1380, 2022]
- Business accounts lose access to trending and cooperative sounds, get less reach, and cannot post a link without 1,000 followers. [L1380, 2022]
- Business accounts run into copyright blocks when posting music, because the trending sound library is restricted for them. This is a main reason to stay on a personal account for product videos. [L1380, 2022]

### Profile Picture, Display Name, and Bio

- Set the profile picture to a high-quality product image, a product logo, or the brand name. [L1380, 2022]
- Set the display name (not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com. [L1380, 2022]
- Write a bio of 2 to 3 lines with emojis. [L1380, 2022]
- Place an emoji at both ends of each bio line. This format was used on TikTok product accounts and read well in the profile. Keep each line short so the slogan, offer, and link pointer stay readable on a phone screen. [L1380, 2022]
- Do not put a website link in the bio. The bio is not a link location. [L1380, 2022]

### Links and the 1,000-Follower Threshold

| Link type | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Website link in the profile link tab | About 1,000 followers. The link option appears only after this threshold, so a TikTok Shop is a practical starting point until then. | [L1215, 2023+; L1380, 2022] |
| External website link before 1,000 followers | May be restricted. One seller avoided it and used Instagram instead, noting that the risk is not fully known. | [L1381, 2022] |
| Instagram link (social link) | No minimum follower requirement applied at the time of recording. | [L1381, 2022; L1380, 2022] |
| Any link on a Business account | Cannot be posted without 1,000 followers. | [L1380, 2022] |

- A store can be built later, once the link function is available. [L1215, 2023+]
- To add links, tap Edit profile. The fields for a website link and other links (such as Instagram and YouTube) are located there. [L1380, 2022]
- Logging into Instagram from TikTok syncs the Instagram link, which then shows next to the Follow button. [L1380, 2022]

**Adding an Instagram link:**

1. Open Edit profile on the account. [L1381, 2022]
2. Scroll down to Social. [L1381, 2022]
3. Choose Instagram. [L1381, 2022]
4. Link the brand Instagram account, which should look similar to the TikTok account. [L1381, 2022]
5. An Instagram icon appears on the profile and opens the Instagram page with its products and shop-now button. [L1381, 2022]

### Comment Filters

**Turning on keyword filtering:**

1. Tap Settings at the top right of the profile tab. [L1380, 2022]
2. Open Comments. [L1380, 2022]
3. Switch on Filter keywords. [L1380, 2022]
4. Keep the Filter all comments option off. It forces manual approval of nearly every comment and lowers engagement. [L1380, 2022]

**Filter keywords:**

- Add these keywords to hide comments that reveal dropshipping or hurt trust: dropshipping, amazon, aliexpress, CJ dropshipping, cheaper, china, scam, shopify, dropshipper, fake, and don't buy. [L1380, 2022]
- Expand the list for each product, using negative words that appear in comments on that product. [L1380, 2022]

### Privacy and Suggestion Settings

- Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others. This stops people who receive a link or interact with you from seeing which account you run. [L1380, 2022]
- New accounts come with these options enabled by default, so check them at setup. [L1380, 2022]

### Upload Quality Settings

**Enabling high-quality uploads:**

1. On the post screen, tap More options (the last option in the bottom row). [L1380, 2022]
2. Turn on Allow high quality uploads. [L1380, 2022]
3. Do this before posting or drafting. [L1380, 2022]

- Record in 4K at 60 fps. If 4K or storage is not available, 1080p is sufficient. [L1380, 2022]

### Mobile versus Desktop

- Do product research and account setup on the TikTok mobile app, not desktop. [L1380, 2022]
- Desktop has far fewer filters and editing options. It is mainly useful for buying coins or looking at some analytics. [L1380, 2022]

**Desktop upload procedure:**

1. Log in to TikTok in a desktop browser with the account. [L1186, 2022]
2. Upload the video from the computer. [L1186, 2022]
3. Choose the cover, set public or private, and run the copyright check. [L1186, 2022]
4. Add the music inside the editing tool before uploading, because the desktop upload does not allow adding music. [L1186, 2022]
5. Add the hashtags in the caption. [L1186, 2022]

### Setup Checklist for a New Product TikTok Account

Complete these steps in this order:

1. Sign up with an email address, a Gmail that matches the Shopify store email, not a phone number or Apple ID login. [L1380, 2022]
2. Choose a short username with plain words and no symbols that matches the brand. If it is taken, try adding.com or.co, or shop before or after the brand name. [L1380, 2022]
3. Keep the account on Personal, not Business. [L1380, 2022]
4. Set the profile picture to a high-quality product image, product logo, or brand name. [L1380, 2022]
5. Set the display name (not the username) to the store domain, such as brand.com. [L1380, 2022]
6. Write a 2 to 3 line bio with emojis. [L1380, 2022]
7. Turn off every option under Suggest your account to others. [L1380, 2022]
8. Turn on Filter keywords under Comments in Settings and add the blocked keyword list. [L1380, 2022]
9. Turn on Allow high quality uploads under More options on the post screen. [L1380, 2022]
10. Draft at least 3 to 5 days of videos before posting. [L1380, 2022]
11. Add a website link only after 1,000 followers, under Edit profile. [L1380, 2022; L1215, 2023+]
