## Filming, Editing and Sound

### Filming Practice and Locations

- Leave room around the subject when framing, because footage framed too tightly limits zoom, pan and transition options. [L0341, 2023]
- Film in varied real locations, such as a car, outdoors or while out and about. These look more engaging than the same indoor setting, such as a dining table or living room. Avoid glare from strong light. [L0656, undated]
- Use sound-led footage where it fits. ASMR content, such as the sound of opening a soda bottle, can make a bottle opener product more engaging on video. [L0656, undated]

### Phone-First Workflow

- Draft and edit on the phone. Desktop cannot reliably add text, sounds or some editing features, so keep the phone as the main device for all TikTok work. [L1380, 2022]
- Do not edit in the TikTok studio or in-app editor. The original file is lost and cannot be reused in another edit. Use TikTok only for sounds, hashtags, text and text-to-speech. [L1380, 2022]
- Follow this production sequence:
  1. Record the video on the phone in 4K at 60 fps (1080p is acceptable). [L1380, 2022]
  2. Edit the video in an external app such as Splice, keeping the original file. [L1380, 2022]
  3. Export the finished video. [L1380, 2022]
  4. Upload it to TikTok. [L1380, 2022]
  5. In TikTok, add on-screen text, text-to-speech, music, filters and hashtags. [L1380, 2022]
  6. Save as a draft or post, and keep several drafts queued for the upcoming days. [L1380, 2022]
- To add music, tap upload, pick the video, then tap the music icon at the top center of the screen and search for a sound. Trending and copyright-cleared sounds are only available on a personal account. [L1380, 2022]
- Many TikTok sellers execute products poorly. Cleaner editing, better hooks and removal of dull moments can win views over competitors, which makes this an opportunity for a clean, careful creator. [L1380, 2022]

#### Premiere Pro

- For 9:16 video, keep a separate vertical sequence or workspace rather than cutting vertical clips in a landscape sequence. The sequence takes its frame ratio from the first clip, so set the ratio before adding footage. [L0343, 2023]
- Press C to switch to the Razor tool and cut a clip at the point you click. This is a fast way to remove dead time from raw footage. [L0343, 2023]

#### CapCut

- Combine the recorded footage with the A-roll audio and create the final short video from the phone. [L1403, undated]

#### Splice

- Use Splice to edit videos before upload. [L1383, 2022]
- Use Splice to add custom sound effects after the video edit is finished, layering recorded sounds over the cut. [L1384, 2022]

### Keyframes and Transitions

- A keyframe stores a value such as scale or position at one point in time, and the editor generates the change between two keyframes. Movement only appears when at least two keyframes with different values are set. [L0343, 2023]
- Make zooms and moves long enough to show the change in speed and blur before judging the result. Very short transitions hide easing and motion blur, so the effect looks weak or goes unnoticed. [L0343, 2023]
- In CapCut, set the transition curve between keyframes to a preset ease-in, ease-out or bounce option, or shape it in a graph. Start with the presets, because a hand-shaped bell-style ease curve is harder to get right. [L0343, 2023]
- Use transitions and trend formats that are current, such as a soft music intro that cuts into a fast track with a transition. Spend time inside the platform each day to see what is trending. [L1077, undated]

### Vertical Framing and Reframing

- A TikTok-style vertical ad uses a frame of 1080 x 1920 pixels, a 9:16 aspect ratio. [L1427, 2023+]
- After switching to a vertical frame, footage downloaded from TikTok can look smaller in the frame because TikTok has compressed it. Scale the clip back up to fill the frame. [L1427, 2023+]
- In CapCut's main menu, the Ratio option sets the project frame, for example 1:1 for a square Instagram-style ad. After changing the ratio, resize the footage so it fills the new timeline frame. [L1427, 2023+]
- Reframe horizontal footage for a vertical or square ad:
  1. Import the horizontal clip and place it on the timeline. [L1427, 2023+]
  2. Scale the clip up until the subject fills the vertical or square frame. [L1427, 2023+]
  3. Wait until the subject is clearly visible, then add a keyframe on position. [L1427, 2023+]
  4. Move the position keyframe so the frame follows the subject to the next keyframe. [L1427, 2023+]

### TikTok-Style Text

- In Premiere Pro, set the text color to black, add a white background box, add padding around the text and round the corners of the box. [L1427, 2023+]
- Add TikTok-style text in CapCut:
  1. Tap Text and add your text. [L1427, 2023+]
  2. Open Style and tap Canvas to create an instant background. [L1427, 2023+]
  3. Set the background to white and the text to black. [L1427, 2023+]
  4. Make the text bold. [L1427, 2023+]
  5. Pinch the text to resize it, and adjust the background height and width. [L1427, 2023+]
  6. Animate the text, for example with a bounce-in, if needed. [L1427, 2023+]

### Sound Design and ASMR

- Ambient ASMR sound, aesthetic backgrounds and colorful visuals keep viewers engaged and help videos become memorable. Satisfying sounds, such as unwrapping or peeling, were cited as especially effective. [L1384, 2022]
- Recording your own sound effects, such as a stretching or pulling sound for a product, can beat stock audio. Original satisfying sounds were reported to perform better than generic sound effects. [L1384, 2022]
- Record your own satisfying sounds, for example a product being stretched or pulled, and place them on the edit to give the video original audio. [L1384, 2022]
- Follow this sound production procedure for product videos:
  1. Record the product's natural sound with the microphone as close as possible while filming. [L0764, 2022]
  2. If the natural sound does not come out as wanted, record it again separately, for example on a voice memo. [L0764, 2022]
  3. Recreate the sound after the video is done, and import it into Splice. [L0764, 2022]
  4. Align the recording to the exact clip and frame where the sound happens. [L0764, 2022]
  5. Balance the levels: keep the product sound audible over the background music. For ASMR-focused content, lower the music and raise the original audio, or the other way around depending on the goal. [L0764, 2022]
- Splice is used to import a separately recorded sound and align it to the matching frame of the video clip, so a re-created product sound fits the action. [L0764, 2022]
- Video has two sound layers to balance: the background music or trending audio, and the product's own sound. In ASMR-style videos, the product sound should stay clearly audible without being drowned out by music. [L0764, 2022]

### Trending Sounds

- Trending sounds can drive large organic reach because videos using a rising sound are shown to people searching that sound. Find trending sounds early by opening a trending song and reviewing its top posts, which can show product videos already using it. [L0655, undated]
- Hop on a new trending sound quickly. A sound and its hashtag can grow from thousands of users to hundreds of thousands or millions within a short time, and early adoption gives the best chance of being shown first in search. [L0655, undated]
- An early video can rank near the top of the sound's page. As more users adopt the sound, that video is recommended first. The owner reported one video reaching 20 to 30 million views this way. [L1077, undated]
- Hop onto a trending sound or hashtag early, while it is still growing. [L1077, undated]
- Use trending sounds from the For You page rather than the viral tab, since sounds that are already viral are often used by many accounts. Use a sound from the For You page only if it fits the video and the product. [L0372, 2022]
- Sounds taken from the For You page are often better than trending chart sounds, which saturate quickly. Choose sounds that are still in use but not yet overused. [L1383, 2022]
- Adopt a trending sound early, before many product accounts use it, because a format that is already common among sellers gets less reach. Only use the sound if its meaning fits the product. [L0657, undated]
- Trending songs tend to rise quickly and fade within days. If you plan to use a trending sound, post while it is still climbing, not after it has peaked. [L0706, 2020+]
- Trending audio helps videos get found. Using a sound that is already trending places your video among the first ones shown for that audio. Dialogue or theme music from a trending series can be used with the lines shown as on-screen text. [L0764, 2022]
- Organic TikTok growth depends on riding sounds before they peak. Jump onto a trending sound early so viewers who click the sound also watch the other videos in that trend. [L0819, 2020+]
- Cutting clips to the beat of the audio is a meaningful editing technique on TikTok, since sound is on by default. This differs from Facebook, where most viewers watch muted. [L0819, 2020+]
- Trending sounds are a key factor in going viral. Check which sounds are currently trending and use them in your own videos, provided the sound matches the video and is used while it is still popular. [L0821, undated]
- Choose music whose beats or pauses line up with your transitions, and that matches the product and the timing of the video. A trending sound that has no relation to the product is unlikely to help. [L0372, 2022]

### Music Rules and Copyright

- When a creator video runs as a TikTok paid ad, use only audio from TikTok's commercial soundtrack library. Regular artist music in paid ads can lead to copyright problems. [L0784, 2025-26]
- For a video that will run as an ad, download royalty-free sounds from a free library, add them in Splice, then upload. [L1383, 2022]
- If a trending sound is copyrighted, find a bootleg or re-uploaded version that sounds nearly identical and use that instead. Sounds that complement the product also help the video. [L1383, 2022]
- Conflict: [L1383, 2022] suggests bootleg substitutes for copyrighted trending sounds, but [L0784, 2025-26] requires the commercial library for paid ads. For paid ads, follow [L0784, 2025-26]. The source only describes the bootleg substitute for organic use. [L0784, 2025-26; L1383, 2022]
- Trending and copyright-cleared sounds are only available on a personal account. [L1380, 2022]

### Text-to-Speech

- Hook videos can be voiced with TikTok's built-in text-to-speech using the "Jesse" voice, which keeps the hook from sounding flat. [L1383, 2022]
- Use the same voice across hook variations so the format stays consistent for testing. [L1383, 2022]
- Add text-to-speech inside TikTok after uploading the exported video. [L1380, 2022]

## Replicating, Remixing and Refreshing Viral Content

### Principles of Replicating and Remixing

- Start from what already works. Copy the winning angle and format first, and change angles only after the product shows traction and converts well. [L1380, 2022]
- Copying a successful competitor's video closely, even frame by frame, can still produce a viral result, which suggests that video structure drives performance more than timing alone. [L1429, 2022]
- A viral video with weak sharpness and audio can still succeed on its concept. You can beat it by keeping the concept and improving image sharpness and sound quality. [L0657, undated]
- Reposting other creators' videos is risky because TikTok increasingly detects reused content, and the account can suffer. Recreate the concept with your own footage instead. [L0657, undated]
- Study the top videos of a successful account closely, including frame changes, splits, sounds, transitions, recording style and editing pace. Then recreate the structure with your own product rather than copying the content. [L0657, undated]
- Use competitor accounts in the same niche as inspiration. Borrow the theme or structure, then improve it or change it entirely. [L0764, 2022]
- Study a proven viral video from another account and recreate its structure in your own way, using the same type of music, hook and context. Copying the core format while filming your own version is the approach that produced the first sales. [L0821, undated]
- To learn what works on TikTok, spend regular time on the platform looking at which videos go viral and which sounds they use. Then replicate those sounds and formats in your own videos. [L0821, undated]

### Remixing a Winning Ad or Viral Video

#### Remixing a Winning Ad

1. Start from the winning ad's angle and script. [L0341, 2023]
2. Open with a big benefit shot, such as a phone that sticks anywhere. [L0341, 2023]
3. Find footage that shows each benefit and cut it into short clips. [L0341, 2023]
4. Adjust clip audio and volume as needed. [L0341, 2023]
5. Add text in a TikTok-style layout using Open Sans, scale it down to fit, and set the text background to white at 100% opacity. [L0341, 2023]

- One clip is rarely enough to modify a winning video ad well, so gather a full set of footage before cutting. This prevents a copy that looks exactly like the original. [L0341, 2023]
- On TikTok, change the clip roughly every 2-3 seconds when remixing competitor videos, so the video is not flagged as a duplicate. Mixing hooks from several winning videos into one new edit is a common method. [L0637, 2023+]

#### Replicating a Viral TikTok Video

- Keep the same filming style and footage approach, but make slight variations of the spoken text or on-screen words, and cut any weak moments (for example, shorten a 10-second video to 8 seconds). [L0501, 2022]
- Judge each variation against the original. If it performs worse, work out whether the words, the angle or the length caused it. [L0501, 2022]
- Do not repost the same video. Make a new variation of the same style (same filming approach, different wording or cut), and reuse trending sounds that worked on your best video. [L0501, 2022]

#### Repurposing Viral Footage into a Facebook Ad

1. Write a script with ChatGPT based on the footage and the product's problem and benefits. [L0524, 2023]
2. Record a voiceover, or generate one with ElevenLabs. [L0524, 2023]
3. Cut the footage to match the voiceover and add on-screen text. [L0524, 2023]
4. Test the cut as a Facebook ad against a version with a fresh opening. [L0524, 2023]

#### Copying a Viral Product's Video Approach

1. Identify the viral video and its trending sound while the trend is still active. [L0655, undated]
2. Copy the video's format, structure, and sound closely rather than inventing a new angle. [L0655, undated]
3. Ensure your product link is working before publishing. [L0655, undated]
4. Add a clear call to action and urgency to the video text and description. [L0655, undated]
5. Post while the sound is still trending, ideally within days of the viral peak. [L0655, undated]

#### Recreating Your Product Cleanly

- Learn the structure of successful competitor videos, then rebuild it with your own footage, so the content is yours even when the format is copied. [L0657, undated]
- Remove watermarks and create original footage. Reused clips with watermarks can signal low quality and lead to lower distribution. [L1384, 2022]

- Keep testing new angles constantly, because the same content gets harder to keep relevant over time. [L0501, 2022]

### Trends, Sounds and Hashtags

- Trend-based videos are easy to produce and are worth doing even if a trend does not last, as long as the product fits naturally into the trend format. An example is listing benefits over a trending sound on a product-style video. [L0655, undated]
- Trends can be used to reach new audiences from the For You Page. Include your product in a current video trend, filter, or trending sound instead of relying only on your own content format. [L1384, 2022]
- A trend can be used without showing your face. Put the product into a current video trend, a filter or a trending sound. Keep the product presentation within normal limits and avoid anything too revealing. [L1384, 2022]

#### Organic Replication of a Viral Video

1. Use the same trending sounds, the same hashtags, and a very similar text-to-speech voiceover as the viral video. [L1429, 2022]
2. Upload two to three more similar videos after the viral one. [L1429, 2022]
3. Leave at least 2 hours between uploads. [L1429, 2022]
4. Vary posting times, including unusual hours such as 2 am, so different time zones and regions see the content. [L1429, 2022]

### Reusing Your Own Old Videos (Repurposing)

#### Eligibility Rules

- Only repurpose content on an account that has been posting for several weeks or months and already has a large library of videos. Do not repurpose on a new account. [L0795, undated]
- Only consider re-uploading a video that is at least about one month old, and only when it previously performed well. [L0795, undated]
- Use repurposing sparingly. Repeating it too often stops it from working. [L0795, undated]

#### Procedure

To repurpose an existing video:

1. Take a video that already performed. [L0820, undated]
2. Change the hook or the opening. [L0820, undated]
3. Slightly alter the video file itself. [L0820, undated]
4. Re-upload it so TikTok does not treat it as spam. [L0820, undated]

#### File Differences

- A re-uploaded video must differ in its file content, for example through a small trim of a second or a changed frame, even if it looks the same to the viewer. [L0795, undated]
- Re-uploading the identical file is risky, and a viewer who has already seen it will swipe past. [L0795, undated]
- Viewers who have already seen a video on a large account may swipe past it when it reappears, which can lower its performance. This is a reason to avoid identical re-uploads even when a restriction is not triggered. [L0795, undated]

#### Changing the Fingerprint

- To change a video's fingerprint before re-uploading it as an ad, speed it up by 1.01x to 1.05x in a video editor or speed tool. Combined with swapping the hook of one video onto the body of another, this creates variants that TikTok may treat as new videos, which can lower CPM. [L1377, 2022+]

### Metadata and Meta Refresh

- The aim of a meta refresh is to make TikTok treat a re-uploaded ad as new content rather than an old video, so it gets fresh reach. [L1436, undated]
- A metadata-changing method can be used to refresh a copied video so the platform treats it as a new upload. In one test a third-party website was used for this, and the effect lasted only about one day. [L1177, 2023]
- The metadata that can be changed for a refresh includes coding, dimensions, length, timing, and playback speed. Changing these helps the platform treat a reused video as a new ad. [L1382, undated]

#### Meta Refresh Procedure

1. Start with the video at its original 1080 x 1920 (9:16) dimension. [L1382, undated; L1436, undated]
2. Change the dimension to 1920 x 1080 (16:9) in an editor such as CapCut, Sony Vegas Pro or Premiere Pro. [L1382, undated; L1436, undated]
3. Export the video and re-import it into the editor. [L1382, undated; L1436, undated]
4. Change the dimension back to 1080 x 1920 (9:16). [L1382, undated; L1436, undated]
5. Change the coding and other metadata of the video, such as speed or timing. [L1382, undated; L1436, undated]
6. Export the final video and use it for the new ad. [L1436, undated]

#### Results Benchmark

- After using the meta refresh method on competitor videos, a CTR of 1.52 percent was reached on TikTok, which is very high. [L1382, undated]
- A CTR of about 0.7 to 0.8 percent is considered good on TikTok. The 1.52 percent is a single test result, and 0.7 to 0.8 percent is the general benchmark. [L1382, undated]

### Downloaders, Watermark Removal and Editing Tools

- Snap Tick (snaptick.app) and SnapTik are web tools that download TikTok videos without the TikTok watermark. The clean file can be posted on Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts without visible TikTok branding. [L0795, undated; L1186, 2022]
- Use the downloader to get a clean file before re-uploading, since the watermark suppresses reach on other platforms. [L1186, 2022]
- To reuse existing TikTok content, download the source video with SnapTik, then edit it into your own custom ad in CapCut, which is also available as a mobile app for editing on the phone. [L1183, undated]

### Video Replies

- For a video reply, pick a high-liked comment as the hook. The selected comment appears in the top left corner of the reply video, where it works as a strong hook. [L1429, 2022]
- Video replies that respond to a viral video with traction can compound reach, because each reply can ride the momentum of the earlier viral video. The top comments on a new reply then supply topics for the next replies in the same chain. [L1429, 2022]

### Case: Replication That Produced the Breakthrough

- A dropshipper started about 20 months before the interview, initially doubting that dropshipping could be profitable until a friend began making sales. [L0821, undated]
- He tested 6 to 7 products before finding a hit. [L0821, undated]
- For the hit, he noticed another account getting tens of millions of views within a day, bought one unit on Amazon that arrived the same day, and filmed many videos that copied that account's format, music and hook in his own execution. [L0821, undated]
- He posted 4 to 5 videos per day for two weeks, was about to stop just before the breakthrough, and then gained 1,000 followers in 15 days, then 10,000, and about 20,000 as one video reached roughly 10 to 15 million views and earned about $1,000 within 2 hours. [L0821, undated]
- The same product then kept getting about 4 million views per day for roughly a month. The overall path from the first dropshipping attempt to the win took about a year. [L0821, undated]
