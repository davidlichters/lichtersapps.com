## Posting Cadence, Timing and Hashtags

### Posting Cadence and Volume

- Cadence matters more than volume. One video per week posted consistently beats ten videos in one day or one burst per month followed by inactivity. Choose a cadence you can sustain. [L0915, 2025-26]
- Organic short-video accounts should post at least once a day, even when older videos still generate sales. Views keep growing from earlier uploads, but regular new posts keep reach and sales from declining. [L0387, 2022]
- Organic growth depends on consistent posting over time rather than on one video. Early results, such as about 1,200 views and 18 new followers after two days, are best treated as a test of how steady the output is. Plan a regular posting rhythm from the start. [L0888, 2023+]

- The sources give different numbers because they describe different goals. The 1-per-day floor keeps an established account active. The 2-5 range fits testing. The 5-6 range applies only while a video is viral. The 9 cap is an upper limit that should not be exceeded. [L0387, 2022; L0501, 2022; L0706, 2020+]
- The viral figures differ slightly: one source aims for 4-5 and allows 5-6 when a video is viral, so treat 4-5 as the target and 5-6 as the ceiling during a spike. [L0706, 2020+; L0501, 2022]
- The "about 3 per account" figure applies to organic testing across several accounts, and each account needs its own content. [L0561, 2023+]

### Bursts, Gaps and Spacing

- During active testing, post in bursts followed by gaps between posts. [L0820, undated]
- Upload in volume and in batches. One video in a batch can go viral while the others do not. One account in the slipper case uploaded about three to six times per day, and one of its many videos produced most of the views. [L0657, undated]
- Viral success depends on volume. Several uploads a day raise the chance that one video breaks through. The hosts emphasized that a single successful video can drive most of an account's results. [L0657, undated]
- While a video is viral, space uploads about two hours apart to avoid being flagged as spam. [L0706, 2020+]
- On an early account, space posts four to six hours apart. [L1077, undated]

### Consistency and Patience

- A viral video can take up to two weeks to appear after an account starts posting. An account that posts irregularly may take longer to break through, and consistent uploading shortens that wait. [L0656, undated]
- Going viral usually requires sustained posting, because reach often disappears for stretches and then returns. Do not conclude after a dry period that the account is shadow banned. Keep publishing consistently. [L0819, 2020+]

- Align posting with US waking and lunch hours, since the US audience is the reference for these peaks. [L0820, undated]
- Avoid posting near midnight. [L0795, undated]
- Sources differ on the early-morning slot. A seller's 3 a.m. ET uploads performed well because the audience was awake at that hour in another time zone. A personal account did well at 3 a.m. PT. The midnight warning and these early-morning examples do not conflict, because they describe different accounts and audiences. [L1381, 2022; L0820, undated; L0795, undated]
- In one account, a video posted around 6:30 a.m. ET got about 170 views, while videos posted around 12:00-13:00 ET got about 300-400 views. Compare view counts across your own upload times before settling on a schedule. [L1383, 2022]
- Always test upload times against the time zone of the target audience, and test more than three upload times on a new account. [L1381, 2022]
- Varying upload times diversifies who sees the content, because viewers in other regions may be awake at unusual hours. Posting late at night in one region may still need local engagement first. [L1429, 2022]

#### Procedure: Finding the Best Posting Time for a Product

1. Use different devices and accounts.
2. Post the same product content at different hours of the day.
3. Compare views and engagement by time slot to find the best window.

### Drafting and the Content Pipeline

- Draft at least 3 to 5 days of videos ahead of posting day instead of creating them on the day. A buffer of drafts makes consistent uploading possible. [L1380, 2022]
- Drafting regularly signals to TikTok that the creator is a consistent, valuable uploader, which tends to lead to more reach. Edit everything on the phone so the workflow stays quick. [L1380, 2022]
- For steady output while busy, use this routine:
  1. Produce three to nine videos per day in advance.
  2. Keep drafts a day ahead.
  3. Upload one per scheduled slot.

  [L0820, undated]
- The two figures describe different levels of the pipeline. The one-day-ahead draft rhythm is the daily working routine, and the 3-5 day buffer is the reserve kept on hand. [L0820, undated; L1380, 2022]

### Scheduling

- The TikTok desktop uploader lets you set a scheduled upload time, which separates posting from phone activity. It offers fewer editing options than the app, so add music and text before uploading from the computer. [L1186, 2022]
- TikTok Shop now lets creators and brands schedule shoppable videos ahead of time, so shoppable content can be planned and published at set times. [L1504, 2025-26]

### Hashtags

- Hashtags are not the main reason a video goes viral. If no specific hashtag comes to mind, broad tags such as fyp, viral or asmr can be used, but the video itself matters far more. [L0764, 2022]
- Use no more than six hashtags per post: about two niche, two broad and two trending. A very large set can send a video to unrelated audiences. [L1077, undated]
- For a new account with a target audience, use two niche hashtags, one of which names the target audience. [L1077, undated]
- Test with and without hashtags, because their effect varies. [L1077, undated]
- A typical set uses about five hashtags: the product hashtag, about two niche hashtags, one broad hashtag such as "satisfying", and one hashtag for the problem or complaint the buyer has. Every hashtag should be relevant, because irrelevant hashtags narrow the audience to the wrong group. [L1381, 2022]
- The two counts differ. Six is the maximum for a post, and about five is the typical structure. Stay at or below six, and aim for about five. [L1077, undated; L1381, 2022]

### Captions and Search Terms

- TikTok search autocomplete suggestions can reveal what users want within a niche. Using them in captions and content is an early SEO tactic on the platform. [L1077, undated]

### Trend Monitoring

#### Procedure: Daily Trend Monitoring

1. Spend about 30 minutes a day on the For You feed to see what is trending.
2. Note the popular music, sounds and formats that repeat across several videos.
3. When you find a trending audio, open its sound page to see the top videos using it.
4. Use the same audio in your own video, ideally soon after it starts trending.

[L0764, 2022]

#### Procedure: Finding Trending Audio and Formats

1. Spend regular time on TikTok analyzing what is trending.
2. Spot trends one to two weeks before they reach the viral charts when possible.
3. Use the trend format with your product. Older trends, even a few months old, can still perform well.

[L0795, undated]

### Seasonal and Q4 Planning

- Consumer spending increases visibly around October 12-15 every year. Spending stays high despite economic downturns because consumers rely on credit cards and buy-now-pay-later schemes. Plan inventory and campaign capacity for a volume surge in this window. [L0309, 2024+]

#### Procedure: Preparing for the Q4 Holidays

1. Raise posting from one or two to three times per day.
2. Watch for trending sounds, hashtags and filters starting about 24 to 48 hours before Thanksgiving and the Friday after.
3. Use them in your videos.

[L0820, undated]

### Handling Viral Momentum

- While a video is viral, upload more good-quality content, aiming for four to five videos, and space uploads about two hours apart. [L0706, 2020+]
- When a video is gaining momentum, post several times a day. The example was four uploads on the first day, followed by continued consistency. [L1444, 2022]
- Keep posting during the peak first week even if early views are low, because one video can break out and lift the rest. [L1383, 2022]
- A viral spike usually fades within 24 hours to a few days. A strong video can stay elevated for about a week, but only if you keep adding actions that reach new viewers. [L1429, 2022]
- A video that does not go viral on day one can still gain views later, because TikTok sometimes reruns or pushes older videos. One reported video went from 14 million views to 20 million and then to 30 million through reruns. [L1444, 2022]
