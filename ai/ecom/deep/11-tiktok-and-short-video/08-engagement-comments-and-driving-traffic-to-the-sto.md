## Engagement, Comments and Driving Traffic to the Store

### Followers Versus Sales

- Below 1,000 followers, direct sales from TikTok profile traffic are small, because new viewers rarely trust a small account. Sales typically pick up after the account passes 1,000 followers, and a single video with roughly 5,000 to 10,000 views can be enough to reach that number. [L1380, 2022]
- Free-product and giveaway-style angles grow followers and views fast, but the audience that responds mostly wants free items and rarely buys. Use them to build early momentum, not as the main sales angle. [L0352, 2022]
- Giveaway posts grew the follower count quickly but mostly attracted people who wanted free items, not buyers. Sales-focused videos that followed stayed under 5,000 views and drew little traffic. Keep the content aimed at people who want the product, and use giveaways only when the audience matches the product. [L1077, undated]
- Engagement drives distribution. When viewers like, follow, watch to the end and comment, the platform sends the video to a larger audience. Ads optimised for viewers who engage and finish watching the video tend to scale better. [L1383, 2022]

### Audience Matching and Steering

- Engaging with similar creators (liking, commenting and following in your niche) increases your exposure to the audience of those creators. [L0915, 2025-26]
- Steer the audience away from kids after a viral video attracts the wrong viewers:
  1. Find another viral video for a similar product and identify its angle. [L0706, 2020+]
  2. Test new content angles that appeal to adults. [L0706, 2020+]
  3. Use hashtags that adults engage with. [L0706, 2020+]
  4. Once an adult-focused angle goes viral, replicate it and reduce the youth-focused content. [L0706, 2020+]
- Redirecting an existing audience with coins is risky because it can drive away current viewers. Use it only when comments show a clear buying segment, such as all male commenters, and target that segment or an age group expected to buy. [L1429, 2022]

### Comments and Engagement

- Reply to as many comments as possible. Long comment sections raise watch time, and TikTok then pushes the video further. Replying to comments with new videos and asking questions in comments drives engagement. [L0501, 2022]
- To reply to a comment with a video, tap the comment under your viral video and choose **Make video**. The original comment then appears in the top-left corner of the recording screen, and you place your reply video there. [L0501, 2022]
- The TikTok video reply lets you answer a specific comment with a new video. Using it on a top comment that shares a theme can pull in viewers who are already engaged with that topic. [L0764, 2022]
- When a viral video's comment thread is building around its angle, a follow-up video that responds to those comments can extend reach while the trend is active. Only use this if the product really works, because a retraction-style follow-up can backfire. [L0655, undated]
- Controversial content that prompts argument in the comments raises engagement and watch time, because the video keeps playing while people read and reply. Some creators deliberately point viewers to the comments to drive interaction. [L1384, 2022]
- Small deliberate imperfections in a video or caption, such as a visible slip, can draw comments and boost engagement. Keep them mild, because extreme behaviour can backfire on the account. [L0501, 2022]
- Do not share your own video by copying its link and sharing it on your own account, because TikTok can detect self-spam. Share actions should come from genuine viewer behaviour, not self-promotion. [L1384, 2022]

### Calls to Action and Links

#### Calls to action

- Calls to action can boost engagement by asking viewers to comment, share, save or tag. Examples include asking viewers to save the video for later, send it to a gym partner, or tag a friend who needs the product. [L1384, 2022]

#### Link rules

- Do not put link in bio or any direct link call-to-action in captions or on videos. TikTok is believed to penalise it. If a product link must be shown, a friend or second account can comment that they just bought it and that it is in the description, and the comment can be pinned to the top of the comment section. [L0501, 2022]
- Excessive use of link in bio calls on TikTok tends to end in a sharp drop-off after an initial viral spike, and shadow bans after large follower counts have been reported. Play it safe with links and do not risk an account that has reached a large following. [L0501, 2022]
- Avoid tricks such as misspelling link in bio in organic comments to get around algorithm limits. The effect is uncertain, and a mistake can damage an account that took a long time to build. [L0706, 2020+]
- For commenters asking where to buy, redirect them to the link without using the standard link in bio wording. Keep the product visible in the video so viewers have a reason to click. [L0352, 2022]
- Comment template for redirecting commenters:

```
Hey guys, if anyone is asking, you can find it in the product description. I just bought one, and it's in the description if you want one too.
```
[L0501, 2022]

#### Comment placement for the store link

- TikTok removed the option to pin comments, so the store link cannot be pinned any more. Instead, be the first to comment or comment under the most-liked comment, pointing viewers to the store. [L1381, 2022]
- A creator comment placed under the most-liked comment is shown above the other comments. This makes it a reliable place to point viewers to the store. [L1381, 2022]

#### Product link flagging

- TikTok can flag a product page link when its URL has too many characters, so the link may be blocked or fail. Shortening the product page URL by removing characters can make the link work again. A link problem can also be fixed in the Shopify store settings or at the domain provider, such as GoDaddy. [L1385, undated]
- A product link that TikTok flags or that fails may work on mobile while failing on desktop. Test the product page link on both device types before concluding it is broken. Long URLs with many characters are a common cause of flagging, and shortening the product page URL can restore the link. [L1385, undated]

### Giveaways

#### Giveaway format

- A giveaway format can drive comments by promising a free product to a commenter whose comment gets no likes within 24 hours. Viewers often like each other's comments to avoid being the winner, which keeps the comment section active. [L1384, 2022]

#### Fulfilment rule

- Running giveaways you do not actually fulfil, such as promising a free product to a commenter, damages trust and can lead to reports and violations, even if the giveaway drives comments. If you run a giveaway, deliver the prize as promised. If a giveaway is promoted, the prize must be delivered exactly as promised. [L1384, 2022]

#### Running a giveaway for an account

1. Post a link with a set entry date, here the 10th, for participants. [L0352, 2022]
2. Participants click or react to the link to enter a random wheel. [L0352, 2022]
3. The wheel selects a winner, and the result is announced on the set date, here the 15th. [L0352, 2022]
4. The winner receives the login details and can change the username, password and branding. [L0352, 2022]

#### Account as prize

- A giveaway account offered as a prize had more than 10,000 followers in the personal care category and was about two to three weeks old. Its followers were reported as organic and real, which makes the account a stronger prize than a bought one. [L0352, 2022]

#### Case: giveaway growth without sales

- A store was open for one week after a TikTok video went viral. The owner posted three to four videos a day spaced four to six hours apart, mostly giveaway content meant to grow the account from zero toward 1,000 followers, and the account reached about 35,000 followers. One video reached 22 million views within about 36 hours. The store received about 1,220 sessions in the week, with 57 add-to-cart events and 47 checkouts and zero sales. The owner attributed the failure to low-relevance traffic from curious viewers and to the product itself, then stopped posting and closed the store. [L1077, undated]

### Monetisation Programmes

#### Creator Next

- Creator Next is accessed via the top-right icon on the profile tab. The course states these requirements: 1,000 followers, 1,000 views in the last 30 days, at least 3 posts in the last 30 days, and age 18 or older. Confirm the view requirement in the app, as the transcript is unclear. [L1380, 2022]
- The sources give a different follower figure for the shop playlist. At 10,000 followers, apply for TikTok's creator program (transcribed as Creator Next) to get access to a shop playlist, which lets viewers see a shop-now option. Use 1,000 followers as the stated Creator Next requirement and 10,000 followers as the course's point to apply for the playlist. Check current eligibility in the app. [L1381, 2022; L1380, 2022]

#### Creator Fund

- Eligibility conflicts across sources. One source reports that the Creator Fund requires at least 1,000 followers, but an account with 10,000 followers was still rejected in one case, and support replies were slow. Verify the eligibility criteria yourself and do not expect automatic approval. [L0372, 2022]
- Other sources state 10,000 followers as the requirement. Use 10,000 as the planning figure. [L1380, 2022; L1383, 2022]
- Payouts: the Creator Fund was estimated at about 30 dollars per million views, so 40 million views would be worth about 1,200 dollars. This is only an estimate from community experience and should not be used as a fixed revenue forecast. [L0372, 2022; L1381, 2022]
- The range given in the course is about 20 to 40 dollars per million views. It only pays meaningfully for accounts with large view counts. [L1380, 2022; L1381, 2022]
- Product accounts can earn about 1,000 to 3,000 dollars per million views through product-linked content, which is worth much more than the Creator Fund. [L1380, 2022]
- Sources disagree on whether product accounts should apply:
  - For product accounts whose goal is sales, the course advises avoiding the Creator Fund. It risks view suppression that can hurt sales. [L1380, 2022]
  - For accounts with about 10,000 followers whose videos already perform well, the course advises applying for the Creator Fund and turning on sponsorships, and continuing to upload well-performing videos even without direct sales. [L1383, 2022]
- Late-year payouts may slow down because the fund runs out, which can reduce views for creators in the fund. Treat this as a risk to plan around rather than a reliable income source. [L1380, 2022]
- Some sellers avoid the Creator Fund because they believe it limits organic views. This is an unverified belief. One seller estimated that a viral account could have earned about 30,000 to 40,000 dollars from it but never applied. [L1381, 2022]

#### Creator Marketplace

- Creator Marketplace offers brand sponsor deals and is not usually used for product accounts. Large accounts have been offered around 2,000 to 3,000 dollars per sponsored video, so it is worth checking as a later revenue option. [L1380, 2022]

## Shadowbans, Restrictions and Account Health

### Symptoms of a Restriction

- A restriction shows up as a sudden drop in views compared with recent videos, and several consecutive uploads stay at a similar, very low level. Example: one video at 5,000 views followed by repeated uploads near 80 views is a strong sign of restriction. [L1381, 2022]
- Judge restriction by views relative to follower count, not by one low result. Views in the thousands on a small account can be normal, while an account with about 100,000 followers that gets only a few hundred views is likely restricted. [L1381, 2022]

### Diagnosis Before Assuming a Shadowban

1. Check whether the problem is platform-wide. Analytics and Creator Fund issues can affect many accounts and are not specific to yours. Before assuming a shadowban, check whether other accounts report the same delays or problems. [L0372, 2022]
2. For a new account whose videos get almost no views, check the account setup and VPN use before blaming the content. [L0501, 2022]
3. If a video stays in review for a long time, check its upload status with the Copy link step before assuming it failed. [L0501, 2022]

### Causes

- A glitching VPN or other account-level problem can get a new TikTok account shadow banned. [L0501, 2022]
- Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction. [L1381, 2022]
- Videos from new accounts can be shadowbanned or removed quickly if they contain risky content. [L0372, 2022]
- Posting a private copy and then publishing the same video again from the same account is risky, as TikTok may see the duplication as a problem. [L1444, 2022]
- Competitors can file reports against successful accounts, and enough reports can lead to flags even without a violation. [L1384, 2022]
- Most account removals happen after multiple violations or reports, not after a single incident. [L1384, 2022]

### Durations

The sources give different values. Read them together.

| Source | Duration | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Restriction in general | Usually one to two days; the longest observed lasted about two weeks. [L1381, 2022] | Ordinary restriction |
| Shadow ban in the described cases | Typically about 14 days before it lifts. [L0819, 2020+] | Shadow ban; the length varies with the content posted during that time. [L0819, 2020+] |
| Several videos removed in one day | Views stayed very low until the account recovered after about one to two weeks of waiting and re-uploading. [L1381, 2022] | Case of mass removal |

- Expect a few days for an ordinary restriction, and plan for up to about two weeks. [L1381, 2022; L0819, 2020+]

### Recovery

When an account seems shadow banned:

1. Keep posting about once a day on that account. [L0819, 2020+]
2. Run a new account in parallel. [L0819, 2020+]
3. Expect the shadow ban to usually lift after about 14 days in the cases described, but note that the duration depended on the content being posted. [L0819, 2020+]

- After mass removal, wait about one to two weeks and re-upload videos. [L1381, 2022]
- A single restriction usually recovers within a short period after a successful appeal. [L1384, 2022]
- Recovering from a shadow ban can cost weeks of work. [L1444, 2022]

### Appeals and Review

- Appeals are typically resolved in about 24 hours, sometimes three to four days when TikTok is backed up. [L1384, 2022]
- A video removed at least once may cause later uploads to go into review more often. [L0501, 2022]

### Reports and Violations

- Videos that violate Community Guidelines are not pushed to the For You Page and are shown only to the creator's own followers. [L1384, 2022]
- Repeated violations can lead to a permanent ban. Removal of an account means losing its followers and income. [L1384, 2022]
- Enough reports can lead to flags even without a violation. [L1384, 2022]

### Guidelines and Risky Content

- Keep the first videos of a new account safe and avoid borderline jokes until the account is stable. [L0372, 2022]
- Videos that break the rules lose their reach, so check every video against the Community Guidelines before posting. [L1384, 2022]

### Deletion Rules

- Never delete a video, because TikTok treats deleted videos as failed. [L1444, 2022]
- If a video is bad or has an editing mistake, set it to private instead. [L1444, 2022]
- Do not publish the same video again from the same account after posting a private copy, because TikTok may see the duplication as a problem. [L1444, 2022]
- If an account stops performing, test the same content on a new account, which is the safer option. [L1444, 2022]
- Removing several videos in one day may trigger a restriction. [L1381, 2022]
- Do not delete accounts that have brand, business or personal value right away. [L1381, 2022]

### Keeping or Abandoning Accounts

- A restricted account with thousands of followers can be kept. [L1381, 2022]
- For product accounts, a new account is often easier to make go viral than reviving an old one. [L1381, 2022]
- A large follower count builds credibility with shoppers, so an account with tens of thousands of followers is worth keeping as the main brand account even when its organic views drop. [L1381, 2022]
- Shoppers who click a Spark Ad often look at the profile first, which is why the follower count matters. [L1381, 2022]

### Account Roles and Separation

- Keep a separate account for paid ads and for testing new products. [L1381, 2022]
- Keep the personal account separate from ad and test work. This protects the main account and keeps the work organized. [L1381, 2022]
- The sources use "main account" in two senses. In [L1381, 2022] it means the high-follower brand account that you keep. In [L1381, 2022] it means the account kept personal and separate from ads and tests. Keep both roles apart. [L1381, 2022]

### Platform Rule Changes

- Platform rules and TikTok features change often, so check the current rules before relying on any setting or program. [L1380, 2022]
- Changes are a recurring reason setups fail. [L1380, 2022]

### Risk Principles

- Keep the risk of account bans low, because a ban can wipe out the views and progress of accounts on the same device. [L1444, 2022]
- Avoid the mistakes that trigger shadow bans, since recovering from a shadow ban can cost weeks of work. [L1444, 2022]
