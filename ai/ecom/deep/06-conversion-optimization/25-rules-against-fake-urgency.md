### Rules against fake urgency

- Fake urgency, such as a stock count or a deadline that resets every day or week, is dishonest and erodes trust. Customers are a business's most important asset, so urgency built on lies damages it. [L0396, 2021-22]
- Do not use scarcity messages such as `Limited stock on hand` unless they are true and change over time. A stock message that never changes signals fake scarcity. [L0409, 2021-22]
- Use urgency only when it is true. Real limited stock, such as a product that is really down to its last 10 units, can be stated. A blanket claim that every product is low on stock is not believable. [L0473, 2021-22]
- Fake urgency, such as countdown timers that reset, fake stock levels or small token discounts, is not a recommended way to build urgency. Visitors notice it, and it damages trust once discovered. [L0473, 2021-22]
- Fake urgency tactics, such as false countdown timers, claimed low stock, or offers that never actually expire, usually give small, temporary conversion gains. They are often misleading, so they should not replace a stronger offer. [L0414, 2021-22]
- A customer's consideration period cannot be shortened by pressure. People who truly want the product buy it regardless of a small discount, so do not rely on urgency tricks to close the sale. [L0409, 2021-22]
- A strikethrough reference price combined with a large `You save` percentage, shown on a sale that never ends, reads as a fake discount and lowers trust. Show a reference price only when it is a real list or previous price, and make the saving amount match the numbers shown. [L0409, 2021-22]
- Case: a Shopify reseller of high-ticket bathroom products ran a `48 hour sale` banner for at least three months. The countdown date changed by one day each day while the offer stayed at 5% off with code FLASH5, and the page still said `Limited Stock On Hand`. A $2,999.99 product was shown next to a $4,838.69 reference price, with `You Save: $1,838.70 (38%)`. The store also had `Some exclusions apply` on its tax statements, `No Shipping to California Addresses` with no explanation, a financing line with no link, reviews hidden at the bottom, an empty review widget and non-working tabs. Its USP sat in small header text that did not show on mobile. The verdict: this combination makes the store look like a commodity competing on price and reduces trust. [L0409, 2021-22]

### Pop-ups and overlays

- Avoid pop-ups to present offers, since they distract customers and interrupt the buying decision. [L0549, undated]
- Use the notification bar and the exit-intent overlay to make every offer known across the site. [L0757, 2022]
- If the margin does not allow a discount in the announcement bar or exit-intent popup, use free shipping for those locations. (2023.) [L0855, 2023]
- Source conflict: one source advises avoiding pop-ups entirely, while two others use exit-intent overlays for offers. Avoid general pop-ups that interrupt the buying decision. Use an exit-intent overlay only as a last-chance offer, and only where the margin supports it. [L0549, undated; L0757, 2022; L0855, 2023]
