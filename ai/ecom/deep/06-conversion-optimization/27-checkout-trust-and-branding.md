### Checkout trust and branding

**Branding the checkout**

- An unbranded checkout that looks different from the rest of the store can make customers abandon at the last step, even after marketing has built trust. Apply your branding to every step of the purchase process, including checkout. [L0390, 2019]
- The checkout is a different-looking page that may feel untrusted. A branded checkout banner with secure checkout, privacy protection and satisfaction guarantee badges can raise conversion. In one case, conversion rose from 0.29 percent to 2.15 percent after these changes. [L1259, undated]
- The checkout page should look trustworthy. Add a checkout banner with secure checkout and satisfaction guarantee messaging, payment processor logos such as PayPal, Visa and Mastercard, and optionally a 30-day money-back guarantee, because the checkout is otherwise unbranded. [L1260, undated]
- Add the store logo and store information to the checkout. Optionally add a background image in the theme settings under Checkout. This keeps the checkout looking like part of the store, so customers do not feel they have moved to a different site after adding items to the cart. [L1326, undated]
- Showing the business logo on the checkout page reassures customers that they are still on the store. Shopify controls the checkout for security, so it can be branded with a logo and colors but not fully redesigned. [L1371, 2021-22]

**Trust signals**

- Shoppers feel more uncertain at checkout because they must enter email, address and payment details. Add trust icons such as privacy protected, secure checkout and satisfaction guaranteed. Also show a contact number so customers feel supported. [L1377, 2022+]
- Add a trust badge or logo at checkout to increase credibility. [L0067, 2023]
- Show customer review counts or ratings directly on the checkout page as well as on product pages. Buyers who are close to paying benefit from seeing that other customers have completed their purchase successfully. [L1208, 2025-26]
- Payment technical problems and worries about payment security can make buyers leave checkout quickly. Make security signals and the accepted payment methods clearly visible during checkout, and add payment plan options for buyers who may hesitate because of cost. [L1208, 2025-26]
- A PayPal study found that adding a padlock (lock) icon to the checkout button raised mobile conversion by 17 percent. Small, thoughtful design details at checkout can have a measurable effect. [L0390, 2019; L0423, 2019]

**Case**

- A store started with a checkout conversion rate of 0.9 percent, while TikTok traffic showed a high add-to-cart rate. The owner added trust badges and icons to the checkout page, and the conversion rate rose to 2.05 percent within one day. Only two or three changes were made, all on that page. [L1377, 2022+]

### Checkout completion checklist

Steps to improve checkout completion:

1. Ensure the shipping language is attractive, for example "free tracked shipping" rather than "standard shipping". [L0067, 2023]
2. Add a trust badge or logo at checkout to increase credibility. [L0067, 2023]
3. Consider a limited-time free gift offer or an extra product at checkout to incentivize the purchase. [L0067, 2023]
4. Make sure the cart display is not minimized on mobile. Shopify now minimizes cart contents on mobile checkout. [L0067, 2023]

### Testing checkout

**Desktop and mobile**

- Test the add-to-cart and checkout flow on both desktop and mobile. Look for issues such as an unexpected extra fee, a cut-off logo, or a button that does not work. [L0469, 2021]
- Test the complete purchase path after every change, including add-to-cart, subscription selection, upsells and the cart. A cart that stays empty after items were added will stop almost all sales. [L0524, 2023]
- Verify the checkout on the live store. [L1296, 2020-26]

**Test purchase with no conversions**

1. Add a cheap product priced around 1.50 USD.
2. Buy it on a desktop device.
3. Buy it on a mobile phone.
4. If either purchase fails, fix the checkout before running more traffic.

[L0963, 2022]

**Usability test with a tester**

1. Set up the store and add a specific product to the cart.
2. Find the add-to-cart button and confirm it is easy to locate.
3. Start and complete the checkout process.
4. Watch the tester without giving any guidance.
5. Note every place they hesitate or get stuck.
6. Ask them why they got stuck.
7. Optimize each problem point and repeat the test.

[L1098, 2019+]
