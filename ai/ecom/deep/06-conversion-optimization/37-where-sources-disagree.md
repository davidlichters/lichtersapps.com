### Where sources disagree

1. **Design changes before or after sales.** one source says store design tests make sense only after the store makes sales, and before that only when the offer and traffic are confirmed to work. [L0388, 2024+] one source says to start conversion work at the first sale, or before any sale at about 40 unique visitors per day. [L1293, 2014] one source also says not to leave the store unchanged from day one. [L1108, 2023] Apply the design rule to design tests. Use the pre-sale rule for general conversion work, once traffic reaches about 40 unique visitors per day.

2. **Order of first store tests.** One one source sequence starts with the landing page or theme, then prices, lead magnets, discounts, copy, product description and trust signals, and ad breakdown. [L0773, 2023] Another starts with themes, bundles, prices, urgency and copy, then scaling. [L1108, 2023] The first sequence has a stated trigger (a winning product gets traffic but no sales). [L0773, 2023] The second has no stated trigger. [L1108, 2023] Use the first when that trigger applies.

3. **Many small changes versus fundamental changes.** one source says small tips change little and fundamental changes to the store, offer and purchase path come first. [L0598, 2021-22] one source and one source both recommend many small tested changes, and an ongoing process. [L0549, undated; L1364, 2021-22] Use fundamental changes when the offer or purchase path is weak. Once those are planned, run small changes continuously. [L0399, 2021-22; L1364, 2021-22]

4. **Cut-offs for product focus.** The top 100 trafficked products is one cut-off. [L1446, 2020-26] The top 20% of trafficked products is another. [L1446, 2020-26] Both are kept above. Use the top 100 as the working list and the 20% rule to rank within it, if the sources' stated numbers are applied to the same store. (Derived; the sources do not say which applies to which store size.)

5. **Ad cut level.** Ad-level cuts happen within the first 24 hours at $10 spend and $3.50 CPC. [L0229, undated] Product-level cuts happen at the pre-set break-even limit, not on the first disappointing day. [L0639, 2023] These apply at different levels and do not conflict.

#### Further details

- Product recommendation and discovery tests require strong product categorization and merchandising to achieve their full potential lift. Before running tests on product recommendations, out-of-stock alternatives, or quick navigation links, audit and optimize your product catalog structure and categories. [L0197, 2025-26]
