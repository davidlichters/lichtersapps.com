## Collecting and displaying reviews

### Why reviews matter for conversion

- Reviews build trust with new visitors and act as social proof on product pages. Conversion rates rise over time, and the effect compounds: more sales bring more reviews, and more reviews bring more sales. [L0155, undated; L1245, 2020-26]
- Visitors judge whether a product will achieve their desired outcome by reading reviews and looking at photos of other customers using it. Strong social proof raises perceived likelihood and conversion rate. [L0233, undated]
- Reviews from real customers are the main long-term source of social proof, and the review database should be treated as a long-term asset. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Reviews raise conversion rate and can justify selling above MAP. Build reviews before lowering prices. [L0806, 2024]
- Customers trust non-biased review platforms (Trustpilot, productreview.com.au, Google, YouTube) because they cannot be manipulated, so these platforms are your strongest conversion lever. [L0041, 2025-26]
- A widely quoted figure says around 80 percent of shoppers read reviews before buying. The source is not given, so treat it only as a rough benchmark. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Reviews also raise click-through rate. High review counts make ads, organic search listings and some social profiles stand out, and buyers arrive already inclined to trust the store. [L1012, 2021-22]
- Reviewers who have written a review are more likely to buy again, so repeat-purchase conversion is higher for them. [L1012, 2021-22]
- Show reviews as a separate section. The number of reviews matters, not only descriptive wording. [L1209, undated]
- Show reviews with specific wording rather than vague adjectives alone. [L1210, undated]
- Customer reviews are social proof. The more reviews a store shows, the more buyers trust that the product will work for them, and the more reviews show success, the more trust grows. [L1207, undated]
- Show reviews on every product page once you have them, because they are a key trust element. [L1130, 2020-26]
- Product pages need customer reviews to convert well, and the reviews must come from real customers. [L1325, 2020-26]

### Protecting ratings and handling negative reviews

- Every star lost on Trustpilot (for example 4.8 → 4.7 → 4.6) measurably reduces the store's conversion rate. Protect ratings above all else in the early stage. [L0041, 2025-26]
- When a customer complains or leaves a negative review, especially early on, resolve the issue above and beyond what seems fair. Each rating point compounds across all conversions. [L0041, 2025-26]
- Reply to reviews from the brand team to show the brand is responsive. Show qualitative reviews on the product page, including detailed ones. [L1016, 2026]
- Do not hide genuine negative reviews, because customers who find them will trust the store less. Remove a review only if it breaks platform rules or you can verify it was not written by a buyer, and check each one against order records first. [L0657, undated]

### Review volume and rating

- Review volume matters as well as the average rating. 500 reviews averaging 4.8 stars carry far more weight with buyers than 25 reviews at the same average, especially in a niche where competitors already have years of reviews. [L1012, 2021-22]
- A store with very few reviews, such as seven, looks weak. Collect more genuine reviews before scaling traffic. [L0657, undated]
- Before writing a review figure on the page, check the total in your review widget. It can be well above the rounded number in the headline. One store headlined 10,000+ reviews while its widget held about 16,500 reviews, of which about 14,000 were five-star. [L0409, 2021-22]
- Show the star rating together with the number of reviews, for example "4.8 stars from 293 reviews", and label reviewers with a Verified Customer tag when available. [L0600, 2026]
- Use only real reviews. Invented ratings and counts create legal and platform risk. [L0600, 2026]

### Trustpilot and third-party platforms

- Do not create a Trustpilot company profile for the store as a review source. Once a profile exists it cannot be deleted, and the company then receives repeated calls to buy paid plans. Collect reviews through Judge.me and share them to social channels instead. [L0665, 2022]
- Use a review app such as Judge.me to collect reviews across the site and show them on one review page. This page works as your trust signal instead of paid security seals. [L0934, 2023]
- Ask customers to leave reviews on your store and include a review link on the way out. Ask as a routine step, not only when a problem comes up. [L0808, 2026]
- A review request does not need the customer to post publicly on social media. Display the reviews collected through the request on the store, because visible reviews raise conversion. [L0417, 2020-26]
- When a rating block links to a complete, unedited review feed, visitors can see the reviews are not hand-picked. Record whether a competitor offers such a link to judge how much weight its rating carries. [L0893, 2021-22]

### Collecting reviews before launch

- Install the review app before traffic and sales begin, then start collecting reviews as soon as sales start. Having no reviews at launch is normal and expected. [L0155, undated]
- Install the review app before the store gets traffic, so reviews start accumulating with the first sales. Waiting until sales arrive loses the early reviews that build the base. [L1245, 2020-26]
- Collect reviews from every buyer from the first sale onward. [L1245, 2020-26]
- Acquire initial reviews before major marketing, because without social proof conversion rates suffer. Methods include sending products to friends or small customer groups. [L0227, 2014]
- When competitors sell at MAP with no reviews on the product, actively ask your customers for reviews. Even one review adds reassurance and gives you an advantage. [L0894, 2020-26]
- Review request emails should be routine. Each review adds to the snowball effect. [L1245, 2020-26; L0808, 2026]

#### Review count targets

Sources give different targets. Use the one that matches your stage:

| Stage or rule | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Before major marketing | 3-5 initial reviews | [L0227, 2014] |
| Before selling (store start) | About 10-20 reviews, in English, genuine-looking, with photos when possible; add more once the product gets views and sales | [L1444, 2022] |
| Per product, minimum | At least 3 customer reviews; a product with none is unlikely to sell | [L1293, 2014] |
| New store, per product | 2-3 reviews as a workable starting target; publish what exists and keep adding | [L0970, 2023] |
| Supplier reviews, per top seller | 2-3 of the best reviews for each top-selling product of a brand | [L1097, 2025] |
| Text-review rate from requests | At least 50% of customers leaving a standard text review (video and photo reviews are a bonus on top) | [L1012, 2021-22] |
| Automated request emails alone | Typically about 10% response, sometimes up to 20%, so roughly 10 reviews per 100 sales | [L1012, 2021-22] |

- Do not publish reviews until you can see the number and rating on the live page. Verify the count after each import. [L1297, 2023]

### Legitimate sources for early reviews

- A new store without sales can gather reviews from suppliers, friends and family, and later from real buyers. Reviews from real customers remain the main long-term source. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Friends and family who know and trust the store owner may leave a genuine review of the business. Their reviews are a legitimate early source. [L0908, 2020-26]
- Ask the supplier before reusing its reviews on your store. Get explicit permission from the supplier contact person directly and do not assume it. Never copy reviews from competitor stores. Reviews should be collected on your own store once you have customers. [L0908, 2020-26; L0934, 2023; L0667, 2025]
- Use supplier-provided reviews only after the brand gives explicit permission. Use a standard review request process to collect customer reviews. [L0667, 2025]
- Testimonials can start as real reviews from Amazon or AliExpress for the same product. They are safe to adapt with credit to their source. [L1259, undated]
- Do not publish AI-written quotes as if they came from real customers. Invented testimonials are misleading and can break platform rules. Replace any placeholder quotes with genuine customer reviews as soon as sales come in. [L1259, undated]
- Placeholder reviews or testimonials, including AI-written ones, must not be presented as real customer feedback, and star ratings must not be invented. Replace them with real reviews as soon as sales arrive, because fabricated reviews create legal and trust risks. [L1260, undated]
- Placeholder reviews are only for layout and must be replaced before launch. [L1331, 2025-26]
- Remove or avoid reviews that clearly reveal dropshipping, such as wording that thanks the shipper. Reviews should read as genuine customer experience. [L1444, 2022]
- Offer a coupon code in exchange for a customer review. This builds social proof and turns the reviewer into a repeat customer. [L1357, 2018]

#### Video testimonials (cheap method)

1. Set up a YouTube channel and upload the testimonial videos as private or unlisted.
2. Embed the videos on the store using a gallery-style section in the theme or page builder.
3. Order the product yourself and film short testimonial videos, or use real customer clips. [L0387, 2022]

#### Prompt for placeholder testimonials (use only until real reviews exist)

```
I sell [product name], which [what it does]. Write one short customer testimonial for it in the first person, two to three sentences long, that names one specific benefit and the problem it solved. Keep the tone natural, avoid exaggerated claims and invented statistics, and give three versions: plain, enthusiastic, and calm. Use this text only as a placeholder until real customer reviews exist, label it internally as a placeholder, and never publish it as a real customer review.
```
[L1345, 2021+]

### Import rules

- Import only reviews that are real and relevant. Check each imported review one by one, and publish only those with good text and pictures. [L1297, 2023]
- Imported reviews raise trust on a new product page. Verify the rating count on the live page after each import. [L1297, 2023]
- Review incentives and imports must not hide genuine negative reviews. Check each imported review against the policies in the section above. [L0657, undated]

#### Import filters: differing defaults

| Platform | Starting filters | Applies when | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| LUX Reviews | Leave pictures-only and most-recent filters off; import all four-star-and-up reviews | First import | [L0477, undated; L1297, 2023] |
| LUX Reviews | Pictures-only and most-recent filters are optional and can be added later | After the first import | [L0477, undated] |

### Review requests and rewards

- Review request emails alone typically get only about 10% response, sometimes up to 20%. A store that relies only on them will collect roughly 10 reviews per 100 sales. [L1012, 2021-22]
- Set a target of at least 50% of customers leaving a standard text review. Video or photo reviews convert at a lower rate, so treat them as a bonus on top of the text target. [L1012, 2021-22]
- Stamped.io sends the request automatically after an order is placed, not at checkout. It waits a set number of days, and the first email is set up in the app's customization step. [L1298, 2021-22]
- A written review does not need an incentive. A small reward, such as a 10% discount on a future order or store credit, can be offered. Customers should never feel obliged to take a reward for a text review. [L1012, 2021-22]
- Review incentives encourage more customers to leave reviews. Junip's two-tier design pays a basic reward for every review and a stronger reward only for reviews with a photo or video. [L1244, 2025-26]
- LUX Reviews keeps the picture review reward at a 15% discount by default. [L1297, 2023]

#### Review request timing by app

| LUX Reviews | 28 days | Counted so the product should have arrived after fulfillment | [L1297, 2023] |

### Review app setup

#### Judge.me

- Judge.me is a review app. The Awesome plan is used for it, and the review widget text can be edited to show a rating and review count. [L1299, 2026]
- After Judge.me is installed on the product page, visitors see a Write a review button under the review widget and can submit their own review directly on the page. Submitted reviews are published, and the star rating updates with them. [L0908, 2020-26]
- On a product page with reviews, Judge.me shows a prominent star rating, customer reviews below the product details with reviewer name and date, and a write-a-review option. [L0155, undated]
- A product page with reviews shows the reviewer, the date and the review text, together with a write-a-review button. A product without reviews shows only the write-a-review button, so no review block needs to be configured for it. [L1245, 2020-26]
- Judge.me marks reviews as verified in the widget on the product page. The verified label helps visitors trust them. [L0908, 2020-26]
- On the Judge.me free plan, the star colour can be changed with the colour picker in the review widget settings. [L0908, 2020-26]

#### Looks (Product Reviews and Photos)

1. Install Looks (Product Reviews and Photos) from the Shopify App Store.
2. Choose the Beginner plan (it starts with a 7-day free trial).
3. Click Approve, Continue, and Add Looks to your theme; keep it enabled and save.
4. Click Add rating widget and place it, for example just after the product title, then save.
5. In the admin, set the review request timing to 14 days after delivery (21 days if delivery is slow; a discount offer to customers can help, and 10 is also mentioned).
6. Enter the admin and click Import reviews. [L0635, undated]

#### Looks: importing AliExpress reviews

1. Drag the Looks import bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar.
2. Open the product on AliExpress and click the bookmark to import it to Looks.
3. Set filters: four stars and up, all countries, translate to English, only reviews with photos, most recent.
4. Click Preview, then Import.
5. Check Manage reviews in the admin; the reviews appear on the product page after a refresh. [L0635, undated]

#### LUX Reviews

1. Install LUX Reviews from the Shopify App Store.
2. Choose the Beginner plan: click the 14 day free trial option, then take Beginner instead, and approve.
3. Click Continue and set the store's primary language to English.
4. Choose the star colour: your brand colour or the default gold.
5. Set the review request timing to 28 days, which means after the product should have arrived following fulfillment.
6. Keep the picture review reward at 15% discount, then continue. [L1297, 2023]

#### LUX Reviews: importing AliExpress reviews

1. Drag the LUX import button to your bookmarks bar.
2. Open the matching AliExpress product page and click the bookmark.
3. Import into your store's product URL.
4. Filter for 4 stars and up, all countries, with reviews translated to English.
5. Select the product, leave extra filters (only with pictures, most recent) off at first, and click Import.
6. Go through the reviews and import only those with good text and pictures, then click Finish importing.
7. Check the Reviews section and the product page to confirm the count and ratings. [L1297, 2023]

- Start with the LUX Beginner plan and upgrade to Growth or Unlimited once the store starts to scale. [L1297, 2023]
- In the example, 19 five-star reviews were imported, and the product page then showed 19 five-star reviews. [L1297, 2023]

#### Stamped.io

- Start Stamped.io on the free-forever plan at launch, because a store with no orders does not need paid review features yet. Move to a paid plan once order volume makes the extra functions worth their cost. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Paying for a higher review plan before the store has orders wastes money. Higher plans only pay for themselves once order volume and display needs grow. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Stamped.io collects product reviews, photo reviews, video reviews, checkout reviews, community questions and answers, Net Promoter Score and Instagram curation. It also supports review requests with an in-email form, manual review requests, and importing AliExpress and Facebook reviews. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Stamped.io's AliExpress reviews importer lets dropshippers import reviews for AliExpress-sourced products with one click. It is part of the Reviews & UGC app. [L1298, 2021-22]

##### Stamped.io setup

1. Install Stamped.io Product Reviews UGC from the Shopify App Store (search for stamped.io).
2. Click through the welcome screens. Skip the selling step because the store is not selling yet, then click Next.
3. In the customization step, set up the automatic review request email that is sent a number of days after the order.
4. On the Choose a Plan page, select the free-forever plan.
5. On the import step, skip importing past orders because the store has none.
6. In the main Reviews & UGC platform, open Customize Main Widget to adjust the review widget. Optionally connect social links and add a logo. [L1298, 2021-22]

#### Junip

- Junip can show review stars under product titles across the whole store, not only on the product page. This puts ratings in front of shoppers before they click into a product. [L0589, 2025-26]

### Widget placement and display

- An empty review widget, with no sign of any store reviews on the page, removes social proof entirely. Add a Reviews tab or section with store reviews, even when the individual product has none yet. [L0409, 2021-22]
- Place a customer review bubble above the collapsible tabs so the most persuasive quote is seen as the buyer scrolls. Keep the quote short and specific to a benefit the product delivers. [L1188, 2026]
- Use real customer testimonials and reviews on the page. The more people report success, the more shoppers trust that the product will work for them. [L1207, undated]
- Reviews placed on product pages are meant to increase conversions. The vendor claims double-digit conversion gains. This is a vendor claim, not a verified benchmark, so measure the effect on your own product pages before relying on it. [L0589, 2025-26]
- Show the star rating and count near the buy button, using real customer feedback. Competitor stores use emoji-style reviews and short testimonials with first name and last initial, plus customer photos when available. [L0600, 2026]

#### Customer video testimonial placement

- Embed customer videos in a gallery-style section of the theme or page builder. [L0387, 2022]

### Styling the widget

- Match the star colour to the store's main brand colour so the stars stay consistent with the store design. A gold or yellow tone is a common alternative if the brand colour does not work. [L0908, 2020-26]
- In Stamped.io, use Reviews & UGC > Customize > Customize Main Widget to match the widget to the store's colours and layout. The widget appears on product pages automatically and displays star ratings. [L1298, 2021-22]
- Style review widgets after reviews are live, so colours and layout match the real content. With no reviews yet, the widget will show little on the storefront. [L1298, 2021-22]
- In LUX Reviews, choose the star colour as your brand colour or the default gold during setup. [L1297, 2023]
