## Other details

### Foundations for conversion

- Market knowledge, content development and customer understanding feed directly into conversion rate optimization. Outdated basics therefore weaken every later marketing and sales effort. [L0761, 2021-22]
- A small store with good suppliers and steady traffic can make money before organic traffic builds. Organic traffic grows over time as Google recognizes the site as high quality. [L1111, 2020]

### Sales channels and store setup

- The Shop sales channel can be added to Shopify sales channels. It rarely generates significant volume but does not hurt to include. [L0278, 2025]

### Product images in Search product ads

- Image extensions in Search product ads have an approval rate of approximately 50%, with no clear rejection reason. [L0210, 2022]
- Upload product images anyway, because approved images generate more clicks. [L0210, 2022]

### SEO titles and SKUs

- Adding the SKU number to the SEO title is fine and helps SEO. [L0805, 2024]
- The product title follows what the top competitors put in their titles. [L0805, 2024]
- Products without variants show the SKU in the SEO title. Variant products do not include the SKU. [L0805, 2024]

### Ad funnel

1. Run targeted Google Shopping ads to product pages optimized for trust. [L0270, 2021]
2. Use dynamic Facebook remarketing ads and email marketing to bring back visitors who left without buying. [L0270, 2021]
3. Optimize lead value by earning more from every sale once a customer purchases. [L0270, 2021]
4. Repeat to maximize revenue per customer. [L0270, 2021]

### Affiliate banners in Refersion

- When uploading a banner creative to Refersion, set the landing page URL to match the banner type. [L0288, 2021-22]
  - General business-wide banner: link to the homepage. [L0288, 2021-22]
  - Product-specific banner: link to the specific product page. [L0288, 2021-22]
- This ensures visitors land on the appropriate destination, where they find details about the offer. [L0288, 2021-22]

### Trade buyer capture (2024)

1. Create a page with a name such as "interior designer discounts" or "to the trade", and link it from the homepage and the footer menu. [L0360, 2024]
2. Add an exit-intent overlay that asks whether the visitor is an interior designer and collects their email for trade discounts. [L0360, 2024]
3. Send the captured emails a personal reply that offers custom quotes. [L0360, 2024]
4. Create a separate trade contact page with its own contact form. The form collects name, email, phone, products of interest and quantity. [L0360, 2024]

### Returns benchmark

- Aim for a return rate under 1 percent. [L1277, 2020]
