### Beginner versus advanced finance path

#### Which module to take

- Every member should complete at least one of the two finance modules, regardless of stage, because the finance content is not optional. Which module to start with depends on whether bookkeeping is already in place. [L0545, 2021-22]
- The beginner finance module is for owners who are brand new to business or who have just launched but do not yet have a solid bookkeeping method. It must be studied as soon as the business launches. [L0545, 2021-22]
- The advanced finance module is for owners who already run a basic bookkeeping system that tracks profit, losses, invoices, sales and costs. It focuses on using financial numbers to grow the business profitably. [L0545, 2021-22]
- The Know Your Numbers Advance module is aimed at businesses that already have a live store with sales and want to grow. It is more advanced than the earlier Know Your Numbers material, which is the Build Phase module 7 in the one source program for high-ticket dropshipping. [L0889, 2021-22]

#### What the beginner module covers

- The beginner finance module has 15 lessons. Among them are an introduction to knowing your numbers, a lesson called What You Need Now, profit per order, generating a profit and loss statement, and when to level up. [L0545, 2021-22]
- The beginner finance module covers calculating profit per order, generating a profit and loss statement, and recognising when to level up. These basics are needed before scaling any high-ticket dropshipping business. [L0545, 2021-22]
