### Brand protection and infringement tracking

- Tracking log for brand infringement reports (one tab per platform, same column setup on every tab):

  ```
  Columns: Item (the infringer or page name) | Complaint ID (received after filing) | Person (who handled the report) | Detection Date | Reporting Date | Removal Date | Location (if detectable) | Link (to the infringing content) | Infringement Type (Copyright or Trademark) | Status (Detected, Reported, Resubmitted, Escalated, Removed).

  Also keep a brand information tab with the details needed for reports: brand name, rights owner, official online presence, registration numbers, mailing address and reporting email.

  Use the status 'Resubmitted' when the platform asks for more details, and 'Escalated' when outside help is needed.
  ```

  [L0991, 2025-26]
- Monthly, weekly or yearly analysis of the infringement log:
  1. Count the infringements by platform.
  2. Count the infringements by type (copyright or trademark).
  3. Identify the most often infringed products.
  4. Identify where the infringers are located.
  5. Use the results to refine the detection and reporting process.

  [L0991, 2025-26]
- Tabs in the infringement tracking board:

  | Tab | Purpose |
  |---|---|
  | Brand Information | Brand name, rights owner, official online presence, registration numbers, mailing address, reporting email |
  | Amazon, eBay, Bol.com, TikTok, Etsy, Shopify | One tab per marketplace or channel searched |
  | Facebook, Instagram | One tab per social platform, sorted by year groups such as 2024 |
  | Other Marketplaces | Other channels not listed above |
  | Stand Alone Websites (current year) | Standalone shops found in the current year |
  | (CLOSED) previous year tabs | Archive of finished years, for example (CLOSED) 2023 Stand Alone Websites |

  Every platform tab uses the same column setup. [L0991, 2025-26]

### Case results and examples

- Case study: Resistance Bands, Day 18. Revenue: $507 on 17 orders. Average order value: $29.85 (full price, no discounts or giveaways). This demonstrates that sustainable growth does not require artificial tactics like free giveaways or deep discounting. [L0228, 2015]
- Case study: Resistance Bands, Week 5-6: 96 units sold, $2,900 revenue, 10-18 units per day (average daily orders remained above 10 for 6 of 7 days). The product price increased from an initial $24.95 to a $30.17 average, then to $35.95, without significant order drop-off. Week 6 single day: 12 orders at the raised $35.95 price. Net profit after Amazon fees: $22 per unit. Within 6 weeks, the initial investment was fully recovered and a reorder was funded. Weeks 7-8 produced profit payouts from the two-week Amazon payout cycle. Key lesson: price increases do not necessarily reduce orders in a low-supply scenario with high demand. [L0228, 2015]
- Success case: 108 orders in one month = $325,000 in sales; average order value $3,000 per order. [L0268, 2020]
- Profitability timeline: the first sale typically comes within 30 days of launch if the 21-day launch plan is followed. Timeline to consistent profitability depends on reinvestment rate and ad optimization. [L0270, 2021]
- Example: A competitor added extra features to their weighted blanket (making it Queen-size by default), which doubled product cost. You kept it simple (single size), spent the margin savings on ads, and won the market. [L0041, 2025-26]
- Creative and product-page measurement, for a product description: aim for a product description of roughly 600 words; this length supports SEO and avoids thin content. Count words in the product copy and aim for that range. [L0461, 2021-22]
- Snapchat's SPECS AR glasses were reported to cost around 2,000 US dollars. The price is an estimate from the update commentary, not an official figure. [L1504, 2025-26]
- At the stage described, the brand had about 185 products on sale and an annual revenue of around 1 million pounds. The founder reported that the business had reached a multi-seven-figure level. [L1438, 2023+]
