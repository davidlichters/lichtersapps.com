### Refunds, timing, cancellations

- The refund timeframe should be a fixed number of business days counted from when the item is received and inspected, for example within 7 business days. Stating the start point of the clock prevents customers from expecting refunds on the day the return is shipped. [L0539, undated]
- Sample refund rule: approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is inspected. [L1250, 2026]
- Partial refunds may apply to items not in original condition, damaged items, or returns after the window. [L1250, 2026]
- Refund wording from the lesson: "If you're unhappy with your products for any reason, we refund the full amount minus shipping. To ask for a refund, email [business email] or phone [phone number]. [Add restocking fee, if any.] [Add brands or products that cannot be returned, if any.]" [L0377, 2020-26]

```
If you're unhappy with your products for any reason, we refund the full amount minus shipping. To ask for a refund, email [business email] or phone [phone number]. [Add restocking fee, if any.] [Add brands or products that cannot be returned, if any.]
```
[L0377, 2020-26]

- The lesson's sample policy refunds the full product price minus the original shipping charges and tells customers to request this by email or phone. [L0377, 2020-26]
- The refund policy must state any restocking fee and any brands or product types that cannot be returned, because a reviewer checks for these. [L0377, 2020-26]
- The refund and return policy should answer: what the refund policy is, how a customer requests a refund, how returns work for damaged items, and how a customer starts a return. [L0377, 2020-26]
- one source's example refunded the original shipping cost for store-caused returns and did not refund it for remorse returns. The 2025 template in Dropship defaults: store refunds original shipping cost for defective or wrong items. [L1048, 2020-26]
- Default refund policy settings for a new dropship store: the customer pays for return shipping, the store pays for defective or wrong items and refunds the original shipping cost, orders canceled before shipping receive a full refund, and exchanges use prepaid shipping labels. [L1048, 2020-26]
- Cancellation policy: full refund if canceled before shipping. [L0539, undated; L1048, 2020-26]
- Original shipping cost policy: the original shipping cost is refunded for store-caused returns. [L0539, undated; L0556, 2021-22]
- For a steam shower or bathtub that fails local inspection, the return is the purchase price minus the original shipping fee, the return shipping fee and a 15% restocking fee if it is in original packaging. If opened or assembled, the restocking fee rises to 25%. [L0556, 2021-22]
- Refund disputes are best prevented with clear product page wording about returns. Keep the wording specific to the supplier and product type. (2020) [L0664, 2020]
- A refund dispute can happen when a return promise is inconsistent (see Consistency). [L0567, 2025-26]

Return address and RMA:

- The RMA process: a customer starts a return and receives an RMA number. The return address is provided after the return is authorised. [L0167, undated]
- Procedure: after you receive an RMA number, send the item only to the return address you are given, quoting the RMA number. [L0724, 2020-26]
- If returns go to a third-party warehouse instead of the business address, the refund policy must state that the return address is provided after the return is authorised. Google may flag a policy that does not clearly name the return address when it differs from the business address. [L0724, 2020-26]

Template for a warehouse return address:

```
Once your return has been approved and you have received an RMA number, we will provide you with the exact return address of the warehouse facility. Please send your item only to that address, quoting your RMA number.
```
[L0724, 2020-26]

### Damage reports and defective items

- Set the two damage report windows separately in the returns policy, because the carriers' claim periods differ. (2023) [L0539, undated]
- Damage reports for shipments by UPS or FedEx must be filed within 48 hours by the customer. For all other carriers the damage report window is 15 days. [L1048, 2020-26]
- Conflict on UPS and FedEx: the Example for high-ticket products sets freight (LTL) damage reports at 48 hours after delivery and small parcel shipments via UPS or FedEx at 15 days after delivery. The Shopify default sets UPS or FedEx at 48 hours and all other carriers at 15 days. Check the carrier's claim period before you publish, because the two sources do not agree on UPS and FedEx. [L0539, undated; L1048, 2020-26]
- Damage report window by carrier type (applies to the high-ticket template):
  - Freight (LTL): 48 hours after delivery. [L0539, undated]
  - Small parcel (UPS or FedEx): 15 days after delivery. [L0539, undated]
- Each area of a competitor's return policy has its own terms: seller errors (wrong or damaged product, incorrect photo), the window for reporting damage, warranty terms, and change-of-mind returns. Record each separately. [L0893, 2021-22]
- Restocking fees and damage: damage to the product can void the return entirely. [L0556, 2021-22]
- For a quality or damage claim, the customer provides photo or video evidence. State that a refund is not granted simply for disliking a product; the customer can leave a review instead. [L0639, 2023]
- The 30-day money-back guarantee applies, with photo or video evidence required for quality or damage claims. [L0639, 2023]
- Keep screenshots of the terms of service, shipping policy and refund policy pages. They serve as evidence in a chargeback dispute. [L0352, 2022]
- Put a specific refund cutoff in the refund policy, such as no refunds after 14 days from delivery, and state it in the terms of service as well. [L0352, 2022]
- Publish clear terms of service, a shipping policy and a refund policy with specific conditions, such as no refunds after a set number of days. [L0352, 2022]

### Exchanges

- Exchanges are only offered for defective or damaged items. The customer must send details of the issue and proof of damage. The customer pays return shipping, which is non-refundable in the sample policy. [L1250, 2026]
- Replacements happen only for defective or damaged products. The customer emails the contact address describing the problem and attaches proof of damage. [L1250, 2026]
- Exchange shipping: "Customer responsible for return and reshipment costs" is the example input. [L0539, undated]
- Exchanges use prepaid shipping labels in the default settings for a new dropship store. [L1048, 2020-26]
- Template for returns and exchanges:

```
Returns and Exchanges

We accept returns and exchanges within [X-day] days for unused products in their original packaging. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us at [email/contact info] to start a return. We will guide you through the process and issue a refund or an exchange once we have received and checked the returned items.
```
[L1250, 2026]

### Blanket policy versus supplier exceptions

Sources disagree on whether to match each supplier individually or use one blanket policy with exceptions. Each applies in the case stated:

- Match each supplier individually: "Don't create uniform policy; match supplier policies individually." [L0268, 2020]
- Use one blanket policy with exceptions only for suppliers that do not accept returns. (2021 onwards) [L0671, 2021]
- Use one general policy for most suppliers and give unique policies to suppliers whose terms differ. (2023) [L1283, 2023]
- Use one standard 30-day return policy for the store and add exceptions only for extreme outliers. This keeps the policy simple. (2023) [L0970, 2023]
- Use a blanket return policy built from the average of your suppliers' return policies. [L0963, 2022]
- Set your return and shipping policy as an average of the supplier policies. Do not copy each supplier's policy into the store. [L0962, 2021]
- For a store with several brands, use one general return policy that works for all suppliers, or add a separate return policy tab on each product page for a brand with different terms. (2023) [L0855, 2023]

When each applies:
- Use the blanket policy when most suppliers share similar terms, and show exceptions separately. [L1283, 2023; L0801, 2021]
- Use an individual supplier policy when supplier terms differ widely, such as a supplier with no returns or a supplier with a 14-day window. [L0470, 2023; L0268, 2020]

- Supplier return terms differ in windows, restocking fees and return shipping. Because of this, a blanket policy can sometimes cost around $100 on a single return. [L0671, 2021]
- Main shipping and return policy covers most products. Any supplier with stricter terms gets its own policy on the product page, and those exclusions are also listed in the main policy. [L0801, 2021]

### Shopify return rules and settings

- Settings > Policies > Manage return rules: turn return rules on with the toggle (Turn off appears when they are active). Choose the return window: 14 days, 30 days, 90 days, Unlimited or Custom days. Choose return shipping cost: Customer provides return shipping or Flat rate return shipping. Optionally enable Charge restocking fee and define final sale items. [L1250, 2026]
- Settings > Policies > Return and cancellation rules (Defaults): set the return window to 3 days starting from when the item is delivered, with the option to extend the window to account for weekends or holidays. Make the customer pay for return shipping. Leave the restocking fee off, and note the rule applies only to items purchased after the rule is turned on or updated. [L1327, 2026]
- Return rules are enabled on the Policies page and let you set return fees, return shipping and define final sale items. Enable them before launch if you want to control returns in the store. [L1167, 2026]
- Return rules in Shopify only apply to orders placed after the rules were turned on or last updated. The written return policy page must be updated separately whenever the rules change. [L1250, 2026]
- Shopify's return rules page shows a summary, for example returns accepted for 30 days and no final sale items. The written return and refund policy must repeat the same return window and final-sale rule, otherwise customers see conflicting terms. [L1250, 2026]
- A return window set to 3 days in Shopify must be matched in the written return policy. The template text in the lesson says 14 days, so the policy text and the Shopify rule disagree if left unchanged. Edit the policy text after setting the rule so the customer sees one consistent return window. [L1327, 2026]
- Publishing a generated policy with the example numbers unchanged is a common error. Every placeholder and figure, such as the return window, processing days and delivery days, must be replaced with what the store really offers. [L1250, 2026]
- Settings > Policies: Shipping policy and Refund and return policy pages. The refund policy initially shows as "no policy set". [L1048, 2020-26]
- Conditional free shipping profiles: see Shipping and free shipping. [L1283, 2023]

### Google Merchant Center requirements

- In the Merchant Center return steps, the return method options are "By mail" with a return label, "Download and print" label, or "Customer's responsibility" for the customer to pay return shipping. Choose the option that matches the return policy on the website. If the store charges no restocking fee, select "No cost" for the restocking fee. [L0729, 2020-26]
- The Merchant Center return window can include a holiday extension, but add one only if the website's return policy states it. If the policy has no extension, keep the standard return window so the Merchant Center setting matches the website. [L0729, 2020-26]
- The refund policy must state the return address clearly when it differs from the business address (see Refunds). [L0724, 2020-26]
- The refund policy summary table ("Returns and Refunds at a Glance") is placed at the top of the refund policy page (see Refunds). [L0724, 2020-26]
- Product returns do not directly hurt Google SEO, and they have no direct effect on search rankings. [L0963, 2022]

### Chargebacks and support

- A visible support policy with a stated response time signals that complaints will be handled. This reassures customers before they buy and reduces the urge to dispute a charge with their bank. [L1250, 2026]
- Support and feedback template: "Our team is here for you. Questions, feedback or complaints about products or services can be sent through [email or contact form]. We aim to answer within [X] business days and will work to settle any problem to your satisfaction."

### Consistency across pages and emails

- Keep the return promise consistent across all emails and match it to the real policy. One email stated a 30-day money-back guarantee while another claimed 100% money back. That conflict creates a potential refund dispute. [L0567, 2025-26]
- Repeat the same wording everywhere a policy appears so customers are not confused. [L0663, 2019]
- Keep the refund and return policy consistent with the Shopify return rules, the Merchant Center setting and the product pages. Shopify rules apply only to new orders, so update the written policy whenever the rules change. [L1250, 2026; L1327, 2026; L0729, 2020-26]
- Clear product page wording about returns prevents refund disputes. Keep it specific to the supplier and product type. [L0664, 2020]
- Policy pages (shipping and terms) must exist before they are linked. Replace a temporary link with the correct page. [L1309, 2020-26]
- Keep the exact wording for a no-return supplier on every page that mentions it. [L0835, 2023]
