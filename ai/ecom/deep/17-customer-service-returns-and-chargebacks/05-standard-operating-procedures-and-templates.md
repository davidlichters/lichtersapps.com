### Standard operating procedures and templates

**Support workflow with a VA:**

1. Create reply templates for the most common messages, such as where is my package, when will it arrive and where is my tracking number. [L0519, 2020+]
2. Translate messages with DeepL (free online tool) when the assistant does not speak the customer's language. [L0519, 2020+]
3. Review the assistant's drafts before sending them, especially for general messages. [L0519, 2020+]
4. Keep separate templates for each target market. [L0519, 2020+]

- Routine questions get a direct reply from the assistant. For unusual cases, the assistant sends the email screenshot to the founder, who dictates the response. [L1432, undated]
- Staff have first responsibility for monitoring and answering emails to the business's customer email addresses. The owner monitors these accounts in the staff member's absence. Emails addressed to the owner by name must be forwarded to the owner's personal email. [L0404, undated]

### Escalation when the answer is unknown

- When you cannot answer a customer's question by phone, chat or email, do one or more of the following, as relevant, in this order:
  1. Contact the business owner through the agreed contact methods. If the owner is available, they will either answer the question or ask you to forward the customer to them. [L0404, undated]
  2. If the owner is not immediately available, tell the customer you will get back to them shortly, take their contact details, and reply as soon as you can. [L0404, undated]
  3. If the owner is not available and you need product information, first check the supplier's webpage. If the information is not there, call the supplier and ask. [L0404, undated]
  4. Do not rush. Reply to the customer once you have the correct information, since a short delay of around 10 minutes is acceptable. [L0404, undated]
- To find product information for a customer, the VA follows this order:
  1. Search Google for the answer. [L1430, 2020-26]
  2. If the answer is not found, take the customer's question and contact details, and pass them to a sales rep contact number or email. [L1430, 2020-26]
  3. The sales rep then calls the customer back once the needed information is available. [L1430, 2020-26]

### Agent training and knowledge base

- Customer service agents are usually the customer's first contact with the company, so improving agent training is typically the first and most powerful step to raise overall satisfaction. [L0401, 2025-26]
- Build an internal knowledge base or wiki for agents containing a guide to the brand voice, canned responses for common scenarios such as angry customers and refund requests, and upselling scripts. [L0401, 2025-26]
- Support agents hear the most direct customer complaints, so ask the support team for their view on what causes low satisfaction. Their input on customer needs is one of the most useful sources for changing how support is delivered and for improving the wider purchase experience. [L0401, 2025-26]
- Check customer questions and contact requests by reviewing chat and phone activity during the first months of a new hire's training, and send corrections privately as needed. Watching the chat app is the simplest way to see the answers given to customers. This helps catch misspellings and rude replies before customers notice. [L1396, 2021-22]

### Quality rubric

- A quality rubric for support replies defines the key elements a high-quality response must have and gives an objective framework for scoring response quality. Typical criteria are response time, alignment with company policy, brand tone and voice, plus any other elements the business considers essential. [L0401, 2025-26]
- Judge the rubric against the actual customer outcome too. A response can score well on tone (for example, playful humor) yet still fail to produce a clear, satisfying interaction. [L0401, 2025-26]

### Response times and KPIs

| Channel | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat, first response | Within 30 seconds [L1111, 2020] | |
| Email, baseline | Reply within 24 hours; if there is no reply, follow up, because the email may have gone to spam or the wrong inbox [L0359, 2020] | |
| Email, staffed window | Set window such as 30 minutes [L1111, 2020] | |
| Email, too slow | A reply in 5 business days is too slow for an active dropshipping student [L1452, 2026] | |
| Phone | Answer 95% of inbound calls [L1111, 2020] | |

- The sources give two email targets. Use 24 hours as the baseline rule for every support email and the trigger for a follow-up. Use the 30-minute window where a dedicated person covers the inbox during staffed hours. [L0359, 2020; L1111, 2020]
- Track customer support response times and set a target time for every channel, such as tickets, phone calls and live chat. [L0962, 2021]
- Set KPIs for each support channel: chat response time, email response time and phone answer rate. Where phone support is part of the job, track the percentage of calls converted to sales. [L1111, 2020]
