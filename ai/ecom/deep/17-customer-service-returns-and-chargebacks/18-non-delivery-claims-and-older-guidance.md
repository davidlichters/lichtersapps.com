### Non-delivery claims and older guidance

**Procedure: contest a non-delivery chargeback**

- A chargeback claiming non-delivery can usually be won with tracking that shows the item was delivered, so submit the shipping information and tracking proof to the bank. [L0466, 2018] **
- To contest a non-delivery chargeback, upload evidence such as a tracking number that shows the order was delivered. The bank or card network then decides whether the merchant keeps the money or the customer is refunded. [L1086, 2019] **

**Procedure: non-delivery and partial shipment**

1. Submit all documentation to the bank or card company, including shipping information and tracking showing delivery. [L0466, 2018] **
2. Contact the customer in ways that can be documented, such as email, not only by phone. [L0466, 2018] **
3. Email the customer about any outstanding item, with its status and options such as shipping it later or a partial refund. [L0466, 2018] **
4. Keep records of every contact attempt, including unanswered calls and voicemails. [L0466, 2018] **

**Procedure: contest a chargeback**

To contest a chargeback: [L0352, 2022]

1. Check whether the customer already emailed and answer those messages if possible. [L0352, 2022]
2. Open the dispute form that Shopify provides. [L0352, 2022]
3. Submit proof that the order shipped, including tracking. [L0352, 2022]
4. Attach screenshots of the terms of service, shipping policy and refund policy that show the customer agreed to them. [L0352, 2022]
5. For damage claims, state that items are quality checked and shipped in good condition. [L0352, 2022]

**Procedure: one source response flow**

1. Check email regularly and respond quickly to any customer who raises a problem or threatens a chargeback. [L0639, 2023]
2. Ask what went wrong and offer a fix first, such as a replacement or a resend. [L0639, 2023]
3. If the customer still wants money back, offer a partial refund of about 20%, or up to 50%. [L0639, 2023]
4. Only offer a full refund if the customer escalates publicly on social media or another platform, where a dispute can cause more harm. [L0639, 2023]
5. If a chargeback is filed, submit the shipping details, a tracking number, an order screenshot, the refund and return policy, and email chats showing that the transaction was approved and the customer was contacted. [L0639, 2023]
6. In the chargeback response, state the evidence and explain what you are currently doing to resolve the situation. [L0639, 2023]
