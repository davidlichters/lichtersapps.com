## Support tone, soft skills and handling difficult customers

### Support mindset and tone

- Customer service drives both repeat purchases and five-star reviews. Reviews act as a trust factor that raises the website conversion rate, so service quality directly affects sales. Communication should always be quick, truthful and friendly, across phone, email, chat and social media. [L0404, undated]
- Empathy is the most important skill in customer service. Understand the customer's situation from their side, listen fully, and reflect back what you heard before you act. [L0909, 2021-22]
- Stay friendly and patient, even with rude customers. Basic courtesy, such as not talking over the customer and not ignoring their message, matters more than any technique. [L0909, 2021-22]
- Greet every customer warmly, ask what problem you can solve for them, and build a genuine personal connection. Customers who feel cared for are more understanding when problems appear during the order. [L1472, 2022]
- Remember details from earlier conversations, such as names and past topics, and use them in later contacts. This familiarity matters most for repeat buyers. [L1472, 2022]
- Keep a consistently positive tone with every customer, because a competitor selling the same product is often only one click away. Service is what sets a store apart when the product and price are similar. [L1472, 2022]
- Be empathetic by imagining you are the customer waiting for a product that is four weeks late with no clear date. Frustration is natural in that situation, so acknowledge it in every reply. [L1472, 2022]
- Even when a delay is caused by a supplier, carrier or processing time outside your control, you remain responsible for solving it. Respond with empathy and focus on the solution. [L1472, 2022]
- Customers often accept a long wait or a problem as long as you communicate regularly and stay polite. Rudeness is the main thing that makes customers leave, not the problem itself. [L1472, 2022]
- Service that does not lead to a sale still has value, because it builds goodwill and a human connection. Listening to a customer who needs to talk can keep them loyal for future purchases. [L1472, 2022]
- Do not sell products you do not carry, but still listen to the customer and help where you can. Being a supportive point of contact can keep the customer loyal even when the sale does not happen. [L1472, 2022]
- Treat your customers with respect and aim to improve their lives, since the best dropshipping businesses excel at service. Customer relationships are where you can separate yourself from competitors. [L1480, 2021-22]

### Chat and proactive contact

- Staff may start chats with website visitors when they have time, but this is optional. [L0404, undated]
- If a visitor does not reply to the first message, do not send a follow-up, so customers do not feel harassed. [L0404, undated]

### Never guess, and admit what you don't know

- Never guess or make up answers to customer questions. Give information only once it has been confirmed through the owner or the supplier. [L0404, undated]
- Never invent an answer to a product or technical question. If the customer receives wrong information and finds out after delivery, you will have to pay to fix the problem. [L0909, 2021-22]
- If you do not know a product detail, do not guess. Tell the customer you will check with the manufacturer and call back with accurate information, then use the answer to update your product page. [L0934, 2023]
- Admitting when you do not know an answer and following up with the correct information builds trust. Customers appreciate accurate information, and the owner gains a stronger knowledge base in the niche over time. [L1094, 2023]

Phone reply when you do not know the answer:

```
Good question, [customer name]. I don't know the answer at the moment, so I'll consult our team and return your call as quickly as possible. May I have your phone number and email address?
```

Can I get your name, phone number and email address? I will follow up with you as soon as I have the answer, because I do not want to give you incorrect information.

#### Handling a question you cannot answer

1. Tell the customer you need to check the answer, and do not guess.
2. Offer a callback within a stated time, such as five or ten minutes, or put them on hold if the phone system allows it.
3. Check the answer with your supplier, by phone or on the supplier's website.
4. Call the customer back and give them the confirmed answer.

[L0909, 2021-22]

Holding reply:

```
Thanks for asking. I want to confirm that exactly before I answer, so you get accurate information. Could I call you back in five minutes?
```
[L0909, 2021-22]

### Voicemail and callbacks

- Sending callers to voicemail loses sales, because callers simply phone the next seller on their list. Customers have said they bought because someone answered the phone, so answered calls are a cheap sales safeguard. [L0337, 2022]
- Voicemail greeting:

```
Hello, you've reached [business name]. We are busy right now and cannot take your call immediately, and we don't want you to have to wait on hold. To use our callback service, leave your name and your contact details after the tone, and we will call you back as soon as we can. Thank you for your patience.
```
[L0909, 2021-22]

#### Handling a voicemail callback

1. Receive the SMS or email notification that a message has been left.
2. Listen to the message and note the caller's name and what they need.
3. Call the customer back as soon as you are able to.

[L0909, 2021-22]

### When to call customers

- Avoid calling customers early in the morning. Use the evening and night hours instead, and in most countries keep calls before about 8 p.m. [L0909, 2021-22]
- Evening callbacks work because customers are usually at home at that time and can actually talk. Calling after a day of work also fits a side-business owner's schedule. [L0909, 2021-22]

### Difficult and angry customers

- Do not return an angry caller's hostility with hostility, and do not block customers in anger. A single heated reaction can turn a fixable problem into a lost customer and a public complaint. [L1472, 2022]
- Angry customers often vent about problems in their own life, so their anger is rarely about the store itself. Stay calm, train yourself to keep your composure, and still follow your own process. [L1472, 2022]
- Some customers will stay unreasonable no matter how well you respond, and that is not your fault. As long as you complete your own steps correctly, their reaction does not change the correct response. [L1472, 2022]
- Do not take personal offense at customers who react badly to a sensible response. Document each step so you can show you handled the situation correctly. [L1472, 2022]

#### Handling objections that products ship from China

- Some customers object that products come from China. This is rare, and it can usually be handled by giving the tracking number and finding a solution with the customer. [L1347, undated]

1. When a customer objects that the product ships from China, first send the tracking number, since customers often ask for it.
2. If they remain upset, point out that many everyday consumer electronics, such as iPhones, are also made or shipped from China.
3. If the problem is serious, offer a solution.

[L1347, undated]

## Reply templates and scripts for pre-purchase questions

### Speed and first response before purchase

- Being the first store to answer a pre-purchase question often decides the sale. Fast responses are a direct sales lever, and speed and quality of service can beat competitors who sell the same product. [L0948, 2020-26]
- Replying within minutes can raise sales, because a quick answer to a product question can convert a visitor into a buyer. Check messages from your phone so questions are not left waiting. [L0661, undated]
- Shoppers who call before buying to check stock or get an answer often choose the store that responds. Answering the phone can win sales that other stores lose. [L0966, 2025]
- Phone calls from prospective customers, such as asking whether an item is in stock, are a normal part of selling higher-priced products. Answer or return them as soon as possible. [L0835, 2023]
- Pre-purchase questions from website visitors typically arrive by phone, live chat or email. They are a daily task to automate or delegate. Post-purchase follow-up questions come through the same channels and need the same handling. [L0592, 2020-26]
- Shoppers fear buying online and then receiving no answer from customer service. Only promise fast customer service if you can actually reply quickly. [L1207, undated]

### Answering known questions in chat

- When an answer is known, give it right away in the chat to close the sale. Shoppers who ask pre-purchase questions should receive a fast reply without extra effort from the store owner. [L0948, 2020-26]
- Example: a shopper asks how fast stickers ship, and the store replies "They ship within two to three days." Quick, specific shipping times answer purchase hesitation directly. [L0948, 2020-26]
- Most customer service questions are simple and concern delivery time, shipping time or discounts. Prepare clear answers to these first. [L0909, 2021-22] **
- Pre-written answers can be stored in the live chat app, so customers get an immediate reply about a specific product, including shipping time and usage. This is useful when you expect repeated questions about one product. [L1428, undated]
- Reply to a chat question you cannot answer yet with the following text, then send the answer as a follow-up once it is ready:

```
When a chat question comes in and you don't know the answer yet, respond right away by saying it's a good question, that you're looking into it, and that you'll reply as soon as possible. Once the answer is ready, send it as a follow-up.
```
[L0948, 2020-26]

### Handling a question you cannot answer yet

#### Standard procedure (phone or chat)

1. Receive a customer question you cannot answer immediately. [L0276, 2024]
2. Respond: "That's a great question. I want to make sure you get an accurate answer. May I have your name, email, and phone number?" [L0276, 2024]
3. Contact your supplier with the customer's exact question. [L0276, 2024]
4. Collect the supplier's answer. [L0276, 2024]
5. Reply to the customer with the accurate response. [L0276, 2024]
6. Update your product page with this information for future customers. [L0276, 2024]

#### Supplier-verified variant

1. Tell the customer you want to confirm the answer and get the correct information. [L0972, 2025]
2. Collect their name, email address and phone number if the question came by phone. [L0972, 2025]
3. Ask your supplier rep the question directly, stating the customer's needs and quantities. [L0972, 2025]
4. Send the verified answer to the customer. [L0972, 2025]
5. If the question is likely to come up again, add the answer to the product page as an FAQ or in the description. [L0972, 2025]

Use this reply when a customer asks a product question you cannot answer yet:

```
That's a great question, and I want to make sure I give you the correct answer. Could you please send me your name, email address and phone number? I will check directly with the manufacturer and get right back to you.
```
[L0972, 2025]

#### Product question not answerable from the product page

1. Tell the customer you do not have the answer right now, and take their name, phone number and email. [L0805, 2024]
2. Contact the supplier rep (or email the supplier) with the product model number and the customer's question. [L0805, 2024]
3. Call the customer back or reply with the answer. [L0805, 2024]
4. Add the answer to the product page so future customers see it. [L0805, 2024]

#### Principles for supplier questions

- Expect customer questions on products you did not know before. Ask the supplier for the answer, then add frequent questions to the product page. [L0806, 2024]
- Each store owner needs to build product knowledge in the niche. Questions from customers should be answered by checking with the supplier, then published on the product page. [L0805, 2024]
- Phone and support answers for product questions should follow a lesson script. A sales phone call should never end without a follow-up: the customer should be called back with an answer rather than left waiting. [L0971, 2024]

### Responding to pre-purchase hesitation

- Using fear, threats or crime statistics to push a purchase repels most buyers. Instead, let the buyer recognize the need, then show the product points they care about most, such as reliability and ease of installation. [L1366, undated]

### FAQ building and product-page content

#### Building the FAQ

1. Answer every customer question by email or phone, then add that question and answer to the FAQ. [L0910, 2015]
2. Keep building the FAQ over the first months of the store. [L0910, 2015]
3. Make new customer service staff read and memorize the FAQ during onboarding, because these are the questions they will receive. [L0910, 2015]
4. Link customers to the FAQ so they can find answers without calling or emailing. [L0910, 2015]
5. Where possible, add the answer to the product description or to a staff note so the question does not come up again. [L0910, 2015]

#### What the FAQ page should cover

- The FAQ page should collect the questions customers ask repeatedly. Cover price guarantee, product guarantee, shipping policy, returns and support. If one visitor has a question, many others probably do too. [L1130, 2020-26]

#### Preparing support for a team

*The following two units describe the same procedure and are merged here.*

1. List the questions customers are likely to ask, both before and after purchase. [L1358, undated; L1360, undated]
2. Write a clear, polished answer for each question. [L1358, undated; L1360, undated]
3. Put the questions and answers into PDFs (compile them into a PDF document). [L1358, undated; L1360, undated]
4. Let the support team copy and paste the answers to customers. [L1358, undated; L1360, undated]

#### Turning questions into product-page content

- Turn customer questions into product page updates so future visitors find the answers without calling. [L0934, 2023]
- Product pages should give customers all information they need before buying, so the store reduces customer inquiries over time. [L0964, 2023]
- Write detailed FAQs on each product page for every recurring customer question, so the answer is available before the customer has to ask. [L1515, 2019]
- Write content that answers questions a buyer has before buying, such as whether to offer a phone number, and link those questions to your products. This keeps the blog focused on purchase decisions rather than general interest. [L0382, 2017]

### B2B and wholesale discount requests

- Standard reply for a B2B or wholesale buyer who wants a discount: create a multi-use discount code named after the business, give it only to that buyer, and set the percent off as the B2B price. [L0472, 2025]

Reply template:

```
Thank you for your question. That's a great question, and I want to make sure I give you the correct answer. Could you please send me your name, email address and phone number? I will look into this and get back to you with the answer as soon as I have it.
```
[L0472, 2025]

### MAP discount requests

Procedure for customers who request a discount on products with strict MAP policies:

1. Acknowledge their request professionally. [L0251, 2023]
2. Explain that the price is set by the manufacturer to protect brand value and that this is industry-wide. [L0251, 2023]
3. Offer alternative value: same-day shipping if possible, expedited service, or reference other benefits. [L0251, 2023]
4. If you cannot offer discounts or special terms, maintain confidence in the price and emphasize service quality and expertise. [L0251, 2023]
