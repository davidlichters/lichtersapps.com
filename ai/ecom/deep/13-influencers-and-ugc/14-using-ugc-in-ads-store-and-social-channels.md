## Using UGC in Ads, Store and Social Channels

### Paid ads: why UGC works

- UGC means real customer material, such as reviews and photos of people using the product. It builds trust and works especially well in retargeting ads, where it answers the doubts of people who already saw the product. [L0648, undated]
- Meta favors content that looks natural. Over-produced ads still run but cost more, so real customer photos and videos, or phone-shot footage, often perform better. [L0649, 2019]
- Use real customer videos or photos in retargeting where possible. A customer describing their experience often outperforms a brand message. [L0649, 2019]
- Spark Ads from influencer posts tend to outperform regular brand creatives because they look like organic content from a trusted creator rather than a sales message. This lowers viewer resistance and makes the ad feel more credible. [L1374, undated]

### Paid ads: where the traffic goes

- Turn collected UGC into paid ads. Cold audiences convert poorly on direct product pages, so send paid traffic to the influencer's profile or to a lead magnet page instead of the product page. [L0789, 2022]

| Audience | Creative | Send traffic to | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold | UGC video ads with real creators | Influencer's profile or lead magnet page | [L0789, 2022] |
| Retargeting (people who already saw the product) | Real customer videos or photos | Not specified in the source | [L0648, undated; L0649, 2019] |

### Paid ads: whitelisting and brand pages

- Brand pages perform well with UGC video ads that feature real creators, sourced from TikTok Shop creator programs or official Instagram influencers. [L0906, 2026]
- Whitelisting means running these ads through a creator or persona page instead of the brand's own page. [L0906, 2026]
- The brand's page can still host the UGC content, so both approaches are used together. [L0906, 2026]

### Paid ads: boosting organic content

- Paid traffic such as Coins or Spark Ads on a brand-new account can speed up reaching the right audience during the new account advantage window, which lasts roughly the first one to two weeks. Use this boost only on new accounts. [L1374, undated]
- Do not boost old accounts with established organic performance. Boosting old successful organic posts has been reported to kill organic sales. [L1374, undated]
- Use organic videos as ad creatives only if they already reached strong views (around 50,000 to 100,000) and carry real social proof. Very new organic videos can lose organic reach when boosted through Spark Ads. [L1377, 2022+]
- Both conditions apply together: the view threshold decides which videos qualify, and the account-age rule decides whether boosting is safe at all. [L1374, undated; L1377, 2022+]

### Paid ads: borrowed clips and copyright

- Using UGC clips taken from other brands or TikTok for ads carries a risk of copyright strikes. Keep this short term, such as a couple of weeks at 10 USD per ad set per day (50 USD total), and replace it with your own UGC once the product gains traction. [L0693, 2024]

### Ad iteration: fixing low-attention ads

- Ads with low attention can be improved rather than discarded. Layer a UGC video with a voice-over or new hooks, because a single hook change can lift attention and sales. [L1426, undated]

### Converting winning UGC into other formats

- For books, UGC AI ads produced higher purchase intent and better traction than static image ads in the tests. Static image ads still work, but UGC AI ads are the better default for this product type. [L1030, 2026]
- Convert a proven UGC AI ad concept back to image ads, in this order:
  1. Confirm the UGC AI concept has produced many purchases and steady profit. [L1030, 2026]
  2. Build image ads that use the same concept, angle and pain point. [L1030, 2026]
  3. Run the image ads alongside the UGC AI ads and compare soft and hard metrics. [L1030, 2026]

### Product pages and testimonial sections

- Place customer photos in a dedicated section on the product page, not mixed into supplier product photos. This section is one of the most important elements for conversion. [L1086, 2019]
- Customer photos also work in blog posts where a product image fits. A standalone customer photo gallery page is a weaker use of the photos than product pages and emails. [L1086, 2019]
- In testimonial sections and product images, use raw UGC photos of real customers in real-life settings instead of high-quality studio shots. Visitors relate more to real people, and the lesson reports that such content converts better than polished lifestyle images. [L0916, 2022+]

### Review proof blocks

- Pattern: place review screenshots from the store, a marketplace or a product page over a blurred version of the product, and show the store URL in the same frame. It works because it puts several genuine reviews in one image while the product remains visible in the background. [L0398, 2023+]
  - Example 1: five screenshots of five-star reviews with the URL of the store at the bottom. [L0398, 2023+]
  - Example 2: three reviews from a marketplace over a blurred kitchen counter with the product. [L0398, 2023+]

### Messenger social proof

- Formula: name a real customer with their location, state that they like the product, and mention one specific benefit. Real names and places increase trust more than generic praise. [L1243, 2017]
  - Example 1: Maria from Ohio told us this kettle heats up in under three minutes, and she uses it every morning. [L1243, 2017]
  - Example 2: Dan from Texas wrote that his backpack survived a full semester of daily use. [L1243, 2017]

### Video bubbles on the website

- Embed a bubble group on the website, as follows:
  1. Create the video campaigns that will be shown as bubbles. [L0951, 2025-26]
  2. Group the campaigns together into one bubble group (playlist) per topic. [L0951, 2025-26]
  3. Embed the bubble group on the website. [L0951, 2025-26]
  4. Customize the bubbles to match the look and feel of the website. [L0951, 2025-26]
  5. Update the content later without re-embedding, because embedded bubbles are fully dynamic. [L0951, 2025-26]
  6. Reuse any video that is already embedded in email marketing campaigns. [L0951, 2025-26]

### Shoppable social galleries

- Add a carousel of social content to the website, as follows:
  1. Pull the content directly from your Instagram or TikTok account onto the website. [L0951, 2025-26]
  2. Tag each video with the specific products shown in it. [L0951, 2025-26]
  3. Place the carousel on the home page, collection pages or product pages, where it works like an endless scroll for product discovery. [L0951, 2025-26]
  4. Let the customer check out from the carousel with a single click. [L0951, 2025-26]

### Scaling case: UGC as the main lever

- Starting point: a brand scaled mainly on bottom-of-funnel static ads for about two years, with three UGC videos. [L1476, 2026]
- Change: UGC video was introduced a couple of months earlier. [L1476, 2026]
- Result: monthly revenue rose about 5x with both formats combined. [L1476, 2026]
- Conclusion: UGC is the main scaling lever, while bottom-of-funnel statics help at small scale or on top of existing UGC. [L1476, 2026]

## Tracking, Measuring and Iterating

### Measuring Influencer Results: Principles and Priorities

- Tracking traffic from influencer partnerships is the most critical step in determining profitability. It shows whether a partnership returns enough to continue long-term. Some influencers become so profitable that they stay active advertising partners for multiple years. [L0183, undated]
- Track referrals from brand ambassadors as their own traffic source, separate from paid social and search. This traffic is expected to be of higher quality than cold paid traffic, and it can feed back into the top of the funnel to create more customers. [L0676, 2020-26]
- Judge TikTok performance over several posts rather than a single post, because engagement on influencer content can rise and fall sharply: attention spans are short and trends move quickly, so expect results to fluctuate. [L0480, undated]
- Organic Instagram posting that reached over 200 followers within a week signals content interest, but it is not sales evidence on its own. [L1016, 2026]
- A cited survey claims that 86% of businesses using influencer marketing effectively report an immediate or long-term effect on their brand. The source and the exact definition of "effective" are not stated, so treat this figure as a rough indication only. [L0786, undated]

### Platform Analytics and Engagement Data

- Check the built-in analytics of the platform for each influencer post:
  - Instagram Insights
  - Facebook Insights
  - Twitter Analytics
  - YouTube Analytics
- Record for each post: comments, shares, likes and interaction rate. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
- Ask each influencer to send their engagement figures (comments, shares, likes, interaction rate) for the campaign posts, so you can compare them with your own platform data. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]

### Influencer Platforms and Campaign Analytics

- Influencer marketing platforms such as Influency, Aspire IQ and Tracker provide detailed campaign analytics, including reach, engagement and metrics specific to each collaboration. [L0630, undated]
- These platforms are most useful when the whole campaign is managed through them. [L0786, undated]
- If the campaign ran through such a platform, pull its analytics for reach, engagement and collaboration-specific metrics. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]

### Brand Mentions and Audience Feedback

- Track brand mentions over the campaign period, either with a social listening tool or by manually searching for the brand, keyword, hashtag or campaign name. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
- Survey the audience and collect feedback (surveys, social media polls) to find out where they first heard of the influencer and how they perceive the campaign. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]

### Sales, Conversion and Unique Codes

- Track sales and conversion rate for each campaign. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
- Give each creator a unique discount code, ideally built from their name, so that sales can be traced back to that creator. A 25% code was affordable at that brand's margins. Any unique code, even free shipping, serves the same tracking purpose. [L0790, 2017]
- Coupon codes unique to each influencer track campaign results and strengthen the relationship with the creator. Any coupon works, even free shipping, as long as it is tied to a specific influencer. [L0790, 2017]
- Use one identical spelling of each creator name across all ads. Consistent creator names allow reports per creator and make creator commission payments easier to match. [L0885, 2025-26]
- An influencer post that does not produce a sale can still be valuable if it earns a follower, an email or a visit that can be retargeted. Plan this fallback before paying for the post. [L0790, 2017]

### Campaign Evaluation Sequence

Measure each influencer campaign in this order:

1. Check the platform's built-in analytics for each influencer post (Instagram Insights, Facebook Insights, Twitter Analytics, YouTube Analytics). [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
2. Record comments, shares, likes and interaction rate. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
3. Ask the influencer to send their engagement numbers as well. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
4. Monitor brand and hashtag mentions with a listening tool, or search manually for the brand, keyword or campaign name. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
5. Pull detailed campaign metrics from the influencer marketing platform, if one was used. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
6. Track sales and conversion metrics. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
7. Survey the audience. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
8. Compare before and after the campaign. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
9. Compare results with your other marketing channels. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]

### Deciding Which Partners to Repeat

- Use the results and audience insights from each campaign to decide which influencer strategies to repeat. [L0868, undated]
- Choose future partners and formats based on what has resonated most with your audience. [L0868, undated]
- Use the sequence above on every campaign before deciding, so that comparisons across creators rest on the same measurements. [L0630, undated; L0786, undated]
- Influencers whose traffic and sales prove profitable can remain active advertising partners for multiple years. [L0183, undated]

### TikTok ad testing

Procedure for launching a test campaign:

1. Create a new campaign named after the product plus "testing one" and choose ABO.
2. Create the first ad group named "Open 1" with the external website option and select your pixel.
3. Set the conversion event to complete payments and set placement to TikTok only.
4. Turn off comments and video download.
5. Select the countries (United States and Canada for the standard setup) and the language English.
6. Choose the gender: all, or female for beauty products.
7. Choose the age group: 18+ for open targeting, or 18 to 24 for teen-oriented products.
8. Check the audience size; for open targeting in the US and Canada it is often above 100 million.
9. Set the start to the next day at midnight, with the schedule all day or from 7 a.m. to midnight.
10. Set optimization to lowest cost.
11. Select the identity: brand name and logo, or your own account if it has engaging videos.
12. Upload the creative and add the ad text.
13. Set the call-to-action button to Shop Now and paste the UTM URL.
14. Submit and publish the campaign.
15. Duplicate the ad group to create the other ad groups, changing only the audience.

### Pinterest group boards

Procedure for using group boards as a traffic source:

1. Build a strong Pinterest profile with relevant boards so it looks credible when you contact group board owners.
2. Find group boards in your niche, for example through the BoardDeckHQ.com directory, and get access to pin on them.
3. Post your product and content pins to the group boards.
4. Drive traffic from the group board pins back to your store.
5. Retarget the visitors who arrive from the group boards.

[L1136, 2014]

### Customer collection letters

- Put a real customer testimonial in every letter, including collection letters. An unpaid bill often reflects doubt about value rather than refusal to pay. [L1369, undated]
- Use the quote to restate value for the customer. [L1369, undated]

### Coaching calls

- Use two one-on-one coaching calls each month. [L0820, undated]
- Unused calls do not roll over. Book them before the month ends, because they reset on the first of the next month. [L0820, undated]
