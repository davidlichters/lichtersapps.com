## Affiliate and Ambassador Programs

### Recruiting Affiliates

#### Where to Find Affiliates

- Start with your Dream 100 prospect list and look for potential affiliates there. [L0202, 2021-22]
- Search Google directly for additional affiliates in your market. [L0202, 2021-22]
- Evaluate whether each candidate is relevant to your market and products. [L0202, 2021-22]
- Reach out by email (using templates) or by phone call. [L0202, 2021-22]
- Present your affiliate program offer. [L0202, 2021-22]
- Affiliate partners sometimes run offers as banner ads, which you can find during Dream 100 outreach or through searches on forums and Google. [L0288, 2021-22]
- A prospect who already runs affiliate deals on a site, YouTube channel or other platform can be added to your affiliate program. They earn a commission per sale and gain new offers to share with their audience. [L0525, 2021-22]
- Use an affiliate or commission plan to reward influencers and buyers who promote the product with a referral code, to keep fans engaged after the first purchase. [L0789, 2022]
- Wholesale accounts such as hotels, cafes and shops can share the product with their own followers, which is a free channel for reaching buyers. Ask each new wholesale partner to promote the product and to let you show their logo on the website. [L1016, 2026]

#### Finding Content Creators via Google

**Principle**

- Affiliate content creators produce material for the information-gathering stage of the buyer journey (reviews, how-to guides, top-10 lists) rather than commercial-intent keywords, because substantial content performs better with research-phase audiences. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Search procedure**

1. Search Google using research-stage keywords: "best [product type]" and "top 10 [product type]" to locate content creators reviewing or comparing products in your category. [L0215, 2021-22]
2. Examine the results for review and comparison guide websites. [L0215, 2021-22]
3. Click through to these sites to identify which products they recommend and whether they use affiliate links. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Mapping the full landscape**

- Test multiple search term variations on Google, such as "best [product]", "top 10 [product]" and related modifiers, to identify all affiliate review and comparison sites active in your product category and geographic market. This comprehensive mapping reveals the full landscape of potential affiliate partners. [L0215, 2021-22]
- To map the landscape, search with these variations:
  1. "best [product type]"
  2. "top 10 [product type]"
  3. "[product type] reviews"
  4. "[product type] comparison"

  For price-sensitive categories, also search "best [product type] under $[price]" (e.g., "best headphones under $200"). Review the first 2-3 pages of results for each search to identify all major affiliate publishers in your niche. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Evaluating candidates**

- Look for reviews comparing multiple products (typically 7 or more), with detailed feature breakdowns, score ratings, and pros/cons for each product. High-effort, comprehensive content indicates a serious publisher worth approaching; thin comparison pages are lower priority. [L0215, 2021-22]
- Major media publications (e.g., New York Times, PC Magazine) frequently rank in "best [product]" searches but typically do not use visible affiliate offers due to editorial policies. Focus outreach on independent review sites and content creators where you can confirm active affiliate links, rather than spending effort on large publishers. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Confirming an active affiliate link**

1. Hover your mouse over the "See Price" button or product link and read the URL shown in the bottom-left corner of the browser. [L0215, 2021-22]
2. If the URL is unclear, right-click the link and select "Inspect" or "Inspect Element". [L0215, 2021-22]
3. In the HTML code, locate the `href=` attribute containing the full destination URL. [L0215, 2021-22]
4. Look for an affiliate tag parameter in the URL (e.g., "tag=rtings" for Amazon or a similar pattern for other platforms). [L0215, 2021-22]
5. If a tag parameter is present, the link is active. If the URL ends at the product ID with no additional parameters, it is not an affiliate link. [L0215, 2021-22]

- Affiliate links contain a tag parameter in the URL that identifies the affiliate partner and tracks commissions. Amazon affiliate links use the format `tag=storeidentifier`. Links without this parameter are not affiliate links. [L0215, 2021-22]
- In Amazon affiliate links, the text after `tag=` is the affiliate's identifier and activates the affiliate commission tracking system (e.g., `tag=rtings`). [L0215, 2021-22]

**Outreach template: message to a potential affiliate** [L0215, 2021-22]

```
Hi [Name],

I came across your [article title/topic] and was impressed by your thorough analysis of [number] products in this category.

I noticed you're currently promoting products through [their affiliate program, e.g., Amazon Associates]. I sell [product category or specific products from their review], and I can offer you commission rates that are competitive with or better than what you're earning through [their current program].

[Add one of these based on your situation:]
- I carry the exact same products you reviewed.
- We offer similar high-quality products that fit perfectly with your audience.
- Our products align with the criteria you outlined in your review.

Would you be open to discussing a partnership? I'm happy to share details about commission rates, cookie duration, and partner support.

Looking forward to connecting.

[Your Name]
```

- When contacting a content creator you have identified, propose a partnership by mentioning that you sell the same or comparable products and offer affiliate rates that may be better than or competitive with their current Amazon or alternative program rates. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Negotiation**

- After pitching a potential affiliate, expect negotiation. They may accept your terms, request adjustments to commission or cookie duration, or decline. Treat each conversation professionally regardless of outcome, since affiliate relationships matter and a rejection today may become a partnership later. [L0215, 2021-22]

#### Recruiting in Facebook Groups

**Procedure**

1. Go to Facebook and search for groups focused on affiliate marketing. [L0215, 2021-22]
2. Join established affiliate-marketing communities that permit merchant recruitment posts. [L0215, 2021-22]
3. Post your affiliate offer in the group. [L0215, 2021-22]
4. Respond to interested affiliates who comment or send direct messages. [L0215, 2021-22]
5. Share your affiliate program details with qualified partners. [L0215, 2021-22]

- Before posting, check the group's pinned posts or description to confirm that merchant recruitment posts are allowed. Some groups restrict posting frequency, require specific formatting, or prohibit direct links; violating these rules can result in post removal or removal from the group. [L0215, 2021-22]

**Post template** [L0215, 2021-22]

```
Hi everyone,

We're recruiting affiliates to promote [product category] to their audiences.

**Affiliate offer details:**
- Product category: [Specific type, e.g., over-ear headphones, fitness trackers]
- Price point: $[price range]
- Commission: [X]% per sale
- Cookie duration: [X] days
- Tracking platform: [Affiliate software name, e.g., Refersion, Impact]

If you think your audience would be interested, send me a DM and I'll get you all the details to get started.

Thanks!
```

- When posting an affiliate offer in a Facebook group, include: the product type or vertical, the retail selling price, the affiliate commission rate you are offering, the cookie duration and tracking system details, and a clear call-to-action directing interested affiliates to message you for program information. [L0215, 2021-22]

#### Affiliates Who Buy Traffic

- Some affiliates buy their own paid traffic and treat the program as a business. They judge a program by whether the commission leaves them profit on offers that convert, not by the size of the catalog. [L0719, 2021-22]

### Commission Rates and Cookies

#### Rate Ranges and Benchmarks

| Source | Figure | Context |
|---|---|---|
| one source (2020) | 5-10% standard | Set toward the lower end unless the affiliate promotes you heavily [L0961, 2020] |
| one source | 3-7% | Used by some stores; affiliate programs were used rarely and depended on the store's profit [L1088, 2020] |
| one source (2023) | 5% or 7% | Per sale made through the partner's link [L0528, 2025+] |
| one source (2023) | Up to 30% | Can be considered, but affiliates are hard to win early because a new store has no reputation [L0934, 2023] |
| one source (2022) | About 8-10% plus a free product | Needed to recruit influencers or sites through an affiliate app, because a link alone generates no sales [L1113, 2022] |
| one source | 10-15% of revenue | Per sale from a unique link; track with Shopify Collabs or another affiliate platform [L1213, 2023] |

- Where sources disagree, the values apply to different situations: 5-10% (2020) and 5-7% (2023) fit standard programs, 8-10% plus a free product (2022) fits recruiting through an app where a link alone brings nothing, up to 30% (2023) fits only a case where an affiliate is won with a strong offer, and 10-15% fits a revenue-based ambassador setup. one source's 2020 and 2022 advice is older. [L0961, 2020; L0528, 2025+; L0934, 2023; L1113, 2022; L1213, 2023; L1088, 2020]

#### Tiered Rates by Partner Status

- Affiliate commission structure: 6% for new affiliate partners, 7% for long-term partners who have consistently performed. [L0226, 2017]
- Long-term influencer partners earn passive revenue: articles they wrote years ago continue generating income through affiliate commissions and ad placements. Paying 7% commission to experienced partners versus 6% to newcomers acknowledges the compounding value from evergreen content. [L0226, 2017]

#### Setting the Rate by Promotion

- Set affiliate commissions based on how the affiliate promotes you, such as email to their list, sidebar placements, articles, social media or a footer link. The more prominent the promotion, the higher the justified rate. [L0961, 2020]

#### Cookie Duration

- Cookie duration is stated in every offer (e.g., "Cookie duration: [X] days") and must be included in outreach and group posts. [L0215, 2021-22]

#### Timing

- Affiliate programs work best once a store has steady sales and history. Early affiliate traffic is usually a small share of total traffic. [L0970, 2023]
- Expect few positive responses from bloggers and YouTube creators until the store has an established sales history. [L0970, 2023]
- Affiliates are hard to win early because a new store has no reputation; paid sponsored placements, such as a blog post or sidebar image on relevant sites, tend to get results faster in the beginning. [L0934, 2023]

#### Payment Logic

- The store pays nothing until link clicks turn into sales. [L0528, 2025+]

### Affiliate Software and Setup

#### Refersion

- Refersion integrates directly with Shopify and can run affiliate and influencer programs. It has been used successfully for this purpose. [L0803, 2022]
- Affiliates who log into Refersion can browse all available banners in the Creatives section, select whichever they want at any time, and test different versions to see what drives more clicks. They do not need to request new assets or wait for custom creation. [L0288, 2021-22]

#### Referson / Shopify app

- The Referson app on Shopify runs an affiliate program, where you choose the commission percentage and cookie duration. It works well when your margins allow commissions. [L0961, 2020]

#### Shopify Collabs and other platforms

- Track affiliate and brand-ambassador deals with Shopify Collabs or another affiliate platform. [L1213, 2023]

#### Shopify listing partners (complementary stores)

- Shopify stores that are not direct competitors but sell complementary products can list your product for you, with no product data required from you. You then fulfill the order through your supplier and pay them an affiliate-style cut, so this works best for products where you can afford an affiliate fee. [L0869, 2021-22]

#### Tracking links and tags

- Use the tag parameter in affiliate URLs to identify the partner and track commissions (see Finding Content Creators via Google above). [L0215, 2021-22]

### Banners and Creatives

- Partners with banner ads place image-based advertisements across website headers, sidebars, or other locations. [L0288, 2021-22]
- Create banner ads either tied to specific products or as general business-wide promotions. General business banners work well for affiliate placements where partners earn a commission whenever their visitors buy anything from you. [L0288, 2021-22]
- You create and provide all banner creatives. Affiliate partners do not create banners for themselves; they expect ready-made, professional options they can immediately use on their sites. [L0288, 2021-22]
- Affiliates can choose and test banners in the Refersion Creatives section at any time, without requesting new assets. [L0288, 2021-22]

### Tiers and Brand Ambassadors

#### Affiliate and Ambassador Commissions

- Affiliate or brand-ambassador deals pay about 10% to 15% of revenue from each sale generated through a unique link. Track these with Shopify Collabs or another affiliate platform. [L1213, 2023]
- Formula: Commission = 15% of the revenue from orders placed through the influencer's link. Example: 100K followers with 2% buying gives 2,000 buyers whose purchases form the revenue base. [L1213, 2023]

#### Upgrading Tiers

- Upgrade affiliates from a Gold to a Platinum ambassador tier once they reach a sales threshold. The upgrade motivates them to bring more sales. [L1213, 2023]

#### Micro-Influencer Affiliate Program

1. Find small influencers who are active on TikTok or Instagram. [L1439, 2024+]
2. Gift them one product. [L1439, 2024+]
3. They promote it on their platforms. [L1439, 2024+]
4. They receive a commission for every sale made through their channel. [L1439, 2024+]

### Referral Programs

- A referral program lets existing customers refer family and friends, who both get a discount such as a set percentage off. It works better early on than recruiting affiliates. [L0804, 2023]
- Referral programs can provide customers with ready-made social posts or emails to share with friends and followers. This turns existing customers into a channel that spreads the offer. [L1234, 2021]

### Brand Ambassadors and Loyalty

- The hourglass funnel turns new buyers into brand ambassadors who are happy to recommend the store. Their referrals bring higher quality traffic back to the top of the funnel, which converts into more customers. [L0676, 2020-26]
- Use an affiliate or commission plan to reward influencers and buyers who promote the product with a referral code, to keep fans engaged after the first purchase. [L0789, 2022]

### Affiliate Apps and Incentives

- An affiliate app only works when you recruit influencers or sites with a strong offer, such as a free product and a commission around 8 to 10 percent, because a link alone generates no sales. [L1113, 2022]

### Affiliate Partnerships with Complementary Brands

- Outreach to magazines, blogs and authority websites you already have a relationship with: invite them to become affiliates and ask whether they would write a review as an affiliate. [L0768, 2021-22]
- Affiliate partners can be asked to add your product to their recommended product list, not only to write a review. This placement on existing lists of trusted sites is a separate step from the review request. [L0768, 2021-22]
- Find indirect competitors in your niche (e.g., drum lessons if you sell drum kits): [L0251, 2023]
  1. Identify indirect competition products related to your niche. [L0251, 2023]
  2. Search Google for "best [related product/service]." [L0251, 2023]
  3. Visit top results and look for "Partner Program," "Affiliate Program," or a similar link in the footer. [L0251, 2023]
  4. Email them directly with your store information and ask about affiliate terms. [L0251, 2023]
  5. Negotiate the commission structure (fixed dollar or percentage per sale). [L0251, 2023]
  6. Get a unique affiliate link and promote it on relevant product pages. [L0251, 2023]

## Paid Placements, Sponsorships and Joint Promotions

### Why Creators Sell Ad Space
- Content creators and large-scale platforms earn revenue by placing advertisements across their websites, YouTube channels, Instagram feeds and audio podcasts. [L0183, undated]
- Offer advertisers a cost-free seven-day evaluation period to check whether the placement delivers measurable results before they sign a binding agreement. This appeals to most companies actively searching for additional advertising opportunities. [L0183, undated]

### Testing a Placement Before You Commit
Procedure for each prospect that runs paid ads:
1. Complete your Dream 100 Worksheet, identifying people and companies in your niche across all influencer categories.
2. Work through the list and segment out only those that run paid ads.
3. Locate an "advertise" or "contact us" button on their site, or contact them directly via their preferred social channel.
4. Send your outreach message proposing a free one-week trial.
5. During the trial week, track traffic using UTM parameters in Google Analytics to measure sales generated.
6. After one week, review your Google Analytics data for that traffic source to see sales and revenue.
7. Compare the cost of the ad to the sales generated and decide whether to sign a contract. [L0183, undated]

### Advertising Early, Before Relationship Building
- Take advertising opportunities with Dream 100 prospects early and directly, without waiting to complete relationship building, provided the cost is within budget. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0202, 2021-22]
- Paid advertising can be offered before you reach the 10-engagement threshold. If a site shows an advertise-with-us option, you can buy ad space right away, and the purchase makes the owner aware of your business. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]

### Booking Advertising on a Partner Site
Procedure:
1. Find the advertise-with-us link, which is often in a footer or on a contact page of a forum, blog or website.
2. Submit the contact form with your details.
3. Wait for the rate card, which lists prices per placement, such as a banner ad or a sidebar ad.
4. Compare the prices with your budget.
5. If you can afford it, start the booking conversation and buy the placement. If you cannot, keep engaging in other ways and return to it once it fits your budget. [L0525, 2021-22]

*Older advice, 2021-2022.*

### Banner Ads on Forums and Blogs
- Forums and blogs often sell banner ad space. You pay the owner to display your banner, which places your brand in front of the same audience every day. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]

### Podcast and YouTube Sponsorships
- You pay for a sponsored episode or a sponsor mention, often read at the start of the episode. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- Podcasters and YouTubers in your niche should be added to the Dream 100 list as well. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- A sponsored YouTube video or podcast episode can stay online and rank for three to five years, delivering traffic and brand awareness long after it is published. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- Sponsorships like this do not necessarily have to be expensive. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- Example: a seller of wine coolers can add wine podcasts to the Dream 100 list and offer to sponsor an episode. Thousands of wine enthusiasts may hear that episode over the years, so the sponsorship reaches a relevant audience. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- Pitch two placements to the same partner in one outreach, such as a podcast guest slot and a guest blog post, because one contact can open two traffic sources. [L0528, 2025+]
- Influencer traffic works better through advertising on niche authority blogs or websites than through paying random individuals in the niche, because authority sites bring more relevant readers. [L0597, 2020-26]

### Paid and Free Product Promotion
- Paid product promotion: some prospects openly publish rates for mentioning your product in a video or sending an email blast to their list. You pay the rate for the placement you choose, so the cost depends on what they offer. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]
- Free product promotion: the prospect promotes your products to their audience or email list at no cost, usually because they like them. In return you promote their channel, business or content to your audience, for example with a recommendation or a blog post about them. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0525, 2021-22]

### Flat Fee Versus Commission
- A flat-fee ad placement can be cheaper than a commission deal. Example: a banner that costs a couple hundred USD per month but brings over 10,000 USD in monthly sales. Pay for placement first when you can afford it. *Older advice, 2020.* [L0961, 2020]

### Product-for-Post and Celebrity Video Deals
- A sponsored YouTube video can be paid in product, for example a board valued at up to $800 sent to a smaller creator with high views and engagement. In return the creator makes a video, posts it to the channel and gives a shout-out naming the brand. [L0528, 2025+]
- A celebrity can be paid to record a video that is sent to you rather than posted on their channel. You then post it on your own social media and website, so the celebrity's name and face lend credibility to your store. [L0528, 2025+]

### Endorsements and Specialist Reviews
- Endorsements from recognized authorities or celebrities in your market, such as PGA Tour golfers endorsing golf products, provide credible third-party approval. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0295, 2021-22]
- A specialist such as a chiropractor or doctor can review the product on camera; a 30 to 40 second review is enough. Pay the specialist and use the review on the site, landing pages and social media. [L0871, 2021+]
- Only ask a specialist for a review if the product quality is good, since their name will be attached to the product. A simple product with a clear problem is a good fit for this approach. [L0871, 2021+]

### Meme Pages and Repeating Promotions
- Paid placements on TikTok meme pages can be used as a repeating promotion for a product, run about every one to two months. [L0819, 2020+]
- Launch a new batch when the audience gained from the previous batch has been used up and the follower base starts to recycle. [L0819, 2020+]
- Meme pages can post paid content for brands without issue, while influencers may refuse if their followers expect them to appear in the video. Use meme pages to repurpose UGC. [L1214, 2023+]

### Joint Promotions and Collaborations
- Joint promotions let you get your message in front of another creator's audience while they reach yours. Most markets are large enough that this mutual exchange works without taking customers from each other. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0478, 2021-22]
- Joint promotion formats include content collaborations, giveaways and competitions. A podcast can be sponsored for one episode, which places your link and product in front of its listeners. *Older advice, 2021-2022.* [L0478, 2021-22]
- Collaborate on social media with suppliers, blogs, forums and Facebook groups in your niche by tagging them or working together on posts, which brings the right reach and followers early. *Older advice, 2021.* [L0801, 2021]

### Free Resources for Influencers
- Offer influencers a free training course, guide or educational resource to promote to their audience. This works as an alternative to affiliate commissions: influencers build credibility by offering exclusive free value, their followers gain access to the resource, and you gain traffic and backlinks. [L0226, 2017]

### Lead Magnet Placements
- Lead magnet traffic can come from blog banner ads, sponsorships of Facebook groups and forums, Facebook ads aimed at people interested in the niche or its events, and influencer posts. Test these placements with a free offer instead of only using direct discount offers. [L1076, 2019]
- The lead magnet must match the site, community or influencer where it appears. An offer that fits the audience gets opt-ins, while an off-topic offer gets clicks that never convert to sales. [L1076, 2019]
