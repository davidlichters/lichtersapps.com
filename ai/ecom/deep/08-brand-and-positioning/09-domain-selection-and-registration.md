## Domain selection and registration

### Domain structure and formulas

- Combine one brandable word with the niche keyword. Put either first: a brandable word plus the niche, or the niche plus a brandable word. Both patterns work equally well. [L0079, undated]
- Formula: [brandable word] + [niche keyword], or the reverse. The brandable part makes the name unique and ownable, and the keyword tells visitors and search engines the topic at a glance. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Default order: brandable word first, niche second. If that.com is taken, swap them. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Examples:
  - ShakaSurfboards.com pairs the brandable word Shaka with the niche keyword Surfboards. [L0079, undated]
  - Verdant + plantstands gives verdantplantstands.com. If taken, plantstandsverdant.com can be tried. [L0504, 2020-26]
  - Tidewear + swimsuits gives tidewearswimsuits.com, or swimsuitstidewear.com as the fallback. [L0504, 2020-26]
  - Lumora + candles gives lumoracandles.com. [L0521, 2020-26]
  - Brindle + pet supply gives brindlepetsupply.com. [L0521, 2020-26]
- When a niche combines two products, do not put both product names plus a brandable word in the domain. Choose one short, memorable name or the main product name. [L0853, 2021]
- If a descriptive.com built on a full product phrase is taken, a shorter variant with a different adjective may still be free. In one case, the longer product-based name was unavailable as.com, but a shorter name built around "active" plus the product word was available. Test several variants before settling. [L0520, undated]

### Extensions:.com and local domains

- Use.com as the default, with no hyphens, no misspellings, and no name nearly identical to a competitor's domain. [L0671, 2021]
- United States and Australian audiences:.com. [L0079, undated]
- Australian market specifically:.com.au. [L0079, undated]
- The two sources on Australia differ in emphasis. Use.com for a broad US and Australian audience, and.com.au when the Australian market is the specific target. [L0079, undated]
- United Kingdom operations:.co.uk. [L0079, undated]
- Use.co.uk when the store sells in the UK. [L0671, 2021]
- A pan-European English-language store fits a.com. Google Ads can target specific European countries and languages, so the store can still reach targeted traffic. [L0671, 2021]
- Check the.com together with the local extensions, such as.com.au and.co.uk, before committing. A name whose domains are open is usually also a name with fewer trademark conflicts. [L0989, 2025-26]

### Length, characters and wording

- Keep the domain to three words at most. [L0079, undated; L0249, 2022; L0521, 2020-26]
- Some sources say two or three words at most. Two words are easier to say and remember, and three words are the upper limit. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Pick a name that is easy to pronounce, distinctive and memorable. A customer should be able to say it, spell it and find it again later. [L0079, undated; L0504, 2020-26]
- A good name can be recalled after hearing it once. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Domain criteria: quick, memorable, readable,.com, no dashes, three words or fewer. [L0249, 2022]
- Exclude numbers, hyphens and complex letter combinations. [L0079, undated]
- Avoid numbers and dashes. Both make the name harder to spell, say and remember. [L0521, 2020-26]
- Avoid generic filler such as best, cheap, online, store or shop, unless the word is genuinely brandable. Also avoid trademark-like names, awkward spelling, and long or confusing word chains. [L0537, undated]
- Domain names cannot contain spaces. Join a multi-word niche into one lowercase string before you search, so "standup desks" becomes "standupdesks". Use the same joined form in generators and availability checks. [L0504, 2020-26]

### Using the niche keyword

- Sources differ on whether the niche must appear in the name.
- 2022 advice: include the niche name for authority if possible. If not possible, use clever wordplay or add small words before or after, such as Max or All. [L0249, 2022]
- 2024 advice: including the niche is preferable but not required. Stores with generic or niche-related domains have succeeded equally well. Prioritize a memorable name over a keyword-heavy one. [L0276, 2024]
- 2021 advice: the domain should reflect the niche, but it does not have to contain the exact keyword. [L0671, 2021]
- A generic approach: use one generic, brandable domain that does not refer to the niche, such as a short invented name under 10 characters. Check names with Namelix. [L1412, 2023]
- A short, memorable name associated with the niche is ideal, but an unrelated memorable name is acceptable. [L0469, 2021]
- Exact-match one-word domains no longer give a meaningful SEO advantage and are usually priced far above their value. Choose a memorable two- or three-word brandable domain instead, since brand recall drives direct visits. [L1279, 2021]

### Generating candidates: tools and procedures

- AI brainstorming tool with a domain-specific prompt. Customize it with two editable sections:
  - The niche keyword, for example surfboards, horse saddles or chandeliers;
  - optionally, a vibe: premium, coastal, adventure, eco-friendly, tech or family.
  The tool generates branded, niche-specific domain ideas. [L0079, undated]
- Select names from AI results that match your niche naturally, are easy to remember and sound like a real brand. Test availability for your final choices. [L0079, undated]
- Prompt for a 25-candidate list (use as written, replacing the niche placeholder):

```
You work as a professional domain name researcher who focuses on brandable eCommerce store names. Your task is to propose 25 brandable.com domains for an eCommerce store in this niche: [PASTE NICHE HERE]. Every domain must include the niche keyword exactly as written. For example, if the niche were surfboards, the word surfboards has to appear in each name, as in OceanSurfboards.com or SurfboardsHub.com. You are permitted, and even encouraged, to run live availability checks through real registrars such as Namecheap, GoDaddy, Cloudflare Registrar, or a WHOIS lookup.

Mandatory rules:
- Only.com extensions
- At most 3 words in total
- No hyphens
- No digits
- The exact niche word must appear in every domain
- The structure must be either BrandableWord + NicheKeyword (e.g., SummitSurfboards.com) or NicheKeyword + BrandableWord (e.g., SurfboardsSummit.com)
- Names must be short, easy to pronounce, easy to remember, and sound brandable and premium
- Avoid trademark-like names, awkward spellings, long or confusing word chains, and generic filler such as best, cheap, online, store, or shop, unless the word is genuinely brandable

Availability: run a live check on every domain and label it Available, Taken, or Premium/For Sale. If a name is Taken, keep generating until you have 25 strong ideas in total. If live checks cannot be run in this session, say so clearly and mark the status as Unverified instead of guessing.

Process: first list niche keyword variations where they apply, then list short, brandable modifier words (examples: Peak, Atlas, Nova, Ridge, Wave, Forge, Harbor, Oak, Lux, Coast, Aura, Sage, Vertex, Tide, Trail, Stone, Bloom, Haven, Apex, Union, Craft, Nest, Collective). Combine the modifiers with the niche in either order (BrandableWord + Niche or Niche + BrandableWord). Favor names that feel like a real business, sound authoritative and premium, and could grow into a larger brand.

Output: return exactly 25 results. For each, list Domain, Word Count, Structure, Availability, Brandability Score (1-10), and Notes. Example row: OceanSurfboards.com | 2 | Brandable + Niche | Available | 9 | Strong, clean, premium ocean association. Optional vibe: Premium / Coastal / Adventure.
```
[L0537, undated]

- A store domain for a niche brand should be.com only, at most three words, with no dashes and no numbers. The exact niche keyword must appear in every candidate, placed before or after one brandable word. [L0537, undated]
- Manual procedure:
  1. List niche keyword variations that fit the product niche. [L0537, undated]
  2. List short, brandable modifier words that sound premium and are easy to say. [L0537, undated]
  3. Combine each modifier with the niche keyword as BrandableWord + Niche and as Niche + BrandableWord. [L0537, undated]
  4. Check each combined domain for availability and mark it Available, Taken, or Premium/For Sale. [L0537, undated]
  5. Replace every Taken domain with a new candidate until 25 strong ideas remain. [L0537, undated]
  6. Score each remaining domain for brandability from 1 to 10 and note its feel, such as premium, coastal or adventure. [L0537, undated]
- Generator procedure:
  1. Open businessnamegenerator.com and enter your niche in the add keywords field. [L0504, 2020-26]
  2. Click generate and wait for the results to load. [L0504, 2020-26]
  3. Note the related keywords and brand ideas, and ignore the exact names shown. [L0504, 2020-26]
  4. Google "words associated with [niche]" and open the lists you find. [L0504, 2020-26]
  5. Combine the best words with your niche, using the brandable-word formula, in a document. [L0504, 2020-26]
  6. Keep many candidates so you are not stuck when one turns out to be taken. [L0504, 2020-26]
  7. Check availability in Shopify and run the safety checks before buying. [L0504, 2020-26]
- businessnamegenerator.com is a free tool. It returns related niche keywords and brand ideas for the niche you enter. Its results are raw material, not final names. Combine the useful words with your niche word, before or after it. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Google search "words associated with [niche]" returns online lists of terms related to your niche. These give extra words to place before or after the niche keyword. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Domain Soup generates random brandable domain names. Good names are short, have no literal meaning and sound like a brand. [L0637, 2023+]
- Do not create a domain by taking a competitor's store name in the same niche and changing one or two letters. The name must be unique to your brand. [L0504, 2020-26]

### Checking availability

- AI tools cannot verify real-time availability. Suggested names may already be taken or listed as premium. Verify each domain by entering it in a browser or Shopify search before purchasing. [L0079, undated]
- Confirm availability for every candidate at a registrar before you treat it as an option. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Before you finalize, add the domain to the cart to confirm it is still available. A domain that looks free can disappear. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Check the exact final spelling at the registrar. A slightly different spelling is a different domain and may be free or taken. A domain checked earlier can be forgotten when the name changes. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Test several candidates, since a single name is often taken. [L0504, 2020-26; L0520, undated]

### Premium, taken and squatted domains

- Many generated.com names may be taken or listed for resale at high prices. [L0989, 2025-26]
- In businessnamegenerator.com results, a dollar symbol next to a.com name means the domain is taken or held by a squatter. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Do not pay a squatter more than the normal registration price of about $15 per year. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Example: a premium.com on a domain marketplace was listed at $2,995 (roughly $3,000). It was skipped rather than bought. Premium resale prices like this are a reason to look for other candidates. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Aftermarket pages on a taken premium.com can show a "Buy It Now" price plus a broker service fee on top. Add that fee to the listed price before comparing candidates, since the total cost is what matters. [L0989, 2025-26]
- If a premium name is taken, put "the" in front of a short brand name. This often makes the.com available, for example thecosery.com. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Two-word fallback: a brand word plus a descriptive word is usually free immediately, because few people register such combinations. Use it when single-word premium names are taken or overpriced, for example a brand word plus "comfort" or "plush". [L0989, 2025-26]

### Price limits

- The sources give different figures for the standard yearly price of a domain. Treat about $13 to $15 per year as the normal range. The 2021 figures are older.
  - About $13 per year: a standard domain costs about this. Do not buy an expensive domain from a third party, such as paying around $1,000 for a better-looking name. [L0671, 2021]
  - About $14 per year: do not pay more than this, and avoid buying from people who hold and flip domains. [L0469, 2021]
  - About $15 per year: do not pay a squatter more than the normal registration price. (Undated source) [L0504, 2020-26]
- Compare total cost, including any broker fee, before you buy. [L0989, 2025-26]
- Do not pay premium resale prices such as $2,995 when a two-word or "the" variant is available. [L0989, 2025-26]

### Matching the domain to the name

- A brand name matters less than whether its matching domains are free. Check the.com and the local extensions before committing. [L0989, 2025-26]
- The domain should reflect the niche and be easy to remember. It should sound like a real brand. [L0079, undated]
- Pick a memorable name over a keyword-heavy one. [L0276, 2024]
- Make the domain unique to your brand. Do not copy a competitor's store name. [L0504, 2020-26]
- Do not put the word Shopify in a domain name or store name. Shopify has contacted stores that used its name in a domain, and ads that targeted the word Shopify were taken down. (2023) [L0964, 2023]
- Check the exact spelling of the final domain and the store name you use, so they match. [L0989, 2025-26]

### Business email

- Set up business emails on the domain alongside the website and branding. When suppliers contact you, consistent emails show that the business is legitimate. Set this up with the site, not afterwards. [L1060, 2021-22]
