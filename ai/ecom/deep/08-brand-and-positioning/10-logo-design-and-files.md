## Logo design and files

### Logo requirements

- Define the brand before designing the logo: its values, what it offers, the customer problems it solves and its tone of voice. These answers guide the logo's shape and style. [L0952, undated]
- The logo is the face of the brand and is often the first thing customers link to it. It reinforces the feelings, beliefs and attitudes people already hold about the brand, which is why it matters for trust. [L0952, undated]
- A good logo meets four criteria: simplicity, memorability, versatility and relevance. Each one is a test to apply when judging a design. [L0952, undated]
- **Simplicity:** the logo is easy to recognize and remember. Simplified versions of objects work well, as the Apple and Twitter bird examples show. A simple mark is recognized at a glance and recalled later. [L0952, undated]
- **Memorability:** the logo makes a strong impression at first glance and stands out enough that a viewer instantly recognizes it on later encounters. [L0952, undated]
- **Versatility:** the logo must work in many formats (social media, websites, letterheads) and on both light and dark backgrounds. Test it in each context before finalizing. [L0952, undated]
- **Relevance:** the logo should visually match the niche. For a dropshipping brand, use concepts from the product's niche, for example pet symbols for a pet brand or baby symbols for a baby brand, so the brand looks like it belongs to that niche. [L0952, undated]
- A logo should be as simple as possible, because simpler logos produce better results. Minimal designs look more professional and more luxurious than busy ones. [L0953, 2026]
- A logo should be simple: text plus one small graphic, nothing showy, so it is noticed but does not compete with the page. Stores whose products are already colorful and busy benefit most from a simpler logo. [L0342, 2020-26; L1045, 2020-26]
- When unsure about a logo design, choose the simpler option and avoid over-engineering or overcomplicating it. Less is more. [L0952, undated]
- A logo does not need to be professional at the start. It should be clean, the right size and not pixelated, and a simple typed wordmark is sufficient and cheap to make. ** [L0663, 2019]
- The logo should use the same words as the domain name, so the store name is the most visible element. Check that the spelling matches the domain exactly. [L0342, 2020-26]
- The logo should use the same words as the domain name, with the store name front and center. This keeps the brand name consistent across the site. [L1045, 2020-26]
- Logo design should be primarily text-based, displaying the business name clearly and legibly as the dominant element. Decorative logo marks are optional and not necessary for success. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- The logo must be rectangular (wider than tall) to fit into website headers without consuming excessive vertical space. Circular logos are difficult to size and display properly. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- Your website logo appears in the header, footer and a few other pages. Rectangular orientation is critical because it fits naturally in the header space without consuming excessive height. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- A logo should be rectangular and no more than about 200 px high, with a width of around 400 to 500 px, so it loads quickly and displays well in multiple places on the site. ** [L1371, 2021-22]
- Logo text style should match the target customer base. Male-dominated markets such as power equipment benefit from bold, chunky, thick fonts. Water sports and movement-focused markets suit softer, flowing typography. Luxury segments need elegant, refined letterforms. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- Logo design quality comes from a brand colour palette and a chosen typeface defined before the logo is built, so colours can be swapped quickly to test how the logo looks. [L0952, undated]
- Build logos from core basic shapes (circles, squares, ellipses, rectangles) instead of drawing everything freehand. Complex icons can be reduced to a few simple shapes that fit together. [L0952, undated]
- Taglines in the logo are optional and make no measurable difference to business success. Include one only if it genuinely resonates with the target market's values or positioning. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- Branding is not permanent. The logo design and colours can evolve over time without changing the business name or domain. ** [L0323, 2021-22]

### Wordmark or icon: where to start

- A simple typed wordmark in a nice font is enough as a logo at the start. Logos matter less than the brand name, so do not spend much time on logo design. [L0361, 2024]
- At the early stage, use text-only wordmarks with no icon or symbol, because a mark is not needed for versatility yet. Each wordmark option should differ only in its letter case or type style. [L0954, 2025-26]
- Paying for a logo is optional during setup. A logo can cost about USD 20 or up to USD 100, and the choice adds to the USD 100 to 500 launch budget. ** [L0438, 2021-22]
- A consistent symbol paired with the brand name builds recognition over time. When later products launch, customers who saw the icon before connect it with past purchases and expect to like the new product. [L1047, 2025-26]
- A brand logo must work across many surfaces, such as a website, packing slips, social media ads and other materials. It needs to be versatile and adaptable, not a single image that only fits one place. [L1047, 2025-26]
- Test a logo by mocking it up on the real surfaces where it will appear, such as an Instagram profile, before deciding. A wordmark can fit poorly in a small profile circle while a compact icon fits well. [L1047, 2025-26]
- There are two ways to build an icon: an automated route where AI brainstorms concepts, and a manual route where you sketch the icon yourself and have AI refine it. Use the AI brainstorm when you need options, and use the sketch when you already have a specific idea in mind. [L1047, 2025-26]
- Logo workflow in four steps: [L1047, 2025-26]
  1. Develop an icon that pairs with the brand name.
  2. Export the logo as a transparent PNG.
  3. Convert the PNG into a vector file.
  4. Finalize the logo into the variations you actually need.
- A logo concept should express the product's core meaning in a simple visual idea, such as motion for speed or growth for progress. A simple symbol is easier to remember and connects to the brand story. Example: a leaf-shaped mark for an eco-friendly cleaning product. Second example: an arrow shape forming a 'U' for an upgrade-focused electronics store. [L1071, undated]
- Logo design pattern: a simple mark made of two or three basic shapes, where one shape is cut out of another (negative space) to suggest a recognizable object. The viewer completes the image in their mind, which makes the mark memorable and easy to recognize. Example of the same pattern: a coffee cup whose handle is formed by a cut-out circle in a solid square, and a leaf built from a single cut-out ellipse on a circle. [L0952, undated]
- A pet-friendly brand logo can pair a rounded display typeface with a lighter, softer secondary typeface. Choose the typefaces together with the brand colours before building the mark, so the text and symbol share one visual tone. [L0952, undated]

### Tools and routes

Choose the route by budget and time to market. [L1044, undated]

| Option | Best when | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Canva logo templates | Fast launch, no design skill | Type logo into the search, edit text, fonts and icons, then take the palette from an icon in the design |
| Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop | Owner has design skills | Build the logo from scratch |
| 99designs | Need visual inspiration | Browse the logo library and recreate a style you like |
| Fiverr logo designer | Little time, budget available for hiring | Brief the designer on look and feel, tone of voice, brand values and example designs, then let them create the logo |

[L1044, undated]

- Logo, colour and typography can be made in Canva, through a graphic design class, or by hiring a freelancer on a third-party service such as Fiverr. [L1071, undated]
- Canva is a free design tool that can produce a simple logo. A professional designer is an option when more polished branding is needed. ** [L0853, 2021]
- Canva can hold a brand kit that combines the logo, colour palette and typefaces in one place. [L1071, undated]
- You do not need a finished logo concept before contacting a Fiverr designer. Provide three colour hex codes, the business name, what the business sells and the target market feel you want, then let the designer create options from that foundation. ** [L0323, 2021-22]
- A logo can be built from overlapping shapes in Illustrator: [L0754, undated]
  1. Set each new shape to about 50 percent transparency so overlapping shapes stay visible.
  2. Draw the basic shapes with the Shape tool and the Pen tool, and shape anchor points with the Direct Selection tool.
  3. Group the shapes you want to combine and open the Shape Builder tool.
  4. Drag across the areas you want to keep to merge them into one shape, and hold Alt or Option and drag across the areas you want to remove.
  5. Lock finished layers using the lock icon in the Layers panel so they cannot be moved by accident.
  6. Polish the outline with the Direct Selection tool, then set the transparency back to 100 percent.
  7. Collect several reference logos for inspiration and iterate until the shape feels balanced.

### Canva: store logo for the one source theme

Canva procedure with the 800 x 300 px template: [L0342, 2020-26]
1. Open the logo template for the one source theme (800 x 300 px) and click Use Template.
2. Click Text, then Add a heading, and type the store name exactly as the domain name.
3. Save the brand colors in Brand Kit first, then select the text and apply a brand color with the text color (A) icon.
4. Enlarge the text until it fills the template, choose a bold or extra-bold font and optionally capitalize it.
5. Adjust letter spacing and line spacing so the text fills the frame and the lines sit close together.
6. Open Elements, search Graphics for a simple icon such as a letter S, drag it in, resize it to fill the template and recolor it to match the brand colors.
7. Keep the logo to text plus one small graphic.
8. Download the logo as a PNG. On the free plan it keeps a white background.

A second version of the same Canva procedure: [L1045, 2020-26]
1. Open the logo template (800 x 300 pixels) through the linked template and click Use template.
2. Click Text, then Add a heading, and type the store's domain name.
3. Select the text and use the A text color button to pick one of the brand colors from the Brand kit.
4. Change the font, make it bold or extra bold, capitalize it if wanted, and enlarge it to fill the template. Adjust letter spacing and line spacing as needed.
5. Go to Elements, then Graphics, search for a simple letter or symbol and pick a free item (items with a crown are paid).
6. Drag the icon into the template, enlarge it to nearly the full template size and change its color to the brand palette.
7. Click Share or the download button, choose PNG and download.

### Canva: square template
- The Canva logo template used in this method is a square 500 × 500 px design. Keep the logo on a square canvas of this size. [L0953, 2026]
- Procedure in Canva Premium: [L0953, 2026]
  1. Decide the brand name first.
  2. In Canva Premium, open Designs in the top-left area.
  3. Search "Logo" in the "Search Logo templates" field and scroll through the results.
  4. Choose a simple, minimalist template that matches a professional look.
  5. Replace the template text with your brand name.
  6. Edit the template's letters to fit the brand name, for example by changing the first and last letter of the template text into an O-shape.
  7. Adjust the position of the logo elements (move up or down) until they are balanced.
  8. Adjust the size of the logo so it is clearly readable.
  9. Check the result against the simplicity rule and finish if it is simple and professional.
- Pick a template whose style is black and white, minimalist and simple, because that style matches the professional look a logo should have. Reject templates with many elements or strong decoration, even if they catch the eye first. [L0953, 2026]

### Wordmarks generated with ChatGPT

- For logo generation in ChatGPT, select the ChatGPT 5 Thinking model and the Heavy thinking mode in the composer before pasting the logo prompt. The full set of four wordmarks, four favicons and a palette took about one minute of thinking in this setup. [L0954, 2025-26]
- Before generating, finalise the brand name and its spelling. The wordmarks and favicons are built from the exact spelling. If the name changes later, all four wordmark options, favicons and the palette must be generated again with the new name. [L0954, 2025-26]
- Keep the wordmark to the brand name only and leave out the domain extension such as.com, even if the domain is pasted into the prompt. The domain is an input for the brand positioning, not part of the logo text. [L0954, 2025-26]
- Steps to create a logo set and palette with ChatGPT: [L0954, 2025-26]
  1. Build a master brand document with the product list, the chosen brand name, the domain name and one brand positioning (three short pillars, each a label with one sentence, plus a short mini story paragraph).
  2. Open a new chat in ChatGPT using the thinking model.
  3. Paste the logo generator prompt and replace the placeholders with the brand name, the domain and the positioning text.
  4. Wait until the generation is finished.
  5. Click download and download all assets: the 4 wordmark PNGs, the 4 favicon PNGs and the palette swatch PNG.
  6. Open the downloaded files in Finder to check that the wordmarks, favicons and palette swatch are all there.
  7. Use the logo, favicon and text options in the Shopify theme.
- Logo generator prompt, to be pasted with placeholders filled in: [L0954, 2025-26]

```text
Act as a senior logo designer. Produce 4 clean wordmarks for the brand [BRAND NAME] that use type alone: no icon, symbol, mascot or other mark, only the name set in lettering. Leave out drop shadows, gradients, 3D effects and ornaments. The background must be transparent.

Mandatory baseline rules:
- Every letter sits on one shared baseline. No letter may be moved vertically on its own.
- Reject any bouncy or playful baseline. Switch off any stylistic set that offsets glyphs.
- Keep the font's default kerning. Adjust tracking evenly across the whole word, never letter by letter.
- Fonts may overshoot for optical reasons, but the baseline must still look straight, with no stair-stepping.
- Run a baseline check before export and regenerate any option that shows vertical jitter.

Four style options:
- A: lowercase
- B: Title Case
- C: UPPERCASE, bold
- D: soft serif (small caps, expanded)

Inspiration, not imitation: draw on recent, high-quality brand aesthetics from the last 10 years. Choose new, free fonts that suit the brand positioning and the products sold.

Deliverables: PNG wordmarks on a transparent background, 3000 x 1200 px, wordmark centred with even padding on all sides. Also create 4 PNG favicons, one per option, each showing the first letter of the wordmark.

Spelling must match the input exactly. Use consistent tracking with optical kerning turned on.

Output: download links for the 4 wordmark PNGs and 1 palette swatch PNG. Under a 'Palette' heading, list 5 colours with HEX and RGB values. Include at least one dark and one light colour with strong contrast for buttons, and make sure the colours work with white. Under each option (A, B, C, D), give a short spec line.

Inputs (paste below):
Brand name: [BRAND NAME]
Domain: [DOMAIN]
Brand positioning: [POSITIONING 1: label and one sentence] [POSITIONING 2: label and one sentence] [POSITIONING 3: label and one sentence]
Mini story: [SHORT MINI STORY PARAGRAPH DESCRIBING THE PRODUCTS, THE CUSTOMER FEELING AND THE BRAND PROMISE]
```

- Logo export checklist for wordmarks and favicons: [L1331, 2025-26]
  1. Use a single straight baseline with no per-letter vertical shifts.
  2. Keep default kerning and even tracking, with no manual glyph nudging.
  3. Make the canvas 3000 x 1200 px, centered, with at least 12% clear margin on all sides.
  4. Check that no letters overlap, there are no unwanted ligatures, and the mark stays clean at small sizes.
  5. Export transparent PNGs for wordmarks and favicons.
