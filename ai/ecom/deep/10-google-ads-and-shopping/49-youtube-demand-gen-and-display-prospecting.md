## YouTube, Demand Gen and Display Prospecting

### Video campaign foundations and content controls

- Turn off auto-generated video in the ad settings to keep full control over creative quality and format. [L0189, undated]
- Exclude these content categories from the campaign: content not yet labeled, all sensitive content categories, and live streaming videos. [L0189, undated]
- Excluded content options include embedded videos on other sites, live streaming videos, games, and content not yet labeled or aimed at mature audiences. Excluding these typically costs little reach because the included inventory types already cover much of it. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Inventory type: Google recommends the standard setting, which excludes profanity, sexual content and violence. The alternative, Limited inventory, reduces the available inventory by about 20% and blocks videos with repeated strong profanity, strong sexual content and violence. This can be adjusted later. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Set the campaign goal to the option that gives full control rather than the smart goal recommendations. The goal list includes Sales, Leads, Website traffic, Product and brand consideration, Brand awareness and reach, and App promotion, plus an option to create a campaign without a goal. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Use a clear naming system, for example naming the campaign by funnel stage and audience, such as bottom-of-funnel sales video retargeting. [L1510, 2020-26]

### Network and placement settings

- Turn off 'Video partners on the display network' in video campaign settings. These non-skippable ads on random sites waste budget because people don't watch and don't convert. [L0210, 2022]
- Start the first YouTube campaign with wide network reach (YouTube search results, videos, channel pages, homepage and Display Network video partners), then measure which placements perform and narrow down based on performance. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Campaign build step 3 for a YouTube retargeting campaign lists the network options as YouTube search results, YouTube videos, channel pages, homepage, and Display Network video partners, with languages and countries matching the store. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Conflict between sources: [L0210, 2022] and the remarketing settings below say to turn video partners off, while [L1510, 2020-26] and [L1510, 2020-26] include them for the first wide-reach campaign. Apply the off setting to remarketing, dynamic and promotion campaigns, and use the wide-reach setting only for the first test campaign that is meant to find where the ads perform. [L0210, 2022; L1510, 2020-26]
- If a site shows Google-served ads in its sidebar or footer and offers no direct ad deal, you can still test ads there by running Google display ads that appear only on that website. [L0528, 2025+]
- Ad schedule: start with all 24 hours on every day to cast a wide net, then narrow later based on performance data. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Devices: start with all devices for the first campaign, since it is unknown which device or network performs best. Then observe results and apply bid modifiers to devices afterwards. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Delivery method: use Standard, not Accelerated, which is almost always the right choice. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Set start and end dates for the campaign. With a limited budget, an end date can be added to cap spend. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Popular video adjustment (raising bids on particularly popular videos) is usually left off, because the goal is a wide net at a controlled cost. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Demographic data in Google Ads for YouTube often shows many viewers as unknown, since Google does not always know age or income. Review demographic information later in the dimensions report. [L1510, 2020-26]

### Video remarketing campaigns

**Dynamic Video Remarketing (setup sequence)**

1. Campaign type: 'Custom video campaign'. [L0210, 2022]
2. Bid strategy: 'Maximum CPV' (cost per view). [L0210, 2022]
3. Turn off 'Video partners on display network'. [L0210, 2022]
4. Link the Merchant Center feed for dynamic product ads. [L0210, 2022]
5. Select the skippable in-stream format. [L0210, 2022]
6. Target 30-day website visitors. [L0210, 2022]
7. Turn off 'audience expansion'. [L0210, 2022]
8. Exclude recent customers. [L0210, 2022]
9. Use the video creator tool with preset templates (3 images + logo + text). [L0210, 2022]

**Retargeting structure**

- Video - WARM - Website Visitors adds another touchpoint to remarketing. It reduces ad fatigue from display and reaches visitors on the video placements where they spend time. [L1121, 2022]
- Discovery - WARM - Monthly Promotions uses ads similar to the Google Display Network but with different aspect ratios, to raise awareness of limited-time offers among warm audiences. [L1121, 2022]
- Video - WARM - Monthly Promotions uses video ads to raise awareness of limited-time offers among warm audiences. [L1121, 2022]
- Video - PAST CUSTOMERS - Goodwill targets people who have already bought. Its purpose is to reduce buyer's remorse after purchase. [L1121, 2022]
- The audience sits at the ad group level. [L1510, 2020-26]

**Build order for a YouTube retargeting campaign**

1. Create the video campaign with a clear name. [L1510, 2020-26]
2. Set the start and end dates. [L1510, 2020-26]
3. Choose network options, languages and countries matching the store (see Network and placement settings). [L1510, 2020-26]
4. Set the bid strategy to Maximum CPV. [L1510, 2020-26]
5. Set inventory type and content exclusions. [L1510, 2020-26]
6. Set devices, frequency cap (1 per day) and ad schedule (24 hours). [L1510, 2020-26]
7. Create the ad group. [L1510, 2020-26]
8. Set the audience, max CPV bid, and optional popular-video adjustment (usually left off). [L1510, 2020-26]
9. Choose the video and format (in-stream or discovery). [L1510, 2020-26]
10. Set the destination URL and companion banner (auto-generate if available). [L1510, 2020-26]

**Example budgets and targets**

- A Google Ads Demand Gen remarketing campaign was launched with a starting budget of $10/day and a target of more than 10x ROAS. [L1509, undated]
- A YouTube remarketing campaign on Google Ads was launched with a starting budget of $10/day and a target of more than 10x ROAS. [L1509, undated]
- Bottom-of-funnel sales video retargeting example: campaign budget set to 3x KPI budget, at 10 per day. [L1510, 2020-26]

**Introduction video as YouTube ad**

- If the business owner has a YouTube channel they want to grow, the introduction video can also run as a YouTube ad. The ad is built like a Facebook video ad and needs a link to the target page. [L0488, 2021-22]

### Goodwill and post-purchase video

**Goodwill video procedure**

1. Create or film a 1-minute personal thank-you video in selfie style. [L0210, 2022]
2. Include your name, thank you for the order, contact information, tracking timeline, and appreciation for customer feedback (no sales pitch). [L0210, 2022]
3. Set campaign to 'Custom video campaign' > bid strategy 'Maximum CPV' > audience 'Purchased 7 days' > frequency cap '1 view per week' > use budget of $5-10 per day. [L0210, 2022]
4. People will only see it once after buying, during their first week. [L0210, 2022]

**Rules and settings**

- Do not ask for reviews in goodwill videos. Send review requests in follow-up emails later, not immediately after purchase when the product has not yet been delivered. [L0210, 2022]
- For goodwill post-purchase video campaigns, set the maximum CPV bid to $1. Actual spend will be much lower, typically cents per view, because recent customers are warm, high-intent audience members. [L0210, 2022]
- Maximum CPV for goodwill versus retargeting: goodwill campaigns start at a $1 maximum CPV bid, while general YouTube retargeting ad groups start at about 10 cents (see Bidding, frequency caps and budgets). [L0210, 2022; L1510, 2020-26]

### Monthly promotion video and Discovery

**Video Monthly Promotion campaign**

1. Choose dates (e.g., October 29-31). [L0210, 2022]
2. Audience: 180-day website visitors. [L0210, 2022]
3. Bid strategy: 'Maximum CPV'. [L0210, 2022]
4. Set frequency cap to 2 impressions per day. [L0210, 2022]
5. Turn off 'Video partners on display network'. [L0210, 2022]
6. Turn off 'audience expansion'. [L0210, 2022]
7. Create the video using the Google video creator template. [L0210, 2022]
8. Use the /promotions URL. [L0210, 2022]
9. Call-to-action: 'save now'. [L0210, 2022]
10. Exclude recent customers. [L0210, 2022]

**Discovery campaign for monthly promotions**

1. Campaign type: 'Discovery'. [L0210, 2022]
2. Choose start and end dates for the promotion. [L0210, 2022]
3. Audience: 180-day website visitors. [L0210, 2022]
4. Turn off 'optimized targeting'. [L0210, 2022]
5. URL: /promotions. [L0210, 2022]
6. Static image ads only (aspect ratio adjusted from Display ads). [L0210, 2022]
7. Headlines with the promotion name (e.g., 'Halloween sale'). [L0210, 2022]
8. Description with offer details. [L0210, 2022]
9. Exclude recent customers. [L0210, 2022]

**Promotion reach**

- Run the monthly promotion across display remarketing, YouTube, Discovery, Facebook, Instagram and email, so the offer appears everywhere the warm audiences are. [L1204, 2022]

### Discovery ads and video formats

- Discovery ads appear as suggested videos in the sidebar, on the YouTube homepage, or in the search results for a keyword. They are shown while someone is watching other content or searching. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Video discovery ad setup: the thumbnail and headline appear in the sidebar and search results. Use a strong headline, since a good headline drives click-through. The destination can be the standard video watch page. A descriptive ad name is recommended. [L1510, 2020-26]
- For discovery in Google Ads, choose the video to promote. Any YouTube video URL can be entered, including unlisted ones. Then choose the format: in-stream (pre-roll) or video discovery. Plan to run both formats, and name each ad group by its format, e.g. in-stream. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Destination URL for YouTube ads: the YouTube channel, the store website, or a specific product page. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Companion banner: auto-generate if none is provided. It appears next to or below the video during playback. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Create a 'Custom video campaign' with the Maximum CPV strategy, a 1-view-per-day frequency cap, and the skippable in-stream format for dynamic video remarketing. [L0210, 2022; L1510, 2020-26]

### Bidding, frequency caps and budgets

**Bid strategy**

- Set the bid strategy to Maximum CPV (cost per view), not Maximum CPM (cost per thousand impressions), because you want to pay when a viewer actually watches the ad. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Starting max CPV bid for YouTube retargeting ad groups: about 10 cents, then increase gradually to see whether the ad gets traction. Views on YouTube are generally less valuable than clicks to the website, so do not start high. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Goodwill and post-purchase video campaigns start at a maximum CPV of $1 (see Goodwill and post-purchase video). [L0210, 2022]

**Frequency caps**

| Campaign type | Frequency cap | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Large audience video and display campaigns | 2 impressions per day, to avoid repeatedly showing the same people the same ad and to control spend | [L0210, 2022] |
| Video Monthly Promotion | 2 impressions per day | [L0210, 2022] |
| YouTube retargeting | 1 impression per day per campaign, so viewers stay aware of the brand without seeing the ad four or five times a day | [L1510, 2020-26] |
| Goodwill (purchased 7 days) | 1 view per week | [L0210, 2022] |

- Frequency caps for large audiences and for retargeting differ. Use 2 per day for broad video and display audiences, 1 per day for retargeting, and 1 per week for goodwill. [L0210, 2022; L1510, 2020-26]

**Budget reference points**

- $5-10 per day for goodwill video. [L0210, 2022]
- $10 per day starting budgets for Demand Gen and YouTube remarketing with a more than 10x ROAS target. [L1509, undated]

### Audience and placement targeting in Google Ads

**Audience and interest visibility**

- To see the profile Google has built about you, search for Google Ad Settings or open adsettings.google.com. It shows the inferred demographics and interests that Google uses for ad personalization and targeting. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Google builds a user's interest and demographic profile from their search behavior and from the websites they visit that run Google Analytics. Users never enter these details themselves, so the profile reflects what they actually search for and read. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Google holds demographic and interest data that is at least as detailed as Meta's, and in some respects more granular. Its demographic targeting is therefore a strong tool, not just a secondary option. [L0720, 2020-26]

**Targeting methods**

- Demographic targeting can select audiences by gender, age range, parental status, homeownership and household income. Interest targeting uses browsing history and search behavior to reach people with matching interests. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Topic targeting selects content categories, such as health and wellness or swimming pools and spas, as well as specific websites and apps. Google's reach estimate for a topic can run into millions of impressions, so the potential audience is large. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Life events targeting reaches people at moments such as moving, getting married, birthdays or college graduation. Custom intent targeting shows display ads on sites to people who have been searching and researching a product category, which makes it an intent-based audience. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Placements let you choose specific websites where your ad appears, for example sites your customers already read. This works as a targeting method alongside search. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Demographic segments can receive different ads rather than one message for everyone. Examples include households in the top versus bottom income bracket, or senior citizens versus people under 30 who are actively searching. [L0720, 2020-26]

**Launch order**

1. Launch targeting methods in order of how well they convert: start with the methods that convert best most often, then move up the funnel to methods that reach earlier-stage audiences. [L0720, 2020-26]
2. Do not activate all methods at once. [L0720, 2020-26]

**Finding placements and interest ideas for YouTube**

1. Browse YouTube channels in the category (e.g. furniture) and check whether their audience matches your customers. [L1510, 2020-26]
2. In Google Analytics, use the Interests report (Overview and Affinity categories) to find audience categories, then search for them in Google Ads. [L1510, 2020-26]
3. Use placements only when the audience is large enough. For smaller audiences, do not narrow the targeting too much. [L1510, 2020-26]

### Competitor and top-of-funnel YouTube

- YouTube allows targeting competitor audiences by selecting specific channels or videos (e.g. a competitor channel), so that a discovery or in-stream ad appears as a suggested video next to that content. Facebook restricts such page-level targeting, while YouTube does not restrict it in the same way. [L1510, 2020-26]
- On YouTube you can target specific channels and specific videos, including videos made by competitors. This lets you reach an audience that already watches content close to your offer. [L0720, 2020-26]
- Use YouTube targeting to reach viewers of specific competitor channels or videos only when that content is relevant to your buyers. The goal is to draw part of that audience's attention toward your offer. [L0720, 2020-26]

**Top-of-funnel campaign for competitor fans**

- Principle: copy the retargeting campaign and modify it. Exclude the website visitors audience (so only new people are reached), and target competitor channels or videos under placements (e.g. competitor YouTube channel) so the ad appears as a suggested video next to their content. Pause the retargeting ad groups if they are not needed. [L1510, 2020-26]
- The first touch should use a top-of-funnel video and offer. Users then flow into retargeting campaigns. The purpose of this campaign is to build a list of people to retarget later, so track cost per view against how many enter the funnel. [L1510, 2020-26]

**Build procedure**

1. Copy the existing campaign and paste it. [L1510, 2020-26]
2. Rename it to "top of funnel views". [L1510, 2020-26]
3. Replace the visitors-who-haven't-converted audience with a competitor-channel audience, by adding placements with YouTube channels or videos. [L1510, 2020-26]
4. Keep the website visitors audience as an exclusion. [L1510, 2020-26]
5. Repeat the same changes for the discovery ad group. [L1510, 2020-26]
6. Pick the funnel offer. [L1510, 2020-26]
7. Measure cost per view to see whether it pushes visitors into the funnel. [L1510, 2020-26]

### When YouTube makes sense

- YouTube ads are not a starting point for a new store. They suit sellers who are already doing well, want to grow further, and are comfortable with video. [L1088, 2020]
- YouTube ads are not needed early on because they take considerable effort to get right and easier gains exist elsewhere. Consider them only when a store already does multiple millions of dollars in sales per year and needs more growth. [L0968, 2021]
- Invest in Google Ads (search, shopping, Performance Max) for strong returns over 2-3 years. Reserve YouTube ads for retargeting only, because YouTube reaches video viewers rather than search-intent customers. [L0324, 2025+]
- The sources differ on cold use. The retargeting-only rule [L0324, 2025+] applies by default. The cold top-of-funnel build [L1510, 2020-26] applies only once the store has passed the starting stage described above [L1088, 2020; L0968, 2021]. [L0324, 2025+; L1510, 2020-26; L1088, 2020; L0968, 2021]
- YouTube ads can serve two purposes: warming up past visitors to raise conversion rate (retargeting), and introducing the brand to cold users who search for the product but have not seen the store yet. The same setup works for both audiences. [L1510, 2020-26]
- YouTube can create ad tools through Google Ads, and a Google support document covers the setup process. It is a separate ad format to consider alongside Shopping and Search campaigns. [L1093, 2023]
- YouTube ads can target any channel regardless of its size, unlike Facebook, where targeting is limited to certain page types. [L0510, 2018]
- A YouTube channel that drives zero direct-attributed conversions may still deliver value through brand awareness, audience building for retargeting, and pixel/cookie placement. Measure its indirect impact using assisted conversions in Google Ads. [L0257, 2020-26]

**How a view is counted and billed**

- Sources describe the counted view in two ways. A view counts when the viewer watches 15 seconds, or the whole video if it is shorter, and these views are relatively cheap. [L0510, 2018] A view counts when the viewer watches a significant portion of the ad or clicks it, and skipping before that point is not billed. [L1510, 2020-26]
- On YouTube you pay per view, and a view only counts when the viewer chooses to watch a significant portion of the ad. Facebook can count a mere impression as a view. Skipping an in-stream ad costs nothing. [L1510, 2020-26]

**Format definitions**

- In-stream ads (pre-roll) are charged only when a viewer watches a significant part of the ad or clicks it. [L1510, 2020-26]
- Discovery ads appear as suggested videos in the sidebar, on the YouTube homepage, or in the search results for a keyword. [L1510, 2020-26]

### Display Network: reach and when prospecting makes sense

**Reach figures (older, 2021-2022 source; verify current values)**

- The Google Display Network can reach about 97% of internet users. This is a reach figure for the whole network and does not mean those users are relevant buyers for a given product. [L0725, 2021-22]
- At the time of the source, Google's own reach figure for US internet users on the Display Network was 92% across millions of sites, videos, and devices. The source said the figure was higher by then. Treat it as a dated figure for the US and check the current value in Google's tools before using it. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Around 2021 to 2022, websites monetized through the AdSense publisher network had about 204 million visitors per month combined in the US. This is a dated US figure and should be verified before use. [L0725, 2021-22]
- At the time of the source, YouTube had over 1 billion video views per day, and Google properties accounted for about 5% of all online time spent. These are dated global figures that show the scale of Google-owned placements. [L0725, 2021-22]
- The DoubleClick Ad Exchange connects hundreds of premium content publishers that offer hundreds of millions of ad placements every day, according to a 2021 to 2022 figure that should be verified before use. This exchange is one of the three groups of properties that make up the Display Network. [L0725, 2021-22]
- YouTube was described as the second largest search engine in the world around 2021 to 2022, with over 1 billion video views per day. Video placements on YouTube are therefore a large Display Network inventory, but the figures are dated and should be checked in current Google sources. [L0725, 2021-22]

**Where display ads run**

- Display ads run on third-party websites and apps monetized through the AdSense publisher network or the DoubleClick Ad Exchange, and also on Google-owned properties such as YouTube, Gmail, Google Maps, Blogger, and Google Finance. They do not appear in the main Google search results. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Search network ads reach people who are actively looking for something, so their intent is visible from the search terms. Display ads interrupt people while they browse other content, similar to Facebook ads, so their intent is much weaker. [L0725, 2021-22]
- The Display Network contains a large share of irrelevant traffic. The source estimates that roughly 99% of available impressions will not match your market. Relevant audiences can still be reached, but only through careful targeting. [L0725, 2021-22]

**When to use display**

- For a high-ticket dropshipping store, the Display Network is best used for retargeting ads (static and dynamic) and targeted lead generation ads. Using it for cold prospecting is an advanced strategy. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Display prospecting should only start once the business is already profitable from other traffic sources, such as Google Shopping, search text ads, and organic search. Retargeting can and should run from the start. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Cold display audiences are usually far from the bottom of the sales funnel, so a direct offer for an expensive product such as a $4,000 item rarely converts from a first impression. People need time and a reason to trust before they buy. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Cold display traffic has a longer gap between the first click and the purchase, so ad spend must continue for a long time before returns come in. Only businesses with cash to keep investing should attempt this. Businesses running on thin margins should first improve the sources they already have. [L0725, 2021-22]
- A lead generation approach to cold display traffic depends on having useful content on the website to send visitors to. Without content pages, the funnel has nowhere to move the visitor next. [L0725, 2021-22]

**Cold display procedure for a high-ticket store**

1. Run a lead generation ad that sends cold visitors to a content page or lead page on your website. [L0725, 2021-22]
2. Retarget those visitors with display ads (static and dynamic) for product pages, content pages, and collection pages. [L0725, 2021-22]
3. Get visitors to opt into your email list and deliver value through content before making a sales pitch for an expensive product. [L0725, 2021-22]

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Display ads from large brands for expensive products, such as car makers in major newspapers, are usually branding campaigns with budgets in the millions and no direct tracking. A small store cannot copy this approach, so its own display plan should be built around direct response and sales within a reasonable time frame. [L0725, 2021-22]
- Before copying a competitor's ad strategy, check what kind of company it is and how much money it can spend. Results that work for a different business model or budget are not a reliable guide for your store. [L0725, 2021-22]
- One advertiser can fill several ad slots on the same large news page at once, which signals a very large budget. A small store cannot copy this kind of placement and should judge display placements by the advertiser's size and goal before treating them as a model. [L0725, 2021-22]

**Sidebar and footer testing**

- If a site shows Google-served ads in its sidebar or footer and offers no direct ad deal, you can still test ads there by running Google display ads that appear only on that website. [L0528, 2025+]
