### Display ad formats and CTA

- On the Google Display Network, the same ad can use a plain text-based link-style CTA or a button-style CTA. Keep the same image and headline when switching between the two styles. [L0415, 2021-22]

### Shopping showcase ads

- Showcase ads in Shopping campaigns show searchers a swipeable carousel of brands and products, leading to a brand store-style page and then to single products. The lesson reports a very high CTR and says the first click is free. Verify that billing claim in current Google Ads terms before relying on it. [L0712, 2020-26]
- Product showcase ads require uploaded images: a logo, a header image, and a lifestyle photo, such as a model wearing or using the product outdoors. The lifestyle photo works best as the top photo of the set. Product images can be added as well. [L0712, 2020-26]
- The product showcase description is limited to 120 characters. [L0712, 2020-26]

**Create a product showcase ad**

1. Create a new campaign and choose the goal Shopping. [L0712, 2020-26]
2. Select your merchant feed and go to the next screen. [L0712, 2020-26]
3. Enter a campaign name and click Save and continue. [L0712, 2020-26]
4. On the showcase format screen, choose to show a selection of products and put your top products first. [L0712, 2020-26]
5. Upload the header image, the logo and, if used, the product images. [L0712, 2020-26]
6. Enter the display URLs the ad should lead to. [L0712, 2020-26]
7. Write a headline and a description of up to 120 characters. [L0712, 2020-26]

### Demand Gen

**Why Demand Gen matters**

- A Google Shopping listing shows only an image, a title and a price, so the image is one of the main reasons a shopper clicks. Demand Gen native image ads run in browsing placements such as YouTube in-feed and Google Discover, where images also drive the ad. [L0882, 2025-26]
- Brands scaling through Shopping and Demand Gen spend about 10,000 to 40,000 US dollars per day across both channels, according to the lesson. Demand Gen image placements are described as one of the most under-used scaling options. [L0882, 2025-26]

**Assets**

- A Demand Gen ad can use up to 20 images, up to 5 headlines and up to 5 descriptions. Google combines these assets into different ad combinations, so each asset should work with any other asset. [L0882, 2025-26]

**Image sizes**

| Format | Aspect ratio | Pixel size | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landscape | 1.91:1 | 1200 x 628 px | YouTube in-feed |
| Square | 1:1 | 1200 x 1200 px | Works across both placements |
| Portrait | 4:5 | 960 x 1200 px | Discover |

[L0882, 2025-26]

**Channels**

- To choose where a Demand Gen ad runs, open the ad group settings and go to Channels. Select All Google channels to run everywhere eligible, or select Let me choose and tick the channels to use, such as YouTube in-stream, YouTube Shorts, Discover, Gmail, Google Display Network or Maps. [L0882, 2025-26]
- YouTube in-stream and YouTube Shorts run on YouTube surfaces. Discover shows ads in personalized content feeds on Google mobile apps. Gmail shows ads inside users' Gmail inboxes. Google Display Network runs ads on third-party sites and apps and may modify the ad to fit each publisher's format. Maps shows ads on Google Maps and is labelled NEW in the interface. [L0882, 2025-26]
- Before saving a Demand Gen ad group, the Channels panel shows an estimate of conversions and impressions for the channels currently selected, plus the total available ad group impressions. Checking these estimates while ticking channels helps compare which placements will bring volume before publishing. [L0882, 2025-26]

**Ad group page**

- Above the Channels section, the Demand Gen ad group page has Ad group name, Locations and Languages fields. The example uses United States (country) and All languages. [L0882, 2025-26]
- The page is saved with the buttons Save all changes, Duplicate ad group and Discard all changes at the bottom. [L0882, 2025-26]

### Pinterest and secondary formats

- Pinterest promoted pins were in beta at the time of recording and were billed on a cost-per-click basis. You only pay when a user clicks through from the pin to your website. [L1136, 2014]
- Pinterest ad targeting uses search keywords, which are displayed as separate keyword segments in search results, plus location, language, device and gender. Choose the keywords that match what users search for, then select the audience filters before paying. [L1136, 2014]
- Promoted pins often earned free repins more easily than they earned paid clicks back to the store in the speaker's tests. Repins still spread the content and can later lead to purchases or viral reach. [L1136, 2014]
- Pinterest promoted pins are new, so there is little public data on the best way to use them. Traffic from them tends to be browsing traffic, so the store needs a strong funnel with retargeting and an email opt-in to capture it. [L1136, 2014]

### Measurement across channels

- Switching on a Google Ads search campaign raised overall website traffic, even though the campaign running at that time was a YouTube video campaign that sent no visitors directly to the site. Track total site traffic alongside per-campaign clicks to see this kind of cross-channel lift. [L1142, 2020-26]
- Targeting an interest category with a highly relevant video ad can produce very cheap views, because relevance drives down cost per view. Relevance of message, audience and timing matters more than budget size for this effect. [L1142, 2020-26]
- Brand recognition built up by earlier video ads raises the click-through rate of later search ads, because searchers already recognize the brand. Plan search ads as a follow-up to awareness campaigns, not only as standalone direct-response buys. [L1142, 2020-26]

**Campaign- and ad-group-level video cost check**

1. Open Supermetrics and choose the last 30 days. [L1236, 2015+]
2. Add cost, clicks and cost per view. [L1236, 2015+]
3. Split the rows by campaign name and ad group. [L1236, 2015+]
4. Apply the cost-per-view KPI of 0.05 USD and conditional formatting that turns cells yellow when they are greater than 0.05. [L1236, 2015+]
5. Drag the formula down for all campaigns and check for flagged rows. [L1236, 2015+]

- In a YouTube awareness campaign, the ad group for retargeting pre-roll and awareness showed a cost per view above the 0.05 USD threshold. Breaking it down by video title identified the specific video and audience combinations that were not performing, which pointed to exactly which ads to change. [L1236, 2015+]
