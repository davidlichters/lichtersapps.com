### Merchant Center Promotions

#### Setup

Procedure for creating promotions (2023):

1. Create the promotion in Google Merchant Center or in Shopify's Google channel settings, following the Shopify support guide for promotions in Google Shopping ads. [L0430, 2023]
2. Set the promotion's discount code, date range and eligible products. [L0430, 2023]
3. Confirm the promotion is approved in Merchant Center before relying on it in ads. [L0430, 2023]

Example procedure with coupon codes (2025):

1. Create the promotion in Merchant Center, for example 10% off with coupon code FF. [L0255, 2025]
2. Ensure the coupon code is active on the website for the entire promotion period. [L0255, 2025]
3. Double and triple check that the code works throughout the duration. [L0255, 2025]
4. If the code expires or stops working before the promotion end date, Google may suspend the Merchant Center account. [L0255, 2025]

Setup facts:

- Promotions are added in Merchant Center as an add-on to the Shopping account. (2025) [L0472, 2025]
- Promotions are created in Merchant Center. A Google support document explains how to set one up for Shopping ads, and promotions attach discount or offer messaging to products shown in Shopping results. [L1093, 2023]
- Google Shopping ads are supported by the Merchant Center promotions setting. (2022) [L0665, 2022]
- Create coupon-code promotions in Merchant Center to show the discount on Google Shopping listings. [L0667, 2025]
- Promotions are created inside Merchant Center to show discount offers in Google Shopping results. Set them up before expecting them to appear on listings. (2025) [L0675, 2025]
- Merchant Center sections to review regularly are Promotions (shown next to product titles in Shopping ads) and Review feeds (uploaded directly or through an app). [L0732, 2020-26]

#### What promotions can show

- Google Shopping promotions can show badges such as low price or great pick alongside ads quickly, without needing store history. They are the fastest way to match competitors' badges (2022). [L0429, 2022]
- Promotions can add extra clickable text next to the store name in results, which can improve CTR (2021). [L0469, 2021]
- Shopping ads can show promotional text such as a price advantage over another retailer. In the example shown, a 31% price drop was displayed in the ad. [L0762, 2020-26]
- Promotions display discount labels such as `save 350, expires in 4 days`. [L0472, 2025]
- Advertise active discounts through the promotion function in Merchant Center. The discounts then appear in Shopping ads, which gives a visual advantage over competitors. [L1357, 2018]
- Use Merchant Center promotions so your offer appears in Google Shopping results. This gives an advantage over stores that do not run promotions there. [L0920, 2018]

#### Where promotions are not shown

- Google Shopping promotions are not shown in the free organic section. Low-margin products can therefore stay in the free listings, while promotions are set up only for the best-selling, highest-margin products in the new best-seller campaigns. (2025) [L0472, 2025]

#### Eligibility and rules

- Promotions accept only percent-off or fixed-amount offers. A bundle is treated as a new product that must be submitted to Merchant Center, not as a promotion on an existing product (2020). [L1088, 2020]
- Google's shipping promotion policy changed, so a free shipping promotion may be rejected for requiring a coupon code. Check the Merchant Center promotion policy before running it (2024). [L1115, 2024]
- Do not create a free shipping promotion with a coupon code just to satisfy the policy, because this can interfere with other coupons. Use another structure, such as a small fixed discount on a large order (2024). [L1115, 2024]
- Free shipping can be shown as a setting or tag in Merchant Center, and these special tags can appear in Google Shopping ads. [L0788, undated; L1094, 2023]

#### Case

- A store with little sales despite on-site optimisations added Google Shopping promotions to its shopping ads. Sales went from near zero to roughly 20 to 25 within a couple of weeks (2025). [L0472, 2025]

### Feed Effects on Quality Score, CTR and Impressions

#### Quality Score

- Complete feed data feeds into Quality Score. [L0879, 2021-22]
- Platform gains stack in a positive feedback loop. A better title raises CTR. Higher CTR raises quality score, which lowers CPC and improves position. Each optimisation makes the next one more valuable. [L0510, 2018]
- Case: an advertiser selling a golf-ball type product optimised its campaign for CTR. CTR rose by 108%, cost per click fell by 25%, and quality score rose by 60%. The change let the advertiser get cheaper clicks than competitors who were paying more for weaker ads. [L0510, 2018]

#### CTR

- CTR can be improved through product titles, descriptions, images, bids and negatives. After the click, the store experience should keep visitors engaged (2022). [L0969, 2022]
- A title that is optimised to raise CTR while keeping the product accurate reduces wasted clicks. [L0510, 2018; L0969, 2022]
- To raise CTR without changing targeting, spell out in the ad why a premium product is worth its price, such as what makes it more premium than cheaper alternatives. Clear reasons for a higher price attract the right clicks. [L0327, 2020-26]
- Intentionally decreasing CTR can improve profitability. Example: if your product costs $900 and competitors charge $800, include the price in ads to reduce clicks from price-focused customers. This lowers CTR but saves money and increases ROAS. [L0327, 2020-26]

#### Impressions

- Moving a commonly searched term to the front of the title raised CTR for those terms by about 88%, and lifted impressions across all products by about 18%. This comes from a study of thousands of Shopping ads. [L0510, 2018]
- Title tests in another study reported about a 33% increase in impressions and about a 320% change in revenue. One example reported a 400% increase in impressions after a single word was added to the front of an old title. [L0510, 2018]
- Adding a converting high-volume generic term to the front of a title increased impressions in the examples shown. [L0454, 2019]
- Titles improved to rank higher and earn more clicks in organic search also flow into the Shopping feed, so one title edit can help both channels. [L0762, 2020-26]

#### Title as an impression lever

- Title is the lever that controls search reach. Image controls click. Keep both in mind when running tests. [L0220, 2025-26; L1508, 2022+]

### Competitive Positioning

- A product that looks like every other listing gets fewer clicks, so visible differences in photo and title matter. [L1508, 2022+]
- Ads that are cheaper per click than competitors' often come from a stronger ad, not a lower bid. The golf-ball case shows a CTR-led change that made clicks cheaper than rivals paying more. [L0510, 2018]

### Review Cadence

- Review converting search terms about once a quarter, around four times a year, and add them to titles. [L0454, 2019]
- Repeat title review regularly, because new search terms appear over time. [L0510, 2018]
- Review the feed regularly, not only at launch. [L1170, 2021-22]
