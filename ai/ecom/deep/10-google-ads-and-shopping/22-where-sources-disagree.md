### Where Sources Disagree

- **Full catalog at launch versus product-level split from the start.** Launch with the full catalog in alpha and beta, then split products by individual product once data arrives [L0941, 2022; L1277, 2020; L1111, 2020]. Older 2020 advice also gives a per-product bid split from the outset [L1277, 2020; L0852, 2020]. Apply the full-catalog launch first. Apply the per-product split when there is data to justify each product's bid.
- **Brand split versus product split.** one source's older default splits Shopping campaigns by brand within three priority levels (high, medium and low). Split by price, margin or product type only when there is a specific reason for that product group [L0879, 2021-22]. one source's guidance splits standard Shopping by individual product so that performance is visible per product [L1092, 2022; L0746, 2020-26]. A brand split is also named as a valid option for bid control [L1112, 2021]. Use the brand split where the three-priority-level structure is in use. Use the per-product split for standard Shopping.
- **Number of campaign tiers.** The default is two tiers, alpha and beta. A three-tier structure also works [L0971, 2024]. The standard Shopping setup named as Generic, Brand and SKU [L1033, 2019] and the three priority levels named by one source [L0879, 2021-22] are both options. The basic starting setup of four campaigns adds remarketing campaigns on Google and Meta [L0972, 2025]. Start with two tiers. Add the others as the account needs them.
- **Negative keywords.** Negative keywords are added to both campaigns from the start [L0961, 2020]. Adding many negative keywords can block searches that would have converted, so they should be used with care [L0805, 2024]. Negative keywords and bid adjustments can wait until there is data [L1111, 2020].
- **Smart Shopping versus smart campaigns.** Smart Shopping as a campaign type is phased out and should not be used [L0854, 2022]. Smart campaigns offered on the goal screen are described as suitable for beginners [L1336, 2020-26]. Advanced advertisers should use custom campaigns [L1336, 2020-26].
- **Starting bid examples.** The 2026 example uses $1.00 for alpha and $0.50 for beta, with a starting range of $0.50 to $1.00 CPC [L0808, 2026]. The 2020 example raises one product's cost per click from 0.20 USD to 0.25 USD [L1277, 2020]. These are examples for different situations. Neither is a fixed rule.
