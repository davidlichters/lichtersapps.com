## Account Suspensions, Appeals and Google Support

### Why Accounts Get Suspended

#### Merchant Center: Misrepresentation and Data Mismatches

- Google suspends Merchant Center accounts mainly for five reasons: business information that does not match the website, incomplete product data, missing required business information on the website, incomplete Merchant Center settings, and products in prohibited categories. A suspension is not a deletion; the account is only blocked from use. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
- Suspensions often carry the reason misrepresentation, which means Google believes some information is missing or wrong. Suspension notices arrive by email to the business email address set up for the account. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
- A suspension is a legitimacy check, not a final verdict. Google suspends to protect searchers and to screen out scam or low-quality stores. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
- New accounts are checked most strictly and are often suspended almost immediately. Established accounts can also be suspended after two years or more of trading. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
- Google tightened account security after the e-commerce boom in 2020, so suspensions can also hit legitimate sellers. A suspension can be reversed, so fix the cause and request reinstatement rather than panic. ** [L0731, 2021-22]
- Common causes of suspension are shipping or tax information in Merchant Center that does not match the Shopify store, product prices in the catalog that do not match the store, missing contact information, and a missing privacy policy. ** [L0801, 2021]
- Missing or inconsistent contact details can cause a contact-information suspension. The contact page should offer at least two contact methods, such as a phone number, an email address or a contact form, plus a privacy policy. ** [L0801, 2021]
- A currency mismatch, missing contact details or a missing privacy policy can flag an account. Fix these and then appeal. ** [L0468, 2020]
- A missing or unverified business phone number in Merchant Center is a recurring trigger for misrepresentation. ** [L0247, 2021; L0962, 2021; L0429, 2022]
- Shipping costs, the shipping policy and the privacy policy URLs in Merchant Center must match what appears in the Shopify store, because Google bots add products to cart to check this. [L0856, 2024]
- Footer social links that point to Shopify social pages or to platform home pages, instead of your own profiles, were a known cause of a Merchant Center suspension. Change them to your own profiles or remove them, then resubmit. *(Advice from 2023.)* [L0934, 2023]
- Misrepresentation often comes from missing policy pages and content. Make sure the store has all required policies and pages. ** [L1281, 2022]
- Vague or missing wording in shipping and return policies is a common cause of a suspension without a clear reason. Check that both policies are clear and complete. [L0808, 2026]
- Never invent a GTIN or enter a third-party GTIN that does not belong to the product. Google treats a mismatched GTIN as misrepresentation, which can lead to suspension. [L1283, 2023]
- A missing GTIN alone does not cause a misrepresentation suspension. The problem arises only when a wrong identifier is placed in the GTIN field. [L1283, 2023]
- A contact detail or policy that differs even slightly from the Merchant Center entry can trigger misrepresentation. Example: a suite number written differently. Compare address, phone, email and every policy character by character with the store. [L1283, 2023]
- A Merchant Center suspension can have many causes. Check the GTIN, verified phone number, shipping, return and privacy policies, and product detail content, and use the full checklist before escalating to support. ** [L0469, 2021]
- Installed dropshipping apps alone were not considered a cause of account rejection. The likely trigger is a red flag on the store that a Google bot detects as dropshipping. [L0577, 2023]
- Blocking Google's checkout crawler to hide abandoned checkouts also leads to automatic feed suspension. Do not try to block it. ** [L1170, 2021-22]
- Test ads with fake URLs or non-existent websites can get a Google Ads account suspended. Ads must point to the business website linked to the account. ** [L0727, 2021-22]

#### Google Ads: Account-Level Reasons

- Google Ads accounts are suspended for specific reasons: incorrect business information; multiple Google Ads accounts tied to the same business or personal information, including old dormant accounts after a prior suspension; a payment method flagged as fraudulent or previously bounced; and ads that point to a URL other than the business website or to a website that does not exist. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
- A Google Ads suspension applies to the whole advertiser account and every related account. While suspended, you cannot open new accounts, and ads stop running on Search, search partner sites and the Display Network at the same time. A store that relies on one paid channel can lose all paid traffic at once. [L0380, 2014]
- Google can flag an ad account as restricted in a country where the advertiser does not sell or advertise. This is usually an automated error. ** [L0803, 2022]
- For suspected bot clicks, contact Google support by live chat for the recommended course of action before relying on a click-blocking tool alone. ** [L1279, 2021]
- A Google Ads suspension for suspicious payment activity is usually caused by billing details that do not match the account, which is covered in the appeal steps below. [L0806, 2024]

#### Circumventing Systems and Linked Accounts

- The circumventing systems suspension usually occurs when Google believes a new account was opened after a previous account was closed. [L1283, 2023]
- The circumventing flag often appears when a previously suspended account used the same billing address, credit card or website as a new account. Creating a new account to get around a policy triggers this flag. [L0857, 2025]
- Google can link new ad accounts to earlier ones through IP address, cookies, name, address and card details. Creating a new ad account after a suspension therefore usually fails. ** [L0963, 2022]
- A suspension on one platform does not automatically affect other platforms. Accounts on Microsoft, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and Instagram can keep running. ** [L0963, 2022]
- Using agency accounts to circumvent bans on Google Merchant Center is not recommended. It can lead to a cycle of banned accounts and costs a lot of money. [L0577, 2023]
- Only use.com domains for the store. Do not switch to a.store or.us domain to fix a Google Ads policy problem, because changing domains to get around a flag can look like circumventing systems. [L0857, 2025]
- The circumventing flag can be a false flag when a new account was opened after a previously closed account. If you never had an earlier account, appeal the suspension instead of creating a new one. [L0965, 2024]

### Response Procedure After a Suspension

#### First Steps

1. Do not reply immediately, and do not reply in anger. [L0727, 2021-22]
2. Read the suspension reason in Google's email. It may be a warning giving 7 days to fix the issue, or an immediate suspension. [L0727, 2021-22]
3. Check that the business information in the account is complete. [L0727, 2021-22]
4. Make sure the website follows the business launch checklist. [L0727, 2021-22]
5. Check product data in the feed for disapproved products and missing attributes. [L0727, 2021-22]
6. Make sure shipping, tax, phone number and other account information match the website exactly. [L0727, 2021-22]
7. Delete old, unused Google Ads accounts. [L0727, 2021-22]
8. Submit a detailed, neutral response through the form or avenue in Google's email, explaining what was checked and changed and asking for a review. [L0727, 2021-22]
9. Expect a review within one to two days, sometimes up to a week. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
10. Unsuspension responses must be neutral and factual. An angry or terse reply makes the problem worse and delays the review. ** [L0727, 2021-22]
11. When a Merchant Center issue is unclear, respond calmly, check the official Google policy pages, and ask an expert or coach rather than attempting a risky fix alone. ** [L0730, 2021-22]

#### Merchant Center Recovery (Current Method)

1. Read the compliance checklist lesson and confirm every recommended setting is in place. ** [L1279, 2021]
2. Log in to Merchant Center and click through all settings, making sure each is complete. ** [L0962, 2021]
3. Check the phone number under contact info in Merchant Center settings. [L1279, 2021]
4. Check whether the business phone number is verified. ** [L0962, 2021]
5. Verify the phone number. Depending on what Merchant Center offers, use the verification by text message [L0247, 2021], the automated call [L0962, 2021], or the PIN sent by phone call [L1279, 2021]. **
6. Reply to Google's suspension email and request a review. In the source example, reinstatement came the same day. ** [L0962, 2021]
7. Follow up with Google until the account is reinstated. [L1279, 2021]

#### Merchant Center: Full Review Request

1. Log in to Google Merchant Center. [L1094, 2023]
2. Add the return policy and make the other changes Google asked for. [L1094, 2023]
3. Find the request review option, which may appear in the first email Google sent. [L1094, 2023]
4. Submit the request. After the first few reviews, Google may impose a waiting period of about one week before the next submission. [L1094, 2023]
5. Before requesting review, correct all mismatches. The first suspension round is usually handled by an algorithm, and a human reviews the store only after a manual review request. [L1283, 2023]
6. Read the reason Google gives and fix the mismatched item. [L1283, 2023]
7. Check that every setting and policy in Merchant Center matches the store exactly. [L1283, 2023]
8. Submit for a review. If suspended again, request a manual review. [L1283, 2023]
9. In the manual review request, list each correction you made and state that you checked all Google policies. [L1283, 2023]
10. Do not request a review again until the problems are fixed. [L0962, 2021]
11. After a Merchant Center suspension is fixed, wait for Google's review result before changing other platforms. Once confirmed, make sure the same errors do not repeat on Microsoft, Bing or Meta. [L0934, 2023]
12. Prepare the fix before appealing, so the first appeal succeeds. A failed appeal can be resubmitted. [L0934, 2023]
13. Manual review requests for suspended products are typically decided within 24 to 48 hours. Use them when a flag appears to be an algorithm misreading rather than a true violation. ** [L1170, 2021-22]

#### Merchant Center: Standard Template

Use this message when requesting a Google Merchant Center account review after a suspension. Replace each bracketed placeholder with the real information and keep every sentence in the same order:

```
To whom this may concern,

We are submitting our store for review after our account was initially suspended for [reason] on [date]. We understand that our initial submission was missing [issues]. All of these issues have now been addressed in their entirety. After reviewing all of the Google Merchant Center terms of service, we now believe our site is in full compliance with your policies. [List each change you made to meet compliance.]

Thank you for your time and review.
```
[L1094, 2023]

#### Google Ads: Response Routine

1. Identify the suspension reason first by contacting support. Do not create a new Google Ads account with the same email, card on file or URL. [L0278, 2025]
2. Fix whatever caused the suspension. [L0359, 2020]
3. Contact Google Ads support by phone first, or by live chat if phone support is unavailable. [L0359, 2020]
4. Ask the support team to reinstate the suspended account. [L0359, 2020]
5. Use the Google Ads support page to find the contact options. [L0359, 2020]
6. Only if reinstatement fails, create another Google account. [L0359, 2020] *(Note: the 2025 rule in step 1 and the rule against new accounts in the Avoiding New Accounts section both apply before this step.)* [L0278, 2025; L0963, 2022]
7. Keep records of every change made. ** [L0801, 2021]

#### Recovery Routines by Suspension Type

**Suspension for misrepresentation (Google Ads)**

1. Re-check the store's content (business details, product claims, policies) against the policy requirements and fix any mismatch. [L0666, 2024]
2. If nothing stands out, ask an outside reviewer to look at the store with fresh eyes. [L0666, 2024]
3. Search community posts for the policy name to see what others changed before their account was reinstated. [L0666, 2024]
4. Contact Google Ads support and request a review of the store against Google's policy requirements before reinstatement. [L0970, 2023]
5. Follow up the compliance lesson, make the policy adjustments, then request a review through support with your store details. [L0855, 2023]

**Suspension for misrepresentation (Merchant Center, with appeal button unavailable)**

1. Follow up with the Google contact you are already waiting on. ** [L0803, 2022]
2. Re-check the compliance checklist and fix any gaps. ** [L0803, 2022]
3. If the appeal button is unavailable in your account, call Microsoft Merchant Center support, which runs 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific, and ask for a manual review. ** [L0803, 2022]

**Suspension for the circumventing systems policy, first Google Ads account**

1. Do not change the domain or create a new account. [L0857, 2025]
2. Request a review from Google. [L0857, 2025]
3. State that this is the first Google Ads account for the store or business, and ask for a human to review it. [L0857, 2025]

**Suspension for suspicious payment activity (Google Ads)**

1. Contact Google Ads support through live chat. This is a different team from Merchant Center support. [L0806, 2024]
2. Ask for the specific reason for the suspension. [L0806, 2024]
3. Check that the billing address on the card matches the address on the account. [L0806, 2024]
4. Ask whether anything else must be submitted, or whether the account can be reviewed again. [L0806, 2024]
5. Submit the appeal only after this check. [L0806, 2024]
6. A suspended account for suspicious payment activity is usually fixed by contacting support, not by hiring an agency. An agency is not needed if the business model has not changed. [L0806, 2024]

**Google Ads suspended, and no reason given**

1. Use live chat with the platform and keep contacting support until a person states the specific reason. ** [L1279, 2021]
2. Fix the stated problem, such as an unverified phone number in Merchant Center contact info, and verify it with a PIN sent by call. ** [L1279, 2021]
3. Keep following up with the platform until the account is reinstated. ** [L1279, 2021]
4. Ask Google for the specific suspension reason, so you can address it. ** [L1281, 2022]
5. If an account is suspended without a clear reason, check that shipping and return policies are clear and complete. [L0808, 2026]
6. Explain the situation calmly on live chat, describe what other stores do, and ask for the terms to be checked before the restriction stays in place. ** [L1090, 2021]

**Disapproval that cites a reason that does not apply**

1. A store received a Google Ads disapproval citing a reason that did not apply, stating the ads were illegal in a country where nothing was advertised. The store contacted support with the details and the issue was resolved in a few hours. ** [L0801, 2021]
2. Google reasons are sometimes wrong, so contact support directly. ** [L0801, 2021]
3. Use live chat through the Google Ads help form to resolve product feed problems such as pending or disapproved products, instead of waiting for phone support. ** [L0852, 2020]

**Store restricted in a country it does not sell in**

1. If the account offers no appeal option, contact Google directly. ** [L0803, 2022]
2. State that the store does not target that country, and ask for a manual check. ** [L0803, 2022]

**Domain may be linked to a suspended ad account**

1. Ask Google Ads support through live chat, describing the earlier suspension. ** [L0963, 2022]
2. Do not guess the policy. ** [L0963, 2022]
3. Screenshot the written answer and keep it on file. ** [L0963, 2022]

**Reactivated Google Ads account where ads are not running**

1. Recreate the campaigns and allow a couple of days for approval. [L0964, 2023]
2. The account and Merchant Center may just need time. [L0964, 2023]

#### Video Ads and Evidence

- Before appealing a suspension for alleged policy circumvention, check that each video ad is an original file and contains no nudity or graphic content. Keep this evidence ready to include with the appeal. [L1214, 2023+]

### Appeals and Review Timing

#### Appeal Rules

- Google allows a limited number of suspension appeals through the review form. Reaching a person through live chat or phone support is a separate route that can be used in addition to appeals. [L0360, 2024]
- Google bots crawl stores and can flag a store wrongly, which leads to a back-and-forth until a human reviews it. A suspension is often resolved by getting a human to look at the store rather than by repeating automated appeals. [L0360, 2024]
- Reinstatement sometimes happens when you reach the right person at the right time, so keep following up. ** [L0671, 2021]
- A Google Ads account and a Merchant Center account can be reinstated separately. A Google Ads account may stay suspended for months after Merchant Center is reinstated. [L0964, 2023]
- A Google Ads appeal for an ad that was not in compliance is a valid route; do not run ads before the store has real products and is open for business. ** [L1111, 2020]
- After a suspension, first resolve the issues on the existing account before creating a new business account. [L0965, 2024]

#### Review Timing by Source

| Review type | Reported timing | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant Center suspension, first review | 1-2 days, sometimes up to a week | [L0727, 2021-22] |
| Google Ads appeal decision | About 3-5 business days | [L0971, 2024] |
| Google appeal response (2023 advice) | About 1-2 weeks; resubmit if it fails | [L0934, 2023] |

| Google appeal, member-reported | Up to about 5 weeks | [L1281, 2022] |

| Merchant Center reinstatement, single example | Same day | [L0962, 2021] |

- After the first few Merchant Center reviews, Google may impose a waiting period of about one week before the next submission. [L1094, 2023]
- Google Merchant Center reinstatement can be followed by a cooldown period before reinstatement, after fixing a missing verified phone number or policy issues and resubmitting. ** [L0429, 2022]

#### Retry and Follow-Up Schedule

1. Keep running other traffic channels in the meantime. [L0806, 2024]
2. Set a weekly phone reminder. [L0806, 2024]
3. Each time, open Google Ads support live chat, which is a different team from Merchant Center support. [L0806, 2024]
4. Ask for the specific suspension reason and keep trying until you reach the right team. [L0806, 2024]
5. Keep records of every change made, and follow up repeatedly, since resolution can take many attempts. ** [L0801, 2021]
6. Keep following up during the review period, and send the reason for the suspension if you have it. ** [L1281, 2022]
7. Contact Google support every day, by phone when available, or by live chat otherwise. ** [L0671, 2021]
8. For untrustworthy promotions suspensions, if you did not violate policy, follow up daily via live chat and phone calls, and escalate to a supervisor. ** [L0268, 2020]
