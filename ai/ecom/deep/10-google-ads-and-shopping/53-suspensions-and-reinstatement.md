### Suspensions and reinstatement

#### Why it happens

- New Microsoft Ads accounts are often suspended automatically within a day or two of creation, sometimes on the same day, for a vague policy reason with no specific details. [L0373, 2024+]
- This happens even when the store is compliant. A suspension alone does not mean the advertiser did something wrong. [L0373, 2024+]
- A new account may be suspended the next day with a notice citing an egregious policy violation and a list of possible causes, without saying which one applies. Treat this as a common platform issue and appeal it, rather than assuming the store broke a rule. [L0373, 2024+]
- Microsoft Ads commonly suspends new accounts. Resolve any Google issues first, then pursue the Microsoft suspension. [L0855, 2023]

#### Appeal procedures

Procedure A (if a new account is suspended):

1. Check the store against a platform compliance checklist. [L0373, 2024+]
2. Submit an appeal through the account. [L0373, 2024+]
3. Wait for a human reviewer to visit the site, which usually leads to reinstatement. [L0373, 2024+]

Procedure B (reinstatement after an immediate suspension, 2024):

1. Visit the Microsoft support page every two to three days. [L0360, 2024]
2. Request help, say that the store follows all policies, and ask for a human review of the store. [L0360, 2024]
3. Keep repeating until a reviewer who can look at the store is reached (about one week in one case). [L0360, 2024]

Procedure C (new account closed automatically without a clear reason, 2023):

1. Set a daily reminder for the same time each day. [L0804, 2023]
2. Send a message to Bing support every day. [L0804, 2023]
3. Try live chat and open support tickets as well. [L0804, 2023]
4. Keep following up until a human reviews the account, because new accounts are often flagged by automated checks. [L0804, 2023]

- For repeated suspensions, request live chat again and ask for a different representative. [L0855, 2023]

#### Merchant Center flags

- Microsoft Merchant Center can flag a store for professional services or unallowed characters in product data. Tobacco or e-cigarette ads are also flagged. [L0803, 2022; older]
- Some flags cannot be appealed from inside the account. Contact Microsoft support directly and ask for a manual review. [L0803, 2022; older]

#### Using Microsoft as a backup

- if Google Shopping is blocked by a suspension, use Microsoft as a backup traffic source:
  1. Set up a Microsoft store. [L0963, 2022; older]
  2. Create a Microsoft Shopping campaign. The traffic volume is smaller, but the campaign can still be profitable. [L0963, 2022; older]
- Case (Google account suspended): a Shopify store open since January with over 100 orders sold only through Microsoft Ads. Net profit was about 10 to 15%, around $2,000 in September. [L1116, 2025]
- Case (Merchant Center and Google Ads suspended): a store with about 200 products from seven brands ran Alpha and Beta campaigns on its full catalog across Google and Microsoft. Microsoft received about twice the spend of Google. See Optimization for the outcome and advice. [L0941, 2022]

### Ad formats and tracking

- Microsoft Advertising offers search text ads, the Bing counterpart to Google search text ads. They carry a small `Ad` label above the listing. [L1034, 2021+]
- Microsoft Advertising offers Shopping ads, the Bing counterpart to Google Shopping. They show when a user searches for a product, or a keyword related to a product sold in the store. [L1034, 2021+]
- Microsoft Shopping ads work the same way as Google Shopping. Set up Shopping ads after the search text campaigns and the organic webmaster setup. [L1034, 2021+]
- Bing Ads offers search text ads and other formats that copy Google's types. Import the Google campaigns into Bing and check Google first if Bing shows errors. [L0468, 2020; older]
- Conversion tracking is set up by adding Microsoft tags to the site as described in the Microsoft setup section of the advanced ads material. [L0855, 2023]
- Connect the UET tag to the Google Shopping app in the Microsoft Advertising Conversion section. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Bing search terms appear in the Microsoft Advertising dashboard. [L0834, 2021; older]

### Optimization

#### Core rule

- Optimize Bing Shopping campaigns weekly when first starting. Review performance numbers, identify negative keywords to add, and adjust bids up or down based on results. Treat Bing management the same as Google Ads optimization after import. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Microsoft Ads is optimized with the same actions as Google Ads: search term review, negative keywords and budget changes. Only the interface differs, so the Google Ads workflow can be applied directly. [L0807, 2025]
- The same optimization rules used for Google Ads also apply to Microsoft Ads (Bing). [L0857, 2025]
- The same Google Ads campaign optimization steps also apply to Microsoft Ads (Bing). The same tweaks can be made on both platforms before raising bids. Microsoft Ads should still be left out of the starting budget until campaigns are profitable. [L0360, 2024]
- Microsoft Ads responds to the same optimization as Google Ads, so apply the same Google Ads optimization process to Microsoft campaigns. [L1116, 2025]

#### Negative keywords

- On Microsoft Ads, removing negative keywords has a different impact than on Google Ads. If you have high impression share but low CTR and want to improve visibility, remove some negative keywords on Microsoft specifically before increasing bid. [L0272, 2022; older]

#### Troubleshooting an unprofitable campaign

- A Bing campaign that sends unprofitable traffic should be cut to about $10 in total until the settings are fixed. [L0857, 2025]
- Check the campaign's location and other settings before adding budget. [L0857, 2025]

#### Case: lowering spend on a losing account

- Case (2025): the owner of a store with over 100 orders feared paying too much per click and had not raised spend. The account had about 1,100 clicks from about 75,000 impressions on $650 of spend, on a shared $20 daily budget that was always maxed out. [L1116, 2025]
- Advice given: do not raise the budget yet. Review which products and keywords convert profitably and which do not, move budget toward the profitable ones, let the account run for a couple of weeks, confirm that return on ad spend rises, and only then increase the budget. [L1116, 2025]
- No final result was reported in the call. [L1116, 2025]

#### Case: Microsoft spend exceeded Google's return

- Case: Microsoft received about twice the spend of Google, yet Google had the better return. Google had four conversions from 566 pounds of spend, and total sales were reported around 4,400 pounds from 10 conversions. [L0941, 2022]
- Advice given: keep Microsoft off until extra profit covers it. Run Google only, review which products already sold, and lower CPC on products that spent at least 50 without converting. [L0941, 2022]

### Scaling

#### Scale only when proven

- Bing Ads should be added after the core setup and only when Google performs. [L0855, 2023; L0544, 2026+]
- Pause Bing Ads until Google Shopping is profitable, then move the proven campaigns to Bing. [L0804, 2023]
- restart Microsoft Ads only once the Google campaigns are profitable. [L0854, 2022; older]

#### Stepwise scaling procedure

1. Check which products and keywords convert profitably and which do not. [L1116, 2025]
2. Move budget away from non-converting or unprofitable keywords and products toward the most profitable ones. [L1116, 2025]
3. Let the adjusted account run for a couple of weeks. [L1116, 2025]
4. Confirm that return on ad spend improved. [L1116, 2025]
5. Only then increase the daily budget. [L1116, 2025]

- Increase budgets gradually. If results are positive after the 80% test, raise budgets step by step. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Start with about 10% of the total ad budget, optimize there, and only then scale. [L0856, 2024]

#### Scaling by account spend

- Below about $5,000 monthly ad spend, export Google Ads campaigns straight into Bing Ads, and later changes in Google Ads update automatically. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Above about $5,000 monthly ad spend, do the initial import and then optimize Bing on its own data. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Signal that Bing has enough own data: around 1% conversion rate with regularly around 100 clicks. [L0866, 2020-26]

#### Case: restarting after losses

- Case: a store with about 200 products restarts at about 30 per day on five to ten proven products. [L0941, 2022]
- It improves product pages, expands the product list later, and adds Microsoft back at a much smaller budget. [L0941, 2022]

#### Secondary platform budget

- A secondary search platform such as Bing can run at $15 a day. If it does not pay off, drop it to about $5 a day to keep visibility until profits can support more spend. [L1285, 2024]

### Market background data

Dated figures from older sources, to be used as background only:

- As of 2015: about one in three US searches happened on Bing, and Google had about 65% of US search share. Google and Bing together reached about 95% of the US market, so skipping Bing meant missing a large share of buyers. [L1328, 2015]
- For the period around 2018: Bing Ads ad spend grew 32% year over year in Q4. Bing handled about one in three US searches. [L1455, 2018+]
- For the same period: audience-based Google search ad clicks grew 34% year over year, so audience targeting became a larger part of search campaigns. [L1455, 2018+]
