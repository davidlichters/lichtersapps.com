## Microsoft/Bing Ads

### Role of Microsoft Ads in the account

- Microsoft Advertising, formerly Bing Ads, is Microsoft's paid search platform and an alternative to Google Ads. Its ads mainly appear on bing.com. [L1034, 2021+]
- Roughly one in three web searches takes place on Bing, so it is a sizeable additional traffic source next to a Google Ads account. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Microsoft Ads is an advanced paid search channel to add later, not part of the core setup. It only makes sense once Google Ads is maxed out. [L0544, 2026+]
- Pause Bing Ads until Google Shopping is profitable, then move the proven campaigns over to Bing. [L0804, 2023]
- Microsoft and Bing ads should not take part of the starting budget. Add them once campaigns are profitable and profits are being reinvested. [L0360, 2024]
- Bing produces real sales with a good return on ad spend, but it is weaker than Google. Focus on Google first and add Bing once Google performs. [L0855, 2023]
- Much of Bing's search volume likely comes from people who kept the default search engine preinstalled on a new PC and never changed it. Paid reach on Bing is therefore smaller but still profitable. [L1034, 2021+]
- Bing works almost like a copy of Google Ads. Account structure, campaign logic and optimization habits transfer directly, so little new learning is needed. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Product feed, conversion tracking and campaigns move to Bing as one set with the same logic. The existing Google setup does not need to be rebuilt from zero. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Google and Microsoft do not share data. Past results on one platform do not show what Google will do. [L0472, 2025]
- If an account that sold well on one platform gets very few conversions on Google from thousands of clicks, the setup or traffic type is likely wrong. [L0472, 2025]

### Account setup

#### Sign-up (detailed route, ads.microsoft.com)

1. Open ads.microsoft.com and click `Sign up now`. If you already have an account, log in. If not, click the option to create one. [L1035, 2020-26]
2. Enter an email address. Use the same email address as your Google Ads account. [L1035, 2020-26]
3. Set up the account, then click `switch to expert mode`, as in the Google Ads setup. [L1035, 2020-26]
4. Enter your business information. Set the primary use of the account to `promote this business` (your own business). [L1035, 2020-26]
5. Accept the Terms and Conditions (a link to read them is provided) and click `create account`. [L1035, 2020-26]
6. On the next screen, do not import from Google yet. Click `skip campaign creation`. It sits at the top of the page. [L1035, 2020-26]
7. Enter your business location (address) and tax information, then enter your billing information. [L1035, 2020-26]
8. In the billing options, choose `Post-pay`. Post-pay means you pay after you have spent money on ads. [L1035, 2020-26]
9. Select `Credit or debit card` as the payment method and enter your card details. [L1035, 2020-26]
10. Confirm. The new account screen opens. It is empty at this stage, which is expected. [L1035, 2020-26]

#### Sign-up (short route)

1. Search for Bing Ads and go directly to the Bing Ads site. [L0866, 2020-26]
2. Click to sign up and create an account. [L0866, 2020-26]
3. Verify the email address, then complete the captcha. [L0866, 2020-26]
4. Enter the company name. [L0866, 2020-26]
5. Enter a business phone number. [L0866, 2020-26]
6. Select the option to manage your own accounts. [L0866, 2020-26]

#### Setup rules

- Use the same email address for Microsoft Advertising as for Google Ads, so both accounts stay linked to one identity. [L1035, 2020-26]
- Entering business address, tax and billing details does not charge anything, because no ads are running yet. [L1035, 2020-26]
- Microsoft Advertising offers `Post-pay` or `Prepay`. Choose `Post-pay` as described above. [L1035, 2020-26]
- The Bing Ads campaign screens can still look like the old AdWords layout after an import. This is cosmetic and does not mean the import failed. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Bing may ask for a credit card during account setup. It can be skipped for now while the imported campaigns are still set up. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Setup order: sign up for Bing Ads, Bing Webmaster Tools and Merchant Center, then create the first Bing Shopping campaign. [L0393, undated]
- Bing Webmaster Tools and Merchant Center are set up alongside Bing Ads. Merchant Center supplies the product data that Bing Shopping campaigns use. [L0393, undated]
- Next step after the account exists: create the Microsoft Merchant Center account. Products are submitted there before any Google Ads campaigns are imported. [L1035, 2020-26]

#### Sign-up import option: sources differ

- one source says that during Bing account setup you should use the import-from-Google option so the existing Google campaigns appear inside the Bing account. [L0866, 2020-26]
- one source says to skip campaign creation at first setup, because importing from Google requires logging in to Google and only works correctly after Microsoft Merchant Center is set up and products are submitted. [L1035, 2020-26]
- When each applies: use one source if Merchant Center is not yet set up. Use one source's import option once Merchant Center is in place and you are importing. [L0866, 2020-26; L1035, 2020-26]

#### Vouchers and credits

- Microsoft Advertising sometimes offers voucher codes that give new accounts ad credits. Availability changes, so search for current `Microsoft advertising vouchers` on bing.com. [L1035, 2020-26]
- Microsoft Advertising offered a $100 credit for new accounts. Search for the current version of the offer, since the URLs change often. [L0852, 2020; older]

### Microsoft Merchant Center

#### What it is

- Microsoft Merchant Center is the Microsoft counterpart to Google Merchant Center. It holds store information and the product feed used by Microsoft Shopping ads on Bing. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Microsoft Merchant Center is where products must be set up and submitted before importing Google Ads campaigns into Microsoft Advertising for Shopping. [L1035, 2020-26]
- The Merchant Center setup follows the same logic as Google Merchant Center: store details and shipping first, products after. The product feed is only submitted once the account is complete. [L1037, 2020-26]
- A Microsoft Advertising account must exist before a Merchant Center store can be created, because the Merchant Center link is opened from inside that account. The new store is attached to the main Microsoft Advertising account. [L1037, 2020-26]

#### Store creation procedure

1. Log in to the Microsoft Advertising account and click the Merchant Center link in the header menu. [L1037, 2020-26]
2. On the empty welcome screen (no stores yet), click `Create store`. [L1037, 2020-26]
3. Enter the store name. [L1037, 2020-26]
4. For domain validation, select the Bing Webmaster Tools account that already holds the store's destination URL. [L1037, 2020-26]
5. Add a store description. [L1037, 2020-26]
6. Answer yes to secure checkout and click `Next`. [L1037, 2020-26]
7. Select `Block` for aggregators and click `Next`. [L1037, 2020-26]
8. Choose the language and enter the store's contact email. [L1037, 2020-26]
9. Answer no to best-practice emails if the main account email already receives them, then click `Create store`. [L1037, 2020-26]
10. Open the store's settings from the main account, go to shipping settings, turn on free shipping, enter a minimum purchase price (0 if there is none), and click `Submit`. Products are not uploaded in this step. [L1037, 2020-26]

#### Store settings details

- Store description works like a short About Us page. It tells shoppers what the store is and who runs it. The field shows a remaining character count. A real business should use more of it to explain what it sells and what makes it different. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Turn on secure checkout when creating the store. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Aggregators collect product offers from many often unrelated businesses. By default they can include your product feed in their ads. Blocking aggregators keeps your products out of their Bing ads. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Block aggregators during store setup. The extra explanation is available by hovering over the question mark next to the option. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Use a dedicated support email address on the store's own domain as the contact email. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Set the minimum purchase price for free shipping to 0 if there is no minimum order value. Free shipping is switched on in the store's shipping settings. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Do not upload products during Merchant Center setup. The account must be complete first, and the feed is submitted afterwards. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Right after the store is created, it appears under the main account with no items listed. The list stays empty until a feed is submitted, so an empty store at this stage is normal. [L1037, 2020-26]

#### Product feed for Bing

- Sources differ on whether the Bing merchant account carries over from Google. one source says it does not and must be created again from scratch, because assuming it comes across leaves Shopping ads without a working feed. [L0866, 2020-26]
- one source says one feed app connection can supply products to both Google and Bing. The connection screen lets you pick an existing Merchant Center store or create a new one from inside the app. [L1036, 2020-26]
- When each applies: if you have no Microsoft Merchant Center store, create one. If you do, pick it in the app's connection screen. [L0866, 2020-26; L1036, 2020-26]
- Before connecting a Shopify store to Bing Shopping through the feed app, a Microsoft Advertising account and a Merchant Center store should already exist. [L1036, 2020-26]
- Feed procedure:
  1. Use the same process that was used to create the Google Shopping feed. [L0866, 2020-26]
  2. Open a Shopify app that builds the feed inside the store. The same feed app serves Google Shopping and Bing Shopping. [L0866, 2020-26; L1036, 2020-26]
  3. Log in to the app and set up the feed. [L0866, 2020-26]
  4. In the app, go to `Settings > Bing Shopping Settings`. The only option that needs changing is the `sale price with price` checkbox, which must be checked. Leave the other Bing Shopping options at their defaults. [L1036, 2020-26]
  5. Sync the feed with Bing Ads. [L0866, 2020-26]
  6. Check that the product feed is now available in Bing Ads. [L0866, 2020-26]
- After a successful import, products show the status `pending`. This alone is not an error and confirms that the feed was received. [L1036, 2020-26]

### Store verification

Procedure for verifying the store for Bing:

1. When Bing asks for the store name, first set up the store in Bing Webmaster Tools. [L0866, 2020-26]
2. Return to Bing Ads once the store is set up there. The remaining fields fill in automatically. [L0866, 2020-26]
3. When Bing asks for the product feed, select the feed that was already created. [L0866, 2020-26]

- Domain validation in Merchant Center uses the Bing Webmaster Tools account that already holds the destination URL. [L1037, 2020-26]
- Bing Webmaster Tools and Microsoft Merchant Center support the Bing Shopping campaign, which runs alongside Google. [L0532, undated]
- Bing Webmaster Tools also shows organic Bing search terms. [L0834, 2021]

### Import from Google Ads

#### Why import, and what not to do

- Existing Google Ads campaigns can be imported into Microsoft Advertising. This takes much less work than building them from scratch. Budgets are then set lower than on Google. [L1034, 2021+]
- Do not use Auto Import when importing campaigns from Google Ads to Microsoft Advertising. Auto Import copies everything in your Google account and becomes unmanageable once you have many campaigns. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Use `Import Specific Campaigns and Ad Groups` to choose exactly which campaigns to copy. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Sources differ on ongoing sync. one source says Auto Import should not be used (above). one source says below about $5,000 monthly ad spend, export the Google Ads campaigns straight into Bing Ads, because later changes in Google Ads are then updated automatically in Bing Ads. [L0302, 2020-26; L0866, 2020-26]
- When each applies: the import settings in the procedure below cover the choice of campaigns. one source's automatic updates depend on `apply updates to existing items` being checked (the import option in step 12). The sources do not give a threshold for Auto Import itself. [L0302, 2020-26; L0866, 2020-26]
- Above about $5,000 monthly ad spend, do the initial import and then optimize Bing Ads using its own performance data. Repeated imports from Google Ads become less useful once Bing has enough data of its own. [L0866, 2020-26]
- Example signal that Bing has enough own data: a Bing conversion rate of around 1% combined with regularly around 100 clicks. Then optimizing on Bing's own numbers can replace constant importing from Google Ads. [L0866, 2020-26]

#### Import procedure

1. Log in to Microsoft Advertising. [L0302, 2020-26]
2. Click `Import` in the header menu. [L0302, 2020-26]
3. Select `Import from Google Ads`. [L0302, 2020-26]
4. Sign in to the Google account linked to your Google Ads account. [L0302, 2020-26]
5. Click `Continue`. [L0302, 2020-26]
6. Change `Schedule` from `Auto` to `Once` and set the time to `Right Now` (your timezone). [L0302, 2020-26]
7. Click `Customize Import`. [L0302, 2020-26]
8. Select `Import Specific Campaigns and Ad Groups`, not `import all existing and new campaigns`. [L0302, 2020-26]
9. Search for and select the campaigns you want to copy (for example, your Alpha and Beta campaigns). [L0302, 2020-26]
10. Click `Next`. [L0302, 2020-26]
11. Uncheck `account level URL options`. [L0302, 2020-26]
12. Check the three sync options: sync items not previously added to your Microsoft account, apply updates to existing items, and remove items from Microsoft Advertising if they were deleted from Google Ads. [L0302, 2020-26]
13. Select your UET tag from the Google Shopping app, so conversion data from Bing Shopping feeds into your Microsoft Advertising account. [L0302, 2020-26]
14. Uncheck the bid and budget minimums options (`Increase any of our bids to reach minimums for Microsoft Advertising`, `Update Bid Strategies`, `Increase Campaign Budgets to the minimum`). [L0302, 2020-26]
15. Select `Customize Budgets for Microsoft Advertising` and reduce by 80%. [L0302, 2020-26]
16. Click `Next`. [L0302, 2020-26]
17. Name the import (for example, Alpha Beta Import). [L0302, 2020-26]
18. Set it to email on errors. [L0302, 2020-26]
19. Click `Run Import Now`. [L0302, 2020-26]
20. Wait for product feed approval in Microsoft Merchant Center. It may show pending while images are crawled. [L0302, 2020-26]
21. Once approved, check the account weekly to review numbers, add negative keywords and adjust bids. [L0302, 2020-26]

#### Import settings explained

- Uncheck account level URL options. Leaving it checked transfers URL tracking from Google to Bing, which incorrectly attributes Bing sales to your Google Ads account. [L0302, 2020-26]
- The UET tag must be connected to the Google Shopping app in the Microsoft Advertising Conversion section. Select this tag during import setup. [L0302, 2020-26]

#### Pending approval

- Do not expect imported Bing campaigns to run immediately while the feed is pending approval in Merchant Center. The feed may show `pending according to image crawling` until Microsoft approves all product images and data. [L0302, 2020-26]
- Campaigns show Delivery errors until approval completes. Wait for the feed status to turn green before expecting ad delivery. [L0302, 2020-26]

#### Other import routes

- To set up Bing ads from an existing Google setup:
  1. Import your campaigns from your Google account to Bing. [L0972, 2025]
  2. Lower the budget for the Bing campaigns. [L0972, 2025]
  3. Keep using the product images from your product pages. No separate assets are needed. [L0972, 2025]
- To launch Bing Shopping from an existing Google feed:
  1. Submit the same product feed you use for Google to Bing Ads. This can be done within about an hour if it is already clean. [L1328, 2015]
  2. Import your Google Shopping campaigns into Bing Ads. The platform supports this with one action. [L1328, 2015]
  3. Review the imported bids and segmentation before turning the campaigns on. [L1328, 2015]
- Remarketing audiences must be created inside Microsoft Ads. Shopping campaigns can be transferred from Google Ads without creating new audiences. [L1283, 2023]
- if import errors occur:
  1. Open the Microsoft Merchant Center account in Microsoft Ads. [L1092, 2022; older]
  2. Delete the existing Google Ads integrations, but do not delete the accounts themselves. [L1092, 2022; older]
  3. Re-add the integrations and confirm the correct Merchant Center accounts are selected, since linking the wrong account causes data errors. [L1092, 2022; older]

### Budgets relative to Google

Sources agree that the Bing budget should be lower than the Google budget. They give different ratios because each uses a different base.

| Starting point | Bing budget | Base | Year of advice | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First import of Google campaigns | Reduce imported budgets by 80%, which leaves 20% | Imported Google budgets | Undated | [L0302, 2020-26] |
| Import into a new Microsoft account | About 75% lower, which is 25% of Google | Google daily budget | 2024 | [L0666, 2024] |
| Launch with Google daily budget | 25% of the Google Ads daily budget | Google Ads daily budget | 2026 | [L0808, 2026] |
| Ratio by search volume | About 25% of Google budgets | Google alpha and beta budgets | 2021, older | [L0671, 2021] |
| Shopping budget | 15 to 20% of Google Shopping daily budget. Bing can be left unfunded at first | Google Shopping daily budget | 2023 | [L0855, 2023] |
| Proportional to traffic | About 25% to 30% of Google's traffic. Google keeps the larger share | Traffic that Bing brings | 2022, older | [L0854, 2022] |
| Total ad budget | Start with about 10% of total ad budget, optimize there, then scale | Total ad budget | 2024 | [L0856, 2024] |
| Secondary platform | $15 per day. If it does not pay off, drop to about $5 per day to keep visibility | Fixed daily amount | 2024 | [L1285, 2024] |
| Troubleshooting unprofitable traffic | Cut the total budget to about $10 until settings are fixed | Fixed total | 2025 | [L0857, 2025] |
| Unfunded start | No budget until Google is profitable | Google profitability | 2023, 2024 | [L0855, 2023; L0360, 2024] |

Examples:

- $100 per day on Google becomes $25 per day on Bing. [L0666, 2024; L0671, 2021]
- $100 per day on Google Shopping gives $15 to $30 per day on Bing. [L0855, 2023]
- Setting Bing to $400 with a $100 Google budget would skew results. [L0671, 2021]

Rules for picking a value:

- For a first import, start with the 80% reduction. The procedure's test is whether Bing delivers any impressions and clicks before committing larger spend. [L0302, 2020-26]
- The 80% reduction leaves 20% of the Google budget, which is the top of the 15 to 20% range given for Shopping. [L0302, 2020-26; L0855, 2023]
- The 25% figures (2026, and the 2021 volume ratio) and the 15 to 20% figure (2023) use different bases. Use the one that matches how your budget is set. [L0808, 2026; L0671, 2021; L0855, 2023]
- If the budget is set as a share of total spend, start at about 10%. [L0856, 2024]
- If Microsoft spends more than Google despite higher Google budgets, the Google daily budget is not being spent. See Bids and CPC below. [L0837, 2025]

Cuts and pauses:

- If Microsoft Ads costs are high while its sales are low compared with Google, cut its budget sharply or turn it off. Focus on Google Shopping first. [L0854, 2022; older]
- Restart Microsoft Ads only once the Google campaigns are profitable. Size its budget in proportion to the traffic it brings, which is typically about 25% to 30% of Google's. [L0854, 2022; older]
- Traffic relation: Microsoft Ads usually brings about 25% to 30% of the traffic that Google brings. Its spend should be proportional to that, and Google should get the larger share because it typically has the better ROAS. [L0854, 2022; older]
- Share of searches: roughly one in three web searches takes place on Bing. This is a share of all searches, not a share of Google's traffic. [L0866, 2020-26]

### Bids and CPC

- Launch Microsoft Ads with 25% of your Google Ads daily budget. Set the cost-per-click to match your Google Ads cost-per-click, and adjust it as data comes in. [L0808, 2026]
- a Bing account received advice to raise every CPC to $2. The better approach was a small increase of about 5 cents, because Google campaigns already produced clicks at 60 cents. [L0962, 2021; older]
- If Microsoft Ads spends more than Google Ads despite higher Google budgets, the Google daily budget is not being spent. This usually means the default CPC is too low. Raise the Google Ads default CPC by about 10 cents. [L0837, 2025]
- if Google impressions are low while Bing shows many impressions for the same products, check the keyword triggers and product matches in both accounts. Bing clicks are often cheaper, so raising the Google cost per click can increase impressions. [L1111, 2020; older]
- Bing clicks cheaper than Google is part of why Bing can add impressions at low cost. Keep the bid adjustments small and check results after each change. [L1111, 2020; L0962, 2021]

### Targeting

- Bing Ads supports the same geo-targeting options as Google Ads and Meta: individual zip codes, whole states, or a radius around a city. Use these instead of a country-wide target when a supplier's shipping cost makes distant customers unprofitable. [L0942, 2022]
- Before adding budget to a campaign with traffic but no sales, check its location and other settings. A campaign with about 5,944 clicks from very low spend and zero sales points to the wrong traffic, often from targeting many countries where clicks are cheap. [L0857, 2025]
