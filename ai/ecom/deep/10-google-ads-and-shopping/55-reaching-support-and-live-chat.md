### Reaching Support and Live Chat

#### Which Route First: Phone or Chat

Sources differ on whether to start with phone or live chat. Use the route the account offers.

| Situation | Route | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Phone support is available | Phone first | [L0359, 2020] |
| Phone support is unavailable | Live chat | [L0359, 2020] |
| Only the web form is available | Phone is still preferred in the 2019 advice; the form alone was judged not enough | [L1069, 2019] |
| Chat is the main route for a stated reason | Live chat, keep contacting until a person names the reason | [L1279, 2021] |
| A phone number or callback option appears | Request it and ask for the specific reason | [L1111, 2020] |

- Keep calling until a person who can help is reached. Accounts suspended by mistake or by an error in the review are often reinstated this way, provided the account has no unpaid balance or fraud issues. ** [L1069, 2019]
- Follow up persistently with the same case and documentation. ** [L1069, 2019]
- Reply directly to any Google email, which may be answered faster than a new request. ** [L0247, 2021]
- If live chat is available, it will appear as an option on the Google Ads help page. ** [L0247, 2021]

#### Finding the Contact Option (Google Ads)

1. Sign in to the Google Ads account you need help with. [L0837, 2025]
2. Open the Google Ads help page for your issue and scroll to the bottom, where it says Need more help? Contact us. [L0837, 2025]
3. Select the account you are contacting about and type your question. [L0837, 2025]
4. Ask to speak with someone. Contact options vary and may include email, live chat or phone. [L0837, 2025]
5. Open the Google Ads support page and scroll down to the Contact us section. Depending on the number of support agents available, you will get either live chat or a phone number. ** [L0801, 2021]
6. In the Contact us section below the templated responses, click Tell us more. Check which option appears: a message form, live chat or a phone number. [L0360, 2024]

#### Getting Live Chat or a Phone Line

1. Open the Google Ads support contact page. [L0360, 2024]
2. If live chat does not appear, try again in two to three hours, and repeat over several days. [L0360, 2024]
3. Once live chat appears, explain the situation so a person can review the store. [L0360, 2024]
4. Keep a browser tab open on the Google Ads support page. [L0429, 2022] **
5. Refresh it about every hour. When phone support is available, a phone icon appears next to live chat. Call then. ** [L0429, 2022]
6. Some Google Ads accounts have no live chat most of the time, so checking the support page for an available chat window is the main route to fast help. ** [L1092, 2022]
7. Try Google's live chat from the support help page, which is rare, so keep that page open and refresh it every 30 to 60 minutes. ** [L1092, 2022]
8. Live chat only appears when a support agent is free and the queue is not backed up. If it does not show, retry at different times of day to reach a person. ** [L0963, 2022]
9. When the chat option disappears, check the contact section of the account, since a support phone number sometimes appears there. [L0666, 2024]
10. A review request for a product disapproval does not show whether the review changed anything, so check the status directly afterwards. [L0666, 2024]
11. Google Merchant Center is free, so advertisers get no dedicated account representative for it. Google Ads and Facebook accounts with spend history usually have direct account contacts, but Merchant Center support has to be reached through its standard help channels. ** [L1111, 2020]
12. Open the Google Merchant Center support help page and go to the contact options. [L1111, 2020]
13. If only email or live chat is shown, leave and return later, because the available contact options change with staff availability. ** [L1111, 2020]

#### Google Ads Problems Inside and Outside the Account

- For Google Ads account problems that cannot be solved inside the account, such as unexplained suspensions, pixel errors or programs that do not appear, search for Google Ads support to reach live chat. Share the exact error or status when you contact them. [L0970, 2023]
- Google Ads support is Google's official help resource for account setup, policies and optimization questions. Check it when an account issue or policy rejection is unclear. [L1095, 2024]
- Google Ads support is a channel for getting help with account problems such as disapproved ads or suspended accounts. ** [L1089, 2021]
- Google Ads offers phone support and live chat for account problems. Use these channels when stuck, but weigh their advice critically. [L0489, 2020-26]
- Google's support teams can be lenient when an account is restricted. ** [L1090, 2021]

### Avoiding New Accounts

#### Rules Against Opening a Replacement Account

- Do not start a new Google Ads account while an existing one is suspended. Creating a second account to get around a suspension is treated as circumventing the system and can lead to a permanent block. ** [L1281, 2022]
- After a Google Ads account suspension, do not create a new Google Ads account. A new one is usually suspended as well, and Google may cite circumvention of policy. ** [L0963, 2022]
- Opening a new ad account right after a suspension can get it blocked or suspended again within minutes. Work through the appeal on the existing account rather than repeatedly creating replacement accounts. [L1214, 2023+]
- A new account with a different email and card may still be linked if it is opened from the same computer or IP address. [L0965, 2024]
- Creating a second Merchant Center account to get around a disapproval causes more problems. Fix the disapproval and request review instead. *(Advice from 2023.)* [L0964, 2023]
- Do not delete the account and create a new one with the same email, because that is flagged as circumventing policies. ** [L1092, 2022]
- Do not run a new account with the same card or domain as the suspended one. [L0278, 2025] *(See the conditional route below.)*

#### Conditional New-Account Routes (Conflicting Sources)

Sources disagree on whether a new account is ever a route. Give each route with its conditions:

- **Current route (2025):** If Google Ads is suspended, identify the suspension reason first. To open a new account, use a completely different business entity, new email address, new billing information and new domain URL. Include a VPN only if reusing information from the suspended account. [L0278, 2025]
- **Older route (2019):** One practical route was to open a new account with a new domain and a new credit card. The new account could be flagged too, so persistent calls to Google support were still needed, and reinstatement was generally possible when there was no fraud and no unpaid balance. ** [L1069, 2019]
- **Merchant Center only (2025):** Multiple Merchant Center accounts can be created, but this does not solve Google Ads suspension issues first. [L0278, 2025]
- **Merchant Center only, reported (2021):** Reported experience suggests a suspension of one Merchant Center account does not automatically affect separate Merchant Center accounts. This was not verified and should not be relied on. ** [L0469, 2021]
- **Untrustworthy promotions, Merchant Center (2020):** If unresolved after a week, create a new Merchant Center account with a different email. ** [L0268, 2020]
- Google accounts are stricter about opening multiple new accounts than Merchant Center. [L0278, 2025]

#### Policy Checks Before Any Ad Account Decision

- If Google Merchant Center asks for business verification such as proof of address or a verified phone number, wait until you have the requested document and upload it. Trying to work around the request can trigger the circumventing systems policy. [L0857, 2025]
- If a domain may be linked to a different ad account that was suspended, ask Google Ads support through live chat, describe the earlier suspension, and keep the written answer on file. ** [L0963, 2022]
- Only use.com domains for the store. [L0857, 2025]
- After a suspension, first resolve the issues on the existing account. [L0965, 2024]

### Reps, Scams and Unsolicited Offers

- Callers who say they are Google ad representatives and offer paid ad management are often third-party sellers rather than Google. Even genuine Google reps tend to push higher ad spend, so decline such offers and manage the account yourself. ** [L0468, 2020]
- Google reps who call or email about account reviews are sales staff who earn money from higher ad spend, so their advice should not be followed blindly. Google's own sales advice is not a reliable guide for account setup. [L0472, 2025]
- If the form route gets no answer after an appeal, ask your Google account rep for help, since phone support pointed to a form. Google ad accounts can be suspended without a clear reason, such as an alleged policy circumvention. [L1214, 2023+]
- When a Facebook account is blocked, a direct contact at the platform can help recover funds or restore access. Keep an agency or platform rep contact available for these cases. [L1214, 2023+]
- Be skeptical of agencies offering negative keyword management services, since such services often do not involve the owner's own oversight of campaigns. Hands-on management of campaigns in early stages is a better approach. [L0471, 2024]
- If any Google account shows errors or problems, contact the platform support or the community for help instead of making ad hoc changes. Report the error so it can be resolved before the learning phase ends. [L1393, 2020-26]
- Keep requesting live chat with Google through the Ad support center whenever that option appears, and do not give up on the appeal. [L0471, 2024]

### Agencies and Outsourcing

#### When Not to Outsource

- Do not outsource Google Ads until the store is already profitable with your own optimization. Only hire for management or scaling once you have proven what works. ** [L0270, 2021]
- Do not outsource Google Ads management until the store has a track record of profitable ads and known winning products. ** [L0834, 2021]
- Before raising ad spend, verify the account is profitable. Outsourcing ads to an agency is not recommended until the store is profitable and the baseline performance is known, since agencies rarely take a brand-new store. ** [L0854, 2022]
- Do not outsource Google Ads early. First prove a baseline on your own account, such as a store live for six months with about 13X return on ad spend over the past four months, before hiring an agency. *(Advice from 2023.)* [L0934, 2023]
- Do not outsource Google Ads before you have a documented baseline on your own account. An example baseline is a store live for six months with about 13X return on ad spend over four months, which gives an agency a measurable target. *(Advice from 2023.)* [L0934, 2023]
- Do not start ads before the store has real products and is open for business. ** [L1111, 2020]

- Google Ads agencies typically charge 1,500 to 2,500 dollars monthly and manage one to ten accounts per person, making per-account costs higher than larger platforms. [L0217, 2025-26]
- Google Ads agencies charge low fees (1,500 to 2,500 dollars monthly) because one person manages 1 to 10 accounts simultaneously. The cost of labor is amortized across multiple clients. In-house requires paying for one full-time person dedicated to your account only. [L0217, 2025-26]
- Google Ads agencies tend to be maintenance-focused rather than proactively scaling because the platform requires more campaign setup work per testing cycle. Clarify scaling expectations upfront and map deliverables week by week. [L0217, 2025-26]
- Google Ads return on ad spend is claimed in the source to be strong, around 4x to 7x in typical cases. Outsourced Google Ads management in the source cost about 1,500 dollars per month. [L0637, 2023+]
- A good agency brief sets a measurable goal: keep a 13X return on ad spend while raising monthly spend from 1,000 to 2,000 dollars, or raise return from 13X to 15X at the same spend. Without a baseline an agency has nothing to be judged against. [L0934, 2023]
- Judge the performance of an outsourced Google Ads manager by ROAS trend, not spend alone. A flat or weak ROAS at a relatively small ad budget should be improved before the budget is raised. ** [L1111, 2020]
- When an agency is paid a fixed monthly fee, check whether the ROAS justifies it. A 10x ROAS on 2,000 dollars monthly spend was judged acceptable for a new account, but needs to improve as spend grows. ** [L1111, 2020]

#### Agency Checks and Options

1. Read the policies and confirm the store meets the compliance requirements before handing over the account. [L1111, 2020]
2. Check whether the account is suspended for a policy reason, and read the reason in the email. [L1111, 2020]
3. Appeal if the reason was an ad that was not in compliance. [L1111, 2020]
4. Search Scientists is an outside Google Ads agency that can be considered for outsourcing once the account is proven. *(Advice from 2025.)* [L1116, 2025]

#### Further details

Handling a Google Merchant Center suspension:
1. Read the email from Google, which usually names a broad reason for the suspension.
2. Send that reason and a link to the store to support for a review.
3. Look for a clear pattern in the flagged stores before changing anything.
[L1088, 2020]
