### Brand names and the two-campaign alternative

- Searches for a brand name convert well, because the shopper already knows the brand. Example: *Lifestyles infrared sauna* converts higher than *infrared sauna* or *should I buy this*, because generic searchers are earlier in the funnel doing comparison shopping. [L0225, 2015+]
- one source's account structure separates Alpha and Beta, and the bids differ by query type. **Procedure (undated):** To segment a Shopping campaign into a branded and a generic campaign:
  1. Open the account and click the edit button, then copy and paste the existing product listing ad campaign so you have two campaigns.
  2. In the generic campaign, go to the Keywords tab, click Keywords, and add negative phrase keywords for your brand terms (brand name, brand possessive, model names) so only generic queries trigger it.
  3. In the branded campaign, add negative exact keywords for the generic terms (for example *sunglasses* and *men's sunglasses*) so only branded queries trigger it.
  4. Set low bids in the generic campaign and high bids in the branded campaign.

  [L1328, 2015]
- Blocking low-converting generic terms completely gives up profitable traffic. Keep them in a separate generic campaign with a low bid instead, where they can remain ROI positive. [L1328, 2015]
- **Two-campaign vs. three-campaign:** one source's segmentation uses two campaigns (branded and generic). one source's funnel uses three priority levels and states that three campaigns are standard for most high-ticket cases. The sources do not state which to use in which case; the three-campaign funnel is the standard the older source sets for high-ticket dropshipping. [L1328, 2015; L0742, 2021-22]

### Alpha and Beta campaigns

- A Google Ads account for e-commerce is organised in two layers: campaign stage (MOFU or BOFU) and campaign type. Each campaign should be placed in one stage and one type so the structure stays readable. [L0867, 2020-26]
- Build Google Ads campaigns bottom-up: start with BoFU audiences (ready to buy). Avoid spending on TOFU (awareness-only) as the entry point, then expand upward to MOFU and TOFU once BoFU revenue scales. [L0023, undated]
- Search terms that convert well in Shopping campaigns are the money terms. They are moved into their own cold-traffic Search campaign, so budget and bids can be controlled for proven keywords. [L0867, 2020-26]
- **2025, rule:** Alpha campaigns should contain only brand names, product names and SKU numbers as keywords. Descriptive generic terms belong in the Beta campaign, where CPC can be raised to win that traffic. [L0472, 2025]
- Example for a chandelier store: an exact product name, model number or SKU search belongs in the Alpha campaign. A generic search such as *16 bulb chandelier* is not added as a keyword anywhere. Beta picks it up, because such users convert less often than people who search for the exact product. [L0746, 2020-26]
- **Undated procedure:** To create Alpha campaigns from high-performing search terms:
  1. Identify all search terms that generated at least one conversion in the Google Ads account.
  2. Create manual exact-match keywords for each converting term in a dedicated Alpha Shopping campaign.
  3. For search terms generating 5 or more conversions, also incorporate those terms into product titles and content strategy for SEO benefit.

  [L0299, 2020-26]

  *Conflict:* The undated procedure in step 2 adds every converting term to Alpha, while the 2025 rule restricts Alpha to brand names, product names and SKU numbers. Use the 2025 rule for keyword selection. Use step 3 of the undated procedure for title and content work on terms with 5 or more conversions. [L0299, 2020-26; L0472, 2025]

### Search term review and funnel maintenance

- Review the search terms that trigger your ads regularly, and exclude the poor ones as negatives. Google does not reliably judge intent, so unreviewed matches burn budget. [L0739, 2021-22]
- Tag each term by journey stage (research, comparing, ready to buy). Terms that are clearly converting deserve their own ad group or campaign, so they can get more budget and tailored copy. Terms with few clicks but a relevant non-brand, top-of-funnel intent can still be kept as potential keywords. [L0876, 2025-26]
- Judge each search term by profit, including the unrelated contacts from broad terms. Do not judge a term on click volume. [L0741, 2021-22; L0837, 2025]
- Broad generic terms such as *leather jackets* can see large marketplaces and fast-fashion sellers take most of the Shopping placements. A small brand in that query needs its own photos, a distinct offer or a clear angle, or its listing blends into the same results. [L1508, 2022+]
- Exclude generic top-of-funnel terms from Shopping and address them through another channel such as content. [L1456, 2021-22]
