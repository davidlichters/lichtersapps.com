### Setup Sequence

one source procedure:

1. Create a free Google Merchant Center account. [L1251, 2024+]
2. In Shopify, install the Google sales channel and follow its step-by-step setup, which takes about 10 minutes. [L1251, 2024+]
3. Wait about 3 to 5 days while Google audits the products, which is required for a new account. [L1251, 2024+]
4. In Google Merchant Center, open linked accounts and link your Google Ads account. [L1251, 2024+]
5. Once linked, run the campaign hands-off with Performance Max. [L1251, 2024+]

one source procedure:

1. Upload all products to the store. [L1437, 2018]
2. Submit the store's product feed to Google Merchant Centre. [L1437, 2018]
3. While the feed is waiting for approval, launch Search text ads so the store can start getting sales. [L1437, 2018]

### Linking Merchant Center to Google Ads

- Connect the Google Ads account under Google Merchant Center > Linked accounts, after the Merchant Center audit is complete. [L1251, 2024+]
- The link lets Google Ads use the product feed for Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. [L1251, 2024+]

### Audit and Approval Timing

- A new account needs an audit of about 3 to 5 days before the link step. [L1251, 2024+]
- Approval of the feed takes time, so the feed is submitted right after products are uploaded. [L1437, 2018]
- Waiting time for feed approval can be used to launch Search text ads. [L1437, 2018]

### Verifying Shipping and Tax Settings

- The shipping and tax rate calculator in Merchant Center previews the rates that will show for a product delivered to a chosen destination. [L1170, 2021-22]
- Use it to verify shipping and tax settings before the feed goes live. [L1170, 2021-22]
- Older source: treat this as older advice and check it against the current Merchant Center interface. [L1170, 2021-22]

#### Further details

- All shipping and return settings must match exactly what is stated on your website policies. If settings do not match, Google may reject your products. [L0181, undated]

- Fill in company information and shipping information in your Google Merchant Center account when setting it up. [L0671, 2021]
