### Link Building Delegation

**What can be delegated**

- Backlink building work, such as skyscraper outreach, can be delegated to a VA or freelancer. Written SOPs make it possible to keep building backlinks to the site on an ongoing basis. [L0943, 2021-22]
- Resource page link building and broken link building can also be handed to a VA, assistant or freelancer. These processes suit delegation once the niche opportunities have been identified. [L0943, 2021-22]
- Link building tasks such as spreadsheet work, email sending and follow-ups are simple and do not need advanced English or specialist skills. They can be outsourced at low cost, unlike coding, development, content writing or creative work. [L0945, 2021-22]
- The outreach work is repetitive and manual but needs little specialist skill, so a general VA can usually perform it. There is no need to pay a high-cost freelancer, and the tasks can be added to an existing VA's workload. [L0565, 2021-22]
- Compared with classic one-by-one skyscraper link building, this system is highly automated and scalable. After setup, the ongoing time commitment stays small, and most of the work can be outsourced. [L0565, 2021-22]
- Broken link checking can be outsourced to a VA at about 5 USD per hour. The assistant runs the checker on every content page of a niche site, lists the in-content broken links with the URL where each was found, and you review the list to decide which links to pursue and which of your pages to offer. [L0332, 2021-22]
- Data pulling, merging and cleaning the likely-linker list are repetitive manual tasks that a VA can complete once the topics are chosen. [L1443, 2021-22]
- Sort the backlinks column or the referring domains column from highest to lowest. Both sort keys give a useful ordering for choosing which domains to process first. [L1443, 2021-22]
- The per-domain backlink exports can be merged into one master sheet by copy-and-paste or by an automated method. The automated method depends on whether you use Mac or Windows and which spreadsheet app you use, so follow instructions that match your setup. [L1443, 2021-22]

**Resource page outreach procedure**

1. Give the virtual assistant your outreach templates and written instructions on the search and filling-in steps. [L1237, 2021-22]
2. The assistant searches for resource pages using the approved search terms and builds a list of candidate sites. [L1237, 2021-22]
3. Review the list, approve or reject entries, and double-check the quality of each site. [L1237, 2021-22]
4. Once the list is approved, the assistant sends the outreach emails and manages the follow-up emails. [L1237, 2021-22]
5. Repeat the search periodically to find new resource pages. [L1237, 2021-22]

**Ownership and priority**

- If the owner keeps doing repetitive link building tasks personally, they drop down the priority list and are rarely completed. Consistent outsourcing prevents this slide. [L0945, 2021-22]
- Outsource link building in this order:
  1. Perform each link building task yourself a few times until you understand it. [L0945, 2021-22]
  2. Once the work is affordable, write or record an SOP for the task. [L0945, 2021-22]
  3. Delegate the task to a VA or freelancer using that SOP. [L0945, 2021-22]
- Existing VAs with spare capacity are a good first choice for link building tasks, and they can take on part of the work immediately. This avoids hiring for a new role before the workload justifies it. [L0945, 2021-22]
- For a serious link building goal in a growing business, a dedicated link building team member, full time or at least part time, is worth hiring. Once content production runs steadily, more link opportunities appear, and a dedicated person speeds up progress. [L0945, 2021-22]
- Extensive outreach also builds industry relationships that can lead to business partnerships, co-promotions, guest posting, featured mentions and promotions to their audience. Over a long-term business, these relationships can pay off well beyond the link itself. [L0565, 2021-22]

### Social Media and Influencer Outreach

**Organic social posting**

- Organic social posting shows that the brand is active and real when customers search for it. It is not a direct sales channel. [L1094, 2023]
- Paid social for high-ticket products often has minimal return on ad spend and a long payback, so outsourcing it is not recommended. [L1094, 2023]
- A social media VA received a brand kit from the owner and had access to the website images. She made slight changes to each image so every post looked unique, then published across all accounts from Hootsuite. [L0921, 2022]
- A VA wrote a blog post every two weeks, which was proofread before publishing. The posts were then shared on social media. [L0921, 2022]
- Delegate social media engagement:
  1. Hire a virtual assistant or use automation tools. [L0226, 2017]
  2. Have them engage on Pinterest (pinning, inviting followers, following accounts). [L0226, 2017]
  3. Manage Instagram, Facebook and Twitter interactions. [L0226, 2017]
  4. Post blog content links to social channels. [L0226, 2017]
  5. Monitor engagement metrics and adjust frequency based on results. [L0226, 2017]

**Posting cadence: sources differ**

- Expected Instagram cadence was two to three posts per day, with Instagram as the primary platform. Creator-made content can supply much of this volume. Use this cadence only when you have creator content to fill it. [L0790, 2017]
- In the first phase, post occasionally, such as when a new supplier approval comes in, rather than several times a week. SEO and blogs are a longer-term play than paid traffic. Use this cadence for a new store. [L0856, 2024]
- Recurring weekly content formats posted on fixed weekdays build loyalty, because followers learn when to come back. Choose a format simple enough to sustain for the long term, since consistency matters more than complexity. [L0576, 2025-26]
- Examples of recurring series: a Monday myth-busting post, a Wednesday behind-the-scenes post, and a Friday weekly recap. Adjust the day and format to the brand's rhythm. [L0576, 2025-26]

**Influencer and outreach**

- Use experienced VAs to research influencer lists, because the manual work is cheap compared with the value of the list. A list of a few hundred influencers typically pays back the cost of the work. [L0790, 2017]
- After a campaign, record which influencer collaboration formats and messages worked best with your audience. Reuse these proven approaches in future partnerships to improve results over time. [L0787, undated]
- For a technical niche where you are not an expert, find people who already have authority in niche Facebook groups or Reddit communities, and offer them a paid freelance content gig. Influencers can also be asked to contribute to your content. [L1120, 2024+]
- Outreach can be scaled by hiring a VA who sends messages from several social accounts, funded from about 500 dollars per month of cash flow. The seller used purchased older Instagram accounts for this. That carries account-ban and platform-policy risk, which should be checked before doing the same. [L0789, 2022]

**Outsourcing vs. owner-led**

- Outsource posting and engagement once the brand's tone is set. The owner should still check the brand kit and approve the first posts. [L0226, 2017; L0921, 2022]

#### Further details

- Aim for at least 50 entries in the attention matrix, with no upper limit. Keep adding entries, remove those that disappear, and review the whole list about once a year. [L0478, 2021-22]

- Building the attention matrix is a simple, repeatable research task that a virtual assistant can do from written instructions. It is a good first task for a new team member, and a founder can do it personally before hiring. [L0478, 2021-22]

- Build the attention matrix in short, regular daily time blocks instead of one long session, and keep working on it until you have a solid number of entries. A virtual assistant can take on this task from written instructions, which makes it a good first onboarding task for a new team member. [L0478, 2021-22]

- Backlinks for product pages are not required to rank; start with product page SEO. Competitive niche keywords need more backlinks and time, while long-tail product pages can rank without them. [L0856, 2024]
