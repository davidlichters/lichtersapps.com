### Shortlisting rules

#### Funnel numbers

Sources give different numbers. Use the one that matches your post volume.

| Stage | Example from source | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Applications to a popular post | Around 50, or hundreds | [L1055, 2021] |
| Applications to a large post | About 180 | [L1077, undated] |
| Applications to a hiring funnel example | About 400 total | [L0263, 2022] |
| Pass keyword or form filter | About 200 → 50-60 | [L0263, 2022] |
| Final interview candidates | 5-6 | [L0263, 2022] |
| Interviewed | Only 3 to 5 | [L1055, 2021] |
| Hired | 1 | [L0263, 2022] |

Note the difference: [L0263, 2022] has 5-6 final interview candidates, while [L1055, 2021] says only 3 to 5 should be interviewed. Keep the shortlist to 3 to 5 if your team can handle that size. [L0263, 2022; L1055, 2021]

Other figures:
- Expect hundreds of applicants, so shortlist, test several candidates at the same time, and compare results before choosing. [L0467, 2019]
- Expect hundreds of applications to a job post, and judge candidates by past work samples. [L0971, 2024]
- Filtering early saves interview time. [L0263, 2022]

#### Shortlisting order

Shortlist applicants in this order: [L1055, 2021]
1. Check whether the application followed the posted instructions. If not, delete it without reply.
2. Check whether the candidate has experience with the task. If the posting asked for proof of experience and it is missing, remove the candidate. People who have never done the task are generally filtered out.
3. Check attention to detail: effort in the application, full written answers versus one-liners, quality of any CV or resume, spelling and sentence structure. Drop candidates who did the bare minimum.
4. Check command of the working language, which must be good for the customer, product and content work involved. Frequent misspellings or poor grammar are a reason to remove the candidate, since this cannot be taught later.

#### Working the shortlist

- Shortlist applicants in a spreadsheet, such as Google Sheets, before sending follow-up questions. The follow-up can repeat the job post questions or be new ones. [L1000, 2017]
- Shortlist the applications you like in a spreadsheet, then email follow-up questions to the shortlisted candidates. [L1000, 2017]
- Reply only to the candidates you shortlist, because replying to hundreds of applicants is too time consuming. If you have an executive assistant, that person can handle the replies to the others. [L1055, 2021]
- If more candidates remain after the four checks than the interview limit allows, rank the remaining candidates and keep the top five. If the candidates still cannot be separated, choose randomly and move on without overthinking. [L1055, 2021]

#### Follow-up questions

- Review the follow-up answers for responsiveness, spelling and grammar, and whether the answers look scripted. Drop candidates who take days to reply. [L1000, 2017]
- Send the remaining applicants a follow-up with specific questions about their knowledge and experience for the job. [L1396, 2021-22]

### Interviews, trials and hiring

#### Interview and trial

- Run a five-minute interview test on a call with the best candidates. Offer a half-day paid trial to the last three or four. [L1000, 2017]
- Test with scenario questions and a small paid trial batch, such as three or four product descriptions with minimal instructions, to see how they show initiative. [L1122, 2016]
- Give a paid trial task and measure how fast it is done. [L1396, 2021-22]
- Give a trial task before a long-term commitment. [L0832, 2019]
- Before offering a paid trial task, check each content applicant against all three requirements. [L1396, 2021-22]

#### Checks before hiring

- Check reviews on the platform, especially recurring themes that need discussion. [L1122, 2016]
- Check portfolios and ask previous clients for references. [L1122, 2016]
- Before hiring a freelancer on Fiverr, check the number of reviews and the feedback left by previous clients. Reviews serve as social proof of reliability. [L1433, undated; L1418, undated]
- Adding a line at the end of an Upwork job post that points to your positive client reviews can help attract more applicants. This works only if you already have good client feedback on the platform. [L0382, 2017]
- A Klaviyo specialist can be found on Upwork or similar sites. Ask for screenshots or a recording of past flows before hiring. [L0855, 2023]
- To find a Shopify bookkeeper, search Upwork for Shopify bookkeeping and review the profiles with direct e-commerce experience. [L0855, 2023]
- Define a list of criteria that every applicant must meet, then check each applicant against it. [L0926, 2018]

#### Five-step VA hiring process (version A)

1. Write a very detailed job post. [L1000, 2017]
2. Shortlist the applications you like in a spreadsheet, then email follow-up questions to the shortlisted candidates. [L1000, 2017]
3. Review the follow-up answers for responsiveness, spelling and grammar, and whether the answers look scripted. Drop candidates who take days to reply. [L1000, 2017]
4. Run a five-minute interview test on a call with the best candidates, and offer a half-day paid trial to the last three or four. [L1000, 2017]
5. Sign an agreement letter, then train the new hire gradually, starting with email and moving to phone calls and live chat. [L1000, 2017]

#### Six-step freelancer and VA sequence (version B)

1. Write a clear, attractive job posting that states the job, expectations, pay, and weekly hours, so good candidates are attracted and weak ones are filtered out. [L1122, 2016]
2. Check reviews on the platform, especially recurring themes that need discussion. [L1122, 2016]
3. Ask your business community or forums for recommendations. [L1122, 2016]
4. For ongoing hires, interview the candidate by video using prepared questions, as you would for an employee. [L1122, 2016]
5. Test with scenario questions and a small paid trial batch, such as three or four product descriptions with minimal instructions, to see how they show initiative. [L1122, 2016]
6. Check portfolios and ask previous clients for references. [L1122, 2016]

#### Five-step paid trial approach (version C)

1. Post a job with a clear list of required skills and tasks. [L1396, 2021-22]
2. Ignore applicants who do not read the requirements, such as those who send only a generic message or a copied resume. [L1396, 2021-22]
3. Send the remaining applicants a follow-up with specific questions about their knowledge and experience for the job. [L1396, 2021-22]
4. Give a paid trial task and measure how fast it is done. [L1396, 2021-22]
5. Keep the people who deliver quality and convert the best part-timers to full-time. [L1396, 2021-22]

#### Small first task

Procedure for a first VA hire: [L0832, 2019]
1. Post a job ad for a small, clearly defined task, such as uploading a batch of products with descriptions, images and prices.
2. Review the candidates' profiles and give a trial task before a long-term commitment.
3. Keep the first task simple so a bad result is easy to correct by deleting and rehiring.

#### Fiverr procedures

To find a VA on Fiverr: [L1418, undated]
1. Type "virtual assistant" into the Fiverr search bar.
2. Compare the sellers on offer and check how many reviews each one has.
3. Contact the sellers you consider directly.
4. Give them a task and see whether they can deliver it.

To find a VA on Fiverr for small tasks: [L1433, undated]
1. Type "virtual assistant" into the Fiverr search bar.
2. Look for sellers who have a good number of reviews.
3. Contact the sellers directly and give each one a concrete small task to see whether they can deliver.
4. Pay only after you accept the finished job, so the seller is paid only if you are satisfied.

Fiverr suits small tasks and small store projects. Use it to test how a VA works before committing to a larger role. [L1418, undated; L1433, undated]

#### Upwork procedure

Hiring a VA on Upwork: [L1433, undated]
1. Post a job with the exact tasks you want to delegate.
2. Write a clear job description that states those tasks.
3. Add screening questions to the job application.
4. Review applicants and filter out everyone who did not answer the screening questions.

#### Agency intake (Monazar VAs)

To request a VA through Monazar VAs: [L1418, undated]
1. Open the Monazar VAs website.
2. Scroll down to the contact form.
3. Enter your name, your email address, and either your Skype ID or your WhatsApp number.
4. Write a brief explanation of what you need and how a VA should help your business.
5. Submit the form and wait for the team to contact you and set up a consultation call to discuss your needs.

Typical intake steps with an agency-based VA service: [L1433, undated]
1. Open the provider's website and fill in the contact form with your name, email address, and either your Skype ID or WhatsApp number.
2. Briefly describe what you need and how a VA could help you.
3. Expect a call from the provider to run a needs analysis, where they confirm your requirements and whether they can help.

#### Hiring for the business's own processes

- Recruit VAs through platforms such as OnlineJobs.ph and Upwork. Use the Empire Flippers guide as a reference. [L1441, 2018]
- Check your own requirements before the first hire. [L1055, 2021]
- Hire a VA with experience solving problems rather than Shopify experience alone, when the role is in the US. [L0337, 2022]
