### Performance review and benchmarks

#### Baseline targets

- A proven 13X return on ad spend can be used as an agency brief. A good brief keeps 13X while increasing spend from $1,000 to $2,000 per month, or raises return from 13X to 15X at the same spend. Without a baseline, an agency has no measurable target. [L0934, 2023]
- Keep Google Ads in-house until the account returns at least 10x ROAS. [L1116, 2025]
- Set a target uplift of 20 percent or 50 percent more ROAS, agreed with a realistic timeframe. [L1116, 2025]
- Compare an agency's period with the same months of the previous year. [L1508, 2022+]

#### Reviewing an agency before firing it

- Review an agency or specialist before firing them: [L0963, 2022] **
  1. Ask what changes they made to scale, including budget increases and campaign type.
  2. If they switched to Smart Shopping, ask them to revert to manual campaigns.
  3. Decide afterward whether to keep or replace them.
- Do not fire a specialist on the first day of trouble. Previous manual work may have been what worked, so first restore the earlier setup. ** [L0963, 2022]

#### Ongoing checks

- Judge the return on the agency only after its work has had time to improve results. [L0694, 2024]
- Monitor agency spend and results closely in the early phase, when there is little historic data. ** [L0801, 2021]
- Verify the specialist's reports instead of trusting them blindly. [L1448, 2020-26]

### Testing agencies against in-house results

- When an agency, freelancer or new approach claims better results, test it in a few markets alongside the existing campaign and compare traced results. This settles the question without a commitment to the new party. [L1261, undated]
- Test procedure: [L1261, undated]
  1. Keep the current campaign running unchanged in most markets.
  2. Give the new agency or approach a few selected towns or segments.
  3. Track responses, cost per customer and sales in both groups using the same keying method.
  4. Compare the traced results and extend the winner only when its figures are clearly better.
- An outsourced agency or new hire must beat your own prior results from the same period, because outsourcing raises your break-even point. [L1508, 2022+]
- Outsourcing Google Ads only makes sense with a stated timeframe and a commitment to beat current results. Without that baseline, a new agency's underperformance cannot be judged. [L1116, 2025]
