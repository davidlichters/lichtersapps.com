#### Platform fit by task type

- OnlineJobs.ph suits a long-term part-time or full-time team member, not a one-off project such as a single product upload. [L0855, 2023]
- Upwork was used to find a large number of qualified VAs for listing and data tasks. [L0801, 2021]
- Upwork worked only for small one-off tasks such as coding and product research and did not produce good full-time hires. [L1473, 2019]
- Over the years a business can build a team of around 15 from OnlineJobs.ph. It is a practical long-term source. [L0970, 2023]
- OnlineJobs.ph was used to hire overseas staff for product uploads and customer service. [L0671, 2021]
- For hiring VAs to post content, use OnlineJobs.ph or Upwork. [L0379, 2021-22]
- For writers, the template-based job post on ProBlogger is the specialised option. On general freelance platforms such as Upwork, many applicants claim writing skills they do not have. Sifting is slower and more error-prone there than on a specialised blog-writer board. [L0379, 2021-22]
- Upwork and Craigslist are better for finding native English speakers for live call work. OnlineJobs.ph is named for back-office work. [L0910, 2015]
- Searching for phone-specific gigs yields few results, because few qualified people want telemarketing or cold calls. Search for an all-around VA or customer service profile, then add answering calls as a side task. [L0910, 2015]

#### Older platform data (2018 to 2022)

- Filipino VAs usually speak English well, can handle phone support and often work reverse hours. A night shift can cover your daytime in the U.S. [L1274, 2018]
- OnlineJobs.ph was used to hire remote customer service staff from the Philippines at low wages, for support tickets, damage claims and technical troubleshooting. [L0270, 2021]
- OnlineJobs.ph was recommended for hiring VAs on an hourly rate basis, and the company built large teams through it over many years. [L0272, 2022]
- For product uploads and similar tasks, look for experienced workers in the Philippines or Vietnam, which offer fair rates. [L1275, 2019]

#### Cost of posting and platform fees

Two sources from 2019 describe OnlineJobs.ph pricing differently. Both are older. Check current terms before relying on either.

- **one source (2019):** Upwork is free to post jobs and hire freelancers. OnlineJobs.ph charges $99 monthly (not per job) to access pre-vetted full-time Filipino employees. [L0243, 2019]
- **one source (2019):** Post hiring jobs on Upwork or OnlineJobs.ph. Expect hundreds of applicants, so shortlist and test several candidates at the same time. OnlineJobs.ph charges more to post a job. [L0467, 2019]

#### Freelancers, agencies and trust

- Upwork and Fiverr carry a high risk of scams. A trusted assistant agency or company, or a free trial period, reduces that risk. [L1432, undated]
- Fiverr carries lower hiring risk than Upwork. The buyer's payment is released to the VA only after the buyer accepts the finished job. [L1418, undated]
- Fiverr lowers financial risk because you pay for a service and the freelancer receives the money only once you accept the completed job. This protects you if the work is not satisfactory. [L1433, undated]
- Some agency-based VA services offer a money-back guarantee if the delivered services are not satisfactory. Read the guarantee terms before committing to any agency. [L1433, undated]
- Monazar VAs is a dedicated VA recruiting service with a money-back guarantee if the client is not satisfied. [L1418, undated]
- Choose Independent as the freelancer type on Upwork, not Agency. An agency is a larger company that assigns workers to you. You want freelancers who look for work directly on Upwork. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Freelancer profiles on Upwork and Fiverr vary widely in quality. Check each candidate's portfolio of emails, flows or designs before hiring. [L0855, 2023]
- Do not hire the cheapest VA. It saves money on paper but usually fails to deliver the required quality, and poor quality work can seriously damage the business. [L0765, undated]

#### Referrals and communities

- Ask current VAs to recommend candidates near them. Well-paid and well-treated VAs stay loyal, and the team turns into a community. Hiring a partner of a current VA worked well in this case. [L1437, 2018]
- One strong VA often recommends other reliable VAs from their own network. Experienced VAs have often worked with many businesses over time. [L0921, 2022]
- A VA referred by a trusted contact can often start without training from scratch. Ask your network whether a team or agency already has a VA you can hire. [L1127, 2024]
- Ask your business community or forums for recommendations. [L1122, 2016]
- To find a VA for store management and supplier outreach, search the community's Discord for terms such as VA and freelancer and look for recommendations from other members. [L0805, 2024]
- Recruit freelancers and VAs through business communities for recommendations, and check reviews on the platform for recurring themes that need discussion. [L1122, 2016]

#### Overseas apprentices

- Apprentices are usually sharp people in their twenties who want out of conventional office work and doubt that job security exists. Offers that combine growth, adventure and a life abroad attract them, so recruit through communities of lifestyle entrepreneurs. [L1359, 2014]
- Describe the living and working conditions of an overseas apprentice accurately before they accept, including basic housing. An expectation gap on arrival damages the fit between employer and apprentice, so the pitch must match reality. [L1359, 2014]

### Writing the job post

#### Principles

- A job post should be simple and direct. State only what is needed, with no fluff or hype. Clear posts make it easier for candidates to understand the role and reduce unsuitable applications. [L1055, 2021]
- Expectations, pay and weekly hours should be stated clearly, so good candidates are attracted and weak ones are filtered out. [L1122, 2016]
- Write a moderate job description and prefer candidates with less experience. You can then train them to your own processes instead of hiring someone with fixed habits. [L1437, 2018]
- State in the post that an editor will help the writer improve. Good writers value feedback, and this attracts stronger applicants. Writers choose clients who signal they will develop their skills. [L0382, 2017]

#### Required contents (version A, one source)

Include the following in every job posting: [L1055, 2021]
1. Job title or position name.
2. Position description.
3. Proposed pay rate, which can be a starting point rather than the final agreed rate.
4. Location preference, if one is required.
5. Hours per week and any specific working hours, such as business hours in your country.
6. Whether weekend work is required.
7. Required software or tools and the existing skills needed, for example Photoshop for image editing or existing Shopify knowledge.

#### Required contents (version B, one source)

A detailed job post should cover: [L1000, 2017]
1. Responsibilities, such as phone answering, email, live chat, and whether the assistant works with other assistants.
2. Required skills, such as sales ability, product knowledge, and whether the owner will train a less experienced person at a lower rate.
3. Working hours and the time zone they must work in.
4. Pay rate.
5. Work environment, such as a quiet space without background noise, for phone roles.
6. Hardware requirements, such as a working computer.
7. Availability and any fixed commitments the candidate has.
8. A link to the store, asking whether the candidate can see themselves selling these products and talking about them.
9. An instruction to reply on the first line that they have read the full job posting, which filters out applicants who skim.

A detailed post on Online Jobs PH or Upwork can be used the same way. [L1000, 2017]

#### Upwork job post setup

Create the Upwork job posting in this order: [L1430, 2020-26]
1. Select the category Customer Service.
2. Select the subcategory Customer Service.
3. Enter a job title and describe the work, for example handling live chat and emails.
4. List the responsibilities the assistant will carry, such as email and live chat handling.
5. Select the skills for email and live chat.
6. Enter the required working hours and the time zone.
7. Choose the payment type: hourly when first testing someone, fixed price once a good candidate is found.
8. Choose Entry-level as the experience level.
9. Answer the additional questions on expected duration (for example more than six months) and hours per week.
10. Set the freelancer preferences and screening filters described in the following rules.
11. Add the candidate questions described in the screening procedure.
12. Publish the posting.

Upwork settings for an email, live chat and order processing role:
- Experience level: Entry-level is the correct choice for a VA who answers emails, handles live chat and processes orders, because this is an entry-level role. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Freelancer type: Independent. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Job success score: set the filter to 90% or higher and reject any candidate below 90%. Freelancers who take on too much work tend to miss deadlines and see their score drop. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Minimum experience on Upwork: 100 hours, which shows the candidate has already done some work on the platform. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Location: does not matter for an email, live chat and order processing role. [L1430, 2020-26]
- English level: set to Fluent. Do not require Native or Bilingual, since the Fluent filter already includes these candidates. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Expect applications within the first 30 minutes after posting, and many of them. Plan time to review them quickly. [L1430, 2020-26]

#### Keyword and phrase instructions in the post

- Include a keyword filter in the job title or description, such as: "To apply successfully, include [specific phrase] in your application" (for example, "include your best color"). This filters out low-effort applicants automatically. [L0263, 2022]
- Ask applicants to put a specific phrase, for example "customer service is my favorite", in the subject line of their application. [L0337, 2022]
- Replace keyword filtering with an Asana form submission, which is the modern approach. Direct applicants to complete the Asana form to apply successfully. Form responses become Asana tasks with the candidate's email as the task name. Customize 6 key screening questions in the form. [L0263, 2022]

#### Template for a VA job post

The template text is in the source as follows. Keep it unchanged.

```text
I'd like to bring on a Virtual Assistant to help run my dropshipping business. Here are the tasks I'm planning to hand off:
1. Handling customer inquiries and support
2. Managing order fulfillment
3. Building out the online store

Drop me a message if this sounds like something you'd want to take on!
```
[L0765, undated]

#### Template for content writers

The source gives no template text for this role. Build your own by listing: [L0379, 2021-22]
- The deliverable,
- The required elements of each piece,
- The pay per project,
- The test project.

When hiring content writers, list three requirements in the job post: time management, customer service experience and writing experience. Check each applicant against all three before offering a paid trial task. Hire separately for other roles rather than expecting one person to cover everything. [L1396, 2021-22]

#### Tasks that need market-based staff

- Tasks that involve calling suppliers in a market are best given to someone based in that market. Matching time zone, business hours and accent makes suppliers more comfortable. The post should require that location and tell people not to apply if they are elsewhere. [L1055, 2021]

#### Delegation posts on Upwork

- When you need to free up time and delegate, post a job on Upwork that describes the exact task you want someone else to do. Applications then arrive from freelancers who want that work. [L1418, undated]
- Use Upwork with a detailed job post and screening questions when you need a VA for ongoing work. Use Fiverr for small, one-off tasks to test how a VA works before committing to a longer hire. [L1418, undated]

### Pay-rate disclosure

- Publish a starting pay rate in the post. It filters out candidates who would reject the pay anyway, so you interview only people already within budget. Candidates should see whether the figure is in their range before applying. [L1055, 2021]
- The pay rate is a required item in the post. [L1000, 2017]
- Pay type on Upwork: choose hourly when first testing someone, and fixed price once a good candidate is found. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Fixed-batch example: a post for a product upload VA offered a fixed price of 200 for 179 products, with descriptions, images and prices supplied by the store owner. A fixed batch with a clear deliverable makes it easy to compare applicants and test them cheaply. [L0832, 2019]
- Freelancers on Upwork with no ratings yet can often be hired at lower rates if you vet them yourself by reading their work samples. The extra vetting time is the trade-off for the lower price. [L0382, 2017]
- VAs on Upwork are priced very differently, so compare rates and profiles before hiring. [L0829, 2019]
- You can state whether you will train a less experienced person at a lower rate. [L1000, 2017]
- Well-paid and well-treated VAs stay loyal. [L1437, 2018]
- Overqualified hires cost more per hour but need fewer explanations, make their own decisions, and are less likely to leave for a better salary. [L1473, 2019]

### Location and time zone

- Location only needs to be stated on platforms that are not location-limited. On OnlineJobs.ph all candidates are based in the Philippines. On platforms such as FreeUp or Upwork, a required location (for example, the US) must be written into the post. [L1055, 2021]
- Time zone matters, but it is not decisive. A location close to the target market's time zone makes working hours easier to share. For example, the Philippines is three to four hours from Australia, depending on daylight saving. [L1000, 2017]
- Factor a VA's time zone and location into the hire. A VA in the Philippines works different hours than one in the US, which affects when calls and questions can be answered. [L0832, 2019]
- Regions with a similar time zone can reduce friction for managers. Some owners use Morocco on Upwork for this reason. [L1077, undated]

### Application filters and screening tricks

#### Filters that run before any interview

- Simple instructions for how to submit the application and what it must contain are the first screening layer. They show whether a candidate reads and follows directions. [L1055, 2021]
- Candidates who ignore the submission format or required content should not be interviewed. Ignoring simple instructions before hiring predicts ignoring SOPs after hiring. [L1055, 2021]
- Ask a screening question in the Upwork application, such as a simple arithmetic question like 2+2. Applicants who do not answer it have likely not read the job description and can be ignored. [L1418, undated]
- A correct answer to a screening question shows that an applicant read the full job description and is serious about the work. Skipping the screening question is a sign of random, mass applications. [L1418, undated]
- A simple screening question, such as a basic arithmetic question, shows whether an applicant read the full job description. Applicants who skip screening questions are usually applying at random and can be ignored. [L1433, undated]
- Filter out everyone who did not answer the screening questions. [L1433, undated]
- Hidden secret word: add a random secret word, for example "tuna fish", to the post with a request to include it in the reply email. Discard applicants who omit the word, since they most likely did not read the post. [L0921, 2022]
- Secret-word filter result: it reduced hundreds of VA applications to three qualified candidates. The remaining candidates were built into a long-term working relationship. [L0921, 2022]
- Easter egg: ask applicants to add a small picture of a purple giraffe at the top of their application, and reject those who miss it. Most applicants miss this instruction, so it separates careful candidates from others. [L1441, 2018]
- Current employment question: "Do you currently hold a similar position in another company? If yes, what type of clients/products are you responsible for?" This is the most selective question. Candidates answering "yes" should not advance. You need people fully committed to your company, not part-timers juggling multiple employers. [L0263, 2022]

#### Screening tests after the first filter

Criteria every applicant must meet, checked against each applicant: [L0926, 2018]
1. Internet speed screenshot.
2. Audio recording of the applicant's voice sample.
3. English grammar test.

Screening steps in order: [L1441, 2018]
1. Require a passing score of 80 percent or higher on an English grammar test.
2. Ask for a screenshot of an internet speed test showing upload and download speeds.
3. Request an audio recording of the candidate reading a short text aloud, to check that their English is good enough for customer calls.
4. Include an easter egg in the job posting (see the purple giraffe filter above).

#### Video and audio answers

- Ask a few questions that require a short video or audio answer, for example "why do you want this job". [L0337, 2022]
- Discard written answers that you would not be willing to send to a customer. Keep those that show the person can talk to customers well. [L0337, 2022]
- For ongoing hires, interview the candidate by video using prepared questions, as you would for an employee. [L1122, 2016]

#### Experience requirements

Sources differ on how much prior experience to require. Each applies in a different situation.

- Experience solving problems matters more than Shopify experience for US roles, because small details can be learned on the job. Hire experienced freelancers for US roles through Upwork and for Philippines-based roles through OnlineJobs.ph. [L0337, 2022]
- For most Shopify high-ticket dropshipping tasks, experienced people already exist, so requiring proof of prior experience in the application is realistic. Candidates without that experience can be filtered out without losing good options. [L1055, 2021]
- Hiring someone overqualified, with good English and years of customer service experience, saves time. They need fewer explanations, make their own decisions, and are less likely to leave for a better salary. [L1473, 2019]
- For a VA who answers emails, handles live chat and processes orders, Upwork's entry-level setting is the correct experience level. This is a separate setting from how overqualified you are willing to hire. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Write a moderate job description and prefer less experienced candidates when you will train them to your own processes. [L1437, 2018]

#### Language

- The working language must be the language the business and its customers use, whatever that language is. A candidate whose written language is weak can be rejected even if the weakness comes from a non-native background, because a writing standard cannot be taught on the job. [L1055, 2021]
- Frequent misspellings or poor grammar are a reason to remove a candidate, since this cannot be taught later. [L1055, 2021]
