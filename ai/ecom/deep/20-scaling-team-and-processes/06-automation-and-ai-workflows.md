## Automation and AI workflows

### Readiness: when a task is ready to automate

- A task that repeats regularly is ready for automation without first being perfected. Repetition is the criterion, not the optimization level. [L0285, 2020-26]
- Automation fits tasks that occur day in and day out. [L0285, 2020-26]
- Automate capturing orders only once suppliers reliably have stock. Until then, capture orders manually and have a team member confirm stock on each order. ** [L0834, 2021]
- Eliminate the cause of a problem before automating the response to it, because a problem that never occurs needs no handling. Automation should follow once the root causes of calls, emails and refunds have been addressed. [L0910, 2015]
- Automate repeated customer-facing steps once volume grows, because manual handling becomes overwhelming. Example: quiz replies were moved to a team member for a personal touch, but the team returned to automation after about a week because the automated version was better. [L0337, 2022]
- Identify repetitive manual tasks documented in Standard Operating Procedures. Any task that recurs consistently can be turned into a Zapier automation, which reduces human error and frees team capacity for high-value work. [L0286, 2018]
- Any task that employees or team members repeat the same way multiple times is a strong candidate for automation. It saves money and frees people for higher-value work. [L0312, 2023+]
- Automation handles rule-based, repetitive tasks (trigger X, perform Y). It does not replace human judgment for exceptions, negotiation or creative decisions. Use the time it frees for those tasks. [L0286, 2018]
- The aim of an automated dropshipping store is to keep daily manual work to 30 minutes or less, even when the store is very profitable. Judge every tool and automation by how much recurring manual work it removes. [L0437, 2020-26]

### Manual first, then automate

- Execute each process once manually to understand it, then transfer it to automation for ongoing execution. [L0285, 2020-26]
- In the early stage, prioritize manual, non-scalable work, such as personal email follow-ups and individual abandoned-checkout outreach. This extracts maximum conversions from existing traffic. As you scale, these tasks can be automated or delegated. The manual work also generates customer feedback that reveals optimization opportunities. [L0208, 2024+]
- For a team, automate the order email step only after manual processing has worked smoothly for several orders. Add automation once the manual process is verified. [L0964, 2023]
- Supplier order emails and the Trello card move are done by hand at first. Once several orders have gone through without errors, add a Zapier automation that moves the card to "sent to supplier" when the order email goes out. Add a note on the card showing it was moved automatically. [L0964, 2023]
- Add an extra note to an automated card when an automation moves it, so staff can tell it was not handled manually. [L0964, 2023]
- Study the automations module only after the store receives paid traffic and runs operationally, because automations are most useful once you understand the baseline workflow. ** [L0268, 2020]
- Learning automation pays off over time. After you understand how one automation works, you start seeing similar opportunities across the business, such as inventory updates, customer emails, competitor monitoring and report generation. [L0287, 2025-26]

### Time saved and where it goes

- Automation is meant to free time. Campaign management, bid optimization, and Google and Facebook ad bid optimization together typically take about 5 hours each. Use the saved hours for growth instead of manual adjustments. [L1033, 2019]
- Time saved by automation should move to higher-leverage work: product titles, product page conversion rate, cross-channel feed distribution, branding and cold social funnels. For example, five hours of bid tweaking can go to product titles or product pages instead. [L0454, 2019]
- Automate in steps rather than everything at once. As paid traffic becomes more consistent, shift attention to branding, conversion rate optimization, SEO, email marketing, social media, content marketing and influencer outreach. [L0454, 2019]
- Automation and outsourcing let order volume, sales and customer count grow without a matching increase in work, manpower or hours, which is what makes the business ready to scale. [L0506, 2020-26]
- Automation templates can be used to automate most store operations, and ready-made templates can be set up directly in the system. [L0506, 2020-26]

### Learning the stack

1. Use each tool in the stack yourself at least once to learn how it works: Shopify, Facebook page, Google Sheets, Trello, Slack and Zapier. [L0437, 2020-26]
2. Only after you understand each tool, set up the automations that connect them. [L0437, 2020-26]
3. Keep the free versions of Slack, Trello and Google Sheets unless a real limit is reached. [L0437, 2020-26]
4. Pay only for Zapier's $20 per month plan when you need the Shopify integration. [L0437, 2020-26]
- Automations work like a virtual employee that handles repeatable tasks such as order tracking, posting and sending notifications. Connect each automation through Zapier and have it report results to Slack or a shared sheet. [L0437, 2020-26]
- Automation platforms cost approximately $20 per month and reliably execute tasks, without the operational costs and risks of employees: no sick days, errors, salary increases or staff turnover. [L0236, 2018]
- Process automation costs $20 per month and runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, including holidays. [L0285, 2020-26]
- Automation provides reliable execution without errors once configured, runs without interruption from illness or other human factors, eliminates requests for raises, preserves business trade secrets and continuously improves its capabilities. This frees the owner for work that needs human judgment and strategic thinking. [L0285, 2020-26]
- Automations are not free of trade-offs. Automated email sequences sometimes arrive at suboptimal times for individual customers, such as during backorders or shipping delays. This timing variance is an acceptable trade-off for operational scalability and simple systems, and it causes minimal harm overall. ** [L0203, 2021]
- Manual updating of order notes in Asana is the weakest operational friction point. Automation or workflow redesign would reduce this burden. [L0230, 2019]

### Zapier and Trello order flows

**Trello order flow:**

1. Customer places order: Zap 1 automatically creates a card in paid orders with complete order details (name, email, product, SKU, shipping address). [L0292, 2020-26]
2. When the supplier ships, mark fulfillment as shipped in Shopify. Zap 2 automatically moves the card to shipped orders. [L0292, 2020-26; L0286, 2018]
3. When tracking shows delivery, manually move the card to delivered and pending review. [L0292, 2020-26]
4. Contact the customer by phone, email or text using the follow-up script to request a review and feedback. [L0292, 2020-26]
5. Once the review is received, move the card to the reviewed column. [L0292, 2020-26]
6. Goal: ensure no order is forgotten and every customer receives follow-up within days of delivery. [L0292, 2020-26]

- When an order is marked as shipped in Shopify, Zapier moves its Trello card from the "Sent to Supplier" list to the "Ship Orders" list, which removes manual card movement. [L0286, 2018]
- **Two ways to set the delivered move are given.** When you check tracking yourself, move the card by hand on delivery (step 3 above). [L0292, 2020-26] When you automate it, set the delay between "fulfilled" and "delivered" in Trello to match the usual delivery time, for example 7 days after fulfillment. [L0359, 2020]
- Supplier order automation: after all configuration is complete, toggle the switch labeled "off by default" to "on". The Zap then immediately processes all new paid orders according to the rules you set. [L0283, 2020-26]
- After activating the Zap, monitor it for the first few orders. Check the Shopify admin and the BCC emails in your inbox, and confirm emails are being submitted to suppliers correctly. Verifying the initial orders confirms the setup works before you rely on it for all orders. [L0283, 2020-26]
- The Trello board is a management dashboard that needs no daily involvement. If the paid orders list holds several cards with old dates, that signals slow supplier processing or internal fulfillment gaps, so you can intervene quickly. [L0292, 2020-26]
- Order tracking and review follow-up can be self-managed or delegated to a virtual assistant. The system scales from low to high volume without manual intervention. As volume grows, systematic tracking stops orders slipping through with new or untested suppliers and keeps customer contact consistent for review collection. [L0292, 2020-26]

### Custom order automations

- This automation applies to stores that offer customized product versions (varying sizes, colors, heights or specifications), or to B2B businesses handling bulk orders of 50 or more units that require price quotes. [L0284, 2020-26]
- B2B bulk orders handled through quote forms typically involve 50 or more units per request. [L0284, 2020-26]
- Custom order forms typically capture: customer name, email address, phone number, geographic location or company, and a description of the desired product customization or specifications. [L0284, 2020-26]

Build the custom order automation in Zapier as follows:

1. Set WuFu (as written in the source) or Typeform as the trigger, selecting the "Form Completed" event. [L0284, 2020-26]
2. Add an action: send a Slack private message containing the customer name, email, phone number, location and inquiry details. [L0284, 2020-26]
3. Add a second action: insert a new row into the Google Sheets tracking sheet with all lead information. [L0284, 2020-26]
4. Both actions run automatically in sequence when the form is submitted on the website. [L0284, 2020-26]

### Ad account and ad launch automation

- *Older advice, one source 2019:* Set up ad account automation once by giving Google, Facebook, Bing and Pinterest a fixed set of rules they follow on their own. Once the rules are active, campaigns run without daily manual management or an agency. [L1086, 2019]

The aim is to shift human time to higher-value work, not to cut people for their own sake. [L1086, 2019]

### Automation versus outsourcing

- Outsourcing means hiring humans to perform business tasks. Automation means using tools, apps and bots to execute processes automatically. [L0285, 2020-26]
- Outsourcing means buying a service from a third party in place of an in-house resource, and it adds capabilities the owner lacks. It is not the same as automation, which removes the human step entirely. The two can be combined, but they solve different problems. [L1122, 2016]
- Example: setting up automated email sequences that nurture leads and close sales is automation, because the repeated manual work of writing and sending disappears. Paying someone to write and send those emails by hand would be outsourcing, which is a different thing. [L1122, 2016]
- Compare the two on cost and reliability: automation runs for about $20 per month with no sick days or turnover, while staff bring the costs and risks listed under readiness and cost above. [L0236, 2018; L0285, 2020-26]

### AI workflows, AI agents and their levels

**Automations progress through three levels.** Start with AI Workflows. [L0287, 2025-26]

| Level | What it does | Example | Current status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workflows | Linear, triggered by time or events, without AI | Daily ad reports to Slack; competitor ads to Google Drive | Reliable | [L0287, 2025-26] |
| AI Workflows | Chain multiple AI steps in a predetermined order | Scrape forum → summarize → generate angles → write scripts → save to spreadsheet | Start here | [L0287, 2025-26] |
| AI Agents | The system decides what to do dynamically | Email agent that chooses between responding, notifying or scheduling | Unreliable, fails frequently | [L0287, 2025-26] |

- AI Agents: current technology is unreliable and fails frequently. Do not prioritize building agents now. Agents often make unpredictable decisions or misinterpret instructions. Most tasks that seem suited to agents work more reliably as AI Workflows, which use predetermined steps with AI at each stage. Plan to revisit agents in a few months as the technology matures. [L0287, 2025-26]
- Example of an email agent: it can notify you in Slack about an incoming email, compose a reply using Claude or another AI model, send the reply directly to the sender, read your previous emails for context, check your calendar for availability, or create a Google Calendar event for a proposed meeting. It makes these decisions dynamically, without a predetermined sequence. [L0287, 2025-26]
- AI org charts: a hierarchy of agents where a CEO agent delegates to specialized agents such as a CMO, COO or CFO agent. Each specialized agent can manage its own sub-agents. For example, a CMO agent might supervise copywriter, competitor research, data analysis and landing page builder agents. This models how human teams scale and allows automation of complex multi-team workflows. [L0287, 2025-26]

### AI creative workflows: setup

**one source setup (Steps 0-8 once, then Steps 9-10 for each new ad script):**

- Steps 0-8 build five reference files in a Claude or ChatGPT project: Voice Guide, Structure Library, Banned List, Avatar and Psychology Lens. Once these five files live in the project, Steps 9-10 create a new ad in a fresh chat every time. The project reads the five files automatically, so you supply only the product, winning scripts, research and chosen angle. [L0039, 2026]
- Set up Steps 0-8 once and upload the five reference files to your project. After that, run only Steps 9-10 for new ads. Every script session is a new chat in the same project, so the five files stay constant and you change only the angle. [L0039, 2026]

Procedure:

1. Gather 5-10 winning ad scripts from Kalodata or the free Meta Ad Library. Any ad running for months is a proven winner. Copy them into one document and keep it. [L0039, 2026]
2. Open Reddit Answers and ask six research questions, one at a time. Copy each complete answer into one research document:
   - (1) What's so hard about [SITUATION]?
   - (2) Why do people struggle with [PROBLEM]?
   - (3) What have people tried for [PROBLEM] that didn't work?
   - (4) What finally worked for people with [PROBLEM]?
   - (5) Why do people give up on [PROBLEM]?
   - (6) What do people dislike about [CATEGORY]?

   Copy everything. Don't pick and choose, because the next prompts sort it. [L0039, 2026]
3. Run the Step 3 prompt to reverse-engineer the concepts in your winning scripts and identify concept families and gaps. [L0039, 2026]
4. to 8. Run the Step 4-8 prompts to generate and download five files (Voice Guide, Structure Library, Banned List, Avatar, Psychology Lens), then upload each into your project knowledge. [L0039, 2026]
9. Run the Step 9 prompt in a new project chat to get a ranked angle bank. [L0039, 2026]
10. Run the Step 10 prompt in the same chat, pick one angle and get your script. [L0039, 2026]

**Conflict hierarchy (Step 10).** The one source source names two files differently: "Voice Guide" in the setup and "Style Guide" in the rule, and "Banned List" in the setup and "Banned Blacklist" in the rule. Use the names as they appear in your project. [L0039, 2026]

Use the five-file conflict hierarchy exactly as written:

1. Style Guide governs voice first. [L0039, 2026]
2. Structure Library governs beats second. [L0039, 2026]
3. Banned Blacklist supplies hard negative constraints third. [L0039, 2026]
4. Avatar & Context tells you who to write to fourth. [L0039, 2026]
5. Psychology Lens informs but never overrides voice, fifth. [L0039, 2026]

Flag any brief element that contradicts a reference file instead of overriding it silently. [L0039, 2026]

Bottom-of-funnel ad production:

1. Ask Claude: "Make me four [concept type] bottom-of-funnel ad prompts for ChatGPT." [L0316, 2026]
2. Copy each prompt to ChatGPT and generate image descriptions or ad text. [L0316, 2026]
3. Upload each generated image to Canva. [L0316, 2026]
4. Re-download from Canva to remove AI metadata. [L0316, 2026]
5. Upload all images to Meta. [L0316, 2026]
6. Create one ad set per concept (e.g., testimonial, free gift, benefit) with 4-8 ads inside each. [L0316, 2026]
7. Set the budget to $50 per day (or $15-20 AUD minimum) and test for 2-3 days. [L0316, 2026]

### AI creative workflows: the reference-file build

- Assemble the reference files a brand's custom AI assistant needs, and keep them inside the assistant's project so its research follows the brand's own process:
  1. A brand or product overview. [L0485, 2025-26]
  2. A strategy knowledge file describing the creative method. [L0485, 2025-26]
  3. A copy quality rules file. [L0485, 2025-26]
  4. A team process overview. [L0485, 2025-26]
  5. A discovery call template. [L0485, 2025-26]
  6. Production documents for static ads and for video ads. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Use AI to analyze what customers say. A weekly review scraper, for example, can turn customer language into ad ideas. [L0485, 2025-26]
- AI is often seen only as an idea generator, script writer or visual generator. In practice, almost any task that can be written down can be completed, automated or assisted by AI. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Tools, templates and software built with AI can assist current team members or replace some roles entirely. This reduces slow manual work in creative production. [L0485, 2025-26]
- A coded workflow connects AI tools and software so each step passes its output to the next. A typical chain:
  1. The custom AI assistant creates a prompt-based output. [L0485, 2025-26]
  2. The workflow sends it to a platform such as Arcads to create the ad. [L0485, 2025-26]
  3. The result is saved to a spreadsheet. [L0485, 2025-26]
  4. A human is notified on Slack to review it. [L0485, 2025-26]
- Workflows save admin time and speed up the process, but a custom assistant or pre-built platform already speeds up work without them. Understanding how to build and use the assistant and the platforms comes first. [L0485, 2025-26]
