### Warnings, termination and hiring

**Termination for culture violations**

- Automatic termination for business culture violation: cheating (faking time tracking, falsifying reports), fraud, or dishonesty. No second chances. When trust is broken at this level, the person cannot stay. This is an automatic line in the sand; communicate it clearly during onboarding. [L0263, 2022]

**Yellow card procedure**

1. Note missed deadlines or quality problems, and refer to what was agreed. [L1431, 2020+]
2. Send a written warning, called a yellow card, that states the expectation. [L1431, 2020+]
3. If the problem repeats, end the work relationship. [L1431, 2020+]
4. Only dismiss someone with stated reasons and prior warnings, and never for reasons such as lack of money. [L1431, 2020+]

**Early termination and hiring**

- Expect to let go 30-35 people during the journey to building a 35-person team. Most early terminations are because of inadequate onboarding, unclear SOPs, or poor standards. As you improve systems, processes, and expectations, fewer people fail. The mistakes are yours; learn from them and improve the hiring and onboarding process. [L0263, 2022]
- If a virtual assistant does not work out despite careful hiring, let them go early rather than late, because a quick replacement costs less than a prolonged poor fit. Hire slowly and dismiss quickly. *Older (2019).* [L0356, 2019]
- Repeatedly missing details despite clear instructions leads to replacement. [L0337, 2022]

**When to act**

| Problem | Action | Timing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheating, fraud or dishonesty | Automatic termination, no second chances [L0263, 2022] | Immediately | one source |
| Missed deadlines or quality problems | Yellow card, then end the relationship if it repeats [L1431, 2020+] | After a stated written warning | one source |
| Repeated detail misses despite clear instructions | Replace the team member [L0337, 2022] | After several repeated misses | one source |
| Below standard with no visible improvement | Performance improvement plan, then end the arrangement [L1054, 2021] | Default one month, shorter for high-impact tasks | one source |
| Poor fit despite careful hiring | Let them go early [L0356, 2019] | As soon as the poor fit is clear. *Older (2019).* | one source |
| Output late | Check whether the training or instructions were unclear first [L0337, 2022] | Before judging the person | one source |

### Offboarding

**Ongoing staff offboarding procedure** **

1. Before telling them, remove their access to everything, ideally overnight, including Shopify, Google Drive and any other accounts. [L1054, 2021]
2. Remove access to shared payment cards, and order a new credit card if the person had card information. [L1054, 2021]
3. Remove access to the phone system if they had it. [L1054, 2021]
4. Remove them from all team group chats. [L1054, 2021]
5. Send a short written message saying the employment is ending because the required improvement did not happen, referring to the earlier discussion. [L1054, 2021]
6. Pay out any remaining pay. Do not hold a call to explain the decision. [L1054, 2021]

**Project-based freelancers**

- For project-based freelancers rather than ongoing roles, you can simply stop giving them work without a conversation or message. *Older (2021-22).* [L1054, 2021]
