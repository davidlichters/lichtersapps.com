## Interviewing, paid trials and selecting hires

### Screening the applicant pool

#### Filtering on application answers and written instructions

1. Ask each applicant whether they have done a similar customer service role before. If yes, ask for a link to a company they worked for. Ask for examples of typical email correspondence they handled, to judge how they communicate. Decide whether they are the right hire based on these answers. [L1430, 2020-26]
2. Use the follow-up answers to judge spelling, grammar, and whether the reply is a copied script. A candidate who will handle customer emails must write accurately and professionally. [L1000, 2017]
3. Treat slow replies to follow-up questions as a sign of low interest, because a candidate who takes a week to answer is likely applying to many jobs. [L1000, 2017]
4. Eliminate unsuitable candidates by providing specific written instructions and requesting a test task that mirrors actual work. [L0230, 2019]
5. Add a simple test question to the job listing, such as "What's 2+2?", to check whether applicants actually read the listing. [L0765, undated]
6. Set the pass mark of any screening test from your own data: score a few hundred applicants from each country you hire from, then choose the cutoff based on those averages. A test you write yourself can be faked, so treat a candidate who skips it as the strongest reason to reject them. [L1359, 2014]

#### Full screening sequence from one source

The source describes these steps in order. About 50 percent of applicants eliminated themselves at step 1, and roughly another 30 percent were removed at step 2.

1. Write a job post with specific application instructions, such as how to apply, and remove applicants who do not follow them; about 50 percent of applicants eliminated themselves this way.
2. Ask screening questions about English level; this removed roughly another 30 percent of applicants.
3. Send the remaining candidates a test task with three written questions: a reply to a very angry customer, a reply to a bad review, and an action plan for an unshipped order that was the company's fault.
4. Choose the best candidates and send them a scheduling link (Calendly, free) for a 15-minute video call via Skype.
5. Spread the interviews over two or three days, about two to three hours in total.
6. Record every candidate's results in a Google spreadsheet immediately after each call, so the best can be compared later.
7. Sign the contract before training starts. [L1473, 2019]

#### Setting the right candidate profile

- Hire for talent and problem-solving ability, not for the lowest hourly cost. A cheap hire who does not know the products forces the owner to answer the unusual customer questions personally. [L0337, 2022]
- Hire for qualifications rather than for the lowest cost, because weak candidates need more training than they are worth. A junior hire who needed too much training was dropped after the first month. [L0832, 2019]
- Hire people who are overqualified or already run their own business, because they add skills and can later run other team members. Being replaced by a strong hire is less of a threat than having weak support. [L0832, 2019]
- Freight and logistics problems will recur in a store that ships freight, so a team member who can call freight companies, vendors and customers and resolve the issue is essential. Look for this problem-solving skill when hiring, since product knowledge and Shopify experience can be taught. [L0337, 2022]
- Consider personality fit when hiring, since owners and assistants with very different styles can clash. A free 16 Personalities test taken by both parties, with the results compared, can show strengths and weaknesses to manage. [L1000, 2017]
- Disallow team members from working for other online companies while employed by you. This full-time commitment rule must be screened during hiring and enforced consistently to protect your knowledge, systems, and competitive advantage. [L0263, 2022]
- When screening virtual assistants, give work history the most weight. Favour candidates who worked for large, established companies, often in the US or Australia, and find out why they left, since a job at a small online store is a weaker signal. ** [L0356, 2019]
- Recruit virtual assistants with the same care as a local employee, with a longer screening process and more checks. In one year using this approach, about 8 assistants were hired and only 1 was let go. ** [L0356, 2019]

#### Using the one source customer success hiring procedure

This is a reference procedure from the source. Interview rounds and pay differ between units; both values are shown in the section on interview formats below.

1. Start with a Google Form screening on dropshipping, e-commerce, and tools such as Zoho, Zendesk or email tools.
2. Run a multi-round interview, six rounds in the source.
3. Hire via Zoom and offer a salary of about $250 per month.
4. Onboard with Loom recordings about the brand and systems.
5. Spend week one on shadow testing, then take over all tasks from week two. [L0637, 2023+]

### Interview formats

#### Choosing a format: typed chat or video call

Sources disagree on the medium. Use the format that fits the role:

| Format | Source | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Typed chat on Skype or Slack, one question at a time | one source | Hiring a VA or freelancer for mostly written, message-based work; you want a searchable written record |
| 15-minute video call via Skype, after a written test | one source | Adding a voice check to a written screening; the short call assesses fluency and fit |

- Conduct hiring interviews via message exchange using standardized template questions rather than phone or video calls, as this method is more efficient. ** [L0223, 2021]
- Interview through typed messages instead of voice or video calls. Written chat shows responsiveness and written communication skills, which matter because most day to day communication with a VA or freelancer is by message. ** [L1055, 2021]
- Run candidate interviews by typed chat rather than recorded voice or video, because the written record is quick to search. Finding a specific answer in an audio or video recording is much slower. ** [L1055, 2021]
- Schedule video interviews via Skype within 2-3 days of application; keep each interview to 15 minutes. Use Calendly to schedule. [L0230, 2019]

#### Question set and interview opening

- Prepare a set of up to 15 interview questions and use the same core set for every candidate, changing only small details to suit the role. This keeps the candidates comparable. ** [L1055, 2021]
- Ask a set of open questions during onboarding and interviews: a strong virtual assistant asks many questions, while one who asks few will get stuck later without raising it. Asking questions during onboarding is a good sign of a future good hire. [L1000, 2017]

Shortlist invitation, ready to paste:

```
Hello [Candidate name],

Thank you for applying. You have been successful and shortlisted for an interview.

Can you attend an interview at [proposed time, adjusted to the candidate's time zone]? If this does not suit you, please tell me a time that works for you.

The interview will be held by typed message on [Skype or Slack]. Please send me your [Skype ID or email address for the Slack invite] so I can set it up.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
```
[L1055, 2021]

Interview opening message, ready to paste:

```
Hi [Candidate name], welcome to the interview. I will send you one question at a time. Please answer each question fully before I send the next one. There is no need to rush, but please reply in detail.
```
[L1055, 2021]

Run the typed interview in this order:

1. Send the candidate a success notice with a proposed interview time that works for both time zones.
2. State that the interview will be held by typed message on Skype or Slack and ask for the Skype ID or the email address for the Slack invite.
3. Once the candidate has joined, post the introduction in the chat and send it.
4. Send the first question and wait for the full typed answer.
5. Send the next question only after the answer arrives, and repeat until all questions are asked.
6. Thank the candidate and say when you will reply, which is typically within three days.
Keep the chat as a written record for later comparison. ** [L1055, 2021]

#### Stage-based video interviews

Interview stage 1 (Short Call):

1. Duration: 10-15 minutes.
2. You have already reviewed their written application answers and recording.
3. Reintroduce the company, explain what you do, what you need.
4. Ask candidate to introduce themselves.
5. Assess English fluency, communication, and fit.
6. Communicate this is one of many short calls; only best advance.
7. Follow up with email to selected finalists for stage 2. [L0263, 2022]

Interview stage 2 (Final Call):

1. Duration: 45-60 minutes.
2. Prepare case study materials: link to website, SOPs, FAQs, product catalog.
3. Send materials to candidate before the call so they can prepare.
4. During call, ask realistic scenarios (e.g., 'Does this product have [feature]?' or 'What is the estimated delivery time?').
5. Answer must come from the materials provided (candidate hasn't seen products before).
6. This tests problem-solving, product learning ability, and communication under pressure.
7. Select top 5-6 candidates for final interviews from approximately 400 applicants. [L0263, 2022]

- Conduct the final interview call yourself, even as you scale. This final call is when you decide who joins the team. You need to know the person, they need to know you, and you both commit to working together. This builds accountability and culture from the hire's first day. [L0263, 2022]

#### Multi-round interviews

- Run six to seven interview rounds before hiring anyone, to test the candidate's eagerness to learn. Starting pay for candidates who passed was often 200 to 300 USD per month. [L1431, 2020+]
- Hiring a virtual assistant frees up your time so you can keep growing the business or scale it. Choosing the right person matters more than hiring quickly, because a poor fit costs money and progress. [L1433, undated]

Note: the one source procedure above states six rounds and about $250 per month as salary; [L1431, 2020+] states six to seven rounds and 200 to 300 USD per month as starting pay. Use the range that matches your budget and the round count you can staff.

### Practical tests

#### Phone and site-navigation test

Interview line to add phone duties to a virtual assistant role:

```
By the way, we also get about one or two calls a day. Would you be able to answer those as they come in, in addition to the email work?
```
[L0910, 2015]

Testing a phone-answering VA candidate:

1. Ask the candidate for a phone number and say you will call later in the week.
2. Call at random times Monday to Friday between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. without announcing a test.
3. Count the rings before the candidate picks up.
4. Assess how professional they sound and whether they are a native English speaker.
5. Casually ask them to log into the store and open the homepage, and time how long it takes.
6. Ask them to describe the homepage and to read back the phone number shown on it.
7. Ask them to find a specific product through the search bar or the menu.
8. Ask them to read the note from the seller on that product.
9. Ask them to find a second product and tell you how long it takes to ship, and time the search.
The test shows availability, professionalism, computer readiness and store knowledge in one short call. [L0910, 2015]

- Testing a candidate by phone and site navigation reveals more than a CV or interview, because it shows real availability and skills. Those skills are the same ones that customers will need during a real call. [L0910, 2015]

#### Product uploader sample

1. Post a job or search for candidates with relevant experience.
2. Review the work samples they have sent before.
3. Give a small sample project of 10 to 30 products.
4. Triple-check the sample work for correctness.
5. Only then give the full project. [L0971, 2024]

#### Test projects for freelancers

- Start every new freelancer with a test project, such as writing part of an article, before committing to multiple pieces. Use test projects to confirm quality and turnaround time, and to choose between several candidates by comparing their results. ** [L0379, 2021-22]
- Before hiring a virtual assistant for a task, test whether they can complete it on their own without constant checking. If you cannot trust them to work independently, they are not the right fit for the role. [L0832, 2019]

### Paid trials

#### Trial offer and pay

- Offer a paid half-day trial to the last three or four candidates, and walk them through the processes as if they were already hired. The trial reveals how they actually work, which a phone call cannot show. [L1000, 2017]
- An owner with two strong candidates on paper could not choose between them after interviews. A paid half-day trial made one candidate's superior performance clear, and that candidate was hired. [L1000, 2017]
- A paid half-day trial carries a small risk that a candidate who sees the business processes will start a competing store. The owner judged this risk highly unlikely and accepted it, because real work on the trial shows how a candidate performs better than an interview. [L1000, 2017]
- Do not hire the successful candidate outright. Tell them they are successful and offer a paid trial first, and pay the trial at the full rate for the position because they do the real work. ** [L1055, 2021]

#### Trial steps

1. Tell the successful candidate they are selected and offer a trial.
2. Provide the SOP for the task so they can do it properly.
3. Walk them through an onboarding session to confirm they understand the task.
4. Define the trial parameters, such as its length or deliverable, which depend on the task.
5. Review the result at the end of the trial and decide whether to continue. ** [L1055, 2021]

#### Trial length and deliverable

| Role | Trial length or deliverable | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Customer service | One-week trial, with a review at the end | one source [L1055, 2021] |
| Content production (e.g., blog writing) | First piece of work, for example one blog post | one source [L1055, 2021] |
| Any role, owner-led | Paid half-day trial, walking through processes as if already hired | one source [L1000, 2017] |

Trial and delivery length depend on the role. The half-day trial is the approach for the final three or four candidates in the one source source; the one-week trial is the one source approach for customer service. [L1055, 2021; L1000, 2017]

#### Judging the trial

- A trial does not need to be perfect, since everyone makes mistakes in a new role. Small fixable issues are acceptable because you can develop the person, but a large quality gap means the trial is failed. ** [L1055, 2021]

Trial failure message, ready to paste:

```
Hi [Candidate name],

Thank you for your work on the trial. Here is your payment for the trial.

Unfortunately you were not successful in the trial, so we will not be offering you ongoing work. Thank you for your time, and good luck for the future.

[Your name]
```
** [L1055, 2021]

### Evaluating candidates

#### Checks after each interview

Evaluate each interview using these checks, in order:

1. Did the candidate clearly agree to the pay, the hours and any specific hours? If not, it is a red flag and the candidate is not hired.
2. How many other clients does the candidate have? A large number is a red flag for part time or full time roles, because they will not be able to give the hours they promise.
3. Did the candidate answer in detail? Short answers of a few words are a red flag, while multi-line answers are a positive sign.
4. How quickly did the candidate reply? A long delay for a simple question suggests divided attention, and slow understanding of the question is also a red flag.
5. Did the answers feel honest and genuine rather than like fluff written to please you? Trust your gut feeling here.
6. Does the candidate fit your company culture?
Any red flag on these checks means the candidate is removed. ** [L1055, 2021]

- The interview is not used to test the task itself, since most people learn the task quickly. It is used to assess speed, attention to detail, culture fit and red flags, and the job application already gives a good indication of experience. ** [L1055, 2021]
- Attention to detail is required for any VA or freelancer role, because those roles follow SOPs and cannot be run with loose work. High level thinking is the owner's job and does not replace attention to detail in these roles. ** [L1055, 2021]
- A candidate who takes about 10 minutes to start replying to a single interview question is probably dividing attention with other work. Slow replies during the interview are a warning sign about future responsiveness. ** [L1055, 2021]
- If a part-time or full-time candidate already works with around 10 other clients, they are unlikely to deliver the hours they promise, even if they say otherwise. Treat a high number of other clients as a red flag for hours. ** [L1055, 2021]

### Reference checks and verification

- On Upwork, recent hires have sometimes claimed experience that they did not have. Verify work samples and experience before hiring. ** [L1274, 2018]
- Ask for a link to a company the candidate has worked for in a similar role, and ask for examples of typical email correspondence they handled. [L1430, 2020-26]
- Find out why the candidate left previous large, established companies, since this shows more than the CV alone. ** [L0356, 2019]
- Compare the sample work sent by the candidate with a small sample project before the full project is given. [L0971, 2024]

### Selecting the hire

#### Deciding between finalists

- If two or three candidates pass all checks, choose the one with the strongest gut feeling, the strongest relevant experience, or the best fit with your culture. Give candidates a decision date, typically around three days after the interview. ** [L1055, 2021]
- A negative gut feeling during the interview stage rarely improves later, and you will work with the person often, sometimes daily. Move on to another applicant when the gut feeling is negative. ** [L1055, 2021]
- The first 3-6 months reveal whether a win-win exists: for you (business value), the team (culture fit), and the employee (fair pay, growth, respect). Set this expectation during hiring so both sides know early termination is possible if it's not working. This removes surprises and keeps focus on actual fit. [L0263, 2022]

### Rejecting candidates and avoiding rushed hiring

- Thank unsuccessful applicants and keep good relations with them, because they can be called on for a second assistant hire later. [L1000, 2017]
- Rushing the hiring process, skipping steps or skipping paid trials leads to the wrong hires. You then pay for work that does not return value, and either pay someone to redo it or spend your own time fixing it. ** [L1055, 2021]
- Hiring a virtual assistant frees up your time so you can keep growing the business or scale it. Choosing the right person matters more than hiring quickly, because a poor fit costs money and progress. [L1433, undated]
- Remove any candidate who fails a check in the evaluation sequence, and send the failure message to those who completed a paid trial without success. [L1055, 2021]

#### Further details

- Maintain a candidate tracking spreadsheet for all applicants, interview notes, and hiring decisions. [L0230, 2019]
