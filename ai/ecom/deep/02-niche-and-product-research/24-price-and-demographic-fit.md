### Price and Demographic Fit

- Do not mark a niche as wealthy or lower-income and drop it right away. First check whether a related sub-niche, such as general chandeliers instead of real crystal chandeliers, fits the upper middle class. (Source year not stated.) [L0457, 2020-26]
- Consider pivoting when price points reach the $5,000 to $10,000+ range, or when the product is mostly bought as a first or temporary purchase. Adjust the niche to move it back into upper middle class price territory. [L0457, 2020-26]
- A broad niche such as rugs covers an enormous price range, from about $30 to $40 for house rugs up to $500 or more for premium rugs. Broad niches make it hard to tell which demographic they serve, so split them into narrower sub-niches before researching. [L1517, 2020-26]
- Narrow niches differ in price: a niche with a $30 to $40 product and a $500 product serve different buyers. Pick the sub-niche that matches the target price tier before you test demand. [L1517, 2020-26; L0801, 2021]

### Fallback When All Ideas Fail

**Shortlist cycle**

1. Take the next five ideas from the shortlist that have not been researched yet.
2. Research each of them in full.
3. Run the niche approval procedure on the five ideas.
4. Repeat the cycle until at least one niche is green. [L0490, 2021-22]

**Fallback order when all ideas fail**

- Work down your brainstorm list first. If the niche fails the competition test, niche sideways into a related category, or niche up one level, and pick the path that gets you to at least 20 suppliers. [L0804, 2023]
- If a niche fails, pivot from the closest near-miss niche rather than brainstorming new ones. A niche that failed only one test is a good pivot starting point. [L0855, 2023]
- Expand into a lateral niche when the current niche has no good options left. Use the same analytics to compare these options. [L0920, 2018]
- Keep a near-miss niche alive by pivoting it into a related niche that passes, or broaden it one level so the list gets more candidates. [L0807, 2025]
- Rescue dismissed ideas by combining similar products and retesting the combined version against the criteria. [L0803, 2022]

#### Further details

- Niche up (broaden to a higher category) when you cannot find 20 dropship-friendly suppliers in your specific niche but can reach that number at a broader level, such as selling kayaks plus stand-up paddle boards plus surfboards as water sports. [L0249, 2022]

- When your niche has few suppliers, look at adjacent product categories, such as broader lines of similar products, to reach the minimum number of suppliers. [L0671, 2021]
