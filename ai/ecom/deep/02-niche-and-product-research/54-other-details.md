### Principles for Choosing a Niche and Product

- Find the X factor: the single variable in your business with the largest impact on results, sometimes called the lead domino. It differs for every business and is found by becoming an expert in your industry. [L0413, 2016]
- Choose niches you know and use. Bonus ideas come easily when you understand what buyers need, and you can see what a competitor's buyers lack. [L1293, 2014]
- You do not need to be an expert in a product to sell it. Lack of prior product knowledge should not stop you from choosing any product type, because you learn the product as you sell it. [L0769, 2020-26]
- You do not need to know everything about a product before building the store. Learn as you go: ask the supplier technical questions, and when a question comes up often, add the answer to the product page as a Q and A. [L0806, 2024]
- No niche is guaranteed to be a home run. Some niches expected to be big underperform, and some modest ones grow strongly, so treat the niche as a project that needs ongoing optimization. [L0962, 2021]
- Thorough competitor research before building a store, contacting suppliers and buying traffic raises the chance of success. It takes time up front, but a rushed list weakens the decisions that follow. [L0891, 2020-26]
- Validate ideas with the criteria used in published top-10 niche lists rather than using the list as a ready-made answer. The criteria are what matter. [L0854, 2022]
- A November 2021 coaching call presented a ranked list of the top 10 niches for 2022, meaning niche selection was reviewed annually against the market. [L1089, 2021]
- Niche research never really ends. Set a recurring calendar reminder, for example every six weeks, to check your top competitors' brand pages and look for new brands and suppliers. Source from 2018: [L1274, 2018]
- Revenue figures that a store owner publicly reports are unverified and should be treated as a hint, not as proof. Verify such claims before using them to justify a model or a layout choice. [L1325, 2020-26]

### Domain Names and Store Setup

- Brainstorm domain ideas with an AI chat tool using a prepared prompt:
  1. Open the prompt document linked under the lesson.
  2. Copy everything from the 'role' line (the professional domain name researcher specializing in brandable e-commerce stores) to the end of the document.
  3. Replace the niche placeholder ('paste niche here') with your niche keyword, e.g. surfboards, horse saddles or chandeliers.
  4. Optionally replace the 'vibe' placeholder at the bottom with a style you want, such as premium, coastal, adventure, eco-friendly, tech or family; the vibe is not required.
  5. Paste the finished prompt into the AI tool and send it.
  6. Review the returned list and pick the names that look best.

  [L0521, 2020-26]
- The same domain prompt works in ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok, so any of these AI chat tools can be used for domain brainstorming. The prompt text itself is not included in the lesson transcript; only its structure is described here. [L0521, 2020-26]
- On Shopify one domain can only be linked as the primary domain of one store, so two stores cannot use the same domain. [L0656, undated]
- To build a new store from a proven one:
  1. Extract the Shopify theme from a successful dropship store.
  2. Create a new Shopify store for a related niche, such as free weights next to a treadmill store or home gyms.
  3. Upload the extracted theme to the new store.
  4. Change the logo to the new store's brand.
  5. Find suppliers that sell the products of that niche and upload them to the new store.

  [L0923, 2015]

### Catalogue Focus, Upsells and Featured Products

- Apply the 80/20 principle to the catalogue: in stores with many products, a small share of products usually drives most sales, such as 20% of products for 80% of results, or even 5% for 95% of sales. Upsell planning should focus only on that top group. [L0448, 2021]
- Plan upsells for the top products only, not for the whole catalogue. Example: with 500 products of which 20 sell regularly, plan for the 20. [L0448, 2021]
- Find your best sellers:
  1. Log into Shopify and run a report on most sold products, or use the equivalent report in Google Analytics.
  2. If you already know your best sellers from daily work, list them directly.

  [L0448, 2021]
- List up to 20 core products by exact product title, based on sales volume. If you have fewer, use 10 or 5; even a single product that makes up most of sales is enough to start. [L0448, 2021]
- The chosen unique mechanism should also support the story or thought command used earlier in the copy, so the whole text builds one line of argument. [L0465, 2021-22]
- Three ways to choose products for the featured block:
  1. Use trending products based on Shopify sales of the last 7 days.
  2. Check store reports for the most searched products or items.
  3. Pick products manually, such as a new product, a bundle of the week or any item you want to push.

  [L0475, 2026]

### Bonus and Bundle Ideas

- Find bonus and bundle ideas by looking at Amazon's 'Customers who bought this also bought' section on your product page, which shows what buyers actually purchase together with it. [L1293, 2014]

### Traffic Quality and Channels

- The most valuable visitors search for your brand names, exact product names or SKU numbers. The more specific the search, the higher the chance the person buys. [L0597, 2020-26]
- Cheap bulk visitor packages sold on freelance marketplaces, such as 20,000 niche visitors for $5 to $10, are not worth the money. Those visitors are not buyers, so the traffic is worth less than $5. [L0597, 2020-26]
- Traffic channels change over time, and many disappear or get renamed within a decade. Do not build the business on a single source; keep testing new channels and keep only those that stay profitable. [L0597, 2020-26]
- For products that people rarely search for on Google, do not start with Shopping ads because buyers do not know the product name to type. Such launches usually need top-of-funnel Facebook ads first. [L0510, 2018]
- Outreach should target websites whose audience is relevant to your target customers, your products, or their other interests. Relevance matters more than sheer link count. [L0565, 2021-22]
- A landing page should answer three questions quickly: what is the main benefit, why should the visitor trust the brand, and how is the risk of buying reduced. If it does not answer these, visitors with buying intent may leave before deciding. [L0918, 2023]

### Customer Questions and Product Knowledge

- Handling a product question from a customer:
  1. Acknowledge the question and tell the customer you will check with the manufacturer or supplier.
  2. Ask for the customer's phone number or email, depending on the channel they used (email, live chat or phone).
  3. Send the question to the brand or manufacturer.
  4. Relay the answer to the customer.
  5. Add the question and its answer to your website so future visitors find it.

  [L0769, 2020-26]
- Suggested reply to a customer question:

  ```
  That's a great question. Let me check with my manufacturer and I'll get right back to you. What's your phone number or email address so I can reach you?
  ```

  [L0769, 2020-26]
- Answering customer questions builds your knowledge of both the products and the industry over time. Even a lead who does not buy leaves you with an answer you can publish on your site. [L0769, 2020-26]

### Product Images and Creative Tools

- Finding royalty-free product images in Google Image search:
  1. Search for the niche keyword in Google.
  2. Click 'Images'.
  3. Click 'Tools'.
  4. Click 'Usage rights' (named 'usage rates' in the transcript).
  5. Choose 'Creative Commons licenses'.
  6. Scroll past the sponsored ads at the top, since the first row is paid.

  [L0964, 2023]
- Canva's Pro plan includes an AI image generator that can create product photos for any niche. It is only available on the paid plan, and a free trial (30 days in the transcript) can be used before cancelling. [L0964, 2023]

### SEO: Featured Snippets

- Keyword research for featured snippets, in order:
  1. In Ahrefs or Semrush, list all keywords where your site ranks in positions 1 to 4.
  2. Filter that ranking report by the featured snippet filter (available in Ahrefs; Ubersuggest does not offer it).
  3. For the filtered keywords, note which of your pages ranks in positions 1 to 4.
  4. Check the type of snippet Google shows (definition or list).

  [L0662, 2021-22]
