- Competitor ad links point to Meta Ad Library pages with these filters: active_status=all, ad_type=all, country=ALL, search_type=page and media_type=all. This shows every ad a competitor page has run, active or inactive, in all countries and media types [L1159, 2023+].
- The AfterLib column stores the AfterLib link for each product. It is an additional research source next to the competitor store, AliExpress and TikTok links. Keep all four links for a product in the same row for quick checking [L1159, 2023+].
- Build a product testing sheet in this order [L1162, undated]:
  1. Make your own copy of the shared template.
  2. Enter the product name in the first field.
  3. Paste the ad link, taken from the Facebook Ad Library or an ad-analysis tool, for inspiration.
  4. Paste the competitor's store link to study what they do and what you can improve.
  5. Paste the AliExpress link so you can source the product from CJ or your current supplier.
  6. Mark engagement as high or low and competition as high, medium or low, then note your findings.
  7. Mark market size as small, medium or large.
  8. Make the go/no-go decision (test or not).
  9. Enter COGS (cost of goods sold, i.e. what it costs to fulfil one order with your supplier).
  10. Enter your selling price and the competitor's selling price.
  11. Calculate the breakeven ROAS using the calculator linked at the top right of the sheet, entering COGS and selling price.
  12. Write the reason for your product choice in the mentor notes field.
  13. Send the list to your mentor so they can comment on each product.
- Keep a separate tab for Facebook products and another for TikTok products in the testing sheet, so each channel's test candidates are tracked apart. Use the 'my notes' section for personal notes on each product [L1162, undated].
- Use the product testing sheet as one overview of every product you have tested or plan to test, so you can track your progress. A shared sheet also lets a mentor scan the candidates and comment on each product while you are still researching [L1162, undated].
- Build a product list in a spreadsheet, with one sheet for each research session. Add each candidate with its active ad count and store link. Aim for about ten candidates per session, then test them in the following week [L1173, undated].

### one source's market idea tracker

- The working file for market idea research is a high ticket dropshipping market research tracker template, a spreadsheet where candidate market ideas are listed and later assessed for viability [L0996, 2021-22].
- The research tracker is a spreadsheet with one row per market idea. It is updated as each market idea is researched [L1486, 2021-22].
- Build a market research tracker with at least 20 market ideas before researching. Extra ideas are fine, but detailed research should not be run on all of them because it takes too long [L1329, 2021-22].
- In the first exercise, fill in only the Market Idea column and leave all other criteria columns empty until the later steps. Trying to research criteria during brainstorming slows down idea generation [L0996, 2021-22].
- The market research tracker is the single working document for the whole first phase. The idea list is filled in now, and the scoring criteria are filled in during later steps. Keep working in the same copy or file throughout [L0996, 2021-22].
- Record customer research in a spreadsheet rather than in multiple word-processing documents, because a spreadsheet is more flexible for comparing and updating customer data. Keep the research in one living file that the whole team can review [L0872, 2021-22].
- The Market Idea and Competition columns are both part of the template. The averaged competitor count is recorded for each market idea in the Competition column [L0896, 2021-22].

### Dream 100 tracker

- The Dream 100 tracker is a spreadsheet with columns for Name, Platform/Website, Online Forum, Facebook Group, Instagram, YouTube, Other Networks, Contact Details, Advertising Opportunity? and Notes. Start by filling in the target channels and names; contact details, advertising and notes can be filled in later [L0527, 2021-22].
- Dream 100 tracker column layout, one row per person or organization (Numbers or Google Sheets) [L0527, 2021-22]:

| NAME | PLATFORM/WEBSITE | ONLINE FORUM | FACEBOOK GROUP | INSTAGRAM | YOUTUBE | OTHER NETWORKS | CONTACT DETAILS | ADVERTISING OPPORTUNITY? | NOTES |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Notes on use [L0527, 2021-22]:
  - A person can appear in several channel columns.
  - The Advertising Opportunity column should contain a yes or no plus a link to the advertising page if one exists.
  - A forum or Facebook group name can be left blank until you join and see the admin.
