### Counting Keyword Volume with Ubersuggest

- Older advice. Ubersuggest requires a paid account for full search volume data. Use this procedure for every shortlisted market idea:
  1. Sign up for an Ubersuggest account to access full search volume data.
  2. Open the Keyword Ideas section of Ubersuggest.
  3. Review the market ideas on your research tracker and enter the main keyword for one idea, for example 4x4 accessories.
  4. Set the country in the country dropdown to the market you will sell in, such as English / United States, English / United Kingdom, English / Australia, English / Canada or Japanese / Japan.
  5. Click Search.
  6. Read the VOL column for the main keyword and hover over the volume to see the exact monthly searches.
  7. Go through the suggestion list and keep only keywords that match the products you would sell.
  8. Enter product-specific keywords, such as winches for trucks, to narrow results to the right product type.
  9. Check for regional wording, such as truck accessories in the US, and search those terms too.
  10. Add up the monthly volumes of all relevant keywords for the market idea.
  11. Enter the combined total in the Traffic Volume column of the research tracker.
  12. Repeat steps 3 to 11 for every shortlisted market idea. [L1486, 2021-22]

### Filtering Keywords

- Count a related keyword only if you could realistically sell that product. For example, winches for UTV could be counted, while winches for trailers should be excluded when the store sells truck accessories. Narrow broad terms with a qualifier such as for trucks to remove results that do not match your product. [L1485, 2021-22]
- Remove keyword variations that do not match what you sell or how you sell, such as local phrases like near me when the store is online. Only count terms that a buyer would type when looking for your products. [L1486, 2021-22]
- Broad keywords can show very large volumes but include unrelated uses, so they overstate the market. For example, the broad term winches shows about 60,000 monthly searches but includes trailer and other non-truck winches. Narrow the term to product-specific phrases before counting volume. [L1486, 2021-22]
- Leave out keywords that bring in products you would not sell, such as trailer winches. If a keyword is close but off-core, such as winches for UTVs, decide whether you could sell that product. To narrow results, add a product detail such as for trucks and search again. [L1486, 2021-22]

### Competition Signal in Keyword Tools

- Older advice. Ubersuggest keyword overviews show the average top-10 ranking page's backlinks and domain authority, and the domains that rank in Google for that term. Use this to see how many established sites already compete for the keyword before entering the niche. [L1485, 2021-22]
- Averages for the top-10 ranking page, US market at the time of recording:

| Keyword | Average backlinks | Average domain authority |
|---|---|---|
| 4x4 accessories | 536 | 56 |
| truck accessories | 513 | 59 |
| winches | 68 | 71 |
| bumpers for trucks | 36 | 55 |

  Compare these averages to see how strong the established sites already ranking for a keyword are before entering that niche. [L1485, 2021-22]

### Google Trends as a Complement

- Google Trends can show whether interest in a product is rising and how it compares to other search terms, for example to see whether one color is trending more than another. Use it alongside click data when deciding what to test. [L1508, 2022+]

#### Further details

- Target a niche with at least 10,000 average monthly searches; a single narrow product keyword with around 600 monthly searches is too low to build a store on. [L1113, 2022]
