### Digital products: validation and competitor research

#### Ebook product research

1. Look at what big brands sell and which problems they target.
2. Choose problems that buyers actively face, such as gut health, skin, dental health or joints in pets.
3. Build one ebook per problem.
4. Check that the solution helps before creating the full product. [L1131, 2018+]

- A recipe ebook for one problem should contain 15 to 20 recipes. With 20 recipes, a buyer using one recipe every second day has enough content for more than a month. [L1131, 2018+]

#### Niche and competitor validation for a digital product

1. Choose a broad niche with proven money potential that you understand.
2. Narrow it to a sub-niche with a specific problem people want solved, for example weight loss for men above 40, memory loss, anti-aging or gut health.
3. Find the top five competitors selling digital products in that sub-niche.
4. Analyse each competitor's offer, copy structure, headlines, price and guarantee.
5. Build a better offer with more value and a stronger angle.
6. Create the ebook cover first and sell before the ebook is written.
7. Once orders come in, create or source the ebook and fulfil it. [L0513, undated]

Find competitors by searching these places: [L0513, undated]

- ClickBank (rank top offers by gravity).
- Digistore24.
- Warrior Plus.
- The Facebook Ads Library (search the problem term to see who runs ads).
- TikTok (who promotes a product on the problem).
- Google (first two result pages).
- Twitter/X.

ClickBank is the largest affiliate marketing marketplace and shows the top offers by gravity. Open the offers to study how they name the problem and persuade the buyer. Use it to learn new offers and copy structure, never to copy content. [L0513, undated]

#### Pre-sale validation before writing the ebook

1. Create a book cover and a table of contents.
2. Run ads to an offer priced at 19 USD per ebook.
3. Only after people actually pay, write the full ebook, for example with ChatGPT.
4. As an alternative, buy a private label rights ebook from PLR Hustle, download it, add your own branding, and resell it.

[L1212, undated]

A low-cost ebook test at about 19 USD per book was run to see whether people spend money on the offer before the full product is created. [L1212, undated]

#### Pricing digital products for ads

- Before selling a product for ads, check the competitor price and the cost of acquiring one customer via Facebook ads, then price the product so the margin stays large enough. Example from the lesson: if one customer costs 13 dollars to acquire, do not price the ebook at 15. [L0513, undated]
- Example: a competitor selling an ebook on quitting gaming addiction charged 27 dollars and used a personal story on the sales page. The response was judged a good but improvable offer. [L0513, undated]

### Digital products: stock video and organic reach

- Digital products can be promoted by downloading free stock videos from Pexels, adding a quote on top, and posting them as short-form videos for organic reach. This aims at zero ad spend and zero fulfillment costs, but takes more time than paid ads. [L1439, 2024+]

Stock video steps: [L1439, 2024+]

1. Download a free video from Pexels that fits the product topic.
2. Add a short quote on top of the video that explains what the product helps with in which situation.
3. Upload the video as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
4. Post consistently to generate organic traffic.

### Digital product variants: monthly plans and white-label

- Custom phone icon packs can work as a digital product for an influencer's niche, with the designs commissioned from Fiverr. Offering a monthly plan that adds new icon sets every month or season turns a one-time sale into recurring revenue. [L0789, 2022]
- For a physical product in a specific niche, such as photography gear, a white-label item like a tripod can be branded with the influencer's name. This gives the influencer a product that fits their audience. [L0789, 2022]

### Print-on-demand designs

- Print-on-demand designs sell when they tie into a trend or a strong emotional interest, such as a football team. Use names, quotes, players or memes that suggest a team instead of official logos, to avoid trademark problems. [L1214, 2023+]

Pattern for trend-based slogans: [L1214, 2023+]

- Formula: take a well-known phrase or catchphrase from a popular show or trend, and change it to describe an everyday activity that many people share at the same time.
- Why it works: buyers recognize the reference and enjoy wearing a joke that matches their current situation, so the product sells during that moment.
- Examples in the same pattern: "Remote and rested" for people working from home, and "Snooze and bake" for people stuck indoors during a lockdown.

### Customized and personalized products

#### Customized jewelry and products

- Customizable products with multiple options (parent and child variants, personalization features) sell better because buyers perceive higher value. [L0209, 2023]
- Customizable products such as engraved jewelry, bracelets, necklaces and frames can perform well, especially on TikTok. Customization creates an emotional attachment for the buyer. [L0363, 2022+]
- Jewelry products require only still photography for ad creatives. Video content is optional. [L0209, 2023]
- Customizable jewelry performs well because it combines jewelry's photo-only requirement with personalization appeal. [L0209, 2023]
- Customized products sell better in a narrow niche than as generic items, for example jewelry tailored to a specific region or country, a specific religion, or a specific language. Pet products are another customization option. [L1017, undated]
- Customized jewelry tends to sell better when it targets a narrow group, such as a necklace featuring a specific country's map, a religious symbol, or text in one language. Choose the audience first, then design the piece around it. [L1017, undated]
- Customized or personalized products, such as gifts for a sister, mother or father, can work better than broad jewelry because they target a narrower buyer with less competition. Broad jewelry has many competitors and is harder to sell. [L1185, 2023]

#### Finding a customization niche

1. Search small, broad keywords such as "engraved", "custom", "bracelet" or "necklace" on the marketplace or research tool.
2. Repeat with other short keywords to surface more listings. [L0363, 2022+]

#### Production, delivery and beginner fit

- Customized products justify longer delivery times, so the product page can state 7-12 days or up to 7-15 business days. [L0363, 2022+]
- For made-to-order products like custom furniture, state the production lead time on the product page, for example four to six weeks. Customization options can be added with the Bold product options app, which has a 14-day free trial. [L0804, 2023]
- Custom-sized or custom-configured products sold via dropshipping work this way: the customer chooses options, the supplier builds the item to order, and then ships it to the customer. [L0805, 2024]
- Personalized products often sell well, but they are not recommended for complete beginners because they need more production effort and logistics to arrive undamaged. In the lesson's experience, personalized products work best in the US market. [L1172, 2023+]

The sources give both signals: customized gifts can outperform broad jewelry [L1185, 2023], but personalized production is not recommended for complete beginners [L1172, 2023+]. Start with a narrow customized niche only once production and logistics are under control.

### Category-specific warnings

- Before selling a smart home device such as a light-bulb security camera, confirm whether it needs a subscription, how footage is stored (for example an SD card slot for local playback), and what the companion app requires. Check these points in the product listing or the manufacturer's information so you can answer customer questions. [L0372, 2022]
- For electronics that the buyer uses on the body, order a sample and test it yourself before listing, or check the product description and manual for safety and performance. This prevents selling a poor-quality product that causes chargebacks and disputes. [L1218, undated]
- Gadget niches such as locks often get attention without converting. Treat them as low-priority tests unless you have seen them convert. [L1186, 2022]
- Ingredient-led supplements such as ashwagandha need extra scrutiny before testing. [L1187, 2026]
