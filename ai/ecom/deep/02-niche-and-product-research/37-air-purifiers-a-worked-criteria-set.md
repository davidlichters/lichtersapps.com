### Air purifiers: a worked criteria set

Competitor guides weigh tool-free filter changes, ozone emissions, upkeep costs over the unit's life, warranty length, weight and portability, and filter-change monitoring. Use these criteria to judge which brands are worth listing for buyers with allergies, asthma or chemical sensitivities. [L0937, 2021-22]

- A unit should exchange the room's air about 5 times per hour, with 2 air exchanges per hour as the minimum, depending on room size. A unit rated for a larger room than needed can run on lower speeds, which makes it quieter. [L0932, 2021-22; L0937, 2021-22]
- Room size is a major factor when choosing an air purifier, because the unit must exchange the room's air several times per hour. [L0937, 2021-22]
- A reliable warranty covers at least 5 years on the main fan and motor parts. The filters are the only part expected to need regular cleaning or replacement. [L0932, 2021-22; L0937, 2021-22]
- Any ozone emission from an air purifier is undesirable. It matters most for buyers with allergies, asthma or multiple chemical sensitivities, because ozone irritates the respiratory system. Filter out ozone-emitting models when selecting products for these buyers. [L0932, 2021-22; L0937, 2021-22]
- CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate), certified by AHAM, shows how much purified air a unit delivers and is used to compare air purifiers. Higher CADR figures indicate higher output for the same type of unit. [L0937, 2021-22]
- Combining gas or odour filtration and HEPA filtration in a single cartridge is criticised, because the two filter types degrade at different rates. Separate filters are generally preferred. [L0937, 2021-22]
- Products with filters that are easy to change without tools are preferred. The best designs let the user open a door, pull out the filter and insert a new one by hand. Filters that need a screwdriver, the unit turned upside down or are hard to reach are often changed late, past the recommended date, which reduces performance. [L0932, 2021-22; L0937, 2021-22]
- Upkeep cost is often overlooked by buyers. Some of the most expensive air purifiers cost less to maintain over their lifetime, and units without filters, such as some air sterilisers, have no filter cost at all. [L0932, 2021-22]

### Product idea examples and brainstorm inputs

- Product ideas can come from items visible in everyday life. Each brainstorm candidate is a candidate to test for a price of $200 or more. [L0474, 2020-26]
- Product ideas on the first brainstorm list are hypotheses, not confirmed winners. [L0474, 2020-26]
- Selection should be based on criteria, not on the example products. Apply the selection criteria rather than copying the examples. [L1111, 2020]
- Passion is not a selection criterion. Product ideas should be selected on the expected selling price. [L0474, 2020-26]

**Conflict: passion, understanding and personal use**

- Passion for a product is not a valid selection criterion, and it is not required. [L0474, 2020-26; L1088, 2020]
- Only sell products you believe in and would use yourself, because selling something you understand makes the marketing and customer answers easier. Spend a couple of weeks researching which brands are actually best before choosing a product. [L0910, 2015]
- Do not try to sell products you do not understand, because you will struggle to connect authentically with the product, create convincing marketing or answer customer questions. [L0280, undated]
- Choosing products only because you like them or would buy them is a mistake. Test for the average customer in your target market. A novelty window-mounted cat bed and phone cases sold little or nothing for one seller, because they had no wow factor and no culture around them. [L0549, undated]
- Choosing a product because of personal passion is not a valid selection criterion. Product ideas should be selected based on the expected selling price. [L0474, 2020-26]
- Niche selection should be based on research criteria and market knowledge, not on familiarity alone. [L1111, 2020]
- Products that are not sold in many stores but show strong ad counts and a clear niche can be tested. [L1176, 2023]
- Only sell products you would use or consume yourself. [L1171, 2024+]

Reconciling these: understanding the product and being able to use it is useful; passion and personal taste are not criteria. Judge a product by its problem, its price, its margin and the market's response. [L0280, undated; L0549, undated; L0577, 2023; L0910, 2015]

### Testing mindset and rules of thumb

- Pick a niche that meets the criteria and follow the process, because a perfect niche rarely exists. [L1112, 2021]
- Product quality matters more than virality. [L0372, 2022]
- Product selection matters more than platform specialisation. [L0349, 2024]
- Do not run products with no ad proof without a small test. Use image ads and friend and family checks first. [L1316, 2024+]
- Build the list of product ideas continuously; keep adding ideas from daily life. [L0474, 2020-26]
- Brand-new products with few sellers can sell with minimal marketing, but this depends on luck, so keep research as the main method. [L1158, 2023]
- Before testing a product, confirm it solves a problem and stands out. [L0950, 2023]
- Prefer products with only 2 to 3 variants. [L1174, undated]

### Problem-First Product Selection

1. Identify a group of customers who already buy from you. In the lesson's example, these were women over 45. [L1432, undated]
2. List the daily problems that group faces. [L1432, undated]
3. Search for a product that solves one of those specific problems. [L1432, undated]

- Example from the lesson: for a customer group that struggles with fitness, hair loss or weight, choose a product that addresses that specific problem. For women over 45 in Switzerland, emptiness and depression were noted as problems, and meditation-related products were considered as a response. [L1432, undated]
- A proven audience can be used to test products across several niches. For example, a winter clothing brand and an astrology-themed product were both aimed at women over 45. [L1432, undated]
- Choose test products by asking which daily problems the audience already has, not by copying random winning products. [L1432, undated]

### Conversion Before Traffic

- Traffic alone does not produce sales. A high-converting product is essential. [L1429, 2022]
- Products that appeal to emotions tend to trigger impulse buys. [L1429, 2022]

### Problem-Solving Versus Wow-Factor Products

#### Two Requirements for a Product Worth Testing

- A product worth testing needs two things: room to scale, and support for many engaging short videos. [L1444, 2022]
- A product can fit a popular category and still fail if it is poorly built or poorly structured. [L1444, 2022]

#### Wow-Factor Products

- A wow-factor product that is hard to buy locally can go viral quickly, because viewers assume the seller has exclusive access. [L1444, 2022]
- Once viewers discover that the product does not work as shown, conversion drops sharply and the product is exposed. [L1444, 2022]
- A wow factor only carries a product if the product actually delivers what the video shows. [L1444, 2022]
- Before you publish a store for a wow-factor item, check that it works properly. [L1444, 2022]
- A simple, common item that buyers can easily get themselves is hard to sell at a high price. Example: an ice product listed at about 17 to 18 USD. [L1444, 2022]

#### Problem-Solving Products

- Products that solve a problem, especially medical-type or health-related solutions, were described as the best performers. [L1444, 2022]
- Slight improvements on proven products are also worth testing. [L1444, 2022]

#### Quick Money-Grab Products

- Quick money-grab products may not solve a real problem but can generate a few thousand USD. [L1444, 2022]
- Use them as a reasonable test to practise hooks, angles and video production before testing a product with higher potential. [L1444, 2022]

| Proven product, slight improvement | Worth testing | When a proven product already sells | [L1444, 2022] |

### Quality, Safety and Red Flags

- Check product quality with extra care for beauty and health products. A poor product in these categories can harm the customer and creates a red flag. [L1436, undated]
- Burns, poor quality, or a product that stops working after a few uses spread quickly through comments and buyer discussion. [L1444, 2022]
- Once buyers realise a product does not work, conversion can drop sharply within days. [L1444, 2022]

### Product Types to Favour

#### Economy-Driven Choices

- Choose products by the economy. In a cost-of-living crisis, people go out less and spend more at home. [L1440, 2021+]
- In that period, home cooking, home entertainment and kids' board games sold well, and TikTok cooking content performed well. [L1440, 2021+]

#### Household and Seasonal Items

- Household items were described as strong sellers in general. [L1444, 2022]
- A product priced too low may not carry a whole store, but it can work as an upsell. [L1444, 2022]
- Sunny locations near water were mentioned as a helpful factor for certain beach or summer products. [L1444, 2022]

#### Established Branded Products

- Start with established branded products that already sell in your target market, not with new untested products. [L1480, 2021-22]
- New products cost more, take more time, and carry a much higher risk of failure. [L1480, 2021-22]
- The wow-factor and quick money-grab guidance above ([L1444, 2022]) is more experimental. Use [L1480, 2021-22] when choosing the core product for a store in a known market, and use the experimental tests on a small scale to practise and find new angles. [L1480, 2021-22; L1444, 2022]

### Cross-Sell and Bundle Candidates

#### Jewellery

- Choose cross-sell products that are highly relatable and easy to explain with one image. [L1410, 2023]
- Jewellery is one of the best niches for collection and bundle offers, if each product is simple to understand from a single photo. [L1410, 2023]
- Simple one-image pieces suit bundle and cross-sell offers. [L1412, 2023]
- Designs tied to meaning or feeling need more explanation and can lower conversion when cross-sold. [L1412, 2023]

| Design type | Cross-sell suitability | Units |

#### Further details

- Do not test branded products; focus on unbranded or generic alternatives. [L0209, 2023]

- Local-brand products are already sold on the market, are tested, and are often trusted brands with histories of 20 years or more. Choosing such products lowers the risk compared with selling new, unknown items. [L0458, 2021-22]
