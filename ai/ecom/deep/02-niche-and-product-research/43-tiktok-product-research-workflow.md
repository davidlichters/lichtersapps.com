### Kalodata and FastMoss Research

- FastMoss scans TikTok Shop and Shopify data and shows trending products with real sales numbers. It replaces hours of manually scrolling TikTok to find what sells in a niche. [L1459, 2026]
- Keep product research to two tools: Kalodata to find products with rising revenue, and Meta Ad Library to confirm that other advertisers run ads for them. Use only the filters you need, such as Revenue Uptrend and Category, rather than stacking many filters. [L1479, 2026]

#### Kalodata Setup

- In Kalodata, set Dates to Last 30 Days and the region to United States. Then add the Revenue Uptrend filter under Revenue Filters to show products whose revenue is growing, and use Category to compare niches. [L1479, 2026]
- Sort Kalodata's video list by Publish Date (New) to surface recently published videos. This shows which creatives are working now rather than what worked months ago. [L1479, 2026]
- Kalodata's video lists show GPM next to Revenue and Views. GPM is revenue per 1,000 views, so compare videos by GPM when their view counts differ widely. [L1479, 2026]

#### Kalodata Product Research Procedure

1. Set the region to United States and Dates to Last 30 Days. [L1479, 2026]
2. Add the Revenue Uptrend filter under Revenue Filters to surface growing products. [L1479, 2026]
3. Browse the Category list to narrow the niche you want. [L1479, 2026]
4. Do not stop at the first page of results. Check pages 4 to 5 for products that are selling but have fewer sellers. [L1479, 2026]
5. Open the product detail and check Avg. Unit Price, Creator Number, Commission Rate, Shipping Option and Launch Date. [L1479, 2026]
6. Open the top videos of the product and study the creatives that already sell. [L1479, 2026]
7. Confirm the product has active ads on Meta before you commit to it. [L1479, 2026]

#### Kalodata Product Detail Fields

| Field | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Product Rank in United States | Position of the product in the US ranking |
| Avg. Unit Price ($) | Average selling price |
| Is Affiliate Product | Whether creators sell it as an affiliate |
| Creator Number | How many creators promote the product |
| Creator Conversion Ratio | How well creators convert viewers into buyers |
| Commission Rate | Affiliate commission offered |
| Shipping Option | Shipping method listed for the product |
| Launch Date | When the product started selling |
| Revenue Source (Content) and Revenue Source (Channel) | Where the revenue comes from |

#### Reading Rankings

- The first page of a ranking usually shows saturated products that many sellers already run. Look at the first pages only to see what works, then go deeper for products with a growing trend, people actively searching for them, and fewer advertisers. [L1479, 2026]

### Viral Account Vetting

- Two accounts that share a website domain, have similar follower counts and post on the same day suggest split testing or paid ads on both. Look for linked accounts before assuming one account's results are organic. [L1385, undated]
- A viral account can pivot: the account may privately hide or delete its old videos, change its name and domain and switch to a new product while keeping the same website. Track these changes before assuming the product that went viral is still the one selling. [L1385, undated]

#### Viral Account Verification Procedure

1. Find the viral account and note its website domain and product. [L1385, undated]
2. Compare its upload history: count total uploads, check dates and look for uploads that do not fit a normal pattern. [L1385, undated]
3. Search for a second account that links to the same domain, has similar followers or posts on the same dates. [L1385, undated]
4. Place a dummy test order on the website and record the order number and the sales it implies. [L1385, undated]
5. Place a second dummy order about 24 hours later and compare the sales estimate with the first. [L1385, undated]
6. Check the store for other products and look for products that are reachable only through a direct URL. [L1385, undated]
7. Buy from another account running the same product, so you have at least two reference points. [L1385, undated]
8. Only then decide whether the product is worth testing, and do not commit budget before the checks are complete. [L1385, undated]

### Facebook Feed Research

- Beauty and health products aimed at women are riskier on Facebook, which often bans these niches if the ad account profile is not solid. Test such niches with open targeting from the start and read the gender and demographic breakdown in Facebook Ads Manager to decide whether to narrow the audience. [L1440, 2021+]
- Use Meta Ad Library to confirm that other advertisers run ads for the product before committing. [L1479, 2026]

### Pinterest and YouTube Research

#### YouTube Keyword Discovery

- For a surfboard seller, YouTube auto-suggest for the core term 'surfboards' returned variations by skill level (beginners, intermediate), fin setup (two fins, long fins), use case (roof racks, big waves, small waves), body type (big guys), and reviews. Each variation can be a separate video target because people already search for it. [L1511, 2021-22]
- Brand-based YouTube keywords combine the brand, the product, and a review term, such as '[brand] [product] review' or '[brand] [product] reviews'. If you stock a specific brand, a review video targeting these phrases matches searches from people already interested in that brand. Add these phrases to the keyword research tracking spreadsheet alongside the generic terms. [L1511, 2021-22]

### Virality Versus Real Sales

- Viral views can cause sellers to believe a product works when it does not. The lesson recommends checking sales data across several accounts before copying a viral product, since many sellers reportedly lost time and money on a weak converter. [L1385, undated]
- Products that worked well on paid Facebook ads can be tested organically on TikTok and sometimes perform strongly, so test products from several sources rather than relying only on TikTok discovery. [L1444, 2022]
- Sort by recent publish date and compare GPM rather than raw views to judge which creatives convert now. [L1479, 2026]
