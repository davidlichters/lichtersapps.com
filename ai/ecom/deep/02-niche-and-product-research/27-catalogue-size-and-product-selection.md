### Catalogue size and product selection

- Upload only the products you want to sell, even if a supplier offers hundreds. A supplier with 100 products may have only 10 you need; uploading all dilutes niche focus. [L0274, 2023] (2023)
- Do not upload all products from a supplier's list. Upload only the products that fit your niche and are profitable. [L0671, 2021]
- Minimum product count per store: at least 100 products; no defined maximum. [L0969, 2022]
- Aim for as many sellable products in the niche as the market supports, not for a fixed number. About 300 products can be enough to launch, but more should be added whenever the niche has thousands of products available. [L0962, 2021]
- Start a new store with about 10 products, then test them one by one rather than building a large list. Products that are not yet proven can be kept as a collection on the store. [L0661, undated]
- Conflict on launch size: about 10 products to start and test one by one [L0661, undated]; about 300 to launch when the niche supports it [L0962, 2021]; at least 100 products per store as the minimum [L0969, 2022]. Use 10 to start and test if proof is still missing, and the larger counts when the niche is verified and the supply supports it.
- Prioritizing products when there are many supplier products:
  1. Go to the top future competitors' sites and open the category or subcategory. [L0666, 2024] (2024)
  2. Sort products by best sellers. [L0666, 2024] (2024)
  3. Upload the top 20 best sellers first, from brands you are approved to sell, then add the rest over time. [L0666, 2024] (2024)
- Adding products to a store increases the chance of finding new winners that may be more profitable than the current winning product. Do not rely on one or two winners; keep expanding the offer and adding products. [L0434, undated]
- Each new category can be tested as a separate product with its own ads. Any category that performs well can become an additional winner to scale on its own. [L0434, undated]
- A broader product range increases the chance of finding a new winning product that can outperform the current best seller. Stores should keep adding products instead of relying on one or two winners. [L0435, undated]
- Where a niche has a wide price range, sell the tiers under one store. [L1112, 2021]
- Check whether product models differ in a meaningful way, such as size, power or technology, before building separate pages for them. Models that differ only in color rarely justify their own category page. [L0949, 2021-22]
- If suppliers and products are sold as a pair or set, upload them as sold, with the photos showing the pair or set. [L0671, 2021]
- In niches where buyers want matching sets, such as furniture, offer complete sets rather than individual pieces, because buyers prefer to purchase the matching items together. [L0663, 2019]
- Choose the collection list before creating products. Build the collection list from competitor stores before creating anything in Shopify:
  1. Open two to three future competitor stores. [L0371, 2020-26]
  2. Hover over the main categories to see their subcollections. [L0371, 2020-26]
  3. Note how they split the category, for example by type, by brand or by feature. [L0371, 2020-26]
  4. Open a document in Google Drive, Notes or similar. [L0371, 2020-26]
  5. Write each main collection first and indent its subcollections below it. [L0371, 2020-26]
  6. Keep the list short at the start and skip brand collections until suppliers are approved. [L0371, 2020-26]

### Demo products before supplier contact

- Demo store preparation: 5 to 10 demo products are sufficient before contacting suppliers. More than 50 products is excessive and delays supplier outreach. [L0249, 2022]
- Demo products only need to look professional: 4 to 5 products with complete pages, no blank images and no dummy text. Full catalogs are not needed before contacting suppliers. [L1085, 2018]
- Conflict on demo count: 5 to 10 products are sufficient [L0249, 2022], and 4 to 5 per collection is the working limit [L0452, 2021-22; L1085, 2018]. Both stop well short of a full catalogue. Use 4 to 5 when you need the fewest pages, and up to 10 if you want more coverage before supplier contact.
- Demo products are added in the Shopify admin under Products in the left sidebar. The Products page opens the product list; the **Find products** button is not useful for this task and can be ignored. To add one, click **Add product**, which opens the product editor. [L0452, 2021-22]
- Find demo products from another store that sells the same product type but carries brands you do not plan to target, so suppliers on your list do not see their own products on your site. A supplier seeing its own products can create awkwardness. [L0452, 2021-22]
- Demo product procedure:
  1. Open your initial supplier list from the earlier step. [L0452, 2021-22]
  2. Turn on a VPN and set it to a country that is not your target market. [L0452, 2021-22]
  3. Run a Google search for the product type you plan to sell. [L0452, 2021-22]
  4. Pick a store from the results that carries brands not on your supplier list. [L0452, 2021-22]
  5. Choose 4 to 5 products from that store's collection for your demo products. [L0452, 2021-22]
- A VPN is used to search from another country so that Google returns e-commerce stores from that market. The video recommends NordVPN and notes that many other VPNs can be found through a Google search for good VPN. [L0452, 2021-22]
- Choose the VPN country as a market where the brand is sold but not in your own country. For a store in Australia, set the VPN to the US and look for brands sold only there. For a US store, use Australia, the UK or a European country. This way there is no confusion with local suppliers about the products. [L0452, 2021-22]
- Upload only products that fit one of the product-type collections you already created. Choose one collection and upload 4 to 5 demo products for it, not more than that. [L0452, 2021-22]
- Before supplier contact, the demo store is a sample of the look and feel, not the catalogue. Use it to show suppliers the store style and the product type. [L1088, 2020] [L0249, 2022]

### After approval

**Starting the build**

- Approval is the signal to start: a green light means you start the store build and supplier contact. [L0360, 2024] (2024)
- If a niche passes all checks and you have confirmed it, move on to store building. [L1080, 2020-26]
- Once you have confirmed the niche or completed the submission, move on to store building. [L1080, 2020-26]
- If a niche passes every validation test, proceed to store building without additional validation. [L0058, undated]
- Once the niche is verified, the next step is the store-building step, where the store is built. [L0058, undated]
- After confirmation, the niche becomes the store niche. Store build and supplier contact follow the collection list in the procedure above. [L0360, 2024; L0371, 2020-26]
- Niche verification requests on the one source course come back with the approved product. Wait for that ticket result, which names the approved product, before starting any store work. [L0491, 2026] (2026)
- Once a niche is confirmed, a new product type within that niche does not require a new verification. [L0666, 2024] (2024)

**Using the verdict**

- If the verdict is a pivot, use the suggested related niche. The reviewer either confirms the niche or suggests a related niche that fits the selection criteria better. [L0970, 2023] (2023)
- Use the verdict and reasons when stuck between several viable candidates. [L1083, 2024+]
- A niche that passes all criteria should not be dropped because of saturation rumors or a recession. [L0804, 2023] (2023)
- A niche that passes the research can still be a base hit, a double or a grand slam. Early results show profitability; scale becomes clear only after months of running it. [L0806, 2024] (2024)
- Higher demand does not automatically mean higher results. The actual outcome only shows after the store is built. [L1079, 2020-26]

**Brand, reviews and affiliate content**

- Bulk-uploading manufacturer reviews is acceptable if a supplier agrees in writing that you can use their product reviews on your store. You can then bulk-upload reviews from the manufacturer's website in one day without risk of Google account suspension. Always confirm supplier permission first. [L0253, 2024] (2024)
- Affiliate links belong in the blog or content section of a store, not on product pages. Only link to directly related products that the store does not sell, such as a heating pad alongside a massage chair, or a drum lesson subscription alongside a keyboard. [L0472, 2025] (2025)
- Affiliate examples for related niches: a gun safe store can promote a firearms safety subscription; a keyboard store can promote online drum lessons. [L0472, 2025] (2025)
- The first filter and the collection list (see above) set the store structure before the first products go live. [L0890, 2020-26; L0371, 2020-26]

### Comparing graded niches

- A long candidate list is a reason to stop researching and choose, not to keep searching. With 100 or more candidates, assume at least 20 are viable, then choose one you feel confident about. [L1274, 2018]
- Prefer a narrower sub-niche over a broad one when it passes all criteria. Dining sets, for example, convert better than whole home furniture if they pass these tests. [L1279, 2021]

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Demand | Enough demand [L1279, 2021] |
| Seasonality | Evergreen [L1279, 2021] |
| Competition | Low competition [L1279, 2021] |
| Brands | At least 20 brands [L1279, 2021] |
| Products | At least 100 products across those brands [L1279, 2021] |

- Focus on one specific niche rather than a broad market. A clear niche makes the offer and ads more relevant. A jewelry brand that focused on a single cultural or geographic theme connected better with its audience. [L1439, 2024+]
- Before a final decision, fill in both the recurring revenue answer and the traffic volume total for each shortlisted market idea. The traffic volume check is the last research step for the shortlist. [L1486, 2021-22]

### Choosing the first niche

- Launch one niche first. Start a second niche only after the first is profitable, and reinvest profit into it. A niche with much higher search volume and a niche with much higher prices can both become successful stores over time. [L1275, 2019]
- A store built around one motif or theme across several product types can work if buyers of one item are likely to buy related items. It is less focused than a single high-ticket niche store, so it needs clear categories and strong differentiation. [L1325, 2020-26]

### Submitting for external verification

- Submit a niche for verification only after you have completed every niche research step, including validating it against all criteria. The verification team typically responds within about 24 hours. [L1275, 2019]
- Submit the niche idea through the 'get your niche verified' section, which is linked from the sidebar of any blueprint lesson in the members area. Wait for feedback before ordering a store build (2024). [L1115, 2024]
- Niche Verification is a tool shared in the September 2025 coaching call for checking a niche before committing to it. [L1286, 2025]
- Shortlist size differs between sources:

| Source | Shortlist size | Notes | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | Two or three niches, after your own research is done | Submitting two is fine if you are unsure which one to pick | [L1279, 2021] |
| 2022 | One or two ideas | Collect ideas through the research criteria first | [L1281, 2022] |

The sources do not say when each range applies. Both require your own research to be complete before you submit. [L1279, 2021; L1281, 2022]

**Verification procedure:**

1. Collect a handful of niche ideas through the research criteria. [L1281, 2022]
2. Open the 'get your niche verified' item in the members area sidebar. [L1281, 2022]
3. Submit one or two ideas for review. [L1281, 2022]
4. Read the feedback. It either confirms the niche or recommends a pivot with reasons, and reviewers can give personal feedback on each idea. [L1281, 2022]

### Niche-specific store rules

- Build niche-specific stores around one specialized product category rather than a general store that sells everything. Core products are the main niche products the store is built around. [L1147, 2020-26]
- Avoid general stores that sell random items with no shared identity. Focus each store on one niche where the audience is passionate and willing to spend. [L1131, 2018+]
- Two sources give different rules on how many niches a store should carry:

| Source | Rule | When it applies | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 | A mixed-category store is acceptable at launch. Once you know your best sellers, split them into a dedicated store, if enough search volume exists for that narrower category. | Launch stage | [L1274, 2018] |
| 2022 | One niche per store is the rule. Expand into a second niche only when you have to niche up to reach enough suppliers. | Store structure in general | [L1281, 2022] |

- More focused stores usually convert better, so segmentation is worth doing as long as the search volume for each segment supports it. [L1274, 2018]

### After approval

- Once the niche feedback confirms your niche, you can order the store build. If the feedback recommends a pivot, the reasons are given with it. [L1115, 2024; L1281, 2022]
- Product approval comes before store building. It prevents the costliest early mistake, which is investing in a store and ads around a weak product. Run four checks on each shortlisted product: margin, demand, competition and timing. [L1452, 2026]

**Product approval workflow:**

1. Complete the product research module. [L1452, 2026]
2. Submit the top product picks to the team through a support ticket. [L1452, 2026]
3. The team checks margins, demand, competition and timing. [L1452, 2026]
4. The team answers either go-ahead or suggests a better product. [L1452, 2026]
5. Do not build the store until the product is approved. [L1452, 2026]
