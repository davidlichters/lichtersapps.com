## Finding Products on TikTok and Social Platforms

### Where to Search and Why

- TikTok shows products while they are already viral, which is faster than waiting for research tools to list them. The trade-off is the time you spend browsing every day. [L0372, 2022]
- Find product ideas on TikTok before paid research tools, because many winning products appear there first. Products that performed well in Facebook ads can also be tested organically. [L1380, 2022]
- Use TikTok to spot trending products, then check that the product fits the target audience's needs. A trend alone is not enough. [L0492, 2023+]
- Going viral is the first step. It is harder and more expensive to achieve through ads than organically. Once viral reach exists, the time spent finding a winning product is better spent. [L0764, 2022]
- Facebook users are usually not in active buying mode, so targeting and creatives carry more weight there. Problem-solving products generally perform better on Facebook than on TikTok. [L0708, undated]
- Test a product on both Facebook and TikTok before dismissing it. Facebook ads are not limited to older users, because they also run on Instagram, which has a younger audience than Facebook itself. [L0995, 2023]
- Match the platform to where your audience asks questions rather than where most ads run. Test with a small budget before scaling. [L0637, 2023+]
- Products recommended by YouTube channels are often not saturated, because most viewers only watch and never implement. About one in ten viewers actually runs ads on the recommended product. [L1184, 2023+]
- Reverse-engineering the TikTok feed is a method of its own. [L1219, undated]

#### Reverse-engineering TikTok for products

1. Shape the TikTok feed toward dropshipping content.
2. Note products that perform well there but have not been widely used on Facebook or Instagram.
3. Test those products on Facebook and Instagram.
4. Reuse the TikTok videos as UGC where possible. [L1219, undated]

### Finding Candidates on TikTok

#### Searches and hashtags

- Search "TikTok Made Me Buy It" in the TikTok search bar. Use the "Top" tab, not the hashtag tab, because the hashtag tab does not let you filter out recent uploads. [L0706, 2020+]
- The hashtag "Amazon Finds" works like "Made Me Buy It" and can surface AliExpress products that can be dropshipped. Filter results by time range as in other TikTok product searches. [L0706, 2020+]
- Giveaways and similar viral content attract a young audience, and TikTok can keep showing your videos to younger viewers. Those viewers rarely buy, which can lower conversion. [L0706, 2020+]
- Watch daily which products get unusually high views on other accounts. When a product shows this traction, act quickly and move to testing before the trend fades. [L0821, undated]
- Look for decoration items with a "wow factor". They have viral potential, and niche products such as viral lamps achieve significant traction alongside paid advertising. [L0209, 2023]

#### Made Me Buy It: filter procedure

1. Search "TikTok Made Me Buy It" in the TikTok search bar.
2. Select the "Top" tab, not the hashtag tab, because the hashtag tab does not let you filter out recent uploads.
3. Open the filter settings and set the time range to "This month", "This week", or "Last 3 months". The 3-month range can reveal older products that are still selling.
4. Sort by "Most liked" only as an option, because good products can have few likes.
5. Use the phone app for the filter, since the filter is not available on desktop. [L0706, 2020+]

- Do not rely only on the most-liked results. Strong products are often posted with few likes, so review lower-engagement posts too. [L0706, 2020+]

#### Free discovery with a research account

- Use a new TikTok account created without your main account to search for terms such as "dropshippers", "Etsy finds" and "Amazon finds". This is the free method; spy tools are the paid alternative for the same goal. [L1382, undated]
- Searching exposing-dropshipper content can reveal products that have already gone viral with lower visibility. Those videos often get modest views, but the product videos themselves can reach millions. [L1384, 2022]
- Engage with every product video in your feed so the For You Page shows more organic product content over time. [L1384, 2022]

#### Free discovery procedure (exposing-dropshipper search)

1. Search TikTok for keywords such as "Amazon finds", "TikTok made me buy it", or "exposing dropshippers" to find product videos.
2. Filter results by upload date to see the most recent week or month, which surfaces newer products.
3. Open the product videos, follow the tagged product link or search the product name on AliExpress.
4. Engage with every product video in your feed so the For You Page shows more organic product content over time. [L1384, 2022]

#### Dummy research account procedure

1. Create a second account on the same phone. You do not need a new device.
2. Do not upload any videos from this account.
3. Search for keywords such as "exposing dropshippers" and "Amazon finds".
4. Like and comment on the product videos that appear.
5. Repeat for a few hours to a few days until the For You page shows mostly product content and dropshipping ads.
6. Scroll this feed during product research to spot products that show up. [L0706, 2020+]

#### Free product search (broad method)

1. Use a mobile phone, preferably, or a computer.
2. On TikTok, search for these phrases: "TikTok made me buy it" / "Amazon finds" / "50% off".
3. While browsing the feed, look for videos showing products that have more than 5 million views.
4. Search the product name on TikTok and identify the biggest accounts posting about it.
5. Search for those accounts on Instagram, or go to their website and find their Instagram link.
6. Check whether the product has gone viral on Instagram or whether those accounts post on Instagram. If neither is true, that is a good sign.
7. Search for the product on AliExpress using the names people use online, so you can source it. [L1367, undated]

#### Personal research account

1. Create a personal TikTok account that is set up only for product research.
2. Search TikTok directly for the product or product idea.
3. Check whether any account has had a viral video with it, since a viral result from up to a year ago still shows that the product can work.
4. If you find only paid ads and no organic viral examples, treat the product as unproven. [L0372, 2022]

- A product uploaded a year ago that got little attention is not automatically dead. A product that has never shown viral traction anywhere on TikTok deserves more caution. Treat previous attempts as information, not as a final verdict. [L0372, 2022]
- Older products with no dominant account can be revived if the video quality is improved. A product from a few months or a year ago can still be worth testing. [L1380, 2022]

#### Seasonal and gift products

- Use the TikTok search for "mother's day gift" or "shop now", open the hashtag page, and filter to this week. [L1379, 2020+]
- Seasonal gift products can be found by checking products that performed well in past seasons on TikTok, and by using the hashtags "link in bio" and "shop now" to find trending products. [L1379, 2020+]
- A novel, hard-to-demonstrate product such as a glowing toy can struggle alone. Comparison videos, such as comparing it with a well-known competitor, performed well in this case. [L1379, 2020+]
- A proven seasonal video format can be reused for a new holiday by keeping its structure and hook and changing only the theme and product visuals. [L1186, 2022]
- For a gift-focused test, consider following only gift-related content on a new account, since the feed fills with product posts within about a day. [L1379, 2020+]

#### Hashtag and Instagram sourcing

- Target products that already sell well as short videos on TikTok but have not yet gone viral on Instagram. Focus first on fashion, beauty, entertainment and sports products, and read the comments under winning videos to spot trends and community demand. [L1367, undated]
- Search Instagram by hashtag, for example #product-name or #weighted-hula-hoop, to discover real user photos and videos. Filter for clear product shots, authentic user context and good lighting. [L0321, undated]
- On Instagram, search several variants of the product term, such as "pet hair cleaner" and "dog hair removal", because different wording returns different accounts. Keep accounts with real followings and posts, and skip those with few followers or no content. [L0450, 2022+]

### Creative Center

#### TikTok Creative Center procedure

1. Open TikTok Creative Center from TikTok Business Center.
2. Scroll to the trending hashtags and choose an industry filter that matches your product, for example "Sports & Outdoors" or "Travel".
3. For trending songs, set the time range to "Last 30 days" and the country to "United States" if you are targeting the US.
4. Scroll past the songs and click "View more" to reach the top-performing ads.
5. Choose an industry such as "Apparel & Accessories" and set the duration to "Last 30 days" or "Last 7 days".
6. For e-commerce ads, select "E-commerce", choose "Non-app", and select all.
7. Scroll through the ads and note products that match AliExpress dropshipping listings. [L0706, 2020+]

### Kalodata

#### Access and data coverage

- Kalodata's Claude integration runs through the third-party tool 2Suite, which costs 39.99 USD per month and does not allow CSV export. An enterprise Kalodata subscription gives API access and can be integrated with an AI tool directly. [L0354, 2026]
- Kalodata's enterprise data covers 365 days, so trends can be selected for the last 30, 90, 180 or 365 days. [L0354, 2026; L1187, 2026]
- Kalodata product pages show an earliest record date for their data (07/25/2025 in one example). Trends for a product cannot be checked further back than that date. [L0333, 2026]
- Toosuite is a third-party platform that bundles several research tools, such as spy tools and CapCut-style editing tools, and is used alongside Kalodata. Kalodata remains the main data source for the product research described here. [L1175, 2026]
- Kalodata is a TikTok Shop product research tool that shows revenue, units sold, growth rate, creator count, commission rate, shipping fee and video ads for each product. Its Explore Category, Top New Products and Video & Ad views are used to find and inspect candidates. [L1187, 2026]

#### Product detail page: metrics

- The product detail page shows the product image, price, commission rate, shipping fee and rating. In the example, a $17.99 book had a 15% commission rate, a $0.00 shipping fee and a 4.7/5 rating. The same page can be used to pick a product and copy its image for cover editing. [L0333, 2026]
- The product detail page shows the category path, the number of product SKUs, a "View on TikTok" button and a "Generate Promo Video" button. For books the category path can read Books, Magazines and Audio, then Literature and Art, then Literature and Fiction. [L0333, 2026]
- The product detail page shows revenue and revenue per day, items sold, video revenue, product card revenue, live revenue, creator count, creator conversion ratio, concentration rate, commission rate, shipping fee and the earliest recorded date. [L0354, 2026]
- Kalodata is used to see a product's revenue, items sold, average unit price, review count and rating, plus the video and ad items linked to it, filtered by country and a 30, 90 or 180 day window. In the sample page the rating was 4.2 out of 5 from about 3,400 reviews, and the category was Beauty and Personal Care > Skincare > Moisturizers and Mists. [L0707, 2026]
- To check a TikTok Shop product's performance, use the product detail page with a chosen window, such as the last 30 days: revenue, items sold, creator conversion ratio, average unit price, live revenue and concentration rate. It also shows commission rate, shipping fee, rating and linked videos. [L1188, 2026]
- Kalodata's product view shows revenue, items sold, launch date, revenue growth rate, average unit price, creator number, revenue split by source (video, ad, livestream), highest-revenue videos and revenue trend. Filters for category, date, country, revenue, growth rate and price narrow the list. [L1175, 2026]
- The product Overview splits total revenue into Live Revenue, Video Revenue, Product Card revenue and Interactive Mode revenue. In the NAD+ drink mix example, live revenue was far above video revenue over 30 days, so check which channel actually sells before choosing which ad style to copy. [L0901, 2026]
- The product page shows "The earliest date recorded", which tells you how long the product has been tracked on TikTok. An older date means a longer selling history in the data. In the drink mix example it was 01/10/2025. [L0901, 2026]

#### Product detail page: tabs

- Product pages are split into tabs: Overview, Creator, Sales Strategy, Reviews, Video & Ad and Live. Use Video & Ad for the library of ad videos and Overview for revenue and items sold. [L0901, 2026]
- Product detail pages are split into tabs for linked Video & Ad content, Creators, Sales Strategy and Review Insights. Check these tabs to see how a product is marketed before you commit to building a store. [L1249, 2026]
- Listings for TikTok Shop products can carry a "Ship From Local" label, which indicates the product is stocked in the local market. Treat it as a signal of faster delivery when comparing products for a store in that market. [L1249, 2026]

#### Filters, date windows and item-sold buckets

- Date presets for product metrics are Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Last 180 days and Last 365 days. The Item Sold filter offers the buckets All, under 100, 100 to 1k, 1k to 10k and Max. [L1187, 2026]
- The date selector for a product offers Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days and Last 180 Days. Use the same window when comparing products. [L0901, 2026]
- The Video & Ad list shows each ad with views and sales, with a time filter of Last 30 Days, Last 90 days and Last 180 days. Use Last 30 Days to find current winners. [L0704, 2026]
- Kalodata's Explore view has tabs such as "Top New Products" and "High Potential Affiliate". Product lists can be sorted by revenue, items sold, revenue growth rate and average unit price. Each product has an "Is Affiliate Product" column that shows whether it can be promoted through affiliates. [L0333, 2026]

#### Category and market structure

- Kalodata product pages list a category path and a price range, for example Health > Nutrition & Wellness > Beauty Supplement for the NAD+ drink mix. Use the category to find related products whose ads you can study for hooks and testimonials. [L0901, 2026]
- On a category page, read Revenue Growth Rate, Top 3 Shop Revenue Ratio, Top 10 Shop Revenue Ratio, Number of Shops and Average Revenue per Shop together. In the Beauty & Personal Care L1 example for the last 30 days, the top 3 shops held 10.46% and the top 10 held 19.80% of revenue. Low shares suggest many sellers share the market; high shares suggest a few shops dominate. [L0901, 2026]
- The "Save selected filters" button on a category page keeps your filter combination for reuse. [L0901, 2026]

#### Health product filter procedure

1. In Kalodata, change the category to Health.
2. Set the revenue range to 10,000 to 45,000 dollars and press Apply.
3. Set Revenue Source (Content) to Video and Revenue Source (Channel) to Affiliate.
4. Set Revenue Growth Rate to at least 30 percent.
5. Press Submit.
6. Skip the first five result pages and start the product review on page 6.
7. Check the Last 30 Days and Last 90 Days revenue graphs and the video ads for each product that passes the filters. [L1187, 2026]

- Filter panel values for this Health research: Category = Health; Revenue = 10k to 45k dollars; Revenue Source (Content) = Video; Revenue Source (Channel) = Affiliate; Revenue Growth Rate = minimum 30 percent; date range = Last 30 days. A Creator Conversion Ratio filter with a threshold above 30 percent is also available in the same panel. [L1187, 2026]
- Products that rise steadily without dips are less interesting than products whose revenue graph goes up and down. A fluctuating curve with recovering peaks suggests real, repeated demand rather than a single spike. [L1187, 2026]
- Skipping the first five pages of filtered results is a deliberate way to avoid the most obvious, heavily tested winners and find less crowded products. Review results from page 6 onward. [L1187, 2026]
- Products whose ads are TikTok Shop videos that rely on the TikTok Shop platform are not suitable to run as Meta ads and should be skipped. [L1187, 2026]

- Kalodata's sidebar contains links to the TikTok Ads Library and the Facebook Ads Library. Use them to see how competing listings of a candidate book are advertised before deciding to test it. [L1175, 2026]

### Other Research Tools

- Fastmoss is an AI-driven product research tool used to spot viral products early. [L0914, 2026]
- Catalog Data is an AI-driven tool used together with Fastmoss to identify viral products early in product research. [L0914, 2026]
- The TikTok Shop explorer in WinningHunter lists products with revenue, revenue growth, sales growth, price, units sold, creator count, launch date, product score and review count. It can be filtered to Yesterday, 7 days or 30 days, with the default set to Last 30 days. [L0892, 2026]
- The TikTok Shop product list has the tabs All Products, Best Sellers, High growth and Top New Products. Each product row offers a "Find Shopify competitors with AI" action, and the list has Export and Refresh buttons. [L0892, 2026]
- PPAds (about 1 USD for a 3-day trial with a virtual card, cancelled before the trial ends) and Seller Count are paid TikTok research tools. Their product search shows direct competitors and ad impressions for each product. [L0895, 2021+]
- PP Ads has the largest TikTok ad library with strong filters, and its 3-day trial for $1 suits a short research sprint. Menia is a TikTok ad spy tool with a TikTok research section. Afterlib can be used for similar product discovery. [L1183, undated]
- Menia filter settings for product research: Date set to creation date within the last 14 days; Likes at least 200; Plays at least 10,000; E-commerce platform Shopify; Countries United States, Canada, United Kingdom and Italy. PP Ads supports the same filters. [L1183, undated]
- The Chrome extension VidIQ shows the reach and engagement graph of a competitor's video, revealing whether views drop off after the video starts running paid ads or Spark Ads. [L0819, 2020+]
- My Ad Finder is a free browser extension that shows only ads in the Facebook feed, so you can spot products with strong engagement and positive comments. Record promising products in a spreadsheet. [L1107, 2023]

#### Free competitor research on TikTok

- Search the product name in the TikTok app and count the organic videos. Many videos, or a few with tens of millions of views, signal saturation, though paid tools are more precise. [L0895, 2021+]
- A competitor whose first and last ad dates are recent and still running shows the product is working now. If the first and last ad dates are old with no recent ads, the product has likely faded. [L0895, 2021+]

### Recency Rules

- For TikTok product research, filter for posts created in the last 2 weeks. For Facebook Ads research, the window can go up to 1 month. [L0363, 2022+]
- A recent TikTok post with strong engagement signals a new product, while an older product that someone has only just launched may still qualify. If it is unclear whether a product is new, check with an experienced mentor. [L0363, 2022+]
- Note that Menia's filter uses a 14-day creation window, and TikTok's "Made Me Buy It" filter offers "This month", "This week" or "Last 3 months". The sources give different windows for different tools, so match the window to the tool you are using. [L0363, 2022+; L1183, undated; L0706, 2020+]

### Likes and Engagement Thresholds

- Use the number of likes on trending videos over the last month as a product signal. About 300 likes in a month was cited as a starting threshold. For a six-month window, extrapolate it (3 times 300 gave about 1,500 likes). [L0639, 2023]
- Menia's search filter used a minimum of 200 likes for ads created in the last 14 days. This is a search filter setting, not the same threshold as the 300-per-month starting point, so apply each one only in its own context. [L1183, undated]
- Products with weak video quality can still be good products. Strong hooks can draw attention to weak content, while good products with plain videos get overlooked. Judge the product itself, not the production quality of the ad. [L0639, 2023]
