## Deciding whether phone sales belong in your store

### Price bands where calls matter

| Price level | What to expect | Source ids |
|---|---|---|
| Up to about $1,000 without phone capacity | Keep the average price in a lower range, around $1,000. This is older advice from 2019 and 2021. | [L0356, 2019; L1112, 2021] |
| About $1,000 to $2,000 | Products generally convert with phone support, so have a team or staff ready to answer or return calls quickly. | [L0667, 2025] |
| About $1,000 to $2,000 (listing) | High-ticket items above this range are worth listing, because a short phone call can close the sale. | [L0934, 2023] |
| Above about $1,500 to $3,000 | Expect many inbound calls that check the business is legitimate before buyers order. | [L0667, 2025] |
| Above roughly $2,000 | Many buyers call before ordering, so expect inbound sales calls. List these products and return calls quickly. | [L1081, 2020-26; L0934, 2023] |

- The sources give different thresholds, so apply the one that matches your situation. The 2025 source puts conversion with phone support at $1,000 to $2,000 and expects heavy inbound calls above $1,500 to $3,000 [L0667, 2025]. The 2023 sources put the listing threshold at $1,000 to $2,000 [L0934, 2023] and at roughly $2,000 [L0934, 2023]. Older advice from 2019 and 2021 points to around $1,000 as the ceiling without phone support [L0356, 2019; L1112, 2021].
- Use the lower end of each range if you are unsure whether you can handle calls, and the higher end only when you have capacity to answer them [L0667, 2025; L0934, 2023].

### Phone as a trust signal

- A visible phone number tells customers they could call if needed, even when few customers actually call [L0359, 2020].
- Phone calls matter for trust even when few customers call, and the few who call usually buy [L0359, 2020].
- Keep a working phone number on the store and answer customer calls promptly, because phone responsiveness affects conversions for higher-priced items. This is older advice from 2020 [L0468, 2020].

### Inbound calls as a buying signal

- A phone call on the store is usually a buying signal, not a waste of time [L0910, 2015].
- Over about 2.5 years, more than 90 percent of calls either led to a sale or came from existing customers [L0910, 2015].
- Many shoppers call a store before buying, and those who get an answer tend to buy from that store [L0966, 2025].
- Answering the phone quickly and reliably is a competitive advantage [L0966, 2025].
- Answering the phone or returning calls generates significant sales volume that competitors miss. Customers are often surprised that a human answers and frequently place orders as a result. For established businesses, this represents millions in annual revenue [L0251, 2023].
- Phone calls before purchase are a normal part of selling products above about $1,500 to $3,000, because buyers check that the business is real [L0667, 2025].
- Calls decrease as the store builds reviews and credibility over time [L0667, 2025].

### Capacity you need before taking calls

- A store with high-priced products needs someone who can answer customer phone calls. Without that capacity, keep the average price in a lower range [L0356, 2019].
- For high-ticket products, the store owner should be willing to handle phone calls and extra emails, since that is part of how higher order values are closed [L0359, 2020].
- Only enter the $1,500 to $3,000 price range if you are willing to take these calls [L0667, 2025].
- Return calls from high-ticket buyers quickly [L0934, 2023].
- Respond to or call back high-ticket callers quickly [L0934, 2023].

### High-ticket offers and live selling

- Changing the selling environment is needed for high-ticket offers, because few people will buy a very expensive product from a sales letter alone [L0523, 2015+].
- Move high-ticket offers to a phone call, direct mail or a live event [L0523, 2015+].
- An 8,000 USD buy button is unlikely to be clicked from an email, while a booked phone appointment makes the sale far more likely [L0523, 2015+].
- A phone conversation gives live feedback, so the salesperson can answer objections and help the prospect decide on the spot [L0523, 2015+].
- Listeners pay closer attention to a voice than to a page [L0523, 2015+].
- Use phone sales for offers that are too costly to close online [L0523, 2015+].

### Decision: phone work or lower prices

1. Check whether you can answer and return calls quickly and reliably. If yes, you can consider products in the higher bands above $1,000 [L0667, 2025; L0966, 2025; L1112, 2021].
2. If you cannot, keep prices in the lower band around $1,000 and do not move into high-ticket niches [L0356, 2019; L1112, 2021].
3. If you want a hands-off business with no phone sales, do not pick niches with an average price above roughly $2,000 [L1081, 2020-26].
4. If you enter the $1,500 to $3,000 range, expect calls from buyers checking legitimacy and accept that they decline as reviews grow [L0667, 2025].

## Choosing a business phone number and provider

### Why a separate business line

- Business numbers signal professionalism to suppliers and customers and keep business calls apart from personal calls. [L0122, undated; L0664, 2020]
- A dedicated business number receives less spam than a personal cell number, and business calls are easier to recognise and prioritise. [L0122, undated]
- Use a business phone line and business address on the store, not personal details. A business line also allows a phone team to handle inbound and outbound calls. [L0852, 2020]
- Each store needs one phone number that belongs only to the business. Show it on the contact page or in the header. [L1092, 2022]

### Toll-free numbers

#### Prefixes and rules

| Country | Prefix | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| US | 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, 833 | Each prefix is assigned by a Responsible Organization (RespOrg) under FCC rules. [L1198, 2021-22] |
| Australia | 1300 | Equivalent of the US toll-free number. [L1198, 2021-22] |

- Toll-free numbers in the formats 800, 855 or 866 look more professional on store websites and work especially well for supplier-facing stores. [L0122, undated]
- Older advice (2019) lists 888 or 855 for customer calls. A phone line may be a Google number or a toll-free number. [L0960, 2019] **
- For callers in the same country, a toll-free number is free. International callers pay charges. [L1198, 2021-22]
- The provider forwards calls to any mobile or landline you choose, so callers hear an ordinary phone call. [L1198, 2021-22]
- Toll-free numbers are portable. You can move one to a different provider without changing the number. [L1198, 2021-22]

#### Vanity numbers

- Do not pay extra for a memorable or vanity number if the number only appears on your website and in supplier emails. The digits after the prefix do not matter for this use, and a plain number with a toll-free prefix looks just as professional. [L1198, 2021-22]

### Providers

#### Mighty Call / MightyCall

- MightyCall is a business phone system for handling calls. [L0518, undated]
- Entering the code `dropship50off` at checkout gives 50% off for the first 3 months. [L0518, undated]
- Mighty Call provides a toll-free business number with an app that rings the owner's cell phone and transcribes voicemails. It suits a single business number shown in the site header or on the contact page. [L1092, 2022] *(source 2022)*
- If Mighty Call does not work in a country such as Australia, Google Voice is a workable alternative for a business phone line. [L1092, 2022] *(source 2022)*

#### Grasshopper

- Grasshopper (grasshopper.com) is a US option for toll-free and vanity numbers. It has desktop and mobile apps, call forwarding and voicemail transcription. [L1198, 2021-22]
- Pricing at the time of recording: plans from $26 per month for a business line, and up to $75 off an annual plan. [L1198, 2021-22]

**Setup steps**

1. Open grasshopper.com and click Try Free. [L1198, 2021-22]
2. Go to Types of Numbers and select Toll Free Numbers. [L1198, 2021-22]
3. Pick a random number or specify your digits, then choose a plan. [L1198, 2021-22]
4. Download the desktop and mobile apps. [L1198, 2021-22]
5. Configure your settings, which the setup guides you through. [L1198, 2021-22]
6. Place the number on your website and in supplier communication. [L1198, 2021-22]

#### Zintel

- For an Australian business number, Zintel (zintel.com.au) sells 1300 numbers for about $10 per month. [L1198, 2021-22]
- Sign-up takes under 5 minutes. There is no lock-in contract, and you can cancel at any time. [L1198, 2021-22]

#### JustCall

- JustCall costs about $20 per month and lets you buy numbers in over 70 countries. Calls are answered through desktop and mobile apps from anywhere. [L1198, 2021-22]
- Example: a US number can be answered from the UK or Thailand, which suits a business run from abroad. [L1198, 2021-22]
- At the time of recording the provider advertised 70% off the first month. [L1198, 2021-22]

#### Google Voice

- Google Voice is a workable business phone line where Mighty Call does not operate, for example in Australia. [L1092, 2022] *(source 2022)*
- Google numbers are an accepted option for a phone line. [L960.043] **

## Setting up the line: voicemail, greetings, hours and routing

### Routing calls by time of day

- The phone number is directed to different people at different times of the day, based on the business time zone. [L0404, undated]

### Overflow to voicemail or personal phone

- Unanswered calls overflow automatically to voicemail or the owner's personal phone, so no manual switching is needed. [L0404, undated]
- Staff must check voicemail regularly during their answering periods. [L0404, undated]

### After-hours handling

- Outside advertised business hours, the phone line plays a prerecorded message and goes to voicemail. [L0404, undated]
- Voicemail left after hours is checked in the next business period. [L0404, undated]
- Alternatively, outside advertised business hours the call diverts to the owner's personal phone. [L0404, undated]

### Staff answering rules

- Staff must always state their first name and the business name when answering a call. [L0404, undated]

### Ringing several devices

- Google Voice can be installed on multiple phones logged into the same Google account so that calls ring on several devices. [L0971, 2024]

### Answering service

- Older advice: Most number providers offer an answering service. When a call is missed, it plays a pre-recorded greeting that names your business and asks for a message. The service then notifies you of the message by email or app so you can call back as soon as possible. [L1198, 2021-22]

### Voicemail transcripts

- Quo's automatic voicemail transcripts convert incoming messages to text, enabling faster responses to customer and supplier inquiries without listening to full recordings. [L0122, undated]
- Example (Quo Starter plan): The Quo Starter plan includes voicemail transcripts, which turn each voicemail into text. A caller asking whether a product is in stock can be answered from the transcript later, so a sale is not lost while the owner is busy. [L0338, 2020-26]

### Recorded voicemail greeting

Current version:

```
Hello, you've reached [store name, e.g. storename.com]. Thanks for calling. Our team is currently away from the desk or helping other customers, so please record a voicemail including your name, email address, phone number, and the purpose of your call. We'll respond as soon as we're able.
```
[L0934, 2023]

Older version:

```
Thanks for reaching out to [store name]. Our team is either away from the phone or busy assisting another customer at the moment. Leave a voicemail stating your name, email address, and question, and we'll call back as quickly as we can. Alternatively, send us a message at [support email address].
```
[L1090, 2021]

### Missed-call text reply

```
Hi, this is {store_name}, and I'm sorry I missed your call. I'm helping other customers right now, so I'll reply by text or phone as soon as I can. If your call is urgent, you can reply to this message with your order number and a short note about what you need.

{your_name}
```
[L1472, 2022]

## Showing the number and using phone calls to action

### Why phone contact matters

- For expensive products, a phone-based call to action can convert better than the buy-now button for many buyers, because they want to speak to a sales or customer service person before committing. Test "Call today" with the phone number in the copy against the buy-now button. Keep the buy-now button as the default option. [L0461, 2021-22]
- Business buyers often want to call before they order, to confirm stock or negotiate a volume discount. If you sell B2B, put a phone number on your website. [L0769, 2020-26]
- Visitors who call before ordering are usually close to buying. Leaving the number off can lower conversion. [L1088, 2020]
- A visible phone number builds trust, and callers are often ready to buy. [L0960, 2019]
- Older advice (2019 to 2022): one source (2019) recommends a phone number at any price point, including products with an average order value around $500. one source (2021 to 2022) limits the rule to expensive products. Use both: for high-ticket products, show the number prominently; for lower-priced products, the number still builds trust, so it does no harm to include it. [L0960, 2019; L0461, 2021-22; L1371, 2021-22]

### Showing the number on the site

#### Header and contact page

- Show a business phone number in the website header with a "Call us today" heading. High-ticket buyers want to speak to someone, and Google Shopping and similar marketing channels require a visible phone number. [L1371, 2021-22]
- Keep the phone number on the website even if you cannot answer every call. [L0934, 2023]

#### Trade (B2B) page

- B2B buyers usually want to talk on the phone and ask for bulk pricing. Make a phone contact visible on the trade page. [L0961, 2020]

### Handling calls you cannot answer

Follow these steps when the store cannot answer every call:

1. Keep the phone number on the website. [L0934, 2023]
2. Set up a voicemail greeting that collects the caller's details. [L0934, 2023]
3. Return every missed phone call on the same day. [L0961, 2020]
4. Outsource phone support later, when the store earns enough to justify it. [L0934, 2023]

### Offering a choice of channels

- When customers can choose between writing, calling or visiting a store, the phone option is the one most people use. In one test, 70 percent of inquiries for a sample came by telephone. [L1261, undated]
- Offer the phone option as a primary response channel, since people find it easier than writing. [L1261, undated]

### Testing phone-based calls to action

| Element | Control | Test variant |
|---|---|---|
| Call to action | Buy-now button (default, stays) | "Call today" with phone number in the copy |
| Product type | Expensive products | Test first here [L0461, 2021-22] |

Run the test on expensive products first. Keep the buy-now button as the default option whatever the result. [L0461, 2021-22]

## Answering inbound calls

### Greeting and first contact

- Answer every inbound call with a person, not a machine. Do not use AI to answer sales calls. A person should answer, even if they do not know every product answer right away. [L0808, 2026]
- Use this phone greeting, adjusting the wording to match how formal your company is. Use the same structure for opening chats, and make sure the staff member knows the next question to ask after the greeting. [L1396, 2021-22]

```
Hello, thank you for calling [company name], this is [your name]. How can I help you today?
```
[L1396, 2021-22]

- Staff with little phone experience should practice this greeting until it feels natural. [L1396, 2021-22]

### Understanding what callers want

- Inbound callers usually want to confirm that the business is legitimate, or check whether a product is in stock before ordering. The phone line therefore also answers pre-purchase questions. [L0700, 2020-26]
- Most incoming calls concern stock or shipping times. The real question is usually whether the store can be trusted. [L0910, 2015]
- Most early calls to a store are simple checks, such as "is this a real business" or "is this product in stock". [L1092, 2022]
- Answers should reassure the caller that a real person will help if something goes wrong. [L0910, 2015]

### Building trust on the call

- A phone number builds trust, and calls often convert to sales. [L1092, 2022]
- Ask what the caller's specific concerns are first. [L0253, 2024]

Trust-concern script:

1. Ask what their specific concerns are.
2. Respond: "We are an authorized retailer for [Brand Name]. We have been since [year]."
3. Ask which product they're interested in.
4. Say: "Our website is hosted on Shopify. All of your payment information is completely secure."
5. Offer: "You can also purchase directly on our website without speaking to us if you prefer. Or, if you ever have questions, you can reach me directly at [your email]."
6. Your goal: build confidence by proving you are legitimate and authorized. Most callers are satisfied once someone picks up the phone.

[L0253, 2024]

### Answering questions about physical locations

- If a customer asks whether they can visit a store or pick up in person, answer as follows:

```
We do not have physical locations. We ship directly from our suppliers' warehouses to customers, and those warehouses are not open to the public.
```
[L0803, 2022]

### Handling missed calls and voicemail

- Calls to a store should always be answered by someone who can handle the common questions, because an unanswered call can mean a lost sale. [L0910, 2015]
- Check voicemail and return calls promptly. [L0910, 2015]

### Treating callers as real prospects

- Treat each inquiry as a real prospect of a salesperson, and give them the full story rather than cutting the conversation short. [L1261, undated]
- A prospect who has already shown interest is worth more effort than a cold one. [L1261, undated]
