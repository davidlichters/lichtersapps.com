## Promotions, Holidays and Q4 Retargeting

### Offer-based retargeting

#### Two types of deal

- Irresistible deal ads come in two types: offers aimed at one person based on their stage in the customer journey, and offers tied to a holiday or seasonal event such as Black Friday, Christmas, Thanksgiving, Veterans Day or Easter. Event deals can also be niche-specific or global. [L0799, 2021-22]
- For a holiday deal, retarget everyone, including previous customers, with a campaign that runs only for the deal period. Audience position in the funnel does not matter here. [L0799, 2021-22]
- For a specific offer, target product viewers who are near the point of no return. This is generally around 30 days after the product view, so a 14 to 30 day viewer window is the usual target. [L0799, 2021-22]
- If your funnel moves fast, shorten the specific-offer window, for example to 7 days after the product view. High-ticket products usually have longer buyer journeys, so their offers should come later. [L0799, 2021-22]
- Do not offer a discount just because someone viewed a product. A buyer who needs more time may not need a discount to convert, so do not rush the deal and protect profit by timing it to the customer journey. [L0799, 2021-22]

### Deal timing

- Schedule the Christmas promotion in early December. Running it on Christmas Day misses buyers, who have spent their budgets and are not shopping online. [L1224, 2025+]
- Publish holiday content weeks ahead of the event. A holiday blog post published just before the sale is wasted effort, because it will not have time to rank and few people will see it. [L1224, 2025+]
- Run promotion-specific Google and Meta ads only during that promotion's dates, so each holiday has its own creative and a short exposure window. [L1224, 2025+]
- Run retargeting ads from November 24 through the end of Cyber Monday. [L0307, 2023+]
- High-CPM periods are ideal for retargeting, because the warm audience has high conversion intent despite CPM increases. [L0298, 2023]
- Build the visitor audience early, then spend on remarketing when ad costs rise in Q4. [L1110, 2019]

### Monthly promotions

- Retargeting ads work alongside email to capture additional revenue during holidays and promotions. They are run as a standard part of the promotion plan. [L1097, 2025]
- During a short seasonal sale, point paid ads at remarketing audiences so people who already visited the store are pushed toward the discount. Use the same offer, coupon code and dates across the emails and the ads so the message stays consistent. [L1105, 2024]
- Promotion ads are not product-specific like dynamic ads. They push one store-wide deal, bundle or bonus to lapsed contacts. The goal is to re-ignite the reason the shopper searched for the product in the first place. [L1204, 2022]
- Remarketing to warm audiences costs less than reaching cold traffic, so promotions can run on a small budget if the offer is strong. Good offers in these audiences can produce a very high return on ad spend. [L1204, 2022]
- Static remarketing ads aimed at older website-visitor lists are not limited to holidays. They also run during regular monthly promotions, showing store products with the current coupon code and linking to the store. [L0623, 2020-26]
- During the promotion window, run Facebook and Google remarketing ads to the same audience that receives the promotion emails. This maximizes reach and increases sales from people who have already visited but not bought. [L1205, 2020-26]

#### Promotion audience and sequence

- The promotion audience is the catch-all group of people who engaged with the store (website visitors, email opt-ins, Facebook and Google remarketing lists) but have not bought. Promotions are sent to them only after they have gone through abandoned cart emails and dynamic product remarketing ads without purchasing. [L1205, 2020-26]

Procedure:

1. Visitor views a product and leaves without buying; add them to Facebook and Instagram remarketing and Google remarketing. [L1205, 2020-26]
2. Send them through the email flows, starting with the abandoned cart sequence, and show dynamic product remarketing ads that return them to the product they viewed. [L1205, 2020-26]
3. Anyone who still has not purchased after these automations is added to the promotion audience and receives the promotional offers. [L1205, 2020-26]

### BFCM and holiday campaigns

#### Standard BFCM campaign

Procedure:

1. Use 5-6 different creatives per campaign. [L0298, 2023]
2. Prioritize image ads (perform best during holidays). [L0298, 2023]
3. Highlight discount (e.g., '60-70% Off') and show price comparison (e.g., 'Was 159, Now 89.99, Limited Time Only'). [L0298, 2023]
4. Run this on warm audience (recent visitors, cart abandoners). [L0298, 2023]
5. High-CPM periods are ideal for retargeting because warm audience has high conversion intent despite CPM increases. [L0298, 2023]

Procedure, audience and traffic routing (for the Black Friday to Cyber Monday window):

1. Primary audience (prioritize): People who entered the email sequence (newsletter subscribers). [L0307, 2023+]
2. If primary audience is too small for viable campaign: Expand to hot audiences = anyone who engaged with your brand: [L0307, 2023+]
   - Visited your website
   - Watched 50%, 75%, or 90% of your videos
   - Engaged with your Facebook page (likes, comments, shares)
   - Engaged with your Instagram page
3. Run retargeting ads from November 24 through end of Cyber Monday. [L0307, 2023+]
4. Send all retargeting traffic to the hidden category page (not product pages). [L0307, 2023+]

#### Stacked campaign for a short, high-demand event

- For a short high-demand event such as Black Friday or Christmas, one approach was to stack all retargeting audiences into one ad set, use at least 10 creatives that combine testimonials with offer images, and add roughly 10 to 30 dollars to the budget. [L0646, 2023]
- The retargeting audience is small, so it needs fresh proof and new offers rather than the same ad shown repeatedly. [L0646, 2023]

Creative count, two values:

| Campaign type | Creatives | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Standard BFCM retargeting campaign | 5-6 per campaign | Each campaign run on the warm audience [L0298, 2023] |
| Stacked ad set for a short high-demand event | At least 10, combining testimonials with offer images | All retargeting audiences in one ad set for Black Friday or Christmas [L0646, 2023] |

#### Holiday-specific Facebook audiences

- For holiday Facebook remarketing, target all visitors from the past 180 days instead of only the recently viewed-product carousel used normally. Use creative that is specific to the holiday, such as gift boxes with the offer shown on the image. [L0467, 2019]
- Run holiday-specific Facebook ads to a custom audience of website visitors from up to 180 days ago, in addition to dynamic product ads. Example offers: 10 percent off the entire store for Cyber Monday, or a free water bottle with every purchase. [L1110, 2019]
- Each visitor can see several different holiday offers within about three months when the same offer and creatives as the holiday email campaign are used. [L1224, 2025+]

### Static holiday ads

- Standard remarketing uses dynamic ads, but holiday promotions use static image ads with uploaded images. Each holiday gets its own campaign on Meta and its own display and demand gen campaigns on Google. [L1223, 2024+]
- For Q4 remarketing on Meta, use static remarketing ads, one for each holiday, uploaded as images. Do not use dynamic product ads for these holiday campaigns. [L1285, 2024]
- For Q4 remarketing on Google, use static display ads and static demand gen ads targeted to the same audience of website visitors from the last 180 days, excluding customers from the last 30 days. [L1285, 2024]
- Dynamic remarketing ads that show the exact products a visitor viewed should target recent website visitors who have not bought, such as 7-, 30- or 60-day audiences. These audiences form the main group used to bring buyers back during regular promotions. [L623.008]
- Visitors who were last active 30, 60 or 90+ days ago may no longer remember which products they viewed, so dynamic product ads are a weak fit for them. Static ads that show store products and the holiday offer work better for these older audiences. [L623.009]
- Example: A static holiday remarketing ad shows several store products with a holiday logo placed over them. The ad copy states that a promotion is running, names the saving and the coupon code, and links to the store. [L623.010]

Conflict on dynamic ads during holidays:

- Use dynamic product ads alongside holiday static ads on a 180-day custom audience (2019 advice). [L1110, 2019]
- Do not use dynamic product ads for the holiday campaigns; use static ads only (2024-2025 advice). [L1285, 2024]
- Both approaches appear in the source. The 2019 advice pairs holiday ads with dynamic product ads on the 180-day audience. The 2024-2025 advice drops dynamic ads from holiday campaigns entirely. [L1110, 2019; L1285, 2024]

### 180-day audiences and Q4 remarketing

- For Q4 remarketing, target all website visitors from the last 180 days and exclude all customers who purchased in the last 30 days, on both Meta and Google. (2024) [L1285, 2024]
- Meta (Facebook) ads for the promotion should carry the same message as the emails and be shown to all website visitors from the last 180 days. Exclude all customers from the last 30 days from this audience. (2024) [L1221, 2024+]
- Google Ads for the promotion should carry the same message as the emails and be shown to all website visitors from the last 180 days. Exclude all customers from the last 30 days, and run them as Display Ads and Demand-Gen Ads. (2024) [L1221, 2024+]
- Meta remarketing targets all website visitors from the last 180 days, excluding customers from the last 30 days. Use the same offer and creatives as the holiday email campaign, so each visitor can see several different holiday offers within about three months. [L1224, 2025+]
- Google remarketing uses the same audience as Meta: site visitors from the last 180 days, excluding customers from the last 30 days. Run both Display and Demand Gen ads on this audience, because the two formats add placements for short promotion windows. [L1224, 2025+]
- For Google display and demand gen retargeting, use an audience of all website visitors from the last 180 days and exclude customers from the last 30 days. Run both ad types on the same audience to get more placements for short promotion windows. (2025) [L1287, 2025]
- Matching the ad message to the email message keeps the offer consistent across channels during the same promotion window. Excluding recent buyers stops the promotion from following customers who just purchased. (2024) [L1221, 2024+]

#### Holiday remarketing audience plan

| Audience | Ad type | Exclusion | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 180-day website visitors | Static holiday ads | 30-day website visitors | Reach older leads who have not bought |
| 30-day website visitors | Dynamic product ads | Recent customers | Bring back recent visitors who have not bought |

The same structure can be built on both Facebook and Google by creating separate ad sets for the older audience and excluding the newer one. [L0623, 2020-26]

#### Email and ad exclusions

- Email promotions go to all contacts except those who purchased in the last 30 days and those currently in an evergreen automation, such as an abandoned cart sequence. The same segment applies whether you use Shopify Email or Klaviyo. [L1224, 2025+]
- Do not run holiday promotions as cold campaigns to people who have never visited the store or joined the list. Promote only to email subscribers and recent site visitors, and exclude recent buyers so they do not see the same discount again. [L1223, 2024+]

### Ad copy and message consistency

- Remarketing ads on Meta and the Google Display Network can reuse the offer, code, and deadline from the promotion's email copy so that the message stays consistent across channels. Build the ad copy from the emails rather than writing it from scratch. [L620.005]
- Prompt for writing remarketing ad copy from the email copy:

```
Using the email copy provided above, write copy for the remarketing ads I will run on Meta and the Google Display Network. Please write a separate version for each platform, and for each include a headline, primary text, and a call to action. Keep the discount code, the 10% discount, the promotion dates, and the delivery-before-Christmas guarantee identical to the emails.
```

[L1286, 2025]

### Budget math per promotion

#### Small-budget principles

- Holiday remarketing ads can run on a very small budget and still reach many people. Budget is therefore rarely the reason to skip this step. [L623.012]
- Remarketing budget for a small holiday audience is about $5 per day, which makes the ad spend a small cost compared with the email sales it supports. The budget can be scaled with the audience size. [L1223, 2024+]
- Remarketing to warm audiences costs less than reaching cold traffic, so promotions can run on a small budget if the offer is strong. [L1204, 2022]
- Remarketing to people who have already visited the website is typically the lowest cost advertising, because those visitors are cheaper to reach than new traffic. [L1110, 2019]

#### Budget procedure

1. Count how many people are in each Facebook and Google audience. [L757.033]
2. Decide how many times you want each person to see your ads (frequency). [L757.033]
3. Find the current remarketing CPM for each platform from your historic data. [L757.033]
4. Calculate the budget for each audience and total it per platform. [L757.033]
5. Divide each platform total by the number of promotion days to get the daily budget. [L757.033]

#### Formula

Daily budget = total platform budget / number of promotion days. For a 3-day promotion, divide the Google total by 3 and the Facebook total by 3. [L757.035]

#### Worked example

Small-audience example: seven-day website visitors, 2,000 people each on Google and Facebook, frequency 6. At a $1.50 Google CPM the total is $18 (about $6/day over 3 days), and at a $5.00 Facebook CPM the total is $60 (about $20/day over 3 days). [L757.037]

### Ending promotions cleanly

- Leaving bundle pages, bonus gifts or progress trackers live after a promotion ends sends visitors to dead ends and rewards that no longer exist. Revert every change in the closing procedure. [L1204, 2022]
- Run promotion-specific ads only during that promotion's dates, so each holiday has its own creative and a short exposure window, and stop them when the window closes. [L1224, 2025+]
