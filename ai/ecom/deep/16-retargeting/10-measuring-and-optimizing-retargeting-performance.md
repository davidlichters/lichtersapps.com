## Measuring and Optimizing Retargeting Performance

### Core KPIs (incl. remarketing ROAS target: above 10x within 2 to 5 days)

- Retargeting ROAS is revenue from the ads divided by ad spend:

```
ROAS = revenue from the ads / ad spend
```

  [L0360, 2024]
- Facebook Dynamic Remarketing and Google Remarketing should keep a conversion rate of at least 2.5% as a baseline. These are the warmest audiences and should outperform cold traffic. [L0272, 2022]
- Define business measurables for each horizon: customer count, average order value, cost per acquisition or cost per sale, repeat purchase rate, and number of products sold. Choose the measurables that directly drive revenue and profit figures. [L1519, 2021-22]

- Target window for remarketing ROAS (December 2021 coaching call, question from a store with a 2,000 to 3,000 USD average order value whose customers do not buy right away): judge ROAS within a typical 2 to 5 day window, not a month later. A good Google Shopping campaign should reach at least 10x ROAS within 2 to 5 days of the click; hot remarketing to your best customers should return MORE than the 10x accepted elsewhere (above 10x within the first 2 to 5 days), because those buyers see the brand a second time. No upper limit: higher is better. [L0469, 2021, 03:38 and 05:07; older advice, newer courses may differ] See also https://lichtersapps.com/ai/ecom/deep/10-google-ads-and-shopping/47-targeting-and-optimized-targeting.md (section "Performance Expectations and Strategy").

### Sample size and attribution caveats

- With fewer than 300-400 qualified people in the audience, results appear random. Larger audiences show clearer conversion patterns. [L0276, 2024]
- Retargeting audiences from Google and from Facebook or Instagram can be built from site visitors. Include them when calculating the conversion rate for paid traffic. [L0963, 2022]
- Traffic from days earlier can convert later through remarketing and abandoned cart emails. Do not judge a campaign on the last day's orders alone. [L1279, 2021]
- Mobile shoppers often add products to the cart on a phone and finish the purchase later on desktop. Retargeting brings them back, so mobile traffic should not be judged only by immediate sales. [L1275, 2019]
- If retargeting converts worse than cold-traffic campaigns in an account, the result is unusual and should be investigated: check audience size, lookback windows, and tracking errors. [L0867, 2020-26]

### Testing retargeting against cold audiences

- Retargeting is often the cheapest audience, but this does not always hold. One advertiser found that a retargeting audience cost 10 to 20 percent more than a cold audience of similar businesses in a similar niche that competitors had not yet targeted. Test cold audiences against retargeting audiences rather than assuming. [L0643, 2015]
- Both results apply at once. Warm audiences should outperform cold traffic on conversion rate [L0272, 2022], but cost per result must be tested, because retargeting can cost more than a cold audience [L0643, 2015].

### Diagnosing weak results

| Signal | First check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Retargeting underperforms | How qualified the cold traffic is, and what the copy in the retargeting ads says | [L1277, 2020] |
| High click-through rate, zero conversions | Traffic quality is usually fine. The bottleneck is the store, typically at product page level. Fix the conversion side before changing the audience. | [L1083, 2024+] |
| Retargeting converts worse than cold traffic | Audience size, lookback windows, tracking errors | [L0867, 2020-26] |

- The quality of the remarketing audience depends entirely on the quality of the traffic that creates it. The best remarketers are people who arrived through well-optimized cold traffic campaigns. [L0276, 2024]
- Better cold traffic, such as traffic from Google Shopping, improves warm and hot retargeting results. Retargeting only works with the quality of the first visits that feed it. [L1083, 2024+]
- Keep Meta ads running for remarketing at a small budget even when they have not yet produced conversions. Remarketing only performs well when the initial traffic was qualified, so weak traffic from Google also weakens remarketing. [L0837, 2025]

#### Learning from non-buyers

1. Create a custom audience of visitors who did not buy.
2. Retarget them with a short video in which you ask why they did not buy and what would change their mind.
3. Ask them to comment with the reason.
4. Use the answers to improve the product, price or page, so more visitors convert.

[L0643, 2015]

### Reviewing and improving ads on a schedule

- Track remarketing ROAS and units sold as the retargeting KPIs, because more organic visitors grow the remarketing list. Compare them with the earlier month to see whether the growth is working. [L0941, 2022]
- Review results across accumulated days rather than the last day's orders, since delayed conversions from earlier traffic also count. [L1279, 2021; L0941, 2022]

### Scaling budgets

1. Check that the audience is large enough for the result to mean something. Below 300-400 qualified people, results appear random. [L0276, 2024]
2. Check the return. A retargeting campaign should reach a ROAS of at least 10 (10x) before its budget is increased. [L0360, 2024]
3. Keep the Meta remarketing budget small until conversions appear, and only increase it once the campaign has passed the ROAS threshold. [L0837, 2025; L0360, 2024]

## Common Mistakes and Operating Rules

### Strategy Principles

Three principles drive remarketing success:

1. Stay consistently visible so customers do not forget you. [L0240, 2019]
2. Directly address the specific objections or concerns that prevented their initial purchase. [L0240, 2019]
3. Deeply understand what your target customer wants and why. [L0240, 2019]

### Product Ad Mix

- Running only dynamic product ads is a common mistake. They fit some visitors but not all, and repeatedly showing a product someone already rejected for 14 to 30 days rarely changes their decision. [L0563, 2021-22]

### Ad Angles and Segmentation

- Retargeting works best when ad angles are varied and audiences are segmented by the pages visited and the actions taken on the site. Each ad type can expose the customer to different aspects of the business and its products. [L1239, 2021-22]
- Retargeting ads work best when they use different angles and communicate different things about the business and its products. They are a chance to educate customers, not only to bring them back to the site. [L1240, 2021-22]

### Repetition and Message Fatigue

- Showing the same retargeting ad again and again over weeks, for example a full month, is one of the biggest mistakes and a common error. Once a customer has seen an ad a few times, repeating it adds little value, so change the angle or message instead. [L1239, 2021-22; L1240, 2021-22]

### Exposure Limits

- Retargeting visitors for too long and too often wastes budget and annoys people. Limit how long and how frequently each audience sees ads. [L1390, 2021-22]

### Operating Rules

1. Do not run only dynamic product ads; combine them with other ad types, because each type fits different visitors. [L0563, 2021-22; L1239, 2021-22]
2. Segment audiences by the pages visited and the actions taken on the site. [L1239, 2021-22]
3. Vary the angle, and after a customer has seen an ad a few times, change the message instead of repeating it. [L1239, 2021-22; L1240, 2021-22]
4. Use each ad to educate the customer, not only to bring them back to the site. [L1240, 2021-22]
5. Limit how long and how frequently each audience sees ads. [L1390, 2021-22]
6. Stay visible, address the specific objection that blocked the first purchase, and understand what the customer wants and why. [L0240, 2019]

## Other details

### Retargeting Principles

- Retargeting covers several ad types. Each type needs its own setup, and audiences should be segmented so every group sees the message that fits where it is in the buying process. [L1497, 2021-22]

### Retargeting Setup Sequence

Work through these nine follow-up actions in this order:

1. Set up all required pixels and tracking codes from Google Ads, Facebook and Google Analytics. [L0493, 2021-22]
2. Build the relevant retargeting audiences in Google Ads and Facebook. [L0493, 2021-22]
3. Plan the retargeting and email strategy before building anything. [L0493, 2021-22]
4. Identify email opt-in opportunities across the site. [L0493, 2021-22]
5. Review the email list and its segmentation structure. [L0493, 2021-22]
6. Plan the email sequences for post-purchase and each opt-in. [L0493, 2021-22]
7. Review and update the abandoned cart sequence. [L0493, 2021-22]
8. Build the first 30-day master flow. [L0493, 2021-22]
9. Replicate the master flow for each new entry point. [L0493, 2021-22]

### Meta Account Setup

Set up a new Meta account in this order:

1. Create a free Meta Business Manager at business.facebook.com. [L0649, 2019]
2. Create a Facebook page for the business. [L0649, 2019]
3. Create an Instagram page for the business. [L0649, 2019]
4. Install the Meta pixel on the Shopify store. [L0649, 2019]
5. Turn on auto advanced matching in the Meta account. [L0649, 2019]
6. Create the audiences listed below in Business Manager. [L0649, 2019]
7. Create the campaigns. If no lead generation or testing is running yet, start with hot dynamic, hot static, customers goodwill and promotions. [L0649, 2019]
8. Fill each ad set with 3-4 ads that look similar to the creative examples. [L0649, 2019]

### Landing Pages by Audience

- Speak to different target audiences with separate landing pages when they have different needs or experience levels, for example beginner, intermediate and advanced. [L0918, 2023]
- Match each landing page to the messaging of the ad that brings that audience in. This increases relevance and conversion. [L0918, 2023]

### Traffic Sources and Affiliate Offers

**Amazon Associate stores**

- Do not send traffic from Google Shopping, Google Performance Max or search ads to an Amazon Associate store. It is not allowed and can get the account banned. [L0923, 2015]
- Use Amazon Associate stores only for retargeting people who already know your brand, because batch-imported products get almost no organic traffic. [L0923, 2015]

**Raw affiliate links**

- Never run paid traffic to a raw affiliate tracking link, such as a long ClickBank hop link. Facebook dislikes affiliate promotion and the account can be lost. [L0923, 2015]
- Instead, write a review or informative blog post on your Shopify store, add the affiliate link there, and retarget the blog post visitors. [L0923, 2015]

### Display Placements

- Remarketing display ads can appear on unrelated websites because they follow where the visitor goes next. [L0968, 2021]
- Unrelated placements are normal and are not an error on their own. [L0968, 2021]

### Video Production

- Video editing is not a skill a store owner needs to learn. Freelance editors can cut a video cheaply, and at a rate of one or two videos per month the cost stays low. [L1424, 2021-22]
