## Retargeting Ad Types and Creative

### Retargeting ad types and networks

- Every retargeting ad fits one of four basic types: static image ads (a fixed image, optionally with text on it, and no separate ad copy), responsive ads (fixed images plus separate ad copy variants that the network mixes and tests automatically), static image and text ads (fixed image plus separate ad copy, from a headline and call to action to long body copy), and video ads (a video alone, as on YouTube with optional overlays, or a video with separate ad copy, mainly on Facebook). The type determines which assets you must prepare. [L1238, 2021-22]
- Each type runs mainly on specific networks: static image ads mostly on the Google Display Network and smaller display networks; responsive ads mostly on the Google Display Network; static image and text ads mainly on Facebook, Taboola and Twitter; video ads on YouTube (video alone, with optional overlays) or Facebook (video with separate ad copy). [L1238, 2021-22]
- Choose the ad type first, then prepare the assets that type needs. [L1238, 2021-22]
- Static image ads are the simpler format to set up for retargeting on the Google Display Network and Facebook. The same setup logic carries over to most other ad networks. [L1342, 2021-22]

### Image sizes and asset preparation

- Image-based retargeting ads need good-quality images. Dynamic product ads are excluded from this work because they reuse existing product ads from the store. [L1238, 2021-22]
- When running across several networks, the same ad must be recreated quickly in many sizes, so a fast resizing tool is needed. [L1238, 2021-22]
- The Google Display Network offers about 20 different image and ad sizes for static retargeting ads. Produce only the most-used sizes with the highest inventory and performance first. [L1238, 2021-22]
- Facebook retargeting ads of every type use a single image size of 1200 x 628 pixels, so one resized image set covers all Facebook placements in retargeting. [L1238, 2021-22]

### Dynamic and site-wide static ads

- Use dynamic remarketing for product-specific ads on Google display and Meta, because it shows the actual products the visitor viewed. [L0470, 2023]
- Use static site-wide ads only for promotions that cover the whole store. [L0470, 2023]
- Keep dynamic product ads in the retargeting plan, but pair them with other ad types that approach the visitor from different angles. Use these combinations to address the specific reason each visitor has not bought. [L0563, 2021-22]

### Product reminder ads

- A product reminder ad targets people who already know a specific product and reminds them of it. It is meant for audiences that have already seen the product, not for cold traffic. [L1169, 2021-22]
- Build the product reminder audience from visitors who viewed a specific product page, and show the ad 1 to 7 days after that view. The goal is to keep the product top of mind while the shopper decides. [L1169, 2021-22]
- Use product reminder ads only for high-volume products such as best sellers, because the audience has to be big enough to fill.
  - If the store has hundreds or thousands of products, do not build this for every product.
  - If the store has only a handful of products, it can be used for all of them. [L1169, 2021-22]

### Trusted advisor ads

- Trusted advisor ads show social proof to people in retargeting audiences who visited the site without buying. They address doubt about whether the product lives up to its promises or whether the business can be trusted. [L1398, 2021-22]
- Audiences and windows for the trusted advisor ad:

| Audience | Window |
|---|---|
| Viewers of specific product pages (generally high-volume product pages) | 7 to 14 days |
| Viewers of specific content pages | 7 to 14 days |
| Viewers of any product page | 7 to 14 days | [L1398, 2021-22]
- Run several variations of the trusted advisor ad with different reviews, so the audience does not see the same ad repeatedly. [L1398, 2021-22]

### Introduction ads

- An introduction ad is a brand ad that shows the business owner's face and invites contact. It works best in retargeting at roughly 14 to 30 days after the first contact with the visitor, and not as the first retargeting ad a person sees. [L0488, 2021-22]
- It can be shown to anyone who interacted with the business, for example people who viewed product or all-visitor sequences. [L0488, 2021-22]

### Informant ads

- The content of an informant ad must be genuinely valuable. It may link to products inside the content or mention them without a hard sell. [L0415, 2021-22]
- Products should come up naturally in the content rather than dominate it. [L0415, 2021-22]

### Promotion ads

- Retargeting image offers can push a viewer who already saw a video to buy. Examples: 50% off with an end-today deadline, a free gift, or a testimonial. [L1476, 2026]
- Run these offers in separate image campaigns in the same ad account. [L1476, 2026]
- Video ads bring in colder customers, similar to a sales letter. Image ads in a separate campaign for the same product can act as retargeting for people who watched a video but did not buy. [L1476, 2026]
- Pattern: a caption that leads with a discount and a coupon code for a specific percentage off, then two real customer review images (for example, the product in hand or worn). The offer addresses price doubts and the reviews address trust doubts in one ad. [L0648, undated]
  - Example: Still thinking about it? Use code SAVE15 for 15 percent off today, plus two photos of customers with the product.
  - Example: Your cart is waiting. Take an extra 10 percent off with CART10, with two real reviews from buyers in your region.

### Testimonial and proof videos

- Testimonial and proof videos perform especially well in retargeting, because warm visitors who saw the brand for the first time need trust, not just an offer. [L0398, 2023+]
- Trust is the bigger barrier for people who do not know the store, so show real reviews and people using the product. [L0398, 2023+]
- Classic dropshipping-style videos also perform well in retargeting, even though they convert less in cold traffic. Use them as retargeting creative alongside UGC and testimonial videos. [L0398, 2023+]
- Static trust ads matter because visitors leave for reasons other than lack of interest, such as needing to check their card, asking someone, or not knowing the brand. Showing videos, testimonials and product photos helps them come back. [L1076, 2019]

### Creative that works

- Retargeting should be subtle. Use each ad to deliver value, state product benefits and offer the next step in the sales funnel without mentioning past browsing. Visitors connect the dots themselves, and the ads stay effective. [L0563, 2021-22]
- Using one retargeting message for all audiences fails, because visitors have different pain points: trust, not understanding the product's value, not understanding why the business is better than competitors, and doubting that the product fits their problem. One ad cannot address all of these, so the same person should see different ads across the retargeting window. [L0563, 2021-22]
- Procedure for building the message set [L0563, 2021-22]:
  1. Create several ad creatives, messages, styles and angles for retargeting. [L0563, 2021-22]
  2. Address each likely pain point with its own ad, such as trust, product value, competitor comparison or product fit. [L0563, 2021-22]
  3. Match each ad as closely as possible to the pages the visitor previously viewed on your site. [L0563, 2021-22]
  4. Test different combinations and keep the ones that perform best. [L0563, 2021-22]
- Customer research makes retargeting ads more personal and relevant. Retargeting ads should hit the points customers need to hear before they buy. [L0872, 2021-22]
- A first ad must contain the full case, instead of depending on a later reminder. Readers who see an ad once may never see it again, and a repeat exposure does not replace a complete message. [L1261, undated]

### Copy rules

- The goal of retargeting ad copy is to get the viewer to click through to the website, not to make the sale. The conversion happens on the website, where the selling takes place. [L1238, 2021-22]
- On some placements, such as many Google Display image-only ads, no ad copy is shown, so the product image alone must do the persuading. [L0563, 2021-22]
- On Facebook, ad copy always appears, so weak copy hurts dynamic product ads there. [L0563, 2021-22]

### Recycling best offers

- Identify your best-performing promotional offers and ad creatives from past campaigns. Then systematically recycle and repurpose them across different remarketing audience segments: abandoned carts, one-time buyers, inactive visitors and email subscribers. This maximizes ROI while reducing the time and cost of creating new creative assets. [L0240, 2019]
