### Company identification and contact details on legal pages

- Each legal page should include the company name. [L1345, 2021+]
- Each legal page should include at least an email address so customers can contact you. [L1345, 2021+]
- The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy templates use `{Store name}` and `{Business email}` for these details. [L1327, 2026]

### Checkout and pre-traffic readiness

- Refund policy and shipping policy appear in the checkout. [L1345, 2021+]
- Both must be in place before traffic starts. [L1345, 2021+]

### Visibility of shipping policy and guarantees

- Publish the shipping policy and any guarantee, such as a 30-day guarantee, on the store where buyers can see it. [L1346, 2020-26]
- Examples of placement: tabs on the product page. [L1346, 2020-26]
- Visible policies signal that the business is legitimate. [L1346, 2020-26]

### Footer links and Google ads compliance

- Link the policy pages in the store footer. [L1446, 2020-26; L1331, 2025-26]
- Do this before running any ads, for Google compliance. [L1446, 2020-26]
- Do not start ads until the policy pages are linked in the footer. [L1446, 2020-26]

### Country-specific content: legal notice (Impressum)

- In Germany, an online store must show a legally required legal notice (Impressum) with the business information on the website. [L1428, undated]
- Missing information can lead to warning letters from lawyers and complaints from customers. [L1428, undated]
- The Impressum must be complete before the store goes live. [L1428, undated]
- Check the legal requirements of the country you sell from and the countries you sell to. [L1428, undated]

#### Further details

- TermsFeed is an all-in-one wizard tool recommended by Google for generating privacy policy agreements without legal expertise. [L0014, undated]

- Google Adwords provides sample privacy policy templates to use as a starting point. [L0014, undated]

- A store should have a privacy policy and terms of service live on the site before launch. These pages are part of the basic legal setup, separate from marketing copy. [L1264, 2021-22]
