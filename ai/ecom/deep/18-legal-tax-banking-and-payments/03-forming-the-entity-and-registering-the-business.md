## Forming the Entity and Registering the Business

### Order of registration

- Choose the legal form first: sole proprietor, LLC or S-Corp. The sales tax certificate is needed only where the seller must collect sales tax. [L0532, undated]
- Form the LLC before you apply for the EIN. The EIN application uses the LLC name. [L0695, 2020-26; L0118, undated]
- Register the business early, even before it earns money, because the LLC, EIN and any sales tax certificate will be needed anyway. This avoids later delays and makes the business look more professional to suppliers. [L0695, 2020-26]
- The business name and the store name can differ. A business name is often generic and not tied to one niche. [L0941, 2022; L0251, 2023]
- Do not set up ad accounts until supplier products are on the store. While waiting for the EIN or company paperwork, work on supplier research and niche understanding. [L0962, 2021]

#### Full procedure: US LLC with EIN and sales tax certificate

1. Decide between sole proprietorship and LLC. Choose an LLC unless there is almost nothing to lose. [L0695, 2020-26]
2. Choose a generic LLC name that is not the store name. [L0695, 2020-26]
3. Form the LLC online in the state where you are located, using LegalZoom's $0 basic option plus the state filing fee (no more than $97). Decline all upsell offers. [L0695, 2020-26]
4. Confirm with LegalZoom, or the chosen service, that the LLC name is available and belongs to you. [L0695, 2020-26]
5. Apply for an EIN on the IRS website using the "Apply for an EIN" option. Answer the questions, which usually takes 5 to 10 minutes. You receive a 9-digit number. [L0695, 2020-26]
6. Only if your state has sales tax: search Google for "[your state] sales tax certificate", open the state's.gov sales and use tax registration page, choose to register online, and enter the LLC name. [L0695, 2020-26]
7. Download the PDF that shows your sales tax certificate number. If none is offered, the certificate is sent by mail. [L0695, 2020-26]
8. Use the LLC name, EIN and sales tax certificate when registering with suppliers. [L0695, 2020-26]

#### Step-by-step details (per step)

- The EIN can usually be requested at the same time as the LLC, or right after the state approves it, depending on the filing service. [L0468, 2020]
- The EIN is a unique tax number for the business and is needed for Stripe, Mercury and most platforms. [L1127, 2024]
- Use the EIN as the business tax number for Stripe, Mercury and other platforms. [L1127, 2024]
- Open the business bank accounts only after the LLC and EIN are in place. [L0693, 2024]
- Entity, tax ID and bank setup should be done before supplier applications. Suppliers ask for the business name, business structure, registration and tax numbers. Missing these can delay progress by about a week while government registrations are processed. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Keep the EIN and sales tax certificate under the LLC's name, not the store name. [L1115, 2024]

### Choosing the legal form

- Sole proprietor or LLC: choose an LLC unless there is almost nothing to lose. [L0695, 2020-26]
- Before the EIN and sales tax certificate, get an accountant's advice on the structure that fits you, and track every business expense from the start so all deductible costs can be written off. [L1344, 2020-26]
- Describe the business as "online retail" or similar. No physical location is required in the formation documents. [L0268, 2020]
- An S-corp can be set up with an accountant after the EIN and LLC are in place. Many owners set up the EIN, LLC and S-corp as a package. [L0468, 2020]
- Converting a sole proprietorship to an LLC (DBA, EIN, bank accounts): consult a CPA or the state where the DBA was registered. One call did not give the steps. [L0805, 2024] (2024)

### Forming an LLC: DIY, services and fees

- Forming an LLC means filing articles of organization with your state. This is a formal registration step separate from any federal number. It can usually be done without a paid service, although many online ads push paid helpers. [L1415, 2021-22]
- You can form a business entity yourself. Paying someone is optional, and many owners pay only after the business is making money. [L1112, 2021]
- An LLC and EIN can be set up directly online for little cost. Third-party formation services often charge about $99 and then upsell extra services. [L0855, 2023] (2023)
- An LLC filed online through LegalZoom costs about $99 and is optional. It keeps business and personal funds separated from the start. [L1275, 2019]
- Do not pay a service just to obtain a document you can file yourself. Compare quotes before paying. [L0468, 2020]
- A service that forms LLCs for a monthly fee charges only the state filing fee. Typical formation services add a markup of roughly $100 to $150 on top of it. [L0960, 2019]
- Forming a business entity directly through a local government website typically costs about $100 to $300 at most. [L0698, 2020-26]
- Paying third-party intermediaries to form a legal entity abroad can be far more expensive than the official government registration website. Use the government portal whenever it is available. [L0698, 2020-26]
- Use the official state website to apply for a business entity or the state's reseller certificate. A virtual address can be used where needed for business documents. [L0964, 2023] (2023)
- Formation services usually handle the state filing for you. Confirm with the service what is included. [L1113, 2022]

### Naming the entity

- Choose a general business name that does not tie you to a specific niche, for example "AM Stores LLC". [L0251, 2023] (2023)
- All domain names for different niches can be owned by the same LLC. [L0251, 2023] (2023)
- When contacting suppliers, reference the LLC name, not individual niches. [L0251, 2023] (2023)
- Confirm that the LLC name is available and belongs to you before you apply for the EIN. [L0695, 2020-26]

### Obtaining the EIN

- The EIN is free when requested directly from irs.gov during IRS business hours. Have it ready for suppliers. [L1344, 2020-26]
- Apply on the IRS website, not through paid third-party sites. [L0695, 2020-26]
- A formation service may charge extra for the EIN as an add-on. Add it when placing the LLC order if you use the service. [L1127, 2024]
- The EIN can usually be requested at the same time as the LLC or right after the state approves it. [L0468, 2020]

### Sales tax certificate (state level)

- A sales tax certificate is needed only in the state where the business is registered, and only if that state has sales tax. There is no single federal source. It is obtained through the state's government website. [L0695, 2020-26]
- States that charge sales tax require a state sales tax certificate. Get it early rather than late. [L0468, 2020]
- Depending on your state, you may need a sales tax certificate. It can be applied for online and usually arrives within a few days. Not every supplier asks for it at the start. [L0671, 2021]
- Search for the state name with "sales tax certificate" or "sales tax permit" to see whether your state requires one. [L1285, 2024; L1405, 2021-22]
- Use only the official.gov website. Commercial.com sites offer to file the same registration for a fee, although it can be done directly and quickly online. [L0695, 2020-26]
- After online registration, the state usually provides a downloadable PDF with the certificate number. If no PDF is provided, the state mails the certificate. [L0695, 2020-26]
- Example, North Carolina: the sales and use tax registration page has an online form that asks for the LLC name. There is no fee for the certificate of registration. [L0695, 2020-26]
- To get a certificate: search Google for your state's name with "sales tax certificate", open the state's official online form, and fill it out. It usually costs $0 and is often issued online within about a day. [L1285, 2024] (2024)
- In the US, sales tax permits are issued by each state. Many states let you apply online through the state government's website. [L1405, 2021-22]
- The sales tax certificate is only required in cases where the seller must collect sales tax. [L0532, undated]
- Check your state at taxjar.com/states, click your home state, and read the registration FAQ to see whether you need a sales tax certificate there. [L1344, 2020-26]
- After getting a seller's permit in your home state, register separately for any sales and use tax obligations and for payroll if you will pay employees. These registrations are separate from the seller's permit itself. [L1415, 2021-22]
- Apply for a sales tax certificate once the niche is picked. It can often be done online in a short time, depending on the state. [L0803, 2022]

### Business licenses and state-specific rules

- Business license and permit requirements differ by state in the US. Look up the rules for the state where you operate instead of copying another state's steps. [L0801, 2021]
- A business license may be requested by suppliers. If the home state issues no license, ask the supplier whether an alternative form can be used instead. [L0855, 2023] (2023)
- Business license filing services can charge very different fees for the same filing, with one quote at $250 and another at $100 (2020 example). [L0468, 2020]

### California and out-of-state entities

- If you live in California but incorporate in another state (for example a Wyoming S-Corp), California requires you to register as a foreign business entity in California. [L0253, 2024] (2024)
- California has the strictest state tax requirements. Consult the one source Discord community for specific steps, as this varies by situation. [L0253, 2024] (2024)

### S-corp election

- The S-corp election (IRS Form 2553) can be filed early. It has no effect until the business has revenue. When to file is a personal choice. [L0667, 2025] (2025)
- An S-corp can be set up with an accountant after the EIN and LLC are in place. [L0468, 2020]
- Converting a sole proprietorship to an S-corp: update the legal name and tax details with Shopify, update the legal details with each supplier account, update the financial accounts that hold the business's money, and keep a list of who has confirmed the change and who is still pending. Some accounts respond slowly. [L0834, 2021]

### Banking and payments timing

- Open bank accounts after the LLC and EIN are in place. [L0693, 2024]
- Identify which banking options you will use and open the business accounts early. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Before the store goes live, open a business checking account, a business savings account and a business credit card, in that order after registration and EIN. [L0966, 2025] (2025)
- Set up a business PayPal account linked to the business bank account, and ask PayPal support to enable auto sweep. [L1415, 2021-22]
- Use the EIN as the business tax number for Stripe and Mercury. [L1127, 2024]

### Non-US equivalents (Australia)

- In Australia, a sole trader needs an ABN and a company needs an ACN. Other countries have an equivalent business number that identifies the business for tax purposes. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Do business in the country where you live. If that is not possible, do it in the US. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Registering as a sole trader and getting an ABN online costs about 36 Australian dollars. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Forming a company with an accountant's help typically costs between 1,500 and 2,000 Australian dollars. [L0702, 2021-22]
- An Australian sole trader needs an ABN, obtained through a short online form and issued quickly. A company gets an ACN from its accountant when it is registered. Both numbers may be required on supplier documents. [L1405, 2021-22]
- A sole trader can begin trading as soon as the ABN is issued online. No further registration is needed before the first sales. Company formation usually takes longer and involves an accountant. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Government registrations can take one to two weeks to process. Start them early and have your business name, company name, company number and tax ID ready when you contact suppliers. [L0702, 2021-22]
- In Australia, a.com.au domain requires a registered Australian business, identified by an ABN. Buying an ABN is one route to obtain that domain. [L0549, undated]

#### Legal and banking setup checklist (Australia, in order)

1. Decide the country where you will do business. [L0702, 2021-22]
2. Start the process to form the business entity now. For a sole trader, use the government registration page for your country (in Australia, apply online for an ABN). For a company, work through the setup with an accountant. [L0702, 2021-22]
3. Obtain the tax ID required in your country (in Australia, an ABN or an ACN). [L0702, 2021-22]
4. Start looking for a business accountant now, even if you are not using one yet. Ask people you know for recommendations. [L0702, 2021-22]
5. Identify which banking options you will use and open the business accounts now. [L0702, 2021-22]

### Tax setup checklist (US e-commerce)

1. Decide which business structure fits you (sole proprietor, LLC or S-Corp), and get an accountant's advice on it. [L1344, 2020-26]
2. Track every business expense from the start so all deductible costs can be written off. [L1344, 2020-26]
3. Form the business type you chose. [L1344, 2020-26]
4. Get a free EIN directly from irs.gov during IRS business hours, and have it ready for suppliers. [L1344, 2020-26]
5. Check your home state at taxjar.com/states to see whether you need a sales tax certificate there. [L1344, 2020-26]

### Post-formation updates

- Update the legal name and tax details with Shopify after any change of legal form. [L0834, 2021]
- Update the legal details with each supplier account. [L0834, 2021]
- Update the financial accounts that hold the business's money. [L0834, 2021]
- Keep a list of who has confirmed the change and who is still pending. Some accounts respond slowly. [L0834, 2021]

### Case: US company (Wyoming LLC, reported)

- Formation cost around 300 to 400 USD, with formation time about 2 to 3 weeks. Filing by a CPA cost about 200 USD at most and took under 3 to 4 days. [L1512, 2023]
- Stripe payouts came within 1 to 2 days. The first year reached about 300,000 USD in revenue, with zero taxes reported as paid under that structure. [L1512, 2023]
