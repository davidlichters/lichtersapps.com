## Selling the Business: Legal, Tax and Transaction Matters

### Preparing the Store for Sale

- Keep accurate, complete books from the start, because brokerages and marketplaces typically require several years of complete financial records (the example given was four years). Older guidance from 2021 to 2022 gives the same requirement. [L0449, 2021-22]
- Store all supplier invoices in Google Drive so they are easy to show during buyer due diligence and verification. [L0231, 2019]
- Turn off WHOIS privacy in the last month before listing, so your own name shows as domain owner. Buyers look up WHOIS to confirm ownership. [L1468, 2014]
- Make sure you are verifiable as a seller: real social profiles with real friends, and a LinkedIn profile with genuine connections. Sellers without an online presence may be asked for ID, a passport or a social security card, which makes the sale harder. [L1468, 2014]

### Asset Sale or Stock Sale

- Decide before listing whether you will do an asset sale (specific assets transfer) or a stock sale (transfer of ownership of the entire company). [L0231, 2019]
- Asset sales are simpler. Stock sales require deeper buyer due diligence. [L0231, 2019]
- On this marketplace, more than nine out of ten sales are asset sales, where websites, files and domains are transferred but the LLC stays with the seller. [L0554, 2019]
- A stock sale of an LLC requires much deeper due diligence, because the buyer must consider lawsuits arising from earlier actions. [L0554, 2019]
- The usual way to sell a store is an asset sale in which the LLC sells the Shopify store, ad account access, supplier contacts and email lists. The LLC stays with the owner and can be dissolved or reused later. This is older advice from 2022. [L0803, 2022]

### LLC Structure and Transfer

- You typically do not have to sell your LLC structure. Buyers conduct more extensive due diligence when purchasing an LLC than when purchasing specific assets. [L0231, 2019]
- Large portfolio buyers usually set up a separate LLC for each store, which makes record keeping straightforward. This setup is common among portfolio buyers. [L0554, 2019]

### Trademark Risk in Listings

- A business with a partially trademarked name can be listed for sale. Some buyers may avoid it because of perceived legal risk. [L0231, 2019]
- A partially trademarked name can be listed if the seller has not received a warning or cease and desist. [L0554, 2019]
- Well-known brands, such as Marvel, should be avoided in listings. [L0554, 2019]
- Some buyers may still be uncomfortable with the risk, even when the listing is allowed. [L0554, 2019]

### Brokerage or Private Sale

| Factor | Brokerage | Private sale |
|---|---|---|
| Sale price | Typically higher, through professional buyer matching. [L0231, 2019] | You keep the full sales price. [L0231, 2019] |
| Negotiation | Broker guides you through negotiations and earn-out scenarios. [L0231, 2019] | You handle negotiation yourself. [L0231, 2019] |
| Buyer qualification | Broker qualifies buyers to protect your time. [L0231, 2019] | Harder to qualify serious buyers. [L0231, 2019] |
| Competitors | Broker screens competitors using deposit systems. [L0231, 2019] | Financial records are exposed to potential competitors. [L0231, 2019] |
| Migration | Broker handles business migration. [L0231, 2019] | You are responsible for business migration. [L0231, 2019] |
| Buyer list and protections | Broker's buyer list and protections apply. [L0554, 2019] | No broker buyer list or protections. [L0554, 2019] |
| Fee | 15% of the sales price. [L0231, 2019; L0554, 2019] | No broker fee. [L0554, 2019] |

### Broker Fees

- Brokerage commission on dropshipping store sales under $1 million is 15% of the final sale price. [L0231, 2019]
- The broker charges a fee of 15% of the sales price, which the source describes as standard in the industry. [L0554, 2019]
- Private sales avoid this fee, but they lack the broker's buyer list and protections. [L0554, 2019]

### Escrow and Payment Protection

- Escrow protects both sides by holding the buyer's money until the site has been fully transferred and checked. The seller is paid only after the new owner confirms that everything works. [L1468, 2014]
- Third-party escrow services such as escrow.com move money safely but do not handle the migration. Moving the store, its accounts and its domain still needs someone to manage the details. [L1468, 2014]
- An escrow service can protect a private sale when everything goes smoothly. Escrow services are not specialised in online businesses, so arbitration can be difficult if a problem arises. [L0554, 2019]
- A broker that holds the buyer's funds until the transfer is complete may not be allowed to call itself an escrow company under US banking rules. Before paying, confirm how the money is held and whether the arrangement is legally valid in your jurisdiction. [L1468, 2014]

### Due Diligence

- Buyers must complete due diligence themselves. The sale platform vets listings before publication, but the responsibility to check suppliers, relationships and whether the business suits them stays with the buyer. [L0553, 2018]
- Prepare records that answer a buyer's checks on suppliers, relationships and the store's history, since the buyer will run these checks regardless of the platform's vetting. [L0553, 2018; L0231, 2019]

### Addbacks in Valuation

- Addbacks allow you to reintroduce expenses from your tax return into net profit for valuation when they differ from what a buyer would actually incur. [L0231, 2019]
- Prepare to justify and negotiate these adjustments. [L0231, 2019]

### Seller Financing and Earn-Outs

- Seller financing, such as an earn-out or a share of profits paid over several months, becomes more common as deal sizes grow. It bridges the gap between the buyer's ability to pay upfront and the seller's asking price. [L0554, 2019]
- Seller financing can take the form of an earn-out or a share of profits over several months, for example $5,000 per month for six months. [L0554, 2019]
- In this structure, the broker holds the domain until the buyer makes the final payment. The broker can transfer it back or use it as leverage if the buyer breaks the contract. [L0554, 2019]

### Non-Competes

- When buying or selling a business, the buyer should require a non-compete agreement. Without one, the seller can open the same niche again soon after the sale. This is older advice from 2020. [L0852, 2020]
- Observed non-compete terms ranged from 12 months to 5 years. This is older advice from 2020. [L0852, 2020]

### Cross-Border Transfers

- Transferring accounts to a buyer in another country can take months. [L0553, 2018]
- One American buyer who had to set up an EU company for an EU migration was three months into a migration that was not going smoothly. Check cross-border handovers early. [L0553, 2018]

### Deal Process on a Broker Platform

1. The seller submits the store for vetting, and a team reviews it. [L0553, 2018]
2. Once approved, the store is listed. [L0553, 2018]
3. The sales team screens buyer interest. [L0553, 2018]
4. A prep call with the seller takes place. [L0553, 2018]
5. A buyer-seller call led by the broker takes place. [L0553, 2018]
6. A post-call follow-up with the seller takes place. [L0553, 2018]
7. The broker helps negotiate. [L0553, 2018]
8. After the asset purchase agreement is signed, the broker's team migrates the business and hands it over. [L0553, 2018]

If you use Google Analytics integrated with Shopify's native tracking, you already have sufficient performance data and do not need tracking templates. ### Payments and banking

- **Shopify Payments address verification:** Provide a utility bill or any mail that proves you own the address. If you are outside the US but have a US address, provide a contract with the address company or mail from a supplier. Older advice (2022). [L0249, 2022]
- **Payment details at onboarding:** These can include a bank account for transfers through TransferWise or a PayPal account email. Keep them in the standardized questionnaire responses so they are easy to find later. Older advice (2021-22). [L1054, 2021]
- **Online-only banks such as Mercury:** Shopify Payments may not accept them, and Shopify support may be unsure. The practical test is to connect the account and see whether it is accepted. Then check community groups for other sellers' experiences with that bank. Older advice (2021). [L1279, 2021]
- **Shopify subscription payment methods:** Credit card, debit card and PayPal are accepted. You can update the payment method later, for example when a business credit card is available. [L1304, 2020-26]
- **Email autoresponder billing:** Autoresponder subscriptions are billed through the Shopify account. The charge appears on the Shopify bill after the trial ends. Older advice (2021-22). [L1298, 2021-22]
- **Gemini API billing:** Usage is billed through a Google Cloud billing account that needs a credit or debit card. Charges go to the account linked to the key's project. If a tool runs on someone else's key, their account pays, so every user of a shared tool should enter their own key. [L0794, 2026]
- **Shared logins:** Sharing one login across several people usually breaches the terms of service of paid software such as ChatGPT Plus, Canva Pro or Helium 10. The provider can suspend or close the account, so read each tool's terms before you depend on shared access. [L1387, 2026]
- **Buying software bundles through Whop:** The bundle is sold through a third-party checkout platform, Whop, using an affiliate-tracked sign-up link. The seller is therefore not the software vendors themselves. Before paying, check:
  - who the seller is,
  - The refund terms,
  - whether the subscription renews automatically, and at what price. [L1387, 2026]

### Shipping costs and carriers

- **Shipping your own account:** Open a UPS or FedEx account online, which is free. Give the supplier your account number so they can ship and bill it to you. You then pay the shipping bill. [L0933, 2020-26]
- **Cutting shipping costs for large or heavy products.** Older advice (2021). [L0853, 2021]
  1. Ask the supplier whether they can give a shipping discount through their own carrier accounts.
  2. Get quotes from several freight brokers for recent shipments.
  3. Compare the quotes, because they can differ by hundreds of dollars.
  4. Use the cheapest reliable option.
- Pricing a large item with a fixed carrier rate can waste a lot of money over time. Get shipping quotes regularly and avoid relying on one carrier, since UPS quotes for large, heavy items can be very high. Older advice (2021). [L0853, 2021]
- Products that are unusually costly to ship or need special handling should be checked with the carrier. This course handles them as standard shipping with insurance, not as a separate cost line. Older advice (2022). [L1281, 2022]
- **Setting up shipping details in Smart SEO:**
  1. Turn on the shipping details option.
  2. Enter the country code for the country you ship to.
  3. Enter the minimum handling days, for example 0 if some products ship the same day.
  4. Enter the maximum handling days, for example 3 if orders cannot ship on weekends.
  5. Enter the minimum transit days, for example 1 to 5 days.
  6. Choose the shipping price type: minimum, maximum or fixed amount.
  7. For a fixed amount, enter the currency (USD in the example) and the rate. For free shipping, enter a rate of 0.
  8. Leave the setting turned on and save. [L1337, 2020-26]

### Shopify store setup: domains, SSL and licences

- **Connecting a domain in Shopify.** Older advice (2021-22). [L1292, 2021-22]
  1. In the Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Domains.
  2. Click Connect existing domain.
  3. Enter the domain name you purchased (no https://), then click Next.
  4. If the registrar is a major one such as GoDaddy, choose Connect automatically. Shopify asks you to authorize the connection in the registrar's window. Then click Connect and close it.
  5. Shopify verifies the connection and alters the domain's DNS settings. Verification can take a minute or two.
  6. For a smaller registrar that Shopify does not connect to automatically, choose Connect manually and follow the instructions Shopify shows to change the DNS settings.
  7. Once the domain shows as connected, wait for propagation, which can take up to 24 hours.
- Shopify detects and connects domains from major registrars such as GoDaddy automatically. Smaller registrars require a manual DNS connection. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292, 2021-22]
- **SSL:** Shopify provides a free SSL certificate for connected custom domains, so you do not need to buy one separately. The status shows SSL pending for a short period, around 30 minutes. After that, HTTPS is active. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292, 2021-22]
- HTTPS is essential for e-commerce. While the SSL status is pending, the site shows as not secure in the browser until the certificate is issued. Older advice (2021-22). [L1292, 2021-22]
- **Shrine licences:** One Shrine licence per store is enough. Buying more licences for a single store is unnecessary. [L1330, 2025-26]
- Shopify does not lock your store code. You can export all products as a CSV file and download image assets to move them elsewhere. A full rebuild on another platform is not a one-click process. Older advice (2021). [L0968, 2021]

### Software costs and subscriptions

- Shopify is the only paid software that is a must-have at the start. Canva's free version is enough for design. Stripe Atlas can help a founder operating from abroad set up a US company. Advice from 2023. [L0934, 2023]
- Klaviyo offers a free tier, but billing starts once the contact list grows past a set size. Check the contact count against the pricing before the list grows. Advice from 2023. [L0934, 2023]
- Canva's subscription cost about 11 GBP (roughly 14 USD) at the time of recording. It included a library of stock images, audio and stock videos that would otherwise cost extra. Check the current price and the licence terms before relying on this figure. [L0754, undated]

### Marketplace fees and paid promotion

- **Amazon Associates commissions:** Rates range from about 4.5 percent to 8.5 percent, depending on the number of transactions sent per month. Lower-volume affiliates earn the lower rate, so commissions stay small until the volume grows. [L0923, 2015]
- **eBay fees:** Commission depends on the product category. The category used in the lesson had about 7 percent commission. Payment processor fees such as PayPal's come on top of this. Calculate the full fee per category before you set prices. [L1441, 2018]
- **Paid boosts (coins):** Coins can start at $5, but $10 per day for the first three days is the usual starting level. Avoid overspending on paid boosts, because heavy spending defeats the organic purpose. [L1429, 2022]

### Health insurance abroad

- Compare the total annual cost of a plan, meaning the premium plus the copay or deductible, rather than the monthly premium. One example US plan at about 500 USD per month carried a 7,000 USD annual copay. [L1515, 2019]
- Many long-term travellers combine travel insurance for large catastrophic events, such as helicopter evacuation from a hike, with paying cash for routine care. Private care abroad can be affordable. For example, an emergency visit with ultrasound in Thailand cost about 120 USD in cash. [L1515, 2019]
- SafetyWing offers health and travel cover built for digital nomads and expats. It is an alternative for people who need coverage without a home-country plan. [L1515, 2019]
