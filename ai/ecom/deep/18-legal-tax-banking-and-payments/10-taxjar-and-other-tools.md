### Australia: GST

**Registration threshold**

- In Australia, a new business does not have to register for GST until its revenue reaches $75,000 or more in a single financial year. Before that it does not have to charge GST on sales [L0677, 2021-22].
- GST sales tax registration is not required for a new business until revenue reaches 75,000 Australian dollars in a single financial year. A business already GST-registered under an existing structure may continue as it is [L0702, 2021-22].
- GST registration only becomes mandatory once revenue in a single financial year reaches $75,000. Revenue from several businesses under the same legal entity is added together toward this threshold [L1405, 2021-22].
- A new business that has not made any sales does not need to register for GST yet. Registration can be done later, either directly or with an accountant, once it becomes relevant [L0702, 2021-22].
- In Australia you need at least an ABN from the start. GST registration is not required straight away for a sole trader. Registering early makes you collect and remit GST on every sale [L1405, 2021-22].

**Obligations once registered**

- In Australia, once a business is registered for GST it must collect GST on sales. Not collecting it when registered is illegal [L0677, 2021-22].
- In Australia, retail prices must include tax. Shopify's setting "All prices include tax" must be ticked for this [L0677, 2021-22].

**ABN and Shopify billing**

- A business registered for GST should add its Australian Business Number under General settings (Add ABN) so Shopify does not charge GST on its own subscription invoices. In the US the equivalent federal number is the EIN [L0677, 2021-22].
- In Australia, Shopify charges 10% GST on invoices. Adding an Australian Business Number (ABN) under Settings > General exempts invoices if you are GST registered [L1145, 2021-22].
- For a store based in Australia, Shopify may show a missing-tax notice. A GST-registered merchant can enter an ABN so that Shopify bills are exempt from GST [L1166, 2021-22].
- For a store based in Australia, Shopify bills are exempt from GST if the business is GST registered and enters its ABN in the tax settings. Without the ABN, the store will see a notice that tax information is missing [L1300, 2021-22].

**Shopify GST setup procedure**

1. Go to Settings > Taxes and open the Australia tax region [L0677, 2021-22].
2. Click Set up [L0677, 2021-22].
3. Click Collect GST [L0677, 2021-22].
4. Enter your ABN [L0677, 2021-22].
5. Click GST; Shopify then collects 10% GST on each order [L0677, 2021-22].
6. Under Tax calculations, tick "All prices include tax" [L0677, 2021-22].
7. Click Save [L0677, 2021-22].

**Australia: Process once threshold is reached**

1. Register for GST [L1405, 2021-22].
2. Lodge a BAS (Business Activity Statement) regularly [L1405, 2021-22].
3. Remit to the ATO the difference between GST collected from customers and GST paid to suppliers [L1405, 2021-22].

### Tax on Ad Spend

**Singapore GST on ad spend**

- Singapore GST on ad spend equals 9% of the spend converted into SGD. The speaker's example of $100k USD gave S$9,000 in GST. At about S$1.30 per USD, $100k is about S$130k, and the GST would be about S$11,700. Verify the conversion before budgeting [L0351, 2026].
- When estimating Singapore GST on USD ad spend, convert the spend to SGD at the current exchange rate first, then apply 9%. Treating USD 100k as S$100k understates the taxable base [L0351, 2026].

**Other countries**

- Ad spend in some other countries carries VAT rates higher than Singapore's 9% GST. The total tax on scaling spend must be included in the budget [L0351, 2026].
- Ads taxes in the UK and EU were flagged as a risk in the one source lesson [L0552, 2023].

**Sales tax on ad spend in the US**

- Sales tax obligations for ad spend are not separate from nexus. Ad spend in a state can be a factor, so check the nexus rules for your situation [L0357, 2019].

#### Further details

- Sales tax nexus is typically triggered at around $100,000 in sales per year per state. Collect sales tax only where you have nexus, and avoid collecting or paying it in states where you have none. [L0469, 2021]

- Sales tax applies in the state where the business is registered if that state has sales tax, for orders shipped to addresses in that state. Not every US state has sales tax. [L0806, 2024]

- For most states, economic nexus (a tax obligation in another state) starts when sales in that state exceed 100,000 dollars in a year. Check each state's rules with an accountant. [L0806, 2024]

- Before any state reaches its economic nexus threshold, a store charges sales tax only on orders shipping to the state where it is registered and holds a sales tax certificate. Each other state is added only when the store reaches that state's threshold. [L0972, 2025]
