## Business Credit Cards, Rewards and Supplier Payments

### Whether a business card is required

- A business credit card is not required to run a dropshipping business, and a debit card is enough to pay suppliers. [L0005, undated; L0699, 2020-26]
- A card is still recommended because business spending accumulates points quickly, and cash back, travel rewards or other benefits are substantial enough to make getting and keeping one worthwhile. [L0005, undated; L0255, 2025]
- Pay as many business expenses as possible with a business credit card, including advertising, Shopify subscription fees and cost of goods sold, because points, miles or cashback accumulate quickly in this model. [L0697, 2020-26]
- Many suppliers require payment by card and do not accept bank transfers for orders, so at least one business card, credit or debit, is needed before approaching suppliers. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Suppliers typically accept ACH transfers, checks and credit cards. [L0255, 2025]
- Suppliers generally do not care whether a personal or business credit card pays, as long as the payment is accepted. [L1069, 2019]
- Suppliers generally accept debit card payment, so orders can be paid with a debit card as well as a credit card. [L1111, 2020]
- Credit card terms are preferred with suppliers: the supplier keeps the card on file, charges it on each order, ships immediately, and the business earns points. [L0665, 2022]
- Credit card versus debit card for supplier payments: both work, but a credit card is preferred to build points or miles and to request limit increases based on daily payoff history. [L0268, 2020]
- An alternative to credit is to order from the supplier only after the customer's payment has been received. Points-earning credit cards are a useful option for supplier payments. [L1275, 2019]
- Prepaying suppliers by credit card earns card points on those purchases, which is an extra benefit of a dropshipping store, but this only applies when the card is in your name. [L0933, 2020-26]
- Use a personal credit card to buy inventory until the business is formed, then use a business credit card for purchases. [L0359, 2020]
- With good or decent credit, a rewards credit card can be used from the start to build points and miles for the business. [L1068, 2019]
- If you have no credit yet, give the supplier a debit card number to start with. Once you have built up credit, replace it with a credit card on file. [L0933, 2020-26]

### Starting with a checking account and debit card

- Open a business checking account and use its debit card for early business expenses before applying for a business credit card. This keeps business and personal funds separate and delays annual credit card fees until the business is profitable. [L0853, 2021]
- A business debit card should run on the Visa or Mastercard network. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Where a business credit card is not possible because of personal credit history or finances, a debit card linked to the business bank account can be used to buy products. [L1405, 2021-22; L0702, 2021-22]
- A debit card linked to the business bank account can serve as the card when credit is unavailable, and it is needed when a supplier requires a card or when a card must be linked to a Google Ads account. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Keep business cash flow positive rather than taking loans or funding programs. Pay ad spend and cost of goods on a business credit card and pay it off in full each month to earn rewards without carrying debt. [L0804, 2023]

### Scaling with multiple cards

- Use business credit cards for scaling, since AmEx offers a near-limitless limit. The balance must be paid off within the month or face high interest. [L0308, 2023]
- Build a business credit rating by using cards consistently and paying on time. [L0308, 2023]
- When ad spend outgrows one card's limit, combine several cards: personal credit cards at different banks, a business card and partner cards. [L0308, 2023]
- Example from the Black Friday run: personal cards at about 5,000 GBP, a business card at about 10,000 GBP and a partner's card at about 10,000 GBP. Total leverage was roughly 25,000 to 35,000 GBP combined. [L0308, 2023]
- Ask American Express for additional business cards under the same company once the 4X category limit has been reached. Each card can earn 4X on its own first 150,000 USD of ad spend, and cards can be used for separate businesses to keep books apart. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Rotating American Express cards over time, for example moving ad spend to a new card every few months, allows 4X earning to continue beyond the first 150,000 USD of spend under one company. The author reports no known limit on the number of cards. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Ask the card issuer for a credit limit increase while paying the balance off every day. Repeated increases over a few months can take a small starting limit to tens of thousands of dollars per card. [L0468, 2020]

### Building credit from a low limit

#### Credit path for a new store

1. Start with the debit card linked to the business bank account, and accept that the first orders wait for the 48-hour payout before they go to suppliers. [L0360, 2024]
2. Once profits build up in the account, open a secured credit card. [L0360, 2024]
3. Secure the card with a certificate of deposit (CD) equal to the wanted credit limit, for example a 5,000 USD CD for a 5,000 USD limit. [L0360, 2024; L0699, 2020-26]
4. Use the card and pay it off as orders come in. The credit score improves quickly. [L0360, 2024]
5. Once credit is strong, close the secured card and cash out the CD. [L0360, 2024]

#### Building business credit without a credit history

1. Open a business checking account, which comes with a business debit card. [L0699, 2020-26]
2. Pay suppliers with the business debit card while cash on hand is small. [L0699, 2020-26]
3. Build a cash reserve from sales over time. [L0699, 2020-26]
4. Once the reserve is large enough, open a secured credit card. [L0699, 2020-26]
5. Use the secured card and pay the full balance each cycle so credit builds. [L0699, 2020-26]
6. As credit improves, move to cards with better rewards, benefits and higher credit limits. [L0699, 2020-26]

- A secured credit card requires opening a certificate of deposit (CD) at the issuing bank. The bank holds the deposit as collateral against the credit limit, so a 5,000 USD limit needs a 5,000 USD CD, and the bank pays interest on the CD for its term. [L0699, 2020-26]
- Using a secured card and paying it off in full each cycle builds credit quickly. Strong credit can then qualify a business for cards with more rewards, benefits and higher limits. If the card is not paid, the bank can collect the balance from the CD. [L0699, 2020-26]
- Building credit with a new card: use a low-limit card, pay it in full each night, and request a limit increase every few weeks. A secured card can help if credit is poor. [L0963, 2022]
- Paying card balances down promptly as sales come in can raise credit limits quickly. With a low credit limit, suppliers can still be paid by debit card or wire transfer. [L0853, 2021]
- A card with a credit limit of about 2,500 USD allowed several orders a day when paid off regularly. Requesting credit limit increases every few months grew that capacity over time. [L1275, 2019]
- A store owner can build card credit by paying balances off promptly and asking the card issuer for periodic credit limit increases. This helps cover supplier orders before customer payments settle, which typically takes about 48 hours. [L1111, 2020]

#### Daily payoff and limit increases

- Build available credit limits by paying the balance every couple of days instead of waiting for the full billing cycle. This renews the credit line frequently and keeps credit available for continuous business purchases without waiting 30 days. [L0243, 2019]
- If the personal credit limit is low (300 to 1,000 USD), provide the credit card to suppliers. If the limit is insufficient for orders, offer a debit card instead or request multiple charges. Build credit over time, and eventually upgrade to a secured credit card with a limit of 5,000 USD or more. [L0270, 2021]

#### Daily payoff strategy (small limit)

1. Put business charges on the card throughout the day. [L0699, 2020-26]
2. Each morning, check that the day's store deposits have reached the business bank account. [L0699, 2020-26]
3. Pay off the full card balance. [L0699, 2020-26]
4. Repeat every day until the credit limit has grown. [L0699, 2020-26]

#### Requesting a credit limit increase

1. Set a recurring reminder to call the card company every week, every two weeks or every month, depending on sales volume. [L0699, 2020-26]
2. Call the card company. [L0699, 2020-26]
3. Explain that the card balance is paid off every night or morning. [L0699, 2020-26]
4. Ask whether the credit limit can be increased. [L0699, 2020-26]

#### Case: daily payoff and recurring calls

- **Starting point:** a small Chase credit card with a very low limit, paid off every morning after store deposits arrived. [L0699, 2020-26]
- **Actions:** full payoff every day, plus recurring calls to the card company asking for limit increases. [L0699, 2020-26]
- **Results:** the limit rose from about 5,000 USD to 10,000 USD, then 25,000 USD, then 100,000 USD, and later reached spending of up to 1,000,000 USD per month without problems. [L0699, 2020-26]
- Repeated payoffs and limit requests over a few months grow the limit. Ask the supplier to split charges, pay in full daily, and request increases monthly, as set out in the procedure below. [L0664, 2020; L0468, 2020]

#### Paying a supplier when the limit is below the order

1. Ask the supplier to split the charge into several smaller charges, for example 500 USD today and 500 USD tomorrow. [L0664, 2020]
2. Pay the card balance in full every day, not at the end of the month. [L0664, 2020]
3. Alternatively, use a debit card linked to your checking account. [L0664, 2020]
4. Call the card company every month and request a credit limit increase, pointing to the daily payments as proof. [L0664, 2020] The same approach, in short:

1. Use the business credit card to pay suppliers. [L0468, 2020]
2. Pay the card balance off every day while the credit limit is small. [L0468, 2020]
3. Build the limit over a few months by repeating the daily payoff. [L0468, 2020]
4. Consider a rewards card once the limit is larger. [L0468, 2020]

### Rewards math

- Card reward points from ad spend equal the spend in the capped bracket multiplied by the earn rate. For example, 100,000 USD of annual ad spend at 4x on the first 100,000 USD earns 400,000 points. Check current terms, since reward rates and caps change. [L0358, 2020]
- At 1 to 2 reward points per dollar, a card used for 100,000 USD in monthly supplier and ad spend would earn about 100,000 to 200,000 points per month. [L0702, 2021-22]
- An AmEx Business Gold card used for 150,000 USD of ad spend earns 600,000 points, which transfer one-to-one to 600,000 Delta SkyMiles. [L0903, 2021-22]
- At 100,000 USD monthly cost of goods sold on the Capital One Spark card, 2% cash back equals 2,000 USD per month. At 1,000,000 USD monthly spend across all cards, the reward equals 20,000 USD per month. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Break-even on the Capital One Spark card's 95 USD annual fee from year two: 95 USD divided by 2% equals 4,750 USD of annual spend. Spending 4,750 USD on the card in a year covers the fee. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Example: spending 5,000 USD in the first three months on a Capital One Spark Business card meets the 4,500 USD threshold for the 500 USD cash bonus and also earns 100 USD of 2% cash back. These figures assume the offer is still active. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Choose cash-out or spending on rewards according to a tax adviser's advice, since the reward can be used as cash, to cover purchases or in other ways. Confirm the tax treatment with an accountant before choosing how to use it. [L0903, 2021-22]

### Card split and bookkeeping

#### Keeping business charges on one or two cards

- Use a business credit card only for business expenses, and keep all business charges on one or two cards so month-end bookkeeping and reconciliation stay simple. Choosing a separate card for each expense category also helps avoid hunting for charges during reconciliation. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Use the American Express card for advertising spend (Google, Bing, remarketing, Facebook lead ads, Snapchat, Pinterest, TikTok and similar) and for shipping costs, since these categories earn 4X points. Use the Capital One card for cost of goods sold and all other business expenses such as Shopify, email and virtual assistant tools. [L0903, 2021-22]

#### Two-card setup

1. Apply for an American Express Business Gold card and use it only for advertising spend and shipping costs. [L0903, 2021-22]
2. Apply for a Capital One Spark Business card and use it for cost of goods sold (supplier orders) and all other operating expenses such as Shopify, apps and virtual assistants. [L0903, 2021-22]
3. Keep spending mostly on each card as assigned, so that the monthly statements map directly to the books. [L0903, 2021-22]
4. Pay off both cards in full each month. [L0903, 2021-22]

#### Separate cards per allocation account

1. Get a separate credit card for each expense account, or a card with a different number if the issuer allows it. [L1200, 2021-22]
2. Pay supplier invoices with the supplier card. [L1200, 2021-22]
3. Pay the supplier card off from the COGS account. [L1200, 2021-22]
4. Pay operating expenses with a separate operating card. [L1200, 2021-22]
5. Pay the operating card off from the operating expenses account. [L1200, 2021-22]

#### Statements, payoff and balances

- A business credit card is usually backed personally, so the balance appears on your personal credit file even when the card is issued for a business. Never carry a balance month to month. Pay the statement in full every month to avoid interest and protect your credit. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Pay credit card balances off in full every month. Any balance carried reduces the cash needed for other business costs and creates interest. As sales grow, the balance rises faster, so repayment must keep pace with spending. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Use the business credit card only for product purchases and marketing spend, never for personal purchases. Carrying personal debt on a business card is bad practice. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Use a credit card for the business as a whole, but only when the owner is personally comfortable with the credit arrangement. [L0702, 2021-22]
- Paying a supplier with a credit card covers the gap between a customer's payment and the payout from the store's payment processor. The card balance then gives roughly 30 days or more to repay, so inventory can be ordered before sales money reaches the bank account. [L0903, 2021-22]

### Billing cycles and statement timing

- Some credit cards have a 30-day billing cycle, while others, such as Amex in the example, bill in about 16 days. A card with a longer billing window gives more time before ad spend has to be paid. [L0351, 2026]
- The source describes Amex billing as roughly 30 days or more to repay. Check the actual cycle on your statement, since the one source example gives about 16 days for Amex. [L0903, 2021-22; L0351, 2026]
- Capital One Spark limits are based mainly on the owner's income and what the issuer will extend. Very high monthly sales will not automatically unlock a matching limit. Plan payments so the balance is cleared before the next statement closes. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Use the daily payoff method to make the balance clear before the statement closes. [L0699, 2020-26]

### Limits, estimators and charge card rules

- American Express business card limits are not a fixed number. The issuer sets them by purchase estimator and risk, so a large monthly spend can still be declined. Test a large purchase with the issuer's estimator before relying on it. [L0903, 2021-22]
- American Express charge cards must be paid in full each month. In exchange, the issuer gives very high credit limits and no preset spending limit is stated, though a practical ceiling still exists. [L0699, 2020-26; L0934, 2023]
- (2023) The Gold and Platinum charge cards were described as having no preset spending limit, but they must be paid in full every month. Apply early for one as a second card when the business credit line is too small. [L0934, 2023]
- (2023) Qualifying for a US business card generally requires a US business with its information on file. [L0934, 2023]
- The 4X Amex reward on the first 150,000 USD of ad spend is stated without a time period in the source, so it may not reset each year as an earlier summary assumed. Confirm the period with the issuer before planning spend around the cap. [L0903, 2021-22]
- The terms cited in the source, including the Amex and Capital One annual fees, the 4X and 2% earn rates, and the Capital One sign-up bonus, reflect offers as of September 2021. Check current issuer terms before applying, since fees, earn rates and bonuses change. [L0903, 2021-22]
- The source states that the newer Capital One Spark Business card requires the balance to be paid in full each month. Verify this with the issuer before assuming a revolving balance is allowed. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Apply for a business credit card with a rewards program such as cash back or travel points, since ad and product spending can earn meaningful rewards. The credit limit does not need to be high, and approval is what matters. [L1405, 2021-22]
