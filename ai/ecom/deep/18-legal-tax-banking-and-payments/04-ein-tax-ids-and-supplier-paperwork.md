## EIN, Tax IDs and Supplier Paperwork

### Which tax numbers apply to you

| Your situation | What applies | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US business | An EIN is required and free from the IRS. A sales tax certificate applies where your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one. | [L0278, 2025; L0118, undated; L1095, 2024] |
| Non-US resident starting a US business | Apply for an EIN through the international route. An ITIN is used for tax filing without an SSN. | [L1090, 2021; L0804, 2023; L0963, 2022] |
| European business not selling into the US | A VAT number is enough. An EIN is not needed. | [L0360, 2024] |
| Canadian seller | Sources disagree (see Conflicts below). | [L1277, 2020; L0853, 2021; L1116, 2025] |
| Store that does not sell in the US | No EIN is needed for that store. | [L0360, 2024] |

### Decision order: what to get and when

1. **Get the EIN from the IRS first.** It is the minimum to have before contacting suppliers. [L0006, undated; L0468, 2020; L1415, 2021-22]
2. **Get a sales tax certificate if your state has sales tax and suppliers ask for one.** [L0006, undated; L0118, undated; L0359, 2020]
3. **Keep a W-9 ready for suppliers that accept one.** [L0667, 2025; L0972, 2025]
4. **Apply before supplier outreach where possible.** Some suppliers require these documents and others do not. [L1116, 2025; L0808, 2026]
5. **If you are close to approval, have the documents ready within one to two weeks rather than re-contacting the supplier later.** [L0934, 2023]

### The core numbers and what they are

#### EIN (federal tax ID)

- An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a 9-digit number the IRS issues to a business. It works like a Social Security number for the company and is given to suppliers instead of your personal SSN. [L0118, undated; L0695, 2020-26; L1344, 2020-26]
- The format is two digits, a dash and seven digits. A tax ID in that format is the same kind of number as an EIN. [L0855, 2023]
- It is a federal tax ID. It is free from the IRS and is needed for tax reporting and for many business tasks in the US. [L0671, 2021; L1344, 2020-26]
- A US business typically needs an EIN for tax filings and for opening a business bank account. [L0675, 2025; L1095, 2024]
- It is needed to open a business bank account, apply for business licenses, file business taxes and fill in most supplier application forms. [L1415, 2021-22]
- It keeps business tax and accounting records separate from personal records. [L0468, 2020]
- Do not use your Social Security number in place of an EIN. Using the EIN keeps business and personal finances separate. [L1344, 2020-26]
- US business suppliers require an EIN. [L0278, 2025]
- Many suppliers ask for an EIN during approval. [L0247, 2021]
- Suppliers prefer an EIN over a personal SSN. [L0272, 2022]
- Suppliers use the EIN and the sales tax ID as interchangeable terms when they request a tax ID. [L0961, 2020] See Conflicts below.

#### Sales tax certificate, resale certificate and related names

- A sales tax certificate is the same thing as a reseller permit. [L0671, 2021]
- A sales tax certificate and a resale certificate are the same document, not two separate ones. They can be obtained online per state, and the process is typically fast and automated. [L0967, 2020]
- A sales tax permit and a reseller certificate are the same thing. They are based on the EIN and cover collecting and remitting sales tax. Suppliers asking for either are requesting the same document. [L0969, 2022]
- In the US, a reseller permit and a sales tax certificate are the same document. [L0934, 2023]
- In many states a sales tax license and a resale certificate are the same document, and suppliers may ask for either one. [L0855, 2023]
- A resale certificate and a sales tax ID refer to the same thing. Use them interchangeably when registering for sales tax. [L0963, 2022]
- A reseller number is essentially a sales tax ID. Some suppliers ask for it, and depending on your state you may need one. [L1274, 2018]
- A seller's permit is also called a sales tax permit, sales tax license or reseller's permit. [L1415, 2021-22]
- A business license, sales tax certificate and sales tax registration number usually refer to the same thing, though the name varies by state. [L1285, 2024]
- A supplier's request for a business license number generally means the same thing as a reseller permit or sales tax ID. [L0971, 2024]
- Names differ by state. Search for the state name plus "sales tax certificate" or "resale certificate." [L0667, 2025; L0934, 2023]

#### W-9 (form, not a number)

- The W-9 is an IRS form that a US business uses to request the taxpayer identification number of a US-based supplier or contractor. It collects this information in a standard format and applies only when the business pays US-based payees. [L1008, 2025]
- A W-9 is usually not needed, but fill it out if a supplier asks for it. [L1085, 2018]

#### ITIN

- An ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a tax ID for individuals who are not US citizens or residents and need to file US taxes. Use it when you are a non-US resident starting a US business. [L1090, 2021]
- An ITIN is used only for non-US citizens filing taxes without a Social Security number. [L0278, 2025]
- For non-US residents, an ITIN works similarly to an EIN, and suppliers may ask for one or the other. Ask each supplier what they require, and check forums and communities for answers. [L1277, 2020]

#### VAT and corporate tax numbers

- VAT is specific to Europe and an EIN to the US. A VAT number is enough for European business. [L0360, 2024] (2024 advice)
- A corporate tax number is not the same as a VAT number. Suppliers may ask for a specific one before authorizing a store. Asking which number a supplier requires before submitting the application avoids delays. [L0360, 2024]

### Getting your EIN

#### Free application through the IRS

- An EIN is free on the official IRS website (irs.gov). Many third-party sites charge for the same EIN, so use only official sources. [L0667, 2025; L1274, 2018]
- A third-party service is not needed. Some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing. [L0468, 2020]
- Paid services that advertise EIN setup are unnecessary for US residents, though non-residents may need paid help to obtain one. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Paid helpers are not needed for this simple process, since many online ads charge fees to do these steps for you. [L1415, 2021-22]

**Procedure: free EIN in the US** [L1344, 2020-26]

1. Search for "EIN number" on Google.
2. Do not click the paid results marked as ads, because these companies charge a fee for setting up an EIN.
3. Click the first organic result, which is the IRS site at irs.gov.
4. Apply for the EIN online and answer the questions the IRS asks. No paid helper is needed.
5. Expect to receive the EIN typically within about 15 minutes. [L1344, 2020-26]

**Procedure: apply for an EIN** [L0963, 2022]

1. Use only the IRS website, not paid third-party services.
2. If you live outside the US, use the international applicant route, which requires a phone call.
3. Check the IRS phone hours before calling. [L0963, 2022]

**Procedure: apply for an EIN** [L0468, 2020]

1. Go to the IRS website and apply for an employer identification number (EIN) for free.
2. The application takes about five minutes.
3. Do not pay a third-party service for the EIN, since some companies charge about $200 or more for the same free filing.
4. Use the EIN instead of your social security number when applying for supplier accounts. [L0468, 2020]

#### How long it takes

| Time stated | Source |
|---|---|
| 5 to 10 minutes, number received immediately on completion | [L0118, undated] |
| About 10 minutes online | [L0359, 2020] |
| About five minutes | [L0360, 2024] (2024), [L0468, 2020] |
| 5 to 10 minutes, 9-digit EIN produced | [L0695, 2020-26] |
| 5 to 10 minutes | [L0671, 2021] |
| About 15 minutes online | [L1274, 2018] |
| About 15 minutes, received typically within this time | [L1344, 2020-26] |

- An online EIN application usually takes 5 to 10 minutes and produces a 9-digit EIN. [L0695, 2020-26]
- The IRS online EIN application only works during IRS business hours, even though it is an online form. If you try early in the morning or late at night, it may not be available, so plan the application for business hours. [L1344, 2020-26]
- Get the EIN as early as possible. It is free on the IRS website, is required by many suppliers, and takes about 10 minutes online. [L0359, 2020]

#### Timing relative to your LLC

- Apply for your EIN directly on the IRS website after your LLC is officially approved. [L0118, undated]
- An EIN can be obtained without an LLC. When reaching out to suppliers without an LLC yet, get an EIN from the IRS website for free in about five minutes. [L0272, 2022]
- Form an LLC first only if you want to give company details at the time of approval. [L0852, 2020]
- Not having an LLC or S-Corp usually does not affect supplier approvals. The coach had never seen that happen. [L1277, 2020]
- A business license and insurance are optional for an online store. However, get an EIN at a minimum if you do business in the US. [L1277, 2020]

#### Sole proprietors

- You can contact suppliers as a sole proprietor once the store is built, and no legal entity is needed. [L0852, 2020]
- A US business without a Social Security number can get an EIN from the IRS website. [L0801, 2021]
- Get an EIN even as a sole proprietor. It is only needed for businesses in the US. [L1277, 2020]
- A sole proprietor without employees does not strictly need an EIN. They can obtain one or use the owner's SSN instead. [L1415, 2021-22]
- A sole proprietor can be approved by some US suppliers, according to reports from store owners, but this was not verified by the lesson. [L0359, 2020]

#### Reusing an existing EIN

- An existing EIN from a previous business may be reusable for a new store, although the business type on the EIN may need updating. Verify with a tax professional before reuse. [L1111, 2020]
- Reusing an old EIN from a business that never started is probably allowed, but verify it with a lawyer or a legal forum before you do. [L1277, 2020]

#### Who applies and how the form is filled

- An EIN can be applied for directly by the company. The title used on the form (president, CEO) is a matter of how you describe yourself. A partnership with several officers requires more care. [L0834, 2021]

#### Why the EIN matters for pacing

- Tax documents, EIN and state registrations can be completed after you start, because many suppliers still approve new retailers without them. [L0934, 2023] (2023 advice)
- For a foreign founder, an EIN can take a long time, sometimes up to around 60 days. Keep building the store and contacting suppliers in the meantime, and hold back top-tier suppliers until your business documents are complete. [L0968, 2021]

### Applying from outside the US

#### Non-US citizens and residents

- Non-US citizens must call the IRS to apply. US citizens can apply online at irs.gov. [L0270, 2021]
- Non-US residents cannot get an EIN online and must call the IRS, which begins its process at IRS.gov. [L0469, 2021]
- A non-US resident can get an EIN on the IRS website, though a phone call may be part of the process. [L0359, 2020]
- Non-US applicants can get an EIN by calling the IRS international applicant number listed on the IRS EIN page. US citizens can use the same online process as in the course. [L0804, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Applicants outside the US may be directed to call in instead of applying online, so check the foreign applicant route before applying. [L0968, 2021]
- Non-US businesses can apply for the equivalent tax identification number on the same site. [L0801, 2021]
- To obtain a US resale certificate as a non-US resident, form a US business entity first and apply through that entity. A virtual address is not enough, a real company address is required. [L0836, 2024] (2024 advice)
- Stripe Atlas was named as one common formation service, but a state-issued resale certificate, not Stripe Atlas, is what suppliers usually ask for. [L0836, 2024] (2024 advice)

#### Procedure for the international route

- See the international applicant procedure under Getting your EIN above. [L0963, 2022]

#### Canadian sellers

- A Canadian seller does not need an EIN, because it is an IRS number for US businesses. [L1277, 2020]
- A Canadian business selling into the US probably needs a US tax ID, which is a FEIN that can be obtained online from the IRS. This is not certain, so confirm it with Canadians who already run US businesses or with a tax professional. [L0853, 2021]
- Some US suppliers ask Canadian sellers for an EIN or federal tax ID, which requires a short call to the IRS. Requirements differ by brand, so check with other Canadian sellers in community forums. [L1279, 2021]
- A business registered in Canada can get a US EIN from the IRS by phone and should not get a resale certificate first. When an application asks for one, leave that section blank or state that the business is based in Canada and has no nexus in any US state. A state tax ID and resale certificate follow later, once a state threshold is reached. [L1116, 2025] (2025 advice)

#### Foreign entities and sales tax

- A foreign entity without a nexus in any US state probably does not need a sales tax certificate, but this is unverified. Submit the supplier application and check whether approval requires it. [L0853, 2021]
- Do not collect resale certificates for states where you have no obligation, just because a supplier asks for one. Only obtain them for states where you have physical presence or where you crossed the sales threshold in a year. [L0968, 2021]

### Sales tax certificate and state resale documents

#### When you need one

- A sales tax certificate is required by most suppliers if your state has a sales tax. If your state has no sales tax, you do not need one. [L0118, undated]
- A sales tax certificate is needed only if the supplier requires it and the business is in a state that has sales tax. In states without sales tax, no certificate is needed. In other states, the certificate can be applied for online in about 10 to 15 minutes. [L0359, 2020]
- Some suppliers ask for a state sales tax certificate and some do not. Requirements depend on the state where the business is based. Having the certificate does no harm and is required in some states. [L0664, 2020]
- Whether you need a resale certificate depends on the supplier and on your state. Some suppliers ask for one and others do not, and some states do not issue them. Before a state's sales tax threshold is reached, you usually do not need a tax certificate in that state. [L0961, 2020]
- Many suppliers ask for a seller's permit or sales tax certificate, but not all do. Requirements are state specific, and states without sales tax do not need a certificate there. [L1088, 2020]
- Taxes and sales tax: for each state, look up the state's reseller certificate. The certificate allows collecting and remitting sales tax and is required by most suppliers. [L0964, 2023] (2023 advice)
- In a state where the business is based, a sales tax certificate is required, and suppliers may ask for a tax-exempt form. The tax-exempt certificate usually requires an existing sales tax certificate. [L0665, 2022]
- Whether suppliers require an EIN and a sales tax ID before approval varies by supplier and by state. Ask each supplier directly rather than assuming the same requirement. [L0359, 2020]
- Some suppliers ask for a resale certificate and others do not, so ask each supplier before assuming you need one. [L0468, 2020]
- Get a resale certificate early, since many suppliers ask for one even though not all do. Requirements depend on the state where you do business. [L0808, 2026] (2026 advice)
- Apply for a state resale certificate or sales tax permit early, as suppliers often ask for it when you are approved. [L0667, 2025] (2025 advice)
- Do not apply for resale permits in every state a supplier asks for if you have no sales nexus there. Offer a W-9 instead, along with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667, 2025] (2025 advice)
- Provide a resale certificate for the state where your business operates when a supplier asks for one. Do not treat the certificate as a general document for every state. [L0470, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Get a sales tax certificate and an EIN tax ID eventually. Obtaining them sooner rather than later avoids delays with suppliers that ask for them. [L1088, 2020]
- A reseller number is not urgent in the early stage. [L1274, 2018]
- Purchases from suppliers are not taxed when you resell, because you give the supplier a W-9 if requested or your sales tax certificate, also called a resale certificate. Sales tax is separate from income tax. [L0972, 2025] (2025 advice)

#### Getting a state certificate

**Procedure: sales tax certificate (general)** [L0118, undated]

1. Search Google for "[Your State] sales tax certificate" and use only official.gov websites; do not pay third-party sites.
2. Complete online registration if available in your state (most allow this and often free).
3. You will receive a downloadable PDF or physical certificate by mail. [L0118, undated]

**Procedure: resale or sales tax certificate** (2023 advice) [L0251, 2023]

1. Go to your state's.gov website.
2. Search for "[Your State] resale certificate" or "[Your State] sales tax certificate."
3. Complete the online form (typically 5 minutes).
4. Receive your certificate number.
5. Provide this number to suppliers to purchase at wholesale without sales tax. [L0251, 2023]

**Procedure: resale certificate** (2025 advice) [L1116, 2025]

1. Search Google for the registration state plus sales tax certificate or resale certificate.
2. Open the official.gov page of that state.
3. Complete the online form. Many states give a PDF immediately; some mail it, which can take about a week.
4. Get it before supplier outreach, because some suppliers require it and others do not. [L1116, 2025]

**Procedure: sales tax certificate online** [L0934, 2023]

- In a state with sales tax, search for the state name plus sales tax certificate or reseller certificate and apply through the state's online form. [L0934, 2023]

**Procedure: sales tax ID after your EIN** [L1113, 2022]

- Get a sales tax ID after your EIN, as early as practical, because many suppliers ask for it. The setup is usually done online, and states without sales tax do not need one. [L1113, 2022]

**Procedure: when a supplier asks for a resale number and the state has no certificate** [L0854, 2022]

1. Search for an exemption form on the official state government website.
2. If none is listed, call or email the state's contact number and ask for a document stating you are exempt.
3. Send that document to the supplier. [L0854, 2022]

**Procedure: resale certificate in a state with no registration yet** [L1092, 2022]

1. Ask the accountant for the exact name of the state sales and use tax form.
2. Search that form name online to check whether it can be filed online.
3. If it is available online, file it there instead of visiting a city or county office. [L1092, 2022]

#### Multi-state and specific states

- Do not apply for resale permits in every state a supplier asks for if you have no sales nexus there. Offer a W-9 instead, along with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667, 2025]
- A seller's permit is required in your own home state, and most suppliers will ask for it. You generally do not need a permit in every state, although some suppliers may request one per state. [L1415, 2021-22]
- A supplier that does not receive a Florida annual resale certificate may offer to charge 7% Florida sales tax on orders shipped within Florida, or may refuse shipments to Florida. Send your home-state resale certificate first and state that you have no economic nexus in Florida. [L0835, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Resale certificate rules differ by state. A business formed in Delaware may not need a sales tax certificate because Delaware has no sales tax. A Delaware wholesaler license was mentioned as an option for businesses set up there. [L0836, 2024] (2024 advice)
- For a business in Delaware, the supplier example is handled with a W-9 instead. See the procedure under What to tell suppliers. [L0253, 2024]

### W-9 forms

#### When and how to use a W-9

- Fill out a W-9 when a supplier asks for one. [L1085, 2018]
- Give a W-9 if requested, or your sales tax certificate, when purchasing for resale. [L0972, 2025] (2025 advice)
- Offer a W-9 instead of applying for resale permits in states where you have no nexus, together with your home-state resale certificate. [L0667, 2025] (2025 advice)
- A D&B (Dun & Bradstreet) number is not necessary for the business. [L1085, 2018]

**Procedure: handling a W-9 request from a large business customer** [L1396, 2021-22]

1. Keep a blank Form W-9 PDF in the PDF files section of the corporate information board.
2. When a customer asks for it, print the form.
3. Fill in the business name and the tax information.
4. Send the completed form to the customer.
5. Keep the file ready because large companies request it repeatedly. [L1396, 2021-22]
