### Creator content and usage rights

- Usage rights define how long the brand may use the video, in which territory, on which channels and formats, and what extra costs apply to keep using it. Include all of these in the brief before filming. [L0579, 2025-26]
- Define usage rights in the offer: duration, start date (for example six months from the date the first post goes live on the creator's account), territory, and every format where the brand may use the content. Formats should include websites and e-commerce product pages, email marketing, digital ads, and both computer and mobile platforms. [L0783, 2025-26]
- In many contracts a set usage period, such as 30 days, is included in the agreed rate. Continued use as paid media beyond that period can be charged an additional fee per month. [L0579, 2025-26]
- Example usage terms from a keyboard brand's brief: 6 months of use, the term ending six months after the first social post goes live on the creator's account, worldwide, across brand-controlled social accounts, brand and third-party websites, e-commerce product pages, email marketing, digital ads, and on computer and mobile platforms. Fee: $20,000 for this package. [L0579, 2025-26]
- Before reusing creator-made video, get written usage rights from the creator covering ownership, how long you may use it, and where it may appear. Posting creator content without approval can cause legal trouble. [L0784, 2025-26]
- If creator content will run as paid media, the agreement needs a clause stating the usage duration. Such clauses usually carry a fee for the usage period. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Example: if a brand uses creator content for 60 days and wants to renew for another month, the creator might be paid about 250 USD per extra month of renewal. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Some brands negotiate perpetual usage rights, but this is less favourable for the creator, so the request often meets resistance. Agree on a finite term and a renewal option instead. [L0784, 2025-26]
- Define the usage terms clearly before agreeing on a fee. State which exclusivity and which usage rights you need. Exclusive usage rights, or the right to repurpose content across channels and paid ads, usually require a higher fee. [L0785, undated]
- Negotiate usage terms openly with the creator so both sides are satisfied. Document the rights granted to each party in writing. [L0785, undated]
