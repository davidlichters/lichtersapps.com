## Store Policies and Legal Pages

### Store Policies and Legal Pages

### Which pages are required, by source

The required set differs by source and by platform. Each source's list is kept below, so you can see what it asks for.

| Source | Required set | Context | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Merchant Center approval | Four: privacy policy, terms of service, shipping policy, refunds/returns/exchanges policy | Approval requirement for ecommerce stores | [L0014, undated] |
| Five pages before opening accounts | Privacy policy, terms and conditions, contact page, shipping policy, refund/return policy | Before Google Merchant Center, Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, Facebook Ads, or the Facebook Shop channel | [L0377, 2020-26] |
| Footer list before traffic | Privacy policy, terms and conditions, contact us, shipping policy, refunds and exchanges | Create and link in the footer before sending traffic | [L0162, undated] |
| Four footer-linked policies | Return/refund policy, shipping policy, privacy policy, terms of service | All four must exist and be linked in the footer | [L0167, undated] |
| Google Ads core pages | Privacy policy, terms of service, returns and refunds | Add before running paid traffic | [L0421, 2020-26] |
| Supplier-ready store | About Us, Contact Us, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service | Before supplier outreach; privacy and terms are also required for a store that runs ads | [L0509, 2020-26] |
| one source launch gate | Shipping policy, refund policy, privacy policy | Missing any of these should block the launch | [L1324, 2026] |
| one source pre-launch | Privacy policy, terms of service, contact information, refund or return policy | The pages shoppers look for and that Shopify links at checkout | [L1250, 2026] |
| one source written policies | Return and refund policy, privacy policy, terms of service, shipping policy, contact information | Each shows "No policy set" until added | [L1167, 2026] |
| one source full legal section | Terms and conditions, shipping and delivery, returns and exchanges, customer service and complaint handling, data privacy and security | Every page must describe what the shop really does | [L1250, 2026] |
| one source cross-border store | Privacy, terms of service, returns and refunds, shipping, payment terms, contact details | Builds trust, shows compliance, helps resolve disputes with customers and payment providers | [L1233, undated] |
| one source checkout minimum | Refund and shipping pages with company name and at least an email address; privacy and terms are secondary but good to have | Pages shown in checkout | [L1260, undated] |
| one source Shopify list | Return policy, privacy policy, terms of service, shipping policy, contact information | Shown under Settings > Policies | [L1233, undated] |

- Practical rule: build the five pages from the 5-page set (privacy, terms, contact, shipping, refund/return), which covers every list above, and add payment terms, which the cross-border list requires [L0377, 2020-26; L1167, 2026; L1233, undated]. Add About Us if you will approach suppliers [L0509, 2020-26].
- Launch with all required pages live: a missing privacy policy can lead to Google Merchant Center account suspension, and repeated violations can lead to a ban [L0014, undated].
- Merchant Center flags an account automatically when required pages are missing, and you must then request a manual human review, which slows the process [L0377, 2020-26].
- Create the pages before opening any ad or merchant account; this helps avoid suspensions and closures [L0377, 2020-26].
- Google Merchant Center requires the merchant to confirm they have read the policies and that the account complies with them [L0377, 2020-26].
- Completing these pages does not make the store fully compliant; other compliance topics are handled in each platform's own setup [L0377, 2020-26].
- Policies can be refined after supplier relationships are finalised. The launch goal is supplier-ready functionality, not perfection [L0090, undated].
- Policies must be published on the site and must match what is entered in Google Merchant Center [L0966, 2025].
- Policies must be easy to find. Some traffic platforms require easy access before approving ads and may refuse to run ads otherwise [L1303, 2021-22].
- Privacy policy and terms of service must match what Google Ads requires [L0965, 2024].

### Work order in Shopify

#### Compliance setup order (complete sequence)

1. Generate the privacy policy with Shopify's privacy policy generator. Answer the company questions, then request the document by email [L0377, 2020-26].
2. Add the required extra privacy text to the generated policy, if it is missing [L0377, 2020-26]:
   - a remarketing sentence at the end of the introduction or closing;
   - a Google Analytics sentence as the last line of the third-party services section;
   - a Google "pref" cookie line as the last line of the cookies section.
3. Generate the terms and conditions with Shopify's terms generator. Skip the trial, enter company and website information, and receive the document by email [L0377, 2020-26].
4. Review the generated terms, modify anything needed, then publish them on the store [L0377, 2020-26].
5. Create or confirm a contact page with at least two contact methods, for example phone number and business email, optionally Shopify's contact form [L0377, 2020-26].
6. Create a shipping policy stating when orders ship, average transit time, shipping cost, and any free or upgraded shipping options [L0377, 2020-26].
7. Create a refund and return policy stating the refund rules, how to request a refund, how returns work, and how a return is started [L0377, 2020-26].
8. Link all five pages in the footer menu [L0377, 2020-26].
9. Add two contact methods to the footer [L0377, 2020-26].
10. Confirm accepted payment methods are displayed in the footer [L0377, 2020-26].

#### Publish order
1. Go to Settings > Policies (the legal settings page) [L1167, 2026].
2. For each policy (return and refund, privacy, terms of service, shipping, contact information), either turn on the automated policy option or click Insert template [L1167, 2026].
3. Replace every placeholder with your real business details: trading name, business address, business phone number and contact information [L1167, 2026].
4. Click Save, then Publish for each policy [L1167, 2026].

#### Generate with Shopify's free tools

- Shopify has free privacy policy and terms generators. They ask for company information and send the generated document by email, so you can paste it onto the policy pages [L0377, 2020-26].
- Shopify Policy Generator (shopify.com/tools/policy-generator): enter company name, email address, business address and website link. Shopify emails a ready-made privacy policy or terms text that can be pasted without changes. Other generators on the same page create refund and terms texts [L1233, undated].
- Privacy generator, skip-trial step: at the bottom of the page, click the link labelled "I'll skip the Shopify trial now, just create my policy". Enter the company information and click "send me my privacy policy" to receive the generated document by email [L0377, 2020-26].
- Generated text changes over time. Regenerate privacy and terms pages from the current generator and replace older text when the generator updates [L0965, 2024].
- Check the generated privacy policy regularly against the current store setup [L0855, 2023].
- a privacy policy from Shopify's generator, used with only a few modifications, was roughly 95% standard Shopify text [L0247, 2021].

#### Automated policy or template

| Option | How to use it | Behaviour | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use automated policy (toggle) | Settings > Policies, open the policy, turn on "Use automated policy", click Save | Text stays in sync with your store settings and Shopify's latest templates and updates on its own. Still check that business details are correct | [L0090, undated; L1048, 2020-26; L1167, 2026] |
| Insert template | Click Insert template (Terms of Service), or Create from template (privacy, terms) | Static text. You replace every placeholder and edit the text yourself | [L0090, undated; L0509, 2020-26; L1048, 2020-26] |

- The automated option exists on each policy page under Settings > Policies [L1167, 2026].
- Creating a page from a template means you confirm you have read the template disclaimer [L0677, 2021-22].

### Privacy policy

#### Creating the page

- Shopify admin steps (template route):
  1. Click Settings in the bottom left corner of the Shopify Admin.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page and click Policies.
  3. In the Privacy policy section, click Create from template.
  4. Read the highlighted instructions at the top of the generated text.
  5. Delete the highlighted instructions after reading them.
  6. Enter today's date as the last updated date.
  7. Replace the generic example domain with your store's actual URL.
  8. Edit any other template parts that refer to your business [L0509, 2020-26].
- Automated route: go to Settings > Policies, click Privacy policy, turn on the Use automated policy toggle at the top, then click Save [L1048, 2020-26]. The same toggle is described as Turn on the automated policy option [L0090, undated].

#### Editing the content

- Delete the "notes to merchant" section for now. It applies only once you run ads. Update the privacy policy with ad-specific details before ads go live [L0509, 2020-26].
- In the contact section, delete any phone number or physical location you do not yet have. Keep an email address there: the default is the email used at Shopify sign-up, and it can be changed in the same place at any time [L0509, 2020-26].
- Add the extra privacy text if missing: the remarketing sentence, the Google Analytics sentence and the Google "pref" cookie line described in the compliance order [L0377, 2020-26].
- The privacy policy should cover data privacy and security as part of the full legal section [L1250, 2026].

#### Business name in the policy

- The trade name (DBA) is used as the store name in terms and privacy policies. The speaker gives no legal advice on whether the LLC name must be included. Older (2023) [L0964, 2023].
- Conflicting values, both kept:
  - Practice: use the DBA as the store name and do not add the LLC name. Applies where the speaker's practice is followed [L0964, 2023].
  - Caution: the privacy policy may need to name both the store name and the legal LLC name, since they often differ. Unverified. A lawyer should confirm the exact wording required in your jurisdiction. Older (2021) [L0853, 2021].

#### Missing pieces

- The ready-made policy set (terms, shipping, returns, customer support and data protection) contains no privacy policy text. Shopify's written policies list still expects a Privacy policy page, so write and publish it separately [L1250, 2026].

### Terms of service

#### Creating the page

- Shopify admin steps (Insert template route):
  1. Go to Settings > Policies.
  2. Click Insert template. Shopify generates the full policy.
  3. Replace any bracketed placeholders with your business name and support email.
  4. Optionally leave address and phone number blank.
  5. Click Save [L0090, undated].
- Full template procedure:
  1. Go to Settings > Policies.
  2. In the Terms of service section, click Create from template.
  3. Replace the sentence that names the operator of the website with your store name.
  4. Read the whole text and replace every generic item with your own information.
  5. Remove references to policies you have not created yet, such as the refund policy.
  6. Replace the link to the privacy policy with a real link to the Privacy Policy you just created.
  7. Fill in the contact information section with your details, or remove lines that do not apply.
  8. Click Save [L0509, 2020-26].
- Shorter variant:
  1. Go to Settings > Policies and click Terms of service.
  2. Click Insert template at the top to have Shopify generate the template.
  3. Replace the link to privacy policy placeholder with a link to the Privacy Policy page you created.
  4. Replace the trading name placeholder with the store name.
  5. Replace the contact email placeholder with the support email address.
  6. Leave the business address and phone number fields empty if only an email address is provided.
  7. Click Save [L1048, 2020-26].

Both variants above describe the same Shopify route. Their wording differs, and both are kept.

#### What the terms must cover

- Terms set the rules for using the website and buying products. They should cover payment, shipping, returns and warranties [L1233, undated].
- The terms should state that the store may update them at any time and that changes are posted on the page. They should also cover correct use of the site, prohibited activities and age restrictions [L1250, 2026].
- Standard template text. Use it as a starting point and edit it to fit your business:

```
Terms and Conditions

By visiting our website and buying products from us, you agree to follow these terms and conditions. This includes correct use of the site, activities that are not allowed, and age restrictions. We may change these terms at any time, and any changes will be posted on this page.
```
[L1250, 2026]

#### Publishing and reading

- The generated terms are the store's real legal terms, not placeholder text. Read them, adjust anything that does not fit the business, and only then publish [L0377, 2020-26].
- In one store the generated terms were published unchanged. The owner must still read them before going live [L0377, 2020-26].
- Templates are a starting point. Edit each one to match how your business handles orders, refunds and customer data before publishing [L1124, 2026].
- Remove any reference to a policy you have not built yet, and link the privacy policy to the real page [L0509, 2020-26; L1048, 2020-26].

### Shipping policy

#### Creating the policy (Shopify route)

1. In Shopify Admin, go to Settings and select Policies. Open Shipping Policy [L0090, undated].
2. Get the Shipping Policy AI Prompt that starts with "You are a professional e-commerce operations copywriter" and copy it completely. The full text of this prompt is not included in these source units, so take it verbatim from the course prompt file and do not rewrite it [L0090, undated].
3. Fill in the Student Inputs: store name, support email address (use support@yourdomain.com format), shipping region, handling time, transit time, delivery timeframe, and shipping method details [L0090, undated].
4. Use the standard timeframes below unless different circumstances apply [L0090, undated].
5. Create the policy using the prompt. Insert the generated policy into Shopify and save [L0090, undated].

| Stage | Standard timeframe | Ids |
|---|---|---|
| Handling | 3-5 business days | [L0090, undated] |
| Transit | 3-7 business days | [L0090, undated] |
| Total delivery | 7-12 business days | [L0090, undated] |

#### Dropshipping timeframes

| Item | Value | Ids |
|---|---|---|
| Processing time | 1-3 business days | [L1233, undated] |
| USA delivery | 7-12 business days | [L1233, undated] |
| UK delivery | 5-10 business days | [L1233, undated] |
| Australia and New Zealand delivery | 7-15 business days | [L1233, undated] |
| European countries delivery | 6-10 business days | [L1233, undated] |

- These figures are rough estimates in business days. Before publishing, replace them with the delivery times your supplier and carrier actually achieve, because promises the store cannot keep lead to customer complaints [L1233, undated].
- Which figure applies: the standard table applies for the usual case, and the dropshipping table applies to supplier-fulfilled stores, as the source describes them. Both values are given here so you can choose the one that matches your supply chain [L0090, undated; L1233, undated].
- Example values from a 2026 course: orders leave in 2-3 working days and reach customers in 6-10 days [L1250, 2026].

#### What the shipping policy must contain

- Shipping methods, estimated delivery times, shipping costs and international shipping details, and which customs duties or taxes the customer must pay [L1233, undated].
- Processing time, delivery times per country, shipping fees charged, and the domestic shipping time for the store's home country [L1233, undated].
- When orders ship, average transit time, shipping cost, and any free or upgraded shipping options [L0377, 2020-26].
- Confirmation and tracking email, how shipping rates are calculated at checkout, which countries are served and how international shipping times can differ, and that customs or weather can delay delivery [L1250, 2026].
- If the offer includes free shipping, state it clearly, and make the shipping cost terms transparent [L0167, undated].
- Google Shopping requires the customer to know the exact shipping cost before checkout begins. A free-shipping header must be matched by an explicit free-shipping statement in the shipping policy. Vague wording about shipping costs is a common reason for Merchant Center flags [L0724, 2020-26].
- If products are built for one market, say so on the shipping page, for example that they need a 110V electrical system and are certified only to US standards. This protects the store from electrical-compatibility complaints from buyers elsewhere. It is a condition-specific notice, not a general legal disclaimer. Older (2021-22) [L1264, 2021-22].
- Shipping and refund policies must be shown in checkout with the company name and at least an email address [L1260, undated].
- Text for the shipping policy prompt, with placeholders, from the 2026 course:

```
Write a shipping policy for my Shopify store called [Your Store Name]. Orders are shipped within [X-X] working days and usually arrive within [X-X] working days. Cover the processing time, the confirmation and tracking email, how shipping rates are calculated at checkout, which countries are served and how international shipping times can differ, and that delivery can be delayed by customs or weather. Use [placeholders] for any values I have not given you.
```
[L1250, 2026]

- Second shipping prompt, for a store with known figures, from the 2026 course:

```
Draft a shipping policy for my Shopify store, [Your Store Name]. Orders leave our hands in 2-3 working days and reach customers in 6-10 days. The text must cover processing time, how checkout calculates shipping rates, international shipping, and the tracking email sent when an order ships. State that customs or weather can delay delivery. Put [placeholders] wherever I haven't supplied a figure.
```
[L1250, 2026]

### Refund, return and exchange policy

#### Creating the policy (Shopify route)

1. In Shopify Admin, go to Settings > Policies [L0090, undated].
2. Click Return and Refund Policy [L0090, undated].
3. Use the Return & Refund Policy AI Prompt. Its full text is not included in these source units, so take it verbatim from the course prompt file [L0090, undated].
4. Fill in the Student Inputs: store name, support email, return window (recommended: 30 days), condition requirements, restocking fee (recommended: none), return shipping responsibility, cancellation policy, damage reporting timeframe [L0090, undated].
5. Leave most other defaults as-is for now [L0090, undated].
6. Generate the policy [L0090, undated].
7. Paste into Shopify [L0090, undated].
8. Click Save [L0090, undated].

#### Return window: values by context

| Context | Window | Applies when | Ids |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard recommendation | 30 days, no restocking fee | Default for a new store | [L0090, undated] |
| Dropshipping, defect cases | 14 or 30 days money-back guarantee | Defective, damaged or not-as-described items. Chinese suppliers usually do not accept returns | [L1233, undated] |
| 30-day guarantee in sales | 30-day money-back guarantee | Removes purchase risk; shown near the purchase decision | [L1209, undated] |
| EU shipping | 14-day right to cancel or return for any reason | Applies whenever the store ships into the EU, even if the normal window is shorter | [L1327, 2026] |

- Make the 30-day guarantee visible near the purchase decision [L1209, undated].
- If the store ships into the EU, keep the 14-day right to cancel or return for any reason in the returns policy, even when the normal return window is shorter [L1327, 2026].
- Chinese suppliers usually do not accept returns but do refund or replace items that arrive damaged, defective or not as described. The return policy should therefore offer a 14-day or 30-day money-back guarantee for these cases only [L1233, undated].
- Dropshipping clause to adapt. Replace the bracketed placeholder with your support email and choose 14 or 30 days:

```
We provide a 14-day (or 30-day) money-back guarantee if you receive a defective, damaged product or if the product is not as described. Please contact us at [support email] and we will do our best to help. Please send us a photo or video as proof so our team can investigate. If our team approves your request, we will issue a full refund or a replacement, as agreed.
```
[L1233, undated]

#### Return address and return conditions

- For a new store without a return address, the owner's own address or a virtual address can be used as the return address. Customers pay the return shipping label [L1233, undated].
- A returned item is accepted only if it is in good condition with intact, unopened packaging. Add these conditions to the return policy [L1233, undated].
- If you have no return address, remove or change that line in the generated policy [L1233, undated].

#### Content of the refund policy

- Rules for refunds, exchanges and returns, the conditions for returns, time limits and any restocking fees [L1233, undated].
- Eligibility, return window in days from purchase, proof of purchase, non-returnable item types, how refunds are processed and how long they take, when partial refunds apply, when exchanges are allowed, who pays return shipping and whether it is refundable, sale-item rules, and how customers contact you [L1250, 2026].
- Refund rules, how to request a refund, how returns work, and how a return is started [L0377, 2020-26].
- Return prompt from the 2026 course:

```
Write a return and refund policy for my Shopify store called [Your Store Name]. Cover eligibility, the return window in days from the date of purchase, the proof of purchase requirement, non-returnable item types, how refunds are processed and how long they take, when partial refunds apply, when exchanges are allowed, who pays return shipping and whether it is refundable, the rules for sale items, and how customers can contact us. Use [placeholders] for any values I have not given you, and keep the wording plain and customer-friendly.
```
[L1250, 2026]

#### Specific product risks

- Fashion products have high return rates, so return and shipping policies must be clear before launch. Suppliers should also agree to refund defective or wrong-size items [L0335, 2023].
- Do not copy another store's policy. Write the refund, return and shipping text from your actual return window, conditions and exceptions, and check it against consumer rules in each country you sell to [L1188, 2026].
- Before writing your own return and shipping policies, review established dropshipping stores' policies as a reference. Adapt the structure to your own supplier terms rather than copying their wording [L1233, undated].
- The policy should protect the business while being fair to customers [L1124, 2026].

#### Dropshipping return procedure

- Open Shopify Admin > Settings > Policies [L1233, undated].
- Click the policy you need (for example Return and refund policy) to generate the default template [L1233, undated].
- Replace the store's contact email with your support email [L1233, undated].
- Insert a return address if you have one; otherwise remove or change that line [L1233, undated].
- Adjust any other line to your needs [L1233, undated].
- Copy the finished text into a page, or save it in the policies section [L1233, undated].
