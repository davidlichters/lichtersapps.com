### What to tell suppliers

#### Before you contact suppliers

- Ask each supplier directly what they require before assuming a requirement. [L0359, 2020; L0468, 2020]
- Get the EIN before contacting suppliers. The EIN is the minimum to have before contacting suppliers. [L0468, 2020]
- Get a resale certificate before supplier outreach, since some suppliers require it. [L1116, 2025]
- Store EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. D-U-N-S numbers are not required for this model. [L0835, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Ask which number a supplier requires, a corporate tax number or a VAT number, before submitting the application. [L0360, 2024]

#### What to give the supplier

- Provide the official LLC name and the business address where the LLC is registered, not a private mailing address. [L0801, 2021]
- The company name and EIN are entered on supplier approval forms, which usually ask for company details in a one-page form. The company name is not shown to customers. [L0962, 2021]
- One LLC can run several stores. When calling suppliers, use the store name, and on approval forms list the store names you will sell under alongside the corporation's legal name. [L0853, 2021]
- Provide a resale certificate for the state where your business operates when a supplier asks for one. [L0470, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Store EIN numbers and resale certificate numbers are needed for supplier applications. [L0835, 2023] (2023 advice)

**Procedure: when a supplier asks for a state tax certificate and your state does not issue one** (2024 advice) [L0253, 2024]

1. Contact the supplier asking for their tax certificate requirement.
2. If they ask for a state tax certificate, start with your business registration state (e.g., Delaware).
3. If your state does not issue a sales tax certificate (e.g., Delaware), provide a W9 form from the IRS instead.
4. Tell the supplier: "I am a Delaware business; please see the attached W9."
5. Ask if the W9 is acceptable.
6. If not, follow up with any documents your state requires. [L0253, 2024]

### Credit, references and business details

- Suppliers typically request credit and banking information as part of the retailer approval process. Use a business credit card or debit card for authorization rather than bank account details to maintain privacy and control. [L0243, 2019]
- Leave the business references blank if you are not applying for credit. Use cash or a credit card only, since no credit terms are needed in the dropshipping model. [L0274, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Drop shipping stores do not require credit for supplier approval. A low credit score caused by past insolvency or bankruptcy does not block working with suppliers. [L0801, 2021]
- D&B numbers are not required for this model. [L0835, 2023; L1085, 2018]

### Supplier agreements and dropship policy

- For high-ticket items ($5,000+), you can request a supplier agreement. Search "dropshipping supplier agreement" on Google or check competitor sites for templates to adapt. The one source material does not provide standard agreement forms. [L0268, 2020]
- You do not need your own dropship policy. Suppliers that ask for one are rare, and a simple agreement from a template is enough. [L0666, 2024] (2024 advice)

*Conflict: [L0268, 2020] says high-ticket suppliers may ask for a supplier agreement, while [L0666, 2024] says a dropship policy is rarely requested. Use a template agreement for high-ticket suppliers, and a simple agreement otherwise.* [L0268, 2020; L0666, 2024]

### MAP, MSRP and coupons

#### Definitions

- On a supplier price sheet, MSRP is the manufacturer's suggested retail price, while MAP is the minimum advertised price you may list at. [L0470, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Check the case pack size, since the wholesale cost may cover a set of several units rather than one. [L0470, 2023] (2023 advice)
- Some markets have no legally enforceable minimum advertised price (MAP), but suppliers may follow unwritten rules, and discounting can reduce their supply. In the UK, undercutting a supplier's price can cause the supply to dry up because of damage to the supplier's reputation with other retailers. [L1441, 2018]

#### Coupons and MAP compliance

- MAP compliance is judged by the price displayed on the store, not by the price after a coupon. A coupon at checkout is usually allowed, but a displayed price below MAP is a violation unless the supplier agreement permits it. [L1112, 2021]
- Check the supplier's MAP agreement before offering coupon codes. A 20 dollar coupon on a 200 dollar MAP product is allowed as long as the product page still shows 200 dollars. [L1112, 2021]

#### Further details

- The IRS EIN Number page is listed as a resource for obtaining a US Employer Identification Number (EIN) for a US-based business. The lesson gives no further detail. [L0426, 2020]

- Applying for a seller's permit typically requires your SSN or EIN. It is the first administrative step before finding suppliers. [L1415, 2021-22]
