### Before the First Sign-Up

- Settle the business structure before any supplier, platform or ad account sign-up, because those accounts use the registered business details. Get an EIN and a sales tax certificate only when they are needed for your situation (US context). [L0393, undated]
- You do not need an LLC or business license before contacting suppliers. You can start as a sole proprietor, then hire an accountant to set up a corporation after revenue starts. ** [L0268, 2020]
- A business structure is not required to get approved by suppliers. Starting as a sole proprietor is valid, and an LLC or S corporation can be added later. ** [L1085, 2018]
- Entity formation is optional and is not needed before contacting suppliers. ** [L1088, 2020]
- *Conflict:* Many US suppliers prefer to deal with a company structure such as an LLC or S corp rather than a sole proprietor. This is an additional reason to form one. ** [L1405, 2021-22]
- Australian suppliers generally do not care whether you trade as a sole trader or through a company, so the early choice does not limit supplier approval. ** [L1405, 2021-22]
- An EIN can be used for more than one business in the view presented, but confirm this with an accountant before relying on it. ** [L1090, 2021]
- Get an EIN as part of the setup path. [L1277, 2020]
- Running a store under your own name typically requires being at least 18 years old. A younger founder would normally need an adult to sign the accounts and agreements. Confirm the rules for your country or state before opening business accounts. ** [L0803, 2022]

### Structure by Country

#### United States

- The usual US business forms are a sole proprietorship (trading as yourself), an LLC and an S corp. The last two are legal entities. [L0698, 2020-26]
- *Conflict:* A sole proprietorship can be used at the start, and an LLC can be formed later. [L0698, 2020-26; L0962, 2021]
- *Conflict:* A sole proprietorship gives no separation between the business and the owner. The owner is personally liable, and personal assets such as a home can be seized to pay business debts. Do not run a US store as a sole proprietor. [L1405, 2021-22]
- Sole proprietorship, LLC and S corp differ mainly in liability and tax treatment. Use the LLC and S-corp options above for the relevant decision. [L0698, 2020-26; L0703, 2020-26]

#### Australia

- ** In Australia the two structures are the sole trader and the Proprietary Limited company (Pty Ltd). [L1405, 2021-22]
- ** A sole trader can be set up in minutes. A Pty Ltd costs about $800 to $2,000 to establish and requires an accountant. [L1405, 2021-22]
- ** In Australia, a sole trader is personally liable for business claims. A company director's personal assets are protected if the business operates legally. Liability protection and tax treatment are the two main differences. [L1405, 2021-22]
- *Older advice (2021-22). Recommended path for most Australian beginners:*
  1. Start as a sole trader to keep setup cheap and fast. [L1405, 2021-22]
  2. Once the business earns meaningful revenue and liability exposure grows, switch to a Pty Ltd company with an accountant's help. [L1405, 2021-22]
  3. If you already own a Pty Ltd, use it from the start. [L1405, 2021-22]

#### Sole Trader and Company Basics

- ** A sole trader has no legal separation from the owner. [L0702, 2021-22]
- ** The choice between sole trader and company has no universal right answer. It depends on liability exposure, tax situation, cash available and cost. Advice from an accountant is recommended when tax is complex. [L0702, 2021-22]
- ** Starting as a sole trader is a valid low-cost choice, and it is easy to convert to a company later. Many owners do this when cash is limited and convert once the company's cost is financially justified. [L0702, 2021-22]

#### Other Countries

- Most other countries use different names but the same two-tier choice between trading as an individual and forming a company. [L0698, 2020-26]
- ** Outside the US and Australia, a local accountant or lawyer should confirm the structure choice. [L1405, 2021-22]

### Company Path for Beginners

- Check whether Shopify Payments can be opened with personal documents. If so, a company may be unnecessary at first. [L1512, 2023]
- If a company is needed, form a basic company in your home country, not abroad. [L1512, 2023]
- After you have proof of concept, plan a better overseas setup for scaling. [L1512, 2023]
- At about 100,000 USD monthly revenue, the advice was to consider Dubai for tax savings. Hong Kong was recommended for payment stability if the seller lives at home. Both need a home-country basic company for tax reporting. [L1512, 2023]

#### Further details

- When structuring multiple stores, consult an accountant or lawyer about whether to use one LLC for all stores or separate entities; benefits of separate entities include asset protection from lawsuits and cleaner separation if selling a store later. [L0245, 2020]
