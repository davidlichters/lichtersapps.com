### Redeeming points

#### Options and value

- Points can be redeemed for travel, gift cards, Amazon purchases, merchandise, PayPal payments and airline miles. Airline transfers give the best value per point. The other options are simpler but lower value. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Redeeming points for Amazon purchases at checkout is a lower-value option than airline transfers, but it is a simple way to use points for ordinary purchases. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Amazon checkout accepts American Express points when the Amex card is linked to the Amazon account. At checkout, the buyer can choose to pay with points instead of dollars. [L0903, 2021-22]
- American Express points can be used at checkout with many merchants. For example, a ride-hailing app such as Uber may offer to pay with points when it recognises the American Express card. [L0699, 2020-26]
- Only transfer American Express points to an airline partner. Transferring to a hotel chain gives poor value per point. Compare the points needed on the airline's own search before deciding where to move points. [L0903, 2021-22]
- American Express points transfer one-to-one to airline loyalty programmes such as Delta SkyMiles. [L0903, 2021-22]
- On Delta, a first-class one-way flight from the Minneapolis hub to many US destinations cost roughly 25,000 to 70,000 SkyMiles, depending on destination and how close the booking date is. Shorter routes such as Minneapolis to Denver sometimes cost under 20,000 miles, while long routes and smaller airports cost more. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Using 100,000 Amex points for a single hotel night gives far less value than using the same points on a first-class domestic flight that would cost well over 1,000 USD in cash. This comparison is the reason hotel transfers are avoided. [L0903, 2021-22]

#### Portal or transfer

- The American Express travel portal lets cardholders search flights, hotels and rental cars and pay with Amex points. Compare its price with the airline's own points price before choosing between portal booking and a transfer. [L0903, 2021-22]
- When an airline award price is close to the portal price for the same flight, booking through the portal can be preferable because you keep the airline's loyalty earning and status progress. If the portal price is clearly higher, transfer the points to the airline instead. [L0903, 2021-22]
- One flight cost 56,000 Amex points in the Amex portal, while the same booking on Delta cost 46,000 Delta SkyMiles. Transferring to Delta costs a small fee, but booking on Delta earns more value for the airline loyalty account than the portal booking. [L0903, 2021-22]
- If the preferred airline is not on the American Express transfer list, transfer points to one of its partner airlines, such as a European partner, and book the flight through that partner. Check the partner's award availability before transferring. [L0903, 2021-22]
- Flying roughly once a month on one airline and booking through the card portal when its price is close to the airline award price can build elite status without paying for flights. The source reports reaching Delta Platinum Medallion this way. [L0903, 2021-22]
- A charge billed to a hotel room that posts to an American Express card as a travel charge can be covered with Amex points. This works for purchases such as shopping inside a resort hotel that are billed to the guest room. [L0903, 2021-22]

### Funding supplier orders and payment methods

#### Timing and float

- A business credit card can float supplier orders, with the balance paid off every day or two so no interest accrues. [L0247, 2021]
- Early on, with little credit available, supplier orders can be charged to a credit card and paid off each day as payment settles. Customer payments to the bank take about 48 hours. [L1111, 2020]
- If no credit line exists, a debit card can pay suppliers, with customer funds arriving about 48 hours later. [L0247, 2021]
- Paying a supplier immediately by ACH can speed up processing of the purchase order. Use this when suppliers are slow to fulfil orders. [L1472, 2022]
- Place supplier orders with a credit card where possible, because it makes payments simple and the card can earn points or cash back. As order volume and advertising spend grow on the same card, the rewards accumulate. [L1480, 2021-22]
- Free domestic ACH transfers (Brex) meant payments to suppliers arrived within about one day, and recipients were notified within seconds. This makes it practical to pay suppliers from a US company account (verify current terms). [L1512, 2023]
- (2024) Freight booking payment: in the Payment section, click the "Stored Debit/Credit Card" button, which charges the company account automatically. Leave the card dropdown on its preset card, tick the confirmation box, then click "Pay Now". [L1291, undated]

#### Choosing the payment method

- Suppliers' payment options commonly include ACH transfers, checks and credit cards. [L0255, 2025]
- Bank transfers keep payment order-by-order like credit terms and eliminate the processing fee that some suppliers charge on cards. [L0274, 2023]
- If a supplier requests a credit card processing fee (for example 4 percent), ask whether it accepts ACH or bank transfers instead. [L0274, 2023]
- Wise and bank transfer are the two payment methods used to pay private suppliers per order. [L1156, 2026]
- Paying suppliers or CJ Dropshipping through a wallet makes little difference to the cost. A wallet is useful only when you want a fixed budget for fulfilling orders. Otherwise, paying directly by card or PayPal works the same way. [L1216, undated]
- Some suppliers add extra fees when paid with American Express, which can cancel out the rewards. In Australia, avoid Amex for supplier payments, while it is believed the same surcharge does not apply in the US. [L1405, 2021-22]

#### Prepaying and card on file

- Prepaying suppliers by card earns points on those purchases. This only applies when you have a credit card in your name. [L0933, 2020-26]
- Replace a debit card on file with a credit card once credit has been built. [L0933, 2020-26]
- The supplier keeps the card on file, so the card can be updated with the supplier later by email. [L0358, 2020]

### Personal liability, guarantees and co-signing

- Suppliers generally do not care which credit card you use, as long as it processes orders. A new business is usually personally liable for its card until it builds its own credit history, so put the business name on the card when possible. You can update the card on file with a supplier by email later. [L0358, 2020]
- AmEx personally guarantees you for any loans against the card. If you take Shopify Capital (which uses AmEx), the business is liable, but you personally guarantee it. Review the terms and conditions carefully before using business credit for company loans. [L0308, 2023]
- (2023) For first-time business credit card applications, expect to co-sign personally even though the card is in the business name. Once business credit is established, you can remove yourself as co-signer. [L0251, 2023]
- A new business credit card usually still requires a personal co-signature and relies on personal credit in the early years. A UPS Store mailbox address is a convenient option, and a standard forwarding address is an acceptable alternative. [L0968, 2021]
- A sole proprietor can still open a separate card for business expenses only. For an LLC or S-corp, a new business has no credit history, so the owner will usually have to co-sign the card. [L1069, 2019]
- If a business card application is denied while a credit freeze is active, lift the freeze and reapply. Many banks offer business credit cards, and American Express and Chase were cited as popular options. [L0671, 2021]

#### Further details

- AMEX Business Gold is used to maximize rewards on paid advertising, earning 4X points per dollar spent on ads, making it suitable for businesses with significant ad budgets. [L0005, undated]

- Separate virtual cards can be created per advertising or software platform. A virtual card can be closed immediately to stop recurring charges, which helps with free trial sign-ups. [L1512, 2023]
