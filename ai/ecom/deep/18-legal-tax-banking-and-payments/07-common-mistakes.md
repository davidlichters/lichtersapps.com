### Common mistakes

- A UK LTD opened with a USD currency setting and US bank details led Stripe to ask for tax residency documents before activating the account. Keep the company jurisdiction, currency and bank details consistent when opening a payment account. [L1127, 2024]
- Mixing up the owner's residence country and the business country can cause problems with Shopify Payments, checkout functionality and payouts. [L1314, 2021-22]
- Opening PayPal in one country while the company is registered in another led to repeated permanent bans in one reported case. [L1347, undated]
- A cheap UK company later caused account problems, so compare total setup cost before choosing. [L1127, 2024]
- Presenting dropshipping as a marketing-services export to avoid VAT is only the coach's own setup, and it needs local tax advice before use. [L0363, 2022+]
