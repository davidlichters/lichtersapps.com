### Mindset & Data (lesson 009): mindset-daten-die-grundlage-zum-erfolg-0018f.md

- [00:23-01:17] Person A vs person B. Person A tests a single product, decides it is a scam, takes "two full weeks" to get ads live on one product, feels they must test five different creative themes, builds the "perfect custom site" for one product, then panics in the ad account and decides the only way to win is not shipping orders or scamming. Person B never stops, so "failure isn't possible."

- [06:22-07:17] E-com is a very small community: anyone can be found out. Be genuinely good to people, do not scam, and give real value to agents, fulfillment partners, the direct factory and suppliers, so they give more value back, for example credit terms or net terms.

### Change Your Mentality (lesson 017): mindset-ist-alles-99422.md

- [01:09] "Linear predictable scaling does not exist" (said twice). Your ability to stay calm and execute when things go wrong is the only skill that matters; if you cannot master your response to chaos, you will fail.
- [01:09-01:39] Expect massive peaks and massive lows. The default safety-seeking mindset reads every low as a signal to quit. It feels like up and down, but real progress is a slow, steady increase.
- [01:39-02:10] Failures are data, not defeats. Each failure gave three things: "I can do it again, I can do it better next time and I can do it bigger next time." A failure is data you purchase with time and money; a mistake is not a reason to retreat to safety.
- [02:10-02:42] Focus is the most important weapon against the conservative desire to play it safe.
- [02:42] The teacher's intention when starting brands: build something massive and sell the business in the future "for six eight figures", not test random products to look at metrics.
- [03:44] The inputs to create are: launching new creatives, improving operations, fulfilling orders and talking to customers. Customer service is "the most important thing" for progressing, because speaking to real buyers shows the bottleneck so you can solve it.

### Expectations (erwartungen-fuer-deine-dropshipping-reise-f0947.md)

- [00:00] The lesson names three mental traps to avoid, in this order: 1. shiny object syndrome, 2. quitting too early, 3. comparison.
- [00:35] Put 100% into one thing rather than splitting your energy "25 25 25 25". The speaker had tried crypto trading, stocks and running an agency at the same time before committing.
- [01:01] Quitting too early: "most people give up right before things start to click. Don't let impatience rob you of success." The speaker calls it the most common thing he sees with beginners.
- [01:01] Comparison: "everyone's timeline is different". Some take a week, a month, a couple of months or a year. "Your only competition is yesterday's version of you."
- [01:29] A seven-figure operator mindset also means building systems so the business runs even when you are not working.
- [01:29] Analogy: give a homeless person a million dollars and two months later he has zero, because he lacks the mindset to use money. Scale income step by step, from $1 a day to $100 a day to $1,000 a day, instead of jumping straight to $10K.
- [03:02] "The only way you're going to learn is by losing money." Beginners who lose $50 or $100 and start complaining have the wrong mindset. "You're not going to be rich by penny pinching": you need to spend money to collect data.
- [03:27] The speaker's own start: long nights, dozens of products tested and mistake after mistake before real results.
- [03:27] The journey is the best part. Going from $1K to $10K is the destination, but the journey matters more.
