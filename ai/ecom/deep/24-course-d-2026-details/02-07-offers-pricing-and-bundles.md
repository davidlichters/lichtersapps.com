## 07. Offers Pricing and Bundles

### Creating and testing offers for higher AOV (angebote-erstellen-und-testen-fuer-hoehere-aov-d9e27.md, lesson 041, video 8:42)

- [00:31] Slide rule: before setting up any offer, always use the profit calculator to set the new ROAS and CPA targets, so you know whether the offer is successful, or use Intelligems testing instead.
- [01:46] Keep the offer box as simple as possible so the customer is not overwhelmed; show under each option how much money they save.
- [04:59] Charging about $4.99 for shipping usually does not hurt your CVR ("You guys overthink it"). Free shipping is only worth it when your calculated margins allow it.
- [05:54] Example urgency banner from a brand: "Free shipping on all orders over $45, 40% off until midnight". The sale does not really stop at midnight; the line exists so the customer orders before midnight.
- [05:54] Bundle & Save slide: group commonly purchased products and bundle them at a discount; give the bundle a REASON and a name. Examples: "Summer T-Shirt Bundle, Regular $400 Now for $299.99" and "Stylist Bundle - Hair Dryer + 2 Nozzles & Travel Bag, Regular $200 now $169.99".
- [08:17] Final step: talk to ChatGPT about your offer and apply the slide rules to your product.

### Post Purchase Upsell (lesson 091): post-purchase-upsell-automatische-zusatzverkaeufe-nach-dem-k-05ab8.md

- [00:00-00:32] Setup path: search "one click upsell" in the Shopify app store, install, open Funnels and create a funnel. The funnel can sell a selected product or an ebook.
- [04:23] Upsell headline wording, "as simple as possible": "Why not buy one more" / "Why not one more". Second upsell: "Why not one more again". Final downsell (40%): "Fine, this is the best we can offer."
- [04:23] Countdown timer on the upsell: "three minutes I think it's fairly good enough" for urgency.
- [04:53] Shipping on the upsell offer: normally free shipping.
- [02:32-05:56] Price check per step: the first upsell (same product, 25%) must give a total equal to or slightly lower than the buy-two price on the product page; the second upsell (35%) total must be equivalent to or lower than bundle 3 on the product page, "It cannot be more." Wrong math leads to angry emails, refunds and chargebacks.
- [05:56-07:07] Funnel 2: upsell a compatible second product, or an ebook. For an ebook, create a separate PDF product and add it as the upsell product; an ebook has no COGS once you have the PDF, so it raises AOV without extra ad spend.

### Example of Whitelist Pages (L082, whitelist-pages-erklaert-f9fd8.md)

- [00:28] Brand page example: store "GlowSkin", Facebook page "GlowSkin", ad "GlowSkin - Try our new face mask." Baby version: the store is talking about its own product.
- [01:01] Whitelist example: the same GlowSkin face mask, but the ad is shown from a page called "Sarah's Beauty Finds", which recommends or talks about the product. Baby version: someone else is talking about your product, with permission.
- [01:38] Comparison table from the lesson doc: Brand page = your brand talks, your product, feels like "the company is advertising", main benefit "builds your brand". Whitelist/partner page = another approved page or creator talks, can be the same product, feels like "a person/page is recommending", main benefit "gives you another angle / identity to test".
- [02:11] Simple example: Brand page ad = Nike says "Buy our new shoe." Partner/whitelist ad = an approved fitness creator says "I've been wearing this Nike shoe." "Same shoe. Different person speaking."
- [02:11] Easy flow: PRODUCT -> choose who should be the "face" of the ad -> BRAND PAGE or APPROVED PARTNER PAGE -> run the ad -> compare results. "Same product. Different face. Different angle."
- [03:11] Ad Library example (search "murphys law"): brand page "Your Kid's Growth" and whitelist pages "Nicole Kiser" (category Beauty, cosmetic & personal care) and "Emily Bennett" (category Health & medical, 53 followers, stock-photo profile picture) all advertise the same children's book, "Murphy's Law Life Principles for Children", with the same first-person copy ("I've been getting comments lately about how mature my kid seems for their age..."). The brand and the whitelist pages are probably run by the same person.

### Whitelist Page Breakdown (L083, whitelist-seiten-fuer-vertrauenswuerdige-facebook-anzeigen-aa3a3.md)

- [12:57] Social proof from a student: started whitelist pages six days earlier and got about $300 of spend at about 5 ROAS.
- [25:58] Why run image ads as well as video: video ads bring colder customers; images then work like retargeting. Someone who watched the video and did not buy sees an image ad the next day with "50% off, ends today", a free gift or a testimonial, which can push them over the edge.
- [26:29] Video and image ads in separate campaigns in the same ad account for the same product capture every placement type and the different moods people are in, providing you have the budget and are scaling profitably.
- [38:50] Facebook itself recommends combining images and videos in one ad set; the coach does not, because control over each format's budget is "significantly more important".
- [39:54] Structure is secondary to the ad: "a winning ad is a winning ad". People with 10 campaigns and people with one campaign and all ads in one ad set can both do 100K to 500K a month. Separate campaigns only if you want to control and scale images separately; otherwise put everything in one ad set and let Facebook decide.
- [35:42] Pixel within one broad niche: a pixel trained on kids' books can stay for a medical book, and a nursing book to a kids' book is the same case. Keep the pixel; it is more trustworthy than a fresh one and gives slightly lower CPMs/CPCs sooner. A pixel stores buyer patterns and predictability, not your avatar.
- [40:54] If you make a pixel per product you end up with 10+ pixels; old pixels can be deleted. Pixels cost nothing (in the call, Timo says you can have up to 1 million).
- [41:52] Profit check with AI: connect Claude to Facebook (search YouTube for "how to connect Claude to Facebook"), tell it your break-even ROAS, and ask which campaigns are currently profitable and which are not.
- [42:47] Student kill rule, which the coach says "works too": after every six days, turn off an ad that has spent less than $50 and is below break-even ROAS.
- [44:38] Advertorials "don't really apply to books so much", they apply more to supplements.

### How To Properly Test Products (produkttesting-messaging-vor-demand-9dc04.md)

- [07:16] A CBO is a funnel with top, middle and bottom of funnel ads. Most ads you see in a CBO are bottom to middle of funnel: "I can assure you 90% is bottom of funnel ads".
- [20:32] Custom ads go into CBO 2. A separate CBO 3 for custom is "also okay, but I think you will cannibalize a lot", so stick to CBO 2 with custom. Only open one more CBO "if you have a lot of credits".
- [22:40] The full testing, scaling and graveyard structure is an advanced stage for someone running "at least two to three months" with rich data, roughly 500 or 1,000 website purchases.
- [23:02] Country choice for the bid cap scaling campaign: the teacher puts "the top four and with New Zealand, that's it. Forget about Singapore and the rest of the countries." Putting countries into separate campaigns is also fine.
- [25:49] If you need more time for customer research, do "at least seven days or maybe five days", reading line by line in Reddit forums and running the research prompt in Claude, then condense the messaging into VSLs, statics and advertorials.
- [26:16] Reporting rhythm: test at least three days, then update your coach with your P&L, instead of updating daily. After three days the coach judges whether the messaging is good, whether there is product demand, and checks the Meta Ad Library for saturation; if too saturated, cut and move on.

### Product test setup: Meta pixel, CBO campaign, creatives (produkttest-setup-meta-pixel-kampagne-und-creatives-79de0.md)

- [05:18] Ad name: today's date plus the ad number, for example "19 4 1"; select the Facebook page and choose Manual setup.
- [06:09] Upload all ripped videos into the media box in one go before building the ads.
- [07:05-07:23] ChatGPT prompt used: "help me generate five Meta headlines and primary text for this product that highlights the pain points and benefits of the product", followed by the landing page URL pasted into the chat.
- [09:02] Put in all creatives you have: duplicate the ad once per creative, then publish all of them.
