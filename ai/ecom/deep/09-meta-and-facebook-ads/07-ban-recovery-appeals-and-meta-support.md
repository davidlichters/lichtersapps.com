## Ban Recovery, Appeals and Meta Support

### Ban recovery: first moves

- Open Ads Manager, click the ban notice at the top, then See details. This leads to Business settings, where you choose Request review. [L0637, 2023+]
- If you know the reason (ad issue or billing issue), explain it in the appeal. If you do not, paste the appeal template below. [L0637, 2023+]
- Use Meta's appeal forms where available: ad account, page restriction, or business restriction. [L0637, 2023+]
- Use the form that matches the restricted asset, and describe the business in detail. [L0637, 2023+]
- Check replies in the Support inbox, which is in the dropdown on your personal profile. [L0637, 2023+]
- If the account is not reinstated, appeal again and make a final, polite request for a human review. [L0637, 2023+]
- Before appealing, check the store against the ad policy and state the reason in the appeal, because some suspensions are false flags. [L0468, 2020]
- Read Meta's terms of service, make sure you follow them, then submit the appeal and let the process run. Many suspensions were resolved when the account was not doing anything wrong. [L0801, 2021]

### Appeal wording

Template for a banned ad account:

```
I believe you have mistakenly disabled my ad account. My account does not violate any Facebook terms of service. Please have a human review it. Thanks.
```

[L0637, 2023+]

- In the appeal, state that the block was a mistake and ask for a manual review. [L0650, undated]
- Use a final, polite request for a human review on the last attempt. [L0637, 2023+]

### Restricted and blocked pages

- When the Facebook page behind the ads is blocked, the ad sets show ad errors and no ads can run until the block is resolved. [L0650, undated]
- Submit the appeal for the page itself before changing anything in the ad sets. [L0650, undated]
- Page restrictions can follow multiple ad rejections or a feedback score below 2. Use the page restriction appeal form. [L0637, 2023+]
- A flagged Facebook commerce account does not block remarketing ads that rely on a linked product feed. [L0962, 2021]

### Commerce account rejections

- If a commerce account is rejected, work through the checklist first:
  1. Confirm that every required element is on the site, such as required footer links and business information. [L0803, 2022]
  2. If nothing obvious is missing, keep contacting Facebook through live chat in Meta Business Manager. [L0803, 2022]
  3. Keep working through the checklist until someone identifies the specific reason for rejection. [L0803, 2022]
- For a rejected store review, read the commerce eligibility policy carefully, then request a review and state that your store complies after reviewing the policy. [L0855, 2023]

### Identity verification

- Upload identity documents when requesting a review of a blocked account. [L0650, undated]
- Ask for a manual review when you appeal. [L0650, undated]
- For a disabled account that requests identity verification:
  1. Upload the front and back of the required ID. [L0963, 2022]
  2. Wait for a human review, which can take days. [L0963, 2022]
  3. If the resolution link fails, contact Business Manager support through live chat to reach a person. [L0963, 2022]
- A ban for an ID issue can often be resolved by submitting ID for review, which typically takes 24 to 48 hours. If the review does not restore the account, contact the account provider or the support contact for another solution. [L0774, 2023]
- For an early-phase ban, upload a personal ID to recover the account. Recovery often takes one to two days. [L1431, 2020+]
- The Facebook account name must match the name on the personal ID used for verification exactly. Stylized or fancy names are rejected. [L1431, 2020+]
- For identity verification, when asked which ad categories you run (social issues, elections or politics), select 'None of the above' and complete two-factor authentication. Choosing the political or social issue option only complicates the review. [L1218, undated]
- For Instagram accounts disabled for misrepresentation with no posts, submit the business license and resale certificate, and verify identity with the requested photo and security code. Verification can take a few days to about a week, after which a person usually reviews the case and the account is restored. [L1090, 2021]

### Live chat and support routes

- Chat support from a Facebook representative is normally provided only after spending $5,000 to $10,000. Beginners without this spending level must rely on automated review processes. [L0296, 2021+]
- Older advice says Facebook account representatives can help advertisers with spend history. Use this only when you have that spend history. [L1111, 2020]
- To reach Facebook advertising support, go to Business > Help > Support, select your issue, and use the live messenger option when offered. It is only available when support is open. [L0950, 2023]
- Facebook support is slow and first-line. Persistent follow-up is often needed, and a verification review can take a long time, so plan for waiting. [L0950, 2023]
- Meta Business Support live chat is available through the Business Center. If chat is not shown, retry later in the day, since availability changes with time. [L855.145]
- Meta Business Support is the official help centre for Meta ad accounts, Business Manager, and advertising policy questions. Use it when an ad or account is restricted and the reason is unclear. [L1105, 2024]
- For a restricted ad account:
  1. Log in to the ad account. [L0804, 2023] (2023)
  2. Open the support flow and go through the problems you have until a live chat option appears. [L0804, 2023] (2023)
  3. Accept the callback if offered. Live chat was reported as faster than the account rep. [L0804, 2023] (2023)
- Meta Business Support live chat can ask for a phone number and then call the account holder back. Use this to escalate account problems that chat cannot solve. Callbacks may come from an unfamiliar number, sometimes in another country, so expect an unknown caller. [L0666, 2024] (2024)
- For a disabled Facebook account:
  1. Open Facebook's support page. [L1088, 2020]
  2. Under the question about being an advertiser, choose support. [L1088, 2020]
  3. Accept the wait of about nine minutes for the live chat team, then ask for a review of the disabled account. [L1088, 2020]
- For a new ad account that Meta disables at creation (2023):
  1. Use the official support page in Business Manager, since there is no reliable shortcut beyond it. [L0470, 2023] (2023)
  2. Check that page about once a day and resubmit the request, because reaching a reviewer can take several attempts. [L0470, 2023] (2023)
  3. Ask paid-ads communities for working contact routes as a supplement to the official channel. [L0470, 2023] (2023)
- Support paths differ by source and date. Use the route that is open to you. [L0950, 2023; L0470, 2023; L1088, 2020; L0804, 2023; L0855, 2023]
- Live chat is often not shown because the queue is too long. Retry it each day. [L0468, 2020]
- When you reach the agent, ask what caused the ad account or store disapproval and whether it can be fixed. Stay polite and say the issue has been resolved and will not happen again. [L0468, 2020]

### Recovery and securing the account

- To recover and secure a Meta ad account after a ban:
  1. Complete business verification. [L0691, 2024]
  2. Add a valid payment method. [L0691, 2024]
  3. Make sure the personal account is linked to the Business Manager so the business and personal accounts are merged correctly. [L0691, 2024]
  4. Create the pixel inside the Business Manager and connect it to the Shopify store. [L0691, 2024]
  5. Add a second trusted person as an admin on the Business Manager and ad account. [L0691, 2024]
  6. Read Meta's advertising policies before publishing creatives. [L0691, 2024]
  7. Keep two payment methods on file. [L0691, 2024]
- For a reinstated or new ad account, the pixel must be created inside the Business Manager, not on the personal account. The personal profile is only used to open the Business Manager. The Business Manager holds the ad account and the pixel, and business verification must be completed. [L0691, 2024]
- Confirm the pixel shows in the Shopify pixel dropdown before creating campaigns. [L0691, 2024]
- Recovery case: a Meta ad account was fully locked after the business account was registered under the company name, the personal profile was not linked to the Business Manager, and the Shopify pixel sat on the business account. Recovery went through Meta's support channels and business verification. The personal and business accounts were then merged, a new payment method was added, and campaigns were held back until the setup was confirmed correct. [L0691, 2024]
- After a Meta account ban, recover the banned account before creating new accounts. New accounts on the same identity and card are usually blocked. [L0855, 2023]
- Do not try to evade a permanent ban by creating a new setup, new profiles or bought accounts. This violates Meta's terms, and the account and profile trust work of this procedure stops being useful. Focus on a proper appeal instead. [L0637, 2023+]
- A restart from a fresh profile often gets banned again. The problem is common for new advertisers, so review the Facebook ad ban guidance before running any ads. [L1218, undated]
- Under an ad-based ban, the account can often be restored once the advertised content is changed:
  1. Request a review of the banned account. [L1431, 2020+]
  2. Change the ad creatives to more compelling content that follows the platform rules. [L1431, 2020+]
  3. Remove the offending video or ad from the ad account. [L1431, 2020+]
  4. Do not insist on the same creative, because the account will not be returned if it is pushed again. [L1431, 2020+]
  5. Expect the account to be restored within hours to about two days after the offending ad is removed. [L1431, 2020+]
- If an ad account is banned after a before-and-after video, remove the video or image that is being advertised, then request a review. The account can often be restored after that. [L1184, 2023+]

### Ban frequency and false flags

- Facebook bans are rare for properly run stores. Most are caused by user error that can be fixed quickly. [L1111, 2020]
- New stores are often blocked in the first three to four days. [L0650, undated]
- Some ad account suspensions are caused by false flags. Check the store against the ad policy before appealing, and give the reason in the appeal. [L0468, 2020]
- Fashion usually faces fewer Facebook ad restrictions than beauty or health products, which are more prone to ad-policy problems. Keeping the campaign structure as simple as possible also helps. [L0660, 2019+]

### Fallback channels while restricted

- While a Meta account is restricted, run ads on Google and Microsoft instead, including Google remarketing, and try Meta again later. [L0855, 2023]
- If an ad account suspension appeal is delayed, a paid verification option has been reported to speed up review and unlock the account. It is not guaranteed, so use it only as a possible path while waiting for the appeal. [L0835, 2023]
- The paid verification option was reported to cost about £9 per month and to speed up review of a suspended ad account, possibly within an hour. It is not guaranteed, so use it only as a parallel option while the appeal is still pending. [L0835, 2023]
- Ask paid-ads communities for working contact routes as a supplement to the official channel. [L0470, 2023] (2023)

### Restricted categories: health and fitness

- Health and fitness ads must avoid before-and-after comparisons, anything showing skin, and visual comparisons such as tummy and abs. Use a reviewer or testimonial instead where possible. [L0871, 2021+]
- Weight-loss products are considered too risky for dropshippers. Health and fitness products in general can be advertised with a solid ad account setup and careful creative and copy. [L0871, 2021+]
- The Facebook Advertising Blueprint certification is a short course of about 15 minutes that explains the ad rules for health and fitness before a launch. [L0871, 2021+]
- Meta is strict with health advertising, so ban and ad rejection rates are high in health niches. Plan for rejections and avoid building the whole launch on brand pages with direct health claims. [L0906, 2026]
- Health promises, weight-loss claims, and before-and-after beauty claims can get an ad account banned. Test these products only in a separate ad account and avoid these claim types in the ad. [L1171, 2024+]
- Products or claims that promise health results, weight loss, slimmer bodies or looking younger lead to account bans. Before-and-after comparisons that look offensive or misleading can also trigger a ban. [L1431, 2020+]
- Holiday gift cards promoted in Meta ads triggered an ad account suspension that lasted more than 30 days. Check the Meta advertising policies for restricted offers such as gift cards before launching campaigns. [L0468, 2020]

### Restricted categories: before and after, and beauty

- Before-and-after images in ads on Facebook, especially for beauty or dental products, can cause ad account bans for misleading claims. Avoid promises of quick results and use review-style videos instead. [L1184, 2023+]
- Show features and benefits without bold claims, and use a customer review video instead of a before-and-after comparison, to reduce the risk of bans. [L1184, 2023+]
- A strong ad performance cannot be guaranteed on Facebook, so do not make bold guarantee claims in beauty or cosmetic ads. [L1184, 2023+]
- Facebook treats its platform as an entertainment space, so shocking before-and-after or explicit imagery harms the user experience and can get ads restricted. Controversial ads are reviewed more strictly than ads that people enjoy. [L1171, 2024+]

### Restricted categories: medical products

- Meta restricts ads for medical devices, especially personalised ads that address the viewer directly about a medical need. If the ad is restricted, rewrite the ad and remarketing copy to be less personal and focus on the offer. [L0807, 2025] (2025)
- To appeal a Meta restriction on medical products (2025):
  1. Compare an approved product page with a rejected one side by side, and check whether the copy addresses a specific person individually. [L0807, 2025] (2025)
  2. Edit the rejected copy to remove direct personal calls to action. [L0807, 2025] (2025)
  3. Log into your Facebook account and click Support at the top right. [L0807, 2025] (2025)
  4. Open Meta Business Support and request a human review of the rejected products. [L0807, 2025] (2025)

### Restricted categories: celebrity claims

- Do not use celebrity names or claims such as an endorsement by a famous person in ads, because this can lead to Facebook blocking the ad account. [L1172, 2023+]
- If you still test such a product, use a new ad account rather than your main account. [L1172, 2023+]
- A new ad account for testing is not a way to evade a permanent ban. Do not create new setups to get around a permanent ban. [L1172, 2023+; L0637, 2023+]

### Special ad categories

- In the Special Ad Categories section of a campaign, leave the declaration empty for ordinary product ads. [L0348, 2026] (2026)
- Declare a category only when the ad is about social issues, elections or politics, or concerns financial products and services, housing or employment. Requirements differ by country. [L0348, 2026] (2026)
