## Ad Creative, Copy and Placement Formats

### Ad Text Anatomy and Field Priorities

- A Facebook ad has three text elements: Primary Text above the creative, Headline in bold below the creative, and Description in small text below the Headline. [L0204, undated]
- The Description is often hidden on Reels and Stories placements. [L0204, undated]
- Primary Text and Headline are the two most important elements because they stay visible in all placements. Description visibility is unreliable, so optimise the first two first. [L0204, undated]
- Viewers see only the first 1-2 lines of Primary Text before deciding to watch the video or skip it. These lines must stop the scroll with a bold claim, a direct question about a pain point, or a benefit statement. [L0204, undated]
- Put the website URL in the Primary Text field so users can click from the copy straight to the store. [L0205, 2026]
- Users see the video first and the text overlay second. Ad copy must not look pushy, so the whole ad reads as organic and native to the platform. [L0205, 2026]
- In a Facebook video ad, the headline and some call-to-action buttons below the image disappear, so the website link belongs at the top of the Primary Text. [L0488, 2021-22]

### Primary Text Length and Templates

- Long-form Primary Text runs more than 5-6 lines. Short-form runs 3-4 lines. [L0204, undated]
- Select copy length by audience age: for 40+, use long-form copy; for under 40, test short-format copy. [L0204, undated]
- Conflicting age thresholds exist in the sources. [L0204, undated] gives 40+ for long-form and under 40 for short-format. [L0637, 2023+] gives above 40 or 50 for Facebook-style captions with product, solution and benefit messaging, and under 40 or 30 for TikTok-style UGC. Use the threshold that matches your audience data. [L0204, undated; L0637, 2023+]

Long-form Primary Text template [L0204, undated]:

```
Line 1 (bold claim or problem): Introducing [Product Name], the ultimate solution for [Customer Problem].

Line 2-3 (agitation): Are you tired of [specific pain point]? We have got the perfect solution for you. Say goodbye to [problem] once and for all with our innovative [product category].

Line 4 (benefits): Key benefits: [Benefit 1] • [Benefit 2] • [Benefit 3]

Line 5 (offer): [Discount]% off, limited time only / [Discount]% off with code [CODE]

Line 6 (action + outcome): Shop now and transform your [problem] into [desired outcome].
```

Short-form Primary Text template [L0204, undated]:

```
Line 1 (problem question): Struggling with [pain point]?

Line 2 (solution intro): Say hello to [Product Name], your answer to [problem].

Line 3 (benefits): [Benefit 1] • [Benefit 2] • [Benefit 3]

Line 4 (exclusive offer): [Discount]% off, exclusive offer

Line 5 (CTA): Shop now
```

### Headlines and Descriptions

Headline formula library [L0204, undated]:

```
**Attention-grabbing:** Discover [Product Name] [your solution to problem] OR Get ready to transform your life with [Product Name].

**Urgency + offer:** Limited time offer: [Discount]% off OR Hurry, [Product Name] sales ending soon. Grab [Product Name] now!

**Benefit-driven:** Experience [key benefit] with [Product Name] OR [Key benefit] way to [solve problem]

**Problem-solving:** Say goodbye to [problem] with [Product Name] OR Tired of [customer pain point]? Try [Product Name].
```

- The Description is a small, often-hidden field. Fill it with limited-time offer details (for example "Free shipping" or "30-day money-back guarantee") or social proof (for example "Rated 4.8 out of 5 by 15,000+ happy customers"). Avoid long text. [L0204, undated]
- On the Facebook news feed, the ad copy can be more detailed and name both the problem and the solution the content offers. The Headline and CTA stay the same as in other placements. [L0415, 2021-22]

### Writing and Sourcing Copy

- Gather copy inspiration from three sources: customer psychology research (deepest desires and insecurities), direct customer interviews and feedback, and competitor ad analysis via the Facebook Ads Library. [L0204, undated]
- ChatGPT and Copy.ai are free tools for drafting Primary Text and Headlines. Enter your product details and customer pain points, or paste a competitor ad and ask for a rephrased version for your product. Generate 3-5 variations. [L0204, undated]
- Lead with the customer's transformation, not the mechanism. "Includes 360-degree compression technology and cooling materials" is weaker than "Migraine relief you can feel in minutes" because the customer cares about the result. [L0313, 2026]
- Symptom-led headlines name the visible problem the buyer already feels, such as leaking, damage or lost time, rather than the root cause, because people recognise symptoms more easily than causes. [L0701, 2015]
  - Formula: [symptom the buyer sees] + [what it costs them in time, money or image] + question. [L0701, 2015]
  - Examples: "Shoes still soaking after your morning walk?" and "Keep finding ruined shirts after the rain? Stop losing money on replacements." [L0701, 2015]
- Avoid direct claims such as weight-loss guarantees or fitness guarantees in captions, voiceover and on-screen text. Rephrase them so they do not read as a violation. [L0871, 2021+]

### Hooks

- Meta defines the hook as the first one to three seconds of an ad. Other platforms and sources use different time definitions, so the exact length matters less than the element that grabs attention immediately. [L0508, 2025-26]
- Hook length in the sources:
  - First 1-3 seconds: Meta's definition. [L0508, 2025-26]
  - First 1-5 seconds: the proven hook to reuse from a competitor's ad. [L0637, 2023+]
  - First 3-5 seconds: the part that differs between creative variations in testing. [L0648, undated]
- Test idea, style and hook before smaller variables. These three have the largest impact on results even though they take more effort to change. After Meta's Andromeda update, this lets the platform test a wide range of very different ads. [L0508, 2025-26]
- Use the Hook Test strategy on Meta, Facebook and Instagram: run several different ad hooks or angles and keep the ones that scale. [L0914, 2026]
- Judge hooks by hook rate to find the best-performing hook. [L0902, undated]

### Offers, Urgency and Guarantees

- Risk-reversal structure (works because it removes fear of wasted money and sets explicit expectations for when to judge results) [L0313, 2026]:
  1. State the specific benefit timeline ("relief in as little as 7 days").
  2. Include a money-back guarantee with an exact period ("30-night risk-free trial").
  3. Show the price clearly and prominently.
  - Examples: "Feel the difference in 5 days. 60-night money-back guarantee, $44.99" and "Sleep better by night one. 90-night satisfaction guarantee, $59.99 today." [L0313, 2026]
- Competitor comparison with negative indicators (works by making the alternative look worse and clarifying your unique safety or benefit advantage) [L0313, 2026]:
  1. Display your product's key attributes with checkmarks or positive phrasing.
  2. List what alternatives include that customers want to avoid, marked with X, "no" or "risk of".
  - Examples: "Natural formula ✓, Zero chemicals X, Long-lasting ✓, No rebound effect X" and "Effective immediately ✓, No grogginess X, No prescription X, No dependency ✓." [L0313, 2026]
- Urgency with specificity (vague urgency such as "soon" creates no action, while specific deadlines trigger fear of missing out) [L0313, 2026]:
  - State the end day, for example "Offer ends Sunday".
  - Or use "Today Only" or "Limited time only".
  - Or show inventory scarcity.
  - Examples: "Launch offer ends Thursday. Get 20% off now" and "This weekend only: buy now at launch price, $39.99." [L0313, 2026]
- Offer testing: start without an offer, check visitor behaviour, then test offers such as Buy one get the second item 20% off, Buy one get the second 50% off, or Buy two get the third free. The presenter's winning offer at the time was Buy one get the second item 20% off. [L0642, undated]
- Place the offer text in the Primary Text of a single image ad, the most visible spot, where it also appears as the first line on Instagram. For traffic from both Facebook and Instagram, put the offer in the Primary Text. It can also go in other parts of the ad. [L0650, undated]
- Run the first ads as conversion campaigns with urgency and fear-of-missing-out messaging so first-time visitors buy and the product is validated. Use retargeting and email to win back visitors who did not buy. [L0661, undated]
- Use only evergreen promo codes, such as a permanent 3% or 5% code created in Shopify. Expiring or changing codes break remarketing ads once they stop working. Confirm codes are active before publishing. [L1029, 2020-26]

### Social Proof, Reviews and Comments

- Include text-based customer reviews as quotes under the initial copy in Facebook ads and other ads with enough copy space. This adds credibility and increases curiosity and click-through motivation. [L0241, 2021-22]
- Review-led ad structure [L1398, 2021-22]:
  1. Place a headline that summarises a review above the body copy.
  2. Start the body copy with a review text, so the shopper sees social proof first.
- A Facebook ad can carry four or five reviews in the top text, ending with a link back to the product. Choose reviews that highlight different aspects of the product or service so all key points are covered. [L1398, 2021-22]
- If many reviews praise the business itself, such as seller service or the buying experience, the trusted advisor ad can focus on the business instead of one product. Product images can still be used, but the text should say plainly what the business is known for. [L1398, 2021-22]
- Social proof scale: "50K+ users" stated as "relieved and relaxed" gives concrete credibility, compared with an unspecified "thousands of happy customers." [L0313, 2026]
- Reply to every comment with helpful answers such as sizes or shipping times. Do not pitch or post sales links. Ask a follow-up question to keep the conversation going, which builds engagement that lowers CPM. [L0645, 2023]
- CPM was reported to fall from about $15 at launch to $5-$8 after building engagement with comments. Typical dropshipping CPMs are $20-$30. A new campaign can still show $20-$30 before it gets engagement. [L0645, 2023]
- An ad with zero comments looks untrustworthy, so people do not ask questions or comment. Seed the first comments and reviews yourself from the brand page at launch. [L0645, 2023]
- Ads with high engagement, such as comments, likes and shares, get a lower CPM and reach more people for the same spend. Creatives that invite viewers to comment or share can produce this effect. [L1171, 2024+]
- Example: an ad inviting viewers to post a photo of their own pet in the comments triggered many comments and likes. The higher engagement lowered the CPM, so the same budget reached more people. [L1171, 2024+]

### Single Image Ads

- Image ads are cheaper on Facebook and often convert better than video. Use images for products such as jewellery, fashion and some lifestyle products. [L0577, 2023]
- Image ads often have a lower CPM than video, so they reach more people for the same budget. Facebook may also show image ads more to users with a slow internet connection. Video ads cost more but can be useful for retargeting. [L0642, undated]
- Products that need no explanation, such as home decor, clothing, shoes and jewellery, can be sold with picture ads without video. Videos are needed for products that must be explained or demonstrated. [L0642, undated]
- Scaling with image ads alone is possible. One operator reached seven-figure revenue over six-plus months, with roughly 99% of sales from static image ads and no video ads. Image ads are easy to produce in Canva and carry better targeting than many alternatives. [L0349, 2024]
- For Facebook ads promoting clothing, image ads perform best compared with other formats. [L0779, 2023+]
- Include natural, non-graphic photo ads alongside designed graphics, because designed ads can still look like ads. Carousel ads suit lifestyle-heavy products such as fashion, and single image ads can also be strong. [L0341, 2023]
- For a single image ad, Meta's Ad Setup > Format offers Single image or video. Use Carousel only when several cards are needed on purpose. [L0865, 2025-26]
- Meme ad procedure: place a text on top of the image, add the product picture below, and add an emoji. The presenter builds these in Canva. Test multiple texts with one image. [L0642, undated]

### Video Ads

- A Facebook video ad is built in almost the same way as a static image ad. A static image version also works, though video is generally preferred by audiences. [L0488, 2021-22]
- Video length in the owner's clothing test: ads from 15 seconds to under 20 seconds, with the benefit delivered immediately, tended to outperform one-minute videos, with better click-through and conversion. A before-and-after format showing the result early, then the product, drove more clicks. A one-minute video still has value for retargeting viewers who watched more than 50% of it. [L1077, undated]
- Repurposing a standard Facebook ad into a TikTok aspect ratio performed very badly. Even uploaded as an organic video it got fewer than 100 views. Build creative native to each platform instead. [L1077, undated]
- Ad owner's early lesson: the early Facebook ads worked because the advertiser was also a target customer who knew the audience's interests and followed the same creators. Use a hook-pain-solution-benefit structure and show the product's benefits directly. [L1077, undated]
- Paid ads follow the same content logic as organic posts, because the ad only adds paid distribution and audience targeting. Content that performs organically tends to perform as ads with the right targeting. [L1217, 2023+]
- Transcript procedure for video ads:
  1. Get the video transcribed with an automatic online transcription tool such as Rev.
  2. Paste the exact transcript into the ad copy at the top.
  - Reason: some people do not listen to audio, so they can read the message instead. Always include a link to the page you want visitors to reach. [L0488, 2021-22]
- Rev is an online transcription tool that automatically turns video audio into text, useful for producing ad copy from video. [L0488, 2021-22]
- Facebook ads do not offer a click-to-call button. A call-to-action that asks people to phone the business must link to the website's contact page instead. Use the contact page URL as the ad destination. [L0488, 2021-22]
- To promote an existing post, use the version that already has the engagement. Likes, comments and views carry over from the original post, but the other platform's copy starts at zero. Example: use the Facebook post if it has far more views than the Instagram copy. [L0865, 2025-26]

### Carousel, Catalog and Collection Ads

- Carousel ads perform best on Facebook, and carousels with five cards perform very well. Catalog ads also work well on Facebook. [L0577, 2023]
- Carousel setup:
  - Pick 3 to 5 product images. The presenter uses 4. [L0642, undated]
  - Add an offer and a link to each card. Free shipping text is optional. [L0642, undated]
  - Use the collection page link as the destination. [L0642, undated]
  - Add a headline and description to each card. [L0642, undated]
  - Use the same Primary Text as the single image ad. [L0642, undated]
  - CTA: Shop Now or See more. [L0642, undated]
- Carousel ads perform well for product tests and show several product angles in one ad. [L1184, 2023+]
- Use a single image or video for most Facebook retargeting ads. Collection ads tend to underperform, and a carousel is an option only when several product views need to be shown. [L1342, 2021-22]
- Collection ads combine a video or slideshow at the top with dynamic product ads underneath. Shoppers can click through to the exact product they viewed, which makes them suitable for retargeting. [L1243, 2017]

### Slideshow Ads

- Slideshow ad procedure in Meta Ads Manager [L0642, undated]:
  1. Choose Create video instead of image or video.
  2. Select 4 product images.
  3. Click Edit and set the display time per image to 1 second.
  4. Arrange the image order.
  5. Click Play to preview.
  6. Click Save to computer and wait for the video to render.
  7. Upload it, then add headline, description and Shop Now CTA, and check the link.

### Specifications, Ratios and Safe Areas

- Feed ad ratio is 4:5, which is 1080 x 1350 pixels. Reels, Stories and TikTok use 9:16 portrait. Placing a 9:16 edit into a feed placement crops it to 4:5, so text outside the 4:5 area can be cut off. [L0344, 2023]
- Change the project ratio with the ratio button next to the playhead. 9:16 is the usual portrait ratio for Reels, Stories and TikTok. Build the edit in 9:16 and keep all text and graphics within the 4:5 area so the feed crop does not remove them. [L0344, 2023]
- Placing text high on the frame, near the face, is a common mistake because feed placements crop the top and bottom of a 9:16 edit. Move the text down so it stays inside the 4:5 area. Use a 4:5 overlay or a locked guide layer while editing. [L0344, 2023]
- Blend modes for an overlay [L0344, 2023]:

| Blend mode | Effect |
|---|---|
| Screen | Removes dark areas and keeps light ones |
| Multiply | Keeps dark areas and removes light ones |
| Soft light | A softer version of these two |
| Darken | Keeps only dark areas |
| Brighten | Keeps only light areas |

  - Lower the overlay opacity if the effect is too strong. [L0344, 2023]
- Facebook crops portrait video to 4:5 in feed placements. Keep the main subject and captions near the centre of the frame and place captions higher than the bottom edge, so the crop does not cut them off. [L1423, undated]
- Overlay banners at the very top of a video can be cropped out when Facebook shows the feed version at 4:5. Export a 4:5 feed version with sale banners inside the visible area, and use a 9:16 version for Stories and Reels where the top banner stays visible. [L1363, 2023]
- Ad image specification for link ads: exactly 1200 by 627 pixels, with no more than 20% of the image as text. Ads with more than 20% text on the image are rejected immediately. [L0641, 2014]
- Images with more than 20% text get the ad denied right away, so keep text on the image well under that threshold. Ads are also commonly denied in review, which is why a support ticket is the next step. [L0641, 2014]
- Keep text on Facebook ad images under 20% of the image area. Check with a grid overlay before launch. Images that pass this rule have the best chance of approval. [L0799, 2021-22]
- Facebook ad images should be JPG or PNG. Use square 1:1 for feed and in-stream video placements, and fullscreen vertical 9:16 for Stories. Use an image with little or no overlaid text to maximise delivery. [L1342, 2021-22]
- In Ads Manager, Automatically Crop to Square creates a square version of an uploaded image for placements that need it. Crop Image lets you adjust the crop manually for each placement. [L1342, 2021-22]
- A square ad uses a 1080 x 1080 pixel frame, a 1:1 aspect ratio. [L1427, 2023+]
- Set the frame size in the sequence settings of your editor, entering the horizontal and vertical pixel values for the ad format. Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and other editors have an equivalent setting. [L1427, 2023+]
- When Meta's ad preview shows dimension warnings for a 4:5 video, the warnings can be ignored in most cases, because a 4:5 file is served to most placements. Check that the upload shows the dimensions as fine, then continue. [L0647, 2024+]

### Placements, Previews and Destinations

- Preview each ad on Instagram, the Facebook feed and Facebook Marketplace before publishing. The same carousel or picture can look different in each placement. [L0642, undated]
- Separate formats for placements (procedure) [L0773, 2023]:
  1. Upload the horizontal video.
  2. Click Edit video, then Edit placements.
  3. Upload the story-format version for Stories.
  - Using the same video in two formats (feed and story) is also acceptable. [L0773, 2023]
- For a video that runs on Facebook and Instagram in different formats, upload the horizontal version first. Then click Edit video > Edit placements and upload the story version. Feed placements on Facebook and Instagram were the best placements for the lesson's account, so switch from automatic to manual placement once data shows feeds perform best. [L0774, 2023]
- The same image, headline and body copy can run on Instagram news feed placements. Keeping one creative across Facebook and Instagram supports consistent recognition. [L0415, 2021-22]
- Facebook creates the Instagram version of a Facebook feed ad automatically, so a separate Instagram ad does not have to be built. The Instagram version is identical to the Facebook version unless you change it. [L1169, 2021-22]
- Facebook feed ads can use a short or long copy version. Long copy gives more room for product detail, and a View more link lets readers expand it, so the most important text should sit at the top. [L1169, 2021-22]
- On Instagram, only the first line of body copy shows before the more link. The opening line must summarise what the ad is about so the viewer clicks to read the rest. [L1398, 2021-22]
- Set the ad destination to the product page or landing page for one-product stores. Use a collection link only when customers should choose between several products, such as fashion or jewellery. [L0648, undated]
- Set the ad CTA to Shop Now when the ad sends traffic to a collection page. Use Shop Now with the product page link when the ad sends traffic to one product. [L0642, undated]
- Multi-advertiser ads is on by default in the ad format section. Meta can personalise the ad and resize the creative for each viewer. Check the creative in the preview because it can be resized. [L0865, 2025-26]
- Ad-level Languages can translate the ad text based on the viewer's location or default language. It is optional and can stay off in a standard sales setup. [L0865, 2025-26]
