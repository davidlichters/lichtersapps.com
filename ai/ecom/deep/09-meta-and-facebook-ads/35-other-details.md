## Other details

### Platform reps and budget scaling

- Advertising platform reps are usually paid to increase spend. Meta's higher-level reps tend to focus on long-term results and give advice that produces faster results, while reps from other platforms often push large budgets toward new cold audiences. [L0801, 2021]
- When a platform rep suggests tripling a profitable campaign budget, test the advice cautiously. Scale what already works manually, and add prospecting only in small amounts instead of putting most of a larger budget into cold traffic. [L0801, 2021]
- Case: a store's Pinterest account manager advised raising monthly ad spend from 300 to 1,000 dollars, with most of the new money going to prospecting new cold customers and a small part to retargeting. The store's ads were already profitable, so the advice was to test prospecting carefully rather than quadruple the budget at once. [L0801, 2021]

### Meta ad setup and checks

- Meta changes the labels, defaults and options in Ads Manager often, so a setup guide can be outdated within months. Check every field against the current screen before applying a setting, and treat old screenshots and names as a guide only. [L0865, 2025-26]
- To see why you are being shown or retargeted by an ad, click the small arrow in the top-right corner of any Facebook or Instagram ad. This shows which pixel, page or audience triggered the ad. [L1243, 2017]
- Run all campaigns in the background rather than posting them to your Facebook business page with threads. [L0238, 2019]
- Advanced Meta ad strategies are later-stage options. Add them only after the core Meta setup is maxed out and still needs more levers. [L0544, 2026+]

### Feed training and organic reach

- The same feed-training approach works on Instagram Reels and Facebook. On Facebook, you can engage with ads from your own account to get more ads of the same type, so a separate account is optional. [L0706, 2020+]
- To target a specific country when posting organic Instagram reels, use VPN-based account setup. Without a VPN, reach is mostly in the account's home region, such as Switzerland, Germany, and Austria. [L1212, undated]
- As of the week of 29 June to 5 July 2026, Instagram's "Your Algorithm" control sits in the top-right corner of the Explore feed. Users can use it to tell Instagram which kinds of content they want to see more or less of, which helps brands find relevant creators and audiences. [L1504, 2025-26]
- Instagram was reported to be adding "more like this" and "less like this" buttons to Reels, with the "Your Algorithm" control testable from the Home feed. These feedback buttons were described as coming soon, not yet fully available. [L1504, 2025-26]

### Google Shopping campaign types

Google's campaign types changed in 2022. The guidance below is from that period and may no longer match Google's current options.

| Campaign type | Guidance | ID |
|---|---|---|
| Smart Shopping | Being discontinued between July and September 2022, replaced by Performance Max. | [L0249, 2022] |
| Smart Shopping | Results start strong but diminish over time, because Google exhausts warm and hot audiences (retargeting lists and brand searches) and then expands into colder audiences. Do not launch now due to discontinuation. | [L0249, 2022] |
| Performance Max | Currently in testing phase at small budgets. Do not recommend for shopping. It acts as a black box without data on why campaigns work or don't work, making scaling impossible. | [L0249, 2022] |
| Standard Shopping | No discontinuation planned. Use these as your primary campaign type. | [L0249, 2022] |
| Smart Shopping | If testing, run in parallel with your alpha/beta campaigns. Never replace your main campaigns with Smart Shopping. | [L0249, 2022] |

### Spark ads on TikTok

- Spark ads may slow the organic growth of an account if they run for a long time, though opinions on this are divided. Testing with two separate accounts, one for ads and one for organic, can show the effect on your own account. [L0352, 2022]
- Run spark ads for a short initial period, such as the first day or first week, to get past the early slump, then pause the ads. Continuous ad spending may tell TikTok to push organic content less. [L0352, 2022]
- Skip Spark Ads on a TikTok video that is already spreading organically, because boosting it adds little. Consider Spark Ads only when a new video shows solid early views but has not taken off on its own. [L1186, 2022]
- Spark ads budget example: load $100 into the account, set a $50 daily budget, and run about $20 per day per ad group. The minimum per ad group was reported as $20 to $25, and budget can be raised once a video produces strong impressions. [L0352, 2022]
- Spark ads require a TikTok Ads Manager account, with the TikTok account added or a code generated in the Ads Manager. This method is more efficient than other promotion options but carries a higher risk to organic reach. [L0352, 2022]

### TikTok campaign structure, timing and upkeep

- Testing structure: one campaign with three to four ad groups (open, stacked interest, hashtags, open or stacked interest again). Give each ad group a budget of around $25 to $30, because a $20 ad group budget often stalled spending on new accounts. Launch ad groups at 6 a.m. or 8 a.m. Eastern time. [L1382, undated]
- Best performance times for TikTok ads: 9 to 11 a.m., 12 to 3 p.m., and 7 to 11 p.m. Ads launched at 8 a.m. often start spending after about 30 to 40 minutes, so they effectively begin around 9 a.m. [L1382, undated]
- CBO campaigns on TikTok reach different audience pockets within a huge audience. Each campaign with several ad groups tends to hit different pockets, and the campaign's budget flows to the pocket that performs. Overlap between campaigns is therefore low on TikTok compared to Facebook. [L1382, undated]
- Check and rotate TikTok campaigns frequently, often every two to three days, because they lose performance over time. New campaigns with the same structure can replace the old ones without changing the store. [L1382, undated]
- If a campaign's budget keeps decreasing while CTR and CPM show zero, there may be a tracking issue. Check the date range in the top right of the dashboard. If the data still does not appear, stop the campaign and duplicate it to restart the data flow. [L1347, undated]

### TikTok ad rules and comments

- TikTok scans landing pages and enforces strict rules for health and fitness claims such as weight loss. Keep claims compliant and make product pages distinct. [L1213, 2023]
- Turn off comments on TikTok ads. The 18 to 24 age group discusses openly, and comments can reveal dropshipping publicly. [L1213, 2023]

### Store landing pages

- A landing page should combine a large value-proposition hero, early trust signals, benefit-focused copy, a clear offer, and calls to action repeated lower on the page. Each element exists to make a buyer with existing intent cross the decision threshold without friction. [L0918, 2023]
- The hero section should give a snapshot of the main outcome the customer gets, followed by heavy trust-building directly around it. The goal is that visitors believe the promised result is achievable with this brand. [L0918, 2023]
- On a Shopify free theme such as Dawn, a quick add to cart button on the collection page can be enabled without installing an app. Products with options open a small page for choosing the variant before adding to cart. [L0642, undated]

### Order value and return on ad spend

- When the paid ad cost per customer stays the same, a higher order value from that same customer directly improves the return on ad spend. Raising AOV through bundles, cross-sells and upsells therefore gets more out of every paid acquisition. [L0435, undated]
- Example Facebook ad spend over 90 days: $300,000. [L0222, 2025-26]
- Paid traffic for high-ticket stores runs on Google Ads, Bing Ads and Facebook Ads, and the same operator can manage budgets across all three. Being able to manage several ad platforms is an advantage when scaling. [L1014, 2021-22]

### Audience timing and urgency

- For financial products affecting retirees, customer decision urgency and timing follow demographic-specific patterns. Urgency peaks sharply 90 days before the filing deadline, and again at age 64.5-65 when benefits become available. Adjust ad frequency, budget allocation, and messaging intensity to align with these psychological urgency peaks. [L0221, 2026]
- Volume of decision-makers also signals opportunity: 71 million people currently receive social security, with Boomers moving through peak claiming ages now. [L0221, 2026]

### Local agency outreach and offline ads

Cold outreach to a local dentist using Facebook ads follows these steps:

1. Find dentists in the chosen city with D7 Lead Finder. [L0440, 2023+]
2. Check whether each business is running Facebook ads, and review those ads. [L0440, 2023+]
3. If the ads are weak or missing, call the business and ask for 10 minutes to present the agency. [L0440, 2023+]
4. Show a short presentation on what the agency can do. [L0440, 2023+]
5. Offer to test the ads at no up-front cost to the client, with the client paying the ad spend directly. [L0440, 2023+]
6. Set up a Facebook campaign focused on the Invisalign product. [L0440, 2023+]
7. Once the test produces leads, set a monthly fee for managing the ads. [L0440, 2023+]

- Run the client's ad test for about two weeks at no charge, with the ad spend paid directly by the client. Aim for around 30 to 40 leads in that period before stopping the ads and presenting results. [L0440, 2023+]
- Billboards, mall events and similar offline advertising are generally not worth it for sales. Consider them only if very cheap and used for photos and social proof. [L0969, 2022]
