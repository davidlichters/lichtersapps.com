# Legal, taxes, banking and payments

> Keep business money separate, use Shopify Payments, publish honest policies you can keep, hold cash for slow payouts, and ask a local accountant about taxes early.

## Rules

1. If you are under 18, Shopify, payment providers and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so set them up with your parents.
2. Open a separate business bank account in the country where you sell; most banks do it online in about 10 minutes.
3. Never mix personal and business money, because it makes bookkeeping hard and can cause tax trouble.
4. Use Shopify Payments as your main checkout; one 2026 seller skips PayPal because it approves transactions slowly.
5. A new Shopify account pays out after about 21 days, so keep cash for ads and suppliers until then (2026).
6. Pay a credit card in full every month, because you are personally liable for it, even with a company.
7. Publish refund, shipping, privacy and terms pages and link them in the footer.
8. Only promise delivery times and returns your supplier can really deliver.
9. Your business details must match exactly on your site, Shopify, payment and ad accounts, or accounts can get frozen.
10. Never make result claims about health, money or life in ads, because ad networks block them and can ban the account.
11. Only post content you own or license; Instagram can disable accounts that post content without rights (2026).
12. Start bookkeeping with your first sale.
13. Taxes differ by country and state, so check the rules in your country.

## Steps

1. Choose a business form. Many start as a sole trader and switch to a company later.
2. Get your business or tax number, because suppliers and payment providers ask for it.
3. Open a low fee business bank account in the selling country.
4. Create the Shopify account with the country where the business is based.
5. Turn on Shopify Payments and add bank details.
6. Write the four policy pages, paste the same texts into Shopify's policy settings, and add them to the footer.
7. Set Shopify's customer privacy settings, such as the cookie banner and data sale opt out.
8. Set up taxes only where you must collect them.
9. Track revenue, product cost, ad spend and fees per sale in a sheet.
10. Find an accountant now, even if you need one later.

## Key numbers

- **21 days**: payout delay for a new Shopify account; it drops to 14, 7, 5, 3 days, then daily as you fulfill orders (2026).
- **2.9%**: card fee on Shopify Basic with a US company; a 2026 Singapore example showed 3.2%.
- **30 days**: payment delay a credit card gives you, useful for ad bills before payouts (2026).
- **5 to 7 business days**: refund time to the original payment method in a 2026 example policy.
- **14, 30, 90 days or custom**: return windows in Shopify return rules (2026).
- **14 to 21 business days standard, 7 to 10 express**: delivery times in a 2026 shipping policy template.
- **3 to 6 months, 30 days notice**: first contract length and exit notice with an agency (2026).
- **9%**: Singapore GST on ad spend; $100,000 of ads meant about 9,000 SGD tax (2026).
- **90 to 120 days**: how long payment providers can hold your money if your bank account is in another country than the one you sell in (older advice, 2020).
- **A$75,000**: yearly revenue before GST registration in Australia (older advice, check current rules).

## Common mistakes

- Taking payments into a personal bank or personal PayPal account.
- Setting the wrong country in Shopify, which breaks payments and payouts.
- Spending all cash on ads, then waiting weeks for the first payout.
- Copying a refund policy your supplier will not support.
- Policy texts in Shopify that differ from the real pages, or two privacy policies.
- Hiding terms and privacy links; a pre-launch check wants them in the header and footer.
- Waiting with bookkeeping until tax time. One seller paid all profit of a record month to the tax office.
- Paying strangers who DM you claiming to be a coach. Real helpers in a paid community do not message first to ask for money.
- Signing a long contract with an agency you do not know yet.

## FAQ

**Do I need a company to start?** No. A sole trader is fine at first. A company protects personal assets, so check your country's rules.

**Can I use my home address?** Yes, older lessons say that is fine. A cheap virtual address adds privacy.

**Shopify Payments or PayPal?** Shopify Payments first. Older advice added PayPal for customers who want it.

**Why has Shopify not paid me yet?** New accounts wait about 21 days.

**Shopify wants my ID again after launch. Normal?** Yes, checks can come a month or two later. Send what they ask.

**Do I need a credit card?** No, a debit card works. A credit card adds about 30 days before you pay.

**Do I have to charge tax?** It depends on where buyers live and on revenue limits. Ask an accountant.

**My payment or ad account is frozen. What now?** Stay calm and reply with clear, matching information. No reply means no fix.

**Someone copies my ads or photos?** Use the platform's infringement form. Some platforms need a registered trademark, and removal can take days to months.

**Is it safe to paste business data into AI tools?** Treat anything you type into AI as a small data risk.

**Do I need insurance?** Product liability insurance matters most, and the product needs its required certifications; a China supplier or testing agency can help.
