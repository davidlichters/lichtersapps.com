# Apps and tools

> Start with a short list of apps and one AI tool, add each new app only when you need it, and check that every app pays for itself.

## Rules

1. Install only apps with a clear job, because each app on your store adds code and can slow it down.
2. Do not stack cart apps: pick one cart tool (for example a slide cart) instead of stacking several cart extensions (2026).
3. Use one bundle app for all bundle offers; one 2026 lesson says the bundle app it used started billing (the speaker believed $15) only once a customer checked out with a bundle.
4. When you test a new product, skip extras like shipping protection in the cart, and add them later when you scale.
5. Use Shopify Functions for cart discounts, not old Shopify Scripts, which are being phased out (2026).
6. Use one AI chat tool; a 2026 lesson says ChatGPT and Claude both work well, so you only need one of them.
7. When you get stuck, describe the problem to your AI tool and ask it for three possible fixes (2026).
8. Never share an API key; if one leaks, revoke it at once and create a fresh key.
9. Only put files into an AI work folder that the AI may see, because it can read everything you drag in, card details included.
10. Use free research before paid research: the Meta Ad Library is free, and an ad that has run for months counts as a winner.
11. Start automations with the simplest tool, because one 2026 lesson calls the developer tool confusing for beginners.
12. Do not run A/B tests when your store has too little traffic; fix obvious problems directly instead.

## Steps

1. Set up a good theme first; a 2026 lesson says it covers most needs, so you need few apps.
2. Add the basics only: a review app, a bundle app, a cart app, and a free Shopify app for digital files if you give a free ebook.
3. Pick one AI tool and make one project per product, so all chats share files and memory.
4. Research competitors in the Meta Ad Library. Look for pages with more than 80 active ads and ads that have run for months.
5. If you use a paid spy tool, add the competitor brand and wait at least 48 hours before you trust its data.
6. Write ad ideas with your AI tool: give it real customer reviews and ask for 5 marketing angles to test.
7. Make images and simple creatives in a design tool like Canva.
8. After launch, add a post-purchase survey. Export answers once you have 50, 100 or 200+ and give them to your AI.
9. Add email marketing and pop-ups when traffic starts to come.
10. Build automations later, starting with one simple workflow.

## Key numbers

- **$15**: what one speaker believed a bundle app bills, starting when a customer first checks out with a bundle (2026).
- **About $5**: a back-in-stock or pre-order notification app (2026).
- **Maybe $20 a month**: typical price of an app for conversion elements (2026).
- **$0**: Shopify's own app for digital downloads, used to deliver a free ebook (2026).
- **4 tiers**: the maximum steps in a tiered progress bar in one cart app (2026).
- **More than 80 active ads**: a page worth studying in ad research (2026).
- **At least 48 hours**: data time a spy tool needs after you add a brand (2026).
- **5 to 10**: top ad scripts in your niche to collect into one document before you write your own (2026).
- **50, 100, 200+**: survey answers to collect before you export them (2026).
- **20 people**: if that many customers mention the same thing, talk about it more in your ads (2026).
- **About 1200 px**: image width used in a bulk resize to help load speed (2026).
- **A couple of dollars per automation per month**: rough cost of the easiest automation tool (2026).
- **30% of sales**: share one store owner once got after a live chat (older advice, 2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- Installing piles of apps at once. An older lesson warns that a long app list hurts page speed.
- Turning on shipping protection and extras while still testing a product.
- Trusting every AI answer. A 2026 lesson notes that AI often falls back on generic, safe advice, so check its tool picks yourself.
- Pasting API keys into public chats or shared documents.
- Using image compression apps inside Shopify. An older lesson found them unreliable and compressed images before upload.

## FAQ

**Which apps do I need on day one?**
A review app, a bundle app and one cart app. Email comes later.

**Which page builder is best?**
One 2026 lesson says it does not matter which builder you use. One builder has better templates, another is easier.

**Claude or ChatGPT?**
Either works. A 2026 lesson says both do the job well, so pay for only one.

**I hit the free AI limit. What now?**
One lesson recommends a paid plan if you keep hitting the free limit. The desktop work modes need the Pro plan.

**Do I need a paid spy tool?**
No. Start with the free Meta Ad Library. Paid tools save time but are optional.

**Should I A/B test my store?**
Not if you have little traffic. Fix clear problems first.

**Which automation tool should I start with?**
The simplest one. A 2026 lesson says to move to a cheaper tool only when you build many automations.

**How do I protect my API key?**
Keep it private, run tools that hold it only on your own computer, and revoke it if it leaks.

**Do chat apps slow my store?**
Some do. An older lesson found one chat app blocked the whole page. Test speed after every new app.

**How do I get help with an app?**
Contact the app developer. You find their contact details on the app's listing in the Shopify App Store.

**Can I run all this if I am under 18?**
Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so a parent must own those accounts.
