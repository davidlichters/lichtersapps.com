# Customer service, returns and chargebacks

> Ship fast, answer fast and refund before a customer goes to the bank, because a chargeback rate above 1% can freeze 10% of your payouts.

## Rules

1. Fulfill every order quickly, because unfulfilled orders lead to chargebacks and held payouts.
2. Keep your chargeback rate under 1%, since anything above that can trigger a 10% payout reserve.
3. Offer a refund, a replacement or a partial refund to an unhappy customer before it turns into a chargeback.
4. Use a contact form instead of a public support email, because a listed email gets scraped and flooded with spam.
5. Put your help center link in the footer and, if possible, in the header.
6. Write a clear return policy with a time window (30 days after delivery is the most common in recent lessons).
7. Ask customers to report lost or damaged parcels within 7 days of the delivery date.
8. Make customers tick a terms and conditions box before checkout, and link it to your real policy pages.
9. Never invent an answer; say you will check and get back to the customer.
10. Never send a different product than the one you advertised; it causes "not as described" chargebacks.
11. If you are under 18, a parent usually has to own the Shopify, payment and ad accounts.

## Steps

1. Write your policies: shipping, returns, refunds and terms. Adjust any template to how your business really works, and check the rules in your country (the EU gives buyers 14 days to return for any reason).
2. Set up a contact form, a chat widget and a help center, ideally all in one inbox.
3. Turn your most common questions (tracking, returns, discount codes, order changes) into quick answers and saved replies.
4. Send automatic shipping updates so customers know where their parcel is before they ask.
5. On a complaint, read the whole email history and offer a replacement or partial refund.
6. When a chargeback or dispute arrives, collect evidence: the customer emails as a PDF, the packing slip, the order page with billing and shipping address, tracking and delivery date, and your policies.
7. Write a short reason: why the order was not fraud (same billing and shipping address, the card was charged only once), when it shipped, when it was delivered, and whether the customer ever contacted you.
8. Proofread everything, then submit before the deadline.
9. For PayPal, Klarna or Afterpay disputes, reply inside their portal, address the message to the customer (not the platform) and sign as your shop.
10. Track satisfaction (CSAT) and look at every rating under 4 out of 5 to find what to fix.

## Key numbers

- **Above 1% chargeback rate:** can trigger a 10% payout reserve (2026).
- **25% reserve:** Shopify Payments may hold this if you fulfill too slowly over about 6 weeks; holds can last 1 to 4 months (2026).
- **About 2 months without fulfilling:** one shop had its whole payment account frozen, 100% (2026).
- **3 chargebacks in one week:** one 2026 lesson warns this can cost you the store if your dispute tooling is not set up.
- **About 75 days:** how long a bank takes to decide a chargeback (2026).
- **About 20 to 30 USD fee per chargeback** you lose, on top of the order amount (2026).
- **Evidence files on Shopify:** png, jpg or pdf, each under 2 MB (2026).
- **Return rate:** about 20% on average, up to 30% on the high end (2026).
- **Return window:** 30 days is most common; some shops use 14 days or even 3 days (2026).
- **Lost or damaged parcel report window:** 7 days after delivery (2026).
- **Refund time:** a refund takes 5-10 working days to reach the card or account the customer paid with (2026).
- **Return fee:** 64% of brands charge one; average about 8 USD in the US and 5 AUD in Australia (2026).
- **Package protection:** usually priced around 2% of the cart value.
- **Quick answer buttons in a chat widget:** up to 6; fill all of them with your top questions (2026).
- **Outsourcing support:** from about 400 USD per month, after doing it yourself for a few months (older advice, 2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- Fulfilling late or not at all. This brings chargebacks and can freeze your payouts.
- Listing your support email openly and drowning in spam.
- Fighting a customer instead of refunding a small order, then paying the order, the fee and a reserve.
- Submitting too little evidence. Send more proof than feels necessary.
- Leaving automatic dispute refunds (RDR) on blindly. They can refund customers even after the order shipped; one seller lost several thousand USD (2026).
- Running a subscription on a seasonal product, which brings many chargebacks.
- Selling products you are not allowed to resell.

## FAQ

**What is a chargeback?** The customer asks their bank to reverse the payment. If you lose, you lose the money plus a fee.

**What happens if I get too many?** Above 1% your payment provider can hold 10% of payouts. If it keeps going, the account can be closed.

**Should I fight every chargeback?** Fight with evidence when you delivered. Before it reaches the bank, try a refund or replacement.

**What evidence do I send?** Emails, packing slip, order page, tracking with delivery date, your policies and the accepted terms checkbox.

**How long should my return window be?** 30 days is the common choice. Shorter windows exist, but EU customers have 14 days by law.

**Can I refuse some returns?** Yes. Many shops exclude food, hygiene items, used goods, final sale items and gift cards.

**Can I charge a return fee?** Yes. Return and repeat purchase rates stayed stable after brands added one.

**What if a customer asks something I do not know?** Say you will check, ask your supplier, then reply. Never guess.

**When should I outsource support?** After a few months of doing it yourself, when order volume grows (older advice).

**Does my PayPal account have risks too?** Yes. Many returns or chargebacks can get it limited.

## Where courses disagree

- **Return window:** 2026 lessons show 30 days, 14 days and even 3 days. Older 2021-22 shops gave 30 days only as store credit with a 10% restocking fee.
- **Phone support:** 2021-22 lessons pushed a public phone line, call-backs and post-purchase calls 7 days after purchase. 2026 lessons focus on chat, help center and automation.
- **Reserve size:** one 2026 lesson says 10% above 1% chargebacks, another says 25% for slow fulfillment, and an older one mentions a 60,000 USD hold.
