# Suppliers and fulfillment

> Use a China agent who ships single orders with no minimum, promise honest delivery times, check every bill and batch, and keep a backup supplier.

## Rules

1. In the 2026 model a China agent or private supplier ships each order to your customer, so ask for the exact product with no minimum order quantity (MOQ) before you start.
2. Never promise 3 to 5 day delivery from China; set 12 to 21 or 10 to 14 business days at checkout.
3. Check that your supplier can really handle the returns your refund policy promises before you publish it.
4. Ask for samples from every production run and have the first batch sent to you to check.
5. Have at least two suppliers for your main product, so one can take over if the other is late.
6. Read every supplier invoice, because suppliers sometimes overcharge, for example double shipping on a two item order.
7. Do not sign long locked contracts with suppliers, 3PLs or freight forwarders, because these costs drop as you grow.
8. Be polite but firm, and replace a supplier who keeps failing.
9. Treat good agents well, because that is how you later get net terms (paying 2 weeks or 20 days after shipping).
10. Every product must leave at least 25% margin including shipping, or it does not go in the store (2021-22 lesson, still a useful floor).

## Steps

1. Pick the product, then message a supplier or agent (WhatsApp is common with China suppliers) with a product photo or link, and ask: exact product, no MOQ, ships to your main country, price per unit plus shipping.
2. Put cost and shipping into a margin calculator. Example from a 2026 lesson: $20 product plus $10 shipping means $30 total cost per order.
3. Order a sample and check quality, size and packaging yourself.
4. Give the agent collaborator or partner access to your Shopify store, so their software can pull and fulfill orders.
5. Ask which tracking site they use, so customers can follow their parcel.
6. Set shipping in Shopify (Settings, Shipping and delivery, General profile). One 2026 lesson names the rate "Free Express Delivery" and makes separate zones for the US, UK, Canada and Australia.
7. Set inventory to a high number such as 9999, but check with the agent that real stock exists.
8. Pay the supplier per order or per batch by bank transfer.
9. Each week, ask for photos or videos of products being packed, and compare shipping charges with your orders.

## Key numbers

- **12 to 21 or 10 to 14 business days**: delivery window a 2026 lesson sets at checkout when shipping from a China agent.
- **3 to 4 weeks**: old China dropshipping delivery time that customers hated (2021-22, older advice).
- **$20 + $10 = $30**: example product cost plus shipping in a 2026 margin calculator.
- **25%**: minimum margin including shipping before a supplier's product goes in the store (2021-22).
- **5% or 10%**: price cut to ask for once you buy a lot from one supplier (2021-22 example after $700,000 of yearly purchases).
- **2 weeks or 20 days**: net terms from an agent, called even better than paying up front (2026).
- **9999**: inventory number set in Shopify for a dropshipped product (2026).
- **About $2,500**: first order some brand suppliers asked for before dropshipping single orders (2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- Promising fast shipping you cannot deliver. It leads to angry customers and refund requests.
- Writing a return policy your supplier cannot support.
- Trusting the first sample. A later batch can differ from the sample you approved, so check every production run.
- Relying on one supplier. In a 2026 call, a store owner said she now keeps two manufacturers, so she can switch when one cannot deliver in time.
- Locking into long contracts too early.
- Running affiliate offers on products that often go out of stock. Orders get cancelled, but you already paid the affiliate.

## FAQ

**How do I find a new manufacturer when mine is bad?** Contact many suppliers, ask all your questions, and test samples until one fits. Chinese suppliers are often easy to work with.

**Can I put quality rules in a contract?** Yes. Start with tech packs and spec sheets, approve a sample, then check each batch against it and ask for weekly photos or videos.

**Do I need to visit the factory?** Not early on. Later a visit helps, or you can send a representative to do checks for you.

**Is everything negotiable?** Yes. But a very hard line at the start can stop the relationship before it begins.

**When can I ask for better prices?** After you have sent real volume. A 2021-22 example asks for 5 or 10% off, or a lower shipping rate for the next year.

**What if the supplier ignores me or makes mistakes?** Push firmly but politely. If it keeps happening, switch to your backup.

**How do I send orders to a supplier?** An agent's software pulls orders from Shopify. Smaller suppliers may get the order details by email.

**Do I pay the supplier before or after the customer pays?** After. The customer pays you first, then you pay the supplier for that order.

**Can the supplier leave me?** Yes. A supplier can pull a product anytime without asking you. In a 2021-22 Q&A, someone who bought a store lost its main supplier the same day he tried to change the payment card.

**How do I handle returns?** Ask the supplier what they accept, then write a policy that matches it.

**I am under 18. Can I do this?** Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so ask your parents. Import duties and taxes differ, so check the rules in your country.

## Checklist

- [ ] Supplier confirmed exact product, no MOQ, ships to my country
- [ ] Unit cost plus shipping is in my margin sheet
- [ ] Margin is at least 25% after shipping
- [ ] Sample received and checked
- [ ] Agent has collaborator access to my store
- [ ] Checkout shows honest delivery times
- [ ] Return policy matches what the supplier allows
- [ ] Tracking site known and shared with customers
- [ ] Backup supplier found

## Templates

**Supplier inquiry**
> Hi, I run an online store and want to sell this product (photo attached). Can you ship single orders with no minimum order to the USA? Please send the unit price, shipping cost per order, delivery time and which tracking site you use. Thank you!

**Customer delay message**
> Hi [name], thanks for your order! Your parcel is on its way. Delivery takes [X to Y] business days, and you can follow it here: [tracking link]. If anything looks wrong, just reply and we will help.

**Overcharge message to supplier**
> Hi, order [number] had 2 items in one parcel, but shipping was charged twice. Please correct the invoice. Thank you.

## Where courses disagree

- **Local vs China suppliers**: 2021-22 lessons preferred suppliers with stock in the customer's country, because China shipping took 3 to 4 weeks. 2026 lessons use China agents with 10 to 21 business day delivery. Our model is the 2026 one.
- **Calling vs messaging**: 2021-22 lessons built the website first and then phoned brand suppliers for approval, with just over 60% saying yes. 2026 lessons message China suppliers on WhatsApp.
- **Credit terms**: a 2021-22 lesson said to tell brand suppliers you pay up front and want no credit. A 2026 lesson says net terms from an agent are even better.
