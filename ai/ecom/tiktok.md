# TikTok and short video

> Every piece of content should be a short vertical video, posted on a steady schedule at the time your audience is active, and reused on all big short-video apps.

## Rules

1. Make videos, not photos: in 2026 lessons, static posts are described as doing almost nothing for reach.
2. Post on a schedule you can keep: one video a week beats ten videos on one day and then nothing for a month.
3. Post at the same time each time, based on your insights (for example, if 7 a.m. works best, always post around 7 a.m.).
4. Keep each video under 3 minutes, because longer videos hurt Reels reach.
5. Export in 1080 resolution when you can.
6. Use only 3 to 5 hashtags, and they must fit your niche.
7. Do not stuff captions with emojis or hashtags; platforms treat that as spam and stop pushing the video.
8. Do not use engagement bait ("comment YES if..."), because Instagram has said it suppresses it.
9. Mix content about 30% trends and 70% evergreen videos that still make sense months later.
10. Upload one file and one caption to all 5 main short-video apps, and keep that job under 10 minutes.
11. Answer comments, and if someone replies again, keep answering so the thread stays alive.
12. Engage with other creators in your niche, because the algorithm rewards accounts that interact with others.
13. When you test a product with video ads, plan around 6 to 8 video variants.

## Steps

1. **Train your feed.** Spend time watching, liking and commenting on the kind of videos you want to make. This teaches the app what your account is about.
2. **Pick your apps.** Use the 5 main short-video apps: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and Snapchat.
3. **Plan a mix.** For every 10 ideas, make about 3 trend videos and 7 evergreen videos (product in use, problem and solution, how-to, behind the scenes).
4. **Film vertical and short.** Stay well under 3 minutes. Export in 1080.
5. **Write a clean caption.** One clear line, 3 to 5 niche hashtags, no emoji walls, no bait.
6. **Post at your best time.** Open your insights, see the hour your viewers are online most, and use that hour for every upload.
7. **Cross-post.** Upload the same file and caption to all 5 apps in under 10 minutes. On Instagram, Reels, carousels and photos also post to Threads automatically.
8. **Test on Instagram with Trial Reels.** A Trial Reel is shown only to people who do not follow you. Use it to test new ideas without risk to your main profile. If one performs, you may move it onto your main profile, but you do not have to.
9. **Try new features.** Instagram tends to give videos that use its newest features an early boost.
10. **Talk to people.** Reply to comments and keep threads going. Comment on similar creators' videos.
11. **Turn winners into ads.** When you move to paid Meta ads, test 6 to 8 video versions per product. Read your ad copy one more time, then launch.

## Common mistakes

- Posting photos and wondering why nothing grows.
- Posting a lot for a few days, then going silent for weeks.
- Posting at random times instead of your audience's active time.
- Using 20 hashtags and a row of emojis.
- Asking for comments in a baity way.
- Making only trend videos that are dead in two weeks.
- Ignoring follow-up questions in the comments.
- Testing a product with only one or two videos.

## FAQ

**Photos or videos?**
Videos. 2026 lessons say static posts do almost nothing.

**How often should I post?**
At least once a week, every week. Steady beats a lot.

**What time should I post?**
Your insights show the hour your viewers are online most. Use that hour for every upload.

**How long can a video be?**
Under 3 minutes for Reels. Shorter is usually safer.

**How many hashtags?**
3 to 5 that fit your niche.

**Should I just copy trends?**
Use some trends, but about 70% of your videos should still make sense months later.

**Can I post the same video on every app?**
Yes. Same video, same caption, all 5 apps, under 10 minutes.

**What is a Trial Reel?**
An Instagram post shown only to non-followers. Good for testing ideas. You decide later if it goes on your profile.

**Can I repost the same video many times?**
Some creators repost a winner as a Trial Reel up to 10 times a day. Be careful: spammy behavior gets pushed less.

**How do I get more reach without paying?**
Reply to comments, keep conversations going, comment on similar creators, and use new app features early.

**How many videos do I need to test one product?**
About 6 to 8 variants.

**Which ad videos scale best?**
One 2026 lesson reports that native-style ads never scaled as well for the speaker as UGC-style videos or bottom-of-funnel ads.

**I am under 18. Can I do this?**
Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, often a parent. Check the rules in your country.

## Where courses disagree

- **Funnel role.** Older advice (2021-22, high-ticket era) saw social media mainly as a way to reach people who do not know you yet and said YouTube works across the whole funnel. 2026 lessons treat short video as the main way to grow on any platform and as raw material for ads.
- **Reposting.** One 2026 lesson reports creators reposting a viral video up to 10 times a day as Trial Reels. Another 2026 lesson warns that spammy behavior is pushed less and says steady weekly posting matters more than volume. Test carefully.
