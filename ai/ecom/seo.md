# SEO and content

> SEO catches people who already search for what you sell, so set up clean pages now, keep paid ads as your main engine, and build content slowly with one keyword per page.

## Rules

1. Treat SEO as demand capture, not demand creation: it serves people who already search and brings no traffic tomorrow (2026).
2. Give every keyword exactly one page, because Google usually shows only one page per site for a search.
3. Match the page type to the search: if the top results are guides, write a guide; if the top 5 are product pages, use a product page.
4. Make collection pages your main SEO pages, with the target keyword as the H1 and inside at least one H2 (2026).
5. Write a blog post within 100 to 200 words of the median length of the top 10 results (2026).
6. Never publish AI text without a human edit; in one 2026 test only 100 of 300 AI link ideas were usable.
7. Give product pages the most internal links, link blogs to collections, and never link product pages to blogs, since that moves buyers away from checkout (2026).
8. Use the focus keyword in the title, URL, first 100 words, one H2 and one image name and alt text, 5 to 10 times per page in total (2021-22).
9. Keep at least 300 unique words on pages you want to rank, and at least 75% of each page unique (2021-22).
10. Do not change the URL of a live page; when you delete a page, 301 redirect it to a related page.

## Steps

1. Connect Google Search Console, submit your sitemap, and check that every product's search preview text makes sense.
2. Run your root keywords (the broadest terms for your products) through a keyword tool set to your target country and sort by volume.
3. Drop terms under 100 monthly searches and terms for things you do not sell.
4. Group the rest by topic and map each to a page type: product names to product pages, product types to collections, questions to blog posts.
5. Build collection pages first: keyword H1, short intro, about 100 words under each subheading.
6. Fix product pages: title as brand, model, product type, descriptor; a unique file name and alt tag for each image; keyword 2 to 3 times on short pages.
7. For each blog post, study the top 10 results (length, H2 and H3 count, lists, tables). Copy the structure, not the words, and add something only you know.
8. Link while you write: blog to blog, blog to collection, collection to collection. No page more than 4 links from the homepage.
9. Promote each new piece for 2 weeks to 30 days through 3 to 4 channels.
10. Regularly export 30 to 95 days of Search Console data and rewrite the title and intro of pages that already get clicks. Check changes in a spreadsheet first.

## Key numbers

- **About 75%** of clicks for a search go to the top 3 organic results (2021-22).
- **1 to 3%** click rate is normal for a result low on page one (2026).
- **Top 10 median, plus or minus 100 to 200 words:** blog length target; results of 1200 to 1800 words give about 1500 (2026).
- **300 words:** minimum unique text so a page is not seen as thin (2021-22).
- **5 to 10 uses** of the focus keyword per page, counting headings, image names and alt text (2021-22).
- **100 monthly searches:** cut-off for a keyword list (2021-22).
- **Keyword difficulty above 10** (scale 0 to 100) is a good target in most niches (2021-22).
- **5 to 10 good backlinks** are often enough, not 100 (2021-22).
- **800 x 800 px** product images, never under 600 x 600, saved as compressed JPEG (2021-22).
- **2 pieces of content per month** for a steady plan (2021-22).
- **About 30K in gross sales:** when a paid SEO app starts to matter (2026).

## Common mistakes

- Several pages or posts chasing the same keyword, so none ranks.
- Too few internal links, or random links everywhere, so every page looks equally important.
- Blog posts nothing links to (orphan pages).
- Collection pages with only a product grid; only giant brands rank with those.
- The same long shipping or returns block on every product page.
- Copying supplier text with a few words changed.
- Hiding products instead of setting stock to 0, which creates broken links.
- Hard "buy now" calls on articles with no buying intent; an email signup fits better (2026).

## FAQ

**I wrote two posts for the same keyword. What now?** Keep the stronger one. Delete the weaker one if it has no backlinks; if it has links, move it to a new keyword.

**I bought an old domain with strong links. Will SEO work right away?** Not for sure. One experienced seller did this and still struggled to rank a new store.

**How many backlinks do I need?** More or better ones than the pages you compete with. One student read a difficulty of 20 as roughly 20 backlinks.

**My site's authority score is 7. What now?** Get some backlinks plus as many brand mentions and press stories as you can.

**My buyers do not search for single products.** Target the style or use instead, one step higher in the funnel. That is what content is for.

**Is a "my brand vs big brand" page a good idea?** Yes. A coach suggested comparison pages plus a video comparing the products.

**Can AI write my articles?** Yes, it drafts fast, but edit and fact check. Give it your real product catalog so it does not invent specs.

**Do meta descriptions help ranking?** No (2026), but a clear title and description can still raise clicks.

**Should I hide a product I no longer sell?** Set stock to 0 if it may return. If it is gone for good, delete it and redirect to a similar product.

**What is the fastest fix for a page that already ranks?** Rewrite its intro paragraph around the keyword with the best opportunity (2026).

## Checklist

- [ ] Search Console connected, sitemap submitted
- [ ] Each keyword mapped to one page
- [ ] Collection pages have keyword H1, intro and text sections
- [ ] Product titles follow brand, model, type, descriptor
- [ ] Every image has a unique file name and alt tag, compressed
- [ ] Every blog links to at least one collection; no orphan pages
- [ ] AI text edited and fact checked by a human
- [ ] Deleted pages redirected with a 301
- [ ] Under 18: Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so ask your parents; check the rules in your country

## Where courses disagree

- **Post length:** 2021-22 says always 1500+ words; 2026 says match the top 10 median, because too long also fails.
- **Meta descriptions:** 2021-22 says make each unique with the keyword; 2026 calls that box checking.
- **Who writes:** 2021-22 says hire a freelance writer; 2026 uses AI drafts plus a human edit.
- **Volume vs quality:** 2021-22 says 2 quality pieces a month beat more weak ones; 2026 social lessons put consistency above quality.
