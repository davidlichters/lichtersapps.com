# Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads

> Set up your own account and pixel cleanly, test with about $50 a day in one simple CBO, judge only by cost per purchase after enough days, and scale slowly in 15 to 20% steps.

## Rules

1. Use your own Business Manager, ad account, page and pixel, connected to Shopify before you spend anything.
2. Always pick the Sales objective, the Website conversion location and Purchase as the conversion event.
3. Start with one CBO (campaign budget) campaign, broad targeting (18 to 65+, all genders) and automatic placements.
4. Give every ad set a single concept with 3 to 5 creatives, and never mix concepts in one ad set.
5. Spend at least $50 a day, because less does not create enough learnings for Meta to get out of the learning phase.
6. Leave a new campaign alone for at least 3 days, and give evergreen campaigns 7 days before you cut budget.
7. Raise a winning budget by 15 to 20% at a time, then wait about 3 days before the next raise.
8. Keep the same pixel while you stay in one niche, and use a new one when you change niche.
9. Keep bottom-of-funnel image ads in a campaign without videos, or the videos take all the budget.
10. Run no more than 3 CBOs at once, because more campaigns split your data.
11. Exclude buyers of the last 180 days, so most of your spend goes to new and engaged people.

## Steps

1. Finish the store and wait until Shopify has reviewed it.
2. Create your brand page (logo, banner, short bio, website link) and link Instagram.
3. In Shopify, install the Facebook and Instagram app, connect your Business Manager, pick the right pixel and share Maximum or Enhanced data.
4. Verify your domain, then check the pixel with Test Events (add to cart, start checkout).
5. Warm up a new ad account: one small engagement ad with a $20 budget, turned off after it spends $8 to $10.
6. Save a column preset: spend, purchases, cost per purchase, ROAS, CPM, CTR, CPC, frequency, add to cart, and conversion rate (purchases divided by link clicks).
7. Build the test: Sales campaign named with date and product, CBO at $50, the 5 top countries (US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand), 3 ad sets with 4 to 5 creatives each.
8. Ads: link to the product or collection page, 2 primary texts, 2 headlines, "Shop now", overlays off.
9. Read results after 3 days, decide after 7 days.
10. Move the ads that win into your main CBO, then scale in small steps.

## Key numbers

- **$50 a day**: minimum test budget for new advertisers. Some 2026 lessons start at $75.
- **$100 to $200 a day**: spend level where results start to look consistent.
- **48 to 72 hours / 3 days**: first look at CTR, CPM, add to carts and conversion rate.
- **7 days**: lots of traffic but zero sales after a week means fix the website, not the ad.
- **15 to 20%**: safe budget raise that does not reset learning.
- **CTR**: under 0.5% bad, 0.5 to 1% below average, 1 to 1.5% average, 1.5 to 2.5% good, above 2.5% excellent.
- **Frequency 2.5 to 4**: normal range for an ad that has run for a while; watch it as it climbs.
- **$50 gross margin per order**: below this you have little room to make Meta work.
- **7 to 10 touchpoints** for a $50 to $60 product, **20 to 30** for a $200 product.
- **8 to 15** truly different concepts beat 2 to 3 versions of one idea.
- **180 days**: exclusion window for past buyers. **30 to 60 days**: window for engaged audiences.
- **80 to 100 website purchases**: before you try ABO (ad set budgets).
- **Bottom-of-funnel ads**: 1 ad set per concept, 4 to 8 ads each, start at $50, or $15 a day on the side.

## Common mistakes

- Killing a campaign after day one. In one 2026 case a $100 test CBO had a $104 cost per purchase on day 1 and $15 on day 3, with no changes.
- Adding new creatives to a running ad set. This can reset learning; make a new ad set instead.
- Copying a competitor's creatives. Matching ads can lead to copyright (DMCA) takedowns of your Shopify store and ad account.
- Logging in through a VPN, from many devices, or adding payment methods again and again. These cause fast bans.
- Duplicating an ad and only changing the hook or text. Since early 2026 that no longer reaches a new group of people.

## FAQ

**What budget should I start with?** $50 a day, so you can test a full week.

**No sales on day one?** Normal. Wait at least 3 days before you change anything.

**Why test a full week?** Many people get paid later in the week and buy then, so 3 days can miss them.

**When do I turn an ad off?** One student pauses ads after 6 days with under $50 spent and below break-even ROAS.

**My ad set gets no spend. What now?** Meta is telling you something. If you still want to test it, give it a separate CBO.

**CBO or ABO?** CBO. ABO is for advanced users and only makes sense with real purchase data.

**Do I need a new pixel for every product?** No. Keep it within a niche. A pixel that already knows your buyers gives lower CPMs and better targeting.

**Should I run my reviews as ads?** Yes. One coach would run all 11 testimonials of a student as ads.

**Should I narrow my audience?** Usually no. Broad works best; if clicks are expensive, add more top countries.

**How fast can I scale?** In 15 to 20% steps. One 2026 lesson scales faster ($75, 150, 300, 500, 750, 1,000) once results are stable.

**Should I worry about "Learning limited"?** Not much. One coach has profitable ad sets that have shown this status for months.

**Can I run ads from someone else's page?** Only with their permission through Meta's partnership ads.

## Checklist

- [ ] Store reviewed and live with the right domain
- [ ] Own Business Manager, ad account, brand page and Instagram linked
- [ ] Pixel connected through the Shopify app, domain verified, test events seen
- [ ] Warm-up engagement ad done
- [ ] Sales objective, Purchase event, attribution window chosen
- [ ] Past buyers (180 days) excluded
- [ ] One CBO, $50, broad, top 5 countries, a single concept in each ad set
- [ ] Hands off for 3 days, decision after 7
- [ ] Under 18: Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so ask your parents; check the rules in your country

## Where courses disagree

- **Attribution:** a 2026 setup lesson uses 7-day click only; a March 2026 update recommends 7-day click plus 1-day engage-through, switched over slowly instead of all at once.
- **Pixel:** one 2026 lesson uses a new pixel for each product in a different genre, and only starts over after 3 to 5 failed products in a row; others keep one pixel per niche.
- **Test length:** some 2026 coaches read results after 2 to 3 days; others wait 7 days.
- **Scaling speed:** 15 to 20% steps vs. doubling the budget ladder.
- **Campaign type:** one lesson promotes a page with engagement or traffic campaigns and interest targeting. To sell products, use Sales (conversion) campaigns with broad targeting.
