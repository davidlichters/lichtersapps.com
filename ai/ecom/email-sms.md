# Email and SMS marketing

> Collect an email and phone number from every visitor, build a few automated flows first, then send short campaigns several times a week only to people who open them.

## Rules

1. Build the 4 core flows first (welcome, abandoned cart, abandoned checkout, abandoned browse), because in a 2026 lesson they made 80% or more of all flow revenue.
2. Add a post purchase flow as the fifth flow, split into new and returning customers.
3. Turn on your popup only after the welcome series behind it is finished.
4. Aim for an open rate above 30% on every campaign.
5. Send 3 to 5 campaigns per week (2026), about 30% brand building and education and 70% direct selling.
6. Who you do NOT email matters more than who you email, so use exclude segments on every campaign.
7. Exclude people who bought in the last 7 days, or better 14, from sales campaigns.
8. Keep each email under 200 words with one call to action.
9. Never offer a discount in the first abandoned cart email.
10. Give every flow a goal and a deadline, for example 72 hours or the code expiring.
11. Use SMS only for messages with real value.
12. Only use scarcity or a countdown when the limit is real, like a true deadline or low stock.

## Steps

1. Set up a business email address for your store (a 2021-22 lesson used a paid workspace plan for this).
2. Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC in your domain DNS so your emails are authenticated.
3. Use single opt-in and name every list after its source, for example "popup 10%".
4. Build the popup: 10% off for an email, upgrade to 15% by adding a phone number. Send the code by text so people enter a real number.
5. Build the welcome series. A 2026 example: email 1 at once with the code, then emails when 48, 24 and 12 hours are left on the offer, and a last chance email with 4 hours left. Filter out anyone who orders.
6. Build the abandoned cart flow. A 2026 example: SMS after about 20 minutes, email 1 on desire and objections, email 2 only to non-openers, email 3 on the biggest pain after 23 hours, email 4 on trust (guarantee, payment options, social proof), then a 15% offer ending at midnight with a reminder near 8 pm.
7. Test every discount link and code in the real checkout.
8. Build engagement segments (opened or clicked in 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days). Use "opened or clicked" because Apple Mail hides real opens.
9. Build exclude segments with a star (*) in front of each name: recent buyers, 3+ bounces, spam traps, and addresses with words like support, help@ or reply@.
10. After every send, check opens by segment and by inbox provider (Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail).
11. Run your main A/B tests in campaigns, then move winners into flows and test them there.

## Key numbers

- 10% for email, 15% for email plus phone: popup offer (2026).
- 72 hours: deadline for the welcome and abandoned checkout flows.
- 20 to 30 minutes: wait before the first cart SMS; test 20 minutes to 4 hours before the first email.
- 23 hours: wait before the third cart touchpoint (2026); a 4 hour gap can land at 2 am, so watch send times.
- 8 pm: best send time named in a 2026 lesson.
- Above 30%: target open rate; 40% was called healthy; around 11% at one provider is a problem.
- About 15% opens and 0.5% clicks: stop sending to that segment.
- 2 or 3 opens in 7 days: hyper engaged segment; send it 3 to 7 times a week for about 2 weeks to repair deliverability.
- Under 3,000 emails on day 1, then often 10 to 15% more per day: warm up after a new tool or domain.
- 1,000 per variant: bare minimum for a split test, which gives only about 80% confidence.
- Over 80%: SMS open rate (2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- One popup and one list for the whole store.
- A discount in the first cart email.
- Telling shoppers they "forgot" something instead of assuming checkout had a problem.
- Showing other products in early cart emails, which restarts the buying journey.
- Sending sales emails to people who just bought.
- Emailing the full list right after switching tools, which damages sender reputation.
- Sending so rarely that people forget you and unsubscribe.
- One-off texts instead of automated, tested SMS flows.

## FAQ

**Which SMS or email tool?** A 2021-22 Q&A said most tools are similar, and how you use one matters more than which one.

**Do I need SMS?** Collect numbers and test it, especially for younger buyers. Keep texts rare.

**How often should I email?** 3 to 5 campaigns a week. If unsubscribes jump after an increase, go back down.

**Discount right away in the cart flow?** No. Remind and handle objections first, then give a time limited offer.

**My open rate dropped. What now?** Mail only your most engaged segment for about 2 weeks, then widen it step by step.

**One provider has very low opens?** Exclude unengaged contacts at that provider for about 7 days, then add them back slowly.

**What is a spam trap?** An old address that never opens or clicks. Exclude profiles with zero opens, clicks, views and orders, but keep sign ups from the last 14 days.

**Do I need a winback flow?** A 2026 lesson says no, use campaigns instead.

**Should emails look fancy?** Keep them simple, readable on mobile and in dark mode, with your logo and brand colors.

**Can I A/B test with a small list?** Barely. You need about 1,000 people per variant.

**What before Black Friday?** One coaching example switched the popup to early access, which grew the list for a strong sale day.

**Any legal limits?** One lesson says only one cart SMS is allowed in the first 24 hours, and many countries require a business address in emails. Check the rules in your country. If you are under 18, your parents usually need to own the Shopify and payment accounts.

## Checklist

- [ ] Business email on own domain, SPF, DKIM, DMARC set
- [ ] Popup with email and phone step, list named by source
- [ ] Welcome series live before the popup
- [ ] Cart, checkout, browse and post purchase flows live
- [ ] Discount links tested in checkout
- [ ] Engagement and exclude segments built
- [ ] 3 to 5 campaigns planned this week
- [ ] Opens checked by segment and provider

## Where courses disagree

- Frequency: 2026 says 3 to 5 campaigns a week; 2021-22 said one blast a week plus automated emails.
- Design: 2021-22 preferred simple, lean emails with a personal signature; 2026 uses reusable modules tested on mobile and in dark mode.
- Font size: 2021-22 lessons say 18 point minimum in one and 14 in another.
- Winback: 2026 uses campaigns, not flows; 2021-22 sent buyers a 4 email series at once, then 15-30, 60 and 120 days after purchase.
