# Sources of the deep e-commerce reference

> Every statement in https://lichtersapps.com/ai/ecom/deep/index.md ends with its sources in square brackets, for example [L0012, 2023] = lesson L0012, recorded 2023. Several sources: [L0458, 2021; L0771, 2025]. Look the lesson up here to see which course it comes from. When sources disagree, the newer one wins unless the statement says otherwise; "older" in a bracket marks advice the source itself calls outdated.

## How to read the year

- `2023`: date or year taken from the lesson's own title or file name (e.g. a monthly coaching call), or the fixed recording year of the course.
- `2021-22`, `2025-26`, `2020-26`: course recorded in that range, lesson not dated more precisely.
- `2024+`: lesson not dated; the latest year it mentions itself is 2024, so it was recorded in 2024 or later.
- `undated`: no date and no year mentioned.
