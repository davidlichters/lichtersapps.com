# Dropshipping business model and strategy

> Dropshipping is a marketing business: a supplier ships the product, your ads and service decide the profit, and cash from short trends should later fund a real brand.

## Rules

1. Run your shop as a marketing and customer service business, because you cannot change or improve the product yourself.
2. Focus on one store until it works, because a second store almost never fixes a weak first one and doubles your support work.
3. Use trend products for cash and practice, and expect most of them to sell well for only about 2 to 4 months.
4. Move the cash from trend products into a long-term brand, because a trend store builds no lasting brand value.
5. Grow in only three ways: more new customers, a higher average order value, or more repeat purchases.
6. Lower your cost per unit, so you have more money left for ads.
7. Test many products, because a single flop only means you try another.
8. Decide with data (ROAS, conversion rate, cost per customer), not with feelings.
9. Aim for your first sale, then your first 10 to 20 sales, before you think about building a community.
10. Guard your rating, because each small drop (for example 4.8 to 4.7 to 4.6 stars) hurts your whole conversion rate.
11. Never rely on one traffic source forever; if that channel stops, your sales stop too.

## Steps

A 2026 lesson splits the first 90 days into six phases:

1. **Day 1 to 14, setup:** learn the basics, then create Shopify, a domain, a shop email, your own Meta Business Manager and the pixel.
2. **Day 15 to 30, product research:** look for a product that already sells and could carry a brand.
3. **Day 31 to 45, store:** make the store feel trustworthy at first glance: a clean product page, bundles or "buy one, get one" offers, real reviews and a guarantee.
4. **Day 45 to 60, creatives:** produce 12 to 20 ads that show what the buyer gains, such as hooks, how-to clips and problem and solution videos.
5. **Day 60 to 75, ad launch:** you spend to learn, not to profit yet. Wait 48 to 72 hours before you judge a test.
6. **Day 75 to 90, improve and scale:** repair the weakest step of your funnel, add fresh ads every week, push the angle that works, and add retargeting, upsells, bundles and subscriptions.

## Key numbers

- **2 to 4 months:** how long most trend products sell well; a few run 4 to 5 months (2026).
- **1 to 3 months:** how long a trend store usually lasts before it burns out (2026).
- **About $30 to $50:** usual price of a trend product, low enough for an impulse buy (2026).
- **At least $50 per day:** minimum Meta ad spend for someone new, because less does not give the algorithm enough data (2026).
- **12 to 20 creatives:** minimum before your first launch; one teacher tests about 15 per day himself (2026).
- **15 to 30 creatives:** what you should have launched after 90 days (2026).
- **$5,000 to $20,000 per month:** steady revenue goal for a good product in the last phase of the 90-day plan (2026).
- **ROAS 3:** example where $100 in ads brings $300 in revenue, before ad and product costs are taken off (2026).
- **About 4 to 5 percent, sometimes 6:** average conversion rate one teacher names for the US (2026).
- **$10,000 per month:** the level where the focus shifts to hiring, systems, customer lifetime value and brand (2026).
- **Under $500:** start cost named for a high-ticket store, while stock models like Amazon FBA can cost far more up front (older advice, 2021-22).

## Common mistakes

- Testing one product, seeing it fail and calling the model a scam.
- Running a trend product as if it were a permanent brand, then having nothing left when the trend ends.
- Opening a second store because the first one is slow. You repeat the same errors twice.
- Spending thousands on a branding package before you have sales.
- Depending only on paid ads with no email list or second channel.
- Forgetting refunds: gimmick products often disappoint buyers and bring many refund requests.

## FAQ

**Is dropshipping dead?** No. The model is more than 20 years old. Methods changed: cheap ads and slow shipping (2016 to 2018), branded stores (2019 to 2021), now AI.

**Do I need experience?** No. You can run it next to school or a job. The model is simple, but the work is still hard.

**How long until I make money?** Expect to learn first. A 2026 plan says 90 days can show real progress if you stay consistent.

**Brand or trend products?** Many 2026 lessons say: start with trend products to earn cash and learn, then use that money for a brand.

**When can I open a second store?** Only when the first one works. People who reached very high revenue usually ran just one or two stores.

**What should I focus on first?** Basics, product research and store setup. Above $10,000 a month: systems, hiring, brand.

**Why care about repeat buyers?** A second sale is easier and cheaper, often with a better margin.

**Should I buy an existing store?** Buying saves steps but costs a lot of money; building takes longer but teaches you more.

**Can I sell my store later?** A profitable site can be sold after a few years. A trend store has little brand value to sell.

**Which country should I sell to?** Older advice says your own country, or the US if your country has no big online market. Our 2026 model ships from China and uses Meta ads.

**I am under 18. Can I start?** Shopify, payments and ad accounts usually need an adult owner, so a parent must own them. For tax and law, check the rules in your country.

## Checklist

- [ ] I focus on one store and one goal.
- [ ] Shopify, domain, shop email, my own Business Manager and pixel are set up (adult owner if I am under 18).
- [ ] I know if my product is a trend product or a brand product.
- [ ] My product page has a clear offer, real reviews and a guarantee.
- [ ] I have at least 12 creatives ready.
- [ ] I spend at least $50 per day and wait 48 to 72 hours before I judge a test.
- [ ] I know my break-even ROAS and track conversion rate and cost per customer.
- [ ] I have a plan for repeat buyers (email flows, upsells, bundles).
- [ ] I save part of the profit for a long-term brand.
- [ ] I checked the tax and legal rules in my country.

## Where courses disagree

- **Trend products:** most 2026 lessons call them a good cash engine for beginners. One 2026 mentor says a product that only works as a short trend should be dropped for one that can carry a brand.
- **Market and suppliers:** 2021-22 lessons sell in your own country with local suppliers and products around $800 or more. 2026 lessons sell cheaper products from China.
- **Traffic:** 2021-22 lessons start with Google Ads and Google Shopping. 2026 lessons start with Meta ads and AI-made creatives.
- **Speed:** a 2021-22 lesson promises profitable sales within 30 days; a 2026 plan expects 90 days.
- **Number of stores:** one 2021-22 lesson talks about building up to five profitable stores over time; a coach from the same years warns that a second store rarely helps.
