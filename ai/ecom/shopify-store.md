# Building the Shopify store

> Build a clean, simple store fast, check it on a phone, make sure checkout works, and send ad traffic straight to one product page.

## Rules

1. Keep the design clean, simple and trustworthy, because extra effects rarely help sales.
2. Edit the theme in mobile view, because about 90% of shop traffic comes from phones.
3. Send ad traffic straight to the product page, not the homepage, until you know which angles win.
4. Start on the Basic plan, because the bigger plans mostly add reports a new store does not need.
5. Buy your domain outside Shopify, because that makes moving it and setting up email easier later.
6. Duplicate the theme before every bigger change, so you can go back if something breaks.
7. Activate every market you sell to, because customers in an inactive market cannot check out.
8. Set the store currency and units to match your buyers (USD and pounds for the US), not where you live.
9. Run one test checkout after you turn on payments, before you spend any money on ads.
10. Give every product its own page template, or one edit changes all your product pages at once.
11. Pick a fast theme and keep few apps, because the theme has the biggest effect on speed and app code can stay behind.
12. Turn off the store password before launch, or nobody can buy.

## Steps

1. **Create the account.** Pick "sell online" and "dropshipping" in the setup questions.
2. **Settings first:** store name, business address and email, currency, time zone, unit system.
3. **Buy and connect the domain.** Use "Connect existing domain", then set it as the primary domain so customers never see the myshopify address. Shopify adds HTTPS for free.
4. **Install the theme.** Upload it as a zip file in Online Store, then publish it while the store is still password protected.
5. **Brand it:** logo, favicon (square PNG with a transparent background), up to three brand colors, one simple font.
6. **Build the product page:** title, short bullet points, price, at least two styled square images, an FAQ block with about six questions, payment icons. Set the product to Active and tick the Online Store channel.
7. **Add the policy pages:** privacy, refund, shipping, terms, contact, about. Read every generated text and replace all placeholders.
8. **Set up shipping:** one shipping zone with all countries you sell to, a free shipping rate, and delivery times with a buffer.
9. **Set up Markets:** add your main countries and switch the market to active.
10. **Turn on Shopify Payments,** then place a test order.
11. **Check everything on desktop and on a phone,** remove the password and launch.

## Key numbers

- **About 90%:** share of traffic on phones (2026), so mobile view comes first.
- **About 2 days:** target time from finding a product to a live product test (2026).
- **A few hours to half a day:** build time for one store for an experienced builder (2026). Your first one takes longer.
- **3 days free, then 30 days for $1:** a 2026 Shopify trial offer seen in one lesson. Cancel before the trial ends if you do not want to pay. Older lessons show 14 days free, then $29 per month for Basic.
- **5 countries:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand as the main markets to add first (2026).
- **13%:** average cross-border conversion lift Shopify reports for adding translations.
- **14 days:** default return window in Shopify's refund policy text.
- **7 to 10 days:** express delivery time one 2026 lesson writes into the shipping setup.
- **Up to 3 options** per product (size, color, style) without an extra app; use variants, not separate pages.
- **1000 x 1000 px:** maximum for product images; larger files slow the site.
- **1600 px wide, 500 to 600 px high:** hero banner size, so content is not pushed below the screen.

## Common mistakes

- Spending days on the homepage. Most visitors land on the product page and rarely see it.
- Leaving products in Draft or unticking the Online Store channel, so they stay invisible.
- Setting stock to 0, which shows the product as sold out.
- Forgetting to activate the market, so checkout fails.
- Editing a shared template and changing every product page by accident.
- Uninstalling an app and assuming its code is gone. It often stays in the theme.
- Copying a whole competitor store. Take only small ideas from several stores and use your own text and images.
- Forgetting to remove the password at launch.

## FAQ

**Which theme should I use?**
A popular dropshipping theme is fine. Theme choice has the biggest effect on speed, so test it on a phone before you build.

**Do I need a fancy homepage?**
No. Keep it short and lead visitors to your products quickly. It mainly builds trust.

**Should I add many products before launch?**
No. You can launch with one product or a few and add more later.

**Where should my ads point?**
To the product page. Build special landing pages only after you know which angles sell.

**Why can't customers check out?**
Usually an inactive market, no shipping rate for their country, or payments not connected.

**Do I need a currency or language switcher?**
Only if you sell to several countries. Translations can help cross-border sales.

**How do I let someone help build my store?**
Send them your collaborator code. You approve their request by email, so you never share your password.

**My store looks slow. What first?**
Remove unused apps, compress images, cut slideshows and lazy-load videos.

**How big should product images be?**
Square, all the same size, and no larger than 1000 x 1000 px.

**I am under 18. Can I do this alone?**
Usually not. Shopify, payment providers and ad accounts normally need an adult owner, so a parent should own the accounts. Check the rules in your country for taxes and business registration.

## Checklist

- [ ] Store name, address, email, currency and units set
- [ ] Domain connected and set as primary
- [ ] Theme uploaded, published and duplicated as a backup
- [ ] Product page with title, bullets, price, images and FAQ
- [ ] Product Active and Online Store channel ticked, stock above 0
- [ ] Privacy, refund, shipping, terms, contact and about pages live and linked in the footer
- [ ] Shipping zone and rates set
- [ ] Markets active for every country you sell to
- [ ] Shopify Payments on and a test checkout done
- [ ] Store checked on a phone and on desktop
- [ ] Password removed
