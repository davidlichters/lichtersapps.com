# Research results: everything we asked Perplexity, condensed

When to read this: before any decision on ads, pricing, products, suppliers, shipping, payments, tax or law for a US shop; check here first whether we already paid for the answer.

Source: about 400 unique paid Perplexity answers (Perplexity Max, mostly GPT-6.1 Sol Thinking, many "triple" runs = 2-3 independent answers plus a contradiction check), 28 Sep to 7 Nov 2026, plus our own decision notes written from them. Pure duplicates and test questions were dropped. Dates are the day the answer was fetched. All outside numbers are the cited sources' claims as reported by Perplexity and were NOT re-measured by us unless marked "measured"; treat them as guesses for our shop until our own data replaces them. "Decision" lines are what we decided from the answer at the time. Shop context in most questions: Porchnap, a US-only Shopify dropshipping store run from Germany, Christmas sleigh bed for small dogs and cats at 69.99 USD, cost 18.90 USD incl. DDP, 6-10 business days from China, break-even CPA 47.01 USD.

The single most repeated finding: for almost every narrow question ("what conversion for X at price Y in December") no public measured number exists. Benchmarks are cross-industry, vendor-produced or self-reported. Plan with them, then replace them with your own data as fast as possible.
