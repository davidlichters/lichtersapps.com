## Market and demand (US pet gifts, seasons, breeds)

**When US pet owners buy Christmas gifts** (2026-09-29)  
No measured pet-gift monthly split. Adobe US online total Oct/Nov/Dec 2025: 25.6 / 35.5 / 38.9 %. APPA (24 Nov 2025): 50 % of dog owners buy Christmas gifts, avg 29.80 USD per gift; 41 % of cat owners, 30.70 USD. NRF (16 Oct 2025): 42 % start before November, 63 % wait for most until Thanksgiving, 60 % finish in December, 55 % plan online. Consequence for us: do not save in October.

**AKC 2025 small breeds (published 18 Mar 2026)** (2026-09-29)  
French Bulldog rank 1 but up to 28 lb. Dachshund 5, Poodle 6, Beagle 7 (miniature variants not ranked separately), Cavalier King Charles 12, Yorkshire Terrier 13, Miniature Schnauzer 17, Pomeranian 19, Shih Tzu 21, Chihuahua 24. No US ranking of which breeds get most gifts. Our consequence: Frenchie ads only if size S fits, else Cavalier.

**Search interest for Christmas dog bed terms** (2026-09-30)  
Amazon.com (Similarweb estimates, data 13 Sep 2026): "christmas dog bed" peak 23-29 Nov 2025 at 3,680; "dog christmas gift" peak 14-20 Dec 2025 at 7,332; week 28 Dec-3 Jan both down about 90.8 % / 90.4 % (340 / 704). No data for "dog sled bed". Google Trends weeks not obtained.

**Which dog bed sizes sell most in the US** (2026-09-30)  
No national S/M/L unit split for 2025-2026 and no current share of US dogs under 25 lb; the 52 % figure is household size ownership from 28 Feb 2015. Amazon shows larger/XL products prominently: only a test hypothesis. Show outer size, usable area, access and weight guidance separately.

**Google Trends peak weeks for Christmas dog terms** (2026-09-30)  
Not obtainable (no usable exports). Method: October weekly index divided by the yearly maximum of the same series; Trends is normalized interest, not absolute searches.

**Monthly split of US online dog bed sales Oct-Dec** (2026-10-01)  
Not determinable: no time series on Amazon bestseller pages, Keepa/Jungle Scout/Helium 10 history not evaluable, Google Trends not retrievable.

**Gift buyers who do not own the pet ("granddog", "dog aunt")** (2026-10-04)  
Best measure: "almost a third" of grandparents give the "grand-furbaby" a holiday gift (survey of 1,200 US pet owners, Oct 2023). No representative total share from APPA, Chewy, Rover, Petco, NRF. Planning value 30 % of grandpet grandparents. Real buyers say "granddog", "grandpup", "great-grandpup". Follow-up (2026-10-08): no reliable share of Meta Christmas pet-gift buyers without own pet.

**Gift buyers without own pet: numbers** (2026-10-04)  
No current US rate. Older single values: 15 % (Level 7, 2018), 28 % of non-owners and 66 % of owners (Vetstreet readers, 2022), do not average. J.M. Smucker (16 Dec 2024): 25 % of seasonal pet product purchases from households without a dog (method unknown). Nulo/OnePoll (13 Dec 2023, n=2,000 owners): brother 37 %, father "grandpaw-rent" 36 % give gifts. Usable only as audience proof, not size.

**Pet bed demand in Jan/Feb vs Nov/Dec** (2026-10-08)  
Not quantifiable for 40-90 USD cave beds; winter offers and post-Christmas ads documented.

## Pricing, bundles and shipping-fee tests

**Compare-at price, bundle and urgency rules for a 70 USD seasonal bed** (2026-09-29)  
Conservative start: 69.95 USD single without compare-at price (no unproven former price), two for 125.90 USD (exactly 14.00 USD saving vs 139.90). A holiday sale may run with a real end (e.g. 30 Nov 2026 23:59 ET), but no fake timers, invented stock or unproven Christmas delivery. "6-10 business days" only after proof of the full delivery span. Meta can reject deceptive ads and restrict repeat offenders.

**Price of the same sleigh bed that jinglenap sells** (2026-10-03)  
Assumed 18.90 USD COGS and 33-47 USD ad cost per order. Recommendation: 69.99 USD honest launch price, no struck-through compare price, free shipping, a real time-limited launch offer. 59.99 USD not viable at these CACs; 79.99 likely too much without differentiation.

**Free shipping vs shipping fee** (2026-10-03)  
No published uplift for this case. Recommendation: test C = 74.99 USD incl. US delivery against B (69.99 + shipping); C may convert about 8.91 % relatively worse than B before its contribution per visitor falls below B (own math). Never unqualified "Free US shipping" if conditions apply.

**Conversion at 59.99 / 69.99 / 79.99 USD for Christmas dog beds** (2026-10-07)  
No public product-specific conversion for Christmas dog beds at these prices (3 independent runs plus cross-check agree). Only general: Meta median CVR 1.53 % (1 Aug 2025-31 Jul 2026, Triple Whale 18 Aug 2026). Use 69.99 as provisional price; decide by controlled test on contribution per visitor.

## Offer mechanics (gift cards, printables, inserts, editions)

**Personalized pet trading cards / portraits as inserts (10 offers)** (2026-09-28)  
Best as surprise insert after a qualifying repeat purchase: Chewy reports higher customer lifetime value for "surprise and delight" recipients; Friskies 2026 gave free personalized trading cards tied to product samples.

**Numbered limited first editions with yearly new edition (10 offers)** (2026-09-29)  
Made In Washington started 2004 with 500 numbered ornaments, 2006 1,000, 2011 2,011, 2024 2,024. League of NH Craftsmen since 1988, 2026 the 39th edition at 32 USD per ornament. No source proves extra revenue or price premium from numbering alone.

**Selling physical gifts after the shipping cut-off (printable gift card)** (2026-09-30)  
Model proven: Walnut Studiolo (US Shopify) printable gift announcement (15 Dec 2024), Mail Order Mystery documented post-Christmas delivery start for Christmas 2025, Bean Box PDF card in the email receipt. Recommendation: clearly announced delivery after Christmas plus printable gift announcement in the order confirmation, optional Shopify's free Digital Products app; Flow can tag/flag, but "Send internal email" cannot send dynamic customer mails.

**Gift experience offers (envelope, card with dog name, digital announcement)** (2026-09-30)  
Sugarwish best documented: digital eCard in about 5 minutes, recipient chooses, box later; 27-139 USD for 2-24 items. Beds with cards: Charley Chau, Orvis, MiaCara. Made By Cleo, Yappy, Gaby's: packaging, name cards, handwritten notes. No offer combines bed plus automatic pre-announcement plus physical dog-name card.

**"Arrives before Christmas or money back" offers** (2026-09-30)  
None of 10 compared offers proven as a full "delivered before Christmas or full refund" guarantee; what exists: ship-date guarantees, express fee or shipping cost refunds, Christmas delivery advertising without compensation, explicit exclusion of Christmas guarantees.

**Printable placeholder gifts after the shipping cut-off (10 offers)** (2026-09-30)  
Benchmarks: KiwiCo, Dorfkram, Redbubble, CrunchLabs, Driftaway Coffee, Bloom & Wild, Finders Seekers, Universal Yums, Presseplus, The Curious Drinker: printable placeholder plus later physical delivery. **Early-bird tiered Christmas prices** (2026-10-01)  
Lynch Creek Farm "Pre-Order for Christmas & Save 10 %" seen in search index; evidence contradictory.

**Printable treasure hunt instead of a Christmas parcel** (2026-10-01)

## Upsells and add-ons

No public measurement for US buyer + Shopify pet bed + cheap bandana one-click. Best dataset (2026, global, cross-industry): median sales per offer view 1.4 % in cart drawer, 1.9 % cart popup, 2.4 % product page popup. Planning value: 5 % paid attach rate only as an assumption.

## Meta ads: CPM, CPC, CTR, CPA benchmarks

**Meta CPM/CPA Q4 and order-by date for 6-10 day China shipping** (2026-09-28)  
Expect a CPM peak in November; purchase CPA rises less because purchase intent is higher. 2026 values are forecasts: 2025 seasonal curve plus 12-20 % (Meta reported Q2 2026 average ad price +12 % worldwide, about +20 % US/Canada). For 6-10 day China shipping the answer suggested 10 Dec 2026 as public order-by date for the lower 48, or 8-9 Dec if business days or customs are not reliably priced in.

**Cheapest Meta formats/placements, BFCM 2025** (2026-09-28)  
No public source splits CPA by format for pet products at Christmas 2025. Closest: Triple Whale BFCM 2025 (28 Nov-1 Dec 2025, all industries, by placement): Facebook Reels Overlay cheapest CPA 10.50 USD, ROAS 8.68, CPM 3.36 USD (LinkedIn post on Triple Whale data, 3 Dec 2025). Reels had the cheapest CPM. Pets CPA median 2025: 38.18 USD.

**What drives pet CPA at Christmas** (2026-09-28)  
Pets & Animals benchmark: CPM 16.27 USD, CTR 2.37 %, CVR 1.63 %, CPA 38.51 USD (Triple Whale 2026). BFCM 2025 all industries: CPM 22.26 USD, CPA 34.06 USD; CPM +7.6 % vs prior-year event. CPA = CPM / (1000 x CTR x CVR); levers: creative, offer, checkout, seasonal budget. Do not merge US CPM, global pet benchmark and BFCM numbers into one forecast.

**Meta CPM/CPA for US pet products Oct-Dec 2025** (2026-09-29)  
No full public US-pet monthly series. Global pet 2025: median CPM 15.45 USD, median CPA 38.18 USD (Zeely, 24 Mar 2026). Broad US monthly (Superads): CPM Oct 2025 22.85 USD, Nov 27.47 USD (+20.2 %). BFCM 2025 (Triple Whale, all industries): CPM 22.26 USD, CPA 34.06 USD, CPM +7.6 % YoY (4 Dec 2025). Our break-even CPA was 47.01 USD.

**CPA and ROAS of small direct-ship shops on cold Meta traffic** (2026-09-30)  
No public series. Anchors Q4 2025 global e-commerce CPM: Oct 13.31, Nov 21.96, Dec 12.74 USD; BFCM Meta panel CPA 34.06, ROAS 2.26x. Historic references, not US pet cold benchmarks.

**Funnel planning bands for cold US Meta traffic, 50-80 USD product** (2026-09-30)  
Planning bands (not benchmarks), October: link CTR 1.0-2.0 %, link CPC 0.60-1.10 USD, ATC / landing page views 4-7 %, purchase / initiate checkout 35-55 %. November: mainly tolerate higher CPC. Pause at 100 LPV without ATC (after technical check), at about 200 LPV without purchase, or at 3x allowed target CPA.

**Orders of a new one-product seasonal dog shop in November** (2026-09-30)  
Model (not measured cohort), 1-14 Nov 2025, cold Meta: at 50 USD/day 7-18 orders / 440-1,100 USD revenue; at 100 USD/day 15-37 orders / 880-2,200 USD; midpoints about 13 / 25 orders. Inputs: 2025 Animals & Pets CPC 0.78 USD, Shopify CVR 1.4 %, pet supplies AOV 59 USD.

**Weekly Meta CPM/CPA in the US 1 Oct-20 Dec 2025** (2026-10-01)  
No public weekly series for small accounts; no proven cheapest week. Best anchor: Triple Whale Meta CPM 22.26 USD for 28 Nov-1 Dec 2025 (published 4 Dec 2025).

**Country CPMs and break-even** (2026-10-03)  
No full country/week matrix with CPM, CPC, CPA. Math: at 1.0 % link CTR and 2.0 % purchases per link click, a 47 USD CPA limit means a 9.40 USD CPM ceiling in every country. Decide country budgets by own purchase CPA and contribution, not CPM.

**Link CTR and purchase rate per click Nov/Dec 2025** (2026-10-04)
