# Scam-baiting: why "scamming scammers" is not a business

In June 2023 I wrote "Scammer beklauen" ("steal from scammers") into my list of business ideas. I was looking for a trick, not a customer. **Result: 0 euros.** It did not work and earned nothing. Later I wrote a line about myself that sums it up: too honest for scams.
