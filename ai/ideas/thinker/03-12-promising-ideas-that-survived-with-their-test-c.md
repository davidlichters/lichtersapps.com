## 12. PROMISING: ideas that survived, with their test cards

Format: name. Pitch. Test (cost, days). Pass threshold fixed in advance. Expected value (EV) as estimated by the card. "Repaired" = the surviving form is the repaired one. None of these was tested.

### Run 2026-09-27 (12 checked, 11 alive, 3 repaired, 1 dead, 1 control; all 12 judged "teils" by stage 4, occupied rate 0/12)

1. **Red-flag check before buying a small company (repaired), 1,500 EUR, EV 315 EUR.** Buyers of small firms (succession, MBI, price under 2 M EUR) skip financial due diligence because it starts at 25,000 EUR. We deliver in one day a red-flag report from annual accounts, management reports (BWA) and trial balance, pure number analysis without tax judgment, plus the target's register history fetched individually by register number, plus checking the other directorships the seller lists himself in the DD questionnaire (with his written consent). No people search, no own person directory, no archive of register pulls, report only to the buyer. Test: fixed-price page, explicitly without auditor/tax-advisor seal, 150 EUR fully refundable deposit via Stripe, or order request with the target's register number; Google Ads 50 EUR, 7 days. Pass: at least 1 paid deposit OR at least 3 requests with a real register number (checked), price range under 2 M EUR and LOI date. Fail: at least 20 clicks and none of that. Under 20 clicks: invalid. Decides: does a buyer pay without a seal?
2. **Backlog list: all running liquidations in your radius as a one-off purchase, EV 156 EUR.** Buyers, recyclers and clearers opening up dissolutions as a source buy the whole stock of running liquidations in their area once (firm level, notice date, end of blocking year); a subscription from today would see almost nothing (see backlog signal). Test: 55 personal letters to recyclers/buyers who advertise buying at company dissolution, list for 50 km with remaining time, 390 EUR one-off or 39 EUR deposit, North Data's 49 EUR named openly; page 2 shows 10 real cases from their area. Pass: by day 7 at least 1 paid list or 2 deposits. 0 payments: one-off sale dead for these buyers; exactly 1: second wave of 55. Visits do not count.
3. **Blocking-year calendar per federal state, only from the Bundesanzeiger, no personal names (repaired), 99 EUR/month, EV 150 EUR.** Every month per state all GmbH/UG whose blocking year ends in 4 to 8 weeks: firm, seat, date of creditor call, computed end per § 73 (1) GmbHG. No register portal pulls, no liquidator names, no natural persons. Tax firms, liquidation-balance providers and lawyers with deletion packages write themselves to "the liquidator of X GmbH i.L.". We prepare no balance and no return, contact nobody for a buyer, charge nothing per address or mandate (so §§ 5, 9 StBerG do not bite); not exclusive. Test: 40 letters, each with a real sample list of 20 firms from the recipient's state (personal-name firms filtered) and the true hook "In January 2027, 515 blocking years end in Berlin, 376 in Hamburg"; personal short URL with QR to an order page (Stripe 99 EUR/month or binding order form); delivery in 48 h. Pass: at least 2 of 40 pay or bind-order by day 7. Reading fixed before: 1 = fail; 0 with at least 8 letter codes visited = list without names does not carry 99 EUR, dead in this form; 0 with under 4 visits = finding about the letter, not the idea. Before scaling: lawyer question § 87b/§ 5 UrhG.
4. **Domain pre-purchase from the liquidator, 32 EUR/month, EV 77 EUR.** SEO agencies and domain investors pay 37 USD/month today for lists of domains that already expired. We deliver domains of GmbHs in liquidation or with announced deletion, with the liquidator's name and the deadline, months before the domain is released (the liquidator must turn assets into money, § 70 GmbHG, and a domain is an asset). Gate at 0 EUR first: map 200 archive cases (100 liquidations, 100 deletions) to domains via the register number in the imprint; only if at least 10 of 200 have a domain with at least 20 referring domains, spend 50 EUR. Then a page with 10 real domains this week, pre-order 32 EUR via Stripe, says openly we do not know if the liquidator sells. Pass: at least 3 payments in 7 days (about 17 EUR acquisition cost per customer vs 32 EUR monthly revenue); 1 to 2: repeat with another channel; 0: dead.
5. **Takeover instead of deletion: dissolutions for succession buyers, 150 EUR/month, EV 72 EUR.** Searchers, search funds and small M&A boutiques in DACH pay for firms whose shareholders just decided to dissolve, pre-filtered to IT, staffing and holdings (the over-represented sectors). US anchor: Grata at 25,000 to 50,000 USD/year, which does not know German register events [probable]. Stage 0 (0 EUR): check 30 recent IT/staffing dissolutions (website active, job ads, headcount). Gate: at least 2 of 30 still operating with more than 10 staff, then both arms; at least 5 operating, only arm B; else no send. Stage 1 (45 EUR): letters to 50 buyers (25 search funds/boutiques, 25 acquirers of IT system houses and staffing firms). Pass: at least 2 payments of 150 EUR in 7 days.
6. **Disposal broker for liquidators on commission, EV 54 EUR.** In a voluntary liquidation in Germany there is no administrator who hires recyclers and clearers (in the UK a licensed insolvency practitioner does this for every MVL using auction houses). We get the liquidator offers from clearers and buyers; a clearance firm in Hannover offered 15 % of the order value on its own. Gates at 0 EUR: inventory pre-check, and a written yes from the clearance partner to 15 % with viewing within 5 working days. Test: 25 letters to liquidators of fresh dissolutions within 30 km of Hannover, free quotes, commission named openly, liquidator chooses freely, 30 EUR. Pass: at least 2 of 25 agree a viewing with address, description of contents and proposed date within 7 days; "interested" does not count.
7. **Proof list of dead contact forms for US web agencies, 45 USD, EV 42 EUR.** WordPress maintenance and web design agencies pay 44.99 USD/month for raw Google Maps lists. We sell firms whose form endpoint provably returns 404/500 since a given date, or whose certificate expired, with date and status code as proof for the first mail. Form monitoring for owners is occupied (FormPulse, FormTracker.io, MoniTao); deleted assumption: "an agency lead must know he has a problem". Gate (0 EUR): of 67 persistent-404 forms, at least 34 must really be broken today and at most 1 of 20 control forms may return 404, else nobody is contacted. Test: about 130 personal mails, each with a free real case from their city; 25 firms from their state for 45 USD prepaid, money back if fewer than 25; 12 EUR. Pass: at least 3 paid pre-orders with at least 120 delivered mails; 0 = dead; 1 to 2 = grey.
8. **Dissolution list for recyclers, without register pulls (repaired), 49 EUR/month, EV 41 EUR.** Industrial recyclers, auction houses and surplus traders get every new voluntary dissolution (creditor call per § 65 (2) GmbHG) in their radius, pre-sorted for firms with goods (trade, gastro, machines/metal, furniture, cars, fashion), firm data only plus a link to the register search; no personal names; the buyer looks up the liquidator himself for an interesting case (§ 9 (1) HGB) or writes to the company. Test: 50 used-machine dealers get their real list of the last 30 days on page 2 and the offer 49 EUR/month on page 1 (QR payment link, money back if no case that month), 45 EUR. Pass: at least 2 paid first months from 50 letters by day 7. Decides: does a recycler pay for the list without liquidator names while North Data shows names for 49 EUR?
9. **Legal-successor matching per hit ("Postadress Move for business customers"), 1.50 EUR per hit, EV 40 EUR.** B2B mailers, trade publishers and wholesalers submit their business customer list once before the next mailing or dunning run and pay per hit like Deutsche Post's change-of-address service. Hit = merged into X with register number, in liquidation, or deletion announced. No subscription. Gate at 0 EUR: match against our own 9,269 businesses with legal form, 30 hits re-checked; under 60 real hits per 10,000 or under 27 of 30 correct means no letter. Pass: by day 7 at least 2 uploaded real lists (500+ firms, 70 % found in register) AND at least 1 paid delivery of at least 30 EUR. 2+ lists without payment = counting interests but price or competition beats us.
10. **AI cost audit on success fee: 50 % of savings found, EV 35 EUR.** AI automation agencies and small SaaS teams with 500 to 5,000 EUR monthly API spend pay half of the waste we find and stop, for 12 months, nothing otherwise. We put card receipts, token journal and delivered results side by side (wrong model behind a code path, paid work never delivered, measurements failing silently). Test: 50 letters, 45 EUR. Pass: at least 2 of 50 send their 30-day cost export (at least 500 EUR/month on own keys) plus agreement to the 50 % deal with company and name; exactly 1 = follow-up batch at 25 %; 0 = dead.
11. **Liquidators near the end of the blocking year, for record-storage firms, 79 EUR, EV 24 EUR.** Per § 74 (2) GmbHG every liquidated GmbH must store its books 10 years with a custodian the liquidator chooses. Archive services that court liquidators pay for liquidators in their area whose blocking year ends in 60 days (one order is worth from 23 EUR per binder at one provider). Test: letters to 40 archive services with the measured count in their area and 20 real sample rows, 79 EUR first month or a 15-minute call. Pass: at least 1 paid order or 3 booked calls in 7 days.
12. **CONTROL (killed by pessimist 2): fresh dissolutions with liquidator name and address for liquidation-balance providers, 79 EUR/month, EV 63 EUR.** Killed because taking all liquidation calls daily from a database the Bundesanzeiger publisher claims database protection for, and selling it explicitly as a cheaper substitute for the publisher's own product, is the core case of § 87b (1) sentence 2 UrhG. Control test: letter pre-orders to 47 providers with open note that start depends on legal clearance and nothing is charged until then. Pass: at least 3 pre-orders from 3 firms (6.4 %); 0 to 2 confirms the kill from the market too.

### Run 2026-10-04 (11 checked, 9 alive, 3 repaired, 2 dead, 1 control)

1. **Duplicate-payment list for German mid-size firms, 3,500 EUR, EV 560 EUR.** Controllers and accounts payable pay for a script plus model searching 12 to 24 months of the vendor ledger for duplicate and near-duplicate payments, delivering a documented recovery list; the client recovers himself, we never touch money or suppliers. Test: real free pre-check (upload one quarter's vendor export, hit list in 48 h), then full audit at 3,500 EUR fixed. Pass: at least 2 different firms (company email) upload a real export within 7 days; form without file does not count; 0 uploads under 20 clicks = unclear, not dead. Decides: does a controller give real vendor data to a stranger?
2. **Sole trader becomes a company: weekly list for insurance brokers (repaired), 149 EUR/month per region, EV 358 EUR.** When an e.K. spins its business off into a company (only possible as Ausgliederung, § 152 UmwG), all policies still run on the old owner, plus new D&O and managing-director liability. Only what the notice says (firm, place, court, date) plus website and contact from the open web; no per-firm register pull, no tax advisors as buyers (§ 57a StBerG forbids them advertising aimed at a single mandate). Territory protection one broker per region; contact by letter or visit, not cold call or cold mail (UWG § 7). Test: letters to 40 commercial brokers (10 each Cologne, Munich, Stuttgart, Dusseldorf) with a real sample week without firm names; 149 EUR deposit for the first month, start at 2 booked regions, else full refund. Precondition: Perplexity on § 34d GewO (tipster), register portal terms and Art. 14 GDPR; "forbidden" = no send. Pass: at least 2 paid deposits from 40 within 7 days of delivery.
3. **Real-estate GmbH in liquidation: acquisition list only for brokers (repaired), 199 EUR/month, EV 239 EUR.** Weekly list per state of GmbH/UG in liquidation whose registered purpose is own real estate, with liquidator name, address, court and creditor-call date; the broker gets a sales mandate before the property reaches a portal. Sold only to brokers; terms forbid passing to buyers and self-purchase; no sales to investors or developers so we do not "prove a purchase opportunity" (§ 34c (1) no. 1 GewO). Model: US probate lists for agents [guess, unchecked]. Pre-stage 0 EUR: read 30 register extracts; under 10 of 30 with property, no ad. Test: honest pre-order page "start after legal check", 3 sample rows without personal names, 49 EUR refundable deposit on the first month. Pass: at least 2 paid deposits from 2 different broker firms in 7 days.
4. **Bounty per real owner reply through contact forms, 100 EUR per reply, EV 210 EUR.** US firms selling to trades, law firms or tax offices pay only for a real owner reply, sent through forms that provably deliver, autoresponders filtered. Test: pilot offer to 300 US vendors, rule for "positive" signed beforehand, replies via an address the client reads too, 300 EUR deposit for 3 replies, full refund if not delivered in 14 days; 15 EUR (domain). Before: Perplexity on CAN-SPAM for forms, state laws, captcha and the "pay per positive reply" competitor; only forms without captcha. Pass: at least 3 of 300 send the filled briefing and at least 1 pays.
5. **Silent disposals: industry feed nationwide for dealers and auction houses, 149 EUR/month, EV 150 EUR.** Used-machine dealers, industrial auction houses and gastro buyers pay per goods type for voluntary liquidations with subject and liquidator (the ~93 % in no insolvency feed). Competitor infobroker sells this only for insolvencies at 125 EUR per industry report. Gate (free): check 20 names for visible goods; stop if under 8 of 20 or under 6 rows per goods type per month. Test: free sample of 3 real cases, then 149 EUR prepaid for a month; about 100 calls; 20 EUR. Pass: at least 2 prepayments from 40 decision makers reached by day 7.
6. **Register watch for anti-money-laundering obliged firms: monitoring protocol, 149 EUR/month, EV 119 EUR.** Tax firms pay for continuous checks of their corporate clients for liquidation, § 394 deletion announcement, continuation and change of representatives, with a monthly protocol per § 10 (1) no. 5 GwG. In the UK, Companies House monitoring for AML-obliged firms is standard [guess]. Test: 40 letters to tax firms with GmbH focus, founding month at 149 EUR up to 300 firms, pay before first protocol. Pass: at least 1 paid month from a firm that already has GwG software, with uploaded client list and signed agreement per § 62a StBerG, by day 10. 0 payments and 0 lists = dead. (Caution from research: monthly register checks collide with the register portal ban on systematic pulls, and DATEV's GWP-Digital is in the market.)
7. **Logo license, consumer-test style, for email verifiers tested on German business addresses, 900 EUR/12 months, EV 105 EUR.** We test verification services on addresses with known outcome from our own craft mailing (delivered with human reply vs bounce); the winner pays for the test logo. Test: honest page "independent verifier test, Q4 2026", everyone tested, result not for sale; order form 900 EUR, 250 EUR refundable deposit; personal mail to 15 verifiers plus one reminder; 0 EUR. Pass: at least 1 of 15 pays the deposit or signs. Calls do not count. Before passing any addresses: clear privacy via Perplexity.
8. **Liquidation watch for marketplace sellers from the Bundesanzeiger (repaired), EV 40 EUR.** Mid-size EU marketplaces upload their seller list (firm, seat, register number); we match daily against our Bundesanzeiger archive of 32,846 liquidations and creditor calls and report each hit with source and an ambiguity flag; never query the register portal. Announced price 25 EUR per firm per year [guess]. Decides: does a marketplace pay for ongoing warnings although DSA Art. 30 only requires a one-off check? Test: free pilot via 60 LinkedIn requests. Pass: at least 1 real list upload or 2 booked calls.
9. **Exclusive auction per dissolution with machinery, 30 EUR per case, EV 36 EUR.** Each new dissolution with machines or forklifts goes for 48 h exclusively to one dealer, pay per case like trade lead portals but not shared with 3 to 5. Test: 10 dealers/recyclers get 2 expired and 3 current cases (region, goods type, no firm names); after payment firm and register number, no liquidator name, no notice text. Pass: at least 1 paid case by day 7. Note: needs an exception to LR's own rule against selling a single notice as the product.
10. **CONTROL (killed by pessimist 2): deletion look-back for collection agencies, 149 EUR/month, EV 60 EUR.** Collection agencies would run their debtor stock against our archive of deletion announcements so they stop paying enforcement against asset-less GmbHs. Killed because the sold core is a permanent archive of announcements from handelsregister.de explicitly as a substitute for the portal's 8-week window, which the register portal terms forbid ("systematic pulls to build, extend or update parallel full or partial registers are not permitted"). Control test: honest waitlist, binding pre-order at 149 EUR/month from 150 agencies, nothing charged before start. Pass: at least 2 pre-orders with real firm, contact and portfolio size.

### Probe runs and research (2026-09-19 and 2026-09-25)

- **Liquidation shield: your customer base against the Bundesanzeiger** (2026-09-19). This is Liquidationsradar, reinvented by the machine. v1 killed it as "occupied" (North Data Premium watchlists, Insolvenzmonitor.online list upload, Firmium watchlist, Creditreform monitoring, STP, Firmenkontor24, Insolvenz-Radar, Insolvenz-Kontor, the Unternehmensregister notification service, credit insurers Coface/Atradius/Allianz Trade). v2 let it live (EV 280 EUR). This case is why the proof rule exists.
- **Register data products ranking (research 2026-09-25, scored on price anchor x2, data coverage, reachability, time to first customer, max 25).** No idea killed; only forms the field reports themselves rejected.
  1. **Payday matching for collection firms with titled business claims, 21 points.** A GmbH in voluntary liquidation may only distribute after debts are paid or secured, else the liquidator is personally liable (§ 73 (1) and (3) GmbHG): for an agency with an old title that is the day money must exist. Insofy, InsolvenzTracker and Insolvenzmonitor read only insolvency notices and do not see these cases; Insolvenz-Radar already sells liquidations explicitly to collection agencies (Expert 999 EUR/month; Business 149), Insolvenzmonitor Enterprise 399, insolvenzradar.info 349.99. 1,859 registered collection agencies (BDIU 2025). Price 249 EUR/month up to 10,000 debtors, 149 up to 2,000. Test (0 EUR, ~3 h): write to 20 agencies that advertise long-term title monitoring individually, ask for three debtor names (not the list), answer by hand within 24 h with blocking-year end, then offer 249 EUR/month, no free month. Pass: at least 3 of 20 send names within 10 working days AND 1 pays within 30 days. Stop if 0 of 20 reply or 2 say "we have that via Creditreform or Insolvenz-Radar". Fix first: a matching bug where "P & W Immobilien GmbH" returned no hit.
  2. **Real-estate GmbHs in liquidation, off-market list for brokers, 19 points** (became run idea 3 above). Price 199 EUR/month per region with territory protection; anchors ZvgScout Enterprise 480 EUR net, owner leads 35 to 120 EUR, terminated contacts 150 to 600 EUR. Pre-measure: at least 9 of 30 recent cases show land/buildings above 0 in the last balance sheet, else grey.
  3. **"e.K. becomes a GmbH" for insurance brokers, 18 points** (became run idea 2). Anchors: handelsregister.ai Max 499 EUR/month (only tier with transformation signals), startupdetector 485, Riek 800 EUR per 1,000 new registrations, makler.de business leads 35 EUR each. Pre-measure: at least 30 of 50 random e.K. rows show a running business. Open: Art. 14 GDPR for owner names (show examples without names until solved).
  4. Liquidation add-on for large creditors / customer-base guard (18): best anchor (North Data Max 3,950 EUR/year for 5,000), but big firms buy through procurement and the measured hit rate was 2 per 205 names per year; belongs to LR as a price tier.
  5. Silent disposals feed (17): no data price over 200 EUR for this buyer type (became run idea 5).
  6. Liquidation data feed for small monitoring providers (17): fastest technically (anchors Firmium API 490, startupdetector API 695 EUR/month) but exactly the § 87b UrhG case; jumps to place 2 if the publisher agrees in writing.
  7. Register watch for GwG-obliged firms (16): good anchors (GWP-Digital 60 EUR per firm plus 25 EUR monitoring per year, KanzleiDrive 349, DS|GwG 220 EUR/month) but monthly register checks collide with the portal ban; DATEV in market since 2026-04-14.
  8. Merger alerts for IT system houses (16): type of conversion unknown (full-text pull 0 of 20 worked).
  9. Takeover instead of shutdown (14): the only monthly anchor is a tiny-business provider (Übernahme-Radar 300/833 EUR), DUB charges 2 %, min 10,000 EUR on sale; 76 searchers in DACH.
  10. KYB company file at fixed price (11): no monthly anchor, highest liability (wrong beneficial owner).

## 13. REJECTED: ideas killed with proof

- **Spam-folder counter-test: "your 10-of-10 certificate is worthless as proof"** (2026-09-19, stage 4, occupied). GlockApps (free plan 2 tests, Essential 59 USD/month: send your real campaign to 70+ seed addresses at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, AOL and business servers, inbox vs spam per provider, SpamAssassin score, blocklists), plus GMass, MailGenius, unspam.email, Instantly, Smartlead, Folderly, MailReach, deliverability audits by agencies, a German Hamburg-based audit, the Certified Senders Alliance. Got a control card (EV 468 EUR), never tested.
- **Fresh dissolutions with liquidator name for liquidation-balance providers** (2026-09-27, pessimist 2, § 87b (1) s. 2 UrhG, database right of the Bundesanzeiger publisher). Control card above.
- **Supplying § 394 deletion announcements to small insolvency watchers** (2026-10-04, pessimist 1): daily pulling all deletion announcements from the register portal, keeping an archive since 07-20 and selling it as a feed to resellers is building a parallel partial register, forbidden by the register portal terms (also LR's own ban no. 9).
- **Deletion look-back for judgment debtors** (2026-10-04, pessimist 2, same register portal clause). Control card above.
- **From the 2026-09-25 field reports (rejected forms):** running debt collection ourselves (needs registration with the Federal Office of Justice); trade credit insurance (insurance supervision); credit-score repair (licensed legal service); own credit bureau or score (Creditreform, SCHUFA, CRIF dominate inside customer software, high switching costs); reselling transparency-register extracts (access only for obliged parties or legitimate interest); mirror of the commercial register or own register API (portal terms forbid systematic pulls); debtor register (Schuldnerverzeichnis) as a source (ban on passing on); pure sanctions-list screening (same result free at the official German sanctions list service).
- **Calibration cases confirmed forbidden:** paying lawyers per referred client (§ 49b (3) BRAO); collection without RDG registration; VAT pre-returns as a non-tax-advisor (§§ 2, 5 StBerG). Occupied: forwarding rejected US injury cases (Attorney Share), expert-witness exclusion profiles (Expert Witness Profiler).
