# Vending machine: a gumball machine as a first business

In 2023, when I was 13, I tried the gumball machine idea. I was nervous about asking, but I pushed through it and got a gumball machine set up at the bakery in my school. The machine and the gumballs cost all my savings. **Result: 5 euros.** Each gumball was supposed to cost 20 cents, but the machine also gave out gumballs for 1 cent coins. It was one of many ideas that summer, and I jumped to a new idea every few days. Later I wrote one summary for all my 2023 tests: nothing worked, and together they cost me about minus 160 euros.

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
