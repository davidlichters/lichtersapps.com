# TikTok account plus your own course

**2023, the fake version.** At 13 I ran a TikTok account called get_rich_today and tried to sell a ChatGPT course on making money. I had never made money myself. It reached about 300 followers. Nobody bought it.

**2026, a fake persona again.** I built an Instagram fitness coach character with a weight loss story and income claims that were never mine. It cost me more than it brought in, and I shut it down.

**2025, the honest version.** In January 2025 I uploaded my first fitness TikTok as myself. My first sale came in October 2025: one guide for 25 euros. By January 2026 I had about 18,000 followers.

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
