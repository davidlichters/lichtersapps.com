# Data products: selling lists made from public data

In August 2026 I had the idea for "Liquidationsradar": read official notices in Germany about companies going bankrupt or closing, and tell nearby trade businesses, so they could buy equipment cheaply or win the customers. My first goal was 13 customers at 79 euros a month. With AI I built a system that checked over 9,000 small trade businesses for a working email address and sent emails automatically every 40 minutes. My mistake: I spent weeks on the machine and almost no time on two questions: does someone already sell this, and will one real person pay? **Result: 888 emails delivered, 10 clicks, 0 paying customers.**

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
