## 9. Contradictions between the area files, decided

- Shipping: D18 (files 01, 02, 06 against 04), because page, policy and all 29 ads said "Free US shipping".
- Switching off: D6 plus D7 (the old rule R4 only switches off earlier, never later).
- Scaling: D12 (file 02 ladder vs file 06 +20 %; "daily" rejected). Later overlaid by Timo's bump ladder (section 2) and the course-judge version (Part G).
- Pixel: A2 (the old dataset belonged to the old book portfolio).
- Budget: D3 (David's 200 USD at 15:10 beat 100 USD in the 14:25 path; then replaced by the warm-up budget at 19:35).
- Visa RDR: D27 (file 05 against 01).
- Reviews (decided 1 to 9 Oct for Porchnap): permanently only reviews of real Porchnap buyers of this bed. An imported list of 647 reviews from 1 Oct never went into the shop; reviews from other shops or other products are not Porchnap reviews; widget off until the first real one. A broad "use review data we provide" rule in Timo's workspace had no course source and did not enter the playbook. Note: David later delegated legal questions to a separate lawyer list for every shop; for any NEW shop follow that list, not this line.
