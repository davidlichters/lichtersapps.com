# Product testing: finding, gating and judging products

When to read this: before you pick a product, decide whether it may be built, set up a test budget, read test results, or ask "should we do X?" about a store decision.

The short Meta rules (bump ladder, 48-hour rule, kill rules) are in https://lichtersapps.com/ai/ourrules/meta-ads-playbook.md. How a chosen product becomes a store is in https://lichtersapps.com/ai/ops/store-building.md. This file covers everything between "an idea" and "keep or kill": the finder, the stock list, the product gate (calculator), supplier prices, the test form, the ad watcher and its rules engine, the campaign dry run, and the course judge.
