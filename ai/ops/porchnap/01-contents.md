## 1. Short story and timeline

- **Who:** Timo (David's partner, runs the Shopify store and presses all money buttons) and David. The AI worked as co-founder and operator. Goal set 29 Sep 2026: **200,000 USD profit with Porchnap by 31 Dec 2026, counted from 0 on 1 Oct 2026** (92 selling days, about 2,174 USD profit per day). Timo's dream goal: 1,000,000 USD revenue by 31 Dec 2026. All money in **USD** (Timo, 22 Sep: "always usd").
- **Before 30 Sep:** the same Shopify store was called "AmaVerde" and sold children's books (section 2). Timo declared the book product dead on 23 Sep ("it is dead"); never propose it again.
- **24 Sep:** domain porchnap.com registered (Porkbun). Name won a test against "houndiva": Porch Nap had 0 USPTO trademark hits; "Houndiva" collided with live US marks (PUP DIVA, DIVA DOG CLOTHING). Instagram/Pinterest @porchnap were taken, @theporchnap free. Logo hearing test: lowercase "porchnap" was read as "pork nap" by 7 of 36 people, so the logo is written PorchNap; in text the brand is "Porchnap" (small n).
- **27 to 30 Sep:** supplier quotes for a Christmas sleigh dog bed; store renamed to Porchnap; offer, pages, policies and ad drafts built; 6 playbook area files written from the courses.
- **1 Oct:** planned launch. **3 Oct:** full playbook; store rebuilt in the look of competitor Jingle Nap. **4 Oct:** David and Timo forbid any further pre-launch gates ("stop holding us up"). **5 Oct:** David's ad rules (section 6).
- **Through at least 13 Oct:** 0 orders, 0 USD profit; the ads never got running in a usable ad account (section 7).
- **Late Oct:** product research by scanning ecom videos for leaked dashboards (section 10).
- **From 1 Nov:** David builds **Curlmoon**, his own separate store, alone (section 11).

## 2. The predecessor: the book store (measured, Shopify and Meta, 22 to 23 Sep 2026)

Kept as history and lesson only. Timo, 29 Sep: forget all old numbers; they are never a benchmark, risk or comparison for the new shop.

- Revenue 7 Jul to 22 Sep: **10,320.11 USD from 214 orders** (48.22 USD per order). July 2,665.95 (59 orders), August 7,058.09 (143 orders), September to 22nd 596.07 (12 orders).
- 98.2 % of August revenue was one book ("The Curious Child's Encyclopedia"), 181 pieces, net 38.10 USD per piece (gross 63.97, 40.5 % discount), 1.29 pieces per order. Discounts overall: 6,528.94 USD of 16,830.43 gross (38.8 %).
- Ad spend all 8 Meta accounts: **7,087.48 USD** (68.7 % of revenue). Main account: 6,778.71 USD, 200 Meta purchases, CPA 33.89, ROAS 1.43. The encyclopedia campaigns: 4,175.61 USD, 134 purchases, CPA 31.16, ROAS about 1.61.
- Ad cost per Shopify order: 33.12 USD. This became Porchnap's "planned CPA" (an unmeasured assumption for the bed).
- Supplier cost: 2,317.37 USD (10.83 per order). Payment fee measured 5.83 %. Fixed costs about 97.40 USD/month.
- **Gross profit from ads and sales: +313.60 USD (3.0 % of revenue, 1.47 USD per order)**. After start-up costs (course, laptop and other) the store was at about **-3,375.69 USD**.
- Of 8 Meta ad accounts on 29 Sep, 6 were disabled, restricted or lacked business info. One business portfolio was restricted on 28 Sep for "created or used with an automation that doesn't follow our rules ... Account Integrity" (likely related to an AI reading Meta through a browser extension).
- Lessons: (1) a product that looked good "over all time" was dead in the last 14 days; only judge on the last 7 to 14 days. (2) A 1.43 to 1.61 ROAS with heavy discounts leaves almost nothing. (3) Count received money, never agreed money.

## 3. The product, prices and margins

**Product:** a Christmas "sleigh" bed for small dogs and cats, sold in 4 designs: Candy Cane, Reindeer, Santa, and a crocheted "Tree Cave". Store sold only size S at launch; M planned.

**Sizes (supplier Bonnie, 4 Oct):** S for animals under 4 kg (8.8 lb), M under 9 kg. The shop wrongly said "up to about 6 lb (3 kg)" for days; correct text: "under 8 lb (4 kg)". Always ask the supplier for dimensions, care instructions and multi-piece price in the first message, otherwise you have no size chart or FAQ.

**Landed cost (delivered duty paid, USA):** S 18.90 USD, M 28.30 USD (Bonnie, 27 Sep). Other agents quoted S 19.68 and 21.27, M 28.55. Tree Cave via agent John 25.67 USD (US shipping, duties, size unchecked).

**Prices (playbook D17):** 1 bed 69.99, 2 beds 119.00 (15 % off), 3 beds 167.98 (20 % off). No strike-through price. Hold until 20 purchases. Never discount the single bed. Competitor reference: Chewy "Sherry Kline" small 57.68 USD with free shipping, medium 89.99.

**Margin math (formula: contribution = price x 0.9417 minus landed cost; profit = contribution minus CPA 33.12):**

| Tier | Price | Contribution | Profit at CPA 33.12 | Gross margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 bed | 69.99 | 47.01 | 13.89 | 73.0 % |
| 2 beds | 119.00 | 74.26 | 41.14 | 68.2 % |
| 3 beds at 25 % off | 157.50 | 91.62 | 58.50 | 64.0 % (below the 67 % floor, so changed) |
| 3 beds at 20 % off | 167.98 | 101.49 | 68.37 | 66.2 % |
| M single | 89.99 | 56.44 | 23.44 | |

- Break-even CPA single bed **47.01 USD** (ROAS 1.49). Safety CPA for scaling 37.61.
- Lowering to 59.99 would leave 4.47 USD profit: no room. A price test at 79.99 only with data.
- Lesson: an early check computed M only at 59.99 and 69.99 and concluded "M loses"; at 89.99 M earns more per sale than S. Compute a variant at every sensible price before dropping it.
- Rule for the 3-pack: if the supplier's 3-bed package costs at most 47.25 USD landed, the 3-tier can go back to 25 % off (still 70 % margin). For the 2-pack the 70 % limit is 35.70 landed.
- **Post-purchase upsell (D20):** second bed 48.99 on the thank-you page only when exactly 1 bed was bought (69.99 + 48.99 = 118.98, never above the 2-bed price). Accepted: third bed 48.99. Declined: second bed 41.99 (40 % off). Buyers of 2: third bed 47.99. Contributions 27.23 / 20.64 / 26.29 USD with no ad cost. Lesson: an upsell discount must never undercut your own bundle (an early draft had 69.99 + 41.99 = 111.98 below 2 beds at 118.98).
- **Free gift:** "The Cozy Christmas Guide (PDF)" in the 2 and 3 tiers via Shopify's free Digital Downloads app, no value claim.
- **Later add-ons only when scaling:** optional shipping protection 2.99 (not preselected, with a real reship promise), paid gift box 4.99 if the supplier can do it.
- **Black Friday (D24):** 27 Nov 00:00 to 30 Nov 23:59 ET, 2 beds 111.98 (20 %), 3 beds 157.48 (25 %), single 69.99; profit per order 34.53 / 58.48.
- **Planning target:** 14.01 USD profit per bed means about 14,300 beds (about 1 million USD revenue) for 200,000 USD profit, about 155 sales a day. The bed only sells until the Christmas order cutoff (4 Dec 23:59 ET), so a second product was required for December.
- **Shipping decision:** USA only, 0.00 USD for every order (6 to 10 business days plus 1 to 2 processing). The course says never free shipping on the first tier (charge 4.99); we chose free because every page and ad already promised it, and a mixed state (page says free, checkout charges 4.99) is worse than either. 4.99 on the single bed is a test only after 20 purchases.
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back, customer keeps the bed, one refund per household; name it the same everywhere ("money-back", not "satisfaction"). Write guarantee sentence and refund policy together; an early page promised "if your dog does not love it we will make it right" while the course template refund policy only took back unused goods.
- **Deadline:** "Order by December 4, 11:59 PM ET for Christmas delivery in the US" only after the supplier confirms in writing (4 Dec + 12 business days = 22 Dec). Shipping page and cutoff must carry the same math: a 2 + 10 business-day promise means last Christmas order 8 Dec, not 10 Dec.

## 4. Suppliers and agents

Supplier contacts and quotes are also on [suppliers.md](https://lichtersapps.com/ai/suppliers.md).

- **Bonnie** (China agent, WhatsApp): cheapest for the sleigh bed. Ships in 6 to 10 days, vacuum packed. 2 beds ship as **separate parcels** (2 to 3 parcels per bundle, otherwise oversize); add an FAQ line about multiple parcels. **No returns**; replacement or refund only for damage or loss. Daily capacity depends on factory stock, no fixed number. Gives no fixed shipping date and announces price increases only 2 to 3 days ahead (instead of 7). She knows us as "David", not Timo: before sending any supplier text, check in the chat export which name they last used.
- **Bonnie asked repeatedly for Shopify collaborator access** (orders and fulfilment). Refused: such access is store-wide, shows names, addresses and sale prices of all customers, and "fulfill and ship orders" allows changing delivery addresses. Better: Shopify's free "Custom order fulfillment" (Settings > Shipping and delivery) emails the supplier only name, address and items per order. Before refusing any access request, look for the built-in Shopify way that covers the real need.
- **Four objective warning signs of supplier failure** (instead of re-asking every time): a paid order exceeds the processing window; tracking stays "Pending"; over 7 days without tracking number and no explanation; stock, processing and communication unreliable at the same time. Only then escalate.
- **John** (agent, JNY): backup; Tree Cave design; sleigh bed S 21.27, M 28.55. **Adam** and **Joel (YYSH)**: further agents. **The Course D coach is not a supplier** (David, 29 Oct): a WhatsApp group name containing his name misled the AI into sending him a price request. Supplier questions go to the agents in individual chats, or Timo checks in AutoDS.
- Rule: every supplier answer with conditions goes immediately, verbatim, into the supplier register, not only into a reminder line.
- Payment to Chinese suppliers: Wise was researched; the decision stayed with Timo (money button).

## 5. The shop

- **Platform:** Shopify (Basic plan has 0 staff accounts; staff logins start at Grow). Theme: Elixir (ecomelixir.com, 200 USD license), later a copy named "glamorous-style" rebuilt in the look of competitor Jingle Nap (3 Oct, see skill "shop nachbauen"). Domain porchnap.com pointed to Shopify.
- **Apps:** Kaching Bundles (quantity tiers, 1 bed preselected), Track123 (tracking page; showed the old store name for a while), Judge.me (reviews), Digital Downloads (gift PDF), Disputely (chargeback app, planned before first budget raise), a post-purchase upsell app (Zipify OCU in the course, Selleasy planned), AutoDS (removed to cut mobile load time).
- **Markets:** USA only (Canada, UK, Australia deliberately off, 3 Oct). Empty shipping rows for those countries are intentional, not a bug.
- **Checkout:** no PayPal at launch (course: PayPal holds money), re-decide after 30 orders; Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay cover express checkout. Remove PayPal and Discover icons from the footer.
- **Mobile speed:** Lighthouse mobile LCP was 9.5 s (score 43, render delay 5.94 s). Target under 4 s. Video in a product gallery with autoplay loads fully on page load (2 x 1 MB) even on slide 2: use preload="none" and play on slide change.
- **Email:** support@porchnap.com via registrar forwarding; DKIM and DMARC were never set (David forbade reminders about it). Registrar forwarding supports up to 20 named forwards free, no catch-all.
- **SEO/link previews:** if "Homepage title" and "Meta description" are empty, the theme puts the store name everywhere (og:title, twitter); after a rename every ad link preview shows only the name. Fill both. This field cannot be set by API; only a human in Online Store > Preferences.
- **Policies:** written to match the US FTC Mail Order Rule (16 CFR 435): if an order is not shipped in time the customer gets all amounts including shipping back; that also applies to buy-2 discounts. Read all policies side by side: shipping policy granted the legal cancel right while the refund policy said "can no longer be cancelled after 12 hours". Privacy text may only promise what Shopify or Meta guarantee in writing. "Taxes are included" on the page while US sales tax was on in checkout: always check page promises against checkout settings, not only numbers against numbers.
- **Reviews:** for Porchnap only real reviews of buyers of this bed; widget off until the first one. Judge.me had THREE separate embeds in the theme (app embed in settings, an orphan section, and an active product-template section pulling 649 foreign reviews at 4.78 stars); switching off the app embed did nothing. Check every place with a text search for "judg" in the product template. App-embed switches fall back to the app default on every theme change; re-measure, never trust an old "done". For new shops the lawyer list on this site decides review questions.

## 6. Meta ads

- **Accounts:** old book ad accounts mostly restricted. A fresh business portfolio ("BM3") was to run Porchnap with its own dataset/pixel (niche change books to dogs requires a new pixel). David and Timo handled BM3, card, account limit, page, pixel connection and domain verification themselves and **forbade the AI to ever mention, remind or escalate them** (4 Oct 14:19 to 14:24). The AI's own Meta connection only saw a private ad account in EUR with no business and no payment method; changing currency in Meta creates a new account with a new ID.
- **Campaign build (playbook A10, D2, D3, area file 02):** warm-up engagement ad 20 USD budget, off at 8 to 10 USD spent; image CBO "date + product + BILD cbo", Sales, Advantage campaign budget, Highest volume, objective Purchase, Maximize number of conversions, attribution 7-day click + 1-day engage-through (no view-through, cannot be changed after publish), Advantage+ audience, USA, 18 to 65+, all genders, Advantage+ placements; 3 ad sets = 3 concepts (benefit "The coziest spot under the tree", deadline, two beds "One for your dog. One to gift."), 4 images each (one per design), 4:5 plus 9:16 via placement asset customization; each creative its own ad, single image/video, multi-advertiser ads off, all Advantage+ creative enhancements off, 2 primary texts, 2 headlines, 1 description, CTA "Shop now", link to the product page with UTM. Separate video CBO (videos would starve images in the same budget). Columns: spend, CPM, link clicks, cost per link click, link CTR, add to cart, cost per ATC, checkouts, cost per checkout, purchases, cost per purchase, ROAS, frequency, delivery.
- **Bulk import gotchas:** Meta's bulk import does not reliably attach images by file name (only "Image Hash" works) and does not set the 9:16 placement asset, so 12 image ads are better built by hand with Duplicate. "Review and publish" lists ALL drafts of the account; deselect unwanted ones. Shopify's "Facebook & Instagram" channel does not guarantee that a newly created dataset is linked to the ad account (then it is missing in the ad set picker).
- **David's ad rules (5 Oct 2026, apply to every shop):** never write "for small pets"; A/B test ads that say only "dog" against ads that say only "pet", never mixed; never mention the order deadline ("Order by Dec 4") because a deadline delays the purchase; never mention shipping or "free shipping" anywhere in ads. Bottom-of-funnel image ads: 5 for pets, 5 for dogs. Check the rendered text of every creative against these rules; old creatives made before the rules are not automatically compliant.
- **Added 2 Nov 2026 (Curlmoon, every shop):** professional magazine-quality photos only; no sticker boxes, no small "cheap" fonts, no customer phone photos as ads (they belong in reviews); clean large Apple-like type. Put each ad next to the competitor winners and ask whether David would call it professional. 5 Nov: if an ad is removed, the replacement comes first; never drop below the course minimum of 12 creatives. The dog in the image must fit the largest bed size. 6 Nov: video ads allowed, made with AI from real product photos only (no own footage); check every clip that the bed stays identical to the real photos; same product means same size (four "giant bed" videos were once shown as our 40 to 70 cm bed).
- **Creative production lessons:** never upload a competitor video 1:1 (the AI once flagged our own TikTok clips as foreign; check ownership before judging). "ChatGPT with product photo attached" redraws and falsifies the product in about 7 of 10 cases: keep the product as a cut-out layer and let AI make only the empty scene. Two supplier photos blended with multiply show a seam where white edges overlap: soft mask per photo edge. 9:16 headlines need the same font size as 4:5 at equal width. A percentage in an ad image must be re-measured on the live checkout right before upload (the bundle discount changed from 15 to 20 % within 17 minutes once). A new shop wording ("pet" instead of "dog") applies retroactively to all old creatives: search all build scripts and the CSV.
- **Course comparison numbers used:** test with 10 to 15 creatives minimum (target 21), Ad Library check (skip if 2 to 3 competitors run the same creatives), decide on day 3, emergency brake per single ad at 94.02 USD with no purchase (141.03 with documented exception), product verdict at 173.41 USD with 0 purchases, retargeting not measurable at launch budget (10 USD/day at 22 USD CPA = 6 purchases in 14 days; always compute budget x 14 / CPA against 20 purchases before giving a module its own ad set).

## 7. Results (honest)

- **Porchnap revenue since 1 Oct 2026: 0 orders, 0 USD profit** (measured via Shopify GraphQL repeatedly up to 13 Oct; the newest order was still an old book order from 19 Sep). The AI never got confirmation that the Porchnap ads ran in a working account.
- **Why it stalled (our reading):** (1) the launch depended on account work only the two founders could do, and ad accounts kept getting restricted; (2) weeks of pre-launch perfection (A-gates, tests, tickets) before David struck all gates on 4 and 5 Oct: being slow is course mistake no. 1; (3) the shop-publishing lane stopped on 4 Oct and 18 to 44 finished fixes piled up unpublished; (4) supplier messages lay unsent 5 to 9 days; (5) the automation produced escalations and reminders instead of sales (section 9).
- **What was proven:** the product math, a full course-checked playbook, a working research pipeline, and how to change a Shopify store fully via API.
