# Porchnap Playbook (complete, with every rule ID and course source)

**When to read this:** before you launch, test, kill, scale or run Q4 for any one-product dropshipping store on Shopify plus Meta ads, especially a China-shipped seasonal product sold to the USA. This is the rulebook David and Timo built for Porchnap (Christmas dog and cat beds, autumn 2026), checked line by line against the courses. Rule IDs (A1, B1, D15 ...) are the ones used in all our chats and files.

## Short version: our Meta ads playbook (moved here from ourrules/meta-ads-playbook.md on 2026-11-07)

> The rules we actually run our US dog-bed stores with, October and November 2026. Every rule was checked against the courses before it went in; rules marked "own" are ours and have no course source. Numbers are in USD.

Words: CPA = ad cost per purchase. ROAS = revenue divided by ad spend. CBO = a campaign that splits its budget across ad sets itself. ABO = budget set per ad set. Concept = one message ("the coziest gift"). Creative = one image or video.

### The numbers everything hangs on

- **Break-even first.** Before any ad runs, compute the CPA at which a purchase stops making money. For our single bed (69.99) that was a CPA of 47.01, a ROAS of 1.49. Above it, every sale costs money.
- **Every offer tier keeps at least 67% gross margin** (price minus product and shipping, before ads). Below that, ads eat the profit.
- **Plan CPA is a guess until 20 purchases.** We planned with 33.12, which left 13.89 profit per single bed. Real numbers only count after 20 purchases.
- **Do the honest math early.** At that margin, 1,000 a day in ads makes about 419 a day profit. Big goals need more profit per order (bundles, post-purchase offers), not just more budget.

### Start: warm-up and first budget

1. A brand-new ad account gets a warm-up: wait 1 to 2 hours after creating it, run one engagement ad at about 20 a day in the US with one image, stop it at 8 to 10 spent, only then start the sales campaign. Wait 1 to 2 hours after any business-settings change.
2. Account spend: day 1 about 10 to 20, day 2 about 20 to 30. Normal campaigns from day 3. Never a high budget on day 1.
3. From day 3: up to 3 CBO campaigns at 50 a day each, one concept per campaign.
4. Structure: one concept per ad set, 4 images per ad set (benefit, deadline, two-pack). At most 3 CBOs. ABO only after 80 to 100 purchases.
5. Objective purchase, broad US, ages 18 to 65+, attribution 7-day click plus 1-day engaged view.
6. No gate before launch. Being too slow is the most common beginner mistake; we lost 2 days 15 hours on checklists before our first ad (own lesson).

### Reading results

- **First 48 hours: touch nothing** except technical problems (rejected ad, broken checkout, purchases not tracked).
- **Decide only on purchases and CPA.** The other numbers are for finding faults: CPC around 1 is good, from 2 bad; link CTR target 3 to 7%; under 1% after 3 days with no add-to-cart means replace the creative.
- **Kill an ad set** after 72 hours with no purchase or a CPA above break-even; launch a new concept as a new ad set instead.
- **Emergency brake before 72 hours:** only at 0 purchases and twice the break-even CPA spent in the ad set (94.02 for us).
- **Kill a product** after 7 days of traffic with 0 purchases: all ads off, rework the page.
- **Fatigue:** frequency from 2.5 means a new concept, not more budget.
- **Funnel check after 7 days:** find out which half is weak (ad or page) and work only on that half.
- **Product ideas only from the last 7 to 14 days of numbers,** never from all-time totals. We almost restarted a dead product because its all-time ROAS looked fine while the current month was dead.

### Scaling: the bump ladder (never delete)

- Start at 50 or 100 a day.
- Raise the budget **only at ROAS 2.0 or better**, and only at 17:00 or 05:00 New York time.
- Ladder from 50: 50, 75, 110, 150, 200, 275, 350, then +25% per raise.
- Ladder from 100: 100, 150, 200, 275, 350, then +25% per raise.
- The +25% rule starts at about 200 to 300 a day. Doubling under 500 is the upper limit, never more.
- Profit falls after a raise: one step back and hold 48 hours. CPA above break-even 2 days in a row: one step back.
- Never raise every day; daily +20% resets learning.
- Scale big only at about 100 profit a day, 3 days in a row. Stuck at 50: add new ads, keep the budget.
- Safety margin: CPA at most 80% of break-even (37.61 for us, own). Cash in the bank at least 6.3 times the next daily budget (own). Never scale on credit while not profitable.
- Profit test before doubling: (budget / CPA) x (break-even CPA minus CPA) must grow.
- Vertical first (budget), then horizontal (new campaigns). Images at about 30% of the video budget.
- Advertorials, listicles and whitelisted pages only from 500 to 1,000 a day.

### Creatives

- Start with at least 12 creatives, aim for 21 (12 images, 9 videos).
- A new hook on an old image is not a new creative. Never use someone else's video 1:1; cut and mix.
- Image layout: headline on top, product in the middle, no price.
- Refill Monday, Wednesday, Friday: one new concept each time as a new ad set (4 to 8 images or 3 to 5 videos), never into a running ad set. Test lane 15 to 20% of budget.
- When an ad goes off, the replacement goes live first. The minimum count never drops.
- Our own ad rules: magazine-quality photos only, clean large type, no sticker boxes, no customer phone photos as ads (they belong in reviews). Say only "dog" or only "pet" per ad, never "small pets". No deadline and no shipping claim in ads; both slowed instant buying for us. AI videos made from real product photos are fine if the product stays exactly the same, including its visible size.

### Offer, price, shipping

- Prices 69.99 / 119.00 / 167.98 for 1 / 2 / 3 beds, no strike-through price, hold until 20 purchases, never discount the single bed.
- Free US shipping on every order. A shipping fee on the single item only as a test after 20 purchases.
- Post-purchase offer: second item at 48.99, if declined 41.99, never more than the bundle. A timer only if the offer really disappears.
- 30-day money-back guarantee, customer keeps the item; a line under the buy button. Refund rather than risk a chargeback.
- Trust: only real deadlines, no fake timer, no fake "sold out", only real reviews.
- Ads go straight to the product page; description tab open; check it on an iPhone. Mobile load (LCP) under 4 seconds; remove heavy apps.
- Offer test only after 20 purchases: 1 item vs 2 items preselected, winner by profit per visitor.
- Checkout without PayPal at first, decide again after 30 orders.

### Daily operations

- Morning: orders older than 12 hours go to the supplier, paid. One line into the profit sheet (spend, CPM, CTR, CPC, carts, purchases, revenue, cost of goods, fees, profit).
- Tracking numbers in by midday. Inbox three times a day, answer the same working day.
- Parcels without a new scan: write the customer before they ask.
- Supplier: tracking within 48 hours; after 72 hours inform the customer and check a backup supplier. From 10 orders a day, one week of stock.
- Chargebacks under 1%, a dispute tool before the first budget raise, answer each case the same day with evidence.
- Second product: from week 2, 1 to 2 tests a week, same buyer, stop at 300 spent without a sale (own).

### Q4 and Black Friday

- From late October: gift message in store and ads, without a discount.
- After 2 to 3 weeks of traffic: retargeting with 5 to 6 offer images.
- By mid-November: written supplier confirmation of capacity for Black Friday week.
- By the Friday before Black Friday: put a banner on the 2 to 3 best creatives; no new ones. Schedule the campaign in the ad account's time zone.
- Black Friday to Cyber Monday: bundle discounts only, single item at full price. Read every 4 hours: +20% per interval, double only at ROAS 2.0+, CPA above break-even means back to the day's start budget.
- Order cutoff for Christmas only after 5 real deliveries were measured and the longest took at most 12 days.
- January: only a capped test (for example 100 a day for 7 days). CPMs in January were 16 to 24% lower than December in three winters in a row, but cheaper reach is not demand.
- Value rules: none at launch. From 50 purchases, read breakdowns; a group with 10+ purchases and CPA above break-even gets -50%. Remove the rule after 7 days if CPA rose without more profit.

### The 10 beginner mistakes we made or almost made

1. Too slow to launch. 2. Wrong pixel, so Meta learns nothing. 3. Too few creatives, concepts mixed in one ad set. 4. Counting a new hook as a new creative. 5. Promises you can't keep (shipping times), which bring chargebacks. 6. Turning everything off after one red day. 7. Not measuring profit daily. 8. Raising budget daily. 9. Scaling without chargeback protection. 10. Judging a product on all-time numbers.

### Where we stand

As of 6 November 2026: average ROAS about 1.46, best ad 1.61; our rules need 2.0 to raise budget and 2.5 to 3 to make real money. No profitable month yet. The bottleneck is creative supply (of 55 images, 12 were running) and profit per order, not the budget.
