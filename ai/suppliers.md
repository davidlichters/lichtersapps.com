## How we pay suppliers safely: the rule (moved here from ops/gaps-2-1.md)

Sources: payment rule set 2026-10-04 15:10 after a deep Perplexity research, extended 2026-10-09 00:00 after a second research, corrected 2026-11-02. Applies to every agent, including John.

1. **Pay per order only after the customer has bought.** Never a deposit, never prepay stock no customer has bought. (Suppliers' 30/70 terms are for bulk buying, not our model.)
2. **First payments small:** only the first real orders, at most about 100 USD [guess]; the next batch only after tracking numbers have arrived for the previous one.
3. **Payment method, in this fixed order:**
   a. PayPal Goods and Services (PayPal invoice) or card: reversible. Card fee about 1 to 3 % (Chase, 2025-10-20).
   b. Otherwise Wise to a **company** account. Example fee USD to CNY: 52.21 USD per transfer (Wise, 2026-03-26), so bundle small amounts.
   c. Payoneer only to a confirmed company account (0 USD from Payoneer balance; by card up to 3.99 % plus 0.49 USD, Payoneer 2026-09-03).
   Switch from (a) to Wise Business or Payoneer ACH only after delivery is proven. Since 2026-11-02 our standing ask to agents is "we pay each order by PayPal invoice or card, not Wise".
4. **Before the first Wise or bank payment, require:** company name in Chinese, the 18-digit USCC (Unified Social Credit Code, China's company number), a photo of the business licence, and account holder identical to the company name. Contract party = invoice issuer = payee.
5. **Identity check for a new agent:** legal name, an invoice, account holder = company, and a call-back through a second channel (not the chat that sent the bank details). Have the DDP chain shown: destination, importer of record, customs representative, shipping line.
6. **Stop immediately on:** a private account, crypto or Western Union, 100 % prepayment with time pressure, changing account or company data, refusal to give the company number. Never PayPal Friends and Family. A new bank account is never confirmed only through the message that announces it.
7. **New agent = pilot:** about 30 to 50 shipments with a daily check of deadlines and tracking, scale only after that.
8. Who presses the button: every payment is pressed by a human (David or Timo); the AI prepares amount, payee and order numbers.
