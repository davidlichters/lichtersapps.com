# The research behind Lichters

Friends, Training and Sleep rest on strong research. Offline and Eating rest on good research, but it observes people and does not prove cause. Business is mostly judgment, and this page says so.

"Sure" means large studies agree. "Medium" means good but fewer studies. "Judgment" means it fits the research, but nobody tested this exact rule.

## Friends: sure

- **Harvard Study of Adult Development.** People with the warmest relationships stayed healthy and lived longest. Those happiest in their relationships at 50 were the healthiest at 80. Quality mattered more than number. https://weforum.org/agenda/2016/01/what-one-of-the-worlds-longest-studies-tells-us-about-happiness and https://bigthink.com/?p=574116
- **Rohrer and others, 2018.** In a German sample, people who planned social goals were happier a year later. Non-social goals changed nothing. https://www.diw.de/de/diw_01.c.779088.de/s_10732.html
- **Hall, 2018.** About 50 hours makes a casual friend, about 90 a friend, over 200 a close friend. https://news.ku.edu/2018/03/06/study-reveals-number-hours-it-takes-make-friend

## Training: sure

- **Ortega, 2012.** Over a million Swedish boys aged 16 to 19: the strongest had a 20 to 35% lower risk of dying before 55 and fewer psychiatric diagnoses. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3502746/
- **Noetel, 2024.** Exercise works so well against depression that it can count as a core treatment, more so when it is harder. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-075847
- **Recchia, 2023.** Exercise lowered depression symptoms in children and teens, a small effect. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9857695
- **White, 2019.** From about 120 minutes in nature per week, people more often reported good health and wellbeing. https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2019/06/190613095227.htm
- **World Health Organization.** Ages 5 to 17: on average 60 minutes of movement a day, hard and strengthening on at least 3 days.

## Sleep: sure

- **US sleep doctors (AASM).** Ages 13 to 18 need 8 to 10 hours, from 18 it is 7 to 9. https://depts.washington.edu/dbpeds/pediatricsleepdurationconsensus.pdf
- **Roenneberg.** The body clock gets later until about 20, then earlier again. https://www.nature.com/news/2004/041229/full/news041229-5.html
- Too little sleep raises the risk of depression in teens. https://sleepreviewmag.com/sleep-health/demographics/age/sleep-key-help-teens-recharge-success-school-year/
- **Axelsson, 2010.** After too little sleep, faces were rated less healthy, less attractive and more tired. https://news.ki.se/a-good-nights-sleep-the-key-to-being-attractive

## Offline: medium

- **Hunt, 2018.** Students who cut social media to about 30 minutes a day felt less lonely and less depressed. This is where the 30 minutes comes from. https://doi.org/10.1521/jscp.2018.37.10.751
- **Castelo and others, 2025.** Two weeks without mobile internet improved mental health, wellbeing and measured attention. https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf017

## Eating: medium

- **Aune and others, 2017, International Journal of Epidemiology.** A review of 95 studies: about 800 g of fruit and vegetables a day went with a roughly 31% lower risk of dying early. That is about 10 handfuls, so 5+ is the start. https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw319

This study observes people, so it shows a link, not proof. Water instead of soda is judgment, not tested here.

## Business and money: judgment

- **Killingsworth, Kahneman and Mellers, 2023.** For most people happiness keeps rising with income. https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/does-money-buy-happiness-heres-what-the-research-says/
- **Ruberton, 2016.** Savings you can reach predicted life satisfaction beyond income and debt. https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1519781
- **Dittmar, 2014.** People who make money and things their life goal are less happy on average. https://research.brighton.ac.uk/en/publications/the-relationship-between-materialism-and-personal-well-being-a-me/
- **Azoulay and others, 2020.** Founders of the fastest-growing firms were 45 on average, and industry experience predicted success. https://www.ipr.northwestern.edu/our-work/working-papers/2018/wp-18-11.html
- **Gilovich, 2000.** For the scary part of business, asking: students in an embarrassing T-shirt guessed half the room noticed. Really it was about a quarter. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.78.2.211
- **Sandstrom and others, 2022.** A week of talking to strangers lowered fear of rejection, and it lasted. https://sussex.figshare.com/articles/journal_contribution/Talking_to_strangers_a_week-long_intervention_reduces_psychological_barriers_to_social_connection/23490227

No study shows "5+ hours a day on your own business as a teen" makes you happier. It is a bet.

## Attitudes

- **No substances: sure.** Self-control in childhood predicted health and wealth at 32, and early smoking was one of the teen traps. https://dunedinstudy.otago.ac.nz/news-and-events/2011/children-with-more-self-control-turn-int Drinking before 14 led to dependence in 47%, from 21 in 9%. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16818840/ Teen cannabis went with 37% higher odds of depression. https://www.oxfordhealth.nhs.uk/orka/title/association-of-cannabis-use-in-adolescence-and-risk-of-depression-anxiety-and-suicidality-in-young-adulthood-a-systematic-review-and-meta-analysis/
- **Ticks first: sure.** "When X, then Y" plans clearly help people reach goals. https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f
- **Start early, keep going: medium.** A habit took about 66 days to become automatic, and one missed day barely mattered. https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2009/aug/how-long-does-it-take-form-habit
- **Ignore the crowd: judgment.**

## Why no meditation habit

**MYRIAD:** mindfulness lessons in 85 schools (about 8,400 teens) did no better than normal lessons. https://arc-sw.nihr.ac.uk/?p=39416
