# Print on demand: selling your own T-shirt designs

In summer 2023, when I was 13, I started selling printed T-shirts. I had no target group, no sample order and no plan for how long to test. Three days later I was on the next idea. **Result: 0 sales.** No sale is written down anywhere.

**Why it didn't work:** the only reason was that I had very little experience overall. This model can still work today. Just because it didn't work for me back then doesn't mean it can't work now. With my experience today, I'm convinced almost any of these would work for me.
