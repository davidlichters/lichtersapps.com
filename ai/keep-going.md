# Keep going

When you want to quit, look at months, not days. One bad day barely matters. Stopping for a year does.

## What it is

Lichters shows each habit as a year grid: 7 rows for the weekdays, about 52 columns for the weeks. One tick is one small square, so a single day looks like nothing. That is the point.

After a few months the grid shows your real average. 80% filled with a few gaps means you have the habit.

## Why

**Habits take about two months, not 21 days.** Phillippa Lally at University College London found it took about 66 days on average until a new daily habit felt automatic, under 20 for some people and over 200 for others.

**Missing one day does almost nothing.** The same study found that skipping a single day did not really hurt the habit. Missing often or stopping for long stretches did.

**That is why there is no streak that falls to zero.** A streak says one missed day erased everything. That is false, and it makes people quit. The grid keeps every tick.

**If everything has felt pointless for weeks, or you think about hurting yourself,** talk to a trusted adult today or call a crisis line in your country. That comes before any habit.

## How to start

1. **When you miss a day, tick the next one.** Never miss two in a row if you can help it. Missed three days? Do not restart from zero. Tick today.
2. **Make the bad-day version tiny.** Sick or tired? Training becomes a 30-minute walk. Business becomes 20 minutes on the smallest task.
3. **Zoom out once a month.** Is this month greener than last month?
4. **Write down why you missed.** Same excuse every time? Fix that one thing.
5. **Change the method, not the habit.** If your business idea earns nothing for months, switch the idea. Keep the 5+ hours a day.

## Mistakes

- **Restarting from zero in your head.** One missed day is a 1-day gap, not a new day 1.
- **Waiting until you feel motivated.** A fixed time works on the days motivation does not show up.
- **Quitting the habit because one project failed.** The person who keeps working daily gets better at picking the next one.
- **Chasing shortcuts when you are frustrated.** That is when "easy money" ideas look good. They are usually scams.

## What happened when I tried it

My honest record is mostly a list of things that did not work. Since 2022, when I was 12, I tried:

- Minecraft YouTube (Oct 2022 to Apr 2023). One video got 100,000 views.
- Fiverr, affiliate marketing, printed T-shirts, a bubblegum machine, Amazon Influencer, selling tennis balls. All in 2023, most for a few weeks. Together about 160 EUR down.
- Two bad ideas: a fake-persona TikTok selling a ChatGPT course, and "scamming scammers".
- Fitness TikTok with a cheap guide (Jan 2025 to Nov 2025), then 1:1 coaching (Nov 2025 to Aug 2026).
- Ads through the contact forms of US companies (August 2026): 5,820 messages, 15 replies, 0 sales. That was spam.

From March 2024 to January 2025 my notes show no business project at all. School was hard and I stopped. Nothing moved for ten months. That gap is the biggest hole in my record.

My first sale came on Halloween 2025: 25 EUR for a training guide, about three years after my first try. In January 2026 I wrote that I had reached 0 of my goals for 2025: 18,000 followers instead of 100,000.

What kept me going was not a result. It was the daily tick. My best months were the ones with a green tracker, no matter what the money did.

The only time I really lost was the year I stopped.
