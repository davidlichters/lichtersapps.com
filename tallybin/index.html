Tallybin

Update stock with plus and minus or a barcode scan, see what to reorder and count your inventory without paper. 50 items free, no account, no ads.

Support

Contact Support Privacy Policy Imprint

Questions or a problem? Write to support@lichtersapps.com. We usually answer within two days. Purchases are restored in the app under Settings, subscriptions are managed in the iPhone Settings under your name.

Features

All items grouped by location, with plus and minus right in the list
Hold a barcode or QR code in view: Tallybin finds the item and takes one out, adds one in, or just shows it
Log any amount as out, in or counted, with a note like “Order 1042”
Set a minimum stock for each item
Count list of all items by location
Name, SKU, barcode, location, minimum stock, unit cost and a note
