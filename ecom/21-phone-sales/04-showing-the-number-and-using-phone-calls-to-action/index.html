Showing the number and using phone calls to action

Section 21.4 · 4 of 26 in this chapter · 2 min read

In short

  • For expensive products, show a phone number. Many buyers want to talk to a person before they pay.
  • Test a "Call today" button against the normal buy-now button, starting with expensive products.
  • Keep the buy-now button as the default. Only change it if the test clearly shows a better result.
  • Always call back every missed phone call on the same day.

Why phone contact matters

  • For expensive products, a phone button can work better than the buy-now button for many buyers.
  • More sales from a phone button is called conversion (the share of visitors who buy).
  • Many buyers want to speak to a sales or customer service person before they commit.
  • Test (compare two versions and see which one sells more) "Call today" with the phone number in the text, against the buy-now button.
  • Keep the buy-now button as the default option.
  • Business buyers (B2B, selling to other companies) often want to call before they order.
  • They call to check stock or to ask for a volume discount (a lower price for buying in large amounts).
  • If you sell B2B, put a phone number on your website.
  • Visitors who call before ordering are usually close to buying.
  • Leaving the number off can lower conversion.
  • A visible phone number builds trust.
  • Callers are often ready to buy.
  • Older advice (2019 to 2022): Source A (2019) recommends a phone number at any price point.
  • This includes products with an average order value (the average amount spent per order) around $500.
  • Source B (2021 to 2022) limits the rule to expensive products.
  • For high-ticket products (expensive products), show the number prominently.
  • For lower-priced products, include the number anyway. It builds trust and does no harm.

Showing the number on the site

Header and contact page

  • Show a business phone number in the website header (the top bar of your site), under a "Call us today" heading.
  • High-ticket buyers want to speak to someone.
  • Google Shopping (Google's product ads service) and similar marketing channels require a visible phone number.
  • Keep the phone number on the website, even if you cannot answer every call.

Trade (B2B) page

  • B2B buyers usually want to talk on the phone and ask for bulk pricing (a lower price per unit when buying in large amounts).
  • Make a phone contact visible on the trade page (the page for business buyers).

Handling calls you cannot answer

Follow these steps when the store cannot answer every call:

  1. Keep the phone number on the website.
  2. Set up a voicemail greeting (a recorded message that plays when nobody picks up) that collects the caller's details.
  3. Return every missed phone call on the same day.
  4. Later, when the store earns enough to justify it, outsource phone support (hand it to an outside company).

Offering a choice of channels

  • When customers can choose between writing, calling or visiting a store, the phone option is the one most people use.
  • In one test, 70 percent of inquiries for a sample came by telephone.
  • Offer the phone option as a primary response channel (the main way you answer customers). People find it easier than writing.
