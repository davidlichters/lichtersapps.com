Cold calling local businesses and agency clients

Niche choice and target selection

  • Choose a niche (a group of customers with the same need) that has healthy budgets (plenty of money to spend), such as dentists.
  • Dentists are a local business type. They often do not think about ads or growth in business terms. This changes how the first talk must be led.

Cold email first

  • Send a cold email (an email to someone who did not ask for it) before you call. Then the business has already seen the agency's name when the call comes.
  • Keep the email short. Open with one specific observation about the business's current online presence (how they appear on the internet) or ads. Ask for a 10-minute call.

Call procedure

  1. Send a cold email first to introduce the agency.
  2. Call the business. If they are busy, remind them that the email was sent.
  3. Ask for 5 to 10 minutes to talk about their ads or online presence.
  4. Ask about their goals for the coming year, for example more clients, fewer low-value treatments, or more high-value services.
  5. Explain how running ads (paid online adverts) can help them reach those goals.
  6. Propose a meeting, ideally at the business's office, to close the sale (get the customer to say yes).

Opening the conversation

  • Use a shared-interest opener (a first sentence about something you both care about). For example, say that the business owner and the agency both sell a product that solves a customer problem. Then say the agency wants to help the business sell that product online.
  • Local business owners are often not thinking about ads or growth in business terms. So the operator (the person running the agency) must lead the talk toward growth. Explain why ads and product offers matter.

Leading the owner toward growth

  • Ask the owner about their goals for the coming year. Then link ads to those goals.
  • If the business already has an online shop, show a demo store (a sample shop built to show what can be better). Show an easier checkout (the page where people pay), relevant apps (add-on tools for the shop), and automated customer emails (emails the shop sends on its own). The gaps in their shop then become visible.
  • Quote a price based on what the client is able to pay.

Payment plan offers for expensive treatments

  • Offer local businesses a payment plan for expensive treatments, such as Invisalign (clear plastic braces). Customers pay monthly instead of one large sum.
  • Let a third-party installment provider (an outside company that splits a price into payments) take over collecting the payments. This reduces the risk for the business.
  • Example from a Swiss dentist's competitor: the offer was Invisalign on installments of about 60 francs per month, instead of a single payment of 3,000 to 5,000 francs. The installment provider paid the full amount to the dentist upfront (right away). It then collected the monthly payments. It charged late fees (extra charges for missed payments) if payments were missed.

Cold call and email scripts

Use this prompt to generate the scripts. Replace the bracketed placeholders before use.

Write me a cold calling script and a cold calling email script for my [type of agency, e.g. digital marketing agency] that offers [services, e.g. Facebook ad management and website design] to [target business, e.g. dentists] in [city]. The email should be short, open with one specific observation about the business's current online presence or ads, and ask for a 10-minute call. The phone script should be in the order it will be spoken and include: an opening line that introduces the agency and the reason for calling, one question about the business's goal for the next year, one sentence on how ads can help reach that goal, and a request for a 10-minute meeting at the business's office. Use simple, friendly language without jargon, and give me the script and the email as separate sections.

Lead magnet and monetisation

  • Offer a free sample of an improved website as a lead magnet (a free gift that brings in possible clients).
  • Sell ad management (running the client's paid ads for them) as an upsell (a bigger add-on sale) once the website is sold.
  • Charge a referral fee (payment for introducing someone) or a per-lead fee (payment for each new contact) for every client introduced.

Delegated influencer outreach

  • Influencer outreach (contacting online creators who have large audiences) can be delegated to an assistant. Use a cold-calling script and a course from the resources section.
  • In the speaker's case, outreach led to two influencer partnerships.
  • A DM (private message) alone rarely gets a response, because creators often do not see DMs. Combine the DM with an email and a comment on their latest video.
