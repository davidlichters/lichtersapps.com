Taking orders over the phone

Section 21.8 · 8 of 26 in this chapter · 3 min read

In short

  • Staff may take orders by phone and enter them on the website for the customer.
  • Phone orders can only be paid by credit card or bank deposit (money sent straight into the bank account).
  • Phone calls mainly help close big orders, like 10 items for an office, often with a discount.
  • A phone sale can be sent as a draft order (an unfinished order the customer completes), and it counts as a phone sale.

Staff authorisation and payment limits

  • Staff are authorised (allowed) to take customer orders over the phone. They place the order on the website for the customer.
  • Phone orders can only be paid by credit card or bank deposit (money sent directly into the company's bank account).

When phone sales matter

  • most sales come through the website, not the phone.
  • phone calls are used to close (finish) bulk orders, which are large orders of many items at once.
  • Example: 10 items for an office.
  • A discount (lower price) is offered.
  • The sale is then placed over the phone.

Taking the call

  1. Answer the customer's question about a product or stock (how many are available).
  2. If they want to buy, add the item to the cart (the online basket) in the store.
  3. Collect the customer's name, shipping address (where the goods go), billing address (the address linked to the payment) and payment method.
  4. Enter the details as if the customer were checking out (paying on the website), using their email.
  5. Process (handle) and follow up the order like any other online order.

The sources also give a fuller version of the call:

  1. Ask what the customer would like to buy.
  2. Open the website, put those products in the cart, then go to checkout (the payment steps).
  3. Collect the customer's contact details with them: name, email address, delivery address and phone number.
  4. Move on to the payment page and ask which payment method they prefer. Phone orders can be settled by credit card or by bank deposit.
  5. If the customer is paying by credit card, ask for the card numbers, type them into the checkout page, then click finish to complete the purchase.

Routes for entering the order

The sources describe two routes.

  • Phone quotes are sent as draft orders (unfinished orders the customer completes).
  • Direct orders are entered through the normal checkout.

Normal checkout

  • Take phone orders by entering them through the normal online checkout. Ask the customer for the same information as on the site.
  • No separate POS (point-of-sale, a cash-register system) is needed for this.
  • A phone-only order can be placed through the normal checkout in the back end (the admin area where staff manage the shop).
  • This makes the order record match a customer-placed web order.

Shopify draft orders

Creating an order for a customer by phone in Shopify (online shop software):

  1. Click "Create order" in the Orders section. This creates a draft order.
  2. Add the products the customer wants.
  3. Apply any discounts.
  4. Send the customer a link so they can complete checkout themselves.

Channel attribution

Attribution means which sales route (channel) gets credit for a sale.

  • Phone quotes are sent to customers as draft orders in Shopify.
  • When the customer buys from a draft order, the sale is attributed (credited) to the phone channel.
  • The sale is not credited to the online store, Facebook or Pinterest.
  • Because of this attribution, the phone sales commission (the pay owed for phone sales) can be tracked by channel.

Referral questions

  • Ask about referrals (customers sent to you by someone else) only if they are part of the sales workflow (the usual steps of a sale).
  • Referral status does not change the process.
