Choosing a business phone number and provider

Section 21.2 · 2 of 26 in this chapter · 3 min read

In short

  • Give each store one phone number that belongs only to the business.
  • A toll-free number starting with 800, 855 or 866 looks professional, and callers in the same country pay nothing.
  • Do not pay extra for a fancy number. A plain number with a toll-free start works just as well.
  • Prices in this text were correct when recorded, so check them again before you buy.

Why a separate business line

A business line is a phone number used only for the business. Keep it apart from your personal phone.

  • Business numbers signal professionalism to suppliers and customers. They also keep business calls apart from personal calls.
  • A dedicated business number (used only for business) gets less spam than a personal cell (mobile) phone number. Business calls are also easier to recognise and prioritise.
  • Use a business phone line and a business address on the store, not personal details. A business line also lets a phone team handle inbound (incoming) and outbound (outgoing) calls.
  • Each store needs one phone number that belongs only to the business. Show it on the contact page or in the header (the top strip of the website).

Toll-free numbers

A toll-free number is a phone number that callers can dial at no cost. It starts with a set of digits called a prefix.

A RespOrg (Responsible Organization) is a company allowed to assign these numbers. The FCC is the US body that sets the rules for them.

Prefixes and rules

Country Prefix Notes
US 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, 833 Each prefix is assigned by a Responsible Organization (RespOrg) under FCC rules.
Australia 1300 Equivalent of the US toll-free number.
  • Toll-free numbers starting with 800, 855 or 866 look more professional on store websites. They work especially well for stores aimed at suppliers.
  • Older advice (2019) lists 888 or 855 for customer calls. A phone line may be a Google number or a toll-free number. **
  • For callers in the same country, a toll-free number is free. International callers (from other countries) pay charges.
  • The provider forwards calls (sends them on) to any mobile or landline (home phone) you choose. Callers hear an ordinary phone call.
  • Toll-free numbers are portable: you can move one to a different provider without changing the number.

Vanity numbers

A vanity number is an easy-to-remember number, such as one that spells a word.

  • Do not pay extra for a memorable or vanity number if the number only appears on your website and in supplier emails. The digits after the prefix do not matter for this use. A plain number with a toll-free prefix looks just as professional.

Providers

Mighty Call / MightyCall

  • MightyCall is a business phone system, a service that handles business calls.
  • Enter the code dropship50off at checkout to get 50% off for the first 3 months.
  • Mighty Call provides a toll-free business number with an app. The app rings the owner's cell phone and transcribes (turns into written text) voicemails (recorded messages). It suits a single business number shown in the site header or on the contact page. (source 2022)
  • If Mighty Call does not work in a country such as Australia, Google Voice (a phone service from Google) is a workable alternative for a business phone line. (source 2022)

Grasshopper

  • Grasshopper (grasshopper.com) is a US option for toll-free and vanity numbers. It has desktop and mobile apps, call forwarding (sending calls on to another phone) and voicemail transcription.
  • Pricing at the time of recording: plans from $26 per month for a business line, and up to $75 off an annual plan.

Setup steps

  1. Open grasshopper.com and click Try Free.
  2. Go to Types of Numbers and select Toll Free Numbers.
  3. Pick a number: choose a random one or specify your digits, then choose a plan.
  4. Download the desktop and mobile apps.
  5. Configure your settings. The setup guides you through them.
  6. Place the number on your website and in supplier communication.

Zintel

  • For an Australian business number, Zintel (zintel.com.au) sells 1300 numbers for about $10 per month.
  • Sign-up takes under 5 minutes. There is no lock-in contract (a contract that stops you leaving early), and you can cancel at any time.

JustCall

  • JustCall costs about $20 per month and lets you buy numbers in over 70 countries. Calls are answered through desktop and mobile apps from anywhere.
  • Example: a US number can be answered from the UK or Thailand. This suits a business run from abroad.
  • At the time of recording, the provider advertised 70% off the first month.

Google Voice

  • Google Voice is a workable business phone line where Mighty Call does not operate, for example in Australia. (source 2022)
  • Google numbers are an accepted option for a phone line. [L960.043] **
