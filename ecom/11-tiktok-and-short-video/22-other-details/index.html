In short

  • Before you build a book, plan its structure, page count and a visual hook.
  • Connect your Shopify store to your own.com domain before you contact suppliers.
  • Shopify does not let you change server or CDN settings, so technical SEO has limits.
  • Test your store on a phone before you spend more on TikTok ads.
  • Test short video yourself. Do not assume it works.

Product before filming

  • Before building an original book based on a public method, define its structure, page count and a visual hook.
  • A visual hook is an eye-catching look or idea that works on camera.
  • A hook that films well makes the product usable in short video from the start.

Store setup before contacting suppliers

  • Before you contact suppliers, link your Shopify store to a purchased custom domain. Shopify is the software you use to build your online shop.
  • A custom domain is your own web address, for example a brand name.com.
  • Do not leave the store on the default myshopify.com subdomain. A subdomain is a smaller address that sits under a bigger one. Here it is the free address Shopify gives you.
  • A subdomain looks unprofessional when a supplier asks where you operate from.

Technical limits on Shopify

  • On Shopify, the merchant (the store owner) has no access to hosting settings. Hosting is the server that keeps your site online.
  • You also cannot manage or change content delivery networks. A CDN is a network of servers that delivers your pages quickly around the world.
  • So technical SEO work is limited to what the platform allows. Technical SEO means behind-the-scenes setup that helps search engines read your site.
  • Any optimization that needs server or CDN control is not possible on a hosted Shopify store. A hosted store runs on Shopify's own servers.

Testing before scaling

  • Test the store on a phone before scaling paid TikTok traffic. Paid traffic means visitors who arrive through your ads. Scaling means spending more once an ad works.
  • Traffic from TikTok is mostly mobile, meaning people using phones. So the mobile layout (how the page looks on a phone) and offer placement (where your deal sits on the page) drive most of the sales.
  • The call gives no short-video platform guidance. Do not assume short video will work without testing.

Local reach with TikTok's local feed

  • TikTok's local feed (a list of videos from people nearby) was being rolled out in the UK, France, Germany and Italy in the week of 29 June to 5 July 2026.
  • The local feed shows your content to users in your region.
  • This is useful for brick-and-mortar stores (shops with a real building people walk into) and for local events such as conventions (large gatherings of people with shared interests).
