Search retargeting (RLSA) and branded search

Section 16.12 · 12 of 24 in this chapter · 3 min read

In short

  • Retargeting shows ads only to people who already visited your website.
  • Returning searchers are close to buying, so give them higher bids.
  • Search retargeting had the best results: 9.4x return on ad spend, versus 3.5x for product listing ads.
  • Set the visitor list to keep people for the maximum of 540 days.
  • Do not split visitors by how recent their visit was.

Search Retargeting (RLSA) Overview

  • Retargeting means showing ads to people who already visited your website.
  • Retargeting can be combined with search campaigns (text ads that show when people type into Google) or Shopping campaigns (product ads with pictures and prices).
  • Then the text ads or Shopping ads run only for people who previously visited your website.
  • This lets you show different ads or offers to returning visitors than to cold traffic (people who have never visited your site).
  • A Performance Max campaign is a Google campaign type that runs automatically across many Google channels.
  • A branded search campaign is a search ad that uses your brand or product name as its keyword.
  • Google retargeting through a Performance Max campaign plus a branded search campaign caught people who saw a Meta ad (an ad on Facebook or Instagram) and then searched for the product name.
  • This Performance Max and branded search combination was described as the main retargeting channel once Facebook retargeting stopped working well for the account. (The "account" is your Google Ads account.)

Why Returning Searchers Convert Better

  • "Convert" means turning a visitor into a buyer.
  • Google search retargeting is very high converting, meaning many of these visitors buy. Returning visitors who search again are comparison shopping (comparing products and prices) and are close to a purchase.
  • Showing them special ads at this point catches them when they are most ready to buy.
  • People who visited earlier and are searching again are more relevant (a better match for your product) than cold searchers, who have never visited your site.
  • This is true even if the earlier visit was months ago.
  • Their earlier research, or money saved for a high-ticket item (an expensive product), makes them worth a stronger bid (the amount you are willing to pay for a click).
  • So do not split the audience (the group of people your ads target) by how recent the visit was.
  • Visitors who came back across several separate visits (multi-session visitors) were reported to convert 2.17 times more often than other visitors.
  • This is the basis for giving this audience higher bids and a wider reach (how many people see your ads).

Results Compared with Product Listing Ads

  • ROAS means return on ad spend: the sales earned for each unit of money spent on ads. A value of 9.4x means 9.4 in sales for every 1 spent.
  • Product listing ads are Shopping ads that show your products.
Campaign (same account) ROAS
Search retargeting 9.4x
Product listing ads 3.5x
  • In that account, search retargeting was the highest-converting campaign, so treat it as a priority.

Audience Settings

  • An audience is the list of people your ads target.
  • Set the membership duration (how many days a visitor stays on the list) of the website visitor audience used for search retargeting to the maximum of 540 days.
  • 540 days is the longest period Google keeps the cookie (a small file in the visitor's browser that remembers the visit).
  • Shorter time periods discard earlier visitors who are still searching.

Setup Steps

  1. Find the AdWords retargeting code in the back end (the settings area behind the main screen) of your Google Ads account. (AdWords is the old name of Google Ads.)
  2. Place that code on every page of the website. (The code is a small piece of tracking code.)
  3. Create a retargeting list of site visitors, meaning a list of people who visited your site.
  4. In Google Ads Editor (a Google program for editing campaigns), copy the existing search campaign.
  5. Add the retargeting audience to the copied campaign and upload the change (send it to Google).
  6. Expand the keyword list, including broad match (a setting where ads show for related searches, not only exact words), because visitors are already in market (ready to buy). A keyword is a word people type into Google. A coupon keyword is a search for a discount code. Coupon keywords are only worth targeting here if the store actually offers coupons.
  7. Rewrite the ad copy (the written words of the ad) for returning visitors, for example with a returning-visitor offer.
  8. Apply the same retargeting audience to the product listing ads.
